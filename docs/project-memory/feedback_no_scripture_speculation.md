---
name: No scripture speculation
description: When scripture doesn't explain something, report what it says without offering our own interpretation or filling doctrinal gaps.
type: feedback
---

When scripture leaves something unexplained (e.g., "seven demons" cast out of Mary Magdalene), report exactly what the text says and stop. Do not offer alternative interpretations, even well-intentioned ones like "probably physical and mental afflictions."

**Why:** While writing an article on Mary Magdalene, I tried to reinterpret the "seven demons" as physical/mental afflictions. The user corrected: "No tratemos de reinterpretar los siete demonios. Estaríamos especulando." The goal is to debunk Gregorio's false interpretation without substituting our own.

**How to apply:** In articles and corpus answers: (1) Quote what scripture says. (2) If a historical figure added meaning the text doesn't support, say that. (3) Do NOT fill the gap with a new interpretation. "Las escrituras no explican qué significaba" is a valid and complete sentence.
