---
name: Citation style rules
description: FCD citations must be inline (parenthetical), never isolated on a separate line; GA titles use highest known rank
type: feedback
---

Two citation style rules:

1. **References always parenthetical, never isolated.** A citation on its own line is wrong — it must be inline within or at the end of the paragraph it supports: `...contra los demás (Ezra Taft Benson, "Cuidaos del orgullo," Conferencia General de abril 1989).`

**Why:** An isolated reference line breaks reading flow and looks like a footnote without a footnote system.
**How to apply:** Every `(Author, "Title," Source)` must attach to the text it cites — either mid-sentence or closing the paragraph.

2. **General Authority titles use the highest known rank.** Benson → presidente Benson (was Church President). Bednar → élder Bednar (apostle). Nelson → presidente Nelson. If unsure, use the most commonly known title or the one held at the time of the cited talk.

**Why:** This follows Church convention and shows proper respect. Using a first name or no title is inappropriate.
**How to apply:** In all commentary text (not inside literal quotes from the corpus). The formal citation itself uses the full legal name: `Russell M. Nelson, "Title," ...`
