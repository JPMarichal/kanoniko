# LDS Scripture Structural Reference

## Book of Mormon
- 15 named books, 239 chapters, ~6,604 verses
- Books: 1 Nephi(22), 2 Nephi(33), Jacob(7), Enos(1), Jarom(1), Omni(1), Words of Mormon(1), Mosiah(29), Alma(63), Helaman(16), 3 Nephi(30), 4 Nephi(1), Mormon(9), Ether(15), Moroni(10)
- Special front matter: Title Page (part of translated text), Introduction (1981 editorial), Testimonies of Three/Eight Witnesses, Joseph Smith testimony
- Each book has internal title/preface before chapter 1

## Bible (KJV, LDS Edition)
- OT: 39 books, 929 chapters, ~23,145 verses
- NT: 27 books, 260 chapters, ~7,957 verses
- LDS Edition adds: footnotes cross-referencing all 4 standard works, Topical Guide, Bible Dictionary, JST excerpts, maps

## Doctrine and Covenants (D&C / DyC) — STRUCTURALLY UNIQUE
- **The volume IS the book** — no sub-books
- 138 **sections** (NOT chapters) + 2 Official Declarations
- Each section has editorial heading (italics): date, location, historical context — NOT part of revealed text, copyrighted
- Official Declarations 1 (1890, plural marriage) and 2 (1978, priesthood) have NO verse numbers — prose documents
- Section lengths vary: Section 13 = 1 verse, Section 76 = 119 verses
- Historical layers: Sections 1-134 (Joseph Smith), 135 (John Taylor), 136 (Brigham Young), 137-138 (added 1976)
- Citation: "D&C 76:22-24" (EN) / "DyC 76:22-24" (ES)

## Pearl of Great Price — HETEROGENEOUS
- **Book of Moses** (8 chapters): From JST of Genesis. Ch 1 = vision to Moses (no Genesis parallel), Ch 2-8 ≈ Genesis 1-6
- **Book of Abraham** (5 chapters): From Egyptian papyri. **Includes 3 Facsimiles** with numbered figure explanations (NOT verse format)
  - Facsimile 1: before Abraham 1
  - Facsimile 2: between chapters 3 and 4
  - Facsimile 3: after chapter 5
- **Joseph Smith—Matthew** (1 chapter, 55 verses): Extract from JST of Matthew 24. Abbrev: JS—M
- **Joseph Smith—History** (1 chapter, 75 verses): 1838 history extract. Contains First Vision (vv 15-20). Abbrev: JS—H
- **Articles of Faith** (13 items): Numbered 1-13, cited as "A of F 1:1-13" — technically one "chapter"

## Citation Conventions

### Format
- Standard: `Book Chapter:Verse(s)` — e.g., `Alma 32:21`
- Ranges: `32:21-23` (contiguous), `32:21-23, 26-27` (non-contiguous)
- Multiple chapters: `1 Nephi 3:7; 4:1-3` (semicolon separates)
- Cross-book: `Alma 32:21; Ether 12:6` (semicolon separates)
- D&C: `D&C 76:22-24` — "section" used in speech, but citation uses same format

### Standard Abbreviations (EN)
Gen., Ex., Ps., Isa., Matt., 1 Cor., Rev., 1 Ne., 2 Ne., Mosiah, Alma, Hel., 3 Ne., 4 Ne., Morm., Ether, Moro., D&C, OD, Moses, Abr., JS—M, JS—H, A of F

### Spanish
DyC (not D&C), 1 Nefi, Mosíah, Helamán, Mormón, Éter, José Smith—Mateo (JSM), José Smith—Historia (JSH), Artículos de Fe (A de F)

## Extended Hierarchy (user's mental model for analysis)

Both hierarchies coexist — the short chain is the practical default; the long chain is a complementary tool for structural analysis, not a replacement.

**Short (practical default):** `Volume → Book → Chapter → Verse`
**Long (structural analysis):**

**Volume → Division → Book → Part → Chapter → Pericope → Verse**

- **Division**: Major groupings within a volume (e.g., OT Law/History/Poetry/Prophets, NT Gospels/Acts/Epistles/Revelation, BoM Small Plates/Large Plates/Moroni)
- **Part**: Sub-divisions within a book (e.g., Isaiah 1-39 "First Isaiah" vs 40-66 "Deutero-Isaiah", Genesis patriarchal narratives)
- **Pericope**: A thematic unit spanning one or more verses — the natural "reading unit" (e.g., the parable of the prodigal son = Luke 15:11-32)

**Why it matters:**
- Gospel harmonies compare **pericopes** across Gospels (not just verses)
- Book commentaries analyze **parts** and their structural role
- Structural analysis reveals chiasmus, parallelism, narrative arcs
- The pericope is the natural unit for a "concept map" or "thematic analysis"

**How to apply:** When modeling scripture references in the KG or for synthesis products, support grouping at these levels. A timeline of Jesus's life is organized by pericopes. A commentary is organized by parts. A harmony is a pericope-to-pericope mapping.

## Key Data Modeling Notes
1. D&C: no sub-books, "sections" not "chapters", ODs have no verses
2. PGP: facsimiles are NOT chapter:verse (need special handling)
3. Chapter headings/summaries are **copyrighted** editorial content (Intellectual Reserve, Inc.)
4. Articles of Faith: one "chapter", items function as verses
5. The em dash (—) in JS—M, JS—H is part of the name, not a range indicator
