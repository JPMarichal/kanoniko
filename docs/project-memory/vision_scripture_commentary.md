The conference talk corpus (6,883 talks, 37,403 scripture citations) is the foundation for a **scripture commentary system**.

**Why:** The goal is not just indexing talks — it's building the data layer for exegesis. For any verse (e.g., Moroni 10:32), the system should surface WHO cited it, WHAT they said about it (precise textual quote), and HOW different talks interpret it. This enables correct, well-founded interpretation backed by authoritative sources.

**How to apply:**
- Scripture↔talk relations must preserve the **context** (the passage around the citation), not just the link
- Thematic cross-referencing between talks matters: talks that cite the same scriptures or teach similar concepts form clusters
- The KG should model talk↔scripture (CITES), talk↔talk (thematic), and speaker↔topic patterns
- This is a base layer for future products (chat, study aids, commentary generation)
