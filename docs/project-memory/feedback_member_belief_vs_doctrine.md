When writing about topics where LDS member beliefs, official Church doctrine, and pop culture narratives overlap, treat them as three distinct layers. Never conflate them.

**Why:** While writing about Mary Magdalene, the Da Vinci Code section originally debunked the idea of Jesus being married. The user corrected: many LDS members believe Jesus was married and María Magdalena may have been his wife — that's a legitimate theological reflection, not the same as Brown's conspiracy theory. It's also not official Church doctrine. The article should debunk the "secret lover + conspiracy" narrative without dismissing the marriage question.

**How to apply:** (1) Pop culture myths (Da Vinci Code, etc.) → debunk with evidence. (2) Common member beliefs that aren't doctrine → acknowledge respectfully, note they're not official, suggest they merit their own treatment. (3) Official doctrine → cite authoritative sources. Never let debunking pop culture accidentally dismiss sincere member beliefs.
