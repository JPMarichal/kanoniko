## Testimony (Testimonio) — Concept Reference

### Definition
- **True to the Faith:** "A testimony is a spiritual witness given by the Holy Ghost."
- **GEE:** "Conocimiento y confirmación espiritual que da el Espíritu Santo."
- **PMG ch.10:** "A testimony is a spiritual witness given by the Holy Ghost. To share your testimony is to give a simple, direct declaration of knowledge or belief about a gospel truth."

### Dual Nature
1. **Spiritual state** (interior): conviction confirmed by the Holy Ghost — exists whether or not expressed
2. **Rhetorical act** (exterior): public declaration of that conviction — "doy testimonio de que..."
- Circular relationship: sharing strengthens the state. Packer: "A testimony is to be found in the bearing of it."

### Five Pillars
1. God is our Heavenly Father and loves us
2. Jesus Christ lives, is the Son of God, our Savior
3. Joseph Smith was the prophet called to restore the gospel
4. The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is the Savior's true Church
5. A living prophet leads today

### How Obtained
- **Mechanism:** Holy Ghost (no substitute — not reasoning, not evidence, not angels)
- **Conditions:** sincere desire, study, prayer with real intent, obedience, fasting
- **Modes (Brown 2025):** sudden (light bulb), gradual (sunrise), intermittent (rays)
- **Spectrum:** believe → hope → trust → sure witness (all valid)

### Growth and Loss
- Never static — growing or weakening
- Grows through: service, obedience, study, prayer, fasting, sharing
- Lost through: neglect, disobedience, leaving covenant path
- Oliver Cowdery as archetype of loss (Wilford Woodruff)
- Nelson: "with frightening speed, a testimony not nourished daily can crumble"

### Eternal Consequence (DyC 76)
- Celestial: received testimony AND were valiant in it (76:51-52)
- Terrestrial: received testimony but NOT valiant (76:75, 79)
- Telestial: did NOT receive testimony in the flesh (76:82, 101-103)

### Biblical Roots (ISBE)
- *Martus* = witness, forensic origin — testifying before a judge
- Requires minimum 2 witnesses (Deut 17:6; Matt 18:16)
- Witness participated in execution (Deut 17:7) — proving conviction
- *Martus* → *martyr* (Acts 22:20; Rev 2:13) — sealing with blood
- Witness of the Spirit (Rom 8:16): concurrent testimony — your spirit says "Thou art my Father"; the Spirit says "Thou art His child"

### Testimony vs. Illustration (for product design)
- Testimony IS a specific form of illustration: experience + proof from the speaker's own life
- But in SUD context it carries additional weight: it invites the Spirit to confirm, not just persuade
- In a discourse: testimony = illustration + spiritual proof + covenant obligation
