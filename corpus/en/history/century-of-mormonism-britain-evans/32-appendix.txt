246BRITISH-BORN GENERAL AUTHORITIES OF THE CHURCH

246JOHN TAYLOR—Born November 1, 1808, at Milnthorpe, England, ordained an Apostle December 19, 1838, under the hands of Brigham Young and Heber C. Kimball. Sustained October 10, 1880, as President of the Church, at the age of 72; died July 25, 1887, at Kaysville, Davis County, Utah.

246GEORGE Q. CANNON—Born January 11, 1827, at Liverpool, England; ordained an Apostle August 26, 1860, at Salt Lake City, Utah, by Brigham Young, at the age of 33 years, advanced to be First Counselor in the First Presidency October 10, 1880. Died April 12, 1901, at Monterey, California.

246GEORGE REYNOLDS—Born January 1, 1842, at Marylebone, London, England; ordained a Seventy March 18, 1866; set apart as one of the first seven president, April 10, 1890, at Salt Lake City, Utah, at the age of 54 years. Died August 9, 1909, at Salt Lake City, Utah.

246GEORGE TEASDALE—Born December 8, 1831, at London, England; ordained an Apostle October 16, 1882, at Salt Lake Utah, by John Taylor, at the age of 51 years; died June 9, 1907, at Salt Lake City, Utah.

246BRIGHAM H. ROBERTS—Born March 13, 1857, at Warrington, Lancashire, England; ordained a Seventy, March 8, 1877; set apart as one of the first seven presidents October 8, 1888, at Salt Lake City, Utah, at the age of 31 years. Died September 27, 1933.

246JOHN R. WINDER—Born December 11, 1821, at Biddendew, England; ordained a high priest, March 4, 1872, by Edward Hunter; sustained October 17, 1901, as first Counselor in the First Presidency, at the age of 80; died March 27, 1910, at Salt Lake City, Utah, at the age of 89 years.

246CHARLES W. PENROSE—Born February 4, 1832, at London, England; ordained an Apostle, July 7, 1904, by Joseph F. Smith at Salt Lake City, Utah, at the age of 72; advanced to be second Counselor in the First Presidency December 7, 1911; and as first Counselor in the First Presidency March 10, 1921; died May 16, 1925, at the age of 93 years.

246CHARLES W. NIBLEY—Born February 5, 1849, at Hunterfield, Midlothian, Scotland; ordained and set apart as Presiding Bishop of the Church, December 11, 1907, at Salt Lake City, Utah, at the age of 58; set apart as second Counselor in the First Presidency, May 28, 1925, at the age of 76 years. Died December 11, 1931, at Salt Lake City, Utah, at the age of 82 years.

246JAMES E. TALMAGE—Born September 21, 1862, at Hungerford, Berkshire, England, ordained an Apostle December 8, 1911, by Joseph F. Smith, at Salt Lake City, Utah, at the age of 49 years. Died July 27, 1933, at Salt Lake City, Utah.

246JOHN WELLS—Born September 16, 1864, in Carlton, Nottinghamshire, England; ordained a Bishop July 18, 1918, and set apart as second Counselor to the Presiding Bishop.

246CHARLES A. CALLIS—Born May 4, 1865, at Dublin, Ireland; ordained an Apostle October 12, 1933, at the age of 68 years, by Heber J. Grant.
