Acknowledgments

Although it is impossible to thank adequately all those who have shared their views and offered me valuable suggestions, a special note of appreciation is extended to Dr. A. Thurber, book editor of the Review and Herald Publishing Association, for reading the selection in this work pertaining to the Seventh-day Adventists and suggesting helpful revisions.

This work also could not have been completed without the cooperation of the staffs of many libraries, especially the librarians who serve the students and faculty of the Brigham Young University. Grateful acknowledgment is further extended to my wife, Kathleen McLatchy Backman, for her encouragement, her timely advice, and assistance in preparing the original draft of this work for publication.
