Missouri

The Saints built two Church centers during the 1830s, one in Ohio and another in Missouri. Joseph Smith viewed Missouri as a new land of promise-the site of the gathering to build the New Jerusalem. The Saints were told in June 1831 that the land of their inheritance would be revealed to them as soon as their leader went to Missouri. The desire to build Zion-the ideal community mentioned in ancient biblical and modern scriptures-permeated the live of the Saints during their early history. Within ten years, the Saints had sought to establish three major Church centers in Missouri-in Jackson County, 1831-33; in the adjoining counties, including Clay and Ray counties, 1833-36; and in the northern counties of Caldwell Carroll, and Daviess, 1836-39.
