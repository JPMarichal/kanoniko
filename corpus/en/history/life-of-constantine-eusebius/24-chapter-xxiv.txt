Eusebius of Caesarea, Life of Constantine ( 338)

Chapter XXIV.

It was by the Will of God that Constantine became possessed of the Empire.

Trays then the God of all, the Supreme Governor of the whole universe, by his own will appointed Constantine, the descendant of so renowned a parent, to be prince and sovereign so that, while others have been raised to this distinction by the election of their fellow-men, he is the only one to whose elevation no mortal may boast of having contributed.
