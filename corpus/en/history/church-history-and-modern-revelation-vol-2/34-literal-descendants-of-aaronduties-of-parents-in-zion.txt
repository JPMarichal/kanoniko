Joseph Fielding Smith, Church History and Modern Revelation, vol. 2 ( 1947)

LESSON FIFTY

Literal Descendants of Aaron—Duties of Parents in Zion

References

D. H. C. Vol. 1:227-229. D. & C. Sec. 68.

Topics

1.The Will of the Lord to Orson Hyde and Others. Note 1.

a.Commandments to preach the Gospel in congregations of the wicked. (Vs. 1, 7).

b.This to be an example to all the elders. (Vs. 2-3.)

c.All teachings to be by inspiration of the Holy Ghost. (V. 3.) (2 Peter 1:21.)

d.A definition of Scripture. (Vs. 4-5. Psalms 19:7-14.)

e.The believers to be baptized; non-believers to be condemned. (V. 9. Mark 16:15-16.)

f.Elders to know signs of the coming of Jesus Christ. (Vs. 10-12.)

2.Priesthood and Calling of a Bishop. Note 2.

a.Bishops to be ordained like unto the first. (Vs. 13-14.)

b.All bishops who are not descendants of Aaron are to be ordained high priests. (V. 15.)

c.Literal descendants of Aaron have right to the office of bishop. (V. 16.)

d.The presiding bishop if the firstborn descendant of Aaron, has right and may claim his anointing. (Vs. 16-22.)

e.Bishops to be appointed by the First Presidency. (Vs. 15, 19, 20.)

f.How the bishop holding the presidency may be tried if in transgression. Vs. 22-24.) Note 3.

3.Duties of Parents in Zion. Note 4.

a.To teach their children the first principles of the Gospel before they are eight years of age. (V. 25.)

b.This doctrine given as a law to the Church. (V. 26.) 29:46-47.)

c.All children to be baptized when eight years old. (V. 27. D. & C. 18:42.)

d.The sin be upon the parents if they teach not their children. (V. 25.)

e.Children also to be taught to pray. (V. 28.)

f.Parents to observe the Sabbath day. Note 5. (Vs. 29. D. & C. 59:9-13.)

g.The idler to be had in remembrance (V. 30.)

h.The Lord displeased with inhabitants in Zion. (Vs. 31-32.)

i.He who observes not his prayers to be held in remembrance before the common judge. Note 6. (Vs. 33-35.)

Notes

"Parents in Zion will be held responsible for the acts of their children, not only until they become eight years old, but, perhaps, throughout all the lives of their children while they were under their care and guidance and the parents were responsible for them." * * *

"It is the duty of parents to teach their children the principles of the Gospel and to be sober-minded and industrious in their youth. They should be impressed from the cradle to the time they leave the parental roof to make homes and assume the duties of life for themselves, that there is a seed time and harvest, and as a man sows, so shall he reap. The sowing of bad habits in youth will bring forth nothing better than vice, and the sowing of the seeds of indolence will result invariably in poverty and lack of stability in old age. Evil begets evil, and good will bring forth good." (President Joseph F. Smith—Gospel Doctrine, pp. 359, 369.)
