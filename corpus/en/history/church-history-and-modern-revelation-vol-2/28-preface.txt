Joseph Fielding Smith, Church History and Modern Revelation, vol. 2 ( 1947)

Preface

This course of study is a continuation of the study for the Melchizedek Priesthood Quorums of the Church commenced in the year 1947. As stated in the Preface of the compilation for the year 1947 "The General Church Authorities decided to present a course dealing with the history of the Church with special stress upon the revelations and commandments given to the Prophet Joseph Smith. The brethren of the Committee realize that such a study cannot be completed in the course of one year, therefore this same theme will be presented in the following year, or until such time as the period of history covering the life and ministry of the Prophet Joseph Smith is completed."

"We feel that there is a need for the study of our own Church History, to impart to members of Priesthood quorums generally, a better understanding of the revelations and commandments which have been given in this Dispensation for our temporal and eternal welfare."

In harmony with this decision this Second Series has been prepared and we send it forth hoping that it will meet with the same favorable response which greeted the course of study in the year 1947.

Very sincerely,

THE COUNCIL OF TWELVE APOSTLES

By George F. Richards, President
