Satan's Opposition to the Introduction of the Gospel to England

LaMar Garrard

Introduction

In light of other events which took place in the early history of the Restored Church up until 1837, how important was the taking of the gospel to the British Isles? If we were to attempt to measure the importance of an event in church history in terms of "opposition by Satan," then how would the introduction of the gospel to the British Isles in 1837 be ranked with other major events such as the First Vision, the coming forth of the Book of Mormon, and the growth and expansion of the Church in America? This paper shows how each important step that was taken to restore the gospel, and then present it to the world up until 1837, was in turn met by severe opposition from either Satan, his angels or devils, or their mortal followers here on earth. I will then examine the nature and strength of the opposition hurled against the early leaders of the Church as they began to preach the gospel in England in 1837, to see if this event could be ranked in importance with other events such as the First Vision, the coming forth of the Book of Mormon, and the growth and expansion of the Church in America.

Satan Opposes Joseph Smith

Several events in Joseph Smith's life before the First Vision seem to indicate that Satan was attempting to take the young boy's life even before the restoration of the gospel. [^1] However, the power of Satan manifested against the Prophet at the time of the First Vision gives evidence that this event was probably the most important event in the restoration of the gospel: rarely is Satan allowed to come out directly and use such power in an attempt to stop God's work upon the earth. Joseph Smith describes the attack of Satan as follows:

84After I had retired to the place where I had previously designed to go, having looked around me, and finding myself alone, I kneeled down and began to offer up the desire of my heart to God. I had scarcely done so, when immediately I was seized upon by some power which entirely overcame me, and had such an astonishing influence over me as to bind my tongue so that I could not speak. Thick darkness gathered around me, and it seemed to me for a time as if I were doomed to sudden destruction.

84But, exerting all my powers to call upon God to deliver me out of the power of this enemy which had seized upon me, and at the very moment when I was ready to sink into despair and abandon myself to destruction-not to an imaginary ruin, but to the power of some actual being from the unseen world, who had such marvelous power as I had never before felt in any being-just at this moment of great alarm, I saw a pillar of light exactly over my head, above the brightness of the sunÂ�.

84It no sooner appeared than I found myself delivered from the enemy which held me bound. (JS-H 1:15-17)

84Although Satan failed to destroy Joseph Smith at this time-thanks to the intervention of God-he continued in his efforts to stop the young prophet. Joseph related to Heber C. Kimball on one occasion that he had had many encounters with the evil one, adding that "the nearer a person approaches the Lord, a greater power will be manifested by the adversary to prevent the accomplishment of His purposes." [^2]

85Satan also helped in the persecution of Joseph Smith in an attempt to prevent the restoration of the gospel: between the time of the First Vision and the appearance of the Angel Moroni, Satan influenced many of his mortal followers on earth to persecute and revile against the young prophet. Joseph expressed surprise that so much persecution was raised against such an obscure person as he:

85It seems as though the adversary was aware, at a very early period of my life, that I was destined to prove a disturber and an annoyer of his kingdom; else why should the powers of darkness combine against me? Why the opposition and persecution that arose against me, almost in my infancy?Â�

85I soon found, however, that my telling the story had excited a great deal of prejudice against me among professors of religion, and was the cause of great persecution, which continued to increase; and though I was an obscure boy, only between fourteen and fifteen years of age, and my circumstances in life such as to make a boy of no consequence in the world, yet men of high standing would take notice sufficiently to excite the public mind against me, and create a bitter persecution; and this was common among all the sects-all united to persecute me. (JS-H 1:20, 22)

85At this critical time when his kingdom on earth was being threatened, Satan also tried to tempt Joseph Smith to commit sin, which would have rendered him unfit to be instrumental in furthering God's work on earth. Joseph states that in the interim between the First Vision and the appearance of the Angel Moroni, he was led into many temptations. Evidently Satan was frustrated in these attempts, however, because the young prophet explained that he was not guilty of any serious sins:

85I was left to all kinds of temptations; and, mingling with all kinds of society, I frequently fell into many foolish errors, and displayed the weakness of youth, and the foibles of human nature; which I am sorry to say, led me into divers temptations, offensive in the sight of God. In making this confession, no one need suppose me guilty of any great or malignant sins. A disposition to commit such was never in my nature. (JS-H 1:28)

86Joseph was correct in disclaiming guilt of any great sins, for he was worthy enough for the Angel Moroni to appear to him in September of 1823, and inform him of his future role in translating and publishing the Book of Mormon to the world.

Satan Opposes the Translation and Publication of the Book of Mormon

Those who read and accept the message of the Book of Mormon are converted to the Gospel of Jesus Christ. By accepting and living the gospel, men achieve happiness in this world as well as in the world to come. Since Satan's motives are to make men miserable like unto himself, he went to great efforts in an attempt to prevent the translation and publication of the Book of Mormon to the world. He first attempted to prevent the translation of the golden plates, by tempting Joseph to use the plates for personal gain. When the Angel Moroni appeared to Joseph Smith on September 21, 1823, he told him where the plates were located but at the same time warned him that Satan would try to tempt him (since his family was so poor at that time) to obtain the plates for the purpose of getting rich. "This he forbade me, saying that I must have no other object in view in getting the plates but to glorify God, and must not be influenced by any other motive than that of building his kingdom" (JS-H 1:46). According to Oliver Cowdery, as Joseph walked from his residence to the Hill Cumorah the next morning, two invisible powers were operating upon his mind, and "that the one urging the certainty of wealth and ease in this life, had so powerfully wrought upon him, that the great object so carefully and impressively named by the angel, had entirely gone from his recollection." [^3] [^4]

87At that instant he looked to the Lord in prayer, and as he prayed, darkness began to disperse from his mind and his soul was lit up as it was the evening before, and he was filled with the Holy Spirit; and again did the Lord manifest his condescension and mercy: the heavens were opened and the glory of the Lord shone round about and rested upon him. While he thus stood gazing, and admiring, the angel said, "Look!" and as he thus spake he beheld the prince of darkness, surrounded by his innumerable train of associates. All this passed before him, and the heavenly messenger said, "All this is shown, the good and the evil, the holy and impure, the glory of God and the power of darkness, that you may know hereafter the two powers and never be influenced or overcome by that wicked one. Behold, whatever entices and leads to good and to do good, is of God, and whatever does not is of that wicked one: It is he that fills the hearts of men with evilÂ�. You now see why you could not obtain this recordÂ�. They are not deposited here for the sake of accumulating gain and wealth for the glory of this world. [^5]

87Satan failed in this attempt to persuade Joseph Smith to use the golden plates for personal gain. Consequently, in September 1827, Joseph was allowed to take the plates from the Hill Cumorah. However, Satan again attempted to stop the translation of the golden plates by use of wicked disciples on earth: Satan influenced them to attempt to take the plates from Joseph by force. However, Moroni told Joseph Smith that if he used all his "endeavors to preserve themÂ�they should be protected" (JS-H 1:59). Joseph describes Satan's efforts to stop the work in this manner as follows:

For no sooner was it known that I had them, than the most strenuous exertions were used to get them from me. Every stratagem that could be invented was resorted to for that purpose. The persecution became more bitter and severe than before, and multitudes were on the alert continually to get them from me if possible. But by the wisdom of God, they remained safe in my hands, until I had accomplished by them what was required at my hand. (JS-H 1:60)

88Since the opposition against Joseph and the efforts to wrest the plates from him became so severe in Manchester, New York, he moved to Harmony, Susquehanna County, Pennsylvania, where he began to translate the plates.

88Realizing he had failed in his efforts to stop the translation and coming forth of the Book of Mormon by these methods, Satan now devised "a cunning plan" ( D&C 10:12 ) to discredit both Joseph Smith as a prophet of God, and the translated and published account of the Book of Mormon as the word of God. It appears that this was to be accomplished by Satan persuading Martin Harris to obtain the first part of the translation (the first 116 pages of manuscript) of the golden plates, and then causing it to fall into the hands of wicked men. Satan would then influence these men to make changes in this account and publish the altered version after Joseph had written a new account retranslated from the same original record, and published it as the first part of the Book of Mormon. Since the new translation published in the Book of Mormon would disagree with the altered version published by these wicked men, they would falsely claim that Joseph Smith was not a true Prophet of God, since he could not get the same translation a second time from the same original record. Once men were convinced that Joseph Smith was not a prophet of God and hence the Book of Mormon was not the Word of God, the Book of Mormon would be ineffectual in spreading the gospel to the world. God revealed this plan of Satan's to Joseph Smith in a revelation given shortly after the lost manuscript fell into the hands of Satan's followers:

88And, behold, Satan hath put it into their hearts to alter the words which you have caused to be written, or which you have translated, which have gone out of your hands.

89And behold, I say unto you, that because they have altered the words, they read contrary from that which you translated and caused to be writtenÂ�.

89For, behold, if you should bring forth the same words they will say that you have lied and that you have pretended to translate, but that you have contradicted yourself.

89And, behold, they will publish this, and Satan will harden the hearts of the people to stir them up to anger against you, that they will not believe my words.

89Thus Satan thinketh to overpower your testimony in this generation, that the work may not come forth in this generation. ( D&C 10:10, 11, 31-33 )

89God thwarted Satan's plans by instructing Joseph to translate from a different record among the golden plates which covered the same period of time as that covered by the previously translated account that was lost. In this manner, the Lord said, "I will confound those who have altered my words. I will not suffer that they shall destroy my work; yea, I will show unto them that my wisdom is greater than the cunning of the devil" ( D&C 10:42-43 ).

89Once the records were translated, Satan still attempted to prevent the printing and publishing of the account while the manuscript was in the hands of the printer. In spite of his efforts, the Book of Mormon was published to the world in 1830. [^6]

Satan Opposes the Expansion and Growth of the Church

Having been unsuccessful in preventing the translation and publication of the Book of Mormon, it appears that Satan now turned his efforts to stop the preaching of the gospel to the world by impeding the growth of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The Church is the vehicle or means the Lord uses to take the Book of Mormon to the four corners of the world: there could be no great missionary program without an organized church with sufficient members to provide missionaries, train them, send them out, and then sustain them in the mission field. Consequently, once the Book of Mormon was published to the world, Satan apparently made every effort to slow down or stop the expansion and growth of the Church with its attendant missionary program. He attempted to accomplish this goal by two methods: first, opposition and persecution of the Church arose from without; second, the Church was weakened from within by the apostasy of its own members.

90As soon as the Church was organized, efforts were made by the enemy to prevent the baptism of new members, and to prevent missionaries from preaching the Gospel. Satan resorted to such means as having his followers tear down dams used to make ponds for baptisms, hauling away prospective members by force to prevent their baptism, mob action against members, attempted disruption of church meetings, and the false charges and court actions against Joseph Smith. [^7] There was constant persecution and plotting against the lives of Joseph Smith and his associates. To escape these dangers, in December of 1830 the Lord commanded Joseph and other members of the Church to move to Ohio, "because of the enemy and for your sakes" ( D&C 37:1 ). In January of 1831, the Lord revealed to Joseph Smith some sort of a conspiracy against the leaders of the Church and again commanded them to move from New York to Ohio to escape the power of the enemy:

90And now I show unto you a mystery, a thing which is had in secret chambers, to bring to pass even your destruction in process of time, and ye knew it notÂ�.

90And again, I say unto you that the enemy in the secret chambers seeketh your livesÂ�.

90And that ye might escape the power of the enemy, and be gathered unto me a righteous people, without spot and blameless-

90Wherefore, for this cause I gave unto you the commandment that you should go to the Ohio. ( D&C 38:13, 28, 31-32 )

91Shortly after Joseph Smith arrived in Ohio, the Lord revealed to him that those who were converted in the East should "flee to the west, and this in consequence of that which is coming on the earth, and of secret combinations" ( D&C 42:64 ).

91In the summer of 1831, the Lord sent Joseph Smith and other leaders of the Church to Missouri to dedicate the land of Zion, and then to have some of the Saints settle there as well as in Ohio. However, by 1833 Satan raised severe persecution against the members in Jackson County, Missouri, and they were later mobbed and driven north across the Missouri River into Clay and Ray Counties. The persecution became so intense that they were never able to return to their homes in Jackson County. In 1836, persecution arose in Clay County; the members consequently moved northward and settled Caldwell and Daviess Counties. Meanwhile, Joseph Smith was among the Saints in Ohio; and there in 1835 the Twelve Apostles were chosen. In March of 1836, the Kirtland Temple was dedicated, after many trials and opposition. Shortly thereafter, the Church received the keys and endowments necessary to prepare the elders to preach the Gospel to the nations of the earth. "Following these blessings they began to go forth, spreading abroad in all parts of the land, preaching the word in power as they had never experienced it before, and many received their testimony and were numbered among the people of the Lord." [^8]

Evidently Satan renewed his efforts to stop this missionary effort and growth of the church, since in 1837 there arose a severe apostasy from within as well as severe persecution from without the Church. Part of the problem from within the Church was a result of the Financial Panic of 1837, with the subsequent failure of the Kirtland Safety Society. Many members of the Church had been unwise in their investments, despite warning counsel from Joseph Smith. As a result, they suffered great financial loss. This factor and others caused many members in Ohio to develop the spirit of apostasy, described as follows:

92"It seemed," said the Prophet, "as though all the powers of earth and hell were combining in their influence in an especial manner to overthrow the Church at once, and make a final end." Enemies abroad, aided by the apostates within, united in various schemes to overthrow the Prophet as if he had been the sole cause of the evils, not only in the communities of the Church, but throughout the entire land. Most of this evil which befell the Church might have been avoided if the Prophet's counsel had been accepted by the Saints. Apostasy developed within all the counsels of the Church, and many of the leading brethren, who previously had been true and faithful, were involved. [^9]

With this apostasy from within the Church in Ohio, and persecution from without in both Ohio and Missouri in 1837, the Church needed new and stalwart members to strengthen the weakened Church. In an earlier revelation the Lord had instructed Joseph Smith to send missionaries out to continue to preach the gospel, so that they could gain many converts. With the increased membership in the Church, the Lord explained, "Ye shall obtain power to organize yourselves according to the laws of man; That your enemies may not have power over you; that you may be preserved in all things;Â�that every bond may be broken wherewith the enemy seeketh to destroy my people" ( D&C 44:4-5 ). Consequently, in 1837-when Satan was having success in weakening the Church in America-the Lord instructed Joseph Smith to call Heber C. Kimball to carry the gospel to the great nation of England. Accompanying Elder Kimball on that mission were Orson Hyde, Willard Richards, Joseph Fielding, Isaac Russell, John Goodson, and John Snyder. The results of this opening of England to the gospel was that "members were baptized by the thousands in the course of a few months following. Many of them emigrated and became stalwarts in the church." [^10]

Satan Opposes Missionary Work in England

The missionaries arrived in Liverpool, England, on 20 July 1837. At that time Heber C. Kimball was very depressed, for he had been "reflecting on the wretched state of affairs in far away Kirtland, where the Prophet of God, whom he loved as his own soul, was surrounded by enemies, and his own family in lowly circumstances in the midst of persecution." [^11] [^12]

Meanwhile, the powers of darkness had taken counsel against these servants of the Lord. Not without a struggle would Satan loose his hold, and permit the gates of salvation to open for the eastern, as they had already opened for the western hemisphere. The evil one had seen that the Church in America was trembling on the verge of dissolution. To give it fresh impetus, and infuse new life into the seemingly sinking system, was the object of the Apostles' mission to the shores of Albion. The opening of that mission it was Satan's fell purpose to thwart, and for which he was now gathering, far and near, the embattled hosts of hell.

94The Elders might be said to have "stolen a march" on the Adversary, in securing, already, three hearings at Vauxhall Chapel, with the favorable results before noted. This much could not be retrieved, but the enemy of righteousness hoped to prevent a repetition of such scenes, and to hinder those who believed, from obeying the Gospel by going down into the waters of baptism. [^13]

94Satan's reaction to this foothold gained by the Lord's servants gives ample evidence to just how important it was at this time to introduce the gospel to England. Satan began his counterattack against Heber C. Kimball and the other missionaries by first turning the Reverend Fielding against them. As soon as he saw some of the members of his flock leaving, he closed the doors of his chapel to the missionaries, so that they were obliged "to preach at night in private houses, which were opened in every direction, when numbers came to hear and believed the Gospel." [^14] In addition to this opposition, Satan resorted to an attack against the missionaries somewhat similar to that made against the young prophet Joseph Smith in the Sacred Grove. A vivid account of this episode comes to us from Heber C. Kimball and Orson Hyde, with editorial comments by Orson F. Whitney.

99In still another discourse, Elder Kimball pointed out that some of these evil spirits who attacked them were persons who previously had lived upon the earth with a body, but who had been overcome by Satan as mortals and had since died:

It is written, "resist the devil and he will flee from you." Some people do not believe that there are any devils. There are thousands of evil spirits that are just as ugly as evil can make them. The wicked die, and their spirits remain not far from where their tabernacles are. When I was in England, twenty-eight years ago next June, I saw more devils than there are persons here to-day; they came upon me with an intention to destroy me; they are the spirits of wicked men who, while in the flesh, were opposed to God and his purposes. I saw them with what we call the spiritual eyes, but what is in reality the natural eye. [^19]

Summary

In America, Satan opposed the coming forth of the Book of Mormon and the growth and expansion of the Church. When the missionaries came to England in 1837, they also met opposition by means of persecution brought against them by Reverend Fielding and his associates. In addition, the attack against the missionaries by Satan's angels and evil spirits were quite similar to Satan's attack on Joseph Smith in the Sacred Grove. Keeping this in mind, as we examined the history of the Restored Church up through the year 1837, it was apparent that Satan opposed the opening of England to the gospel just as he opposed Joseph Smith receiving his vision in the Sacred Grove and the consequent spread of the gospel in America. Hence, the introduction of the gospel to England in 1837 was truly one of the important events that took place as God restored, and began to spread his kingdom here upon this earth.

Heber C. Kimball, "Elders Called to Go on Missions, etc.," Journal of Discourses, III (1856), 229-230.
