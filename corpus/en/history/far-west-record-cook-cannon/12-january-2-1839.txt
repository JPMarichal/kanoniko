Lyndon W. Cook, Donald Q. Cannon, Far West Record: Minutes of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, 1830-1844 ( 1983)

January 2, 1839

Far-West, Mo. January 2nd 1839.

The High Council of Zion assembled according to adjournmt Simeon Carter (1) Jared Carter (2) Solomon Hancock (6) John Badger (7) John Murdock (8) George W. Harris (10) David Dort (11) and Samuel Bent (12)

The Council was opened by prayer by Simeon Carter

The Council adjourned untill two weeks from today at 10 o'clock P. M.

E. Robinson Clerk.

January 16, 1839

The High Council of Zion met Far West, January 16th 1839. [^1]

The Council was organized as follows

Simeon Carter (1) Jared Carter (2) Solomon Hancock (6) John Badger (7) John Murdock (8) George W. Harris (10) and Samuel Bent (12)

The Council was opened by prayer by the Clerk

Resolved That the vote be recinded which was passed last spring ( ) which was passed last appointing Isaac Morley, Recorder of Patriarchal Blessings.

The Council adjourned untill two weeks from to day at 10 o clock A. M.

Council Closed in prayer by the prest B Young

E. Robinson Clerk.

Footnotes

Notas:
[^1] These high council minutes differ from the others in the Far West Record in that they were not transcribed at a later date, but apparently were recorded at the time of the meetings. They are also unique in being a record of events that transpired in Nauvoo as opposed to Missouri or other places. These notes were not included in the official typescript of the Far West Record, and are available only in handwritten manuscript form.
