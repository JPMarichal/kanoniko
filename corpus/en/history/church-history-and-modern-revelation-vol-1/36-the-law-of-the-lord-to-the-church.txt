Joseph Fielding Smith, Church History and Modern Revelation, vol. 1 ( 1946)

LESSON THIRTY-SIX

The Law of the Lord to the Church

(This lesson may cover two class periods if desired)

Topics

a. The promise of the law. (see lesson 33.)

b. The Saints assemble in Ohio according to commandment. (verse 1)

c. The Saints commanded to obey the law. (verses 2-3.)

a. The Elders to travel two by two, declaring the word like angels. (verses 4-7.)

b. The journey to be westward building up branches on the way. (verse 8.)

c. This missionary work to be preparatory to the making known where the city New Jerusalem shall be built. (verse 9.)

d. Edward Partridge to stand in his office as Bishop. (verse 10.)

e. None to go forth to preach who have not been ordained by the heads of the Church. (verse 11.)

f. Elders, Priests and Teachers to teach Gospel as found in Bible and Book of Mormon. (verse 12.)

g. The ministers to observe all church covenants. (verse 13.)

a. The Spirit to be given by the prayer of faith, and if any receive it not they shall not teach. (verse 14.)

b. The Comforter to be the guide. (verses 15-17.)

c. The Comforter knoweth all things. (verse 18.)

a. Thou shalt not kill. (verses 18, 19, 79.)

b. Thou shalt not steal. (verses 20, 84, 85.)

c. Thou shalt not lie. (verses 21, 86.)

d. Thou shalt love thy wife and cleave unto her and none else. (verse 22.)

e. He that looketh upon a woman to lust, will deny the faith. (verses 23.)

f. The adulterer who repents not to be cast out, but if guilty again not to be forgiven. (verses 24-26. 75-81.)

g. Thou shalt not bear false witness. (verse 27.)

h. All other commandments not specifically stated, but found in the scriptures, renewed. (verses 28-29.)

i. Those who offend many, to be chastened before many. Those who offend openly to be rebuked openly. Those who offend in secret to be rebuked in secret, if they repent. (verses 88-93.)

a. Properties consecrated for support of poor. (verses 30-31.)

b. A covenant not to be broken. (verses 31-32.)

c. How properties are to be imparted. (verses 31-33.)

d. Every man to be made accountable, and a steward. (verse 32.)

e. The residue of property to be kept in the Bishop's storehouse, and for purchase of lands, etc. (verses 34-36.)

f. The Saints to become the covenant people of the Lord through consecration. (verse 36.)

a. The sinner who will not repent to be cast out. (verses 37-38.)

b. What the prophets have spoken, to be fulfilled. (verse 39.)

c. None to be proud in heart, and garments to be plain and workmanship of the hands of the Saints. (verse 40.)

d. Cleanliness in all things commanded. (verse 41.)

e. The idler not to eat the bread or wear the garment of the laborer. (verses 42, 53-55.)

a. The sick to be nursed by friends, not enemies. (verse 43.)

b. Elders of the Church to lay hands on the sick, etc. (verse 44.)

c. Saints to live in love, and weep for the loss of them that die. (verse 45.)

d. Death to be sweet to the righteous, but bitter to the unrighteous. (verses 45-47.)

e. Faith will heal the sick who are not appointed unto death. By faith the blind are to see, the deaf to hear, etc. (verses 48-51.)

f. They who do not have faith to receive these blessings, but who believe, and keep the laws, have power to become the sons of God. (verse 52.)

a. Revision of the Bible to be made by Joseph Smith.

b. The scriptures to served. (verse 56.)

c. To be taught to all nations. (verses 57-58.)

d. The revelations and scriptures to be received, as the law of the Lord. (verses 59-60.)

a. Revelation to be given as needful. (verses 61, 67, 68.)

b. Site of New Jerusalem to be revealed. (verse 62.)

c. Saints to gather to the west in consequences of tribulation and secret combinations. (verses 63-66.)

d. Church covenants sufficient to establish the Saints in Zion to be given. (verses 67-69.)

e. Church officers giving their time to receive support. (verses 70-73.)
