Joseph Fielding Smith, Church History and Modern Revelation, vol. 1 ( 1946)

LESSON THIRTY-EIGHT

Preparation for the Coming of Christ—The New Jerusalem

Topics

a. Jesus Christ laid foundation of earth, and by him all things live and have being. (verses 1, 7-8), Note 2.

b. Summer shall be past, and your souls not saved. (verse 2.) Note 3.

c. Jesus Christ the Advocate with the Father. (verses 3-5.)

d. Listen while it is called today.

e. Jesus rejected by his own. (verse 8.)

f. As many as receive him to be sons of God and have eternal life. (verse 8.)

g. The everlasting covenant sent as a light to the world and a standard to the Gentiles. (verses 9-19.)

a. Why the City of Enoch was taken from the earth. (verses 11-14.)

b. A day of righteousness sought by all holy men, and they found it not. (verse 12.)

c. The day of righteousness near at hand. (verse 14.)

a. The disciples ask for signs of the Savior's coming.

b. "Desolation shall come upon this generation."

c. Jerusalem to be destroyed—the Temple to be thrown down—the Jews to be scattered among all nations and remain until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled.

a. In the times of the Gentiles a light shall break forth among them that sit in darkness. (verse 28.)

b. The people receive not the light because of the precepts of men. (verse 29.)

c. In that generation shall the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled. (verse 30.)

d. An overflowing scourge and desolating sickness to cover the land. (verse 31.)

e. Men will curse God and die, but disciples to stand in holy places. (verse 32.)

f. Earthquakes and many desolations. (verse 33.)

g. When all these things come to pass the hour of Christ's coming is nigh—Sign of the fig tree. (verses 34-38.)

h. The faithful shall be looking for the great day of the Lord. (verse 39.)

i. Signs and wonders to be shown. (verses 40-42.)

a. A remnant of the Jews to be gathered to Jerusalem (Palestine). (Verse 43.)

b. "They shall see me in the clouds of heaven." (verse 44.)

c. The Saints that have slept to be raised. (verse 45.)

d. Then shall the arm of the Lord fall upon the nations. (verse 47.)

e. The Lord shall set his feet upon the Mount of Olives and it shall cleave, and the earth shall tremble and reel.

f. The heathen nations to be redeemed, and they without law to receive their resurrection. (verse 54.)

g. Satan to be bound. (verse 56.)

h. The parable of the ten virgins. (verses 56-57.)

i. The earth to be given to the Saints who will possess it and multiply and wax strong.

a. Wars to be waged on this land. (verse 63.)

b. Saints commanded to go to western lands. (verse 64.)

c. The Saints to purchase inheritances in Zion with their riches. (verse 65.)

d. The New Jerusalem to be built in the western land, and to be a city of refuge. (verse 66.)

e. The glory of the Lord to be there, and the wicked will not come to it. (verses 67-70.)

f. The righteous to be gathered out from all nations. (verse 71.)

g. When the Lord shall appear he shall be terrible unto the ungodly, who shall fear and tremble. (verses 72-75.)

Notes

The signs predicted of Christ's near approach are here. The hearts of men are failing them. After this second world conflict there are still numerous rumors of wars, and actual conflicts. Earthquakes and other commotions are frequent: the nations are in distress and wickedness prevails among the nations of men. Now is the time when the faithful should be looking forth for the great day of the Lord.
