Appendix B

Callings of Participants in Zion's Camp

A broadside printed in Kirtland in April 1836 identified members of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles and the first and second quorum of seventies. This broadside has been reproduced below in the same format as the original. Members of these priesthood bodies who participated in the march to Zion have been identified by an asterisk, a mark that the author of this volume has added to the printed page.
