## Learn

Families can make homes places where the Holy Ghost can help them learn. They can talk about Jesus Christ and try to be more like Him. You can do many things to invite the Holy Ghost into your home and to keep your family strong. You can pray and read the scriptures as a family every day, live the gospel, and serve others.

When the Holy Ghost is in your home, your family will treat each other with kindness and love. When your children feel loved, they will feel the support they need to be successful.

These things will help your home be a place of love and learning:

- Be patient.

- Try not to get angry.

- Spend time together.

- Have fun.

- Listen carefully.

- Praise your children as they try to learn.

- Use kind words.

## Think and Pray

Think about what you have learned. Write down any thoughts you have or ideas that come from the Holy Ghost. What do you think the Lord wants you to do? What things can you do to have or show more love in your home?

## Activity

Think about things that will help your home be filled with love. Write two or three ideas of things that we see, hear, and feel if our homes are filled with love.

### What do you see when you look around your home?

- Example: People helping each other.

### What do you hear family members saying to each other?

### What do family members feel when they are at home?

## Talk with Your Family
