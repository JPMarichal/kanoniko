# Additional Resources

The Joseph Smith Papers website, josephsmithpapers.org, offers many resources that enrich the documents presented in this volume and can aid further research. These include the following:

- High-resolution color images and searchable transcripts of documents

- A glossary of terms that have particular meaning in Latter-day Saint usage, defined as they were used in JS’s time

- More maps, photographs, charts, and other media that contextualize the documents and events herein

- A detailed chronology of JS’s life

- Finding aids that link to JS documents related to selected topics
