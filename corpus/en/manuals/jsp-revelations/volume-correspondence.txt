# JSPVolume Correspondence Chart

The texts and annotation in this study companion were taken from thirteen volumes of The Joseph Smith Papers. The following table identifies the JSP volume from which the content of each Doctrine and Covenants section in this compilation was taken. The left column lists the twelve JSP volumes, chronologically by publication date. Full bibliographic entries are given, allowing readers to identify the historians responsible for the scholarship in each of the volumes. The right column lists the Doctrine and Covenants sections, as found in the modern (2013) Latter-day Saint edition, that are treated in each JSP volume. In addition to the volumes listed below, the first two volumes of the Revelations and Translations series of The Joseph Smith Papers feature images and transcripts of most of the texts in these Doctrine and Covenants sections; they are also the source of the two appendixes herein.
