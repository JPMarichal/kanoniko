General Conference Leadership Meeting—April 2023

# Helping the Rising Generation Bind Themselves to the Savior through Temple Covenants

Instruction from Elder David A. Bednar and Elder Gerrit W. Gong

## Making Sacred Promises

### Leaders and councils help members of the rising generation:

- Focus on Heavenly Father’s plan of happiness and our Savior’s Atonement

- Learn for themselves the conditions and promised blessings of ordinances and covenants

- Worship and serve in the House of the Lord as often as feasible

An individual and personal covenant connection with Heavenly Father and the Lord Jesus Christ provides purpose, direction, strength, and protection.
