# You can help with God’s work

Moses 1:39

Heavenly Father loves all of His children. He wants to help them find happiness and joy, return to Him, and gain eternal life. This is called His work of salvation and exaltation, and He invites you to help!

## Eternal Truths

God’s work and glory is to bring to pass your immortality and eternal life. Immortality is to live forever with a resurrected body. Eternal life, or exaltation, is to become more like God and live with Him eternally as families. All of this is possible because of Jesus Christ and His Atonement. You can trust Him.

You can help with God’s work by coming unto Christ and helping others do the same. Anytime you do anything to help someone follow Jesus Christ, you are helping with God’s work.

## Invitations

Live the gospel. Build your faith in Jesus Christ. Repent daily. Receive God’s priesthood ordinances and keep your covenants. Make that the pattern for the rest of your life.

Care for those in need. Find ways to serve and minister to others, including your family, your friends, and your community. One great way to help people in need is to obey the law of the fast and give fast offerings.

Invite all to receive the gospel. Let your words and actions reflect your faith in Jesus Christ. Be ready to tell anyone who asks you about the hope and happiness you feel as His disciple. For young men, serving a full-time mission is a priesthood responsibility. For young women, it is a powerful opportunity; pray to know if the Lord would have you serve. Prepare to share the gospel throughout your life.

Unite families for eternity by doing family history and temple work. Learn about your ancestors. Find out who needs temple ordinances. Go to the temple as often as you can to be baptized and confirmed for them. If you need help, go to FamilySearch.org.

## Promised Blessings

You will have God’s help. Heavenly Father does not expect you to do this work on your own. He is with you. When you humbly offer your time and heart, He will do amazing things through you. Jesus Christ is your strength!

You will be filled with joy. God’s work is a joyful work! As you take part in it, you can feel the joy He feels. Imagine the joy you’ll share with Heavenly Father and His children who you helped bring to Him!
