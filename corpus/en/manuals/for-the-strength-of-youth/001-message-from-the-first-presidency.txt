# Message from the First Presidency

Our dear young brothers and sisters,

We love you, and we have confidence in you. You are truly among Heavenly Father’s choice spirits, sent to earth at this time to do important things.

You are stronger and more capable than you may realize. And when you rely on the Savior, your strength grows even more. He is the “strength of youth.”

This guide will help you build a solid foundation for making choices to stay on the path that leads to eternal life. It will help you prepare to make sacred covenants in the temple, prepare to serve a mission, and find joy in following Jesus Christ throughout your life. You have an important place in His Church. Jesus Christ will help you fulfill His purposes for you.

We know God lives. It is our prayer that you will stay firmly on the covenant path—the path of making and keeping sacred covenants with God. That is the path that leads back to your Father in Heaven. As you do, you will be an influence for good, sharing the gospel joyfully, gathering God’s children, and preparing the world for the Second Coming of the Savior. Remember: Jesus Christ is your strength!

The First Presidency
