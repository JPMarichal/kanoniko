# July 7, 1842 • Thursday

Minutes of the

Thirteenth Meeting of the

Society.

Grove July 7^(th)

Meeting opened by singing “When I survey the wondrous cross” &c.¹

Pray’r by sister [Sophia] Packard.

Mrs. President not present,² the busines of the Society was defer’d, and the time spent in exhortation and speaking, singing and prayer, much to the comfort and edification of those present.

The following Donations were made to wit.

  ------------------- ---- ----
                      $    
  Mary Akerly         ..   50
  Catharine Spencer   ..   20
  ------------------- ---- ----

+------------------------------+---------------+

+------------------------------+               |

+------------------------------+---------------+
| Mrs. D[e]uel appropriated    | 27 do [ditto] |
+------------------------------+               |
| to Mrs. Birch                |               |
+------------------------------+---------------+
| Mrs. Winchester appropriated | 25 do         |
+------------------------------+               |
| for Mrs. Hawkes.             |               |
+------------------------------+---------------+

Prayer for the children earnestly recommended by Councillors [Sarah] Cleveland, [Elizabeth Ann] Whitney and others.

Meeting adjourn’d to next week.

[p. [73]]
