# How to Use This Book

This book is your primary resource to help you serve and teach effectively at a historic site of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It is designed for personal study, companionship study, and training with other missionaries at the site.

Consider reading the book at least once from beginning to end—perhaps in the first week or two after you receive it. This will help you understand the scope and significance of your calling at a historic site. After that, return to individual sections of the book often for inspiration, encouragement, and practical help.
