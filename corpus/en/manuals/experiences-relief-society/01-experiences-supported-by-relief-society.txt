# Experiences Supported by Relief Society

The purpose of Relief Society is to help God’s children prepare to return to His presence. Relief Society accomplishes this by supporting experiences for sisters to give and receive the Savior’s love and relief. As they feel of His love, sisters will gain a desire to develop their covenant relationship with God. Experiences supported by the Relief Society help sisters fulfill this important purpose.

The accompanying resources can help you learn about gospel principles that support these experiences. Relief Society leaders prayerfully consider what experiences to focus on in your wards and stakes. You may also use the resources to teach sisters how to strengthen their connection with Jesus Christ.

## Effective Gospel Teaching and Learning in Relief Society

Sisters participate in Sunday instruction and counsel about how the principles taught apply to current challenges, needs, and opportunities. Focusing on the Savior will help sisters strengthen their testimonies, receive temple ordinances, keep covenants, and deepen their discipleship. Counseling as part of Relief Society instruction and discussion helps build unity and a sense of belonging in Relief Society.

- “Gospel Learning in Relief Society Meetings,” General Handbook, 9.2.1.2
