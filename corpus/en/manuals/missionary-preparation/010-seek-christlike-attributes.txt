Lesson 10

# Seek Christlike Attributes

Preach My Gospel, Chapter 6

“One important way to seek Jesus is to make a diligent effort to learn about Him and become more like Him” (Preach My Gospel: A Guide to Sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ [2023], 124). Seeking to develop the attributes of Jesus Christ can help us share the Savior’s gospel more effectively. With the Savior’s help, we can gradually develop attributes that help us become more like Him.

## Review and Follow Up

## Suggested Learning Activity

### How can I become more like the Savior?

#### Study

- What are some of the Savior’s attributes that you admire most? Why?

Read “Introduction” and “Seek This Jesus” in chapter 6 of Preach My Gospel (pages 123–24). Highlight blessings associated with developing Christlike attributes.

- How can striving to become more like Jesus Christ help you become a better missionary?

- What role does the Savior play in helping you become more like Him?

- What has helped you make progress toward becoming more like the Savior?

#### Practice

1.  Complete the “Attribute Activity” at the end of chapter 6 of Preach My Gospel (pages 136–38).

2.  Based on your responses, select a Christlike attribute you want to better understand and develop.

3.  Find and study the section on your chosen attribute in chapter 6 of Preach My Gospel.

#### Set a goal

Create a plan for how you will seek to develop the Christlike attribute you studied. You could use the suggestions under the heading “A Pattern for Becoming More Christlike” (Preach My Gospel, 134) to help you. Don’t worry if you aren’t perfect in this attribute. Keep striving and the Savior will help you.

My goal

- I will accomplish my goal by … (a simple plan).
