Lesson 13

# Go Forward in Faith

This lesson is intended to help learners evaluate their progress and growth as disciples of Jesus Christ during their participation in the Missionary Preparation course.

## Review and Follow Up

## Suggested Learning Activity

### Reflect on spiritual growth

- What do the scriptures say both the Savior and Joseph Smith did to grow spiritually?

- How was Joseph Smith’s life changed by reflecting on what he had read in James 1:5?

Reflect on your spiritual growth since you began this class. Also, consider progress you still want to make. Write down your thoughts and spiritual impressions. Questions such as these can help:

#### Looking back

- Compared to your readiness prior to taking this class, what are some ways you now feel better prepared to serve a mission or to share the gospel with others?

- How has studying Preach My Gospel affected you?

- How has your relationship with Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ changed?

- What experiences during or outside of class have made a difference for you?

#### Moving forward

- What do you still want to do to continue preparing for missionary service? What do you feel your next steps should be to achieve these goals?

- Who do you want to help come closer to Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ? What will you do to help them?

  

  You can increase in your desire to serve God, and you can begin to think as missionaries think, to read what missionaries read, to pray as missionaries pray, and to feel what missionaries feel. You can avoid the worldly influences that cause the Holy Ghost to withdraw, and you can grow in confidence in recognizing and responding to spiritual promptings. Line upon line and precept upon precept, here a little and there a little, you can gradually become the missionary you hope to be and the missionary the Savior expects. (“Becoming a Missionary,” Ensign or Liahona, Nov. 2005, 46)
