Skill Practice 3

# Teach People How to Pray

Many of the people missionaries teach lack experience with prayer. “Inviting people to pray is one of the most important invitations you can extend as a missionary” (Preach My Gospel [2023], 43).

## Define

Our loving Heavenly Father desires His children to communicate with Him through prayer. Missionaries often teach people who have little or no experience with prayer.

Read the section titled “Pray Often” (Preach My Gospel, 77). Look for guidelines that can help you teach others why it is important to pray and how to pray.

- What did you learn about the importance of prayer?

- What did you find that can help others know how to pray?

- Begin by addressing Heavenly Father.

- Express the feelings of our hearts, such as gratitude for the blessings we have received.

- Ask questions, seek guidance, and ask for blessings.

- End by saying, “In the name of Jesus Christ, amen.”

## Demonstrate

## Practice

With your partner, take turns teaching each other about prayer (refer to the guidelines from page 77 of Preach My Gospel as needed). Be sure to explain both why we pray and how to pray. You could also testify that God hears and answers prayers.

## Apply

During the next week, look for an opportunity to practice teaching someone how to pray. This could be with a family member or friend who already knows how to pray, or if you know someone who is unfamiliar with prayer, you could teach them. Also consider how you might improve your personal prayers.
