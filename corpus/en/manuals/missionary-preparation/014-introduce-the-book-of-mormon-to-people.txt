# Introduce the Book of Mormon to People

The Lord has given us the Book of Mormon to aid in conversion. This activity can help learners practice sharing a brief introduction to the Book of Mormon with others.

## Define

“The Book of Mormon, combined with the Spirit, is your most powerful tool in conversion” (Preach My Gospel [2023], 113). As a missionary, you will have many opportunities to introduce people to the Book of Mormon.

Imagine you have a friend who asks you, “What is the Book of Mormon?”

- If you only have a minute or two to respond, what might be some of the most important things for them to understand?

Read “Introducing the Book of Mormon to People” in the “Teaching Insights” box (Preach My Gospel, 42). Look for helpful points to focus on when introducing people to the Book of Mormon.

## Demonstrate

## Practice

Prepare to introduce the Book of Mormon to someone by doing the following:

1.  Write down key points you might share, including what the Book of Mormon is about and how it has influenced you personally.

2.  With a partner, take about 1–2 minutes each and alternate introducing the Book of Mormon to each other.

## Apply

Look for an opportunity to introduce the Book of Mormon to someone in the next day or two. This could be someone who is currently unfamiliar with the book, or you could practice your introduction with a friend or family member.
