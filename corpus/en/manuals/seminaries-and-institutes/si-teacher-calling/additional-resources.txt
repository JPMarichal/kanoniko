# Additional Resources

In addition to the General Handbook, please consider these additional resources to support you in your calling.

Scriptures.The standard works are the words of God and our primary source in teaching youth and young adults.

Current Seminary Curriculum.This year’s curriculum and life lessons focus on the scriptures and the teachings of Church leaders.

Teaching in the Savior’s Way.This resource can be your guide to learn about how the Savior taught. As you strive to teach in His way, the Lord will help you become the teacher He knows you can be.

Objective of Seminaries and Institutes.The objective statement describes the outcomes of our efforts as seminary and institute teachers

Thank you for exercising your faith to the blessing of more and more of Heavenly Father’s children as you provide a spiritual anchor they so desperately need. (Brother Chad H Webb, Administrator of Seminaries and Institutes of Religion, “Spiritual Anchor” [S&I Annual Broadcast January 2024], Gospel Library)
