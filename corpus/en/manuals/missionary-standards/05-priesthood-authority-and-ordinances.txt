5. Priesthood Authority and Ordinances

5.0 Introduction

Priesthood authority is the power of God delegated to man to act in His name for the salvation of His children. All missionary work is conducted under priesthood authority. Understanding the role and proper exercise of priesthood authority is essential for every missionary, regardless of gender or office.

5.1 Priesthood Authority in Missionary Work

Elder missionaries who hold the Melchizedek Priesthood or the Aaronic Priesthood exercise their priesthood authority in performing ordinances and blessings as authorized by their mission president. The Aaronic Priesthood authorizes elders to perform baptisms. The Melchizedek Priesthood authorizes them to confirm new members, confer the Holy Ghost, consecrate oil, and administer to the sick.

All ordinances and blessings must be performed according to the established procedures of the Church. Missionaries should be thoroughly familiar with the proper forms and words for each ordinance they may be called upon to perform. Detailed instructions for performing priesthood ordinances and blessings are provided in the supplement (section 7.11).

The mission president holds the priesthood keys for the mission and must authorize all ordinances performed by missionaries within the mission boundaries. Missionaries should not perform ordinances without proper authorization from the mission president or his designee.

5.2 Women and Priesthood Authority

Sister missionaries serve with full authority under the direction of priesthood leaders. Though they do not hold priesthood office, they are set apart and given authority to teach, testify, and serve. Their contributions are essential and equal in importance to those of elder missionaries.

Sister missionaries participate in the work of salvation by teaching the gospel, bearing testimony, serving in their communities, and ministering to those in need. They may also be called to serve in young missionary leadership roles such as sister training leader.

The authority by which sister missionaries serve comes through their setting apart by priesthood leaders. This setting apart grants them the power and right to represent the Lord and His Church in their missionary calling.

All missionaries, both elders and sisters, should understand and respect the role of priesthood authority in the Church. Priesthood authority is exercised in a spirit of love, service, and humility, never for personal gain or domination.

5.3 Reference to Supplement

For detailed instructions on performing specific priesthood ordinances and blessings, including baptizing, confirming, consecrating oil, administering to the sick, and giving comfort blessings, refer to section 7.11 of the supplement.
