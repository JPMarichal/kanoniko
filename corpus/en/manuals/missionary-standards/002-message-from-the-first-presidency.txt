Message from the First Presidency and Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

Dear Fellow Missionary:

After His Resurrection, Jesus Christ commanded His disciples to "make disciples of all nations, baptizing them...teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you."

You have been called by revelation through God's chosen servants to assist the Apostles in this great work of taking the gospel of Jesus Christ to the world.

When you serve with complete dedication, you fulfill the two great commandments: loving God and loving your neighbor as yourself. Being an effective missionary requires you to be a faithful disciple of Jesus Christ.

You are invited to develop your talents and skills for God's glory. Jesus Christ will magnify your efforts. Heavenly Father loves you and will help you love and bless His children wherever you serve. Through the Atonement of Jesus Christ, He will empower both you and those you serve to become truly "converted unto the Lord."

These missionary standards are approved by Church leadership. They provide physical, spiritual, and emotional protection while helping you become the disciple Jesus Christ needs you to be.

If you have questions about any standard, pray for understanding of its importance. Seek help from your companion, young missionary leaders, or mission leaders as needed. Study these standards regularly with your companion and commit to obeying them.

The Church congratulates you on accepting this call and prays you will experience true peace and joy serving the Lord Jesus Christ.
