1. Your Full-Time Missionary Experience

1.0 Introduction

Your mission represents a continuing chapter in your covenant path rather than a time-bound assignment. The experience begins before your formal setting apart and extends beyond your release date. This period serves as a transformative event within your larger life journey as a disciple of Jesus Christ.

From an eternal perspective, the mission is more than a checkbox to be marked off. It functions as a means to deepen your discipleship. The Prophet Joseph Smith encouraged early Saints, asking them to advance confidently in this great work of salvation, maintaining courage and rejoicing in the ultimate victory ahead.

The handbook invites missionaries to find joy in their service by loving God and serving neighbors. This time is meant for experiencing lasting happiness and peace through Jesus Christ.

1.1 Obedience

True discipleship requires obedience to divine commandments. The Savior taught the connection between love and obedience, establishing this as a foundational principle. Keeping commandments means willing, faithful adherence coupled with joy and gladness.

Jesus Christ exemplified perfect faithfulness by doing only the Father's will. Missionaries should follow this example through best efforts in keeping commandments and living by established standards, learning continuously, correcting mistakes promptly, and accepting personal responsibility.

Even obedient followers may experience troubles and challenges. The handbook acknowledges that hardship can occur despite faithfulness, though the Savior promises comfort and support.

The handbook warns against attempting to negotiate with God by imposing additional sacrifices or adjusting requirements. Approved standards exist for all missionaries; the handbook specifically warns against bargaining through extra effort, fasting beyond the monthly fast, or skipping preparation days.

1.2 Standards for Life

Mission standards function as lifelong guiding principles. Standards addressing personal study, goal-setting, and righteous technology use benefit missionaries during service and throughout their lives. These scriptural commandments and principles should guide decisions beyond the mission years, with God directing and blessing those who remain faithful.
