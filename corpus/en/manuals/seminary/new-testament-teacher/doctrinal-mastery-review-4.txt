# Doctrinal Mastery Review 4

Apply Doctrinal Mastery Scripture Passages

One of the purposes of doctrinal mastery is to help you learn how to apply to your life the doctrine taught in the doctrinal mastery scripture passages. This lesson will give you opportunities to practice applying doctrinal mastery scripture passages to scenarios similar to ones you may encounter in your own life.

## Possible Learning Activities

### How do the scriptures relate to my life?

A young man named Nico has spent a lot of time studying the scriptures. He knows many scripture stories and has memorized a lot of verses. Despite his efforts, Nico has a hard time seeing how the scriptures relate to his own circumstances and questions.

- What might you say to Nico to help him see how the scriptures apply to his life?

- When have you applied a truth from the scriptures to your life?

This lesson will help you apply the truths taught in the doctrinal mastery scripture passages to real-life situations.

### Applying the doctrine to real-life situations

- Your friend asks you, “What is so important about Jesus Christ?”

- As a friend begins to learn about the gospel, they ask, “Why do I need to be baptized? Can’t I just be a good person?”

- You are feeling overwhelmed by life and wonder where you can turn for relief.

- Your parents emphasize how important it is for you to be an example to others.

- Which doctrinal mastery scripture passage do you think relates to this scenario?

- What are some specific words or phrases from this doctrinal mastery scripture passage that would be especially helpful in this situation?
