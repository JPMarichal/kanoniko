Lesson 176: For the Strength of Youth: Making Choices

# Our Divine Identity and Purpose

We Have a Divine Nature and Destiny

As children of heavenly parents, we can become as they are and inherit what they have. These blessings are available to each of us because of Jesus Christ and His Atonement. This lesson can help students feel that as children of God, they have a divine nature and purpose.

## Possible Learning Activities

### Who am I and what is my purpose?

Amelia is 15 years old. She often feels alone and unnoticed by others. She wishes she had more friends and looked different than she does. Amelia sees others doing important things but doesn’t think she is good enough to do anything important. She doubts her self-worth.

- What can make it hard to remember our identity as children of God?

### Divine nature and purpose

Read the scriptures and statements on the handout. Find phrases you think could help Amelia, and consider why they might be helpful.

### The significance of who I am and can become

- What thoughts or feelings did you have as you studied about your divine identity and purpose as a child of God?

- How do you think this knowledge could impact decisions you make or the way you treat others?

- What experiences have you had that have helped you feel that you are a child of God with a divine destiny?

You could collect the papers and select a couple of questions to address during the lesson.</p> </section> <section> <header> <p class="title" data-aid="157052501" id="aside2_title3">Sensing the love of God</p> </header> <p data-aid="157052503" id="aside2_p2">Some students may benefit from learning how they can better feel God’s love for them. Students could identify what they learn about God’s love from the following statement by Elder Dale G. Renlund of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles.</p> <blockquote> <div class="image-cropper"></div> <p data-aid="157052505" id="aside2_p3">You are a beloved [child of God]. Nothing you do—or do not do—can change that. God loves you because you are His spirit [child]. Sometimes we may not feel His love, but it is always there. God’s love is perfect. Our ability to sense that love is not. (Dale G. Renlund, “<a class="cross-ref" href="/study/general-conference/2022/04/36renlund?lang=eng&amp;id=p2#p2">Your Divine Nature and Eternal Destiny</a>,” <cite>Liahona</cite>, May 2022, 70)</p> </blockquote> <p data-aid="157052507" id="aside2_p4">You could ask students a question like this:</p> <ul class="bullet"> <li> <p data-aid="157052509" id="aside2_p5">What can you do that can help you feel God’s love for you?</p> </li> </ul> <p data-aid="157052511" id="aside2_p6">You could then show the segment of his talk that immediately follows this statement. As you do, students could list things they can do to feel God’s love and what they should avoid. Students could then select one thing from their list that they desire to start or stop doing.</p> </section> <section> <header> <p class="title" data-aid="157052514" id="aside2_title4">Videos</p> </header> <p data-aid="157052516" id="aside2_p7">The following videos could help students understand and feel the importance of their divine identity and purpose.</p> <p data-aid="157052518" id="aside2_p8">The video “<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/media/video/2017-01-0005-our-divine-destiny">Our Divine Destiny</a>” (3:42) includes youth sharing how knowing their divine destiny helps their lives.</p> <p data-aid="157052519" id="aside2_p9">The video “<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/media/video/2021-04-0030-running-toward-the-light">Running toward the Light</a>” (17:35) can help students feel the significance of knowing their divine identity and destiny.
