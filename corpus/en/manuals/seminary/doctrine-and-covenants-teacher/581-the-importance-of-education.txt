Lesson 191: Preparing for Future Education and Employment

# The Importance of Education

Education Is Vital to Our Progression

Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ want us to learn and obtain education throughout our lives. Doing so is vital to our progression on earth and throughout eternity. This lesson can help students understand the importance of education.

## Possible Learning Activities

### Why is education important?

Imagine there is a student named Sara who doesn’t like school. She thinks it is a waste of time and causes her too much stress. In addition, she doesn’t have a lot of motivation for learning because she has never done well in school. She would rather hang out with friends, relax at home, or go to work.

At the top of a page in your study journal, create a heading titled “Why is education important?” As you study today, seek revelation from Heavenly Father through the Holy Ghost to see the value of education in your life. Add your insights and spiritual impressions to the list in your journal.

### Study the importance of education

1.  Summarize what you learned from the scripture and statement you studied.

2.  After students have shared all the summaries, discuss the following questions:

3.  What similarities do you find in the statements and scriptures you learned about?

4.  How were the scriptures or statements you studied unique from one another?

5.  Is there anything in one quote or scripture that gives you insight about something in a different quote or scripture?

### Truths about the importance of education

- Which of the truths you identified was most meaningful to you? Why?

- How do you think education can impact your relationship with Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ?

- How have you seen the blessings of obtaining education in your life or the lives of others?

### Explain it in your own words

Using what you have learned and felt today, create a response you could share with Sara that could help her better understand the importance of education. In your response, include at least one reference to a scripture or statement from a Church leader. You could also include your testimony or describe experiences you have had.

Bednar in the handout within the lesson and in the “Additional Resources” section mention learning to love learning. If students would benefit from additional instruction about how to love learning, consider discussing the following questions:</p> <ul class="bullet"> <li> <p data-aid="157092962" id="aside2_p2">What is there to love about learning? Why do we sometimes lose sight of that?</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="157092963" id="aside2_p3">Who do you know who seems to love learning? What do they do that demonstrates their love of learning?</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="157092964" id="aside2_p4">What can help you increase your love for learning?</p> </li> </ul> <p data-aid="157092965" id="aside2_p5">Invite students to share ways they have developed an appreciation or even a love for learning and educational opportunities. Consider listing these on the board. Help students see that the more we come to appreciate our learning and education, the more we will work hard to learn, grow, and improve.</p> </section> <section> <header> <p class="title" data-aid="157092966" id="aside2_title3">How can I become a better learner right now?</p> </header> <p data-aid="157092967" id="aside2_p6">Students can get discouraged when they feel they are not good at learning. Some may struggle in formal or informal settings but the Lord will help them. The following suggestions can guide students as they pursue emotional, intellectual, and spiritual learning.</p> <ol class="decimal" start="1"> <li data-marker="1."> <p data-aid="157092968" id="aside2_p7"><strong>Exercise faith.</strong> If you believe that God can help you reach exaltation, you may find it easier to believe that He can help you learn math or whatever subject you’re struggling with. Take an eternal perspective. Exercising faith helps you be grateful for feedback even if it means losing points or having to do something over again.</p> </li> <li data-marker="2."> <p data-aid="157092969" id="aside2_p8"><strong>Be purposeful in your motivation to learn.</strong> Students who try to meet course requirements and deadlines in a hurried, dismissive, or “check-the-box” approach can miss out on opportunities for learning. In contrast, students who look for connections, opportunities, and a sense of purpose can become transformed by the education experience. They learn so they can change, improve, and grow.</p> </li> <li data-marker="3."> <p data-aid="157092970" id="aside2_p9"><strong>Be willing to change.</strong> Be willing to change and identify how your current way of thinking and acting might be limiting your ability to learn. Look for ways to improve when your performance on a test or assignment is lower than you expected. Change can produce new results.</p> </li> <li data-marker="4."> <p data-aid="157092971" id="aside2_p10"><strong>Seek opportunities to learn and grow.</strong> Because education is important to the Lord, as you seek opportunities to learn and grow, you will grow closer to Jesus Christ and find emotional, intellectual, and spiritual strength.</p> </li> </ol> </section> [/SIDEBAR]
