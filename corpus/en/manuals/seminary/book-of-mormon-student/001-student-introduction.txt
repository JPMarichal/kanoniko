# Introduction to theBook of Mormon Seminary Student Manual

The objective of Seminaries and Institutes of Religion is to help youth and young adults deepen their conversion to Jesus Christ and His restored gospel, qualify for the blessings of the temple, and prepare themselves, their families, and others for eternal life with their Father in Heaven.

The lessons in this manual are designed to help you achieve this objective as you study the Book of Mormon. This manual contains 255 lessons. You will not need to complete all these lessons. Most are aligned with the Come, Follow Me scripture block that individuals and families will be studying. Other lessons are associated with doctrinal mastery. Your teacher will help you know which lessons to complete at home and which to do together as a class.

The work for each lesson includes assignments you will submit to your teacher. These assignments are indicated with this icon: . Carefully consider how you will complete these assignments. This will help you have more meaningful experiences as you learn and apply truths from the scriptures. Pray before you begin your lessons and seek guidance and direction from the Holy Ghost to help in your study. Find opportunities to share with your family and others what you are learning. As you do, you will deepen your conversion to the Savior and His restored gospel.
