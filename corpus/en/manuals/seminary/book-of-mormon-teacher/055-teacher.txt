# Book of Mormon Doctrinal Mastery Review 1

Mark and Memorize

One of the purposes of doctrinal mastery is to help you memorize the doctrinal mastery scripture references and key scripture phrases. This lesson is intended to help you locate, mark, and practice memorizing the scripture references and key scripture phrases for the first 12 Book of Mormon doctrinal mastery scripture passages.

## Possible Learning Activities

### Scripture references and key scripture phrases

- Why would it be useful to know the scripture references and key scripture phrases for doctrinal mastery passages?

To help you learn the scripture references and key scripture phrases for the first 12 doctrinal mastery passages, consider copying the following chart into your study journal or downloading the Doctrinal Mastery app on a mobile device and using the activities in the app to help you with memorization.
