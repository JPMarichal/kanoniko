# Doctrinal Mastery Review 3

Understand and Explain

Understanding specific scripture passages and being able to explain the truths they teach is central to achieving doctrinal mastery. This lesson is intended to help increase your understanding and allow you to practice explaining the doctrine taught in some of the Book of Mormon doctrinal mastery passages.

## Possible Learning Activities

### Understanding doctrinal mastery passages

- Which of these passages do you feel you understand well?

- What experiences have helped you understand the truth(s) of that passage?

Choose at least one of the Book of Mormon doctrinal mastery passages that you would like to understand better and complete one of the following activities to help you deepen your understanding of the doctrine it teaches. If you finish quickly, choose another doctrinal mastery passage and complete another activity.

#### Activity A: Visualizing

Create a drawing, collage, meme, word art, word cloud, or other visual representation that reflects what your passage teaches.

#### Activity B: Sharing with others

Create a plan to teach your passage. This could include questions you would ask to help those you are teaching understand the passage better. You could also consider creating a video in which you answer the questions you created or explain the meaning of key words and phrases.

#### Activity C: Studying independently

Choose one or two key words from the passage to study. Use study helps in your scriptures, the Gospel Library app, or other divinely appointed sources to identify definitions, possible cross-references, or statements by Church leaders that deepen your understanding. Record what you learn, including what these things teach you about Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.

#### Activity D: Making comparisons

Identify tangible objects or create an analogy that could represent the passage or words and phrases from the passage. For example, for 2 Nephi 2:27 you could place a key in one hand to illustrate that we “are free to choose liberty and eternal life” and a lock in the other hand to represent “captivity and death.”

- Why do you think it is important for you to understand the passage you chose?

- How could understanding this passage help you increase your faith in Jesus Christ or become more like Him?
