# Moroni 10

Overview

In some of his last writings, Moroni emphasized that God is the source of all truth. He taught that if we ask God with a sincere heart, real intent, and faith in Christ, we can know the Book of Mormon is true by the power of the Holy Ghost. He testified of gifts that God has given His children through the Spirit to benefit them. In the final verses of the Book of Mormon, Moroni invited all to follow the Savior and be perfected in him.

## Prepare to Teach

The following information provides teachers with ideas of what may need to be prepared in advance for each lesson.

### Moroni 10:1–7

Lesson purpose: This lesson can help students know for themselves that the Book of Mormon is true.

- Student preparation: Invite students to read Moroni 10:1–7, looking for any words or phrases that help them understand what it takes to know the truth about the Book of Mormon.

- Videos: “Moroni Invites All to Come unto Christ” (8:37; watch from time code 4:39 to 5:32); “A Living Testimony” (20:29; watch from time code 13:51 to 14:31)

- Content to display: Instructions for the activity organized by the three phrases of Moroni’s counsel

- Videoconference teaching suggestion: Consider dividing students into breakout groups to discuss the three phrases of Moroni’s counsel using the content included in the lesson. Be sure to copy the content for each group and share it with them in the chat or via email to guide their discussion.

### Doctrinal Mastery: Moroni 10:4–5

Lesson purpose: This lesson can help students memorize the doctrinal mastery reference and key scripture phrase for Moroni 10:4–5, explain the doctrine, and apply principles of acquiring spiritual knowledge in real-life situations.

- Student preparation: Invite students to memorize the scripture reference and key scripture passage for Moroni 10:4–5: “Ask with a sincere heart, with real intent, having faith in Christ. … And by the power of the Holy Ghost ye may know the truth of all things.”

- Videoconference teaching suggestion: After students have had time to prepare their response to the “Questions and Answers” post, consider giving them time to share. This could be done in pairs, in small groups in breakout rooms, or with the entire class.

### Moroni 10:8–25

Lesson purpose: This lesson can help students seek after the gifts of the Spirit.

- Student preparation: Invite students to learn about gifts of the Spirit. They could use scripture study aids such as the Gospel Topics entry “Spiritual Gifts” (topics.ChurchofJesusChrist.org).

- Video: “Serve with the Spirit” (19:12; watch from time code 5:33 to 5:48)

### Moroni 10:26–34

Lesson purpose: This lesson can help students “come unto Christ, and be perfected in him” (Moroni 10:32).

- Student preparation: Invite students to come prepared to share what they think the phrase “come unto Christ” means. Students could search the phrase in the Gospel Library app if they need help.

- Objects: A few items (or images of items) that have distinct purposes

- Content to display: The chart listing Moroni’s invitations and Heavenly Father’s promises

- Video: “Come unto Christ: 2014 Theme Song” (4:48)

- Videoconference teaching suggestion: Students could use the chat feature to share what Moroni’s invitations and promises mean in their own words (see Moroni 10:30–34).

### Assess Your Learning 10

Lesson purpose: This lesson is intended to help students evaluate the progress and growth they have experienced during their study of the Book of Mormon this year.

- Student preparation: Students could reflect on their study of the Book of Mormon and prepare to share an insight or truth they learned that has increased their love for Jesus Christ.

- Videoconference teaching suggestion: For the section “What do you understand about Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ?” consider allowing students to use the whiteboard or chat function to share truths they have learned from the Book of Mormon about Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.
