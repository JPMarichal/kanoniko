# Ether 8

“The Lord Worketh Not in Secret Combinations”

Secret combinations plagued several groups of people throughout the Book of Mormon. This pattern emerged among the Jaredites and even led to their destruction. As he wrote about the Jaredites, the prophet Moroni added important truths we can learn from these destructive occurrences. This lesson can help you understand the need to reject secret combinations by following Jesus Christ.

## Possible Learning Activities

### Acts in secret

- What are some ways Satan uses secrecy to accomplish his evil purposes?

- How would such secrecy impact a person’s relationship with Jesus Christ? Why?

- What would you want someone to know who feels that they can get away with doing evil because they keep it a secret?

Today you will learn about a group of people who participated in secret acts of wickedness known as a secret combination. As you study, look for teachings that help you understand the danger of secret combinations.

### Wickedness among the Jaredites

You might recall that after the Jaredites arrived in the promised land, they appointed a king to rule over them despite the brother of Jared’s warnings that doing so would lead to captivity (see Ether 6:22–23). In the generations that followed, wicked kings caused divisions and suffering among the people.

Read Ether 8:1–10, looking for what took place when a man named Omer became king.

Jared agreed to his daughter’s wicked plan. When Akish asked for Jared’s daughter in marriage, Akish, Jared, his daughter, and other family members entered into a secret combination to kill Omer (see Ether 8:11–15).

### The dangers of secret combinations

A secret combination is “an organization of people bound together by oaths to carry out the evil purposes of the group” (“Secret Combinations,” Guide to the Scriptures, scriptures.ChurchofJesusChrist.org). Satan is the originator of secret combinations (see Helaman 6:25–30).

As the narrator of this story, the prophet Moroni shared several important teachings and warnings about secret combinations. Read Ether 8:18–25, looking for how the Lord feels about secret combinations.

- What did you learn about secret combinations from these verses?

One truth we can learn from these verses is that secret combinations are abominable in the sight of God (Ether 8:18).

To help you further understand why secret combinations are abominable to the Lord, read the following scriptures, looking for what you learn about Jesus Christ:

- 2 Nephi 26:22–24

- Alma 39:8

- What did you learn from these verses that could have helped the people you studied about in Ether 8?

- What did these verses teach you about the Lord?

- How could these teachings help teenagers today?

Moroni concluded his teachings about secret combinations by explaining why he included these warnings. Read Ether 8:26, looking for what Moroni wanted us to know.

- What did you learn about why Moroni included these teachings in the Book of Mormon?

- What have you learned today that could persuade you to follow Jesus Christ more fully?
