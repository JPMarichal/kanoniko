Genesis 5; Moses 6: Lesson 13

# Moses 6:1–39

The Calling of Enoch

When Enoch was called to preach repentance to a wicked group of people, he felt inadequate. The Lord helped and strengthened him to accomplish what he thought he could not do. This lesson can help students feel confidence in the Lord and His power to help and strengthen them.

## Possible Learning Activities

### Feelings of inadequacy

- What are situations where teenagers might experience these kinds of feelings?

Think about a situation in your own life that might be causing you to feel inadequate or overwhelmed. Take a moment and ponder this question:

- How confident do you feel that the Lord will help you in this situation?

### Enoch experiences the Lord’s power

A few generations after Adam, one of his descendants, Enoch, lived among hard-hearted and wicked people. Read Moses 6:26–28, looking for what the Lord called Enoch to do.

- How might you have felt if you were Enoch? Why?

### The Lord’s help and strength in your life

- How have you seen or experienced the Lord’s power to give help and strength in your life?

### Reflect on what you learned

- What have I learned or felt today about the Lord that can help me when I feel inadequate, discouraged, or not strong enough?

Invite students to read <a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/6?lang=eng&amp;id=p31#p31">Moses 6:31</a>, looking for the assumptions Enoch might have had about himself, other people, or the Lord. Then, read <a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/6?lang=eng&amp;id=p32-p39#p32">Moses 6:32–39</a>, looking at those same assumptions from the Lord’s view.
