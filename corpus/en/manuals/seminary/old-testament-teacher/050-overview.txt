# Genesis 5;Moses 6

Overview

The Lord called Enoch to preach repentance to a wicked group of people. Although Enoch felt inadequate, the Lord strengthened him. Enoch taught the people why they needed a Savior and how they could come unto Him and receive the blessings only He can offer.

## Prepare to teach

The following information provides you with ideas of what you may need to prepare in advance for each lesson.

### Moses 6:1–39

Lesson purpose: To help students feel confidence in the Lord and His power to help and strengthen them.

- Student preparation: Invite students to think of times people experienced the Lord’s power as he helped them overcome difficulties. Encourage them to look for examples in the scriptures.

- Content to display: Statements found under the heading “Feelings of inadequacy”

- Handout: “Enoch Experiences the Lord’s Power”

- Video: “Becoming True Millennials” (30:29; watch from time code 9:05 to 12:29)

### Moses 6:47–68

Lesson purpose: To help students understand how to come unto Jesus Christ and receive blessings only He can offer.

- Student preparation: Invite students to ask a family member or friend why Jesus Christ is significant to them and what it means to them to come unto Him.

- Image: A picture of Jesus Christ

- Content to display: Self-evaluation in the section titled “Come unto Christ”; chart for study activity

### Doctrinal Mastery Practice 1

Lesson purpose: To give students opportunities to memorize doctrinal mastery references and their key scripture phrases as well as learn and apply divine principles for acquiring spiritual knowledge.

- Student preparation: Consider inviting students to select a doctrinal mastery passage and to work on memorizing the scripture reference and key scripture phrase. They could then consider the value in having those memorized.

- Materials for students: Strips of paper for memorization activity

- Content to display: Doctrinal mastery passages and key scripture phrases; instructions for memorization activity; discussion questions for the acquiring spiritual knowledge activity
