# Easter

Overview

We observe Easter to celebrate the Atonement and Resurrection of the Savior Jesus Christ. It is a wonderful opportunity to reflect on what He has done for us.

## Prepare to Teach

The following information provides suggestions for what you may need to prepare before each lesson.

### Easter

Lesson purpose: This lesson can help students reflect on the peace and hope Jesus Christ offers them.

- Student preparation: Invite students to do one of the four numbered activities at the end of the lesson. For example, study “The Living Christ” document, looking for phrases that testify of the peace and hope the Savior offers. Encourage them to come prepared to share their favorite phrases.

- Content to display: Display a picture of Jesus Christ on the cross, such as “The Crucifixion” and a picture of the Savior’s Resurrection, such as “Christ and Mary at the Tomb”

- Videos: Encourage students to watch the following videos: “The Peace and Hope of Easter” (5), “The Prince of Peace” (2:32), and “Because He Lives” (2:28)
