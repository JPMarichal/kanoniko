# Teachings of Church Leaders

Overview

As evidence of His compassion for us, the Lord sends His messengers to counsel and warn His people (see 2 Chronicles 36:15). We can receive personal revelation from the Lord through the voice of His servants. As we listen to and study the messages of His servants, we can hear the Lord’s voice. These lessons can help students study the Lord’s messages given by His servants.

## Prepare to teach

The following information provides you with ideas of what you may need to prepare for each lesson.

### Preparing for General Conference

Lesson purpose: This lesson can help students prepare to receive messages from the Savior in general conference.

- Student preparation: Invite students to ponder the blessings that could come from watching the next general conference and how they could prepare for it.

- Items to display: “Just for You” note or image, statement from Elder Neil L. Andersen

- Handout: “Getting the Most out of General Conference”

- Materials for students: Sticky notes or a piece of paper for each student

- Video: “The Blessings of General Conference” (2:24)

### Studying the Messages of the Lord’s Servants

Lesson purpose: This lesson can help students include studying the Lord’s messages given by His servants as part of their personal gospel learning.

- Student preparation: Invite students to think about some of the blessings they have received as they have studied recent messages from Church leaders. They may also want to ask a family member or local Church leader for blessings they have experienced. They will have an opportunity during class to share these blessings.

- Items to display: Two statements from President Russell M. Nelson

- Video: “Hear Him” from time code 13:07 to 14:16

### Template: Teachings of Church Leaders

Lesson purpose: This template is intended to be used as a tool to help you teach messages from leaders of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

- Student preparation: Share with students the title of the talk they will study in class. Encourage them to read, watch, or listen to all or a portion of the talk before class. Invite students to prayerfully prepare their hearts for the Holy Ghost to teach them as they study messages of the servants of Jesus Christ.

### “Hope of Israel”

Lesson purpose: This lesson can help students act on President Russell M. Nelson’s invitations.

- Student preparation: Share with students the title of President Nelson’s talk “Hope of Israel.” Encourage them to read, watch, or listen to all or a portion of the talk before class. Invite them to prayerfully prepare their hearts for the Holy Ghost to teach them as they study messages of the servants of Jesus Christ.

- Items to display: Statement from President Russell M. Nelson

- Handouts: “The Gathering of Israel”; “Invitations to Enlist in the Gathering of Israel”

- Video: “Hope of Israel” from time code 29:51 to 33:37
