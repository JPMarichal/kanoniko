Isaiah 58–66: Lesson 129

# Isaiah 60–66

The Savior’s Millennial Reign

With the world in turmoil, we all long for moments of peace. The Savior Jesus Christ will return to the earth and reign for a thousand years, ushering in a time of peace. Isaiah prophesied of this period, which is called the Millennium. This lesson can help students feel an increased excitement for the Savior’s Second Coming and millennial reign.

## Possible Learning Activities

### The Millennium

- What are some problems in the world that you wish would change?

Take a moment to ponder any problems in your life or in the lives of those you love that you wish would change. As you study today, seek the guidance of the Holy Ghost as you look for truths that may help us and the world.

### The final chapters of Isaiah

The final chapters in the book of Isaiah contain Isaiah’s teachings and prophecies about the Savior’s Second Coming and the Millennium.

Read the following verses, marking phrases that help you feel excited for the Savior’s return:

- Isaiah 60:18–20

- Isaiah 62:11–12

- Isaiah 65:17–25

- How would you summarize what the Savior will do for His people when He returns?

### Deepening understanding

- Why do you think having Jesus Christ on the earth will lead to happiness, peace, and joy?

Take a moment to ponder any questions you have about the Millennium and the blessings of the Savior’s return. The Lord has revealed more on the subject.

Select one or two of your own questions about the Millennium or the following questions you are most interested in. Spend several minutes looking for answers in the scriptures and divinely appointed sources. The section titled “Millennium” in Guide to the Scriptures and Topics and Questions on ChurchofJesusChrist.org may be useful.

1.  What does it mean that the Lord will “create new heavens and a new earth?” (Isaiah 65:17). Read Doctrine and Covenants 88:25–26 and Articles of Faith 1:10. Think of what these verses teach about the Savior’s power and His feelings for the righteous.

2.  Will there be death during the Millennium? What does it mean that because of the Savior a child “shall die an hundred years old?” (Isaiah 65:20). Read Doctrine and Covenants 101:29–31 and ponder what these verses teach you about the Savior’s power.

3.  What will happen to Satan because of the Savior’s return? Read some of the following verses: Doctrine and Covenants 45:55, 101:28, 1 Nephi 22:26, and Revelation 20:1–3. Think of what these verses teach you about the Lord.

4.  What roles will the Savior have during the Millennium? What will he do? Read Doctrine and Covenants 29:11 and 45:59 and Articles of Faith 1:10.

5.  What else has the Lord revealed about the Millennium? Read some of the following: Doctrine and Covenants 45:54, 56–58; 101:26–29; and “Millennium” in Topics and Questions on ChurchofJesusChrist.org.

### Visualizing what you learned

Visualizing what you are learning can be effective at times. Close your eyes and visualize what you have learned about the Savior and when He reigns on the earth during the Millennium. Pray for the help of the Holy Ghost to make this activity meaningful for you. Picture the Savior returning and how He may bless you, your loved ones, and all mankind.

Elder Neil L. Andersen of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles testified:

  

  The thought of His coming stirs my soul. It will be breathtaking! The scope and grandeur, the vastness and magnificence, will exceed anything mortal eyes have ever seen or experienced.

  … He will appear “in the clouds of heaven, clothed with power and great glory; with all the holy angels” (Doctrine and Covenants 45:44).

  … We will kneel in reverence, “and the Lord shall utter his voice, and all the ends of the earth shall hear it” (Doctrine and Covenants 45:49) …

  There will be unforgettable reunions with the angels of heaven and the Saints upon the earth (see Moses 7:63). But most important, as Isaiah declares, “All the ends of the earth shall see the salvation of our God,” (Isaiah 52:10) and He “shall reign over all flesh” (Doctrine and Covenants 133:25). (“Thy Kingdom Come,” Ensign or Liahona, May 2015, 122)
