Physical and Emotional Health: Lesson 187

# Developing Healthy Habits

Jesus Christ Can Help Us Change Our Habits

Actions that we repeat over time become habits. Some habits can help us better follow the Savior. Other habits might be unhealthy ways of dealing with difficult emotions and could lead to our feeling distant from Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ. Establishing healthy habits helps us feel close to and become more like Them. This lesson can help students seek help from Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ to develop healthy habits.

## Possible Learning Activities

### Habits are like a garden

Imagine a friend wanted to know what they would need to do to turn a piece of weed-covered land into a garden.

- What would you counsel them to do?

- How could the process of planting a garden be compared to establishing healthy habits?

- What effect do habits have on our lives?

- What can make correcting unhealthy habits or establishing healthy habits difficult?

### Jesus Christ can help us change our habits

Read Ezekiel 36:26–27; Mosiah 3:19; and Ether 12:27, looking for what these scriptures teach about how Jesus Christ can help us change.

- How might these verses apply to changing our habits?

President Jeffrey R. Holland of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles also taught about changing our habits:

  

  Of course, we all have some habits or flaws or personal history that could keep us from complete spiritual immersion in this work. …[T]here is divine help for every one of us at any hour we feel to make a change in our behavior. God gave Saul “another heart.” Ezekiel called on all of ancient Israel to cast off her past and “make…a new heart and a new spirit.” Alma called for a “mighty change” that would cause the soul to expand, and Jesus Himself taught that “except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.” Clearly the possibility of change and living at a more elevated level has always been one of the gifts of God to those who seek it. (“The Greatest Possession,” Liahona, Nov. 2021, 9)

- How would you summarize what these scriptures and President Holland teach about habits into a statement of truth?

- Why do you think we need the help of Jesus Christ to change our habits?

- What are some ways we can come unto Him so He can help us change our habits?

### Gideon’s example

After a period of wickedness, the Israelites were oppressed by the Midianites. The Lord prepared a man named Gideon to help deliver Israel from their enemies (see Judges 6:11–14).

As he received the Lord’s assignment, Gideon expressed concern that he would not be able to fulfill it.

Read Judges 6:15–16, looking for how the Lord responded to Gideon’s fears.

Gideon took his concerns to the Lord. The Lord reassured Gideon that He would not be facing this task alone (see Judges 6:17–24).

Read Judges 6:25–26, looking for what changes the Lord asked Gideon to make.

- Why do you think the Lord instructed Gideon to first tear down the altar to Baal and then build a new altar to the Lord?

- If we compare these two altars (building the new altar to the Lord after tearing down one to Baal) to our habits, what can this example teach about what the Lord would have us do to change? What are some ways He can help us?

### Establishing healthy habits

Read Alma 13:28–29, looking for ways we can come unto Jesus Christ so He can help us change our habits.

- What did you find?

- What are some additional ways we can come unto Him?

### The Savior will help us change our habits

- How has the Savior inspired you or someone you know to make changes to become more like Him?

- If someone expressed that it is hard to change their habits, what would you want them to remember about Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ?
