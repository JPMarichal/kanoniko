# Isaiah 60–66

The Savior’s Millennial Reign

Visit “Studying the Scriptures” to help prepare yourself spiritually to learn.

With the world in turmoil, we all long for moments of peace. The Savior Jesus Christ will return to the earth and reign for a thousand years, ushering in a time of peace. Isaiah prophesied of this period, which is called the Millennium. This lesson can help you feel an increased excitement for the Savior’s Second Coming and millennial reign.

## Study the Scriptures

What are some problems in the world that you wish would change? Take a moment to ponder any problems in your life or in the lives of those you love that you wish would change. As you study today, seek the guidance of the Holy Ghost. Look for truths that may help you.

The final chapters in the book of Isaiah contain Isaiah’s teachings and prophecies about the Savior’s Second Coming and the Millennium.

Read the following verses, marking phrases that help you feel excited for the Savior’s return:

- Isaiah 60:18–20

- Isaiah 62:11–12

- Isaiah 65:17–25

Take a moment to ponder any questions you have about the Millennium and the blessings of the Savior’s return. Record your questions in your notebook.

In your notebook, summarize what you have learned about the Savior’s Second Coming and Millennium in one or two sentences.
