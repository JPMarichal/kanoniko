2 Samuel 11–12; 1 Kings 3; 6–9; 11: Lesson 80

# Assess Your Learning 5

Numbers–Judges

Visit “Studying the Scriptures” to help prepare yourself spiritually to learn.

Reflecting on and assessing your spiritual learning can help you draw closer to Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ. This lesson can help you reflect on the goals you have set and the growth you have experienced during your recent study of the Old Testament.

## Review the Scriptures

Look at the image below that shows the growth of a seed into a plant. You will refer to this image when doing the activities that follow.

Think about the following questions:

- Why is it helpful to take the time to regularly check the growth of a plant?

- How might caring for the plant be different at each stage? What might be similar?

Ponder where you felt you were spiritually as you began your study of the Old Testament and where you are today. Relate your spiritual growth to the image of the growing seed. In your notebook, complete the following exercise:

1.  On the left page of your study journal, draw the plant (A, B, C, D, or E) that best represents where you feel you were spiritually at the beginning of the year.

2.  On the right page of your study journal, draw the plant (A, B, C, D, or E) that best represents where you feel you are spiritually today.

3.  Write a paragraph describing the changes you have experienced in seminary this year. Then invite the Holy Ghost to help you recognize one or two adjustments you could make to grow closer to the Savior. Write those adjustments in your notebook.

Review the following study options. Complete one or more.

## Share Your Learning

Lesson Purpose: To help you reflect on goals you have set and the growth you have experienced during your recent study of the Old Testament.

Share one of the following with your teacher:

- The paragraph you wrote describing your effort to progress toward Jesus Christ in your spiritual growth in seminary this year.

- The outline you wrote about the role or title of Jesus Christ.

- Your responses to the questions about how you can increase your desire to worship God and Jesus Christ in the temple.

- Your responses to the questions about how you can receive and recognize revelation.
