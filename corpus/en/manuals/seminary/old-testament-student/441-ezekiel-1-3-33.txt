# Ezekiel 1–3;33

“I Have Made Thee a Watchman”

Visit “Studying the Scriptures” to help prepare yourself spiritually to learn.

How can we stay safe with so many spiritual dangers in the world? The Lord calls prophets to serve as watchmen to warn and protect us. The Lord called Ezekiel to serve as a watchman for the Israelites while in Babylonian captivity (see Ezekiel 3:16–17). This lesson can help you feel a greater desire to heed the warnings of the Lord’s prophets.

## Study the Scriptures

Having 165 floors and standing 828 meters (2,717 feet) tall, the Burj Khalifa in Dubai offers a unique view. From its observation deck, you can see up to 95 kilometers (59 miles), more than 20 times farther than the distance visible standing on flat ground.

While the Burj Khalifa is an extreme example, people have used towers to get a better view for thousands of years. The Lord used this concept to teach Ezekiel a valuable lesson about prophets.

Ezekiel had been captured and taken to Babylon, along with other Israelites. While in captivity, he was visited by the Lord and called to be a prophet (see Ezekiel 1–2).

Read Ezekiel 3:16–17 and mark what the Lord compared Ezekiel to.

In ancient times, watchmen were positioned on towers so that they could see what those below them could not. Their job was to warn the people of approaching enemies, fires, predators, or other threats.

In Ezekiel 33, the Lord again taught Ezekiel his responsibility to serve as a watchman and share God’s message with Israel (see Ezekiel 33:1–7).

Link Ezekiel 3:16–17 to Ezekiel 33:1–7. Then read Ezekiel 33:1–7, looking for the Lord’s explanation of how prophets are like watchmen.

Now that you’ve studied about watchmen, make a note next to Ezekiel 3:17 explaining why having spiritual watchmen is so important.
