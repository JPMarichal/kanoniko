## Learn How

### Crossing the Jordan River

When Joshua and the children of Israel arrived in Canaan, the Lord promised them all the land on the other side of the Jordan River (see Joshua 1:1–7). However, they faced the obstacle of crossing the overflowing river to achieve their goal (see Joshua 3:15).

Read Joshua 3:6–8, 13–17. Mark the following:

- The faithful actions the priests took to enable the children of Israel to cross the Jordan River

- God’s help in enabling the children of Israel to cross over the Jordan River

Complete the following statement based on what you learned from the story of Joshua and the priests of Israel that could apply to overcoming obstacles to furthering your education. Add your completed statement in a note next to one of the passages you marked.

- As I move forward with faith in Jesus Christ, He will …

Moving forward with faith in Jesus Christ includes any effort you might make that is in accordance with God’s will. For example, the Lord might inspire you to talk with a counselor, earn money, or faithfully pay tithing. These are among ways to move forward with faith in Jesus Christ to overcome obstacles to education.

How would acting with faith in Jesus Christ to pursue education help us become more like Him? Why might faithfully seeking God’s help be necessary to overcome obstacles to education?

### Practice overcoming obstacles

In your notebook, answer the following questions.

- What resources might be available to help this person overcome the obstacle you just listed? Whom could they talk to about the obstacle?

Now consider your own situation. What challenges might you need to overcome to further your education? Here is a list of ideas to help you address these obstacles.

#### Financial Resources

- Scholarships, grants, student loans

- Work-study programs

- Part-time jobs

- The Perpetual Education Fund or other financial resources through the Church

#### Academic Resources

- Academic tutors or mentors

- Free online tutoring programs

- Standardized-test preparation courses

#### Support Resources

- Teachers, family, or friends

- Counseling professionals

- Ward or branch members and leaders

- Welfare and Self-Reliance Services, including self-reliance courses

- Family Services

#### Spiritual Resources

- Scripture study

- Prayer to seek the Lord’s help in:

  - Recognizing your strengths and abilities

  - Making decisions

  - Knowing what the Lord would want you to do

- Studying your identity as:

  - A child of God

  - A child of the covenant

  - A disciple of Jesus Christ

- Paying a full tithe

- Fasting and fast offerings

- Priesthood blessings

Choose one or more ideas from the list. Search online or ask someone for resources to share with your class. Here are some examples:

1.  Find a list of local scholarships.

2.  Share scriptures that boost your confidence.

3.  Talk with a school counselor about available resources.

Share on the discussion board the resources you found.
