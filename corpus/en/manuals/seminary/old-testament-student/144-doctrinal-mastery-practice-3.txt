# Doctrinal Mastery Practice 3

Memorizing Key Phrases and Applying Principles of Acquiring Spiritual Knowledge

Visit “Studying the Scriptures” to help prepare yourself spiritually to learn.

Doctrinal mastery can help you build the foundation for your life upon Jesus Christ and His gospel. This lesson can help you memorize the key scripture phrases in doctrinal mastery passages. It can also help you apply the divine principles of acquiring spiritual knowledge.

## Memorize

Think about some benefits of having friends.

Elder Richard G. Scott (1928–2015) of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles stated:

  

  Great power can come from memorizing scriptures. To memorize a scripture is to forge a new friendship. (“The Power of Scripture,” Ensign or Liahona, Nov. 2011, 6)

How do you think a memorized scripture phrase or passage could be like a friend?

Elder Scott continued:

  

  It is like discovering a new individual who can help in time of need, give inspiration and comfort, and be a source of motivation for needed change. (“The Power of Scripture,” Ensign or Liahona, Nov. 2011, 6)

Respond to one or more of the following questions in your notebook:

- When has a scripture phrase or passage you have memorized been like a friend to you?

- What strategies could help you memorize scripture phrases or passages?
