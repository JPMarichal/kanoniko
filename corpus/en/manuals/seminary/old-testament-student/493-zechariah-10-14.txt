Haggai 1–2; Zechariah 1–4; 7–14: Lesson 157

# Zechariah 10–14

“The Day of the Lord Cometh”

Visit “Studying the Scriptures” to help prepare yourself spiritually to learn.

Through His prophets, the Lord reveals details about future events so we can recognize and look forward to them with confidence. Zechariah prophesied of when the Savior will return to the earth and protect His people from the nations gathered to fight against them. This lesson can help you feel more confident that the Savior will be with you during the events preceding the Second Coming.

## Study the Scriptures

As the world prepared to move from 1999 to the year 2000, some people were very concerned. Fears spread that a computer glitch known as the Millennium Bug could cause major technological failures. Many thought the world might come to an end. Other people prepared to celebrate.

Another event that sparks strong emotions for people is the Savior’s Second Coming.

In your notebook, answer the following questions:

- What words describe how you feel about the Savior’s Second Coming?

- What influences how you feel about this event?

Zechariah prophesied that in the last days, a great battle, known as the battle of Armageddon, will take place near Jerusalem. All the nations of the earth will be gathered against Jerusalem (see Zechariah 12:2–3). As part of the battle, Jerusalem will be under siege as armies encompass the city (see Zechariah 14:2 and Luke 21:20). The Savior will then appear on the Mount of Olives (see Zechariah 14:4).

Reading about future events with armies, battles, and sieges can be frightening. But the Savior will be with us. As you study about the Savior’s Second Coming, let the Holy Ghost help you feel the Savior’s comfort and love. 

One way we can experience joy when we think about Christ’s Second Coming is by keeping our focus on Him and learning what He will do for us at His coming.

Read the following passages to learn what the Savior will do for people during His Second Coming. Mark words or phrases that you want to remember.

What will the Savior do for …

- His covenant people before His Second Coming? Zechariah 10:6, 8–9, 12 (Note: In verse 8, hiss means “call.”)

- The people under attack at Jerusalem? Zechariah 12:8–9; 14:2–5, 8–9

- The Jews who acknowledge Him as the Messiah? Zechariah 12:10; 13:6, 8–9 (see also Doctrine and Covenants 45:51–53)

In your notebook, record the truth Jesus Christ will return in the last days and gather, save, and protect His people. Record your responses to the following questions:

- How can knowing this truth about Jesus Christ help us face His Second Coming with confidence?

- What are some ways Jesus Christ gathers, saves, and protects His people today?
