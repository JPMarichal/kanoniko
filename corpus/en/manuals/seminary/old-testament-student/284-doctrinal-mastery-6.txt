## Review the Doctrine

Think of a time when you read a scripture and it was confusing. How did you react when this happened? Why do you think Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ are happy when we try to understand scripture?

It is normal not to understand everything in the scriptures. With time and with practice, your ability to understand the scriptures will increase.

Complete one of the following activities:

1.  Link
    Find other scriptures that might increase your understanding of the doctrinal mastery passage. You might use the passage’s footnotes. You could also identify a doctrine taught in the passage and use the Guide to the Scriptures or the Topical Guide to find scriptures that teach more about that doctrine. Consider linking the other scriptures you find with your doctrinal mastery passage. You could also record in your notebook how using these resources helped increase your understanding of the doctrinal mastery passage.

2.  Define words
    Identify words in the doctrinal mastery passage that you do not understand. Look up the definitions of those words in available resources. You could check the Guide to the Scriptures, the Bible Dictionary, or a general dictionary. Record the definitions you discover in your notebook, your scripture margins, or the Gospel Library. Read the scripture passage again, using the definitions you found.

Respond to one of the following questions in your notebook:

- How did linking increase your understanding?

- How did defining words help you see these scriptures more clearly?
