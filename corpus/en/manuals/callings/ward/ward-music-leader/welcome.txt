# Welcome

Welcome to your new calling. We are grateful for the love and commitment to Heavenly Father you have shown by your willingness to serve His children. Through you, He can touch hearts, strengthen conversion, and guide His sons and daughters along the covenant path.

## Resources to Know

The Church’s General Handbook and My Calling resources will help you navigate your calling. Read the descriptions below to better understand how to use these two resources.

General Handbook: General Handbook: Serving in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints provides detailed instructions for your various responsibilities. Please make use of this handbook throughout your service and prayerfully consider the direction it offers.

My Calling: My Calling provides an overview of your new responsibilities. Included with these summaries are several links to the General Handbook for more detailed information.
