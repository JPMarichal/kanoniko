# Additional Resources

In addition to the General Handbook, please consider these additional resources to support you in your calling.

Children’s Songbook.Music for children to help them learn the gospel in class and singing time.

“Appendix B: For Primary—Preparing Children for a Lifetime on God’s Covenant Path,” Come, Follow Me—For Home and Church.Learning activities to help prepare children for the covenants they will make.

Teaching in the Savior’s Way: For All Who Teach in the Home and in the Church (2022).Guidance for how to teach children and support teachers.

Simplified Instruction for Primary Meetings.A simple overview of singing time and class time in Primary.
