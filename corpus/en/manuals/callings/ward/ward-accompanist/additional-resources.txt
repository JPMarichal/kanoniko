# Additional Resources

In addition to the General Handbook, please consider these additional resources to support you in your calling.

Church Music Library.The Church’s full collection of congregational, choral, solo, and instrumental music.

Basic Keyboarding CourseBeginners piano course to learn to play the hymns.

New Ward Organist—Training Materials.Training for accompanying the hymns on the organ.

Hymns Organ Transformations.Simplified adaptations of the hymns for organ prelude and postlude.

Simplified Organ ArrangementsCollection of simplified organ accompaniments for select hymns.

Using the Hymnbook.Instruction for using the resources included in the hymnbook.

Using the Children’s Songbook.Instruction for using the resources included in the Children’s Songbook.

Owners’ Manuals for Church OrgansUser instruction for Church-supplied organs and pianos.

Guitar Chord ChartSimple chart with guitar fingering for playing hymns and children’s songs.
