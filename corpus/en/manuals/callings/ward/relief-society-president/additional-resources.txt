# Additional Resources

In addition to the General Handbook, please consider these additional resources to support you in your calling.

Relief Society Home Page, ChurchofJesusChrist.org.Home page for the Relief Society organization of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

2024 Worldwide Relief Society Devotional, Gospel Library.President Russell M. Nelson and the General Relief Society Presidency teach about giving and receiving the Savior’s relief and the blessings and power of a covenant relationship with God.

Caring for Those in Need, Gospel Library.Resources for leaders (counseling resources), life help, self-reliance, temporal preparedness, helping those in need, and addiction recovery.

Sydney Walker, “How Counseling Together Can Increase Unity and Revelation in Sunday Relief Society Meetings,” Church News, Nov. 16, 2022, thechurchnews.com.How counseling in Sunday meetings can bless Relief Society sisters.

General Handbook: Serving in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, 9.2.1.2, Gospel Library.Resource for organizing Relief Society meetings.

Global Initiative for Women and Children, caring.ChurchofJesusChrist.org.Global progress starts with women and children. Learn what Relief Society sisters can do in their own families and communities to be a part of our global initiative.
