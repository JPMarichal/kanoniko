## How to Use This Manual

My Foundation helps individuals learn and live doctrinal principles that lead to spiritual and temporal self-reliance. It works best when reviewed in a small group of 8 to 12 people or as a family. Each week a different group member takes a turn being the facilitator. The facilitator does not teach the principle to the group. Instead, he or she follows the material and encourages all to participate. All videos can be found online at srs.lds.org/videos.

## Self-Reliance Groups

Self-reliance groups can help you improve your personal finances, job, small business, or education. To join a self-reliance group, contact your stake self-reliance specialist.
