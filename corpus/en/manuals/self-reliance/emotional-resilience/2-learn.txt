## 3. Responding to Triggers

[SIDEBAR] <header> <p class="title" data-aid="145667438" id="aside1_title1">ACTIVITY (10 minutes)</p> </header> <p data-aid="145667448" id="aside1_p1"><strong>Step 1:</strong> We have defined what triggers are and how and when you might experience them. Answer the following questions on your own; they will help you identify where, when, and with whom your triggers may happen. Then list a few triggers that occur repeatedly in your life.</p> <p data-aid="145667453" id="aside1_p2">What mood(s) do I struggle with the most?</p> <span class="record-your-impressions" data-rows="2"></span> <p data-aid="145668469" id="aside1_p3">What days of the week or times of day are hardest for me?</p> <span class="record-your-impressions" data-rows="2"></span> <p data-aid="145668482" id="aside1_p4">What types of people are difficult for me to be around?</p> <span class="record-your-impressions" data-rows="2"></span> <p data-aid="145668500" id="aside1_p5">What situations or events are most difficult for me?</p> <span class="record-your-impressions" data-rows="2"></span> <p data-aid="145668516" id="aside1_p6">What are a few triggers that occur repeatedly in my life?</p> <span class="record-your-impressions" data-rows="2"></span> <p data-aid="145668530" id="aside1_p7"><strong>Step 2:</strong> As a group, review some common triggers. Read the example and then fill out the other two triggers together. First, identify how the trigger might make you feel. Then fill out common responses and healthier alternatives.</p> <p data-aid="145668670" id="aside1_p24"><strong>Step 3:</strong> On your own, select one of the triggers you wrote down in step 1. Write down how you feel because of the trigger. Then fill out common responses you have and healthier alternatives.</p> [/SIDEBAR]
