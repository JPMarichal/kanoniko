# My Foundation: Receive Temple Ordinances—Maximum Time: 20 Minutes

[SIDEBAR] <header> <p class="title" data-aid="145665397" id="aside1_title1"><span class="uppercase">Activity</span> (5 minutes)</p> </header> <p data-aid="145665399" id="aside1_p1"><strong>Step 1:</strong> With a partner, read the quote by President Boyd K. Packer and the scripture passages below from the Kirtland Temple dedication prayer. Underline the promised blessings for those who worship in the temple.</p> <p data-aid="145970042" id="aside1_p2">“The Lord will bless us as we attend to the sacred ordinance work of the temples. Blessings there will not be limited to our temple service. We will be blessed in all of our affairs” (Boyd K. Packer, <cite>The Holy Temple</cite> (1980), 182).</p> <p data-aid="145665401" id="aside1_p3">“And that they may grow up in thee, and receive a fulness of the Holy Ghost, and be organized according to thy laws, and be prepared to obtain every needful thing” (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/109?lang=eng&amp;id=p15#p15">Doctrine and Covenants 109:15</a>).</p> <p data-aid="145665403" id="aside1_p4">“And when thy people transgress, any of them, they may speedily repent and return unto thee, and find favor in thy sight, and be restored to the blessings which thou hast ordained to be poured out upon those who shall reverence thee in thy house” (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/109?lang=eng&amp;id=p21#p21">Doctrine and Covenants 109:21</a>).</p> <p data-aid="145665406" id="aside1_p5">“And we ask thee, Holy Father, that thy servants may go forth from this house armed with thy power, and that thy name may be upon them, and thy glory be round about them, and thine angels have charge over them” (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/109?lang=eng&amp;id=p22#p22">Doctrine and Covenants 109:22</a>).</p> <p data-aid="145665407" id="aside1_p6">“We ask thee, Holy Father, … that no weapon formed against them shall prosper” (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/109?lang=eng&amp;id=p24-p25#p24">Doctrine and Covenants 109:24–25</a>).</p> <p data-aid="145665409" id="aside1_p7"><strong>Step 2:</strong> On your own, ponder, “What do I need to change in my life to participate in temple ordinances more often?”</p> [/SIDEBAR]
