## 3. Be Understanding and Nonjudgmental

[SIDEBAR] <header> <p class="title" data-aid="145668117" id="aside1_title1"><span class="uppercase">Activity</span> (5 minutes)</p> </header> <p data-aid="145668120" id="aside1_p1"><strong>Step 1:</strong> We can remember the Savior’s example of compassion for others when we are tempted to judge or criticize. “When you meet someone, treat them as if they were in serious trouble, and you will be right more than half the time” (Henry B. Eyring, “<a class="cross-ref" href="/study/general-conference/2018/10/try-try-try?lang=eng&amp;id=p4#p4">Try, Try, Try</a>,” <cite>Ensign</cite> or <cite>Liahona,</cite> Nov. 2018, 90).</p> <p data-aid="145668123" id="aside1_p2"><strong>Step 2:</strong> Below are some thoughts that can help us be more understanding when we are tempted to be judgmental. As a group, take turns reading the following statements and discuss any thoughts you have.</p> <ul class="bullet"> <li> <p data-aid="145668127" id="aside1_p3">“They are also a child of God.”</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="145668131" id="aside1_p4">“They are probably doing the best they can.”</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="145668135" id="aside1_p5">“I don’t know their situation.”</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="145668139" id="aside1_p6">“They could be going through a major trial.”</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="145668142" id="aside1_p7">“We are more similar than different.”</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="145668146" id="aside1_p8">“I don’t know everything.”</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="145668151" id="aside1_p9">“Everyone has strengths and weaknesses.”</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="145668155" id="aside1_p10">“The Savior loves them as much as He loves me.”</p> </li> </ul> <p data-aid="145668160" id="aside1_p11"><strong>Step 3:</strong> As a group, discuss other thoughts that help you when you are tempted to judge others.</p> [/SIDEBAR]

## 4. Communicate with “I” Messages

| Examples of “You” Messages                                                     | Examples of “I” Messages                                                                                              |
+================================================================================+=======================================================================================================================+
| Examples of “You” Messages                                                     | Examples of “I” Messages                                                                                              |
|                                                                                |                                                                                                                       |
| “You never listen to anyone, and you’re not really listening to me now.”       | “I feel discouraged when I don’t think I am understood. Keeping eye contact helps me feel like you care.”             |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Examples of “You” Messages                                                     | Examples of “I” Messages                                                                                              |
|                                                                                |                                                                                                                       |
| “You are so inconsiderate and thoughtless for missing dinner and not calling.” | “I feel neglected when you miss dinner without calling. I also feel worried that something may have happened to you.” |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

Write out a few of them in the first column of the table.</p> <p data-aid="145668313" id="aside2_p4"><strong>Step 2:</strong> In the second column, write “I” messages to rephrase the negative “you” messages and make them more positive.</p> <p data-aid="145668319" id="aside2_p5"><strong>Step 3:</strong> Pair up with another person, and if you feel comfortable doing so, share what you wrote.
