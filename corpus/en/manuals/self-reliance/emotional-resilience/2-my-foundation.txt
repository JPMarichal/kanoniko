# My Foundation: Our Divine Identity and Purpose—Maximum Time: 20 Minutes

Satan uses discouragement to cause us to doubt God’s love for us and our divine nature and purpose.</p> <p data-aid="145667206" id="aside1_p2"><strong>Step 1:</strong> With a partner, read the differences between <i>worth</i> and <i>worthiness</i> in the table below (see Joy D. We don’t have to “earn” it.</p> </td> <td><div class="card-label"> <p class="label" data-aid="145667215" id="aside1_p4">Worthiness</p> </div> <p data-aid="145667250" id="aside1_p10">If we sin, we are less worthy but never worth less to God.
