# My Foundation: Become One, Serve Together—Maximum Time: 20 Minutes

[SIDEBAR] <header> <p class="title" data-aid="145668163" id="aside1_title1"><span class="uppercase">Activity</span></p> </header> <p data-aid="145668168" id="aside1_p1"><strong>Step 1:</strong> As a group, think of someone who needs help.</p> <p data-aid="145668173" id="aside1_p2"><strong>Step 2:</strong> Discuss the talents, contacts, and resources you have to offer.</p> <p data-aid="145668178" id="aside1_p3"><strong>Step 3:</strong> Make a plan to serve that person. For example, you could:</p> <ul class="bullet"> <li> <p data-aid="145668184" id="aside1_p4">Perform a service project in your community.</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="145668190" id="aside1_p5">Help that person prepare a family history using the booklet <cite><a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/bc/content/ldsorg/topics/my-family-booklet-pdf/english-whole-pdf.pdf">My Family: Stories That Bring Us Together</a>.</cite> Then go to the temple and perform sacred ordinances for family members who have died.</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="145668196" id="aside1_p6">Help the person on his or her path to self-reliance.</p> </li> </ul> [/SIDEBAR]
