# My Foundation: Live a Balanced Life—Maximum Time: 20 Minutes

Ponder:How do I keep the daily demands of life in balance?

Watch:“Of Regrets and Resolutions,” available at srs.lds.org/videos. (No video? Read page 55.)

Discuss:What did the Savior do to achieve a balanced life?

Read:Mosiah 4:27, 2 Nephi 9:51, and the quotes by Elder M. Russell Ballard (on the right) and President James E. Faust (on page 55)

[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="133533423" id="aside1_p1">“See that all these things are done in wisdom and order; for it is not requisite that a man should run faster than he has strength.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="133533424" id="aside1_p2"><span class="uppercase">Mosiah</span> 4:27</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="133533425" id="aside2_p1">“Do not spend money for that which is of no worth, nor your labor for that which cannot satisfy.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="133533426" id="aside2_p2">2 <span class="uppercase">Nephi</span> 9:51</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="133533427" id="aside3_p1">“Just do the very best you can each day. Do the basic things and, before you realize it, your life will be full of spiritual understanding that will confirm to you that your Heavenly Father loves you. When a person knows this, then life will be full of purpose and meaning, making balance easier to maintain.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="133533428" id="aside3_p2"><span class="uppercase">M. Russell Ballard</span>, “Keeping Your Life in Balance,” <em>Liahona,</em> Sept. 2012, 50</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="133533429" id="aside4_p1">“Balance in large measure is knowing the things that can be changed, putting them in proper perspective, and recognizing the things that will not change.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="133533430" id="aside4_p2"><span class="uppercase">James E. Faust</span>, “The Need for Balance in Our Lives,” <em>Ensign,</em> Mar. 2000, 5</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]

Discuss:What things can be simplified or eliminated from our lives so we can experience more joy?

Commit:Commit to do the following actions during the week. Check the boxes when you complete each action:

- Act on your idea to bring more balance to your life.

- Share with your family or friends what you have learned about finding balance.
