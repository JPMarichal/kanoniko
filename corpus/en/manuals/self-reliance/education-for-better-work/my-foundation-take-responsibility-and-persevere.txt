# My Foundation: Work: Take Responsibility and Persevere—Maximum Time: 20 Minutes

Ponder:Why does Heavenly Father want me to take personal responsibility for my life?

Watch:“Sedrick’s Journey,” available at srs.lds.org/videos. (No video? Read page 99.)

Discuss:How do we learn to keep going, even when the work is difficult?

Read:Doctrine and Covenants 42:42 and the quote by President James E. Faust (on the right)

[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="132877469" id="aside1_p1">“Thou shalt not be idle; for he that is idle shall not eat the bread nor wear the garments of the laborer.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="132877471" id="aside1_p2"><span class="uppercase">Doctrine and Covenants</span> 42:42</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="132877473" id="aside2_p1">“Perseverance is demonstrated by those who … don’t give up even when others say, ‘It can’t be done.’”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="132877475" id="aside2_p2"><span class="uppercase">James E. Faust</span>, “Perseverance,” <em>Ensign</em> or <em>Liahona,</em> May 2005, 51</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]

Discuss:Read the quote by Elder D. Todd Christofferson (on page 99). Why does the Lord expect us to work for what we receive?

[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="132877479" id="aside3_p1"><span class="page-break" data-page="99"></span>“God has designed this mortal existence to require nearly constant exertion. … By work we sustain and enrich life. … Work builds and refines character, creates beauty, and is the instrument of our service to one another and to God. A consecrated life is filled with work, sometimes repetitive, … sometimes unappreciated but always work that improves, … lifts, [and] aspires.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="132877481" id="aside3_p2"><span class="uppercase">D. Todd Christofferson</span>, “Reflections on a Consecrated Life,” <em>Ensign</em> or <em>Liahona,</em> Nov. 2010, 17</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]

[SIDEBAR] <header> <p class="title" data-aid="132877483" id="aside4_title1"><span class="page-break" data-page="98"></span>Activity</p> </header> <p data-aid="132877485" id="aside4_p1"><strong>Step 1:</strong> Choose a partner and read together each step in the pattern below.</p> <p data-aid="132877487" id="aside4_p2"><strong>Step 2:</strong> Ask each other to talk about a very hard task or challenge the other currently faces.</p> <p data-aid="132877489" id="aside4_p3"><strong>Step 3:</strong> Help each other apply the four steps below to the difficult task or challenge.</p> <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/bc/content/shared/content/images/gospel-library/manual/PD60002307/EBW_98.pdf"> </a><a class="open-pdf" data-icon="pdf" href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/bc/content/shared/content/images/gospel-library/manual/PD60002307/EBW_98.pdf" target="_blank">Open PDF</a> <p data-aid="132877514" id="aside4_p16"><strong>Step 4:</strong> Write two or three ways you can move forward with faith, trusting that God will provide.</p> <span class="record-your-impressions" data-rows="2"></span> [/SIDEBAR]

Ponder:Read the quote by President Thomas S. Monson (on the right). How do I react when I experience failure?

[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="132877520" id="aside5_p1">“Our responsibility is to rise from mediocrity to competence, from failure to achievement. Our task is to become our best selves. One of God’s greatest gifts to us is the joy of trying again, for no failure ever need be final.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="132877522" id="aside5_p2"><span class="uppercase">Thomas S. Monson</span>, “The Will Within,” <em>Ensign,</em> May 1987, 68</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]

Commit:Commit to do the following actions during the week. Check the box when you complete each action.

- Choose something that is hard or uncomfortable and finish the task. Write it below.

- Share what you’ve learned today about work and perseverance with your family and friends.
