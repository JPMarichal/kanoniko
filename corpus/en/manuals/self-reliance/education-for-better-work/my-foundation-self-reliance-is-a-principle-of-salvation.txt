Discuss:What is an abundant life?

Discuss:Do you believe there are solutions to your problems? How can we qualify for the Lord’s power to assist us?

Read:The Handbook 2 reference and the quote by Elder Dallin H. Oaks (on the right). Being self-reliant does not mean that we can do or obtain anything we set our mind to. Rather, it is believing that through the grace, or enabling power, of Jesus Christ and our own effort, we are able to obtain all the spiritual and temporal necessities of life we require for ourselves and our families. Self-reliance is evidence of our trust or faith in God’s power to move mountains in our lives and to give us strength to triumph over trials and afflictions.

[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="132876252" id="aside2_p1">“Self-reliance is the ability, commitment, and effort to provide the spiritual and temporal necessities of life for self and family. As members become self-reliant, they are also better able to serve and care for others.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="132876253" id="aside2_p2"><em><span class="uppercase">Handbook 2: Administering the Church</span></em> (2010), 6.1.1</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="132876254" id="aside3_p1">“Whatever causes us to be dependent on someone else for decisions or resources we could provide for ourselves weakens us spiritually and retards our growth toward what the gospel plan intends us to be.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="132876255" id="aside3_p2"><span class="uppercase">Dallin H. Oaks</span>, “Repentance and Change,” <em>Ensign</em> or <em>Liahona,</em> Nov. 2003, 40</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]

Discuss:How has Christ’s grace helped you obtain the spiritual and temporal necessities of life?
