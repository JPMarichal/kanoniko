# Day 9: “Peace Be unto You” (John 20:19)

Easter celebrates the beginning of a new life in Christ. After His Resurrection, Jesus continued to minister and teach. He appeared to His disciples in Jerusalem (see John 20) and later visited people in the Americas (see 3 Nephi 11). Jesus brought peace, taught His gospel, and invited all to come unto Him. His ministry continues even now through living prophets, scripture, and personal revelation. We can be disciples of Jesus Christ as we follow His example and share His message of peace.

## Scriptures

## Invitation

- Christ’s Atonement and Resurrection are not just events in history; they are invitations to live as Christ’s disciples every day. How can you continue to experience the blessings of the Savior’s Atonement in your daily life?

- Set a Christ-centered goal this week. It could be daily prayer, scripture study, service, or anything you feel will bring you closer to Christ.
