# Day 7: “I Am the Light” (3 Nephi 9:18)

After Jesus’s body was placed in the tomb, His disciples waited. Even though Christ had told them He would rise again, they were confused, heartbroken, and uncertain about what would come next. There are times when we may feel like we are waiting in darkness. But even in our own moments of uncertainty, we can have confidence that God will fulfill His promises.

## Scriptures

- Matthew 27:59–60

- 3 Nephi 9:18

## Inspiration from Church Leaders

- President Camille N. Johnson

## Invitation

- In what aspect of your life do you feel like you’re waiting—perhaps without clear answers or hope?

- How can trusting in Christ’s sacrifice bring you peace even in those moments of uncertainty?

- If you’re able to attend the temple, schedule an appointment to serve in the house of the Lord. Serving in the temple can bring light and hope into your life. If you’re not able to serve in the temple, write on a piece of paper one thing you have been praying or hoping for. Place the paper somewhere safe as a symbol of trusting Christ with what you are praying or hoping for.
