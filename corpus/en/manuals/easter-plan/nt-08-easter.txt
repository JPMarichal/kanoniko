Easter Sunday

# Day 8: “He Is Risen” (Matthew 28:6)

On the third day, the tomb was empty. Jesus Christ rose from the dead, completing His Atonement and breaking the bonds of sin and death. He was resurrected, meaning His spirit body was reunited with His physical body after death, and He became immortal. His Resurrection means that we too will live again and be resurrected. We can find joy and hope in Him every day.
