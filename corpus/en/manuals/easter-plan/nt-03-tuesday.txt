# Day 3: “Love Thy Neighbour” (Matthew 22:39)

At times, it can feel like the world is full of hate, despair, and loneliness. But Jesus Christ taught that we can make a difference when we keep the two great commandments—loving God and loving our neighbors. Christ’s life was the perfect example of showing love for God and others. And then He showed the greatest love of all through His Atonement, which included His suffering for our sins, His death on the cross, and His Resurrection from the grave.
