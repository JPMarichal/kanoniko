Palm Sunday

# Day 1: “Hosanna in the Highest” (Matthew 21:9)

Jesus Christ entered Jerusalem to fulfill His sacred mission. The crowd welcomed Him with palm leaves and cried, “Hosanna,” or “Please save us.” For generations, the people in Jerusalem had been waiting for the Messiah to come and save them from oppression. Imagine the joy of being in the crowd, welcoming the Savior with palm leaves. What would you do to welcome someone who could free you?
