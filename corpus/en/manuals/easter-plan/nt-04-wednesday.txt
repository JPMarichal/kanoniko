# Day 4: “A Good Work” (Mark 14:6)

A woman anointed Jesus with costly ointment. Some people were critical of the way she showed her devotion to the Savior. But Jesus Christ knows that our best efforts to show our love and devotion will not be perfect. Being a devoted disciple doesn’t mean being perfect but rather continually trying to be more like Jesus Christ.
