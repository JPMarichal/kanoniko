## Acknowledgments

Many have generously given time to help publish these histories. Thank you to Hillary Errante, Eliza Nevin, Greg Scoggin, Sabrina Clifford, Katie Parker, Lindsey Maughan, and Preston Shewell in the Publishing Services Department for their careful, thorough, and thoughtful reviews. Thank you also to Jason Mumford and Justin Dahl for their excellent design work and to Julia Crawford and Cathy Cooper for managing all of the many moving pieces involved in the publication process. Our heartfelt thanks also to Jay Burton, Bart Atkin, Audrey Dunshee, Jake Dowdle, Brandon Metcalf, Elizabeth Miles, Camille Coons, and Ben Whisenant from the Church History Library for their help in digitizing sources and gaining permission to use the material.

Their contribution was invaluable to the process.
