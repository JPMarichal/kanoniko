# Asa Dodds

(Birth and death dates unknown)

Asa Dodds was baptized into The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and ordained an elder by 4 February 1831. Later that year, he accompanied Orson Pratt on a mission from Missouri to Indiana. In January 1832, Dodds was called to accompany Calves Wilson on a mission to “the western countries” (Doctrine and Covenants 75:15).
