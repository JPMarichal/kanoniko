# Suggested Music for Families

Families may choose to use the following hymns and children’s songs during family scripture study or family home evening to support the doctrine taught in the Book of Mormon. Children will be singing many of these songs in their Primary classes and singing time.

## January

- December 30–January 5 (Introductory Pages of the Book of Mormon): “Book of Mormon Stories” (Children’s Songbook, 118–19)

- January 6–12 (1 Nephi 1–7): “Keep the Commandments” (Children’s Songbook, 146–47)

- January 13–19 (1 Nephi 8–10): “Search, Ponder, and Pray” (Children’s Songbook, 109)

- January 20–26 (1 Nephi 11–15): “The Iron Rod” (Hymns, no. 274)

- February 17–23 (2 Nephi 11–25): “I Love to See the Temple” (Children’s Songbook, 95)
