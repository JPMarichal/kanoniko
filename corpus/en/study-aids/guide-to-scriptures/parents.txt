Fathers and mothers. Worthy husbands and wives who have been properly sealed in marriage in a temple of God may fulfill their role as parents throughout eternity. “Parents have a sacred duty to rear their children in love and righteousness, to provide for their physical and spiritual needs, and to teach them to love and serve one another, observe the commandments of God, and be law-abiding citizens wherever they live” (“The Family: A Proclamation to the World,” ChurchofJesusChrist.org).
