See also Disciple; Nephi4

eldest son of Nephi2, given charge of plates, 3 Ne. 1:2.
sorrows for wickedness of people, 3 Ne. 1:10.
cries unto the Lord in behalf of his people, 3 Ne. 1:11–12.
hears voice of the Lord, sign of Christ’s birth to be given, 3 Ne. 1:12–13.
performs baptisms, 3 Ne. 1:23.
makes record on plates of Nephi1, 3 Ne. 5:9–10.
was visited by angels and voice of the Lord, 3 Ne. 7:15, 18.
ministers with great power, 3 Ne. 7:17.
casts out devils, raises brother from dead, 3 Ne. 7:19 (19:4).
arouses people’s anger because of many miracles, 3 Ne. 7:20.
Jesus calls Nephi forward, 3 Ne. 11:18–20.
is given power to baptize, 3 Ne. 11:21–22 (12:1).
is called by Jesus to minister as one of twelve disciples, 3 Ne. 19:4.
is baptized, baptizes other eleven disciples, 3 Ne. 19:11–12.
is told to bring forth records, 3 Ne. 23:7–8.
is commanded to write missing parts of record, 3 Ne. 23:9–13.
