Nephi1 likens all scriptures unto his people, 1 Ne. 19:23.
the Lord’s marvelous work is likened unto Israel’s being nourished by Gentiles, 1 Ne. 22:8.
words of Isaiah1 concerning house of Israel may be likened unto Nephites, 2 Ne. 6:5 (11:2, 8).
Israel is likened unto tame olive tree, Jacob 5:3 (6:1).
they who do Christ’s sayings are likened unto man who built house upon rock, 3 Ne. 14:24.
parable of kingdoms that are likened unto man having field, D&C 88:46–61.
children of Zion are likened unto parable of woman and unjust judge, D&C 101:81, 85.
Joseph Smith is likened unto servant in parable, D&C 103:21.
