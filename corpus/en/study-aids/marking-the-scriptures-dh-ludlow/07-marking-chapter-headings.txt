Marking Chapter Headings

The system you select for marking and annotating the King James text can also be used for marking chapter headings. For example, if blue underlining is used to designate passages dealing with Israel (origin, scattering, gathering) in the King James text, then those sections of the chapter headings concerned with Israel would also be underlined in blue.
