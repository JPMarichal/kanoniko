Marking Footnote Entries Containing Editorial Notes

Some of the footnote entries contain editorial notes not introduced by the capitalized headings GR, HEB, IE, JST, TG, and OR.

For example, Genesis 4:17 refers to Enoch the son of Cain and to the city that was called after his name. Footnote 17b (page 7) pertaining to this scripture notes: "Do not confuse Enoch of Cain's lineage and the city of his name with the Enoch of Seth's lineage and the city (Zion) of his name."

You might want to block such footnotes in a pale color (such as pink).

Footnote entries containing important and informational editorial notes are on the pages listed below. Read and review each of these notes; then decide whether you want to block them in a distinctive color.
