Samuel was a prophet who was a Lamanite. Heavenly Father told Samuel to tell the wicked Nephites to repent. The Nephites wouldn’t let Samuel into their city, so he stood on the city wall. They threw stones and shot arrows at him. But Heavenly Father protected him. Samuel told the people what Heavenly Father wanted them to hear.

  One time I was scared because I had to bear my testimony. When I bore my testimony, I wasn’t scared.

  Chase C., age 6, Arizona, USA
