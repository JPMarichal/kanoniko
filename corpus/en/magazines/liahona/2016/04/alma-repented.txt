Alma was a priest of the wicked King Noah. He listened to the prophet Abinadi teach about the commandments. Alma knew he needed to change his life and follow God instead of doing wicked things. He wrote down what Abinadi taught. He began teaching others the gospel. Later he had a family and named one of his sons Alma.
