[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="129972302" id="aside1_p1">“I can emphatically state that because of the Atonement of Jesus Christ, ultimately, in the eternal scheme of things, there will be no unfairness. ‘All that is unfair about life can be made right’ [<em>Preach My Gospel,</em> 52]. Our present circumstances may not change, but through God’s compassion, kindness, and love, we will all receive more than we deserve, more than we can ever earn, and more than we can ever hope for. We are promised that ‘God shall wipe away all tears from [our] eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away’ [<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/nt/rev/21?lang=eng&amp;id=p4#p4">Revelation 21:4</a>].”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="129972303" id="aside1_p2">Elder Dale G. Renlund of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, “That I Might Draw All Men unto Me,” <em>Liahona,</em> May 2016, 42.</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]
