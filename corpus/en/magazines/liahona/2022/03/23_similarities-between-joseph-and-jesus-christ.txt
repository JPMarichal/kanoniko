The Old Testament contains many prophecies, stories, and symbols that foreshadow the life and mission of the Savior, Jesus Christ. One of these stories is that of Joseph of Egypt.

  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Joseph of Egypt                                                                                                Jesus Christ
  Joseph was a shepherd (see Genesis 37:2).                                                                      One of Christ’s names is “the good shepherd” (John 10:11).
  Joseph was sold as a slave into Egypt (see Genesis 37:26–28).                                                  Jesus was betrayed for 30 pieces of silver—the price of a slave (see Exodus 21:32; Matthew 26:15).
  Joseph’s brothers did not recognize him when they came to Egypt to buy food (see Genesis 42:7–8).              Christ’s people did not recognize Him as the Savior (see Luke 4:22, 28–29; John 1:10).
  Joseph revealed himself to his brothers when they came to speak with him a second time (see Genesis 45:1–5).   Christ will be revealed to Israel at His Second Coming (see Matthew 24:30–31; Doctrine and Covenants 88:104).
  By following God’s guidance, Joseph had enough bread to save Egypt from famine (see Genesis 47:13–19).         Jesus Christ is “the bread of life” (John 6:35; see also verse 51).
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
