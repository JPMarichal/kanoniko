With so much misinformation in the world, it can be difficult to sort through it all and find eternal truth. Heavenly Father knows this, and He knows that many of His children are seeking answers. Thankfully, many of the answers to life’s toughest questions can be found in the scriptures, in general conference talks, or through personal revelation. Heavenly Father will never lead us astray.

In Proverbs, we often read about wisdom. Read chapters 2 and 3 individually or with a group and identify verses that discuss how we seek wisdom. For example, Proverbs 1:5 says, “A wise man will hear, and will increase learning; and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels.”
