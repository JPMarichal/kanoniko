What can we learn about repentance by studying David’s pleadings in Psalm 51? He acknowledged his sin (verses 3–4) and prayed for mercy (verse 1), cleansing (verse 2), and “a right spirit” (verse 10). He recognized the importance of “a broken and a contrite heart” (verse 17). He desired to “teach transgressors [the Lord’s] ways” (verse 13).

Consider these important aspects of repentance:

- Faith in our Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ

- Sorrow for sin

- Confession

- Abandonment of sin

- Restitution

- Righteous living
