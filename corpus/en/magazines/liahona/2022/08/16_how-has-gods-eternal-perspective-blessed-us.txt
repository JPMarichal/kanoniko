After gaining a better understanding of God’s power, Job realized his limited vision. He said, “Therefore have I uttered that I understood not” (Job 42:3). Like Job, we too may not always see what God sees for our lives. These three modern examples show how the Lord knows the trials we face and gives us guidance through His prophets. When have you seen the Lord’s perspective bless your own life?

To help us avoid the negative effects of drugs, alcohol, and other harmful substances, the Lord revealed the Word of Wisdom through Joseph Smith in 1833 (see Doctrine and Covenants 89).

Knowing the struggles families would face, the Lord inspired living prophets and apostles to publish the family proclamation in 1995 (see “The Family: A Proclamation to the World,” ChurchofJesusChrist.org). This is a Church standard to strengthen the family.

The home-centered Come, Follow Me program became available for all ages in 2019, about one year before COVID-19 closed Church meetings for a time.
