The Liahona Local Pages give our readers in the Africa South Area an opportunity to read inspiring messages from local leaders, hear the experiences and testimonies of other members in South Africa, and receive direction that is pertinent to the challenges we face as members living the gospel right now in South Africa.

Starting in December 2022, these eight pages of local content will be made available every three months. In every edition you can look forward to messages from our Area Presidency and other Area leaders, local news, and inspiring stories from members just like you.

These pages are also available on the Gospel Library App.

To access them on the app, click on Library >> Magazines >> Liahona >> Choose the monthly edition you would like to read, and then scroll down to the bottom of the menu and click on Africa South Local Pages.

We hope you enjoy them!
