[SIDEBAR] <ul class="bullet"> <li> <p data-aid="127097355" id="aside1_p1"><span class="page-break" data-page="10"></span>Try to arrange the seating so you can see each class member and so everyone can see the chalkboard and other visual aids.</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="127097357" id="aside1_p2">When trying to promote discussion, avoid yes-and-no questions. Instead of asking, “Did Nephi have faith?” ask, “How did Nephi show his faith?”</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="127097358" id="aside1_p3">Be attentive when class members are answering questions or commenting so they know their thoughts and opinions are appreciated.</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="127097359" id="aside1_p4">If your meetinghouse has a library, ask the librarian to show you what’s available to use with your lesson.
