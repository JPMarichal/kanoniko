Elder Spencer J. Condie shares seven lessons we can learn from the life of Handel, the composer of Messiah (see page 20). To hear the Tabernacle Choir perform part of Messiah, visit www.liahona.lds.org.

He says, “The whole point was to share our testimonies of the Savior through the music and the words of the cantata.” To see a video of portions of the cantata, go to www.liahona.lds.org.
