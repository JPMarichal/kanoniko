There, Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ appeared to him in answer to his prayer.</p> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="163912888" id="aside2_p1">“I saw a pillar of light exactly over my head, above the brightness of the sun, which descended gradually until it fell upon me.</p> <p data-aid="163912892" id="aside2_p2">“… When the light rested upon me I saw two Personages, whose brightness and glory defy all description, standing above me in the air. Hear Him!</i>” (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/pgp/js-h/1?lang=eng&amp;id=p16-p17#p16">Joseph Smith—History 1:16–17</a>).</p> [/SIDEBAR]
