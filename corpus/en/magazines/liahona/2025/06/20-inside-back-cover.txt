[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="165666196" id="aside1_p1">In 1831, the Saints in New York followed the Lord’s command to move to Kirtland, Ohio. The Whitney family—Kirtland residents who had been converted and baptized the previous year—immediately welcomed Joseph and Emma Smith into their home. Here, Joseph received several revelations now recorded in the Doctrine and Covenants.</p> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="165666198" id="aside2_p1">“Verily I say, men should be anxiously engaged in a good cause, and do many things of their own free will, and bring to pass much righteousness;</p> <p data-aid="165666200" id="aside2_p2">“For the power is in them, wherein they are agents unto themselves. And inasmuch as men do good they shall in nowise lose their reward” (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/58?lang=eng&amp;id=p27-p28#p27">Doctrine and Covenants 58:27–28</a>).</p> [/SIDEBAR]
