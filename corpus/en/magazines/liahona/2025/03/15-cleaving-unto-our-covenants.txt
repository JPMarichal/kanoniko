Articles in this month’s Church magazines can help you as you seek to “cleave unto the covenants” that you have made (Doctrine and Covenants 25:13).

On page 2, Elder Neil L. Andersen of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles reminds us that the noise of the world can distract us from the voice of the Spirit. Are there distractions in your life that make it difficult to connect with God? You could make a goal this month to reduce these distractions, such as spending more time worshipping in the temple or doing a fast from social media.

The article on page 18 discusses how Church members can find joy and belonging in the Lord’s temple, regardless of marital status. What can you do to help your fellow Saints understand and receive the blessings Heavenly Father has offered to all His children who keep covenants with Him?

Read the article on page 44 by Elder Christophe G. Giraud-Carrier of the Seventy to find five ideas for receiving and recognizing revelation. In what areas of your life do you need divine guidance? Consider applying the principles taught by Elder Giraud-Carrier as you seek inspiration from God this month.
