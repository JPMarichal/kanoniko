[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="167768087" id="aside1_p1">After staying in several different homes in Ohio, Joseph and Emma Smith finally moved into a home of their own in Kirtland in late 1833. Here, Joseph received revelations about many matters, including how to organize high councils (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/102?lang=eng">Doctrine and Covenants 102</a>), how to help the suffering Saints in Missouri (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/103?lang=eng">Doctrine and Covenants 103</a>), how to handle the Church’s financial affairs (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/104?lang=eng">Doctrine and Covenants 104</a>), and how to fulfill priesthood offices (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/107?lang=eng">Doctrine and Covenants 107</a>).</p> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="167768091" id="aside2_p1">“I, the Lord, stretched out the heavens, and built the earth, my very handiwork; and all things therein are mine.</p> <p data-aid="167768095" id="aside2_p2">“And it is my purpose to provide for my saints, for all things are mine.</p> <p data-aid="167768097" id="aside2_p3">“But it must needs be done in mine own way” (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/104?lang=eng&amp;id=p14-p16#p14">Doctrine and Covenants 104:14–16</a>).</p> [/SIDEBAR]
