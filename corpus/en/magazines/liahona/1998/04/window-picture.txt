This is a picture to hang on your window, where the sun can shine through it. Carefully remove page 15 and tape it tightly against your window, with the picture of Jesus Christ facing you. The pictures will remind you of who made the world. Doctrine and Covenants 88:7 [D&C 88:7] says of Christ, “He is in the sun, and the light of the sun, and the power thereof by which it was made.” The light shining through your window picture is Jesus Christ’s own light.
