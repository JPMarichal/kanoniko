MY MISSIONARY JOURNAL

“Every member a missionary …”

“… How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things! …” Romans 10:15 [Rom. 10:15]

Some of my friends I want to share the gospel with:
_______________________________

I am going to think about each of my friends. Then I will decide on one special friend to be a missionary to. I will pray and ask Heavenly Father if I’ve made the right choice. If the feeling I get is a positive one, I’ll continue working with that friend. If the feeling I get is that my choice was wrong, I will choose another friend.

The friend I chose is _______________________________

What I can do to fellowship my friend

1. Invite my friend to dinner to experience the joy of family prayer around the dinner table.

2. Invite my friend to ward parties and special programs.

3. Share a family night with my friend.

4. Take my friend on family outings.

5. Invite my friend to Primary and Sunday School.

Other things I can do:
_______________________________

If my friend is showing interest, I will:

1. pray

2. invite him/her to my home and to share in other activities that are fun

3. take my friend to church meetings

4. tell my friend how I feel about the Church

5. suggest to my father or home teacher that my friend’s family might be interested in having the missionaries call.

MY MISSIONARY JOURNAL INSTRUCTIONS:

To assemble missionary journal, remove page from magazine. Cut paper in half horizontally. Place title page on top of bottom half. Fold in half vertically and staple on fold. Now you are ready to begin your missionary journal.
