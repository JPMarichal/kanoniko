In 2 Nephi 2, Lehi teaches about agency and our opportunity to choose joy and eternal life. But we couldn’t make that choice if there weren’t opposition. Lehi explains that without opposition, “righteousness could not be brought to pass” (verse 11).

Take a look at 2 Nephi 2:11–13 to fill out the following chart:

  --------------------- -------------------------
  If we didn’t have …   Then we wouldn’t have …
  Death                 
  Sin                   
  Corruption            
  Wickedness            
  Punishment            
  Misery                
  --------------------- -------------------------

None of us is exempt. <span class="dominant">Opposition permits us to grow toward what our Heavenly Father would have us become.”</span></p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="158465355" id="aside1_p2">President Dallin H.
