This year in Primary you will be taking a wonderful journey following the footsteps of Jesus and learning about His life from the pages of the New Testament. Your parents will be studying the same scriptures in their classes too.

Here are some activities to help you and your family learn more about the New Testament together.

[SIDEBAR] <ul class="bullet"> <li> <p data-aid="127388090" id="aside1_p1">“Tell Me the Stories of Jesus” (<em>Children’s Songbook,</em> 57)</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="127388091" id="aside1_p2"><a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/nt/matt/11?lang=eng&amp;id=p29#p29">Matthew 11:29</a></p> </li> </ul> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="127388093" id="aside2_p1">Why is it important and exciting to study the New Testament? How will learning more about the Savior help us be stronger? Be sure to give each person a chance to share. Afterward you could sing “The Books of the New Testament” (<em>Children’s Songbook,</em> 116). You could also make cards and write the names of the books and place them in order.</p> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="127388095" id="aside3_p1">Marking your scriptures can help you find your favorite verses later!</p> <p data-aid="127388096" id="aside3_p2">You can use a pencil, a pen, or a colored pencil.</p> <p data-aid="127388097" id="aside3_p3">Underline verses where Jesus is speaking—or just circle the verse numbers.</p> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="127388098" id="aside4_p1">Your parents can order a copy of the New Testament for you in English or Spanish for $2.50 at <a href="https://store.lds.org"><span class="language" lang="eng">store.lds.org</span></a>.</p> [/SIDEBAR]
