[SIDEBAR] <div class="image-cropper"></div> <p data-aid="127408298" id="aside1_p1">“As we walk the path of spiritual liberty in these last days, we must understand that the faithful use of our agency depends upon our having religious freedom. …</p> <p data-aid="127408299" id="aside1_p2">“There are four cornerstones of religious freedom that we as Latter-day Saints must rely upon and protect.</p> <p data-aid="127408301" id="aside1_p3">“The first is freedom to believe. No one should be criticized, persecuted, or attacked by individuals, or governments either, for what he or she believes about God. …</p> <p data-aid="127408302" id="aside1_p4">“The second … is the freedom to share our faith and our beliefs with others. …</p> <p data-aid="127408303" id="aside1_p5">“The third … is the freedom to form a religious organization, a church, to worship peacefully with others. …</p> <p data-aid="127408304" id="aside1_p6">“The fourth … is the freedom to live our faith—free exercise of faith not just in the home and chapel but also in public places.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="127408305" id="aside1_p7">Elder Robert D. Hales of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, <a class="cross-ref" href="/study/liahona/2015/05/sunday-afternoon-session/preserving-agency-protecting-religious-freedom?lang=eng">“Preserving Agency, Protecting Religious Freedom,”</a> <em>Liahona,</em> May 2015, 112.</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]
