In Galatians 5, the Apostle Paul teaches how the Spirit speaks to us. Note the differences between “the fruit of the Spirit” and “the works of the flesh.”

  --------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------
  Fruit of the Spirit (see Galatians 5:22–23)   Works of the Flesh (see Galatians 5:19–21)
  Love                                          Adultery, fornication, lasciviousness
  Joy                                           Hatred
  Peace                                         Strife
  Long-suffering                                Variance (contention)
  Gentleness                                    Wrath
  Goodness                                      Envyings
  Faith                                         Heresies
  Meekness                                      Seditions
  Temperance                                    Uncleanness
  --------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------
