- To Church members in Rome

- Written from Corinth in about AD 55–56

- Purpose: To encourage unity, clarify doctrine, and prepare for Paul’s visit to Rome

- Key teachings: Faith, salvation, and becoming more Christlike in spite of our fallen nature

- To Church members in Corinth

- Written from Ephesus in about AD 55–56

- Purpose: To strengthen the Saints against division and immorality

- Key teachings: Unity, spiritual gifts, charity, and building a foundation on Jesus Christ

- To Church members in Corinth

- Written from Macedonia in about AD 55–57

- Purpose: To express thanks to those who heeded his previous epistle and to give warning about false teachers

- Key teachings: The spirit of the law, overcoming trials, and the authority to preach the gospel

- To Church members in Galatia

- Written from Macedonia in about AD 55–57

- Purpose: To teach God’s higher law while Saints were reverting to the law of Moses

- Key teachings: The Atonement of Jesus Christ, the fruit of the Spirit, and the saving power of faith in Jesus Christ
