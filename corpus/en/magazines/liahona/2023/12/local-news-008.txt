Over this Christmas season, look for ways to help others feel Christ’s love through service.

Here are 25 ideas to get you started. You might find a few things on this list that you can add to your own ideas!

1.  Laugh with a friend.

2.  Call someone you miss.

3.  Carry an extra snack for someone in need.

4.  Make a homemade Christmas card for a friend.

5.  Plan some one-on-one time with a loved one.

6.  Let a stranger go ahead of you in line.

7.  Learn to say “Merry Christmas” in a new language.

8.  On social media, share a link to a Christmas song that you love.

9.  Apologize to someone.

10. Complete an item on a loved one’s to-do list.

11. Send a thank-you note to a healthcare worker.

12. Help someone research their family genealogy.

13. Text a photo of a fond memory to a family member.

14. Give your neighbour a compliment.

15. Let someone else be kind to you.

16. Learn how another culture celebrates Christmas.

17. Help a loved one clean their home.

18. Invite others to a Christmas celebration.

19. Leave a gift on a neighbor’s doorstep.

20. Watch The Christ Child with family or friends.

21. Make a meal with a family member.

22. Invite a neighbor to a worship service.

23. Visit with an elderly neighbor.

24. Offer to teach someone a new skill.

25. Forgive someone.
