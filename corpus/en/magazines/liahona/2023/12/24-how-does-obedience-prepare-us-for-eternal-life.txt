John the Revelator understood the importance of commandments in how they prepare God’s children to inherit all that He has. An angel instructed John that the righteous who follow the commandments of God will inherit a holy city: “Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city” (Revelation 22:14). The “holy city” (Revelation 22:19) that Heavenly Father wants to prepare us for is the celestial kingdom.
