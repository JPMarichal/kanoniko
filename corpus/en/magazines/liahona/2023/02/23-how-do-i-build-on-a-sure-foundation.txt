The parable of the wise man and the foolish man teaches us the importance of building on a firm foundation by not only hearing the Lord’s words but also doing them (see Matthew 7:24–27). Helaman 5:12 teaches us that our spiritual foundations should be built on “the rock of our Redeemer, who is Christ, the Son of God.” Building on a strong foundation does not come easy. What did the wise man do to make his house unshakable? (see Luke 6:48).

Consider reviewing some of the Savior’s teachings from the Sermon on the Mount listed below. Is there a particular teaching that you feel inspired to practice more in your life? How might that teaching strengthen your relationship with Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ? You could make a plan to apply that teaching in your life and reflect on your progress over time.

Humility: Matthew 6:1–8, 16–18

Prayer: Matthew 6:5–13

Forgiveness: Matthew 6:14–15

Priorities: Matthew 6:19–24, 33; 7:6, 21–23

Trust in God: Matthew 6:25–34; 7:9–11

Judge righteously: Matthew 7:1–5, 15–20

Ask in faith: Matthew 7:7–8

Kindness: Matthew 7:12

Obedience: Matthew 7:21–27
