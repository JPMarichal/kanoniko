President Russell M. Nelson tells how, as a young man, he studied lists of things to do or not do on the Sabbath. He later learned from the scriptures that his actions and attitude on the Sabbath represented a sign between him and Heavenly Father (see Exodus 31:13).

He said: “With that understanding, I no longer needed lists of dos and don’ts. When I had to make a decision whether or not an activity was appropriate for the Sabbath, I simply asked myself, ‘What sign do I want to give to God?’” (“The Sabbath Is a Delight,” Liahona, May 2015, 130; emphasis in original).

Read Christ’s teachings about the Sabbath day in Matthew 12:1–13; see also Isaiah 58:13 and Doctrine and Covenants 59:9–13.
