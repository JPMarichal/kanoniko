Some people brought a deaf man to Jesus. The man could not hear, nor could he talk very well. The people wanted the Savior to heal him. Mark 7:32

Jesus and the man walked away from the others. Then Jesus put His fingers in the man’s ears and touched the man’s tongue and blessed him. Mark 7:33–34

Now the man could hear and talk, and the people could understand him. Jesus asked them not to tell anyone what had happened, but they told everyone. Mark 7:35–36
