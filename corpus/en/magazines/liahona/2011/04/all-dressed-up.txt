1.  Elise wanted to dress up. She put on Dad’s shoes and a fake red nose.

    I’m a funny clown.

2.  Elise ran back to her room. She put on a yellow construction hat and grabbed a plastic hammer.

3.  Elise hammered the floor before she ran back to her room.

    I’m a strong builder.

4.  Elise put on a glittery purple and silver gown and twirled out of her room.

5.  Elise liked being a princess. She stayed dressed in her gown for the rest of the day.

    I’m a beautiful princess.

6.  At bedtime Elise changed into her favorite green pajamas. She walked out of her room and sat on Mom’s lap.

7.  I am Elise. I am a child of God.
