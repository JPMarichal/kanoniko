[SIDEBAR] <div class="image-cropper"></div> <p data-aid="127176110" id="aside1_p1"><span class="page-break" data-page="6"></span>“The Savior came to the earth to show us how to live the plan that was authored in heaven—a plan that, if lived, will make us happy. His example showed us the way to return home to our Heavenly Father. No other who has ever lived has been so ‘steadfast and immovable’ (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/5?lang=eng&amp;id=p15#p15">Mosiah 5:15</a>). He was never distracted. He was focused on carrying out the will of the Father, and He remained true to His divine mission. …</p> <p data-aid="127176111" id="aside1_p2">“You are a part of that wondrous plan that was presented in the premortal realms. Your coming to the earth now has been anticipated since the plan was accepted. Your position in time and place is no accident. Your ‘exceeding faith and good works’ (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/13?lang=eng&amp;id=p3#p3">Alma 13:3</a>) then has laid the foundation for what you may accomplish now if you are faithful and obedient. … You have a great work to do. In order to accomplish your divine mission and live the plan of happiness, you too must be steadfast and immovable.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="127176112" id="aside1_p3">Elaine S. Dalton, Young Women general president, “At All Times, in All Things, and in All Places,” <em>Liahona</em> and <em>Ensign,</em> May 2008, 116.</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]
