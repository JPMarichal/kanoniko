You can make a poster to help your family grow stronger!

- With the help of an adult, cut out the blocks. Glue onto a piece of paper the ones that show things your family is already doing. These blocks will represent your family’s foundation.

- Choose another block that shows something your family would like to do or do better. After you complete this goal, add it to your tower. Place the poster where everyone can see it, and keep adding blocks to make your family strong!
