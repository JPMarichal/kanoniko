Jesus Christ is our Savior. He loves us. He wants us to have faith in Him and follow the example He set for us when He lived on the earth. Remove this year’s poster from the magazine, and hang it in your room, where it can remind you to think about the Savior. In each issue this year, you will find a coloring page with a theme and a scripture. Color the picture, and hang it near the poster. As you look at the pictures, strive to develop your faith and testimony, follow the Savior, and live His gospel.
