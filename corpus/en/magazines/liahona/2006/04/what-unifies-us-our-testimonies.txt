Having encountered missionaries many times since my teens, I was, at the age of 34, blessed to see, recognize, and know the truth.

Through the years, I had always been too egotistical to believe the story of a boy, a vision, and the gold plates. Now I know through the confirmation of the Holy Ghost that Joseph Smith is a chosen prophet of God. Because of the Prophet’s guileless innocence and yearning, the true Church of our living Savior has been restored. I know we can return to loving heavenly parents as we come unto Christ.

Marianne Lipps of Sydney, Australia
