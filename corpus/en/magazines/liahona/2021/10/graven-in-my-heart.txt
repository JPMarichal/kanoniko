I still count to ﬁve

when I gather forks

to set the dinner table

or when I calculate

the brownie cuts

so that all have equal parts.

I still count you in

my unconscious movement,

the momentary minutia of my days.

You are absent

but not from my thoughts

or pulse

or breath.
