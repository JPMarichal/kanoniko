In these sections we learn about the Lord’s command to the early Saints to build a temple.

In section 97, the Lord promises that if the Saints build a temple and keep it pure, “all the pure in heart that shall come into it shall see God” (verse 16). After quoting this verse in a general conference talk, Elder David B. Haight (1906–2004) of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles said:

“It is true that some have actually seen the Savior, but when one consults the dictionary, he learns that there are many other meanings of the word see, such as coming to know Him, discerning Him, recognizing Him and His work, perceiving His importance, or coming to understand Him.

“Such heavenly enlightenment and blessings are available to each of us.”

In what ways have you seen God manifest Himself in the temple?

These scriptures describe some other blessings that come to the pure in heart:

- Psalm 24:3–5

- Jacob 3:1–2

- Doctrine and Covenants 56:18; 101:17–18; 136:11
