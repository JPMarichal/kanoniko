Mary, blessed among women:

In earliest days

did He in whose name our cries reach heavenward

have His cries answered hourly

by the loving care of the woman most blessed

who kept all these things

and pondered them in her heart?

In unseen hours

did He who carried the burden of our suffering

learn pain, comforted and hunger filled,

while wrapped in the arms of the woman most blessed

who passed through the valley of the shadow of death

for Him who would overcome it?

In tender youth

did He who took upon Himself our sorrows

know heartache, disappointment, and grief,

secure by the stature of the woman most blessed

who presented herself a handmaid

to give life to Life and Light?

Did you heal the Great Healer,

soothe the balm of Gilead,

calm the Master of earth and skies?

And in that day, will it be with some tender remembrance

that He wipes our tears away?
