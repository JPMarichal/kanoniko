The Church has announced the publication of the Book of Mormon in Malay, making it the 109th language for this book of scripture. The Slovak Book of Mormon was made available in March of this year. Malay is spoken in the Southeast Asian countries of Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, and India.

The Malay Book of Mormon is now available through local Church distribution centers and the online store at store.lds.org (item 35607348) and at scriptures.lds.org.
