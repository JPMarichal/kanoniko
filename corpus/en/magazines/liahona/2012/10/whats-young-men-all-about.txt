Passing the sacrament

Collecting fast offerings

Strengthening your family

Learning and doing new things

Sharing with others

Developing a closer relationship with Heavenly Father

Standards to make you strong

Serving with your quorum

Mutual fun!

Being baptized for those who have died

Preparing to make covenants

Feeling the Holy Ghost

For a mission

For marriage and fatherhood

This is very important. When God gives you His priesthood, He will be telling you He trusts you. It will be your sacred duty to serve others as the Savior would.</p> <p data-aid="127265538" id="aside1_p3">Now is the time to start preparing. Watch and listen to the priesthood holders around you as they bless and pass the sacrament, baptize, give blessings, serve missions, and lead families. Then you will be prepared for that great day when you will receive the Aaronic Priesthood.</p> <p data-aid="127265540" id="aside1_p4">Sincerely,</p> <p data-aid="127265542" id="aside1_p5">David L.
