God sent His Son Jesus Christ to help us. You can have power from Jesus Christ when you do these things:

- Learn about the Savior. Several years ago I read and underlined every verse in the scriptures about Jesus Christ. When I finished, I told my wife, “I am a different man!”

- Study “The Living Christ.” It is a testimony about Jesus Christ from the prophets and apostles.

- Be willing to stand out, speak up, and be different from the people of the world.

- Try to look to Him in every thought (see Doctrine and Covenants 6:36). When you do, your doubts and fears will flee.

- Make sacred covenants and keep them.

Jesus is the Christ! His power is available to each one of you.
