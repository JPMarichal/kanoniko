Help your family get ready for Christmas! Starting on December 15, do one of these activities together each day. Then color the star.

- Watch the video “The Nativity.”*

- Sing a Christmas song or hymn.

- Be especially kind today, like Jesus would.

- Review a talk from the First Presidency Christmas devotional.*

- Draw a picture of baby Jesus.

- Act out the Nativity (see Luke 2).

- Share a favorite scripture about Jesus.

- Help someone who is sick or lonely, like Jesus would.

- Read something in the scriptures Jesus said.

- Share why you’re thankful for Jesus.
