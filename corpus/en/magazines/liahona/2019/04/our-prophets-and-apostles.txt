Jesus Christ leads The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints through prophets and apostles. Read about them below. Then cut out the pictures on page F23 and tape them in place on the chart. Put tape at the top of each picture so you can lift them to read the facts underneath!

17th President of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

- Was a heart surgeon

- Studied several languages, including Mandarin

- Has 10 children: 9 daughters and 1 son

First Counselor in the First Presidency

- Cleaned a radio repair shop as his first job

- Became a lawyer and Utah Supreme Court judge

- Was president of Brigham Young University

Second Counselor in the First Presidency

- Played basketball in high school

- Learned physics from his father on their family chalkboard

- Was president of Ricks College, now BYU–Idaho

Acting President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Was nicknamed “the bishop” in college because of his high standards

- Owned a car dealership

- Served as a missionary in England and as a mission president in Toronto, Canada

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Was a mission companion with Elder Cook in England

- Worked for the Church Education System

- Was president of BYU

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Was a refugee twice as a child

- Was an airplane captain

- Enjoys skiing with his children and grandchildren

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Was a quarterback on his high-school football team

- After serving a mission in Germany, he baptized his father a member of the Church

- Was president of Ricks College when it became BYU–Idaho

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Gained a testimony after studying scriptures and praying with his older brother

- Saw his future wife at a junior high school talent show

- Served as a Church leader in the Philippines and Pacific Islands

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Baked homemade bread for his family growing up

- Participated in the Hill Cumorah Pageant in New York as a teenager

- Worked as a lawyer before being called as an Apostle

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Grew up on a dairy farm in Idaho, USA

- Served as a missionary and mission president in France

- Speaks French, Portuguese, Spanish, and English

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Was a mission president in New York City, USA

- His motto is “It’s people that matter most”

- Dedicated the first Latter-day Saint building in the Czech Republic

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Served as a missionary and later as mission president in Japan

- Started a business building and selling exercise equipment

- Served as bishop over the whole Church

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- His middle name, Gunnar, means “brave soldier”

- Moved with his family from Utah to Sweden when he was 11

- Worked as a heart doctor

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Likes animals, including penguins

- Loves meeting people in every country

- Has family history records back to First Dragon Gong in AD 837

Of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

- Is from Brazil and served as a missionary there and as mission president in Portugal

- Learned about the Church with his family as a young boy

- Started getting ready for a mission when he was 12
