The Savior, Jesus Christ, commanded His disciples to share the gospel with everyone on earth. He said,“Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature” (Mark 16:15).

We need every member of the Church to help take the gospel to all the world.

Here are some ways you can help:

1.  Love everyone as your brothers and sisters, like Jesus taught.

2.  Keep the commandments so you can be a good example.

3.  Pray to know who is ready to learn about the gospel and how you can share it with them.

4.  Help others learn more about Jesus Christ.

Missionary work means having an attitude of love and helping others. No matter how the other person responds, you are a successful missionary if you share the gospel with love!
