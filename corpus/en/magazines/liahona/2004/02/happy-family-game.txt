Families are very important to Heavenly Father. He has provided many blessings to help families be happy. You and your family can play a game to help you remember some of these blessings.

Instructions

1.  Remove this page from the magazine, glue it to heavy paper, and cut out the cards. Put the cards in a sack.

2.  Take turns choosing a card from the sack, looking at the picture, and telling what blessing the pictured item brings to your family. Keep taking turns until all the cards have been chosen.
