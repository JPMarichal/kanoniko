“The family is ordained of God.”

“Happiness in family life is most likely to be achieved when founded upon the teachings of the Lord Jesus Christ.”

“Successful … families are established and maintained on principles of faith, prayer, repentance, [and] forgiveness.”

“Successful … families are established and maintained on principles of … respect, love, compassion, work, and wholesome recreational activities.”

See the First Presidency and Council of the Twelve Apostles, “The Family: A Proclamation to the World,” Liahona, Oct. 1998, 24; Ensign, Nov. 1995, 102. This proclamation (item nos. 35602, 35538, and 35571) is also available at Church distribution centers.
