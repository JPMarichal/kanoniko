Throughout history, Heavenly Father has called courageous and honorable men to be prophets. They are special witnesses of Jesus Christ.

Prophets are called to testify of the Savior, to teach us that He died so we might repent and return to live with our Father in Heaven. They tell us of Jesus’ great love for us. Many prophets’ testimonies are found in the scriptures. To hear the testimony of our living prophet, we can listen to or read his general conference talks.

Match each testimony at right with the prophet who gave that testimony. Write the number of each testimony under the correct prophet’s name. If you need help, look up the reference at the end of each testimony. Then record your own testimony of Jesus Christ in your journal. Share your testimony during family home evening.
