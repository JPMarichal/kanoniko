Prayerfully studying the truths in the Book of Mormon can help you acquire the spiritual knowledge you need to guide your life and to answer questions that you or others may have. Here is a sample of the truths you can learn during your study of the Book of Mormon at seminary, at home, and at church. As you study, ponder, and pray over them, consider ways to share and act upon what you have learned to deepen your understanding, testimony, and conversion to these important truths.
