This year in the Liahona, we’re learning about Church history. Below is a list of scriptures you and your family can read during the year. After reading, talk about what you learned; then color the matching numbered spaces. Each picture here represents a part of Jesus Christ’s true Church that was restored.

Most of these scriptures are revelations from the Lord to Joseph Smith. Color the illustration above as you read them!
