You are invited to create new works of art for the 11th International Art Competition, sponsored by the Church History Museum in Salt Lake City, Utah.

- Theme: “Meditations on Belief,” inspired by Psalm 77:11–12. All artistic media, styles, and cultural approaches are welcome.

- Submission dates: February 1 to June 1, 2018.

- Ages: Participants must be age 18 or older.

- Awards: Chosen by a jury, awards will be announced in October 2018. Selected entries will be displayed at the Church History Museum and online.

Visit lds.org/artcompetition for detailed rules, eligibility requirements, and online registration.
