The Holy Ghost is the third member of the Godhead. He testifies of Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.

He teaches us. He gives us hope. He helps us do good.

He gives us spiritual gifts. He gives us knowledge and helps us remember important things.

He helps us be clean from sin. He comforts us.

The Holy Ghost also helps us make important choices.

He speaks to our minds and hearts gently. We each might hear Him differently.
