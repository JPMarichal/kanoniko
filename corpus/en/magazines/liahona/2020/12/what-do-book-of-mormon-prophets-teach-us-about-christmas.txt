As you read the following scriptures, what does each prophet teach us about the birth of the Savior? Consider recording your thoughts or sharing them with your family.

“I beheld a virgin … bearing a child in her arms. And the angel said unto me: Behold the Lamb of God, yea, even the Son of the Eternal Father!” (see 1 Nephi 11:13–33).

“All the prophets … since the world began—… have they not said that God himself should come down among the children of men … ?” (see Mosiah 13:33–35).

“The Lord … shall come … among the children of men, … and he shall be called Jesus Christ, the Son of God, … and his mother shall be called Mary” (see Mosiah 3:5–11).

“He shall be born of Mary, … even the Son of God” (see Alma 7:7–13).

“Five years more cometh, and behold, then cometh the Son of God” (see Helaman 14:1–13).

“The voice of the Lord came unto him, saying: … on the morrow come I into the world” (see 3 Nephi 1:4–22).
