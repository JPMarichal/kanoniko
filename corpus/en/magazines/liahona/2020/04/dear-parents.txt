In the early spring of 1820, Joseph Smith prayed and saw Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ. This magazine talks about that special time. Here are a few pages to look for:

- A picture story tells how the Restored Church is part of God’s plan (F4–10).

- President Nelson tells how we can help with the Restoration (F2).

- A new song teaches about the First Vision (F16–17).

- A story teaches how the Restoration can bless our families (F20–21).

Isn’t it wonderful that we can belong to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints? Let’s share the good news of Jesus and His Church with the world!

Happy Easter,

The Friend
