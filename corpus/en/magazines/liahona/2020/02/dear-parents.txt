Nephi was faithful. Your children can learn about him in this month’s magazine. On page F2, President Eyring talks about how we can follow Nephi’s example. Use pages F12–F13 to act out the story of Nephi building a ship. On pages F20–F23, read about when Nephi’s hunting bow broke. Ask your children what words they would use to describe Nephi. What are some words that could describe your family?

Stay faithful!
