Dear Seeking,

Even if your family doesn’t study the scriptures, remember that you can! Do your best to read on your own, even if it’s just a little each day. You could start with the reading chart on pages F12–F14. Read some more ideas on page F11!

Whether you read the scriptures with others or by yourself, the Holy Ghost is with you.

You are not alone!

The Friend
