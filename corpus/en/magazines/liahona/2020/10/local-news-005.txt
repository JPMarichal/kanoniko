Starting in January 2021, the Church will offer three separate magazines in 48 languages:

- Liahona for adults

- An expanded Friend for children

- And the new For the Strength of Youth for youth.

Each magazine will be available in English every month. Magazines in other languages will be available every month or every other month, depending on the language.

Current Liahona subscribers will continue to receive the Liahona through the end of their subscription, but after January it will include only material for adults. Those who want to receive the new Friend or For the Strength of Youth will need to subscribe to those magazines. Subscribe using whatever process is available in your area, including at store.ChurchofJesusChrist.org, at a distribution center, or through local unit orders. Digital magazine content will continue to be free in the Gospel Library app or at liahona.ChurchofJesusChrist.org.
