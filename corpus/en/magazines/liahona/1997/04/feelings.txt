Your face can show how you feel. When you have done something wrong, you should feel sad and ask for forgiveness. Then you should try not to make the same mistake again. Heavenly Father and Jesus know that if you repent, you will be happy.

1.  Carefully remove this page from the magazine. Mount it on heavy paper; then cut out the head and face.

2.  Cut slits along the broken lines; slip the face through the slits.

3.  Change the face from sad to happy by moving the bottom part up and down.

4.  In a family home evening, use this face to tell your family what you have learned about repentance.
