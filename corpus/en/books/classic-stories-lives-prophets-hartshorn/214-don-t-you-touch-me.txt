Leon R. Hartshorn, Classic Stories from the Lives of Our Prophets ( 1971)

372"Don't You Touch Me"

372As told by Elder Boyd K. Packer.

372He makes those around him happy. His abundant humor is always in good taste.

373On one occasion he was returning from Canada with one of his associates. The stewardess on the flight offered them all kinds of refreshments that are not proper for Latter-day Saints. After failing with coffee, tea, and alcoholic beverages, she asked, with some concern, "Isn't there anything I could get for you?"

373"I would like some lemonade, if you have some," President Kimball replied.

373"I'm sorry," she said. "We don't have any aboard."

373She turned to go down the aisle and then no doubt remembered that lemon slices are often served with alcoholic beverages. She had some lemons aboard, for she turned and said, "But perhaps I could squeeze you a little."

373Brother Kimball threw up his arms good naturedly as a barrier and in an expression of mock concern replied, "Don't you touch me!"

373He is happy and positive and always reassuring to be around. His handshake is hearty and warm and sincere, and he is ever alert to reach out to those who otherwise might be over-looked or ignored. Those who meet him for the first time are impressed at once with his courtesy.

373Ensign, March 1974, p. 6.
