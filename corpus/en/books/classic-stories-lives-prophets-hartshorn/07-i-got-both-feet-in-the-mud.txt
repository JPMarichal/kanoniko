13Margaret McIntyre Burgess, who, as a child, lived with her parents in Nauvoo, Illinois, related this incident of her cherished recollections of the Prophet Joseph.

13My older brother and I were going to school, near to the building which was known as Joseph's brick store. It had been raining the previous day, causing the ground to be very muddy, especially along that street. My brother Wallace and I got both feet in the mud and could not get out, and, of course, child-like, we began to cry, for we thought we would have to stay there. But looking up, I beheld the loving friend of children, the Prophet Joseph coming to us. He soon had us on high and dry ground. Then he stooped down and cleaned the mud from our little heavy-laden shoes, took his handkerchief from his pocket, and wiped our tear-stained faces. He spoke kind and cheering words to us and sent us on our way to school rejoicing.
