Missionary WorkKimball, Heber C.47"One York Shilling"

47As Brigham Young and Heber C. Kimball arrived in Kirtland, Ohio on their way to England, Elder Kimball recorded the following.

47Brother Brigham had one York shilling left, and on looking over our expenses we found we had paid out over $87.00 out of the $13.50 we had at Pleasant Garden, which is all the money we had to pay our passages with. We had traveled over four hundred miles by stage, for which we paid from eight to ten cents a mile, and had eaten three meals a day, for each of which we were charged fifty cents, also fifty cents for our lodgings. Brother Brigham often suspected that I put money in his trunk, or clothes, thinking that I had a purse of money which I had not acquainted him with; but this was not so. The money could only have been put in his trunk by some heavenly messenger, who thus administered to our necessities daily as he knew we needed.

47Whitney, Life of Heber C. Kimball, p. 273.
