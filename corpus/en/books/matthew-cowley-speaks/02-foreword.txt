Matthew Cowley, Matthew Cowley Speaks ( 1954)

Foreword

THERE WAS ONLY ONE MATTHEW COWLEY

Gifted with great faith—with broad human sympathies—with rare affection for all men, but particularly for the unfortunate and recreant—with that eloquence which speaks from the heart: to the heart—with unquestioned loyalty and devotion to the Church and to Him who restored it—with power to bless and to heal—

Blessed with all these gifts, he nurtured them through long years among his beloved friends of the Pacific until his spirit was mellowed and chastened and warmed.

Punctuate all these attainments with a delicious and spontaneous humor and you have a rare soul.

The time spent in the reading of these sermons will put a lump in the throat of the reader and a new urge in his life.

Adam S. Bennion

Los Angeles, California

March 16, 1954
