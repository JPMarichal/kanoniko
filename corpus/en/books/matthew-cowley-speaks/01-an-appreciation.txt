Matthew Cowley, Matthew Cowley Speaks ( 1954)

An Appreciation

We express our appreciation to those who have assisted in publishing this book.

To the many interested and enthusiastic friends who have provided us with written copies and tape recordings of Elder Cowley's talks, most of which have been included in this book.

To Elder Adam S. Bennion of the Quorum of the Twelve for his wise counsel, cooperation, and encouragement as well as diligent proofreading which helped so much in getting this book published.

To Marba C. Josephson for her untiring efforts in gathering, compiling, selecting, editing, and proof-reading all material, for her suggestions and supervision of all work in the publication of this book.

To Elva Cowley whose wholehearted cooperation and painstaking reading of every address has made this book possible.

To Bishop Glen L. Rudd for his suggestions and cooperation in planning and producing this book.

DESERET BOOK COMPANY
