HumorYoung, BrighamA Cart Full of Apples

Brigham Young

A man was going to market, a pretty wicked swearing man, with his cart full of apples. He was going uphill, and the hindboard, as the Yankees call it—the Westerners call it the hindgate, slipped out of his cart, and his apples rolled down the hill. He stopped his team and looked at the apples as they rolled down the hill, and said he, "I would swear if I could do justice to the case, but as I cannot I will not swear a word."

Journal of Discourses, 15:19-20.
