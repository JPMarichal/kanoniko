MotherhoodLee, Harold B.The Faith of a Mother

Harold B. Lee

As just a high school boy, I went away on a high school debating team trip. We won the debate. I came back and called Mother on the telephone only to have her say, "Never mind, son. I know all about it. I will tell you when you get home at the end of the week."

When I came home she took me aside and said, "When I knew it was just time for this performance to start, I went out among the willows by the creekside, and there, all by myself, I remembered you and prayed God you would not fail."

I have come to know that that kind of love is necessary for every son and daughter who seek to achieve in this world.

Harold B. Lee, Ye Are the Light of the World, 327-28.
