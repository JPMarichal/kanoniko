David O. McKay was the first great voyaging LDS Church leader of the twentieth century, traveling around the world as an Apostle in 1921. Twenty years later, as a counselor in the First Presidency, President McKay and his wife, Sister Emma Ray Riggs McKay, visited Hawaiʻi to dedicate the Oʻahu Stake Tabernacle. Courtesy LDS Church Archives.
