Marvin J. Ashton, One for the Money ( 1975)

Foreword

In recent years sound management of money has become increasingly difficult for families as well as individuals. The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints has long advocated that its members avoid getting into debt except for basic long-range investments, such as buying a home or investing in advanced schooling.

Elder Marvin J. Ashton of the Council of the Twelve offers in this little booklet twelve basic guidelines for establishing sounder money management practices.

Following presentation of these guidelines at a general welfare meeting of the Church, President Spencer W. Kimball declared: "I've been thinking of what Brother Ashton has said. I think if I were starting out I would like to get his twelve points and teach myself, my children, my family, and everybody I come in contact with. These are basic. . . . I agree with all Brother Marvin has said this day in regard to the financing of the family and the home."

We are pleased to present these most helpful guidelines for the benefit of all who are just starting to cope with financial matters as well as for those who are already well established with homes and families.
