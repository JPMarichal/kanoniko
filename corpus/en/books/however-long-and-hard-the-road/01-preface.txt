Jeffrey R. Holland, However Long and Hard the Road ( 1985)

Preface

The following pages come from a large stack of essays and addresses I have written over the past decade or so. They were, of course, not written—or spoken—to be published together, but they do have one thing in common. They are intended to be messages of hope. All of my professional life has been spent with young people, and universally they (like the rest of us) have needed support, encouragement, reassurance, and confidence. They have, in short, needed hope—that incentive to keep moving, keep trying, and keep believing until hope's sister virtues of faith and charity can also work their miracles. The thoughts contained in the pages that follow, including the invitation to repent when and where necessary, are shared with the intent that they might give encouragement to those who are struggling, that they might "succor the weak, lift up the hands which hang down, and strengthen the feeble knees."
