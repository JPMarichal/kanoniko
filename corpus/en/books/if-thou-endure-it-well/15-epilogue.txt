Neal A. Maxwell, If Thou Endure It Well ( 1996)

Epilogue

SUBMISSION By Neal A. Maxwell

When from Thy stern tutoring I would quickly flee, Turn me from my Tarshish To where is best for me. Help me in my Nineveh To serve with love and truth— Not on a hillside posted Mid shade of gourd or booth. When my modest suffering seems So vexing, wrong, and sore, May I recall what freely flowed From each and every pore. Dear Lord of the Abba Cry, Help me in my duress To endure it well enough And to say, . . ". "Nevertheless.
