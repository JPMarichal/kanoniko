Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 2 ( 1971)

Paul Glories in His Sufferings for Christ

SC2 Cor 11 162 Cor 11 172 Cor 11 182 Cor 11 192 Cor 11 202 Cor 11 212 Cor 11 222 Cor 11 232 Cor 11 242 Cor 11 252 Cor 11 262 Cor 11 272 Cor 11 282 Cor 11 292 Cor 11 302 Cor 11 312 Cor 11 322 Cor 11 33Blessed is Paul who was persecuted for righteousness' sake, for his is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed is he because he was reviled and persecuted and men spake all manner of evil against him falsely. Why then should he not rejoice and be exceeding glad? Why should he hesitate to recount his scourgings, stonings, and shipwrecks, suffered while on the Lord's errand? Why not glory in overcoming perils, weariness, pain, cold, and nakedness? Truly the sufferings of the faithful are swallowed up in the glory of Christ's service. (D. & C. 84:80.)
