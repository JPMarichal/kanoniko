Apostles Continue Miracles of Jesus

SCActs 5 12Acts 5 13Acts 5 14Acts 5 15Acts 5 16"These signs shall follow them that believe." (Mark 16:17.)This is the everlasting, eternal, immutable decree of the Almighty. Where there are apostles and prophets, there will be gifts and signs; where there is faith, there will be miracles; where the true saints are, God's power is manifest—and where these wonders are not found, there the saving truth is not. See Commentary I, pp. 870-871; Acts 3:1-16; 16:22-40; Heb. 11:1-3.

Acts 5 1515. The shadow of Peter] So confident and sure were the people that the healing power of God rested with the apostles, that any contact, however slight, sufficed to bring miracles to pass. It was as when the woman touched the border of Jesus' garment (Luke 8:43-48), or the sick had contact with handkerchiefs used by Paul (Acts 19:11-12), or the dead man revived through touching the bones of Elisha. (2 Kings 13:20-21.)
