Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 2 ( 1971)

Saints Seek Tabernacles of Immortal Glory

2 Cor 5 11. Our earthly house] Our mortal body, the temporary tabernacle of the spirit.

2 Cor 5 1An house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens] An immortal body, one that houses the spirit eternally and dwells in the realms of glory.

SC2 Cor 5 22 Cor 5 32 Cor 5 42-4. The saints desire to be raised "in immortality unto eternal life," that is to come forth in the resurrection and be clothed with robes of righteousness. (D. & C. 29:43.)

2 Cor 5 55. The earnest of the Spirit] See Eph. 1:13-14.

2 Cor 5 66. We are absent from the Lord] We left the pre-mortal estate and are no longer in the personal presence of the Lord.

2 Cor 5 77. We walk by faith, not by sight] In the pre-mortal life we were in the presence of God; we saw him and knew the course he had charted for us. Now we are out of his personal presence, no longer remember our association in his household, and are being tested under circumstances where we must accept him and his laws on faith.

2 Cor 5 1010. See Rev. 20:11-15.

2 Cor 5 10Salvation] See 1 Pet. 1:1-16. Pre-existence] See Heb. 12:9-10.
