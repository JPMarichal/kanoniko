Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 2 ( 1971)

Walk Uprightly as Children of Light

SCEph 4 31Eph 4 32Ethical principles, godly conduct, personal righteousness, everything that is good and right—all such grow out of the gospel.

SCEph 4 31Eph 4 32Exhortations to walk in the light, to pursue a course leading to celestial peace, to keep the commandments—all such bear fruit when men know the doctrines of Christ.

SCEph 4 31Eph 4 32If there were no God, no gospel, no eternal life, men might as well eat, drink, be merry, and let lust and passion rule.

SCEph 4 31Eph 4 32But there is a God; he has ordained a gospel of salvation; the saints were foreordained to receive the plan of salvation; they are sealed by the Holy Spirit of Promise; salvation does come by grace through faith; the blood of Christ does save, redeem, and justify; the Gentiles are fellow heirs with Israel; there is one true Church; apostles and prophets guide its destiny and give revelation to the saints and to the world—all of which Paul has already told the Ephesians with power and in plainness.

SCEph 4 31Eph 4 32Building on this foundation—a foundation of God and his gospel—Paul is now continuing his mighty exhortations, exhortations that change the lives of men who believe in Christ and his gospel.

Eph 4 314:31. Bitterness] Caustic and acrimonious attitudes; feelings of harshness or cruelty toward others.

Eph 4 31Wrath] See Gal. 5:16-26.

Eph 4 31Anger] "As with nearly all strong emotions or passions, anger is manifest both in righteousness and in unrighteousness. Always there is a sense of displeasure attending it, and usually this is accompanied by a feeling of antagonism, excited by a sense of injury or insult.

Eph 4 31"Righteous anger is an attribute of Deity. His anger is everlastingly kindled against the wicked. (D. & C. 1:13; 5:8; 60:2; 63:11, 32; 84:24.) Similarly, an inspired man might speak or act in righteous anger, as when Moses broke the tablets upon which the Ten Commandments were written, or as when our Lord drove the money changers from the temple." (Mormon Doctrine, 2nd ed., p. 37.)

Eph 4 31Clamour] A vocal and violent outcry giving vent to discontent.

Eph 4 31Evil speaking] False, corrupt, wicked, degrading, profane, or belittling words spoken about persons or things.

Eph 4 31Malice] Ill will or enmity of heart toward others; feelings of dislike which are deep seated and unreasonable and which take pleasure in seeing others suffer.

Eph 4 3232. Tenderhearted] Compassionate; easily moved to love, sorrow, or pity.

Eph 4 32God for Christ's sake hath forgiven you] Forgiveness comes from the Father, because of Christ's atonement, to those who obey the gospel.

Eph 5 15:1. Be... followers of God] 'Walk in the paths he walked in, obey the laws he obeyed, do the things he did, until you become "perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect" (Matt. 5:48), until you become like him and have exaltation with him.' God is the great prototype; Jesus is next. "What did Jesus do? 'Why; I do the things I saw my Father do. ... My Father worked out his kingdom with fear and trembling, and I must do the same.'... So that Jesus treads in the tracks of his Father." (Teachings, pp. 347-348.)

Eph 5 1Dear children] Sons of God, adopted members of the family of God the Father, sons who are joint-heirs with Christ and who are thus followers of God because they tread in the tracks of Jesus.

Eph 5 22. Christ. . . a sacrifice to God for a sweetsmelling savour]Even as each sacrifice offered anciently, as it prefigured the coming sacrifice of the Lamb of God, was "a sweet savour . . . unto the Lord" (Ex. 29:18), so was Christ's offering of himself a pleasing thing to God. The sweet smell of the burning sacrifices in Israel symbolized the pleasing blessings flowing from our Lord's personal offering.

Eph 5 33. Fornication] See 1 Cor. 6:9-20.

Eph 5 3Uncleanness] Moral filthiness, obscenity, or unchastity; any unholy or impure practice, as masturbation.

Eph 5 3Covetousness] See 1 Cor. 6:9-20.

Eph 5 44. Filthiness] Obscenity, or any form of moral corruption or transgression.

Eph 5 4Foolish talking] Talk of fools, as conversation without depth or substance which makes light of the plan of salvation and sacred things.

Eph 5 4Jesting] Not something said in banter or raillery which is not to be taken seriously, as is our current definition. But, as the original Greek word indicates, a form of discourse "which turns about and adapts itself, without regard to principle, to the shifting circumstances of the moment, and to the varying moods of those with whom it may deal." (Jamieson, p. 353.) However, the true saints are expected to maintain a sound and balanced sobriety in harmony with the command: "Cast away your idle thoughts and your excess of laughter far from you." (D. & C. 88:69.)

Eph 5 55. Whoremonger] A whoremaster, that is, one given to whoring, a lecher.

Eph 5 5Covetous... idolater] See 1 Cor. 6:9-20.

Eph 5 66. Children of disobedience] See Eph. 2:1-10.

Eph 5 88. Children of light] The saints who have come out of the darkness of the world into the marvelous light of the gospel.

Eph 5 1414. Paul is either quoting a lost scripture or giving a paraphrasing interpretation to Isaiah 60:1. "Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the Lord is risen upon thee."

Eph 5 1616. Redeeming the time] Buying up or making wise use of the time.

Eph 5 1818. Be not drunk with wine] "Inasmuch as any man drinketh wine or strong drink among you, behold it is not good, neither meet in the sight of your Father." (D. & C. 89:5.)

Eph 5 18Be filled with the Spirit] 'Gain the constant companionship of the Holy Ghost.'

Eph 5 18Obedience] See Jas. 1:22-25.
