The multitude of details which of themselves bear witness to the veracity of the account; and 2. The spiritual fortitude and power exhibited by Paul, including his exercise of the gift of seership. By fasting and faith Paul prevailed upon the Lord, who sent his angel to deliver the message, to give divine assurance of preservation for all aboard the ill fated vessel. Then he announced in advance exactly what was to happen, exhibiting thereby his powers of seership. See Commentary I, pp. 132-134.
