Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 2 ( 1971)

Crucify the Flesh and Walk in the Spirit

SCGal 5 16Gal 5 17Gal 5 18Gal 5 19Gal 5 20Gal 5 21Gal 5 22Gal 5 23Gal 5 24Gal 5 25Gal 5 26In this mortal probation our objective is to follow God and flee from Satan, to overcome the world, to put off the natural man and put on Christ, to bridle our passions, to rise above "the lusts of the flesh and the things of the world," to refrain from "all manner of iniquity" (1 Ne. 22:23)—to crucify the flesh and walk in the Spirit. Overcome the world] See Rev. 2:1-7.

Gal 5 1616. Walk in the Spirit] Keep the commandments after baptism and thus gain the promised companionship of the Holy Ghost.

Gal 5 1717. There is eternal warfare between good and evil, virtue and vice, the saints and the world, the flesh and the Spirit.

Gal 5 1818. Or: 'If you are led by the Spirit, you have left the world and become a saint.'

Gal 5 1919. Works of the flesh] All unrighteous acts.

Gal 5 19Adultery, fornication] See 1 Cor. 6:9-20.

Gal 5 19Uncleanness] State of accountable persons whose sins have not been washed away in the waters of baptism, and who are not keeping the commandments so as to walk in the Spirit. "No unclean thing can inherit the kingdom of heaven." (Alma 11:37.)

Gal 5 19Lasciviousness] See 2 Cor. 12:11-21; 13:1-4.

Gal 5 2020. Idolatry] See 1 Cor. 6:9-20.

Gal 5 20Witchcraft] Sorcery; intercourse with evil spirits; a system of false religion wherein mediums or other persons communicate with devils. (Mormon Doctrine, 2nd ed., pp. 839-840.)

Gal 5 20Hatred] "Hate is the opposite of love; in its full force it is to abhor, abominate, and detest; in lesser degree it is merely to dislike or regard with displeasure. Hatred is a proper and holy emotion when channeled properly. 'Hate the evil, and love the good.' (Amos 5: 15.)" (Mormon Doctrine, 2nd ed., p. 344.)

Gal 5 20Variance] Disagreement on gospel principles; dissension and discord in the Church; unwarranted differences of opinions about the law of the Lord.

Gal 5 20Emulations] Envious rivalries born of jealousy and worldly ambitions.

Gal 5 20Wrath] Violent anger and rage, often leading to attempts at revenge for real or supposed wrongs.

Gal 5 20Strife] Strugglings for superiority; altercations and conflicts.

Gal 5 20Seditions] Acts of resistance to either church or governmental authority; stirring up of discontent against either of these authorities.

Gal 5 20Heresies] See 1 Cor. 11:16-19.

Gal 5 2121. Envyings] Begrudging discontent at the excellence and good fortune of others. "Preach against all lyings, and deceivings, and envyings, and strifes, and malice, and revilings, and stealing, robbing, plundering, murdering, committing adultery, and all manner of lasciviousness, crying that these things ought not so to be." (Alma. 16:18.)

Gal 5 21Murders] See 1 John 3:10-18.

Gal 5 21Drunkenness] See 1 Cor. 6:9-20.

Gal 5 21Revellings] Acts of unseemly merrymaking, carousing, and conviviality.

Gal 5 2122. Fruit of the Spirit] Attributes of God.

Gal 5 21Love] See Rom. 13:8-10.

Gal 5 21Joy] A state of happiness and bliss. "Men are, that they might have joy." (2 Ne. 2:25.)

Gal 5 21Peace] Both freedom from war and an inner sense of tranquillity and satisfaction.

Gal 5 21Longsuffering] Patient and extended endurance of offenses.

Gal 5 21Gentleness] Refinement, gentility, and honorableness.

Gal 5 21Goodness] Righteousness, virtue, and excellence.

Gal 5 21Faith] See Heb. 11:1-3.

Gal 5 2323. Meekness] "The meek are the godfearing and the righteous. They are the ones who willingly conform to the gospel standards, thus submitting their wills to the will of the Lord." (Mormon Doctrine, 2nd ed., p. 474.) "Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth" (3 Ne. 12:5), meaning in that day when celestial beings abide forever on its surface. (D. & C. 88:16-32.)

Gal 5 23Temperance] A course of moderation and control of the passions and appetites; also, abstinence from the use of intoxicants.

Gal 5 2424. 'The saints have overcome the world.'

Gal 5 2525. 'If we are members of the Church, let us be guided by the Holy Ghost.'

Gal 5 2626. Vainglory] Excessive pride in one's own accomplishments, abilities, or achievements, as manifest through boasting and vaunting of one's self.
