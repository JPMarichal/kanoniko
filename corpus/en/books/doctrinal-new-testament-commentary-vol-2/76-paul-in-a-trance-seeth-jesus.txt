Paul in a Trance Seeth Jesus

Acts 22 1717. A trance] See Acts 10:9-20.

SCActs 22 18Acts 22 19Acts 22 20Acts 22 2118-21. For the first time we here learn that the Lord Jesus appeared again to Paul when the Apostle first visited Jerusalem to report his labors and participate in the council which determined the issue of circumcision. See Acts 15:1-35. It was in this appearance that the converted Paul received the direct and personal commission to go to the Gentiles. See Acts 13:42-49.
