Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 2 ( 1971)

True Ministers Set Examples of Righteousness

Philip 3 1414. I press toward the mark] 'I press forward with a steadfastness in Christ. I endure to the end. I keep the commandments after baptism. I do the works of righteousness. I work out my salvation.' The prize] Eternal life.

SCPhilip 3 16Philip 3 1716-17. All saints should seek salvation in the same way Paul does.

Philip 3 1717. Be followers . . . of me] As with Christ so with his ministers, those who stand in his place and stead in administering life and salvation to men, all are exemplars. How many souls have been saved because a righteous man has been able to say, with Christ, "Follow thou me." (2 Ne. 31:10.)

Philip 3 1919. Whose God is their belly] They honor sensual appetite, which is symbolic of the appetites and passions of the flesh, like a god.

Philip 3 2020. Our conversation is in heaven] 'Our citizenship is in heaven; as citizens of the kingdom of God on earth, we are preparing for eternal citizenship in his kingdom in heaven.'

Philip 3 2121. Our vile body] 'Our corruptible, mortal body.' His glorious body] 'His celestial, immortal body.'

Philip 3 21Righteousness] See Gal. 5:5-15.
