Analysis and Summary

Chapter 19 continues the tempo of rejoicing begun in chapter 18. John hears the multitude of heaven doing one complete unified act of praise: âHallelujah! Salvation, glory and power belong to our Godâ (19:1). The adoration focuses on those aspects of God revealed through his victory over Babylon. Out of this grows the rest of the praise: âHe hath judged the great whore . . . and hath avenged the blood of his servantsâ (19:2). Judgment always comes. During this act of praise, a voice from the throne speaks. When a voice near the altar spoke in a former vision (16:7), the focus was on the witness and suffering of the Saints. In the present vision, the Saints suffer no longer. Thus, from the source of governmentâfor God is on his throneâgoes forth the call to praise (19:5). All the heavenly hosts join in the anthem.
