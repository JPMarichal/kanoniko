Analysis and Summary

With the promise that men and women can truly become as God, the magnificent visions of John close. The sweep of his prophecy has been vast, stretching from the premortal existence to the postmortal glory. He has placed all history in its cosmic setting and shown its movement to the end of time. Analysis and Summary

With the promise that men and women can truly become as God, the magnificent visions of John close. The sweep of his prophecy has been vast, stretching from the premortal existence to the postmortal glory. He has placed all history in its cosmic setting and shown its movement to the end of time.
