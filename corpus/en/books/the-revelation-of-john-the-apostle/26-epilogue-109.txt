The book of Revelation contains, as John clearly stated in his introduction, the revelation of Jesus Christ. That revelation was given to the Son by God, the Father, and with little wonder since it showcases what the Savior would do. In it he is revealed as the divine, visible, and invincible Lamb, and the Father as the omnipotent, curtained God. Though unseen, God is a God of action.
