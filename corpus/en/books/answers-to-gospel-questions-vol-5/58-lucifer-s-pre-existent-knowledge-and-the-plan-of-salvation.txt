Joseph Fielding Smith, Answers to Gospel Questions, vol. 5 ( 1966)

56

Lucifer's Pre-existent Knowledge and the Plan of Salvation

Question: "Assuming that Lucifer, too, could discern between good and evil, account for the fact that he played into the program of the fall and salvation so well."

Answer: If Lucifer had not known the effects of his rebellion, how would he ever have become perdition? If he were ignorant he could not have become perdition. He was not ignorant, and therefore, he became perdition. They who followed him were sons of perdition, because he and they sinned knowingly. They did what they did with their eyes open, and he was in rebellion against God.

However, he did not know all the purposes of the Father as we learn from the Pearl of Great Price:

And Satan put it into the heart of the serpent, (for he had drawn away many after him,) and he sought also to beguile Eve, for he knew not the mind of God, wherefore he sought to destroy the world. (Moses 4:6.)

Therefore, the Lord turned Lucifer's evil into good to accomplish his purposes.
