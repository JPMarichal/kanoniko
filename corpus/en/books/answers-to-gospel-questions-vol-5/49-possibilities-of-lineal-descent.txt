Joseph Fielding Smith, Answers to Gospel Questions, vol. 5 ( 1966)

47

Possibilities of Lineal Descent

Question: "When one receives a blessing, is he to understand that the lineage of which he is of descent is the only lineage of which he is from? To explain this a little more, if one is told in his blessing that he is of the seed of Joseph through the lineage of Ephraim, may he still be of descent of Reuben, or Benjamin, or Simeon? Or is this literal descent of which you spoke of in your book to be recognized as being of that lineage only that he is told in his blessing?"

Answer: This is in answer to your question about patriarchs and patriarchal blessings.

The patriarch has the right by revelation to declare the lineage of those who are blessed. It is true that we are of mixed lineage. A man said to be of the lineage of Ephraim may also be a "descendant of Reuben, Benjamin or Simeon," but the blood that predominates is the one that counts. Therefore, two brothers could be assigned to a different lineage. I call your attention to the difference between Jacob and Esau.
