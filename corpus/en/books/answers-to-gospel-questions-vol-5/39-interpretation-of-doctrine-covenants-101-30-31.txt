Interpretation of Doctrine Covenants 101:30-31

Question: "Our question concerns D. & C. 101:30-31. Will the nonmember living upon the earth in the millennium also be changed in the twinkling of an eye to immortality, or does he go into the grave? Is the member of the Church the only one to whom this applies?

Answer: This is my answer to your question in relation to the following verses in the Doctrine and Covenants.

In that day an infant shall not die until he is old; and his life shall be as the age of a tree.

And when he dies he shall not sleep, that is to say in the earth, but shall be changed in the twinkling of an eye, and shall be caught up, and his rest shall be glorious. (D. & C. 101:30-3l.)

CHANGE WILL COME OVER ALL WHO REMAIN

Evidently verses 30 and 31 indicate that a change will come over all who remain on the earth after it is cleansed. Turn to the preceding question in this volume and read the words of Isaiah, particularly verse 20.

Since the earth is going to be changed from a telestial to a terrestrial earth then all on the face of it that are left will be changed to terrestrial bodies. Therefore, there will be no sickness nor death, but there will be upon the face of the earth all those who have lived a terrestrial law. Among these will be Catholics, Protestants, Mohammedans, and whatnot, and we will continue to preach the gospel until all are converted.

If there are any who will not receive the gospel under those conditions the curse of the Lord will be upon them.
