Where Was Adam's Body Created?

Question: "In Volume 1 of Doctrines of Salvation we read that Adam's body was made from the dust of this earth, and further on we read and understand that he was translated from another planet. Did we understand this correctly?"

Answer: The subject matter and scriptural references on pages 90-91 of Doctrines of Salvation, Volume 1, seemed to be sufficiently clear to establish the fact that Adam's body was created from the dust of this earth. However, I shall re-emphasize the matter in hopes that I might be clearly understood.

Adam and Eve, like all the rest of us, were in the spirit world before the earth was formed. When the earth was prepared the Father prepared a body of flesh and bones for Adam and Eve and their eternal spirits were placed in them. They were so created that they were not subject to death. (2 Nephi 2:22.) By partaking of the forbidden fruit they brought the mortal condition upon themselves and became subject to death. Their bodies were made from the dust of this earth.

ADAM WAS NOT A TRANSLATED BEING

Through the atonement of Jesus Christ Adam and Eve were redeemed from the physical (or mortal) death; so were we ALL. Adam was not a translated being from another planet. He came from the spirit world just as we all do. He had not passed through any resurrection on any other earth. A resurrected person cannot die. The scriptures are clear on this point; therefore Adam was not an immortal person in the sense of having passed through the resurrection on another planet. It is true that there was no death when he was placed here. That came because of the Fall. If Adam had been a resurrected person when he came here he could not have died, or become mortal again. (For scriptural references read Chapter 6, "Michael Our Prince," Doctrines of Salvation, Vol. 1, commencing on p. 90. )
