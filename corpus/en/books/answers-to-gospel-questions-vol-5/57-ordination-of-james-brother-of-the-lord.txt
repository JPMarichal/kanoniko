Joseph Fielding Smith, Answers to Gospel Questions, vol. 5 ( 1966)

55

Ordination of James, Brother of the Lord

Question: "Can you please tell me who ordained James, the brother of the Lord, to be an apostle?"

Answer: While James the brother of the Lord was evidently called to be an apostle, we do not have any history of his ordination. Remember that the books of the New Testament tell us only fragments of history. Even the four books of the ministry of our Lord Jesus Christ, are fragmentary and do not contain one hundredth part of what the Savior did and taught. John has informed us of this fact. (John 21:25. ) Therefore we will have to wait until the full record is revealed and it is useless for us to speculate on matters which are not clearly proved.

The James who came with Peter and John to the Prophet Joseph Smith was the brother of John and son of Zebedee.
