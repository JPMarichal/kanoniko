Joseph Fielding Smith, Answers to Gospel Questions, vol. 5 ( 1966)

49

Marriage and Kingdoms

Question: "If a member of the Church has been sealed to his parents, but marries out of the Church, what kingdom will he inherit in the resurrection?"

Answer: No matter how a person is sealed he will be assigned to his proper place. If a person is sealed to his parents, or is born under the covenant, and then he lives a telestial law, to the telestial kingdom he will go, or to the terrestrial. Salvation is an individual responsibility. Only through living the gospel can we enter the celestial kingdom.

They who are clean in their lives; who are virtuous; who are honorable; but who will not receive the covenant of eternal marriage in the house of God, shall come forth—and they may even enter into the celestial kingdom, but when they enter there, they enter as servants—to wait upon those "who are worthy of a far more, and an exceedingly, and an eternal weight of glory." (D. & C. 132:16.)
