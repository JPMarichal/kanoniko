Saints Exhorted to Be Holy

SCCol 3 5Col 3 6Col 3 7Col 3 8Col 3 9Col 3 10Col 3 11Col 3 12Col 3 13Col 3 14Col 3 15Col 3 16Col 3 17Holiness becometh the saints of the Lord! "Be ye holy," saith the Lord. (Lev. 20:7.) There is no substitute for personal righteousness, for cleansing and sanctifying the human soul. Holy beings are saved beings. And so we now find Paul telling even those who are sealed up unto eternal life that they must press forward, forsaking all evil and cleaving unto all good.

Col 3 55. Mortify . . . your members] 'Deaden and control your carnal desires; discipline your appetites; control your worldly desires.'

Col 3 5Fornication] See Commentary II, pp. 337-340.

Col 3 5Uncleanness] See Commentary 11, pp. 482-484.

Col 3 5Inordinate affection] An excessive and unrestrained display of affection which exceeds the bounds of propriety or decency.

Col 3 5Evil concupiscence] Evil and lustful sexual desires.

Col 3 5Covetousness, which is idolatry] Those strong and inordinate desires for the possessions or things of another which make up covetousness are here equated with idolatry, which is the excessive love or veneration of anything other than Deity. See Commentary II, pp. 337-340.

Col 3 66. Wrath of God] The angry and indignant outpouring of divine justice upon the wicked; the meting out of just punishment to those who rebel against God and his laws.

Col 3 6Children of disobedience] See Commentary II, pp. 497-500.

Col 3 88. Anger] See Commentary II, pp. 514-517.

Col 3 8Wrath] See Commentary II, pp. 482-484.

Col 3 8Malice] See Commentary 11, pp. 514-517.

Col 3 8Blasphemy] "Blasphemy consists in either or both of the following:1. Speaking irreverently, evilly, abusively, or scurrilously against God or sacred things; or 2. Speaking profanely or falsely about Deity.

Col 3 8"Among a great host of impious and sacrilegious speaking that constitute blasphemy are such things as: Taking the name of God in vain; evil-speaking about the Lord's annointed; belittling sacred temple ordinances, or patriarchal blessings, or sacramental administrations; claiming unwarranted divine authority; and promulgating with profane piety a false system of salvation." (Mormon Doctrine, 2nd ed., p. 90.)

Col 3 8Filthy Communications] Obscene, pornographic, or vulgar pictures, speech, or writing. See Commentary II, pp. 514-517.

Col 3 99. Lie not] Lying is worldly, perfect honesty is saintly. Liars shall be damned, the perfectly honest shall be saved. (Mormon Doctrine, 2nd ed., pp. 440-441.)

Col 3 9The old man] The natural man, the man of sin, who lives after the manner of the world.

Col 3 1010. The new man] The saint of God, the man of righteousness, whose life is now patterned after Christ's.

Col 3 1212. Elect of God] Those foreordained persons who believe in Christ, accept his gospel, gain the blessings of the temple, and thereafter continue to walk in paths of truth and righteousness. (Mormon Doctrine, 2nd ed., pp. 217-218.) See Commentary II, pp. 271-278.

Col 3 12Bowels] The seat of such feelings as compassion, tenderness, pity, and kindness.

Col 3 12Mercies] Manifestations of such things as compassion, forgiveness, clemency, charity, and benevolence.

Col 3 12Kindness] Acts embodying such things as good will, sympathy, tenderness, or benevolence, which show forth an interest in the welfare of others.

Col 3 12Humbleness of mind] Freedom from pride and arrogance; an unassuming attitude.

Col 3 12Meekness] See Commentary II, pp. 482-484.

Col 3 12Longsuffering] See Commentary II, pp. 482-484.

Col 3 1313. "Ye ought to forgive one another; for he that forgiveth not his brother his trespasses standeth condemned before the Lord; for there remaineth in him the greater sin. I, the Lord, will forgive whom I will forgive, but of you it is required to forgive all men." (D. & C. 64:9-10.)

Col 3 1414. "And above all things, clothe yourselves with the bond of charity, as with a mantle, which is the bond of perfectness and peace." (D. & C. 88:125.) See Commentary II, pp. 377-380.

Col 3 1515. Be ye thankful] "Thou shalt thank the Lord thy God in all things." (D. & C. 59:7.)

Col 3 1616. Lot the word of Christ dwell in you] 'Feast upon the words of Christ; let the doctrines of salvation sink into your soul.'

Col 3 16Teaching and admonishing one another] "Teach one another the doctrine of the kingdom." (D. & C. 88:77.) Singing with grace in your hearts to the Lord] "My soul delighteth in the song of the heart," the Lord says, "yea, the song of the righteous is a prayer unto me, and it shall be answered with a blessing upon their heads." (D. & C. 25:12.)

Col 3 1717. Any word which cannot be spoken in the name of Christ should be left unsaid, any deed that cannot properly bear our Lord's name should be left undone.
