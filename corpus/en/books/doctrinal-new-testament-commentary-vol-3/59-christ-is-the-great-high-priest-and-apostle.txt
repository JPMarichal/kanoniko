Christ Is the Great High Priest and Apostle

Heb 3 11. Wherefore . . . consider] In the light of all that is said in the first two chapters of Hebrews—that Christ is the Son; that he is pre-eminent over the angels; that he became mortal to work out the infinite and eternal atonement; and that through him man is reconciled to God—let us now consider him and his doctrine.

Heb 3 1Holy brethren] Faithful members of the Church who, as the subsequent presentations show, held the Melchizedek Priesthood.

Heb 3 1The heavenly calling] The Melchizedek Priesthood, which is the "calling" from God through which eternal life is gained. (D. & C. 84:33-41.)

Heb 3 1The Apostle] Christ, who was and is his own chief witness, who testifies plainly to the faithful of all ages: "I am the Son of God."

Heb 3 1High Priest] "Christ is the Great High Priest; Adam next." (Teachings, p. 158.) That is, Christ is the chief minister of salvation for men on earth, in that through his atoning sacrifice salvation itself comes. See Heb. 5:4-14.

Heb 3 1Our profession] 'Our faith; our gospel; our priesthood.'

As the Hebrew Saints knew, the Lord's house anciently was his people, his congregation, his saints, concerning which he said: "My servant Moses . . . is faithful in all mine house." (Num. 12:7.) Now Paul is saying that the Meridian Saints are the Lord's house, among whom is Christ the Lord who excels even Moses in glory and greatness. Moses was the servant in the house for his day and age, but Christ is the Son who made the house and whose it is.
