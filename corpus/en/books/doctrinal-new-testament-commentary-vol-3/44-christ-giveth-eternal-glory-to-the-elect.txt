Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 3 ( 1973)

Christ Giveth Eternal Glory to the Elect

2 Tim 2 88. Jesus Christ . . . was raised from the dead] See Commentary II, pp. 390-392.

2 Tim 2 99. The word of God is not bound] The gospel cannot be put in prison; the truth of heaven cannot be locked in a jail cell.

2 Tim 2 1010. Christ's ministers suffer persecution in order to make the truths of salvation available to the saints.

2 Tim 2 10The salvation which is in Christ] See 1 Pet. 1:1-16.

2 Tim 2 10Eternal glory] Exaltation; eternal life; the fulness of salvation in the highest heaven of the celestial world.

2 Tim 2 1111. Dead with him] Dead to sin. Live with him] Live in exaltation in his kingdom.

2 Tim 2 1212. Reign with him] Stand with him as kings and priests, ruling and reigning over kingdoms and dominions forever.
