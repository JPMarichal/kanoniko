Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 3 ( 1973)

Be Strong in the Faith

2 Tim 1 131:13. Sound words] True doctrine.

2 Tim 1 1414. That good thing] The gospel.

2 Tim 1 14The Holy Ghost which dwelleth in us] "The Holy Ghost has not a body of flesh and bones, but is a personage of Spirit. Were it not so, the Holy Ghost could not dwell in us." (D. & C. 130:22.) That is, the Holy Ghost is a Spirit Man, an individual, an entity, who because of his spirit state has power to speak to the spirit within man, thus dwelling in man in the sense of conveying truth to the human heart.

SC2 Tim 1 162 Tim 1 172 Tim 1 1816-18. God grants mercy to the merciful; those who succor the saints temporally shall receive rich spiritual rewards from the Lord.

2 Tim 2 12:1. Be strong] Be valiant and courageous in the war with sin. Keep the commandments.

2 Tim 2 22. Paul was one of many witnesses. And he now hopes that the truth shall go from witness to witness until all the world is warned.

2 Tim 2 33. Endure hardness] Rather, 'Suffer hardship with me.'

2 Tim 2 3Soldier of Jesus Christ] There is war on earth, as there was in heaven; Christ's ministers are soldiers in the war with sin.

SC2 Tim 2 42 Tim 2 54-5. Only the valiant warriors are saved.

2 Tim 2 66. The reward of eternal life is for the minister first and the convert second. Those who labor in the Lord's vineyard bring salvation to their own souls first and then to the souls of those who believe and obey their witness of the truth. (D. & C. 4.) "He that reapeth receiveth wages, and gathereth fruit unto life eternal." (John 4:36.)

2 Tim 2 6Valiance] See Rev. 3:14-22.

2 Tim 2 6Salvation] See 1 Pet. 1:1-16.
