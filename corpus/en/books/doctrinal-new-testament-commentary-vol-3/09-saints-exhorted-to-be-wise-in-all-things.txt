Col 4 16Col 4 10The First Epistle of Paul the Apostle to the Thessalonians

Col 4 16Col 4 10Many doors of gospel investigation are opened by Paul in this first epistle to the Thessalonians. Supposed to be one of his earliest writings, he takes occasion in it to remind his beloved associates in Thessalonica of the coming of our Lord, of the power resident in his gospel, of how diligent they must be in walking as becometh saints, and of summarizing many church and ministerial duties. His explanations relative to the Second Coming contain what is probably the best approach in any inspired writing to an interpretation of when the signs of the times shall be fulfilled.
