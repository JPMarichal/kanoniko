God Poureth Out Plagues upon the Wicked

Rev 16 1Rev 16 2Rev 16 3Rev 16 4Rev 16 5Rev 16 6Rev 16 7Rev 16 8Rev 16 9Rev 16 10Rev 16 11Rev 16 12SCRev 15 5Rev 15 6Rev 15 7Rev 15 8God in his mercy shall pour out destructive plagues upon the wicked and ungodly in the last days. These diseases and calamities shall sweep great hosts of men from the face of the earth, preparatory to that final Millennial cleansing which shall prepare our planet as an abode for the righteous. As shown to John, the seven last plagues are:

Rev 16 1Rev 16 2Rev 16 3Rev 16 4Rev 16 5Rev 16 6Rev 16 7Rev 16 8Rev 16 9Rev 16 10Rev 16 11Rev 16 12SCRev 15 5Rev 15 6Rev 15 7Rev 15 81. A noisome and grievous sore. (Rev. 16:2.)

Rev 16 1Rev 16 2Rev 16 3Rev 16 4Rev 16 5Rev 16 6Rev 16 7Rev 16 8Rev 16 9Rev 16 10Rev 16 11Rev 16 12SCRev 15 5Rev 15 6Rev 15 7Rev 15 8Could this be the plague spoken of by Zechariah in these words: "And this shall be the plague wherewith the Lord will smite all the people that have fought against Jerusalem; Their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, and their eyes shall consume away in their holes, and their tongue shall consume away in their mouth." (Zech. 14:12.) And is the Lord referring to it in these words of latter-day revelation: "And their flesh shall fall from off their bones, and their eyes from their sockets"? (D. & C. 29:19.)

Rev 16 1Rev 16 2Rev 16 3Rev 16 4Rev 16 5Rev 16 6Rev 16 7Rev 16 8Rev 16 9Rev 16 10Rev 16 11Rev 16 12SCRev 15 5Rev 15 6Rev 15 7Rev 15 82. Turning the waters of the sea to blood with the consequent death of the life therein. (V. 3.)

Rev 16 1Rev 16 2Rev 16 3Rev 16 4Rev 16 5Rev 16 6Rev 16 7Rev 16 8Rev 16 9Rev 16 10Rev 16 11Rev 16 12SCRev 15 5Rev 15 6Rev 15 7Rev 15 8Could this be the curse to which the Lord was referring when he said: "Behold, I, the Lord, in the beginning blessed the waters; but in the last days, by the mouth of my servant John, I cursed the waters. Wherefore, the days will come that no flesh shall be safe upon the waters"? (D. & C. 61:14-15.)

Rev 16 1Rev 16 2Rev 16 3Rev 16 4Rev 16 5Rev 16 6Rev 16 7Rev 16 8Rev 16 9Rev 16 10Rev 16 11Rev 16 12SCRev 15 5Rev 15 6Rev 15 7Rev 15 83. Turning of the rivers and fountains to blood so that all drinking water is diseased. (Vs. 4-7.)

Rev 16 1Rev 16 2Rev 16 3Rev 16 4Rev 16 5Rev 16 6Rev 16 7Rev 16 8Rev 16 9Rev 16 10Rev 16 11Rev 16 12SCRev 15 5Rev 15 6Rev 15 7Rev 15 84. Changes in the effects of the sun so that men are scorched with fire and great heat. (Vs. 8-9.)

Rev 16 1Rev 16 2Rev 16 3Rev 16 4Rev 16 5Rev 16 6Rev 16 7Rev 16 8Rev 16 9Rev 16 10Rev 16 11Rev 16 12SCRev 15 5Rev 15 6Rev 15 7Rev 15 85. Spread of darkness (probably spiritual darkness) and pain and sores in the kingdoms of the world. (Vs. 10-11.)

Rev 15 5Rev 16 115:5; 16:1. The Temple] See Rev. 11:15-19.

Rev 15 515:5. The tabernacle of the testimony in heaven] The courts of heavenly glory from which spiritual truth is dispensed, even as the earthly tabernacle (itself a temple) was the place from which light and knowledge was dispensed to the faithful in Israel.

Rev 15 77. Vials] Bowls; small vessels for liquids.

Rev 16 116:1. Pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth] Pour out vengeance, anger, and plagues upon the earth and its inhabitants.

SCRev 16 5Rev 16 6Rev 16 75-7. Men receive what they merit. The plagues of latter-days are a just retribution heaped upon those whose lives have been such as to reap the very harvest of pain, sorrow, and damnation which they receive.

Rev 16 55. The angel of the waters] Not a figurative expression, but the angel who poured out his vial upon the rivers and fountains of waters.

Rev 16 77. "The judgments of the Lord are true and righteous altogether." (Psalm 19:9.) How true it is, from everlasting to everlasting! What the Lord does is right! In "the day of judgment," the wicked shall "shrink with awful fear . . . and be constrained to exclaim: Holy, holy are thy judgments, O Lord God Almighty—but I know my guilt; I transgressed thy law, and my transgressions are mine; and the devil hath obtained me, that I am a prey to his awful misery." (2 Ne. 9:46.)
