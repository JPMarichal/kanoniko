Joseph Smith, Alma P. Burton, Discourses of the Prophet Joseph Smith ( 1977)

Acknowledgments

The first edition of this book was published in 1956. President David O. McKay and his counselors approved of using the doctrinal statements of the Prophet Joseph Smith for this purpose. Further assistance was rendered by A. Hamer Reiser, who was assigned by the First Presidency to act as a consultant. Sincere appreciation is expressed for the kindness of the First Presidency and the assistance of Elder Reiser. In 1965 this publication was enlarged and revised. The present format was first issued in 1977.

Special appreciation is expressed to my wife, Clea, who has been extremely helpful with the initial preparation of the manuscript and the revision of this edition.
