"Let's Put a Dollar in Each Shoe"

OSCAR A. KIRKHAM

Two boys, playing along a ditch bank, found a pair of shoes at the edge of a field. One boy said, "Let's fill them with rocks and see what the owner does."

The other boy said, "Let's put a dollar in each shoe and see what happens."

They followed the second suggestion.

The man was watering the field and finally came back to get his shoes, which he had put aside so carefully in order to save them.

When he put his foot into his shoe, he pulled it out again quickly and found the dollar. Then he looked in the other shoe and found another dollar.

Tears came to his eyes, and he kneeled on the ground and thanked his Heavenly Father in a voice filled with emotion that now he could buy some food to take home to his hungry family. The two boys also went home thankful and happy.

Oscar A. Kirkham, Say the Good Word (Deseret Book Co., 1958).
