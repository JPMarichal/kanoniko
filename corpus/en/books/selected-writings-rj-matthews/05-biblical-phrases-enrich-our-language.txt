Chapter 2

Biblical Phrases Enrich Our Language

The Bible has contributed a number of phrases to our speech that seem to be heard everywhere, both in religious and nonreligious conversation. Sometimes these phrases are used in a casual way, sometimes in prayer. They illuminate a sentence with meaning and enhance the thoughts.

Bible Influence

Consider also these expressions, which although not exact quotations from the Bible, yet carry with them a distinct memory of some ancient story:

Â The flesh pots of Egypt

Â Manna in the wilderness

Â A mess of pottage

Â Joseph's coat

Â Mantle of Elijah

Â Widow's mite

Â House of Martha

Â Friend of publicans

Â Steady the ark

Â Handwriting on the wall

By such phrases familiar to us all, the Bible has poured a wealth of material and meaning into our language far beyond the measure of bare words.
