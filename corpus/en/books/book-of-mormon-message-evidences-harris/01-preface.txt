Franklin S. Harris, Book of Mormon: Message and Evidences ( 1961)

Preface

A series of radio talks given during the summer and fall of 1946 on Sunday Evening on Temple Square form the basis for the present volume. New material has been added and rearrangements made to better fit book form. An attempt has been made to give appropriate references where further material may be found in addition to material directly quoted. A selected bibliography has been added.

3 The author wishes to express his appreciation to the Church Missionary Committee, and particularly to the committee Executive Secretary Gordon B. Hinckley who has been most helpful at all times. Valuable suggestions and other forms of aid have been given by, among others, Dr. M. Wells Jakeman, Dr. C. Lowell Lees, the late Mrs. Huberta Berg Robison, Dr. Sidney B. Sperry, Thomas Stuart Ferguson, Dr. Milton R. Hunter; the Church Historian's Library, in particular A. William Lund, the late Alvin Smith, E. Cecil McGavin and Earl Olson; the Brigham Young University Library and Miss Anna Ollorton; and the University of Utah Library. The late Dr. John A. Widtsoe with whom the author first started studying seriously the Book of Mormon gave aid and advice. To the great host of students of the Book of Mormon, friendly and otherwise, who have produced the great body of material on which this brief study is based, the debt is partially indicated in the appropriate references. The selection of material and views expressed are the author's own.

3 Note to Second Edition: Some corrections and additions have been made with the aid of suggestions, in addition to the above, of Dr. Ross T. Christensen, Dr. Welby W. Ricks and John L. Sorenson.

FRANKLIN S. HARRIS, JR.
