KEY TO ABBREVIATIONS USED IN FOOTNOTES

The Bible, Book of Mormon, and Pearl of Great Price references are made by name of book, chapter, and verse. Thus—Genesis 4:3, Mosiah 2:9, or Moses 3:7.

The Doctrine and Covenants references are made by the abbreviation D&C followed by the section and verse. Thus—D&C 93:36.
