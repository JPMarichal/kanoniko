James E. Faust, To Reach Even unto You ( 1990)

Preface

Since becoming a General Authority on October 6, 1972, I have not managed time well enough to write for the simple satisfaction of writing. Time and effort in this area have been directed toward the preparation of talks to be given in general conferences, June conferences, area conferences, and at devotionals and firesides at Brigham Young University and other places. This book is composed of some of the speeches given, after some editing for the benefit of the readers.

All mankind ultimately reaches down to its smallest part—to each individual. Each soul has a value that is infinite and priceless. It is the one person who really matters.

Part of the searching each of us goes through in order to find our own identity involves an inward reach. It is prayerfully hoped that there might be found in these pages some messages that will strengthen feelings of self worth and of an enlightened human purpose. It is also hoped that there may be found messages of hope, of optimism, and of new beginnings.

Appreciation is expressed to many people, mostly to my beloved Ruth, to my family, and to Elder Neal A. Maxwell and Elder Marion D. Hanks for many helpful suggestions. Without their encouragement this effort would never have been. A thought from Paul seems to be appropriate: "But we will not boast of things without our measure, but according to the measure of the rule which God hath distributed to us, a measure to reach even unto you." (2 Corinthians 10:13. Italics added.)
