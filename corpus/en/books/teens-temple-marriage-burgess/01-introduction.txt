Introduction

"The most important things that any member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ever does in this world are: 1. To marry the right person, in the right place, by the right authority; and 2. To keep the covenant made in connection with this holy and perfect order of matrimony—thus assuring the obedient persons of an inheritance of exaltation in the celestial kingdom." (Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine [Salt Lake City: Bookcraft, 1966], p. 118.)

Because of the importance of temple marriage, Satan is doing everything in his power to keep us from making the proper decision about marriage. It will take more than a few firesides and some lessons on temple marriage to counteract this influence. And then, not only do we need to marry in the temple but we also need to have prepared ourselves to assume the responsibilities of entering the temple and making covenants with the Lord.

Nine factors seem to affect whether or not a young person marries in the temple and then builds a successful marriage. Each of the first nine chapters is based on one of these factors. Chapter 10 deals with the temple ordinances and some of the things that take place in the temple.Chapter 11 focuses on what couples can do after they are married in the temple to help their children want to go to the temple themselves.
