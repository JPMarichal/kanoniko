Neal A. Maxwell, Time to Choose ( 1972)

Dedication

TO BECKY, CORY, NANCY, AND JANE

With deep appreciation for the anxious honor of welcoming you and giving you the orientation tour around your "second estate," for the patience you've shown my efforts to be your guide, for often helping to show me the way, for making my duties such a delight, and for reaffirming that the joy of my own efforts at discipleship can never be complete without you!
