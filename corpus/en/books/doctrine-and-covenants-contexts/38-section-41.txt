Joseph Smith learned as a youth that when he had a dilemma he could not resolve, he could ask God in faith and be answered, not upbraided (Joseph SmithâHistory, 1:10â19). The purpose of this book is to aid understanding of the Lord's revelations by telling the rest of the story.
