Preston Nibley, Missionary Experiences ( 1942)

FOREWORD

THE missionary efforts of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, during the one hundred and twelve years since the Church was organized in April 1830, have been unique and outstanding. One of the first commandments given to the youthful Prophet, Joseph Smith, was that the "fulness of the everlasting gospel should be proclaimed unto the ends of the world, and before kings and rulers." This was an arduous task for the early Saints, who were poor in this world's goods and were subject to constant persecution by their enemies; but they went forward fearlessly with the knowledge that they were teaching the truth and that the Lord was with them. The missionaries prospered in their humble and persistent efforts, and the work thus begun has continued to the present time.

Out of the wealth of material in the Church Historian's Library, the personal "missionary experiences" contained in this volume have been compiled. The purpose of reprinting these experiences is to acquaint the youthful members of the Church today with the noble and unselfish efforts that have been put forth by the missionaries of former years, in the great task of "building up the Kingdom of God in these last days."

This book has been prepared and published under the direction of the Presiding Bishopric.

The Compiler.
