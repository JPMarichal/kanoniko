The law of Moses encompasses hundreds of laws, rules, and regulations. The Jews have identified 613 particular laws as recorded by Moses in the books of the Torah (or the Pentateuch: Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy). The Ten Commandments and other familiar laws are included in this body, but most of the laws are unfamiliar, and seem outdated and strange to contemporary Bible readers.

Rather than trying to identify every single law, it is helpful to recognize that there are two major types of commandments within the law of Moses--eternal and temporary.
