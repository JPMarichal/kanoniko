(Ex. 19:19-25)

The Israelites spent two days cleansing themselves physically and spiritually prior to the manifestation of God to them on Mount Sinai. As a whole community, they heard the voice of the Lord as he delivered the Ten Commandments. (See Deut. 4:33; 5:4, 24.) This experience so humbled and terrified them that they requested that any future words from the Lord should come through Moses. (See Ex. 20:18-22; Deut. 5:5, 23-33.)
