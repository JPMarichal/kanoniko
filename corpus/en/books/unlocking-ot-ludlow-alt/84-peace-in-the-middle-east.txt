(Isa. 19:24)

As late as the summer of 1977, hardly any expert on the Middle East would have dared predict that Egypt and Israel would complete a peace treaty within two years. Yet a modern, political miracle occurred after the November, 1977, visit of Anwar Sadat to Jerusalem. It culminated on March 26, 1979, when these two countries signed a formal peace treaty.

As of the summer of 1980, hardly any expert on the Middle East would dare predict that Iraq (modern country ruling the ancient area of Assyria), with its Soviet leanings and strong anti-Zionist and anti-Egyptian feelings, will ever sign a peace treaty and open commercial routes with Egypt and Israel. Yet according to Isaiah 19:24, it will happen.
