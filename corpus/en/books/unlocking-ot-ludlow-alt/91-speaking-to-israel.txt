(Isa. 48-52)

In these few chapters, Isaiah defines the relationship of the Lord to various segments or types of Israelites and tells them how they can find strength in the Lord. He also includes some servant songs and promises about the gathering of Israel. All of these chapters were quoted in the Book of Mormon, especially chapter 52, which was quoted by Jacob (2 Ne. 8:24-25), Abinadi (Mosiah 12:21-24; 15:14-19, 28-30), and Jesus Christ (3 Ne. 20). These chapters helped many ancient Israelites understand their covenants with the Lord and his promises to them. Modern members of covenant Israel continue to find great inspiration in these chapters.
