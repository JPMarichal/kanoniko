There were three divisions or offices in the Levitical Priesthood: Levite, priest, and high priest. Officers of these divisions wore special attire. These vestments are mentioned and sometimes described in the scriptures (see TG "Clothing," "Garment," "Robe," "Breastplate," and "Apparel") and in later rabbinical literature.
