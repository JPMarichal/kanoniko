Richard Eyre, Linda Eyre, Teaching Children Charity ( 1986)

Epilogue

Homebase: An International Parents Organization

After we read a book and find ourselves in agreement with its principles, what we often need is some form of follow-up, some program or assistance to help us implement the principles consistently in our lives and within our own families.

This book and the others in the series (Teaching Children Joy and Teaching Children Responsibility) have spawned parenting organizations that provide additional materials (teaching manuals, music tapes, children's storybooks, and a monthly newsletter) to assist parents in teaching joy, responsibility, and charity to their children.
