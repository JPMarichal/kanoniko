DOCTRINE AND COVENANTS 80

Stephen Burnett and Eden Smith Are Called to Preach in Whatever Place They Choose

DOCTRINE AND COVENANTS 80:1-5

80:3 It mattereth not, for ye cannot go amiss. Not every assignment is made because the Spirit dictates that specific people are to serve in specific areas. Many can serve effectively in various callings as needs dictate. Those called to serve may find that the spirit in which they fill the assignment is of greater importance than either the place they serve or the nature of the calling in which they serve. The Lord molds the lives of his servants like the master potter. If his servants follow the Spirit and do his will, he can make them the right persons in the right place at the right time.
