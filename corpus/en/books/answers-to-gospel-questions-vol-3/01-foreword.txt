Joseph Fielding Smith, Answers to Gospel Questions, vol. 3 ( 1960)

Foreword

This third volume of Answers to Gospel Questions, like Volume II, contains many answers to religious questions that have never before appeared in print. Material for these questions has been gathered from the private files of President Joseph Fielding Smith and represent but a few of the thousands of personal answers by letter to members and non-members of the Church. These answers, printed herein, supplement the articles that have appeared in The Improvement Era under the title, "Your Question."

Concise rather than exhaustive treatment has been given each explanation. Careful consideration was given as to the value of information and importance of each question in doctrinal theology of the Restored Church.

The Lord has commanded that we should seek wisdom out of the best books. (D. & C. 88:118) In view of this admonition, the Deseret Book Company has made these volumes available to the reading public to assure them an authoritative answer to their questions.

Earlier this year, the Brigham Young University conferred a Special Service Award on President Smith for his many years of service to the Church and the university. The following is quoted from this special citation:

"Since his youth he has been an assiduous student of history and especially of the history of God's latter-day work. He is unquestionably one of the greatest scriptorians of this dispensation, one who knows whereof he speaks."

Though President Smith has prepared three volumes of Answers to Gospel Questions for the benefit of the reading public, it is hoped that members of the Church will be stimulated to search the scriptures and seek guidance and understanding through their own research.

—JOSEPH FIELDING SMITH, JR.
