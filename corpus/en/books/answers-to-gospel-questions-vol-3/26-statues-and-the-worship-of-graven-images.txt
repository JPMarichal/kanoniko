Statues and the Worship of Graven Images

Question: "How do we explain why we have statues of the Angel Moroni and Brigham Young when in Exodus 20:4 we are told not to have graven images, or likenesses?"

Answer: There is no difficulty in explaining why we place the angel on the temple and have a monument or statue of Brigham Young in the street. We do not worship President Brigham Young nor bow down to the monument. The image of an angel on the temple is symbolical of the angel flying through the midst of heaven. (Revelation 14:6.) Exodus 20:4 has nothing to say about erecting monuments or placing on a temple images of angels proclaiming the gospel.

WORSHIP OF FALSE GODS CONDEMNED

Exodus 20:4 is only half the sentence. Verses 5 and 6 are all part of the same sentence and the same thought. Now read these verses carefully and you will see that the condemnation is the bowing down and serving them as false gods:

Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth:

Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for I the Lord thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the Fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me;

And shewing mercy unto thousands of them that love me, and keep my commandments. (Exodus 20:4-6.)

Now I am going to ask two questions. Kindly read Exodus 25:10-20 and Exodus 37:7-9. From your thinking, why did the Lord place two cherubims on the Ark of the Covenant? A cherubim being an image of an angel, don't you think it strange according to your idea, that the Lord would make an ark embracing a covenant and place angels on it for the children of Israel?

Remember, these cherubims were made by command of the Lord.
