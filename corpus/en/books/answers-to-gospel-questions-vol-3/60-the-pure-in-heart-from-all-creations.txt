Joseph Fielding Smith, Answers to Gospel Questions, vol. 3 ( 1960)

57

The Pure in Heart from All Creations

Question: "In the Pearl of Great Price, Moses 7:64, the Lord speaks of Zion (the pure in heart) coming forth out of all the creations which I have made. Does the Lord mean all of the creations strictly of this earth? Or does he mean also the world where Enoch's translated city abides? Or does he mean out of all of the millions of worlds that he has created at the instance of his son Jesus Christ?

"Is this earth when celestialized, after the millennium, to be the final abode of the Savior?"

Answer: Answering your question: Moses 7:64, Zion (the pure in heart) coming forth out of all the creations.

The Lord is not restricted in giving invitations to other creations to visit this earth, so you need not be surprised if some visitors from other worlds do visit this one.

If you will read the second chapter in the Book of Moses, you will find that Christ was instrumental in the creation of many worlds. Reason would teach us that he would have power to retain them.

This earth, when celestialized, will be under the direction of Adam, and he will be under the direction of Jesus Christ.
