Llewelyn R. McKay, David O. McKay, Home Memories of President David O. McKay ( 1956)

Preface

The first section of this book is biographical, the second section contains father's diary relating to his trip around the world with Hugh J. Cannon, and the third section is a compilation of stories about father, the fourth section gives a glimpse into his family life, the fifth section contains selections from his talks and writings relative to practical living, and the sixth relates to tributes and honorary degrees which elucidate the personality, character, and religious depth of this great leader.

During his trip around the world, father kept his family informed of his travels by means of a day-by-day account. In re-reading this diary, I have been so impressed with the beautiful imagery, poetic descriptions, practical philosophy, keen wit, and vivid observations that I feel convinced others should have the joy of reading it. Fortunately, father has given me permission to have this interesting account published. The detailed recording of the numerous meetings which were held has been omitted, and the section headings are mine. Otherwise, the wording is father's; no one could paraphrase his writing and do justice to his authorship.

I have purposely sub-divided the sections into many headings in order to supply Church members and others—particularly the young people—with a rich source of illustrative material for talks.

It is my sincere hope that the reader will become more intimately acquainted with father through this book because to know him is to love him!

LLEWELYN R. MCKAY

Salt Lake City, January 5, 1956
