Llewelyn R. McKay, David O. McKay, Home Memories of President David O. McKay ( 1956)

ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Special acknowledgment should be made to those who have contributed to this book.

I am under great obligation to Clare Middlemiss, secretary to President McKay, who has collected material concerning his life and works and has compiled it neatly in numerous "scrapbooks."

I should like to express appreciation to Marba Josephson, Associate Managing Editor of the Improvement Era, for having undertaken the arduous task of editorial work, including critical reading, technical assistance and friendly suggestions.

Thanks also are due to Alva H. Parry, manager of the Deseret Book Company, for his cooperation and valuable help.

Finally, I wish to express appreciation to all people whose quotations appear in this volume.
