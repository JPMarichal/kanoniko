431David Lawrence McKay

432My father, David O. McKay, served as President of the Church from 1951 to January 1970, when he died at age ninety-six.] During these final years, Mother was always first in Father's heart, even when the Church came first in his time. Some of our sweetest memories from this time are of their continuing love story. Once, after they were both in wheelchairs, Brother Darcey Wright had wheeled Father out of the apartment door and down the hallway. While they were waiting for the elevator, Father suddenly exclaimed, "We have to go back. I didn't kiss [Emma] Ray good-bye." Brother Wright dutifully wheeled him back for this loving ritual that had only become more important as the years passed.
