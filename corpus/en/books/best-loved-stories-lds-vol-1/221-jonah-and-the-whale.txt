457Thomas E. Cheney

457An acquaintance met J. Golden [Kimball] on the street one day and in conversation asked, "Do you believe that Jonah was swallowed by the whale?"

457"When I get to heaven I'll ask Jonah," J. Golden answered.

457"But," said the man, "what if Jonah is not there?"

457"Then you will have to ask him," Golden quickly replied.

457Thomas E. Cheney, The Golden Legacy: A Folk History of J. Golden Kimball (Santa Barbara and Salt Lake City: Peregrine Smith, 1974), p. 109.
