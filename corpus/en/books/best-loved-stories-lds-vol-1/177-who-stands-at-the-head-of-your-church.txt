Missionary WorkLewis, Theodore B.386"Who Stands at the Head of Your Church?"

386-387Theodore B. Lewis

387At another time, Elder [Jedediah M.] Grant was challenged by a very eminent Baptist preacher, named Baldwin, to a discussion. Brother Grant consented. The place chosen was the fine, large church of his proud and imperious antagonist. Mr. Baldwin was described to me, as a man, overbearing in his manner—a regular browbeater. When the time came for the discussion, the house was densely crowded. Umpires were chosen, and everything was ready to proceed, when Brother Grant arose and said: "Mr. Baldwin. I would like to ask you a question before we proceed any farther."

387"Certainly so," said Baldwin.

387"Who stands at the head of your church in southwest Virginia?"

387Mr. Baldwin very quickly and austerely replied, "I do, sir; I do."

387"All right," said Brother Grant; "I wished to know that I had a worthy foe."

387Mr. Baldwin looked a little confused for a moment, and then said: "Mr. Grant, I would like to ask you, who stands at the head of your church in southwest Virginia?"

387Brother Grant arose and with bowed head replied, "Jesus Christ, sir."

388The shock was electrical. This inspired answer completely disarmed the proud foe, and the humble servant of God again came off victor.

388Andrew Jenson, Latter-day Saint Biographical Encyclopedia, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City: Andrew Jenson History Co., 1901), 1:58.
