GenerosityWorkman, Andrew405"I Feel Sorry for This Brother to the Amount of Five Dollars"

405Andrew Workman

405I saw the Prophet Joseph for the first time in May [of 1842]. He was with about a dozen others on the stand in a meeting. I knew him as soon as I saw him. Although I was young I knew him to be a man of God.

406A few days after this I was at Joseph's house; he was there, and several men were sitting on the fence. Joseph came out and spoke to us all. Pretty soon a man came up and said that a poor brother who lived out some distance from town had had his house burned down the night before. Nearly all of the men said they felt sorry for the man. Joseph put his hand in his pocket, took out five dollars and said, "I feel sorry for this brother to the amount of five dollars; how much do you all feel sorry?"

406Juvenile Instructor 27:641.
