HealingsSmith, George Albert350"He Blessed Me That I Might Be Healed"

350George Albert Smith

351When I was a child I became very ill. The doctor said I had typhoid fever and should be in bed for at least three weeks. He told mother to give me no solid food, but to have me drink some coffee.

351When he went away, I told mother that I didn't want any coffee. I had been taught that the Word of Wisdom, given by the Lord to Joseph Smith, advised us not to use coffee.

351Mother had brought three children into the world and two had died. She was unusually anxious about me.

351I asked her to send for Brother Hawks, one of our ward teachers. He was a worker at the foundry and a poor and humble man of great faith in the power of the Lord.

351He came, administered to me, and blessed me that I might be healed.

351When the doctor came the next morning I was playing outside with the other children. He was surprised. He examined me and discovered that my fever was gone, and that I seemed to be well.

351I was grateful to the Lord for my recovery. I was sure that he had healed me.

351 Jeremiah Stokes, Modern Miracles (Salt Lake City: Bookcraft, 1945), p. 135.
