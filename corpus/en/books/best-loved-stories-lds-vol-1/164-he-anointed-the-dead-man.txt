HealingsCowley, Matthew 355"He Anointed the Dead Man"

355 Matthew Cowley

355I was called to a home in a little village in New Zealand one day. There the Relief Society sisters were preparing the body of one of our Saints. They had placed his body in front of the Big House, as they call it, the house where the people came to wail and weep and mourn over the dead, when in rushed the dead man's brother.

355He said, "Administer to him."

355And the young natives said, "Why, you shouldn't do that; he's dead."

355"You do it!"

356 Matthew Cowley, Matthew Cowley Speaks (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1954), p. 247.
