Ed J. Pinegar, Especially for Missionaries, vol. 3 ( 1997)

Be Happy That We Can Honestly Repent

What is required of us in order to be cleansed from iniquity? Fifty-five thousand years of righteousness? No, just honest repentance. This is the key: if you do something wrong, repent. Don't muck in the mire, don't live in the past. Repent. You will also see how incredibly happy people are when they finally realize that they can repent, and you will watch the light come into their eyes because of what you've given them--the knowledge that they can repent.
