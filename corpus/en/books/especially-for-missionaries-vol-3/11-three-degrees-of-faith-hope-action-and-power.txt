Ed J. Pinegar, Especially for Missionaries, vol. 3 ( 1997)

Three Degrees of Faith: Hope, Action, and Power

Now, let's think of some personal things we must do that involve our faith, our attributes, and our skills. First of all, you will teach with power no faster than you grow in faith, because faith is the foundation of all righteousness. Faith has three degrees: that of hope, that of action, and that of power. You've read the definition of faith in Alma 32 and Hebrews 11: "Faith is the substance of things hoped for." That is the first degree of faith. James said, "I will show you my faith by my works"--meaning action. The brother of Jared said, "By faith they moved the mountains"--meaning power. If you want to read more on that, then read Lectures on Faith by the Prophet Joseph Smith and you'll begin to understand. A simple little book on the same subject is Drawing on the Powers of Heaven. Both these books will teach you how you can grow in faith to become strong.
