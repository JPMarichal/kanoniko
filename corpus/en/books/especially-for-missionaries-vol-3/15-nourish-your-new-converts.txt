Ed J. Pinegar, Especially for Missionaries, vol. 3 ( 1997)

Nourish Your New Converts

Now read the rest of John 15:16, and pay particular attention to the phrase, "that your fruit should remain." It isn't enough to teach with power and baptize. You have to make sure that your fruit remains. What are you learning six discussions for now? You are learning them for the new converts, the new members of the Church, and those six discussions are imperative for their retention. Make sure you go with a member or ask a stake missionary to come with you to give that lesson. This is vital! If you don't take a member with you, the convert will probably have a hard time making the social leap to being accepted by the members. Those six lessons are as vital to making the fruit remain as your six discussions are to their getting baptized.

You want to have as many of your investigators remain as possible; therefore, you must do the following things: Make sure you teach those six discussions (the brethren have recommended at least the first three, unless you are so well organized that the stake missionaries are right there with you, going forward in teaching all six of them). Then make sure your investigators understand the discussions. In Moro. 6:4 we read, "And after they had been received unto baptism, and were wrought upon by the power of the Holy Ghost, they were numbered among the people of the church of Christ; and their names were taken, that they might be remembered and nourished by the good word of God, to keep them in the right way. . . . " In other words, remember to keep nourishing your great converts.
