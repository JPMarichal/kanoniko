Ed J. Pinegar, Especially for Missionaries, vol. 3 ( 1997)

Purify Ourselves and Remember Who We Are

So, we've got the desire and we have the knowledge or we're working on the knowledge. Now we need to purify ourselves. Do you ever wonder why some missionaries can't do things that others can do? Why some people can perform miracles that other people can't? In 3 Ne. 8:1 we read: "And now it came to pass that according to our record, and we know our record to be true, for behold, it was a just man who did keep the record . . ." Now, if you'd like to, cross reference that to D&C 76:69, which teaches us, "These are they who are just men made perfect." They are the ones who enter the celestial kingdom.

Do you want to be a just person? Let's read that scripture again: "We know our record to be true . . ." Why? Because a just man kept the record. "For he truly did many miracles in the name of Jesus"--now here is the catch--"and there was not any man who could do a miracle in the name of Jesus save he were cleansed every whit from his iniquity" (3 Ne. 8:1; emphasis added).

We read in Moro. 7:23-30 that angels ceased to come. Why? Because the people lacked faith. If you have faith, what happens? You repent, just like Enos did. He asked, "Why am I free of this guilt, Father?" "Because of thy faith in Christ, whom thou hast never before heard nor seen" (Enos 1:8; emphasis added).

You might say, "President Pinegar, that's the trouble! You quote from Ammon and Omni here, and you quote from Enos there, and they're free from iniquity. You want me to be like them,and you forget who I am." No, that's the trouble. You've forgotten who you are. Angels appear and help in every form of godliness, and the sons and daughters of God have power to call down angels. That's part of the power of the Aaronic Priesthood.
