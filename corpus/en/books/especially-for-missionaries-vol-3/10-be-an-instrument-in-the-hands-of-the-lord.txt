Ed J. Pinegar, Especially for Missionaries, vol. 3 ( 1997)

Be an Instrument in the Hands of the Lord

When we repent in righteousness through confession and forsaking, and through good works, we become sanctified. We become holy and pure, without spot, so the Lord can work with us. That's why repentance is a daily ritual. Do you know what happens as we become purified? We become worthy instruments.

We learn about "worthy instruments" in Alma 29:9-10. Alma said, "I know that which the Lord hath commanded me [Now remember to apply this scripture to yourself], and I glory in it. I do not glory of myself, but I glory in that which the Lord hath commanded me; yea, and this is my glory, that perhaps I may be an instrument in the hands of God to bring some soul to repentance; and this is my joy." Purity and purification make you an instrument in the hands of the Lord, and the Lord will use us if we desire it in our hearts and if we're trying to be purified.
