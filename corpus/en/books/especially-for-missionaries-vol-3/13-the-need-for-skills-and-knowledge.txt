Ed J. Pinegar, Especially for Missionaries, vol. 3 ( 1997)

The Need for Skills and Knowledge

When we have shown that we have the desire, have sought knowledge and faith, and have sought to purify ourselves, we need to acquire the appropriate skills. There are many good and kind men in the world, but for a heart operation, who would you rather trust to do it, me or Elder Russell M. Nelson? All of you would choose Elder Nelson. Why? Because he's a heart surgeon and I'm not. Now, who would you choose to fill a cavity in your tooth? Elder Nelson or President Pinegar? You would choose me, and why? Because I'm a dentist.

If you don't have the skills, things won't work out the way you want them to. When you take things lightly, like your role-playing with your companion or others in your district, your skills go downhill. Let me give you an example. When you are not finding and teaching people on your mission, you don't feel good. You say things like, "Man, I want to teach somebody. I learned all this teaching stuff in the MTC and I haven't got anybody to teach."

But faith, knowledge, diligence, patience, and acquiring the skills can make all the difference. I once prayed and said, "Father, I need help." Do you know what happened? An idea came to my mind. Now when the Lord gives us knowledge, the Prophet Joseph Smith said that is revelation.
