Ed J. Pinegar, Especially for Missionaries, vol. 3 ( 1997)

Four Ways to Acquire Faith

Spencer W. Kimball, Ezra Taft Benson, and Marion G. Romney (who was in the First Presidency at one time) gave us these four ways to acquire faith. We can also increase our faith in other ways, but these are the primary ones.

Isn't it interesting that we're given the same formula to acquire the Spirit that we use to grow stronger in love and faith? Having the love of God, the love of Christ, the Spirit, and faith--all require the same four things. President Benson said that searching the scriptures, the word of God, is the only way you can have faith. That's based on Rom. 10:17: "So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God."

The next thing President Benson said was to acquire faith through fasting and prayer. Now think of Hel. 3:35 and Alma 17:3: "They did fast and pray oft, and did wax stronger and stronger in their humility, and firmer and firmer in the faith." Fasting + prayer = Faith. When you fast and pray, you will also be filled with love, just as it says in Moro. 7:48: "I will pray with all the energy of my heart, Father [I'm paraphrasing here], that I will be filled with the love of Christ, that I might preach the gospel and share it with my fellowman."

The third thing is personal righteousness. Righteousness will give you the Spirit, righteousness will give you faith, and righteousness will give you love.

The last way to acquire faith is building up the kingdom. You can be righteous by doing many things, but you are especially so when you're anxiously engaged in building up the kingdom.
