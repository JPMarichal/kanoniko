Ed J. Pinegar, Especially for Missionaries, vol. 3 ( 1997)

Teaching by the Spirit: Recognize Heavenly Father's Children

I was at the hospital a few days ago, smiling at everyone I passed in the halls, when I realized that each one of them was one of Heavenly Father's children. Then I realized that there are more than five billion children of Heavenly Father on this earth, and that there have probably been that many before now, and for all I know, there may even be five billion after now. I wondered how righteous people should feel about Heavenly Father's children. Then I thought of Ammon and Omner and Himni and Aaron (they were righteous after they repented, as all of us become righteous after we repent) and how they felt about Heavenly Father's children.

In Mosiah 28:3, they had just talked to their father, King Mosiah, who was a very great man (his best friend was Alma the prophet, so he must have been a great man). They said, "Daddy" (I don't know if they said, "Daddy," but whatever they called him, they said something like this) "could we please go to the Lamanites and preach?" Those Nephites with minimum mentality probably wondered, "Why would you want to work with those savages who are trying to kill us?" But this is how the sons of Mosiah thought: "They were desirous that salvation should be declared to every creature, for they could not bear that any human soul should perish; yea, even the very thoughts that any soul should endure endless torment, did cause them to quake and tremble" (Mosiah 28:3).
