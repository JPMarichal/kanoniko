1855-May 7-Letter on file at Church Historian's Office, Salt Lake City

ELDER ORSON HYDE

Dear Brother:

As you will be connected with the press, and in some degree control its matter, I counsel you not to write or publish anything that will require an apology from you, but be guarded in this thing to avoid back action. Let an article be well matured, and the policy of its publication considered before it is thrown to the public gaze, thereby preserving a better influence of the paper, and avoiding mortificationÂ�.
