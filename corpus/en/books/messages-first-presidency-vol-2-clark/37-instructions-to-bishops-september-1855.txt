Instructions to Bishops, September, 1855

1855-September-Circular on file in Church Historian's Office, Salt Lake City

INSTRUCTIONS TO THE BISHOPS.

To you brethren of the Bishoprick, who are or should be acquainted with every man in your respective wards, we look to make these collections, and forward them to this office, with the names of such as pay.

There are none so poor, that have health, but can do something towards liquidating their indebtedness. They should not only be willing to pay what they owe, but give liberally by donation besides, for such a benevolent purpose.

You are particularly instructed not to let any person owing the fund, leave your ward without first paying, or securing what they owe.

Be energetic brethren, and attend to this business as you value the interests of Zion, and the building up of the Kingdom of God upon the earth.

Report all the names on the accompanying list in your respective wards to this office immediately, that we may know where those indebted to the Fund reside. BRIGHAM YOUNG.
