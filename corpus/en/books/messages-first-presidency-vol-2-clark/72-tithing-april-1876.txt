A strict account must be kept by all the Elders on missions, of the tithing that passes through their hands, with the names of those who pay it. They must also have the same conveyed and reported to the General Tithing Office in this city. In those countries where a mission is organized into conferences and branches, the traveling Elders and branch Presidents must report to the President of the mission, who will gather together in one the reports from the various conferences and forward them to the General Tithing Office in Salt Lake City. In those regions where the Church is not thus permanently organized, the traveling Elders will report to the President of the district, if there be one, and he to us, or if they have no President Â�. . . they will kindly send their reports direct to headquarters in Salt Lake City. It is not sufficient that condensations or totals be represented in these reports, it is required that the name of each person who pays a tithing, or any portion of his tithing, no matter how small the amount paid, be found on the records in this office.

If it should be necessary that any of the tithing be used by those who have received permission from this office to do so, it should also be reported strictly. The account thereof must be itemized, and expressed in sufficient detail to enable the presiding authorities of the Church to clearly and correctly understand what use has been made of the money, and who should be credited therewith.

Each missionary should keep a strict account of his own doings, and an account should also be kept with each branch through its President, showing the amounts of moneys received by them for all Church purposes and the disposal that is made thereof. These reports should be forwarded with promptness to the presiding authorities in the various missions at such stated periods (never to exceed three months), as may be deemed most desirable by them.

It should be well understood by the Elders called on missions that, though they are required to travel without purse or scrip like the apostles of old, yet the laborer is worthy of his hire, and if any one give a gift unto the Elders, let these receive it with thankfulness in the name of Jesus, and with a blessing to those who give it; furthermore, let them use the means thus received with wisdom and prudence to the best interests of the work of the Lord, and to the supply of their own necessities whilst traveling amongst the people or filling the purposes of their mission. The Lord will hold all to a strict accountability for the use made of the means they receive which is donated for the up building of His kingdom BRIGHAM YOUNG, JNO. W. YOUNG, DANIEL H. WELLS. First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
