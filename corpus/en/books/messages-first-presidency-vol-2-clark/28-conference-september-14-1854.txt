James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 2 ( 1965)

Conference, September 14, 1854

CONFERENCE ON THE 6TH OF OCTOBER NEXT.

As the time for our next Conference is close at hand, it is thought best to offer a few suggestions to those Saints who intend to come to and remain in this city during that time, lest circumstances unthought of, or overlooked, may arise to mar the union, and good feelings so highly desirable.

You are well aware that those living in the smaller settlements have far greater facilities for abounding in butter, cheese, eggs, a variety and abundance of vegetables, &c., than the inhabitants of this city; hence it is but reasonable for you to bring them, or beforehand, such quantity of the above articles as will allow the men upon the Public Works the blessing of comfortable meals, at least while attending Conference, and enable the families with whom you eat, and lodge, to furnish palatable food without being obliged to run in debt for those very articles, or do without, since they are high priced, and scarce in the market here.

And again, as many of your brethren here have no use for hay, and of course do not wish to buy it, you will see the propriety of bringing forage for your animals. Come then prepared to be of mutual benefit, and inasmuch as many have gone out of this city because it was too straight for their feelings, do not throw an extra burden upon its citizens, but of the two, out of your chosen advantages, furnish more than an equal proportion, rather than fall short. BRIGHAM YOUNG, HEBER C. KIMBALL, JEDEDIAH M. GRANT.
