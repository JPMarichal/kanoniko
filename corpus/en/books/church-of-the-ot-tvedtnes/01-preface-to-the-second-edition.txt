John A. Tvedtnes, Church of the Old Testament ( 1980)

PREFACE TO THE SECOND EDITION

The first edition of The Church of the Old Testament was written when I was twenty-three years of age and published when I was twenty-six. The thirteen years that have intervened have brought me many rich experiences, both academically and spiritually, that have added to my conviction of the basic truths of the gospel and of the organization of the Church. A certain measure of intellectual maturity, coupled with four university degrees and eight and a half years living in Israel, have convinced me of the necessity of making some few changes. A few important points have also been added to the text. My writing style has also changed over the years (for the better, I hope), and thus I have made changes in the style at many points.

There was a certain amount of frustration involved in the preparation of this second edition, primarily because I know much more about the subject of this book than I can possibly tell in so few pages. I hesitated for a while, trying to decide whether to do a much larger and more comprehensive book. Ultimately, I decided that the additional material accumulated over the years should form the basis of other printed works, now in preparation. The concise nature of this second edition is an advantage in that it provides an introduction to the subject. The real meat is to be found in the text of the Old Testament itself.
