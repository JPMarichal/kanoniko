BOOK OF MORMON

By

Jerry D. Grover, Jr. PE, PG

Jerry D. Grover, Jr., is a licensed Professional Structural and Civil Engineer and a licensed Professional

Geologist. He has an undergraduate degree in Geological Engineering from BYU and a Master’s Degree

in Civil Engineering from the University of Utah. He speaks Italian and Chinese and has worked as a

freelance translator with his wife off and on for the past 25 years. He has provided geotechnical and civil

engineering design for many private and public works projects. He took a 12-year hiatus from the

sciences and served as a Utah County Commissioner from 1995 to 2007. He is currently employed as the

site engineer for the remediation and redevelopment of the 1750-acre Geneva Steel site in Vineyard,

Utah.
