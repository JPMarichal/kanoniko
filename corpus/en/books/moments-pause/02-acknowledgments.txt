Acknowledgments

The responsibility for creating the weekly message known as "The Spoken Word" falls to several people. Bonneville Media Communications producer Ed Payne assigns a writer to a given week. The five writers who share the rotating assignment for each week's script are Ray Haeckel, Duane Hiatt, Clifton Jolley, Maurine Proctor, and Michael Robinson. The draft of the script is given to Carroll Smith, of BMC, to retype and send to me to edit or rewrite. To each of these people and many others who share the creativity, the labor, and the joy of this weekly endeavor, I give my thanks.

J. Spencer Kinard
