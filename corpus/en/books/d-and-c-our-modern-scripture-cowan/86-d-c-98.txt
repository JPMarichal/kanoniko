The Saints knew from bitter experience that "when the wicked rule, the people mourn" (verse 9), so the Lord counseled his people to take positive steps to seek and uphold good men rather than resorting to further violence (see verse 10).

152Latter-day Saints were not to be afraid of their enemies, because these afflictions were to prove their faith, and even if they had to lay down their lives for the Lord's sake, the righteous still had the promise of eternal life. (See verses 11-15.) Verse 16 contains what is perhaps the key theme in D&C 98-that above all, the Saints were to work for peace.
