Section 133: The Appendix

Because this section is designated "The Appendix," it stands at the end of the revelations given through Joseph Smith-sections 134, 135, and 136 being of a different order. This revelation was given a day or so after D&C 67 was received at the conference considering publication of the first compilation of revelations. Another revelation given at about the same time, known as "the Lord's Preface," now stands as D&C 1. Notice that many of the ideas in the "Preface" are paralleled in D&C 133. (See the discussion of D&C 1 herein.)

202The Command to Gather (Verses 1-15). This revelation was given to answer the elders' questions concerning what they should preach. Verses 7-9 directed that the missionaries' message should be a call for gathering. For several decades the Perpetual Emigrating Fund and other Church programs assisted converts in "gathering to Zion" or, in other words, actually leaving home to join the main body of saints in America. Today the missionaries' message is still "gather," but more in the sense suggested in verse 14; this is a spiritual gathering out of the wicked world into the community of Saints who are building strong branches of the Church in their respective homelands.
