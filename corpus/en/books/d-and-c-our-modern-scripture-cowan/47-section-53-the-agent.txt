Richard O. Cowan, Doctrine and Covenants, Our Modern Scripture ( 1984)

Section 53: The Agent

88The bishop had a weighty role in administering the temporal affairs of the Church under the law of consecration. (See the discussion of the law of consecration under D&C 42 herein.) In this revelation, Sidney Gilbert was called to be the first "agent" only four months after the revelation on consecration had been given. The Lord later explained (D&C 84:113) that the agent was to take charge of secular or temporal business affairs under the direction of the bishop.
