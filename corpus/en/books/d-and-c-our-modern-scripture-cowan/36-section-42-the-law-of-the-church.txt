Section 42: The Law of the Church

The Lord's law contained in this section was given in fulfillment of the promise in D&C 38:32 and according to the Lord's arrangement made in 41:2-3. D&C 42 is actually composed of three revelations. Verses 1-72 were received February 9, 1831. Verses 74-77 and 78-93 were received February 23.
