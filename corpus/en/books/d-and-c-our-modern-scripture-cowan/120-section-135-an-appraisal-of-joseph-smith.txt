Section 135: An Appraisal of Joseph Smith

John Taylor was well qualified to write this statement. Not only was he an eyewitness to the martyrdom, but his position as one of the Twelve Apostles enabled him to assess the spiritual significance of the Prophet's contributions. (Consider, for example, the lofty claim set forth in verse 3.) With their deaths, Joseph and Hyrum Smith sealed their testimonies with their blood. (See Heb. 9:16-17. For a discussion of Hyrum's unique standing at this time, see "Priesthood Instructions," under D&C 124 herein.)
