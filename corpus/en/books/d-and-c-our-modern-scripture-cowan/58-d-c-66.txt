Richard O. Cowan, Doctrine and Covenants, Our Modern Scripture ( 1984)

D&C 66

During the summer of 1831, William E. M'Lellin (pronounced and sometimes spelled McLellan) was converted by Samuel Smith and accompanied the elders on their mission to Missouri. He then returned to Kirtland seeking to know the Lord's will. In this revelation he was not only called to further service, but he was also warned about a serious temptation which had been a problem in the past. At a special conference held only a week later, M'Lellin challenged the wording of the Lord's revelations. (See the discussion of D&C 67 below.)

103Ultimately, however, Brother M'Lellin must have heeded the Lord's warning, because he was later selected as one of the original Twelve Apostles in this dispensation.
