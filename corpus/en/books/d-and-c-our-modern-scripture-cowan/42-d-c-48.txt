Richard O. Cowan, Doctrine and Covenants, Our Modern Scripture ( 1984)

D&C 48

Pursuant to the directions given in D&C 37 and 38, most of the New York saints moved to Ohio during the spring of 1831. This revelation was given for their benefit, instructing them concerning the law of consecration. (See the discussion of consecration under D&C 42 herein.)
