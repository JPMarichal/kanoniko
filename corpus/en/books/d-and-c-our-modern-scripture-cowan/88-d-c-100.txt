Richard O. Cowan, Doctrine and Covenants, Our Modern Scripture ( 1984)

D&C 100

The Saints' difficulties in Jackson County continued during the fall of 1833. Before leaving on his mission to the East, Joseph Smith sent Orson Hyde and John Gould to check on conditions in Missouri. In D&C 100 the Lord assured the Prophet that these elders were safe and that his own family was well and gave the missionaries encouragement in their work.

153Sidney Rigdon as Spokesman (Verses 9-11). Sidney Rigdon's former experience as a minister prepared him well for his new calling as Joseph Smith's spokesman. He had also served as the Prophet's scribe (D&C 35) and more recently had been called as his first counselor (D&C 90:6). Elder Rigdon's new calling was put in proper perspective when the Lord reminded him that Joseph Smith would still be the revelator. Thus Joseph and Sidney served in the same relative positions as had Moses and Aaron anciently.
