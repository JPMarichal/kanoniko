Section 13: Restoration of the Aaronic Priesthood

In the Prophet's history, D&C 13 was recorded chronologically before D&C 11. Read the entire account of the restoration of the Aaronic Priesthood in the Pearl of Great Price, JSH 1:68-75. John the Baptist, last in a long series of Aaronic Priesthood bearers, was privileged to prepare the way for and to baptize the Savior. (See D&C 84:26-28; Matt. 3:13-17. For a consideration of the history and scope of the Aaronic and Melchizedek priesthoods, see the discussion of D&C 107 herein.)

36The statement that the priesthood will remain "until Â�" does not mean that it will be withdrawn at some future time. (Compare D&C 84:18.) When Oliver Cowdery recorded John the Baptist's words, he quoted the heavenly messenger as saying that the priesthood must remain "that the Sons of Levi may yet offer an offering unto the Lord in righteousness." (Quoted in the Pearl of Great Price, 1981 edition, p. 58 fn.)

36The identity of the "Sons of Levi" and of their "offering" can be understood in two senses: First, literal descendants of Levi will return with the Ten Tribes before the Second Coming and will make an actual blood sacrifice as a necessary token of the "restoration of all things." (See Teachings, p. 172.) Second, in a more figurative sense, D&C 84:33-34 indicates that faithful bearers of the Aaronic and Melchizedek priesthoods become the sons of Aaron and of Moses respectively; they thus become the "Sons of Levi" because Aaron and Moses were both descendants of Levi. The "offering" given by these faithful Latter-day Saints may include "a broken heart and a contrite spirit" (3 Ne. 9:19-20) as well as an acceptable record of their dead. 37 (D&C 128:24.) (For a further consideration of the Sons of Levi and their offering, see Sperry, Compendium, pp. 74-84.)
