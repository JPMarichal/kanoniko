Richard O. Cowan, Doctrine and Covenants, Our Modern Scripture ( 1984)

D&C 92

The "united order" was a group of top Church leaders whose responsibility it was to administer the law of consecration. (See discussion under D&C 78 herein.) Because Frederick G. Williams had recently become the second counselor in the First Presidency (D&C 90:6), he was admitted to the order in which he was to be a "lively" (or active) member.
