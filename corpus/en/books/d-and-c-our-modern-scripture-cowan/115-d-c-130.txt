Richard O. Cowan, Doctrine and Covenants, Our Modern Scripture ( 1984)

D&C 130

While on a short trip to Ramus, Illinois, with the Prophet, Elder Orson Hyde delivered a sermon on 1 Jn. 3:2, suggesting that the Savior will come as a warrior and that we would be like him, having some of that same spirit. Afterward, the Prophet offered some corrections about the Second Coming, and these and other items are included in D&C 130. (See HC, 5:323.)
