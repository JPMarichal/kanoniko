Richard O. Cowan, Doctrine and Covenants, Our Modern Scripture ( 1984)

D&C 79-81

These revelations gave important calls to three brethren: Jared Carter, Stephen Burnett, and Frederick G. Williams. Frederick G. Williams's call as a counselor to Joseph Smith (D&C 81) was consummated just a year later with the organization of the First Presidency (D&C 90). Elder Williams became the Prophet's second counselor, holding the keys with him. (Compare D&C 81:2 with 90:6.)
