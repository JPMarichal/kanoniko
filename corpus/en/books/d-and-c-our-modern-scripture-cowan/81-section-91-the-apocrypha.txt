Richard O. Cowan, Doctrine and Covenants, Our Modern Scripture ( 1984)

Section 91: The Apocrypha

The word apocryphal means "of doubtful or questionable origin"; this term is also used to refer in general to non-canonical or nonscriptural writings. "The Apocrypha" is a collection of writings included in Catholic but not in most Protestant Bibles; it comes from the period of Hebrew history roughly between the Old and New Testaments.

145The term apocryphal should not be confused with apocalyptic, which describes a special form of prophetic and highly symbolic Hebrew literature. "The Apocalypse" is the name given to the Revelation of John.
