Richard O. Cowan, Doctrine and Covenants, Our Modern Scripture ( 1984)

D&C 74

Following the Lord's instructions in D&C 73, Joseph Smith resumed his "translation" or inspired revision of the Bible. Questions about the meaning of specific biblical passages became the occasion for receiving several revelations now in the Doctrine and Covenants (sections 74, 76, 77, 86, 91, and 113, for example). D&C 74 is related to Paul's teachings about marriage in general, and contains the Lord's explanation of 1 Cor. 7:14 in particular.
