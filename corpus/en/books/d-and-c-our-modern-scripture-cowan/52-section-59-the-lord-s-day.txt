Richard O. Cowan, Doctrine and Covenants, Our Modern Scripture ( 1984)

Section 59: The Lord's Day

Soon after the arrival of the Colesville Saints, Sister Polly Knight (wife of Joseph Knight, Sr.) passed away, the first Latter-day Saint to die in the land of Zion. Her funeral was held on Sunday, August 7, 1831. It was in this setting that D&C 59 was received. Verses 1-4 were a message of comfort and encouragement to the Saints. Beginning in verse 5, the Lord reviewed with them some of the basic commandments which can be grouped under the two great commandments-love of God and love of neighbor. Verse 21 warns that one should recognize the Lord's hand in all things, and verse 23 summarizes the fruits of righteous living in a most meaningful way.
