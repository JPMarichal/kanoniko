Leon R. Hartshorn, Outstanding Stories by General Authorities, vol. 2 ( 1971)

"Home Plate Doesn't Move"

I am thinking right now of an interview held once with Satchel Page. He was a great baseball player—a great pitcher. And someone came up and said, "Satchel, what's your secret?"

"Well," he said, "you know, I take that ball and I throw it right over home plate. That's what I try to do every time. You know, home plate doesn't move."

And in his philosophy he knows and has recognized that there is one thing to do, and that is always to live according to the law.

(Speeches of the Year, "Be Leaders with Spiritual Wisdom and Stability," March 4, 1969, p. 7.)
