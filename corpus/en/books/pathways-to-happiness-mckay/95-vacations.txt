David O. McKay, Pathways to Happiness ( 1957)

* Vacations

VACATIONS are commendable. A change of environment and of occupation is restful and in most cases healthful. During the summer months, particularly, those who are able to take even short trips to nearby canyons find themselves out of the enervating heat of the lower valley into an invigorating, energy-producing atmosphere that gives renewed life and activity. Fortunate indeed are they who can spend a week or more strolling along the clear mountain streams or climbing the pine-clad hills.
