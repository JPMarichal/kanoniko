Thomas S. Monson, Inspiring Experiences That Build Faith: From the Life and Ministry of Thomas S. Monson ( 1994)

An Inscription in a Book

Some years ago I attended a stake conference in the southern part of the United States where Elder Delbert L. Stapley of the Council of the Twelve had served as a missionary in his youth. After the conference concluded, a sister came forward and opened for my view a rather old copy of the Book of Mormon. She asked, "Do you know the man who inscribed this book when he was a missionary and presented it to my grandparents?"

I looked at the signature, immediately recognized its authenticity, and replied, "Yes. I serve with Elder Stapley."

She asked, "Would you please take this book to Elder Stapley with our love? Tell him that his testimony and this book guided my entire family to become members of the Church."

I wholeheartedly consented to her request. I waited for an appropriate opportunity and then went to Elder Stapley's office, told him of my experience, and handed him the copy of the Book of Mormon he had presented many years before. He read the inscription he had written on the title page of the book and saw his name; then tears came to his eyes. Teaching through testimony had brought to him indescribable joy and profound gratitude.
