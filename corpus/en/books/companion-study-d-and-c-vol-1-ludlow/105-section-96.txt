Section 96

Background information on section 96

"On the 4th of June, 1833, the High Priests met in Conference, in the Translating Room, to consider what disposition to make of the French farm, which, together with other real estate, had been bought for the Church. The Conference was not able to agree on a manager, but all were willing to submit the question to the Lord, in prayer. In answer to their petitions, they received this Revelation, in which the Lord directed them (1) to give Bishop Newel K. Whitney charge of the farm (1-5); and (2) to accept John Johnson as a member of the United Order (6-9)." (Smith and Sjodahl, DCC, p. 606.)
