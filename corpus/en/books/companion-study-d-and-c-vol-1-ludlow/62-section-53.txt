For biographical information on Sidney Gilbert, see Appendix B.

53:5

"take your journey with . . . Joseph Smith . . . and Sidney Rigdon"

"It was on the 19th day of June, 1831, that Joseph Smith departed from Kirtland, Ohio, to go up into Missouri, the place promised as an inheritance for the Saints and at which the New Jerusalem should sometime be established. The Prophet was accompanied by Sidney Rigdon, Martin Harris, Edward Partridge, W. W. Phelps, Joseph Coe and A. S. Gilbert and wife." (George Q. Cannon, LJS, p. 96.)
