Spencer W. Kimball and Ezra Taft Benson

I Am a "Mormon" Boy

The twelfth and thirteenth presidents of the Church remember with fondness the Evan Stephens' song, "I Am a 'Mormon' Boy."

How proud I was when we were to sing in the congregation:

A "Mormon" Boy, a "Mormon" Boy I am a "Mormon" Boy. I might be envied by a king, For I am a "Mormon" Boy. [^1]

I liked this song; I have always gloried in those words: "I might be envied by a king, For I am a 'Mormon' Boy." [^2]

President Benson has a great love for music and . . . has delighted audiences throughout the Church by singing, "I Am a Mormon Boy," a favorite song he learned in Primary. [^3]

One such incident occurred in the Salt Lake Tabernacle on February 16, 1986, when the Saints of the Salt Lake Liberty and Salt Lake Monument Park stakes were assembled in regional conference. He remembered his Primary teacher had assigned him to sing a song for ward conference. He said he would never have had the courage to sing it were it not for his mother and his wonderful Primary teacher.

He then proceeded to sing all three verses of "I Am a 'Mormon' Boy." [^4]
