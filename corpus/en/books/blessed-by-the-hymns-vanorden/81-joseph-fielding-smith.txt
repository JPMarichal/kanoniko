Come, Come, Ye Saints

We'll make the air with music ring, Shout praises to our God and King; Above the rest these words we'll tell—All is well! All is well! (Hymns, 1985, no. 30.)

Joseph Fielding Smith

Prayer and Love of God

Now, in this . . . song, "All is Well," is a familiar household song with us, but to my mind the inspiration, the beauty and strength of this song will some day make it prized as one of the gems of American literature. . . .

What a magnificent outpouring of the human soul that song is! When you think of a band of people, driven from the confines of civilization, and facing the necessity of thousands of miles of travel to an unknown, new world, with all the deprivation and hardship such an undertaking would entail, to my mind the expression—and it was a true expression of the spirit of the people who composed that great band—is one of the finest utterances of the human soul.
