Spencer W. Kimball

President Kimball Himself Played "I Need Thee Every Hour"

Upon the sudden and unexpected death of his beloved friend Harold B. Lee, the mantle of the prophet rested upon Spencer Kimball. He said, "We prayed it would never happen; our prayers were for President Harold B. Lee. Night and morning every day we prayed for a long life and the general welfare of President Lee. I knew the responsibility could fall to me, but I did not seek it. Now I will do my best." (Church News, 5 January 1974, p. 4.)

On December 30, 1973, the brethren gathered in the Salt Lake Temple to sustain and set apart the new President of the Church. As they convened, President Kimball himself played "I Need Thee Every Hour" on the piano and the apostles sang. Then each of them in turn bore his witness that Spencer Kimball was the man the Lord wanted to preside over the Church. [^1]
