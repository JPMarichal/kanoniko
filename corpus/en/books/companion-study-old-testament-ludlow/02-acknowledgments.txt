And special and eternal thanks to our Heavenly Father and to his divine Son, known by many honored and appropriate titles, including Jehovah and Jesus Christ, for making the sublime scripture of the Old Testament available to us, and for encouraging us to search it diligently so we might know and then to follow their teachings so we will also do. My prayer is that this book might serve in some small way to encourage people to read and ponder the Old Testament, thus demonstrating willingness to receive the word They have already given, so They might give more for our eternal benefit.
