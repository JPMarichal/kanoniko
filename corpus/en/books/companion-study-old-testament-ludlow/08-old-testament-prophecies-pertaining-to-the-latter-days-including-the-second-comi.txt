OLD TESTAMENT PROPHECIES PERTAINING TO THE LATTER DAYS, INCLUDING THE SECOND COMING AND THE BEGINNING OF THE MILLENNIUM

Still other Old Testament prophecies pertained to events in the last days ("the dispensation of the fulness of times"), when there would be a restoration or restitution of all things. Many of these prophecies were concerned with the restoration of the gospel and the true Church; others with the restoration of priesthood authority and keys; others with events which would help prepare the earth and peoples of the earth for the coming of the Messiah in power and great glory (the second coming of Jesus Christ); still others pertained specifically to the second coming.

Following are some of the many Old Testament prophecies concerning these great events of the last days with sources of additional information, including selected references from other scriptures. The numbers following the scripture references refer to section numbers printed in the margins of Part I.
