(Note: This book has been written for use with the LDS edition of the King James Version of the Bible published in 1979 by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. All references to chapter headings, footnotes, and entries in the Topical Guide and Bible Dictionary pertain to that edition of the Bible.

The abbreviation TG refers to the Topical Guide; BD refers to the Bible Dictionary.

The numbers in the margin of each page in Part I indicate major segments; these numbers are used with the designation "Part I" for ease in reference. For example, the commentary for Micah 4:7 says that additional information on that verse appears in Part I 204, meaning segment 204 of Part I, found on page 84.)
