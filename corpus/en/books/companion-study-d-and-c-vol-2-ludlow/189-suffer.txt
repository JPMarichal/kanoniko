Suffer

"suffer it to be so"

Some of the accepted meanings of the word suffer are "to permit" and "to allow." It is used in these senses numerous times both in ancient and modern scriptures.

Scriptural References:

D&C 10:14, 43; 61:8; 109:49; Luke 8:32; 9:22, 41, 59; 17:25; 18:16; 22:15, 51; 24:46.
