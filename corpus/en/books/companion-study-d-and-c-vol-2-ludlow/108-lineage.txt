Lineage

The term lineage refers to line of descent or genealogy and can refer either to a person or to power and authority (priesthood). It is used in all of these senses in the seven verses of the Doctrine and Covenants where it appears: 68:21; 84:14-15; 86:8, 10; 107:41; 113:8.

Additional Scriptural References:

Abr. 1:27; Luke 2:4.
