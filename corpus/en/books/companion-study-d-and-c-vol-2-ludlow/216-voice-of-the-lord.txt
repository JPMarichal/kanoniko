Voice of the Lord

voice of the Lord's Spirit

The voice of the Lord can be perceived by man either through his physical senses (the ear) or directly to his spirit (or mind). Sometimes the voice of the Lord is heard audibly but at other times it is perceived as a still small voice or as a whisper, and at still other times it is perceived only by its impressions upon the mind.
