Sabaoth

Lord of Sabaoth

The word sabaoth is a Greek form of a Hebrew word (tsebaoth), which means "armies." Jesus Christ is the Lord of Sabaoth, or Lord of Armies, also translated as Lord of Hosts. (Isa. 1:9; Rom. 9:29; James 5:4.) The Lord of Sabaoth (Jesus Christ) is also "the creator of the first day, the beginning and the end." (D&C 95:7.)

Scriptural References:

D&C 87:7; 88:2; 95:7; 98:2; Rom. 9:29; James 5:4.
