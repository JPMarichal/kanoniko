Nauvoo, Illinois

Nauvoo, Illinois, was the major gathering place of the Saints between 1839 and the great exodus westward in 1846. It was built from a village previously known as Commerce, Illinois. According to Joseph Smith, the word Nauvoo means "beautiful."

Scriptural References:

D&C 124:60, 109, 111, 117, 119, 121; 125:3-4.
