Twinkling of an eye

Twinkling of an eye is an expression used in the scriptures to indicate that when a certain thing happens it will occur quickly, immediately—virtually in the time it would take for a person to blink his eyes.

Scriptural References:

D&C 43:32; 63:51; 101:31; 1 Cor. 15:52; 3 Ne. 28:8.
