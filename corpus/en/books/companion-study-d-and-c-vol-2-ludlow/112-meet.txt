Meet

"meet in mine eyes"

Earlier definitions of the word meet, which are now listed as archaic in some modern dictionaries, include the idea of being proper, fit, acceptable, permissible, right, necessary, or desirable. Thus, the statement it is "not meet that I should command in all things" (D&C 58:26) essentially means that it is not necessary or desirable for the Lord to tell us everything we should know.

Scriptural References:
