Living God

(see also God)

God is a vital, active, living Personage of flesh and bones and spirit. He is not a God who lived a long time ago and has already accomplished all his work, nor is he a God who is dead and no longer has any power or force or interest in the world. At the time of the First Vision in 1820, many of the creeds of Christianity contained elements suggesting that God had either already accomplished his work or was no longer exercising his power in the earth. However, in his revelation to the Prophet Joseph Smith, as recorded in the Doctrine Covenants, God frequently reiterates that he is a "living God" and that his church is a "living church."
