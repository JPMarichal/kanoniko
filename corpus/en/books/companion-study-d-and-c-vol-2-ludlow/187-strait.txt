Strait

strait gate

The word strait should not be confused with straight, which is pronounced the same although spelled differently. Strait means "narrow, close fitting, limited in space or time." The strait gate leading to the kingdom of God on the earth (the Church) is narrow and restricted, meaning certain conditions must be met before the person can enter. Thus, "few there be that find it." Similarly, a strait gate protects all the ordinances of the gospel, including the path "that leadeth unto the exaltation and continuation of the lives." (D&C 132:22.)

Selected Quotations

"There are those who complain that to follow the straight and narrow path requires limitations, restrictions, overcoming, and doing without things that are very tempting. We must remember, however, that it guarantees victory and achievement of our goal, which is gained by setting a goal and being able to concentrate and follow an undeviating course.

"Narrow is a very meaningful word. Often people accuse us of being narrow-minded if we are following the straight and narrow path, which certainly does require self-denial. We must realize and be prepared to accept the fact that it confines us, restricts us, and limits us in certain areas. But let us fully realize that it does not fetter or shackle mankind. On the contrary, it is the way to emancipation, independence, and liberty." (N. Eldon Tanner, Ensign, May 1975, p. 76.)
