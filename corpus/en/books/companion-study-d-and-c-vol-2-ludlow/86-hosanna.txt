Hosanna

Hosanna is a transliteration of a Hebrew (or Semitic) word that literally means "save now" and that could be translated "grant us salvation." Most of the prayers said by the Jews at the Feast of Tabernacles begin with this word, and it was also used by the multitude as they greeted Jesus Christ when he came into Jerusalem during the last week of His life upon the earth. (Matt. 21:9, 15.) This term appears in five sections of the D&C—19:37; 36:3; 39:19; 109:79; 124:101.

Scriptural References:

Matt. 21:9, 15; Mark 11:9-10; John 12-13.
