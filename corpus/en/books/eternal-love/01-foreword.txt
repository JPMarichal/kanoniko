Boyd K. Packer, Eternal Love ( 1973)

Foreword

Few men are better qualified to speak on the subject of eternal love than is Elder Boyd K. Packer of the Council of the Twelve Apostles of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

His own companionship with his wife, his large family of exemplary children, and his untiring service as an educator and Church leader make his voice strong in proclaiming the true manner of love.

Deseret Book is pleased that Elder Packer has permitted this stirring address, originally given to students at Brigham Young University, to be published in this format. We commend it to your earnest consideration.

The Publishers
