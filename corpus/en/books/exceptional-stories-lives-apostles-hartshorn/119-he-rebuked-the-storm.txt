MiraclesRichards, Franklin D."He Rebuked the Storm"

While on one of his trips across the Atlantic, returning from the British Mission in charge of a company of saints and Elders, the weather was very stormy, and the waves were rolling so high that the officers of the vessel were fearful of its being dashed to pieces. When the hearts of strong men were failing them, he remembered he held the holy Priesthood, which authorized him to rebuke the angry elements and command them to be still, as was done by the Savior and the Apostles in their day. Withdrawing to a part of the ship where he was unobserved, and raising his hands to high heaven, he rebuked the storm, the furious winds and waves, in the Name of the Lord Jesus Christ, commanding them to cease their violence and to be calm. The tempest immediately subsided; none of the passengers were lost,and no damage was done to the vessel.

224West, Life of Franklin D. Richards, pp. 85-86.
