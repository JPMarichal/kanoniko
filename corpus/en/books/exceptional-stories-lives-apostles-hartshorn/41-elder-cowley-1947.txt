Leon R. Hartshorn, Exceptional Stories from the Lives of Our Apostles ( 1972)

InspirationCowley, Matthew69"Elder Cowley—1947"

69Elder Cowley arrived at the Samoan Islands Wednesday July 2, 1947. A reception was held by the saints in behalf of Elder Cowley's arrival. President John Adams, of the Samoan Mission, reported as Elder Cowley arrived, "The greeting was spontaneous and warm." . . . "The older men, and some women crossed to the large Samoan style building on the Chapel lot, where the impressive island ceremony of passing the native drink of respect, Ava, with accompanying speeches from both sides, was done in royal fashion. All were impressed and delighted with Elder Cowley's spirit and response and congenial handling of his part."

69Some of the natives had erected a large sign for the welcome of Elder Cowley. They had printed on the sign "Angel Moroni-1823; Elder Cowley-1947" with a big WELCOME in-between. The Church leader asked the significance of the sign and they pointed skyward from whence Elder Cowley had just arrived by airplane and explained that both the Angel Moroni and Elder Cowley had come from the heavens to bring them the truths of the everlasting gospel.

69Church News, August 2, 1947, p. 8; August 16, 1947, p. 7.
