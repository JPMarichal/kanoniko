Leon R. Hartshorn, Exceptional Stories from the Lives of Our Apostles ( 1972)

PRESIDENT GEORGE Q. CANNON

Biographical Sketch

33Elder Cannon was born on January 11, 1827, at Liverpool, England, the son of George Cannon and Ann Quayle.

33As a boy, he immigrated from England to the United States with his parents who had been converted to the Church. His mother died at sea.

33Two months after the assassination of the Prophet Joseph and Hyrum, George Q. Cannon was ordained a seventy and chosen secretary of the quorum. (His father had died shortly before.) Elder Cannon served missions to California, Hawaii, and the Eastern United States and England. He was ordained an apostle on August 26, 1860, at thirty-three years of age.

33He served as President Brigham Young's private secretary.

33Elder Cannon served as president of the Deseret News.

33He was sustained as a counselor to Brigham Young on April 8, 1873. He was called to be first counselor to President John Taylor on October 10, 1880. Elder Cannon also served as first counselor to President Wilford Woodruff and President Lorenzo Snow.

34He was a brilliant scholar and editor and a voluminous writer.

34George Quayle Cannon died on April 12, 1901, at Monterey, California. [^1]
