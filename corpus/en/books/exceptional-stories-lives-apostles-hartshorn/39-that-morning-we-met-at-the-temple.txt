Leon R. Hartshorn, Exceptional Stories from the Lives of Our Apostles ( 1972)

67"That Morning We Met at the Temple"

67I was struggling to earn my way in Washington, to get an education, and I didn't have enough money to come and get married in the temple. We even had the announcements printed, and then all of a sudden my father heard about it. . . . He wasn't a man of means. He was never successful in a business way, and I guess things looked pretty dark for him as far as I was concerned. So he went out and put his hand into the hand of God. I know he did it on his knees, and in response to that prayer, I received money from him, and he said, "You have to come home and get married right in the first instance."

67I came home, and we went to the temple. It was the 13th of July. That morning we met at the temple, my father and my mother, and some others of our family. We came around from West Temple where we lived, just across the street from Temple Square. My wife and her family came together, and we met at the little gate in the east wall of the temple.

67Smith, Matthew Cowley—Man of Faith (Bookcraft), p. 82.
