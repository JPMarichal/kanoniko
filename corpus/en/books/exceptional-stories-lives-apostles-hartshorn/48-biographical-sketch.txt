Leon R. Hartshorn, Exceptional Stories from the Lives of Our Apostles ( 1972)

PRESIDENT JEDEDIAH M. GRANT

Biographical Sketch

79Jedediah M. Grant was born on February 21, 1816, at Windsor, New York, the son of Joshua Grant and Athalia Howard.

79At age seventeen he was converted to the Church and was baptized March 21, 1833.

79Elder Grant served many missions. His first was in 1835.

79Elder Grant was ordained a seventy February 28, 1835, under the hands of Joseph Smith, Sidney Rigdon and Frederick G. Williams. He was set apart on December 2, 1845, as one of the first Seven Presidents of the Seventies at age twenty-nine.

79Elder Grant was elected as the first mayor of Salt Lake City. He was elected to that office in 1851.

79He was chosen speaker of the house of representatives of the Utah territory in 1852.

79Elder Jedediah Morgan Grant was ordained an apostle by President Brigham Young on April 7, 1854. He was sustained as President Young's second counselor on the same day. He was thirty-eight years old.

President Jedediah M. Grant was a zealous servant of God and perhaps over-extended himself physically. He died at the early age of forty on December 1, 1856, in Salt Lake City.

80At the time of his death one of his sons, Heber Jeddy Grant, who was to become the President of the Church, was just nine days old.

80President Brigham Young paid Jedediah Morgan Grant a great compliment at Elder Grant's funeral services.

80In essence he said:

80"Jedediah has been a member of this Church some twenty-five years but in that time he has given the Lord one hundred years of church service."
