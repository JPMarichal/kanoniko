Leon R. Hartshorn, Exceptional Stories from the Lives of Our Apostles ( 1972)

PRESIDENT HEBER C. KIMBALL

Biographical Sketch

109Elder Kimball was born June 16, 1801, at Shelden, Vermont, to Solomon Kimball and Anna Spaulding.

109In the fall of 1831 Elder Kimball first met Mormon missionaries. In April 1832 he was baptized.

109He was called to be an apostle in 1835 and was a member of the first Quorum of Twelve in this dispensation.

109He served as a missionary to Canada and New England in 1835. In 1837 he was called to open the missionary work in England. During this mission of less than a year he was responsible for 1,500 baptisms.

109Elder Kimball returned to England with other members of the Twelve in 1840.

109He was one of the first pioneers to come into the Salt Lake Valley in 1847.

109Elder Kimball was sustained as first counselor to President Brigham Young in 1847, a position he held until his death on June 22, 1868, at age sixty-seven.

109Heber Chase Kimball lived an exemplary life; he had a great gift of prophecy and was deeply loved by the Latter-day Saints.

110Few men in the history of the Church have been loved and honored as was Heber C. Kimball. He was fearless, and no one could doubt his integrity and his complete devotion to the Lord Jesus Christ.
