230"I Cut the Ice in the Creek"

230My father had been for several years very feeble in health and for about six months previous to his baptism had not been able to visit his barn. His neighbors all believed that baptism would kill him. I cut the ice in the creek and broke a road for forty rods, through the crust on two feet of snow, and the day was very cold. The neighbors looked on with astonishment, expecting to see him die in the water, but his health continued improving from that moment.

230George A. Smith, "Memoirs," Brigham Young University library, p. 5.
