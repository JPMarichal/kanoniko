Leon R. Hartshorn, Exceptional Stories from the Lives of Our Apostles ( 1972)

ELDER ORSON PRATT

Biographical Sketch

179Orson Pratt was born September 19, 1811, at Hartford, New York, the son of Jared Pratt and Charity Dickinson. He was a younger brother of Apostle Parley P. Pratt.

179Elder Pratt was baptized on September 19, 1830. He traveled two hundred miles to meet the Prophet Joseph Smith in Fayette, New York. He also became acquainted with the three witnesses at this time.

179He was ordained an apostle on April 26, 1835, under the hands of Oliver Cowdery, David Whitmer, and Martin Harris. He was one of the original members of the Quorum of the Twelve in this dispensation. He was only twenty-three years old when he was chosen by the Lord. Elder Pratt served numerous missions for the Church. He was a brilliant scholar, writer, and teacher and an eloquent speaker.

179Elder Orson Pratt died October 3, 1881, at Salt Lake City.
