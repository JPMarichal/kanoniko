Leon R. Hartshorn, Exceptional Stories from the Lives of Our Apostles ( 1972)

28"Thank God for America"

28Recently I attended in an adjoining state a testimonial in honor of the outstanding young man in his community. He was to be presented a medal of distinction. The man who was chosen to make the presentation asked the indulgence of the crowd while he told the story of the young man who was to be honored. This was his story:

28There was a time in this town when a widow was left with a group of children and little to go on. Neighbors offered to help but she said she wanted to try to hold them together. And, like other noble ones of her sex, she did. She carried on by doing the washing for the community. And this young man, distinguished here tonight, is the "kid that gathered up the bundles for her to wash"!

28As I shook his hand to congratulate him after the presentation, I asked him what he would have said could he have put into words what was in his heart.

28"Only this—thank God for a mother who cared enough to hold us together.

29"Thank God for America—the land in which a kid like me has a chance."

29Church News, June 20, 1942, p. 8.
