Clare Middlemiss, David O. McKay, Cherished Experiences from the Writings of President David O. McKay ( 1955)

SECTION III

INCIDENTS OF DIVINE HEALING

INTRODUCTION

PRESIDENT DAVID O. MCKAY has spent and is continuing to spend his life in traveling throughout the world to bless and encourage both members and nonmembers of the Church. Wherever he goes, the people form a veritable cordon about him, nor will they disperse until each has the opportunity to shake him by the hand. Many of the lands visited by him have never had an Apostle of the Lord within their borders, and many of the people have had a burning faith that if they could only grasp him by the hand they would receive increased strength thereby. Their desire has been increased by the fact that perhaps there would never be another opportunity in a lifetime to grasp the hand of a chosen representative of the Lord. Between meetings, it has been almost impossible for President McKay to take a needed rest, for all the people have been desirous of receiving a blessing from him. All who have come to him have been greeted and blessed as sincerely and prayerfully as his soul could bless. There are perhaps hundreds who have been healed through his administration, and thousands of others have been blessed and encouraged by the warm clasp of his hand.

In an address given January 3, 1953, President McKay said: "Faith is operating today as it has always operated. Our Father in heaven is just as near now as he was when he stood by the Savior's side and appeared to the Prophet Joseph Smith."

The following incidents are given as a testimony that the power of healing is operative today through the priesthood properly bestowed upon the servants of the Lord. It will be noted that in nearly all of these cases, faith in the priesthood was operative in the individual who received the blessing.
