Various General Authorities, Joseph E. Cardon, Samuel O. Bennion, Testimonies of the Divinity of the Church ( 1930)

Bishop John Wells

Second Counselor in the Presiding Bishopric from July 26, 1918, to the present time. [Publication date, 1930.]

I am deeply indebted to the Gospel of Jesus Christ for the many blessings that have come to me through obedience to its laws and ordinances.

254The Gospel came to me in my native land. It appealed to me so much that it impelled me to thoroughly investigate and later to accept it.

254Through obedience to its precepts I have received a lasting and abiding testimony of its truth and of the great work of the latter-day Prophet, Joseph Smith; that he was an instrument of our Father in Heaven in establishing His work in these latter days, and my testimony is that it will grow in power and influence.

254There has also been given to me a testimony concerning the Book of Mormon. This was given to me in response to my prayers as a young man when investigating the Gospel. This book is a new witness for God and contains in fulness the principles of the Gospel of Jesus Christ as taught by him to the ancient inhabitants of this continent.

254I sincerely pray that similar testimonies may be given to earnest and honest investigators and seekers after the truth, that they may become recipients of the same blessings which have come to me through obedience to the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
