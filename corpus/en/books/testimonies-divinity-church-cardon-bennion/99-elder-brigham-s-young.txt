Various General Authorities, Joseph E. Cardon, Samuel O. Bennion, Testimonies of the Divinity of the Church ( 1930)

Elder Brigham S. Young

President of the Northwestern States Mission from June, 1922, to January, 1927.

"God has spoken." To me that is the surest thing I know; Of this tremendous fact I have had verified suggestion as distinct as though it came by audible word, and "I know of the doctrine" because "I have done the will of the Father."

285The unassailable consistency of the Gospel constantly confirms my conviction that through the Prophet Joseph Smith and other inspired men, God has revealed anew the Gospel of Jesus Christ. As we eat and drink in remembrance of Him, keep His commandments and "always remember Him," so may we walk and live and breathe in His Spirit and know the joy of His presence; then will the earth be the place of our feet and not the shrine of our worship. The surest thing I know is, that "God has spoken."

285The world needs the restored Gospel of Jesus Christ, because of the almost hopeless condition of human spiritual thought. Compared with the idealism of "Mormonism," there is nothing in literature or science that reaches the wonderful possibilities of that which God has supplied in giving us a knowledge of the truth. Men can only be brought to a comprehension of these marvelous truths by accepting the Gospel.

285My testimony is that this is the Gospel of Jesus Christ. This is the work of God; and our missionaries are learning this truth by contrasting the Gospel with other religions, so called, of the world; they are learning that "Mormonism" is indeed the truth of the Lord Jesus Christ.
