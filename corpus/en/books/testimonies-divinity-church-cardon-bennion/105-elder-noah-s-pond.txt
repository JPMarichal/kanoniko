Various General Authorities, Joseph E. Cardon, Samuel O. Bennion, Testimonies of the Divinity of the Church ( 1930)

Elder Noah S. Pond

President of the Northern States Mission from April, 1928, to the present time. [Publication date, 1930.]

From the earliest recollection of my life it has been an easy matter for me to bear testimony of the divine mission of the Savior of the world, likewise it has been easy and pleasurable to testify that Joseph Smith was a Prophet of God.

299The Savior's baptism was signalized by the presence of the Holy Ghost, so likewise was the baptism of the Latter-day Prophet. The Savior's mission was a brief period of little over three years. The Prophet's official career was about 14 years.

299The Savior said he had no place to lay his head-the Prophet could not offer wealth but consecrated not only his own life but the lives of his wife and children. The reputation of the Savior was bad in the eyes of the Jews, Scribes, Pharisees and Romans, but his character was irreproachable. The Prophet's life was burdened with false charges, but he was never convicted of crime.

299The Savior was crucified, the Prophet was assassinated, and both were martyrs for the sake of truth. Both proved the "blood of the martyrs was the seed of the Church" and I testify that both died for the same noble cause.
