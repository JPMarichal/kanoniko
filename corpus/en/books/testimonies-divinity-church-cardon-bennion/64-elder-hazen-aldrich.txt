Various General Authorities, Joseph E. Cardon, Samuel O. Bennion, Testimonies of the Divinity of the Church ( 1930)

Elder Hazen Aldrich

Member of the First Council of Seventy from March 1, 1835, to A pril 6, 1837.

202Elder Aldrich joined the Church at an early date and was ordained a high priest. As a member of Zion's Camp he marched to Missouri and back in 1834; he was ordained a seventy under the hands of Joseph Smith and others, February 28, 1835, at Kirtland, Ohio; and when the first quorum of seventy was fully organized soon afterwards, he was set apart as one of the seven presidents of the same.

202As Elder Aldrich had been ordained a high priest previous to his ordination as a seventy the Prophet Joseph invited him to take his place in the high priests' quorum again which he did, April 6, 1837. During the great apostasy in Kirtland in 1837 and 1838, Hazen Aldrich was numbered among those who rebelled against the legitimate authorities of the Church, and became an apostate. Subsequently he joined the Brewster movement and published a paper at Kirtland called "The Olive Branch," the first number of which was dated August, 1848. He became a leader among the Brewsterites, and finally went to California where it is supposed he died.
