The third step in the formula consists of explaining that the answer given in step number two has to be right if we really do have modern prophets and modern revelation. This step is a natural outgrowth of the previous one. In fact, it is practically forced upon you by your investigator. He may say, " But I don't accept the Book of Mormon!" Then you simply reply, "Well, that is the real problem then, not sprinkling or infant baptism."

10 You might go on to say, " Suppose you knew the Book of Mormon were true and came from God. Would you then accept baptism by immersion as being necessary and correct?"

10 He may reply, "Well, yes; but I do not think that the Book of Mormon would contradict the Bible if it came from God."

11 Then you could say, "We see no contradiction between those two books. And we know the Book of Mormon came from God; so we accept its teachings. Would you like to know how you can find out if the Book of Mormon is true?"

11 Now you are right into step number four of the formula. And everything is going as it should be. You are declaring the message of the restoration: this is preaching the gospel of the kingdom. God has restored the gospel. His prophets walk the earth. We have no quarrel with anyone. We have good news to all who care to hear it. We do not wish to force it on anyone. We have no intention of proving it to anyone. But we will tell anyone who wants to know how he may prove it to himself
