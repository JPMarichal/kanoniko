Glenn L. Pearson, Book of Mormon, Key to Conversion ( 1963)

Dialogue on "Soul-Sleeping"

Elder: Is it your belief that there is no conscious existence between death and the resurrection?

21 Joe Whitney: That's right. The body goes to Sheol or the grave and the spirit, which is simply the breath of life, " returns to God who gave it. " (Eccl. 12:7.)

21 Elder: The problem then is that we disagree on the interpretation of the passages in the Bible which discuss the fate of the soul between death and the resurrection. May I ask you if you believe the Bible is inspired?

21 Whitney: I certainly do. It is the word of God.

21 Elder: In other words, when the ancient prophets and apostles spoke or wrote by inspiration, they were saying what God would have said if He had been there in person?

21 Whitney: That's right.

21 Elder: Would you agree then that God would know what Isaiah, and Peter, and Paul meant better than anyone else, since they spoke His words?

21 Whitney: Well, yes, I suppose He would. What are you getting at?

21 Elder: We base our whole interpretation of the Bible and all of our doctrine on the teachings of modern apostles and prophets whom we claim the Lord has ordained through angelic visitations. God has revealed to us that there are intelligent spirits in men which have a conscious and separate existence between death and the resurrection. If you were to find out that we do have such communications from God, would you believe what He has revealed concerning the soul of man and the meaning of the Bible?

22 Whitney: Well, if you could prove that statement, I would have to believe.

22 Elder: We are not in the business of proving anything. We have only been commissioned to declare the message that God has spoken again. But can tell you how you can find out for yourself just as I have found out. Here is a book called the Book of Mormon.
