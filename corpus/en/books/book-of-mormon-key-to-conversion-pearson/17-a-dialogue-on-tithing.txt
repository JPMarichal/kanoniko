Glenn L. Pearson, Book of Mormon, Key to Conversion ( 1963)

A Dialogue on Tithing

Joe Whitney: Tithing should not be practiced. It was done away in Christ with the rest of the Law of Moses.

22 Elder: Do you believe in an all-wise and all-powerful God?

22 Whitney: Yes, I do

22 Elder: Then he could command you to do anything he knew was best, couldn't he?

22 Whitney: Yes, of course.

22 Elder: Would you have to know why in every case before obeying his commandments?

22 Whitney: No. We must obey God regardless.

23 Elder: I can testify to you that God has commanded the members of the LDS Church to pay tithing. If you really knew that God had commanded us to pay tithing, would you then feel it proper that we should?

23 Whitney: Yes, of course I would. But I don't know that.

23 Elder: Then your problem is not whether or not we should pay tithing, but whether or not we have been commanded by revelation to do so, isn't it?

23 Whitney: Yes, I see that it is.

23 Elder: Would you like to know how to find out that we do have revelation?
