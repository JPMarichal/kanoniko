Glenn L. Pearson, Book of Mormon, Key to Conversion ( 1963)

Summary

The foregoing dialogues illustrate the equation which every question, problem, or objection fits. All it takes is the ingenuity of the missionary to see it and demonstrate it to his contacts. It also is the formula for avoiding contention and arguments over tenets. We have been told to preach the gospel and things of the kingdom (D&C 71:1) and "nothing but repentance." (D&C 19:21) We are to " contend against no church save it be the church of the devil." (D&C 18:20) If we follow this formula we can fulfill those commandments. Each time a situation arises which presents an opportunity for contention or digression, the missionary leads the investigator right back to the story of the restoration, the good news of the new dispensation.
