The Problem and the Procedure

Your investigator may say, " Our Church has revelation, too. " Or he may be very friendly and be coming along nicely when it occurs to him that his church or other churches than the LDS Church might have revelation. He then may want you to explain why our modern revelation is to be preferred to theirs.

30 This predicament sometimes arises through mismanagement. That is, the missionary may bring it on by using a negative approach, one which sets out to tear other churches down instead of just declaring our message. This is the reason God has commanded us to "contend against no church save it be the church of the devil." (D&C 18:20) It is true that we have to teach in such a way as to contrast the truth with error; but we need not say what the other church teaches which is wrong and then proceed to tear it down.

30 Let us consider some typical cases which will indicate what seems a reasonable way to proceed.
