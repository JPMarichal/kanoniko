12 Sometimes a member of the LDS Church will think that emphasizing the Book of Mormon as the keystone is an attempt to downgrade the other scriptures. This is not the case. The Bible, Doctrine and Covenants, and Pearl of Great Price are, in truth, the equal of the Book of Mormon. In scope, they are more grand, in beauty of language, more sublime. But the Book of Mormon is the keystone in the scriptural arch. Your investigators will have no trouble seeing this. Their background will have prepared them for it. If they have heard of any of our books it will be the Book of Mormon. Our enemies can see that it is the tower they must cast down before they can span our walls. Their main attack is centered on it. They know that, if this bastion falls, all the rest will fall into their hands without a struggle. And, if your investigator has never heard of any of our books, he will believe you when you tell him that this is the place to start his investigation.
