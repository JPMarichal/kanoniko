A Preliminary Agreement

Missionary: If the Bible is true, it cannot contradict itself, can it? That is, it could not clearly teach in one place that revelation has ceased, and then teach in another that it will continue could it?

53 Fred: No. I agree with you. It must be consistent.

53 Missionary: Then, if I can show you that the Bible clearly teaches that there will be revelation and that there must be revelation, you will agree that it could not also teach that revelation would and should cease?

53 Fred: Yes—except, what about those passages which say revelation will cease?

54 Missionary: It is a matter of interpretation. I will show you that there really are no passages which say revelation will cease. But first let me show you how the Bible teaches that there will and must be revelation.
