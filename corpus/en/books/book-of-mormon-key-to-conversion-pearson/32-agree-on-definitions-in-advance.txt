Glenn L. Pearson, Book of Mormon, Key to Conversion ( 1963)

Agree on Definitions in Advance

Most investigators are not as difficult as John. But, if the missionary has any intimation that he is going to have a problem on revelation, he should get his definitions agreed upon in advanced Then it will be less likely that there will be any trouble in coming to an agreement on these definitions.

46 It is not that we want to trap people or win arguments. Missionaries should be divested of the kind of pride that causes them to want to win arguments. They have a job to do. This job is to declare the glad tidings of Christ and His restored gospel. The obstacle most likely to prevent them from getting this job done is contention. The Book of Mormon method of handling problems will make it possible for a missionary to get his job done without contention. He will never win or lose an argument. So no one's pride needs to suffer.

47 There may be times when an argument is forced upon you, and there is no escape. Here, again, the Book of Mormon comes to the rescue. If the missionary learns to use the Book of Mormon system described in part one of this book, he will be able to convince anyone who wants to argue that the only issue can -be whether or not we have revelation, and the only sensible way to settle it is for the investigator or opponent (in whichever role he chooses to cast himself) to find out if the Book of Mormon is true.
