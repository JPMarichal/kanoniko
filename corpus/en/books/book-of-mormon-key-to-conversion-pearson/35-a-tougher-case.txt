A Tougher Case

The chances are pretty good that, if you have a teachable contact you can escape any further discussion of whether or not the Bible teaches that revelation should cease. But suppose Fred says something like this:

53 "I have already heard the story about the Book of Mormon and the Angel Moroni. I won't say it isn't true; but I can't believe it as long as I feel that the Bible teaches that there should be no more revelation. "

53 This sort of comment calls for some discussion of revelation from the Bible.
