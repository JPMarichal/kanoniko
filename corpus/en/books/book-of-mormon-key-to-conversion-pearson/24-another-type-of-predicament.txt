Glenn L. Pearson, Book of Mormon, Key to Conversion ( 1963)

Another Type of Predicament

John: Why do you people practice fasting one day a month? This was a part of the Law of Moses and Jesus did away with it.

33 Elder: God has revealed to the prophets who are at the head of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints that fasting is very important to spiritual development and to give us power over the will of the flesh.

33 John: I don't believe in your prophets. I only believe in the Bible. There is to be no more revelation besides the Bible.

34 Elder: Don't you believe in prayer?

34 John: Of course I do. The Bible teaches us to pray.

34 Elder: Prayer would be meaningless unless prayers could be answered. Do you believe prayers can be answered?

34 John: Of course I do. What are you getting at?

34 Elder: If you asked for guidance in any matter, and your prayer was answered, wouldn't that be revelation?

34 John: Oh no ! I believe prayers can be answered; but I don't believe that this is revelation like the ancient prophets received. It is a sort of an inspiration. We believe that the Holy Spirit can guide us and give us grace. But we don't believe in revelation besides the Bible.
