John Taylor, G. Homer Durham, Gospel Kingdom: Selections from the Writings and Discourses of John Taylor ( 1943)

HIGHLIGHTS IN THE OFFICIAL MINISTRY of JOHN TAYLOR

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1808—November 1: Born at Milnthorpe, Westmoreland County, England, the son of James and Agnes Taylor.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1832—Emigrated from England first to Brooklyn and Albany, New York, finally taking up residence in Toronto, Canada.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1836—>May 9: Baptized a member of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1838—July 8: Called to the apostleship by revelation to the Prophet Joseph Smith (D. & C. section 118).

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1838—December 19: Ordained an apostle by Brigham Young and Heber C. Kimball.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1839—August 8: Left Nauvoo, Illinois, for a mission to England.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1840—January 11: Arrived in Liverpool.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTPHe introduced the gospel into Ireland and the Isle of Man, extending his labors into Scotland.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1841—July 1: Arrived in Nauvoo, Illinois, from his completed mission in England.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1841—October: Appointed with Elias Higbee as a committee to petition Congress for a redress of wrongs in Missouri. He was appointed by the Prophet Joseph Smith to present the petition.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1842—November 15, 1842, to February 15, 1846: Editor of the Times and Seasons.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTPNauvoo city councilman.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTPRegent of the University of Nauvoo.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTPJudge Advocate of the Nauvoo Legion.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1843—May 3, 1843, to October 29, 1845: Editor of the Nauvoo Neighbor.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1844—June 27: Seriously wounded by the same mob which killed the Prophet Joseph and his brother, the Patriarch Hyrum Smith, at Carthage, Illinois.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1846—Assisted in organizing the Mormon Batallion at Winter Quarters.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1846—October 3: Arrived in England for a second mission.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1847—Returned with a number of British emigrating Saints, and arrived in the Salt Lake valley in late fall.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1849—March 12: Chosen one of the associate judges of the Supreme Court of the Provisional State of Deseret.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1849—October: Called on a mission to France.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1851—Published in the papers Etoile Du Deseret and Zion's Panier in Paris and Hamburg, respectively.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1851—Completed manuscript of The Government of God.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1852—Book of Mormon translated into French and German under his direction and published in Paris and Hamburg.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1852—August 20: Arrived in Salt Lake City after completing European mission.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1854—Elected a member of the Territorial Legislature, but subsequently resigned to fill a mission in New York and preside over the Eastern States Mission.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1855—February 17, 1855, to September 19, 1857: Published the Mormon in New York City.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1857—Called home at the coming of Johnston's army.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTPSpeaker of the House of Representatives, Utah Territorial Legislature, for five successive sessions after 1857, member until 1876.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1877—Elected Territorial Superintendent of Schools, an office later abolished by Act of Congress in 1887.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1880—October 10: Sustained as President of the Church.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1882—Completed manuscript of The Mediation and Atonement of Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1884—May 17: Offered the dedicatory prayer at the Logan Temple.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1885—February 1: Made last public appearance and withdrew to voluntary exile in view of anti-polygamy laws and persecutions thereunder.

Taylor, Johna-Taylor, JohnTP1887—July 25: Died at Kaysville, Davis County, Utah, aged 78 years, 8 months, 25 days.

Taylor, Johna-Young, BrighamTP"With regard to Brother John Taylor, I will say that he has one of the strongest intellects that can be found. He is a powerful man, and we may say that he is a powerful editor. But I will use a term to suit myself and say that he is one of the strongest editors that ever wrote."
