Explanation:

A more detailed illustration of chart 66, this chart illustrates the concise but thorough discourse of Alma the Younger as he explains the ways of life and death to the people of Ammonihah (see Alma 12:31–37). Alma's explanation of the first death can be applied to three different situations: Adam and Eve's transgression in the Garden of Eden, Israel's disobedience to the law of Moses in the wilderness, and personal sin.
