Explanation:

In Mosiah 26:20–28 the voice of the Lord builds upon Abinadi's discourse, telling Alma the Elder that the ways of life and death can be determined in terms of responding to the Lord's voice. Those who obey the word of the Lord will be found on the right hand of God, while those who do not will dwell in everlasting fire with the devil.
