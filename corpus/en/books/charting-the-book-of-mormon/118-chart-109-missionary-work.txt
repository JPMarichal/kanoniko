Explanation:

The importance of missionary work is a prominent theme in the Book of Mormon. This chart lists and compares the work of many of the ancient American missionaries, the people they preached to, the degree of success they had, and the scriptural references pertaining to their missionary labors. Reactions to the gospel message varied, despite the quality of missionaries in the field.
