The Meaning of the Law of Moses Is Lost If Its Connection to Christ Is Overlooked MLM

While todayÂs experts on Judaism are very learned in all the details of the feast day, the fast days, the calendars, the minutiae of legal technicalities, and the history of the law of Moses, they rarely know the most essential fact--that the law of Moses had no meaning without Christ. Without faith in Christ and a great understanding of the New Testament commentary on the law of Moses, it would be difficult to find this most important aspect of the law of Moses in the Old Testament. In fact, the meaning of the law of Moses is obscure even in the New Testament. Too many of "the plain and precious things" have been deleted. But the Book of Mormon supplies all of these things which are most precious to a proper understanding of the law of Moses.
