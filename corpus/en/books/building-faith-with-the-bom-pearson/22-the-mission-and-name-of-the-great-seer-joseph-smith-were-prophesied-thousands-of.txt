(2) He was a descendant of that Joseph, son of Jacob, who was sold into Egypt by his brethren. (3) That same Joseph, top leader in Egypt, foretold Joseph SmithÂs coming, his name, the name of his father, and many details of his greatness and his ministry. (4) The things which are said about the great seer, Joseph Smith, make it abundantly clear that anyone who has said that he become a fallen prophet has denied the truth of the Book of Mormon and is, himself or herself, an apostate.
