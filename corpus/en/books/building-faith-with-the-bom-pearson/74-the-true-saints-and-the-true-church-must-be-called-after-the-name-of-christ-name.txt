The True Saints and the True Church Must Be Called After the Name of Christ NAME

There is a name that all the faithful must bear and not be ashamed of, and that name is the name of Christ. That is the name King Benjamin promised the faithful. That is the name Abinadi reminded them of. That is the name Christ himself designated as the only name for his Church. "There is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved." (Acts 4:12.)
