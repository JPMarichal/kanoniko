Glenn L. Pearson, Reid E. Bankhead, Building Faith with the Book of Mormon ( 1986)

Faith in Christ Is a Great Motivator FAITH

Faith is a gift or quality of character which moves people to good action. It is the opposite of fear and doubt. Faith in God as they perceived him and in themselves caused the Founding Fathers of the United States to establish a free nation in which men and women could let their own resourcefulness and a bounteous nature work together to create the highest living standard any nation has enjoyed as far as we know.

105 It is not just faith, however, but faith in Christ that is the first principle of the gospel. Faith in Christ causes us to hope for a better life here and in the world to come. This hope causes us to come down into the depths of humility and experience a broken heart and a contrite spirit ad to dedicate our lives to Christ. It causes us to pattern our lives after his life and to keep his commandments.
