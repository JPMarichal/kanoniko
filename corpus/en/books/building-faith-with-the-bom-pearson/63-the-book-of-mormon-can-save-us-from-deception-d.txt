Glenn L. Pearson, Reid E. Bankhead, Building Faith with the Book of Mormon ( 1986)

The Book of Mormon Can Save Us from Deception D

There is a difference between being deceived and making a mistake. When you are deceived, you think you are right when you are wrong; so you are less likely to make a correction and get back on course. You go on living and doing things that are wrong, but you think they are right. Since we reap as we sow, the harvest of deception is an empty granary. Life is frustrating at best; but when we are led by men like Nehor, Sherem, Korihor, Gadianton, and Amalickiah, we are led down into destruction both temporally and spiritually. The lives of these men are case histories of deceivers and the deceived. If you will study them, you will see that many of the people of the world are being led today by men just like them.
