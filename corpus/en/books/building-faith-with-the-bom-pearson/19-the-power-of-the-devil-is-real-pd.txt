Glenn L. Pearson, Reid E. Bankhead, Building Faith with the Book of Mormon ( 1986)

The Power of the Devil Is Real PD

The devil is a real person with a real power that is the counterfeit or opposite of good. This is the power of evil. The devil is an enemy of God. He seeks the misery of all mankind. He has the ability to tempt people who have reached the age of accountability if they let him. The Book of Mormon calls this "listing" to obey him. Christ is the power we need to reject Satan. That is, if we exercise the will to live righteously and seek his help, Christ will give us his spirit and power. We are free to follow Christ, the good shepherd, or the devil, who is the hireling or the wolf, who doesn't care for the flock.

2 Ne. 2:15-18, 28.
