Doctrine and Covenants, 1854 & 1902 editions, November 25, 1905

1905-November 25-Original letter. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The "present edition" of the Doctrine and Covenants referred to in this letter presumably was the edition of 1902. The first edition of the Doctrine and Covenants appeared in 1835. The early editions had fewer revelations in them and the material was not verified. The Doctrine and Covenants was divided into verses, with references by Orson Pratt in 1876. It was first issued with footnotes by Orson Pratt in 1879.

In 1900 the First Presidency authorized a revision of the Pearl of Great Price and a new edition of the Doctrine and Covenants by Dr. James E. Talmage. They were both published in 1902.

November 25, 1905 Mr. J. B. Redford, DuBois, Idaho

Dear Brother:

This is in answer to yours of the 22 inst.

The Book of Doctrine and Covenants, published in 1854, referred to by you, is not so complete as our present edition, in which the revelations have been put in the order of the dates at which they were given, and they have also been divided into shorter verses, thereby making the book better for reference purposes. Revelations taken from the Prophet Joseph Smith's history have also been added in the later edition, but the revelations themselves have not been changed.

Your Brethren, JOSEPH F. SMITH, JOHN R. WINDER, ANTHON H. LUND, First Presidency.

1905-December 1-Original pamphlet. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Special instructions on handling Church funds had appeared in April, 1905. When these annual instructions of December, 1905, were issued, however, they were virtually word for word with the annual instructions of December, 1904.
