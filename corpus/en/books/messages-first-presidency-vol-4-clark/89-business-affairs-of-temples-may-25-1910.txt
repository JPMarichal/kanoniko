Business affairs of Temples, May 25, 1910

1910-May 25-Original letter. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Following the death of President John R. Winder on May 25, 1910, the First Presidency was reorganized and on April 7, 1910, Anthon H. Lund, former second counselor, was sustained as first counselor and John Henry Smith was sustained as the new second counselor. He died October 13, 1911, after serving in the First Presidency for one year and a half.

The instructions in this letter to Temple Presidents looked toward improved methods of record keeping and reporting for the Temples of the Church with John Wells authorized to gather information for later recommendation and action of the First Presidency.

Salt Lake City, Utah, May 25th, 1910.

TO THE PRESIDENTS OF TEMPLES,

Dear Brethren:

With a view to securing a more uniform and perhaps improved method of accounting and recording and the general arrangement of the business affairs of our temples, we have instructed Bro. John Wells, of the Presiding Bishop's Office to visit the different temples and secure what information he can that will assist us in reaching conclusions as to the best methods to be adopted.

Please render Brother Wells such assistance as you can and be perfectly free to make such recommendations as in your judgment will help to bring about the result desired.

Your brethren in the Gospel, (Signed) JOSEPH F. SMITH, ANTHON H. LUND, JOHN HENRY SMITH.
