James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 4 ( 1970)

Uintah Indian Reservation-opening to white settlement, December 10, 1904

1904-December 10-Original letter. Reed Smoot collection, Brigham Young University Library, Archives. Provo, Utah.

The First Presidency here conveyed a request from President William H. Smart that the Senator from Utah assist the people of his Stake with information relative to the opening for white settlement of the Uintah Indian Reservation in eastern Utah.

Office of The First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

Salt Lake City, Utah, December 10th, 1904 Senator Reed Smoot, Washington, D.C.

Dear Senator:

President William H. Smart has just called upon us in the interest of his people with respect to the opening of the Uintah Reservation; and as a result of the conversation we are led to ask that if you can do anything in their behalf by way of affording him information in relation to the opening or otherwise render timely assistance, he and we shall feel very much obliged.

Brethren, JOSEPH F. SMITH, JOHN R. WINDER, ANTHON H. LUND, First Presidency.
