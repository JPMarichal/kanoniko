Committee to answer Burrows and DuBois, January 7, 1907

1907-January 7-Original letter. Reed Smoot collection, Brigham Young University, Archives. Provo, Utah.

See notes to previous letters of Gibbs to Smoot under date of January 4, 1907.

Office of The First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

Salt Lake City, Utah, January 7th, 1907. Senator Reed Smoot, Washington, D.C.

Dear Senator:

Just a line to say that the committee appointed for the purpose of answering in behalf of the Church certain allegations made by Senators Burrows and Dubois, of which I wrote you last Friday, has concluded to publish nothing until your case is closed. I would like to say also that reference to this committee's work was personal information to you.

Yours, &c. GEO. F. GIBBS, Secretary.
