Calling of Seventies on missions, May 10, 1906

1906-May 10-Original letter. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The procedure for calling Seventies in the Church on missions outlined in this letter constituted a change in procedure of the Church. It gave the First Council of Seventies more direct jurisdiction over the Seventies of the Church The procedure has since that time been reverted to the original procedure of the Bishops recommending and the First Presidency calling all missionaries.

Office of The First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

Salt Lake City, Utah, May 10th, 1906. Prest. Joseph Morrell and Counselors, Cache Stake.

Dear Brethren:

In the matter of calling Seventies on missions, the First Council of Seventies have made the request to the effect that this labor and duty be assigned to themselves alone, but with the understanding that the names of all Seventies to be called on missions by them shall first be submitted in the usual way to the Bishops for their approval.

Regarding this as a consistent request, we readily granted it. Therefore you and your Bishops will hereafter please regard the quorums of Seventy in your Stake as the field for the First Council of Seventies to operate in, as far as calling Seventies on missions is concerned; and this will leave all other eligible brethren under your jurisdiction subject to the direct recommendation of yourselves and Bishops.

Your Brethren, JOSEPH F. SMITH, JOHN R. WINDER, ANTHON H. LUND, First Presidency.
