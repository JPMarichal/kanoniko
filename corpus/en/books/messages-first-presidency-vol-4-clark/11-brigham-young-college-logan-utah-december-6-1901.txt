Brigham Young College, Logan, Utah, December 6, 1901

1901-December 6-Original letter. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The Brigham Young College mentioned in this letter, sent to the Cache Stake Board of Education, is not to be confused with the Brigham Young Academy in Provo, Utah. Both institutions, Brigham Young College in Logan, Utah, and Brigham Young Academy (later University) in Provo, were first private and then L.D.S. schools. Brigham Young College was founded by Brigham Young on July 24, 1877. It first opened for admission of students on September 9, 1878, in rented rooms in the Logan City Hall. In 1883-1884, the "East Building" was constructed on the nine thousand acres deeded to the College by Brigham Young. In 1897-98 the "West Building" was constructed. This letter is notice of a general Church appropriation of $12,400 for operating expenses for the year 1901-1902. The College was discontinued in 1926.

Office of the First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

President Joseph Morrell, Cache Stake Board of Education

Dear Brother:

At a meeting of the General Church Board of Education held on the 26th of November last, the sum of $12,400. was appropriated in favor of your Stake Board of Education to be used in meeting the running expenses of the Brigham Young College during the present school year (1901-2), and I, as Trustee-in-Trust, have issued instructions for the payment of said amount, or such parts thereof (supposing you have already drawn on account in anticipation of this appropriation) as now remain unpaid, to be drawn from time to time as needed. You will receive your orders, on application, through Brother James Jack of this office.

As ever, Your Brother, JOSEPH F. SMITH Trustee-in-Trust.
