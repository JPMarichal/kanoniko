New Apostle chosen, October 24, 1901

1901-October 24-Millennial Star 63:748 (November 14, 1901)

Hyrum M. Smith, the new Apostle, was the son of President Joseph F. Smith. The notice illustrates how re-organization of the Church at the top may affect a number of other offices in the Church.

A NEW APOSTLE CHOSEN

The Deseret News of Thursday, October 24th, says:

At the regular meeting of the First Presidency and Apostles, held today in the Salt Lake Temple, Elder Hyrum M. Smith was unanimously chosen and ordained by that body to fill the vacancy in the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles.

At the same meeting Elder Orrin P. Miller, President of the Jordan Stake, was chosen as the second counselor of Presiding Bishop William B. Preston, to fill the vacancy occasioned by the selection of Bishop Winder as first counselor to President Joseph F. Smith.

Elder Hyrum Goff, first counselor to President Orrin P. Miller of the Jordan Stake was nominated to succeed President Miller. JOSEPH F. SMITH, JOHN R. WINDER, ANTHON H. LUND, First Presidency
