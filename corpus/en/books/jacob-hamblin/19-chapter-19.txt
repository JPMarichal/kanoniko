Visit of Tuba and his wife to the Washington factory and flouring mill--Many Navajoes come to trade with our people--Take Tuba and his wife home--More talk about the death of Young George A. Smith--Saints called to settle in Arizona--They become discouraged and return to Utah--Navajoes murdered in Grass Valley--War imminent--Sent to settle the difficulty--Two miners accompany me--Indians call a council.
