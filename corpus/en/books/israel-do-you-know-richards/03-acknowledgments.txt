LeGrand Richards, Israel! Do You Know? ( 1954)

ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

Several friends read manuscript copies of this book. To all of them, too numerous to mention, I express my gratitude for their time, their candid opinions and suggestions.

Since this book deals largely with the Jews, I invited some of my Jewish friends to read the manuscript and to express their reactions. All of them have been most cooperative and their suggestions have been invaluable. The manuscript was passed so freely from one to another among so many of them as to make it hazardous to mention names lest some are omitted. I am sure they will understand and will accept this expression of my appreciation for their friendly assistance.

The Embassy of Israel, Washington, D. C., gave me full cooperation in all requests made of them as detailed elsewhere.

Words cannot express my feelings of gratitude to my very dear friend and colleague, Lee A. Palmer, for his willingness to assist me with this book in so many ways. His tireless assistance, given without remuneration, in the editing of A Marvelous Work and A Wonder, [^1] gave me courage to call upon him again. Since the time he so generously devoted to editing the manuscript, to proofreading, and to relieving me of the details of publication was taken from his family at home, I express to him and to them my profound appreciation.

Vella Wetzel, Frances Cardall, and Liliu Peery were so kind and helpful in typing the manuscript copy and reference material with such meticulous care.

LeGrand Richards

Salt Lake City, Utah

October 1, 1954
