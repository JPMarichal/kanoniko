# The Home Front

### The Home Front
Chad Hawkins, Faith in Service (Salt Lake City 2008)
 
 

The Home Front

A few
weeks from now I will be sending a son as a missionary to Brazil and my husband
as a soldier to Afghanistan. As a mother of a missionary and the wife of a
soldier, I am proud of their willingness to serve God and country.
Although I will miss them tremendously, I trust the Lord will bless and comfort
them while sustaining us back home. As they serve honorable causes, I
place them in His care.

—Colette
Fillmore
