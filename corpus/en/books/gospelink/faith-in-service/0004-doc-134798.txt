# Events DownRange

### Events DownRange
Chad Hawkins, Faith in Service (Salt Lake City 2008)
 
 

Events DownRange

We
live in a time of wars and rumors of wars. 
Many Church members have personally felt the effects of war, and many more will
likely participate in these conflicts. We are grateful for the personal
sacrifices they and their families have made in 
defense of freedom and liberty. Especially during these challenging times,
we want them to know of our love and heartfelt thanks 
for their courageous and devoted service.

—Frank
W. Clawson, LDS Church Director of Military Relations
