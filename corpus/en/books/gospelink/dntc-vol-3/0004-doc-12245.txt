# Gospel Preached to Every Creature

### Gospel Preached to Every Creature
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 3 ( 1973)
 
 

Gospel Preached to Every Creature

SCCol 1 20Col 1 21Col 1 22Col 1 2320-23. Through the atonement of Christ, coupled with obedience to the laws and ordinances of the gospel, men are reconciled to God and to Christ. Reconciliation] See Commentary II, pp. 421-423.

Col 1 2020. Reconciliation comes by the shedding of Christ's blood and is between God in heaven and man on earth.

Col 1 2121. Even wicked men (through repentance!) are reconciled to God through Christ.

Col 1 2222. Those who are reconciled become clean; they are pure and blameless; their sins are forgiven.

Col 1 2323. But the reconciliation remains in force only on the condition of continued obedience.

Col 1 23Gospel] See Commentary II, pp. 213-216.

Col 1 23Which was preached to every creature which is under heaven] What Paul probably wrote was that the gospel shall be preached to every creature which is under heaven. This is what we commonly say today to emphasize the importance of the gospel message and the universality of its application. However, two truths are known with reference to who has and who will hear the gospel:1. Every living soul did hear the gospel in pre-existence, and 2. Every living soul shall hear the gospel again, either in this life or in the spirit world before the day of resurrection and judgment.

Col 1 23The gospel is the plan of salvation. It consists of the laws, ordinances, and eternal truths by conformity to which the spirit children of God can progress and advance until they become like their Eternal Parent. It is centered in Christ, whose atoning sacrifice guarantees immortality to all men and offers eternal life to all who obey its laws. The gospel was first revealed in the pre-mortal life; it was known to all the spirit hosts of heaven in that day when Christ was chosen and foreordained to be the Redeemer. Those who then rejected it were cast out with Lucifer and his angels.

Col 1 23This same gospel was revealed to Adam and to all of the saints of old. Christ restored it in the meridian of time. Paul and the ancient apostles preached it to the extent of their strength and abilities. And it has been restored again, for the last time, through Joseph Smith, in this final dispensation. With its restoration has come the decree that it shall be preached in every nation and among every people before the Second Coming of Christ. All men however will not hear it while in mortality. Rather, untold hosts will hear the message in the spirit world, for the eternal decree is that "there is no eye that shall not see, neither ear that shall not hear, neither heart that shall not be penetrated." (D. & C. 1:2.)

SCCol 1 24Col 1 2524-25. Paul was a minister of the gospel, sent to teach those truths dispensed to him by revelation.

SCCol 1 26Col 1 27Col 1 28Col 1 2926-29. His preaching was the mystery of Christ; that is, his teachings were incomprehensible to the carnal mind; they could not be understood by worldly power, only by revelation from God.

Col 1 2828. Warning every man] True ministers always raise the voice of warning—a voice which invites men to flee from the desolation awaiting the world and come to the safety and security of the gospel. (D. & C. 1:4; 38:41; 63:37, 58; 88:81.)
