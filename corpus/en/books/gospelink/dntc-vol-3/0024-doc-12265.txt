# Paul Preacheth the Gospel of Work

### Paul Preacheth the Gospel of Work
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 3 ( 1973)
 
 

Paul Preacheth the Gospel of Work

SC2 Thes 3 72 Thes 3 82 Thes 3 92 Thes 3 102 Thes 3 112 Thes 3 12Temporal work is essential to salvation. Man cannot be saved in idleness. It is not enough simply to believe the great spiritual realities. We could do that as spirits in pre-existence. But we are now placed on a temporal earth, to gain the experiences of mortality, with the command: "In the sweat of thy face shalt thou eat bread, till thou return unto the ground." (Gen. 3:19.) Work, as such, is an essential part of eternal progression. Hence: "The idler shall be had in remembrance before the Lord." (D. & C. 68:30.) And: "The idler shall not have place in the church, except he repent and mend his ways." (D. & C. 75:29.)

SC2 Thes 3 72 Thes 3 82 Thes 3 97-9. Even Paul and his ministerial associates, who were in fact entitled to temporal help from the saints, chose to set an example of self-support. There are perils in a paid ministry. Of the elders of the Church in general the Lord says: "Let the residue of the elders watch over the churches, and declare the word in the regions round about them; and let them labor with their own hands that there be no idolatry nor wickedness practised." (D. & C. 52:39.)

SC2 Thes 3 102 Thes 3 112 Thes 3 1210-12. Work is a commandment of the Lord. "Thou shalt not be idle; for he that is idle shall not eat the bread nor wear the garments of the laborer." (D. & C. 42:42.)

SC2 Thes 3 102 Thes 3 112 Thes 3 12Work with your own hands] See 1 Thess. 4:9-12.
