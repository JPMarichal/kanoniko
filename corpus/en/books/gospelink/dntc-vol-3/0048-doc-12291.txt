# "Preach the Word"

### "Preach the Word"
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 3 ( 1973)
 
 

"Preach the Word"

SC2 Tim 4 12 Tim 4 22 Tim 4 32 Tim 4 42 Tim 4 5What message do the Lord's ministers bear? What should they preach? The gospel of Jesus Christ! The word! The truths of salvation as recorded in the revelations! How sobering is Paul's charge! In God's holy name the command is to preach doctrine, sound doctrine, saving doctrine, the doctrine of Christ!

SC2 Tim 4 12 Tim 4 22 Tim 4 32 Tim 4 42 Tim 4 5"These shall be their teachings," the Lord commands in this day: "Teach the principles of my gospel, which are in the Bible and the Book of Mormon," and in "the fulness of my scriptures." (D. & C. 42:12-15.)

2 Tim 4 22. Preach the word] In sending forth his latter-day ministers, the Lord commanded: "Let them journey from thence preaching the word by the way, saying none other things than that which the prophets and apostles have written, and that which is taught them by the Comforter through the prayer of faith." (D. & C. 52:9.)

2 Tim 4 33. They will not endure sound doctrine] See 2 Tim. 3:1-13.

2 Tim 4 44. Fables] "All false doctrines are fables. That is, they are stories which have been imagined, fabricated, and invented as opposed to the gospel which is real and true. (2 Pet. 1:16.) Apostasy consists in turning from true doctrine to fables." (Mormon Doctrine, 2nd ed., p. 261.)

2 Tim 4 55. An evangelist] See Commentary ii, pp. 508-512.

2 Tim 4 5Teach true doctrine only] See 1 Tim. 1:1-11.

2 Tim 4 5Preach by the Spirit] See Commentary II, pp. 317-319.

2 Tim 4 5Take no thought beforehand] 1 Pet. 4:7-11.
