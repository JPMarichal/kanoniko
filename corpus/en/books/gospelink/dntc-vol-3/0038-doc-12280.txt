# Paul Names Divers Church Policies

### Paul Names Divers Church Policies
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 3 ( 1973)
 
 

Paul Names Divers Church Policies

1 Tim 5 195:19. Christ's ministers should not be condemned on hearsay and gossip. Formal charges should be made through the court system of the Church.

1 Tim 5 2020. Public sins should be dealt with publicly. "And if thy brother or sister offend many, he or she shall be chastened before many. And if any one offend openly, he or she shall be rebuked openly, that he or she may be ashamed." (D. & C. 42:90-91.)

1 Tim 5 2121. The elect angels] Those angels, as Moroni and Elijah, who inherit exaltation in celestial glory. "Then shall the angels be crowned with the glory of his might, and the saints shall be filled with his glory, and receive their inheritance and be made equal with him." (D. & C. 88:107.)

1 Tim 5 21Doing nothing by partiality] God is no respecter of persons; let his ministers be as he is.

1 Tim 5 2222. Lay hands suddenly on no man] Brethren should be seasoned, tried, and found worthy before they are ordained and set apart to serve in positions of power and influence in the Church.

1 Tim 5 2323. Wine] "The fermented, or, loosely, the unfermented, juice of any fruit or plant used as a beverage; as, currant wine." (Webster's New Collegiate Dictionary.) Having knowledge of Timothy's physical infirmities, Paul is probably here counseling him that fruit juices will be more healthful than water. It is not reasonable to suppose that Timothy was being told to drink an alcoholic beverage, unless such was under limited medicinal circumstances.

SC1 Tim 5 241 Tim 5 2524-25. Concerning those to be ordained to priestly office (verse 22): 'Some are known to be unworthy when considered for ordination; the unworthiness of others is revealed later. Some are recognized as worthy in advance; the fitness and qualifications of others is manifest later.'

1 Tim 6 21 Tim 6 16:1. Servants . . . 2. Masters] See Commentary II, pp. 522-523.

1 Tim 6 33. Wholesome words] The words of Christ and his servants, words of truth and righteousness, words which teach sound doctrine, words that edify.

SC1 Tim 6 41 Tim 6 54-5. How aptly these descriptive phrases of Paul's describe apostate religionists! And how universal is their application!
