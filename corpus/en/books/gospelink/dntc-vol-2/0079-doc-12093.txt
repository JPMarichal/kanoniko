(Alma 37:25-31; Hela. 6:15-30; 4 Ne. 42; Ether 8; 10:33.) God permitted his ancient saints to swear with an oath that they would perform some righteous act at the peril of their lives. See Commentary I, pp. 225-227. Satan on the other hand directed those who had fully given themselves into his hands and service to covenant by solemn adjuration to spread evil and iniquity on earth even at the peril of their lives.

Acts 23 1212. Bound themselves under a curse] A perfect description of a devil-directed oath. In effect these ancient gangsters pledged, in God's name, that their lives or Paul's would be forfeit—thereby bringing the eternal curse of God upon them.

Acts 23 2929. Nothing... worthy of death or of bonds] As with Jesus, so with Paul. (Luke 23:4.)
