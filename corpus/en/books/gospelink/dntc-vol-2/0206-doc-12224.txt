# Saints Sealed Up Unto the Day of Redemption

### Saints Sealed Up Unto the Day of Redemption
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 2 ( 1971)
 
 

Saints Sealed Up unto the Day of Redemption

Eph 4 30Those saints who keep the commandments, who overcome the world, who are faithful in all things, who are married in the temple, who live by every word that proceedeth forth from the mouth of God, have their calling and election made sure. "They are sealed by the Holy Spirit of promise... unto the day of redemption." (D. & C. 132:26.)

Eph 4 30Holy Spirit of Promise] See Eph. 1:13-14.

Eph 4 30Calling and election sure] See 2 Pet. 1:1-19.

Eph 4 30Patriarchal blessings] See Heb. 11:17-22.
