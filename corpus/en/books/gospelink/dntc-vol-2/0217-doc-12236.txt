SCPhilip 2 17Philip 2 18Philip 2 19Philip 2 20Philip 2 21Philip 2 22Philip 2 23Philip 2 24Philip 2 25Philip 2 26Philip 2 27Philip 2 28Philip 2 29Philip 2 30There is always the human element in church administration. Some of Christ's ministers serve well, others poorly, and some decline calls to service, even for personal and selfish reasons. Sickness and disease plague the saints as well as the world. Thus, Timothy was faithful; Epaphroditus incurred serious illness while engaged in his appointed missionary work; and some who should have responded to gospel calls preferred to pursue their own interests rather than "the things which are Jesus Christ's."
