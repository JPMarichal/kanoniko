# 'as Ye Sow, So Shall Ye Reap'

### 'as Ye Sow, So Shall Ye Reap'
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 2 ( 1971)
 
 

'As Ye Sow, So Shall Ye Reap'

SCGal 6 7Gal 6 8Gal 6 9Gal 6 107-10. The law of the harvest! Men (the saints included!) reap what they sow! These Galatians are in the Church; they have received Christ and his gospel; they are Paul's converts; to some extent at least they are in harmony with God and his laws. But salvation does not come to them because they believed, because they once confessed the Lord Jesus with their lips, because of the grace of God alone; they are not justified by faith only, as such is generally understood and taught in modern Christendom.

SCGal 6 7Gal 6 8Gal 6 9Gal 6 10But in addition to accepting Christ, in addition to baptism and the receipt of the Holy Ghost, they are now required to keep the commandments, to overcome the world, to "press forward with a steadfastness in Christ, having a perfect brightness of hope, and a love of God and of all men." They are now required to feast "upon the word of Christ, and endure to the end." (2 Ne. 31:20.)

SCGal 6 7Gal 6 8Gal 6 9Gal 6 10Members of the Church who keep the commandments shall be saved; those who disobey them shall not be so blessed. Salvation is not a matter of belief alone, of grace alone, of the cross of Christ only; it is all this coupled with "obedience to the laws and ordinances of the Gospel." (Third Article of Faith.)

Gal 6 88. Corruption] Spiritual death. Life everlasting] Eternal life. See 2 Tim. 1:1-12.

Gal 6 99. We shall reap] 'We shall gain eternal life.' If we faint not] 'If we endure to the end.'

Gal 6 1010. Though the saints, as their strength and circumstances permit, do good to all men, their special obligation is to bless and help their fellow saints, the members of their church-family.

SCGal 6 12Gal 6 13Gal 6 1412-14. Those Galatian Saints who advocated and practiced circumcision did so to avoid persecution; they were not in fact attempting to keep the whole law of Moses, of which circumcision was the symbol. But because they were compromising the gospel with the law of Moses, they were not and could not center their whole hearts on Christ and the salvation made possible through his atoning sacrifice.

Gal 6 1515. The gospel is for all, Israel of that day who are circumcised and the Gentiles who are not. Salvation, regardless of circumcision, comes to all who accept Christ, are baptized, receive the Holy Ghost, and are born again, becoming new creatures, alive to the things of the Spirit.

Gal 6 1616. Peace and mercy are bestowed upon the obedient. (Mormon Doctrine, 2nd ed., pp. 483-486.)

Gal 6 1717. The marks of the Lord Jesus] The scars in Paul's flesh which testified of his steadfastness in the face of persecution (2 Cor. 11:23-27); also, the holy symbols typifying faith in Christ and his gospel, which are borne by all the faithful.
