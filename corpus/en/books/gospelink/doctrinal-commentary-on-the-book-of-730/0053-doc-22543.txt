# All That Is Good Comes from God Omni 1:25

### All That Is Good Comes from God Omni 1:25
Robert L. Millet, Joseph Fielding McConkie, Doctrinal Commentary on the Book of Mormon, vol. 2 ( 1988)
 
 

All That Is Good Comes from God

Omni 1:25

25. And it came to pass that I began to be old; and, having no seed, and knowing king Benjamin to be a just man before the Lord, wherefore, I shall deliver up these plates unto him, exhorting all men to come unto God, the Holy One of Israel, and believe in prophesying, and in revelations, and in the ministering of angels, and in the gift of speaking with tongues, and in the gift of interpreting languages, and in all things which are good; for there is nothing which is good save it comes from the Lord: and that which is evil cometh from the devil.

Omni 1 2525. Of necessity, all true religion is revealed religion. A religion that does not claim God as its Father can hardly expect God to claim its adherents as a part of his family. Thus it is fundamental that all true religion contain the ongoing testimony of prophecy, revelation, the ministering of angels, and the manifestation of all spiritual gifts and all good things- "for there is nothing which is good save it comes from the Lord." (Cf. Alma 5:40; Moroni 7:12.)
