# To Serve One Another Is to Serve God Mosiah 2:16-17

### To Serve One Another Is to Serve God Mosiah 2:16-17
Robert L. Millet, Joseph Fielding McConkie, Doctrinal Commentary on the Book of Mormon, vol. 2 ( 1988)
 
 

To Serve One Another Is to Serve God

Mosiah 2:16-17

16. Behold, I say unto you that because I said unto you that I had spent my days in your service, I do not desire to boast, for I have only been in the service of God.

17. And behold, I tell you these things that ye may learn wisdom; that ye may learn that when ye are in the service of your fellow beings ye are only in the service of your God.

Mosiah 2 1616. I have only been in the service of God] We serve God by serving each other. Of acts of goodness and kindness the Savior said, "Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me." Conversely, "inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me." (Matthew 25:40, 45.)

Mosiah 2 16To stain our clothes and soil our hands in the service of others is but to cleanse our own souls. We are sanctified through properly serving others. The world would be a poorer place indeed had Christ and his prophets sought to sequester themselves in secluded monasteries rather than lose themselves in the service of their fellowman.

Mosiah 2 1717. Learn wisdom] The Psalmist who told us that "the fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom" also told us that the knowledge of God is reserved for those who "do his commandments" (Psalms 111:10). Wisdom, which is the coinage of heaven, is acquired by him who labors in heaven's cause. "The same layeth up in store that he perisheth not, but bringeth salvation to his soul" (D&C 4:5). In contrast, "the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it is written, He taketh the wise in their own craftiness." (1 Corinthians 3:19.)
