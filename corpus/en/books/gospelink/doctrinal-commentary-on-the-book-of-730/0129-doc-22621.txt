# Salvation Is Only in Christ Mosiah 16:6-13

### Salvation Is Only in Christ Mosiah 16:6-13
Robert L. Millet, Joseph Fielding McConkie, Doctrinal Commentary on the Book of Mormon, vol. 2 ( 1988)
 
 

Salvation Is Only in Christ

Mosiah 16:6-13

6. And now if Christ had not come into the world, speaking of things to come as though they had already come, there could have been no redemption.

7. And if Christ had not risen from the dead, or have broken the bands of death that the grave should have no victory, and that death should have no sting, there could have been no resurrection.

8. But there is a resurrection, therefore the grave hath no victory, and the sting of death is swallowed up in Christ.

9. He is the light and the life of the world; yea, a light that is endless, that can never be darkened; yea, and also a life which is endless, that there can be no more death.

10. Even this mortal shall put on immortality, and this corruption shall put on incorruption, and shall be brought to stand before the bar of God, to be judged of him according to their works whether they be good or whether they be evil-

11. If they be good, to the resurrection of endless life and happiness; and if they be evil, to the resurrection of endless damnation, being delivered up to the devil, who hath subjected them, which is damnation-

12. Having gone according to their own carnal wills and desires; having never called upon the Lord while the arms of mercy were extended towards them; for the arms of mercy were extended towards them, and they would not; they being warned of their iniquities and yet they would not depart from them; and they were commanded to repent and yet they would not repent.

13. And now, ought ye not to tremble and repent of your sins, and remember that only in and through Christ ye can be saved?

SCMosiah 16 6Mosiah 16 76-7. Had there been no Christ, had Mary not brought forth the Father's Only Begotten Son, had the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world not taken upon himself a body of flesh and blood, there could have been no redemption-no freeing of men from the dominion and power of Satan. Nor would there have been a breaking of the bands of death in a glorious resurrection. Adam and all of his children would have remained as if there had been no redemption made. They would have been as Jacob declared, "devils, angels to a devil, to be shut out from the presence of our God, and to remain with the father of lies, in misery, like unto himself" (2 Nephi 9:9).

Mosiah 16 66. As though they had already come] With the Nephites, as with all the faithful Saints before the Savior came into mortality, their faith was in the promise of a redemption which was sure, in a God before whom all things-past, present, and future-were and are one eternal now. For them, prophecy was as history.

Mosiah 16 77. The grave should have no victory] See Isaiah 25:8; Hosea 13:14; 1 Corinthians 15:55.

Mosiah 16 88. The victory is the Lord's! He has broken the bands of death, has robbed the grave of its awful sting. All who witness the return of the resurrected Christ will "see the salvation [i.e., victory] of the Lord." Joseph Smith said it perfectly: "Salvation is nothing more nor less than to triumph over all our enemies and put them under our feet. And when we have power to put all enemies under our feet in this world, and a knowledge to triumph over all evil spirits in the world to come, then we are saved, as in the case of Jesus, who was to reign until He had put all enemies under His feet, and the last enemy was death." (Teachings, p. 297.)

Mosiah 16 99. He is the light and the life of the world] Light brings freedom, deliverance, and hope; it is a symbol of happiness, prosperity, and joy. To the Jews Christ declared himself to be the "light of the world" (John 8:12; John 9:5). To those of the latter days he announced himself to be "the light which is in all things, which giveth life to all things, which is the law by which all things are governed, even the power of God who sitteth upon his throne, who is in the bosom of eternity, who is in the midst of all things" (D&C 88:13). Without Christ all is darkness, misery, and death. See also Alma 38:9; 3 Nephi 9:18; 3 Nephi 11:11.

To this ancient testimony we add the words of a modern prophet: "In his love and in his mercy, a gracious God seeks the salvation of all his children. But he cannot save the righteous without damning the wicked; he cannot reward the obedient without condemning the rebellious; he cannot fill the hearts of the righteous with unmeasured blessings without pouring out his wrath upon the wicked. Indeed, how could a just and holy Being who cannot look upon sin with the least degree of allowance do other than send wrath and vengeance upon those who worship Satan and rebel against Him?" (Millennial Messiah, p. 499.)

Mosiah 16 1010. Immortality] See commentary on 2 Nephi 9:13.

Mosiah 16 1010. Corruption shall put on incorruption] That which is corruptible is that which is subject to disease, decay, or death. In the Resurrection Christ obtained the victory over all that is subject to corruption.
