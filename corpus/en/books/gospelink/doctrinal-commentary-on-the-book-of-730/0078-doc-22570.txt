# The Wicked Will Be Justly Rewarded Mosiah 3:25-27

### The Wicked Will Be Justly Rewarded Mosiah 3:25-27
Robert L. Millet, Joseph Fielding McConkie, Doctrinal Commentary on the Book of Mormon, vol. 2 ( 1988)
 
 

The Wicked Will Be Justly Rewarded

Mosiah 3:25-27

25. And if they be evil they are consigned to an awful view of their own guilt and abominations, which doth cause them to shrink from the presence of the Lord into a state of misery and endless torment, from whence they can no more return; therefore they have drunk damnation to their own souls.

26. Therefore, they have drunk out of the wrath which justice could no more deny unto them than it could deny that Adam should fall because of his partaking of the forbidden fruit; therefore, mercy could have claim on them no more forever.

27. And their torment is as a lake of fire and brimstone, whose flames are unquenchable, and whose smoke ascendeth up forever and ever. Thus hath the Lord commanded me. Amen

SCMosiah 3 25Mosiah 3 26Mosiah 3 2725-27. Justice cannot be denied. Those who have sown the seeds of righteousness will harvest as they have sown. Those who have revelled in the sowing of evil seeds and remained unrepentant must endlessly feast on the bitter fruits of their own planting.

Mosiah 3 2525. An awful view of their own guilt] See commentary on 2 Nephi 9:14, 46.

Mosiah 3 2525. Endless torment] Endless, as used in such texts, refers to the quality or nature of the punishment rather than to its duration. See D&C 19:6-15.

Mosiah 3 2727. Is as] The fact that the phrase "lake of fire and brimstone" was never intended to be more than metaphorical is attested here by the words "is as."
