# Those Who Have Been Taught Are Accountable Mosiah 3:21-24

### Those Who Have Been Taught Are Accountable Mosiah 3:21-24
Robert L. Millet, Joseph Fielding McConkie, Doctrinal Commentary on the Book of Mormon, vol. 2 ( 1988)
 
 

Those Who Have Been Taught Are Accountable

Mosiah 3:21-24

21. And behold, when that time cometh, none shall be found blameless before God, except it be little children, only through repentance and faith on the name of the Lord God Omnipotent.

22. And even at this time, when thou shalt have taught thy people the things which the Lord thy God hath commanded thee, even then are they found no more blameless in the sight of God, only according to the words which I have spoken unto thee.

23. And now I have spoken the words which the Lord God hath commanded me.

24. And thus saith the Lord: They shall stand as a bright testimony against this people, at the judgment day; whereof they shall be judged, every man according to his works, whether they be good, or whether they be evil.

SCMosiah 3 21Mosiah 3 2221-22. It is incumbent upon all who enjoy mortal citizenship to seek after the truths of salvation. Having obtained those truths, men and women are responsible to embrace and live them. To do otherwise is to be in a state of rebellion, to be a captive to the natural man, and thus to become an enemy to God and righteousness.

Mosiah 3 2323. The true servant of God speaks only those words and teaches only that doctrine which he has received from the Lord. He neither adds to nor takes from the gospel message. (See 3 Nephi 11:40.)

Mosiah 3 2424. They shall stand as a bright testimony] It is by the testimony of the word that all are judged. It is the message, not the messenger, that is important. Benjamin received the word from an angel. His people received it from him. Both have the same obligation to embrace and live it. Under the direction of the Spirit, Peter, James, and John can bring none other message than that brought by a humble elder in the Church.

Mosiah 3 2424. According to his works] Judgment is by works, as near countless scriptural texts attest. In that eternal tribunal in which each person must give an accounting for his life, the only admissible evidence for the profession of belief will be the way he lived and the desires of his heart. See Alma 41:3; D&C 18:38; D&C 137:9.
