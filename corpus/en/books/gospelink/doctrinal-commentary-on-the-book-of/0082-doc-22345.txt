1 Ne 21 66. It is a light thing] The charge given by God to his servant the prophet, and through the prophet to his servant, the Church, is not only to gather Israel but to be "a light to the Gentiles." Thus the gathering of Israel as momentous as that is, appears to be a small matter or a "light thing" when compared with the taking of the light of the gospel to the Gentiles.

1 Ne 21 66. A light to the Gentiles] Both Christ and Joseph Smith are spoken of as "a light unto the Gentiles" (Isaiah 42:6; D&C 86:11). Similarly, all who labor to take the light of the gospel to the Gentiles are properly referred to as "a light unto the Gentiles" (D&C 86:11).

1 Ne 21 77. To him whom man despiseth] The promise made to Joseph Smith was that his name would be had for "good and evil among all nations, kindreds, and tongues, or that it should be both good and evil spoken of among all people" (Joseph Smith History 1:33).

1 Ne 21 77. Kings shall see and arise] Again the promise through Joseph Smith is "that the fulness" of the gospel is to be proclaimed "by the weak and the simple unto the ends of the world, and before kings and rulers" (D&C 1:23). To the Nephites, Christ promised that a day would come when "kings shall shut their months at him; ... for that which had not been told them shall they see; and that which they had not heard shall they consider" (3 Nephi 20:45; 3 Nephi 21:8).

1 Ne 21 88. In an acceptable time] "Make haste, and also proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord, and the gospel of salvation" (D&C 93:51) is the Lord's directive to those who go forth to raise the warning voice and testify of the Restoration.

1 Ne 21 88. I will preserve thee, and give thee my servant] Those sustaining the Lord's prophet are promised a blessing of protection. "I the Lord, knowing the calamity which should come upon the inhabitants of the earth, called upon my servant Joseph Smith, Jun., and spoke unto him from heaven, and gave him commandments; and also gave commandments to others, that they should proclaim these things unto the world; and all this that it might be fulfilled, which was written by the prophets-the weak things of the world shall come forth and break down the mighty and strong ones, that man should not counsel his fellow man, neither trust in the arm of flesh" (D&C 1:17-19).

1 Ne 21 88. For a covenant of the people] The revelation cited immediately above continues, "That faith also might increase in the earth; that mine everlasting covenant might be established." (D&C 1:21-22)
