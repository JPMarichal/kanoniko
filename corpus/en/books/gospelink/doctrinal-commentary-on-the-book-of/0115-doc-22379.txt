### Lehi Is a Descendant of Joseph of Egypt 2 Nephi 3:4
Robert L. Millet, Joseph Fielding McConkie, Doctrinal Commentary on the Book of Mormon, vol. 1 (Salt Lake City: Bookcraft, 1987)
 
 

Lehi Is a Descendant of Joseph of Egypt

2 Nephi 3:4

4. For behold, thou art the fruit of my loins; and I am a descendant of Joseph who was carried captive into Egypt. And great were the covenants of the Lord which he made unto Joseph.

2 Ne 3 44. I am a descendant of Joseph] Lehi was a descendant of Manasseh (Alma 10:3); Ishmael was of Ephraim (JD 23:184). Thus the Book of Mormon peoples represented both branches of Joseph's posterity, in fulfillment of prophecy (Genesis 49:22), and their record is referred to as the "stick of Joseph" by Ezekiel (Ezekiel 37:19) and the "stick of Ephraim" in modern revelation (D&C 27:5).
