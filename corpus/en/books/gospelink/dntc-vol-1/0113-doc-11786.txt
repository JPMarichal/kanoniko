# Parable of the Seed Growing by Itself

### Parable of the Seed Growing by Itself
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)
 
 

Parable of the Seed Growing by Itself

SCMark 4 26Mark 4 27Mark 4 28Mark 4 29This parable is addressed primarily to those called to carry the message of salvation to the world. Their obligation is to preach the gospel, to plant the seeds of truth in the hearts of men, and then to leave the event in the hands of Deity. Conversion cannot be forced upon a person anymore than man can require a seed to grow. The harvest of converted souls is not gained by the power of the minister who plants the seed. Paul plants, Apollos waters, but it is God who giveth the increase. (1 Cor. 3:6.)

SCMark 4 26Mark 4 27Mark 4 28Mark 4 29True it is that the soil should be cultivated, that the planted seeds should be watered, fertilized, and given every opportunity for growth. But the ultimate sprouting and growth depends upon a power beyond that of the sower. The earth brings forth of herself; seeds grow by the power of God. And so the minister continues on the Lord's errand, preaching to others, planting more seeds, raising the warning voice, offering salvation to others of the Father's children, and later returns to thrust in his sickle and harvest the original field.

Mark 4 2626. Kingdom of God] Church of Jesus Christ. A man] Any legal administrator sent to preach the gospel. Cast seed into the ground] Preach the gospel.
