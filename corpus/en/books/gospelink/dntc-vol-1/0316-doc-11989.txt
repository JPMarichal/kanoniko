Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)
 
 

Jesus' Friends Weep at His Death

SCMatt 27 55Matt 27 56"Thou shalt live together in love, insomuch that thou shalt weep for the loss of them that die." (D. & C. 42:45.)

Matt 27 55SCMatt. 27:55. Many women... which followed Jesus] Though the apostles and mighty missionaries of Jesus' day are the ones of whom the New Testament makes particular mention, it should be noted that there were many great and faithful women who followed our Lord and who, for their faith and righteousness, shall be exalted to thrones of glory along with the more renown male members of the kingdom.

Luke 23 49Luke 23:49. And all his acquaintance] Jesus' friends were there. No mention is made of any of the apostles save John to whom Jesus gave the care of Mary. But who can feel that any were absent, save Judas whose soul was already with Satan?
