# "He That Reapeth Receiveth Wages"

### "He That Reapeth Receiveth Wages"
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)
 
 

"He That Reapeth Receiveth Wages"

SCJohn 4 31John 4 32John 4 33John 4 34John 4:31-34. Though weary and hungry Jesus' first concern was to plant the seeds of salvation in the hearts of the approaching multitude. Similarly, those sent forth by him to preach the gospel, who have the spirit of their calling, are so imbued with divine zeal that they scarcely take time to eat or rest as they herald the message of salvation to the world.

SCJohn 4 31John 4 32John 4 33John 4 3435-36. Blessings flowing from ministerial service accrue first to the minister sent forth to harvest the souls of his brethren and then to the receptive persons who heed his testimony. "Lo, he that thrusteth in his sickle with his might, the same layeth up in store that he perisheth not, but bringeth salvation to his soul." (D. & C. 4:4.)

SCJohn 4 31John 4 32John 4 33John 4 3437. Jesus is here quoting some ancient scripture, well known to his disciples, but lost from our knowledge. Paul apparently had the same scripture in mind when he said, "I have planted, Apollos watered; but God gave the increase." (1 Cor. 3:6.)

I. V. John 4:40. Jesus' disciples were building on the foundation laid by the ancient prophets; in due course they would reap a harvest of souls from among the Samaritans because that people had some belief in the writings of the ancient prophets. Similarly today the elders of Israel reap a harvest from seeds planted in the writings of the apostles and prophets of old.
