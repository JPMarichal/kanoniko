# "Jesus Is the Christ, the Son of God"

### "Jesus Is the Christ, the Son of God"
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)
 
 

"Jesus Is the Christ, the Son of God"

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25And so endeth the gospels—

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25Those sacred scriptures which tell of the birth, ministry, mission, atoning sacrifice, resurrection, and ascension of the Son of God;

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25Those revealed records which teach with power and conviction the eternal truths which men must believe to gain salvation in God's kingdom;

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25Those true histories of the life of Christ which lead men to love the Lord and to keep his commandments;

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25Those sacred and solemn testimonies which open the door to the receipt of peace in this life and eternal life in the world to come.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25In this holy writ, in these gospel accounts, in these testimonies of the life of our Lord—

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We see Jesus the Almighty, the Creator of all things from the beginning—receiving a tabernacle of clay in the womb of Mary.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We stand by an Infant in a manger and hear heavenly voices hail his birth.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We observe him teaching in the temple and confounding the worldly wise when but twelve years of age.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We watch him in Jordan, immersed under the hands of John, while the heavens open and the personage of the Holy Ghost descends like a dove; and we hear the voice of the Father speak approving words.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We go with him into a wilderness place apart and behold the devil come, tempting, enticing, seeking to lead him from God—directed paths.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We view in wonder and amazement his miracles: He speaks and the blind see; at his touch the deaf hear; he commands and the lame leap, paralytics rise from their beds, lepers are cleansed, and devils desert their ill-gotten abodes.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We rejoice at the miracle of sin-crippled souls being made whole, of disciples who forsake all to follow him, of saints who are born again.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We stand in awe as the elements obey his voice: He walks on the water; at his word storms cease; he curses the fig tree and it withers; water becomes wine when he wills it; a few small fish and a little bread feed thousands because of his word.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We sit with the Lord of life, as a man, in the intimacy of a family circle in Bethany; we weep with him at Lazarus' tomb; we fast and pray at his side when he communes with his Father; we eat and sleep and walk with him down the lanes and in the villages of Palestine; we see him hungry, thirsty, weary, and marvel that a God should seek such mortal experiences.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We drink deeply of his teachings; we hear parables such as never man spake before; we learn what it means to hear one with authority announce his Father's doctrine.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We see him:

In sorrow—weeping for his friends, lamenting over doomed Jerusalem;

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25In compassion forgiving sins, caring for his mother, making men whole spiritually and physically;

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25In anger—cleansing his Father's house, blazing forth with righteous indignation at its desecration;

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25In triumph—entering Jerusalem amid shouts of Hosanna to the Son of David, transfigured before his disciples on the mount, standing in resurrected glory on a mountain in Galilee.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We recline with him in an upper room, apart from the world, and hear some of the greatest sermons of all time as we partake of the emblems of his flesh and blood.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We pray with him in Gethsemane and tremble under the weight of the burden he bore as great drops of blood come from every pore; we bow our heads in shame as Judas plants the traitor's kiss.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We stand at his side before Annas and again before Caiaphas; we go with him to Pilate and to Herod and back to Pilate; we partake of the pain, feel the insults, shudder at the mocking, and are revolted at the gross injustice and mass hysteria which hurl him inescapably toward the cross.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We sorrow with his mother and others at Golgotha as Roman soldiers drive nails into his hands and feet; we shudder as the spear pierces his side, and live with him the moment when he voluntarily gives up his life.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We are in the garden when the angels roll back the stone, when he comes forth in glorious immortality; we walk with him on the Emmaus road; we kneel in the upper room, feel the nail marks in his hands and feet and thrust our hands into his side; and with Thomas we exclaim: "My Lord and my God!"

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We walk to Bethany and there behold, as angels attend, his ascension to be with his Father; and our joy is full for we have seen God with man.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25We see God in him—for we know that God was in Christ manifesting himself to the world so that all men could know those holy beings whom to know is eternal life.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25And now what shall we say more of Christ? Whose Son is he? What works hath he wrought? Who today can testify of these things?

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25Let it now be written once again—and it is the testimony of all the prophets of all the ages—that he is the Son of God, the Only Begotten of the Father, the promised Messiah, the Lord God of Israel, our Redeemer and Savior; that he came into the world to manifest the Father, to reveal anew the gospel, to be the great Exemplar, to work out the infinite and eternal atonement; and that not many days hence he shall come again to reign personally upon the earth and to save and redeem those who love and serve him.

SCJohn 20 30John 20 31John 21 25And now let it also be written, both on earth and in heaven, that this disciple, who has prepared this work, does himself also know of the truth of those things of which the prophets have testified. For these things have been revealed unto him by the Holy Spirit of God, and he therefore testifies that Jesus is Lord of all, the Son of God, through whose name salvation comes.
