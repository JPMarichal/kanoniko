Isaiah's prophecy of the death and burial of Jehovah specifies that he would be "with the rich in his death." (Isa. 53:9.) And now cometh one Joseph of Arimathea, "a rich man," a man of power and influence, "who also himself was Jesus' disciple," who owned a new and elaborate tomb, which then became the sepulchre for the Son of God. And now cometh also Nicodemus, he too being a man of means and position, who brought great quantities of costly ointments and spices to anoint and embalm as befitted a King and as only the wealthy could do.
