### Jesus Travels Without Purse Or Scrip
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)
 
 

Jesus Travels without Purse or Scrip

SCLuke 8 1Luke 8 2Luke 8 3Jesus apparently traveled without purse or scrip, devoted his entire time to the ministry, was not engaged in carpentry or other temporal pursuits, and gained his sustenance from disciples who contributed freely to his upkeep.

Luke 8 1Luke 8:1. Glad tidings of the kingdom] Identically the same message which the latter-day elders carry to the world: The Church and kingdom is again set up; the gospel has been restored; salvation in its fulness is again available through faith, repentance, baptism, obtaining the Holy Ghost, and enduring in righteousness and truth to the end.

Luke 8 2112. Mary called Magdalene] One of the most virtuous and righteous women ever to follow Jesus. So great was her faith and so extensive her good works that she was singled out to stand as the first mortal person to see our Lord after his resurrection. (Mark 16:9; John 20:11-18.) She is not to be confused with the unnamed though repentant sinner who anointed Jesus' feet in the home of Simon. (Luke 7:36-50.) It is one of the basest slanders of all history to suppose that Mary of Magdala was a fallen woman and therefore to use the term Magdalene as a appellation descriptive of reformed prostitutes. (Jesus the Christ, pp. 263-265.)

Luke 8 33. Ministered unto him of their substance] Our Lord obeyed his own law, which is, "They which preach the gospel should live of the gospel." (1 Cor. 9:14.)
