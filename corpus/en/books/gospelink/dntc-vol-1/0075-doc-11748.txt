SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38"Whenever the Lord reveals the gospel, confers priesthood or keys upon men, or sends a message to a people, he acts in accordance with the law of witnesses which he himself ordained. This law is: 'In the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word be established.' (2 Cor. 13:1; Deut. 17:6; 19:15; Matt. 18:15-16; John 8:12-29.)

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38"Never does one man stand alone in carrying the burden of the Lord's message and warnings to the world. In every dispensation, from Adam to the present, two or more witnesses have always joined their testimonies, thus leaving their hearers without excuse in the day of judgment should the testimony be rejected." (Mormon Doctrine, pp. 398-399.)

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38This law was well known to and understood by the Jews. Here Jesus, in the course of his sermon, is showing them that he has conformed to it in fullest particular; and therefore they are bound, at the peril of losing their souls, to accept him as the promised Messiah.

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38Who bears testimony that Jesus is indeed the Christ?

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38(1) Jesus himself, repeatedly, bluntly, plainly, over and over again testified of his own divine Sonship;

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38(2) His Father, by his own voice out of heaven, by personally coming to earth to introduce the Son, and by sending the Holy Ghost to speak to the spirits of the contrite;

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38(3) The Holy Ghost, the Spirit member of the Godhead, whose mission is to bear record of the Father and the Son;

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38(4) The works performed by Jesus in his mortal ministry, including his miracles, teachings, resurrection, and atoning sacrifice which made immortality and eternal life a reality;

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38(5) Prophets and apostles of all ages—Moses, John, Peter, Nephi, Joseph Smith, the elders of Israel, a great host which no man can number; and

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38(6) The scriptures and recorded revelations of the past and present.

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5 38"How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, that publisheth peace; that bringeth good tidings of good, that publisheth salvation; that saith unto Zion, Thy God reigneth!" (Isa. 52:7.)

SCJohn 5 31John 5 32John 5:31-32; I. V. John 5:32-33. Though worded differently, the meaning of these passages is virtually identical. If Jesus was the only witness testifying of his Messiahship, then according to the law of witnesses, his hearers would have been justified in rejecting his testimony.

John 5 32John 5:32. Another that beareth witness] His Father. In the full and final sense no one but the Father could bear the conclusive and absolute testimony of the Son's Messiahship. Anyone else, as was the case with John the Baptist, would have to rely on revelation from the Father, and therefore in a sense would be repeating the witness of Deity.

SCJohn 5 33John 5 34John 5 35John 5 36John 5 37John 5:33-37; I. V. John 5:35. John's testimony was binding; it was sufficient to damn those who rejected it. Among those born of woman there was not a greater prophet than John. Yet his testimony is not to be compared in binding power and force with the witness of the Father or with the works performed by Jesus.

John 5 37John 5:37. Seen his shape] God the Father has a shape; he is a personal being in whose image man is created. He is not a congeries of laws, floating like a fog in the universe; he is not an immaterial, uncreated, spirit essence that is everywhere and nowhere in particular present. He is Man of Holiness, a holy man, whose body has shape, occupies space, travels from place to place, and is in but one place at one time, though his influence may be felt throughout the immensity of space.

John 5 3838. No one can believe in the Father without first believing in the Son; and no one has the Father's word abiding in him who has not first accepted the Son.
