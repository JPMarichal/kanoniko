# Thou Shalt Not Commit Adultery-Physically

### Thou Shalt Not Commit Adultery-Physically
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)
 
 

Thou Shalt Not Commit Adultery—Physically

SCMatt 5 27Matt 5 28There are no grosser personal crimes than adultery, except murder and the commission of the unpardonable sin. (Alma 39:5-6.) Adulterous acts are committed mentally before the physical debauchery ever takes place, and sensual and evil thoughts are in themselves a debasing evil. (Mormon Doctrine, pp. 23-24, 638-639.) "He that looketh on a woman to lust after her, or if any shall commit adultery in their hearts, they shall not have the Spirit, but shall deny the faith and shall fear." (D. & C. 63:16.)
