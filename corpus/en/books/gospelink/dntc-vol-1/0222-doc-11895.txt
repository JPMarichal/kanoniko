# Jesus Seeks and Saves Repentant Persons

### Jesus Seeks and Saves Repentant Persons
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)
 
 

Jesus Seeks and Saves Repentant Persons

SCLuke 19 2Luke 19 3Luke 19 4Luke 19 5Luke 19 6Luke 19 7Luke 19 8Luke 19 9Luke 19 10Ever and always our Lord sought to bring sinners to repentance, to save the lost, to bring the sanctifying power of his gospel to those bound by the chains of sin. Were they despised publicans whose extortions at the customs gate made them odious in the public eye? No matter; through repentance salvation was available to all. Such is the message of his visit in the house of Zaccheus the wealthy Jewish-publican.

Luke 19 5Luke 19:5. I must abide at thy house] No waiting for an invitation here; the Royal Visitor invited himself. 'Come and care for my needs, for the laborer is worthy of his hire.'

Luke 19 88. Restitution for sin is part of true repentance. Zaccheus vowed to right the wrongs of the past and make due amends for all his transgressions. "By this ye may know if a man repenteth of his sins—behold, he will confess them and forsake them." (D. & C. 58: 43.)

Luke 19 99. Salvation come to this house] The gospel message has been received and repentance has taken place. Now by making the covenant of salvation in the waters of baptism, and by continuing in the avowed course of righteousness, the new convert shall gain eternal salvation.

Luke 19 9A son of Abraham] Literally, by birth; spiritually, by virtue of conversion and doing some at least of the works of Abraham.
