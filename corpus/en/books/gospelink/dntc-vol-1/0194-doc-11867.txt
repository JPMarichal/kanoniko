### Jesus Again Heals on the Sabbath
Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)
 
 

Jesus Again Heals on the Sabbath

See John 5:1-16; Matt. 12:1-8, 9-15a.

SCLuke 14 1Luke 14 2Luke 14 3Luke 14 4Luke 14 5Luke 14 6Why did Jesus, again and again and again, seek occasion to exercise his healing powers on the Sabbath? Apparently this was one of his ways of keeping in the headlines. He was teaching the truths of salvation and inviting men to believe in him as the Son of God so that they might become heirs of eternal salvation. His miracles testified of his divine mission, and miraculous works wrought on the Sabbath would be known to more people, discussed in more synagogues, investigated by more truth seekers than those performed at any other time.
