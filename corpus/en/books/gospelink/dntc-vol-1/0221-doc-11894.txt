Minor conflicts in the details of the story (all of which no doubt would be clarified if we had more information) attest to the independent authorship of the synoptic gospels. Matthew says there were two blind men; Mark and Luke mention only one, Mark naming him as Bartimeus, Luke designating him as "a certain blind man." Enroute to Jerusalem, Jesus passed through Jericho. Luke says the healing took place as our Lord's party approached the city; Matthew and Mark acclaim that it occurred as the group departed on their way. Matthew says Jesus touched the eyes of the blind; Mark and Luke record only that he gave a spoken command.
