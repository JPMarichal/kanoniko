# Messenger of Salvation

### Messenger of Salvation
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Messenger of Salvation

See ATONEMENT OF CHRIST, CHRIST, MESSENGER OF THE COVENANT. Christ is the Messenger of Salvation, meaning that he was sent by his Father to bring salvation to men by working out the infinite and eternal atonement. (3 Ne. 27:13-22.)
