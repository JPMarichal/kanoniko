# Mission Presidents

### Mission Presidents
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Mission Presidents

See CHURCH ORGANIZATION, MISSIONARIES, MISSIONS. Presiding officers in missions are mission presidents. They hold the keys of their ministry and are assisted by two counselors (thus forming a mission presidency). Stake and regional mission presidents, as with other officers in the stakes and wards, devote full church service time to their callings, but are also engaged in the normal temporal pursuits. Presidents of foreign missions are called by the First Presidency and devote their full time to the missionary ministry. They direct both the proselyting work and preside over (as a stake president does in a stake) all of the programs of the Church in the missions.
