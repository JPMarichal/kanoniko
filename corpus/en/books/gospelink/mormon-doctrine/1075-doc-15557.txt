# Lemuelites

### Lemuelites
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Lemuelites

See BOOK OF MORMON, NEPHITES AND LAMANITES. That portion of the Lamanites who were lineal descendants of Lemuel the older brother of Nephi were sometimes called Lemuelites. (Jac. 1:13-14; 4 Ne. 37-39.)
