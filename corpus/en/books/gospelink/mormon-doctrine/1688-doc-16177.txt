# Son of the Eternal Father

### Son of the Eternal Father
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Son of the Eternal Father/Son of the Eternal Father

See CHRIST, SON OF GOD. Christ is the Son of the Eternal Father (1 Ne. 11:21; 13:40), a name-title calling attention both to his divine Sonship and to the Eternal nature of his Father.
