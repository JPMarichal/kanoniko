# Ascension Day

### Ascension Day
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Ascension Day

See CHRIST, ASCENSION OF CHRIST, EASTER. Since our Lord ascended in dramatic manner to his Father 40 days after his resurrection, it has become traditional among sectarians to celebrate the Thursday, 40 days after Easter, as ascension day. But since Easter is only the traditional day of his resurrection, it follows that ascension day is not the actual day of his formal return to his Father.
