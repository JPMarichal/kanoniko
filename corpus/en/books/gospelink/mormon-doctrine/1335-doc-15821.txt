# Pantheism

### Pantheism
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Pantheism

See ATHEISM, DEISM, FALSE GODS, GOD. Pantheism is in effect the worship of nature. It assumes that the universe and all the phenomena existing in it, including man and nature, are the ever-changing manifestation of God. It is the doctrine that there is no God except the great forces and laws which are manifested in the existing universe. Such apostate concepts are the end result of the philosophy which worships and serves the creature more than the Creator. (Rom. 1:25.)
