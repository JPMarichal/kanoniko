### Druidism
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Druidism

See GHOSTS, MAGIC, SORCERY, WITCHCRAFT. Druidism is one of the ancient apostate religions which, as far as is known, specialized in magic, conjuration, and the like. It prevailed during the dark ages in Britain, Ireland, and Gaul.
