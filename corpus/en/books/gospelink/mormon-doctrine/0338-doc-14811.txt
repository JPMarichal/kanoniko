### Common Enemy
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Common Enemy

See DEVIL. This designation of Satan describes his position as the general enemy of all mankind. No accountable persons are free from his wiles. (D. & C. 29:47.)
