# Spirit Birth

### Spirit Birth
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Spirit Birth/Spirit Birth

See BORN AGAIN, FATHER IN HEAVEN, PRE-EXISTENCE, SPIRIT BODIES, SPIRIT CHILDREN, SPIRIT ELEMENT. 1. In the literal sense, the expression spirit birth has reference to the birth of the spirit in pre-existence. Spirits are actually born as the offspring of a Heavenly Father, a glorified and exalted Man. They will be born in a future eternity to future exalted beings for whom the family unit continues. (D. & C. 131:1-4; 132:19-24, 29-32.)

2. In a figurative sense, the expression spirit birth has reference to those who are born again of the Spirit, who have become new creatures of the Holy Ghost. (John 3:3-12; Mosiah 27:24-31.)
