# Devotion

### Devotion
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Devotion

See CONSECRATION, OBEDIENCE, RIGHTEOUSNESS, WORSHIP, ZEAL. True devotion consists in a man loving and worshiping Deity with all his heart, and with all his might, mind, and strength. It presupposes that he will keep the commandments, walk uprightly, serve in the Church with an eye single to the glory of God, and put first in his life the things of God's kingdom. True devotion to the end gives an absolute guarantee of eternal salvation.
