# Austere Man

### Austere Man
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Austere Man

See CHRIST, JUDGE OF ALL THE EARTH, JUSTICE. In the parable of the pounds, the slothful servant who did not put his money to usury justified himself because he feared the Lord who was an austere man. In answer the Lord — who is Christ, the Judge of all the earth — agreed that he was an Austere Man, a man who would mete out justice to all men and impose severe penalties for sins of omission as well as those of commission. (Luke 19:12-27.)
