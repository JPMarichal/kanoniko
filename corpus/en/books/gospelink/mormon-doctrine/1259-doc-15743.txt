# Nazarene

### Nazarene
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Nazarene

See CHRIST, JESUS OF NAZARETH. Christ is the Nazarene. Matthew had access to a Messianic prophecy which has not been preserved for our day, which enabled him to write of our Lord: "He came and dwelt in a city called Nazareth: that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophets, He shall be called a Nazarene." (Matt. 2:23.)
