# Christhood

### Christhood
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Christhood

See APOSTASY, CHRIST, JOINT-HEIRS WITH CHRIST. There is only one Christ, because there is only one Son of God, only one Only Begotten, only one person ever born into the world with life in himself with the power to lay down his life and to take it up again. (John 10:11-18.) False doctrines found in some cults to the effect that a number of persons have attained Christhood or the state of being a Christ are direful perversions of the truth. They are the devil's substitute for the true doctrine which teaches that men may become joint-heirs with Christ.
