# Lord of Sabaoth

### Lord of Sabaoth
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Lord of Sabaoth

See CHRIST, LORD, LORD OF HOSTS. Christ is the Lord of Sabaoth. (Isa. 1:9; Rom. 9:29; Jas. 5:4; D. & C. 87:7; 88:2; 98:2.) Sabaoth is a Hebrew word meaning hosts or armies; thus, Jehovah Sabaoth means the Lord of Hosts. Also, as revealed to the Prophet, "The Lord of Sabaoth, . . . is by interpretation the creator of the first day, the beginning and the end." (D. & C. 95:7.)
