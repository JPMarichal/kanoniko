# Hades

### Hades
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

H

Hades

See GEHENNA, HELL, SPIRIT PRISON, TARTARUS. In Greek the word for hell is hades; it is a place of outer darkness where the spirits of the wicked go at death to await the day of their eventual resurrection. Sorrow, anguish, and "the fiery indignation of the wrath of God" attend those cast down to this fate. (Alma 40:13-14; Luke 16:23.)
