# Restorer

### Restorer
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Restorer/Restorer

See CHRIST, ELIAS, RESTORATION OF THE GOSPEL. When Christ came in the meridian of time, he came as the great Restorer, the one who brought back again to earth the fulness of the gospel, for he then gave again the same truths he had given originally to righteous saints of old. Christianity did not begin a mere 2000 years ago; it existed first in eternity, and then had its beginning on earth with Father Adam.
