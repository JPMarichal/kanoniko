# Bastards

### Bastards
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Bastards

See SONS OF GOD. Since a bastard is an illegitimate child, one born out of wedlock, Paul aptly and pointedly uses the term to describe those who are not sons of God, who have not been adopted into the family of God as joint-heirs with Christ. (Heb. 12:5-8.) According to his terminology there are sons on the one hand and bastards on the other. The sons inherit the fulness of the Father's kingdom; the bastards — never having been born of God — are cast out of the eternal family as though they were illegitimate; they become "servants, to minister for those who are worthy of a far more, and an exceeding, and an eternal weight of glory." (D. & C. 132:16.)
