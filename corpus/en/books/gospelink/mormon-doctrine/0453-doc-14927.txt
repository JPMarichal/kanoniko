# Destroyer

### Destroyer
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Destroyer

See ABADDON, APOLLYON, DESTRUCTION OF THE SOUL, DEVIL. This name for Satan signifies that his great labor is to destroy the souls of men. Incident thereto he rejoices in bringing to pass temporal, spiritual, and mental ruin and waste of all degrees. William W. Phelps, in daylight vision, saw the destroyer riding in power upon the face of the Missouri River; and thereupon the Lord revealed to the Prophet the perils to be wrought upon the waters in the last days by the destroyer. (D. & C. 61.)
