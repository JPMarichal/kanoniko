# True Vine

### True Vine
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Husbandman/True Vine

See CHRIST, PERSONIFICATION. Christ is the True Vine, his Father is the Husbandman his prophets are the branches, and the fruit which the branches bear is eternal life for the souls of men. (John 15:1-8.) Thus without the Father there would be no Christ, without Christ there would be no prophets, without prophets there would be no fruit of eternal life.
