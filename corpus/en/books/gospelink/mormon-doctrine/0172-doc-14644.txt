# Bishop

### Bishop
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Bishop

See CHRIST, GOOD SHEPHERD, OVERSEERS. Christ is "the Shepherd and Bishop" of the souls of the saints, by which is meant that he is the Overseer who has led the saints to salvation through his atoning sacrifice. (1 Pet. 2:21-25.)
