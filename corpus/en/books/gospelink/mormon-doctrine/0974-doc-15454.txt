# Jealous

### Jealous
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Jealous

See CHRIST, PERSONIFICATION. To keep ever before his people the exacting and exclusive devotion which be requires of them, and acting on the principle of personification, our Lord uses the word Jealous as one of his names. "For thou shalt worship no other god: for the Lord, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God." (Ex. 34:14.) Among other things, use of this name is a complete refutation of the sectarian heresy (found in the creeds) that Deity is devoid of passions (Ex. 20:5; Deut. 4:24; 5:9; 6:15; Josh. 24:19.)
