# Journals

### Journals
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Journals

See BOOK OF REMEMBRANCE, RECORD KEEPING. Members of the Church should keep accurate family records of births, marriages, deaths, blessings of children, baptisms, ordinations, and items of this sort which pertain to their own welfare and which may be of benefit to posterity. There is no particular obligation to keep a daily journal or diary, but special worthwhile events should be recorded and preserved. (Doctrines of Salvation, vol. 2, pp. 204-206.)
