# Lucifer

### Lucifer
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Lucifer

See DEVIL. This name of Satan means literally lightbearer or shining one. It is thus intended to convey a realization of his high status of prominence and authority in pre-existence before his rebellion and fall. (D. & C. 76:25-27; Isa. 14:12-20; Luke 10:18; 2 Ne. 2:17-18.)
