# Clinic Baptisms

### Clinic Baptisms
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Clinic Baptisms

See APOSTASY, BAPTISM, INFANT BAPTISM. "Baptisms by pouring or sprinkling were exceptional in the early ages of the Christian Church. They were called clinic baptisms, because administered as a rule to the sick, who could not be taken from their beds to be immersed; but they were rare, and were regarded only as quasi-baptisms." (Orson F. Whitney, Saturday Night Thoughts, pp. 252-253.)
