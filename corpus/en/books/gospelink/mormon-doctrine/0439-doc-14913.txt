# Demons

### Demons
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Demons

See DEVIL, DEVILS, WAR IN HEAVEN. Devils are demons, the spirit beings cast out of heaven for rebellion. (Rev. 12:7-9.) "We are surrounded by demons," the wicked Nephites cried, "yea, we are encircled about by the angels of him who hath sought to destroy our souls." (Hela. 13:37.) Demonism is belief in demons; a demoniac is one thought to be possessed of an evil spirit.
