# Omegus

### Omegus
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Omegus

See ALPHA AND OMEGA, ALPHUS, CHRIST. One of the name-titles of Christ is Omegus (D. & C. 95:17), a derivative of the Greek Omega. Use of this title emphasizes the eternal continuance of Christ in his exalted station as the Eternal One forever.
