# Eternal Father

### Eternal Father
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Eternal Father

See CHRIST AS THE FATHER, ETERNAL, ETERNAL GOD, FATHER IN HEAVEN, GOD. On very formal occasions, as in the revealed sacramental prayers, Deity is addressed as, "God, the Eternal Father." (D. & C. 20:77-79) This exalted and sacred name-title combines in one expression the concept of God as an Eternal, exalted Being and his position as the personal Father of the spirits of all men. In the sense in which Christ is called the Everlasting Father (Isa. 9:6), he is also the Eternal Father for he is both Eternal and (in special ways) the Father.
