# Son of the Living God

### Son of the Living God
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Son of the Living God/Son of the Living God

See CHRIST, SON OF GOD. Christ is the Son of the Living God. (D. & C. 42:1; 55:2; 68:25; Morm. 5:14; 9:29; Matt. 16:16.) Use of this appellation bears record of his divine Sonship and of the present, conscious, active existence of his Father. A Living God is one who is alive today, who gives current revelation, and who is just as much interested in the souls of men today as he was anciently — all of which runs counter to the apostate view that the day of revelation has ceased.
