# Galilean

### Galilean
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Galilean

See CHRIST, JESUS OF NAZARETH. Christ is called the Galilean (Matt. 21:11; Luke 23:5-7; John 7:41-42), because he lived in the city of Nazareth in the Roman province of Galilee. His prophetic powers were belittled by the Jews on the specious contention that "out of Galilee ariseth no prophet." (John 7:52.)
