# Peculiar People

### Peculiar People
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Peculiar People

See HERITAGE, MORMONS, SAINTS. In every age the Lord's people are classified by the world as a peculiar people, a designation which the saints accept and in which they rejoice. (Ex. 19:6; Deut. 7:6; 14:1; 1 Pet. 2:5, 9.) They are peculiar, distinctive, unusual, not like any other people, because they have overcome the world. Their doctrines, practices, and whole way of life runs counter to the common course of mankind.

To illustrate: It is peculiar to believe in and receive latter-day revelation; to heal the sick; to raise the dead; to abstain from tea, coffee, tobacco, and liquor; to have visions; to pay one-tenth of one's interest annually as tithing; to marry for eternity; to go on missions; to serve in the ministry without financial pay — and so on through all the beliefs and practices of the true saints.
