# Vainglory

### Vainglory
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

V

Vainglory/Vainglory

See BOASTING, PRIDE, VANITY. Vainglory is an excessive pride in one's own performances or attainments. It is manifest through boasting or undue vaunting of oneself. "Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory," Paul taught, "but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves." (Philip. 2:3.)
