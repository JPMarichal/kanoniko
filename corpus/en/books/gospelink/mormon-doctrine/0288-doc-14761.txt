# Children of Zion

### Children of Zion
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Children of Zion

See CHILDREN OF LIGHT, CHILDREN OF THE KINGDOM, ZION. Those who seek the welfare of Zion, whose desires and strivings are that Zion may be built up and her glory spread through all the earth, are the children of Zion. Since the great latter-day Zion is to be built up by the Church and kingdom of God on earth, it follows that the children of Zion are members of that divine institution. (D. & C. 84:56-58; 101:41, 81-85; 103:35.)
