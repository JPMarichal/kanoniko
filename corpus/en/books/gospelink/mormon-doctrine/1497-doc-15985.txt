# Refiner

### Refiner
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Refiner

See CHRIST, HOLY GHOST, PURIFIER, SECOND COMING OF CHRIST. Christ is the Refiner. (Isa. 48:10; Zech. 13:9; 1 Ne. 20:10.) His mission is to cleanse, purify, and refine the human soul so that it can return to his Father's kingdom in purity, free from dross. (3 Ne. 27:19-21.) His cleansing power "is like a refiner's fire, And he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver" in that great day when he comes to judge the world. (Mal. 3:2-3; 3 Ne. 24:2-3; D. & C. 128:24.)
