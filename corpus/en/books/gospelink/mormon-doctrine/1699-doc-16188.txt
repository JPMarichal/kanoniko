# Soothsayers

### Soothsayers
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Soothsayers/Soothsayers

See DIVINATION, FORTUNE TELLING, MAGIC, NECROMANCY, PROPHETS, SEERS, SORCERY, SPIRITUALISM. A soothsayer is one of Satan's substitutes for a seer or a prophet. His mission is to foretell the future by the power of the evil one. Such false prophets were common anciently in the Eastern nations (Isa. 2:6; Dan. 2:27; 5:11; 2 Ne. 12:6), but they were not permitted in Israel (Josh. 13:22), and the penalty for practicing soothsaying was death. (Lev. 20:6, 27.) When the final latter-day triumph of Israel is achieved the Lord has promised to cut off witchcrafts and soothsayers out of the land. (3 Ne. 21:16; Mic. 5:12.)

Paul, the apostle, during his ministry, had a dramatic experience with a soothsayer. In this instance the false foreteller was a female, a "damsel possessed with a spirit of divination ... which brought her masters much gain by soothsaying." To the great embarrassment of Paul and Silas she followed them calling out: "These men are the servants of the most high God, which shew unto us the way of salvation," thus associating her evil practice with the true powers of God's ministers. Of necessity Paul cast the evil spirit out of her, and her power of soothsaying ceased. (Acts 16:16-18.)
