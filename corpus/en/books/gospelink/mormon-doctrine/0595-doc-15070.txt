# Everlasting Father

### Everlasting Father
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Everlasting Father

See CHRIST, CHRIST AS THE FATHER, ETERNAL, EVERLASTING, EVERLASTING GOD, FATHER IN HEAVEN, GOD. One of Isaiah's great Messianic prophecies names Christ as "The everlasting Father" (Isa. 9:6), an expression having reference both to our Lord's everlasting godhood and to the special senses in which he stands as the Father. Since God the Father is both an everlasting Being and the Father of the spirits of men, he also may properly be called the Everlasting Father.
