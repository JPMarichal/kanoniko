# Madonna

### Madonna
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

M

Madonna

See MARY. Madonna (Latin for my lady) is a designation originally applied by Catholics to Mary the mother of our Lord. It is a title now in general usage among most Christians and commonly refers to a picture or statue of Mary, usually with the infant Jesus.
