# Angel of the Bottomless Pit

### Angel of the Bottomless Pit
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Angel of the Bottomless Pit

See BOTTOMLESS PIT, DEVIL. John used this expressive language, angel of the bottomless pit, to describe Satan, having particular reference to his status as the king of hell, the ruling authority over those cast into the pit which is hell. (Rev. 9:11.)
