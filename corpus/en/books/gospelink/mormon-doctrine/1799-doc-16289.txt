# Tartarus

### Tartarus
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Tartarus/Tartarus

See GEHENNA, HADES, HELL, SHEOL, SPIRIT PRISON. In one instance in the New Testament the classical Greek word tartarus is used to mean hell. Peter used it in reference to the abode of "the angels that sinned" and were cast out in the war in heaven (2 Pet. 2:4), perhaps selecting it rather than the usual hades, sheol or gehenna because these latter usually designate the abode of wicked mortals in the realm following this life.
