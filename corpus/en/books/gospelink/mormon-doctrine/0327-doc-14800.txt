# Clerks

### Clerks
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Clerks

See CHURCH HISTORIAN AND RECORDER, RECORD KEEPING. Since record keeping is such an important part of church operation, qualified and able persons are chosen and set apart to serve as clerks and secretaries in all of the organizations of the Church. Among others, stake clerks assist stake presidencies ward clerks and bishoprics, quorum secretaries carry much of the priesthood load, and auxiliary organization secretaries preserve the records of those units.
