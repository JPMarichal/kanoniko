# Minister of Salvation

### Minister of Salvation
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Minister of Salvation

See CHRIST, MINISTERS, SALVATION. Christ is the Minister of Salvation (Rom. 15:8; Gal. 2:17; Heb. 8), meaning that through his mediation and atoning sacrifice the whole plan and system of salvation becomes operative.
