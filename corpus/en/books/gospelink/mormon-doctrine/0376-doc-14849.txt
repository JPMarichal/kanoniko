# Counselor

### Counselor
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Counselor

See CHRIST, MAN OF COUNSEL. Christ, the Son of Man of Counsel (Moses 7:35), bears the name-title Counselor. Isaiah so designates him in one of the great Messianic prophecies. (Isa. 9:6-7.) The name bears record of his pre-eminent position among men where the exercise of deliberate judgment and prudence are concerned. His counsel is: Come unto me and be saved.
