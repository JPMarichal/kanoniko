# Man of Counsel

### Man of Counsel
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Man of Counsel

See COUNSELOR, FATHER IN HEAVEN, GOD, MAN OF HOLINESS. One of the revealed names of God the Father is Man of Counsel (Moses 7:35), a designation signifying that he is a Man and that the perfection of counsel and direction come from him. In similar manner, he might be called Man of Wisdom, Man of Righteousness, Man of Power, Man of Love, or any other name-title which points attention to the perfection and beauty of a particular one of the godly attributes embodied in his Person.
