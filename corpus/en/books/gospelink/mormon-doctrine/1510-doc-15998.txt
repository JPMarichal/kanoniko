# Remorse

### Remorse
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Remorse/Remorse

See ANGUISH, CONSCIENCE, HELL, REPENTANCE, SORROW. Just as righteousness begets joy, so wickedness breeds remorse. (Alma 29:5.) Hence, the ungodly suffer a prolonged and insistent self-reproach; the painful sting of conscience wraps them in intense mental suffering; a poignant uneasiness is born of their sense of personal guilt. Remorse of conscience results from the violation of divine law. (Alma 42:18.) "A man is his own tormentor and his own condemner." (Teachings, p. 357.) As Alma taught, the wicked shall be "brought before the tribunal of God" with their "souls filled with guilt and remorse, having a remembrance of all" their "guilt, yea, a perfect remembrance of all" their "wickedness, yea, a remembrance that" they "have set at defiance the commandments of God." (Alma 5:18.)
