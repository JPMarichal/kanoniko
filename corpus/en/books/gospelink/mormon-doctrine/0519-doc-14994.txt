# Elders Courts

### Elders Courts
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Elders Courts

See EXCOMMUNICATION. In the missions of the Church where there are no bishoprics or regularly organized high councils to try transgressors against the Lord's laws, the proper church officers form elders courts by appointing several Melchizedek Priesthood holders so to serve. These courts exercise jurisdiction similar to that of high councils in the stakes.
