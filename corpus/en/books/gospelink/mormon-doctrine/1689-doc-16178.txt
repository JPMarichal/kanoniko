# Son of the Everlasting God

### Son of the Everlasting God
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Son of the Everlasting God/Son of the Everlasting God

See CHRIST, SON OF GOD. Christ is the Son of the Everlasting God. (1 Ne. 11:32.) Usage of this designation certifies to his divine Sonship and to the Everlasting pre-eminence and power of the Deity who is his Father.
