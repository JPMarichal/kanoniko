# Messiahship

### Messiahship
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Messiahship

See CHRIST, JOINT-HEIRS WITH CHRIST, MESSIAH. There is only one Messiah (2 Ne. 25:18), and his status as the Anointed One, the promised Savior, is what constitutes his Messiahship. Others do not and cannot attain a status of Messiahship (for they are not born, as Christ was, inheriting life in themselves from an Immortal Parent), though others may become joint-heirs with him in his Father's kingdom.
