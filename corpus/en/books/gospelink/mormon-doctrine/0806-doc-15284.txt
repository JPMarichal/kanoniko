# Harem

### Harem
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Harem

See PLURAL MARRIAGE. Under Moslem practices the wives and female relatives of a Mohammedan are a harem. From the Christian standpoint harems are frequently associated with sensuality and excess — a perspective that no doubt was and is justified in many instances. But harems have no part in the gospel. The Mohammedan practice of plural marriage is an apostate perversion of a true principle once properly in force among their ancestors.
