# Messias

### Messias
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Messias

See CHRIST, MESSIAH. Messias is the Greek form of the Hebrew Messiah, and it is this Greek rendition that is found in the only New Testament passages using this particular name of our Lord. (John 1:41; 4:25.) This Greek-inspired translation is not found elsewhere in the scriptures.
