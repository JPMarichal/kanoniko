# King of Israel

### King of Israel
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

King of Israel

See CHRIST, HOLY ONE OF ISRAEL, KING. Christ is the King of Israel (Isa. 44:6; John 1:49; 12:13), meaning both that he as the Eternal King would be born of the lineage of Israel and that he would rule on the throne of David over the house of Israel forever. (Luke 1:31.33.)
