# Sanctifier

### Sanctifier
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Sanctifier/Sanctifier

See BAPTISM OF FIRE, BORN AGAIN, HOLY GHOST, SANCTIFICATION. The Holy Ghost is the Sanctifier. It is through his power as a Spirit Being that men may be sanctified and washed clean from all sin. (Alma 13:11-12; 3 Ne. 27:19-21; D. & C. 84:33.)
