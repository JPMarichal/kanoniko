# Sincerity

### Sincerity
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Sincerity/Sincerity

See HONESTY. Sincerity is a saving virtue only when it is grounded on truth; gospel sincerity consists in centering one's whole heart and mind on the truth. It is the opposite of double-mindedness, deceit, and hypocrisy. (Philip. 1:10; 1 Pet. 2:1-2.) Sincere adherence to false principles keeps a person from salvation. No matter how honestly and genuinely a person may believe, for instance, that Deity is a three-in-one spirit essence that fills the immensity of space, such belief does not make it so. To gain salvation men must "fear the Lord, and serve him in sincerity and in truth." (Josh. 24:14; Eph. 6:24.)
