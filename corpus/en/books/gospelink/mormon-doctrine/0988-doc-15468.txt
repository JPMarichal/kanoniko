# Judge of All the Earth

### Judge of All the Earth
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Judge of All the Earth

See CHRIST, JUDGES, JUDGMENT, JUDGMENT DAY, JUDGMENTS OF GOD. Christ is the Judge of all the Earth, "For the Father judgeth no man, but hath committed all judgment unto the Son." (John 5:22; Ps. 50:6; Acts 10:42.) His judgment is administered with perfect justice (Isa. 9:7); "with righteousness shall he judge the poor, and reprove with equity for the meek of the earth." (Isa. 11:4.) "As I hear, I judge," he said, "and my judgment is just; because I seek not mine own will, but the will of the Father which hath sent me." (John 5:30.) In the coming day of judgment when He judges whose judgment is just, perfect justice will be administered to all men. "Shall not the Judge of all the earth do right?" (Gen. 18:25; Ps. 94:1-2.)
