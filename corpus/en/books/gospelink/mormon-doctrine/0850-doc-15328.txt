# Holy One of God

### Holy One of God
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Holy One of God

See CHRIST, HOLY, HOLY ONE, HOLY ONE OF ISRAEL, HOLY ONE OF JACOB, HOLY ONE OF ZION. To couple the concept of Christ's holiness with the pointed realization that he came forth from and is the Son of God, he is called the Holy One of God. It was by this name that a devil addressed him, when that unclean spirit was commanded to leave his enforced tenancy in a stolen tabernacle. (Mark 1:24; Luke 4:34.)
