# Carpenter's Son

### Carpenter's Son
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Carpenter's Son

See CHRIST, SON OF GOD, SON OF JOSEPH. In a spirit of unbelief and derision, the citizens of our Lord's own community referred to him as the carpenter's son. (Matt. 13:53-58; Mark 6:1-6; Luke 4:16-29.) Joseph, his foster father, earned his daily bread through carpentry, and Jesus himself was schooled in that trade.
