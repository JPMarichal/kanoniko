# Bigotry

### Bigotry
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Bigotry

See TOLERANCE. Bigotry — that is, blind, obstinate, intolerant, and unreasoning adherence to a particular view — has no place among the true saints. They have the truth; their doctrines will bear investigation; there is no reason to close the mind against light and inquiry where any principle of the gospel is concerned.
