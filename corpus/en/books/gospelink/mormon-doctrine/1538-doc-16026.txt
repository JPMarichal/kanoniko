# Risen Lord

### Risen Lord
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Resurrected Lord/Risen Lord

See CHRIST, LORD, RESURRECTION. To point attention to his pre-eminent position as "the firstfruits of them that slept" (1 Cor. 15:20; Col. 1:18), and to emphasize the reality of his resurrection with a tangible body of flesh and bones (Luke 24:36-48), Christ is often called the Risen Lord. (Luke 24:34.)
