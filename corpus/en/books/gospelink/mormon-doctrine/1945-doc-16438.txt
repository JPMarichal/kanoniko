# Waters of Judah

### Waters of Judah
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Waters of Judah/Waters of Judah

See BAPTISM. Isaiah says that the "house of Jacob" has "come forth out of the waters of Judah" (Isa. 48:1), a statement of great interest to Latter-day Saints in view of the fact that his words as recorded on the brass plates added the phrase, "or out of the waters of baptism" (1 Ne. 20:1), thus preserving in purity an Old Testament text about baptism.
