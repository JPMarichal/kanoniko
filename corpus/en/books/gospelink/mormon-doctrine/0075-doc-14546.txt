# Anointed One

### Anointed One
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Anointed One

See CHRIST, MESSIAH. Literally interpreted the Hebrew Messiah means Anointed One, and accordingly Christ is the Anointed One. (Ps. 2; Acts 4:23-30.) He was the Anointed of the Father to carry the eternal truths of salvation to the living and the dead. (Isa. 61:1-3; Luke 4:16-32; Acts 10:38.)
