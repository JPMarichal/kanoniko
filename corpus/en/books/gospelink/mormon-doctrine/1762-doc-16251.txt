# Stem of Jesse

### Stem of Jesse
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Stem of Jesse/Stem of Jesse

See BRANCH, CHRIST, SON OF DAVID, SON OF GOD. Christ is the Stem of Jesse (Isa. 11:1-5; D. & C. 113:1-2), by which is meant that our Lord came as a descendant of that noble Israelite who sired David the King. (Ruth 4:17.)
