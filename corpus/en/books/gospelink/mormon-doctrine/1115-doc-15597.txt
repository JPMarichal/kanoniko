# Lord of the Harvest

### Lord of the Harvest
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Lord of the Harvest

See CHRIST, LORD, LORD OF THE VINEYARD. Christ is the Lord of the Harvest (Matt. 9:36-38); the elders of Israel are the laborers who thrust in their sickles to reap (D. & C. 4); the harvest is one of souls, souls who shall have eternal life. (Matt. 13:24-30; D. & C. 86:1-7.) "He that reapeth receiveth wages, and gathereth fruit unto life eternal." (John 4:35-36.)
