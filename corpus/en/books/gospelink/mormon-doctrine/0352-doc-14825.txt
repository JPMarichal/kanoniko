# Confirmation

### Confirmation
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Confirmation

See BAPTISM, GIFT OF THE HOLY GHOST, LAYING ON OF HANDS. In the name of Christ and the authority of the Melchizedek Priesthood, baptized persons are confirmed members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. As part of this ordinance of confirmation the gift of the Holy Ghost is bestowed. (D. & C. 20:38-43; 33:15.) It is also proper for the legal administrators performing the ordinance to give expression, as led by the Spirit, to a few brief words of blessing, promise, counsel, and exhortation.
