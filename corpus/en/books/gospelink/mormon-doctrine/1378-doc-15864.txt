# Pilgrims

### Pilgrims
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Pilgrims

See IDUMEA, PLAN OF SALVATION. Literally, a pilgrim is a stranger or traveler passing through a field. Thus men are "strangers and pilgrims on the earth," that is, they are strangers here who are traveling from one eternity to the next, from pre-existence to immortality. (D. & C. 45:11-14; Heb. 11:8-13; 1 Pet. 2:11.) Man's sojourn on earth constitutes the period of his pilgrimage. (Gen. 47:9; Ps. 119:54.)
