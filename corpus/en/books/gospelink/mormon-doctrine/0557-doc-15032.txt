# Epistles of Commendation

### Epistles of Commendation
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Epistles of Commendation

See EPISTLES, RECOMMENDS. It appears from 2 Cor. 3:1 that the practice prevailed among the primitive saints of introducing faithful members of the Church from one group of saints to another by means of epistles of commendation or letters of commendation. That is, the saints were commended, introduced, or recommended to the various local churches by these written certifications. These would correspond to "recommends" in modern times.
