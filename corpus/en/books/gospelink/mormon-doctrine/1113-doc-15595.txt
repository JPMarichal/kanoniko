# Lord of Lords

### Lord of Lords
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Lord of Lords

See CHRIST, EXALTATION, GOD, GODHOOD, GOD OF GODS, KING OF KINGS, LORD, PLURALITY OF GODS. Both the Father and the Son are properly known by the title Lord of Lords, a designation that means literally what it says. (Deut. 10:12-22; 1 Tim. 6: 5; Rev. 17:14; 19:16.) The purpose of this name is not simply to reveal that Christ, for instance, is the Lord of mortal persons who are ranked as lords, but rather it is to show that he is Lord and God to others who themselves also are Lords and Gods in their own right. Thus Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob (among others) "have entered into their exaltation, according to the promises, and sit upon thrones and are not angels but are gods." (D. & C. 132:29, 37.) Since exalted beings themselves become Lord and Gods, and since they still stand in subjection to Christ their Lord and their God, it follows that he is a Lord of Lords. Whatever is true of him is also true of his Father, and in due course it will be true of all exalted beings.
