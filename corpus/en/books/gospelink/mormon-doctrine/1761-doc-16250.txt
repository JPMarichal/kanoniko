# Stealing

### Stealing
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Larceny/Stealing

See HONESTY, ROBBERY, TEN COMMANDMENTS, WICKEDNESS. "Thou shalt not steal; and he that stealeth and will not repent shall be cast out." (D. & C. 42:20; 59:6; Ex. 20:15; Matt. 19:18; 2 Ne. 26:32.) That is, the unrepentant thief is to be excommunicated from the Church.

Thieves "shall be delivered up unto the law of the land." (D. & C. 42:85.) Unless they repent, they shall not inherit the kingdom of God. (1 Cor. 6:9-11.) Rather, they shall be thrust down to hell to suffer the wrath of God until the resurrection of damnation, at which time they shall come forth to receive the just reward for their ungodly conduct.
