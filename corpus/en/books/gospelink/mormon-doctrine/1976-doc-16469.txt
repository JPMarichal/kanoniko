# Wizards

### Wizards
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Wizards/Wizards

See DIVINATION, MAGIC, SORCERY, SPIRITUALISM, WITCHCRAFT. A wizard is a sorcerer, one who has magical skills, who engages in witchcraft, who communes with familiar spirits. The practice of wizardry is an abomination (Lev. 19:31; Deut. 18:9-12), and in ancient Israel the penalty therefor was death. (Lev. 20:6, 27.)
