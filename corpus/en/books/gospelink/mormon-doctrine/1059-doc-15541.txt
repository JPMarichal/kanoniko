# Lawgiver

### Lawgiver
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Lawgiver

See CHRIST, GOSPEL, JUDGE OF ALL THE EARTH, KING, LAW OF MOSES, RULER, SHILOH. Christ is the Lawgiver (D. & C. 38:22; 45:59; 64:13; Gen. 49:10; Jas. 4:12); "he hath given a law unto all things." (D. & C. 88:42.) "I am the law," he said. (3 Ne. 15:9.) "The Lord is our judge, the Lord is our lawgiver, the Lord is our king; he will save us." (Isa. 33:22.)
