# Revelator

### Revelator
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Revelator/Revelator

See HOLY GHOST, REVELATION, REVELATORS, TESTATOR, TESTIMONY. "The Holy Ghost is a Revelator," the Prophet said. "No man can receive the Holy Ghost without receiving revelations." (Teachings, p. 328.) Those who enjoy the companionship of the Holy Ghost have the spirit of revelation. (D. & C. 8:2-3.) A testimony of the gospel comes from the Holy Ghost. "By the power of the Holy Ghost ye may know the truth of all things." (Moro. 10:5.)
