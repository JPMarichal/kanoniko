# Vanity

### Vanity
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Vanity/Vanity

See BOASTING, PRIDE, VAINGLORY. As commonly used today, vanity means shallow pride. Biblical passages relative to vanity, however, have been translated from original words having the meanings of emptiness, fruitlessness, and worthlessness. The Hebrew word translated vanity in David's statement, "How long will ye love vanity" (Ps. 4:2), for instance, is one which indicates a sense of empty failure. This same Biblical meaning of vanity seems to have been carried over into the modern revelations. (D. & C. 20:5; 84:54-55.)
