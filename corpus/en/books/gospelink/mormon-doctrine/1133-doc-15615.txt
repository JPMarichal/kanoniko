# Loving-Kindness

### Loving-Kindness
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Loving-Kindness

See KINDNESS, LOVE, MERCY. Old Testament reference is found to the loving-kindness of the Lord. The connotation is one of steadfast love; it signifies the kindly, merciful, and loving relationship that exists between God and those who keep his commandments. For instance, the saints who abide the day of the Second Coming "shall mention the loving kindness of their Lord, and all that he has bestowed upon them according to his goodness, and according to his loving kindness, forever and ever." (D. & C 133:52-53; Isa. 63:7-9.)
