# Religious Syncretism

### Religious Syncretism
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Religious Syncretism/Religious Syncretism

See APOSTASY, GNOSTICISM. With the exception of the restored gospel, every system of religion in the world today is born of religious syncretism. That is, all existing religious systems have resulted from the mingling of previously existing systems; they have been created by the reconciliation and union of a host of different principles, practices, and beliefs.

Revealed religion is the only pure and perfect system of worship. But after the Lord has spoken there are always those who take pleasure in compromising the truth with the ways of the world. Modern Christian sects espouse religious systems that have grown out of revealed Christianity as such was diluted, intermixed and amalgamated with such worldly philosophies as gnosticism. These very philosophies had themselves been born of previously existing systems as these had intermingled with prior revelations of the gospel. Much of this syncretism has been traced and outlined by Milton R. Hunter in The Gospel Through the Ages.
