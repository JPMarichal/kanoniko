# Alphus

### Alphus
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Alphus

See ALPHA AND OMEGA, CHRIST, OMEGUS. One of the name-titles of Christ is Alphus (D. & C. 95:17), a derivative of the Greek Alpha. Use of this title emphasizes our Lord's high status of godhood in pre-existence; he was God from eternity; by diligence and obedience, while yet a Spirit Being, he became "like unto God" the Father. (Abra. 3:22-24.)
