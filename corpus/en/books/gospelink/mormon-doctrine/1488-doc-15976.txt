# Reconciler

### Reconciler
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Reconciler

See ADVOCATE, ATONEMENT OF CHRIST, CHRIST, EXPIATOR, INTERCESSOR, MEDIATOR, PROPITIATOR. Christ is the great Reconciler in that through his atoning sacrifice power is given men to become reconciled to God, to be absolved from all guilt, and to be in harmony with his mind and will.
