# Master

### Master
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Master

See CHRIST, RABBI. Christ is the Master, meaning he stood as a teacher, ruler, and commander. "Ye call me Master and Lord: and ye say well," he said, "for so I am." (John 13:13.) After his ascension into heaven, he was called by Paul, the Master in heaven. (Eph. 6:9; Col. 4:1.)
