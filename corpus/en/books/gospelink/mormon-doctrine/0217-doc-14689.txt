# Bread of Life

### Bread of Life
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Bread of Life

See CHRIST, LIGHT OF CHRIST, MANNA, SACRAMENT, SYMBOLISMS. Christ is the Bread of Life. Just as manna was showered down as bread from heaven to save ancient Israel from starvation and temporal death, so Christ came down from heaven to give living bread and living water to all men so that they might gain everlasting life. (John 4:10-38; 6:30-65.)
