# Jesus of Nazareth

### Jesus of Nazareth
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Jesus of Nazareth

See CHRIST, GALILEAN, JESUS, NAZARENE. Because he lived in Nazareth of Galilee, Christ was referred to by friend and foe alike as Jesus of Nazareth. (Matt. 21:11; Mark 1:24; Luke 18:37; 24:19; Acts 3:6.)
