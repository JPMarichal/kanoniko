# Selfishness

### Selfishness
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Selfishness/Selfishness

See GREEDINESS. Selfishness consists in caring unduly or supremely for oneself; it is one of the lusts of the flesh which must be overcome by those who gain salvation. A selfish person clings to his own comfort, advantage, or position at the expense of others. Men are commanded to repent of their pride and selfishness. (D. & C. 56:8.) In practice the way to do this is to serve in the Church and make generous financial contributions to sustain its programs.
