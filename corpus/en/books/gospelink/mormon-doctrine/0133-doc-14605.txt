# Backbiting

### Backbiting
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Backbiting

See GOSSIPING. To backbite is to slander one who is not present. It is a wicked, evil practice, hated of God and fostered by Satan. (Ps. 15:3; Rom. 1:30; 2 Cor. 12:20.) It is the express appointment of the teachers in the Aaronic Priesthood — as part of their mission to do home teaching — to see that there is no backbiting in the Church. (D. & C. 20:54.)
