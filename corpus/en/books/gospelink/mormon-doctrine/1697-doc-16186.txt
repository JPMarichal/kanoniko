# Sons of Moses and Aaron

### Sons of Moses and Aaron
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Sons of Moses And Aaron/Sons of Moses and Aaron

See AARON, ADOPTION, MELCHIZEDEK PRIESTHOOD, MOSES. Faithful holders of the Melchizedek Priesthood, no matter what their natural lineage, become by adoption the sons of Moses and Aaron. (D. & C. 84:6, 31-34.)
