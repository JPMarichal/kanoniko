# Captain of Salvation

### Captain of Salvation
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Captain of Salvation

See ATONEMENT OF CHRIST, AUTHOR OF SALVATION, CAPTAIN OF THE LORD'S HOST, CHRIST, SALVATION. Christ is the Captain of Salvation (Heb. 2:10), meaning that he is the leader, chief officer, and agent who made salvation available to his brethren through his atoning sacrifice.
