# Ruler

### Ruler
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Ruler/Ruler

See CHRIST, GOVERNOR, KING, LAWGIVER, SHILOH. Christ is the Ruler, meaning that he has the natural and legal right to rule and reign over the earth and all flesh on its face. (D. & C. 38:21-22; 41:4; 58:20.) It is only because of apostasy and rebellion that he is kept from ruling in the hearts of men in the present state of things. During the millennium he will reign personally upon the earth. (Tenth Article of Faith.)
