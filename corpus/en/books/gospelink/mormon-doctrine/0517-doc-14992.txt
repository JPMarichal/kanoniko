# Elder Brother

### Elder Brother
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Elder Brother

See CHRIST, FATHER IN HEAVEN, FIRSTBORN, PRE-EXISTENCE. Christ is literally our Elder Brother. Since all men are the personal spirit children of the Father, and since Christ was the Firstborn spirit offspring, it follows that he is the Elder Brother of all men.
