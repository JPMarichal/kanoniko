# Bishops Storehouses

### Bishops Storehouses
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Bishops Storehouses

See BISHOPS, CONSECRATION, UNITED ORDER, CHURCH WELFARE PLAN. To help bishops discharge their obligation to care for the temporal needs of the poor the Lord has directed the building, stocking, maintenance, and use of bishops storehouses. (D. & C. 42:30-36; 51:9-20; 58:24, 37; 70; 72:9-16; 78:3-7; 82:15-24; 83; 90:22-23; 101:96.) These are operated as part of the Church Welfare Plan.
