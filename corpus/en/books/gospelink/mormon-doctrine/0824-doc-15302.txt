# Heredity

### Heredity
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Heredity

See ENVIRONMENT, PRE-EXISTENCE. In his eternal providences and omniscient wisdom, an Almighty Deity has ordained laws whereby the characteristics and qualities of parents are transmitted to their descendants. From a mortal viewpoint this is called heredity. From an eternal perspective it is the Lord's way of sending through particular lineages the very spirits who have developed the mental, spiritual, and other qualifications entitling them so to be born. The complexities and intricacies of the laws prevailing in this field are beyond finite capacity to comprehend.
