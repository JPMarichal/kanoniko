# Abaddon

### Abaddon
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Abaddon

See APOLLYON, DESTROYER, DEVIL, HELL. This expression, of Hebrew origin, was used by John as a name for Satan. (Rev. 9:11.) It is the same as the Greek Apollyon and means literally the Destroyer. It was used anciently to mean hell, as shown in the marginal reading of Psalm 88:11.
