# Redeemer

### Redeemer
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Redeemer

See CHRIST, DELIVERER, REDEMPTION, SAVIOR. Christ is the Redeemer (Isa. 41:14; 54:5; Alma 37:9; 3 Ne. 10:10; D. & C. 15:1; 18:47) because it is he who worked out the redemption, which ransoms and redeems men from the effects of the fall of Adam. He is so named nearly a score of times in both the Old Testament and the Doctrine and Covenants and more than 40 times in the Book of Mormon.
