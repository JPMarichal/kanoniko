# Terrestrial Law

### Terrestrial Law
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Terrestrial Law/Terrestrial Law

See TERRESTRIAL BODIES, TERRESTRIAL GLORY, TERRESTRIAL KINGDOM, SALVATION. To attain a terrestrial kingdom it is necessary to abide a terrestrial law, which consists in living an upright, honorable life but one that does not conform to the standards whereby the human soul is sanctified by the Spirit. (D. & C. 76:71-80; 88:16-32.)
