# Rock of Heaven

### Rock of Heaven
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Rock/Rock of Heaven

See CHRIST, LORD OF HOSTS, STONE OF ISRAEL. Christ is the Rock (Deut. 32:3-4, 18, 30-31; 1 Cor. 10:1-4), or the Rock of Heaven. (Moses 7:53.) Such name-titles carry a connotation of strength and stability (1 Sam. 2:2), as for instance when David exulted: "The Lord is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; The God of my rock, in him will I trust: he is my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower, and my refuge, my saviour." (2 Sam. 22:1-4; 23:3; Psa. 18:1-3, 31.)

One of Isaiah's great Messianic prophecies was that the promised Messiah would be "for a stone of stumbling and for a rock of offence to both the houses of Israel, for a gin and for a snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem. And many among them shall stumble, and fall, and be broken, and be snared, and be taken." (Isa. 8:14-15.) Both Paul (Ram. 9:33) and Peter (1 Pet. 2:7-8) record the fulfillment of this prophecy.
