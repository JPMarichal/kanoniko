# Yahweh

### Yahweh
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Y

Jahweh/Yahweh

See CHRIST JAH, JEHOVAH, LORD, TETRAGRAMMATON. Yahweh is believed to be the most accurate reconstruction of the Hebrew word meaning Jehovah (who is Christ). Other reconstructions of this name of Deity are: Yahwe, Yahve, Yahveh, Jahweh, and Jahveh.
