# Epiphany

### Epiphany
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Epiphany

See APOSTASY, CHRISTMAS. According to tradition it is supposed that the wise men from the east came to Jerusalem and found the newly born Son of God 12 days after his birth. Hence portions of the sectarian world celebrate Epiphany (Twelfthtide or Twelfth-day) on January 6. This is the traditional and customary end to the Christmas season.

It appears from Matthew's account however, that in reality the wise men came two or three years after the birth of our Lord. It was a "young child," not a baby they were seeking; he was found in a "house, not a manger; and Herod "sent forth, and slew all the children that were in Bethlehem and in all the coasts thereof, from two years old and under, according to the time which he had diligently enquired of the wise men." (Matt. 2.) Those who had not reached the third anniversary of their births were "two years old and under." The true Church, of course, pays no heed to Epiphany and conducts no celebration thereon.
