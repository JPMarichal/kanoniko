# Despair

### Despair
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Despair

See CHEERFULNESS, HOPE, INIQUITY, REJOICING. Despair is a feeling of hopelessness and futility, a feeling that there is no chance for continued progression or forgiveness, or salvation. The Spirit of the Lord sheds forth cheerfulness and hope; the spirit of the devil casts men into despair and despondency. "And if ye have no hope ye must needs be in despair; and despair cometh because of iniquity." (Moro. 10:22.) Though the saints may be perplexed they are not in despair. (2 Cor. 4:8.)
