# Sons of Belial

### Sons of Belial
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Children of Belial/Sons of Belial

See BELIAL, DEVIL, FALSE GODS. In ancient times it was common for the chosen people to refer to those among them who turned to the worship of false gods and the practices of iniquity as sons or children of Belial. They were sons of Satan in the sense that they followed his enticements and subjected themselves to his will. Our Lord spoke similarly to certain wicked Jews when he said: "Ye are of your father the devil, and the lusts of your father ye will do." (John 8:44.) Moses gave pointed instructions relative to the temporal destruction of those led away by the enticements of the children of Belial. (Deut. 13.)
