# Door of the Sheep

### Door of the Sheep
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Door of the Sheep

See CHRIST, GOOD SHEPHERD, KEEPER OF THE GATE, PERSONIFICATION; WAY, TRUTH, AND LIFE. Christ is the door or the Door of the Sheep. By so designating himself, he teaches that no one can enter his sheepfold either in time or in eternity without his approval He personally stands ever on guard to reject the ungodly. (2 Ne. 9:41-43.) He is the Way. "By me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and out, and find pasture," he said. (John 10:7-9.)
