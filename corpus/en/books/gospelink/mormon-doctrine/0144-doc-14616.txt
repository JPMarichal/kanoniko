# Beelzebub

### Beelzebub
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Beelzebub

See BAAL (BAALIM), DEVIL. This name for Satan signifies his position as the prince or chief of the devils. It is the same name (Baalzebub) as was given to an ancient heathen god. (2 Kings 1:3.) In their rebellion against light, the ancient Jews applied the name Beelzebub to Christ (Matt. 10:25), and also said that he cast out devils by the power of Beelzebub. (Matt. 12:22-30.)
