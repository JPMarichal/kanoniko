# Our Lord

### Our Lord
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Our Lord

See CHRIST, LORD, REVERENCE. One of the appropriate and reverential titles properly applied to Christ is Our Lord. This terminology helps avoid the too frequent repetition of the name of Deity, and it signifies our personal acceptance of him as Lord and God.
