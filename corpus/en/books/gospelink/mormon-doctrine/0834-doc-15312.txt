# High Priest

### High Priest
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

High Priest

See AUTHOR OF SALVATION, CHRIST, HIGH PRIESTS. "Christ is the Great High Priest." (Teachings, p. 158; Heb. 3:1.) "Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek," are the words of one of the great Messianic prophecies. (Ps. 110:4; Heb. 5:5-11; 7:14-17.) The designation of our Lord as the great High Priest points to his position as the chief minister of salvation, as the author of salvation in the sense of having worked out the infinite and eternal atonement. (Heb. 2:1-18; 5.)
