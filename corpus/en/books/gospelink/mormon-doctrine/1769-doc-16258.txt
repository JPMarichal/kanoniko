# Stone of Israel

### Stone of Israel
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Stone of Israel/Stone of Israel

See CHRIST, ROCK OF HEAVEN, SON OF GOD. Christ is the Stone of Israel. (Gen. 49:24.) "I am the good shepherd, and the stone of Israel. He that buildeth upon this rock shall never fall." (D. & C. 50:44.) Christ is thus the stone or foundation upon which all men must build. Of him the psalmist prophesied: "The stone which the builders refused is become the head stone of the corner." (Ps. 118:22; Matt. 21:42; Mark 12:10-11; Luke 20:17-18.) Peter used this truth to teach that the saints "as lively stones" should build "a spiritual house," with Christ, the Stone of Israel, as the foundation. (1 Pet. 2:1-9.)
