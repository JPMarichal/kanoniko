# Innocence

### Innocence
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Innocence

See BAPTISM, CLEANLINESS, SALVATION OF CHILDREN, YEARS OF ACCOUNTABILITY. In the gospel sense, innocence is the state of purity and freedom from sin which men must possess to gain salvation in the kingdom of God. (Alma 11:37.) Little children live in a state of perfect innocence and consequently are saved without works on their part. "Every spirit of man was innocent in the beginning; and God having redeemed man from the fall, men became again, in their infant state, innocent before God." (D. & C. 93:38.) But "those who are accountable and capable of committing sin, ... they must repent and be baptized, and humble themselves as their little children, and they shall all be saved with their little children." (Moro. 8:10.)
