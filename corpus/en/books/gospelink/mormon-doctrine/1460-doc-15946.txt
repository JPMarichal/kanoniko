# Pulpits

### Pulpits
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Pulpits

See MEETINGHOUSES, TEMPLES. Raised desks or stands called pulpits are commonly erected in church buildings to be used as places from which sermons are delivered. In the Kirtland and some other temples built in this dispensation pulpits have been placed on one side of the assembly rooms for officials of the Melchizedek Priesthood and on the other for officers of the Aaronic Priesthood.
