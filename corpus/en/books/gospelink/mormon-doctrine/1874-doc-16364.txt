# Traveling Presiding High Council

### Traveling Presiding High Council
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Presiding High Council/Traveling Presiding High Council

See APOSTLES, APOSTOLIC SUCCESSION, FIRST PRESIDENCY, HIGH COUNCIL. "The Twelve are a Traveling Presiding High Council, to officiate in the name of the Lord, under the direction of the Presidency of the Church, agreeable to the institution of heaven; to build up the church, and regulate all the affairs of the same in all nations, first unto the Gentiles and secondly unto the Jews." (D. & C. 107:33.)
