# Lion of the Tribe of Judah

### Lion of the Tribe of Judah
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Lion of the Tribe of Judah

See CHRIST, SHILOH, SON OF DAVID, STEM OF JESSE. Christ is the Lion of the Tribe of Judah. (Rev. 5:5.) When Father Jacob gave Judah his patriarchal blessing, Judah was likened both to a lion's whelp and to an old lion and was promised that the sceptre should not depart from his descendants until the coming of Christ. (Gen. 49:8-12.) accordingly, to denominate our Lord as the Lion of the Tribe of Judah is to point to his position as a descendant of Judah, to is membership in that tribe from which kings were chosen to reign, and also to show his status as the most pre-eminent of all that house, as the one who bore the banner of the tribe so to speak.
