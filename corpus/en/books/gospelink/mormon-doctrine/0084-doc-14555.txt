# Apollyon

### Apollyon
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Apollyon

See ABADDON, DESTROYER, DEVIL. This is one of the names of Satan. It is of Greek origin and means literally the Destroyer. Abaddon is the Hebrew equivalent. (Rev. 9:11.)
