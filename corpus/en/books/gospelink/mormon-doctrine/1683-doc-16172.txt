# Son of Joseph

### Son of Joseph
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Son of Joseph/Son of Joseph

See CARPENTER'S SON, CHRIST, SON OF GOD, SON OF MAN, SON OF MARY. When Jesus began his active ministry he was "about thirty years of age, being (as was supposed) the son of Joseph." (Luke 3:23.) That is, he was considered by the people to be "the son of Joseph." (John 1:45.) Though Joseph did sire sons, they were only the half-brothers of our Lord, and the husband of Mary was in reality only the foster and not the natural parent of Jesus. (Matt. 12:46; 13:55; Gal. 1:19.)
