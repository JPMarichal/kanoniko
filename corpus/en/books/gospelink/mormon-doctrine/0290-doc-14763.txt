# Chosen One

### Chosen One
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Chosen One

See CHRIST, ELECTION OF GRACE. Christ is the Chosen One. (Moses 4:2; 7:39.) By this is meant that from the beginning, because of devotion, obedience, and righteousness, he was chosen by the Father to play the chief part in the great creative and redemptive enterprises of Deity.
