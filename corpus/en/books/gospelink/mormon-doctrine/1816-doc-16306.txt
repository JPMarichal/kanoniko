# Tempter

### Tempter
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Tempter/Tempter

See DEVIL. Lucifer is the tempter. Such name signifies his aim of leading all men to do evil, to forsake righteousness, and to follow that carnal existence which will take them to hell.
