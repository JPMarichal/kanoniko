# Clergy

### Clergy
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Clergy

See MINISTERIAL TITLES, MINISTERS. In the sectarian world, the clergy is the whole body of so-called ordained ministers; those ordained to the ministry (as they suppose) are called clergymen. The ministry involved differs so radically from the Lord's true ministry that the terms clergy and clergymen are not ordinarily applied by the true Church to its own ministers.
