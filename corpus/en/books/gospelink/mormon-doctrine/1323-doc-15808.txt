# Orthodoxy

### Orthodoxy
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Orthodoxy

See BELIEF, BELIEVING BLOOD, FAITH, HERESY, TRUTH. In the true sense, orthodoxy consists in believing that which is in harmony with the scriptures. Thus gospel orthodoxy requires belief in the truths of salvation as they have been revealed in this dispensation through Joseph Smith, and as they are understood and interpreted by the living oracles who wear the mantle of the Prophet. Orthodoxy is the opposite of heterodoxy or of believing heretical doctrines.

There are degrees of orthodoxy exhibited by members of the Church. Those who believe the whole law — and who believe it sanely, sensibly, realistically, according to its true meaning and purport — are completely orthodox. Those who intermingle gospel truths with the educational or philosophical theories of the world have not yet attained perfect orthodoxy, the orthodoxy which is essential to salvation.
