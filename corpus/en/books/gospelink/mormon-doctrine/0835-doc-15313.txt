# High Priestesses

### High Priestesses
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

High Priestesses

See HIGH PRIESTS, PRIESTESSES, PRIESTHOOD. There is no such thing in the true Church as a high priestess. Where this office is found in a church, it is an unauthorized and apostate innovation. Women do not hold the priesthood.
