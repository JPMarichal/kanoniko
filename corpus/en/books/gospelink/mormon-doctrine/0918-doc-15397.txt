# Incantations

### Incantations
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Incantations

See EXORCISM, MAGIC, SORCERY, WITCHCRAFT. By incantations is meant the use of spells or verbal charms, which are chanted, spoken, or sung, as part of the ritual of magic. They are in effect the formula by which devils are supposed to be exorcised and by which sorceries are practiced. (Rev. 18:23.)
