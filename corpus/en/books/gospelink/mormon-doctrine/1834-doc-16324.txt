# Theism

### Theism
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Theism/Theism

See ATHEISM, DEISM, GOD, HENOTHEISM, MONOTHEISM, POLYTHEISM. Theism is simply the belief in the existence of a God or gods; it carries a connotation of a belief in monotheism in that it places one god as supreme in the universe.
