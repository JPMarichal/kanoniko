# Equity

### Equity
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Equity

See EQUALITY, JUSTICE, MERCY, RIGHTEOUSNESS. Equity is the principle which tempers the harshness of justice. Equity dictates that the law shall be administered according to its spirit and not merely its letter. (D. & C. 102:16; 134:3; Hela. 3:20; 3 Ne. 6:4.) It is an attribute of Deity (Ps. 98:9; 99:4; Alma 9:26), the companion of justice and mercy, and the friend of righteousness. One of the great Messianic prophecies says of our Lord: "With righteousness shall he judge the poor, and reprove with equity for the meek of the earth." (Isa. 11:4.)
