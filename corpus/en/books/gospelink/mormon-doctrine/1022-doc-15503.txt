# King of Glory

### King of Glory
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

King of Glory

See CHRIST, KING, LORD OF GLORY. Christ is the King of Glory (Ps. 24), a title signifying his status as King and Ruler and also the transcendent glory that attends him and his marvelous works.
