# God of Truth

### God of Truth
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

God of Truth

See CHRIST, PERSONIFICATION, TRUTH; WAY, TRUTH, AND LIFE. Christ is a God of Truth (Ex. 34:6; Deut. 32:4; Ps. 31:5), meaning he is the embodiment and personification of truth. In the same sense he is the God of grace, mercy, love, righteousness, charity, integrity, and all of the attributes of godliness.
