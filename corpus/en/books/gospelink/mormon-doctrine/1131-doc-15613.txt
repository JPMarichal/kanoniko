# Lotteries

### Lotteries
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Lotteries

See GAMBLING, RAFFLES. Lotteries are one of the most flagrant forms of gambling. Ordinarily they are organized enterprises in which lots or chances are sold with prize winners being chosen by drawings. The fact that lotteries are often sponsored by governments social and civic organizations and even some churches merely shows how far these organizations have departed from true gospel standards.
