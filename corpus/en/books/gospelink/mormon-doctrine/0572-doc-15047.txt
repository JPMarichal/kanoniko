# Eternal God

### Eternal God
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Eternal God

See CHRIST, ETERNAL, ETERNAL FATHER, ETERNITY TO ETERNITY, EVERLASTING FATHER, EVERLASTING GOD, EVERLASTING TO EVERLASTING, GOD. Both the Father and the Son carry the exalted name-title, Eternal God. Both are exalted Beings and as such are Eternal; both are from everlasting to everlasting, with all that this phrase connotes; both are beyond finite comprehension in power, dominion, godly attributes and eternal glory. (D. & C. 121:32.) By their eternal grace men have been created, redeemed, and placed as possible heirs of all things.
