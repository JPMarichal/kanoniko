# Integrity

### Integrity
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Integrity

See HONESTY, JUSTICE, RIGHTEOUSNESS. The complete development of man's moral character in conformity with principles of justice and uprightness is termed integrity. A man of integrity is sound, incorruptible, and particularly strict about fulfilling the trusts reposed in him by others. The highest manifestation of integrity is exhibited by those who conform their conduct to the terms of those gospel covenants and promises which they have made. Integrity goes hand in hand with uprightness and righteousness, and the Lord loves those who have integrity of heart. (D. & C. 124:15, 20.) "The integrity of the upright shall guide them" (Prov. 11:3), and "The just man walketh in his integrity: his children are blessed after him." (Prov. 20:7.)
