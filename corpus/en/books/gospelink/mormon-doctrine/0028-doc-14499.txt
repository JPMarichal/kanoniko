### Adonai
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Adonai

See CHRIST, LORD. In the Old Testament record, Christ is frequently referred to under the Hebrew word Adonai — a term having reference to God, meaning literally my Lord, but usually translated as Lord.
