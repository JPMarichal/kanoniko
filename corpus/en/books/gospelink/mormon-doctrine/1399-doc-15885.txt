# Potentate

### Potentate
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Potentate

See CHRIST, KING, RULER. Christ "is the blessed and only Potentate" (1 Tim. 6:15), meaning that all earthly power of kings and rulers shall in due course fade away, and he shall stand as the only supreme Sovereign and Ruler of all mankind.
