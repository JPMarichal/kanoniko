# Son Ahman

### Son Ahman
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Son Ahman/Son Ahman

See AHMAN, CHRIST, SON OF MAN, SONS AHMAN. Since Ahman is the name of God the Father in the pure language spoken by Adam, Son Ahman is the name of his Only Begotten Son. In two of the revelations published in the Doctrine and Covenants he so designates himself. (D. & C. 78:20; 95:17.)
