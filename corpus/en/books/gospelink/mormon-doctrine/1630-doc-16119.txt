### Servant
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Servant/Servant

See CHRIST, FATHER IN HEAVEN, MINISTER OF SALVATION, SERVANTS OF GOD. Christ is the Father's Servant. One of the great Messianic prophecies so designates him. (Isa. 42:1-8; Matt. 12:14-21.) Usage of this term teaches that he is subject to the Father in all things, that he came into the world to do the will of the Father, and that it is by the power of the Father that he accomplished his mission. (3 Ne. 27:13-16; D. & C. 76:40:43; John 5:30; 6:38.) It should be remembered that in the sense in which the designation is here used, the Servant shall be as his Lord, thus inheriting the fulness of all things for himself. "Where I am, there shall also my servant be." (John 12:26.)
