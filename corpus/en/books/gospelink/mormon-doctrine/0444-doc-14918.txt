# Deseret

### Deseret
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Deseret

See BOOK OF MORMON, JAREDITES. Deseret is the Jaredite name for honey bee. (Ether 2:3.) Brigham Young and his followers first named their new intermountain empire the territory of Deseret after this unique Book of Mormon name. The name has since been applied to many businesses and enterprises in the Utah area, and the beehive, as symbol of industry, is part of the great seal of the state of Utah.
