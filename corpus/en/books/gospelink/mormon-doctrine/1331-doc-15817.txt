# Pageantry

### Pageantry
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Pageantry

See ORDINANCES, RECREATION. Some false churches have worship services in which elaborate pageantry, pomp, and display are presented. Included in their rituals are ceremonies involving images, altars, miters, robes, genuflections, and Latin incantations. In the true Church pageantry is a form of recreation and entertainment and not of solemn worship. Certain formalities attend the performance of sacred ordinances, but these formalities are beautiful in their simplicity and do not consist in the unsubstantial, empty show and display of those whose worship, in large part, is encased in mystery.
