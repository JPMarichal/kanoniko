# Meetinghouses

### Meetinghouses
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Meetinghouses

See CATHEDRALS, CHAPELS, TABERNACLES, TEMPLES. Houses of worship erected in wards and branches of the Church are commonly called meetinghouses. Normally they include an assembly hall (or chapel) where sacrament meetings and other meetings of worship are held, a cultural hall (often including gymnasium facilities), class rooms for study purposes, and offices for bishops and other church officers.
