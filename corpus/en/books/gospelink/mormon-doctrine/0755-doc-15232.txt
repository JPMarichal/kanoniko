# God of Spirits

### God of Spirits
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

God of Spirits

See CHRIST, FATHER IN HEAVEN, GOD, PRE-EXISTENCE. Our Father in Heaven (Matt. 6:9) is the God of Spirits (Num. 16:22; 27:16) or the Father of Spirits (Heb. 12:9), meaning that he is the literal Parent of the Spirit Christ and of all other spirits. Inasmuch, however, as Christ attained Godhood while yet in pre-existence, he too stood as a God to the other spirits, but this relationship was not the same one of personal parenthood that prevailed between the Father and his offspring.
