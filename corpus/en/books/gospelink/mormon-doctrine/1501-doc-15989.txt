# Rejoicing

### Rejoicing
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Rejoicing/Rejoicing

See CHEERFULNESS, JOY, THANKSGIVING. Faithful saints feel great delight and are uplifted in body and in spirit because of the blessings of the gospel. Theirs is a status of joy and rejoicing which comes because of obedience to gospel law. (D. & C. 52:43.) "Lift up thy heart and rejoice." (D. & C. 25:13.) "Rejoice evermore." (1 Thess. 5:16.)
