### Conjure Man
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Conjure Man

See MAGIC, MEDICINE MEN, WITCHCRAFT, WITCH DOCTORS. In some localities, particularly the West Indies and Southern United States, a witch, practitioner of magic, or witch doctor is called a conjure man or woman. Conjuration includes the practice of magic and the summoning of evil spirits by invocation or incantation.
