# Slavery

### Slavery
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Bond-Servants/Slavery

See AGENCY, BONDAGE, CASTE SYSTEM, FREEDOM. Human slavery is not consonant with the principles of agency, freedom, and justice found in the gospel of Christ. It is not right that any man should be in bondage one to another." (D. & C. 101:19.) It is the great apostate Babylon that makes merchandise of "slaves, and souls of men." (Rev. 18:13.)

The fact that the gospel is in the world in periods when slavery is legally practiced, with the result that the gospel must take cognizance of its existence and make certain regulations with reference thereto, does not mean in the slightest degree that true Christianity approves of slavery. "We believe it just to preach the gospel to the nations of the earth, and warn the righteous to save themselves from the corruption of the world; but we do not believe it right to interfere with bond-servants, neither preach the gospel to, nor baptize them contrary to the will and wish of their masters, nor to meddle with or influence them in the least to cause them to be dissatisfied with their situations in this life, thereby jeopardizing the lives of men; such interference we believe to be unlawful and unjust, and dangerous to the peace of every government allowing human beings to be held in servitude." (D. & C. 134:12.)
