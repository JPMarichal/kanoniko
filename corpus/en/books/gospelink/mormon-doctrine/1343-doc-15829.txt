# Passover

### Passover
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Passover

See DAY OF PENTECOST, SACRAMENT, SACRIFICES. To commemorate Israel's deliverance from Egyptian bondage, the Lord commanded his people to keep the feast of the passover, a celebration pointing particularly to the fact that the angel of destruction passed over the homes of the faithful sons of Jacob, when the firstborn in all the families of Egypt were slain. (Ex. 12.)

It was during the week of this feast, some 1500 years after the exodus, that our Lord was crucified. Just before his betrayal he had partaken of the feast with his disciples, using it as the occasion to introduce the ordinance of the sacrament to the Church. (Matt. 26; Mark 14; Luke 22.)

Keeping of the passover, with its sacrifices and unleavened bread, ended (except among apostate peoples) with the sacrifice of "Christ our passover." The saints were to keep the feast only in a spiritual sense, as Paul said: "Let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, neither with the leaven of malice and wickedness; but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth." (1 Cor. 5:6-8.)
