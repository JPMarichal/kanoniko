# Children of the Prophets

### Children of the Prophets
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Children of the Prophets

See ADOPTION, CHILDREN OF THE COVENANT, PROPHETS, SEED OF CHRIST. Those who follow in the footsteps of the prophets, who believe as they believed and live as they lived, are the children of the prophets. (D. & C. 84:33-34.) They are children in the sense of being followers or disciples, and they may also be their literal seed. (3 Ne. 20:25-27; Acts 3:25.) However, the rebellious literal seed cut themselves off from the blessings of their fathers, and they become the children of the devil rather than the children of the prophets. (John 8:33-59.)
