# Son of the Highest

### Son of the Highest
Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine ( 1966)
 
 

Son of the Highest/Son of the Highest

See CHRIST, HIGHEST, MOST HIGH, SON OF GOD. Christ is the Son of the Highest. (Luke 1:32.) Gabriel so announced him to Mary. Both his own divine Sonship and the supremacy of the Father in the Godhead are shown by the usage of this name-title.
