# The Need to Receive

### The Need to Receive
James E. Faust, Stories From My Life (Salt Lake City 2001)
 
 

The Need to Receive

I hope none of our people will be too proud to accept help
when they are in need.

My father was a successful lawyer,
but not many years after the Depression he had two sons on a mission at the
same time—my older brother, Gus, and me. Father fell ill and
was hospitalized, and financially things became difficult. Our good bishop went
to him and said, “George, this month the ward is going to send the money to
your two missionary sons while you get back on your feet.”

I did not learn of this until some years
later, but I was grateful that my father, who gave generously to the Church all
of his life, was not too proud in a moment of need to accept the help of the
loving brothers and sisters in the ward. After all, that is the spirit of the
gospel. In order to have the blessing of giving, one needs to receive.
