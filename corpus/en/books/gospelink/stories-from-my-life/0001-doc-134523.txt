# Preface

### Preface
James E. Faust, Stories From My Life (Salt Lake City 2001)
 
 

Preface

Throughout my life, I have been blessed
with countless experiences that, on reflection, have helped me to better
understand the Lord’s plan for His children, as well as my need to live a life
that is in complete harmony with His teachings. In many cases, these
experiences have been my own, while at other times I have benefited greatly
from observing the faith and strength of others.

In the many talks I have given over the
years, I have, as it seemed appropriate, shared a number of these stories to
help illustrate various principles of the gospel. It is in that same spirit
that some of these stories have been collected in this volume—that the
reader’s faith, understanding, and commitment might be strengthened, in the
same way mine has been throughout my life.

James E. Faust

January 2001
