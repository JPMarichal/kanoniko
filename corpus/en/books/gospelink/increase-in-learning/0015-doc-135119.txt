### Related Readings for Chapter Three
David A. Bednar, Increase in Learning (Salt Lake City 2011)
 
 

Related Readings for Chapter Three
 

The principle of prayerful inquiry and the pattern of asking, seeking, and knocking are vital in obtaining the knowledge, understanding, and intelligence that are necessary for us to know what we need to know, to live as we know we should live, and to become what the Master would have us become—even living members of the Savior’s living Church.
 

The following readings focus upon the importance in our daily lives of faith-filled and meaningful prayer, feasting upon the words of Christ through diligent scripture study, and the spirit of revelation.
