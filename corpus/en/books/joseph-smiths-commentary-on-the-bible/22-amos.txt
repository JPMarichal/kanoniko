Amos 3 7The grand rule of heaven was that nothing should ever be done on earth without revealing the secret to his servants the prophets. [^1]

Amos 3 7The prophet says that God will do nothing but what he will reveal unto his servants the prophets. Consequently, if it is not made known to the prophets, it will not come to pass. [^2]
