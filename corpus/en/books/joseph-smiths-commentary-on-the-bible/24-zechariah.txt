SCZech 14 16Zech 14 17Zech 14 18Zech 14 19The wicked [^1] will not all be destroyed at the coming of Christ, and there will be wicked during the Millennium. . . . There will be wicked men on the earth during the one thousand years. The heathen nations who will not come up to worship will be destroyed. [^3]
