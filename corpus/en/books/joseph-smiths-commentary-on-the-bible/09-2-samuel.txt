2 Samuel 12:7-13

SC2 Sam 12 72 Sam 12 82 Sam 12 92 Sam 12 102 Sam 12 112 Sam 12 122 Sam 12 13A murderer, for instance, one that sheds innocent blood, cannot have forgiveness. David sought repentance at the hand of God, carefully with tears, but he could only get it through Hell. He got a promise that his soul should not be left in Hell. Although David was a king, he never did obtain the spirit and power of Elijah and the fulness of the priesthood. And the priesthood that he received and the throne and kingdom of David is to be taken from him and given to another by the name of David in the last days, raised up out of his lineage. [^4]
