Joseph Smith, Kent P. Jackson, Joseph Smith's Commentary on the Bible ( 1994)

Deuteronomy

Deuteronomy 30:4

Deut 30 4Mark carefully what the prophet says: "If any are driven out unto the utmost parts of heaven," which must mean the breadths of the earth. Now this promise is good to any, if there should be such, that are driven out, even in the last days. Therefore, the children of the fathers have claim unto this day. [^1]
