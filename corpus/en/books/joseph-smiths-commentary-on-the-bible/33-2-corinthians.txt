2 Corinthians 5:10, JST

2 Cor 5 10[God] holds the reins of judgment in his hands. He is a wise lawgiver and will judge all men, not according to the narrow contracted notions of men, but "according to the deeds done in the body, whether they be good or evil," or whether these deeds were done in England, America, Spain, Turkey, or India.
