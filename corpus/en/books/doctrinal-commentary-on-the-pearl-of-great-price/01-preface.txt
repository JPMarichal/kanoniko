Hyrum L. Andrus, Doctrinal Commentary on the Pearl of Great Price ( 1967)

Preface

For several years I have been teaching courses in the Pearl of Great Price at Brigham Young University. This volume is in many ways a product of these years of study and discussion. In presenting it to the public, I would like to express appreciation to those students who have challenged me in my thinking and contributed to my understanding of the Pearl of Great Price. Though the historical events associated with the Pearl of Great Price are of great importance, I have taken the view that the message of the book is of primary value. This volume therefore deals almost exclusively with the teachings of the Pearl of Great Price.

Special thanks should be given to Professor Roy W. Doxey, Acting Dean of the College of Religious Instruction, and to Dr. Chauncey C. Riddle, Chairman of Graduate Studies of Religion at Brigham Young University, for assistance rendered in preparing this material for publication; also to Dean B. West Belnap and Dr. James R. Harris. Professor Ralph A. Britsch of the Department of English has given valuable help by reading the manuscript and checking its style and grammar. I would also like to thank two of our secretaries, Mary Tischner and Jeanne Merrell, for typing the manuscript. Finally, I express deep love and appreciation to my wife, Helen Mae, for her patience and for her assistance in writing this volume. Without her sustaining help this work would not have been possible.

Although others have read the manuscript and have offered helpful suggestions, I alone am responsible for the views expressed in this volume and for the conclusions that have been drawn from the sources quoted.

Hyrum L. Andrus
