Satan

Quotations

Ben E. Rich: It is the work of the adversary to try to creep in between that individual and the people of God. That has always been the work of the evil one; and he holds out false promises to the people as an inducement to get them to turn their backs upon the prophet of Almighty God, just as he held out false promises to the Savior Himself when he . . . showed Him the glories of the world and promised to give them all to Him, if He would fall down and worship him. The old sinner never owned a foot of it (Conference Report, Apr. 1910, 92).
