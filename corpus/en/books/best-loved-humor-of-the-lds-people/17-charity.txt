Jay A. Parry, Jack M. Lyon, Linda Ririe Gundry, Best-Loved Humor of the LDS People (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1999)

Charity

Jokes

A trainee police officer was asked, "How would you go about breaking up a crowd?"

His answer: "Take up a collection. That'll do it every time."

Stories

Thackeray told of an Irishwoman begging alms from him, who, when she saw him put his hand in his pocket, cried out:

"May the blessing of God follow ye all yer life!" But when he only pulled out his snuff box, she immediately added: "and never over- take ye."
