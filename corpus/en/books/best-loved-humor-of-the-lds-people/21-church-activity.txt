Jay A. Parry, Jack M. Lyon, Linda Ririe Gundry, Best-Loved Humor of the LDS People (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1999)

Church Activity

Jokes

Bishop Jones to a prospective missionary: Rob, the Church needs you. It's time for you to join the army of the Lord.

Rob: I'm already in the army of the Lord, Bishop.

Bishop Jones: What do you mean? You hardly even come to Church.

Rob (whispering): Well, you see, I'm in the Secret Service.

1999 Deseret Book Co.
