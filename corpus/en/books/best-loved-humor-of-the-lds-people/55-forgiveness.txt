Forgiveness

Stories

When Frederick the Great was seized with his mortal illness, he asked an advisor whether it was necessary to forgive all his enemies. On receiving the proper answer, he said to the queen, "Dorothy, write to your brother that I forgive him all the evil he has done me; but wait till I am dead first."

1999 Deseret Book Co.
