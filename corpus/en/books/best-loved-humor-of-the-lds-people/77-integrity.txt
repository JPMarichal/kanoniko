Integrity

Quotations

J. Golden Kimball: When I look over this body of men, I do not discover that you are very distinguished in appearance. Why, you are no better looking than I am, and I look pretty bad. . . . I am only a remnant of what I ought to be. I am not very well groomed, and I do not look distinguished; neither do you. . . . You can't boast very much about your appearance. We are a hard working people, and we would not take a very good picture, unless you take the better side of us: but I tell you, in the name of the Lord, we have got clean hearts; we love the Lord; we love truthfulness; we desire to be honest, truthful, and virtuous. You can't judge us by our appearance. If you knew the hearts of this people, there would not be the bitterness there is against the Latter-day Saints (Conference Report, Oct. 1910, 34).
