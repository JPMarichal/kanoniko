Jay A. Parry, Jack M. Lyon, Linda Ririe Gundry, Best-Loved Humor of the LDS People (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1999)

Commandments

Jokes

A woman went to the post office to mail her daughter a new set of scriptures. The clerk asked if the package contained anything breakable. "Just the Ten Commandments," she replied.

Stories

A newspaper editor who needed to fill some space ran the Ten Commandments without comment. A few days later he heard from an angry subscriber: "Cancel my subscription. You're getting too personal."
