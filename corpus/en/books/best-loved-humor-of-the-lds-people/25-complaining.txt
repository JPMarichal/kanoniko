Jay A. Parry, Jack M. Lyon, Linda Ririe Gundry, Best-Loved Humor of the LDS People (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1999)

Complaining

Jokes

A special group of monks was able to utter only two words every ten years. After the first ten years, Brother Francis said to his superior: "Bed hard."

After the twentieth year, he said to the same superior: "Food cold."

Finally, after the thirtieth year, he said to the same superior: "I quit."

The superior responded: "I'm not surprised. All you've done in thirty years is complain, complain, complain!"
