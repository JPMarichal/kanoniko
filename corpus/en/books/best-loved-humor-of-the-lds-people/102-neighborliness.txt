Jay A. Parry, Jack M. Lyon, Linda Ririe Gundry, Best-Loved Humor of the LDS People (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1999)

Neighborliness

Jokes

Brother Bernstein was thoroughly annoyed when his neighbor called at two o'clock in the morning and said, "Your stupid dog is barking so loud I can't sleep!" The neighbor slammed the phone down without even waiting for an answer.

The following night Brother Bernstein waited until 2:00 a.m., then called his neighbor back and said, "I don't have a dog."

1999 Deseret Book Co.
