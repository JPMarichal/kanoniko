Jay A. Parry, Jack M. Lyon, Linda Ririe Gundry, Best-Loved Humor of the LDS People (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1999)

Judgment

Jokes

A Gospel Doctrine teacher was exhorting the class to prepare themselves so they might avoid the wrath that would be visited upon the unrighteous. "I warn you," he thundered, "there will be weeping and wailing and gnashing of teeth!"

At this moment an old woman stood up. "Brother Bowen," she exclaimed, "I have no teeth!"

"Sister Wendell," replied the teacher, "teeth will be provided!"
