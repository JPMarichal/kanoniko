Once when Spencer W. Kimball was too weak to shave, a nurse shaved him with an electric shaver, but with no apparent effect. Finally he suggested, "Perhaps it would work better if you took the cap off" (Kimball, "Spencer W. Kimball," BYU Studies[25[4]: 66).
