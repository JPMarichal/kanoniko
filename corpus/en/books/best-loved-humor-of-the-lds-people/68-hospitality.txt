Hospitality

Stories

Alice James, wife of William James, said that often during evenings her husband would exclaim, "Are we never to have an evening alone? Must we always talk to people every night?" And she would answer, "I will see that whoever calls tonight is told that you are strictly engaged." So they would settle down to their quiet evening. Presently the doorbell would ring and Alice would go to the entry to make sure that her instructions were carried out; but close behind her would be William, exclaiming, "Come in! Come right in!"
