Stories

Dr. Truman Madsen, a member of the faculty of Brigham Young University . . . , once asked [Elder Richard L. Evans of the Quorum of the Twelve]: "How do you account for your composure, your serenity of soul, under the frenetic schedule you must keep?" Elder Evans' answer came back without hesitation: "Exhaustion!" (Evans, Richard L. Evans, 14).
