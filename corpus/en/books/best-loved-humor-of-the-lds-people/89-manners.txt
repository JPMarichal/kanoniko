Jay A. Parry, Jack M. Lyon, Linda Ririe Gundry, Best-Loved Humor of the LDS People (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1999)

Manners

Jokes

As a young student entered her classroom, the teacher noticed his hands were dirty. She stopped him and said, "John, please wash your hands. My goodness, what would you say if I came into the room with hands like that?"

"Oh," the boy replied, "I think I'd be too polite to mention it."

Mother: Sam, I expect you to behave yourself here in your own home! What would your Scoutmaster say if you acted like that in Scout meeting?

Sam: He'd say, "Behave yourself, Sam—remember you're not at home now!"

Little Zachary had been to a birthday party, and when he got back home, his mother asked him questions about his manners at the party. "You didn't ask for a second piece of cake, did you?" she said.

"No, Mom," Zachary answered. "I just asked Jessica's mother for the recipe so that you could make some cake like it because it was the very best cake I'd ever tasted in my whole life. And she gave me another piece, of her own free will."
