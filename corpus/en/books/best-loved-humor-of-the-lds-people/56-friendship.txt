Stories

As a General Authority, Spencer W. Kimball traveled a lot, and he loved to joke with his friends. He wrote to one, "I thought you might have been at the depot as my train went through at five this morning." To another he wrote, "I looked down as my airplane passed over your town today but didn't see you out and around" (Kimball, "Spencer W. Kimball," BYU Studies [5[4]: 67).
