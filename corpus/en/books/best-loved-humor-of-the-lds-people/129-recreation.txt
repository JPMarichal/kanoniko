Richard Eyre: We took a business associate to our [favorite] lake for a long weekend one summer. He spent the first day worrying about the calls he couldn't make since we had no phone. The second day he gave in a little and started relaxing and enjoying himself. At the end of the third day he said, "You know, I think I've forgotten how to play." Then a pause and smile. "But I'm starting to think I have a natural aptitude for it and can relearn it if I just apply myself" (Don't Just Do Something: New Maxims to Refresh and Enrich Your Life, 39).
