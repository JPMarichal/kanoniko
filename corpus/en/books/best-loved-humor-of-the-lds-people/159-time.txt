Time

Stories

Joseph Fielding Smith was so conscious of time and so determined to use it wisely that he "wore two watches, one on each wrist, and kept another pair in reserve. He was up every morning before six and worked hard all day long. One of his sons recalls, 'Somehow it seemed immoral to lie in bed after 6. Of course, I only tried it once. Father saw to that.' One of President Smith's maxims was 'People die in bed. And so does ambition'" (West, Profiles of the Presidents, 251-52).
