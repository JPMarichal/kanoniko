Jokes

At basic training a sergeant was training a bunch of recruits at the rifle range. He had the men line up at a hundred yards and told them to fire. They did so—but when the smoke cleared the sergeant saw the target was untouched. He had them move forward fifty yards and try again. The results were the same. He had them try it at twenty-five yards. Not one mark on the target.

Finally, exasperated, the sergeant faced his troops and yelled, "Fix bayonets and charge!"
