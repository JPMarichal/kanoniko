Jay A. Parry, Jack M. Lyon, Linda Ririe Gundry, Best-Loved Humor of the LDS People (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1999)

School

Jokes

A clever schoolteacher sent home a note to the parents of her new students on the first day of school. It read, "If you promise you won't believe everything your child says happens at school, I promise I won't believe everything she says happens at home."

Child to mother: Guess what? My teacher thinks I'm going to be famous someday!

Mom: Really?

Child: Yup. She said if I get in trouble one more time I'm history!

1999 Deseret Book Co.
