Courage

Stories

A Confederate soldier was seen by General Lee, who met him retiring from the front with what Lee considered unbecoming haste. Lee said to him, "Why don't you go back to the front? That's the place where a soldier should be when a battle is going on."

The reply was, "General, I have been there, and I give you my word of honor it is not a place where any self-respecting man would care to be."

One day Chauncey Depew met a Civil War soldier who had been wounded in the face. He was a Union man, and Depew asked him in which battle he had been injured.

"In the last battle of Bull Run, sir," he replied. "But how could you get hit in the face at Bull Run?" "Well, sir," said the man, half apologetically, "after I had run a mile or two I got careless and looked back."
