Peace

Jokes

An old man who had had more than his share of challenges was asked how he managed to stay so cheerful. "Well, I'll tell you," he replied. "I've just learned to cooperate with the inevitable."

Stories

Shortly before the death of Thoreau, a pious aunt visited him and asked, "Have you made your peace with God, Henry?"

"I don't know that we ever quarreled," he replied.
