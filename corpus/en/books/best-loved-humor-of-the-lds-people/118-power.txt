Power

Quotations

Brigham Young: We are not the Almighty. I am glad of it. I am happy in the reflection that I have not the power [to destroy the Saints' enemies], and I hope and pray I may never possess it until I can use it like a God, until I can wield it as our Father in heaven wields it, with all that eternity of majesty, glory, charity, with his judgment, discretion, and with every faculty of compassion. I am happy in the reflection that I do not possess the power. I am glad you elders do not, I am really glad you do not (Journal of Discourses 15:1-4).
