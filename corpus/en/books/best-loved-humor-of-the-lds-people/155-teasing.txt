Teasing

Stories

Spencer W. Kimball loved to tease. For example, he would say to one of his children, "I see you are in the newspaper!"

"Where? Where?"

"It says right here, 'Crowd of Thousands Watches Parade'" (Kimball, "Spencer W. Kimball," BYU Studies [5[4]: 62).
