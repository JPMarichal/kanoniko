Jay A. Parry, Jack M. Lyon, Linda Ririe Gundry, Best-Loved Humor of the LDS People (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1999)

Curiosity

Jokes

Grade school teacher (giving lesson about Ben Franklin and electricity): So you see, Mr. Franklin's curiosity was a good thing. He discovered electricity. Now, where would we be if no one had ever been curious?

Student: In the Garden of Eden!
