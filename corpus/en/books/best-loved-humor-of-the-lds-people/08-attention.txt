Attention

Jokes

"Who's absentminded now?" the professor challenged his wife as they were walking home from church. "You left your umbrella in the rack, but I remembered both yours and mine."

And he proudly exhibited both umbrellas.

"But, dear," protested his wife, "neither of us brought an umbrella today."

Quotations

J. Golden Kimball: Think of God. How many of us think of God thirty minutes out of twenty-four hours? There is not one out of five hundred that actually thinks of God and his Son Jesus Christ thirty minutes a day. I do; but the first thing I know, my mind wanders off on something else (Conference Report, Apr. 1926, 62).

Brigham Young: Study to preserve your bodies in life and health, and you will be able to control your minds. And when you come to meeting, bring your minds with you (Journal of Discourses 8:135).
