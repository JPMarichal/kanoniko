Jay A. Parry, Jack M. Lyon, Linda Ririe Gundry, Best-Loved Humor of the LDS People (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Co., 1999)

Parenthood

Jokes

College student to parents: "Dear Mom and Dad: Don't forget to write, even if it's only ten or twenty."

On his first visit to the zoo, a little boy stared at the caged stork for a long while. Then he turned to his father and exclaimed, "Gee, Dad, he doesn't recognize me!"

The problem with being a parent is that by the time you've learned how to do your job, you're unemployed.

1999 Deseret Book Co.
