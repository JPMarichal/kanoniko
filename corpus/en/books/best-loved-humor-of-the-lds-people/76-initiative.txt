Jokes

A motorist got bogged down in the sticky clay of an unpaved Georgia road, and paid ten dollars to be pulled out by a Georgia cracker with a team of mules. "I should think," said the motorist, just about to get into his car to continue, "that you would be2pulling people out of this stuff day and night."

"Nope," drawled the man, "at night's when we tote the water for the roads."
