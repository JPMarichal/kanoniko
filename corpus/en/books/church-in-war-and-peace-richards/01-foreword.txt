Stephen L Richards, Church in War and Peace ( 1943)

FOREWORD

The discussions comprising this volume were given as a series of radio talks originating in the Tabernacle in Salt Lake City on the weekly Sunday evening Church program presented over Station KSL. They were so generously received by listeners over a vast area, and the requests for reprints so numerous that the series is now presented in book form.

As such it becomes another outstanding contribution to the literature of Mormonism. The subjects dealt with are many and varied, and they reveal the depth of perception and the broad understanding of the man who prepared them.

Stephen L Richards has long been admired for the simple, yet profound beauty of his language. To many thousands of the Church, who have heard him in one capacity or another during the more than a quarter of a century that he has served as a member of the Council of the Twelve, this compilation will be welcomed as an added treasure to the contributions he has made to an understanding of truth.

Elder Richards distinguished himself as a lawyer and business leader, as well as a Church worker, before his call to the Council of the Twelve. His rich background is evident in the clarity of judgment he brings to problems perplexing men and women in a world torn by war and strife.

His keen insight, his broad sympathies, his outstanding scholarship, his unfailing courtesy and respect for the opinions of others, his great love for the gospel of Jesus Christ, and his testimony concerning its power and mission are all evident in these writings.

Gordon B. Hinckley.
