James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 5 ( 1971)

Church policy on charitable "drives," May 1, 1922

1922-May 1-Original letter. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The Presiding Bishop of the Church, Charles W. Nibley, to whom this letter was addressed, was ordained and set apart as Presiding Bishop on December 11, 1907. The letter sets the policy of the First Presidency and Quorum of Twelve Apostles on the matter of charitable "drives" by placing all such matters in the hands of the Presiding Bishopric of the Church with special cases to be referred to the First Presidency. This letter of instructions and policy evidently made possible a more orderly handling of the type of problems mentioned in the announcements of the First Presidency for June, 1920, and January 23, 1921, which appear earlier in this volume.

Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints Salt Lake City, Utah May 1st, 1922. Bishop C. W. Nibley and Counselors, City.

Dear Brethren:

The subject of charitable "drives" received attention at our meeting of First Presidency and Twelve on Thursday last, resulting in a unanimous action that hereafter what is known as "drives" shall receive your endorsement to make them effective among our people, and when approved that our Church machinery shall be used by our own people in our own way entirely free from connection with or assistance from outsiders. It was the mind of the Council also that rather than turn away anything which in your mind should receive special consideration, in such an event you are to consult the First Presidency before acting on it.

Your Brethren, HEBER J. GRANT, CHARLES W. PENROSE, ANTHONY W. IVINS, First Presidency.
