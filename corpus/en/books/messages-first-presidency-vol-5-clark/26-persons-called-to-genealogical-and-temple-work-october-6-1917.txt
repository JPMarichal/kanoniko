Persons called to genealogical and temple work, October 6, 1917

1917-October 6-Original letter. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

A form letter of call of individuals to special work in the genealogical and temple work programs of the Church.

Dear Brother:

It is desired that you will make yourself familiar with the information contained in the accompanying circulars of instruction, etc., so that you may be enabled to assist the members of the Church, in your district, in acquiring an understanding of their duties and privileges, in relation to Temple ordinances; also to show them how to prepare records of that sacred work for the dead, and inform them concerning what the Genealogical Society of Utah can do in securing genealogical data, etc.

Trusting that it will be agreeable and convenient for you to give this important matter needful attention, without infringing upon your other labors in Church affairs, I remain, with affectionate regards, Your brother in the Gospel, JOSEPH F. SMITH.
