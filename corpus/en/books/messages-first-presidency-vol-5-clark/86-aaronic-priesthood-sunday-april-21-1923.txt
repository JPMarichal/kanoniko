Aaronic Priesthood Sunday, April 21, 1923

1923-April 21-Deseret News, April 21, 1923, p.4

NOTICE

It has been decided that on Sunday, May 13th, 1923, at all the Sacramental meetings throughout the Church to be held on that day, the Lesser Priesthood shall be requested to take charge of those meetings; to do all the singing, the praying, administering the Sacrament, the preaching and giving of testimonies; that it may be strictly a Lesser Priesthood service, in commemoration of the restoration of this Priesthood, which was conferred upon Joseph Smith, the prophet, and Oliver Cowdery, by the laying on of the hands of John the Baptist, on the fifteenth day of May, 1829, in these words:

"Upon you my fellow servants, in the name of Messiah, I confer the Priesthood of Aaron, which holds the keys of the ministering of angels, and of the gospel of repentance, and of baptism by immersion for the remission of sins; and this shall never be taken again from the earth, until the sons of Levi do offer again an offering unto the Lord in righteousness."

The Presiding Bishopric holds the Presidency of this Priesthood, and further instructions and suggestions in respect to these meetings will be sent from the Presiding Bishop's Office. HEBER J. GRANT, CHARLES W. PENROSE, ANTHONY W. IVINS, First Presidency.
