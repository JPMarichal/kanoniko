Approval of Presiding Bishopric's "Circular of Instructions," March (?), 1921

1921-March?-Original circular. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

See historical note for the previous document.

It is interesting to note that these "Instructions," which were circulated by the Presiding Bishopric of the Church to Presidents of Stakes, etc., with this cover letter by the First Presidency, are referred to by the First Presidency as "precepts, regulations and rules" to be made known "to the priesthood and others affected, with a view to concerted action, unity and harmony in the labors of the priesthood." The First Presidency did not use the words doctrine or revelation, specifically, in referring to these operational instructions and policies. This "Circular of Instructions" was the forerunner of the later "Handbook" for Stake Presidencies and Bishoprics.

TO PRESIDENTS OF STAKES AND COUNSELORS, BISHOPS OF WARDS AND COUNSELORS, AND ALL CHURCH AUTHORITIES.

Dear Brethren:

Your attention is directed to the instructions in the following pages, compiled and prepared by the Presiding Bishopric to aid you in transacting the business of the Church, within your jurisdiction.

We heartily approve the precepts, regulations and rules set forth in this "Circular of Instructions," and desire that as Presiding Authorities of stakes, wards and missions, you will observe them and make them known to the priesthood and others affected, with a view to concerted action, unity and harmony in the labors of the priesthood.

With sincere appreciation of your faithful efforts, and helpful cooperation in the past, in looking after the spiritual and temporal interests of the people under your care, we remain.

Your brethren, HEBER J. GRANT, CHARLES W. PENROSE, ANTHONY W. IVINS.
