James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 5 ( 1971)

Site for meetinghouse in Washington, D.C., January 30, 1919

1919-January 30-Original letter. Reed Smoot files, Brigham Young University, Archives, Provo, Utah.

This letter seems to constitute the inauguration of a move to have a Permanent "Mormon" Church building in the nation's capital. Apostle-Senator Smoot's role is clearly defined in the letter.

The First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints Salt Lake City, Utah January 30th, 1919. Senator Reed Smoot, Washington, D.C.

Dear Senator:

We take pleasure in informing you that we have been considering of late the advisability of building a meeting house in Washington, D.C., and in discussing this question at our last Council meeting it was decided that you be requested to give the matter of looking up a suitable and eligible building site your personal attention, and we trust that you will be able to do this without interfering at all with your official duties.

With kind love, we are, Your Brethren, HEBER J. GRANT, ANTHON H. LUND, CHARLES W. PENROSE, First Presidency.
