Commemoration of Aaronic Priesthood restoration, May 12, 1928

1928-May 12-Liahona, the Elders' Journal 26:568, May 12, 1928.

Advance notice of the coming one hundredth anniversary of the restoration of the Aaronic Priesthood to be commemorated during the week of May 12-19, 1929.

NOTICE

On May 15, 1929, it will have been just one hundred years since the restoration of the Aaronic Priesthood through the visitation of an angel, known while on earth as John the Baptist, to Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery, and the ordination by him of these young men to this important authority.

It is therefore very essential that this wonderful event should be appropriately commemorated in all the wards and branches throughout the Church. Since the actual anniversary occurs on Wednesday, May 15th, it is suggested that the main celebration should take place in the Sunday School and sacramental meeting on Sunday, May 12th next, and that a special program and social gathering be held also in lieu of the Priesthood-M.I.A. meeting on Tuesday evening, May 14th. Instructions and suggestive programs for these various gatherings have been sent out by the Presiding Bishopric, the Deseret Sunday School Board and the General Boards of M.I.A. It is desired that careful, thorough preparation be made well in advance in order that the results may be in every way satisfactory. If, for any reason, there are other meetings on Sunday, May 12th, that will conflict with this celebration, the following Sunday, May 19th, should be reserved for the event. HEBER J. GRANT, ANTHONY W. IVINS, CHARLES W. NIBLEY, First Presidency.
