James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 5 ( 1971)

Corporation of the Presiding Bishop, September 7, 1916

1916-September 7-Original letter. Reed Smoot collection, Brigham Young University Library, Archives, Provo, Utah.

Office of The First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints Salt Lake City, Utah, September 7th, 1916 Senator Reed Smoot, Washington, D.C.

Dear Brother:

We send you herewith a copy of the laws of Utah providing for corporation sole and also a copy of the articles of incorporation of the Corporation of the Presiding Bishop of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. These copies have been duly certified by the Secretary of State here and we desire to have them attested by the Secretary of State at Washington and then mailed to the following address: Hyrum W. Valentine, Rhinelander Strasse 10, Basel, Switzerland.

It is very desirable that this matter should receive prompt attention, as President Valentine needs the papers in order to consummate the purchase and conveyance of property belonging to the Church before he leaves for home, which he desires to do as soon as possible.

With kindest regards, Your Brethren, JOSEPH F. SMITH, ANTHON H. LUND, CHARLES W. PENROSE, First Presidency.
