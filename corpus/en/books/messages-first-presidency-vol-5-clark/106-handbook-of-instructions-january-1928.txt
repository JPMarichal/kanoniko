Handbook of Instructions, January, 1928

1928-January-Original handbook. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Note that the instructions set forth in this handbook prepared by the Presiding Bishopric are referred to as "regulations and rules." The handbook is not here reproduced.

HANDBOOK OF INSTRUCTIONS

To the Presidents of Stakes and Counselors, Bishops of Wards and Counselors, Stake and Ward Clerks, and Mission Presidents:

Dear Brethren:

Your attention is directed to the instructions in the following pages, compiled a prepared by the Presiding Bishopric, to aid you in transacting the business of the Church, within your jurisdiction.

We heartily approve the regulations and rules set forth in this "Handbook" and desire that, as presiding authorities of stakes, wards and missions, you will observe them and make them known to the Priesthood and others affected, with a view to concerted action, unity and harmony in the labors of the Priesthood.

With sincere appreciation of your faithful efforts, and helpful cooperation in the past, in looking after the spiritual and temporal interests of the people under your care, we remain, Your brethren, HEBER J. GRANT, ANTHONY W. IVINS, CHARLES W. NIBLEY, First Presidency.
