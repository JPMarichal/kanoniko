Seventies distribution of Liahona, December 4, 1916

1916-December 4-Original letter. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Letter of congratulation to the publishers of the Liahona and a recommendation that Priesthood Quorums throughout the Church cooperate in a plan to donate to send the Liahona to investigators of the Church.

Office of The First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints Salt Lake City, Utah, December 4, 1916. Zion's Printing and Publishing Co., Independence, Jackson Co., Missouri.

Dear Brethren:

Our attention has been called to the good work now being done by some of the quorums of Seventy in sending donations to enable you to distribute the Liahona to investigators and others throughout the United States. We heartily commend to all the Seventies' Quorums the letter of President Seymour B. Young, in which he suggests that each Seventy endeavor to send at least $1.00 per year to the Liahona Office to be used to forward the Liahona to investigators and other worthy people who may be unable to subscribe to your excellent magazine.

We also recommend to the High Priests' and Elders' Quorums throughout the Church, this very excellent way to assist in preaching the Gospel; and we most heartily concur with your suggestion that this matter be taken up with these quorums of the Priesthood so that they may become interested in the Liahona, the organ of the missions of the United States.

We congratulate the management of the Liahona for the very excellent work that is being done in promulgating the Gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ, and we trust that the quorums of High Priests, Seventies and Elders throughout Zion will avail themselves of this privilege of assisting in the great missionary work of the Church.

Wishing you continued success, we are, Your brethren in the Gospel JOSEPH F. SMITH, ANTHON H. LUND, CHARLES W. PENROSE.
