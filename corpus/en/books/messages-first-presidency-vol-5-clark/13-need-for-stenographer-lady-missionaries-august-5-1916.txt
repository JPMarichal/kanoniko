Need for stenographer lady missionaries, August 5, 1916

1916-August 5-Original letter. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Four missions of the Church were particularly in need of lady missionaries who were also stenographers.

Office of The First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints Salt Lake City, Utah August 5, 1916. President Serge F. Ballif and Counselors, Cache Stake.

Dear Brethren:

We are very much in need of lady stenographers to labor in the Eastern, Southern, Western and California Missions. Those suggested should be good, steady young ladies, qualified to do regular missionary work, as part of the time of our lady stenographers is devoted to that labor. They should also be physically and financially able.

We will greatly appreciate it if you will take this matter up with your bishops and try to suggest some good, capable sisters, who will be prepared to leave in the near future. Your Brethren, JOSEPH F. SMITH, ANTHON H. LUND, CHARLES W. PENROSE, First Presidency.
