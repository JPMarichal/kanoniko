Christmas pastoral epistle, December 18, 1926

1926-December 18-Millennial Star 89:24-25, January 13, 1927; Deseret News, December 18, 1926.

Commenting on the issuance of these annual Christmas messages, the editor of the Millennial Star said:

"These pastoral communications carry the spirit of encouragement, associated, when necessary, with admonition and timely warning."

FROM THE FIRST PRESIDENCY

An eventful year has passed since our last greeting to the members of the Church and the people of the world. We are one year nearer a realization of the hope and expectation of all members of the Church and of true Christians the world over, when Christ, our Lord, shall come to establish His kingdom upon the earth, when peace and goodwill shall prevail among men, and the will of the Father be done on earth, as it is done in heaven.

The year 1926 goes out and the new year comes in with the Church in the enjoyment of a full measure of peace and prosperity.

Brotherly love and good fellowship prevail among the members; their confidence in the men who preside over the Church is unshaken; and their faith in the Lord, who stands at the head, is manifested by their good works.

Never before in the history of the Church have more encouraging results been realized from the efforts of the missionaries in the field, and new converts are constantly being added to the membership and strength of the Church. Never before has there been more willing response to the call for missionaries to carry the gospel message to the people of the world. Never before has greater interest in temple work, both for the living and the dead, been manifested. Through devoted observance of the law of tithing the finances of the Church are in better condition than ever before, and the administration of its business affairs is on a sound basis.

During the past year a larger portion of the revenues of the Church has been applied to the maintenance of missions, the support of the school system of the Church, and the erection and enlargement of temples and houses of worship, than ever before. This applies particularly to the building of new chapels, the year 1926 being a banner year in this respect.

All of this has been made possible through the liberality and devotion of the members of the Church in the payment of their tithes and offerings.

We pray that the Lord may reward their faithfulness by opening the windows of heaven and showering down blessings upon them until they will not have room to contain them.

As Christmastide approaches, and we review the events of the past year and the few years immediately preceding it, we are forcefully reminded of the words of Christ, our Lord, uttered just prior to His crucifixion. His disciples said to Him, "What shall be the sign of thy coming, and of the end of the world?" He answered, "Ye shall hear of wars and rumors of warsÂ�. For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in divers places."

In the present dispensation He says to the Church-"After your testimony cometh the testimony of earthquakes, that shall cause groanings in the midst of her, and men shall fall upon the ground and shall not be able to stand. And also cometh the testimony of the voice of thunderings, and the voice of lightnings, and the voice of tempests, and the voice of the waves of the sea heaving themselves beyond their bounds."

During the past year earthquakes, with great resultant loss of life, have been of frequent occurrence. Disastrous floods have brought devastation and death to many people. Tornadoes have left death and destruction in their wake, while famine and civil war have taken a tremendous toll of human life.

According to the most reliable information available the loss of human life since the commencement of the World War reaches the following appalling figures: The World War 9,000,000, Civil wars 6,000,000, Famine 6,000,000, Epidemics 40,000,000, Earthquake and Flood 2,000,000.

In all this we recognize the voice of the Lord, spoken through His servants the prophets, calling the people of the world to repentance, and warning us that the hour of His judgments and the time of His second coming is at hand.

We appeal to all people to come unto Christ and to accept Him as the Creator and Controller of the world. The salvation of the world depends upon their acknowledgment of the Saviour and obedience to His commandments. In this way only can they secure lasting safety and peace.

To the members of the Church we say: Stand ye in holy places; keep yourselves clean and unspotted from the sins of the world, that when His judgments pass over the earth, like Israel of old you may be preserved. HEBER J. GRANT, ANTHONY W. IVINS, CHARLES W. NIBLEY.
