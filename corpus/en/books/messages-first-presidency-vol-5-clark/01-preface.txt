James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 5 ( 1971)

Preface

The same format has been followed in this volume as in the previous volumes in this series.

While both Roberts (CHC) and Joseph Fielding Smith (ECH) give brief summaries of the administration of President Heber J. Grant, which forms the major portion of the material in this volume, I have relied increasingly on my own historical background for the notes for the volume.

Acknowledgment is made, as for the previous volumes in the series, to the many agencies and persons who have given helpful suggestions and have cooperated in the locating of the Messages comprising this volume.

I am indebted once again to the entire staff of the L.D.S. Church Historian's Office Library for their excellent cooperation in this project. Without this cooperation and their unselfish interest the volumes in this series would not be possible.

I also express my gratitude and appreciation to Mr. Marvin Wallin, manager of Bookcraft, Inc., who made it possible to continue the series beyond volume four.

To the reading public I express appreciation for your continued support of the series. JAMES R. CLARK, ED. D.
