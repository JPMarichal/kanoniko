Arizona Temple Day, August, 1920

1920-August-Improvement Era 23:1030, September, 1920.

The following concise statement on the Arizona Temple is given by Andrew Jenson, Assistant Church Historian:

"A site for a temple having been selected at Mesa, Maricopa Co., Arizona, the ground was dedicated Nov. 28, 1921, by Pres. Heber J. Grant. Construction work commenced April 25, 1922, and the building dedicated by Pres. Heber J. Grant Oct. 23, 1927." (Encyclopedic History, p. 30.)

In this announcement appearing in the Improvement Era, the full membership of the Church were given an opportunity to contribute to its erection.

ARIZONA TEMPLE DAY

In order that every Latter-day Saint who desires to contribute may do so, Sunday, September 12 has been set aside as "Arizona Temple Day." It is requested that each bishop in the Church provide means by which all who desire may make these donations, and we hope each bishop will encourage every member of his ward to become a donor. Further details concerning the handling of the funds, record of donors, and the manner of remitting the donations will be given by the Presiding Bishopric, who will collect these funds. HEBER J. GRANT, ANTHON H. LUND, CHARLES W. PENROSE, First Presidency.
