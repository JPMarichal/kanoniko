James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 5 ( 1971)

Notice of postponed General Conference, May, 1919

1919-May-Improvement Era 22:636, May, 1919.

As indicated in notes earlier in this volume, an influenza epidemic of serious proportions had caused the cancelling of all Latter-day Saint Church services during the latter part of 1918 and the forepart of 1919. The General Conference of the Church, normally held annually early in April, was postponed. This announcement by the First Presidency called for the convening of the postponed Conference on June 1, 1919.

President Heber J. Grant and his counselors who signed this notice had been sustained and set apart in a meeting of the Council of Twelve Apostles on November 23, 1918, held in the Salt Lake Temple. They were not sustained in a solemn assembly of the quorums of the priesthood until this postponed General Conference in June, 1919.

GENERAL CONFERENCE NOTICE

The postponed eighty-ninth annual General Conference of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints will convene in the Tabernacle, Salt Lake City, on Sunday, June 1, 1919, at 10 o'clock a.m., with sessions also on Monday and Tuesday, June 2 and 3.

The general Church Priesthood meeting will be held in the Tabernacle Monday evening, June 2, at 7 o'clock; reunions should be arranged so as not to conflict with this meeting. HEBER J. GRANT, ANTHON H. LUND, CHARLES W. PENROSE, First Presidency.
