Naming of Alberta Temple, May 20, 1921

1921-May 20-Original letter. Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

On July 27, 1913, President Joseph F. Smith dedicated the temple site. On November 9, 1913, the ground-breaking ceremony took place. On September 19, 1915, David O. McKay laid the cornerstone. On September 23, 1917, the capstone was laid. The edifice was completed during the summer of 1921 and here given its name of Alberta Temple by the First Presidency and Council of the Twelve on May 20, 1921. President Heber J. Grant offered the dedicatory prayer on August 26, 1923.

The First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints Salt Lake City, Utah May 20th, 1921 Bishop C. W. Nibley and Counselors, City.

Dear Brethren:

At our Council meeting of First Presidency and Twelve yesterday it was unanimously decided to name our temple in Canada the Alberta Temple, after the Province of Alberta.

Your Brethren, HEBER J. GRANT, CHARLES W. PENROSE, ANTHONY W. IVINS, First Presidency.
