Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)

Jesus Healeth a Woman on the Sabbath

SCLuke 13 10Luke 13 11Luke 13 12Luke 13 13Luke 13 14Luke 13 15Luke 13 16Luke 13 17This particular healing is recorded in detail, not because of its miraculous nature, for many others equalled or surpassed it in this respect, but because it took place on the Sabbath day. See John 5:1-16. Out of the occurrences surrounding it we learn:

SCLuke 13 10Luke 13 11Luke 13 12Luke 13 13Luke 13 14Luke 13 15Luke 13 16Luke 13 17(1) For 18 years the woman had been bound by Satan with "a spirit of infirmity," presumably meaning that some mental or spiritual affliction attended her physical illness. Though Satan may rejoice in the afflictions—whether physical, mental, or spiritual—which befall mortal men, it is not to be assumed that he has power to impose them, except in isolated instances where people have complied with laws which permit such an imposition; otherwise, Satan would shackle all men with ills so drastic as to destroy them.

SCLuke 13 10Luke 13 11Luke 13 12Luke 13 13Luke 13 14Luke 13 15Luke 13 16Luke 13 17(2) Jesus appears to have sought out this woman and performed the miracle on his own initiate on the Sabbath to teach the principle that it is lawful to do good and work righteousness on that holy day.

SCLuke 13 10Luke 13 11Luke 13 12Luke 13 13Luke 13 14Luke 13 15Luke 13 16Luke 13 17(3) Though it was not always his habit so to do, in this instance he performed the healing by the laying on of hands, a physical performance which helps faith increase in the hearts of afflicted persons.

SCLuke 13 10Luke 13 11Luke 13 12Luke 13 13Luke 13 14Luke 13 15Luke 13 16Luke 13 17(4) Fake beliefs—as those relative to sabbath observance lead to bigotry and place their adherents in a position where they deny and reject the pure mercies of God and become hypocrites.

SCLuke 13 10Luke 13 11Luke 13 12Luke 13 13Luke 13 14Luke 13 15Luke 13 16Luke 13 17(5) Every soul is honorable and deserving of dignified respect in the eyes of Deity. This woman, though decrepit and infirm, though shackled with atrophied muscles and seemingly afflicted mentally and spiritually as well, was hailed by Jesus as a daughter of Abraham, a soul worthy of the blessings and mercies of Abraham's God.
