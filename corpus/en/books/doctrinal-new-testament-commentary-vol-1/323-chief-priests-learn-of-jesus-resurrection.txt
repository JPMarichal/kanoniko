Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)

Chief Priests Learn of Jesus' Resurrection

SCMatt 28 11Matt 28 12Matt 28 13Matt 28 14Matt 28 15Pilate had placed soldiers at the disposal of the chief priests to use in guarding the tomb. (Matt. 27:62-66.) These now reported to those under whose temporary command they served what had happened, thus bearing testimony that Jesus had risen from the dead. The resultant scheme to claim the body was stolen while they slept, aside from the affirmative evidence to the contrary, is absurd on its face, for they could not have known what transpired while they slept.

SCMatt 28 11Matt 28 12Matt 28 13Matt 28 14Matt 28 15Thus to those who loved and served him, the Lord appeared, and they saw and knew of his risen status. But to those who hated and slew him, word of his resurrection came by way of Gentile overlords who themselves neither believed nor understood the strange events of which they were witnesses.
