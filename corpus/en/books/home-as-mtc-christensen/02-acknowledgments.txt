Joe J. Christensen, Barbara K. Christensen, Making Your Home a Missionary Training Center ( 1985)

Acknowledgments

We appreciate the suggestion and invitation that came to us to write this book. As a result, we were launched into a joint process that has proved to us to be stimulating and motivational.

To bring any writing chore to the point of publication requires the skilled and cooperative efforts of a multitude. Among them, we particularly thank Ronald A. Millett, Eleanor Knowles, Jack Lyon (who edited the manuscript), Kent Ware, Leslie Jack, Cheryl Coultas, and the rest of the effective team at Deseret Book Company.

The patient and effective typing and re-typing by Jan Westbrook are deeply appreciated.

Of inestimable value was the reading of the various drafts of the manuscript by family and friends who were kind enough to be candid.

Our children were of assistance not only in writing this book, but also in setting an example in our home throughout the years. Their lives lent much of the experience that has blessed us beyond our capacity to describe.

And finally, we thank the missionaries by the thousands who collectively and individually were and continue to be an inspiration. In many ways they constitute a modern miracle of unselfishness and idealism in a world where those qualities are not everywhere apparent.
