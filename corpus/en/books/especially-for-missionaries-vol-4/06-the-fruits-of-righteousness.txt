Ed J. Pinegar, Especially for Missionaries, vol. 4 ( 1997)

The Fruits of Righteousness

To those who don't belong to the Church, Alma spoke "by way of invitation, saying: Come and be baptized unto repentance, that ye also may be partakers of the fruit of the tree of life" (verse 62).

What is that fruit? It is the love of God. It is the thing that will bring us happiness. It is desirable above all other things to make one happy.

My beloved brothers and sisters, elders and sisters of the most high God, disciples and ambassadors of Christ, I testify to you that God lives; I know him. Christ is our Savior; he died for us. Joseph Smith was the prophet of the Restoration, and he is my friend, as is my Savior. We are led by a prophet today. This is Jesus Christ's church, and the Book of Mormon is the word of God. May we all be like Christ today, and each day, by being obedient, loving, and faithful missionaries.

Now is the time, and you are the ones who are instruments in the hands of God to bring souls unto Christ. I love you; I honor you. I thank God that you are here to carry the gospel to his children here upon the earth. May God bless you in your endeavors to do so.
