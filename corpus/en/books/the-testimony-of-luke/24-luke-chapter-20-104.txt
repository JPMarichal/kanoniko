Introduction

Analysis

All of the noxious smells from the conspiracy against the Savior pour out of these verses, daubing his last days with the sticky gasses of hate and loathing. Naturally, Jesus, if left unchecked, presents a direct challenge to both the status quo and the powers that rest comfortably and luxuriously on temple authorities.
