Analysis

As before, the Savior brings us inside a home (see 11:5â8), in this case to witness interactions between a father and son. At the top of his list, Jesus compellingly illustrates that God will give both more generously and more regularly than humans do. He frames this divine gift-giving within the larger picture of prayer, if we will only ask and knock. To achieve his end, he speaks in hyperbole, in exaggerated terms.
