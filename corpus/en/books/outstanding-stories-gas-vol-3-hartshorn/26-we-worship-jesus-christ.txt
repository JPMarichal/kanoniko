"We Worship Jesus Christ"

While I was a mission president in Germany, I one day received a letter from an investigator who asked this question: "I am told that you believe in Joseph Smith. Tell me frankly, don't you believe in Jesus Christ? Aren't you Mormons Christians?" I was delighted to receive that letter, for nothing pleases a missionary more than to be asked, "Tell me more about the Mormons!"

I could answer that letter readily and did so. I wrote that if this were the church of Joseph Smith, it would bear the name of Joseph Smith. However, it is The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and bears the name of Jesus Christ. We believe that Joseph Smith was a prophet of God who bore personal witness of Jesus Christ and God the Eternal Father, for he saw them and talked with them and testified to the whole world that they live! Because of this knowledge which he taught us, we love Joseph Smith and revere his memory as a prophet. But we worship Jesus Christ as our God, the Only Begotten Son of God in the flesh, our living Advocate with the Father.

We testify with Joseph Smith that he was born, lived, and died on this earth. Greater than this, however, is our faith and testimony that he was resurrected from the dead and that through the atonement which he made for us he became our God and our Savior. He is a living Lord who sits on the right hand of the Father. Through the blood of Jesus Christ we are saved, and through his grace we become his sons and daughters in and through the waters of baptism. Indeed, we do believe in Jesus Christ; every prayer we utter and every priesthood ordinance we perform is done in the name of Jesus Christ. There are no more ardent worshipers of Jesus Christ, the Lord, than the so-called Mormons.

"Ye Shall Know His Name," BYU Speeches of the Year, November 14, 1967, pp. 1-2.
