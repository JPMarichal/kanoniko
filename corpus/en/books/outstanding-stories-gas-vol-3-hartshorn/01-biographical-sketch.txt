Leon R. Hartshorn, Outstanding Stories by General Authorities, vol. 3 ( 1974)

ELDER WILLIAM H. BENNETT

Biographical Sketch

Elder William H. Bennett, an Assistant to the Council of Twelve of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, was appointed to this position at the 1970 April general world conference of the Church in Salt Lake City.

At the time of his appointment he was director of extension services at Utah State University in Logan, Utah. Prior to that he had been dean of the College of Agriculture, assistant director of extension services, and associate professor of agronomy. He has bachelor and master's degrees from Utah State University and a Ph.D. from the University of Wisconsin.

He has been awarded the President's Citation from the Soil Conservation Society of America. During World War II he was an infantry captain.

At the time of his appointment as an Assistant to the Council of Twelve, he was Regional Representative for the Church for the Cache and Logan regions in Northern Utah and Southern Idaho, a post he had held since 1967. His other Church positions have been as a member of the Church's Priesthood Welfare and Priesthood Missionary committees, a counselor in the East Cache Stake presidency, counselor in a stake Sunday School superintendency, member of a stake Sunday School board, and genealogical chairman for the East Cache and Carbon stakes.

An avid booster of athletics, he participated in the Canadian Olympic Trials in 1936 in the shot put and discus events. He also participated in the Pacific Army Olympics in 1946.

Elder Bennett, a native of Taber, Alberta, Canada, was born November 5, 1910, a son of William Alvin and Mary Walker Bennett. He married the former Patricia June Christensen. The Bennetts have five daughters and one son.
