Leon R. Hartshorn, Outstanding Stories by General Authorities, vol. 3 ( 1974)

ELDER THORPE B. ISAACSON

Biographical Sketch

Thorpe B. Isaacson, an Assistant to the Council of Twelve, was widely known in educational, business, and religious fields. He served as a counselor to the late President David O. McKay from October 29, 1965, to January 18, 1970.

Prior to his call to the First Presidency, Elder Isaacson served for fifteen years as an Assistant to the Council of Twelve and as a member of the Presiding Bishopric of the Church. He resumed his position as an Assistant to the Twelve upon the death of President McKay.

Elder Isaacson was born in Ephraim, Utah, September 6, 1898, to Martin and Mary Beal Isaacson. He attended Snow Academy in Ephraim, Brigham Young University, and Utah State University, all in Utah; and the University of California at Berkeley. On June 10, 1920, he married Lula Maughan Jones of Wellsville, Utah, in the Salt Lake Temple. They are the parents of a son and a daughter.

During his school years, Elder Isaacson was prominent in athletics, being chosen all-state basketball guard in 1917. He later served sixteen years as an athletic coach, teacher, principal, and superintendent in various school districts in Idaho.

He was a member of the Utah State University Board of Trustees for twelve years and was elected seven consecutive terms as president of the board. On June 2, 1956, he was honored with an LL.D. degree from that institution.

In business, Elder Isaacson was eminently successful in life insurance and real estate. He was general agent in Utah and Idaho for the Lincoln National Life Insurance Company, which named him as its most outstanding representative in America. Elder Isaacson passed away on November 9, 1970.
