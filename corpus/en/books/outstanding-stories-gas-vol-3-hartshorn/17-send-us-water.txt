Leon R. Hartshorn, Outstanding Stories by General Authorities, vol. 3 ( 1974)

"Send Us Water"

Some may feel that in some far-off part of the Church there is not much hope. Sometimes we say, "Well, if you could send us a General Authority more often, we would be more inspired." The Church is getting too big, as Brother Lee has explained, for the General Authorities to attend all of the conferences.

I am reminded of what happened to a captain of a ship down in the South Atlantic. He had run out of fresh water. His crew were athirst. Another ship hove in sight, and he signaled, "Send us water" and the signal come back, "Let down your buckets, there is fresh water all around you. You are in the Gulf Stream." They let down their buckets and found it was true. They had not realized that the course of the Gulf Stream, driven out into the briny deep, had maintained its virtues, so to speak. They were able to save themselves by that which was all around them, yet they did not know it.

Brethren, there is available to you, wherever you are, the blessings, the opportunities, the privileges of teaching the gospel of Christ, if you will let down your bucket into the gulf stream of the Holy Spirit, which is everywhere.

Conference Report, October 1963, p. 85.
