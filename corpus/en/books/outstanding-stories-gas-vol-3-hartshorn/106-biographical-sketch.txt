Leon R. Hartshorn, Outstanding Stories by General Authorities, vol. 3 ( 1974)

BISHOP H. BURKE PETERSON

Biographical Sketch

H. Burke Peterson, a well-known engineer from Phoenix, Arizona, was named first counselor in the Presiding Bishopric of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints April 6, 1972.

For two years prior to moving to Salt Lake City on his full-time assignment as a General Authority of the Church, Bishop Peterson was a Regional Representative of the Council of the Twelve. He served previously as president of the Phoenix North Stake for five years and as bishop of Phoenix Eighth Ward for six years.

A partner in the Engineering Corporation of America, Bishop Peterson received the B.S. degree in civil engineering from the University of Arizona in 1947 and the M.S. degree in 1948 from Utah State University, Logan, Utah. He is a member of the American Society of Civil Engineers and the National Society of Professional Engineers.

Bishop Peterson was born September 19, 1923, in Salt Lake City. He served in the U.S. Navy from 1942 to 1946 as an officer in the Civil Engineering Corps. In 1947 he married the former Brookie Cardon in the Mesa Temple, and they are the parents of five daughters.
