ELDER ELRAY L. CHRISTIANSEN

Biographical Sketch

Elder ElRay L. Christiansen, Assistant to the Council of the Twelve, has led an active and varied life in civic and cultural affairs and in the field of education.

He is a graduate of the Utah State University, where he earned a degree in agronomy. This was followed by graduate work at the University of Utah and at Brigham Young University. An agriculturalist, he was engaged to make a soil survey of lands along the Bear River. He assisted in establishing in secondary schools the Smith-Hughes program for agriculture. Employed by the United States Government, he spent time in grazing reconnaissance work for the U.S. Forest Service.

He also served in the public school system and was principal of two high schools.

He married Lewella Rees in the Manti Temple. They have one son and two daughters: Mrs. R. Eugene Elwood, Logan, Utah; Dr. John R. Christiansen, Brigham Young University, Provo, Utah; and Mrs. Robert Murdock, Salt Lake City.

After their marriage, Elder and Sister Christiansen were called to the Central States Mission as missionaries. Later he presided four and one-half years as president of the Texas-Louisiana Mission. He has served in Sunday School and MIA stake presidencies and as a high councilor. He has served as a bishop, counselor in two stake presidencies, and later as president of the East Cache Stake. While residing in Logan, he affiliated with Rotary International.

In 1943 he was appointed president of the Logan Temple, where he served more than eight years. From 1954 through 1961 he was president of the Salt Lake Temple.

Since 1951, he has served as an Assistant to the Council of the Twelve, and in 1961 was named coordinator of all the temples in the Church. In 1971 he was assigned supervision of missionary work in the mid-American states.
