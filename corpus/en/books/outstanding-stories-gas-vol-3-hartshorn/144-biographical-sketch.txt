ELDER HENRY D. TAYLOR

Biographical Sketch

Elder Henry D. Taylor, who for fourteen years was managing director of the Mormon welfare program, is an assistant to the Council of the Twelve. He was called to this position in April 1958 while serving as president of the California Mission.

Born in Provo, Utah, on November 22, 1903, he is a son of Arthur N. and Maria Dixon Taylor. He was graduated from Brigham Young University in 1929 and in 1960 was presented the Alumni Distinguished Service Award from that school. He earned a master's degree from the New York University School of Retailing in 1937.

Elder Taylor was a prominent business executive in Provo, Utah, before his assignment to the California Mission in 1955. Previous Church experience includes a mission to the Eastern States in 1924-26, where he served as president of the Connecticut District and mission secretary. He also served as president of a seventies quorum, 1927-33; on the Sharon Stake high council, 1933-40; as Sharon Stake clerk, 1940-44; as bishop of Pleasant View Ward, 1944-46; as president of Sharon Stake, 1946-52; as president of East Sharon Stake, 1952-55; and as welfare chairman of Central Utah and Mt. Timpanogas regions, 1951-55.

He married Alta Hansen of Richfield, Utah, in the Salt Lake Temple on December 26, 1929. She died on July 6, 1967. On September 9, 1968, he married Ethelyn Peterson Taylor.
