Jerreld L. Newquist, George Q. Cannon, Gospel Truth: Discourses and Writings of President George Q. Cannon ( 1957)

Publisher's Preface

George Q. Cannon served as an apostle in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints for more than forty years and was first counselor in the First Presidency of the Church for approximately twenty years. A printer by trade, he was editor of such publications as the Juvenile Instructor, the Millennial Star, and the Deseret News, and established a publishing company, George Q. Cannon and Sons, the parent organization of Deseret Book Company.

Through his writings and sermons, President Cannon taught clearly and explicitly the doctrines of the Church. He had great understanding of the principles and a remarkable ability to convey that understanding to his readers and listeners.

In February 1957, Jerreld L. Newquist compiled two volumes of excerpts from President Cannon's writings that were published under the title Gospel Truth. These volumes went through several prints, including a 1974 revision. This Classics in Mormon Literature Series volume combines those two volumes to bring this material to a new generation of Latter-day Saints. Those who read and study these words of wisdom will find them to be an important source of gospel doctrine and truth. George Q. Cannon himself summarizes the benefits that can come to the honest seeker of truth:

"Too great a value cannot be placed upon the possession of truth. It is indeed beyond estimate. . . . The great strength of the latter-day work in which we are engaged is found in its truth. This constitutes its great power and makes it all powerful. . . . Truth cannot be changed. It cannot be altered. It is eternal. All other things may perish; the heavens and the earth may pass away; but the truth cannot be destroyed." (Juvenile Instructor 30:273-74.)
