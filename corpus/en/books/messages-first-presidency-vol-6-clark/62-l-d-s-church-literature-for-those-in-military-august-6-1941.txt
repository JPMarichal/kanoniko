James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

L.D.S. Church literature for those in military, August 6, 1941

1941-August 6-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Reports reaching the First Presidency indicate that L.D.S. young men in the military service are not being supplied with Church literature and Church books. A program to supply that need on the part of L.D.S. military personnel, especially the youth in "army camps," is outlined. (Compiler's resume.)
