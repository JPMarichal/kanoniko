Living allowances of General Authorities, April 18, 1950

1950-April 18-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The living allowances of the General Authorities of the Church increased 30 percent retroactive to January 1, 1950. (Compiler's resume.)
