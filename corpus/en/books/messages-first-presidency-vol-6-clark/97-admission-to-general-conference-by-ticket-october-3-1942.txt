James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Admission to General Conference by ticket, October 3, 1942

1942-October 3-Improvement Era 45:640, October, 1942.

Easing of earlier restrictions on travel during wartime now allowed the First Presidency to expand the attendance at the October General Conference of the Church from the General Authorities of the Church and Stake Presidencies as had been the case at the April 1942 Conference by "broadening the base of representation of our Priesthood organizations." Admission was still by ticket, but now stake patriarchs, high councilmen, presidencies of Melchizedek Priesthood quorums, ward bishoprics, mission presidents, temple presidencies and branch presidencies were invited. Attendance still did not include the general membership of the Church. (Compiler's resume.)
