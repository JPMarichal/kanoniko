James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Mormon pioneer commemoration, June 28, 1941

1941-June 28-Deseret News, Church Section, June 28, 1941.

Outline of a program to be presented in commemoration of the arrival of the Mormon pioneers in the Great Salt Lake Valley. The program to be presented in July, 1941, would also look forward to the Mormon Centennial in Utah in 1947. (Compiler's resume.)
