James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Special day of fasting and prayer, August 18, 1945

1945-August 18-Deseret News, Church Section, August 18, 1945.

All Latter-day Saints urged to make Sunday, August 19, a day of fasting and prayer in accord with a proclamation of Harry S. Truman, President of the United States. The surrender of Japan had come on August 14, 1945.
