James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Ward fast meeting not to be held on General Conference Sundays, March 20, 1946

1946-March 20-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Time for the monthly fast meetings in the wards of the Church to be adjusted so that they do not fall on the same Sunday as the General Conference of the Church. (Compiler's resume.)
