James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Invasion of Europe, June 6, 1944

1944-June 6-Improvement Era 47:440, July, 1944.

The First Presidency of the Church issued the following statement soon after hearing that Allied forces had begun the invasion of Europe.

June 6, 1944

We have been asked for a comment upon the invasion.

We feel this is a day, not for comment, but for prayer for our loved ones who are in the service and for the triumph of righteousness. HEBER J. GRANT, J. REUBEN CLARK, JR., DAVID O. MCKAY, First Presidency.
