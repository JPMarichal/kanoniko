James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Survey of Church members in military service, December 19, 1942

1942-December 19-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Stake Presidents and Mission Presidents are instructed concerning a Church-wide survey of members of the Church in military service world-wide.

The first Church-wide survey had been made in March, 1942. In October, 1942, a second survey was made calling for names and addresses of persons rather than statistical information called for in the first survey. Church headquarters now had (December, 1942) an alphabetical file. It was important to keep the file updated so that the Church might keep in touch with these military personnel. "We feel that no greater missionary responsibility rests upon the Church today than that of keeping in touch with the thousands of Latter-day Saint men who are so suddenly thrown into new and strange environments, which too frequently have connected with them great temptations cunningly devised by the Adversary to alienate their affections, undermine their faith, and corrupt their morals."
