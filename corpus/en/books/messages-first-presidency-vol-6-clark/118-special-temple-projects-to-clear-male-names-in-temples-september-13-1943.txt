James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Special temple projects to clear male names in temples, September 13, 1943

1943-September 13-Original circular letter, L. D. S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah; also Improvement Era 46: 698, November, 1943.

Special temple projects inaugurated for Melchizedek Priesthood quorums to clear the backlog of male names awaiting endowments for the dead in all L.D.S. temples. (Compiler's resume.)
