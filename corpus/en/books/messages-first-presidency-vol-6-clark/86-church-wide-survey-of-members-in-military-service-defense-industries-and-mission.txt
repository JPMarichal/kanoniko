James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Church-wide survey of members in military service, defense industries and missionary service, March 23, 1942

1942-March 23-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

This cover letter and the document following it elicited information for a Church-wide survey of the population of the Church engaged in military service, defense industry service and missionary service.

Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints Office of the First Presidency Salt Lake City, Utah March 23, 1942 To Presidents of Stakes:

Dear Brethren:

We enclose herewith a copy of an instruction which we are to-day sending out to the Bishops of Wards, including, of course, the Bishops of the Wards of your Stake.

Will you be good enough immediately to get in touch with the Bishops and assist them in any way that they may need assistance in furnishing us immediately the information called for? We would appreciate your immediate and urgent attention to this matter.

Faithfully your brethren, HEBER J. GRANT, J. REUBEN CLARK, JR., DAVID O. MCKAY, First Presidency.

Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints Office of the First Presidency Salt Lake City, Utah March 23, 1942

IMPORTANT-IMMEDIATE

To Bishops of Wards Presidents of Branches Presidents of Missions

We wish you to use the utmost care to see that the figures you give us are accurate.
