James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Expense Allowances of Stake Presidents, January 29, 1941

1941-January 29-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Expense allowances of Stake Presidents should not be considered as compensation for services. Readjustment of these allowances was being made from a uniform amount for all Stake Presidents to a pro-rata basis based on actual travel and other expenses. (Compiler's resume.)
