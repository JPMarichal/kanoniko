Conference, March 20, 1947

1947-March 20-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

A special meeting in the Salt Lake Temple planned as one of the sessions of the April 1947 General Conference, with instructions for admission to this meeting. (Compiler's resume.)
