James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Serological tests before marriage in Utah temples, January 31, 1949

1949-January 31-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Instructions of the First Presidency of June 30, 1941, August 17, 1942, and July 6, 1943, on the necessity of couples being married in the temples in Utah complying with Utah laws requiring medical serological tests. Bishops should not issue recommends to Utah couples who go out of the state to be married and then return expecting to be sealed in Utah temples without complying with the serological tests required by law. (Compiler's resume.)
