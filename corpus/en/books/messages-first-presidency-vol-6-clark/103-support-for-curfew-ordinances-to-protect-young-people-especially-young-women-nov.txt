James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Support for "curfew" ordinances to protect young people, especially young women, November 23, 1942

1942-November 23-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The Salt Lake City Commission had passed a "curfew" ordinance calling for all minor persons under the age of fourteen years to be off the streets and out of public places between the hours of 10 p.m. and 4 a.m. and for minor girls under eighteen years of age to be off the streets and out of public places between the hours of 12 midnight and 5 a.m.

The Salt Lake Commission was commended for the ordinance and all Church officers throughout the Church were advised to schedule Church meetings so that the young people of the Church would be in compliance with the spirit of this ordinance, though not legally bound by it outside the confines of Salt Lake City.
