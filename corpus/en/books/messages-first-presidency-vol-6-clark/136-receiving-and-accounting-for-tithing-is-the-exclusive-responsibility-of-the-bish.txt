Receiving and accounting for tithing is the exclusive responsibility of the Bishop, February 24, 1945

1945-February 24-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints Office of the First Presidency Salt Lake City, Utah February 24, 1945

To Stake Presidencies and Bishoprics:

One of the most important duties of the Bishop of a Ward is to receive and account for the tithes of the people. By divine appointment the Bishop should give this duty his personal supervision.

It has come to our attention that there are some Bishops in the Church who share this responsibility with their wives or with other members of the family. Such a practice is contrary to Church instruction, and should be discontinued at once. In this respect there should be no deviation from the following instructions.

"Neither the Bishop's wife, nor any member of his family, should have access to the tithing records, and should not be authorized to issue tithing receipts.

"There should be no open discussion by the Bishopric of the amount of tithing paid by any of the members. The amount of tithing paid is strictly a confidential matter and should not be given any publicity.

"Managers of Bishops' storehouses are not authorized to issue tithing receipts. Bishops of Wards and Presidents of Independent Branches are the only ones authorized to receive tithing and they alone will issue tithing receipts."

Will you please call this matter to the attention of the proper authorities at the earliest opportunity.

Sincerely yours, HEBER J. GRANT, J. REUBEN CLARK, JR., DAVID O. MCKAY, First Presidency.
