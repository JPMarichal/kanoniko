James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Christmas and Church Security Plan, December, 1936

1936-December-Improvement Era 39:739, December, 1936.

With launching of the Church Security Plan, reference to it became more and more frequent. The final two paragraphs of this message read:

As Christmas approaches let us double our efforts to bring faith, happiness, and cheer into the lives of our fellow men. Let those who are blessed with comforts and luxuries share with those who are less fortunate, not with an air of superiority, but with an attitude of appreciation expressive of the feeling that it is a privilege to invite another out of the shade and cold of penury and discouragement into the sunshine of kindness and brotherly love.

Let us continue our efforts to make life better and brighter, and thus assist in carrying good tidings of great joy to all people. May all who contribute to the success of the Church Security Plan work with an eye single to God's glory, applying the principles of righteousness as personified in the life and teachings of the Savior to the end that comfort and peace may abound in homes, may be practiced in society, and applied by the nations of the world.

Only thus can peace and goodwill be established among men. (Excerpt.)
