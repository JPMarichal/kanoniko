James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Expiration dates for temple recommends, July, 1942

1942-July-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

As a matter of Church procedure, the dates of expiration of temple recommends was changed so that Bishops would not have interviews for temple recommends and tithing settlement both falling the last week in December. (Compiler's resume.)
