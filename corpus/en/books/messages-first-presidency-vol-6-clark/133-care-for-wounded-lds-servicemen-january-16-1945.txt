James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Care for wounded LDS servicemen, January 16, 1945

1945-January 16-Improvement Era 48:84, February, 1945.

Wounded L.D.S. servicemen were being returned to the United States for treatment. Their "dogtags" did not always identify them as L.D.S. Parents and relatives are requested to supply Church headquarters with names and addresses of the wounded so that the Church could see to their needs. (Compiler's resume)
