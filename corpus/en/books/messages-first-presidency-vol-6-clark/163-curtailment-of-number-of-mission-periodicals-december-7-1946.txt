James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Curtailment of number of mission periodicals, December 7, 1946

1946-December 7-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

To curtail the proliferating number of L.D.S. mission newspapers and periodicals in the interest of saving paper, ink, and the time of missionaries. (Compiler's resume.)
