James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Ward and stake clerks not members of bishoprics and stake presidencies, September 24, 1942

1942-September 24-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Ward and stake clerks are not members of Bishoprics or Stake Presidencies. Their services nonetheless are valuable. (Compiler's resume.)
