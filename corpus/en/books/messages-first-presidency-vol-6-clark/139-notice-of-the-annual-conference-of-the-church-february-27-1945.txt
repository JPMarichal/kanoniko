James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Notice of the Annual Conference of the Church, February 27, 1945

1945-February 27-Liahona 42:421-422, February 27, 1945.

Notice of the forthcoming April 1945 General Conference of the Church. The War Committee on Conventions of the United States had ruled that the holding of the General Conference would not be contrary to the rules and regulations of the committee governing the holding of conventions in wartime.
