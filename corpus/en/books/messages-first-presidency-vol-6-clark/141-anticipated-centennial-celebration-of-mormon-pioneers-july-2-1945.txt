James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Anticipated centennial celebration of Mormon pioneers, July 2, 1945

1945-July 2-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

President Heber J. Grant died May 14, 1945. One week later on May 21, 1945, Elder George Albert Smith was sustained as President of the Church in a meeting in the Salt Lake Temple. He chose J. Reuben Clark, Jr., and David O. McKay as his counselors, both of whom had been serving in the same capacity with President Grant.

The centennial celebration of the arrival of the Mormon pioneers in Great Salt Lake Valley is anticipated by this letter, although still two years away.
