James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Those receiving induction notices for military service not to be called as missionaries, July 10, 1941

1941-July 10-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

At the time this statement was made, the Japanese bombing of Pearl Harbor which precipitated entrance of the United States into the war was six months in the future. After the entrance of the United States, the First Presidency in the April 1942 General Conference of the Church made a more extended statement in which they said, "The Church is and must be against war . . . but the Church membership are citizens or subjects of sovereignties over which the Church has no control. The 1941 statement provided that young men who had registered under the Selective Service Act and who had received notice of induction for military service were not to be recommended for missionary service by the Church.
