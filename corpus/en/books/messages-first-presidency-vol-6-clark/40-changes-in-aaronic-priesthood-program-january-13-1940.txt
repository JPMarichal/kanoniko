Changes in Aaronic Priesthood Program, January 13, 1940

1940-January 13-Deseret News, Church Section, January 13, 1940, p. 3.

Changes in the Aaronic Priesthood plan of the Church. Quorum supervisors now to be known as quorum advisors. Average attendance at meetings for standard quorum awards reduced from 60 percent to 50 percent. Bishop as president of the priests' quorum to attend all of their meetings. (Compiler's resume.)
