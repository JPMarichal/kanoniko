James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Patriarchal Blessings, September 13, 1935

1935-September 13-Copy of original letter L. D. S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Members of the Church coming to the Stakes from Missions where there are no patriarchs may bring a recommend signed by their Branch President and Mission President and receive a patriarchal blessing from a Stake Patriarch. (Compiler's resume.)
