James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

No missionary farewells on Sunday, December 26, 1945

1945-December 26-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

A request that farewells for missionaries not be held on Sunday. When missionaries return from an honorable mission it will be appropriate to ask them to occupy time in a ward sacrament meeting to report their missionary labors. (Compiler's resume.)
