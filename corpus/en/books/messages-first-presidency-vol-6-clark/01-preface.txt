James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Preface

Acknowledgment is made, as for the previous volumes, to the many agencies and individuals who have given helpful suggestions and have cooperated in the locating of the Messages comprising this volume. The debt I owe for this assistance has now widened until I hesitate to be specific lest I fail to acknowledge portions of that indebtedness. Again, as with previous volumes the staffs of the Library and Archives Departments at both the headquarters of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Salt Lake City and at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah, have been very helpful.

Two colleagues of mine at Brigham Young University, Dr. Richard Cowan and Edward J. Brandt, were extremely helpful in providing copies of documents from their own collections.

Again I acknowledge the kindness and cooperation of Marvin Wallin and George Bickerstaff of Bookcraft, Inc. Their continued support of the series is hereby acknowledged and profound thanks given. JAMES R. CLARK
