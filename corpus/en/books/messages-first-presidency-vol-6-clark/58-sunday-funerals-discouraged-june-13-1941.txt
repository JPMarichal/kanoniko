James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Sunday Funerals Discouraged, June 13, 1941

1941-June 13-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The holding of funerals for L.D.S. Church members on Sundays should be discontinued for two reasons: interference with regular Church meetings and the necessity of Sunday work by morticians and their employees. (Compiler's resume.)
