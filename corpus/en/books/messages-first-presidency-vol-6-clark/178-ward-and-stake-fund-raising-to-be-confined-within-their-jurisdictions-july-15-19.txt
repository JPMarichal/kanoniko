James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Ward and stake fund-raising to be confined within their jurisdictions, July 15, 1947

1947-July 15-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Local L.D.S. ward or stake fund-raising projects are not to be extended on a regional or Church-wide basis for reasons of justice and fairness and because of the confusion and chaos that would result from such action within the Church. (Compiler's resume.)
