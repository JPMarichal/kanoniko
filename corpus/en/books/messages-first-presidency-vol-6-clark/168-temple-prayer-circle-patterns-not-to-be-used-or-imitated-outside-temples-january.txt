James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Temple prayer circle patterns not to be used or imitated outside temples, January 30, 1947

1947-January 30-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Prayer circle patterns as held in the temples of the Church are not to be duplicated or imitated outside of the temples. (Compiler's resume.)
