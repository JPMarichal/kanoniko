James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Meeting the spiritual needs of young men drafted into military service, March 10, 1947

1947-March 10-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

This letter of instructions to Presidents of Stakes and Missions outlines a plan to meet the spiritual needs of young men in the armed services.

If the plans of the military authorities of the United States are put into operation, it will mean the entry into the Service of great numbers of our young men. The spiritual guidance of these young men while in the Service is a matter of prime concern to the parents of the men and to the Church. (Excerpt.)
