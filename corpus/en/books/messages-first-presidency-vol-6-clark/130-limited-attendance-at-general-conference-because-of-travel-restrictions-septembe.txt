James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Limited attendance at General Conference because of travel restrictions, September 13, 1944

1944-September 13-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The attendance at the Conference (115th Semiannual) because of increasing difficulty in transportation, both by automobile and by railroad train, will be limited. (Excerpt.)
