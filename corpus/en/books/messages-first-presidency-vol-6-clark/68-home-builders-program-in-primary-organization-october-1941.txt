James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Home Builders program in Primary organization, October, 1941

1941-October-Original broadside, J. Reuben Clark, Jr., papers, Harold B. Lee Library, Brigham Young University.

Dear Home Builders:

Next to motherhood, the holiest thing we know on earth, is radiant, innocent, obedient girlhood.

Father and mother are ever watchful of each of you, their love runs out to you, to protect you from ill, to feed and clothe you, to nurse you when you are sick, to cherish you day by day that your lives may be filled with happiness, to teach you the restored Gospel that you may have joy here and exaltation hereafter.

We ask you to listen to the teachings of your parents, of your Primary and Sunday School teachers, of your bishop; do what they ask you to do. Then in the due time of the Lord you will become the new Mothers of Israel, the loftiest calling of womanhood and a prime purpose of your creation.

We bless you that you may be obedient, that you may live as the principles of the Gospel require, that you may be always pure and chaste.

The Lord loves you. His angels are always near to help you. Your guardian angels stand by you to see that no harm shall touch you, no evil thought disturb you. HEBER J. GRANT, October, 1941 J. REUBEN CLARK, JR., DAVID O. MCKAY, First Presidency.
