James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Church Security Survey, December 31, 1937

1937-December 31-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The First Presidency had conducted a Churchwide survey in 1933, and again in 1935, in connection with the launching of the Church Security plan or program. A survey is requested, to enable the General Church Security Committee to estimate food requirements for 1938-39. (Compiler's resume.)
