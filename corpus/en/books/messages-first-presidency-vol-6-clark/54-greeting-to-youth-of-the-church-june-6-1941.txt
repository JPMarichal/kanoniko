James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Greeting to Youth of the Church, June 6, 1941

1941-June 6-Improvement Era 44:393, 447, July, 1941.

(A greeting to the youth of the Church by President Heber J Grant, presented by President J. Reuben Clark, Jr., at the opening session of the 46th Annual June Conference of the M.I.A.)

President Grant recalls the circumstance of his call to the presidency of the YMMIA and his suggestion to President Wilford Woodruff on the establishment of the Improvement Era. He urged the youth of the Church to study the revelation contained in Section 121 of the Doctrine and Covenants.

Twice in my life I have prayed to the Lord, asking him to give me positions. The first prayer was when Moses Thatcher, having lost his standing as one of the Apostles, lost also his position as a counselor to President Woodruff, who presided over the Young Men's organization. (Excerpt.)
