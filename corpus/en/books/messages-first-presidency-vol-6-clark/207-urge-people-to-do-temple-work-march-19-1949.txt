James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Urge people to do temple work, March 19, 1949

1949-March 19-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Bishops and Stake Presidents not to call "as if they were called on a mission" persons to do work in the temples. "The people should be exhorted and urged to do temple work ." (Compiler's resume.)
