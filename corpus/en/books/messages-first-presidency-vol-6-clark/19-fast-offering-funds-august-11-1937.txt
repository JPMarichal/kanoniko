James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Fast Offering Funds, August 11, 1937

1937-August 11-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Fast offering funds of any ward will be used, first, for the benefit of the needy of that ward. Stakes having funds not needed in any ward of the stake will remit such funds to the welfare region. Regions having excess fast offering funds will remit them to the Presiding Bishop's Office. (Compiler's resume.)
