Special program on Scouting, January 20, 1942

1942-January 20-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Request of the General Superintendency of the Y.M.M.I.A. approved for a special program on Scouting in connection with the birthday of the Boy Scouts of America on February 8, 1942. (Compiler's resume.)
