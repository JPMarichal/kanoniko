James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Issuance of Temple Recommends, January 6, 1941

1941-January 6-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Bishops and Stake Presidents are urged to use thoroughness and care in the issuance of temple recommends to members.

The giving of a recommend to enter into the House of the Lord and to participate in the ordinances therein performed, is a most serious matter, not a mere formality to get the member past the entrance to the temple. Bishops should, of course, urge all members of their wards to fit themselves by right thinking and living, to enter the temple to do temple work, and should urge the worthy members to get recommends; nevertheless, Bishops should not indiscriminately urge all members of their wards to come and get recommends for that purpose. (Excerpt.)
