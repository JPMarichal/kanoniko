James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Trail Builders in Primary organization, September, 1941

1941-September-Original broadside, J. Reuben Clark, Jr., papers. Harold B. Lee Library, Brigham Young University.

Dear Trail Builders:

To you boys of the Church, you boys who will soon be men, who will become the leaders of the Church, who will in due time become the fathers of other boys who will grow as you boys are growing and will be leaders in their own turn, we send our love and greetings.

Just as the Lord loved the boy Samuel of old, so He loves you and watches over you. And just as Samuel did what Eli the high priest told him to do, and just as he helped Eli in his work as he was asked to help, for Eli was father and mother to Samuel, so you boys should listen to and do what your bishop and your parents-your fathers and mothers-teach and ask you to do.

We promise you that when you do this, the Lord will bless you and that you will grow up to be kind and good men, and useful to the Lord; and the Lord will love you and make you His servants to do His work here, and then you will make yourselves happy, and your parents, your bishops, and all the people will be happy with you. HEBER J. GRANT, September, 1941 J. REUBEN CLARK, JR., DAVID O. MCKAY, First Presidency.
