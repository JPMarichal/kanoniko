James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

New fieldhouse for Brigham Young University, May 12, 1950

1950-May 12-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

A new fieldhouse for Brigham Young University approved to be financed on a plan similar to ward buildings, with the Church general funds assuming 50 percent of the cost and the University, through its alumni in all the wards and stakes of the Church and from other donors, to raise the other 50 percent of the cost. (Compiler's resume.)
