Serological tests, summary of previous instructions, February 28, 1949

1949-February 28-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

This letter of instructions summarizes the various previous letters of instructions of June 30, 1941, June 27, 1941, August 17, 1942, and January 31, 1949, on serological tests by persons to whom temple recommends are to be issued.

February 28, 1949 To Presidents of Stakes, Bishops of Wards, Presidents of Missions and Presidents of Temples

Dear Brethren:

Heretofore, to wit on June 30, 1941, June 27, 1941, August 17, 1942, and January 31, 1949, we have communicated with you and issued instructions concerning the granting of temple recommends for couples to be married in our temples for time, or for time and eternity, or for eternity only, with reference to the necessity or non-necessity of securing certificates of serological tests by the persons to whom the recommends are to be issued.

As we still have questions arising with reference to these matters, we have deemed it wise to summarize the various instructions heretofore given for your information and convenient use:

Will you be good enough to be guided by the foregoing rules in the granting of all recommends and the marrying of all couples in our temples.

Faithfully yours, GEO. ALBERT SMITH, J. REUBEN CLARK, JR., DAVID O. MCKAY, First Presidency.
