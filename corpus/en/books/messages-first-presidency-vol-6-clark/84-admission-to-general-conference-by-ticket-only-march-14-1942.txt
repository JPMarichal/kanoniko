James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Admission to General Conference by ticket only, March 14, 1942

1942-March 14-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Admission to all sessions of the coming April General Conference to be by ticket only and confined to the General Authorities and Presidencies of Stakes. The priesthood sessions only of the conference to be held. (Compiler's resume.)
