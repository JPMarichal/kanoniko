James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Stake patriarchs and high priest quorum presidencies are not stake administrative officers, May 21, 1948

1948-April 26-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Stake patriarchs and presidencies of high priests quorums are not stake administrative officers and should meet with the Stake Presidency and high council only to receive special assignments or instructions. (Compiler's resume.)
