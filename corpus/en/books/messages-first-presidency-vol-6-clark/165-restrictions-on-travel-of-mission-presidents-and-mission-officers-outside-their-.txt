James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Restrictions on travel of Mission Presidents and mission officers outside their jurisdictions, January 18, 1947

1947-January 18-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Mission Presidents or their wives to leave the mission only with permission of the First Presidency. Similar restrictions on mission officers and missionaries on travel outside the mission to which they were called. (Compiler's resume.)
