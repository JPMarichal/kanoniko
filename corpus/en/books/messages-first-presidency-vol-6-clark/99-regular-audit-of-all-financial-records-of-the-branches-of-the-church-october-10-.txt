James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Regular audit of all financial records of the branches of the Church, October 10, 1942

1942-October 10-Original circular letter, L. D. S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

(Instructions calling for a regular audit of all financial accounts in the branches of the Church in the missions of the Church. A follow-up letter of October 19, 1942, gave further instructions.)
