James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Care in issuing temple recommends, August 23, 1941

1941-August 23-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah; also Improvement Era 44:616, October, 1941.

Greater care should be exercised in issuing temple recommends. It should never be a perfunctory matter. Every applicant should be considered as if the request he now makes for a recommend were the first request he had ever made. (Compiler's resume.)
