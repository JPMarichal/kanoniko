James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Ordination of men in military service to the Melchizedek Priesthood, June 19, 1942

1942-June 19-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Instructions covering the ordination of men in the military service to the Melchizedek Priesthood. The Bishop and Stake President of the man's home ward should carry out regular Church procedures and then the Stake President should notify the First Presidency who will make the arrangements for the "proper Church officer nearest where the young man is located" to perform the ordination. (Compiler's resume.)
