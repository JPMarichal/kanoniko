James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Christmas message by President George Albert Smith, December, 1948

1948-December-Improvement Era 51:785, 831, December, 1948.

A message for Christmas by President George Albert Smith.

This is a day when the hearts of many are failing them with fear. . . We need have no fear if we do what the Lord has asked us to do. This is his world; all men and women are subject to him. (Excerpt.)
