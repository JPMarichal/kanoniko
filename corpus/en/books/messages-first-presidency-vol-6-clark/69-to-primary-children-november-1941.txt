James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

To Primary children, November, 1941

1941-November-Original broadside, James R. Clark collection, Provo, Utah.

Dear Primary Children:

We are happy to send our loving greetings to you boys and girls who have just been baptized and confirmed members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and to all you younger boys and girls who are looking forward to your baptism and confirmation.

Nothing in all your lives is more important than this. Even the Lord Himself was baptized, thus showing that we must all be baptized if we are to be able to live with Him hereafter.

The Lord loves you. He has said so many times. He reproved his disciples who wanted to keep little children away from Him. He has said that everybody must become like a little child if we are to belong to Him.

When the Lord visited the Nephites, the angels themselves came down from Heaven and cared for the little children whom the Lord had just blessed. Angels are all around you all the time, watching over and caring for you. They can help and guard you best; they can keep harm away from you most when you do what your father and mother ask you to do. Thousands of years ago, the Lord said to His Chosen People, "Honor thy father and thy mother that thy days may be long in the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee." The Lord asks you now to keep this commandment.

Go regularly to your Primary and your Sunday School. Remember what your teachers tell you, do what they ask you to do, then the Lord will bless you.

We ask the Lord to bless all of you and keep you well and safe from harm. HEBER J. GRANT, November, 1941 J. REUBEN CLARK, JR., DAVID O. MCKAY, First Presidency.
