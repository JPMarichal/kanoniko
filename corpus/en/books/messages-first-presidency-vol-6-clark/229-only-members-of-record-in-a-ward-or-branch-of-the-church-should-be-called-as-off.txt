James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Only members of record in a ward or branch of the Church should be called as officers, November 15, 1950

1950-November 15-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Not only should membership of record in the Church be in the ward where the person resides, but only members of record of a ward should be made officers of a ward. (Compiler's resume.)
