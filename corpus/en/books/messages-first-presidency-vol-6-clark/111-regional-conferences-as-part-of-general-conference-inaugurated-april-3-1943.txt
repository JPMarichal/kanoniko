Regional conferences as part of General Conference inaugurated, April 3, 1943

1943-April 3-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Attendance of the general Church membership at the General Conferences had been restricted since the United States had entered the global conflict in December, 1941. Regional conferences as a part of the 113th Annual General Conference were inaugurated. The holding of regional conferences would retain a wider base of attendance and still conform to the restrictions on travel during wartime called for in U.S. Government regulation.
