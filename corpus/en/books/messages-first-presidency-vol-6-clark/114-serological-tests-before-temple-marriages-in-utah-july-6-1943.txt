James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Serological tests before temple marriages in Utah, July 6, 1943

1943-July 6-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Serological tests as required by civil law in Utah are required for all temple marriages in Utah. Similar instruction had already been issued by the First Presidency in letters of June 27, 1941, June 30, 1941, and August 17, 1942. (Compiler's resume.)
