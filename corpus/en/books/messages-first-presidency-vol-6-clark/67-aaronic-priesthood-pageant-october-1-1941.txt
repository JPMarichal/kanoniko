James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Aaronic Priesthood pageant, October 1, 1941

1941-October 1-Aaronic Priesthood Pageant Program, Prepare Ye the Way.

You young men have accepted, among other duties, two great responsibilities; viz, (1) to maintain in your daily life and activity the moral standards of the Church; and (2) to render service to others at every opportunity. (Excerpt.)
