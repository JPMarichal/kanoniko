James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Brigham Young University Board of Trustees, January 19, 1939

1939-January 19-Original Letter. Franklin S. Harris papers, Brigham Young University Archives.

With this and the letter of the First Presidency of May 3, 1939, President Franklin S. Harris of Brigham Young University was appointed a member of the Board of Trustees and of the Executive Committee of the Board, an important step in the history of that institution and of Mormon education.
