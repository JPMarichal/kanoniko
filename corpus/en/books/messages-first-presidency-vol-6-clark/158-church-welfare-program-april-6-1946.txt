Church Welfare Program, April 6, 1946

1946-April 6-Church News 4:1, April 6, 1946.

The accomplishments of the Church Welfare Program, summarized in Part III of the Annual Financial Statement of the Church, as published in the Conference Report for April, 1946, pp. 10-14. Members of the Church urged to continue their "devotion to this great program." (Compiler's resume.)
