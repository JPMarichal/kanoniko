James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Christmas greeting from First Presidency, December 15, 1948

1948-December 15-Deseret News, Church Section, December 15, 1948.

(Christmas greetings from the First Presidency of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, 1948.)

Today, the destiny of Nations is involved in this all-important question of 'What think ye of Christ?', and in whether the world will receive Him as the Saviour of Mankind. (Excerpt.)
