James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Idaho Falls and Los Angeles Temples, July 2, 1940

1940-July 2-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Sunday, September 1, 1940, is designated as a special fast day on which contributions will be taken in all wards of the Church for the building of the Idaho Falls Temple. Plan for building a temple in Los Angeles, California, is also announced. (Compiler's resume.)
