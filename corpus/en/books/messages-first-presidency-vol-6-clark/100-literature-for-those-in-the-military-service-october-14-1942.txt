Literature for those in the military service, October 14, 1942

1942-October 14-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Additional pocket-size literature of the Church to be published for the benefit and strengthening of members of the Church serving in the armed forces during World War II. (Compiler's resume.)
