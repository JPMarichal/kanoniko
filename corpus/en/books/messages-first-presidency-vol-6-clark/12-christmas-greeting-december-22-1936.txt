James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Christmas Greeting, December 22, 1936

1936-December 22-Liahona, the Elders' Journal 34:313-315, December 22, 1936.

Wickedness shall be overcome, hatred, enmity, strife, poverty, and war abolished. This will not be accomplished, however, with bombs and battleshot, with submarines or poison gas, but with a slow but never- failing process of changing men's mental and spiritual attitude. (Excerpt.)
