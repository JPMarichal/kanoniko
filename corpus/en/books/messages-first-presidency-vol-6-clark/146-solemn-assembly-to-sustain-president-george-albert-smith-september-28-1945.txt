Solemn Assembly to sustain President George Albert Smith, September 28, 1945

1945-September 28-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

(Instructions for admission to the Solemn Assembly at the coming October Conference for the sustaining of Elder George Albert Smith as President of the Church.)
