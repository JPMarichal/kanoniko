James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Discontinuance of the Church periodical, Liahona, February 27, 1945

1945-February 27-Liahona 42:409, February 27, 1945.

Announcement by the First Presidency of the discontinuance in the United States of the Liahona, the Elders' Journal to he published hereafter in Mexico in Spanish as the official organ of the Mexican Mission of the Church.

Liahona, the Elders' Journal was commenced with the amalgamation of Elders' Journal published at Chattanooga, Tennessee, in the interest of the Southern States Mission, with the Liahona, published at Independence, Missouri, in the interest of the Central States Mission of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The first number of the amalgamated paper was issued June 22, 1907, with Benjamin F. Cummings, Jr., as its first editor. Its purpose was later extended to serve the interests of all the L.D.S. missions in the United States and Canada.
