James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Mormon Pioneer Commemoration, June 6, 1941

1941-June 6-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah; also Deseret News, Church Section, June 28, 1941.

Mormon pioneer commemoration.

Every passing year emphasizes the importance of awakening in the heart of every true American a deep appreciation of the self-sacrificing lives and great accomplishments of our pioneer fathers and mothers. (Excerpt.)
