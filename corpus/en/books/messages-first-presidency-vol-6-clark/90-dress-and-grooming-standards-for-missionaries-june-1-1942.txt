James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Dress and grooming standards for missionaries, June 1, 1942

1942-June 1-North Central States Mission Bulletin, July, 1942, pp. 6-8.

Periodically the First Presidency issues instructions on appropriate dress and grooming expected of missionaries representing the Church. This letter was reproduced by the North Central States Mission.
