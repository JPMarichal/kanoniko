James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Temple marriages must meet legal requirements of state in which couple resides, February 3, 1949

1949-February 3-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Temple recommends should not be issued to couples to be married in the temples unless they meet all of the legal requirements for a civil marriage in the state in which they reside. (Compiler's resume.)
