James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

No Church auxiliary organization meeting during war except by special permission, January 15, 1943

1944-January 15-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Due to wartime restrictions on gas and rubber, no permission is given to hold Union Meeting by the auxiliary organizations of the Church except by special permission of the First Presidency. (Compiler's resume.)
