James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Merchandising of books or other materials in L.D.S. chapels or buildings of worship against Church policy, March 27, 1950

1950-March 27-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Merchandising of books or other materials should not be carried on in L.D.S. Church chapels or meetinghouses. Such operations are contrary to the spirit of worship and may make the buildings subject to taxation. (Compiler's resume.)
