James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Anti-Liquor-Tobacco Campaign, January, 1939

1939-January-Improvement Era 42:7, January, 1939.

President Heber J. Grant commended the anti-liquor-tobacco campaign being conducted by the Council of the Twelve and recommended the program to all stake, ward and priesthood quorums. (Compiler's resume.)
