James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Returning missionaries to be interviewed at stake conferences, July 16, 1948

1948-July 16-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Returning missionaries will now be interviewed by a General Authority of the Church at Stake Conference in the home stake of the missionary rather than the previous policy of having missionaries report to Church headquarters. Identical but separate letters were sent to Stake Presidents and to Mission Presidents bearing this date. (Compiler's resume.)
