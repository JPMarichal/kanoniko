James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Performance of Civil Marriages by Church Officers, August 22, 1939

1939-August 2-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The authority for performing L.D.S. civil marriages is restricted to Stake Presidents and Bishops, or their counselors, if so designated by the Stake President or Bishop. Authorization for marriages to be performed by "other Elders in the Church" is discontinued. (Compiler's resume.)
