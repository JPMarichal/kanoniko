James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Age restriction on calling of bishops during war, May 24, 1944

1944-May 24-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Pursuant to its belief in compliance with civil laws and governments, in addition to the restrictions on calling certain classes of men into its missionary service, the First Presidency had also agreed with the Selective Service Administration in Utah to restrict by age those it would call into Church service as Bishops. (Compiler's resume.)
