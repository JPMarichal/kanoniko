James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Medical serological tests before marriage, June 27, 1941

1941-June 27-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah; also letter of June 30, 1941.

Medical serological test required for all temple marriages within the state of Utah and for couples from Utah who were being married in all other temples. This ruling was made to comply with recently passed Utah statutes. (Compiler's resume.)
