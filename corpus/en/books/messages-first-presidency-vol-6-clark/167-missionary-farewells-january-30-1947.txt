James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Missionary farewells, January 30, 1947

1947-January 30-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The policy of the Church regarding the details of holding missionary farewells changed from time to time. The instructions in the Handbook for Bishops and Stake Presidents and also the letter of instructions of December 26, 1945, were modified by stating that missionary farewells could be held on Sunday provided that certain stated policies were followed. (Compiler's resume.)
