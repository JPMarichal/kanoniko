James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Death of Henry H. Blood, July 14, 1942

1942-July 14-Liahona, the Elders' Journal 40:61, July 14, 1942.

(Statement of the First Presidency upon the death of Henry H. Blood, former governor of Utah and former president of the California Mission of the Church, who died of cerebral hemorrhage and pneumonia on June 19, 1942.)
