James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Health Standards for Missionaries, July 1, 1940

1940-July 1-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Prospective missionaries should report to the mission home in good health. There is a tendency on the part of some parents of missionaries to shift pending medical costs for missionaries to the Church. (Compiler's resume.)
