James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Ban on publication of lists of servicemen, August 31, 1943

1943-August 31-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Compliance with the U.S. War Department, Military Intelligence Division ban on the publication of all lists of members serving in the armed services to be effectively carried out within the Church immediately. (Compiler's resume.)
