James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Temple work for those killed in war, March 9, 1945

1945-March 9-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Where men lose their lives while engaged in military service, either by accident or in combat, their parents or other relatives desiring to have temple work done for them may do so as soon as it is definitely known that they are dead. (Excerpt.)
