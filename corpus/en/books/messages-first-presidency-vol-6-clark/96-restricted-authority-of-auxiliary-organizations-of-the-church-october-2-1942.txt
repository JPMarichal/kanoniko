James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Restricted authority of auxiliary organizations of the Church, October 2, 1942

1942-October 2-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Stake Presidents and superintendents and presidents of Church auxiliary organizations are reminded that the auxiliaries are just that, auxiliaries to the priesthood and have not the right "to use part of the regular meetings of the stake quarterly conference for themselves." (Compiler's resume.)
