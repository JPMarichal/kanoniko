James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 6 ( 1975)

Serological tests before temple marriages, August 17, 1942

1942-August 17-Original circular letter, L.D.S. Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

All couples being married in L.D.S. temples in Utah must comply with the laws of Utah requiring serological test. "Unfortunately, an unwillingness to submit to a serological test raises in itself a question which rather accentuates the need for the test." (Compiler's resume.)
