Second Elder and Assistant Counselor

OLIVER COWDERY: October 3, 1806-March 3, 1850

Received the Melchizedek Priesthood from Peter, James, and John in 1829; sustained as second elder of the Church April 6, 1830; sustained as assistant counselor in the First Presidency September 3, 1837. Excommunicated April 11, 1839; rebaptized November 12, 1848.
