Communicating with Investigators: Just Smile

I'd like to move into the missionary aspect of communication during your initial contact with your investigator. We've talked about the principles of knowing our Heavenly Father and working with each other. Now that we're building these relationships of trust, what is the most important thing we can do every time we come in contact with another human being? Smile! What does a smile say? Does it say, "My zygomaticus can contract from the obicularis oris to the zygoma"? (The zygomaticus on the anterior portion of our zygoma is the muscle that makes us smile.) No, a smile communicates that we bear a message of happiness and friendliness. When we meet an investigator, we are communicating something so powerfully that we must smile! Of course, that doesn't mean we have to do it every minute, but we should make sure our first communication is accompanied with a smile.
