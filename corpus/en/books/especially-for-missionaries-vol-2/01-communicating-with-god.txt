Communicating with God

Until we learn to communicate, no one will know our hearts, no one will know the love we have for them, and no one will know us. To communicate with others, we must first learn to communicate with the Father who gave us life; learning to communicate with our Heavenly Father becomes the most important step of communication.

We usually call this communication prayer; we even say mighty prayer. In John the Beloved's gospel he said, "And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent" (John 17:3). Knowing God is an essential element of communication. The Prophet Joseph Smith said that before you can love God, before you can worship God, and before you can have faith in God, you must know him and know his character. (See Lectures on Faith) Those of you who have had the privilege of reading the Lectures on Faith know whereof I speak, for the first lecture is all about the character and nature of God. Now that's interesting, because if you look closely at your first discussion, you will see it teaches that God is our Father, that he knows all, and that he is all loving and all powerful. In that sense, when we teach that discussion, we plant the seed of desire to know our Heavenly Father.
