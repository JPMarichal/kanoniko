The Nephite record adds the following words between Isaiah's prophecy regarding the Lord smiting the earth with the rod and the prophecy of the Lord's millennial reign: "For the time speedily cometh that the Lord God shall cause a great division among the people, and the wicked will he destroy; and he will spare his people, yea, even if it so be that he must destroy the wicked by fire" (2 Ne. 30:10). The Lord will destroy the wicked with fire but will spare the righteous.
