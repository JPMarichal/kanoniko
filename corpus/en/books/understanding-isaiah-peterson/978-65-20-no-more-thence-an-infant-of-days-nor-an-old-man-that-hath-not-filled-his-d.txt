the sinner, living to be an hundred years old, shall be accursed. The curse may be that the sinner, having lived out his years, will suffer for his lack of repentance in the flesh. Rather than having a period of time in the spirit world to meet the demands of justice for his sins, he will live to be one hundred years old and then will be resurrected. That would be a curse if a person needed more time to prepare himself for the judgment of God.
