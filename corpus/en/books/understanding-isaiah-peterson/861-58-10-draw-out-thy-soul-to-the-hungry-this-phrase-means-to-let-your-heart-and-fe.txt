thy light rise in obscurity/thy darkness be as the noonday. This passage refers to the future glory of Israel (54:1-13; 55:13). Her humiliation and captivity are as the darkness just before daybreak. And just as her (and our) deliverance from slavery and oppression is as the dawn (v. 8), so the eventual state of Zion will be as the brightness of the sun at noon (Job 11:17). This also has an individual application: As we fast, we seek to become like the Bright and Morning Star (Rev. 22:16), the Dayspring (Luke 1:78), the Light of the World (John 8:12; Alma 38:9; D&C 11:28), Jesus Christ.
