His heart "deceived" by idolatry and his soul unable to receive the spiritual nourishment it requires, the idol worshiper cannot find the Lord and thereby deliver his soul from the gates of hell (D&C 76:81-86). In other words, even if the idolater can create his own god, he still cannot save himself.

Is there not a lie in my right hand? Right hand here speaks of covenant making. An individual blinded by idolatry cannot recognize that covenants made with idols are nothing more than lies (Ps. 144:8). This also means that by creating his god with his own (right) hand, the idolater relies on his own hand ("the arm of flesh") for salvation, which is a lie.
