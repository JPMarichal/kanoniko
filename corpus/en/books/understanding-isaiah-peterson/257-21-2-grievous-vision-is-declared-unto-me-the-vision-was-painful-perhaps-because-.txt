Go up, O Elam: besiege, O Media. Elam (the western portion of ancient Persia) was famous for its archers (Jer. 25:25; 49:34). "Media was the ancient name for NW Iran, southwest of the Caspian Sea and north of the Zagros Mountains." 4 Under King Cyrus's leadership, Elam and Media captured Babylon in 538 b.c.

Isaiah may have envisioned some of the actual bloodshed and warfare connected to this historical event, but he presents here a simple war cry uttered by a military leader (or perhaps by one of the warriors): "Go up, O Elam; besiege, O Media." Jeremiah received a portion of this same vision (Jer. 51:11, 28).
