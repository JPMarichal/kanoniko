There is also another possible way to understand this phrase. Anciently, the birth of infants was not always certain. A woman could come to the point of delivery and yet because of complications (such as the cord being around the baby's neck or the baby being in an unworkable position), she would find her womb, in effect, to be "shut," and both she and the baby could die without delivery. The Lord says, however, that what he brings to delivery will indeed come forth.
