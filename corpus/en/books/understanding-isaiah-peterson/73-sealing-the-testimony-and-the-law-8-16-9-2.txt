This section discusses the following:

Isaiah is first instructed to "bind the testimony" and "seal the law" (8:16).

Isaiah and his family are living symbols unto the house of Israel. Their names also are prophetic and serve to instruct the house of Israel according to God's plan (8:18).

Israel wanders lost and alone in the telestial world because of her sins, which have separated her from God (8:21). She does not follow the prophetic word; instead she seeks the word of God through wizards and familiar spirits (8:19).

The world will be walking in darkness, apostasy, and the "shadow of death" when a "great light," Jesus Christ, will make an appearance (8:22; 9:2).
