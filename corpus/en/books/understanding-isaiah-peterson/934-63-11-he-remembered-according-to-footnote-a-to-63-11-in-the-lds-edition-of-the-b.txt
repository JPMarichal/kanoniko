Where is he . . . ? After remembering how Jehovah saved the people during the Exodus, the people now long for his saving presence in their lives, for his guidance, for his loving comfort and tenderness (Jer. 2:6).

out of the sea. The Lord delivered Israel by miraculous means, taking them through the Red Sea. He does the same with us, bringing us safely past seemingly impassable obstacles in our lives.

shepherd of his flock. Moses was the shepherd the Lord used to bring his flock (the Israelites) through the Red Sea to safety (Ps. 77:19-20; Micah 6:4). Ultimately, of course, the good shepherd for us all is Christ (John 10:14).

put his holy Spirit within him. The him used here seems to refer to Moses, the shepherd, rather than to Israel, the flock. Moses was blessed with the Spirit (Num. 11:17, 25).
