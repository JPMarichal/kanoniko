young and old lion/viper/fiery flying serpent. These same creatures plagued the Israelites as they escaped Egypt's pharaoh and armies (Num. 21:4-6; Deut. 8:15; see the discussion of fiery flying serpent in commentary on 14:29).

they will carry their riches upon . . . young asses. Judah's leaders will carry gifts to Egypt, a people that shall not profit them. Similarly, we sometimes devote our time, energy, or resources (our riches) to purposes or causes that are contrary to God's will. In the end, all such activities will not profit us.
