Isa. 63:1-14

Likening Isa. 63 unto Ourselves

The Lord has suffered greatly for us, as is symbolized by the blood-red color of his garments when he returns. He will come in fury and in anger to punish the wicked. Will we be among that group? Or will we be among those who feel his mercy and loving-kindness? With Isaiah, we can plead with the Lord for his mercy, asking him to return to earth with his deliverance.
