This short passage is a sweet invitation to come to the living waters of Christ and drink freely (55:1). The gospel and the blessings of the Atonement have no temporal cost (55:1-2). Why would we spend our time, effort, and money for that which has no lasting value? (55:2). Rather than spending money and labor on things that "cannot satisfy" (55:2), we can come unto the Lord and "feast" on things of eternal value, which never will perish (55:2). Such things are a delight to the soul (55:2). Come unto Christ, then, hearken to him, and live (55:2-3).

Isa. 55:1-3

Ho, every one that thirsteth, come ye to the waters, and he that hath no money; come ye, buy, and eat; yea, come, buy wine and milk without money and without price. 1 (55:1) Wherefore do [not] spend money for that which is [of no worth,] nor your labour for that which [cannot satisfy]. Hearken diligently unto me, and [remember the words which I have spoken; and come unto the Holy One of Israel, and feast upon that which perisheth not, neither can be corrupted,] and let your soul delight itself in fatness. 2 (55:2) Incline your ear, and come unto me: hear, and your soul shall live; and I will make an everlasting covenant with you, even the sure mercies of David. (55:3)
