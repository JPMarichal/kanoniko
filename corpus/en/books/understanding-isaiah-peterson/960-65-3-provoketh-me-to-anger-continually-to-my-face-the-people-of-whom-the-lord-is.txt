sacrificeth in gardens. Sacrificing in gardens, a practice often used in worshiping idols, was forbidden in the law of Moses (2 Kgs. 21:3-6; Ezek. 20:28). The expression means that the people have turned from the right way of worship.

burneth incense. The verb used here means "to make to smoke" and can be used in describing all burnt offerings, not just offerings of incense (Jer. 44:3-5).

altars of brick. According to the law of Moses, incense was to be burned only on altars of unhewn stone (Ex. 20:24-25; Deut. 27:5; Josh. 8:31). Burnt offerings to pagan gods may have been made on rooftops or in the streets (Jer. 19:13; 32:29), or in the case of incense offerings, bricks may have been heated and then incense sprinkled on the bricks. 4
