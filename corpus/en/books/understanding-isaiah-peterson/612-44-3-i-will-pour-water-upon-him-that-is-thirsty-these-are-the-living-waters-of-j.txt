floods. God is generous with salvation; he will send floods, not simply a canteen full of water upon us to quench our thirst.

dry ground. This term refers to Israel's offspring who, upon receiving the spiritual water and floods, will spring up like willows (44:4). They will recognize the source of their spiritual light and will say, "I am the Lord's" (44:5).

I will pour my spirit upon thy seed. This phrase parallels I will pour water upon him that is thirsty, for the words I will pour and upon are found in both phrases. The Lord promises to pour his Spirit on Israel's seed (which includes us) as one pours water on the thirsty (32:15; Ezek. 39:29). This prophecy was partially fulfilled when the Spirit descended upon many of Christ's followers after Christ was resurrected (Acts 2:1-4) however Peter indicated that the fulness of this prophecy would "come to pass in the last days" (Acts 2:17; see D&C 95:4).
