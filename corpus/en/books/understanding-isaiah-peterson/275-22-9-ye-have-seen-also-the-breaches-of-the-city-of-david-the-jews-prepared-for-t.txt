The pool refers to Hezekiah's Tunnel, an engineering marvel that was created to permit Jerusalem's inhabitants to safely obtain outside water through the city's walls during a siege. Isaiah selects his terms well; Judah relied on the waters that originated from the ground (Hezekiah's Tunnel) but rejected Jehovah, the "waters of Shiloah that go softly" (8:6) and the "fountain of living waters" (Jer. 2:13; 17:13; Isa. 33:21).
