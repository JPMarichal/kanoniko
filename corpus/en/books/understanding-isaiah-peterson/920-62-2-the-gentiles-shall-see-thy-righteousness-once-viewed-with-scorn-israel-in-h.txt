The new name given to Israel may be The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. 4 The name also, as given in this passage, is both Delightful and Union, both of which are names of Zion. These names stand in contrast to the old names of Forsaken and Desolate.

In addition, those who come unto Zion will receive other names: "I will write upon him the name of my God, and the name of the city of my God, which is new Jerusalem, . . . and I will write upon him my new name" (Rev. 3:12).
