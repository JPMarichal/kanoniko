Twice the Lord tells us, "Ye are my witnesses" (43:10, 12). We are to witness and bear testimony to "all the nations" (43:9) that Jehovah is "God" (43:12) and "Savior" (43:11). Note Jehovah's repeated self-testimony—"I am he" (43:10), "before me there was no God formed, neither shall there be after me," "I, even I, am the Lord," "beside me there is no savior," "I am God," "I am he" (43:13).
