bare them/carried them. God is always there to tenderly care for his people, as he did in leading Israel forth from bondage in Egypt and caring for them in the desert (Deut. 32:11; Isa. 46:3-4; Acts 13:17-18). Descriptions of bearing and carrying another usually refer to a mother and her child. The Lord would have us see that he cares for us as a mother cares for her newborn child.

all the days of old. This phrase means the time of Israel's exodus from Egypt and her wanderings in the wilderness (63:11).
