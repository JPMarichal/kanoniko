children of transgression/seed of falsehood. The New International Version says "brood of rebels, the offspring of liars." The sin of Israel has become so pervasive that it passes from generation to generation, ever perpetuating itself (1:2). Children of transgression—those who follow the ways of the world and the whisperings of the adversary—are called a variety of names in the scriptures: "children of this world" (Luke 16:8; 20:34), "children of disobedience" (Eph. 2:2), "children of wrath" (Eph. 2:3), "children of the kingdom of the devil" (Alma 5:25), and "the children of them which killed the prophets" (Matt. 23:31). Jesus said bluntly that they follow their "father the devil" (John 8:44).
