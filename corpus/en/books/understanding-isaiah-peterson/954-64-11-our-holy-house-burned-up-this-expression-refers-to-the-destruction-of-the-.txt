One translation is "All that we treasured lies in ruins" (NIV), perhaps referring to the sacred vessels and other accoutrements of the temple, or possibly the temple itself. Other translations read, "All our pleasant places have become ruins" (RSV); and "All the things of beauty are destroyed" (LB).
