ships of Tarshish. These were the merchant ships, often used to carry metals (including precious metals), from the Phoenician port of Tartessus (see commentary on 2:16; 23:1). The symbolism is that the Lord will provide means to enable the covenant people—and their wealth—to gather to Zion.

first. Some translations render this word as "as at first" or "as in former days." As the Lord provided means for Israel to come to the promised land anciently, so will he help the people of his covenant to come to Zion at the last.
