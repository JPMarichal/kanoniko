Kir uncovered the shield. Kir was a Mesopotamian city situated east of the Tigris River on the main highway that ran from Elam to Babylon. A leather covering protected a warrior's shield when it was not in use, and the phrase uncovered the shield indicates that Kir's warriors held their shields in readiness; it parallels the anticipation of Elam's soldiers who had their arrows removed from the quivers, ready for an impending battle.
