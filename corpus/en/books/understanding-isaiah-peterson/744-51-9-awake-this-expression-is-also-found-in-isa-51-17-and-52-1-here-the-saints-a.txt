Put on strength/arm of the Lord . . . ancient days. This statement is reminiscent of the deliverance of Israel from Egypt, which came by the outstretched arm of the Lord (Ex. 6:6; 15:16). So also will he deliver his people in all ages.

cut Rahab/wounded the dragon. Rahab has several possible interpretations: Egypt, primordial chaos, or Satan. More important than the specific identification of Rahab, however, is the understanding that God has power over all, even his mightiest enemies (27:1; Job 26:12; Ps. 74:13-17).
