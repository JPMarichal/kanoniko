day of visitation. A time of the Lord's judgment and indignation (Hosea 9:7; Micah 7:4; D&C 56:1, 16). It is also the time of the Second Coming, when Christ will visit the earth with power and glory (D&C 124:8, 10). The Hebrew term for visitation (pequdah) also refers to keeping one's promise; hence the day of visitation is the day when the Lord will keep his promise to visit the earth with judgment.

come from far. This refers both to the Assyrian army that will come from a far country to conquer Israel and to Jehovah, who resides far off and will come to judge the world.

to whom will ye flee for help? This is a rhetorical question, showing that during the day of visitation the wicked will have no one to turn to for help. The only one who could assist them is God, and they have turned away from him.

where will ye leave your glory? Many translations read, "Where will ye leave your riches?" On the day of judgment, personal wealth will be meaningless and will have no power to save.
