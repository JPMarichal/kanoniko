turneth wise men backward/maketh their knowledge foolish. The diviners and wise men who counsel the kings of the ancient Middle East believe they have access to supernatural counsel, but their knowledge and promises are useless, for only God's true servants and messengers are able to confirm God's word. The wise men here refer to those who are "learned" and "think they are wise," but "they hearken not unto the counsel of God, for they set it aside, supposing they know of themselves" (2 Ne. 9:28).
