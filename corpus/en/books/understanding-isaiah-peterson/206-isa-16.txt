Isa. 16

Likening Isa. 16 unto Ourselves

The desolation of historical Moab (chapter 15) anticipates the destruction of the modern nations that work evil and worship false gods; the mourning and lamentation of Moab's inhabitants after her destruction (16:6-14) foretells the anguish that will be experienced by earth's inhabitants during and after the latter-day wars; and Moab's refugees who sought protection in Judah (16:1-5) parallel those in the last days who will flee unto Zion from the warring nations (45:68-69).
