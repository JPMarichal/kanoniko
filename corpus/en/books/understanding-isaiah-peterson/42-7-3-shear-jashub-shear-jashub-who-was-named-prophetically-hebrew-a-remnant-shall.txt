The pool was probably located near the Gihon spring in the Valley of Kidron. 9 Ahaz may have been at the pool with his officers to check Jerusalem's water supply for the pending siege. The Lord, who knew Ahaz's location, inspired Isaiah to go there with his son Shear-jashub. This is also the place where Rabshakeh, messenger of the king of Assyria, delivered a message to King Hezekiah (36:2; 2 Kgs. 18:17).
