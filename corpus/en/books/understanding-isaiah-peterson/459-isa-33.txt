Isa. 33

Likening Isa. 33 unto Ourselves

The Lord is the constant support of the righteous—we pray "be thou [our] arm every morning" (33:2). But the wicked have no such promise, and they will be destroyed by burning at the last day. In contrast to the fire of destruction, Isaiah sets forth six statements that describe the fire of holiness. If we are holy, we will not burn at Christ's coming but will dwell with him in "everlasting burnings."
