### Likening Isa. 4 unto Ourselves

When Moroni visited Joseph Smith in September 1823, he quoted 4:5-6 and said that this prophecy would soon find fulfillment. 1 Many of the events will take place in our day, and this prophecy has direct relevance for us. For example, the section speaks of temples, temple work, and the Saints' becoming holy through temple worship. It also prophesies of the establishment of Zion in the latter days.
