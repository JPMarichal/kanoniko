Thus the king was seeking to become like God, who has authority to end life. We recall also that Jehovah is the great forester identified in 10:33-34, and hence the king of Babylon attempts to imitate God's ability to cut down nations and peoples. Yet, as J. Alec Motyer observes, "With exact justice the arrogant woodsman [the king of Babylon] has 'had the chop!'" 12 Satan, similarly, attempts to make people suffer the second death.
