burning/fire. Symbolically and prophetically, the boot and garments identified in 9:5 refer to all unclean and corruptible things that will be burned with fire at Jesus Christ's glorious second coming (3 Ne. 25:1; D&C 64:23-24). Specifically, the weapons and chariots of the nations' armies (boot of the soldier) will burn when the Messiah comes to rule. (See also 2:4, which describes weapons being made into agricultural instruments for mankind's benefit.)
