sift the nations with the sieve of vanity. In the last days the Lord will sift the nations, removing the tares from the wheat with the sieve of vanity or "the sieve of destruction." 3 Compare Amos 9:9, in which God uses the same "sieve" to "sift the house of Israel, . . . yet shall not the least grain fall upon the earth."

bridle in the jaws of the people. As a beast of burden is held in its path by the bridle it wears, so will the wicked be restrained, unable to escape God's judgments.
