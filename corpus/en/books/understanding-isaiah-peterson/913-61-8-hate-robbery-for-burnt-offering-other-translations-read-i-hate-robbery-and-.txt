I will direct their work in truth. The Lord is the head of his church and the director of the covenant people. As a God of truth, he leads his people to proceed in the spirit of truth and obedience in all they do for him.

everlasting covenant. The Lord will make the righteous his covenant people (55:3; 59:21). The everlasting covenant is the gospel with all its requirements of man and its promises from God.
