The New International Version reads that Lucifer "would not let his captives go home," which is a possible reference to the fact that Lucifer does not want his spiritual captives, or those bound by sin, to be released. Lucifer's goal, of course, stands in direct opposition to Jesus Christ's righteous and perfect desire, which is to release from sin all those who come unto him.
