thine eyes shall see thy teachers. The phrase thy teachers should read the singular, thy teacher, because it refers to the Lord, who was Israel's great teacher and eternal lawgiver. When this prophecy is fulfilled, the Lord will not "be removed into a corner"; he will no longer need to withdraw himself from his people because they will no longer be idolatrous or rebellious. Rather, their eyes shall see their teacher when he comes in glory to Israel as a whole and to her people individually.
