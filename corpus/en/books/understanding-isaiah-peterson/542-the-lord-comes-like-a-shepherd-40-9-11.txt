Isaiah calls out to Zion and Jerusalem to prepare for the coming of their Lord in strength and judgment to rule on the earth and reward her inhabitants according to their works. He will be a shepherd who will care for, protect, and lead his followers.

Isa. 40:9-11

O Zion, that bringest good tidings, get thee up into the high mountain; O Jerusalem, that bringest good tidings, lift up thy voice with strength; lift it up, be not afraid; say unto the cities of Judah, Behold your God! (40:9) Behold, the Lord God will come with strong hand, and his arm shall rule for him: behold, his reward is with him, and his work before him. (40:10) He shall feed his flock like a shepherd: he shall gather the lambs with his arm, and carry them in his bosom, and shall gently lead those that are with young. 5 (40:11)
