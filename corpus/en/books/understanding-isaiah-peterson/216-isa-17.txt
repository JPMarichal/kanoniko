Isa. 17

Likening Isa. 17 unto Ourselves

The prophecy of Isaiah in this chapter may be likened unto our own generation. The idolatrous practices of the ancient countries of Syria and Israel resulted in their downfall and destruction (17:1-11). People and nations in the last days who put their trust in idols, such as riches, power, false security, or illicit sex, will similarly be destroyed.
