Is this the man that made the earth to tremble, that did shake kingdoms? On seeing Lucifer in hell, the inquiry will come, Is this the man who caused such great disturbances upon the earth ("made the earth to tremble, . . . destroyed the cities"; 14:16-17), and now he is like "an abominable branch," like "a carcase" that others trample on (14:19)? By calling him man, Isaiah emphasizes here that Lucifer is by no means a god.
