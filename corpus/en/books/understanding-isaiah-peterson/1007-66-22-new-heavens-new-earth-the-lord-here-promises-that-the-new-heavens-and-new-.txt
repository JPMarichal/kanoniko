Their name is the name of Christ, because they have become his sons and his daughters. As King Benjamin said, that is the name that will never be blotted out (Mosiah 5:10-11). The Lord here promises that the seed and the name of the righteous will remain as long as the new heavens and the new earth will remain, which is forever (see commentary on 62:2).
