Isa. 48

Likening Isa. 48 unto Ourselves

Isa. 48 very much speaks to people of our day, particularly members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. In it, the Lord addresses those who have come up out of the waters of baptism—those who have joined themselves to the covenant but who are not true to their covenants and do not submit themselves to God. They have become stubborn and slow to hear the counsel of the Lord.
