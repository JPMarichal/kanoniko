Elsewhere Isaiah warns, "Woe unto him that striveth with his Maker! . . . Shall the clay say to him that fashioneth it, What makest thou?" (45:9). Rather, let us acknowledge, "O Lord, thou art our father; we are the clay, and thou our potter; and we all are the work of thy hand" (64:8).
