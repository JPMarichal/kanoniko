when thou walkest through the fire, thou shalt not be burned. This statement refers to the Lord's judgments that fall upon Israel (42:25) and the world (24:6) because of wickedness, with specific application to Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego (Dan. 3:27). It also applies to the last days, when the wicked are destroyed by the burning that will accompany the Lord's coming, while the righteous of Israel remain unscathed.
