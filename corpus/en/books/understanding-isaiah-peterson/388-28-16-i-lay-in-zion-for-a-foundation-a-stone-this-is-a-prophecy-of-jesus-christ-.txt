Peter's quotation of Isaiah here reads, "He that believeth on him shall not be confounded" (1 Pet. 2:6), and Paul's quotation reads, "shall not be ashamed" (Rom. 9:33; 10:11). The meaning of this phrase, then, is: "Whosoever believes on him [Christ, the stone] shall not be [confounded or] ashamed."
