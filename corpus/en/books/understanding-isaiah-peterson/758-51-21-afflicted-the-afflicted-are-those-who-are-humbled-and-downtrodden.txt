Jeremiah casts further light on this phrase: "For thus saith the Lord . . . ; Take the wine cup of this fury at my hand, and cause all the nations, to whom I send thee, to drink it. And they shall drink, and be moved, and be mad, because of the sword that I will send among them. Then took I the cup at the Lord's hand, and made all the nations to drink, unto whom the Lord had sent me" (Jer. 25:15-17; see Ezek. 23:32-34). Jeremiah's words mark the beginning of the curse; Isaiah's words mark its removal in the last days.
