who shall declare his generation? The New International Version renders this phrase as "who can speak of his descendants?" implying that because he was "cut off from the land of the living" he had none. But Jesus did indeed have descendants, those who become his children through righteousness; see the commentary on 53:10, he shall see his seed.

cut off. He was cut off, or removed, from the land of the living.

stricken. This word repeats the word used in verse 4. There, he was considered stricken by those who would not recognize him. Here, he truly is stricken as he bears on himself the "transgressions of my people."
