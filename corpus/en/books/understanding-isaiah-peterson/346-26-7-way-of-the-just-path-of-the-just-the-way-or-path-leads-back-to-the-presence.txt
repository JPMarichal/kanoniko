most upright, dost make level. Jesus Christ is the most upright, or the most righteous of all, who makes level the path of the just. To make level in the physical sense is to clear the path of bumps, boulders, and large holes so that the traveler can journey thereon. In the spiritual sense, Jesus Christ, through his atonement, provides a clear path for all who will make their way back to the Father (Ps. 25:8; 2 Ne. 31:16).
