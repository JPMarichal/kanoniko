those who are in the east/mountains. Those in the east may be the Israelites in Jerusalem; mountains may refer to Zion in America. The heavens and the earth shall join in rejoicing when the Lord's people in both his capital cities are protected, comforted, and established.

his people/his afflicted. The Lord's people are afflicted because of their dispersion and long exile. They also are afflicted spiritually as a consequence of sin, but through the Atonement the Lord will comfort and be merciful to the repentant, who are truly the Lord's people.
