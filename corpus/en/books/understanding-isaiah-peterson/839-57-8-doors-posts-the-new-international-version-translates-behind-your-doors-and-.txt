discovered thyself/gone up/enlarged thy bed. The New International Version renders this statement in painful plainness: "Forsaking me, you uncovered your bed, you climbed into it and opened it wide; you made a pact with those whose beds you love, and you looked on their nakedness." This phrase has a literal meaning, referring to the pagan practices of temple prostitution, and also a symbolic meaning, referring to the unfaithfulness of Lady Israel (in our age, as well as anciently) in joining herself to other gods after making a covenant with Jehovah.
