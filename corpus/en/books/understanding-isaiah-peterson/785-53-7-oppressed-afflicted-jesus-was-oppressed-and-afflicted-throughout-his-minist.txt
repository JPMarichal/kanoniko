lamb to the slaughter. Not only is Christ the Good Shepherd (John 10:14) but he is also the sacrificial lamb, which went without protest or resistance to his death. This is in contrast to us, as the sheep of the previous verse, who willfully went astray. The sacrifice of an unblemished lamb under the law of Moses prefigured the atoning sacrifice of Christ (Gen. 22:7-8; Ex. 12:3; Lev. 5:7): the Atonement fulfills the symbolism of the lamb.
