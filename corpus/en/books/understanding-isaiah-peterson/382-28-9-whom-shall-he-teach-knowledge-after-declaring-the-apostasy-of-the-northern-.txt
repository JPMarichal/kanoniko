weaned from the milk/drawn from the breasts. Isaiah likens milk to the basic principles of the gospel—faith, repentance, and baptism—that are the basis for further learning. Paul built upon this imagery: "For every one that useth milk is unskilful in the word of righteousness: for he is a babe. But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil" (Heb. 5:13-14; JST 1 Cor. 3:2; Alma 12:9; D&C 19:21-22).
