eating swine's flesh/abomination/mouse. Abomination refers to all unclean animals (Lev. 7:21; Isa. 65:4; 66:3). Swine and mice were both forbidden as food for the Lord's covenant people under the law of Moses (Lev. 11:7, 29).

consumed together. All the wicked will be destroyed at the time of the coming of the Lord (JST Matt. 13:41-51).
