Although the king of Babylon may have enjoyed luxury and wealth during mortality, his dead body, like that of all the deceased, will lie in the dust, and worms will eat his flesh (Job 21:26). Spread or cover suggests a bed, since the king, who sat on his throne in temporal glory, now sleeps in a cold bed in hell; he leaves his pomp on earth, and his body becomes like that of all sinful people who preceded him.
