Jerusalem's leaders sought to strengthen themselves by establishing an alliance with Egypt, ruled by Pharaoh Psamtik I, who seemed to offer strength and protection. The consequence of Judah's alliance with Egypt was the eventual enslavement and deportation of the people by the Assyrians, to their shame and confusion. It is always a sin to trust in the arm of flesh rather than the power of God.

trust in the shadow of Egypt. Judah trusted in Egypt's military might and political strength. Isaiah's reference to shadow of Egypt indicates that the might and strength of Egypt are as a mere shadow—fleeting, dark, and insubstantial—when compared to the power of God.
