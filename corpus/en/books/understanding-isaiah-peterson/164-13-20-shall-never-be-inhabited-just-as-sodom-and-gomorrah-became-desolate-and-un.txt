from generation to generation. This idiomatic expression refers generally to a lengthy period of time (34:10; 51:8; Ex. 17:16; Lam. 5:19). Contrary to the wicked who will be removed from Babylon and will not inhabit it from generation to generation, the righteous will be planted, well-rooted, and "shall grow up on the land of Zion, to possess it from generation to generation, forever and ever" (D&C 69:8; emphasis added).
