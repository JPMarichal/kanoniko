Isa. 15

Likening Isa. 15 unto Ourselves

Moab's inhabitants were enemies of Israel and constantly warred against them (Num. 22-25; Judg. 3:12-30). As such, they serve as a type and a shadow of the wicked today who are Zion's enemies. Also, as ancient Moab was "laid waste" and her inhabitants were "brought to silence," so will the wicked of our day be destroyed at the Lord's coming.
