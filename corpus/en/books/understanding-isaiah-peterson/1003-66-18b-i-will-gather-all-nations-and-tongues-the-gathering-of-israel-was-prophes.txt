This statement may refer to standing in the presence of the Lord in the New Jerusalem, where "the Lord shall be unto thee an everlasting light, and thy God thy glory" (60:19; 60:3; 62:2; Rev. 21:23-24). The righteous may also behold the glory of the Lord in his temple.
