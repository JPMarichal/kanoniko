Egypt/Assyrian. These are the two great oppressors of Israel, Egypt at the beginnings of Israel's history and Assyria in Isaiah's time. They symbolize the world and its oppression of the Saints, including Satan and the oppression of sin. Another way to read this passage is that the Father's children lowered themselves to the standards of the world and were oppressed by the enemies of the righteous.
