As the New International Version says, "I bring prosperity and create disaster." Job gave expression to this idea of the duality of God's power when he said, "Shall we receive good at the hand of God, and shall we not receive evil?" (Job 2:10). As the New International Version puts it: "Shall we accept good from God, and not trouble?" The point of 45:6-7 seems to be that God is in charge: he has power over all things and directs the affairs of men to their ultimate good.
