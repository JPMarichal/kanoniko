foundations of Kir-hareseth shall ye mourn. Kir-hareseth is the modern Kerak, located seventeen miles south of the fords of Arnon (16:2) and some eleven miles east of the Dead Sea. Kir-hareseth's economy was founded on its wine industry. Verses 7-10 contain references to this famous industry that existed in ancient Moab. They include fields of Heshbon, vine of Sibmah, principal plants, branches, vine of Sibmah, summer fruits, harvest, plentiful field, vineyards, wine, and vintage. Moab's inhabitants will mourn because its economic mainstay, in which it placed so much trust, will be lost.

Some translators read "raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth" rather than "foundations of Kir-hareseth." Such a reading fits well into the context of this section of Isaiah, for raisin cakes, used in apostate religious practices (Hosea 3:1), were made of dried and compressed grapes (2 Sam. 6:19; 1 Chr. 16:3).
