my chosen. Israel is the chosen people, as the Lord said through Moses: "The Lord thy God hath chosen thee to be a special people unto himself, above all people that [are] upon the face of the earth" (Deut. 7:6). Many are called to come unto Christ and receive his power and blessings, but few are chosen to receive all he offers (D&C 95:5; 121:34). Those who are chosen are the faithful (3 Ne. 19:20, 28), who come with broken and honest hearts.

slay thee. The Lord will destroy wicked Israel and replace her with a righteous people.

another name. Because of the wickedness of the covenant people, the Lord will no longer call the righteous by the name of Israel but will call them by another name. The new name might be Christian rather than Israelite. Or, in our dispensation, the name could be The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. To that name, as Israel increases in holiness and harmony, will be added another name, Zion (1:26- 27).
