the kingdom [shall cease] from Damascus. With the fall of Syria's capital city and other municipal centers, the kingdom ceased to exist.

the remnant of Syria . . . shall be as the glory of the children of Israel. Those who remain in Syria after it becomes a heap of ruins will have little glory, similar to the "thin" glory Israel will have after it becomes desolate (17:4).

saith the Lord of hosts. Jehovah is the source of this prophecy.
