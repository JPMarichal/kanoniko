When the Lord comes in his glory, he will come with fire and the sword, bringing judgments on the wicked. The idea of fire is repeated five times in three verses: "fire" (three times), "flames," and "consumed" (66:15-17). The idea of the anger of the Lord is repeated four times in two verses with such words as indignation, anger, fury, and rebuke (66:14-15). Through the fire and the sword, many will die (66:16). None of the wicked will escape (66:17-18). These things are sent by way of deliverance to the Lord's servants but as a judgment against his enemies (66:14). Ps. 18:6-17 gives us an interesting parallel description of this same event.
