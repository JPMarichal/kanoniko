Rezin. This king of Syria (2 Kgs. 15:37), together with Pekah, waged an anti- Assyrian campaign. Eventually, Rezin was killed during a battle against Assyria (2 Kgs. 16:7-9).

Pekah. Pekah, who was the son of Remaliah, "conspired against" Pekahiah (king of Israel and son of Menahem), assassinated him, and became Israel's eighteenth king. Pekah's reign lasted approximately five years (737-732 b.c.; 2 Kgs. 15:22-26). Pekah, who with Rezin battled against Ahaz king of Judah, was eventually murdered by Hosea (not to be confused with the prophet Hosea), who succeeded him on the throne (2 Kgs. 15:30). It was during King Hosea's reign that the northern tribes of Israel (the ten tribes) were captured and deported by Assyria.
