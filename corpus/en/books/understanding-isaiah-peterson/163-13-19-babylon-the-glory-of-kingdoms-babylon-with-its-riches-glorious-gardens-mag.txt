Sodom (Hebrew "bituminous area") and Gomorrah (Hebrew "ruined heap"), two biblical cities that were destroyed when God rained fire and brimstone upon them (Gen. 19:24-25), are typical of any wicked city that will be destroyed by God's judgments (Matt. 10:15; Rom. 9:29). According to Jude, Sodom and Gomorrah are "set forth for an example" of God's divine judgment on all those who are evil (Jude 1:7). In the centuries following Isaiah's prophecy, Babylon was sacked and destroyed by a number of invading armies and became a heap of ruins. Similarly, the world's great cities will be destroyed at Christ's coming.
