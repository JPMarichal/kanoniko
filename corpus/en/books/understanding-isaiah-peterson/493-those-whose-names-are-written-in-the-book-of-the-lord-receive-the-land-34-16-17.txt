The land of the wicked has been made desolate and assigned to chaos by means of the Lord's "line" and "plummet." The Lord will also use these devices to assign the glorified earth to the righteous, whose names are recorded in the book of the Lord (34:17; see also D&C 103:7). These will have an eternal inheritance with the Lord (34:16); thus, they will always remain with their spouses and have an eternal increase (34:16; see also D&C 132:30-31). There are a number of parallel word choices between this and previous sections, especially between 34:10-11 and 34:17.

Isa. 34:16-17

Seek ye out of the book of the Lord, and read [the names written therein]: 8 no one of these shall fail, none shall [lack] 9 [their] mate: for my mouth it hath commanded, and [my] 10spirit it hath gathered them. (34:16) And [I have] cast the lot for them, and [I have] divided it unto them by line: they shall possess it for ever, from generation to generation [they shall] 11 dwell therein. (34:17)
