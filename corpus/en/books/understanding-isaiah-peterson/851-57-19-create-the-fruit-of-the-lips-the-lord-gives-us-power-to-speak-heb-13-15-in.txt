Peace. The true source of peace is Christ, as Paul points out (Eph. 2:12-17). This cry of "peace, peace" may also be the Lord's greeting to those who are coming to Zion: "Shalom, Shalom!" (Ps. 37:11, 37; 119:165; Philip. 4:7; 2 Thes. 3:16).

far off. Israel has been scattered to distant places. Also, many are spiritually far off; those who "draw near to me with their lips, but their hearts are far from me" (JS- H 1:19; Isa. 29:13) may find peace through repentance.
