woe be unto him that rejecteth the word of God. Those who "reject the words of the prophets" will "stand with shame and awful guilt before the bar of God. . . . And according to the power of justice, . . . [they] must go away into that lake of fire and brimstone" (Jacob 6:8-10). Individuals reject God's word because of "the fear of persecution and the cares of the world" (D&C 40:2), as well as because of gross wickedness (Hel. 6:2).
