they shall cry unto the Lord . . . he shall send them a saviour . . . he shall deliver them. The words cry unto the Lord, oppressors, and he shall deliver them recall the period of the judges when Israel, oppressed by her enemies, cried unto the Lord and was sent deliverers (Judg. 3:9, 15; 6:7; 10:10). Now it is Egypt that will cry unto the Lord and receive a saviour who will deliver them.
