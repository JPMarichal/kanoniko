the glory of the Gentiles like a flowing stream. The word glory (Hebrew kabod) may be translated "wealth." The wealth of the gentiles, both spiritual and temporal, will flow like a stream unto Zion. See commentary on 60:5; 61:6.

borne upon her sides. See commentary on 60:4.

dandled upon her knees. To dandle is to play with affectionately. The Lord is telling us that the people of Zion will be comfortable and happy.
