Isa. 21

Likening Isa. 21 unto Ourselves

This prophecy speaks not only of Babylon (and Duman and Arabia) of old but also of modern Babylon, which is the world (Rev. 14:8; 18:2; D&C 1:16). Ancient Babylon was destroyed as quickly as a whirlwind passes through the desert, and so will modern Babylon be devastated. "And again, verily I say unto you, the coming of the Lord draweth nigh, and it overtaketh the world as a thief in the night." For us, the command is, "gird up your loins, that you may be the children of light, and that day shall not overtake you as a thief" (D&C 106:4-5).
