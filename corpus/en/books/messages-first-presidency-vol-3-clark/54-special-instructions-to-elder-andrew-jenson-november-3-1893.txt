James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 3 ( 1966)

Special instructions to Elder Andrew Jenson, November 3, 1893

1893-November 3-Autobiography of Andrew Jenson, p. 211.

Andrew Jenson traveled throughout the Stakes of the Church. On previous occasions he had received special assignments from the First Presidency, for example his assignment to gather historical information on the Mountain Meadow Massacre (see Message of January 21, 1892 earlier in this volume). Seemingly the First Presidency had no special assignment for Andrew Jenson at this time. A later work of his, Church Chronology, was taken over and endorsed by the L.D.S. Church through the First Presidency; for this story see messages of July 9, 1898, December 3, 1898 and October 18, 1899 later in this volume.

Salt Lake City, Utah, Nov. 3, 1893.

Elder Andrew Jenson,

DEAR BROTHER:

Replying to your favor of Oct. 9th, in which you ask if we have any special instructions to give you in relation to your labors in the various stakes of Zion which you from time to time visit, we will say that we desire you in your travels and visits to magnify your calling as a minister of the truth, exhorting the people to righteousness, preaching the gospel, and instructing them in the principles of eternal life. You are a Seventy, and as such should never neglect any occasion of doing good, but should avail yourself of every opportunity to build up the Kingdom of God and to strengthen the saints. We have no special message for you to bear only to have you go in the spirit of your calling, as a herald of salvation, and bless, encourage and correct the saints.

May the Lord make you instrumental of continued good, is the prayer of your brethren,

WILFORD WOODRUFF, GEORGE Q. CANNON, JOSEPH F. SMITH.
