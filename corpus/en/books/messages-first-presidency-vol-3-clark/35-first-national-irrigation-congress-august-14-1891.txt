James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 3 ( 1966)

First National Irrigation Congress, August 14, 1891

1891-August 14-Circular letter, Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The First Presidency cooperated with the Committee on Statistics of the first national Irrigation Congress by sending a list of questions, designed to gather needed statistics, to the Bishops of the L.D.S. Wards with the signed cover letter reproduced below. Sixteen States and Territories were represented in the three day Congress. The meetings were held in the Exposition Building on the Tenth Ward Square in Salt Lake City. The second Irrigation Congress was held in Los Angeles, California in 1893. This is another example of a long-range policy of the First Presidency of the L.D.S. Church in cooperating with worthy civic causes for mutual benefit. For additional background see RCH 3:205-211.

THE IRRIGATION CONGRESS SALT LAKE CITY, SEPTEMBER 15, 16 and 17, 1891. Salt Lake City, Utah, August 14th, 1891. The subject to which Jesse W. Fox, Jun., Esq., Chairman of the Statistical Committee of the Irrigation Congress, calls your attention, is one of great importance to our Territory and people. The information asked for is needed to have our Territory and its agricultural interests appear in their proper light before our neighboring States and Territories. We, therefore, take this method of calling your attention to these requests, and take the liberty of emphasizing what Messrs. Fox and Stevenson have said, that our influence may be joined to theirs in impressing you with the importance of having the fullest information furnished upon these points and that, too, at once.

Very respectfully, your Brethren, WILFORD WOODRUFF, GEORGE Q. CANNON.

LIST OF QUESTIONS answered by Bishop of Ward for Committee on Statistics, Irrigation Congress, Salt Lake City, September 15, 16 and 17, 1891.
