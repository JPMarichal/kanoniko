Payment of Stake Presidents in Tithing produce, April 4, 1898

1898-April 4-Circular letter, Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The allowances to Stake Presidencies from general tithing funds of the Church, at least in the case of Cache Stake, were to be paid in produce rather than cash by these instructions.

The First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

Salt Lake City, Utah, April 4, 1898.

Stake Tithing Clerk, Cache Stake of Zion.

DEAR BROTHER:

The sum of $1000 has been appropriated in favor of your Stake Presidency to be drawn in whole or in part by them during the current year in equitable produce at tithing office prices, and you are authorized and requested to place the same to their credit, as follows: Orson Smith, $600; S. M. Molen, $200; Isaac Smith, $200.

At the close of the year please report the amount drawn by each of the brethren on account of this appreciation, and a regular order will be issued to cover the same.

Your Brother, WILFORD WOODRUFF.
