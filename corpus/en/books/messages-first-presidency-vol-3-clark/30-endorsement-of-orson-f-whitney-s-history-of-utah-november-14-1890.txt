Endorsement of Orson F. Whitney's History of Utah, November 14, 1890

1890-November 14-Original circular letter, Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Orson F. Whitney, whose forth-coming three volume History of Utah, is here endorsed by the First Presidency, was a prominent Mormon Bishop and writer. It was Orson F. Whitney who had been asked by President Wilford Woodruff to read to the General Conference of the Church the previous month (October 6th) the Church's Articles of Faith just prior to the presentation of "The Manifesto" previously discussed. This Conference incident gives some indication of the esteem in which Bishop Whitney was held by the First Presidency of the Church. This endorsement of his History of Utah would logically follow that confidence.

A LETTER to the Presidents of stakes, the Bishops and all whom it may concern:

SALT LAKE CITY, Nov. 14th, 1890.

DEAR BRETHREN:

You have doubtless learned that Bishop Orson F. Whitney, of this city, is engaged in preparing the History of Utah, and have probably received the Author's prospectus with accompanying testimonials from ourselves and other citizens. We wish it understood that we are heartily in sympathy with the project, and that it has our entire support. The author of the proposed work is a native of Utah, one in whom we have great confidence as an able writer and a conscientious historian. We believe his history, which is to be published in three large volumes, will be complete and reliable, and should be in the possession of every family; and can recommend it to the general public who desire to know the truth about our Territory and its people. We trust that the Latter-day Saints will give it their support and encouragement. WILFORD WOODRUFF, GEORGE Q. CANNON, JOSEPH F. SMITH. First Presidency of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
