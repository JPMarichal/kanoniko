James R. Clark, Messages of the First Presidency, vol. 3 ( 1966)

State Association of Irrigation for Utah, April 26, 1895

1895-April 26-Circular letter, Church Historian's Library, Salt Lake City, Utah.

The First Presidency had supported several national Irrigation Congresses. This letter encourages Stake Presidents and Bishops to support the organization of an Irrigation Commission for Utah and also a State Association of Irrigation. For additional background see RCH 3:205-214.

Salt Lake City, Utah, April 26, 1895. TO PRESIDENTS OF STAKES AND BISHOPS OF WARDS: DEAR BRETHREN: Your attention is called to the documents issued from the office of the "Irrigation Commission for Utah" for the creation of a "State Association of Irrigation."

The experience of the old and new States within the "Arid Belt" have been of great value in showing us that a high order of irrigation has of late years been established in nearly all of them, and that it behooves Utah not only to keep pace with its neighbors but continue the lead inaugurated by it.

To this end we feel that such an irrigation organization, as will thus be established among us, cannot fail to aid in all our people in many ways.

The publications of this Association, we feel assured, will be of unusual merit, and thoroughly of a character to develop the resources of Utah.

By your efforts in carrying out the formation of this Association you will not only aid your locality but continue the prestige which Utah has attained in the past.

Your Brethren, Wilford Woodruff, George Q. Cannon, Joseph F. Smith.
