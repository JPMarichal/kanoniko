Suspension of Moses Thatcher from Quorum of Twelve Apostles, October 24, 1896

1896-October 24-Deseret News Weekly, October 24, 1896, p. 584.

This is the official notice of the suspension of Moses Thatcher, member of Quorum of Twelve Apostles, from further exercising the functions of his office in the Priesthood. For details leading up to this notice see the discussion and references for the message of the First Presidency and Quorum of the Twelve of April 1896 earlier in this volume.

NOTICE to the officers and members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

It having been reported to us that Brother Moses Thatcher has on three different occasions recently addressed congregations of the Saints at Logan, Cache Valley, this therefore is to notify you that by action of the Council of First Presidency and Apostles of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, the name of Moses Thatcher was not presented at the General Conferences of April and October, 1896, to be sustained in his office as an Apostle; and that this action of the authorities suspended him from exercising any of the functions of the Priesthood, that is, from preaching the Gospel or administering in any of the ordinances thereof, until he, by making satisfactory amends to his fellow-servants, should be restored to their fellowship and that of the Church.

WILFORD WOODRUFF, GEORGE Q. CANNON, JOSEPH F. SMITH, First Presidency.
