Susette Fletcher Green, Dawn Hall Anderson, Women in the Covenant of Grace ( 1994)

A Valentine for Mel

EMMA LOU THAYNE [^1]

How much difference that we love? Do you, knotted with concerns, need me More for a head rub or for turning down the furnace Or for explaining silence and its offering Or for playfulness, the unbelievably enormous attractions Or for seeing that things happen? Waiting as we are today for four more days before we Know why blood tests highlight a prostate like your Yellow felt tip on a page of scripture, Much as love would like, I cannot be with you on the Inside any more than from this little distance You can feel the spring that wakes in me, Your wife watching the snow melt in the rain And listening for your sweet whistle of arrival Cradling me as I would cradle you In the well-supported shape you have worried And loved into our life. My one hand holding tight to yours I will come celebrate with you That so far what has tried to kill us, either one, Has not succeeded. And that for each other Nothing ever will.
