Susette Fletcher Green, Dawn Hall Anderson, Women in the Covenant of Grace ( 1994)

Family Ties

LUCILE JENSEN [^1]

My mother wanted excellence from me (I thought perfection). She was always instructing me what to And not to do. It was tough to find approval Between her rules— It was always too much or too little Too soon or too late, Never enough. She still tries improving me (Goodness knows I need it). She thinks it makes no difference Now I'm gone from home, But her apron strings can still tie knots In me— I still peel the potato skins too shallow Or too deep, Then wash the rebellion down the sink.

---
Notas:
[^1] Teachers can praise. "Peter, that was an excellent solution to that problem." "Janice, you turned in a perfect paper." "Kurt, that was another perceptive comment."
