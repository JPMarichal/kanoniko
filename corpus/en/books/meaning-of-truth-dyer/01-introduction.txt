Alvin R. Dyer, Meaning of Truth ( 1961)

INTRODUCTION

The preparation of the manuscripts which eventually provided the material for the three articles of this book, (A) The Day of the Gentile, (B) The Meaning of Truth, (C) The Kingdom of Evil, resulted from talks given to missionary and other groups. Publication of them was sought only after frequent requests made by listeners for the material. Even then they would not have come to print had it not been for the frequent importuning of my missionary son Brent and wonderful daughter Gloria who felt that others would enjoy the thoughts expressed in them. Thus this book is dedicated to them.

There are many wonderful aspects of the gospel which do not always seem clear. Answers to these questions, while not affecting our individual pursuit of salvation, can nevertheless provide enrichment of thought and purpose, actually motivating us to the living of a better Christian life.

The thoughts given in treating these three subjects have led many to a greater appreciation of the glorious gospel plan which daily expands before our very eyes.

There can be no other purpose in the preparation of this material than to provide incentive in the lives of those who read its contents to respond to the knowledge that there is a God-chosen people, and incentive to live in the light of that knowledge.
