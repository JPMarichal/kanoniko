For Emma

ANITA TANNER

Mississippi ice. The flux of her cumbersome skirt corrugated in the grip of her children: Joseph to one side, Julia on the other. Her arms midnight aching from the weight of two more babes, Frederick and Alexander hung in embrace. Heavy bags of the Prophet's papers bound to her waist. Her mind behind with him shut in prison. Home, cold-impacted Missouri, left hollow by her flight across the river's frozen slate. The careful tramp of her feet scuffing the ice, her name aching in my throat every time it's heard.
