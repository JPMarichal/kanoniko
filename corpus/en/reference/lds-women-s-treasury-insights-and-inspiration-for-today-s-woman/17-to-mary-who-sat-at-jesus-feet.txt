To Mary—Who Sat at Jesus' Feet

SHIRLEY ADWENA HARVEY

Your dark eyes never left His gentle face As you sat listening near the Savior's feet While Martha's hurried steps kept constant pace With the rhythm of your heart's excited beat. Your sister's call to serve was never heard, You gave no thought to feasting or attire— Your ears were strained to catch each precious word As spirit burned within you like a fire. You knew this was the moment you should seek The needful things to fill your longing heart And joy-tears must have trickled down your cheek When He said you had chosen that good part. Dear Mary, in your sweet, far-sighted way— You teach us of priorities today.
