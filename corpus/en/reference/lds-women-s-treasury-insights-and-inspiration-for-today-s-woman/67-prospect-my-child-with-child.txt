Various authors, LDS Women's Treasury: Insights and Inspiration for Today's Woman ( 1997)

Prospect: My Child with Child

EMMA LOU THAYNE

I remember the terrible joy of you in me, the groping of the unplumbed for signs as if new nerves would sear somewhere in annunciation: The awakening at any hour first to "Who is there?" then "Where?" and "Do you know too?" Some shimmer licked my inner sides with long exploratory tongues of testing whether I could hold my own. Now having held I hear you who occupied that place tell me what fire crazes my vacant fullness on this night when I remember with the brightness of your dark eyes.
