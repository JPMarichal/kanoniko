Pioneer Women

ENOLA CHAMBERLIN

By roofless fires they dreamed of cedar thatch Above a sheltered hearth; in fearful hours They dreamed of homes with doors secured by latch; Of windows fragrant with a lilac's flowers. In poignant cold they dreamed the warmth of down; In stagnant heat, of cool, wind-shaken shade; In loneliness, of love's complacent crown. Day by grim day they bravely stitched the seam To bind fulfillment to the glowing dream.
