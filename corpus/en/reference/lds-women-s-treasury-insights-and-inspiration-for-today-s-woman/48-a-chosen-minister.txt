A Chosen Minister

ELIZABETH BARRETT BROWNING

I thought a child was given to sanctify A woman, set her free in the sight of all The clear-eyed heavens, a chosen minister To do their business, and lead spirits up The difficult blue heights. A woman lives Not bettered, quickened toward the truth and good Through being a mother? Then she is none.
