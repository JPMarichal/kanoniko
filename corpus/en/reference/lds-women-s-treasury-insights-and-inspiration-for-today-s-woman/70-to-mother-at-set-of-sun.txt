To Mother, at Set of Sun

AUTHOR UNKNOWN

As once you stroked my thin and silver hair, So I stroke yours now at the set of sun. I watch your tottering mind, its day's work done, As once you watched with forward-looking care My tottering feet. I love you as I should. Stay with me; lean on me; I'll make no sign I was your child; and now time makes you mine. Stay with me yet a while at home, and do me good.
