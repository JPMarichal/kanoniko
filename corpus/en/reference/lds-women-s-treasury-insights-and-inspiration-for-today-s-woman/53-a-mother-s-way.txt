A Mother's Way

IRIS W. SCHOW

She laid her body like a wall To shield her little one from harm; She made a cover of her heart And her cupped hands to keep him warm. Her footprints marked the narrow way His feet might follow to his goal; And for the nurture of his mind She spread a feast from her own soul.
