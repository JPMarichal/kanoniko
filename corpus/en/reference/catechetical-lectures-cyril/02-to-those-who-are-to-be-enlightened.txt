Lecture 1

Of Our Holy Father Cyril, Archbishop of Jerusalem, Delivered Extempore at Jerusalem, as an Introductory Lecture to Those Who Had Come Forward for Baptism: With a Reading from Isaiah

To Those Who Are To Be Enlightened

Wash you, make you clean; put away your iniquities from your souls, from before mine eyes, and the rest.
