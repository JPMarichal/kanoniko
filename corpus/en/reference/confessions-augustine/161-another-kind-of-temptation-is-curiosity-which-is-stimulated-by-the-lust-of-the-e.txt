Augustine, Confessions ( 399)

Chapter 35

Another Kind of Temptation Is Curiosity, Which Is Stimulated by the Lust of the Eyes

For when this heart of ours is made the receptacle of such things, and bears crowds of this abounding vanity, then are our prayers often interrupted and disturbed thereby; and whilst in Thy presence we direct the voice of our heart to Thine ears, this so great a matter is broken off by the influx of I know not what idle thoughts.
