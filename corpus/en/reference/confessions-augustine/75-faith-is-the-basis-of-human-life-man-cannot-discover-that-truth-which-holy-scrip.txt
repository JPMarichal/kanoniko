Augustine, Confessions ( 399)

Chapter 5

Faith Is the Basis of Human Life; Man Cannot Discover That Truth Which Holy Scripture Has Disclosed

For it was the same thing that was most of all to be believed, since no wranglings of blasphemous questions, whereof I had read so many amongst the self-contradicting philosophers, could once wring the belief from me that Thou art,—whatsoever Thou wert, though what I knew not,—or that the government of human affairs belongs to Thee.
