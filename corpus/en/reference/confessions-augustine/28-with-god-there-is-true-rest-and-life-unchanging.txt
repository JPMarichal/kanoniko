Augustine, Confessions ( 399)

Chapter 10

With God There Is True Rest and Life Unchanging

He who enters into thee enters into the joy of his Lord, a and shall have no fear, and shall do excellently in the most Excellent. I sank away from Thee, O my God, and I wandered too far from Thee, my stay, in my youth, and became to myself an unfruitful land.
