Augustine, Confessions ( 399)

Chapter 12

Having Prayed to God, He Pours Forth a Shower of Tears, And, Admonished by a Voice, He Opens the Book and Reads the Words in Rom. 13:13; by Which, Being Changed in His Whole Soul, He Discloses the Divine Favour to His Friend and His Mother

"But Thou, O Lord, how long?" "How long, Lord? Wilt Thou be angry for ever? Oh, remember not against us former iniquities;" for I felt that I was enthralled by them. I sent up these sorrowful cries,—"zhow long, how long? Tomorrow, and tomorrow? Why not now? Why is there not this hour an end to my uncleanness?"
