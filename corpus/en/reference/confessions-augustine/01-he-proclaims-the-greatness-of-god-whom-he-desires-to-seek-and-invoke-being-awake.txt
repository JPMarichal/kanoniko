About the Author

About the Author

Augstine is known as the first theologian, and his influence on Christianity has been profound. His works include Confessions, a spiritual autobiography; On the Trinity, which systematized Christian doctrine; and The City of God, a defense of Christianity against paganism. He died at Hippo on August 28, 430.
