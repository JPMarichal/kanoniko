Wars And The Prince Of Peace

Higher critics deny Chapter IX - XIV to be from Zechariah’s pen as they have attacked the authorship of Daniel. But the Septuagint, which is the Greek translation of the O.T., 200 B.C., and the compilers of the Jewish canon recognise Zechariah’s authorship. Such ancient witnesses to God’s Word are hard to assail.

It is to be noted that Chapters I - VIII refers chiefly to the times in which the prophet lived, and the subject in view was the rebuilding of the Temple. Chapters IX - XIV, the second part of the Book, looks rather to the future.

While Chapters I - VIII treats of Israel under Medo-Persia rule, Chapters IX, X looks beyond to Greece, Chapter XI to Rome, Chapters XII - XIV to Israel in the very end-times, at the second coming of Christ.
