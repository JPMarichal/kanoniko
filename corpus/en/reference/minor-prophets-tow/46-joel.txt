The plague is so severe that even the meat (food) offering and the drink offering run out because of short supply. Needless to say the farmers and vinedressers are left high and dry with no produce to work on. The mood of a great depression (slump) paralyses the whole land. It is like the great Depression of 1929-30 that brought the rubber planting business in Malaysia to a complete standstill, and people literally were subsisting on a starvation diet. The rubber tappers in my father’s rubber estate trapped rats to supplement their meagre rations.

Would the people seek God now in repentance? At such a time, according to the call of the prophet, the Pastor should call his congregation to fasting and searching of heart (Calvin). As for Joel he sees the locust plague as an adumbration of the great Day of the Lord coming to judge the earth. The only way out of total destruction is repentance.
