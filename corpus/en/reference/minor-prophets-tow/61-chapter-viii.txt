Message Of Basket Of Summer Fruit

This chapter begins with the vision of a basket of summer fruit. That is to say Israel, like the plucked summer fruit, is ripe for judgment.

For all these crimes the land will be shaken under God’s judgment, as in an earthquake and by a flood. That the sun will be darkened at noon refers to what Jesus says, the powers of heaven shall be shaken. Their songs will be turned into lamentations.

Greater judgment will be in the spiritual when there will be a famine of hearing the Word of God. It will be difficult to hear a good sermon. In contrast, idolatry will be multiplied from Dan and Beersheba, i.e. even to the southern ends of Judah. Is this not reflected in Christendom today, especially in the western nations?
