35

In extending His grace to them Jehovah sent prophets to them with multiplied “visions and similitudes.” But they seek after falsehoods through Israel at Gilead on the east and Gilgal on the west of the River Jordan. Once again Israel is reminded of the example of their ancestor, how he kept to the terms of contract in getting a wife. He lived up to his word. She is reminded of Moses whom God sent to save them from Egypt and preserve them.

Instead of being grateful to God for all His mercies, Israel in the days of Hosea “provoked Him to anger most bitterly: therefore shall he leave his blood upon him, and his reproach shall his Lord return unto him.”
