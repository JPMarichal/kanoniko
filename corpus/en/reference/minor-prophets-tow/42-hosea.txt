God would deliver them from the grave and death if Israel would only repent. Though Israel seems to prosper God will send an east wind, that is Assyria, to destroy them. “Samaria shall become desolate; for she hath rebelled against her God: they shall fall by the sword: their infants shall be dashed in pieces, and their women with child shall be ripped up” (verse 16).
