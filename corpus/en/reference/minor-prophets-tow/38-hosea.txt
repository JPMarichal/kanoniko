33

Though His judgment against Israel shall be severe, it will not obliterate them, for God will keep His covenant with them. This leads us to the days of the Millennium.

When He comes He will roar like a lion against Israel’s enemies and those dispersed in the war shall return. Those dispersed to the south will return as will those from the north and east.

As for now Israel is still very sinful and deceitful. Judah by comparison is in God’s favour.
