LORD of hosts: I will go also. Yea, many people and strong nations shall come to seek the LORD of hosts in Jerusalem, and to pray before the LORD. Thus saith the LORD of hosts; In those days it shall come to pass, that ten men shall take hold out of all languages of the nations, even shall take hold of the skirt of him that is a Jew, saying, We will go with you: for we have heard that God is with you.

This prophecy look further to a growing movement of people from many cities and from powerful nations who would fly to Jerusalem on pilgrimage — “to pray for the Lord” (verse 22). Many people from many nations will be led by the Jews to seek the Lord’s blessing in their land. In the Millennium it will be the Jews’ turn to be missionaries to the nations afar off. “God’s presence with the Jews would make the Jewish nation great. Jerusalem, being on top of the world, will be looked up to by all the nations of the earth.” Not America to be sure!
