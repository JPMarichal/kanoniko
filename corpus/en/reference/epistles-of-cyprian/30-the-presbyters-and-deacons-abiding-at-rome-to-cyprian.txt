Cyprian, Epistles of Cyprian ( 100)

Epistle 29

The Presbyters and Deacons Abiding at Rome, To Cyprian

Argument—The Roman Church Declares Its Judgment Concerning the Lapsed to Be in Agreement with the Carthaginian Decrees by Any Indulgence Shown to the Lapsed Is Required to Be in Accordance with the Law of the Gospel by That the Peace Granted by the Confessors Depends Only upon Grace and Good-Will, Is Manifest from the Fact That the Lapsed Are Referred to the Bishops by the Seditious Demand for Peace Made by Felicissimus Is to Be Attributed to Faction.

100
