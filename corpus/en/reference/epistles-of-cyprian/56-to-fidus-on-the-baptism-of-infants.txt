Cyprian, Epistles of Cyprian ( 100)

Epistle 58

To Fidus, on the Baptism of Infants

Argument—In This Letter Cyprian Is Not Establishing Any New Decree; But Keeping Most Firmly the Faith of the Church, for the Correction of Those Who Thought That an Infant Must Not Be Baptized Before the Eighth Day up Its Birth, He Decreed with Some of His Fellow-Bishops, That as Soon as It Was Born It Might Properly Be Baptized—He Takes Occasion, However, to Refuse to Recall the Peace That Had Been Granted to One Victor, Although It Had Been Granted against the Decrees of Synods Concerning the Lapsed; But Forbids Therapius the Bishop to Do It in Other Cases.
