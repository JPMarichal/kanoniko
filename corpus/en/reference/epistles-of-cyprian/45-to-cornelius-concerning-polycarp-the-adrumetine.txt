Cyprian, Epistles of Cyprian ( 100)

Epistle 44

To Cornelius, Concerning Polycarp The Adrumetine

Argument—He Excuses Himself in This Letter for What Had Occurred, in That, During the Time He Was at Adrumetum, Letters Had Been Sent Thence by the Clergy of Polycarp, Not to Cornelius, But to the Roman Clergy, Notwithstanding That Previously Polycarp Himself Had Written Rather to Cornelius—It Appears Tolerably Plain from the Context Itself That This Was Written after the Previous Ones.
