Cyprian, Epistles of Cyprian ( 100)

Epistle 12

To the Clergy, Concerning the Lapsed and Catechumens, That They Should Not Be Left without Superintendence

Argument—The Burden of This Letter, as of the Succeeding One, Is Found Below in the 14th Epistle—"But Afterwards," He Says, "When Some of the Lapsed, Whether of Their Own Accord, Or by the Suggestion of Any Other, Broke Forth with a Daring Demand, as Though They Would Endeavour, by a Violent Effort, to Extort the Peace That Had Been Promised to Them by the Martyrs and Confessors," Etc.
