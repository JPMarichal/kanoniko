Cyprian, Epistles of Cyprian ( 100)

Epistle 26

Cyprian to the Lapsed

Argument—The Argument of This Letter Is Found Below in Letter Xxvii by "They Wrote to Me," Says He, "Not Asking That Peace Should Be Granted Them, But Claiming It for Themselves as Already Granted, Because They Say That Paulus Has Given Peace to All; as You Will Read in Their Letter of Which I Have Sent You a Copy, Together with What I Briefly Replied to Them" But the Letter of the Lapsed to Which He Replies Is Wanting.
