One Month Before Bible Study Begins

Once the participants are confirmed and a month before the initial meeting, send an email to all participants. Let them know how to prepare for the first week of the study, who will be joining the study, logistics, day/time each week, and get a book in their hands to read. This book will provide a first week discussion and ice breaker to get everyone talking. If you don’t have a book in mind, consider Mark Cahill’s One Heartbeat Away at http://www.markcahill.org/request.html

A sample Intro Email to the Bible Study is in the appendix.

Pray daily for yourself and your participants that God will prepare hearts for the weeks ahead.
