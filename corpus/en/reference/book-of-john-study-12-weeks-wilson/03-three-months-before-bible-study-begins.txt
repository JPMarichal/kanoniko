Three Months Before Bible Study Begins

The study of the book of John is a great next intentional step with your guests after the High Tech Prayer Breakfast.

At least three months before you plan to start the study, begin inviting. A sample Invitation Email is in the appendix. The email should be sent separately to each individual you plan to invite. We would recommend a small group study of 10 or less. Begin to pray for who God will bring to the study. Who has He placed on your heart, and coincidently () who has He placed recently in your life?
