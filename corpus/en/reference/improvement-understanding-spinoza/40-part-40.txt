Baruch Spinoza, On the Improvement of the Understanding ( 1662)

Part 40

(1) Again, the more things the mind knows, the better does it understand its own strength and the order of nature; by increased self-knowledge, it can direct itself more easily, and lay down rules for its own guidance; and, by increased knowledge of nature, it can more easily avoid what is useless.

(2) And this is the sum total of method, as we have already stated.
