The first four chapters (poems) are dirges, loaded with pain and agony, except

for two brief outbursts of faith and hope in 3:19–42 and 3:55–60. Chapter 5 is not quite as intense, but even there the throb of deep hurt is present. Throughout the poems, one is aware of theological insights typical of Jeremiah. Sharp contrasts and vivid comparisons are often repeated, and figurative language is abundant.
