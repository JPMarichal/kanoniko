The reTurn frOm exile

When Cyrus the Persian captured Babylon in 539 b.c., government controls on

captive peoples were loosened and the way was opened for exiled Judah to begin the return to her homeland. The ascension and victory of Cyrus fulfilled the prophecy of Isaiah 44:28—45:4. Three major expeditions made the journey: one in 538–537 b.c. under Zerubbabel, the second under Ezra in 458 b.c., and the last under Nehemiah in 444 b.c.
