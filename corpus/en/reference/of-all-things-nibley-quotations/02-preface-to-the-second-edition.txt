Hugh Nibley, Gary P. Gillum, Of All Things! Classic Quotations from Hugh Nibley ( 1981)

PREFACE TO THE SECOND EDITION

There would be no second edition without the encouragement of Noel Reynolds, John Welch, Stephen Ricks, Donald Parry, and the Foundation for Ancient Research and Mormon Studies (F.A.R.M.S.). Signature Books, which originally published the first edition in 1981, has graciously given F.A.R.M.S. permission to publish this expanded edition. Finally, Rosa M. Tillman and Shirley S. Ricks prepared and formatted the text for publication. I am very grateful to all of these friends for their assistance. The enthusiasm for Nibley that my wife Signe and some of our seventeen children have exhibited has also been an effective catalyst for the completion of this expanded edition. My warmest thanks goes to them.

This second edition of Of All Things! Classic Quotations from Hugh Nibley adds quotations from Nibley's works published (or discovered) from 1980, when the first edition was compiled, through 1992. The bibliography has been revised, corrected, and updated to reflect the currently available twelve volumes of The Collected Works of Hugh Nibley (CWHN). The page numbers cited are generally to the most recent printed edition of the selection. For this time-consuming labor, I again give my thanks to Rosa and Shirley.

Gary P. Gillum
