Pessimism

Neither provincialism nor pessimism permits a person to see himself, life, or the universe as these things "really are" and "as they really will be" (Jacob 4:13). ("But for a Small Moment", p. 102.)

Life, or any particular situation, if viewed only through the peephole of pessimism, presents a puzzling or discouraging picture indeed. Instead of wonder, awe, and pattern, which the Christian sees, the disciples of despair disclaim any knowledge of a "big picture" of life in which "all things denote there is a God."
