Work

We ignore the fact that work is a spiritual necessity at the peril of our souls. (A Time to Choose, p. 54.)

The principle of work is also fundamental to spiritual ecology. We shall come to know that work is a spiritual necessity, even if the time comes when it is not an economic necessity. ("For the Power Is in Them Â� " Mormon Musings, p. 2.)

If we ponder just what it is that will rise with us in the resurrection, it seems clear that our intelligence will rise with us, meaning not simply our IQ, but also our capacity to receive and apply truth. Our talents, attributes, and skills will rise with us; certainly also our capacity to learn, our degree of self-discipline, and our capacity to work. Our precise form of work here may have no counterpart there, but the capacity to work will never be obsolete. (We Will Prove Them Herewith, p. 12.)
