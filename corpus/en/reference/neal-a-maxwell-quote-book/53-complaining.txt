Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Complaining

Many reject the scriptures, the moral memory of mankind, and then declare absolutely the absence of absolutes. Others reject the light of the gospel and then grump over the growing darkness. Still others cut themselves off from God and lament the loneliness of the universe. Some pursue the paths of him who openly desires mankind's misery (see 2 Ne. 2:27), and then bemoan their discontent. (Ensign, May 1983, p. 10.)
