Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Duty

Our Christian duties are like keys on a piano keyboard: touch them correctly and in concert and renewing music is inevitable; if one chord doesn't lift us, another will! But one must do the touching himself, for we are not dealing with a player piano. (Notwithstanding My Weakness, p. 114.)

The truly converted will not be diverted from their duties to God or to each other. (If Thou Endure It Well, p. 89.)
