Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Potential

The Lord loves each of us too much to merely let us go on being what we now are, for he knows what we have the possibility to become! ("'In Him All Things Hold Together,'" p. 107.)

Jesus knows the sheep of His fold not only for what they now are but also for what they have the power to become. (Even As I Am, p. 78.)

When a sudden, stabbing light exposes the gap between what we are and what we think we are, can we, like Peter, let that light be a healing laser? (Men and Women of Christ, p. 66.)
