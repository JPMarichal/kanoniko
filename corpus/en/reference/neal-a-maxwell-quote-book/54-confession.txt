Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Confession

All sins are to be confessed to the Lord, some to a Church official, some to others, and some to all of these. A few may require public confession. Confessing aids forsaking. We cannot expect to sin publicly and extensively and then expect to be rescued privately and quickly, being beaten "with only a few stripes." (See D&C 42:88-93.) (Ensign, November 1991, p. 31.)

Those who hold back major sins will be held back. So will those who refuse to work at repentance humbly and honestly with the Lord's appointed. The Lord will not be mocked. (Ensign, November 1991, p. 32.)
