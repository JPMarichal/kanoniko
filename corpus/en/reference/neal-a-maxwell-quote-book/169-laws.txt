Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Laws

The rules of the spiritual universe are as demanding as those of the physical universe. The immutable laws which must be obeyed for happiness on this spaceship Earth are just as fixed as the physical laws which we also violate at our own peril. (A Time to Choose, p. 45.)

The Gospel is the counsel of a Super Intelligence as to how we can progress in a cold universe which responds only to law. Man did not get to the moon with random trajectories and with each astronaut "doing his own thing." The price for reaching the moon was obedience to universal law. (A Time to Choose, p. 22.)
