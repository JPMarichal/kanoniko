Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Renewal

We forget to pace ourselves at times. Rest and renewal permit us to go farther faster in this mortal marathon. (We Will Prove Them Herewith, p. 109.)

Sometimes being worn down is our own fault for not providing space and time for ourselves for spiritual renewal, an area that to the adversary is "off limits." We need the equivalent of designated nonsmoking areas to secure us from secondhand sensuality, inappropriate humor, twisted film, and bad music. ("Not My Will, But Thine", p. 75.)
