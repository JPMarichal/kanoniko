Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Handbooks

There can be no all-purpose handbook to cover all human situations with a page for every problem; each of us must develop his own responses to such dilemmas, and those responses must be given to us by the Holy Ghost, who is the unerring guide in telling us "all things" we need to do. (Wherefore, Ye Must Press Forward, p. 38.)
