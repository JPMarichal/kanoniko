Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Valiance

[Some Church] members are "honorable" but not "valiant." They are not really aware of the gap nor of the importance of closing it(see D&C 76:75,79 These "honorable" individuals are certainly not miserable or wicked, nor are they unrighteous and unhappy. It is not what they have done, but what they have left undone that is amiss. For example, if valiant, they could touch others deeply instead of merely being remembered pleasantly. (Ensign, November 1995, p. 22.)
