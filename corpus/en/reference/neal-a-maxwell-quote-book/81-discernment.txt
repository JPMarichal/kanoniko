Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Discernment

We cannot become like Jesus, attribute by attribute, if we display hesitancy as to our purity, or if we are undiscerning between joy and pleasure. Lust is no more like love than itching is to joy, or talkativeness to wisdom, or indulgence to compassion, or passivity to patience. (Ensign, February 1986, p. 19.)
