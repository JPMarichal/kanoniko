Eternal Life

Upon receiving eternal life, whatever the deprivations of the faithful will have been in mortality, fulness will follow. Mortal deprivations will give way to God's celestial benefactions. And He does not give grudgingly. Being a perfect Father, He delights to honor those who serve Him (see D&C 76:5). (A Wonderful Flood of Light, p. 58.)
