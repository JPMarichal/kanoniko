Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Heaven

Like Alma, we will "long to be there" (Alma 36:22), in the "royal courts on high." It is the only destination that really matters. Resplendent reunion awaits us! What is more reasonable and more wonderful than children going home? Especially to a home where the past, the present, and the future form an everlasting and eternal now! (Men and Women of Christ, p. 51.)
