Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Control

Some of us seek to control others instead of letting them grow. Controlling is, in a sense, keeping one's brother-constraining and hedging him in. Some of us want to tie people to us even if it means limiting their possibilities instead of encouraging and helping them on their way. There is great satisfaction in helping to "launch" another person who may rise to heights we ourselves may not have achieved. Real brothers are real boosters. (Wherefore, Ye Must Press Forward, p. 92.)
