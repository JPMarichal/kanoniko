Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Funerals

Though otherwise "lively," hope stands quietly with us at funerals. Our tears are just as wet, but not because of despair. Rather, they are tears of appreciation evoked by poignant separation. They will change, ere long, to tears of glorious anticipation. Yet the emptiness is so real and so restless it initiates a retroactive inventory of what is now so painfully missing, doing so, however, while forecasting fulness and resplendent reunion! (Ensign, November 1994, p. 36.)
