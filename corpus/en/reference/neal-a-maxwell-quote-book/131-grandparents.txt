Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Grandparents

Grandparents are often the strategic reserve who can overlook dirty clothes and untied shoelaces and sibling rivalries-not because they fail to see such, but rather, because their unconditional love for their grandchildren is so irrepressible. (That My Family Should Partake, p. 101.)

Grandparents have usually experienced enough peaks and valleys with offspring so that they do not overreact and also so that they don't get overinvolved. (That My Family Should Partake, p. 101.)
