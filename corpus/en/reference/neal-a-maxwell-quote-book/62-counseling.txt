Counseling

We should not be candid merely to punish or to meet our own ego needs. Rather, we must do as Paul says: speak "the truth in love." (Eph. 4:15.) And even when we must give reproof, we should, as Paul also suggested, confirm our love lest the other individual "be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow." (2 Cor. 2:7-8.) In the Doctrine and Covenants, we are asked to "show forth an increase of love"-not the same level of love, but a visible "increase." (D&C 121:40-44.) (All These Things Shall Give Thee Experience, p. 75.)

Counseling delayed may be counseling denied so far as the critical moment is concerned. (Wherefore, Ye Must Press Forward, p. 111.)
