Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Goodness

It isn't that random goodness lacks heroism. Rather, in isolation it lacks effectiveness. Sharpshooters can irritate and delay anadvancing enemy, but they cannot mount a counteroffensive. Random goodness leads to Dunkirks, not to D-Days. (Deposition of a Disciple, p. 35.)

General goodness is no excuse for failing to work on those things which we yet lack. (Ensign, May 1995, p. 68.)
