Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Affluence

Affluence is usually no friend of spirituality, and, oh, how we hope that teaching is not true-and, oh, how our teeth chatter when the chills of experience tell us it is true! (A Time to Choose, p. 33.)

Adversity does not always produce appreciation, but affluence rarely does. (Look Back at Sodom, p. 11.)
