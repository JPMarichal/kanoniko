Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Millennium

That millenarian moment will not spring out of senates, will not be propelled by mortal proclamations, and will not be traceable to treaties. Rather, the King, Jesus Christ, will have first established His kingdom and His people, physically and spiritually, and He will then come and judge all societies according to His standards, not secular standards. (Wherefore, Ye Must Press Forward, p. 5.)
