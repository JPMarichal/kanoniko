Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Evil

Evil is not only erotic, it is erratic, since it must entice so many in such a multitude of ways. (Of One Heart, p. 25.)

Evil always seeks company, for it cannot be by itself alone. Satan detests solitude, for solitude turns him in upon himself, reminding him of what glories might have been. (Of One Heart, p. 50.)
