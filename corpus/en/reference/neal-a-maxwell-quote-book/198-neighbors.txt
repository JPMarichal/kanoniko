Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Neighbors

A man who is too busy to notice a neighbor also has no time to smell the flowers. Smallness of soul keeps us from contemplating both bird and brother, when God would have us exult over all his creations. (Of One Heart, p. 32.)

Alas, it is regrettable that those who live in neighborhoods are not necessarily neighbors. (If Thou Endure It Well, p. 91.)
