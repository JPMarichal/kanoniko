Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Rationalizing

Occasionally we see those who do something bad justify it on the grounds that thereby they can thereafter do some good: "If I can just make this deal or gain this office, think of the position I will be in to do much good." This rationalizing might be called looking beyond Lucifer's shoulder-on to imagined possibilities. It is a very different thing from saying, "Get thee behind me!" (See Matt. 16:23.) (Men and Women of Christ, p. 130.)
