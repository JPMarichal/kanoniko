Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Thoughts

In a decaying environment, the mind is the last redoubt of righteousness, and it must be preserved even amid bombardment by evil stimuli. (Ensign, May 1987, p. 71.)

The human mind is remarkably retentive. We must be careful of what we allow in our mind, for it will be there for a long time, reasserting itself at those very times when we may be most vulnerable. Just as harmful chemicals heedlessly dumped in a vacant lot can later prove lethal, so toxic thoughts and the mulching of the wrong memories in the vacant corner of the mind also take their toll. (We Will Prove Them Herewith, p. 44.)
