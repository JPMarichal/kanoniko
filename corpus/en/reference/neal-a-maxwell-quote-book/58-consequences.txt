Consequences

We'd better want the consequences of what we want! (Ensign, November 1995, p. 23.)

Consequences come both quickly and slowly in the lives of individuals and of whole societies. Sometimes scarcely one generation passes before some consequences appear. Other consequences slumber like a silent virus, ready to take a later toll. ("Not My Will, But Thine", p. 67.)

There are no immunities from the consequences of our choices. (We Will Prove Them Herewith, p. 83.)
