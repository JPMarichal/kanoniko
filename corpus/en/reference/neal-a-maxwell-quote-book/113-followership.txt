Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Followership

Serious sinners who regard themselves as free are the most obvious satellites in our human solar system. If one is going to be a satellite, he had better pick his Saturns carefully. Indeed, followership in the kingdom leaves no place for static, satellite relationships, for individual growth based on eternal principles is the criterion. To mix metaphors, Pied Piper situations are not only hard on the followers but are bad for the Pied Pipers too. (Wherefore, Ye Must Press Forward, p. 102.)
