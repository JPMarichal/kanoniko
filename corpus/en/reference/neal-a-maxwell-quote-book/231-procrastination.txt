Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Procrastination

Indeed, one of the most cruel games anyone can play with self is the "not yet" game-hoping to sin just a bit more before ceasing; to enjoy the praise of the world a little longer before turning away from the applause; to win just once more in the wearing sweepstakes of materialism; to be chaste, but not yet; to be good neighbors, but not now. (Ensign, November 1974, p. 13.)

One can play upon the harpstrings of hesitations and reservations just so long, and then one faces that special moment-a moment when what has been sensed, mutely, suddenly finds voice and cries out with tears, "Lord, I believe; help thou mine unbelief." (Mark 9:24.) (Ensign, November 1974, p. 13.)
