Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Disobedience

Those few in deliberate noncompliance[includ[e] some who cast off on intellectual and behavioral bungee cords in search of new sensations, only to be jerked about by the old heresies and the old sins. (Ensign, November 1992, p. 65.)

Those who wrongly and heedlessly do their own thing are really doing Lucifer's thing in an unconscious pattern of sobering servility. ("Not My Will, But Thine", p. 8.)
