Since there are no instant Christians, to withhold what we can do to accelerate the process of the perfection of Zion until Zion is nearly perfected is to misconstrue mortality. To withhold all (or even much) of our fellowship, our talents, or our tithing until the Church and its people meet our "high" standard is like trying tobook passage on Noah's ark without driving a nail in a plank. We simply walk on board and ask to be shown to our stateroom and inquire as we enter the stateroom about what time dinner is served at the Captain's table! We must sign on for the voyage with all our imperfections, and commit to help each other. (Wherefore, Ye Must Press Forward, pp. 5-6.)
