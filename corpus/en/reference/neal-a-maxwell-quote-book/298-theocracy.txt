Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Theocracy

Some dismiss the Church out of hand for not being trendy in its theology and for being authoritarian. To such I say, better a true theocracy with a little democracy than a democracy without any theology. Yes, the kingdom of God is a kingdom; there is no "one man, one vote" rule between its King and its citizens. ("'All Hell Is Moved,'" p. 177.)
