Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Creation

We sing, "The stars in the heavens looked down where He lay." The on-looking universe, created by Jesus under the Father's direction, contained "worlds without number" (Moses 1:33). In that sense, Christ was cradled not only in a manger but also in the midst of His own vast creations. (The Christmas Scene, p. 3.)
