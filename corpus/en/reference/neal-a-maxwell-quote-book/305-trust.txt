Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Trust

It is better to trust and sometimes be disappointed than to be forever mistrusting though right occasionally. This is to endorse empathy, not naivete. ("Insights from My Life," p. 199.)

Mostly, to avoid muttering, we need to trust more. So many of the things muttered about beforehand turn out to be marvelous experiences later, and we are inwardly, and deservedly, ashamed for having grumbled. (Deposition of a Disciple, p. 31.)
