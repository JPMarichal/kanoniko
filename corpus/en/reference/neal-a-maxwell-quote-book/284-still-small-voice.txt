Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Still, Small Voice

Whatever the decibels of decadence, these need not overwhelm the still, small voice! Some of the best sermons we will ever hear will be thus prompted from the pulpit of memory-to an audience of one! (Ensign, May 1993, p. 77.)

Imperviousness to the promptings of the still small voice of God will also mean that we have ears but cannot hear, not only the promptings of God but also the pleas of men. (A Time to Choose, pp. 59-60.)
