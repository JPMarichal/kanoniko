Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Hardness of Heart

Perhaps the worst curse that finally falls upon those who are hard of heart and who fight against God is the curse of being consigned to be with each other. (Of One Heart, p. 50.)

Hardness of heart in many will produce other manifestations of hardness and coarseness. Civility will be one casualty of these conditions, and a lowered capacity to achieve reconciliation, whether in a marriage or between interest groups, will be another. (Notwithstanding My Weakness, p. 18.)
