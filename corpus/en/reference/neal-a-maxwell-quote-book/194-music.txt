Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Music

The symphonic strains of scriptural music can give our lives lilt and tone, a way of tuning our lives by reference to a celestial scale. The melodies are the marching music for the traveler on the strait and narrow way! (A Time to Choose, p. 72.)

High-quality music is so desperately needed to counteract secular sounds. ("Not My Will, But Thine", p. 76.)
