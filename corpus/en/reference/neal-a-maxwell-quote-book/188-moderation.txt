Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Moderation

We are rediscovering "moderation" and seeing afresh the importance of "quiet," of "smallness," and of "green." Sound at shock levels, dazzling strobe lights which titillate the senses, if not overwhelm them, are a poor preparation for those who want to see a sunset or watch the grass grow. (A Time to Choose, pp. 70-71.)
