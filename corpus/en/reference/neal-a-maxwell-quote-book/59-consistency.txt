Consistency

As with our wasteful automobile driving habits that consume extra energy because of quick starts and stops, so it may be that with inconsistent discipleship we actually inflict costs on ourselves in the face of divine counsel. (Men and Women of Christ, p. 25.)

Quiet, sustained goodness is the order of heaven, not conspicuous but episodic busyness. (Notwithstanding My Weakness, p. 5.)

The Prophet finally gave all that he had, but his contribution to the work of the Lord was not in a single project; it was spread over many high priority projects and also over a period of time. The paced performance of Joseph Smith brought results that were to touch millions! We, too, may be ready to give our all, but it is usually not required in one lump sum. (Notwithstanding My Weakness, p. 5.)

When our pace exceeds our strength and means, the result is prostration instead of sustained dedication. (Notwithstanding My Weakness, p. 6.)

A few little flowers will spring up briefly in the dry gulley through which torrents of water pass occasionally. But it is steady streams that bring thick and needed crops. In the agriculture of the soul that has to do with nurturing attributes, flash floods are no substitute for regular irrigation. (Notwithstanding My Weakness, p. 7.)
