Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Stereotyping

The world has been led to lazy stereotypes of prophets as being wild and fanatic people to be ignored. Such stereotyping is the work of him who has also encouraged art that portrays aneffeminate Jesus. Lucifer just cannot bring himself to present things as they really are, for the truth is not in him. (Things As They Really Are, pp. 78-79.)

Stereotyping reflects both intellectual laziness and lovelessness. The straight and narrow path leads us to a wide horizon of discovering and knowing people, but stereotyping confines us into the solitude of a conceptual cul-de-sac. (If Thou Endure It Well, p. 96.)
