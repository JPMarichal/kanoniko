One wonders, even in good people, if peevishness is not one of the last tendencies to be conquered. Littleness in big people is always disappointing. Perhaps such littleness is like litter on an otherwise lovely lawn; we must not be judgmental, of course, but we cannot help noticing. Even so, we'd best look first to our own lawns. (Notwithstanding My Weakness, p. 51.)
