Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Travel

Is it the terrain one traverses which is the true test of his life? If so, should those of us who travel in the jet age view with condescension the mileage logged by Jesus during his mortal ministry? In those days "from Dan to Beersheba" seemed so sweeping, yet it involved only a little over a hundred miles. One day our travels about this planet will seem quite provincial too when we are wafted from planet to planet. ("Grounded, Rooted, Established, and Settled," p. 18.)
