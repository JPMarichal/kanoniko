Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Justice

Moses, in that moment on Mount Pisgah overlooking the Holy Land, must have been sobered by the realization that even sterling past performance, when tarnish is present, cannot rob justice of its polishing part. (Even As I Am, p. 45.)

God's love balances justice and mercy in divine due process-beside which our mortal due process is, comparatively, either a kangaroo court or an orgy of unredeeming indulgence. (Even As I Am, p. 46.)

We must not automatically assume that time wounds all heels, for the bad are not always exposed-but eternity will bring a fulness of justice. (Wherefore, Ye Must Press Forward, p. 32.)

The gospel guarantees ultimate, not proximate, justice. (Wherefore, Ye Must Press Forward, p. 116.)
