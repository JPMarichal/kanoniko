Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Jealousy

For us to jettison our jealousies is to stop yet another form of waste, for we cannot go where He is if we worry rather than rejoice over who else will be there. Jousting for position among peers and friends is to waste what could better be used in the battle against the enemy of evil. (Wherefore, Ye Must Press Forward, p. 94.)
