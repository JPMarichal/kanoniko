Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Community

A person could get so caught up in making civic contributions to his community that he could lose his family. By the same token, one cannot readily save his family in an environment in decay. Thus we have obligations to contribute to the civic betterment of the communities in which we live. (Deposition of a Disciple, p. 68.)

A sister gives commendable, visible civic service. Yet even with her good image in the community, she remains a comparative stranger to Jesus' holy temples and His holy scriptures, two vital dimensions of discipleship. But she could have Christ's image in her countenance (see Alma 5:14). (Ensign, November 1995, p. 23.)
