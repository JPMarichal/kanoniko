Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Covenants

Already too many Church members have broken hearts and broken homes because of broken covenants and broken promises. Society's increasing slide towards pleasure-seeking brings our so-called civilization comparatively closer to Sodom than to Eden. (Ensign, November 1982, p. 68.)

To keep the commandments and to honor our covenants-whether one is a cashier at a grocery checkout counter, a neurosurgeon, an automotive mechanic, or a government official-is what matters, daily and eternally (see Luke 9:23). (A Wonderful Flood of Light, p. 103.)
