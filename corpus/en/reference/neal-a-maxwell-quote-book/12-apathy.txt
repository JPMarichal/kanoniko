Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Apathy

Ironically, if the Master is a stranger to us, then we will merely end up serving other masters. The sovereignty of these other masters is real, even if it sometimes is subtle, for they do call their cadence. Actually, "we are all enlisted" (Hymns, 1985, no. 250), if only in the ranks of the indifferent. (Ensign, November 1995, p. 22.)
