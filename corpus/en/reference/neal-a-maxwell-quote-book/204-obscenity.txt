Neal A. Maxwell, Neal A. Maxwell Quote Book ( 1997)

Obscenity

Grossness in conduct is usually preceded or accompanied by grossness in language. Instead of employing beauty and variety, obscenity exhibits poverty of expression. What jars is not only the mounting crudeness but also the increasing sameness-like the indistinguishable grunts of a herd in a hurry to worship afresh at the shrine of sensualism. (That Ye May Believe, p. 75.)
