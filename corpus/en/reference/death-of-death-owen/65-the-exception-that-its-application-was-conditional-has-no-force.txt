The exception that Christ died for them, and his death would have been available to them if they had believed and fulfilled the required condition, has no force at all, in my judgment.

### First, the condition was unknown by all.

For the most part, they never heard of any such condition.

### Secondly, Christ knew whoever failed the condition would be lost.

Christ at his death knew full well that they had not fulfilled the condition, and were actually cut off from any possibility of ever doing so. Any intent to do them good by his death would necessarily be in vain and frustrated. Such a fruitless sacrifice must not be assigned to the Son of God.

### Thirdly, conditional redemption is rejected.

We shall reject this assertion that redemption is applied if they will believe, shortly.
