This argument may easily be misunderstood. Abraham was not burning in the flames of hell until Christ died for him. Owen’s point here is that Christ did not die for the convinced damned, but only for the convinced elect. He was covenantally obliged to die for them as their surety. We often say that God “looked forward” to Christ’s sacrifice that redeemed Abraham (Lk. 16:23). But Owen shortly points out that God set free those in the past whom Christ promised to pay for in due time.
