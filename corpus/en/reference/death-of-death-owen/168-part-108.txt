See John 12:39,40 – The word “enabled” has been added to the text to clarify Owen’s thought. This is the logic: if Christ’s sheep believe, and those who are not his sheep do not believe, then it must be that those who are not his sheep cannot believe, or else they would be his sheep. It is not a matter of volition and choice. The condition of being his sheep is what enables the elect to believe; and by extension, those who are not his sheep do not believe because their condition will not permit it.
