The good shepherd gives his life for the sheep. I know my sheep and they know me… I lay down my life for the sheep… I have other sheep that are not of this fold. I must bring them also, and they will hear my voice… you do not believe because you are not from my fold. My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me… I give them eternal life. My Father… has given them to me.
