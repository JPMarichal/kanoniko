Synecdoche: A figure of speech in which a part or a component of something is used to refer to the whole (“all hands on deck” for sailors), the whole of something to refer to a part (such as the law meaning a police officer), the specific for the general (such as referring to an assassin as a cutthroat, which is only one way to assassinate), the general for the specific (such as thief for pickpocket), or the material for the thing which is made from it (referring to a sword as steel).
