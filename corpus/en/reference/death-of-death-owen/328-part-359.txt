Arminians assert there is a universal proclamation of God's purpose of mercy towards all – a universal vocation or calling. This is an invitation to receive pardon and salvation. It is accompanied, they say, by a universal sufficient grace — this is God’s gracious assistance that is actually and universally bestowed. It is sufficient to enable all men, if they choose, to obtain salvation. Calvinists admit that pardon and salvation are to be proclaimed to all, but this does not indicate God's intent to save all men, nor does it indicate that His grace and divine help, sufficient to produce faith and regeneration, are given to all men.
