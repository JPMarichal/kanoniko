Ishmael

See this page in the original 1992 publication.

Author: Baker, Christine Purves

Little is known of the Book of Mormon Ishmael. An Ephraimite from Jerusalem (cf. JD 23:184), he cooperated in fulfilling God's command (brought

navigation to him from the wilderness by Lehi's son) that he, his wife, five daughters, two sons, and their households travel into the wilderness to join the

Home exodus of the prophet Lehi from Jerusalem about 600 B.C. (1 Ne. 7:2-5 ).
