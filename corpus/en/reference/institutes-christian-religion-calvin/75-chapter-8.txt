John Calvin, Institutes of the Christian Religion ( 1536)

Chapter 8

Of the Power of the Church in Articles of Faith—The Unbridled Licence of the Papal Church in Destroying Purity of Doctrine

This chapter is divided into two parts,—I. The limits within which the Church ought to confine herself in matters of this kind, sec. 1-9. II. The Roman Church convicted of having transgressed these limits, sec. 10-16.
