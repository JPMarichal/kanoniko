John Calvin, Institutes of the Christian Religion ( 1536)

Chapter 16

Pedobaptism—Its Accordance with the Institution of Christ, and the Nature of the Sign

Divisions of this chapter,—I. Confirmation of the orthodox doctrine of Pedobaptism, sec. 1-9. II. Refutation of the arguments which the Anabaptists urge against Pedobaptism, sec. 10-30. III. Special objections of Servetus refuted, sec. 31, 32.

Sections

10 Second part of the chapter, stating the arguments of Anabaptists. Alleged dissimilitude between baptism and circumcision. First answer.
