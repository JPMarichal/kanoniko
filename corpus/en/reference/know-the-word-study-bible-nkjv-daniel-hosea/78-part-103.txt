To fast is to abstain for a period of time from some important and necessary activity in our lives. The purpose of fasting is to be able to spend that time in prayer before God. Different kinds of fasting are possible. One may for a time refrain from sleep (2 Cor. 6:5; 11:27), marital sex (1 Cor. 7:1–5), or food (Matt. 4:1–2).
