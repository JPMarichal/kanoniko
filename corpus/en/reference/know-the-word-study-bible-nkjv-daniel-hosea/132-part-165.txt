8:14 temples . . . multiplied fortified cities. True security comes from the Creator, but God’s people trusted instead in their own efforts, symbolized by their important buildings.

9:1–2 threshing floor. Because of their association with the harvest, threshing floors were the site of agricultural festivals in which Israel offered up sac- rifices to Baal. The Lord would take away the joy of theharvestbydestroyingthecropsandleavingthe threshing floors and wine vats empty.

9:3 the Lord’s land. Israel had forgotten that their land belonged to the Lord. He alone decided who would or would not live in it (Lev. 25:23).
