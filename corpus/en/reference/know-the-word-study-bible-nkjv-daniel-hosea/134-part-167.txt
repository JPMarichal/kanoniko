10 “I found Israel

Like grapes in the wilderness; I saw your fathers

As the firstfruits on the fig tree in its first season.

But they went to Baal Peor,

And separated themselves to that shame;

They became an abomination like the thing they loved.

11 As for Ephraim, their glory shall fly away like a bird—

No birth, no pregnancy, and no conception!

12 Though they bring up their children,

Yet I will bereave them to the last man.

Yes, woe to them when I depart from them!

13 Just as I saw Ephraim like Tyre, planted in a pleasant place,

So Ephraim will bring out his children to the murderer.”

14 Give them, O Lord— What will You give?

Give them a miscarrying womb

And dry breasts!

15 “All their wickedness is in Gilgal, For there I hated them.

Because of the evil of their deeds

I will drive them from My house; I will love them no more.

All their princes are rebellious.

16 Ephraim is stricken, Their root is dried up; They shall bear no fruit.

Yes, were they to bear children, I would kill the darlings of their

womb.”

17 My God will cast them away, Because they did not obey Him;

And they shall be wanderers among the nations.
