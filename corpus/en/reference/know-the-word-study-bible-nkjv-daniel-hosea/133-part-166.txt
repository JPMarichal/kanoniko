9:8 watchman. He would look for approaching armiesandthenwarnthepeoplesothattheycould secure the city and prepare for battle (Ezek. 33:6). The prophets were like watchmen because they were sent by God to warn the people of judgment and urge them to repent (Ezek. 3:17).

9:9 As in the days of Gibeah. The reference here is to the rape and murder of a young woman by men of Gibeah, an event that started a civil war (Judg. 19). Those who witnessed this violent deed remarked that it was the worst crime committed in Israel’s history until that time. However, the sins of Hosea’s generation rivaled the infamous Gibeah.
