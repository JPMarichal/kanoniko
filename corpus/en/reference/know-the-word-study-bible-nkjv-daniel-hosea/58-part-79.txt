MORE INSIGHT

Once a royal decree had been issued (such as “the law of the Medes and Persians” in Daniel 6:8), it could not be revoked, not even by the king himself. It remained in force until its time of expiration. The practice of creating an unchangeable law may follow from the idea that changing a decree was an admis- sion that it had been faulty.
