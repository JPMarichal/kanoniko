By using items dedicated to God to honor his own deities of “gold and silver, bronze and iron, wood and stone” (Dan. 5:4), Belshazzar crossed a line. He blasphemed God and showed total disregard for His holiness. As a result, the writing was on the wall, and Babylon’s days were numbered.

MORE INSIGHT

The words on the wall were written in some form of Hebrew. “MENE” means “numbered,” indicating that the days of the Babylonian dynast y were expiring. “ TEKEL” means “weighed” and relates to the fact that God had measured Belshazzar’s kingdom and found it lacking. “UPHARSIN” means “divided” and points to the division of the kingdom between the Medes and Persians. Meanwhile, Daniel’s reputation remained intact, and he went on to serve his new masters faithfully, starting with King Cyrus.
