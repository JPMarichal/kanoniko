MORE INSIGHT

What exactly was cultic prostitution? Men would bring their sacrifices to pagan shrines where they would prepare and offer them to idols with the cult prostitutes. Afterward, worshipers would engage in intercourse with prostitutes, believing this ritual act would motivate the god (or gods) to fertilize the land. God said He would hold accountable not just the prostitutes but also the men who interacted with them (Hos. 4:14).
