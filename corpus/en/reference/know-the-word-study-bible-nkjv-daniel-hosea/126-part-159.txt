“I thought I was right” doesn’t help when you’ve broken the law. Say you’re driving the wrong way on a one-way street. Your thinking you were going the right way won’t keep you from getting points on your license, having to pay a fine, or getting hit head-on. Thinking you are right doesn’t make it so.

The Israelites thought they were on good terms with God. They believed they were in good shape spiritually. They claimed to know God, but their actions contradicted their smugness.
