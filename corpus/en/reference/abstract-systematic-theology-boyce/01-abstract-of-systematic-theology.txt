Abstract of Systematic Theology

Author(s): Boyce, James Petigru (1827-1888)

Publisher: Grand Rapids, MI: Christian Classics Ethereal Library

Description: James P. Boyce, the first president of Southern Baptist

Theological Seminary, described his Abstract of Systematic Theology as follows: “This volume is published rather as a practical text book, for the study of the system of doctrine taught in the Word of God, than as a contribution to theolo-gical science.” Since its publication, Boyce’s Abstract has indeed served as a tool for education. Pastors and young people seeking to know more about the Reformed Baptist tradition here find a still-relevant resource.
