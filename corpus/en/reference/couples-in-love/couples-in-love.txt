Solomon and his bride show all of the affection and romance that people uni-

versally associate with being in love (Song 2:16). Theirs is one of a number of sto-

ries about romantic love told in the Bible.

Isaac and Rebekah A father seeks and finds a wife for his son, and the young couple love each (Gen. 24:1–67) other deeply.

Jacob and Rachel Jacob labors 14 years for his father-in-law in order to gain Rachel as his (Gen. 29:1–30) wife.

Boaz and Ruth Legal technicalities bring together a Moabite widow and a wealthy (Ruth 3–4)

landowner of Bethlehem, and through them a king is descended.

Elkanah and Hannah A woman is loved by her husband despite being childless, and God (1 Sam. 1–2)

eventually blesses her with the birth of a son, who becomes a mighty

judge over Israel.

David and Michal Genuine love is manipulated by a jealous king, but instead of ridding (1 Sam. 18:20–30) himself of his nemesis, the ruler gains a son-in-law.

Solomon and the Shulamite The commitments and delights of two lovers are told in a beautiful (Song of Solomon) romantic poem.

Hosea and Gomer God calls the prophet Hosea to seek out his adulterous spouse and restore (Hos. 1:1—3:5) the relationship despite what she has done.

Christ and the Church Having won His bride’s salvation from sin, Christ loves and serves her (Eph. 5:25–33) as His own body, thereby setting an example for human husbands

everywhere.
