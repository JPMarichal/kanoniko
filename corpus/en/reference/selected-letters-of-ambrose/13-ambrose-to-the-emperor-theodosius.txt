Ambrose, Selected Letters of Ambrose ( 390)

Letter 61

Ambrose, to the Emperor Theodosius

St. Ambrose explains his absence from Milan on the arrival of the Emperor Theodosius after his victory over Eugenius, and after expressing his thankfulness for that success he promises obedience to the Emperor's will, and while commending his piety urges him to be merciful to the conquered.
