Ambrose, Selected Letters of Ambrose ( 390)

Epistle 18

Ambrose, Bishop, to the Most Blessed Prince and Most Gracious Emperor Valentianus, the August

Reply of St. Ambrose to the Memorial of Symmachus, in which after complimenting Valentinian he deals with three points of the Memorial. He replies to his opponent's personification of Rome in a singularly tilling manner, and proves that the famine spoken of by Symmachus had nothing to do with the cessation of heathen rites.
