In those days Israel had no King; everyone did as he saw fit (Judges 21:25).

The problem is that once the judge dies the people return to their old ways of disobedience. And the judges themselves are not perfect. Even the good ones rebel and lead the people astray. The system of judges is just not working; Israel needs a King.
