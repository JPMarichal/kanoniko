All this took place to fulfil what the Lord had said through the prophet: ‘The virgin will be with child and will give birth to a son, and they will call him Immanuel’- which means, ‘God with us’ (Matthew 1:22-23).

When Jesus is born He is given many names, one of which is ‘Immanuel,’ which means ‘God with us.’ God has now come to live with His people, not in a building but as a man. There is now therefore no longer any need for a land, or a temple – God’s presence exists in Jesus. This has significance for where God will meet with His people and how He will rule them.
