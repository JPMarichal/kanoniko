So he got up, took the child and his mother during the night and left for Egypt, where he stayed until the death of Herod. And so was fulfilled what the Lord had said through the prophet: “Out of Egypt I called my son” (Matthew 2:14-15).

When Jesus is born, His father Joseph must take Him to Egypt to escape Herod’s slaughter of all the babies in Judea. However, when He comes out of Egypt Matthew says that the words of an Old Testament prophet Hosea are fulfilled (Hosea 11:1).

Interestingly, Hosea’s words are not pointing forward to Jesus; they are pointing back to Israel. Yet Matthew states that when Jesus comes out of Egypt as a little boy these words are fulfilled, which means that Israel was a picture of Jesus in some ways.
