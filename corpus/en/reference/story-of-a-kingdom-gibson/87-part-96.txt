‘He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain, for the old order of things has passed away.’ He who was seated on the throne said, ‘I am making everything new!’ Then he said, ‘Write this down for these words are trustworthy and true’ (Revelation 21:4-5).

The curses that Satan brought into the world will be permanently removed. The sea (which symbolises all opposition to God) will be no more. All things are made new…forever! God’s loving covenant – made with Adam, ruined by Adam, re-promised to Abraham and Israel, and established by Jesus - will be completed. God’s perfect Kingdom will finally have come, and the constitution will never again be broken, for it is secured by the death of the King, who now lives and reigns forever.
