‘And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Counsellor to be with you forever – the Spirit of truth. The world cannot accept him, because it neither sees him nor knows him. But you know him, for he lives in you and will be in you’ (John 14:16-17).

There is only one God, but in God there are three persons – Father, Son and Holy Spirit. This is known as the ‘Trinity’. The Holy Spirit is God Himself, and is equal to the Father and the Son. This is a very difficult concept to understand, but just because we cannot fully understand it, doesn’t mean it isn’t true! When Jesus was on earth He promised His followers that He would not leave them alone, but that He would send the Holy Spirit to live in them.
