Book of Remembrance

See this page in the original 1992 publication.

Author: Gardner, Cynthia M.

From antiquity God has commanded his people to keep records. In the days of Adam the people wrote a book of remembrance "by the spirit of

navigation inspiration" (Moses 6:5 ) to identify the faithful, to "know" their fathers (Moses 6:45-46 ), to define "the right of priesthood" (Abr. 1:31 ), and to
