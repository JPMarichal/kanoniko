EVANS, Richard L. (Louis).

Seventy, Oct. 7, 1938; Apostle, Oct. 8, 1953-Nov. 1, 1971; b. Mar. 23, 1906, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Nov. 1, 1971, Salt Lake City; m. Alice Ruth Thornley, four children; voice of the "Spoken Word" in weekly Mormon Tabernacle Broadcast, 1930-1971; editor, Improvement Era.
