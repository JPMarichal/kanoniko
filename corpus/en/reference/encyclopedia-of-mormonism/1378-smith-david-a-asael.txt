SMITH, David A. (Asael).

Second counselor to Presiding Bishop Charles W. Nibley, Dec. 4, 1907; First counselor, July 18, 1918; First counselor to Presiding Bishop Sylvester Q. Cannon, June 4, 1925; released April 6, 1938, when Bishop Sylvester Q. Cannon was ordained an apostle; b. May 24, 1879, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 6, 1952, Salt Lake City; m. Emily Jenkins, nine children.
