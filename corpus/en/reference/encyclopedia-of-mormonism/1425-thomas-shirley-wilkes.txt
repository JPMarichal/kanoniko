THOMAS, Shirley Wilkes.

Second counselor to general president Barbara B. Smith, Relief Society, Nov. 28, 1978-April 7, 1984; b. Feb. 26, 1925, Englewood, California; m. Robert K. Thomas, three children; Relief Society general board member, stake Relief Society president.
