RIGDON, Sidney.

First counselor to President Joseph Smith, Mar. 18, 1833; excommunicated, Sept. 8, 1844; b. Feb. 19, 1793, Saint Clair Township, Pennsylvania; m. Phebe Brook, eleven children; d. July 14, 1876, Friendship, New York; preacher. (See biography, Rigdon, Sidney.)
