CURTIS, LeGrand R. (Raine).

First counselor to general president Robert L. Backman, Young Men, Nov. 1972-June 23, 1974; Seventy, Mar. 31, 1990; b. May 22, 1924, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Patricia Glade, eight children; dentist; regional representative, mission and stake president.
