ANDERSON, Helen Woodruff.

Second counselor to general president Belle S. Spafford, Relief Society, Jan. 1957-Aug. 1958; b. Jan. 1, 1901, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. June 7, 1990, Salt Lake City; m. Alexander P. Anderson, five children; homemaker; Relief Society general board member, stake Relief Society president.
