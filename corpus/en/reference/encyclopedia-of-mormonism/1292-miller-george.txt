MILLER, George.

Sustained in Nauvoo as Second Bishop of the Church, Oct. 7, 1844; dropped prior to 1847; disfellowshipped, Oct. 20, 1848; b. Nov. 25, 1794, Orange County, Virginia; d. 1856, Meringo County, Illinois; m. Mary Catherine Fry; practiced plural marriage, four children on record; carpenter-lumberman, farmer.
