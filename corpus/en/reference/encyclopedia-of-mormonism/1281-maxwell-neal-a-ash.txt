MAXWELL, Neal A. (Ash).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1974; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; Apostle, July 23, 1981; b. July 6, 1926, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Colleen Hinckley, four children; educator, Commissioner of Church Education; regional representative, Young Men general board member.
