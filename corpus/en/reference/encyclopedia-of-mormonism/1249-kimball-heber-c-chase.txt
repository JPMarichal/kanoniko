KIMBALL, Heber C. (Chase).

Apostle, Feb. 14, 1835; First counselor to President Brigham Young, Dec. 27, 1847-June 22, 1868; b. June 14, 1801, Sheldon, Vermont; d. June 22, 1868, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Vilate Murray; practiced plural marriage, sixty-five children on record; potter, businessman. (See biography, Kimball, Heber C.)
