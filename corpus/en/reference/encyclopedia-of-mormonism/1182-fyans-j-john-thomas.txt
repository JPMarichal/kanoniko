FYANS, J. (John) Thomas.

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1974; Seventy 1976-1985; Emeritus General Authority, Oct. 1, 1989; b. May 17, 1918, Moreland Idaho; m. Helen Cook, five children; business executive; regional representative, mission president.
