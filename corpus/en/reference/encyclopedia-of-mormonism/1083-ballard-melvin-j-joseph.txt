BALLARD, Melvin J. (Joseph).

Apostle, Jan. 7, 1919-July 30, 1939; b. Feb. 9, 1873, Logan, Utah; d. July 30, 1939, Salt Lake City; m. Martha Annabelle Jones, eight children; businessman, civic leader; mission president.
