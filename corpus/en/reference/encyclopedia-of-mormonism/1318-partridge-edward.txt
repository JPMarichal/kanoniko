PARTRIDGE, Edward.

First Bishop of the Church, Feb. 4, 1831-May 27, 1840; b. Aug. 27, 1793, Pittsfield, Massachusetts; d. May 27, 1840, Nauvoo, Illinois; m. Lydia Clisbee, seven children; businessman.
