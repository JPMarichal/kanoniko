SMITH, Nicholas G. (Groesbeck).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1941-Oct. 27, 1945; b. June 20, 1881, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Oct. 27, 1945, Salt Lake City; m. Florence Gay, four children; business executive, mission president.
