HAIGHT, David B. (Bruce).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1970; Apostle, Jan. 8, 1976; b. Sept. 2, 1906, Oakley, Idaho; m. Ruby Olsen, three children; business executive; regional representative, mission and stake president.
