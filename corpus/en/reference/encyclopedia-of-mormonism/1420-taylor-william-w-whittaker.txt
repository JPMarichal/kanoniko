TAYLOR, William W. (Whittaker).

Seventy, April 7, 1880-Aug. 1, 1884; b. Sept. 11, 1853, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Aug. 1, 1884, Salt Lake City; m. Sarah Taylor Hoagland; practiced plural marriage, eight children on record.
