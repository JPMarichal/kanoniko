TALMAGE, James E. (Edward).

Apostle, Dec. 8, 1911-July 27, 1933; b. Sept. 21, 1862, Hungerford, England; d. July 27, 1933, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Mary May Booth, eight children; writer, former president of the University of Utah.
