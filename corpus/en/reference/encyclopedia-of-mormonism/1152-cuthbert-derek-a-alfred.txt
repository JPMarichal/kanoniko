CUTHBERT, Derek A. (Alfred).

Seventy, April 1, 1978-April 7, 1991; b. Oct. 5, 1926, Nottingham, England; d. April 7, 1991, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Muriel Olive Mason, ten children; business executive; regional representative, mission and stake president.
