PYPER, George D. (Dollinger).

Second asst. to general superintendent David O. McKay, Sunday School, Dec. 1918-Oct. 1934; general superintendent, Dec. 1934-Jan. 1943; b. Nov. 21, 1860, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Jan. 16, 1943, Salt Lake City; m. Emmaretta S. Whitney, two children; manager of Tabernacle choir.
