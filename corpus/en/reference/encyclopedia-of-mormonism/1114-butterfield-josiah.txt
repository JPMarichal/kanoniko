BUTTERFIELD, Josiah.

Seventy, April 6, 1837; excommunicated, Oct. 7, 1844; b. Mar. 13 or 18, 1795, Saco, Maine; d. April 1871, Watsonville, California; m. Polly Mouton, one child.
