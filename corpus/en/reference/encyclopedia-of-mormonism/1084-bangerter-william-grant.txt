BANGERTER, William Grant.

Asst. to the Twelve, April 4, 1975; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; b. June 8, 1918, Granger, Utah; m. Mildred Schwantes (d. 1952), three children; m. Geraldine Hamblin, seven children; building contractor; regional representative, mission president.
