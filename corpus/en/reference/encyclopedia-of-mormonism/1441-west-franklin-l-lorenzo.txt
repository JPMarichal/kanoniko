WEST, Franklin L. (Lorenzo).

Second asst. to general superintendent Albert E. Bowen, Young Men, 1935-1937; b. Feb. 1, 1885, Ogden, Utah; d. Oct. 21, 1966, Salt Lake City; m. Violet Madsen, six children; educator; Commissioner of Church Education.
