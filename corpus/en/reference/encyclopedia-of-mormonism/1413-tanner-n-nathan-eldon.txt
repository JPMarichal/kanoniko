TANNER, N. (Nathan) Eldon.

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 8, 1960; Apostle, Oct. 11, 1962; Second counselor to President David O. McKay, Oct. 4, 1963; Second counselor to President Joseph Fielding Smith, Jan. 23, 1970; First counselor to President Harold B. Lee, July 7, 1972; First counselor to President Spencer W. Kimball, Dec. 30, 1973-Nov. 27, 1982; b. May 9, 1898, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Nov. 27, 1982, Salt Lake City; m. Sara Isabelle Merrill, five children; businessman and educator.
