BADGER, Rodney C. (Carlos).

Second counselor to general president Junius F. Wells, Young Men, 1876-1880; b. Sept. 8, 1848, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 12, 1923, Salt Lake City; m. Harriet Ann Whitaker Taylor; practiced plural marriage, seventeen children on record; businessman.
