CHILD, Julia Alleman.

Second counselor to general president Louise Yates Robison, Relief Society, Oct. 7, 1928-Jan. 23, 1935; b. Sept. 8, 1873, Springville, Utah; d. Jan. 23, 1935, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. George N. Child, three children; educator; Relief Society general board member.
