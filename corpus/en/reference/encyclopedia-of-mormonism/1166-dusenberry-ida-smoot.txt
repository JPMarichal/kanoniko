DUSENBERRY, Ida Smoot.

Second counselor to general president Bathsheba W. Smith, Relief Society, Nov. 10, 1901-Sept. 20, 1910; b. May 5, 1873, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 25, 1955; m. George Albert Dusenberry, two children; educator.
