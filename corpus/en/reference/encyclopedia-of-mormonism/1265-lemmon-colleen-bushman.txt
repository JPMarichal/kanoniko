LEMMON, Colleen Bushman.

Second counselor to general president Naomi M. Shumway, Primary, Oct. 5, 1974-April 2, 1977; First counselor, April 2, 1977-April 5, 1980; b. July 14, 1927, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. George Van Lemmon, four children; president New Mexico State American Mothers Committee; Primary general board member.
