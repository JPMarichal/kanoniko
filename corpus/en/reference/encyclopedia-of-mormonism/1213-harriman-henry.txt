HARRIMAN, Henry.

Seventy, Feb. 6, 1838-May 17, 1891; b. June 9, 1804, Rowley (Georgetown), Massachusetts; d. May 17, 1891, Huntington, Utah; m. Clarissa Boynton; practiced plural marriage, nine children on record.
