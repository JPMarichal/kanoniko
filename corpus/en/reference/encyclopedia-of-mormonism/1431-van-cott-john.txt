VAN COTT, John.

Seventy, Oct. 8. 1862-Feb. 18, 1883; b. Sept. 7, 1814, Canaan, New York; d. Feb. 18, 1883, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Lucy Sackett; practiced plural marriage, twenty-eight children on record; businessman, legislator, farmer.
