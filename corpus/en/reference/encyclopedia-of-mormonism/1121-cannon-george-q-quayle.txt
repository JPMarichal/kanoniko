CANNON, George Q. (Quayle).

Apostle, Aug. 26, 1860; Counselor to President Young, April 8, 1873; Asst. counselor to President Young, May 9, 1874; released at death of President Young, Aug. 29, 1877; First counselor to President John Taylor, Oct. 10, 1880; released at the death of President Taylor, July 25, 1887; First counselor to President Woodruff, April 7, 1889; First counselor to President Snow, Sept. 13, 1898-April 12, 1901; b. Jan. 11, 1827, Liverpool, England; d. April 12, 1901, Monterey, California; m. Elizabeth Hoagland; practiced plural marriage, thirty-four children on record; businessman, editor.
