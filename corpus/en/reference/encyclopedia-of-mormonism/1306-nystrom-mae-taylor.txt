NYSTROM, Mae Taylor.

Second counselor to general president Martha Horne Tingey, Young Women, April 5, 1905-July 15, 1923; b. Aug. 11, 1891, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Theodore Nystrom, two children; Young Women general board member.
