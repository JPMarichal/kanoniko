LAMBERT, Edith Elizabeth Hunter.

Second counselor to general president May Anderson, Primary, Dec. 11, 1933-Dec. 31, 1939; b. Mar. 14, 1878, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Mar. 3, 1964, Salt Lake City; m. James N. Lambert, three children.
