BENNION, Milton.

First asst. to general superintendent George D. Pyper, Sunday School, Oct. 1934-May 1943; general superintendent, May 1943-Sept. 1949; b. June 7, 1870, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 5, 1953, Salt Lake City; m. Cora Lindsay, eleven children; educator; Sunday School general board member.
