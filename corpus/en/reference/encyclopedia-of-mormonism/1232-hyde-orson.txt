HYDE, Orson.

Apostle, Feb. 15, 1835; dropped from the Quorum, May 4, 1839; restored to the Quorum, June 27, 1839; President of the Quorum of Twelve Apostles, Dec. 27, 1847; seniority adjusted to date of second entry into the Quorum, April 10, 1875; b. Jan. 8, 1805, Oxford, Connecticut; d. Nov. 28, 1878, Spring City, Utah; m. Marinda Nancy Johnson; practiced plural marriage, thirty-two children on record; editor.
