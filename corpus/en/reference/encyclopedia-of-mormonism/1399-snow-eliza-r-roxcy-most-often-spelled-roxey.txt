SNOW, Eliza R. (Roxcy, most often spelled Roxey).

General president, Relief Society, 1866-Dec. 5, 1887; b. Jan. 21, 1804, Becket, Massachusetts; d. Dec. 5, 1887, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Joseph Smith, Jr. (d. 1844); m. Brigham Young; writer, poet. (See biography, Snow, Eliza R.)
