WELLS, Robert E. (Earl).

Seventy, Oct. 1, 1978; b. Dec. 28, 1927, Las Vegas, Nevada; m. Meryl Leavitt (d. 1960); m. Helen Walser, seven children; banking executive; regional representative, mission and stake president; father of Sharlene Wells Hawkes, Miss America, 1985.
