WHITNEY, Orson F. (Ferguson).

Apostle April 9, 1906-May 16, 1931; b. July 1, 1855, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. May 16, 1931, Salt Lake City; m. Zina Beal Smoot; practiced plural marriage, twelve children on record.
