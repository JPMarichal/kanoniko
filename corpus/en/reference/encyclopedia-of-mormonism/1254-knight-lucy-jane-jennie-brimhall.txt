KNIGHT, Lucy Jane (Jennie) Brimhall.

First counselor to general president Clarissa Smith Williams, Relief Society, April 2, 1921-Oct. 7, 1928; b. Dec. 13, 1875, Spanish Fork, Utah; d. Mar. 31, 1957, Provo, Utah; m. Jesse William Knight, two children; executive for Utah County Red Cross.
