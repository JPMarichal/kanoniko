HART, Charles H. (Henry).

Seventy, April 9, 1906-Sept. 29, 1934; b. July 5, 1866, Bloomington, Idaho; d. Sept. 29, 1934, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Adelia Greenhalgh (d. 1913), ten children; m. LaLene Hendricks.
