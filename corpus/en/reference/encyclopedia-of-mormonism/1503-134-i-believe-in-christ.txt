Daniel H. Ludlow, Encyclopedia of Mormonism 4 vols. (New York: Macmillan Publishing Company, 1992)

#134 I Believe In Christ

Among the forty-four new Latter-day Saint contributions added to the 1985 hymnal is this hymn text closely paraphrasing the Book of Mormon text of 2 Nephi 31:20. The words have broad Christian relevance, and the tune was written specifically for this text. Both the text and tune were by contemporary Latter-day Saints.
