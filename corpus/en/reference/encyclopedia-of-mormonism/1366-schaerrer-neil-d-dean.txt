SCHAERRER, Neil D. (Dean).

General president, Young Men, April 7, 1977-Oct. 1979; b. Apr. 12, 1930, Payson, Utah; d. Jan. 18, 1985, Salt Lake City; m. June Coon, four children; attorney; mission president.
