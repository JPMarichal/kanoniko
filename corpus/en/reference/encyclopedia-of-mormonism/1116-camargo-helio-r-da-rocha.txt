CAMARGO, Helio R. (da Rocha).

Seventy, April 6, 1985-Oct. 6, 1990; b. Feb. 1, 1926, Resende, Brazil; m. Nair Belmira de Gouvea, six children; farmer and ret. Brazilian Army officer; regional representative, stake president.
