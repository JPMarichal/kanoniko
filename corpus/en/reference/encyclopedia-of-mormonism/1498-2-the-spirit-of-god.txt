#2 The Spirit of God

Linked to important events in Church history such as the dedication of the Kirtland Temple in 1836, this hymn has been part of Latter-day Saint hymn tradition since its inclusion in Emma Smith's hymnal in 1835. Today it is sung at all temple dedications, as well as in Sunday meetings and on other joyous occasions. The origin of the hymn tune is uncertain. Its first known publication was in a small hymnal printed in 1844 for the use of Latter-day Saint missionaries in New England.
