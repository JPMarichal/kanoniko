FARNSWORTH, Burton K. (Kent).

Asst. to general superintendent George Q. Morris, Young Men, 1937-1945; b. Mar. 6, 1890, Beaver, Utah; d. Oct. 27, 1945, Seattle, Washington; m. Mabel Pearce, six children; educator; Young Men general board member.
