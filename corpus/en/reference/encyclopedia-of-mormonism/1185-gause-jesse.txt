GAUSE, Jesse.

Counselor to President Joseph Smith, Mar. 8, 1832; sent on mission from which he never returned; excommunicated, Dec. 3, 1832; b. about 1784, East Marlborough, Virginia; d. about 1836; m. Martha Cuntry, five children.
