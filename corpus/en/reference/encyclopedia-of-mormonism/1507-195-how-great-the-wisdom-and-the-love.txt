#195 How Great the Wisdom And the Love

The text of this Sacrament hymn by Eliza R. Snow was first published in 1871. The tune, composed as a setting for this text and first published in 1884, is by Scottish-born Latter-day Saint composer Thomas McIntyre, who emigrated to Utah in 1859.
