WINDER, John R. (Rex).

Second counselor to Presiding Bishop William B. Preston, April 8, 1887; First counselor to President Joseph F. Smith, Oct. 17, 1901-Mar. 27, 1910; b. Dec. 11, 1821, Biddenham, England; d. Mar. 27, 1910, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Ellen Walters Winder (d. 1892), ten children; m. Maria Burnham; business executive.
