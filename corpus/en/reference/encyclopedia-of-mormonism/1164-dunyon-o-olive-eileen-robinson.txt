DUNYON, O. (Olive) Eileen Robinson.

Second counselor to general president LaVern Watts Parmley, Primary, April 6, 1962-June 3, 1963; b. June 3, 1917, Preston, Idaho; m. Joy F. Dunyon, three children; school librarian.
