YOUNG, Joseph Angell.

Seventy, Feb. 28, 1835-July 16, 1881; b. April 7, 1797, Hopkinton, Massachusetts; d. July 16, 1881, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Jane A. Bicknell; practiced plural marriage, twenty children on record.
