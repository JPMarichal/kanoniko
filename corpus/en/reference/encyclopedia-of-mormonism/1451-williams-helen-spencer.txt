WILLIAMS, Helen Spencer.

First counselor to general president Lucy Grant Cannon, Young Women, Nov. 1937-May 17, 1944; b. Nov. 29, 1896, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Aug. 10, 1965, Salt Lake City; m. Rex W. Williams, three children.
