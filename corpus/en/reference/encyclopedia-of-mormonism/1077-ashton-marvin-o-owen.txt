ASHTON, Marvin O. (Owen).

First counselor to Presiding Bishop LeGrand Richards, April 6, 1938-Oct. 7, 1946; b. April 8, 1883, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Oct. 7, 1946, Salt Lake City; m. Rae Jeremy, seven children; businessman; stake president.
