CRITCHLOW, William J. (James), Jr.

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 16, 1958-Aug. 29, 1968; b. Aug. 21, 1892, Brigham City, Utah; d. Aug. 29, 1968, Ogden; m. Anna Marie Taylor, three children; business executive; stake president.
