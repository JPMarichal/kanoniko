PRESTON, William B. (Bowker).

Presiding Bishop, April 6, 1884; released due to ill health, Dec. 4, 1907; b. Nov. 24, 1830, Halifax, Virginia; d. Aug. 2, 1908, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Harriet A. Thatcher; stake president.
