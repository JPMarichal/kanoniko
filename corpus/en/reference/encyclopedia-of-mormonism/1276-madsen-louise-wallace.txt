MADSEN, Louise Wallace.

Second counselor to general president Belle S. Spafford, Relief Society, Aug. 1958-Oct. 3, 1974; b. April 25, 1909, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Oct. 4, 1987, Salt Lake City; m. Francis A. Madsen, five children.
