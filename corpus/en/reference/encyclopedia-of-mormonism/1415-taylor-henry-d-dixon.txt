TAYLOR, Henry D. (Dixon).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1958; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; Emeritus General Authority, Sept. 30, 1978; b. Nov. 22, 1903, Provo, Utah; d. Feb. 24, 1987, Salt Lake City; m. Alta Hansen (d. 1967), four children; m. Ethelyn Peterson; business executive; mission and stake president.
