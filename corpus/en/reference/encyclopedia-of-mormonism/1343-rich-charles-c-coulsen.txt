RICH, Charles C. (Coulsen).

Apostle, Feb. 12, 1849-Nov. 17, 1883; b. Aug. 21, 1809, Campbell Co., Kentucky; d. Nov. 17, 1883, Paris, Idaho; m. Sarah DeArmen Pea; practiced plural marriage, fifty-one children on record; legislator.
