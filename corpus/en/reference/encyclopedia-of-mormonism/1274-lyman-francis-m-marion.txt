LYMAN, Francis M. (Marion).

Apostle, Oct. 27, 1880; President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, Oct. 6, 1903-Nov. 18, 1916; b. Jan. 12, 1840, Good Hope, Illinois; d. Nov. 18, 1916, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Rhoda Ann Taylor; practiced plural marriage; twenty-two children on record; businessman; mission president.
