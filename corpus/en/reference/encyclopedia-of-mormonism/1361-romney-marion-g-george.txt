ROMNEY, Marion G. (George).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1941; Apostle, Oct. 11, 1951; Second counselor to President Harold B. Lee, July 7, 1972; Second counselor to President Spencer W. Kimball, Dec. 30, 1973; First counselor to President Kimball, Dec. 2, 1982; President of the Quorum of the Twelve, Nov. 10 1985-May 20, 1988; b. Sept. 19, 1897, Colonia Ju rez, Mexico; d. May 20, 1988, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Ida Jensen, two children; attorney; stake president, managing director of the Church Welfare Program.
