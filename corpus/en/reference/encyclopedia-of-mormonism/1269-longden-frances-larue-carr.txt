LONGDEN, Frances LaRue Carr.

Second counselor to Bertha S. Reeder, Young Women, June 13, 1948-Sept. 30, 1961; b. April 2, 1901; d. May 16, 1991, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. John Longden, three children; stake Young Women president.
