GRANT, Heber J. (Jeddy).

Apostle, Oct. 16, 1882; President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, Nov. 23, 1916; President of the Church, Nov. 23, 1918-May 14, 1945; b. Nov. 22, 1856, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. May 14, 1945, Salt Lake City; m. Lucy Stringham; practiced plural marriage, twelve children; businessman. (See biography, Grant, Heber J.)
