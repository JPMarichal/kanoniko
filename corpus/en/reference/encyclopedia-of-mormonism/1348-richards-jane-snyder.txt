RICHARDS, Jane Snyder.

First counselor to general president Zina Diantha Young, Relief Society, Oct. 11, 1888-Nov. 10, 1901; b. Jan. 31, 1823, Pamelia, New York; d. Nov. 17, 1912, Ogden, Utah; m. Franklin D. Richards, six children.
