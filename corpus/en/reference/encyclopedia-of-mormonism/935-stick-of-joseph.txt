Stick of Joseph

[For Latter-day Saints, the "Stick of Joseph" and the "Stick of Ephraim" refer to the Book of Mormon. Both phrases appear in the book of the prophet Ezekiel (37:16, 19). The view that the Stick of Joseph consists of a scriptural record receives support from the Book of Mormon and the Doctrine and Covenants (1 Ne. 13:35-40; 2 Ne. 3:11-12, 18-21; D&C 27:5). Articles that deal with this subject are Book of Mormon, Biblical Prophecies about; Ezekiel, Prophecies of; Joseph of Egypt: Writings of Joseph; and "Voice from the Dust."]
