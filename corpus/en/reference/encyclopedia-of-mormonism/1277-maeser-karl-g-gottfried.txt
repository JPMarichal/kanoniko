MAESER, Karl G. (Gottfried).

Second asst. to general superintendent George Q. Cannon, Sunday School, July 1894-Jan. 1899; First asst., Jan. 1899-Feb. 1901; b. Jan. 16, 1828, Meiszen, Saxony, Germany; d. Feb. 15, 1901, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Anna Meith; practiced plural marriage, nine children on record; educator, mission president.
