HIGBEE, Elias.

Church historian, April 6, 1838-June 8, 1843; b. Oct. 23, 1795, Galloway, New Jersey; d. June 8, 1843, Nauvoo, Illinois; m. Sarah Elizabeth Ward, eight children; judge.
