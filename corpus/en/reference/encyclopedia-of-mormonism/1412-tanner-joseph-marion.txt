TANNER, Joseph Marion.

Second asst. to general superintendent Lorenzo Snow, Sunday School, May 1901-Oct. 1901; Second asst. to general superintendent Joseph F. Smith, Sunday School, Nov. 1901-April 1906; b. Mar. 26, 1859, Payson, Utah; d. Aug. 19, 1927, Lethbridge, Alberta, Canada; m. Josephine Snow; practiced plural marriage, seventeen children on record; former president of Utah Agricultural College (now Utah State University).
