SNOW, Erastus.

Apostle, Feb. 12, 1849-May 27, 1888; b. Nov. 9, 1818, Saint Johnsbury, Vermont; d. May 27, 1888, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Artimesia Beaman; practiced plural marriage, thirty-six children on record; mission president.
