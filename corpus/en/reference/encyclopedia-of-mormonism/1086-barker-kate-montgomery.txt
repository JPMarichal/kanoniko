BARKER, Kate Montgomery.

Second counselor to general president Louise Yates Robinson, Relief Society, April 3, 1935-Dec. 1939; b. May 30, 1881, North Ogden, Utah; d. Feb. 13, 1972, Salt Lake City; m. James L. Barker, three children.
