FJELSTED, Christian D. (Daniel).

Seventy, April 6, 1884-Dec. 23, 1905; b. Feb. 20, 1829, Sundbyvester, (near) Copenhagen, Denmark; d. Dec. 23, 1905, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Karen Olsen; practiced plural marriage, fifteen children on record; mission president.
