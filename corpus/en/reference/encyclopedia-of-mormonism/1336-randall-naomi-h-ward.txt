RANDALL, Naomi H. Ward.

First counselor to general president LaVern Watts Parmley, Primary, Oct. 4, 1970- Oct. 5, 1974; b. Oct. 5, 1908, Pleasantville, Utah; m. Earl A. Randall, one child; author; Primary general board member (author of "I Am A Child of God," Hymns, no. 301).
