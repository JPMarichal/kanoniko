IVINS, Anthony W. (Woodward).

Apostle, Oct. 6, 1907; Second counselor to President Heber J. Grant, Mar. 10, 1921; First counselor to President Grant, May 28, 1925-Sept. 23, 1934; b. Sept. 16, 1852, Toms River, New Jersey; d. Sept. 23, 1934, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Elizabeth Ashby Snow, nine children; rancher, businessman; Young Men general board member.
