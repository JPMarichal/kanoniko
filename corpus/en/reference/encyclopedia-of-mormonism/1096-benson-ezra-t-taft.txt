BENSON, Ezra T. (Taft).

Apostle, July 16, 1846-Sept. 3, 1869; b. Feb. 22, 1811, Mendon, Massachusetts; d. Sept. 3, 1869, Ogden, Utah; m. Pamelia Andrus; practiced plural marriage, thirty-four children on record; businessman-contractor; mission president.
