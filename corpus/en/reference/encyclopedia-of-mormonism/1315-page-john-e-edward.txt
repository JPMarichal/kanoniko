PAGE, John E. (Edward).

Apostle, Dec. 19, 1838; disfellowshipped, Feb. 9, 1846; excommunicated, June 27, 1846; b. Feb. 25 or 26, 1799, Trenton Township, New York; d. Oct. 14, 1867, near Sycamore, De Kalb Co., Illinois; m. Lorain Stevens; practiced plural marriage, three children on record.
