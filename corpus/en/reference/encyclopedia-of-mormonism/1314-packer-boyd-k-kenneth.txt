PACKER, Boyd K. (Kenneth).

Asst. to the Twelve, Sept. 30, 1961; Apostle, April 9, 1970; b. Sept. 10, 1924, Brigham City, Utah; m. Donna Smith, ten children; educator, assistant administrator of Seminaries and Institutes of Religion; mission president.
