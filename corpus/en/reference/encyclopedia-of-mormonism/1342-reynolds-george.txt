REYNOLDS, George.

Seventy, April 5, 1890-Aug. 9, 1909; b. Jan. 1, 1842, London, England; d. Aug. 9, 1909, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Mary Ann Tuddenham; practiced plural marriage, thirty-two children on record.
