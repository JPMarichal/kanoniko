YOUNG, Joseph A. (Angell).

Apostle, Feb. 4, 1864-Aug. 5, 1875; b. Oct. 14, 1834, Kirtland, Ohio; d. Aug. 5, 1875, Manti, Utah; m. Clara Federata Stenhouse; practiced plural marriage, eleven children on record.
