ELDREDGE, Horace S. (Sunderlin).

Seventy, Oct. 7, 1854-Sept. 6, 1888; b. Feb. 6, 1816, Brutus, New York; d. Sept. 6, 1888, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Sarah Gibbs; practiced plural marriage, twenty-eight children on record; businessman, superintendent of ZCMI; mission president.
