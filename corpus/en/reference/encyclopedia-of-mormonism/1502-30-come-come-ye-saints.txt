Daniel H. Ludlow, Encyclopedia of Mormonism 4 vols. (New York: Macmillan Publishing Company, 1992)

#30 Come, Come, Ye Saints

Written in 1846 while William Clayton was traveling westward with the first company of Mormons forced out of Nauvoo, this hymn text reflects the strength and devotion of the Mormon pioneers. Along with its accompanying English folk tune, this historically significant text has become today the hymn most readily identified with The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It has appeared in every Latter-day Saint hymnal since 1851.
