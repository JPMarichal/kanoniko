SMITH, Eldred G. (Gee).

Patriarch to the Church, April 10, 1947; Emeritus General Authority, Oct. 4, 1980; b. Jan. 9, 1907, Lehi, Utah. m. Jeanne Ness (d. 1977), five children; m. Hortense Child.
