CLAWSON, Rudger.

Apostle, Oct. 10, 1898-June 21, 1943; Second counselor to President Lorenzo Snow, Oct. 6, 1901; President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, Mar. 17, 1921; b. Mar. 12, 1857, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. June 21, 1943, Salt Lake City; m. Florence Dinwoodey; practiced plural marriage, eleven children on record; businessman; mission and stake president.
