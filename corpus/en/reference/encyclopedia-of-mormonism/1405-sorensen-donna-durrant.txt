SORENSEN, Donna Durrant.

Second counselor to general president Amy Brown Lyman, Relief Society, April 1940-Oct. 12, 1942; b. Dec. 24, 1904, Spanish Fork, Utah; d. June 4, 1990, Salt Lake City; m. Wesley A. Sorensen, three children.

SORENSEN, Lynn A. (Andrew). Seventy, April 4, 1987; b. Sept. 25, 1919, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Janet Elaine Weech, nine children; management; mission president.
