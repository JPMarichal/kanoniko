ROBERTS, B. (Brigham) H. (Henry).

Seventy, Oct. 7, 1888-Sept. 27, 1933. b. Mar. 13, 1857, Warrington, England; d. Sept. 27, 1933, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Sarah Louise Smith; practiced plural marriage, eight children on record; mission president.
