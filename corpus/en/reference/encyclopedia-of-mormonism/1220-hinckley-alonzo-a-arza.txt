Daniel H. Ludlow, Encyclopedia of Mormonism 4 vols. (New York: Macmillan Publishing Company, 1992)

HINCKLEY, Alonzo A. (Arza).

Apostle, Oct. 11, 1934-Dec. 22, 1936; b. April 23, 1870, Cove Fort, Utah; d. Dec. 22, 1936, Salt Lake City; m. Rose May Robison, 14 children; stake president; (christened Arza Alonzo Hinckley, but signed his name Alonzo A. Hinckley).
