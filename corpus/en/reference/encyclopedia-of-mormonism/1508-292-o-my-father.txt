#292 O My Father

First published in 1845, Eliza R. Snow's hymn text sets forth the distinctive Latter-day Saint doctrines of a pre-earth life and of a Heavenly Mother. Many Latter-day Saint composers have written musical settings for these words, but the favorite tune today is by a non-Latter-day Saint composer, James McGranahan, who originally wrote this melody to accompany a text by Philip Paul Bliss.
