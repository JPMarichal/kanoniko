SMITH, Bathsheba Wilson.

Second counselor to general president Zina Diantha Young, Relief Society, Oct. 11, 1888-Nov. 10, 1901; general president, Nov. 10, 1901-Sept. 20. 1910; b. May 3, 1822, Shinnston, West Virginia; d. Sept. 20, 1910, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. George A. Smith, three children. (See biography, Smith, Bathsheba W.)
