FAUST, James E. (Esdras).

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 6, 1972; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; Apostle, Oct. 1, 1978; b. July 31, 1920, Delta, Utah; m. Ruth Wright, five children; attorney; regional representative, mission and stake president.
