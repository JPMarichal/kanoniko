LEWIS, Theodore B. (Belden).

Seventy, Oct. 8, 1882; [on Oct. 9, when he was to be set apart, he reported that he was already a high priest, so he was not set apart and did not serve in this position]; b. Nov. 18, 1843, St. Louis, Missouri; d. July 20, 1899; m. Martha J. Coray; practiced plural marriage, sixteen children on record.
