YOUNG, Zina Diantha Huntington.

First counselor to general president Eliza R. Snow, Relief Society, June 19, 1880-April 1888; general president, Relief Society, April 8, 1888-Aug. 28, 1901; b. Jan. 31, 1821, Watertown, New York; d. Aug. 28, 1901, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Henry B. Jacobs (div. 1841), m. Joseph Smith, Jr. (d. 1844); m. Brigham Young, four children.
