SMITH, Hyrum Mack.

Apostle, Oct. 24, 1901-Jan. 23, 1918; b. Mar. 21, 1872, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Jan. 23, 1918, Salt Lake City; m. Ida Bowman, five children; business executive; mission president.
