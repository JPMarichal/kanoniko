SHARP, Marianne Clark.

First counselor to general president Belle S. Spafford, Relief Society, April 6, 1945-Oct. 3, 1974; b. Oct. 28, 1901, Grantsville, Utah; d. Jan. 2, 1990, Salt Lake City; m. Ivor Sharp, three children; editor of Relief Society Magazine.
