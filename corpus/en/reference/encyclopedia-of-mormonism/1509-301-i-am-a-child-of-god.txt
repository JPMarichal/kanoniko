#301 I Am A Child of God

Children along with adults attend Sacrament meeting, the principal Latter-day Saint worship service, and the 1985 hymnal includes ten children's songs. This song, written in 1957, was well known among Primary children prior to its appearance in the hymnal, and it is a favorite among many adults as well.
