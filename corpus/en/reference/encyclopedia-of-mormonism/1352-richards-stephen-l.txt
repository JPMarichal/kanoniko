RICHARDS, Stephen L.

Apostle, Jan. 18, 1917; First counselor to President David O. McKay, April 9, 1951-May 19, 1959; b. June 18, 1879, Mendon, Utah; d. May 19, 1959, Salt Lake City; m. Irene Merrill, nine children; attorney; Sunday School general board member.
