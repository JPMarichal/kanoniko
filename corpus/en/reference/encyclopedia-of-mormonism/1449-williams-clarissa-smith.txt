WILLIAMS, Clarissa Smith.

First counselor to general president Emmeline B. Wells, Relief Society, Oct. 3, 1910-April 2, 1921; general president, Relief Society, April 2, 1921-Oct. 7, 1928; b. April 21, 1859, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Mar. 8, 1930; m. William N. Williams, eleven children.
