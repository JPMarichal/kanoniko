CLARKE, J. (John) Richard.

Second counselor to Presiding Bishop Victor L. Brown, Oct. 1, 1976; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1988; b. April 4, 1927, Rexburg, Idaho; m. Barbara Jean Reed, eight children; businessman; regional representative, mission and stake president.
