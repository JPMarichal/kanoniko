RICHARDS, Willard.

Apostle, April 14, 1840; Second counselor to President Brigham Young, Dec. 27, 1847-Mar. 11, 1954; b. June 24, 1804, Hopkinton, Massachusetts; d. Mar. 11, 1854, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Jennetta Richards; practiced plural marriage, twenty children on record; physician, editor, historian.
