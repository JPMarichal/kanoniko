MORLEY, Isaac.

First counselor to Presiding Bishop Edward Partridge, June 6, 1831; released at death of Bishop Partridge; b. Mar. 11, 1786, Montague, Massachusetts; d. June 24, 1865, Fairview, Utah; m. Lucy Gunn Blakeslee; practiced plural marriage, ten children; civic leader.
