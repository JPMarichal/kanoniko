WELLS, Emmeline B. (Blanche) Woodward.

General president, Relief Society, Oct. 3, 1910-April 2, 1921; b. Feb. 29, 1828, Petersham, Massachusetts; d. April 25, 1921, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. James Harvey Harris (d. 1845), two children; m. Newel K. Whitney (d. 1850), two children, m. Daniel H. Wells, three children; writer and editor. (See biography, Wells, Emmeline B.)
