MERRILL, Marriner W. (Wood).

Apostle, Oct. 1889-Feb. 6, 1906; b. Sept. 25, 1835, Sackville, New Brunswick; d. Feb. 6, 1906, Richmond, Utah; m. Sarah A. Atkinson; practiced plural marriage, forty-five children.
