WHITNEY, Elizabeth Ann Smith.

Second counselor to general president Emma Hale Smith, Relief Society, May 17, 1842-Mar. 16, 1844; Second counselor to general president Eliza R. Snow, Relief Society, June 19, 1880-Feb. 15, 1882; b. Dec. 26, 1800, Derby, Connecticut; d. Feb. 15, 1883, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Newel K. Whitney, eleven children.
