CORRILL, John.

Second counselor to Presiding Bishop Edward Partridge, June 3, 1831; released Aug. 1, 1837; excommunicated Mar. 17, 1839; b. Sept. 17, 1794, Worcester Co., Massachusetts; m. Margaret, five children.
