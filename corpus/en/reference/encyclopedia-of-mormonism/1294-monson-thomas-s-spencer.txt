MONSON, Thomas S. (Spencer).

Apostle, Oct. 4, 1963; Second counselor to President Ezra Taft Benson, Nov. 10, 1985; b. Aug. 21, 1927, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Frances Beverly Johnson, three children; business executive; mission president.
