CANNON, Abraham H. (Hoagland).

Seventy, Oct. 8, 1882; Apostle, Oct. 7, 1889-July 19, 1896; b. Mar. 12, 1859, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. July 19, 1896, Salt Lake City; m. Sarah Ann Jenkins; practiced plural marriage, seventeen children on record.
