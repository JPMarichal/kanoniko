JACOBSEN, Florence Smith.

General president, Young Women, Sept. 30, 1961-Nov. 9, 1972; b. April 7, 1913, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Theodore C. Jacobsen, three children; homemaker, businesswoman; Church curator of museums.
