LONGDEN, John.

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 6, 1951-Aug. 30, 1969; b. Nov. 4, 1898, Oldham, England; d. Aug. 30, 1969, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Frances LaRue Carr, three children; businessman; stake president.
