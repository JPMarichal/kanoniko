BOWEN, Albert E. (Ernest).

Apostle, April 8, 1937-July 15, 1953; b. Oct. 31, 1875, Henderson Creek, Idaho; d. July 15, 1953, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Aletha E. Reeder (d. 1906), two children; m. Emma Lucy Gates; attorney; Young Men general board member.
