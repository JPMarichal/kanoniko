SCOTT, Verl F. (Franklin).

Second asst. to general superintendent G. Carlos Smith, Young Men, June 9, 1961-Oct. 4, 1961; b. July 8, 1919, Hinckley, Utah; d. April 17, 1989, Salt Lake City; m. Arline Martindale, seven children; Improvement Era business manager; stake president, Young Men general board member.
