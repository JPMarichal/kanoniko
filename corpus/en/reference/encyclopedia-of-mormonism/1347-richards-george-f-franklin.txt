RICHARDS, George F. (Franklin).

Apostle, April 9, 1906; Acting patriarch to the Church, Oct. 8, 1937-Oct. 3, 1942; President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, May 21, 1945-Aug. 8, 1950; b. Feb. 23, 1861, Farmington, Utah; d. Aug. 8, 1950, Salt Lake City; m. Alice A. Robinson, fifteen children; mission president.
