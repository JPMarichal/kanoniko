BOYER, Marian L. Richards.

Second counselor to general president Barbara B. Smith, Relief Society, Oct. 3, 1974-Nov. 1978; First counselor, Nov. 1978-April 7, 1984; b. Dec. 31, 1912, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Harold Boyer, five children; homemaker; Relief Society general board member, stake Relief Society president.
