HOWELLS, Adele Cannon.

First counselor to general president May Green Hinckley, Primary, Jan. 1, 1940-May 2, 1943; general president, July 29, 1943-April 14, 1951; b. Jan. 12, 1886, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Apr. 14, 1951, Salt Lake City; m. David P. Howells, three children; homemaker, educator.
