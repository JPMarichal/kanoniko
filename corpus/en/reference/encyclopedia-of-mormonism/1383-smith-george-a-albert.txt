SMITH, George A. (Albert).

Seventy, Mar. 1, 1835; Apostle, April 26, 1839; First counselor to President Brigham Young, Oct. 7, 1868-Sept. 1, 1875; b. June 26, 1817, Potsdam, New York; d. Sept. 1, 1875, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Bathsheba Wilson Bigler; practiced plural marriage, thirty children on record; farmer, legislator; Church historian and recorder.
