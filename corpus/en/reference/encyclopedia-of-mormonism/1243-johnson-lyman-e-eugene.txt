JOHNSON, Lyman E. (Eugene).

Apostle, Feb. 14, 1835; excommunicated, April 13, 1838; b. Oct. 24, 1811; Pomfret, Vermont; d. Dec. 20, 1856, Prairie du Chien, Wisconsin; m. Sarah Land (Lang, Long), two children; attorney.
