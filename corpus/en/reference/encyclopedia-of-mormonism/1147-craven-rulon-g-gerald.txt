CRAVEN, Rulon G. (Gerald).

Seventy, Dec. 5, 1990; b. Nov. 11, 1924, Murray, Utah; m. Donna Lunt, six children; secretary to Council of the Twelve; regional representative, Sunday School general board member, mission president.
