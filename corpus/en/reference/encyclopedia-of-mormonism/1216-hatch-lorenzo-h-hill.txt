HATCH, Lorenzo H. (Hill).

Second asst. to general superintendent George Q. Morris, Young Men, 1937-1948; b. Feb. 23, 1893, Franklin, Idaho; d. Nov. 27, 1971, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Ina Porter, four children; stake president.
