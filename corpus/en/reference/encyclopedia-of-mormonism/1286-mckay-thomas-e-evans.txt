MCKAY, Thomas E. (Evans).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1941-Jan. 15, 1958; b. Oct. 29, 1875, Huntsville, Utah; d. Jan. 15, 1958, Salt Lake City; m. Faun Brimhall, five children; businessman, educator; mission president.

MCMURRIN, Joseph W. (William). Seventy, Oct. 5, 1897-Oct. 24, 1932; b. Sept. 5, 1858, Tooele, Utah; d. Oct. 24, 1932, Los Angeles, California; m. Mary Ellen Hunter, seven children; teamster, stone cutter; mission president.
