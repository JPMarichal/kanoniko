HALE, Arta Matthews.

First counselor to general president LaVern Watts Parmley, Primary, May 16, 1951-April 6, 1962; b. Oct. 24, 1898, Oakley, Idaho; d. July 11, 1990, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Dewey Hale, two children; Primary general board member.
