#149 As the Dew From Heaven Distilling

This hymn is familiar to people throughout the world because it serves as the traditional closing organ postlude music for the weekly broadcast of the Mormon Tabernacle Choir. Latter-day Saint musician Joseph J. Daynes wrote the setting for this Protestant text by Thomas Kelly based on Deuteronomy 32:2. The text appeared in 1806, and the Latter-day Saint tune was first published in the 1889 Latter-day Saints' Psalmody.
