BENTLEY, Joseph T. (Taylor).

General superintendent, Young Men, July 21, 1958-Oct. 6, 1962; b. Mar. 6, 1906, Colonia Ju rez, Chihuahua, Mexico; m. Kathleen Bench, six children; certified public accountant, educator; mission president.
