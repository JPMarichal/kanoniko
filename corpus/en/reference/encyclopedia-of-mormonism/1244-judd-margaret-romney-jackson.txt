JUDD, Margaret Romney Jackson.

First counselor to general president Florence S. Jacobsen, Young Women, Sept. 30, 1961-Nov. 9, 1972; b. Sept. 7, 1909, Colonia Ju rez, Mexico; m. Junius M. Jackson (d. 1981), five children; m. George E. Judd; homemaker; Young Women general board member.
