HOWELLS, Marcia Knowlton.

First counselor to general president Amy Brown Lyman, Relief Society, April 1940-April 6, 1945; b. May 28, 1888, Farmington, Utah; d. June 10, 1976, Salt Lake City; m. Thomas J. Howells, one child; homemaker; Relief Society general board member.
