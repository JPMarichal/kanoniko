HANCOCK, Levi W. (Ward).

Seventy, Feb. 28, 1835; released April 6, 1837, having supposedly previously been ordained a high priest; restored to place in First Council of the Seventy, Sept. 3, 1837; b. April 7, 1803, Springfield, Massachusetts; d. June 10, 1882, Washington, Utah; m. Clarissa Reed; practiced plural marriage, nineteen children on record; cabinet maker.
