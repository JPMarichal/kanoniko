HARDY, Ralph W. (Williams).

Second asst. to general superintendent Elbert R. Curtis, Young Men, 1948-1957; b. May 6, 1916, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Aug. 6, 1957, Ogden; m. Maren Eccles, four children; radio executive.
