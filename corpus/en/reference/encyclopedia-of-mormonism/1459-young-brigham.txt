YOUNG, Brigham.

Apostle, Feb. 14, 1835; President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, April 14, 1840; President of the Church, Dec. 27, 1847-Aug. 29, 1877; b. June 1, 1801, Whitingham, Vermont; d. Aug. 29, 1877, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Miriam Words (d. 1824), two children; m. Mary Ann Angell; practiced plural marriage, sixty-one children on record (including four adopted children); carpenter-glazier. (See biography, Young, Brigham.)
