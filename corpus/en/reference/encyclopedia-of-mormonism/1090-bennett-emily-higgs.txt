BENNETT, Emily Higgs.

First counselor to general president Bertha S. Reeder, Young Women, June 13, 1948-Sept. 30, 1961; b. June 27, 1896, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Mar. 19, 1985, Salt Lake City; m. Harold H. Bennett, eight children; homemaker; Young Women general board member.
