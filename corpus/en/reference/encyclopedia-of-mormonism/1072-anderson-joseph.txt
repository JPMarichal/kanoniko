ANDERSON, Joseph.

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1970; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; Emeritus General Authority, Dec. 31, 1978; b. Nov. 20, 1889, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Norma Peterson, three children; secretary to the First Presidency.
