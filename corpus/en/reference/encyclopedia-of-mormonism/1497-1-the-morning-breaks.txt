Appendix 6 A Selection of LDS Hymns

#1 The Morning Breaks

This hymn in praise of the Restoration was published as Hymn 1 in the Manchester Hymnal in 1840; it is also the first hymn in the 1985 hymnal. Parley P. Pratt's text is a Latter-day Saint meditation upon a line from Charles Wesley's "Wrestling Jacob," from which he borrowed the first line of the hymn. George Careless composed the hymn tune in 1864 as he and a group of other Latter-day Saint converts from the British Isles sailed into New York on the ship Hudson.
