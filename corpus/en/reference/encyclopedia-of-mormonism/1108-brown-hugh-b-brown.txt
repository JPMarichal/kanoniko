BROWN, Hugh B. (Brown).

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 4, 1953; Apostle, April 10, 1958-Dec. 2, 1975; Second counselor to President McKay, Oct. 12, 1961; First counselor, Oct. 4, 1963; released at the death of President McKay, Jan. 18, 1970, and returned to the Quorum of the Twelve; b. Oct. 24, 1883, Granger, Utah; d. Dec. 2, 1975, Salt Lake City; m. Zina Young Card, eight children; attorney, educator; mission and stake president.
