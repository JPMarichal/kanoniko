SMITH, Hyrum.

Second counselor to the First Presidency, Sept. 3, 1837; Second counselor to President Joseph Smith, Nov. 7, 1837; given all priesthood that had been originally held by Oliver Cowdery including Apostle; Patriarch to the Church and Assistant President of the Church, Jan. 24, 1841-June 27, 1844; b. Feb. 9, 1800, Tunbridge, Vermont; martyred June 27, 1844, at Carthage Jail, Carthage, Illinois; m. Jerusha Barden (d. 1836), six children; m. Mary Fielding, two children; practiced plural marriage, no other children on record; farmer. (See biography, Smith, Hyrum.)
