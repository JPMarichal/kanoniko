BROCKBANK, Bernard P. (Park).

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 6, 1962; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; Emeritus General Authority, Oct. 4, 1980; b. May 24, 1909, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Nada Rich (d. 1967), six children; m. Frances Morgan; building contractor; mission president.
