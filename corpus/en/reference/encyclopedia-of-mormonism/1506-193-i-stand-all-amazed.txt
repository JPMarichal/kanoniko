#193 I Stand All Amazed

In addition to the opening and closing hymns in each Sunday Sacrament meeting, the LDS congregation sings a hymn known as the Sacrament hymn. This example is a borrowed hymn that entered Latter-day Saint hymnody by way of Deseret Sunday School Songs in 1909, ten years after its first publication in a gospel-song collection.
