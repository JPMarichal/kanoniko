TAYLOR, John.

Apostle, Dec. 19, 1838; President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, Oct. 6, 1877; President of the Church, Oct. 10, 1880-July 25, 1887; b. Nov. 1, 1808, Milnthrope, England; d. July 25, 1887, Kaysville, Utah; m. Leonora Cannon; practiced plural marriage, thirty-five children on record; farmer. (See biography, Taylor, John.)

TAYLOR, John H. (Harris). Seventy, Oct. 6, 1933-May 28, 1946; b. June 28, 1875, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. May 28, 1946, Salt Lake City; m. Susan Rachel Grant, two children; dentist; mission president.
