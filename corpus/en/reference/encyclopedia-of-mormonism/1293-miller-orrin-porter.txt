MILLER, Orrin Porter.

Seventy, Feb. 10, 1884-July 7, 1918; Second counselor to Presiding Bishop Charles W. Nibley, Oct. 24, 1901; First counselor, Dec. 4, 1907-July 7, 1918; b. Sept. 11, 1858, Mill Creek, Utah; d. July 7, 1918, Salt Lake City; m. Elizabeth M. Morgan, nine children; businessman, rancher; stake president.
