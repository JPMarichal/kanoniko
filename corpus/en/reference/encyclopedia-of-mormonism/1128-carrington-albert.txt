CARRINGTON, Albert.

Apostle, July 3, 1870; Counselor to President Brigham Young, April 8, 1873; Asst. counselor to President Young, May 9, 1874; released, Aug. 29, 1877 at the death of President Young; excommunicated, Nov. 7, 1885; rebaptized, Nov. 1, 1887; b. Jan. 8, 1813, Royalton, Vermont; d. Sept. 19, 1889, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Rhoda Maria Woods; practiced plural marriage, fifteen children on record; attorney, educator; mission president.
