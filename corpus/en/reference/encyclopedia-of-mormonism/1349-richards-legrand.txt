RICHARDS, LeGrand.

Presiding Bishop, April 6, 1938; Apostle, April 10, 1952-Jan. 11, 1983; b. Feb. 6, 1886, Farmington, Utah; d. Jan. 11, 1983, Salt Lake City; m. Ina Jane Ashton, six children; businessman; mission and stake president.
