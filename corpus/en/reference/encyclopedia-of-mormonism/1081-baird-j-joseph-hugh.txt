BAIRD, J. (Joseph) Hugh.

Second counselor to general president Richard L. Warner, Sunday School, Aug. 1979-Oct. 1979; b. July 25, 1929, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Florence Richards, nine children; educator; Sunday School general board member.
