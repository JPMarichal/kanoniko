CLYDE, Aileen Hales.

Second counselor to general president Elaine L. Jack, Relief Society, Mar. 31, 1990; b. May 18, 1928, Springville, Utah; m. Hal M. Clyde, three children; homemaker, educator; Young Women general board member.
