JOHNSON, Luke S.

Apostle, Feb. 15, 1835; excommunicated, April 13, 1838; rebaptized in 1846; b. Nov. 2, 1807, Pomfret, Vermont; d. Dec. 9, 1861, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Susan Poteet; practiced plural marriage, fifteen children on record; educator, doctor.
