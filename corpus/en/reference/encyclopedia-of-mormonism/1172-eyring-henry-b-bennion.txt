EYRING, Henry B. (Bennion).

First counselor to Presiding Bishop Robert D. Hales, April 6, 1985; b. May 31, 1933, Princeton, New Jersey; m. Kathleen Johnson, six children; Commissioner of Church Education, former president of Ricks College, Rexburg, Idaho; regional representative, Sunday School general board member.
