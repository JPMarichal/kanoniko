ANDERSON, May.

First counselor to general president Louie B. Felt, Primary, Dec. 29, 1905-Oct. 6, 1925; General president, Oct. 6, 1925-Sept. 11, 1939; b. June 8, 1864, Liverpool, England; d. June 10, 1946, Salt Lake City, Utah; editor, Children's Friend, 1902-1940.
