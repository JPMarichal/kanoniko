KIMBALL, Spencer W. (Woolley).

Apostle, Oct. 7, 1943; President of the Quorum of Twelve Apostles, 1970; President of the Church, Dec. 30, 1973-Nov. 5, 1985; b. Mar. 28, 1895, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Nov. 5, 1985, Salt Lake City; m. Camilla Eyring, four children; businessman; stake president. (See biography, Kimball, Spencer W.)
