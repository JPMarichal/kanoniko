PACK, Sadie Grant.

First counselor to general president May Anderson, Primary, Oct. 6, 1925-Sept. 11, 1939; b. Dec. 20, 1877, Bountiful, Utah; d. Aug. 23, 1960, Salt Lake City; m. Frederick J. Pack, four children.
