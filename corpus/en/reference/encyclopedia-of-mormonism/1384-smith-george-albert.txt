SMITH, George Albert.

Apostle, Oct. 8, 1903; President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, July 1, 1943; President of the Church, May 21, 1945-April 4, 1951; b. April 4, 1870, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 4, 1951, Salt Lake City; m. Lucy Emily Woodruff, three children; businessman; mission president. (See biography, Smith, George Albert.)
