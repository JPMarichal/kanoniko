RICH, Leonard.

Seventy, Feb. 28, 1835; released April 6, 1837, having previously been ordained a high priest; m. Keziah.

RICHARDS, Florence Holbrook. Second counselor to general president LaVern Watts Parmley, Primary, May 16, 1951-June 11, 1953; b. July 7, 1905, Logan, Utah; m. Lorin L. Richards, three children; Primary general board member.
