GILES, John D. (Davis).

First asst. to general superintendent George Q. Morris, Young Men, 1937-1948; b. Aug. 1, 1883, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Sept. 23, 1955, Salt Lake City; m. Una Pratt, four children; businessman, Boy Scout official.
