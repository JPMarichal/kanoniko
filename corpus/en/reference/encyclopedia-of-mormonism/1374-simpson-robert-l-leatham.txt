SIMPSON, Robert L. (Leatham).

First counselor to Presiding Bishop John H. Vandenberg, Sept. 30, 1961; Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1972; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; Emeritus General Authority, Oct. 1, 1989; b. Aug. 8, 1915, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Jelaire Chandler, four children; business executive; mission president.
