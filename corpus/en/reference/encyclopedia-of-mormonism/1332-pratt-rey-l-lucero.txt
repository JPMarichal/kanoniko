PRATT, Rey L. (Lucero).

Seventy, Jan. 29, 1925-April 14, 1931; b. Oct. 11, 1878, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 14, 1931, Salt Lake City; m. Mary Stark, 13 children; mission president.
