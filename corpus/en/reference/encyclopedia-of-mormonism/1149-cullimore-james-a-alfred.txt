CULLIMORE, James A. (Alfred).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1966; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; Emeritus General Authority, Sept. 30, 1978; b. Jan. 17, 1906, Lindon, Utah; d. June 14, 1986, Salt Lake City; m. Grace Gardner, three children; businessman; stake president.
