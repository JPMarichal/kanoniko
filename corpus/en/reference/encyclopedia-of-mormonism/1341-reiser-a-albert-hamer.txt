REISER, A. (Albert) Hamer.

First asst. to general superintendent George R. Hill, Sunday School, May 1943-Sept. 1949; b. Aug. 31, 1897, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 25, 1981, Salt Lake City; m. Elizabeth Baxter, seven children; business management; mission and stake president.
