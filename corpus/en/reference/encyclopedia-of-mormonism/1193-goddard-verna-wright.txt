GODDARD, Verna Wright.

Second counselor to general president Lucy Grant Cannon, Young Women, Nov. 1937-July 1944; First counselor, July 1944-April 6, 1948; b. Nov. 24, 1889, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Nov. 26, 1949; m. J. Percy Goddard, four children.
