ROSS, Isabelle Salmon.

Second counselor to general president May Anderson, Primary, Oct. 6, 1925-Sept. 11, 1929; First counselor, Sept. 11, 1929-Dec. 31, 1939; b. Nov. 1, 1867, Percy, Utah; d. Dec. 28, 1947, Salt Lake City; m. Charles James Ross, three children; educator; Primary general board member.
