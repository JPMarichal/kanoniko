ROCKWOOD, Albert P. (Perry).

Seventy, Dec. 2, 1845-Nov. 25, 1879. b. June 5, 1805, Holliston, Massachusetts; d. Nov. 25, 1879; m. Nancy Haven; practiced plural marriage, twenty-two children on record.
