COWDERY, Oliver.

Apostle, May-June 1829; Second Elder of the Church, April 6, 1830; Asst. president of the High Priesthood, Dec. 5, 1834; Asst. counselor to President Joseph Smith, Sept. 3, 1837; excommunicated April 11, 1838; rebaptized Nov. 12, 1848; b. Oct. 3, 1806, Wells, Vermont; d. Mar. 3, 1850, Richmond, Missouri; m. Elizabeth Ann Whitmer, six children; attorney, educator.
