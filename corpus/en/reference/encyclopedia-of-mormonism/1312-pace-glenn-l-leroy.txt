PACE, Glenn L. (Leroy).

Second counselor to Presiding Bishop Robert D. Hales, April 6, 1985; b. Mar. 21, 1940, Provo, Utah; m. Jolene Clayson, six children; certified public accountant; managing director of Welfare Services.
