HARDY, M. (Milton) H.

First asst. to general superintendent Junius F. Wells, Young Men, 1876-1880; b. Sept. 26, 1844, Groveland, Massachusetts; d. Aug. 23, 1905, Provo, Utah; m. Elizabeth Smoot, five children.
