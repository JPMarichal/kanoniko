TAYLOR, Margaret Young.

First counselor to general president Elmina Shepherd Taylor, Young Women, June 19 1880-1887; b. April 24, 1837, Westport, Connecticut; d. May 3, 1919, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. John Taylor, nine children.
