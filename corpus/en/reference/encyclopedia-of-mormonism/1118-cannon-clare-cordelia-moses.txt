CANNON, Clare Cordelia Moses.

Second counselor to general president Louie B. Felt, Primary, June 19, 1880-Oct. 4, 1895; b. April 21, 1839, Westfield, Massachusetts; d. Aug. 21, 1926, Centerville, Utah; m. William H. Mason (d. 1860), two children; m. Angus M. Cannon, three children.
