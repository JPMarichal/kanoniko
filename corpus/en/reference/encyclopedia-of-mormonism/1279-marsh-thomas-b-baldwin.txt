MARSH, Thomas B. (Baldwin).

Apostle, April 26, 1835; President of the Quorum of Twelve Apostles, May 2, 1835; excommunicated for apostasy, Mar. 17, 1839; rebaptized, July 16, 1857; b. Nov. 1, 1799, Acton, Massachusetts; d. Jan. 1866, Ogden, Utah; m. Elizabeth Godkin; practiced plural marriage, one child on record; educator, physician to the Church.
