MCCONKIE, Bruce R. (Redd).

Seventy, Oct. 6, 1946; Apostle, Oct. 12, 1972-April 19, 1985; b. July 29, 1915, Ann Arbor, Michigan; d. April 19, 1985, Salt Lake City; m. Amelia Smith, eleven children; attorney; mission president.
