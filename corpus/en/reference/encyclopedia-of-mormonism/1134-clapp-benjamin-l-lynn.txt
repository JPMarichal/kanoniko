CLAPP, Benjamin L. (Lynn).

Seventy, Dec. 2, 1845; excommunicated, April 7, 1859; b. Aug. 19, 1814, West Huntsville, Alabama; d. 1860, Liberty, California; m. Mary Shultz; practiced plural marriage, twelve children; farmer.
