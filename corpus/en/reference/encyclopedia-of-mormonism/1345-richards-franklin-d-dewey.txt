RICHARDS, Franklin D. (Dewey).

Apostle, Feb. 12, 1849; President of the Quorum of Twelve Apostles, Sept. 13, 1898-Dec. 9, 1899; b. April 2, 1821, Richmond, Massachusetts; d. Dec. 9, 1899, Ogden, Utah; m. Jane Snyder; practiced plural marriage, twenty-two children on record; judge.
