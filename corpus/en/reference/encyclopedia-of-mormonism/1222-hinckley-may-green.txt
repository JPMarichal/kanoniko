HINCKLEY, May Green.

General president, Primary, Jan. 1, 1940-May 2, 1943; b. May 1, 1885, Brampton, England; d. May 2, 1943, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Bryant S. Hinckley; nurse; stake Young Women president.
