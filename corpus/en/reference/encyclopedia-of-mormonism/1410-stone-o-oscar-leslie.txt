STONE, O. (Oscar) Leslie.

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 6, 1972; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; Emeritus General Authority, Oct. 4, 1980; b. May 28, 1903, Chapin, Idaho; d. April 26, 1986, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Dorothy Cobbley, four children; business executive; regional representative, temple and stake president.
