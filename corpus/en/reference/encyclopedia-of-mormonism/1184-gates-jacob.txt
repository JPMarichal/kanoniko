GATES, Jacob.

Seventy, April 6, 1860-April 14, 1892; b. Mar. 9, 1811, Saint Johnsbury, Vermont; d. April 14, 1892, Provo, Utah; m. Mary Minerva Snow; practiced plural marriage, thirteen children on record; farmer.
