RICHARDS, Franklin D. (Dewey).

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 8, 1960; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976-Nov. 13, 1987. b. Nov. 17, 1900, Ogden, Utah; d. Nov. 13, 1987, Salt Lake City; m. Helen Kearnes, four children; temple and mission president.
