KIMBALL, J. (Jonathan) Golden.

Seventy, April 5, 1892-Sept. 2, 1938; b. June 9, 1853, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Sept. 2, 1938, Reno, Nevada; m. Jennie Knowlton, six children; Young Men general board member, mission president.
