Mexico City Temple

Dedicated by President Gordon B. Hinckley, December 2, 1983

Bless thy saints in this great land and those from other lands who will use this temple. Most have in their veins the blood of Father Lehi. Thou hast kept thine ancient promise. Many thousands "that walked in darkness have seen a great light." (Isaiah 9:2) May the harvest that we have witnessed here foreshadow greater things to come as Thy work rolls on in power and majesty in this, the dispensation of the fulness of times. [Church News, pp. 4-5, December 11, 1983.]
