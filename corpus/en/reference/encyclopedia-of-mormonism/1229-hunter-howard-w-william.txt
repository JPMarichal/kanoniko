HUNTER, Howard W. (William).

Apostle, Oct. 15, 1959; Acting president of the Quorum of the Twelve, Nov. 10, 1985; President of the Quorum, June 2, 1988; b. Nov. 24, 1907, Boise, Idaho; m. Clara May Jeffs (d. 1983), three children; m. Inis Egan; attorney; stake president.
