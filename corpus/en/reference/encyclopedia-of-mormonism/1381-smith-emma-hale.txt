SMITH, Emma Hale.

First general president of the Relief Society, May 17, 1842-Mar. 16, 1844; b. July 10, 1804, Harmony, Pennsylvania; d. April 30, 1879, Nauvoo, Illinois; m. Joseph Smith, Jr. (d. 1844), eleven children; m. Lewis Crum Bidamon. (See biography Smith, Emma Hale).
