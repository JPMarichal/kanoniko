YOUNG, Seymour B. (Bicknell).

Seventy, Oct. 14, 1882-Dec. 15, 1924; b. Oct. 3, 1837, Kirtland, Ohio; d. Dec. 15, 1924, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Ann Elizabeth Riter; practiced plural marriage, thirteen children on record; physician.
