DYER, Alvin R. (Rulon).

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 11, 1958; Apostle, Oct. 5, 1967-Mar. 6, 1977; Counselor to President David O. McKay, April 6, 1968; released, Jan. 18, 1970, at the death of President McKay; resumed position as Asst. to the Twelve, Jan. 23, 1970; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; b. Jan. 1, 1903, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Mar. 6, 1977, Salt Lake City; m. May Elizabeth Jackson, two children; businessman; mission president.
