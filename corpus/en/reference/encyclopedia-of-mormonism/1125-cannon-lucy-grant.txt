CANNON, Lucy Grant.

Second counselor to general president Martha Horne Tingey, Young Women, July 15, 1923-Mar. 28, 1929; First counselor to general president Ruth May Fox, Mar. 28, 1929-Oct. 1937; general president, Nov. 1937-April 6, 1948; b. Oct. 22, 1880, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. May 27, 1966, Salt Lake City; m. George J. Cannon, seven children.
