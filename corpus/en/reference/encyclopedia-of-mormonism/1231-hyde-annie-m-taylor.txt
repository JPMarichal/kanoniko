HYDE, Annie M. Taylor.

First counselor to general president Bathsheba W. Smith, Relief Society, Nov. 10, 1901-Mar. 12, 1909; b. Oct. 20, 1849, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Mar. 12, 1909; m. Alonzo E. Hyde, eight children.
