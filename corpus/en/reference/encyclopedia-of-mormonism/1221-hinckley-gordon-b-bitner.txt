HINCKLEY, Gordon B. (Bitner).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1958; Apostle Oct. 5, 1961; Counselor to President Spencer W. Kimball, July 23, 1981; Second counselor, Dec. 2, 1982; First counselor to President Ezra Taft Benson, Nov. 10, 1985; b. June 23, 1910, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Marjorie Pay, five children; business executive; stake president.
