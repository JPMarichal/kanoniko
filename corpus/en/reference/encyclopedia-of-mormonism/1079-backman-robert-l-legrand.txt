BACKMAN, Robert L. (LeGrand).

Second counselor to general president W. Jay Eldredge, Young Men, June 25-Nov. 9, 1972; general president, Nov. 9, 1972—June 23, 1974; Seventy, April 1, 1978; b. Mar. 22, 1922, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Virginia Pickett, seven children; attorney and state legislator; regional representative, mission and stake president.
