THOMAS, Edna Harker.

Second counselor to general president May Anderson, Primary, Sept. 11, 1929-Dec. 11, 1933; b. April 11, 1881, Taylorsville, Utah; d. Apr. 29, 1942, Washington, D.C.; m. Elbert D. Thomas, three children.
