PAULSEN, Sara Broadbent.

First counselor to general president Naomi M. Shumway, Primary, Oct. 5, 1974-April 2, 1977; b. Sept. 15, 1920, Heber City, Utah; m. Finn B. Paulsen, five children; stake Primary president, Primary general board member, temple matron.
