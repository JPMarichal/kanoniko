SHIMABUKURO, Sam K. (Koyei).

Seventy, July 5, 1991; b. June 7, 1925, Waipahu, Hawaii; m. Amy Michiko Hirose, one child; retired state employee; mission and stake president.

SHUMWAY, Naomi Maxfield. General president, Primary, Oct. 5, 1974-April 5, 1980; b. Oct 3, 1922, Provo, Utah; m. Roden Grant Shumway, three children; stake Primary president, Primary general board member.
