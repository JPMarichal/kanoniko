ROBISON, Louise Yates.

General president, Relief Society, Oct. 7, 1928-Dec. 1939; b. May 27, 1866, Scipio, Utah; d. Mar. 30, 1946, San Francisco, California; m. Joseph Lyman Robison, six children.
