THATCHER, Moses.

Apostle, April 9, 1879; dropped from the Quorum, April 6, 1896; b. Feb. 2, 1842, Springfield, Illinois; d. Aug. 21, 1909, Logan, Utah; m. Celestia Ann Farr; practiced plural marriage, eleven children on record; businessman; mission president.
