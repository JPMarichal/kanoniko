RICHARDS, Lynn S. (Stephen).

Second asst. to general superintendent George R. Hill, Sunday School, Oct. 1952-Nov. 1966; First asst. to general superintendent David Lawrence McKay, Nov. 1966-June 1971; b. Feb. 3, 1901, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Lucille Janet Covey, four children; attorney.
