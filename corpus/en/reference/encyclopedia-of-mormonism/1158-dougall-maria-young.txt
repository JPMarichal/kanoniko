DOUGALL, Maria Young.

First counselor to general president Elmina Shepherd Taylor, Young Women, 1877-Dec. 6, 1904; b. Dec. 10, 1849, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 30, 1935, Salt Lake City; m. William B. Dougall, three children.
