READING, Lucile Cardon.

Second counselor to general president LaVern Watts Parmley, Primary, July 23, 1963- Jan. 8, 1970; First counselor, Jan. 8, 1970-Aug. 6, 1970; b. Aug. 16, 1909, Logan, Utah; d. Mar. 22, 1982, Centerville; m. Keith E. Reading, two children; editor of the Friend.
