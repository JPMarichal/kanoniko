#5 High On the Mountain Top

Many Latter-day Saint hymn writers, among them Joel H. Johnson, made use of the parallels between the mountainous Utah landscape of the latter-day Zion and biblical prophecies such as Isaiah 2:2: "The Lord's house shall be established in the top of the mountains, and shall be exalted above the hills; and all nations shall flow unto it." The text of this hymn was first published in 1856, and the tune in the 1889. The tune is similar to an earlier L. Mason tune "Stow."
