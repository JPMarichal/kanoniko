SMITH, Joseph, Jr.

Apostle, May-June 1829, by Peter, James and John; First Elder and President of the Church, April 6, 1830-June 27, 1844; President of the High priesthood, Jan. 25, 1832; b. Dec. 23, 1805, Sharon, Vermont; martyred June 27, 1844, at Carthage Jail, Carthage, Illinois; m. Emma Hale, eleven children (including two adopted); practiced plural marriage, no other children on record; farmer. (See biography, Smith, Joseph.)
