BROWN, Victor L. (Lee).

Second counselor to Presiding Bishop John H. Vandenberg, Sept. 30, 1961; Presiding Bishop, April 6, 1972; Seventy, April 6, 1985; Emeritus General Authority, Oct. 1, 1989; b. July 31, 1914, Cardston, Alberta, Canada; m. Lois Kjar, five children; business executive; temple president.
