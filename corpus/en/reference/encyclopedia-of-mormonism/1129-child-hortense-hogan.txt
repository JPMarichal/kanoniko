CHILD, Hortense Hogan.

First counselor to general president Ruth H. Funk, Young Women, Nov. 9, 1972-July 12, 1978; b. May 6, 1919, Thatcher, Idaho; m. Romel Child, two children; civic worker; Young Women general board member.
