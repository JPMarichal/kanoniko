BURTON, Theodore M. (Moyle).

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 8, 1960; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; Emeritus General Authority, Oct. 1, 1989; b. Mar. 27, 1907, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Dec. 22, 1989, Salt Lake City; m. Minnie Susan Preece, one child; educator; mission president.
