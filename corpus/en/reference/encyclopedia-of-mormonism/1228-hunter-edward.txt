HUNTER, Edward.

Presiding Bishop of the Church, April 7, 1851-Oct. 16, 1883; b. June 22, 1793, Newton, Pennsylvania; d. Oct. 16, 1883, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Ann Standly (Stanley); practiced plural marriage, thirteen children on record; farmer.
