SONNE, Alma.

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1941; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976-Nov. 27, 1977. b. Mar. 5, 1884, Logan, Utah; d. Nov. 27, 1977, Logan; m. Geneva Ballantyne (d. 1941), five children; m. Leona Ballantyne Woolley; businessman; mission and stake president.
