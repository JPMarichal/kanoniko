SMITH, John Henry.

Apostle, Oct. 27, 1880; Second counselor to President Joseph F. Smith, April 7, 1910-Oct. 13, 1911; b. Sept. 18, 1848, Carbunca (now Council Bluffs), Iowa; d. Oct. 13, 1911, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Sarah Farr; practiced plural marriage, nineteen children on record; railroad businessman, legislator; mission president.
