HALES, Robert D. (Dean).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 4, 1975; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; First counselor to general president Hugh W. Pinnock, Sunday School, July 1981-July 1985; Presiding Bishop, April 6, 1985; b. Aug 24, 1932, New York City, New York; m. Mary Elene Crandall, two children; business executive; regional representative, mission and stake president.
