DOXEY, Graham W. (Watson).

First counselor to general president Neil D. Schaerrer, Young Men, April 7, 1977-Oct. 1979; Seventy, April 6, 1991; b. Mar. 30, 1927, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Mary Louise Young, twelve children; business executive; mission and stake president.
