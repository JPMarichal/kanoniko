PRATT, Parley P. (Parker).

Apostle, Feb. 21, 1835-May 13, 1857; b. April 12, 1807, Burlington, New York; assassinated, May 13, 1857, near Van Buren, Arkansas; m. Thankful Halsey; practiced plural marriage, thirty-one children on record. (See biography Pratt, Parley P.)
