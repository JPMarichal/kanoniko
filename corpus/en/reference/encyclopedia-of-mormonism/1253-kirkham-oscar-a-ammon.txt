KIRKHAM, Oscar A. (Ammon).

Seventy, Oct. 5, 1941-Mar. 10, 1958; b. Jan. 22, 1880, Lehi, Utah; d. Mar. 10, 1958, Salt Lake City; m. Ida Murdock, eight children; Young Men general board member, Boy Scouts of America executive.
