ELDREDGE, W. Jay.

General superintendent, Young Men, Sept. 17 1969-June 25, 1972; general president, Young Men, June 25, 1972-Nov. 9, 1972; b. April 27, 1913, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Marjory Hyde, five children; businessman; mission and stake president.
