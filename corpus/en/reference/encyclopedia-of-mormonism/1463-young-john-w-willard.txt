YOUNG, John W. (Willard).

Counselor to President Brigham Young, April 8, 1873; Asst. counselor May 9, 1874; First counselor, Oct. 7, 1876; released at death of President Young; Counselor to the Twelve, Oct. 6, 1877-Oct. 6, 1891; b. Oct. 1, 1844, Nauvoo, Illinois; d. Feb. 11, 1924, New York City, New York; m. Lucy Maria Canfield; practiced plural marriage, ten children on record; builder of Utah railroads.
