SMOOT, Reed.

Apostle, April 8, 1900-Feb. 9, 1941; b. Jan. 10, 1862, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Feb. 9, 1941, St. Petersburg, Florida; m. Alpha M. Eldredge, six children; business executive, U.S. Senator 1903-1932. (See Smoot Hearings).
