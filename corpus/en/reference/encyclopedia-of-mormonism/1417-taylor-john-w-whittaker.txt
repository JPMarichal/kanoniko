TAYLOR, John W. (Whittaker).

Apostle, April 9, 1884; resigned Oct. 28, 1905; excommunicated for polygamy Mar. 28, 1911; b. May 15, 1858, Provo, Utah; d. Oct. 10, 1916, Salt Lake City; m. May Leona Rich; practiced plural marriage, twenty-nine children on record.
