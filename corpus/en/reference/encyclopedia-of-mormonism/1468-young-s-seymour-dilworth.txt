YOUNG, S. (Seymour) Dilworth.

Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976-July 9, 1981; Emeritus General Authority, Sept. 30, 1978; b. Sept. 7, 1897, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. July 9, 1981, Salt Lake City; m. Gladys Pratt (d. 1964), two children; m. Huldah Parker; Boy Scouts of America official; mission president.
