OKAZAKI, Chieko Nishimura.

First counselor to general president Elaine L. Jack, Relief Society, Mar. 31, 1990; b. Oct. 26, 1926, Kohala, Hawaii; m. Edward Yulio Okazaki, two children; homemaker, educator; Primary and Young Women general board member.
