COLTRIN, Zebedee.

Seventy, Feb. 28, 1835; released April 6, 1837, having previously been ordained high priest; b. Sept. 7, 1804, Ovid, New York; d. July 20, 1887, Spanish Fork, Utah; m. Julia Ann Jennings; practiced plural marriage, sixteen children on record; farmer.
