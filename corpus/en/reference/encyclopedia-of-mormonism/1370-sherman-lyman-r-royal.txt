SHERMAN, Lyman R. (Royal).

Seventy, Feb. 28, 1835; released, April 6, 1837, having previously been ordained a high priest; b. May 22, 1804, Salem, Massachusetts; d. Jan. 27, 1839, Far West, Missouri; m. Delcena Didamia Johnson, six children.
