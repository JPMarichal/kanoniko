The role and duties of the Presiding Bishopric of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints change as the Church grows and needs dictate. The following excerpts were selected from many available documents to illustrate a few of the responsibilities of the Presiding Bishopric.

Consecration of Property, 1832

Following is an excerpt from a formal document labeled a "Lease of inheritance in Jackson Co. and loan of property there." It is dated October 12, 1832, and signed by Edward Partridge, Bishop. A printed form was used; words in brackets indicate the handwritten parts. Spelling and punctuation are preserved as in the original.
