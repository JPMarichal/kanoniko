GODDARD, George.

First asst. to general superintendent George Q. Cannon, Sunday School, June 1872-Jan. 1899; b. Dec. 5, 1813, Leicester, England; d. Jan. 12, 1899, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Elizabeth Harrison; practiced plural marriage, eighteen children on record.
