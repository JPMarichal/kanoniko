BENNION, Samuel O. (Otis).

Seventy, April 6, 1933-Mar. 8, 1945; b. June 9, 1874, Taylorsville, Utah; d. Mar. 8, 1945, Salt Lake City; m. Charlotte Trowler, two children; newspaper executive; mission president.
