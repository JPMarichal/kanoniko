BEEBE, Clara M. Woodruff.

Second counselor to general president Louie B. Felt, Primary, Dec. 29, 1906-Oct. 6, 1925; b. July 23, 1868, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Dec. 27, 1927; m. Ovando C. Beebe, eight children.
