CHRISTIANSEN, ElRay L. (LaVar).

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 11, 1951-Dec. 1, 1975; b. July 13, 1897, Mayfield, Utah; d. Dec. 1, 1975, Salt Lake City; m. Lewella Rees, three children; educator; temple and stake president.
