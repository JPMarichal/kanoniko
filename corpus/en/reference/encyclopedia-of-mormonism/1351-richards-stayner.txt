RICHARDS, Stayner.

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 6, 1951-May 28, 1953; b. Dec. 20, 1885, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. May 28, 1953, Salt Lake City; m. Jane Foote Taylor, six children; real estate, home construction; mission and stake president.
