PETERSON, H. (Harold) Burke.

First counselor to Presiding Bishop Victor L. Brown, April 6, 1972; Seventy, April 6, 1985; b. Sept. 19, 1923, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Brookie Cardon, five children; civil engineer; regional representative, temple and stake president.
