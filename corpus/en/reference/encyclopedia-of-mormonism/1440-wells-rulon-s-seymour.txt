WELLS, Rulon S. (Seymour).

Seventy, April 5, 1893-May 7, 1941. b. July 7, 1854, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. May 7, 1941, Salt Lake City; m. Josephine E. Beatie, seven children; businessman; Young Men general board member, mission president.
