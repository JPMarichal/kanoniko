Daniel H. Ludlow, Encyclopedia of Mormonism 4 vols. (New York: Macmillan Publishing Company, 1992)

SMITH, Joseph, Sr.

Patriarch to the Church, Dec. 18, 1833; Asst. counselor to the First Presidency, Sept. 3, 1837-Sept. 14, 1840; b. July 12, 1771, Topsfield, Massachusetts; d. Sept. 14, 1840, Nauvoo, Illinois; m. Lucy Mack, ten children; farmer. (See biography, Smith, Joseph, Sr.)

SMITH, Julina Lambson. Second counselor to general president Emmeline B. Wells, Relief Society, Oct. 3, 1910-April 2, 1921; b. June 18, 1849, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Jan. 10, 1936, Salt Lake City; m. Joseph F. Smith, eleven children; homemaker.
