BOYNTON, John F. (Farnham).

Apostle, Feb. 15, 1835-Sept. 3, 1837; disfellowshipped, Sept. 3, 1837; excommunicated same year; b. Sept. 20, 1811, Bradford, Massachusetts; d. Oct. 20, 1890, Syracuse, New York; m. Susannah (Susan) Lowell (d.), five children; m. Caroline Foster Harriman; businessman.
