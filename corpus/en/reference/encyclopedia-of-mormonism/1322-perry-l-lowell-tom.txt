PERRY, L. (Lowell) Tom.

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 6, 1972; Apostle, April 11, 1974; b. Aug. 5, 1922, Logan, Utah; m. Virginia Lee (d. 1974), three children; m. Barbara Dayton; business executive; stake president.
