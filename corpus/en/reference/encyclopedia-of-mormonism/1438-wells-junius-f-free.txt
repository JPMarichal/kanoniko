WELLS, Junius F. (Free).

Seventy, Oct. 1875-Mar. 24, 1891; general superintendent, Young Men, 1876-1880; b. June 1, 1854, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 15, 1930, Salt Lake City; m. Helena Middleton Fobes, two children; asst. Church historian. (See biography, Wells, Junius F.)
