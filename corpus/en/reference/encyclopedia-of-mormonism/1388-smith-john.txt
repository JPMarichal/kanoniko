SMITH, John.

Asst. counselor in the First Presidency, Sept. 3, 1837; released at death of Joseph Smith, June 27, 1844; Patriarch to the Church, Jan. 1, 1849-May 23, 1854; b. July 16, 1781, Derryfield, New Hampshire; d. May 23, 1854, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Clarissa Lyman; practiced plural marriage, four children on record; stake president.
