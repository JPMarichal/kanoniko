EVANS, Joy Frewin.

First counselor to general president Barbara W. Winder, Relief Society, May 21, 1984-Mar. 31, 1990; b. Jan. 31, 1926, Salt Lake City; m. David C. Evans, ten children; homemaker; Relief Society general board member.
