JEPSEN, Betty Jo Nelson.

First counselor to general president Michaelene P. Grassli, Primary, April 2, 1988; b. Dec. 3, 1940, Boise, Idaho; m. Glen F. Jepsen, four children; educator, homemaker; Primary general board member, stake Primary president.
