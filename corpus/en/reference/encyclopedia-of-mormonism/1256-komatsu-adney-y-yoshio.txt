KOMATSU, Adney Y. (Yoshio).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 4, 1975; Seventy, Oct. 1, 1976; b. Aug. 2, 1923, Honolulu, Hawaii; m. Judy Nobue Fujitani, four children; businessman; regional representative, temple and mission president.
