COWLEY, Matthias F. (Foss).

Apostle, Oct. 7, 1897; resigned, Oct. 28, 1905; priesthood suspended, May 11, 1911; restored to full membership, April 3, 1936; b. Aug. 25, 1858, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. June 16, 1940, Salt Lake City; m. Abbie Hyde; practiced plural marriage, thirteen children on record.
