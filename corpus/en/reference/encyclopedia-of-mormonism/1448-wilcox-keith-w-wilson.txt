WILCOX, Keith W. (Wilson).

Seventy, Oct. 6, 1984-Oct. 1, 1989; b. May 15, 1921, Hyrum, Utah; m. Viva May Gammell, six children; architect; regional representative, temple, mission, and stake president. (Architect of the Washington, D.C. Temple.)
