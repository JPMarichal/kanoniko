ASHTON, Marvin J. (Jeremy).

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 3, 1969; Apostle, Dec. 2, 1971; b. May 6, 1915, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Norma Bernston, four children; businessman; Boy Scouts of America national committee member.
