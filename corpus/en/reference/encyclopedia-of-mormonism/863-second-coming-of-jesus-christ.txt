Second Coming of Jesus Christ

[The second coming of Jesus Christ refers to his return to the earth in glory to reign as King of Kings, as contrasted to his first coming as an infant in Bethlehem. Articles relevant to this topic are David, Prophetic Figure of Last Days; Dispensation of the Fulness of Times; Jesus Christ: Second Coming of Jesus Christ; Marriage Supper of the Lamb; Messiah: Messianic Concept and Hope; Millennium; Parables; Restoration of All Things.

Both the first and second appearances were foretold in the scriptures, with the second advent to be accompanied by earthshaking events of worldwide consequence. See Armageddon; Joseph Smith-Matthew; Last Days; Malachi, Prophecies of.]
