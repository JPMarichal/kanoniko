Appendix 11 Temple Dedicatory Prayers (Excerpts)

The dedicatory prayers of the temples are inspired statements and supplications. The following selections illustrate important points of LDS doctrine, especially related to the nature and importance of temples. The first LDS Temple in Kirtland, Ohio, was dedicated by the Prophet Joseph Smith and because the entire text is given as Doctrine and Covenants section 109, it is not included in this Appendix. Spelling and punctuation of the printed sources are preserved.
