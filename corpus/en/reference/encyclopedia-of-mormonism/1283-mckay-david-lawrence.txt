MCKAY, David Lawrence.

Second asst. to general superintendent George R. Hill, Sunday School, Sept. 1949-Oct. 1952; First asst., Oct. 1952-Nov. 1966; general superintendent, Nov. 1966-June 1971; b. Sept. 30, 1901, Ogden, Utah; m. Mildred Dean Calderwood, four children; attorney; mission president.
