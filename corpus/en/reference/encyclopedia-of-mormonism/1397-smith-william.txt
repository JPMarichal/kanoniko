SMITH, William.

Apostle, Feb. 15, 1835; dropped from the Quorum, May 4, 1839; restored to Quorum, May 25, 1839; dropped from the Quorum, Oct. 6, 1845; excommunicated, Oct. 19, 1845; b. Mar. 13, 1811, Royalton, Vermont; d. Nov. 13, 1894, Osterdock, Iowa; m. Caroline Amanda Grant; practiced plural marriage, seven children on record.
