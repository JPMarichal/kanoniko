WHITNEY, Newel K. (Kimball).

First Bishop of Kirtland Oct. 7, 1844; Presiding Bishop, April 6, 1847-Sept. 23, 1850; b. Feb. 5, 1795, Marlborough, Vermont; d. Sept. 23, 1850, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Elizabeth Ann Smith; practiced plural marriage, fourteen children on record; merchant.
