WEST, Josephine Richards.

Second counselor to general president Louie B. Felt, Primary, Dec. 15, 1896-Nov. 24, 1905; b. May 25, 1853, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 23, 1933, Logan; m. Joseph A. West, six children; stake Primary president.

WHITMER, John. Church historian 1831-1835; excommunicated, Mar. 10, 1838; b. Aug. 27, 1802; d. July 11, 1878, Far West, Missouri; m. Sarah Jackson, five children; farmer.
