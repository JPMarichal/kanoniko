TINGEY, Martha Jane Horne.

General president, Young Women, April 5, 1905-Mar. 28, 1929; b. Oct. 15, 1857, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Mar. 11, 1938, Salt Lake City; m. Joseph S. Tingey, seven children.
