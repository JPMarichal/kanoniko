Daniel H. Ludlow, Encyclopedia of Mormonism 4 vols. (New York: Macmillan Publishing Company, 1992)

Church in the World

[Since it was organized with six members in Fayette, New York, in 1830, The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints has spread throughout the United States and to many countries of the world. The history and development of the Church in the United States are discussed in six major articles found under the heading History of the Church. The history and development of the Church outside the United States are discussed in different articles under the headings Africa; Asia, East; Asia, South and Southeast; Australia; British Isles; Canada; Europe; Hawaii; Mexico and Central America; Middle East; New Zealand; Oceania; Scandinavia; South America; and West Indies.]
