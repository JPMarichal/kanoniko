LAW, William.

Second counselor to President Joseph Smith, Jan. 24, 1841; excommunicated, April 18, 1844; b. Sept. 8, 1809, Tyrone County, North Ireland; d. Jan. 19, 1892, Shullsburg, Wisconsin; m. Jane Silverthorn, eight children; businessman, doctor.
