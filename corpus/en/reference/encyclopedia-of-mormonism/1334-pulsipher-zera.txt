PULSIPHER, Zera.

Seventy, Mar. 6, 1838; released, April 12, 1862; b. June 24, 1789, Rockingham, Vermont; d. Jan. 1, 1872, Hebron, Utah; m. Polly Randall (d.); m. Mary Brown; practiced plural marriage, sixteen children on record; farmer; stake patriarch.
