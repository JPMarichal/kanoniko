BOYLE, Dessie (Martha Deseret) Grant.

Second counselor to general president Adele Cannon Howells, Primary, July 20, 1943-April 14, 1951; b. April 21, 1886, Liverpool, England; d. Sept. 18, 1970, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Ashby D. Boyle, five children.
