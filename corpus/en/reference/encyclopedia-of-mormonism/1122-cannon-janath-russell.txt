CANNON, Janath Russell.

First counselor to general president Barbara B. Smith, Relief Society, Oct. 3, 1974-April 7, 1984; b. Oct. 28, 1918, Ogden, Utah; m. Edwin Q. Cannon, six children; educator and writer; temple matron.
