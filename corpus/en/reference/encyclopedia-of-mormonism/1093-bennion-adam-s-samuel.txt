BENNION, Adam S. (Samuel).

Apostle, April 9, 1953-Feb. 11, 1958; b. Dec. 2, 1886, Taylorsville, Utah; d. Feb. 11, 1958, Salt Lake City; m. Minerva Young, five children; educator, general superintendent of Church Schools; Sunday School general board member.
