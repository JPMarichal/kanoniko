MORRIS, George Q. (Quayle).

Asst. to the Twelve, Oct. 6, 1951; Apostle, April 8, 1954-April 23, 1962; b. Feb. 20, 1874, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. April 23, 1962, Salt Lake City; m. Emma Ramsey, three children; Young Men general board member, mission president.
