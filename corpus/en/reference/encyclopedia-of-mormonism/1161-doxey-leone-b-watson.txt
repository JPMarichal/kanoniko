DOXEY, Leone B. Watson.

Second counselor to general president LaVern Watts Parmley, Primary, Sept. 10, 1953-April 6, 1962; First counselor, April 6, 1962-Oct. 23, 1969; b. Sept. 3, 1899, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Graham H. Doxey, four children; Primary general board member.
