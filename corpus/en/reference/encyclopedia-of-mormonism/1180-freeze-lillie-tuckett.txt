FREEZE, Lillie Tuckett.

First counselor to general president Louie B. Felt, Primary, Dec. 29, 1905-Oct. 6, 1925; b. Mar. 26, 1855, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Mar. 23, 1937, Salt Lake City; m. James Perry Freeze, four children; Primary and Young Women general board member.
