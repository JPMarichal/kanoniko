YOUNG, Clifford E. (Earle).

Asst. to the Twelve, April 6, 1941-Aug. 21, 1958; b. Dec. 7, 1883, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Aug. 21, 1958, Salt Lake City; m. Edith Grant, four children; businessman, civic leader; stake president.
