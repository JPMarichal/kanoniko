MOYLE, Henry D. (Dinwoodey).

Apostle, April 10, 1947; Second counselor to President David O. McKay, June 12, 1959; First counselor, Oct. 12, 1961-Sept. 18, 1963; b. April 22, 1889, Salt Lake City, Utah; d. Sept. 18, 1963, Deer Park, Florida; m. Clara Alberta Wright, six children; attorney, business executive; stake president.
