WELLS, Daniel H. (Hanmer).

Second counselor to President Brigham Young, Jan. 4, 1857; released at the death of President Young, Aug. 29, 1877; Counselor to the Twelve, Oct. 6, 1877-Mar. 24, 1891; b. Oct. 27, 1814, Trenton, New Jersey; d. Mar. 24, 1891, Salt Lake City, Utah; m. Eliza Rebecca Robinson; practiced plural marriage, thirty-five children on record; public servant, educator, farmer; mission president.
