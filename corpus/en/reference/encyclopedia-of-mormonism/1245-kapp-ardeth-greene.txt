KAPP, Ardeth Greene.

Second counselor to general president Ruth H. Funk, Young Women, Nov. 9, 1972-July 12, 1978; general president, Young Women, April 7, 1984; b. Mar. 19, 1931, Glenwood, Alberta, Canada; m. Heber B. Kapp; educator; Church Correlation Committee member.
