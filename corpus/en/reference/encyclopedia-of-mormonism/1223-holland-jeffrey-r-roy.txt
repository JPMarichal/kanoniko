Daniel H. Ludlow, Encyclopedia of Mormonism 4 vols. (New York: Macmillan Publishing Company, 1992)

HOLLAND, Jeffrey R. (Roy).

Seventy, April 7, 1989; b. Dec. 3, 1940; m. Patricia Terry, three children; educator, President of BYU and former Commissioner of Church Education; regional representative.

HOLLAND, Patricia Terry. First counselor to general president Ardeth G. Kapp, Young Women, May 11, 1984-April 6, 1986; b. Feb. 16, 1942, St. George, Utah; m. Jeffrey R. Holland, three children; homemaker; Young Women general board member.
