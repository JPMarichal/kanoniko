of the patriarchs Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph (Gen. 12–50). Those stories,

16—Num. 21), and their arrival at the entrance to Canaan, the Land of Promise

(Num. 22—Deut. 34).

The first book of the Pentateuch, Genesis, covers the vast period of time from

creation to the journey into Egypt. The remaining four books, Exodus—Deuteron-

omy, cover a period of only about forty years.
