Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Flintshire Conference

FLINTSHIRE CONFERENCE, Wales, British Mission, organized July 29, 1849, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in Flintshire (bordering on the Irish Sea) in North Wales. On March 25, 1866, Flintshire Conference was discontinued to become a part of the North Wales Conference.
