Pembrokeshire North Conference

PEMBROKESHIRE NORTH CONFERENCE, Wales, British Mission, was created July 6, 1851, by the organization of the northern part of Pembrokeshire Conference into a separate conference, named the Pembrokeshire North Conference. On Dec. 29, 1855, the conference was merged into the Pembrokeshire Conference.
