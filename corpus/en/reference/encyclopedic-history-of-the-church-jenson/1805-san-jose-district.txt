Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

San Jose District

SAN JOSE DISTRICT, of the California Mission, embraced in 1930 Santa Clara County and parts of San Mateo, Santa Cruz, San Benito and Monterey counties, California, with headquarters at San Jose. The district includes three organized branches of the Church, namely, Monterey, Palo Alto and San Jose. On Dec. 31, 1930, there were in Palo Alto Branch 174 members, including 60 children, but the membership of the Monterey and San Jose branches was not reported.
