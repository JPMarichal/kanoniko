Cardiganshire Conference

CARDIGANSHIRE CONFERENCE, Wales, British Mission, organized July 29, 1849, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in Cardiganshire (bordering on Cardigan Bay), Wales. This conference was discontinued to become a part of the Llanelly Conference in 1861.
