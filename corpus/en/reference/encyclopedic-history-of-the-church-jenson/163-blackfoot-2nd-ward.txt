Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Blackfoot 2nd Ward

BLACKFOOT 2ND WARD, Blackfoot Stake, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of the city of Blackfoot lying east of the Oregon Short Line Railroad tracks. The meeting house, a brick building, is located on the corner of Shilling Avenue and Court Street.

When Blackfoot Ward was divided into two wards May 9, 1909, namely, the Blackfoot 1st Ward and the Blackfoot 2nd Ward, Lorenzo R. Thomas was chosen as Bishop of the Blackfoot 2nd Ward. He acted in that capacity until 1917, when he was succeeded by Oscar L. Rider, who was succeeded in 1924 by William H. Snyder, who acted as Bishop Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the Blackfoot 2nd Ward had 623 members, including 287 children.
