Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Belfast Conference

BELFAST CONFERENCE, Ireland, British Mission, was organized in 1848 and comprised the counties of Down and Antrim and other localities in the north of Ireland. This part of Ireland, known as Ulster, is inhabited largely by Protestants. Belfast Conference was discontinued in 1867 and later the whole of Ireland was known as the Irish Conference of the British Mission.
