Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Froome's Hill Conference

FROOME'S HILL CONFERENCE, British Mission, Herefordshire, England, organized June 21, 1840, consisted of former members of the United Brethren congregation who had been converted to the truth of the gospel largely through the efforts of Apostle Wilford Woodruff. The name of this conference was changed to Mars Hill in 1844.
