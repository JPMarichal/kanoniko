Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Dundee Conference

DUNDEE CONFERENCE, British Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in Dundee, Arbroath, Aberdeen, Perth and Inverness in Scotland, and these branches of the Church were detached from the Edinburgh Conference Dec. 8, 1850, to form a new division to be called the Dundee Conference. This conference existed until June 20, 1868, when the district contained therein was transferred back to the Edinburgh Conference.
