Derbyshire Conference

DERBYSHIRE CONFERENCE, British Mission. In 1843 the Latter-day Saints residing in the southern part of Derbyshire, England, were organized as the Wooden Box Conference, with headquarters in the town of Wooden Box. But as other branches in the county were raised up the name of the Wooden Box Conference was changed in 1845 to Derbyshire Conference, which existed until 1867, when its membership was transferred to the Leicestershire Conference.
