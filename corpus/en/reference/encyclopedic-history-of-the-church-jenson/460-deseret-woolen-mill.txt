Deseret Woolen Mill

DESERET WOOLEN MILL (The) was erected by Pres. Brigham Young near the mouth of Parley's Canyon about 1860. In 1867 the Wasatch Woollen Mill was erected by Abraham O. Smoot about a quarter of a mile below the Deseret Mill and later for several years the two mills were operated by the same management. As part of the exhibit of the Deseret Woollen Mill at the State Fair in Salt Lake City in 1869 were twenty bolts of fine cloth, suitable for men's suits.

This mill continued to manufacture cloth, and in 1893, when Don Carlos Young, a son of the late Pres. Brigham Young, was going on a mission, James Cummings, manager of the Deseret Mills, presented him with two fine suits of clothes made from the hair of goats raised by the mill operators in the vicinity of the mill site. After standing idle for some time the Deseret Woollen Mill was sold and the machinery moved to Idaho.
