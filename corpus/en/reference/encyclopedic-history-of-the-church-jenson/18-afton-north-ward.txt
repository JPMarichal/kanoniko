Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Afton North Ward

AFTON NORTH WARD, Star Valley Stake, consists of the north half of the town of Afton, and was organized June 22, 1919, with Franklin R. Gardner as Bishop. He still presided over the ward Dec. 31, 1930. Afton North Ward, among many other improvements, can boast of a fine meeting house. An elevated cove at the mouth of the canyon, according to the views of many of the local saints, would be an ideal place for the erection of a temple. On Dec. 31, 1930, the membership of the Afton North Ward was 529, including 108 children.
