Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Richardson's Point

RICHARDSON'S POINT in Van Buren County, Iowa, is the place where Pres. Brigham Young made his headquarters from March 7th to 20th, 1846, when traveling westward through Iowa en route for the Rocky Mountains. Richardson's Point, thus named in honor of an early Iowa pioneer, is on a branch of Chequest Creek. It was in 1846 a timbered point about a mile northwest of the present town of Lebanon, seven miles west of Keosauqua, the county seat of Van Buren County, and twenty-five miles northwest of Farmington. Chequest Creek rises in a north and south branch in Davis County, Iowa, takes an easterly course, and empties in the Des Moines River at Pittsburgh, Van Buren County.
