Provo 5th Ward (No. 1)

PROVO 5TH WARD (No. 1), Utah Stake, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in a farming district lying north of the Provo city survey and extending eastward to the mountains and westward to the Provo River.

Provo 5th Ward was created in 1852 when four other wards, numbered respectively Provo 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th, were organized. William Fausett was set apart as Bishop of the Provo 5th Ward and held that position about a year only, as in 1853 the district was vacated on account of Indian troubles. Bishop Fausett, in 1854, succeeded William M. Wall as Bishop of Provo 4th Ward. (See history of Pleasant View Ward, Sharon Stake.)
