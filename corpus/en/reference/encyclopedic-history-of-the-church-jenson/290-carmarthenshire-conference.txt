Carmarthenshire Conference

CARMARTHENSHIRE CONFERENCE, South Wales, British Mission, organized July 29, 1849, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in Carmarthenshire (bordering on the Bristol Channel), Wales. In 1868 this conference was discontinued to become a part of the Glamorgan Conference.
