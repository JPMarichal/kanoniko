Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Independence Conference

INDEPENDENCE CONFERENCE, or District, of the Central States Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the western part of the state of Missouri, and contains five branches, namely, Independence, Kansas City, St. Joseph and Sedalia in Missouri, and Kansas City, Kansas, (across the river from Kansas City, Mo.) The Independence Conference had a total Church population of 1,099, including 159 children, in 1930.
