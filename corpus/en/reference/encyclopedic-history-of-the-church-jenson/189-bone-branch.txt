Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Bone Branch

BONE BRANCH, Idaho Falls Stake, Bonneville Co., Idaho, consists of Latter-day Saints residing in a dry farm district lying 20 miles southeast of Idaho Falls, near the road leading to Grays Lake. It was once known as Canyon Creek Branch of the Ozone Ward. The saints in that locality were organized as the Canyon Creek Branch August 30, 1914. Bryant H. Rockwood acted as presiding Elder of the branch as early as 1925, and presided Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the branch had a Church membership of 79, including 24 children. Services are held in the local school house.
