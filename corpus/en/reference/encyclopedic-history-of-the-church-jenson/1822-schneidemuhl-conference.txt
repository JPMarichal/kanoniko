Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Schneidemuhl Conference

SCHNEIDEMUHL CONFERENCE, or District, of the German-Austrian Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the town of Schneidemuhl, located on the boundary line between Germany and Poland, about 200 miles northeast of Berlin. On Dec. 31, 1930, this district had a total membership of 300, including 72 children. There are six branches in the district, namely, Driesen, Kolberg, Kreuz, Schneidemuhl, Schoenlake and Stolp.
