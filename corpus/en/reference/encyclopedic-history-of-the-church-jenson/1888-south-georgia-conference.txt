Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

South Georgia Conference

SOUTH GEORGIA CONFERENCE, or District, of the Southern States Mission, comprises the southern part of the state of Georgia, with branches of the Church at Axon, Quitman and Waycross. Besides these branches, L. D. S. Sunday schools are in operation at Camilia, Hazlehurst, Kingsland, Ochlochnee and Willacoochee. The South Georgia District on Dec. 31, 1930, had a total membership of 1,686, including 294 children.
