Basel Conference

BASEL CONFERENCE, or District, of the Swiss-German Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the city of Basel, Switzerland, and German and French vicinity. The total membership of the conference Dec. 31, 1930, was 251, including 29 children. Basel is a city of 149,300 inhabitants situated on the River Rhine, bordering on Germany. There are four branches in the district, namely, Basel, Switzerland; Freiburg and Muhlhausen, Germany, and Strassburg in the French department Bas-Rhin.
