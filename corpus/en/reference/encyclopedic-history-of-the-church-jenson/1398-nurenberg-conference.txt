Nurenberg Conference

NURENBERG CONFERENCE, or District, of the Swiss-German Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the city of Nurenberg and vicinity in Bavaria, Germany. Nurenburg is an ancient city containing the famous Nurenberg Castle and was once a strongly fortified city. It had a population of about 400,000. The Nurenberg District had a Church membership in 1930 of 331, including 42 children. The district contains four branches, namely, Furth, Koburg, Nurenberg, and Regensburg.
