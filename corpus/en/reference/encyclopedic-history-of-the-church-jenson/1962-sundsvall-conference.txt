Sundsvall Conference

SUNDSVALL CONFERENCE, Swedish Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the northern provinces of Sweden. It was organized in June, 1859. From April, 1865, it had a president in common with that of the Stockholm Conference, of which it soon afterwards became a part. It has also been known as the Norrland Conference.
