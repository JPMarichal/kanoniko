Lost River Ward

LOST RIVER WARD, Lost River Stake, Butte Co., Idaho, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in a farming district lying between Arco on the south and Moore on the north. The center of the ward, where the meeting house stands, is in Section 9, Township 4 north, Range 26 east, Boise Meridian, five miles south of Moore.

The original Lost River Branch comprised all the saints residing in Lost River Valley, preceding the organization of the Moore Ward (see Moore Ward) but on March 27, 1920, a new ward called the Lost River Ward was organized by taking certain families of saints from the Moore Ward on the north and other families from the Arco Ward on the south and organizing them as a separate ward named Lost River Ward, with Henry N.
