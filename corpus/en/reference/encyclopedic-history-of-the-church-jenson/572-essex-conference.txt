Essex Conference

ESSEX CONFERENCE, British Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the county of Essex, England, bordering on the southeast coast of England. The conference commenced to function January 1, 1852, but on June 19, 1869, it was dissolved and its territory annexed to the London Conference.
