Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Otago Conference

OTAGO CONFERENCE, or District, of the New Zealand Mission, embraces the south end of the South Island of New Zealand with headquarters in the city of Invercargill. The northern boundary of the conference is the boundary line between Otago and Canterbury provincial districts. Otago Conference had a Church membership of 54 at the close of 1930. The conference was organized Oct. 22, 1892.
