Isle of Man Conference

ISLE OF MAN CONFERENCE, British Mission, was organized June 1, 1846, and comprised the Isle of Man lying in the Irish Sea, about midway between the north of England and Ireland. This conference continued until June 21, 1856, when the Isle of Man was annexed to the Liverpool Conference.
