Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Fairport

FAIRPORT, in Lake County, Ohio, situated three miles northeast of Painesville at the mouth of Grand River, dates hack to 1812. Fairport is mentioned frequently in Church history as a port on Lake Erie from which many of the early Elders of the Church set out for their missions and at which they frequently landed on their return.
