Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

West Colorado Conference

WEST COLORADO CONFERENCE, or District, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the western part of Colorado, not included in the Young Stake. In 1930 the total membership of the Church in the district was 926, including 222 children. There were branches of the Church at Grand Junction, Montrose, and Somerset, and Sunday school organizations at Glenwood Springs and Meeker.
