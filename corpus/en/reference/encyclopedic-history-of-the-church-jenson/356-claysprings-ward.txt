Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Claysprings Ward

CLAYSPRINGS WARD, Snowflake Stake, Navajo Co., Arizona, consists of Latter-day Saints living in a farming district 4 1/2 miles west northwest of Pinedale and 22 miles southwest of Snowflake, the stake headquarters.

Claysprings Ward is an outgrowth of Pinedale and Taylor wards, and the saints, who had settled on the edge of the timber on the north slope of the Mogollon Mountains, were organized May 21, 1916, as the Walker Branch with Reuben J. Perkins as presiding Elder, The branch held meetings in the Walker school house and later in the Woodrow school house about 3 1/2 miles southeast of the Walker school. Pres. Perkins died Sept. 7, 1919, and the branch was organized as an independent branch with Lorenzo Standifird as presiding Elder. He was succeeded in 1921 by William Ammon Hunt. On May 7, 1922, this branch became the Claysprings Ward with Wm. A Hunt as Bishop. He acted Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the ward had 95 members, including 29 children.
