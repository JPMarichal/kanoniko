Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Pinetop

PINETOP, Snowflake Stake, Navajo Co., Arizona, consisted of a few saints residing near the top of the Mogollon Mountains, about ten miles southeast of Showlow, and 30 miles southeast of Snowflake, the stake headquarters. A post office was established in 1891, and meetings and Sunday schools were held in a somewhat irregular manner. A branch organization was also effected in 1891 with John Hall as presiding Elder. Brother Hall died in 1899 and there has been no branch organization in Pinetop since.
