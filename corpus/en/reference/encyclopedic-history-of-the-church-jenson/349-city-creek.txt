City Creek

CITY CREEK is the name of a mountain stream in Salt Lake County, Utah, which supplies water for culinary purposes to Salt Lake City. The stream rises near the summit of the Wasatch Mountains, runs in a southwesterly direction and issues from the the mouth of City Creek Canyon into the heart of the city. Originally its waters emptied through two natural channels into the Jordan River, but its surplus water is now conveyed to said river through an aqueduct built in the center of North Temple Street.
