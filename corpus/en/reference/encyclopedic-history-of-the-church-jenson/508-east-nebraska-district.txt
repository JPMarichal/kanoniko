Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

East Nebraska District

EAST NEBRASKA DISTRICT, or Conference, constitutes a part of the Western States Mission and the Latter-day Saints residing in the east part of the state of Nebraska, and in Council Bluffs and vicinity in Iowa. The district had, in 1930, a total membership of 759, including 105 children. There are branches of the Church in Lincoln and Omaha (Nebraska), and Council Bluffs (Iowa), and Sunday schools function regularly at Norfolk and Falls City, Neb. The saints at Council Bluffs and Omaha own their own chapels.
