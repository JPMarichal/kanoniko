Christiania Conference

CHRISTIANIA CONFERENCE, or District, (originally called Brevik and now Oslo) consists (1930) of the Latter-day Saints residing in the southeast part of Norway and comprises six organized branches of the Church. The total membership of the Church in the Christiania District Dec. 31, 1930, was 878, including one Seventy, 42 Elders, 16 Priests, 28 Teachers, 14 Deacons, 674 lay members and 103 children.
