Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Preston Conference

PRESTON CONFERENCE, England, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in Preston, and nearby towns, in Lancashire, England. The actual date of the organization of the conference is not known, but at a conference held in Manchester Oct. 6, 1840, the Preston Conference is referred to as having a membership of 665, including 18 Elders, 23 Priests, 11 Teachers, and 2 Deacons. In 1868 Preston Conference became part of the Liverpool Conference.
