Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Taranaki Conference

TARANAKI CONFERENCE, or District, of the New Zealand Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the provincial district of Taranaki, lying on the west coast of the North Island, extending inland from the seaport of New Plymouth. The total Church membership of the Taranaki District in 1930 was 227, including 59 children. The Taranaki District dates back to 1903.
