Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Tubuai Conference

TUBUAI CONFERENCE, or District, of the Society Islands Mission, consists of the Austral Islands, with headquarters on the island of Tubuai, which island is situated in the South Pacific Ocean, about 350 miles south of Tahiti. At the close of 1930 there were 206 Latter-day Saints in the Tubuai District, including 76 children.

It was on Tubuai that the first L. D. S. Elders called to open up a mission on the Pacific Islands first landed in 1844, and where they organized the first branch of the Church that year.
