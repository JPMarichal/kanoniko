Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Denver Conference

DENVER CONFERENCE, or District, of the Western States Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the city of Denver, Colo., and vicinity, extending northward to the Wyoming line, including Fort Collins (where there is a branch of the Church), and southward to Englewood (where there is another branch of the Church).

In the city of Denver, where the headquarters of the Western States Mission are located, there is a handsome chapel and home for the missionaries. The total Church population of the Denver Conference Dec. 31, 1930, was 1,406, including 191 children. According to the U. S. census Denver had 287,861 inhabitants in 1930.
