Zwickau Conference

ZWICKAU CONFERENCE, or District, German-Austrian Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the town of Zwickau, Germany, and vicinity. Zwickau is in Saxony about 70 miles southwest of Dresden. On Dec. 31, 1930, Zwickau District had a membership of 469, including 65 children. The district has five branches namely, Planitz, Schwarzenburg, Werdau, Wilkau and Zwickau.
