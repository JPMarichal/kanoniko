Carson County

CARSON COUNTY, one of the counties of the Territory of Utah, was designated and its boundaries defined by an act approved Jan. 5, 1854, as follows:

"Be it enacted by the Governor and Legislative Assembly of the Territory of Utah: That all that portion of country bounded north by Deseret County, east by the parallel of longitude 118 deg. south by the boundary line of this Territory and west by California, is hereby included within the limits of Carson County." (Laws of Utah.)

In 1861 the area included in Carson County became part of the newly organized territory of Nevada.
