Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Bluemesa Branch

BLUEMESA BRANCH, Duchesne Stake, Duchesne Co., Utah, consisted of a farming district belonging to the Knight Investment Company, which owned a large tract of land near Duchesne City, and among the employees of the company were a number of Latter-day Saints, who were organized into a branch of the Church in 1916 with Bryan H. Jolley, jun., as presiding Elder. This branch only existed one summer (1916), since which the Latter-day Saints at Knight's camp have belonged to the Duchesne Ward. The buildings of the Knight Investment Company are about five miles northeast of Duchesne City.
