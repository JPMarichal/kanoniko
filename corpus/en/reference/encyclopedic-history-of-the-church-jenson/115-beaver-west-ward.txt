Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Beaver West Ward

BEAVER WEST WARD, Beaver Stake, consists of Latter-day Saints residing in the west part of Beaver City and came into existence Jan. 22, 1905, when the city of Beaver was divided a second time into two wards, namely, the Beaver East and the Beaver West wards. Samuel O. White, jun., was chosen as Bishop of the Beaver West Ward. He was succeeded in 1908 by Charles D. Farnsworth, who in 1918 was succeeded by George Paice, who in 1925 was succeeded by Wesley W. Farrer, who presided Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the Beaver West Ward had 852 members, including 130 children.
