Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Black Hills Conference

BLACK HILLS CONFERENCE, or District, of the Western States Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in parts of South Dakota and Wyoming, with an organized branch of the Church at Belle Fourche, South Dakota, and Sunday schools at Chadton, Neb., Rapid City, South Dakota, and Sheridan, Wyoming. The total Church population in the Black Hills Conference or District in 1930 was 129, including 64 children.
