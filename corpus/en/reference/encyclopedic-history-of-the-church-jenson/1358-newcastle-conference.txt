Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Newcastle Conference

NEWCASTLE CONFERENCE, or District, of the British Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the extreme northeast part of England, with headquarters at Sunderland. Newcastle Conference was organized in 1848 and has had a continued existence ever since. On Dec. 31, 1930, the total membership of the conference was 800, including 86 children.
