Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Erie Conference

ERIE CONFERENCE, or District, of the Eastern States Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the northwest part of Pennsylvania, and the southwest part of the state of New York, in the attractive country bordering on Lake Erie. There are two organized branches of the Church in the district, namely, one at Erie (Pa.), and another at Jamestown (N. Y.) The total membership of the Church in the district Dec. 31, 1930, was 127, including 21 children.
