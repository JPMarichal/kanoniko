Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Belvedere Ward

BELVEDERE WARD, Los Angeles Stake, Los Angeles Co., Cal., consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of the city of Los Angeles lying east of the Los Angeles River and bounded on the north by the Alhambra Boulevard, on the east by Montebello, a suburb of Los Angeles, and on the south by 18th St., and part of the way also by the Los Angeles River. The headquarters of the ward in May, 1928, were at 712 Macdonald St., where there was a L. D. S. chapel accommodating about 350 people.

Belvedere Ward was organized Aug. 26, 1923, with David P. Cheney as Bishop. He acted Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the Church membership of the ward was 500 souls, including 143 children.
