Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Malad 2nd Ward

MALAD 2ND WARD, Malad Stake, Idaho, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the south part of Malad City, and adjoining country district. William H. Thomas was chosen as Bishop of the Malad 2nd Ward, which was organized May 26, 1912, when the original Malad Ward was divided into two wards, namely, Malad 1st and Malad 2nd wards. Bishop Thomas presided until 1928, when he was succeeded by Lorin L. Richards, who in 1929 was succeeded by Hyrum J. Hansen, who presided Dec. 31, 1930, when the Malad 2nd Ward had a membership of 966, including 195 children.
