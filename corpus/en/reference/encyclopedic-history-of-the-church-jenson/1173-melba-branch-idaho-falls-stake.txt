Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Melba Branch, Idaho Falls Stake

MELBA BRANCH, Idaho Falls Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, consisted of a few families of Latter-day Saints residing in what is now Jefferson County, about 25 miles northeast of Idaho Falls. A branch of the Church was organized in that locality in 1912 and was also known as the Heise Branch, with Thomas H. Clifford as president. This branch organization, however, only continued one year.
