Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Tahitian Conference

TAHITIAN CONFERENCE, or District, of the Society Islands Mission, consisted in 1930 of the Society Islands proper, the largest of which are Tahiti and Morea, with headquarters at Papeete, the capital of the islands, which is also the headquarters of the Society Islands Mission. The total membership of the Tahitian District Dec. 31, 1930, was 133, including 24 children.
