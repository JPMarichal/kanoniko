Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

South Texas Conference

SOUTH TEXAS CONFERENCE, or District, of the Central States Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the southern part of the state of Texas. The district includes five organized branches of the Church, namely, Emilee, Freeport, Houston, Jozye and Williamson, with a total Church membership, including scattered members, of 1,294, of whom 267 are children.
