Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

New Portage

NEW PORTAGE, Summit Co., Ohio, was a small town situated about 25 miles southwest of Kirtland, Ohio. Zion's Camp was organized at New Portage in 1834, and from that town commenced its march to Missouri May 5, 1834. The original town of New Portage now constitutes a part of Barberton, which is situated six miles southwest of Akron, Ohio.

The gospel was first preached in New Portage in 1831 by Reynolds Cahoon, David Whitmer and Lyman E. Johnson. Later, Thomas B. Marsh and Sidney Rigdon preached in the same neighborhood. A branch of the Church, organized there as early as 1834, had upwards of 60 members at the time of its organization. For some time the New Portage Branch ranked as one of the flourishing branches of the Church in Ohio.
