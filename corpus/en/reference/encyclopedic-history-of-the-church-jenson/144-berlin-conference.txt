Berlin Conference

BERLIN CONFERENCE, or District, of the German-Austrian Mission, consists of Latter-day Saints residing in and near the city of Berlin, the capital of Germany. On Dec. 31, 1930, the Berlin District had a total membership of 1082, including 128 children. The district contained eight branches, namely, Berlin-Maobit, East Berlin, Berlin-Schoneberg, Berlin-Spandau, Central Berlin, Brandenburg, Eberswalde and Rathenow.
