Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Mesilla Branch

MESILLA BRANCH, St. Joseph Stake, Donovana Co., New Mexico, consisted of Latter-day Saints (mostly Edmund Z. Carbine and family with relatives) who located at Mesilla Park, on the Santa Fe Railroad about 43 miles north of El Paso, Texas. Meetings and Sunday school sessions were held in private houses and occasionally in the district school house. Edmund Z. Carbine was the first president of the branch. He died Dec. 26, 1926, at Mesilla, and Franklin James Graham succeeded him as presiding Elder. He presided until the fall of 1929, when the branch was disorganized because all the saints except one family (Joseph L. Carbine and family) moved away. This family still resided at Mesilla in 1930, and belonged to the El Paso Ward.
