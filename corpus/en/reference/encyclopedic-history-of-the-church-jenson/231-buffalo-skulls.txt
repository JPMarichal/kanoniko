Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Buffalo Skulls

BUFFALO SKULLS, sometimes jokingly called the "post offices of the plains," were used by L. D. S. emigrants and others upon which to write information about the progress of their journeys for the benefit of trains following. These skulls, bleached white by the sun, were placed in conspicuous places along the route so as to draw the attention of later travelers.
