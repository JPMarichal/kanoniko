Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Montpelier 4th Ward

MONTPELIER 4TH WARD consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the southwest part of the city of Montpelier and is bounded on the north by Main St. or Washington Avenue, and east by 7th Street. South and west it extends to the Montpelier city limits.

The Montpelier 4th Ward came into existence Oct. 22, 1922, when the Montpelier 2nd Ward was divided, and the west part of the same organized as the Montpelier 4th Ward with John W. Jones as Bishop. He was succeeded in 1929 by John J. Sarbach, who presided Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the ward had 286 members, including 62 children.
