Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Vienna Conference

VIENNA CONFERENCE, or District, German-Austrian Mission, consists of Latter-day Saints residing in the great city of Vienna, the capital of Austria, and vicinity. The district on Dec. 31, 1930, contained 261 members, including 37 children. There are four branches in the district, namely, Hagg, Linz, Salzburg and Vienna.
