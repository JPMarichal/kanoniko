Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Macclesfield Conference

MACCLESFIELD CONFERENCE, British Mission, organized Dec. 29, 1840, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the important town of Macclesfield, Cheshire, England, and vicinity, and included besides Macclesfield, the towns of Bollington, Sutton, and others, and soon afterwards those which had belonged to the Altrincham Conference. In 1849 the Macclesfield Conference became part of the Staffordshire Conference.
