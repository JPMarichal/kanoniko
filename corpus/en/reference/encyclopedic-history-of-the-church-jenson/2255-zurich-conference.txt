Zurich Conference

ZURICH CONFERENCE, or District, of the Swiss-German Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the northeast part of Switzerland and surrounding country. Zurich is beautifully situated on Lake Zurich near the foot of the majestic Alps, and has about 235,000 inhabitants. The Zurich District had a total membership in 1930 of 599, including 75 children. There are eight branches in the district, namely, Ennenda, Herisau, Lucerne, St. Gallen, Uster, Wadenswil, Winterthur and Zurich.
