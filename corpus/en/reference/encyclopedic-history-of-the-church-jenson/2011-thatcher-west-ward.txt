Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Thatcher West Ward

THATCHER WEST WARD, St Joseph Stake, Graham Co., Arizona, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the west part of the town of Thatcher.

When the Thatcher West Ward was organized in 1919, Ernest H. Shumway was chosen as Bishop of that ward. He was succeeded in 1922 by Orson P. Skousen, who in 1927 was succeeded by Alma P. Tate, who acted until 1929, when the ward was amalgamated with the Thatcher East Ward to become part of the Thatcher Ward.
