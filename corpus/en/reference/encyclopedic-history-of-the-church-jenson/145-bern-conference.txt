Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Bern Conference

BERN CONFERENCE, or District, of the Swiss-German Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the city of Bern, Switzerland and vicinity. Bern is the capital of Switzerland and has a population of about 112,000. At the close of 1930 Bern District had a membership of 576, including 85 children. The district contains seven branches, namely, Bern Biel, Burgdorf, Interlaken, Thun, Langnau and Solothurn.
