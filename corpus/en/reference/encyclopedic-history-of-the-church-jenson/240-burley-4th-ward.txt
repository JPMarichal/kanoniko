Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Burley 4th Ward

BURLEY 4TH WARD, Burley Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the extreme south part of Burley and was organized from the south part of Burley 2nd Ward, Feb. 28, 1921, with William L. Waite as Bishop. The ward was disorganized May 11, 1924, and the membership transferred to the Burley 2nd Ward.
