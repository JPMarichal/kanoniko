Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Virginia Conference

VIRGINIA CONFERENCE, or District, of the Eastern States Mission, embraces the Latter-day Saints residing in the state of Virginia, which on Dec. 31, 1930, had a total membership of 2,267, including 325 children. There were four branches of the Church in the state, namely, Danville, Norfolk, Richmond and Roanoke, and, besides, Sunday schools at Mt. Lake, Petersburg, Portsmouth, Scholfield, Vinten and Oilville.
