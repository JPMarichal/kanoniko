Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Benton

BENTON, Wyoming, was the name of a temporary village, situated on the South Platte River about 12 miles east of Rawlings, Wyoming. Benton was near the terminus of the Union Pacific Railroad in 1868 when a number of Church trains bringing emigrants to Utah commenced their journey with teams to Salt Lake City from Benton.
