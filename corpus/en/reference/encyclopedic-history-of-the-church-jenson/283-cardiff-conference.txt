Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Cardiff Conference

CARDIFF CONFERENCE, Wales, British Mission, organized in 1858, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the city of Cardiff, located on the south coast of Glamorganshire, Wales, bordering on the Bristol Channel. The conference was absorbed by the Glamorganshire Conference by action of a council held at Cardiff, Jan. 8, 1865.
