Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Heber East Ward

HEBER EAST WARD, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing east of Main St. It came into existence July 15, 1877, when Heber City was divided into two wards, namely, the Heber East and the Heber West wards, and Thomas Rasband was chosen as Bishop of the Heber East Ward. He died July 24, 1884, and was succeeded Nov. 2, 1884, by Robert S. Duke, who presided until Feb. 8, 1903, when Heber City was divided into three wards, namely, the Heber 1st, 2nd and 3rd wards.
