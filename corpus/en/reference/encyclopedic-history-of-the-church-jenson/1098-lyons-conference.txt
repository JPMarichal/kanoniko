Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Lyons Conference

LYONS CONFERENCE, or District, of the French Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the city of Lyons in the Department of Rhone, France, and vicinity. The total Church membership of the conference on Dec. 31, 1930, was 32 souls.
