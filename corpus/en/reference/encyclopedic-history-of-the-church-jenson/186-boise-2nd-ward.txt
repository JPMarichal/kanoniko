Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Boise 2nd Ward

BOISE 2ND WARD, Boise Stake, Idaho, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the west part of Boise City. The ward had no meeting house of its own in 1930, but held meetings and Sunday school sessions in the stake tabernacle, which is situated on the corners of Ninth and Washington streets.

The Boise 2nd Ward came into existence March 4, 1923, when the Boise Ward was divided into two wards, viz., the Boise 1st Ward and the Boise 2nd Ward. Joseph Salisbury was chosen as Bishop of the Boise 2nd Ward; he was succeeded later in 1923 by Joseph Emer Harris, who presided Dec. 31, 1930, when the membership of the Boise 2nd Ward was 849, including 214 children.
