Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Middleton

MIDDLETON, St. George Stake, Washington Co., Utah, is a little settlement situated about three miles east of Saint George and about two miles west of Washington. The saints there belong to the Saint George East Ward. Middleton was first settled in the spring of 1863, and the few saints organized there as a branch, with Henry Bryan Manning Jolley as presiding Elder. This, however, turned out to be only a temporary branch organization.
