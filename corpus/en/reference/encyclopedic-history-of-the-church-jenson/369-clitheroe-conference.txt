Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Clitheroe Conference

CLITHEROE CONFERENCE, British Mission, consisted of the branches of the Church in Clitheroe, Chatburn, Downham, Chaigley, Grindleton, Whitmore, Burnley, Blackburn, Ribchester and Thornley in Lancashire, England, which were organized as a conference Oct. 6, 1840, with Thomas Smith as president. In 1850 the Clitheroe Conference became part of the Preston Conference.
