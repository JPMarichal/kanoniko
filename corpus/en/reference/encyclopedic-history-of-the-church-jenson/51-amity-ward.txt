Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Amity Ward

AMITY WARD, St. Johns Stake, Apache Co., Arizona, consisted of Latter-day Saints residing in the upper end of Round Valley. The center of the ward was about three miles southwest of the present village of Eager. The Amity Ward came into existence Oct. 29, 1882, when the Round Valley Ward was divided into two wards, namely, the Amity Ward and the Omer Ward. Almon Nahum Holden was chosen as Bishop of the Amity Ward. He presided until July 4, 1886, when the Amity and Omer wards were amalgamated and organized into the Union Ward to embrace all the saints residing in Round Valley.
