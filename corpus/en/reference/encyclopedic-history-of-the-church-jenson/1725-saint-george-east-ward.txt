Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Saint George East Ward

SAINT GEORGE EAST WARD, St. George Stake, Washington Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the east part of St. George.

This ward came into existence Dec. 14, 1902, when the St. George Ward was divided into two wards, namely, the St. George East Ward with Isaac C. Macfarlane as Bishop, and the St. George West Ward with James McArthur as Bishop, Bishop Macfarlane was succeeded in 1921 by Franklin G. Miles, who in 1925 was succeeded by Karl N. Snow, who on Sept. 20, 1930, was succeeded by Wilford J. Reichman, who presided Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the St. George East Ward had 675 members, including 143 children.
