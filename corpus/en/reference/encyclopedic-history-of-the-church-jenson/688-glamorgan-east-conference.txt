Glamorgan East Conference

GLAMORGAN EAST CONFERENCE, in South Wales, British Mission, was created July 29, 1849, by the division of the Glamorgan Conference. On Jan. 28, 1865, the conference was discontinued to again become a part of the Glamorgan Conference.
