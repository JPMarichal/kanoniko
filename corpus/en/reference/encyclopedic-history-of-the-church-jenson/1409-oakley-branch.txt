Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Oakley Branch

OAKLEY BRANCH, Woodruff Stake, Lincoln Co., Wyoming, consisted of a few families of saints residing in the coal-mining town of Oakley, situated on Hams Fork (a small river), 1 1/2 miles southeast of Diamondville. The few families of saints who had located at Oakley were organized as a branch of the Church June 17, 1906, with John L. Jensen as presiding Elder. This branch organization was discontinued a few years later, and its membership transferred to the Diamondville Ward, where they belonged Dec. 31, 1930. On that date there was a Sunday school organization at Oakley, where about 25 families of saints belonging to the Diamondville Ward still resided. Mining operations continue to function at Oakley.
