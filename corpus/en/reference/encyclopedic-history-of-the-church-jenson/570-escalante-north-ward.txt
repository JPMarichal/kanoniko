Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Escalante North Ward

ESCALANTE NORTH WARD, Garfield Stake, Garfield Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of the town of Escalante lying north of Main Street, or the street running east and west south of the Amusement Hall, which is owned by the ward and in which meetings and Sunday school sessions are held. The ward also includes some scattered ranches up Escalante Creek.

From 1875 to 1921 all the saints in Escalante constituted one bishop's ward, but on Aug. 28, 1921, the ward was divided into two wards, namely, the Escalante North Ward and the Escalante South Ward. Leslie George was chosen as Bishop of the Escalante North Ward. He was succeeded Feb. 12, 1929, by Lorenzo Heber Griffin, who presided Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the Escalante North Ward had 488 members, including 135 children.
