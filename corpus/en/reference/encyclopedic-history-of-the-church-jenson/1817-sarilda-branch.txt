Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Sarilda Branch

SARILDA BRANCH, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, consisted of a few Latter-day Saints living on Sand Creek, adjacent to Ora. The school house in which meetings and Sunday school sessions were held was situated at a point where Sand Creek emerges from the sand hills about three miles southwest of the Ora meeting house. A few of the farmers in the district irrigated their land from Sand Creek but most of them endeavored to operate dry farms. The few remaining members of the Church now residing in the district belong to the Ora Ward.
