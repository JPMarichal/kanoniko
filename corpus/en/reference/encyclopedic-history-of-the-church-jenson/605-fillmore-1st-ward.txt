Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Fillmore 1st Ward

FILLMORE 1ST WARD, Millard Stake, Millard Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the south part of the city of Fillmore. The ward owns a fine meeting house, a brick building, erected in 1926 and 1927 at a cost of $37,000, which contains an auditorium with a seating capacity of 200 and an amusement hall which can accommodate 500 people.

When Fillmore Ward was divided into two wards Nov. 14, 1920, the Fillmore 1st and the Fillmore 2nd Ward, Parker Pratt Robison was chosen Bishop of the Fillmore 1st Ward, which position he held until 1928, when, having been called into the stake presidency, he was succeeded by Burton K. Farnsworth, who acted as Bishop Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the ward had a membership of 696, including 131 children.
