Bay of Islands Conference

BAY OF ISLANDS CONFERENCE, or District, of the New Zealand Mission, consists of the extreme north end of the North Island of New Zealand, and had a total Church membership of 819 in 1930, including 196 children. This conference was first organized Oct. 15, 1887.
