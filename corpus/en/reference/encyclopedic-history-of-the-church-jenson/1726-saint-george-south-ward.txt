Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Saint George South Ward

SAINT GEORGE SOUTH WARD, St. George Stake, Washington Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the south part of the city of St. George.

At a meeting held Sept. 13, 1925, a new ward named the St. George South Ward was organized from parts of the St. George East and the St. George West wards, with Frank Godbe Miles as Bishop. He presided Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the St. George South Ward had 740 members, including 142 children.
