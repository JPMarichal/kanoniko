Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Summit Stake Academy

SUMMIT STAKE ACADEMY, Summit Co., Utah, was established under the direction of the presidency of the Summit Stake in 1906. A fine two-story building, with basement in which mechanic and domestic arts were taught, was erected for the use of the school, which offered, in addition to a full four-year high school course, a preparatory school for 8th grade students. The theology, history and ethics of the Church were stressed. On the fine school campus track work and other athletic sports were featured. The school was closed in 1913 and a L. D. S. theological seminary established near the Coalville high school instead. William Zimmerman Terry, B. S., acted as principal during the entire term of the school's existence.
