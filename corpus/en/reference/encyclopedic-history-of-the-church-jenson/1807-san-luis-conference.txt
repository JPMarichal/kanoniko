Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

San Luis Conference

SAN LUIS CONFERENCE, or District, of the Western States Mission, consists of Latter-day Saints residing in the San Luis Valley, Colorado, in which also the San Luis Stake of Zion is located. The total membership in the district Dec. 31, 1930, was 831, including 200 children. There is a branch of the Church at Alamosa and L. D. S. Sunday schools are operating at Fox Creek and Lake View.
