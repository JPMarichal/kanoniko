Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Southern Indiana Conference

SOUTHERN INDIANA CONFERENCE, or District, of the Northern States Mission, at the close of 1930, comprises the saints residing in the south part of the state of Indiana, representing a total membership of 1,040, including 168 children. The headquarters are at Indianapolis, where there is a flourishing branch of the Church, containing a Relief Society, a Sunday school and Y. M. M. I. A. meetings are held at the L. D. S. chapel, 1060 Villa Avenue, and James Flemming presides.
