Greasewood County

GREASEWOOD COUNTY, one of the counties of the Territory of Utah, was designated and its boundaries defined by an act approved Jan. 5, 1856, as follows:

"Be it enacted by the Governor and Legislature of the Territory of Utah: That all that portion of Utah Territory bounded north by Oregon, west by St. Mary's County, south by Desert County, and east by that portion of the west shore of Great Salt Lake lying between the north line of Desert County and the most westerly point of said Lake, and by a line running north from said most westerly point to the southern boundary of Oregon, is and shall hereafter be called Greasewood County."

Greasewood County became part of Nevada in 1861.
