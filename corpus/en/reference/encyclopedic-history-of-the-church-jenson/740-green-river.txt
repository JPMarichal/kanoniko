Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Green River

GREEN RIVER, which became so well known to the Latter-day Saint emigrants who crossed the plains in 1847-1869, is a main tributary of the Colorado River. It rises in the Wind River Mountains in Wyoming, runs in a southwesterly direction and empties into the Colorado River in the southeast part of Utah.
