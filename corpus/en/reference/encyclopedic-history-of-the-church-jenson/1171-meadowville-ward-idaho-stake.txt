Meadowville Ward, Idaho Stake

MEADOWVILLE WARD, Idaho Stake, Caribou Co., Idaho, consisted of a farming district lying north of Soda Springs, in which the inhabitants lived in a scattered condition and were engaged in dry farming.

Meadowville Ward is an outgrowth of Soda Springs Ward and came into existence Aug. 26, 1917, when the north part of the Soda Springs Ward was organized as the Meadowville Ward with William H. Skinner as Bishop. He was succeeded in 1919 by Keplar C. Sessions, who in 1922 was succeeded by John J. Skinner, who in 1926 was succeeded by Robert S. Hawker as presiding Elder. He presided until June 6, 1926, when the Meadowville Ward was discontinued and a new ward named Conda was organized in its stead. (See Conda Ward.)
