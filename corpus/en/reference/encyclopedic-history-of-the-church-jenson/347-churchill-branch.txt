Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Churchill Branch

CHURCHILL BRANCH, in Cassia Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in a farming district northwest of Oakley. It was a scattered settlement, the center of which was the school house, located 11 miles north of Marion and 15 miles northwest of Oakley. Meetings were held in this school house.

Churchill Branch was an outgrowth of Marion Ward. Some of the members of the Church having taken up land under an irrigation project in the district, a Sunday school was organized for their benefit Jan. 10, 1915, with John H. Hill as superintendent, and on May 23, 1915 a branch of the Church was organized with Wallace Warner as presiding Elder. He presided until 1924, when, on account of misunderstanding in regard to water privileges, he and many others left the settlement. Consequently, the branch was discontinued and the remaining members were transferred to the Marion Ward.
