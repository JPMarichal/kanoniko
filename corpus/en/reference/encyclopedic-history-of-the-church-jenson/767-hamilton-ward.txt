Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Hamilton Ward

HAMILTON WARD, Gunnison Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in a farming district lying between Gunnison and Centerfield. The center of the ward, where a meeting house has recently been built, is about a mile south of the center of Gunnison. The ward is an outgrowth of Gunnison and Centerfield and was organized May 27, 1923, with Joseph A. Larson as Bishop. The place was named Hamilton in honor of Hamilton H. Kearns, the first ordained Bishop of the Gunnison Ward. The ward extends westward to the Sevier River and eastward to the Mayfield precinct line. Nearly all the residents within the limits of the ward are L. D. S. farmers, who irrigate their lands from the Sanpitch and Sevier rivers. Bishop Larson was succeeded in 1925 by Hans C. Olsen, who in 1927 was succeeded by Charles E. Embley, who in 1930 was succeeded by Joseph A. Larson, who presided Dec. 31, 1930, when the Church membership of the Hamilton Ward was 434, including 69 children.
