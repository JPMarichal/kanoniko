Arizona Conference

ARIZONA CONFERENCE, or District, of the California Mission embraced in 1930, the state of Arizona, irrespective of the four stakes of Zion (Snowflake, St. Johns, St. Joseph and Maricopa). The district consists of 12 organized branches of the Church, namely, Binghampton, Bisbee, Douglas, Hayden, Jerome, Pomerene, Prescott, Ray, St. David, Tucson and Whitewater, with a membership in these branches of 2,228, including 597 children.
