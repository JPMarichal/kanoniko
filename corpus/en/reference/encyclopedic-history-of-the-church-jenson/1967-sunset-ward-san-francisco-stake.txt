Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Sunset Ward, San Francisco Stake

SUNSET WARD, San Francisco Stake, San Francisco Co., California, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of San Francisco known as Sunset, which lies south of Golden Gate Park.

A branch known as Sunset Branch was organized in January, 1926, by Apostle George Albert Smith and Pres. Joseph W. McMurrin. This branch was organized as a ward July 10, 1927, with Carl Kjar as Bishop. He presided Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the Sunset Ward had a membership of 289, including 63 children.
