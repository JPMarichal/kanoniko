Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Grampton

GRAMPTON, Beaver Stake, is a mining district in the mountains near Frisco, Beaver Co., Utah. For the benefit of the families of Latter-day Saints employed in connection with the mines, a Sunday school was organized in 1907 with Charles W. Poole as superintendent. He was succeeded in that position in 1909 by Gideon A. Murdock, who still acted in 1915, since which time no report of the school has been filed.
