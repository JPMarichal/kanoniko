Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Pennsylvania East Conference

PENNSYLVANIA EAST CONFERENCE, or District, of the Eastern States Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in eastern Pennsylvania, with a flourishing branch of the Church in Philadelphia. The total membership of the district on Dec. 31, 1930, was 284, including 56 children.
