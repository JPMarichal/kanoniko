Shropshire Conference

SHROPSHIRE CONFERENCE, British Mission, consisted of the /b Latter-day Saints residing in Shropshire, one of the midland counties of England, bordering on Wales. Shropshire Conference was organized Oct. 5, 1850, but was discontinued in 1862, when its branches were annexed to the Staffordshire Conference.
