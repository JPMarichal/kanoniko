Welsh Conference

WELSH CONFERENCE, or District, of the British Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in Wales, and contains three branches, with a total Church population of 200, including 18 children.

The Welsh Conference was organized Dec. 23, 1873, by the amalgamation of the North Wales, Pembrokeshire and Glamorganshire conferences, the only conferences remaining in Wales at that time. Wales as a missionary field has been designated by the name of the Welsh Conference of the British Mission since 1873. The headquarters of the district in 1930 were located at Cardiff.
