Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

"Maid of Iowa"

"MAID OF IOWA" (The) was a small steamboat built at Augusta, Iowa, by Levi Moffit and Dan Jones and run by Dan Jones as a freight and passenger boat on the Mississippi River above St. Louis until September, 1843. In that year Joseph the Prophet became part owner. The "Maid of Iowa" brought a company of British saints up the Mississippi River from New Orleans to Nauvoo in 1842. In 1843, when the Prophet Joseph was kidnapped in Dixon, Lee County, Ill., the little vessel rendered loyal service to the saints in guarding the rivers to prevent the Prophet from being taken to Missouri. The "Maid of Iowa" was lost some time after the saints left Nauvoo.
