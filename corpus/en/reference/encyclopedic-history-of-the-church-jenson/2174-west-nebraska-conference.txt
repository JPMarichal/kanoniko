Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

West Nebraska Conference

WEST NEBRASKA CONFERENCE, or District, of the Western States Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the west part of the state of Nebraska, numbering 293 souls, including 77 children. There were branches of the Church at Grand Island, Hastings and North Platte on Dec. 31, 1930.
