Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Blue Springs

BLUE SPRINGS, Washington Co., Utah, formerly a branch of the St. George Stake, was a summer ranch located in the Kolob Mountains, 25 miles northeast of Virgin City, or northeast of Toquerville. Many of the people of Virgin City and surrounding settlements on the Rio Virgen went to Blue Springs during the summer to engage in dairying, during which time meetings and Sunday school sessions were held quite regularly. The place was usually vacated during the winter.
