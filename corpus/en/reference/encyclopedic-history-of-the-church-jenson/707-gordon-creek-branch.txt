Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Gordon Creek Branch

GORDON CREEK BRANCH, Carbon Stake, Carbon Co., Utah, consists (1930) of a few Latter-day Saints residing at Gordon Creek, a few miles from Price, the headquarters of the stake.

The saints who had located at Gordon Creek were organized into a branch of the Church June 8, 1930, with Arthur E. Robinson as presiding Elder; he acted in that capacity Dec. 31, 1930, when the Church membership of the branch was 41 souls, including 7 children. Small as the branch is, it has the usual auxiliary organizations found in a L. D, S. community.
