Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Chalford Hill Conference

CHALFORD HILL CONFERENCE, British Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the town of Chalford Hill, Gloucestershire, England, and vicinity. The conference was organized in 1844, but as the work spread into other parts of the country, the town of Cheltenham became the headquarters of the conference, and on June 30, 1847, the name of the conference was changed to that of the Cheltenham Conference.
