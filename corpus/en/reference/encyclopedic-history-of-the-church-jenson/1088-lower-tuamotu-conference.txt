Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Lower Tuamotu Conference

LOWER TUAMOTU CONFERENCE, of the Society Islands Mission, consisted of the western part of the Tuamotu group of islands, including the islands of Takaroa, Fakarava, Takapoto, Arutua, Ragiroa, Apataki, Aratiki, Karoochi, Faaite, Tahanea and Anaa.
