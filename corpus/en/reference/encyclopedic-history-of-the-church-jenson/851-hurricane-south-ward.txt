Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Hurricane South Ward

HURRICANE SOUTH WARD, Zion Park Stake; Washington Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the south part of Hurricane and contains about one-half of the business part and many fine residences. The ward dates back to Aug. 26, 1928, when the Hurricane Ward was divided into the Hurricane North and the Hurricane South wards. Ira H. Bradshaw, who presided over the Hurricane Ward, was chosen as Bishop of the Hurricane South Ward. He presided Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the ward had 559 members, including 149 children.
