Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Roberts Ward

ROBERTS WARD, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in a farming district in that part of Snake River Valley which lies north of Idaho Falls. The center of the ward is a station on the Oregon Short Line Railroad, on the west side of Snake River, 15 miles west of Rigby.

The saints who had located in that locality were organized as a ward Nov. 21, 1920, with Horace I. Grow as Bishop. He was succeeded in 1928 by Carl Paul Holm, who presided Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the Roberts Ward had 229 members, including 59 children. The total population of the Roberts Precinct was 806 in 1930, of which 297 resided in the Roberts Village.
