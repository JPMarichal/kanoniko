Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Liverpool Conference

LIVERPOOL CONFERENCE, or District, of the British Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in parts of Lancashire and Cheshire, England, and also includes the Isle of Man. Liverpool Conference, which dates back to 1841, at one time included the northern part of Wales and also Ireland. The conference has had a continued existence and on Dec. 31, 1930, it had a Church membership of 660, including 54 children.
