Stettin Conference

STETTIN CONFERENCE, or District, of the German-Austrian Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the town of Stettin, located in the north part of Germany, near the shore of the Baltic Sea. The district also embraces a number of villages in the vicinity. The total population of Stettin District Dec. 31, 1930, was 541, including 61 children. There are seven branches in the district, namely, Barth, Demmin, Neubrandenburg, Prenslau, Stargard, Nord Stettin and Sud Stettin.
