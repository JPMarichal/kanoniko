Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Prattville Branch

PRATTVILLE BRANCH, Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, consisted of a small settlement founded on the east bank of the Sevier River between Richfield and Glenwood, in April, 1873, by Helaman Pratt and others. A townsite was located and surveyed and several buildings erected on the same. Helaman Pratt, who had formerly acted as Bishop of Glenwood, was placed in charge of the new settlement. He was succeeded in 1875 by Joseph K. Rogers, who in 1876 was succeeded by Henry C. Jacobs, who presided until 1878, when the settlement was abandoned, the location proving unsatisfactory. In 1900 there were only two houses left of what was once the village of Prattville, and the occupants of these were not members of the Church.
