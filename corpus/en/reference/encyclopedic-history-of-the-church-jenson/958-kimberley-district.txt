Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Kimberley District

KIMBERLEY DISTRICT, Nevada Stake, consisted of a small area of country connected with and adjacent to the Kimberley mines in White Pine County, Nevada. Among the employees at Kimberley there are always a few Latter-day Saints, and for the benefit of these, special missionaries are frequently sent to the Kimberley mining camp to hold meetings. Efforts have been made to establish a regular Sunday school there, but owing to the transient population of the district, with no success. The members of the Church at Kimberley generally attend meetings at Ely.
