"Der Wegweiser (Der)"

"WEGWEISER (Der)" is a periodical published in the German language for the Priesthood and auxiliary organizations in the Swiss-German and German-Austrian missions of the Church. The first number was dated Jan. 1, 1927, and for three consecutive years this well-edited guide made its appearance quarterly, furnishing lesson material for the several groups and classes in the German-speaking missions. On January 1, 1930, "Der Wegweiser" was changed from a quarterly to a monthly periodical. It was published at Basel, Switzerland, at the close of 1930.
