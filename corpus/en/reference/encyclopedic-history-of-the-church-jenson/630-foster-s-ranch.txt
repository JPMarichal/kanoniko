Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Foster's Ranch

FOSTER'S RANCH, St. George Stake, Washington Co., Utah, consisted of a few Latter-day Saint families residing on the Santa Clara Creek, about nine miles below Pine Valley, to which ward the branch belonged. The place was first settled in 1863 by David Canfield and Edson Barney and families. Other settlers followed, and when the Pine Valley Ward was organized June 18, 1877, Frank Foster was chosen as presiding Elder of that part of the Pine Valley which extends up and down the Santa Clara, including Foster's Ranch. This branch organization, however, only existed for a short time.
