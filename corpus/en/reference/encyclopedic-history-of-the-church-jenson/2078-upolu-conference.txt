Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Upolu Conference

UPOLU CONFERENCE, or District, of the Samoan Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints on the island of Upolu, the most important island of the Samoan group. In the absence of statistics, we can only say that there were several branches of the Church on the island in 1930, and the headquarters of the mission were located at Apia. On the north coast of the island of Upolu the Church sustains an excellent school in the interest of the native population.
