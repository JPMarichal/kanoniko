Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Escalante South Ward

ESCALANTE SOUTH WARD, Garfield Stake, consists of Latter-day Saints residing in that part of the town of Escalante lying south of Main Street, and also includes a few ranchers residing on the edge of the desert south of the town.

Escalante South Ward came into existence Aug. 28, 1921, when the Escalante Ward was divided into two wards, namely, the Escalante North Ward and the Escalante South Ward. Milton Twitchell was chosen as Bishop of the Escalante South Ward. When he moved to Prove in 1923, he was succeeded by Harvey Clive Bailey, who presided Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the Escalante South Ward had a membership of 521, including 116 children.
