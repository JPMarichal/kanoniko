Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Lovell East Ward

LOVELL EAST WARD, Big Horn Stake, Big Horn Co., Wyoming, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the east part of the town of Lovell. The ward came into existence in 1917, when Lovell was divided into two wards and Cyrus S. Robertson was chosen Bishop of the Lovell East Ward, which position he held at the close of 1930. Lovell East Ward is sometimes called Lovell Ward, without reference to the compass or the division of the town. The Church membership of the Lovell East Ward Dec. 31, 1930, was 659, including 151 children.
