Masuren Conference

MASUREN CONFERENCE, or District, of the German-Austrian Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in a lake area in East Prussia between the Baltic Sea and Russia. On Dec. 31, 1930, there were in this conference 287 members of the Church, including 46 children. The district has five branches, namely, Insterburg, Lotezen, Memel, Salbongen and Tilsit.
