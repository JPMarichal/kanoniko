Toronto Conference

TORONTO CONFERENCE, or District, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the city of Toronto, in Canada, where there is a flourishing branch of the Church, and scattered saints in the vicinity. The headquarters of the Canadian Mission are located at 36 Ferndale Ave., Toronto, Canada. Toronto is one of the largest cities in Canada, having 627,582 inhabitants in 1930.
