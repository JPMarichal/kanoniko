Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Carlisle Conference

CARLISLE CONFERENCE, British Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the town of Carlisle, Cumberland, England, and may be considered an outgrowth of the Brampton Conference which it absorbed at the time of its organization in 1842. In 1862 Carlisle Conference became a part of the Newcastle-upon-Tyne Conference.
