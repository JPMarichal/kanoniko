Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Fruitland Branch

FRUITLAND BRANCH, Duchesne Stake, Duchesne Co., Utah, consisted of a few families of Latter-day saints who lived scattered on farms and ranches in the west part of Duchesne County, Utah, and constituted a part of the Strawberry Ward.

The saints of the Fruitland district were organized as a branch of the Church Nov. 25, 1923, as a part of the Strawberry Ward, with Elmer Sweat as presiding Elder. He was succeeded in 1924 by Lewis Faucett, who presided until Sept. 2, 1925, when the branch was disorganized and the few families of saints still remaining in the locality were made a part of the Strawberry Ward.
