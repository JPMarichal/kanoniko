Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

West Virginia South Conference

WEST VIRGINIA SOUTH CONFERENCE, or District, of the East Central States Mission, consists of Latter-day Saints residing in the southern part of the state of West Virginia. On Dec. 31, 1930, the district had a total membership of 1,397, including 295 children.
