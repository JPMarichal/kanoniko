Dyffryn-Conway and Anglesey Conference

DYFFRYN-CONWAY AND ANGLESEY CONFERENCE, Wales, British Mission organized about Dec., 1853, consisted of the northern part of Carnarvonshire and the island of Anglesey, on the northwest coast of Wales. This conference became the Carnarvon Conference on Jan. 9, 1858.
