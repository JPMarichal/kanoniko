Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Fort Cameron

FORT CAMERON was a U. S. military post established in 1873, at the mouth of Beaver Canyon, Beaver Co., Utah. Eighteen solid rock buildings were erected there for officers and barracks for the soldiers. In 1883 the post was abandoned and later the buildings on the military reservation were sold. Through the instrumentality and generosity of John R. Murdock, president of the Beaver Stake, and Philo T. Farnsworth, a financier, the site and buildings came into the possession of the Brigham Young Academy at Provo, and in 1898 the Beaver Branch of the B. Y. U. was established in some of the old barracks which were renovated for the purpose. The name of this school was later changed to Murdock Academy, in honor of Pres. Murdock and the school functioned on the site of Fort Cameron until 1922, when it was closed.
