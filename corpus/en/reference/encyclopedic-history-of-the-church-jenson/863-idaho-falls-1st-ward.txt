Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Idaho Falls 1st Ward

IDAHO FALLS 1ST WARD, Idaho Falls Stake, Bonneville Co., Idaho, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of the city of Idaho Falls lying west of the Oregon Short Line Railroad tracks and south of H Street. The ward owns a rock chapel erected in 1892 at a cost of about $35,000 and is located on the same block as the Idaho Falls Stake tabernacle and stake office buildings, on Park Avenue and E Street.

When first organized on March 23, 1919, the Idaho Falls 1st Ward embraced all that part of Idaho Falls lying west of the Oregon Short Line Railroad tracks. Charles Crabtree, who had presided over the Idaho Falls Ward before its division, was chosen and sustained as Bishop of the Idaho Falls 1st Ward. He was succeeded later the same year by Charles E. Dinwoodey, who in 1924 was succeeded by Joseph A. Brunt, who in 1925 was succeeded by James Laird, who in 1927 was succeeded by Gustaf Henry Johnson, who presided Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the Idaho Falls 1st Ward had a membership of 730, including 193 children.
