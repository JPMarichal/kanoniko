Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Manassa 2nd Ward

MANASSA 2ND WARD, Conejos Co., Colorado, was created by the division of Manassa Ward into two wards May 30, 1920, and consisted of all that part of the former Manassa Ward lying north of Main St. Silas S. Weimer was chosen as Bishop of the Manassa 2nd Ward, and acted in that position until Sept. 22, 1928, when the two wards were amalgamated as the Manassa Ward.
