Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Blue Ridge Conference

BLUE RIDGE CONFERENCE, or District, of the Eastern States Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the southeast part of Pennsylvania with headquarters at Lancaster. There are three organized branches of the Church in the conference, namely, Buck Valley, Fairview, and York, with a total Church membership of 231, including 51 children.
