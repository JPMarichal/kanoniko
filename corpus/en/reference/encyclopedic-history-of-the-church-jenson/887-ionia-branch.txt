Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Ionia Branch

IONIA BRANCH, Big Horn Stake, Big Horn Co., Wyoming, consists of a few Latter-day Saint families residing in a prosperous farming district about 12 miles east of Lovell on the north side of the Shoshone River.

As early as 1918 there was a branch organization at Ionia, called the Ionia Branch, with Fred H. Bassett as presiding Elder; he presided over the branch Dec. 31, 1930. The Church membership of the Ionia Branch on that date was 96, including 27 children.
