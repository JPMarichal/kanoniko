Pocatello 5th Ward

POCATELLO 5TH WARD, Pocatello Stake, Bannock Co., Idaho, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of the country known as Alameda. Northward the ward extends to the Chubbuck road, or to North Pocatello, east to Washington Avenue, south to the limits of the city of Pocatello, and west to the mountains.

At a meeting held Feb. 27, 1921, the Pocatello 3rd Ward was divided and all that part of the same lying north of the city limits and west of Washington Avenue was organized as the Pocatello 5th Ward with James A. Quinn as Bishop. He presided Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the Pocatello 5th Ward had 587 members, including 158 children.
