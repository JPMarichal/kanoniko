Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Ivins Branch

IVINS BRANCH, St. George Stake, Washington Co., Utah, consists of a few families of saints residing on a bench about three miles northwest of Santa Clara. The branch embraces a farming district and constituted originally a part of the Santa Clara Ward. At a meeting held in November, 1926, the saints who had located on the bench northwest of Santa Clara were organized into a branch of the Santa Clara Ward named Ivins, in honor of Pres. Anthony W. Ivins, with Edward R. Frei, jun., as presiding Elder. A meeting house was built in 1926. Edward R. Frei, jun., was succeeded as presiding Elder March 20, 1930, by Harmon Gubler, jun., who presided Dec. 31, 1930.
