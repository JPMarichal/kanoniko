Monmouthshire Conference

MONMOUTHSHIRE CONFERENCE, British Mission, comprised the Latter-day Saints residing in Monmouthshire, England, a county bordering on Wales, and inhabited by a number of the Welsh-speaking people. Monmouthshire Conference was organized May 31, 1846, but was discontinued on May 30, 1871, and made a part of the Glamorgan (Wales) Conference.
