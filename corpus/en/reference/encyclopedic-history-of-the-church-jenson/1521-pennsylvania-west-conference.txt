Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Pennsylvania West Conference

PENNSYLVANIA WEST CONFERENCE, or District, of the Eastern States Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the west part of the state of Pennsylvania, with branches of the Church in Pittsburg and Wilson. The total membership of the district Dec. 31, 1930, was 510, including 81 children.
