Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Columbus

COLUMBUS, the capital of Ohio, became associated with the Latter-day Saints as early as 1831 and has, on several occasions, figured quite prominently in Church history. The celebrated Zion's Camp traveled through Columbus in 1834 and many of the leading Elders of the Church have preached and lectured on Mormonism in this beautiful capital.
