Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

San Jose (Colonia San Jose)

SAN JOSE (COLONIA SAN JOSE), Juarez Stake, State of Sonora, Mexico, consisted of a colony of Latter-day Saints residing in San Jose, situated on the Batetio Creek, nine miles north of Colonia Morelos. Colonia San Jose was only a temporary settlement of the saints founded mainly by people from Oaxaca, who had lost their property there by flood in 1905. George Albert Martineau acted as Bishop in 1911, but this settlement, like the other L. D. S. colonies in Mexico, was broken up in 1912.
