Ogden 13th Ward

OGDEN 13TH WARD, Ogden Stake, Weber Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of Ogden city which extends north to 22nd St., east to the mountains, south to 24th St., and west to Quincy Avenue. The ward meeting house is located on Jackson Avenue and 23rd St., facing south and west. It is a modern three-story building, erected in 1917 at a cost of $58,000.

Ogden 13th Ward is an outgrowth of the Ogden 4th and 6th wards and was organized Dec. 31, 1916, with John Howard Jenkins as Bishop. He was succeeded in 1925 by Edward H. Chambers, who still presided Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the ward had 807 members, including 100 children.
