Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

West Warren Branch

WEST WARREN BRANCH, North Weber Stake, Weber Co., Utah, consists of about sixteen families of Latter-day Saints who live in a scattered condition on a part of a peninsula which extends into the Great Salt Lake. Thus the branch is bounded on the east and southeast by Weber River, and on the south, west and north by Great Salt Lake. The West Warren Branch meeting house stands in the northeast quarter of Sec. 23, Township 6 north of Range 3 west, Salt Lake Meridian. A district of the Warren Ward, known as West Warren, was organized as an independent branch March 25, 1917, with Samuel Knight as presiding Elder. He was succeeded in 1926 by Joseph L. Muirbrook, who in 1927 was succeeded by Thomas W. Barrow, who presided Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the branch had 120 members, including 33 children.
