Pleasant Valley Railroad

PLEASANT VALLEY RAILROAD, Utah Co., Utah, was a narrow gauge line from Springville, Utah, to the coal mines in Pleasant Valley. Milan Packard, a merchant in Springville, Utah Co., who owned a coal mine at Winter Quarters in Pleasant Valley, organized the company which built and operated the line, which was commenced in 1876 and completed in 1877. Besides the coal, lumber, wood, ties, fence poles, posts, etc., were also hauled and a mixed train, carrying passengers and freight, ran regularly from the years 1877 to 1880, when the Pleasant Valley Railroad with all its belongings was turned over to the Rio Grande Western Railroad.

Among the first employees on the Pleasant Valley Railroad was John Bartlett, of Springville, who, after assisting in building the road, held many responsible positions in connection with it and was later connected with the D. & R. G. Railroad.
