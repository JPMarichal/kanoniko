Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Lanark Ward

LANARK WARD, Bear Lake Stake, Bear Lake Co., Idaho, is an outgrowth of Paris and consists of a farming district lying between Paris and Liberty. The Lanark Ward was organized Aug. 31, 1893, with Joseph Peter Beck as Bishop; he was succeeded in 1897 by Ebenezer Crouch, who was succeeded in 1902 by James F. Bunn, who was succeeded in 1904 by James Hymas, who was succeeded in 1908 by Fred W. Passey, who was succeeded in 1921 by Ernest D. Hymas, who acted as Bishop of. the Lanark Ward in 1930. The Church population of the Lanark Ward Dec. 31, 1930, was 180, including 50 children.
