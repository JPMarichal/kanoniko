Garway Conference

GARWAY CONFERENCE, British Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing at Garway, Herefordshire, England, and vicinity, including those at Euysharold, Longton and Arcop, who were organized as the Garway Conference April 6, 1841, with Levi Richards, an American Elder, as president. Garway became part of the Herefordshire Conference in 1848.
