Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Gleichen Branch

GLEICHEN BRANCH, Lethbridge Stake, Alberta, Canada, consisted of a few Latter-day Saints residing at Gleichen. Gleichen Branch was organized July 8, 1908. Gleichen is a railway town situated on the main line of the Canadian Pacific Railway, 51 miles by rail southeast of Calgary. Here farms, irrigated from the Box River, were located by Latter-day Saints and others about 1906. The canal, which supplies the country with irrigation water, taps the Box River about ten miles east of Calgary. The saints who had located in or near Gleichen were organized as an independent branch in 1921, but as some of the saints moved away, the branch was disorganized Dec. 31, 1924.
