Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Apostate Organizations

APOSTATE ORGANIZATIONS are names given to various factions which arose in the Church, especially after the martyrdom of the Prophet Joseph Smith. Brief histories of some of these organizations will be found under the captions: Bickertonites, Brewsterites, Cutlerites, Emmett's Company, Hedrickites, Millerites, Morrisites, Reorganites, Strangites, Wightites, Whitmerites and a number of others of more or less significance.
