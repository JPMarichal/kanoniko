Gadfield Elm Conference

GADFIELD ELM CONFERENCE, British Mission, organized June 14, 1840, originally called the Bran Green and Gadfield Elm Conference, was the first conference organized in the British Mission and consisted of members of the Bran Green and Gadfield Elm Branch of the Froome's Hill circuit of United Brethren who became members of the Church largely through the efforts of Apostle Wilford Woodruff. At the time of its organization the conference contained the following branches: Bran Green, Gadfield Elm, Kilcott, Twigworth, Ryton, Lime Street, Deerhurst, Apperley, Norton and Leigh in Worcestershire and Herefordshire (England) and vicinity. Gadfield Elm Conference was transferred to the Cheltenham Conference in 1842.
