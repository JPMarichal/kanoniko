Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Nottingham Conference

NOTTINGHAM CONFERENCE, or District, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the central part of England, with headquarters in the great city of Nottingham, an inland town known throughout the world as a great lace manufacturing center. The Nottingham Conference was organized in 1851 and still exists. The membership of the district on Dec. 31, 1930, was 483, including 55 children.
