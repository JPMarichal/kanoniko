Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

San Francisco District

SAN FRANCISCO DISTRICT, of the California Mission, embraced in 1930 thirteen counties of California, namely, San Marco, San Francisco, Alameda, Contro Costa, Solano, Napa, Marin, Sonoma, Lake Mendosino, Trinity, Humboldt and Del Nortia, with headquarters at San Francisco. The district includes two organized branches of the Church outside of the wards of the San Francisco Stake, namely, Haywood and Vallejo, with a Church membership of 165, including 21 children. Sunday school organizations are also functioning at Alameda, Santa Rosa and Walnut Grove.
