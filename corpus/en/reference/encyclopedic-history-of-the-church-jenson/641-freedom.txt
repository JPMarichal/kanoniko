Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Freedom

FREEDOM, Adams Co., Illinois, is noted in Church history as a temporary stake and gathering place for the saints in 1839-1841. In February, 1840, the branch of the Church there numbered 225 members. Henry W. Miller was appointed to preside Oct. 22, 1840. In 1841 most of the members of the Church who had resided at Freedom moved to Hancock County, Illinois.
