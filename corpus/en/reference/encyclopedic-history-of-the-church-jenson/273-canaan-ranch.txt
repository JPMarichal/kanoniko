Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Canaan Ranch

CANAAN RANCH, Mojave Co., Arizona, was the headquarters of the so-called Canaan Cooperative Stock Company, and the Windsor Castle Stock-growing Company, which was organized in St. George Jan. 1, 1879, with Erastus Snow as president. The two companies mentioned, being amalgamated, carried on a successful stock raising business for a number of years in the interest of the saints in southern Utah and northern Arizona, under the administration of a board of directors. The company was finally disorganized, but in 1930 there was still a ranch at the old location owned by William Shanley.
