Bloemfontein Conference

BLOEMFONTEIN CONFERENCE, or District, of the South African Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the city of Bloemfontein and vicinity, located near the center of the so-called Union of South Africa, comprising the south part of the continent of Africa. Bloemfontein is about 500 miles inland northeast of Cape Town, and has about forty thousand inhabitants. The district on Dec. 31, 1930, had a membership of 55, including 4 Elders, 1 Priest, 2 Deacons, 40 lay members and 8 children.
