Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Jackson Ward

JACKSON WARD, San Juan Co., New Mexico, consisted of a few Latter-day Saint families who located on the La Plata River, and where there was at one time a post office. The center of the Mormon settlement on the La Plata, where the meeting house stands, was 14 miles east of Fruitland and about 18 miles south of the boundary line between New Mexico and Colorado. Associated with the saints were a number of non-Mormon settlers on ranches extending up to the Indian Reservation line, which is also the southern boundary line of Colorado. Jackson was abandoned on account of water shortage.
