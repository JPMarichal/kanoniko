Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Lakeside Ward

LAKESIDE WARD, Snowflake Stake, Navajo Co., Arizona, consists of Latter-day Saints residing in the timber regions adjacent to Showlow Creek on the slopes of the Mogollon Mountains.

Lakeside Ward, an outgrowth of Showlow Ward, was organized June 24, 1912, to comprise all the ranches situated south of the top of the Ellsworth Hill; Alof P. Larson was chosen as Bishop. He acted until 1918 when he was succeeded by Leo Ellsworth, who was succeeded in 1920 by John L. Fish, who acted Dec. 31, 1930. Lakeside Ward has also been known as Woodland and Fairview. On Dec. 31, 1930, the Lakeside Ward had 336 members, including 78 children.
