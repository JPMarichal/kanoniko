Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

East Texas Conference

EAST TEXAS CONFERENCE, or District (Central States Mission), consisted at the close of 1930 of a total membership of 1,573, including 245 children. There were four organized branches of the Church in the district, namely, Dallas, Enoch, James and Kelsey. Nearly all the residents of Kelsey and Enoch were members of the Church.
