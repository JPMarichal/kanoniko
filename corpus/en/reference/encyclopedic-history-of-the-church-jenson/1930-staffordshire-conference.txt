Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Staffordshire Conference

STAFFORDSHIRE CONFERENCE, British Mission, also known as the Potteries Conference, consisted of Latter-day Saints residing in Staffordshire, England. At a meeting held at Hanley June 29, 1840, the branches of the Church at the Staffordshire potteries were organized as the Staffordshire Potteries Conference with Elder Alfred Cordon as president. In 1869 this conference was dissolved and its territory divided between the Birmingham and Manchester conferences.
