Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Chicago Conference

CHICAGO CONFERENCE, or District, of the Northern States Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints in the city of Chicago, Ill., and vicinity, numbering 1,340 souls, including 245 children on Dec. 31, 1930. There are two L. D. S. chapels in Chicago, one on North Sawyer Avenue, and another near the Chicago University. The headquarters of the Northern States Mission are in Chicago, the mission office and home of the missionaries being located near the chapel on North Sawyer Avenue.
