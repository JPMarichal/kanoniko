Denbighshire Conference

DENBIGHSHIRE CONFERENCE, British Mission, in Wales, organized July 29, 1849, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the northern part of Wales. Denbighshire borders on the Irish Sea. This conference was disorganized to become a part of the North Wales Conference March 25, 1866.
