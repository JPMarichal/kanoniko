Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Grantsville 2nd Ward

GRANTSVILLE 2ND WARD, Tooele Stake, Tooele Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the east part of Grantsville. The ward meeting house, erected in 1914-1915 at a cost of $28,000, is a modern brick building somewhat centrally located in the ward, on the north side of Main Street. It has a seating capacity of 500, also an amusement hall, Relief Society room and six class rooms.

Grantsville 2nd Ward dates back to March 29, 1914, when the original Grantsville Ward was divided and two new wards organized in its stead named respectively the Grantsville 1st and the Grantsville 2nd wards. John William Anderson was chosen as Bishop of the Grantsville 2nd Ward and he presided in that capacity Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the 2nd Ward had 513 members, including 79 children.
