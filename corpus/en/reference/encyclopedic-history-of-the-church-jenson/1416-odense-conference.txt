Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Odense Conference

ODENSE CONFERENCE, Danish Mission, consisting of Latter-day Saints residing on the islands of Fyen, Langeland and smaller islands in Denmark, was only a temporary organization existing about a year and a half. It was taken from the Aarhus Conference and delivered back to that conference. Two branches, namely, Odense and Schlesvig-Holstein, constituted the conference.
