Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Pembrokeshire Conference

PEMBROKESHIRE CONFERENCE, Wales, British Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the extreme southwest part of Wales. It was organized July 29, 1849. On July 6, 1851, this conference was divided into the Pembrokeshire North and Pembrokeshire South conferences and so remained until Dec. 29, 1855, when the two conferences were amalgamated as the Pembrokeshire Conference. On Dec. 23, 1873, Pembrokeshire Conference was discontinued to become a part of the Welsh Conference.
