Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Merthyr Tydvil Conference

MERTHYR TYDVIL CONFERENCE, in South Wales, British Mission, was organized April 6, 1844, to comprise the Latter-day Saints /b residing in Merthyr Tydvil, Beaufort, Rumney and Aberdare, in South Wales. The name of this conference was changed to Glamorgan Conference Dec. 26, 1847.
