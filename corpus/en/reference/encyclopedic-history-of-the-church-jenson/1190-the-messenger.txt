Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

"The Messenger"

"MESSENGER (THE)" was a periodical published by the Church in the interest of the New Zealand Mission. The first number of this periodical was issued from the press in Auckland, New Zealand, Jan. 31, 1907, under the title "Elders' Journal" and was started as a semi-monthly magazine. At the commencement it was an eight-page octavo pamphlet, five pages of which were published in English and three in Maori.

On July 15, 1907, the name was changed to that of "The Messenger," about one-half being edited in the Maori tongue. This continued until the commencement of Volume 2 (Feb. 5, 1908) when the publication was undertaken of two separate magazines of 16 pages, duplicated, one in English and the other under the caption "Te Karere" in Maori. Later the "Messenger" was again published half in English and the other half in Maori, and under the name of "Te Karere" is still published in the New Zealand Mission. (Deseret News, July 30, 1932, Church Section.)
