Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Clearing House Conference

CLEARING HOUSE CONFERENCE, or District, of the New Zealand Mission, consists of scattered members who, through change of residence or location outside the boundaries of either of the other fourteen conferences of the mission, have not been enumerated in the membership of said districts. The total number of such members Dec. 31, 1930, was 354, including 34 children.
