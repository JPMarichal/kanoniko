Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Durham and Newcastle Conference

DURHAM AND NEWCASTLE CONFERENCE, once a part of the British Mission, came into existence by the amalgamation of the Durham Conference and the Newcastle Conference in 1868, and functioned under this name until 1882, when the name was changed to Newcastle Conference, later called Newcastle District, which still (1930) exists.
