Pembrokeshire South Conference

PEMBROKESHIRE SOUTH CONFERENCE, Wales, British Mission, was organized July 15, 1851, and consisted of the south part of Pembrokeshire Conference, the northern part of which had been organized July 6, 1851, as the Pembrokeshire North Conference. These two conferences remained separate until Dec. 29, 1855, when they were amalgamated into the Pembrokeshire Conference.
