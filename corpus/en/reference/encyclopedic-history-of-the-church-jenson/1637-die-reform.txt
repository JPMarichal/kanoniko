Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

"Die Reform"

"REFORM" (Die) was a periodical published in the interest of the Church at Geneva (Genf), Switzerland, by John L. Smith, president of the Swiss Mission, whose office was at Rue de Chantepoulet 9, Geneva. It was a German periodical issued from the printing office of Karl Ludvig Sabot, Rive 5. Only one volume of "Die Reform" was published, the twelve numbers of which were dated respectively Sept., Oct. and Nov., 1862, Jan., Feb., April, Sept., Oct., Nov., and Dec., 1863, and Jan. and Feb., 1864. The twelve numbers contained altogether 188 pages (octavo size) of reading matter-16 pages in each number.
