Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Twin Falls 1st Ward

TWIN FALLS 1ST WARD, Twin Falls Stake, Twin Falls Co., Idaho, during its existence from 1921 to 1926, consisted of the east half of the city of Twin Falls. George W. Hammond presided as Bishop all the time, the ward being organized Feb. 7, 1921, and disorganized Aug. 1, 1926.
