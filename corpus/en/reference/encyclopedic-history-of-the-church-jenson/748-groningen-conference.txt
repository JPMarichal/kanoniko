Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Groningen Conference

GRONINGEN CONFERENCE, or District, of the Netherlands Mission, embraces the Latter-day Saints residing in the northern part of Holland, including the provinces Groningen, Friesland and Drente, with headquarters in the city of Groningen. At the close of 1930 there were 381 members of the Church in the district, including 42 children.
