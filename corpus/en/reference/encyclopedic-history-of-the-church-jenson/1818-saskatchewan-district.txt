Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Saskatchewan District

SASKATCHEWAN DISTRICT, or Conference, of the North Central States Mission, consists of Latter-day Saints residing in the province of Saskatchewan, in Canada. On Dec. 31, 1930, it had a total Church membership of 145, including 36 children. There are two organized branches of the Church in the district, namely, Moose Jaw and Regina.
