Lehi 4th Ward

LEHI 4TH WARD, Lehi Stake, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the northeast part of the city of Lehi, Utah, and extends northward to the mountains; east to the Highland and American Fork 3rd wards in the Alpine Stake; south to a line running east and west between 3rd and 4th North streets or the Lehi 1st and 5th wards, and west to the Lehi 2nd and 3rd wards. The Lehi 4th Ward meeting house is situated on the corner of 5th East and 9th North streets, facing north and west.

Lehi 4th Ward came into existence when the city of Lehi, on Dec. 20, 1903, was divided into four wards and John Stoker was appointed to preside over the Lehi 4th Ward. Brother Stoker was succeeded in 1923 by Thomas Leonard Petersen, who in 1926 was succeeded by Joseph Ernest Smith, who presided over the Lehi 4th Ward Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the ward had 475 members, including 83 children.
