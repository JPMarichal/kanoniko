Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

"Saint Louis Luminary"

"SAINT LOUIS LUMINARY" was a weekly newspaper, published in the interest of the Church at St. Louis, Missouri. The first number was dated St. Louis, Nov. 22, 1854, and the last (No. 52) Dec. 15, 1855. It consisted of a four-page folio, each page containing five wide columns. The printing matter on each page measured 14 1/2 by 22 inches.

The paper was edited and published by Apostle Erastus Snow, and while it only lasted one year, it fills a very important period in the history of the Church as pertaining to the condition of the Latter-day Saints in the United States in 1854, and the emigration from the British Isles that year, when the outfitting place for crossing the plains was near Atchison, Kansas, at a place known historically as Mormon Grove.

The subscription price to the "Saint Louis Luminary" was $2 per annum. The paper was published with plain good-looking type and reflected credit upon its publisher, both from an editorial and mechanical standpoint. The motto of the "Saint Louis Luminary" was "Light Shineth in Darkness, and the Darkness Comprehendeth it not." (Jesus).
