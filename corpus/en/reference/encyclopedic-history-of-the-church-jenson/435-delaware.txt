Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Delaware

DELAWARE as a missionary field, constitutes a part of the Eastern States Mission, and is one of the original thirteen states of the American Union. It has an area of 1,965 square miles. The population of Delaware was 76,748 in 1830; 78,085 in 1840; 91,532 in 1850; 112,216 in 1860; 125,015 in 1870; 146,608 in 1880; 168,493 in 1890; 184,735 in 1900; 202,322 in 1910; 223,003 in 1920, and 238,380 in 1930.

As early as 1837 Latter-day Saint missionaries labored in the state of Delaware, Elder Jedediah M. Grant being among the first. It belonged from the beginning to the Eastern States Mission and Wilmington was in early days an important rendezvous for missionaries in that and adjacent states. Delaware has been a fruitful missionary field and still belonged to the Eastern States Mission in 1930.
