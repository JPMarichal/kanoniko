Cedar City 3rd Ward

CEDAR CITY 3RD WARD, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of Cedar City lying north of Center Street and west of Main Street, and organized Dec. 5, 1926. Walter K. Granger was chosen as Bishop of the new ward. He presided Dec. 31, 1930, when the membership of the ward was 1,083, including 237 children.
