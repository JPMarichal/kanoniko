Scott's Bluff

SCOTT'S BLUFF is an attractive natural formation standing on the south side of the Platte River, about 20 miles west of Chimney Rock, Nebraska. It was named after a man called Scott, who, in the early trapper days, being sick, was left by his companions to starve to death. Scott's Bluff is associated with several tragedies resulting from warfare between the whites and Indians.
