Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Ruhr Conference

RUHR CONFERENCE, or District, of the Swiss-German Mission, included within its boundaries that part of Germany which borders on the River Rhine, not far from the German-French boundary. The Ruhr District at the close of 1930 had a total Church population of 435, including 67 children. There are ten branches in the district, namely, Altenessen, Bochum, Buer, Dortmund, Duisburg, Essen, Herne, Langendreer, Oberhausen and Radbod.
