Hannover Conference

HANNOVER CONFERENCE, or District, of the Swiss-German Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the province of Hannover, Germany, and vicinity, excepting the northern part of the province which is included in the Bremen District. The headquarters of the Hannover Conference are in the city of Hannover, situated about 90 miles south of Hamburg. Hannover is a city of 438,000 inhabitants. The Church membership of the Hannover District in 1930 was 320, including 30 children. The district contains nine branches, namely, Barsinghausen, Braunschweig, Celle, Goslar, Hameln, Hannover, Peine, Salzwedel and Uelzen.
