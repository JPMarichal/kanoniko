Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Heber West Ward

HEBER WEST WARD, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing west of Main St. and organized at a special conference held July 15, 1877, when Heber City was divided into two wards, namely, the Heber East and Heber West wards, with William Foreman as Bishop of the Heber West Ward. He was succeeded in 1884 by Henry Clegg, who in 1894 was succeeded by Thomas Hicken, jun., who presided until Feb. 8, 1903, when Heber City was divided into the Heber 1st, 2nd and 3rd wards.
