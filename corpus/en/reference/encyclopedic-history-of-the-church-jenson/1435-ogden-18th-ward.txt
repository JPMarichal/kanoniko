Ogden 18th Ward

OGDEN 18TH WARD, Mount Ogden Stake, Weber Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of Ogden which is bounded on the north by 27th and 24th streets, east by Jefferson and Madison avenues, south by 30th St., and west by Washington and Adams avenues. The ward chapel is situated on the east side of Jefferson Avenue between 27th and 28th streets. It is a fine modern house of worship built in U shape, two-stories high, and has an auditorium capable of seating 400 people.

Ogden 18th Ward was organized July 20, 1924, from a part of the Ogden 2nd Ward, transferred from the Weber Stake and also from parts of the Ogden 5th and 9th wards of the Mount Ogden Stake. David George Shorten was chosen as Bishop of the new ward. He was succeeded in 1926 by Wm. Zimmerman Terry, who presided Dec. 31, 1930, at which time the ward had a membership of 905, including 171 children.
