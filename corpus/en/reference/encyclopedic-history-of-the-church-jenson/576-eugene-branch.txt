Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Eugene Branch

EUGENE BRANCH in Vermillion County, Indiana, was organized in 1831, being one of the first branches of the Church organized in the state of Indiana. It was reported regularly until 1836. Another branch of the Church was organized in Green County, Indiana, in 1831, which was reported as late as Sept. 17, 1835.
