Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Western Iowa Conference

WESTERN IOWA CONFERENCE, or District, of the Northern States Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the western part of the state of Iowa (not including Council Bluffs and vicinity)." The district had a membership of 304, Dec. 31, 1930, including 68 children.
