Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Worland Branch

WORLAND BRANCH, Big Horn Stake, Washakie Co., Wyoming, consists of a few Latter-day Saint families residing in the railway town of Worland on the Chicago, Burlington and Quincy Railroad on the Big Horn River, 80 miles south of Lovell. These families were organized into a branch of the Church Aug. 21, 1920, with George M. Porter as presiding Elder. He was succeeded in that capacity in 1924 by William Moncur, who presided Dec. 31, 1930. 'The Church membership of the Worland Branch Dec. 31, 1930, was 69, including 16 children.
