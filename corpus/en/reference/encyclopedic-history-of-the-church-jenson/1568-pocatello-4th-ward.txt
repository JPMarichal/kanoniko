Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Pocatello 4th Ward

POCATELLO 4TH WARD, Pocatello Stake, Bannock Co., Idaho, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of the city of Pocatello which extends north to the North Pocatello Ward, east to the foothills of the mountains, south to Fremont St., and west to 1st Avenue.

At a special meeting held in Pocatello Feb. 27, 1921, the Pocatello 2nd Ward was divided, and the north part of the same organized as the Pocatello 4th Ward; the dividing line between the Pocatello 2nd Ward and the new Pocatello 4th Ward was established on Fremont St. William Porter Whitaker was chosen as Bishop of the Pocatello 4th Ward. He was succeeded in 1924 by James H. Radley, who in 1926 was succeeded by Lawrence D. Clark, who presided Dec. 31, 1930. On that date the Pocatello 4th Ward had 768 members, including 224 children.
