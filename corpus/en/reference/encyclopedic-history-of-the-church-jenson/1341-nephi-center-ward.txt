Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Nephi Center Ward

NEPHI CENTER WARD, Juab Stake, Juab Co., Utah (also called Nephi Ward), consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of the city of Nephi bounded on the north by 3rd North St., and on the south by 1st South St. East and west the ward extends across Juab Valley from the mountains on the east to the mountains on the west and contains most of the business section of the city of Nephi. Thomas Bailey was chosen as Bishop of the Nephi Center Ward when the ward was organized Aug. 9, 1914, and he presided Dec. 31, 1930, at which time the ward had a Church membership of 816, including 141 children.
