Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Mississippi Conference

MISSISSIPPI CONFERENCE. or District, of the Southern States Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the state of Mississippi, which at the close of 1930 had a total membership of 2,170, including 311 children. It only contained two organized branches, namely, Darburn and Red Star, but there were L. D. S. Sunday schools at Bay, St. Louis, Meadville, Raytown, Red Hill, Sarah and Smithville.
