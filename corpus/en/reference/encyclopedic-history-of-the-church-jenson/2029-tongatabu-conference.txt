Tongatabu Conference

TONGATABU CONFERENCE, or District, of the Tongan Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing on the Tongatabu Archipelago. On Dec. 31, 1930, the membership of the district was 554, including 131 children. The headquarters of the district are in the city of Nukualofa, the capital of the islands, on the island of Tongatabu. This city is also the headquarters of the Tongan Mission.
