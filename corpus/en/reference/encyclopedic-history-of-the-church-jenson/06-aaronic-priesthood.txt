Aaronic Priesthood

AARONIC PRIESTHOOD, also called the "Lesser Priesthood" and the "Levitical Priesthood," was restored to the earth May 15, 1829, when Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery were ordained by John the Baptist on the banks of the Susquehanna River near Harmony, Pennsylvania. Among the offices in the lesser Priesthood are those of Bishop, Priest, Teacher and Deacon which are distributed throughout the entire Church wherever branches or wards have been organized. (See Doc. and Cov., Sec. 13.)
