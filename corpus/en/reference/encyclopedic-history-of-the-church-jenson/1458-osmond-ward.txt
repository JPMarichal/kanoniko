Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Osmond Ward

OSMOND WARD, Star Valley Stake, Lincoln Co., Wyoming, is an outgrowth of Afton and was organized as a separate ward Aug. 18, 1910, and named Osmond in honor of George Osmond, the first president of the Star Valley Stake. Up to that time the place was known as the Dry Creek settlement. Andrew M. Nielsen was the first Bishop of the Osmond Ward; he was succeeded in 1917 by Otto Alfred Harrison, who presided Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the Osmond Ward had 170 members, including 50 children.
