Magrath 2nd Ward

MAGRATH 2ND WARD, Taylor Stake, Alberta, Canada, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in that part of the town of Magrath which lies west of 1st West St.

When the Magrath Ward was divided into two wards May 15, 1922, James Alfred Ririe was chosen as Bishop of the Magrath 2nd Ward. He was succeeded in June, 1930, by Ellis Seymour Heninger, who presided Dec. 31, 1930, on which date the ward had a population of 580, including 160 children. The modern meeting house, a brick building, was dedicated Oct. 23, 1925.
