Nauvoo Mansion

NAUVOO MANSION, a two-story building, was the residence of the Prophet Joseph Smith from 1843 until his martyrdom in 1844. Prior to 1843 the Prophet lived in a pioneer building still standing on the corner of Main St. and Water St. The mansion stands immediately north of the Nauvoo House, facing west on Main St. The building is owned by the Reorganites, and kept in good repair.
