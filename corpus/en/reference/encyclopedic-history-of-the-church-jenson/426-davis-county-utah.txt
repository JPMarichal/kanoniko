Davis County, Utah

DAVIS COUNTY, UTAH, contains that part of Great Salt Lake Valley lying between Salt Lake County on the south and Weber County on the north. It was organized by the Provisional Government of the State of Deseret March 3, 1850, and was named in honor of Daniel C. Davis, captain of Company E of the Mormon Battalion.

The county had 14,021 inhabitants in 1930. Farmington is the county seat, and Davis County, small as it is, contains some of the most fertile and productive farms in Utah.
