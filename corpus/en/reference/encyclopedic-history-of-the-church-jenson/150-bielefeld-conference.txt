Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Bielefeld Conference

BIELEFELD CONFERENCE, or District, of Westfallen of the Swiss-German Mission, comprised the Latter-day Saints of Bielefeld and vicinity. It contained a Church membership of 245, Dec. 31, 1930, including 33 children. The city of Bielefeld had 91,000 inhabitants. Bielefeld District contains five branches, namely, Bielefeld, Herford, Minden, Munchehagen and Stadthagen.
