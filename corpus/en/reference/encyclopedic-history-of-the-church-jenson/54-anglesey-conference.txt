Anglesey Conference

ANGLESEY CONFERENCE, Wales, British Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing on the Island of Anglesey in the Irish Sea, off the northwest coast of Wales. Anglesey was organized as a conference July 29, 1849, and continued until Dec. 29, 1855, when it was discontinued to become a part of the Dyffryn Conway and Anglesey Conference.
