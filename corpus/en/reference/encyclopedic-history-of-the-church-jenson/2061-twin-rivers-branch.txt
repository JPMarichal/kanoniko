Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Twin Rivers Branch

TWIN RIVERS BRANCH, Alberta Stake, Alberta, Canada, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the east part of what was formerly known as the Del Bonita Branch and includes a farming district lying east of Milk River and extends south to the boundary line between the United States and Canada. In 1930 it consisted of about 16 families (80 souls) and belonged to the Rinard Independent Branch.

The Twin Rivers Branch came into existence May 19, 1929, when the east part of the Del Bonita Branch was organized as the Twin Rivers Branch with Arthur Thomas Carter as presiding Elder; he presided Dec. 31, 1930.
