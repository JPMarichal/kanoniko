Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Mahia Conference

MAHIA CONFERENCE, or District, of the New Zealand Mission, consists of a tract of country including Waikare, on Hawkes Bay, on the east coast of North Island, New Zealand. On the south the district extends to the mountains beyond Mahanga. West and north it extends far inland. In 1930 there were 706 members of the Church in the conference, including 168 children. Mahia Conference was first organized Jan. 16, 1885.
