Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Manitoba District

MANITOBA DISTRICT, or Conference, of the North Central States Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the province of Manitoba, Canada, and the western part of the province of Ontario, and had a total membership of 197 on Dec. 31, 1930, including 31 children. There were two branches of the Church in the conference, namely, Winnipeg in Manitoba and Bergland in Ontario in each of which the saints owned their own chapel.
