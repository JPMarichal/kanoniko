Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Hawkes Bay Conference

HAWKES BAY CONFERENCE, or District, of the New Zealand Mission, includes that part of the North Island of New Zealand extending from the east coast, far inland. On Dec. 31, 1930, the conference had 708 members, including 207 children. Hawkes Bay Conference was first organized Jan. 16, 1885.
