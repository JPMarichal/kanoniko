Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Walnut Grove Branch

WALNUT GROVE BRANCH, St. Johns Stake, Apache Co., Arizona, was a small settlement of saints consisting of about five families residing on the Little Colorado River, about 20 miles south of St. Johns, (to which ward the families belonged), and 15 miles north of the present Eagar.

The little valley (2 1/2 miles long by one-half mile wide) was settled by Latter-day Saints in 1880. They were organized into a branch of the Church in the early part of 1882 with James W. Wilkins as presiding Elder. He was succeeded in 1886 by James Moroni Richie, who presided a short time only, but in the spring of 1893 William H. Sherwood was appointed to preside. In 1894 there were still five Mormon families and about a dozen Mexican families residing at Walnut Grove, but soon afterwards the branch ceased to exist. For some time there was no Church organization, but later John W. Sherwood presided over the few families left and kept a good Sunday school running. In 1930 there were still half a dozen families at Walnut Grove belonging to the St. Johns Ward.
