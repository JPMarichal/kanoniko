Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Newhouse

NEWHOUSE, Beaver Co., Utah, is a mining camp situated ten miles from Wah-wah Springs on the west side of the same mountain on which Frisco is located on the east side. Bishop William B. Ashworth of the Frisco Ward visited Newhouse Dec. 10, 1905, on which occasion he organized a L. D. S. Sunday school with George Hardy as superintendent. A Mrs. Ogden was chosen as first and a Mrs. Mortensen as second assistant superintendent. A Mrs. Staples was chosen as secretary. This school, however, only existed for a short time.
