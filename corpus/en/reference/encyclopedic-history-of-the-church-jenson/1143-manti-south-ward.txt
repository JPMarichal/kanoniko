Manti South Ward

MANTI SOUTH WARD, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing south of 2nd South St. of Manti extending into the country south and west, and to the mountains on the east. The ward owns a fine chapel situated on the east side of Main St.

Manti South Ward was organized July 4, 1877, when Manti was divided into the Manti South and the Manti North wards, Hans Jensen (Hals) being chosen as Bishop of the Manti South Ward.
