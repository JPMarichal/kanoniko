Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Marseilles Conference

MARSEILLES CONFERENCE, of the French Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the southern departments of France, with headquarters at Marseilles, a noted seaport bordering on the Gulf of Lyons, or the Mediterranean Sea. The conference at the close of 1930 had only 13 members.
