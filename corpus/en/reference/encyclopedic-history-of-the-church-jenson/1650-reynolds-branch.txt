Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Reynolds Branch

REYNOLDS BRANCH, Malad Stake, Oneida Co., Idaho, consists of a few families of Latter-day Saints residing in a farming district lying north of Malad, in a neighborhood commonly called Devil or Double Creek.

The Reynolds Branch is an outgrowth of Malad 1st Ward and was organized into a branch of the Church May 27, 1923, with Taliesen Reynolds as presiding Elder. He acted in that capacity Dec. 31, 1930, when the branch had a membership of 87, including 27 children.
