Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Kimberley Conference

KIMBERLEY CONFERENCE, or District, of the South African Mission, consists of the Latter-day Saints residing in the north central part of the so-called Union of South Africa, comprising the southern portion of the continent of Africa. The city of Kimberley is about five hundred miles inland northeast of Cape Town and has about 45,000 inhabitants. It is the center of the famous South African Diamond Fields.

The Church membership in the conference on Dec. 31, 1930, was 65, including 1 Priest, 2 Teachers, 50 lay members and 12 children.
