Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Endowment House

ENDOWMENT HOUSE (The) was erected in 1854 and dedicated on May 5, 1855, for the purpose of administering certain sacred ordinances belonging exclusively to the Church. The building was located in the extreme northwest corner of the Temple Block and consisted of a two-story adobe structure. In November, 1889, three and a half years before the Salt Lake Temple was dedicated, but after three other temples in the state had been completed, the old Endowment House was torn down.
