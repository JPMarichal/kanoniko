Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

San Diego District

SAN DIEGO DISTRICT, of the California Mission, consisted in 1930 of the Latter-day Saints residing in San Diego and Imperial counties, in the extreme south end of the state of California with headquarters at San Diego. It consisted of four organized branches of the Church, namely, East San Diego, Logan Heights, National City, and San Diego. Exclusive of Logan Heights Branch, not reported, the district had a total population of 836 in 1930, including 235 children.
