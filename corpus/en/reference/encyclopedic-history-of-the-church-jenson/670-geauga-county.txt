Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

Geauga County

GEAUGA COUNTY, Ohio, in which the town of Kirtland was established as a L. D. S. settlement, was created in 1805 from Trumbull County, Ohio. Its boundaries have been considerably reduced. The name Geauga or Skeauga signifies raccoon in the Indian language. In 1840 Lake County, Ohio, was formed from Geauga and Cuyahoga counties.
