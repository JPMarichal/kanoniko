Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

British Columbia Conference

BRITISH COLUMBIA CONFERENCE, or District, of the Northwestern States Mission, comprises the Latter-day Saints residing in the province of British Columbia, Canada. In Vancouver there is a flourishing branch of the Church with a chapel owned by the Latter-day Saints. The total membership of the Church in British Columbia was 1,181, including 320 children, in 1930.
