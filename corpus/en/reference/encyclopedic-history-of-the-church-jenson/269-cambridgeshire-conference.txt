Cambridgeshire Conference

CAMBRIDGESHIRE CONFERENCE, British Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in Cambridgeshire, located in the southeastern part of England. The Cambridgeshire Conference was organized Nov 1, 1851, but on Dec. 12, 1857, it was dissolved and became part of the Bedfordshire Conference.
