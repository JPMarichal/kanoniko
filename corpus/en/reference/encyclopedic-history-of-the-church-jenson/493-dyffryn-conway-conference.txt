Dyffryn-Conway Conference

DYFFRYN-CONWAY CONFERENCE, Wales, British Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in Carnarvonshire in the northern part of Wales. This conference was organized July 15, 1851, but on Dec. 29, 1855, it was discontinued to become a part of the Dyffryn-Conway and Anglesey Conference.
