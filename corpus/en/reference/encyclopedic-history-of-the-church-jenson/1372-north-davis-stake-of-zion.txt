North Davis Stake of Zion

NORTH DAVIS STAKE OF ZION consists (1930) of the Latter-day Saints residing in the north part of Davis County, Utah. The stake extends north to Weber County (or Weber Stake), east to the Wasatch Mountains, south to the South Davis Stake, and west to the Great Salt Lake. It contains the following bishop's wards: Clearfield, Kaysville, Layton, Syracuse, Sunset, West Layton and West Point. The headquarters of the stake are at Kaysville.

At a conference of the Davis Stake held at Kaysville June 20, 1915, the Davis Stake was divided into two stakes, namely, the North Davis Stake and the South Davis Stake. The above named wards, with the exception of Sunset Ward, were designated to comprise the North Davis Stake. Henry H. Blood was chosen president of the newly organized stake with Ephraim P. Ellison as his first and Francis H. Nalder as his second counselor. Frank L. Layton was the first stake clerk. All of these officers acted in the positions named Dec. 31, 1930, except Francis H. Nalder, who had been succeeded as second counselor in 1927 by Arnold D. Miller. On Dec. 31, 1930, the North Davis Stake had a membership of 4,999, including 994 children. Heber J. Sheffield was the Patriarch of the stake.

Sunset Ward was organized July 24, 1916.
