Andrew Jenson, Encyclopedic History of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ( 1941)

"Zion's Panier"

"ZION'S PANIER" (Zion's Banner) was a periodical issued in the interest of the Church in Hamburg, Germany, in the German language. The first number was dated Nov. 1, 1851, the second Dec. 1st, and the third Jan. 1, 1852. As far as we can learn, only three numbers were published, and although Pres. John Taylor, before leaving Germany in the spring of 1852, had made arrangements for continuing the publication of the periodical, it appears that this was not done. John Taylor edited the three numbers at Rosenstrasse No. 27, Hamburg, and the paper was printed by F. H. Nestler and Melle in Hamburg. A storm of persecution arose against the Elders and the few converts to the restored gospel, which had been made in Hamburg, causing the activities of the Elders to stop for several years.
