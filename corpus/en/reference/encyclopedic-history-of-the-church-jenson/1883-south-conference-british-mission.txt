South Conference, British Mission

SOUTH CONFERENCE, British Mission, consisted of the Latter-day Saints residing in the southwestern part of England and was created by the amalgamation of three small conferences, namely, Bath, Bristol and Trowbridge in 1847. The South Conference existed until Jan. 3, 1862, when the South Conference and the Wiltshire Conference were amalgamated under the name of the Bristol Conference.
