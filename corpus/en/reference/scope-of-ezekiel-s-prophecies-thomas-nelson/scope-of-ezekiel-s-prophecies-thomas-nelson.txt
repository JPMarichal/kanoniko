29 Scope of Ezekiel's Prophecies

ezeKiel’s TemPle second proof 10/5/09

Some interpret this prophecy as an exact blueprint of a physical temple to be con-structed in or near Jerusalem during a future millennial (thousand-year) period. Others view Ezekiel’s restored temple not as a blueprint, but as a vision that stresses the purity and spiritual vitality of the ideal place of worship and those who will worship there. Thus, it is not intended to refer to an earthly, physical fulfillment, but expresses the truth found in the name of the new city: THE LORD IS THERE (48:35).
