Andrew Jenson, Church Chronology ( 1914)

This year the Saints in southwestern Colorado and northwestern New Mexico were organized into the Young Stake of Zion, and a new Stake (Boise) was also organized in Idaho. Permanent missionary homes were secured and dedicated in Chicago, Ill., and Los Angeles, Cal. A site for a Temple was dedicated in Alberta, Can., and Joseph F. Smith and other Church leaders made a visit to the Stakes of Zion in Canada and Arizona.

January 1, 1913 (Wednesday)

The "Deseret News" of this date contains the architect's perspective of the proposed Temple in Alberta, Canada.

January 1, 1913 (Wednesday)

Miss Emma Lucy Gates sang in the Tabernacle, Salt Lake City, to a large audience.

Wright Anderson Moore, a veteran Elder in the Church, died at Blackfoot, Idaho.

December 5, 1913 (Friday)

Pres. Joseph F. Smith and party visited the Roosevelt dam, Ariz.

December 5, 1913 (Friday)

Thomas W. Jones accidentally shot and killed himself at Ogden, Utah.

December 6, 1913 (Saturday)

The Saints residing at Chandler, Maricopa Co., Ariz., were organized into a Ward by Elder Joseph F. Smith, jun., with Henry L. Peterson as Bishop. Isaac hunt was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Pine Ward (Maricopa Stake), Ariz.

December 6, 1913 (Saturday)

A branch of the Church was organized at Nyssa, Oregon; E.A. Trent, president.

December 7, 1913 (Sunday)

A branch of the Church at Nampa, Idaho, was organized into a Ward with Alexander Bills as Bishop; another branch was organized at Kuna, Idaho, and made a part of the Nampa Ward.

December 7, 1913 (Sunday)

The Saints at Weiser, Porterville, and Tamerock, in Idaho, were organized as the Weiser Ward, with William E. Stoddard as Bishop.

December 7, 1913 (Sunday)

William Udall Schofield was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Alamo Ward (Moapa Stake), Lincoln Co., Nevada.

December 8, 1913 (Monday)

Ground was broken for a Ward chapel in the recently organized Ensign Ward (Ensign Stake), Salt Lake City.

December 8, 1913 (Monday)

James W. Ferrin, a Utah pioneer of 1852, died at Pleasant View, Weber Co., Utah.

December 11, 1913 (Thursday)

Mrs. Julia Parks Lindsay, formerly president of the Relief Societies of the Bear Lake Stake, died at Moreland, Idaho.

December 11, 1913 (Thursday)

Gustave T. Anderson of Taylorsville, Alberta, Canada, died at Lulea, Sweden, where he labored as a missionary. His remains were sent home in care of Elder Matthias Erickson.

December 12, 1913 (Friday)

Mrs. Lydia M. Thornton Nell died in New Castle, Utah; and Sanford Porter, a Utah pioneer of 1847 and a Mormon Battalion man, died at Logan, Utah.

December 13, 1913 (Saturday)

Mrs. Eliza P. Kingsbury, widow of the late Joseph C. Kingsbury, died in Salt Lake City.

December 14, 1913 (Sunday)

Pres. Joseph F. Smith and party returned to Salt Lake City from their visit to Arizona.

December 14, 1913 (Sunday)

Samuel Allred was ordained a Bishop and set apart to succeed the late Lauritz O. Larsen as Bishop of Spring City, Sanpete Co., Utah.

December 14, 1913 (Sunday)

William A. Seegmiller, succeeded Virginius Bean as Bishop of the Richfield Second Ward, Utah.

December 14, 1913 (Sunday)

Adolph Merz was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Mount Pleasant North Ward (North Sanpete Stake), Sanpete Co., Utah.

December 14, 1913 (Sunday)

John Neild, a Utah pioneer of early days, died at Afton, Wyoming.

December 18, 1913 (Thursday)

Mrs. Eliza Lyons Groo, a handcart veteran and widow of the late Isaac Groo, died in Salt Lake City, and Jacob W. Hendrickson, an early pioneer of Brigham City, died at Collinston, Utah.

December 19, 1913 (Friday)

The Shelter Home of the Relief Societies, established in the Seventeenth Ward, was dedicated by Pres. Joseph F. Smith.

December 19, 1913 (Friday)

A new chapel was dedicated by James E. Talmage in the Richfield Second Ward, Sevier Co., Utah.

December 19, 1913 (Friday)

Mrs. Rebecca Standing a Utah pioneer of 1857, died at Lehi, Utah.

December 21, 1913 (Sunday)

James W. Vance was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Alpine Ward (Alpine Stake), Utah Co., Utah.

December 21, 1913 (Sunday)

Mrs. Sophia K. Cook, a Utah pioneer of 1848, died near Provo, Utah.

December 22, 1913 (Monday)

Mrs. Caroline Berg Osterman, a Utah pioneer of 1868 and widow of the late James Osterman, died in Salt Lake City; Benjamin Franklin Cowley, a faithful Church worker, died at Venice, Sevier Co., Utah, and Mrs. Mary Jane Tuft, a faithful Church member, died at Tabor, Alberta, Canada.

December 23, 1913 (Tuesday)

Mrs. Augusta H. Hansen, a Utah pioneer of 1854, died in Salt Lake City, and Lars L. Olson, a Utah pioneer of 1866, died at Santaquin, Utah.

December 24, 1913 (Wednesday)

Andrew P. Schow, formerly Bishop of Escalante, Utah, died at Manti, Utah.

December 26, 1913 (Friday)

Andrew C. Nelson, State Superintendent of Public schools, died in Salt Lake City, and Christopher Burton, sen., a pioneer, died at Kaysville, Utah.

December 28, 1913 (Sunday)

A new Ward chapel was dedicated by Pres. Joseph F. Smith in the Murray First Ward, Salt Lake City.

December 28, 1913 (Sunday)

A new Ward chapel was dedicated by Apostle James E. Talmage in the Ogden Sixth Ward; the building cost $25,000.

December 28, 1913 (Sunday)

The Saints at Bliss, Idaho, were organized as a branch of the Church, independent of the Manard Ward, with Herbert E. Hansen as president.

December 28, 1913 (Sunday)

A branch reorganization took place at Kuma, Idaho; Robert J. Ord was chosen presiding Elder.

December 29, 1913 (Monday)

A new Ward was organized in the Pocatello Stake, Idaho, named Arimo, with Lucian C. Farr, jun., as Bishop.

December 29, 1913 (Monday)

Mrs. Adelia Ann Bishop Pratt, a Utah pioneer of 1851 and widow of the late Orson Pratt, died in Salt Lake City, and Henry Wood, a faithful Church worker, died in Smithfield, Utah.

December 30, 1913 (Tuesday)

John Webster, a pioneer blacksmith of Utah, died in Taylorsville, Salt Lake Co., Utah, and Mrs. Mary Etherington Stranger, a Utah pioneer of 1855, died at Lincoln, Idaho.
