Andrew Jenson, Church Chronology ( 1914)

Church Chronology

1805-1820

During the two first decades of the Nineteenth Century a number of men who were destined to take a most active part in the ushering in of the new gospel dispensation were born. Chief among these was the Prophet Joseph Smith, to whom the Father and the Son appeared in a glorious vision and revealed the apostate condition of the religious world.
