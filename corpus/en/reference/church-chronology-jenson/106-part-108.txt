Andrew Jenson, Church Chronology ( 1914)

This year witnessed the death of many prominent and faithful Elders in the Church, among them Pres. John Henry Smith and Patriarch John Smith. Charles W. Penrose was chosen as a counselor in the First Presidency and James E. Talmage as one of the Twelve Apostles. The missionaries of the Church met with considerable opposition in some of the European countries.
