Andrew Jenson, Church Chronology ( 1914)

This year four new Stakes of Zion were organized, namely, three (Ogden, North Weber and Bear River) in Utah, and one (Rigby) in Idaho. The membership of the Church increased in a satisfactory manner in the different missionary fields, as well as in the Stakes of Zion.

September 16, 1908 (Wednesday)

Mrs. Georgiana Fox Young, an active Relief Society worker, died in Salt Lake City.

September 17, 1908 (Thursday)

Willard L. Jones was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Overton Ward (St. George Stake), Lincoln Co., Nev.

September 18, 1908 (Friday)

John M. Bunker was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the St. Thomas Ward (St. George Stake), Lincoln Co., Nev.

September 20, 1908 (Sunday)

Wm. F. Moss, a Utah pioneer of 1861, died at East Garland, Box Elder Co., Utah.

September 20, 1908 (Sunday)

Edward A. Olsen was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Ogden Fourth Ward, and Henry W. Gwilliam was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Ogden Sixth Ward (Ogden Stake), Weber Co., Utah.

September 21, 1908 (Monday)

Charles R. Thomason was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Independence Ward, Fremont Co., Idaho.

September 25, 1908 (Friday)

The Stone and Snowville Wards, formerly in Box Elder Stake, were added to the Malad Stake.

September 25, 1908 (Friday)

The Fielding, Plymouth and Riverside Wards were separated from the Malad Stake to form a part of a contemplated new Stake.

September 26, 1908 (Saturday)

Geo. H. Snelgrove died in Salt Lake City.

September 27, 1908 (Sunday)

Carl E. Peterson was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Ogden Third Ward (North Weber Stake), Weber Co., Utah.

September 27, 1908 (Sunday)

James Peter Jensen was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Malad Ward, and Stephen Wright was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Pleasant View Ward, both in Malad Stake, Bannock Co., Idaho.

September 27, 1908 (Sunday)

A new Ward called Garfield was organized in the Rigby Stake, with Hyrum Severson as Bishop.

October 3, 1908 (Saturday)

Mrs. Anna Warren Perkins, a Utah pioneer of 1850, died in Salt Lake City, aged 97 years, and Mrs. Elizabeth Hill Maughan, a Utah pioneer of 1852, died in Wellsville, Utah.

October 4, 1908 (Sunday)

The semi-annual conference of the Church convened in Salt Lake City; it was continued on the 5th and 6th.

October 5, 1908 (Monday)

Hyrum Severson was ordained a Bishop in Salt Lake City and set apart to preside over the Garfield Ward (Rigby Stake), Fremont Co., Ida.

October 7, 1908 (Wednesday)

Marion L. Corbett was ordained a Bishop in Salt Lake City and set apart to preside over the Francis Ward, Summit Co., Utah.

October 10, 1908 (Saturday)

Samuel H. Alexander, a Utah pioneer of 1857, died at Mill Creek, Salt Lake Co., Utah.

October 11, 1908 (Sunday)

Edwin A. Bingham was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Wilson Ward (North Weber Stake), Weber Co., Utah.

October 11, 1908 (Sunday)

The north part of the Box Elder Stake of Zion was separated from said Stake and organized as the Bear River Stake, with Milton H. Welling as president; Peter M. Hansen, first, and Joseph Jensen, second counselor. The new Stake at the organization contained the following Wards: Beaver, Bothwell, Deweyville, East Garland, Elwood, Fielding, Garland, Plymouth, Riverside, and Thatcher.

October 11, 1908 (Sunday)

Arthur R. Capener was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Garland Ward, and Knud H. Fridal was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Elwood Ward (Bear River Stake), Box Elder Co., Utah.

October 13, 1908 (Tuesday)

David E. Arave was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Taylor Ward (Blackfoot Stake), Bingham Co., Idaho.

October 14, 1908 (Wednesday)

Dudley Leavitt, sen., a Utah pioneer of 1855, and a renowned Indian missionary, died at Bunkerville, Lincoln Co., Nev.

October 17, 1908 (Saturday)

Judge Joseph C. Rich, a Utah pioneer of 1847, one of the first settlers of Bear Lake Valley, Idaho, died at Centerville, Davis Co., Utah, and Mrs. Elizabeth Kingsford, a Utah handcart pioneer of 1856, died at Ogden, Utah.

October 18, 1908 (Sunday)

Thomas E. Powell was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Marriott Ward (North Weber Stake), Weber Co., Utah.

October 18, 1908 (Sunday)

Chas. Henry Berrett, a Utah pioneer of 1847, died at Nephi, Utah.

October 19, 1908 (Monday)

Thos. Aubrey, an early missionary in Great Britain and a Utah pioneer of 1868, died in Salt Lake City.

October 22, 1908 (Thursday)

Wm. F. Maylett, a Utah pioneer of 1856, died at Manti, Utah.

October 24, 1908 (Saturday)

Henry W. Valentine was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Richfield Ward (San Luis Stake), Conejos Co., Colo.

October 25, 1908 (Sunday)

A new Latter-day Saints meeting house was dedicated by Pres. Jos. R. Shepherd, at Laketown, Rich Co., Utah.

October 25, 1908 (Sunday)

John Halls was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Huntsville Ward (Ogden Stake), Weber Co., Utah.

October 27, 1908 (Tuesday)

Wm. T. Wayment was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Warren Ward (North Weber Stake), Weber Co., Utah.

October 28, 1908 (Wednesday)

John D.H. McAllister, a Utah pioneer of 1851, was found dead in his barn in Salt Lake City.

October 29, 1908 (Thursday)

Elder Samuel A. Wilcox, a Utah pioneer of 1861, died near Vina, Cal.

October 31, 1908 (Saturday)

Joseph Herpole, a pioneer of Utah, died at Castleton, Grand Co., Utah, aged 107 years.

November 1, 1908 (Sunday)

The Mound Fort Ward (Ogden Stake), Weber Co., Utah, was divided, part of it being made the Ogden Eighth Ward, with James Taylor as Bishop, and the other part the Ogden Seventh Ward, with Miles L. Jones as Bishop.

November 1, 1908 (Sunday)

Miles L. Jones was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Ogden Seventh Ward, and James Taylor was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Ogden Eighth Ward.

November 1, 1908 (Sunday)

Carl Oscar Turnquist was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Lynn Ward (North Weber Stake), Weber Co., Utah.

November 1, 1908 (Sunday)

Willis Twitchell was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Manila Ward, (Woodruff Stake), Uinta Co., Wyo.

November 1, 1908 (Sunday)

Marcus Skeen was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Oasis Ward, Millard Co., h.

November 1, 1908 (Sunday)

John Jeremy, a Utah pioneer of 1849, died in Salt Lake City, and Peter Fredericksen Petersen, a Utah pioneer of 1860, died at Brigham City, Utah.

November 1, 1908 (Sunday)

The first number of the "Ka Elele Oiaio," a semi-monthly periodical published in the interest of the Hawaiian mission, was published in Honolulu.

November 3, 1908 (Tuesday)

The general election in Utah gave the Republicans a universal victory, the national and state tickets being elected by a plurality of about twenty thousand; the American party was defeated everywhere. Wm. H. Taft was elected president of the United States.

November 6, 1908 (Friday)

John L. Herrick was appointed to succeed Joseph A. McRae as president of the Western States mission.

November 7, 1908 (Saturday)

Chas. Guild, a Utah pioneer of 1854, died at Piedmont, Wyo.

November 8, 1908 (Sunday)

Moses Wright was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Lincoln Ward, Bingham Co., Idaho.

November 15, 1908 (Sunday)

Taylor Nelson was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Riverdale Ward, Oneida Co., Idaho.

November 16, 1908 (Monday)

Mrs. Mary Ann Soffe, a Utah pioneer of 1856, died at South Jordan, Salt Lake Co., Utah, aged 90 years.

November 20, 1908 (Friday)

Henry Chas. Barrell, once the body guard Pres. John Taylor, died suddenly in Salt Lake City.

November 23, 1908 (Monday)

John B. Cornwell, a Church veteran, died at Salem, Utah Co., Utah.

November 24, 1908 (Tuesday)

Mrs. Mary Lindberg, one of the early members of the Church in Sweden and a Utah pioneer of 1856, died at Tooele, Utah.

November 25, 1908 (Wednesday)

Edward W. Wade, formerly Bishop of Pleasant View, Weber Co., Utah, died at Ogden, Utah.

November 27, 1908 (Friday)

Seymour L. Clark, deputy sheriff of Weber Co., Utah, was murdered at Uintah while in discharge of his duty.

November 28, 1908 (Saturday)

Oliver A. Hansen, of Bear River City, Utah, who was returning from a mission to Norway, died in Chicago, Ill., and Mrs. Celecia Hyde, widow of Patriarch Chas. W. Hyde, died in Salt Lake City.

November 30, 1908 (Monday)

Arnold H. Conover, a Utah pioneer of 1847, died at Challis, Custer Co., Idaho.

December 1, 1908 (Tuesday)

Mrs. Hannah Forbes, a Utah handcart pioneer of 1856, died at Layton, Davis Co., Utah.

December 5, 1908 (Saturday)

Mrs. Jane Mabey Holt, a Utah pioneer of 1862, and a prominent Y.L.M.I.A. worker, died at Bountiful, Davis Co., Utah.

December 6, 1908 (Sunday)

Martin M. Jensen was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Mantua Ward, Box Elder Co., Utah.

December 6, 1908 (Sunday)

Chas. A. Orme succeeded Wm. Armstrong as president of the Australian mission.

December 12, 1908 (Saturday)

James T. Hoagland was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Bramwell Ward (Union Stake), Union Co., Oregon.

December 13, 1908 (Sunday)

Henry E. Reddish was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Pocatello Second Ward (Pocatello Stake), Bannock Co., Idaho. This Ward was created on this day by dividing Pocatello into two Wards.

December 14, 1908 (Monday)

Elders Thos. E. Orton, of North Ogden, Utah, and Edgar J. Alder, of Preston, Idaho, were arrested in Grand Rapids, Michigan, for selling tracts without a license. They pleaded not guilty in the police court, but were sent to jail, where they received severe treatment from the other prisoners.

December 20, 1908 (Sunday)

Fannie B. Gorrings, a Utah pioneer of 1855, died at Marion, Cassia Co., Idaho.

December 24, 1908 (Thursday)

Mrs. Elizabeth Evans Watson, widow of the late Bishop James C. Watson, died in Salt Lake City, and Patriarch David John, a Utah pioneer of 1861, died suddenly at Provo, Utah.

December 24, 1908 (Thursday)

Carl A. Carlson was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Poplar Grove Ward (Pioneer Stake), Salt Lake City, which was organized on that day.

December 27, 1908 (Sunday)

Robt. Holmes, a Utah pioneer of 1847, died at Willard Box Elder Co., Utah.

December 27, 1908 (Sunday)

Sarah Ann Gregory Hales, an early member of the Church, died at Huntington, Emery Co., Utah.

December 27, 1908 (Sunday)

Joseph N. Arbon was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Arbon Ward (Malad Stake), Oneida Co., Idaho.

December 30, 1908 (Wednesday)

Henry C. Jacobs was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Ogden Fifth Ward (Weber Stake), Weber Co., Utah.

December 30, 1908 (Wednesday)

Mrs. Catherine Gustin, a Utah pioneer of 1847, died at Puyallup, Pierce Co., Wash.
