Biblical genealogies (e.g., in Genesis, 1 Chronicles, etc.) are not necessarily

sequential in the precise sense. In keeping with ancient genealogical practices,

names are sometimes omitted within the list. The Hebrew term translated “begot”

may also be translated “became the ancestor of.”

The patriarchs who lived before the Flood had an average lifespan of about 900 years (Gen. 5). The ages of

post-Flood patriarchs dropped rapidly and gradually leveled off (Gen. 11). Some suggest that this is due to

major environmental changes brought about by the Flood.
