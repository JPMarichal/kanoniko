enCamPmenT Of The TriBes

With the military census completed in chapter 1, instructions for the organiza-

tion of the tribes and their armies are given. The camp is organized with three tribes on each side of the tabernacle with Yahweh’s dwelling place in the midst of the camp. Further, when they break camp and march, the six tribes on the east and south set out, followed by the Levites with the tabernacle traveling in the center (2:17), fol-lowed by the six tribes on the west and north, respectively. Whether encamped or on the march, the tabernacle is central. One tribe is given priority among the three on each side of the tabernacle, Judah on the east (2:9), Reuben on the south (2:16), Ephraim on the west (2:24), and Dan on the north (2:31).
