The Gospel writers devoted many pages to the events leading up to the cruci-

fixion of Jesus. The final week of His earthly ministry began with the triumphal

entry into Jerusalem and the “Hosannas” from the crowd that changed to cries of

“Crucify Him” before the week was over. Jesus apparently spent most of the week

teaching in the temple area during the day. His evenings were spent in the home of

Mary, Martha, and Lazarus in Bethany. Significant events during this week included

the plot of the Sanhedrin, Jesus’ betrayal and arrest, the trials of Jesus, His journey

to Golgotha down the Jerusalem street known today as the Via Dolorosa, and the

resurrection. After His resurrection, Jesus ministered another forty days before His

ascension.
