Second Angel

He who will blow the "second trump," which will signal the resurrection of terrestrial beings, will be the "second angel" (D&C 88:99). At the end of the Millennium he will again blow his trump and "reveal the secret acts of men, and the thoughts and intents of their hearts, and the mighty works of God in the second thousand years" of earth's history (D&C 88:109). His identity is presently unknown.
