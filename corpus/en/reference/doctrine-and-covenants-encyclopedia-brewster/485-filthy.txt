Filthy

To be filthy is to be spiritually unclean, to be unable to stand in the presence of God with a clear conscience and spotless raiment. Those who willingly seek to sin, whose desires are carnal, sensual, and devilish, "cannot be sanctified by law, neither by mercy, justice, nor judgment. Therefore, they must remain filthy still."
