Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Prophet's Time

Joseph Smith stated that prophets reckon time according to the planet whereupon they reside (D&C 130:4-5). "The prophets count days and years as the people among whom they live and to whom they speak, or write. The people of Bible times and lands, it is thought, measured the years according to the phases of the moon and counted 354 days in a year, instead of 365, as now, and the prophets, of course, used the same time measure." (SS, 814.)
