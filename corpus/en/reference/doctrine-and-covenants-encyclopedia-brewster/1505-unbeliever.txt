Unbeliever

The Apostle Paul said that the unbelieving wife or husband is sanctified by the believing spouse (1 Cor. 7:10-16; D&C 74:1). An unbeliever is one who has not accepted the gospel of Jesus Christ and is outside the faith. Unbelievers may also hold membership in Christ's church but lack the commitment to practice the beliefs espoused by that church.
