Lay It to Heart

The admonition to "lay it to heart" was included in instructions given some elders of the Church in 1831 (D&C 58:5). This is similar to the admonition to "treasure up in your heart" (D&C 11:26) or to "ponder it in your heart" (Moro. 10:3). All of these admonitions mean to seriously consider something. Jehovah told the ancient Israelites to "lay up [carefully consider and remember] these my words in your heart and in your soul" (Deut. 11:18).
