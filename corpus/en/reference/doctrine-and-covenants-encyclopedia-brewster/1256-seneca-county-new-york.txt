Seneca County, New York

In the center of the state of New York lies Seneca County. From her bosom sprang the "stone cut out of the mountain without hands" that was destined to "roll forth until it has filled the whole earth" (D&C 65:2; 109:72-73; Dan. 2:34-35, 44). It was within the borders of Seneca County that the translation of the Book of Mormon was completed and the Three Witnesses thereto received manifestations from God (D&C 128:20). Here twenty sections of the Doctrine and Covenants were received and recorded, and the Church had its humble beginnings at the Whitmer home in Fayette (HC 1:75-80).
