Mantle

The Lord has counseled to "clothe yourselves with the bond of charity, as with a mantle, which is the bond of perfectness and peace" (D&C 88:125). A mantle is a robe or a cloak and the term is often used symbolically to express a covering that characterizes a trait or the authority which an individual possesses. For example, when Elijah cast his mantle upon Elisha, this was symbolic of the authority being transferred from the one to the other (1 Kgs. 19:19). Elisha later used both the mantle of cloth and the mantle of authority to perform miracles (2 Kgs. 2:13-14).
