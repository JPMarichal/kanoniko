Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Herbs

Twice in the Doctrine and Covenants herbs are referred to as something that can have a positive effect on the body. The sick should be "nourished with all tenderness, with herbs and mild food" (D&C 42:43). And "all wholesome herbs God hath ordained for the constitution, nature, and use of man" (D&C 89:10).

A clarifying footnote in the 1981 edition of the Doctrine and Covenants indicates that herbs refers to plants. Although it is common to think of herbs strictly in the sense of actual or alleged medicinal properties they possess, Sperry indicates that there is a broader meaning to the word: "For most practical purposes 'wholesome herbs' may be defined as edible plants or vegetables as they are commonly known" (DCC, 454).
