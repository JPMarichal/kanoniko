Fruit of ... Loins

"The loins," said President Harold B. Lee, "are those portions of the body between the lower ribs and the hips in which are located the vital generative organs" (SHP, 330). The fruit of the loins is the product of those pro-creative powers, that is, one's posterity (D&C 132:30; 2 Ne. 3:5).
