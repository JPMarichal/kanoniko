Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Jacobites

The Jacobites spoken of in Doctrine and Covenants 3:17 are descendants of Jacob, a faithful son of Lehi (1 Ne. 18:7). The term Jacobite was a family name used not only in the days of Jacob but also hundreds of years later, even though in a broader sense they were also referred to as Nephites (Jac. 1:13-14; 4 Ne. 1:36; Morm. 1:8).

Jacob beheld the glory of the Lord in his youth and also beheld angels (2 Ne. 2:3-4; 2 Ne. 10:3; 2 Ne. 11:3; Jac. 7:5, 12). He was a prophet among his people and wrote the book of Jacob in the Book of Mormon. His sermons are also found in his brother Nephi's records (2 Ne. 6, 9-11). His descendants, or those having his blood lineage, can be found among some of the Lamanites of our day.
