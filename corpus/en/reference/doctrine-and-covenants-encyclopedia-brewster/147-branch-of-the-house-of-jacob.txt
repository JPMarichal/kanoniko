Branch of the House of Jacob

The term branch is used to denote the posterity of someone in the same sense that branches of a tree derive their existence from the trunk thereof. The specific phrase, branch of the house of Jacob is unique to the Doctrine and Covenants (10:60) and refers to a particular line of the posterity of Israel (Jacob). This specific branch was that body of people whom God led to the Americas shortly after 600 B.C. and whom we presently know as Lamanites. These people are descendants of the "other sheep" of whom the Savior anciently spoke and who received his resurrected presence (John 10:16; 3 Ne. 15:21-24). They are descendants of Jacob through his sons Joseph (1 Ne. 6:2) and Judah (D&C 19:27; Hel. 6:10).
