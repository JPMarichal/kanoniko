Children of Judah

The descendants of Judah, son of Jacob (Israel), are spoken of as the "children of Judah." In his dedicatory prayer at the Kirtland Temple, the Prophet petitioned the Lord to allow these people to return to Jerusalem and the land of their inheritance (D&C 109:61-64).
