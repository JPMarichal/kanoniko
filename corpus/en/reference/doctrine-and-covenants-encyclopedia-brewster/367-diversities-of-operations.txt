Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Diversities of Operations

Among the "gifts of the spirit" is the "diversities of operations" (D&C 46:16; 1 Cor. 12:6). According to Smith and Sjodahl: "This refers to various spiritual influences at work, for instance such as are manifested in Spiritism, anarchism, and the numerous other 'isms.' To know whether an influence with a professedly moral, or reformatory, aim is from the Holy Spirit, or from another source, is a special gift." (SS, 274.)
