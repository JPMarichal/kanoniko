Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Republic

The word republic is found in a declaration of belief regarding governments and laws (D&C 134:3). A republic is simply a government having a chief of state who is not a monarch (king or absolute ruler with unlimited authority) but who is subject to the will of the people. Supreme power in this form of government rests with the people rather than with a single sovereign.
