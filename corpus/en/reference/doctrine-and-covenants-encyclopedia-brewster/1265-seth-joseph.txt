Seth (Joseph)

During a period of time when pseudonyms were being used to disguise the identity of people mentioned in the revelations, Joseph, the son of Jacob, was referred to as "Seth" (D&C 96:7; pre-1981 edition). In most instances, only the names of the living were disguised, but in this case the identity of an Old Testament prophet long since deceased was hidden from the world (see HC 1:255). In current editions of the Doctrine and Covenants, only Jacob's actual name is shown.
