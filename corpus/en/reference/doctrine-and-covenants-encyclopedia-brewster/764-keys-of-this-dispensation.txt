Keys of This Dispensation

The "keys of this dispensation" (D&C 110:16) refer to the keys of the dispensation of the fulness of times. Joseph Smith received these keys and will hold them throughout eternity, under the direction of Adam, who, under the direction of the Savior, presides over all dispensations (CHMR 1:388; TPJS, 157-58).
