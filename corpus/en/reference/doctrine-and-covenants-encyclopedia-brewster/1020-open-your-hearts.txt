Open Your Hearts

Occasionally the Lord will admonish us to open our hearts (D&C 31:7; 63:1). According to Smith and Sjodahl, "To 'open' one's 'heart' is to listen to the Word of God with love and affection, and with an eager desire to understand it and to do the will of God" (SS, 373).

In opening your heart you allow the free-flowing circulation of the eternal life-giving substance of the Spirit, which is the opposite of hardening your heart.
