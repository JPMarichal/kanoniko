Land of Inheritance

Reference to a land of inheritance may have reference to Missouri, the site of the future New Jerusalem (D&C 101:1), or it may have reference to the paradisiacal earth during the Millennium or even thereafter when it attains its celestial state (D&C 38:19; AF, 375-81; MD, 210-11).
