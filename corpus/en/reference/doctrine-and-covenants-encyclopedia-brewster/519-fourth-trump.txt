Fourth Trump

The fourth trump is the signal at the end of the Millennium that will "quicken" the "sons of perdition," bringing them forth from the depths of the grave to receive their resurrected bodies, which will tabernacle their tortured spirits throughout eternity. "They shall return again to their own place ... because they were not willing to enjoy that which they might have received" (D&C 88:32, 102).
