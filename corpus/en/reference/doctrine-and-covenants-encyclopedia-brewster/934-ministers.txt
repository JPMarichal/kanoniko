Ministers

Those who serve as authorized agents of the Lord in seeing to the needs of others and carrying out the work of God are ministers (see D&C 84:111; 107:97; 124:137). They do not need academic credentials but rather a divine commission from Deity (Articles of Faith 1:5; Heb. 5:4).
