Little Children

When the Lord declared that "little children are redeemed from the foundation of the world" (D&C 29:46), he was speaking of children who had not yet reached the age of accountability and were therefore innocent (see D&C 29:46-47; 68:27; JST, Gen. 17:11; Moro. 8:12, 20). We are commanded to become as little children (i.e., innocent) in order to merit God's kingdom (Matt. 18:3; Mosiah 3:19).

The title of "little children" can also be used figuratively in referring to those whose judgment is not mature on a given subject (see D&C 78:17; John 13:33).
