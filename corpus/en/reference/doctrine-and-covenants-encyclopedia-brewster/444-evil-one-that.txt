Evil One, That

"Satan is the Evil One, a name-title signifying that he is the embodiment of all evil and all wickedness, that he is in opposition to all righteousness, and that he is the father of lies and the author of evil. (2 Ne. 4:27; 9:28; Alma 46:8; Hela. 12:4; Ether 8:25.)" (MD, 246.) Because the devil's domain is full of darkness (Rev. 16:10), "light and truth forsake that evil one" (D&C 93:37).
