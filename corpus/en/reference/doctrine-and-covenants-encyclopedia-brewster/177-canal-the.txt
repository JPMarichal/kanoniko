Canal, The

An 1831 revelation warned the Prophet Joseph and his companions not to continue traveling upon the waters they had previously traveled but to "journey ... upon the canal" (D&C 61:23). "By this the Lord probably means canals, of which there were a number in the region concerned" (DCC, 258).
