Broken Hearts

One of the requisites for baptism is that one should manifest a "broken heart" (D&C 20:37; 3 Ne. 9:20). It is usually associated with a "contrite spirit" (D&C 56:17, 18; 59:8; 2 Ne. 2:7; 3 Ne. 12:19; Morm. 2:14; Ether 4:15; Moro. 6:2). "What is a broken heart?" asked Elder Joseph Fielding Smith. "One that is humble, one that is touched by the Spirit of the Lord, and which is willing to abide in all the covenants and obligations which the Gospel entails" (CR, Oct. 1941, p. 93). To one who offers the Lord such a heart will the "gates of hell be shut continually," and the "gates of righteousness" be open (2 Ne. 4:32).
