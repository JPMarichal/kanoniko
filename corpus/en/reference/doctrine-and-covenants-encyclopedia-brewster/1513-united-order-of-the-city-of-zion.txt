United Order of the City of Zion

On February 9, 183l, the Lord revealed the basis of the United Order (D&C 42). This was the law of the Church for the next three years, although all did not subscribe to the conditions thereof and ultimately the law was repealed (MFP 6:200).

Several months before its demise, the Lord instructed the Saints to become organized in two major "orders." One was to be established in Missouri and another in Ohio. The former was to be called the United Order of the City of Zion, which encompassed the Saints in the "land of Zion," Missouri (D&C 104:47-50; see also D&C 57:1-2).
