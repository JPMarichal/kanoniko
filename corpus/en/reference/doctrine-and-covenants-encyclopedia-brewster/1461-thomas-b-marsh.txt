Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Thomas (B. Marsh)

Occasionally in the Doctrine and Covenants, the Lord will refer to a man only by his first name. In most instances, the identity of the individual is understood through previous verses or the preface to that section.

In D&C 118:2 the name Thomas is given without these identifying qualities. Nevertheless, the revelation is addressed to "the Twelve," of whom Thomas B. Marsh was the President. He was the only member of the Twelve with the given name of Thomas.
