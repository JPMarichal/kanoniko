Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Third Trump

Section 88 reveals that two series of seven trumps will sound, beginning with the return of the Savior at the beginning of the Millennium and ending with the last great battle between the forces of good and evil at the end of that thousand-year period. The "third trump" of the first series will sound at the end of the Millennium, announcing the resurrection of those of a telestial glory (D&C 88: 100-101; DS 2:297-98).
