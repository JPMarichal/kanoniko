Quorum of the Nauvoo House

The "quorum of the Nauvoo House" mentioned in Doctrine and Covenants 124:119 has reference to the building committee assigned to see that this special house was built according to the Lord's specifications. Members of this "quorum" were George Miller, Lyman Wight, John Smith,

and Peter Haws (D&C 124:62). Although it was to be a boarding house for strangers, the house was to be built "unto" the name of the Lord-holy, an abode where weary travelers could "contemplate the glory of Zion" (D&C 124:23, 24, 60).

Because of these high objectives, the Lord declared that all who held stock in the Nauvoo House must be believers in the Book of Mormon and the other revelations given through Joseph the Prophet (D&C 124:118). This was no ordinary business enterprise, but a sacred religious project. Those mentioned as worthy to hold stock therein were Joseph Smith, Vinson Knight, Hyrum Smith, Isaac Galland, William Marks, Henry Sherwood, and William Law (D&C 124:56, 74, 77, 78, 80, 81, 82).
