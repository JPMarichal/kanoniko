Testimony of Their Fathers

Based upon a careful reading of verses 16-20 in section 3, "the testimony of their fathers" refers to the records or plates from which the Book of Mormon was translated. These records would bear special testimony of the Savior to all who had descended from the ancient inhabitants of the Americas. These descendants are known today as Lamanites.
