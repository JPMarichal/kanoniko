Crafts of Men

The Lord expressed joy when Warren A. Cowdery "separated himself from the crafts of men" (D&C 106:6). Beyond its use as a synonym for a vessel that travels on water (D&C 60:5), the word craft generally refers to a skilled occupation. However, in the sense in which it is used with Cowdery it refers to something derogatory, an unrighteous activity.
