Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Law of Christ

The Lord declared that those "who are not sanctified through the law which I have given unto you, even the law of Christ, must inherit" kingdoms other than the celestial kingdom (D&C 88:21). The law of Christ encompasses all of the commandments, covenants, and ordinances in the gospel that set the requirements for receiving a fulness of the glory of the Father in his eternal presence.
