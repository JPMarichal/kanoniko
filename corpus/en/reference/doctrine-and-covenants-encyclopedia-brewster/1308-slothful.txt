Slothful

"He that is slothful shall not be counted worthy to stand," declared the Lord (D&C 107:100). The words slothful or slothfulness appear five times in the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C 58:26, 29; 90:18; 101:50; 107:100). They are also found in the Book of Mormon (Alma 37:46; 60:14), the Old Testament (Prov. 15:19; 19:15), and the New Testament (Matt. 25:26; Rom. 12:11; Heb. 6:12).
