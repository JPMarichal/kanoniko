Magna Charta

In a broad sense, the "magna charta of the United States," as mentioned in the memorial written by John Taylor (D&C 135:7), refers to the constitution of our country, which guarantees the rights and privileges that were so flagrantly violated, leading to the martyrdoms of Joseph and Hyrum Smith.

The word comes from the magna charta, or Great Charter, to which the English barons forced King John to affix his seal June 15, 1215. It was based on an earlier charter but included rights and principles which later came into existence and laid the foundation for the security of English political and personal liberty.
