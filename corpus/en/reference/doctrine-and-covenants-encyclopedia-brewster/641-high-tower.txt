High Tower

Anciently, after being delivered from danger, David spoke of the Lord as being his rock, fortress, deliverer, strength, buckler, horn of salvation, and high tower (2 Sam. 22:3; Ps. 18:2). In modern times the Lord promised to be the salvation and high tower of Zion (D&C 97:20).

A tower is symbolic of strength or readiness to repel incursions by the enemy. The higher the tower, the more territory that can be watched. God is the ultimate High Tower. His omniscience and omnipotence can keep one safe from enemies by giving sufficient warning so that appropriate defenses can be made ready.
