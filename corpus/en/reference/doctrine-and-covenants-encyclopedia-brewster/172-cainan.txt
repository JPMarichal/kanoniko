Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Cainan

In a revelation on priesthood, the line of authority from Adam through the early patriarchs reveals that a great-grandson of Adam was a man named Cainan (D&C 107:45). The Lord "called upon Cainan in the wilderness in the fortieth year of his age," and he then received his ordination from Adam at the age of eighty-seven. He was among the great high priests accompanying Adam in the great council held in the valley of Adam-ondi-Ahman, three years before Adam's death (D&C 107:53). Cainan was born in the ninetieth year of his father Enos's life, and he himself begat his son Mahalaleel in the seventieth year of his life. A "land of promise" was named in his honor (Gen. 5:9-14; Moses 6:17-19). He is also mentioned as a forefather of Joseph, the husband of Mary (Luke 3:37-38).
