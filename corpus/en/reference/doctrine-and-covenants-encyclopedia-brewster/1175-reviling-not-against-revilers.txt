Reviling Not Against Revilers

To those called to "declare glad tidings"-the gospel of Jesus Christ-the admonition to "revile not against revilers" must not go unheeded (D&C 19:29-30). To revile is to abuse verbally. Though our enemies may rant, rave, and revile against us, our charge is to return such ill-treatment with the gospel of love, as is befitting the children of our Father (Matt. 5:44-45:3 Ne. 12:44-45).
