Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Presiding Elder

The title "presiding elder" is used in two instances in the Doctrine and Covenants. First, it refers to the President of the high Priesthood of the Church, or the President of the Church, who at the time was Joseph Smith (D&C 20:66-67; 88:140; 124:125). It should be noted that Oliver Cowdery, as the "second elder of the Church," was a presiding elder, but always subservient to the Prophet Joseph Smith (D&C 20:3; DS 1:212; 3:165; GT 1:155). In a more general sense, "presiding elder" refers to one who occupies a presiding position over a body of the Church or quorum of the priesthood. "Of necessity there are presidents, or presiding officers," said the Lord (D&C 107:21). For example, "there must needs be presiding elders to preside over those who are of the office of an elder" (D&C 107:60).
