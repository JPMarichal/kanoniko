Zion

There are over two hundred references to "Zion" in the Doctrine and Covenants. In most instances, reference is made to a particular location-the state of Missouri; and more specifically to Jackson County, even the city of Independence (D&C 57:1-3). This is where the future "Center Stake of Zion" will be organized and a great temple built prior to the coming of Christ. The Prophet Joseph Smith declared, "The whole of America is Zion itself from north to south" (TPJS, 362).Zion is also used to describe theancient city of Enoch (D&C 38:4;Moses 7:19); a hill (D&C 76:66);and, most important, a people(D&C 97:21; Moses 7: 18). A placeof Zion will only be establishedwhen a people of Zion are ready for occupancy.
