Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Morrow

Authorized ministers who go forth to preach the gospel are counseled to "take no thought for the morrow" (D&C 84:81; Matt. 6:34; 3 Ne. 13:34). According to Webster, morrow could mean one of three things: (1) morning, (2) the next day, or (3) the time immediately after a specified event.
