Alphus

The word Alphus is used to refer to the Lord in a revelation received June 1, 1833 (D&C 95:17). It is a derivative of the Greek word alpha, which is the first letter of that alphabet and denotes "the beginning." Alphus is the Greek word alpha with a Latin ending.
