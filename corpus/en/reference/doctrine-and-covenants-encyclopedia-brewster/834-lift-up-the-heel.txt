Lift Up the Heel

To "lift up the heel against" someone (D&C 121:16) is to treat him treacherously, brutally, violently, or without feeling (Dummelow, 345). Judas lifted up his heel against the Savior when he betrayed the Master into the hands of wicked men (Ps. 41:9; John 13:18).
