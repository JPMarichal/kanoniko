Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Ashley, Major N.

At a conference held in Amherst, Ohio, in January 1832, a number of the elders expressed a desire to know what the Lord had in mind for them to do. In response to the inquiry of the Prophet Joseph, Major N. Ashley was informed that he was to rake his missionary journey into the south, along with Burr Riggs (D&C 75:17). No additional mention of Ashley is found in the Prophet's subsequent writings.
