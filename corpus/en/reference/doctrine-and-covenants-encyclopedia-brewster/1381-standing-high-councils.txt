Standing High Councils

In March 1835, a great revelation on priesthood and church government was received which spoke of the "standing high councils" of the Church (D&C 107:36). "At the time this Revelation was given, there were two standing High Councils in the Church: One in Kirtland, organized February 17th, 1834, and one in Clay County, Mo., organized July 3rd, the same year" (SS, 702). The council in Kirtland was presided over by the First Presidency of the Church, which made it unique.

Today, there is a "standing high council" in every stake of the Church, presided over by a stake presidency, and jurisdictionally confined to the areas in which they are located.
