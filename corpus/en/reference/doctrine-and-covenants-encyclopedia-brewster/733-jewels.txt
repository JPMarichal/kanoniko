Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Jewels

Twice in the Doctrine and Covenants the Lord speaks of the day when he shall make up his "jewels" (D&C 60:4; 101:3). "This is an expression found in Malachi 3:17, where 'jewels' refers to the people of God, and where the meaning seems to be that when God segregates His people from the world, His power, as that of a monarch wearing a crown of jewels, will be made manifest to all men. But the testimony concerning the truth must be given to the world before the coming of that day, in order that, when it comes, its portent may be known to all." (SS, 358.)
