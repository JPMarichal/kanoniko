Holy City

The faithful who "die in the Lord" are promised an inheritance in the "holy city" (D&C 63:49). This sanctified city of Saints is the New Jerusalem (TPJS, 86; Ether 13:5). This is the "city of Zion" (D&C 57:1-3); the "city of the heritage of God" (D&C 58:13); the "city of the living God, the heavenly place, the holiest of all" (D&C 76:66).
