Walk Uprightly

The Lord promised Joseph Smith that "all things shall work together for your good, if ye walk uprightly" (D&C 90:24). To walk uprightly is to walk erect; to be morally correct, honest, and just; and to be "pointing upward," according to Webster. It is the opposite of being stooped in sin. One who has walked uprightly can enter the presence of the Lord with a humble head held erect.

Parents are specifically commanded to teach their children to "walk uprightly before the Lord" (D&C 68:28).
