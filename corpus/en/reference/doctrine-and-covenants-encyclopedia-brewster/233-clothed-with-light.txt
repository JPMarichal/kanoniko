Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Clothed with Light

For a servant of God to be "clothed with light for a covering" (D&C 85:7) is to be divinely endowed or enveloped with authority, power, and truth. "God is light" (1 John 1:5), and he will send forth his servants in light. "Where the gospel is there is light; where the gospel is not darkness prevails" (MD, 444).
