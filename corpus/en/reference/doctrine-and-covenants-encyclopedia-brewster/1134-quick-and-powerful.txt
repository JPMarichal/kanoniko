Quick and Powerful

The Lord refers to his word as "quick and powerful" (D&C 6:2; 11:2; 12:2; 14:2; 27:1; 33:1). The Bible Dictionary defines the word quick as "living, alive." Thus, the word of the Lord is not inert but is a source of life and a moving force of action.
