Lemuelites

The Lord indicated his desire to have the Book of Mormon come to the knowledge of the Lemuelites (D&C 3:18). Specifically, the Lemuelites were originally those people who descended from Lemuel, second oldest son of Lehi (1 Ne. 2:5). They were generally known as Lamanites throughout the Book of Mormon, in deference to their following the leadership of that group (Jacob 1:13-14; Alma 3:7-8; Alma 47:35; Morm. 1:9). Lemuelite blood is intermixed among the people known as Lamanites in our day.
