Greater Priesthood

The "greater priesthood" (D&C 84:19) refers to the "priesthood which is after the holiest order of God," (D&C 84:18), which is the Melchizedek Priesthood (D&C 107:1-3). It is "greater" in comparison to the "lesser," or Aaronic, priesthood in that the Melchizedek encompasses the Aaronic.
