Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Alms

The word alms is mentioned in three of the standard works (see D&C 88:2; 112:1; 3 Ne. 13:1-4; Matt. 6:1-4). Alms comes from the Greek word meaning righteousness, or acts of religious devotion. Almsgiving is the act of donating to the poor, whether through organized religious and community activity or through one's own personal efforts.
