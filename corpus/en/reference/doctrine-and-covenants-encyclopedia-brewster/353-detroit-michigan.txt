Detroit, Michigan

An 1831 revelation instructed Hyrum Smith and John Murdock to take a missionary journey from Kirtland, Ohio, to Missouri by way of Detroit (D&C 52:8). The city of Detroit fronts the Detroit River where it connects Lake Erie with Lake St. Clair in Michigan. It was founded in 1701 by a French trader, Antoine de la Mothe Cadillac. At the time of the 1831 revelation it was the capital of the Territory of Michigan. It became a hub of early railroad operations in the 1830s, and this, combined with its accessibility for shipping, made it a prime place for industrial and commercial activity. Today, the metropolitan area of Detroit comprises one of the major industrial-commercial complexes in the United States. (See Encyclopedia Brittanica, 15th ed., vol. 5, pp. 621-22.)
