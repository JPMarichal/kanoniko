King Immanuel

The only time the title "King Immanuel" appears in the Doctrine and Covenants is in section 128, verse 22. The name applies to the Lord Jesus Christ. The title of "King" represents the regal position occupied by the Savior as our Eternal King (D&C 128:23). "Immanuel" literally means "God with us." This is the name which Gabriel announced should be given to the Son of Mary in fulfillment of the prophecy of Isaiah (Matt. 1:23; Isa. 7:14).
