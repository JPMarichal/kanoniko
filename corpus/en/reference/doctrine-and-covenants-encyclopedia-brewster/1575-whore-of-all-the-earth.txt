Whore of All the Earth

Destruction by "devouring fire" will be the ultimate destiny of "the great and abominable church, which is the whore of all the earth" (D&C 29:21; see also Rev. 17:16; 19:2). A whore is one who prostitutes her body, selling sexual favors for some kind of remuneration. The whore of all the earth is symbolic of Babylon or everything that is evil and contrary to the gospel of Jesus Christ (D&C 86:3). It is "the church of the devil Â� the mother of abominations" (1 Ne. 14:10).

This church does not represent a particular organization but includes all people who fight "against Zion" (the pure in heart) (2 Ne. 16:10; see also D&C 97:21).
