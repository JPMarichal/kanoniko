All Heights and Depths

Those who enter into the new and everlasting covenant of marriage and are faithful to those covenants are promised that they "shall inherit thrones, kingdoms, principalities, and powers, dominions, all heights and depths" (D&C 132:19). This descriptive reward indicates that nothing will be withheld from such faithful followers of the Father. In the words of his Only Begotten Son, "all that my Father hath shall be given unto [them]" (D&C 84:38). "Then shall they be gods ... because all things are subject unto them" (D&C 132:20).
