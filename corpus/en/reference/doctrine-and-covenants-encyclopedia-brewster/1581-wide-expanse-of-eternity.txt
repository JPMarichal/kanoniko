Wide Expanse of Eternity

The Savior said he was the One who "looked upon the wide expanse of eternity Â� before the world was made" (D&C 38:1). According to one commentary, the wide expanse of eternity "would, if a Hebraism, mean, 'the wide, eternal expanse,' or 'boundless space,' which is different from the expanse (raquia) of Genesis 1:6-8, that appeared on the second day of the creation. The grand truth conveyed here is, that our Lord existed before the world was made, and that, with some object in view, He surveyed space and all it contained." (SS, 198.)
