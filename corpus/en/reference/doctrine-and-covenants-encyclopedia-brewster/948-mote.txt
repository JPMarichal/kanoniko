Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Mote

The only appearance of the word mote in the Doctrine and Covenants appears in the Lord's promise that "not one hair, neither mote, shall be lost, for it is the workmanship of mine hand" (D&C 29:25). Mote means a small particle or anything minute. Thus, in the resurrection of man, earth, and beast, the Lord will not overlook even the most insignificant speck of dust. His work is all encompassing.
