Billowing Surge

Symbolically speaking, the Lord told the Prophet Joseph Smith that among the adversities which would come into his life was "the billowing surge" (D&C 122:7). A surge is something which rises and moves in waves or billows (rolling masses that resemble high waves). The billowing surges that came against the Prophet were the antagonisms, enmity, and hatred that rolled upon him and upon the cause he represented like unrelenting waves of a fierce storm.
