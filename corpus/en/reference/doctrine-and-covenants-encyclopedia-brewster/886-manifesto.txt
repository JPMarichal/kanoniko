Manifesto

The declaration ending the practice of plural marriage (OD-1) is popularly referred to as the Manifesto. It is identified as such in the 1981 edition of the Doctrine and Covenants, which includes excerpts from three addresses on the subject by Wilford Woodruff. As shown in these excerpts, the Manifesto is the result of revelation from God to his prophet, who said, "I wrote what the Lord told me to write."
