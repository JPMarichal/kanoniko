Standing Presidents

A listing of the various quorums of the priesthood in Nauvoo, with their attendant officers, is found in section 124. Among the offices listed is the presidency of the high priests quorum, which "is instituted for the purpose of qualifying those who shall be appointed standing presidents or servants over different stakes scattered abroad" (D&C 124:133-35; italics added).

It appears that one of the major functions of this priesthood quorum was to train brethren to serve in administrative functions, one of which was that of stake president. Members of high councils and bishoprics also serve in the capacity of high priests. "It is the duty of the High Priests quorum to teach the principles of government," said President Joseph F. Smith. (PCG, 124.)

At the present time, the stake presidency serves as the presidency of the high priests quorum of that stake over which they preside.
