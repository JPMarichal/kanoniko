Heaven

The word heaven often refers to the dwelling place of God and those who abide his holy presence (D&C 1:17; 110:13; 129:1-3; Alma 18:30). The plural heavens may symbolically represent the inhabitants of heaven (D&C 76:1; 1 Ne. 21:13) or the power of God (D&C 121:33). On the other hand, heavens may refer to the expanse of sky above the earth (D&C 67:2; Morm. 9:11; Moses 2:20). The term end of heaven (D&C 133:7; 2 Ne. 23:5) refers to perceived limits of the horizon rather than to an actual end of the firmament.
