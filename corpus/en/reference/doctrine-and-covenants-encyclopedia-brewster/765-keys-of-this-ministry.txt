Keys of This Ministry

According to President Joseph Fielding Smith, "the keys of this ministry" (D&C 7:7) "constituted the authority of Presidency of the Church" in the dispensation over which Peter, James, and John presided (HC 3:387; Matt. 17:1-9; D&C 81:1-2). "These keys were given at the transfiguration to these three Apostles, and they in turn gave them to Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery in this dispensation" (CHMR 1:49; D&C 27:12-13; 128:20).
