Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Eleventh Hour

The term eleventh hour is found only in the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C 33:3) and in the New Testament (Matt. 20:6, 9). The latter instance is in the context of the parable of the workers in the vineyard. President Joseph Fielding Smith wrote, "This is the 'eleventh hour.' The time in which we live is compared to the eleventh hour, and so it is in the Lord's reckoning, for we are in the closing scenes of the present world. Elder Orson F. Whitney referred to our dispensation as the 'Saturday night' of time. And, according to the parable of the men employed in the vineyard, we who labor in this hour will be rewarded if we are faithful, with equal compensation with those who labored in the previous hours, or dispensations, in the history of mankind." (CHMR 1:153; italics added.)
