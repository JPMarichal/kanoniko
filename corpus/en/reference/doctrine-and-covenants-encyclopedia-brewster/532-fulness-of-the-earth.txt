Fulness of the Earth

At the time of the creation, God gave man stewardship over all things to the end that he might use them wisely for his "food and for raiment, for taste and for smell, to strengthen the body and to enliven the soul" (D&C 59:19; see also D&C 49:19; 89; Gen. 1:27-31; Moses 2:20-31). The "fulness of the earth" (D&C 59:16) is everything God has ordained for the use of men which the earth provides. To paraphrase the psalmist, "The earth is the Lord's, and the fulness thereof" is his to give (Ps. 24:1; 1 Cor. 10:26, 28).
