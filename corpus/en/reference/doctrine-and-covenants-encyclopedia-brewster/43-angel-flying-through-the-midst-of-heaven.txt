Angel Flying through the Midst of Heaven

After quoting the prophecy that an "angel Â� having the everlasting gospel" was to appear on the earth (Rev. 14:6-7; see also D&C 133:6), Elder Rudger Clawson proclaimed: "We believe and claim as a people, that that angel has come to the earth and appeared to Joseph Smith, in the person of Moroni, who delivered to the youthful Prophet a record familiarly known as the Book of Mormon, a record containing the fulness of the Gospel, with a message that [the] Gospel must be preached in every nation under the heavens before the end should come" (CR, Apr. 1909, p. 92). The Lord affirmed that this angel had appeared to some, but would yet appear to many.
