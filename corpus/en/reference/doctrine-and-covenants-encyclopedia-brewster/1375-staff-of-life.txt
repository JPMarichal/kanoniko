Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Staff of Life

In the Word of Wisdom, the Lord mentions that "all grain is ordained for the use of man and of beasts, to be the staff of life" (D&C 89:14). According to Webster a "staff" is something which sustains or supports life, such as used in the common expression, "Bread is the staff of life."
