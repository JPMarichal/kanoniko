Bosom

The bosom has generally been seen as encasing the heart-the designated symbol of one's innermost thoughts and feelings, the center of emotion. It is descriptive of closeness, intimacy, or affection. Christ's presence "in the bosom of the Father, even from the beginning" (D&C 76:13, 25, 39: 109:4) is illustrative not only of his close proximity to the Father but more particularly of his being in tune with the sacred thoughts and feelings held within the Father's bosom.

It is this type of "oneness" which can bring all mankind into the "bosom of the Father" (D&C 35:2; 50:43; John 17:20-26). Enoch's righteousness brought him and his city into the bosom of the Lord (D&C 38:4), and it is from the Lord's bosom (presence) that the holy scriptures have gone forth (D&C 35:20). God's throne is in the bosom of eternity; that is, his presence is at the very heart of the galactic bosom which encompasses his creations: he "is in the midst of all things" (D&C 88:13).

The bosom of the Church mentioned in an 1831 revelation had reference to the heart or center of Church activity at that time (D&C 38:38).
