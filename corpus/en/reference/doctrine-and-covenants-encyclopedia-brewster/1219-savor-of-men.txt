Savor of Men

Those who are the true "salt of the earth" bring out the wholesome "savor of men" (D&C 101:39-40: 103:10; Matt. 5:13; 3 Ne. 12:13). Webster defines savor as a verb meaning "to have a specified taste or quality; a special flavor or quality." Faithful members of the Church, the true "salt of the earth," should provide a special quality in whatever social situation they find themselves. Their presence should be edifyingly savory, bringing out the best in others and adding to the righteous pleasure of all.

Elder Mark E. Petersen noted there can be a negative savor: "The savor that the wicked give off becomes a stench in [the Lord's] face."
