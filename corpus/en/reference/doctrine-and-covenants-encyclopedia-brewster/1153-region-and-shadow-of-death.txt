Region and Shadow of Death

The reference to taking the gospel "unto those who sit in darkness and in the region and shadow of death" (D&C 57:10) is similar to Isaiah's Messianic prophecy (Isa. 9:2; Matt. 4:16; 2 Ne. 19:2). The darkness referred to is that which covers the minds of those not exposed to the illuminating light of the gospel (D&C 112:23; 2 Cor. 4:4). In a sense, they sit in the "region and shadow of [that] death" which is spiritual and which shall be pronounced upon the wicked (D&C 29:41).

The gospel is the call to light and life, for Jesus is "the life and light of the world" (D&C 10:70; 88:6-13; Mosiah 16:9; John 8:12). This principle applies to both the living and the dead, for the illuminating light of the gospel penetrates the darkness and shadows that exist in both spheres (D&C 138:30).
