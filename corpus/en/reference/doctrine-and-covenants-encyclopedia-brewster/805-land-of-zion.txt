Land of Zion

"The whole of America is Zion itself from north to south," declared the Prophet Joseph Smith (TPJS, 362). There are over fifty references to the "land of Zion" in the Doctrine and Covenants, however, and these refer to the land of Missouri in which the Saints attempted to establish the cause and the city of Zion (e.g., D&C 57:14; 63:24-48; 78:3; 103).

President Joseph Fielding Smith said, "We accept the fact that the center place where the City New Jerusalem is to be built, is in Jackson County, Missouri.... But we do hold that Zion, when reference is made to the land, is as broad as America, both North and South-all of it is Zion." (DS 3:72.) Thus, the references to building up Zion, found in prophetic utterances, has implications far beyond the borders of Missouri.
