Majesty on High

The title "Majesty on high" is found in Doctrine and Covenants 20:16 and in the New Testament (Heb. 1:3; 8:1). In the latter instance the title is applied to God the Father, on whose right hand the Son stands (compare Heb. 12:2), while in the latter-day revelation it might be applied to either the Father or Son. The word majesty has reference to sovereign power, authority, and dignity. The Father and Son are the Sovereigns of salvation.
