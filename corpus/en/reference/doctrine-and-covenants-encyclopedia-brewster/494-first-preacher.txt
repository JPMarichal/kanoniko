First Preacher

The Lord's proclamation that Oliver Cowdery was to be the "first preacher of this church" (D&C 21:12) is not his designation as the presiding elder, for the Prophet Joseph was the "first elder" and Oliver the "second elder" (D&C 20:1-3). The meaning of "first preacher" is found in an entry in Joseph's journal. "On Sunday, April 11th, 1830, Oliver Cowdery preached the first public discourse that was delivered by any of our number" (HC 1:81).
