Behoove

On several occasions in the Doctrine and Covenants the Lord uses the phrase, "it behooveth me" (21:10; 61:9; 124:49). This simply means that something is his desire, wish, or will. A slightly different use is found in several Book of Mormon passages where the word means an obligation or requirement (2 Ne. 9:5; Hel. 14:15).
