Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Deep

During the days of despair he spent unjustly incarcerated in a foul prison, Joseph Smith pled for relief from the Lord. Although the voice of the Lord reminded the Prophet that he was not forgotten, he was warned of other adversities that might befall him. Among these was the possibility of being "cast into the deep" (D&C 122:7), which was a metaphorical allusion to being cast into the depths of the seas, or suffering great personal discomfort and distress. When one thinks of a struggling soul sinking in the depths of the seas, one pictures a soul very much in distress. Such would be the lot of Joseph Smith.
