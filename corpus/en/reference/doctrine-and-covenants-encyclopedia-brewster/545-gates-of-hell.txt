Gates of Hell

On a number of occasions the Lord promises protection to the Saints from the power of the adversary, stating that if they are faithful and build upon his rock, "the gates of hell shall not prevail against them" (D&C 10:69; 17:8; 18:5; 21:6; 33:13; 98:22; 128:10). "The gates of hell are the entrances to the benighted realms of the damned where the wicked go to await the day when they shall come forth in the resurrection of damnation. Those beckoning gates prevail against all who pass through them. But those who obey the laws and ordinances of the gospel have the promise that the gates of hell shall not prevail against them." (DNTC 1:388-89.)
