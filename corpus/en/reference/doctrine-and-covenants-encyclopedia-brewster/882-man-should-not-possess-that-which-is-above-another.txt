Man Should [Not] Possess That Which Is above Another

President Joseph F. Smith said: "It is written that 'It is not given that one man should possess that which is above another.' [D&C 49:20.] Of course, there is some allowance to be made for this expression. A man who had ability superior to another man, and is able to manage and control larger affairs than another, may possess far more than another who is not able to control and manage as much as he. But if they each had what they were capable of managing and of using wisely and prudently, they would each have alike." (CR, Oct. 1898, pp. 23-24.)
