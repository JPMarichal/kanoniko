Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Contrite Spirit

Inseparably connected with a "broken heart" is a "contrite spirit." Both are required of one who enters the waters of baptism and who desires to walk the path of righteousness (D&C 20:37; 56:17, 18; 59:8; 97:8). These terms are linked together in the Doctrine and Covenants and the Book of Mormon (2 Ne. 2:7; 4:32; 3 Ne. 9:20; Morm. 2:14; Ether 4:15; Moro. 6:2). Elder Joseph Fielding Smith has said, "Every baptized person who has fully repented, who comes into the Church with a broken heart and a contrite spirit has made a covenant to continue with that broken heart, with that contrite spirit, which means a repentant spirit. He makes a covenant that he will do that." (CR, Oct. 1941, p. 93; italics added.)
