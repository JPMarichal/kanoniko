Thrust in His Sickle

There are a dozen occasions in the Doctrine and Covenants when the Lord invites the laborers in his field to thrust in their sickles (e.g., D&C 4:4; 6:3; 14:3, 4; 33:7). A sickle is an agricultural instrument consisting of a curved metal blade fitted on a short handle. It is a common instrument for reaping crops in nonindustrialized nations. The Lord uses the analogy of thrusting in the sickle to illustrate the work required to harvest souls.
