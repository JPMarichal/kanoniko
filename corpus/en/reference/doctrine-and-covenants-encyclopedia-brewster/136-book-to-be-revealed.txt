Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Book to Be Revealed

In recounting his many experiences with heavenly beings, the Prophet referred to Moroni's visit wherein he declared "the book to be revealed" (D&C 128:20). This book, announced to Joseph Smith in September 1823, would be revealed to the world almost seven years later when the Book of Mormon was published.
