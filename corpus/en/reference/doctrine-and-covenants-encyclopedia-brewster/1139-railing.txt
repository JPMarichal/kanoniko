R

Railing

To rail is to revile or scold in harsh or abusive language. In ancient America, the faithful disciples of Christ received railings and unjust persecutions from others but did not respond in kind (3 Ne. 6:13). In latter days, the Lord counseled against using railing accusations (D&C 50:33).
