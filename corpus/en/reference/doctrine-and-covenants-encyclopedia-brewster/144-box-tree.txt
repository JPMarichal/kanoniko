Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Box-Tree

Among the items the Saints were encouraged to bring to Nauvoo for the building of a house for the Most High was the box-tree (D&C 124:26). This is simply an evergreen tree that is also mentioned in the Old Testament (Isa. 41:19; 60:13). Its hard wood is particularly prized by engravers.
