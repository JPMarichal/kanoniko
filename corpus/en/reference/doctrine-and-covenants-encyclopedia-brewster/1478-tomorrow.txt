Tomorrow

The single use of the word tomorrow occurs in section 64, verse 24, where the Lord refers to his second coming. "For after today cometh the burning- Â� for verily I say, tomorrow all the proud and they that do wickedly shall be as stubble; and I will burn them up," said the Lord.

Inasmuch as today in this sense is the time before the coming of the Lord (SS, 394), "tomorrow" is the day of his coming. Tomorrow is that time when "the summer shall be past, and the harvest ended" (D&C 45:2;56:16).See also: Today
