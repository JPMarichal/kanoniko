Keys of the Church

The name of the true Church of Christ is frequently used synonymously with the term kingdom of God. Thus, the keys of the kingdom of God are the keys of the Church. (D&C 42:69.) These keys are the authorized power to preside over and administer the affairs of the Lord's church here upon the earth. Joseph Smith indicated that the parable of the mustard seed (Matt. 13:31-32; Mark 4:30-32; Luke 13:19-20) was given to represent the growth of the Church (kingdom) in the last days (TPJS, 98-99).
