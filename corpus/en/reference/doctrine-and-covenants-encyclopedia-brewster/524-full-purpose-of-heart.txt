Full Purpose of Heart

The Lord declared that the Twelve Apostles "are they who shall desire to take upon them my name with full purpose of heart" (D&C 18:27). To cleave unto God (Acts 11:23) or to turn to him with full purpose of heart (Mosiah 7:33) is to do so with one's entire being, without reservations. It is to set one's course and not look back or turn to the right or to the left (see Luke 9:62).
