Angel of God Who Was in Authority

The "angel of God who was in authority in the presence of God" is Lucifer (D&C 76:25-26). His pre-earthly status was of such significance that one-third of all our Father's spirit children were willing to cast their ballots in favor of his diabolical scheme to wrest total authority from God and assume the throne of ultimate power himself (D&C 29:36-38; Moses 4:1-4; Abr. 3:27-28). President Joseph Fielding Smith said, "Just what authority Lucifer held before his rebellion we do not know, but he was an angel of light, his name, Lucifer, meaning torchbearer" (CHMR 1:281).
