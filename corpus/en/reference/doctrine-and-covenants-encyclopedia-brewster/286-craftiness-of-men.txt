Craftiness of Men

Some will miss an inheritance in celestial worlds because of the "craftiness of men" (D&C 76:75, see verses 71-79). To be crafty is to be adept in the use of subtlety and cunning, to be scheming or sly in seeking to deceive others. Men who resort to craftiness do not have righteous ends in mind but seek to serve selfish purposes and to take advantage of or destroy another.

The Lord has revealed the means whereby the faithful can avoid the craftiness or deception of men and the master deceiver, the devil himself: "For they that are wise and have received the truth, and have taken the Holy Spirit for their guide ... have not been deceived" (D&C 45:57).
