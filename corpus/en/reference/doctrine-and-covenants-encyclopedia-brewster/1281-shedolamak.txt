Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Shedolamak

The only mention of a place called Shedolamak is in a revelation on priesthood, given in March 1835 (D&C 107:45). Adam is reported to have been traveling to this place when he was met by his great-grandson, Cainan, upon whom he conferred the priesthood. Adam was at that point in his 412th year, for Cainan was ordained at age 87. No authoritative description of Shedolamak has been revealed.
