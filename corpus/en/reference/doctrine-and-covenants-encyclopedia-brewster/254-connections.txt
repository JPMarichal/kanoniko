Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Connections

In his dedicatory prayer of the Kirtland Temple, the Prophet Joseph Smith prayed for himself, for his wife and children, and for "all their immediate connections" (D&C 109:68-70). According to the footnote reference in the 1981 edition of the Doctrine and Covenants, immediate connections referred to "close relatives."
