Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

High-Mindedness

As used in Doctrine and Covenants 124:3, high-minded is an adjective denoting honorable, principled, and fair. However, as used in an 1833 revelation, the noun high-mindedness means arrogant and haughty, full of pride (D&C 90:17).
