Workways

The Prophet wrote that "a very large ship is benefited very much by a very small helm in the time of a storm, by being kept workways with the wind and the waves" (D&C 123:16). Workways is a nautical term which means keeping the bow of the ship headed into the wind. This is the safest course during times of storm.
