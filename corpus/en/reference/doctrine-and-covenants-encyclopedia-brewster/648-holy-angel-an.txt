Holy Angel, An

At the time the Church was organized, the Lord reminded Joseph Smith that following his cleansing experience during the First Vision, wherein he saw and conversed with the Father and Son, he again became entangled in the ways of the world. "But after repenting, and humbling himself sincerely, through faith, God ministered unto him by an holy angel" (D&C 20:5-6). This was the angel Moroni, who gave him power to bring forth and translate the Book of Mormon and anciently had hidden the sacred records which he was now authorized to bring forth (JS-H 1:28-54; Ether 5; Moro. 10).
