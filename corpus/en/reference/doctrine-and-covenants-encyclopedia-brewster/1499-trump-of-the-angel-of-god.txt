Trump of the Angel of God

The "trump of the angel of God" is the signal to be given at the time of the Second Coming which will bring to pass the resurrection of those celestial Saints whose bodies had already been laid to rest in mother earth, as well as those of the same glory who are living at the time of Christ's coming. This latter group will pass through death and resurrection in a "twinkling of an eye" (D&C 43:32; 88:98). These combined groups will be "caught up to meet him" (Christ) and will then descend with him upon his return (DS 2:296).
