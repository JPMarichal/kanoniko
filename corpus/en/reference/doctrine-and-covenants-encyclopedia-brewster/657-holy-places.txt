Holy Places

"My disciples shall stand in holy places, and shall not be moved," declared the Lord (D&C 45:32; 87:8). According to Elder Harold B. Lee, holy places are to be found in Zion, which, according to the scriptures, is where the pure in heart dwell (D&C 97:21). Therefore, "the all-important thing is not where we live but whether or not our hearts are pure" (CR, Oct. 1968, pp. 61-62).

Latter-day Saints are to gather upon "holy places" (D&C 101:64); in other words, wherever members of Christ's church are located that place should be holy, as befits the dwelling places of saints. Temples are, of course, designated as special holy places (D&C 124:39); however, should not the home be just as holy a place?
