Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Rills

In a declaration of poetic prose, the Prophet invited the elements to proclaim the "wonders of your Eternal King." Among those so invited were the "rills" (D&C 128:23). A rill is a very small stream or brooklet, the running forth of which would proclaim with gladness the goodness of God.
