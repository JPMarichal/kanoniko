Backbiting

One responsibility of most priesthood bearers is to see that there is no "backbiting" (D&C 20:54). To backbite is to revile against one who is not present, to slander or speak evil of one who is absent. Thus, a backbiter attacks one whose physical absence denies him the opportunity of defending himself.
