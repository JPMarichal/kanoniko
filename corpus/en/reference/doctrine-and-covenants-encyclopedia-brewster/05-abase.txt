Abase

"He that exalteth himself shall be abased," declared the Lord, "and he that abaseth himself shall be exalted" (D&C 101:42; see also 112:3; 124:114). To abase oneself is to humble oneself (Matt. 23:12; Luke 18: 14). To be abased is the opposite of being puffed up in pride.
