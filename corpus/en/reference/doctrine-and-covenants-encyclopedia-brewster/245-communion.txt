Communion

The noun communion is found in Doctrine and Covenants 107:19. One of the blessings of faithful bearers of the Melchizedek Priesthood is to "enjoy the communion and presence of God the Father, and Jesus." In other words, they will enjoy a close relationship with these exalted Beings. Those who exercise faith and are not stiffnecked "have communion with the Holy Spirit, which maketh manifest unto the children of men, according to their faith" (Jarom 1:4).
