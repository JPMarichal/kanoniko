Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Massachusetts

The state of Massachusetts is one of eight states of the Union in which revelations have been received and recorded in the Doctrine and Covenants. The Prophet Joseph Smith received a revelation in Salem, Massachusetts, on August 6, 1836 (D&C 111). Massachusetts was one of the original thirteen colonies that banded together in 1776 to declare their independence from the British empire and form the United States of America. It is of interest that Massachusetts was founded in part by men who were seeking freedom of religious expression.
