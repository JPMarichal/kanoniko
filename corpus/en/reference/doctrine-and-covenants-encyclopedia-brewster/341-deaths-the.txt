Deaths, The

The Lord decreed that "broad is the gate, and wide the way that leadeth to the deaths; and many there are that go in thereat, because they receive me not, neither do they abide in my law" (D&C 132:25; italics added). This is the opposite of the "continuation of the lives" promised to those who faithfully pursue the strait and narrow path (D&C 132:22). President Joseph Fielding Smith explained that "the term 'deaths' ... has reference to the cutting off of all those who reject this eternal covenant of marriage and therefore they are denied the power of exaltation and the continuation of posterity. To be denied posterity and the family organization, leads to the 'deaths,' or end of increase in the life to come." (CHMR 2:360.)
