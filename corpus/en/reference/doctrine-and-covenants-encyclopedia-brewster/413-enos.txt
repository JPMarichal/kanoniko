Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Enos

In a revelation on priesthood, it is mentioned that Adam bestowed the priesthood on his grandson, Enos, when the latter was 134 years of age (D&C 107:44). Enos was the son of Seth, born in the 105th year of his father's life (Gen. 5:6; Moses 6:13). He was taught "in the ways of God" and "prophesied also." He was one of the great high priests who was called by Adam into the valley of Adam-ondi-Ahman, three years prior to the death of the patriarch of our race. He is mentioned as an ancestor in the lineage of Mary's husband, Joseph (Luke 3:38). In his 90th year, Enos begat Cainan, after whom he named "a land of promise" (Gen. 5:9; Moses 6:17). Enos lived a total of 905 years and had "many sons and daughters" (Gen. 5: 10-11; Moses 6:18).
