Last Death

Speaking of those who are "spiritually dead," the Lord referred to this death as both the "first death" and the "last death" (D&C 29:41). This latter death has been described as "the final judgment passed upon the wicked.... banishment from the presence of the Lord" (DS 2:217).

The last death is not "the dissolution or annihilation of both spirit and body," for, once resurrected, they are never again to be divided (DS 2:222; Alma 11:45). The last death is reserved for "those who have sinned unto death" (D&C 64:7), whose loathsome lives have branded them as "sons of perdition" (D&C 76:32-38). They shall die a "second death, for they are cut off again as to things pertaining to righteousness" (Hel. 14:16-18).
