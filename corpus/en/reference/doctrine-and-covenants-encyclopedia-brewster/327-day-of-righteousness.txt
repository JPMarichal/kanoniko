Day of Righteousness

Because of their having achieved a higher level of living than existed among other men on earth, Enoch and his brethren were separated from the earth and taken into the presence of God (D&C 45:11-12; Moses 7:69). Here they will remain "until a day of righteousness shall come." This day will be when the earth is cleansed of the wicked, preparatory for the second coming of Christ. (Moses 7:62-63; WTP, 309-10.)
