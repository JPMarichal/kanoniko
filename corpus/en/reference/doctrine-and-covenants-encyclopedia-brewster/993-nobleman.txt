Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Nobleman

A parable about the redemption of Zion speaks of a nobleman (D&C 101:44-68; see also 103:21). This term is also found in a parable in the New Testament (Luke 19:12) and in reference to a man whose son was sick (John 4:46). A nobleman is a man of high station in society or one of nobility. In the two parables cited, the nobleman refers to the Lord.
