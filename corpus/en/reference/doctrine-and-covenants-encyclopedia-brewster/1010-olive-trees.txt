Olive Trees

It is appropriate that the olive tree should be used as a symbol in parables. The trees are extensively cultivated in the land where Jesus lived. During troubled times in Missouri, the Lord gave the "parable of the nobleman and the olive trees signifying the troubles and eventual redemption of Zion" (D&C 101:43-62). The olive tree is used several times in scripture as a symbol of a truth being revealed. The Apostle Paul referred to the grafting of the Gentiles into the olive tree (Rom. 11:17-24). The classic allegory of Zenos, which has been described as "one of the greatest parables ever recorded," describes the history of the house of Israel (Jacob 5; AGQ4:141-42).
