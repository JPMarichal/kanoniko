Ensign

According to Webster's Unabridged Dictionary, an "ensign," as used anciently by Isaiah (Isa. 5:26), had reference to "a battle cry; a watchword; or, a signal." Its present meaning denotes a banner, flag, or standard. The "ensign" the Lord has raised to the world is Zion, or his Church (D&C 45:9; CR, Apr. 1961, p. 119).
