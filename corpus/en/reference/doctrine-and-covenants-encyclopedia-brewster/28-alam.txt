Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Alam

The name Alam was used in earlier editions of the Doctrine and Covenants as a pseudonym for Edward Partridge (D&C 82:11; pre-1981 edition). He was one of nine individuals whose true identity the Lord initially wanted to be kept secret from the world (HC 1:255). A year after this revelation was given, the Prophet again referred to Partridge as Alam in a letter dated June 25, 1833 (HC 1:363). The letter also referred to Newel K. Whitney as Ahashdah, by which name he was identified in earlier editions of section 82. Because in current editions of the Doctrine and Covenants only the real name is given, the name Alam no longer appears in modern scripture.
