Remnants

According to the footnote reference (D&C 109:65), the term remnants, as used in the prophecy on war (D&C 87:5), refers to the Lamanites. In 1865, President Daniel H. Wells, Second Counselor in the First Presidency of the Church, suggested that at some future point in the history of the American nation, its inhabitants will "be greatly distressed" by the Lamanites, in fulfillment of this prophecy (MS 27:186-87). It does not appear likely that this vexation will come from those who have clothed themselves with the gospel of Christ, but rather from those who have not yet been spiritually subdued and converted.
