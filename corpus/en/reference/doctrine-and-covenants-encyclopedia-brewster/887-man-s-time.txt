Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Man's Time

Man's time is reckoned according to the planet whereon he resides (D&C 130:4-5). Here on earth, 24 hours equal one day and 365 days equal one year. A year on the planet Mercury is equal to 88 of our days, while Saturn's year is equal to 10,767 of our days, or 29 1/2 of our years.

This is still a diminutive figure, however, when one considers that God's residence, the celestial sphere Kolob, passes but one day's time at the same time our earth has passed through one thousand years (Abr. 3:1-9; 2 Pet. 3:8).
