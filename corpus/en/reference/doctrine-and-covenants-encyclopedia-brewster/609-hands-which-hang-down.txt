Hands which Hang Down

There are two instances in scripture where the admonition is given to "lift up the hands which hang down, and strengthen the feeble knees" (D&C 81:5; Heb. 12:12). A related reference is found in Isaiah: "Strengthen ye the weak hands, and confirm the feeble knees" (Isa. 35:3).

Sidney B. Sperry said these "clauses mean ... to help sustain persons who are either weak of body or in a position where they find it difficult to help themselves" (DCC, 377). The description also applies to those who suffer discouragement, despair, and loneliness.
