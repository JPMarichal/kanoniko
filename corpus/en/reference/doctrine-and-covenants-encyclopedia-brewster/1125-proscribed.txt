Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Proscribed

A declaration of belief warned against governments that foster one religious society while "another is proscribed in its spiritual privileges" (D&C 134:19). To be proscribed is to be hindered in the practice of one's beliefs by another's use of civil authority. Anciently, Daniel and his friends were proscribed from practicing their religious beliefs (Dan. 3; 6).
