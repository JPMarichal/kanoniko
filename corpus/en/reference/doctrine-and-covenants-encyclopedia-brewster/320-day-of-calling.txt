Day of Calling

"There has been a day of calling, but the time has come for a day of choosing," declared the Lord in June 1834 (D&C 105:35). This day is the time period in which one receives the call to respond to the invitation to become "fellowcitizens with the saints and of the household of God" (Eph. 2:19). Although the "net" of the gospel catches many, some slip away and others are cast out because of their unworthiness (Matt. 13:47-50). Thus, while many Saints receive the call, some slip through sin back into the murky waters of the world and are lost. Only the faithful Saints were and are to be chosen for the promised endowment (D&C 105:12, 33; SS, 686; DCC, 550-51). The day of calling has not ceased, for the carriers of Christ's message, the "fishers of men," are covering the earth with the gospel net in search of the faithful.
