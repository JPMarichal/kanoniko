Presidency of the School of the Prophets

In section 88, the Lord outlined the responsibilities of the "presidency of the school of the prophets" (D&C 88:127). Several months later, the First Presidency of the Church was organized and by revelation was designated as the presidency of the School of the Prophets (D&C 90:1-7). This presidency consisted of Joseph Smith, Sidney Rigdon, and Frederick G. Williams.
