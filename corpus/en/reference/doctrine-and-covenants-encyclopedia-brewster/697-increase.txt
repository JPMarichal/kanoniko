Increase

The Lord warns that those who do not achieve the highest order of the celestial glory cannot have an increase (D&C 131:1-4). While increase has reference to eternal offspring, it appears to also have connotations in terms of one's eternal progression towards Godhood, which is available only to those who inherit eternal life, or life in the presence of the Father and Son (D&C 132:17-24). The ultimate destiny of these righteous ones will be to follow in the footsteps of the Father and increase in glory through bringing to pass the immortality and eternal life of their own eternal offspring (Moses 1:39).
