Griffin, Selah J.

The name of "Selah J. Griffin" appears in two revelations, both of which were given in June 1831 (D&C 52:32; 56:5-6). Initially he was called to accompany Newel Knight on a mission to Missouri, but this call was shortly thereafter revoked because of the "stiffneckedness" of the Saints in Thompson, Ohio, where Newel Knight was told to remain. Griffin received a new assignment to accompany Thomas B. Marsh to Missouri.

Following his mission, he moved his family to Missouri and suffered with the Saints during those days of persecution. He remained in the East when the Saints moved west, and nothing more is known of him.
