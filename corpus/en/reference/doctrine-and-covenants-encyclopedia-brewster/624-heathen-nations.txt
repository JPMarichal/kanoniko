Heathen Nations

The term heathen nations is employed twice in the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C 45:54; 90:10) and refers to those nations which worship false gods and idols who are generally represented by some tangible material. Heathen nations are the antithesis of Christian nations, or those nations who profess a belief in Jesus Christ.
