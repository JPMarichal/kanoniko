Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Mountains Shout for Joy

In a poetic outburst, the Prophet invited the mountains to shout for joy (D&C 128:23). This expression is related to others which refer to the mountains or hills singing (Isa. 42:11; 44:23; 55:12; 1 Ne. 21:13). This appears to be an expression of the rejoicing of nature on occasions when God is praised by his creations or when he is particularly pleased because of the rolling forth of the work of righteousness and redemption.
