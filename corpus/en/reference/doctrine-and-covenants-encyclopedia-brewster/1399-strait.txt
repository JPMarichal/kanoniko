Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Strait

Many are familiar with the phrase, "strait is the gate, and narrow the way that leadeth unto [life or] the exaltation" (D&C 132:22; see also Matt. 7:14; Luke 13:24; 3 Ne. 14:14). Strait means narrow or constricted. President Spencer W. Kimball noted that strait is "not the shortest distance between two points. Strait means hard, difficult, exacting." (DSY, 1973, 265.)
