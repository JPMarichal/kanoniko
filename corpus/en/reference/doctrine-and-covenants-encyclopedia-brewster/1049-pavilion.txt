Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Pavilion

According to Webster, a pavilion is a covering, canopy, or tent. God's pavilion is a celestial canopy covering his presence. It is a veil that can only be rent by righteousness. In a moment of deep distress, Joseph Smith pleaded with the Lord to part his pavilion and let himself be heard (D&C 121:1, 4).
