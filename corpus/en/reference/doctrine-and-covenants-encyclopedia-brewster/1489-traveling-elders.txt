Traveling Elders

The phrase "traveling elders" appears but once in the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C 124:139) and refers specifically to the First Quorum of the Seventy, who are to be "traveling ministers" (D&C 107:97) under the direction of the First Presidency and the Twelve Apostles. In other words, theirs is not a "standing" ministry, but they are to travel throughout the world in their ministry. The Twelve Apostles, who are a "Traveling Presiding High Council," are in a general sense considered as traveling elders (D&C 124:39, footnote a; 107:33).
