Thrash the Nations

The ministers of the Lord are called upon "to thrash the nations by the power of [His] Spirit" (D&C 35:13; 133:59). Smith and Sjodahl equate thrashing with threshing and offer the following explanation of this expression, which is also found in Habakkuk 3:12: "Threshing, in olden times, was done by treading out the grain on a threshing-floor. The going forth of the messengers of the gospel among the nations is like trampling the wheat sheaves on the hard floor. The valuable kernels are carefully gathered up; the straw is left." (SS, 186.) Thus, to "thrash the nations" is to preach the gospel to them and gather in the Lord's kernels (converts).
