Waters

During the course of a journey that included travel by canoe, a small group of elders was warned about the "destructions upon the waters; yea, and especially upon these waters" (D&C 61:4-5). Sperry said that "these waters" refers specifically to the Missouri and Mississippi rivers (DCC, 255).

The Lord warned that in the last days he had "cursed the waters" (D&C 61:14-19). President Joseph Fielding Smith suggested that the curse upon the waters has been manifest not only in floods and storms but also in warfare, "especially by submarine" (CHMR 1:224).
