Yesterday, Today, and Forever

The phrase yesterday, today, and forever (D&C 20:12; 35:1; Heb. 13:8; 1 Ne. 10:18) simply means the past, present, and future. When God is spoken of as being the same yesterday, today, and forever, it means that he is unchanging, never varying in his course (DNTC 3:238).
