Temperance

The Lord admonished that we be "temperate in all things" (D&C 12:8). According to Webster, temperance is "habitual moderation in the indulgence of the appetites or passions."

Although the word has usually been associated with abstinence from alcohol, it has other implications. The virtue of temperance, which is to be possessed by all who serve in the ministry (D&C 4:6; 107:30), is "not just refraining from drinking liquor!" said President Joseph Fielding Smith.
