Stupor of Thought

In righteously seeking an answer from the Lord, one may meet with the spiritual sensation of a burning bosom if the thing sought for is right, or with a stupor of thought if it is wrong (D&C 9:7-9). Just as a right answer will bring an enlargement of one's understanding, so will a wrong answer cause a diminution of spiritual feelings. Such a "stupor" can cause a literal numbness of feeling or suspension of thought.

According to Elder Melvin J. Ballard, when one experiences a "stupor of thought," the "heart will be turned away from that thing" which is wrong (CR, Apr. 1931, pp. 37-38).
