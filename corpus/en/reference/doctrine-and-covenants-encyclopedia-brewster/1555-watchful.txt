Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Watchful

One of the most important admonitions one should follow is to "be watchful" (D&C 61:38). Amulek exhorted the Nephites to "be watchful unto prayer continually" (Alma 34:39), as did Moroni (Moro. 6:4). Elder Howard W. Hunter suggested that "prayerful watching does not require sleepless anxiety and preoccupation with the future, but rather the quiet, steady attention to present duties" (En., May 1974, p. 18).
