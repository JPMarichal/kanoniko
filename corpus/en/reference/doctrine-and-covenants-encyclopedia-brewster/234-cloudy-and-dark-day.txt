Cloudy and Dark Day

The Prophet Joseph spoke of "the children of Jacob who have been scattered upon the mountains for a long time, in a cloudy and dark day" (D&C 109:61). This has reference to the scattered sheep of Israel who have been without the full light of the gospel and have been in dismal and gloomy circumstances. Anciently the Lord promised that he, as the great Shepherd, would "seek out [his] sheep, and will deliver them out of all places where they have been scattered in the cloudy and dark day" (Ezek. 34:12; see also HC 1:315).
