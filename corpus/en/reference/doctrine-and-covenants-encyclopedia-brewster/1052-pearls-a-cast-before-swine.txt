On three occasions, the Lord counseled against casting pearls before swine (Matt. 7:6; 3 Ne. 14:6; D&C 41:6). The pearls of which the Savior spoke are the sacred truths and precepts of the gospel; his disciples are counseled against being indiscreet in conveying these to others (Talmage, 244). The swine-whose flesh was an abhorrence to the Israelites (Lev. 11:7: Deut. 14:8; Isa. 65:4)-are symbolic of the unbelievers, those who might treat lightly or trample upon the precious pearls of the gospel.
