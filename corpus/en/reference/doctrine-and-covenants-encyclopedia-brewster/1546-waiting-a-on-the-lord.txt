The concept of waiting on the Lord is found in three volumes of scripture (Prov. 20:22; Isa. 40:31; 1 Ne. 21:23; D&C 98:2). The word wait in Hebrew means hope for or anticipate. Nephi tells us that "the people of the Lord are they who wait [hope] for him" (2 Ne. 6:13). One who waits upon the Lord places his trust in him and lives in accordance with his will in anticipation of his coming.
