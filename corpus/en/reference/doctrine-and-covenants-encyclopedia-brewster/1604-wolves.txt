Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Wolves

Although the word wolves is only used once in the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C 122:6), its use as a symbol of predatory people who seek to take advantage of and to destroy the sheep of the Good Shepherd is also found in other scriptures (Matt. 7:15; 10:16; Luke 10:3; Acts 20:29; Alma 5:60; 3 Ne. 14:15).
