D

Damnation of Hell

In March 1839, Joseph Smith languished as a prisoner in Liberty Jail-confined yet not convicted; falsely accused, despised, and persecuted by the world-yet, withal, maintaining his prophetic mantle. In these circumstances the Lord revealed to him that "all those that discomfort my people, and drive and murder, and testify against them" shall "not escape the damnation of hell" (D&C 121:23). The Prophet later discussed the meaning of this phrase: "What is the damnation of hell?" he asked. "To go with that society who have not obeyed his [God's] commands." (HC 4:554-55.)
