Wicked Man, A

As a result of his having lost an early Book of Mormon manuscript containing 116 pages of translation, Martin Harris was called a "wicked man" by the Lord (D&C 3:12; 10:1). "At heart, Martin was not wicked and desired to do what was right.Â� His wickedness consisted in his selfish desire to gratify his own wish contrary to the will of the Lord, after he had been denied this request twice before it was granted. Moreover, he was wicked in that he violated a most sacred and solemn covenant and trust which he made with the Lord through the Prophet Joseph Smith. From his wicked act, or acts, he humbly repented and again found favor with the Lord to the extent that he was privileged to stand as one of the special three witnesses of the Book of Mormon, and to behold the plates in the presence of the holy angel. He was deprived, however, from ever again acting as scribe in the translation of this sacred record of the Nephites." (CHMR 1:28.)
