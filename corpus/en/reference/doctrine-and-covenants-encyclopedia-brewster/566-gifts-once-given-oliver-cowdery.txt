Gifts Once Given Oliver Cowdery

In 1841, Hyrum Smith was released from serving as second counselor in the First Presidency and called as Patriarch to the Church (D&C 124:91) and as Assistant President of the Church (D&C 124:94-95; 1978 CA, 101). In this latter capacity, he was given the "keys" and "crowned with the same blessing, and glory, and honor and priesthood, and gifts of the priesthood, that once were put upon Â� Oliver Cowdery."

Regarding this calling, President Joseph Fielding Smith wrote: "With many members of the Church Hyrum Smith was just the Patriarch. Hyrum Smith received a double portion. He received the office of Patriarch which belonged to his father and came to him by right, and also received the keys to be 'Second President' and precede the counselors as Oliver Cowdery had done. So he would have remained as President of the Church had he not died a martyr." (DS 1:221.)
