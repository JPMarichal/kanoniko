Joy

"Men are, that they might have joy," declared an ancient prophet (2 Ne. 2:25). More recently the Lord spoke of "that which bringeth joy," enumerating such things as revelation, knowledge, mysteries, peaceable things, and life eternal (D&C 42:61). True joy is an intense inner peace and happiness that can only come from righteous living.

The Book of Mormon reminds us that "wickedness never was happiness" (Alma 41:10) and refers to the gospel as "the great plan of happiness" (Alma 42:8). The Savior declared that he experienced a fulness of joy because of the righteousness of his people (3 Ne. 27:31). Resurrected beings are capable of receiving a fulness of joy (D&C 93:33).
