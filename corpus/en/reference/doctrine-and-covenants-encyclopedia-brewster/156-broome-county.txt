Broome County

The only mention of Broome County, New York, appears as a reference point regarding the restoration of the Melchizedek Priesthood. It was in the wilderness between this county and Susquehanna County, Pennsylvania, that this restoration took place (D&C 128:20). Broome County was the site of Colesville, which figured prominently in the history of the Church from 1830 to 1831.
