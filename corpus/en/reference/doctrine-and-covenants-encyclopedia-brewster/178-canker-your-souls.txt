Canker Your Souls

The Lord warns the rich who will not give of their substance to the poor that their riches will canker their souls (D&C 56:16). One of the definitions of canker is "to corrupt with a malignancy of mind or spirit." Those who greedily set their hearts on the temporal treasures of this earth, turning their backs on the needy, leave a festering sore on their spirits that, if not corrected, can consume them. Their substance will perish with their souls. (See Mosiah 4:16-23.)
