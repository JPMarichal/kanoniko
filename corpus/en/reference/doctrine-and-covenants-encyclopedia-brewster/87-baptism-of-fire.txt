Baptism of Fire

The "baptism of fire" is always associated with the Holy Ghost (D&C 20:41; 33:11; 39:6; 2 Ne. 31:13-14; Matt. 3:11). Of this baptism, Elder Bruce R. McConkie has written: "To gain salvation every accountable person must receive two baptisms. They are baptism of water and of the Spirit. (John 3:3-5.)
