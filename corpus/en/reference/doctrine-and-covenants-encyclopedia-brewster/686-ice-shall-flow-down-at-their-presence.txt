Ice Shall Flow Down at Their Presence

One of the prophecies connected with the return of the ten lost tribes from the north countries is that "they shall smite the rocks, and the ice shall flow down at their presence" (D&C 133:26). Some have conjectured that this refers to the breaking down of ice masses that might be barring the way of the returning tribes. However, one scholar suggested a different approach: "Presumably, when our sphere becomes a new earth; when every valley is exalted and every mountain is made low; when the islands become one land, and the great deep is driven back into the north countries-when all these and other changes occur, then there will also be changes in the climate, and the ice masses of the polar areas will no longer be as they are now" (Mill M, 326-7).
