Earth Would Be Utterly Wasted

The prediction of Malachi that without the keys of Elijah the earth would be "utterly wasted" or "smitten with a curse" (Mal. 4:5-6; D&C 2:3; 27:9; 128:17) was discussed in this way by Elder Theodore M. Burton: The smiting of the earth with a curse, in essence, means that "the whole purpose of earth life will have failed in its purpose in exalting the children of God unless there is a welding link of some kind or other between the fathers and the children." (, 208; D&C 128:18; see also DS 2:121-22).

"The spirit of Elijah", according to Joseph Smith, is "that we redeem our dead, and connect ourselves with our fathers which are in heaven, and ... seal those who dwell on earth to those who dwell in heaven" (HC 6:252).
