Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Brunson, Seymour

The name of Seymour Brunson first appears in the Doctrine and Covenants in a revelation given at the Amherst conference in January 1832. He, along with other brethren, was admonished to "be united in the ministry" (D&C 75:33). Brunson joined the Church in January 1831 and was ordained an elder the same month by John Whitmer. He labored as a missionary in Ohio, Virginia, and several other states and experienced the persecution of the Missouri period of Church history. He was called to the Nauvoo Stake high council in October 1839, in which capacity he served until his death on August 10, 1840. (Jenson 3:331.) That he was a faithful follower of the Master is evidenced by the Lord's own testimony, given in January of 1841: "Seymour Brunson I have taken unto myself" (D&C 124:132).
