Eternity to Eternity

The phrase from all eternity to all eternity appears twice in the Doctrine and Covenants, although the second time it is slightly modified (D&C 39:1; 76:4). It is used in connection with the Savior, who, as a God, is "the same today as yesterday, and forever" (D&C 35:1).
