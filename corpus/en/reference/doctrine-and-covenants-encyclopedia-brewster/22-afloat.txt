Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Afloat

During dark days of persecution, the Prophet Joseph urged the scattered Saints to gather documents and data relating to their sufferings and abuses. Among these materials were "libelous publications that are afloat" (D&C 123:1-4). In this sense, the term applies to that which was circulating about the general public.
