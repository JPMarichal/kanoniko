Levi

The sons of Levi are referred to three times in the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C 13; 124:39; 128:24). Levi was the third son of Jacob and Leah (Gen. 29:34). Though there was at least one episode in his life which caused his father some heartache (Gen. 34; 49:5-7), Levi must have been judged sufficiently faithful to have been granted posterity such as Moses, Aaron, and all who would later minister in the ordinances of the lesser priesthood to the other tribes of Israel (Ex. 4:14; Num. 3; 4; 8).
