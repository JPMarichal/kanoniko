Refiner's Fire

The phrase "he is like a refiner's fire, and like fullers' soap" is employed three times in holy writ: first in the Old Testament (Mal. 3:2), then again in the Book of Mormon (3 Ne. 24:2) and finally in the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C 128:24). Webster states that to refine is "to free from impurities to a pure state.Â� to perfect." A refiner is one charged with the responsibility of perfecting the good while discarding the dross. This is usually done through a process employing heat and fire. Christ's second coming will be as a "refiner's fire," for the impure and coarse shall be separated from among the righteous as if by fire and cast off as dross. Those who remain in his presence will be refined, cleansed, and purified (3 Ne. 27:19).
