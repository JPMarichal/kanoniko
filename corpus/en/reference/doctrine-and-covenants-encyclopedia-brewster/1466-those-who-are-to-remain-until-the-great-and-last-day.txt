Those Who Are to Remain Until the Great and Last Day

The fourth trump to sound, announcing the last order of resurrected beings, will be the call of condemnation. For "those who are to remain until that great and last day, even the end.Â� shall remain filthy still" (D&C 88:102). These are they to whom the notes of the trumpet will not be soothingly melodic but rather terrifyingly condemnatory, for they will signal eternal banishment from the glories of God to the outer reaches of darkness. These are the "vessels of wrath" known as sons of perdition. (D&C 76:31-38; 88:32.)
