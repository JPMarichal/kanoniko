Gift, Possessed by Joseph Smith

The "gift" which Joseph Smith temporarily lost in the summer of 1828 was the seeric gift of translating the ancient Nephite records by the power of inspiration (D&C 10:2). His "mind became darkened" because he had lost the divine light which had previously illuminated his understanding. This came about as the natural consequence of his disobedience and willful neglect which led to the loss of 116 pages of translated manuscript.
