Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Esaias

The name Esaias is the Greek form of Isaiah (LDSBD, 667). However, as used in the Doctrine and Covenants, it refers to a prophet who lived in the days of Abraham and who held the Melchizedek Priesthood (D&C 76:100 [note the cross-reference in the 1981 edition]; 84:111-13). The history of this prophet is yet to be revealed.
