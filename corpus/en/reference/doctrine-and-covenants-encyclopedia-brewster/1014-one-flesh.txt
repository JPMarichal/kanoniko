One Flesh

The command that husband and wife should be one flesh is one of the longest standing commandments of the Lord (Gen. 2:24; Matt. 19:5-6; Eph. 5:31; Moses 3:24; D&C 49:16). One flesh is not confined to the physical or affectional aspects of a marital relationship, it is symbolic of the total unity in which the marriage should function.

President Marion G. Romney provided the following commentary on the meaning of one flesh: "They [husband and wife] should be one in harmony, respect, mutual consideration. Neither should plan or follow an independent course of action. They should consult, plan and decide together." (En., Dec. 1978, p. 2.)
