Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Shule

During a period of time in which the names of men and places were being disguised, the lot where the ashery was located in Kirtland was referred to as "Shule" (D&C 104:39; pre-1981 edition, HC 1:255). This is the only use of that name in the Doctrine and Covenants, but it is also found in the Book of Mormon as the name of an ancient Jaredite king (Ether 7). According to Webster, an ashery is a place for ashes, or a place where wood is burned to ashes. Sperry indicated it was used for making soap (DCC, 542). Shule is not used in current editions of the Doctrine and Covenants.
