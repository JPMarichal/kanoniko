Revelations of John

The revelations of John spoken of in Doctrine and Covenants 20:35 refers to the last book of our present New Testament, the book of Revelation. This inspired book was authored by the Apostle John and contains great truths which have been hidden from the world but revealed to prophets and others who spiritually qualify (1 Ne. 14:18-28; TPJS, 287-94).
