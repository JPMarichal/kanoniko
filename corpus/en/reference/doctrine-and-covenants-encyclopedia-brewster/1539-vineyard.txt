Vineyard

The term vineyard is a frequent expression found in the literature of the Lord. The great parable known as the allegory of Zenos, which gives the history of Israel, uses the term extensively to refer to the world in which we live (Jacob 5; AGQ 4:203-6).

The meaning of vineyard is very evident in the three dozen times it is found in the Doctrine and Covenants, of which the following is an example: Speaking of the world in which we live, the Lord said, "And my vineyard has become corrupted" (D&C 33:4). Again, the Lord said: "Thou art called to labor in my vineyard, and to build up my church, and to bring forth Zion" (D&C 39:13).
