Book of the Law of God

In 1832, the Lord warned the Saints in Zion that they must agree to abide by his laws or they would not be worthy to "have their names enrolled with the people of God" (D&C 85:1-5). In this connection he spoke of "the book of the law of God" in which their names should not be found. This book has been defined as follows: "Those records kept by the Church showing the names, genealogies, and faith and works of those to be remembered by the Lord in the day when eternal inheritances are bestowed upon the obedient are, taken collectively, called the book of the law of God. Such records contain both the law of God and the names of those who keep that law. They are in effect a church book of remembrance." (MD, 100.)
