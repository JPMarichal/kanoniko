Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Deign

On two occasions in the Doctrine and Covenants, the word deign is used. In an 1831 revelation the Lord said: "I ... deign to give unto you greater riches, even a land of promise" (D&C 38:18; italics added). Ten years later he said: "I deign to reveal unto my church things which have been kept hid" (D&C 124:41; italics added). According to Webster, the verb deign means to esteem or treat as worthy, to condescend to give or bestow. Thus, the Lord esteemed the Saints as worthy to receive that which he was to bestow on them. He considered it appropriate.
