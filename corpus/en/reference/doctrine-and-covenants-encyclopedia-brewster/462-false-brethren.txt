False Brethren

The Lord intimated that Joseph Smith should be "in perils among false brethren" (D&C 122:5). These are they who feign friendship or loyalty to people or causes but secretly plot against them. They are as Judas who broke bread with the Savior yet secretly sold him for silver (Matt. 26:20-25, 47-50; Mark 14:10-11).
