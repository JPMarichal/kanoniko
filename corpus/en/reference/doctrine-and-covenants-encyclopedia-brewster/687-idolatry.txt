Idolatry

The Lord has warned against the practice of idolatry (D&C 52:39; 3 Ne. 30:2; Lev. 19:4; 1 Cor. 10:14). Idolatry is not confined to the worship of idols made in the image of some false and powerless god but extends to the unnatural worship or pursuit of anything that displaces God as the first and foremost thought of one's heart. The Savior counseled, "Seek ye first the kingdom of God" (3 Ne. 13:33) for "no man can serve two masters....Ye cannot serve God and Mammon [riches or the things of this world]." (3 Ne. 13:24.)
