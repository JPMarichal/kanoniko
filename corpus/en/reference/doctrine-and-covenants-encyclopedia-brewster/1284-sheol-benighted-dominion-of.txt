Sheol, Benighted Dominion of

A word scripturally unique to the Doctrine and Covenants is Sheol (D&C 121:4). The Prophet Joseph Smith used this term in a pleading prayer from his confinement in Liberty Jail. It is a Hebrew word that translated in English in the King James Version of the Bible could mean "grave" (Gen. 37:35), "pit" (Job. 17:16), or "hell" (Ps. 16:10) (LDSBD, 773).

One writer has said that sheol "is the gloomy abode of departed spirits; it is the place the wicked go to await the day of their eventual resurrection Â�; the connotation surrounding its usage is one of evil, sorrow, and anguish" (MD, 710).
