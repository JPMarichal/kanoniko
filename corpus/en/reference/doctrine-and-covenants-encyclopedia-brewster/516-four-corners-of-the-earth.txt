Four Corners of the Earth

Occasional reference is made to the four corners of the earth (D&C 124:3, 128; Isa. 11:12; 2 Ne. 21:12), the four quarters of the earth (D&C 33:6; 45:46; 135:3; Rev. 20:8; 1 Ne. 19:16), or the four winds (D&C 133:7; JS-M 1:37). This is figuration language which refers to the extremities or ends of the earth. The Church or kingdom of God will go forth in every direction under the direction of the Lord's anointed servants.
