Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Dort, David

Although he is mentioned but once in the Doctrine and Covenants (124:132), the name of David D. Dort appears in several other prominent places on the pages of early Church records. He served on high councils at Kirtland, Ohio (HC 2:510-11); Far West, Missouri (HC 3:225); and Nauvoo, Illinois (HC 4:12). He was a member of the famed Zion's Camp, which went to the aid of fellow Saints in Missouri (HC 2:183), and later publicly covenanted to assist the Saints in their exodus from that land of persecution and mobocracy (HC 3:253). He is reported to have died in Nauvoo in 1841 at about age forty-eight.
