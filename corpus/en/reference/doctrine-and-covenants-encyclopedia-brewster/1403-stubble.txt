Stubble

"They that do wickedly shall be as stubble," declared the Lord (D&C 29:9; see also Ex. 15:7; Isa. 5:20-24; Mal. 4:1; 1 Ne. 22:15: JS-H 1:37). Stubble is that part of the stalk of grain-producing grasses that is left after the grain has been harvested. The righteous are as the grain that is harvested for useful purposes, while the wicked are left to burn as waste.
