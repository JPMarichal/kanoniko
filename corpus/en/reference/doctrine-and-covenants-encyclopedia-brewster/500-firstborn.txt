Firstborn

As used in Doctrine and Covenants 68:16-18, the term firstborn refers to the oldest male child among the descendants of Aaron who by lineage has the right to serve as the Presiding Bishop of the Church (DS 3:92). (CHMR 1:259.) However, it should be understood that such an office would always be appointed under the direction of the First Presidency of the Church.
