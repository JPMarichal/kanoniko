Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Most High

The title "Most High" or "Most High God" appears more than a dozen times in the Doctrine and Covenants (e.g., D&C 39:19; 59:10, 12; 76:112) and refers to the exalted and lofty positions occupied by both the Father and the Son. "This designation connotes a state of supreme exaltation in rank, power and dignity; it indicates that each of these Gods is God above all" (MD, 516).
