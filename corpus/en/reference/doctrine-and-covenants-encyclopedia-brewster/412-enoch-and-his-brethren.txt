Enoch and His Brethren

The citation to "Enoch and his brethren" being removed from the earth (D&C 45:11-12) refers to those ancient inhabitants of the city of Zion. They became so righteous and unified that they were removed from this earth as translated beings (Moses 7:18-21, 68-69).
