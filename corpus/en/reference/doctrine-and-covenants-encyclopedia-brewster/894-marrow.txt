Marrow

The word marrow is found six times in the Doctrine and Covenants and refers to the soft substance that fills bone cavities but is often used to represent vitality and strength (D&C 6:2; 11:2; 12:2; 14:2; 33:1; see also Heb. 4:12). Those who keep the Word of Wisdom are promised that they "shall receive health in their navel and marrow to their bones" (D&C 89:18).
