Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Bridegroom

In the parable of the ten virgins (Matt. 25:1-13), "the Bridegroom is the Lord Jesus; the marriage feast symbolizes His coming in glory, to receive unto Himself the Church on earth as His bride" (Talmage, 578-79). References to Christ as the "Bridegroom" appear in five verses of the Doctrine and Covenants (33:17; 65:3; 88:92; 133:10, 19).
