Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Branch

One use of the word branch is as a unit of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. From the time the Church was organized in April 1830, the Lord referred to these units (D&C 20:65-66). In our day, branches are generally the smallest units of the Church and are presided over by a branch president. Symbolically, a branch is a small extension from the main trunk of the tree representing the Church.

The word branch is also used in symbolic language to represent an extension of one's posterity (D&C 10:60) or the future existence of the individual himself. In the second instance, the wicked are warned that if they remain unrepentant they shall be wasted and left with "neither root nor branch" (D&C 109:50-52).
