Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Benighted

In a prayerful petition, the Prophet Joseph Smith referred to the devil's abode as "the dark and benighted dominion of Sheol" (D&C 121:4). Benighted means to be darkened, without light, and could refer to the absence of both physical and spiritual light.
