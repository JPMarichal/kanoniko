Light to the World

As defined by the Lord, the "light to the world" is the everlasting covenant, or, in other words, the fulness of the gospel of Jesus Christ as revealed through his church (D&C 45:9, 28). Isaiah wrote of a "standard" that was to be set up to the people of this world (Isa. 49:22; 1 Ne. 21:22). Elder Marion G. Romney identified the Church as that standard of which Isaiah spoke (CR, Apr. 1961, p. 119).

To the Church the Lord declared: "Arise and shine forth, that thy light may be a standard for the nations" (D&C 115:5). The charge to the Saints in all ages has been to dispel darkness with the light of the gospel (Matt. 5:14-16; 3 Ne. 12:14-16; D&C 115:42-5). For example, Paul declared that his mission was to open the eyes of the people and "to turn them from darkness to light, and from the power of Satan unto God" (Acts 26:18).
