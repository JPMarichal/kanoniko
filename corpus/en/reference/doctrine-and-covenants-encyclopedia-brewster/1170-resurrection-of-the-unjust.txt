Resurrection of the Unjust

The Lord declared that "they who have done evil" shall rise "in the resurrection of the unjust" (D&C 76:17). The phrase also appears once in the King James Bible (Acts 24:15) and also in the Joseph Smith Translation of the Bible (JST, John 5:29).

Finally, in the afternoon of the second resurrection, those who 'remain filthy still,' those who having been raised in immortality are judged and found wholly wanting, those whom we call sons of perdition, shall be cast out with Lucifer and his angels." (DNTC 1:196-97; italics added.)

This resurrection is also referred to as the "last" one (D&C 76:85).
