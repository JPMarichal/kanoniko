Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Hoar Frost

In a moment of despair and discouragement, Joseph Smith was promised that those who falsely charged him should have "their prospects ... melt away as the hoar frost melteth before the burning rays of the rising sun" (D&C 121:11). The word hoar denotes something white; the hoar frost is the white frozen dew produced when once humid air is frozen.
