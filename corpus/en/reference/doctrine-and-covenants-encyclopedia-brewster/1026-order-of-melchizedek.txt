Order of Melchizedek

Those men who obtain the celestial kingdom must be "priests after the order of Melchizedek" (D&C 76:57). This "order" is the order of the holy Melchizedek Priesthood, which encompasses all who have been ordained unto this priesthood and who by their "fruits" have proven themselves worthy of holding it eternally (D&C 84:33-42; 107:1-12).
