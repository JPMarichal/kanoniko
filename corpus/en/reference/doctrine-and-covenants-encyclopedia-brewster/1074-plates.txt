Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Plates

Other than specific reference to "the plates of Nephi," which are discussed elsewhere in this volume, the Lord speaks several times in a general sense of "the plates" (D&C 3:19; 5:1, 4; 17:1). These "plates" are the gold plates given to the Prophet Joseph Smith and from which he translated the Book of Mormon (see JS-H 1:27-59).
