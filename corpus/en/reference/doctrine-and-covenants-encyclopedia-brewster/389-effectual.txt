Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Effectual

An 1837 revelation given through Joseph Smith to Thomas B. Marsh in his position as President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles promised that "an effectual door" would be opened up in places where the name of Christ was proclaimed (D&C 112:19). Similar promises were made in two other revelations (D&C 100: 3; 118:3). The word effectual means effective, or producing the desired results. Thus the Lord promised Thomas Marsh the desired results-effectual doors would be opened-as he faithfully engaged in His ministry.
