Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Dodds, Asa

Among those called on a mission at the Amherst, Ohio, conference of January 1832 was Asa Dodds. He was called to accompany Calves Wilson on a journey to the west in proclaiming the gospel (D&C 75:15). Whether this mission was fulfilled is unknown, for no further mention is made of him or his companion in subsequent writings. Just as the New Testament immortalizes certain unknowns (Rom. 16), so the Doctrine and Covenants speaks of certain men whose deeds and destiny are at present unknown. Dodds is one of these.
