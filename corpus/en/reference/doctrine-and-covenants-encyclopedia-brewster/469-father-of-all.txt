Father of All

Because of his unique position as the first man and first flesh on the earth, Adam is the father of all men who have inhabited this earth in tabernacles of flesh (D&C 27:11: Moses 3:7). All mankind have descended from our first parents, father Adam and his wife, mother Eve (1 Ne. 5:11).
