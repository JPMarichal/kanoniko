Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Ether

Short hours before his name became eternally emblazoned among the martyrs of Christianity, Hyrum Smith quoted from the Book of Mormon. The particular verses he quoted were Ether 12:36-38. These verses were penned by Moroni, who was responsible for abridging the record known as the book of Ether, the fourteenth of the fifteen books within the Book of Mormon. It was named after the last prophet of the Jaredite people, whose history commenced at the time of the Tower of Babel and ended in America about thirty generations later (Ether 1:6-33).
