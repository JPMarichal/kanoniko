Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Holy One

"Christ is the Holy One," declared Elder Bruce R. McConkie, "a designation signifying that he is a holy, pure, sanctified Person, One who was and is without sin, who had no reason for repentance, and who stands perfect in all things" (MD, 360; see also D&C 78:16; Isa. 43:15; 49:7; Ps. 16:10; Acts 2:27; 3:14; 13:35; 2 Ne. 9:20, 41; 3 Ne. 26:5; Morm. 9:5, 14).
