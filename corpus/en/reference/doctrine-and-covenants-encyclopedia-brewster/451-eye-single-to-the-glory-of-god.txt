Eye Single to the Glory of God

There are six instances in the Doctrine and Covenants when the Lord admonishes the Saints to keep their "eye single to the glory of God" (4:5; 27:2; 55:1; 59:1; 82:19; 88:67). The concept of keeping one's "eye single" is mentioned in the masterful sermons given by the Savior on both hemispheres: "If ... thine eye be single," he admonished, "thy whole body shall be full of light" (Matt. 6:21-24; Luke 11:34-36; 3 Ne. 13:19-24). One's spiritual lenses can be darkened and split by sin. Eyes that are "single to the glory of God ... are undimmed by sin and are focused solely on righteousness" (DNTC 1:240).
