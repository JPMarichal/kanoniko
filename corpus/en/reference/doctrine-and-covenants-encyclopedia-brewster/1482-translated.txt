Translated

There are several ways in which the word translated is used in the revelations. The first refers to the process which Joseph Smith used in rendering the language of the ancient Nephites into English (D&C 1:29; 10:10). The Lord also referred to Joseph Smith's work in rendering plainer explanations of biblical passages as a translation (D&C 45:60).

Yet another meaning of translated refers to the condition whereby God alters the physical condition of selected mortals, thus making them temporarily impervious to the frailties of the flesh, including death (D&C 7; 107:49; 3 Ne. 28:4-12; Moses 7:69; JST, Gen. 15:32-34). At the appropriate future time, each of these translated beings will pass through an instantaneous death and be resurrected.
