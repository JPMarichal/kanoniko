Bathed in Heaven

In preparation for executing judgment upon the wicked, the Lord will bathe his sword in heaven (D&C 1:13). Smith and Sjodahl suggest that "this is a very expressive term from Isaiah 34:5, where it is used to signify the pouring out of the indignation of the Lord upon all nations and His fury upon their armies, delivering them to destruction and slaughter" (SS, 7). Another possible interpretation is that the Lord will bathe (wash or clean) his sword in preparation for its use.
