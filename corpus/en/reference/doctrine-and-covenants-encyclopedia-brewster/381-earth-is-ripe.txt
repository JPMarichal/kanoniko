Earth Is Ripe

Both the Doctrine and Covenants (29:9) and the New Testament (Rev. 14:15) speak of a time when "the earth is ripe." This is symbolic language representing a time of harvest. For the righteous it will be a day of gathering into the sheaves of the Master Harvester (see D&C 4:4; 6:3), but for the wicked it will be a day of destruction as they are treated as chaff and burned (1 Ne. 22:15; D&C 64:24).
