Sea of Glass

When the earth is renewed by resurrection and becomes a glorified globe, a celestial sphere, it will be as a "sea of glass" (D&C 77:1; 88:16-26). "This is a figure of speech typifying the earth in its celestial form," wrote President Joseph Fielding Smith (CHMR 1:295).

Speaking of this change in our planet, Brigham Young said: "It will not then be an opaque body as it now is, but it will be like the stars of the firmament, full of light and glory; it will be a body of light. John compares it, in its celestial state, to a sea of glass." (JD 7:163.)
