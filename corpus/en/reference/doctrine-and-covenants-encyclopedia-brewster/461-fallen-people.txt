Fallen People

In addition to the testimony it provides for Jesus Christ (1 Ne. 6:4:2 Ne. 25:18-30), and the marvelous gospel teachings it contains, the Book of Mormon "contains a record of a fallen people" (D&C 20:9). These people lived in civilizations that existed in the ancient Americas; they were brought to these lands by the hand of God and later destroyed because they rejected God and turned to evil (see Ether 13:20-22; 15:19; Moroni 9).

These fallen people include the Jaredites, a group that originated at the time of the tower of Babel (Ether 1:33), and the Nephites, a group that came to the Americas from Jerusalem about 600 B.C. (1 Ne. 1). Intermingled with the Nephites was a people popularly known as the Mulekites but described in the Book of Mormon as the people of Zarahemla (Omni 1:1-19; Mosiah 25:13). These people were descendants and followers of Mulek, a son of the Jewish king Zedekiah (Mosiah 25:2; Hel. 6:10; 8:21).
