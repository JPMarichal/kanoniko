Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Shinelah

The verb "shinelah" was used once in pre-1981 editions of the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C 104:58). Its English meaning is "to print," but its origin is unclear. Based upon a discussion of the word Shinehah, by President Joseph Fielding Smith, one may assume that shinelah might be a word out of the Adamic language (CHMR 2:97). It is a variant of a word which means "sun" and therefore is appropriate when applied to the verb "to print"; for to print (shinelah) is to shed light upon a subject (Abr. 3:13).
