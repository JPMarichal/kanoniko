Everlasting Order

According to Smith and Sjodahl, the "everlasting order" to which the Lord referred in Doctrine and Covenants 82:20 was the Order of Enoch, which was set up among nine early Church leaders. It was a "united order." Orson Pratt said that this was "a law inferior to the celestial law, because the celestial law required the consecration of all that a man had. The law of Enoch only required a part." (JD 16:156.) Elder Pratt emphasized that this "order" had reference to the days of Joseph Smith (who was called "Enoch" in the original wording of the revelation), and not the ancient "Order of Enoch."
