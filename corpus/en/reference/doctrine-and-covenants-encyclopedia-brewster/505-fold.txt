Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Fold

With the exception of the one instance in the Doctrine and Covenants in which the Lord referred to his followers in ancient Palestine as his fold (i.e., a group of people with a common faith: see D&C 10:59), the word fold is used in conjunction with a number, indicating increase to that degree (e.g., D&C 78:19; 98:25-26). When the Lord promises that he will reward righteousness "an hundredfold," it means the person will get one hundred times the increase for his effort.
