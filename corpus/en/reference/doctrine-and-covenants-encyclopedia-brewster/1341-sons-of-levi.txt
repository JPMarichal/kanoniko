Sons of Levi

In a broad sense, a son of Levi is one who is of the posterity of Levi, the son of Jacob and the father of that tribe of Israel charged with the responsibility of ministering the priesthood to the other tribes (Gen. 29:34; Num. 3:12; 8:14-26). More specifically, however, sons of Levi are those brethren who hold the priesthood in our day (DS 3:93; DCC, 81). At some future day, these "sons" will be called upon to reinstate the law of sacrifice in order that the "restoration of all things" may be accomplished (TPJS, 172-73; DS 3:94; Acts 3:19-21).
