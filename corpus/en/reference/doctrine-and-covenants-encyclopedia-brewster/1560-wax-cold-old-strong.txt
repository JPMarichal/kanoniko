Wax Cold/Old/Strong

To wax means to come to be or to increase in size, strength, or intensity. Thus, to "wax strong" is to increase in strength or capacity (D&C 45:58; 121:45; Mosiah 18:26). To "wax old" is to increase in years, grow old, or even useless (D&C 1:16; 2 Ne. 7:9). Jesus gave counsel to seek after things "which wax not old" (Luke 12:33). Those who allow their love of men to "wax cold" lose the warmth of love-it becomes cold and lifeless.
