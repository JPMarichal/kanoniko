Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Continueth in God

"That which is of God is light; and he that receiveth light, and continueth in God, receiveth more light," declared the Lord (D&C 50:24). To continue is to remain steadfast, to persist or endure. Thus, one who continues in God remains steadfast and faithful to him. He persists in keeping God's commandments.
