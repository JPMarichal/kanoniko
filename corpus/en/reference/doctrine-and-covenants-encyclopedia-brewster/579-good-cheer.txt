Good Cheer

The Lord has invited all his people to "be of good cheer" (D&C 61:36; 68:6; 78:18; 112:4; John 16:33; 3 Ne. 1:13). "Good cheer," said Elder Marvin J. Ashton, "is a state of mind or mood that promotes happiness or joy. Â� With God's help, good cheer permits us to rise above the depressing present or difficult circumstances. It is a process of positive reassurance and reinforcement. It is sunshine when clouds block the light." (En., May 1986, p. 66.)
