Washings

The term washinqs is used in only one section of the Doctrine and Covenants and refers to a sacred ordinance performed only within a temple of the Lord (D&C 124:37, 39). The Savior said, "How shall your washings be acceptable unto me, except ye perform them in a house which you have built to my name?" This ordinance must be received by all who enter into exaltation within the celestial kingdom.
