Dispensation of the Priesthood

Inasmuch as a dispensation of the gospel requires that the priesthood be present to administer the ordinances of the gospel, we may assume that the terms dispensation of the gospel (D&C 110:12) and dispensation of the priesthood (D&C 128:9) are synonymous and may be used interchangeably. Adam holds the keys of salvation and the keys of the dispensation of the fulness of times (D&C 78:16; HC 4:207-8). The Prophet Joseph Smith said that the nature of the priesthood is that there are men who hold the presidency of the priesthood in each dispensation and that each is accountable to Adam, who holds the keys of the fulness of times and who in turn is accountable to the Lord (TPJS, 169; HC 3:385).
