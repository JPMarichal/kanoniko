Everlasting to Everlasting

The Lord is spoken of as being "from everlasting to everlasting" (D&C 20:17; 61:1; 109:77; Moro. 7:22). According to President Joseph Fielding Smith, "'Everlasting to everlasting' means from the eternity past to the eternity future as far as man's understanding is concerned, from the pre-existence through the temporal (mortal) life unto the eternity following the resurrection" (AGQ 2:127).
