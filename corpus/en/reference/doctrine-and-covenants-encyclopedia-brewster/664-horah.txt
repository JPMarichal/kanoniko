Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Horah

On various occasions the Lord deemed it wisdom to disguise the identities of individuals to whom he revealed his will. Once the necessity for the secrecy had passed, the true identities were made known (HC 1:255). One of the unnamed individuals in a revelation given April 26, 1832, was a person designated as "Horah" (D&C 82:11). Recent editions of the Doctrine and Covenants identify Horah as John Whitmer.
