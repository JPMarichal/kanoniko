Continuation of the Lives

In a revelation wherein the nature of the eternal marriage covenant was reviewed, the Lord spoke of the "strait gate" leading to "exaltation and continuation of the lives" (D&C 132:22). "The expression 'continuation of the lives,'" according to President Joseph Fielding Smith, "means the right and power of eternal increase or posterity" (CHMR 2:360). This procreative power is restricted to those celestial couples who qualify for the highest heaven and who will receive celestial crowns of glory and the "fulness of the glory of the Father" (D&C 131:1-4; 132:6). All others will be partakers of the "deaths," which means they will be denied posterity and the power of exaltation (D&C 132:25; CHMR 2:360).
