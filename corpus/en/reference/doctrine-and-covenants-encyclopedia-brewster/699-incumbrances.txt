Incumbrances

In 1833 the Lord told John Johnson "to seek diligently to lift incumbrances upon a certain house known to the brethren, in order that he may dwell therein" (D&C 96:9; DCC, 491). An incumbrance is a legal claim such as a mortgage or lien attached to a property which shows that money is owed to the holder of the incumbrance.
