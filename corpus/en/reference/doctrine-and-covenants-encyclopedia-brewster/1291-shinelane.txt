Shinelane

The word shinelane was found but once in scripture (D&C 104:63; pre-1981 editions). The meaning of the term as defined by the Lord is "printing," which is a derivative of the word shinelah, meaning "to print" (D&C 104:58). Both terms are variations of the word Shinehah, which means sun (Abr. 3:13). This term is no longer used in current editions of the Doctrine and Covenants.
