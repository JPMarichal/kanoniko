Priestcrafts

The Lord said that people erred "in many instances because of priestcrafts" (D&C 33:4). Priestcrafts are those activities practiced by religious leaders which seek selfish ends rather than the welfare of the people they are supposed to be serving. The Book of Mormon states that "priestcrafts are that men preach and set themselves up for a light unto the world, that they may get gain and praise of the world; but they seek not the welfare of Zion" (2 Ne. 26:29).
