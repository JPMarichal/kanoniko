That Man

In a revelation that-because of misinterpretation on the part of those who lacked the Spirit-has spawned a number of apostate offshoots from the true Church, the Lord spoke of removing "that man" and replacing him with "one mighty and strong" (D&C 85:7-8). "That man" refers to Bishop Edward Partridge, who at the time was being reprimanded in a letter written from Kirtland by the Prophet Joseph to W. W. Phelps in Missouri.

However, Edward Partridge repented of his erroneous ways to such an extent that following his death the Lord proclaimed that the bishop was among those who shared the presence of Deity (D&C 124:19).
