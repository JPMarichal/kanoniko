Supreme Being

Inasmuch as the Father has bestowed upon the Son all that he has (D&C 93:16-17), both bear the title of "Supreme Being" (D&C 107:4). To be supreme is to hold the highest rank or authority, to hold or exercise power which cannot be exceeded or overruled. Such is the divine position held by these two Supreme Beings, for they are not exceeded nor overruled by any other power. They are omnipotent (D&C 19:3; 61:1; Matt. 28:18; 1 Ne. 9:6; Mosiah 4:9; Alma 12:15; 26:35: Morm. 5:23: Ether 3:4).
