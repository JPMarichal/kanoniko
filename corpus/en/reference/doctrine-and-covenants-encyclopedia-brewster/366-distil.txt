Distil

In both the Old Testament (Deut. 32:2) and the Doctrine and Covenants (121:45) the Lord promises that something shall "distil as the dew." This is a figure of speech which means that the promised item would descend upon the recipient as gently and almost imperceptibly as the moisture of dew descends upon the earth.
