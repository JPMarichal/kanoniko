Shrink

In recounting his excruciating experience in Gethsemane, the Savior recalled how he desired "not [to] drink the bitter cup, and shrink" (D&C 19:18; see also Matt. 26:39; Mark 14:35-36; Luke 22:41-42). To shrink is to withdraw. For all who suffer moments of despair or discouragement and wish to withdraw from a difficult assignment, the words of the suffering Savior can provide great solace: "Nevertheless, glory be to the Father, and I partook and finished" (D&C 19:19; italics added).
