Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Sensual

By transgression of holy laws man becomes carnal, sensual, and devilish (D&C 20:20). As used in scripture, to be sensual is to focus one's energies on the lusts of the flesh, to unduly seek gratification of the senses, to concentrate on indulging one's appetites. It is to love Satan more than God (Moses 5:13).
