Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Derision

While languishing in LibertyJail, the Prophet was told that"fools shall have thee in derision" (D&C 122:1). The word isalso found in the Old Testamentwhere the Psalmist says of thosewho "take counsel together, against the Lord the Lord shall have them in derision" (Ps. 2:2-4). To have someone in derision is to ridicule, scorn, or mock him. While fools may mock Joseph Smith, they in turn shall be scorned by the Lord.
