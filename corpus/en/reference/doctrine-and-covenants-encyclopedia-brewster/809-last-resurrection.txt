Last Resurrection

When the trump sounds, announcing the Second Coming, the Saints will come forth in a glorious resurrection. However, the Lord will declare to the sinners, "stay and sleep until I shall call again" (D&C 43:18). These are those who "are to be judged and are found under condemnation" (D&C 88:100), who shall not come forth until the "last resurrection" (D&C 76:85).

This resurrection will include all beings of a telestial order and also those miserable creatures whose actions have designated them as "sons of perdition" (D&C 76:31-33, 81-85; 88:32). These are they who arise in the "resurrection of the unjust" (D&C 76:17).
