Wheat

The parable of the wheat and the tares was expounded by the Savior during his mortal ministry and again by revelation to Joseph Smith (Matt. 13:24-30, 36-43: D&C 86:1-11). The Prophet said that the wheat represented the Church (TPJS, 98). The "wheat," said the Savior, shall be gathered and "secured in the garners to possess eternal life, and be crowned with celestial glory" (D&C 101:65).
