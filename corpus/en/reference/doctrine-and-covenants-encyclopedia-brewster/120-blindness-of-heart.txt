Blindness of Heart

The phrase blindness of heart is found only in the Doctrine and Covenants (58:15) and in Paul's writings (Eph. 4:18). It is an attribute of the unrepentant sinner. According to Smith and Sjodahl it "means affections not guided by the light of the Spirit. Those who place their affection upon wrong objects, such as belong to the world, in preference to those that pertain to the Kingdom of God, are blind at heart, no matter how clear the physical or mental vision may be." (SS, 338.) Blindness of heart is to be equated with hardness of heart.
