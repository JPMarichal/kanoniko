Arms of My Love

"Be faithful and diligent in keeping the commandments of God," the Lord declared, "and I will encircle thee in the arms of my love" (D&C 6:20). To be encircled in the arms of the Lord's love is to feel the warm and spiritually sustaining effect or influence of his love.
