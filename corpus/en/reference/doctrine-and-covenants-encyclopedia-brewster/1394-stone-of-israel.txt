Stone of Israel

In an 1831 revelation, Christ identifies himself as the "stone of Israel" (D&C 50:44). This title can also be found in the Old Testament (Gen. 49:24). He is "the stone which the builders refused," but which became "the head stone of the corner" (Ps. 118:22; Matt. 21:42). Moses described the Lord as the "Rock of Heaven." Thus, if men build upon this "stone" or "rock," they are on a "sure foundation, a foundation whereon if men build they cannot fall" (Hel. 5:12; see also Jac. 4:14-16).
