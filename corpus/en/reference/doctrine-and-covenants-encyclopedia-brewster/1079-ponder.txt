Ponder

On three occasions in the Doctrine and Covenants the Lord invites us to ponder His words or ways (D&C 30:3; 88:62, 71). The mother of our Savior "pondered" the experiences of that sacred night when her holy Son came into mortality as the Babe of Bethlehem (Luke 2:19). And the ancient prophet Moroni admonished future readers of the sacred record entrusted to his care to "ponder it in your hearts" (Moro. 10:3). The revelation in Doctrine and Covenants 138 came as a result of Joseph F. Smith's "pondering over the scriptures" (D&C 138:1).
