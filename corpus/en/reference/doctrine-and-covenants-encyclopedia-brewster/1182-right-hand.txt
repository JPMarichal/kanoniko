Right Hand

In a symbolic sense, God has used the right hand to show privileged status. The Savior stands on the right hand of the Father (D&C 20:24; Moro. 7:27; Acts 7:56). The righteous, those whose names are recorded in the book of life, who take upon them the name of Christ and know him, are promised an eternal place at his right hand (Mosiah 5:9; 26:23-24; Alma 5:58).
