Spiritual Authorities of the Church

The term spiritual authorities of the church is a singular expression found in a revelation on priesthood (D&C 107:32). It appears to have reference to the three presiding quorums of the Church, namely the First Presidency, the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, and the First Quorum of the Seventy. If any of these quorums should make a decision in "unrighteousness," an appeal could be made to the general assembly of the collective quorums. This group would constitute the spiritual authorities of the Church.
