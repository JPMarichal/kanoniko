Beautiful Garments

The Lord declared that "Zion must increase in beauty, and in holiness Â� [and] arise and put on her beautiful garments" (D&C 82:14). For Zion to "put on her strength is to put on the authority of the priesthood" (D&C 113:7-8; Isa. 52:1) and "what comes about as a result of exercising the Priesthood is equivalent to putting on 'her beautiful garments'" (DCC, 383).
