Prince of This World

Just as Christ is the "Prince of Peace" (2 Ne. 19:6; Isa. 9:6), so is Satan the "prince of this world" (D&C 127:11), or, rather, "the prince of darkness, who is of this world" (JST, John 14:30; italics added). He reigns in the benighted domain of the world of contention, carnality, and corruption-the world which the Apostle John warned us to avoid (1 John 2:15-17). This "prince" shall lose his satanical sceptre of power when evil is rejected for righteousness, "for he hath no power over the hearts of the people [who] dwell in righteousness" (1 Ne. 22:26).
