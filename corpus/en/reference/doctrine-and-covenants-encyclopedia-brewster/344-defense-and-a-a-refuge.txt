The Lord promises that the stakes of Zion will serve as a "defense, and for a refuge" to the faithful who gather to these protective canopies of Christ, there to be nurtured in his ways (D&C 115:6). A refuge is a shelter or protection from danger. Those who accept the gospel of Jesus Christ and gather to his stakes are shielded from spiritual danger and build spiritual strength and defenses to thwart attacks by the adversary. The congregations of the Saints become fortresses for the faithful. It is significant to note the promise that those who faithfully gather to worship and partake of the sacrament will "more fully" keep themselves "unspotted from the world" (D&C 59:9).
