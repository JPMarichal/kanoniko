People of the Lord

In August 1831, the Lord counseled with those who "call yourselves the people of the Lord" (D&C 63:1). According to the Prophet Joseph, "The people of the Lord [are] those who have complied with the requirements of the new covenant" (TPJS, 17). The "new covenant" includes the fulness of the gospel, with every ordinance, covenant, and commandment belonging thereto. Those who comply therewith are they who have fully taken upon themselves the name of Christ with a determination to "always remember him and keep his commandments" (D&C 20:77).
