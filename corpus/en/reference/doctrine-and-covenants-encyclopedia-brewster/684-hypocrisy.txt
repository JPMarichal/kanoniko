Hypocrisy

An undesirable character trait which the Lord has frequently denounced is hypocrisy (Matt. 6:1-18; 7:1-5; 23:23-29; 3 Ne. 16:10). Hypocrisy is to publicly pretend or profess to believe in principles but to privately practice otherwise. Hypocrites will ultimately "be detected and shall be cut off, either in life or in death" (D&C 50:6-8; 101:90; 124:8).
