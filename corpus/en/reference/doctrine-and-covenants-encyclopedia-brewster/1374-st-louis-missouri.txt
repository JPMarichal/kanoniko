Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

St. Louis, Missouri

In 1831, one of the major intersections of travel from east to west was St. Louis, Missouri. Following a conference of elders in Jackson County, Missouri, the Lord instructed many of the elders to return to the East by way of St. Louis. From this juncture, several of the leaders were to make their way to Cincinnati, Ohio, and the rest were to travel in twos in other directions. (D&C 60.)
