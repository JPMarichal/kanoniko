Interpolations

Speaking of the apocryphal writings, the Prophet Joseph Smith said "many things contained therein Â� are not true, which are interpolations by the hands of men" (D&C 91:2). Interpolations are words or phrases not found in original manuscripts but inserted at a later time. These may be deliberate efforts to corrupt the original text (1 Ne. 13:24-26; Morm. 8:33; HC 6:57).
