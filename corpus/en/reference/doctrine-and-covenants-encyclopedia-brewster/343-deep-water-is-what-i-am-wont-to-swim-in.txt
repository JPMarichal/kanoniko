Deep Water Is What I Am Wont to Swim in

In a moment of reflection on the many persecutions and privations he had suffered, Joseph Smith declared, "Deep water is what I am wont to swim in" (D&C 127:2). The Prophet was simply saying that he was accustomed to being in difficult surroundings that required him to be alert and active or he would not have survived. Elder Russell M. Nelson pointed out that "the expression deep water means danger" (En., May 1988, p. 33). The word wont is an alternate translation of the word accustomed from the Greek language (see Matt. 27:15).
