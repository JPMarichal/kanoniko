Economy

The book which John the Revelator saw with seven seals (Rev. 5:1) is said to contain the "mysteries, and the works of God; the hidden things of his economy concerning this earth" (D&C 77:6). This has reference to God's efficient management of the affairs of this earth in accomplishing his divine objectives (see D&C 3: 1-3).
