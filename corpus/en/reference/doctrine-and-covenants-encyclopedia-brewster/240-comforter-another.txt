Comforter, Another

The Lord speaks of "another Comforter" in the great revelation known as the Olive Leaf (D&C 88:3-4). According to Smith and Sjodahl, "This is the Holy Spirit [of Promise], who gives to the Saints the assurance of eternal life. See also John 14:16." (SS, 541.)
