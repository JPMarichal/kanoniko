Children of Disobedience

The title "children of disobedience" is found in the Doctrine and Covenants (121:17) and in the New Testament (Eph. 2:2; 5:6; Col. 3:6). These are those individuals "who walk after the manner of the world, who are carnal, sensual, and devilish, who are subject to the lusts of the flesh" (DNTC 2:499).
