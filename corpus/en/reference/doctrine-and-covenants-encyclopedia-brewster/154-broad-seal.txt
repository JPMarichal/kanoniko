Broad Seal

The word broad generally refers to something that is wide (D&C 132:25; Matt. 7:13; 3 Ne. 14:13; 3 Ne. 27:33; Moses 7:53). However, a different meaning is found in the account of the martyrdom. The spilled blood of Joseph and Hyrum Smith is described as "a broad seal affixed to 'Mormonism' "in the sense that this physical sign of their deaths is emphatic or conspicuous. The witness of their testimonies cannot be easily erased or avoided.
