South Countries

In January 1832, William E. McLellin was commanded to change his mission from the East and to go into the "south countries" (D&C 75:6-8). This charge did not include territory foreign to the United States, but merely meant he was to go into the southern states, or, that country which was south of his present location. Similarly, Major Ashley and Burr Riggs were sent into the "south country" (D&C 75:17).
