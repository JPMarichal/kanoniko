Prophesy

Among the gifts of the Spirit is the ability to prophesy (D&C 46:22). One possessing this gift speaks under the influence of divine inspiration and proclaims some future event that God has decreed shall come to pass. Such pronouncements may be conditional. For example, when Jonah prophesied that the citizens of Ninevah would be destroyed it was based upon their continuing in their wicked ways. Their repentance changed the course of events. (Jonah 3; see also D&C 130:20-21; ER, 92-96.)

Note that the word prophesy is a verb (the action) whereas the word prophecy is a noun (the subject).
