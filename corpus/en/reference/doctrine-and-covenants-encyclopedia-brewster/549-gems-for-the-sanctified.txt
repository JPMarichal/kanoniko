Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Gems for the Sanctified

The names of the prophet-martyrs Joseph and Hyrum Smith shall "go down to posterity as gems for the sanctified," declared John Taylor (D&C 135:6). Just as a gem is a jewel to be prized, so are the names of Joseph and Hyrum Smith to be prized by the Saints through the generations. Their names are held in honor by the pure in heart.
