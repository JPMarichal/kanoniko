Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Zombre

One of the code names used by the Lord to disguise the real identity of individuals was that of "Zombre" (D&C 96:6; 104:24, 34; pre-1981 edition; HC 1:255). This name was applied to John Johnson in June 1833, and again in April 1834. This name is no longer found in current editions of the Doctrine and Covenants.
