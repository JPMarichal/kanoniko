Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Epistle

The word epistle appears twice in the Doctrine and Covenants, once in connection with the writings of Peter (D&C 138:6) and once in relation to a letter to be written by Sidney Rigdon. An epistle is a formal or official letter, generally bearing some special message. A number of the books of the New Testament are epistles written by the early Apostles.
