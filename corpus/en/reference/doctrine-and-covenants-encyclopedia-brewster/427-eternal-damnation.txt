Eternal Damnation

The divine decree that disobedience and unrighteousness shall stop one's progress is known as "eternal damnation" (D&C 19:7; 29:44). Eternal means proceeding forth from God, for he is Eternal (D&C 19:10-12). And to be damned, said President Spencer W. Kimball, is to be "stopped in progress" (DSY 1976, 154).
