Storm

In commenting on the value of some small things, the Prophet refers to the advantage a ship in a storm has when its helm is very small (D&C 123:16). Although in this sense, a literal storm is meant, the word storm is used metaphorically in several other references to imply an existing or pending fierce disturbance or doom (D&C 90:5; 115:6: 127:1).
