Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Gad

The man Gad is an unidentified individual who received the priesthood from Esaias and in turn bestowed it upon Jeremy (D&C 84:10-11). He is not one of the sons of Jacob (Gen. 30:11) nor the Gad who was the prophet-friend of David (1 Sam. 22:5; LDSBD, 676).
