Bosom Shall Burn

In describing the process of revelation, the Lord told Oliver Cowdery that he must first "study it out in [his] mind; then Â� ask me if it be right, and if it is right I will cause that your bosom shall burn within you; therefore, you shall feel that it is right" (D&C 9:8). In describing the nature of this feeling, Elder S. Dilworth Young said: "If I am to receive revelation from the Lord, I must be in harmony with him by keeping his commandments. Then as needed, according to his wisdom, his word will come into my mind through my thoughts, accompanied by a feeling in the region of my bosom. It is a feeling which cannot be described, but the nearest word we have is 'burn' or 'burning.' Accompanying this always is a feeling of peace, a further witness that what one heard is right." (En., May 1976, p. 23; italics added.)
