Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Sixth Angel

A major portion of the book of Revelation is devoted to the missions of seven angels. The Doctrine and Covenants also speaks of seven angels who will sequentially sound their trumps prior to the occurrence of significant world events (D&C 88:98-110). The "sixth angel" will announce the fall of Babylon and reveal the secret acts and thoughts of the men who lived during the sixth period of earth's history (D&C 88:105, 109-10).

Inasmuch as this sixth period includes the dispensation of the fulness of times, in which we now live, Elder Wilford Woodruff said: "Joseph Smith, I expect, will sound the sixth trumpet. He will be at the head of this dispensation; or, if he does not blow the trumpet of this dispensation, I do not know who will." (JD 21:196.)
