Second Elder

When The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints was first organized, Oliver Cowdery was called by the Lord to be "an apostle of Jesus Christ" and "the second elder of this church" (D&C 20:3; HC 1:76-78). President Joseph Fielding Smith said: "We leave [Oliver] out in our list of Presidents of the Church Â� but he was an Assistant President. Oliver Cowdery's standing in the beginning was as the 'Second Elder' [President] of the Church, holding the keys jointly with the Prophet Joseph Smith." (DS 1:212.)

Because of transgression, Oliver lost his high calling and the Lord called Hyrum Smith to receive the "keys Â� and be crowned with the same blessing, and glory, and honor and priesthood, and gifts of the priesthood, that once were put upon him that was my servant Oliver Cowdery" (D&C 124:95; DS 1:216-22; 3:165-66).

With the sealing of Hyrum's testimony in blood, as a martyr of the kingdom, the position of "second elder," or second president, came to an end.
