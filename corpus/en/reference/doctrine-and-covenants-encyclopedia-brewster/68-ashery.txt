Ashery

In an effort to initially disguise its identity from the outside world, the Lord referred to the ashery in Kirtland as "Shule" (D&C 104:39:pre-1981 edition). The ashery was a building where soap was made (DCC, 542). "Shule" no longer appears in current editions of the Doctrine and Covenants.
