Son Ahman

Among the four books of scripture used by Latter-day Saints, the Doctrine and Covenants is unique in its use of the term Son Ahman (D&C 78:20; 95:17). Elder Bruce R. McConkie has given the following information regarding the origin and meaning of this title:

"In the pure language spoken by Adam-and which will be spoken again during the millennial era (Zeph. 3:9)-the name of God the Father is Ahman, or possibly Ah Man, a name-title having a meaning identical with or at least very closely akin to Man of Holiness. (Moses 6:57.) God revealed himself to Adam by this name to signify that he is a Holy Man, a truth which man must know and comprehend if he is to become like God and inherit exaltation. (1 John 3:1-3; D&C 132:19-24.)"

"Since Ahman is the name of God the Father in the pure language spoken by Adam, Son Ahman is the name of his Only Begotten Son." (MD, 29, 740.)

(For an interesting discussion by Elder Orson Pratt of an uncanonized revelation regarding the meaning of this name, see JD 2:342.)
