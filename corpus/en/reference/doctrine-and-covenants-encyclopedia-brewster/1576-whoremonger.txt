Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Whoremonger

Webster defines a "whore-monger" as one who is a lecher or one given to whoring. In other words, such a one is groveling amidst the filth and degradation of illicit sexual thoughts and relations. These are they who will "suffer the vengeance of eternal hell" (D&C 63:17; 76:103-5; Rev. 21:8; 22:15). "For this ye know, that no whoremonger, nor unclean person, nor covetous man, who is an idolater, hath any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and our God" (Eph. 5:5).
