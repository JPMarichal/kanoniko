Tabernacle of God

A revelation given in May 1833, stated that "the elements are the tabernacle of God; yea, man is the tabernacle of God, even temples" (D&C 93:35). Smith and Sjodahl state that "God dwells in the material universe, through His Spirit which pervades everything; not in the pantheistic sense of indwelling, which denies any distinction between matter and mind, body and spirit, God and the world, and affirms, to all intents and purposes, that the universe is God, and God the universe; but in the sense in which we say that God dwelt in the Tabernacle in the wilderness. He dwells in the material world as king in his palace." (SS, 596.)

Man's body becomes the temple or tabernacle of God as it is kept virtuous and clean as an abode for both man's spirit and God's Spirit (D&C 133:5; 1 Cor. 3:16-17; 6:19). Those who defile their "temples" will find themselves banished from God's presence, which is spiritual "destruction."
