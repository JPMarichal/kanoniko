Maker

The title of "Maker" appears three times in the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C 30:2; 121:4; 134:6), and refers to Deity. In the ultimate sense, the Maker is our Father in Heaven, he who created our spirits (Heb. 12:9). However, inasmuch as the Son, Jesus Christ, shares the fulness of the Father (D&C 93:16-17), as well as being the Maker of the heavens and earth (John 1:1-14; D&C 38:1-3; 3 Ne. 9:15), the title appropriately applies to him as well.
