Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Mete

There are two occasions in the Doctrine and Covenants where the term mete is used. Those who go forth in the ministry are promised divine guidance in that which they shall mete (allot) to every man (D&C 84:85). God will mete out (allot or assign a measure of) just recompense to those who oppress his Saints (D&C 127:3).
