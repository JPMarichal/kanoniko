Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Work of Translation

The phrase work of translation occurs four times in the revelations. In the first two instances, it refers to the work being done on the plates from whence the Book of Mormon came (D&C 10:3, 34). In the latter two references, the Prophet's work on revising the Bible is the "work of translation" (D&C 73:4; 76:15).
