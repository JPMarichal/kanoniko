House of Prayer

The "house of prayer" to which the Saints are commanded to go each sabbath day is the location where the sacrament service and other sacred sabbath meetings are held (D&C 59:9). This may be within the walls of a beautiful chapel dedicated to the purpose of worship, or in a humble home where "two or three are gathered in [the Lord's] name" (D&C 6:32).

However, regardless of location, the only authorized "house of prayer" is the place designated by the proper priesthood authority: "For all things must be done in order" (D&C 28:13). All meetings are to be conducted under the direction of the presiding priesthood authority.

Of course, each individual is responsible for maintaining his own personal "house of prayer," making his own abode a house of daily worship (D&C 88:119).
