Southern States

Almost three decades before the outbreak of the Civil War, the Prophet Joseph Smith prophesied that such an event would come to pass (D&C 87:3). Of the thirty-four states in the Union at the time the hostilities commenced, eleven states from the South formed their own confederacy. These seceding states were: South Carolina, Mississippi, Florida, Alabama, Georgia, Louisiana, Texas, Virginia, Arkansas, Tennessee, and North Carolina. The total population in these states was about 9,000,000, of which about 3,500,000 were Black slaves.
