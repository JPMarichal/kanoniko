Murdered in Cold Blood

The martyrdom of the Prophet Joseph Smith fulfilled his prophecy that he would be "murdered in cold blood" (D&C 135:4). Those who wantonly kill in cold blood do so without pity or mercy and with the clear intent to take a life.
