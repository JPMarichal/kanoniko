Stand by the Wall

The Lord declared that the enemies of his Prophet and "their posterity [would] be swept from under heaven.Â� that not one of them is left to stand by the wall" (D&C 121:15). This may be a euphemism ("the substitution of an agreeable or inoffensive expression for one that may offend") for a similar phrase found in the Old Testament (see 1 Kgs. 14:10). In this sense those who stand by the wall essentially means every male; thus, this passage alludes to the destruction of a family or household (1 Kgs. 16:10-12).
