Love of Men Shall Wax Cold

One of the signs of the last days is that "the love of men shall wax cold, and iniquity shall abound" (D&C 45:27; see also JS-M 1:10; Matt. 24:12). Elder Bruce R. McConkie suggests that this has reference to Saints who sin and lose their love of God, thereby apostatizing from the Church (DNTC 1:641).
