Mighty God of Jacob

The title "Mighty God of Jacob" appears but once in latter-day scripture, that being in the dedicatory prayer of the Kirtland Temple (D&C 109:68). The title applies to Jehovah, God of the Old Testament, who is Jesus Christ. The term "God of Jacob" (1 Ne. 19:10; Alma 29:11; 3 Ne. 4:30; Morm. 9:11), "Mighty One of Jacob" (1 Ne. 21:26; 2 Ne. 6:18), "Holy One of Jacob" (2 Ne. 27:34), and "Mighty One of Israel" (D&C 36:1; 1 Ne. 22:12) are variations of the title and appear elsewhere. Jacob, or Israel, was the father of the twelve tribes bearing his name and who were called upon to worship the true and living God, the Mighty Jehovah.
