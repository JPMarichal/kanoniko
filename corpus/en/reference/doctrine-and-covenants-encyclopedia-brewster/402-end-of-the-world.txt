End of the World

The "end of the world" (D&C 19:3; 45:22; 132:49) is defined in Joseph Smith's inspired rendition of the twenty-fourth chapter of Matthew, as found in the Pearl of Great Price. The Savior's disciples asked, "What is the sign of thy coming, and of the end of the world, or the destruction of the wicked, which is the end of the world?" (JS-M 1:4; italics added.) The "end of the world" (or the end of the wicked) will actually occur in two phases: the first phase will be prior to the Second Coming as the earth is cleansed of all but celestial and terrestrial beings; and the second and final phase will be at the end of the Millennium, when Satan's hosts of hell will be defeated and banished to outer darkness.
