Wisdom of the Wise

The "wisdom of the wise," which shall be surpassed by the wisdom of the righteous (D&C 76:9), is the learning of the world which is transitory and not of an eternal nature. It consists of the learning of those who "hearken not unto the counsel of God, for they set it aside, supposing they know of themselves, wherefore, their wisdom is foolishness and it profiteth them not" (2 Ne. 9:28).
