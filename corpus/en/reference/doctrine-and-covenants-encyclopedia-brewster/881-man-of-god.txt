Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Man of God

An 1833 revelation counseled Bishop Newel K. Whitney to search diligently for an agent to handle secular matters. One of his prime qualifications was that he be "a man of God, and of strong faith" (D&C 90:22). In a broad sense, a man of God is one who avoids that which is "foolish and hurtful" and "follow[s] after righteousness, godliness, faith, love, patience, meekness" (1 Tim. 6:5-12). He is full of good works (2 Tim. 3:17). In a more specific sense a man of God is an authorized minister or servant of God such as Moses (Deut. 33:1), Samuel (1 Sam. 9:6-10), or Nephi (Hel. 11:18).
