Prune My Vineyard

The Lord told his early leaders of the Church and all who would be called to the ministry that they were "called to prune my vineyard with a mighty pruning" (D&C 24:19; D&C 39:17; 95:4). The process of pruning involves the cutting or trimming away of branches on a fruit tree to increase the yield.

An allegorical explanation of the pruning of the Lord's vineyard in the last days was described by the ancient prophet Zenos (Jacob 5:61-74). Pruning accomplishes two purposes: (1) the corrupt branches are cast off, and (2) the tree is strengthened with new, vigorous fruit.
