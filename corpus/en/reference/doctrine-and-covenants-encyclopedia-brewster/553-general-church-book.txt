General Church Book

From the beginning of the Church, the counsel has been to maintain accurate records (D&C 20:83; 21:1; 47:1; 69:2-3; 85:1; 128:4). The "general church record," or "general church book," comprises all the recorded information stored by the Church in its archives. Both the Church and the individuals in it will be judged by the contents of these records. Therefore, "the matter of record keeping is one of the most important duties devolving on the Church" (CHMR 1:103).
