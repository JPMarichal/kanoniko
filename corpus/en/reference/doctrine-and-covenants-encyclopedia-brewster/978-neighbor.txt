Neighbor

Throughout scripture we are counseled to treat our neighbors with consideration, kindness, and respect (Ex. 20:16- 17; Lev. 19:13; Prov. 3:29; Matt. 5:43; Eph. 4:25; Mosiah 27:4; D&C 42:27; 82:19). The Savior gave the parable of the good Samaritan in response to the query, "Who is my neighbour?" (Luke 10:29-37.) A neighbor, therefore, is not simply one who lives nearby, but is one's fellow-man or anyone who has needs. In the context of salvation, all who do not presently have a knowledge and witness of the restored gospel of Jesus Christ with all its saving ordinances are in spiritual need and are the neighbors with whom Latter-day Saints should share such a message (D&C 88:81).
