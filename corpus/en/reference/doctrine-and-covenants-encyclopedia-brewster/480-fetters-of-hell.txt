Fetters of Hell

The Prophet Joseph wrote of the weight of the world's iniquity and said that it is as "an iron yoke, it is a strong band; they are the very handcuffs, and chains, and shackles, and fetters of hell" (D&C 123:8). This is the only use of this description in all of scripture. Fetters are things, such as chains and shackles, that restrain movement (Judg. 16:21; 2 Kgs. 25:7; Mark 5:4). The fetters of hell are those shackles of sin that bind one to Satan himself.

One's movement to higher orders is thus restricted by such fetters, for no unclean thing can dwell in the presence of holy beings (1 Ne. 10:21; Moses 6:57).
