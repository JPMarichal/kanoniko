Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Shalemanasseh

W. W. Phelps was one of four individuals who were given code names in an 1832 revelation, their identity was not known until recent years (D&C 82:11, pre-1981 editions). These four, along with five whose real names were discovered earlier, constituted an association under the Order of Enoch. At the time of the revelation the Lord chose not to reveal the identities of any of the nine in order to keep this knowledge from coming into the hands of the enemies of the Church (HC 1:255). Current editions of the Doctrine and Covenants do not use these code names.
