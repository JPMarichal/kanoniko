Hoyt W. Brewster, Doctrine and Covenants Encyclopedia ( 1988)

Compassion

One of the attributes of Deity is compassion (D&C 64:2; 101:9; 3 Ne. 17:6), and mortal man has been commanded to develop this trait also. "Execute true judgment, and shew mercy and compassions every man to his brother," declared the Lord (Zech. 7:9). Webster defines compassion as "sympathetic consciousness of others' distress together with a desire to alleviate it." The ministry of the Savior was marked by acts of compassion (Matt. 20:34; Mark 1:41; Luke 7:13). Truly those who show compassion to others do make a difference (Jude 1:22).
