First Thousand Years

Two references to the "first thousand years" are found in the Doctrine and Covenants (77:6-7; 88:108). This period of time represents the first millennium from the time Adam and Eve were expelled from Eden, or the first one thousand years of earth's temporal history (D&C 77:6; DS 1:78-81). At the end of "time," prior to the last great battle of the forces of good and evil, seven angels will sound their respective trumps and reveal the "secret acts of men, and the thoughts and intents of their hearts, and the mighty works of God" during each of the seven millennia of earth's temporal history (D&C 88:108-15). The "first thousand years" is represented by the first "seal" on the book which John the Revelator saw containing the earth's temporal history (D&C 77:6-7; Rev. 5).
