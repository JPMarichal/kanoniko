Work of the Gathering

The "work of the gathering" is mentioned twice in the Doctrine and Covenants, and in both instances refers to the process of gathering to Missouri-the land of Zion (58:56; 101:20; see also 63:24). In the latter revelation the Lord reaffirmed this location as the place he had appointed for the gathering of his Saints. However, the day is yet to come when the "Center Stake" of Zion will be established in Missouri (JD 11:324).
