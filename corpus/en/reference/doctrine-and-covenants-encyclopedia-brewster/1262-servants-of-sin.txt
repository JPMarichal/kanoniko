Servants of Sin

Those who falsely accuse the Lord's anointed have been divinely declared to be "servants of sin" (D&C 121:17). These are those whose lives are devoted to serving Satan, who cannot follow Christ (Moro. 7:11; see also 3 Ne. 13:24).
