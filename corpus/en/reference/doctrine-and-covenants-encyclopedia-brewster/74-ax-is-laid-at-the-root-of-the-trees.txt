Ax Is Laid at the Root of the Trees

Alma declared that "the Spirit saith: Behold, the ax is laid at the root of the tree; therefore every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit shall be hewn down and cast into the fire" (Alma 5:52). A little over a century later, in a confrontation with the Pharisees and Sadducees-they who relied so heavily on their lineage back to Abraham-John the Baptist was to invoke the same statement. Furthermore, he reminded them that their lofty lineage would not secure a place for them among the branches of Abraham's tree, for the faithful would be the only foliage thereon. (Matt. 3:7-10; JST, Matt. 3:34-37.) The spiritually decayed branches would have the ax (judgmental decrees of Deity) laid at their roots, and they would be severed from Abraham's bosom and God's presence.

Centuries later, the Lord once again reminded the wicked that they will be severed by an ax laid at their roots unless they bring forth "fruit meet for repentance" (D&C 97:7).
