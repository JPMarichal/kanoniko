Order of the Only Begotten Son

"The order of the Only Begotten Son" spoken of in Doctrine and Covenants 76:57 is the same as "the Holy Priesthood, after the Order of the Son of God," which we presently call the Melchizedek Priesthood (D&C 107:1-4).
