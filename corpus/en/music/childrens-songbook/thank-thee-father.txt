# Thank Thee, Father

1. Thank thee, Father, for this day,
For these hours of work and play,
For the shining sun above,
For thy great and tender love.

2. Help us now thy will to do.
Make us loving, kind, and true.
Through the day and through the night,
Lead us by thy holy light.

Psalm 92:1

Doctrine and Covenants 59:7
