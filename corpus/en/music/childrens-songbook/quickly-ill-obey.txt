# Quickly I’ll Obey

1. When my mother calls me,
Quickly I’ll obey.
I want to do just what is best
Each and ev’ry day.

2. When my father calls me,
Quickly I’ll obey.
I want to do just what is best
Each and ev’ry day.

3. Heav’nly Father loves me,
Blesses me each day.
I want to do just what is best
Each and ev’ry day.

Colossians 3:20

Alma 37:35
