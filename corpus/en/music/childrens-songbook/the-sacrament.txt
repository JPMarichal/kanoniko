# The Sacrament

1. As I take the water and bread,
I’ll think of the words the Savior said:
“This do in remembrance of me.
This do in remembrance of me.”

2. Jesus gave his life for me.
I want to serve him thankfully.
I will remember him.
I will remember him.

3 Nephi 18:7, 11

Matthew 26:26–28
