# The World Is So Big

1. The world is so big and, oh, so round,
And in it God’s creations are found;
Mountains and valleys and trees so tall,
Animals big and animals small.

2. The world is so big and, oh, so round,
And in it God’s creations are found;
Stars shining brightly through all the night,
Sun in the day so warm and so bright.

1 Nephi 17:36

Genesis 1
