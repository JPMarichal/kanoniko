Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

324 Rise Up, O Men of God

Text: William Pierson Merrill Music: William H. Walter Scriptures: 1 Tim. 6:11-12; D&C 4:2

Rise up, O men of God! Have done with lesser things. Give heart and soul and mind and strength To serve the King of Kings.

Rise up, O men of God, In one united throng. Bring in the day of brotherhood And end the night of wrong.

Rise up, O men of God! Tread where his feet have trod. As brothers of the Son of Man, Rise up, O men of God!
