Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

164 Great God, to Thee My Evening Song

Text: Anne Steele Music: Edward P. Kimball Scriptures: Ps. 100:4-5; Alma 37:37

Great God, to thee my evening song With humble gratitude I raise; Oh, let thy mercy tune my tongue And fill my heart with lively praise.

My days, unclouded as they pass, And ev'ry onward rolling hour Are monuments of wondrous grace, And witness to thy love and pow'r.

With hope in thee mine eyelids close; With sleep refresh my feeble frame. Safe in thy care may I repose And wake with praises to thy name.
