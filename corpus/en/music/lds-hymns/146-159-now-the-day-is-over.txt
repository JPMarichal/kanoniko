Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

159 Now the Day Is Over

Text: Sabine Baring-Gould Music: Joseph Barnby Scriptures: Prov. 3:24; Alma 37:37

Now the day is over; Night is drawing nigh; Shadows of the evening Steal across the sky.

Jesus, give the weary Calm and sweet repose; With thy tend'rest blessing May our eyelids close.
