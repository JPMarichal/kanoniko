Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

68 A Mighty Fortress Is Our God

Text: Martin Luther Music: Martin Luther Scriptures: 2 Sam. 22:2-3; Ps. 18:1-2

A mighty fortress is our God, A tower of strength ne'er failing. A helper mighty is our God, O'er ills of life prevailing. He overcometh all. He saveth from the Fall. His might and pow'r are great. He all things did create. And he shall reign forevermore.
