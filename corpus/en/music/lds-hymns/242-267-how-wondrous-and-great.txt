Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

267 How Wondrous and Great

Text: Henry U. Onderdonk Music: Johann Michael Haydn Scriptures: Rev. 15:3-4; Rom. 14:11

How wondrous and great Thy works, God of praise! How just, King of Saints, And true are thy ways! Oh, who shall not fear thee And honor thy name? Thou only art holy, Thou only supreme.

To nations long dark Thy light shall be shown. Their worship and vows Shall come to thy throne. Thy truth and thy judgments Shall spread all abroad, Till earth's ev'ry people Confess thee their God.
