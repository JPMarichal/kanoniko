Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

61 Raise Your Voices to the Lord

Text: Evan Stephens Music: Evan Stephens Scriptures: Ps. 69:30; Ps. 100

Raise your voices to the Lord, Ye who here have heard his word. As we part, his praise proclaim, Shout thanksgiving to his name.

Shout thanksgiving! Let our song Still our joy and praise prolong, Until here we meet again To renew the glad refrain.
