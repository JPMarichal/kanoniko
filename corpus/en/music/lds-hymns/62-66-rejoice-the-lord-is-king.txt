Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

66 Rejoice, The Lord Is King

Text: Charles Wesley Music: Horatio Parker Scriptures: Philip. 4:4; Ps. 32:11

Rejoice, the Lord is King! Your Lord and King adore! Mortals, give thanks and sing And triumph evermore.

Lift up your heart! Lift up your voice! Rejoice, again I say, rejoice! Lift up your heart! Lift up your voice! Rejoice, again I say, rejoice!

The Lord, the Savior reigns, The God of truth and love. When he had purged our stains, He took his seat above.

His Kingdom cannot fail; He rules o'er earth and heav'n. The keys of death and hell To Christ the Lord are giv'n.
