Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

222 Hear Thou Our Hymn, O Lord

Text: Frank W. Asper Verse: Frank W. Asper Scriptures: Ps. 86:6, 11

Hear thou our hymn, O Lord, With thankful hearts we pray. Help us thy will to follow now, And walk the narrow way.

Keep thou our hearts aglow With thy eternal word. Give us thy Spirit ev'ry hour, We ask thee, gracious Lord.
