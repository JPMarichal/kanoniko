Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

45 Lead Me into Life Eternal

Text: John A. Widtsoe Music: Alexander Schreiner Scriptures: Ps. 143:10; D&C 42:61

Lead me into life eternal By the gospel's holy call. Let thy promise rest upon me; Grant me ready strength for all.

Father, all my heart I give thee; All my service shall be thine. Guide me as I search in weakness; Let thy loving light be mine.

Hear me as I pray in meekness; Let my strength be as thy day. Give me faith, the greater knowledge; Father, bless me as I pray.
