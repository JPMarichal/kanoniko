Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

79 With All the Power of Heart and Tongue

Text: Isaac Watts Music: Lowell M. Durham Scriptures: Ps. 138:1-8

With all the pow'r of heart and tongue, I'll praise my Maker in my song. Angels shall hear the notes I'll raise, Approve the song, and join the praise.

I'll sing thy truth and mercy, Lord; I'll sing the wonders of thy word. Not all thy works and names below So much thy pow'r and glory show.

Amidst a thousand snares I stand, Upheld and guided by thy hand. Thy words my fainting soul revive And keep my dying faith alive.
