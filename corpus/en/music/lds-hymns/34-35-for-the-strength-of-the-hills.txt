Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

35 For the Strength of the Hills

Text: Felicia D. Hemans Music: Evan Stephens Scriptures: Ps. 95:1-7

For the strength of the hills we bless thee, Our God, our father's God; Thou hast made thy children mighty By the touch of the mountain sod. Thou hast led thy chosen Israel To freedom's last abode;

For the strength of the hills we bless thee, Our God, our father's God.

At the hand of foul oppressors, We've born and suffered long; Thou hast been our help in weakness, And thy pow'r hath made us strong. Amid ruthless foes outnumbered, In weariness we trod;

Thou hast led us here in safety Where the mountain bulwark stands As the guardian of the loved ones Thou hast brought from many lands. For the rock and for the river, The valley's fertile sod,

We are watchers of a beacon Whose light must never die; We are guardians of an altar 'Midst the silence of the sky. Here the rocks yield founts of courage, Struck forth as by thy rod.
