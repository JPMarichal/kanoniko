Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

24 God Bless Our Prophet Dear

Text: Bernard Snow Music: Harry A. Dean Scriptures: D&C 107:22; 3 Ne. 19:23

God bless our prophet dear; May health and comfort cheer his noble heart. His words with fire impress on souls that thou wilt bless To choose in righteousess the better part.

So shall thy kingdom spread, As by the prophets said, from sea to sea. Truth burns in ev'ry soul; As one united whole, We hasten to the goal we long to see.

Oh, may thy Saints be one, Like Father and the Son, nor disagree. United heart and hand, so may we ever stand, A firm and valiant band Eternally.
