154 Father, This Hour Has Been One of Joy

Text: Nan Greene Hunter Music: Lynn R. Shurtleff Scriptures: 1 Chr. 16:8-10; D&C 11:13

Father, this hour has been one of joy; We thank thee for thy many blessings. Our hearts are filled with thy gracious love And thy tender caring.
