Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

18 The Voice of God Again Is Heard

Text: Evan Stephens Music: Evan Stephens

The voice of God again is heard. The silence has been broken. The curse of darkness is withdrawn. The Lord from heaven has spoken. Rejoice ye living and ye dead! Rejoice, for your salvation Begins anew this happy morn Of final dispensation.

O messengers of truth, go forth; Proclaim the gospel story. Go forth the nations to prepare To greet the King of glory. We shout hosanna, shout again, Till all creation blending Shall join in one great, grand amen Of anthems never ending.
