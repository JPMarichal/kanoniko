Various authors, LDS Hymns ( 1985)

225 We Are Marching On to Glory

Text: John M. Chamberlain Music: John M. Chamberlain Scriptures: D&C 27:15-18; 1 Tim. 6:12

We are marching on to glory; We are working for our crown. We will make our armor brighter And never lay it down.

We are marching, marching homeward To that bright land afar. We work for life eternal; It is our guiding star.

Then day by day we are marching; To heaven we are bound. Each good act brings us nearer That home where we'll be crowned.

Then, with the ransomed children That throng the starry throne, We will praise our Lord and Savior, His pow'r and mercy own.
