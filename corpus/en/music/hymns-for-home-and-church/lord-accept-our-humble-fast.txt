# Lord, Accept Our Humble Fast

1. Lord, accept our humble fast.
Comfort those in need.
May our off’rings nourish them
Through this simple deed.

2. Lord, accept our offering
Of a broken heart.
Make our spirits more contrite;
Saving truths impart.

3. Lord, accept the tithes we bring;
May Thy kingdom grow.
Open heaven’s windows wide;
Let Thy blessings flow.

4. Magnify our offerings.
Use our willing hands.
Consecrate our service, Lord,
To Thy mighty plan.

Isaiah 58:6–11

Matthew 6:33
