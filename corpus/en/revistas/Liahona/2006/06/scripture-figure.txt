As you retell scripture stories, this figure can represent any young woman in the scriptures, such as Esther, one of the ten virgins, or a handmaiden. The figure can be mounted on heavy paper, colored, cut out, then made into a stick puppet, flannel board figure, or paper sack puppet. Make several figures, and color the hair and clothing differently for each one.
