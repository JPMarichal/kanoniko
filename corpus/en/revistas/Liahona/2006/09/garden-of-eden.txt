Instructions: Remove these pages from the magazine, mount them on heavy paper, then cut out the trees and figures on the heavy solid lines. On each figure, fold the tabs back along the broken lines, and tape or glue the ends together to make a finger puppet. Use these puppets to help you tell the story of Adam and Eve in a family home evening lesson or Primary talk (see Genesis 2:15–25; 3).

Note: If you do not wish to remove pages from the magazine, this activity may be copied, traced, or printed from the Internet at www.lds.org. For English, click on “Gospel Library.” For other languages, click on the world map.
