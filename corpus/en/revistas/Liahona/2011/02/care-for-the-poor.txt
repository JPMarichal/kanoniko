[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="127169237" id="aside1_p1"><span class="page-break" data-page="9"></span>“Throughout history, the Lord has measured societies and individuals by how well they cared for the poor. He has said:</p> <p data-aid="127169238" id="aside1_p2">“‘For the earth is full, and there is enough and to spare; yea, I prepared all things, and have given unto the children of men to be agents unto themselves.</p> <p data-aid="127169239" id="aside1_p3">“‘Therefore, if any man shall take of the abundance which I have made, and impart not his portion, according to the law of my gospel, unto the poor and the needy, he shall, with the wicked, lift up his eyes in hell, being in torment’ (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/104?lang=eng&amp;id=p17-p18#p17">D&amp;C 104:17–18</a>; see also <a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/56?lang=eng&amp;id=p16-p17#p16">D&amp;C 56:16–17</a>).</p> <p data-aid="127169240" id="aside1_p4">“Furthermore, He declares, ‘In your temporal things you shall be equal, and this not grudgingly, otherwise the abundance of the manifestations of the Spirit shall be withheld’ (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/70?lang=eng&amp;id=p14#p14">D&amp;C 70:14</a>; see also <a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/49?lang=eng&amp;id=p20#p20">D&amp;C 49:20</a>; <a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/78?lang=eng&amp;id=p5-p7#p5">78:5–7</a>).</p> <p data-aid="127169241" id="aside1_p5">“We control the disposition of our means and resources, but we account to God for this stewardship over earthly things. It is gratifying to witness your generosity as you contribute to fast offerings and humanitarian projects. Over the years, the suffering of millions has been alleviated, and countless others have been enabled to help themselves through the generosity of the Saints. Nevertheless, as we pursue the cause of Zion, each of us should prayerfully consider whether we are doing what we should and all that we should in the Lord’s eyes with respect to the poor and the needy.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="127169242" id="aside1_p6">Elder D. Todd Christofferson of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, “Come to Zion,” <em>Liahona</em> and <em>Ensign,</em> Nov. 2008, 39.</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]
