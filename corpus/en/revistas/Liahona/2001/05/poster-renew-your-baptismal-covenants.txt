Renew Your Baptismal Covenants
When you take the sacrament, you remind the Lord—and yourself—of your commitment to be obedient. Elder L. Tom Perry of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles said: “We make mistakes even after baptism. The Lord has, therefore, provided a way for us to renew our baptismal covenants through partaking of the sacrament each week” (Ensign, November 1984, 19).
