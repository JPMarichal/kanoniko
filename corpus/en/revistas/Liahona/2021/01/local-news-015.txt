Brothers and Sisters, we’ve witnessed and experienced many things in the gospel these last few years. Not just internationally, but also in the United Kingdom. The UK Local Pages are for us to share our stories that show why living the restored gospel of Jesus Christ in the UK is great and makes a difference.

To submit written content and photographs please go to the website ensignsubmissions.co.uk and here you will find everything you need to make an easy submission.

If you have questions, contact us through the LDS UK Facebook page (facebook.com/ChurchofJesusChristUK) or by email at ensignukinserts@gmail.com.

Thank you for your love for the gospel and Christlike service in the United Kingdom.
