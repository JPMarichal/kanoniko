Do you have a faith-promoting personal experience you would like to share?

We want to hear from you!

If you’re a faithful member of the Church in the Africa Central Area who has grown stronger or learned something new through a personal experience, we would love to hear from you.

In particular, you are invited to share personal experiences around the themes of the 2021 Area Plan, which include:

- Increasing faith in Jesus Christ

- Studying the Book of Mormon

- Keeping the Sabbath day holy

- Sharing the gospel

- Strengthening new converts

- Rescuing less-actives

- Doing family history and taking family names to the temple

- Preparing to serve a mission

- Paying an honest tithe

- Living the law of the fast

- Write your experience clearly and simply, keeping your focus on gospel principles.

- Your story should be no longer than 800 words.

- Please ensure your article is centered around a personal experience and what you have learned from it. Sermons are unlikely to be published.

- Email your submission to africacentral-communication@ChurchofJesusChrist.org

- Please include your full name, ward, stake, and telephone number at the top of the document.

- Your story will be edited. Please be prepared to work with our editors.

- It may take several months to receive a response from our team.

- Not all submissions will be published.
