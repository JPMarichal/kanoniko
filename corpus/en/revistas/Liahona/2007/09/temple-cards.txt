Magazines in 2003 and the April 2005 issue contained Temple Cards. Since that time, more temples have been dedicated.

Remove this page from the magazine, glue it to heavy paper, and cut out the cards. Add these cards to the cards that have already appeared in the magazine.

Note: To find the cards in the 2003 and 2005 issues or if you do not wish to remove this page from the magazine, go to www.lds.org. For English, click on “Gospel Library.” For other languages, click on “Languages.”
