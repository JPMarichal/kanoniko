[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="127344380" id="aside1_p1">“[Jesus Christ] shall make intercession for all the children of men; and they that believe in him shall be saved” (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/2?lang=eng&amp;id=p9#p9">2 Nephi 2:9</a>).</p> <p data-aid="127344382" id="aside1_p2">In this scripture, the word <em>intercession</em> has great meaning. It means “acting as a mediator to help two parties resolve a problem.” In this case, the Savior is the mediator who helps all people who have become separated from Heavenly Father because of sin.</p> <p data-aid="127344384" id="aside1_p3">This scripture helped me know the importance of our Savior, Jesus Christ. I am grateful for His Atonement. Only through Him can we live again with our Father in Heaven.</p> <div class="bio"> <p data-aid="127344386" id="aside1_p4">Hanisha A., India</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]
