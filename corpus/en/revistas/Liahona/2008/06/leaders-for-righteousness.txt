“President Gordon B. Hinckley has said of you, ‘You are … the finest [and strongest] generation of young people ever in the history of this Church.’ I believe you have been prepared and reserved to be on the earth at this time when the challenges and opportunities are the greatest. I believe that the Lord is counting on you to be a leader for righteousness and to stand as a witness ‘at all times and in all things, and in all places’ (Mosiah 18:9). Indeed, it can be said of you that you are the ‘bright shining hope’ of the future.”

  Elaine S. Dalton, First Counselor in the Young Women General Presidency, “It Shows in Your Face,” Liahona and Ensign, May 2006, 109.
