After his father, Mormon, died, Moroni wrote on the golden plates. He wrote about the priesthood, the sacrament, and baptism. He challenged us to read the Book of Mormon and ask Heavenly Father if it’s true. If we pray and listen for the answer, the Holy Ghost can tell us it’s true. Moroni buried the plates in the ground to keep them safe.

  I have been reading my scriptures with my dad every day. It is helping me get a testimony. I will not give up.

  Spencer P., age 10, Wisconsin, USA
