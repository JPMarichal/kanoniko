When Mary and Joseph traveled to Bethlehem, the only place they could find to stay was a stable with animals. Jesus Christ was born in the stable and laid in a manger. Shepherds and Wise Men followed a star to find Jesus. The Nephites also saw this star and knew that Jesus had been born. We celebrate Christmas to remember His birth.
