Once there was a wicked king. He wanted to rule over the Nephite people.

Moroni was the captain of the Nephite army. He was strong, and he loved God.

Captain Moroni wanted to protect his people from the wicked king’s army.

He decided to make a flag for his people.

Captain Moroni’s flag reminded his people that they should follow God and protect their families. Then they could have peace.
