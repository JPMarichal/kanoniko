Match each animal with the Old Testament person or event it is associated with.

  --------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1 Lamb    a. Returned to Noah with an olive leaf (see Gen. 8:6–11).
  2 Dove    b. One of the ten plagues inflicted on Egypt (see Ex. 8:1–15).
  3 Fish    c. Given to the Israelites when they were tired of eating manna (see Ex. 16:11–13).
  4 Raven   d. David saved it from being eaten by a lion and a bear (see 1 Sam. 17:32–37).
  5 Quail   e. Fed Elijah while he was hiding from King Ahab (see 1 Kgs. 17:1–6).
  6 Frog    f. Daniel was saved from being eaten by them (see Dan. 6).
  7 Lion    g. Jonah spent three days in the belly of one (see Jonah 1).
