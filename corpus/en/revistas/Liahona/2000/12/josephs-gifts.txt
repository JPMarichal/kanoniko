Joseph Smith Jr. was born two days before Christmas in 1805. From the time he was just 14 years old, he shared many gifts from Heavenly Father with the people on earth. Learn about the Prophet Joseph’s gifts by reading the questions below and circling the gift box or boxes with a correct answer. The answers can be found in D&C 115:3–4; D&C 130:22; JS—H 1:17–19, 30–35, 59, 66–67.
