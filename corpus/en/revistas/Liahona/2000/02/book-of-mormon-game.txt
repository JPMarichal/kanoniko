Remove these two pages and mount them on heavy paper. Cut out the cards on the solid black lines, fold them on the dashed lines (picture and text facing out), then tape or glue them shut.

1.  Play as teams or individuals.

2.  Lay the cards picture side up.

3.  Take turns choosing a card, reading aloud the question on the back, and answering the question.

4.  Look up the answer in the scriptures. If the correct answer was given before the scripture was read, that player or team gets the number of points listed on the card. If the scripture was needed, the player or team gets 1 point for questions 1–5, 3 points for question 6.

Note: Question 6 for each color is intended to be difficult. You may want to study it on your own for a Primary talk, or you may want to study it as a family during family home evening.
