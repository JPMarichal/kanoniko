For 120 years, Noah repeatedly “called upon the children of men that they should repent; but they hearkened not. …

“The earth was corrupt before God, and it was filled with violence. …

“And God said unto Noah: The end of all flesh is come before me” (Moses 8:20, 28, 30).

Despite “the longsuffering of God … in the days of Noah, … [only] eight souls were saved” (1 Peter 3:20).
