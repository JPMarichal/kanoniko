The skyline of this capital city is dominated by the tallest twin towers in the world. The government in Malaysia officially recognized The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in 1977. Today in this country the Church has:

- 10,900 members (approximately)

- 5 districts, 31 congregations

- 8 family history centers
