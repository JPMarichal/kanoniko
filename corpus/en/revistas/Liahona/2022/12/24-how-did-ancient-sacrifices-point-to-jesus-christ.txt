Malachi warned against offering polluted sacrifices to the Lord. The Lord required “a pure offering” (Malachi 1:11) and would not have His table polluted (see Malachi 1:12).

The sacrifices had to be pure because they “pointed to the way God has provided to bring us back into His presence—Jesus Christ and His Atonement.” To offer anything less was a mockery of both Christ’s perfection and His sacrifice.
