In Zechariah 3:3–7, Zechariah describes a vision of Joshua the high priest. In the vision, an angel takes away Joshua’s dirty clothes and replaces them with clean clothes. Similarly, if we repent, Jesus Christ can take away our sins. We are forgiven and can become more holy.

What can you learn about the nature of holiness from these scriptures about clothing?

- Daniel 7:9

- Revelation 3:5; 7:9

- Alma 5:27

- Isaiah 61:10

- 2 Nephi 4:33; 9:14

- Isaiah 52:1

- Doctrine and Covenants 82:14

- 2 Corinthians 7:1

- Alma 5:21; 7:25

- Ether 12:37
