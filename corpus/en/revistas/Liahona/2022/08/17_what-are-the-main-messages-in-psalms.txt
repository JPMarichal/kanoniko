Written by seven different authors, the book of Psalms, like hymns of today, sing praises to the Lord, give thanks, and testify of the Messiah. The psalms express trust in the Lord and speak of His power and blessings. Consider studying some of the psalms by topic.

- Goodness of God: Psalm 36; 46; 67; 103; 104

- Greatness of God: Psalm 18; 105; 136

- Messiah: Psalm 16; 40; 118; 132

- Praise: Psalm 9; 47; 67

- Repentance: Psalm 32; 38; 51

- Trust in the Lord: Psalm 11; 27; 62; 91

- Wisdom: Psalm 1; 14; 15; 34; 37; 119

- Zion: Psalm 48; 76; 84

  “Be still, and know that I am God” (Psalm 46:10).

  “Thy word is a lamp unto my feet” (Psalm 119:105).

  “Happy is that people, whose God is the Lord” (Psalm 144:15).
