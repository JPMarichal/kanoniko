Through his trials, Job remained an example of trusting God in patience, and the Lord delivered him from his trials. As the psalmist said, “Our fathers trusted in thee: they trusted, and thou didst deliver them” (Psalm 22:4). Trusting God brought Job relief from suffering and refinement for his soul.
