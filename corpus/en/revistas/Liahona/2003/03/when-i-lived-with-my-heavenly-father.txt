Instructions: Color each picture. Then tell this true story (see Abr. 3–5).

1. We lived with Heavenly Father before we were born.
2. He asked Jesus Christ to create the world for us.
3. He made the sun, the moon, and the stars to give light.
4. Jesus made the world beautiful with trees and flowers.
5. He made fish, birds, and animals and put them on the earth.
6. Then He placed Adam and Eve, our first ancestors, on the earth.
