Jesus taught that we can show our love for Him by “feeding His sheep” (see John 21:15–17). We can do that by helping others. These children are feeding Jesus’s sheep by helping their neighbor pull weeds from her garden. We should always look for people to serve.

See if you can find and circle the 10 sheep hidden in this picture.

Ask them who Jesus Christ’s sheep are. Explain that the Lord expects us to help other people. Tell of a time somebody helped you.</p> [/SIDEBAR]
