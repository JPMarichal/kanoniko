Trying to help others be their best selves.

True

False

Wanting only to have fun and not staying around when things get hard.

True

False

Sharing my standards, my beliefs, and my testimony.

True

False

True

False

Always waiting for people to come up and talk to me.

True

False

Sticking to my immediate group of friends and acquaintances.

True

False

True

False
