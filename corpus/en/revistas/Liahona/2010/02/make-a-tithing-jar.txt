You will need a small glass jar, colored rubber bands or yarn, and colored paper.

1.  Wash and dry the jar.

2.  Decorate the outside of the jar with the rubber bands. If you don’t have rubber bands, you can use string or yarn.

3.  Cut two large circles out of the paper. Place the circles over the top of the jar, and secure them with a rubber band around the rim. Trim the extra paper.

4.  Cut a slit in the top of the paper for the money to slide through.
