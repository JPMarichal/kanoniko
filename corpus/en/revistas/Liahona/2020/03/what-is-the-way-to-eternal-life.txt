We return to Heavenly Father by making and keeping covenants with Him. Jesus Christ showed us the way along this covenant path, and we gain eternal life by following His example.

What is the gateway to the covenant path? (See 2 Nephi 31:17–18.) How do we show our willingness to follow the example of Jesus Christ and keep His commandments?

How do we progress along the path? (See 2 Nephi 31:16, 20; Mosiah 5:7–9.)

Sin takes us off the path. How do we return? (See 1 Nephi 10:18; 3 Nephi 9:13–14.)

What blessing can we receive if we endure to the end? (See 2 Nephi 31:20.)
