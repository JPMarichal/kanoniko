Enos prayed all day and heard God’s voice! Read about it in Enos 1 in the Book of Mormon or in the story “Enos Prayed” on page F20.

- Start with “Dear Heavenly Father …”

- Thank Him for blessings.

- Tell Him about your day or your feelings.

- Ask Him for what you need or pray for people who need help.

- End with “In the name of Jesus Christ, amen.”

- Take time to listen to the Holy Ghost.

You may not hear God’s voice as Enos did, but you can receive answers in other ways, like through thoughts or feelings. Sometimes answers take time. But God always loves you!
