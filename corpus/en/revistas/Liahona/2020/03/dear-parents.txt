This month’s magazine tells the story of Enos. His parents taught him about the gospel many times. Finally, their words “sunk deep” into his heart (Enos 1:3). We should share our testimonies with our children often. We never know when our words will make a difference.

How many times can your family find the word “pray” in this issue?

God is happy when you talk with Him!

The Friend
