In 1820, Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ appeared to Joseph Smith. Heavenly Father said, “This is My Beloved Son. Hear Him!” The Restoration of the gospel began that day 200 years ago. It continues today!

Someday Jesus Christ will come back to the earth again. Here are four ways you can help with the Restoration and prepare for the time when Jesus will return:

- Build your faith in Jesus Christ.

- Find out about your family and help temple work be done for them.

- Prepare to go to the temple.

- Help people learn about Jesus Christ and His Church.

Doing these things will help prepare the world for the wonderful day when Jesus will come again!
