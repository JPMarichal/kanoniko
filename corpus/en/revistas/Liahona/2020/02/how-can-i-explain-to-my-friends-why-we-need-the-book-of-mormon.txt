We believe that the Book of Mormon was prepared by the Lord for our day and is necessary for us. Here are a few reasons why (as explained by President Russell M. Nelson):

- It contains the answers to life’s most compelling questions.

- It teaches the doctrine of Christ.

- It expands and clarifies many of the “plain and precious” truths from the Bible that were lost (see 1 Nephi 13:29–33).

- It provides the fullest and most authoritative understanding of the Atonement of Jesus Christ to be found anywhere.

- It teaches us about the gathering of scattered Israel.

- It exposes the tactics of the adversary (see 2 Nephi 26–33).

- It dispels false religious traditions.

- It abolishes forever the false concepts that revelation ended with the Bible and that the heavens are sealed today.

(From Russell M. Nelson, “The Book of Mormon: What Would Your Life Be Like without It?” Liahona, Nov. 2017, 62).
