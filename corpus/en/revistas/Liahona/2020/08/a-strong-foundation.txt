Helaman taught that we need to build our foundation on Jesus Christ (see Helaman 5:12). That means doing things that will bring us closer to Him. When we do, we will be able to withstand hard things in life.

1.  Cut out the rocks on this page.

2.  Choose a rock and read the scripture on it. How does doing that thing make you strong and bring you closer to Christ?

3.  Put the rock under the temple to build a strong foundation. Keep playing until all the rocks are in place.
