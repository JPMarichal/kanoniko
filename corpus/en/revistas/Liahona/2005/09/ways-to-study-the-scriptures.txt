Instructions: Read the caption under each picture; then color the picture. You could use these captions and pictures for a family home evening lesson or a Primary talk.

1.  Set a goal to read one or more verses of scripture each day.

2.  Read the scriptures aloud with your family.

3.  Read the illustrated scripture stories in the children’s section of the Liahona or in Old Testament Stories, New Testament Stories, Book of Mormon Stories, and Doctrine and Covenants Stories. Read the stories to yourself or to younger children.

4.  After hearing a scripture story in church, find it in the scriptures and read it at home.
