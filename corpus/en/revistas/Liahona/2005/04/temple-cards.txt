Each issue in 2003 contained Temple Cards. Since that time more temples have been dedicated. Nine of those temples are shown on this page. When nine more temples have been dedicated, another page will appear in the magazine. Remove this page from the magazine, glue it to heavy paper, and cut out the cards. Add these cards to the cards that have already appeared in the magazine to remind you of the importance of temples.

Note: To find the cards in the 2003 issues or if you do not wish to remove this page from the magazine, go to www.lds.org. For English, click on “Gospel Library.” For other languages, click on the world map.
