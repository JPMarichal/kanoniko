[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="163914381" id="aside1_p1">Did you know there have been only six proclamations issued by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints? The first three were published between 1841 and 1865. The last three proclamations have been published since 1980:</p> <ul class="bullet"> <li> <p data-aid="163914402" id="aside1_p2"><strong>“<a class="cross-ref" href="/study/general-conference/1980/04/proclamation?lang=eng">Proclamation from the First Presidency and the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints</a>” (1980)</strong>, approved by President Spencer W. Kimball (1895–1985) and read by then-Elder Gordon B. Hinckley on the 150th anniversary of the organization of the Church.</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="163914412" id="aside1_p3"><strong>“<a class="cross-ref" href="/study/scriptures/the-family-a-proclamation-to-the-world/the-family-a-proclamation-to-the-world?lang=eng">The Family: A Proclamation to The World</a>” (1995)</strong>, read by President Gordon B. Hinckley at the General Relief Society Meeting to share the Church’s official statement on the doctrine of the family.</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="163914426" id="aside1_p4"><strong>“<a class="cross-ref" href="/study/scriptures/the-restoration-of-the-fulness-of-the-gospel-of-jesus-christ/a-bicentennial-proclamation-to-the-world?lang=eng">The Restoration of the Fulness of the Gospel of Jesus Christ: A Bicentennial Proclamation to the World</a>” (2020)</strong>, read by President Russell M. Nelson during the April 2020 general conference—200 years after the First Vision. It reaffirmed that Joseph Smith was a prophet of God and that The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is the restored Church of Jesus Christ.</p> </li> </ul> <p data-aid="163914440" id="aside1_p5">Proclamations are meant for everyone across the world. They are “solemn and sacred in nature and … issued with the intent to bring forth, build up, and regulate the affairs of the Church as the kingdom of God on the earth.”<a class="note-ref" href="#note2"></a> Proclamations are a rare and powerful testament that we have a living prophet guiding the Church and the world today.</p> [/SIDEBAR]
