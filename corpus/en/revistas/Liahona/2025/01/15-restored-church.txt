This year’s Come, Follow Me study of the Doctrine and Covenants will highlight how the Lord restored His Church and gospel through latter-day prophets. Many articles in this month’s issue highlight the blessings of the Restoration.

On page 2, President Russell M. Nelson shares ways we can gather to the Savior, gather with the Saints, and gather in God’s service. Consider setting a goal for this month to contribute to one of those three kinds of gathering.

Elder Patrick Kearon of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles talks about the opportunities we have in the restored Church of Jesus Christ to “serve others with love” (see page 8). Are there people in your ward or branch who are struggling? You could pray for these people and ask for inspiration on how to serve them.

In 2020, the First Presidency and Quorum of the Twelve Apostles issued “The Restoration of the Fulness of the Gospel of Jesus Christ: A Bicentennial Proclamation to the World” (see page 40). As you review the Restoration proclamation, you might consider these questions: What key events does it mention, and how did the Lord guide those events?
