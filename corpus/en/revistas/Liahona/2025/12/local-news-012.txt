At this sacred season, we rejoice in the birth of our Saviour, Jesus Christ. His coming into the world is the greatest expression of our Heavenly Father’s love. Because of Him, we have reason to hope, to change and to become. His light continues to shine in our lives and in the lives of those around us.

One of the blessings of Church membership is the strength we receive through one another’s faith and experiences. For many years, the Liahona local pages have been the place where members have shared testimonies and stories of discipleship. We express heartfelt gratitude to all who have contributed so faithfully.

Beginning in 2026, our local faith stories will be shared in a new way. They will now be published online through our country websites, such as uk.churchofjesuschrist.org and on country social media channels. This change will allow inspiring messages to be shared more widely, more often, and without the delays of print publishing.

We warmly invite members throughout the Europe Norih Area to continue submitting stories and testimonies that inspire faith in Jesus Christ. Your voice can lift and encourage others.

As we celebrate the birth of the Saviour, may we feel His love more fully and help others feel it too. He is the Light of the World. As we turn to Him, we find peace, strength, and purpose.

Merry Christmas and a blessed New Year.
