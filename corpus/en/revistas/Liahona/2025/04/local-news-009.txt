We would like to invite all members within the United Kingdom and Ireland to share your stories, testimonies, and experiences for publication in the Liahona Local Pages.

Your stories matter. Each day, members across the United Kingdom and Ireland are lighting the world through service and testimony. Our ancestors did the same. These experiences can uplift and inspire others on their own spiritual journey.

We encourage contributors of all ages to share their perspectives. No testimony or experience is too small!

Content for the UK and Ireland Liahona Local Pages typically ranges from 200 to 300 words, and the focus should always be on the Restored Gospel of Jesus Christ being lived in some form in the United Kingdom and Ireland.

Possible topics for your contributions may include:

- Personal testimonies

- Stories of service rendered and received

- Insights gained from family or church history

- Stories of conversion

- Experiences in ministering and reaching out to others

You do not need to be a confident writer- our team is more than happy to work with you to get your story written and published.

Submissions are emailed to ukiliahona@gmail.com.

Submission Guidelines for the UK and Ireland Liahona Local Pages

1. Submit text only in a word document. Include title, author, stake, and desired image captions (no actual images or paintings).

2. Submit all images separately from the article document.

3. Get written consent (showing name, contact details and signature) from those who are in any images. Consent can be sent online or written to you, but please take a clear picture or screenshot of that consent and include it as a jpeg in your submission.

4. Attach document, images, and consent (if applicable) and email them to ukiliahona@gmail.com.
