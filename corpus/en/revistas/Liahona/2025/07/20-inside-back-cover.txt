[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="166308808" id="aside1_p1">When Joseph Smith and his family needed somewhere to stay in 1831, the Johnson family, who joined the Church earlier that year, welcomed the Smiths and Joseph’s scribes into their home. Joseph received several revelations here, including one in February 1832 where he and Sidney Rigdon saw Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ in vision and learned about the three kingdoms of glory.</p> [/SIDEBAR] [SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="166308817" id="aside2_p1">“And now, after the many testimonies which have been given of him, this is the testimony, last of all, which we give of him: That he lives!</p> <p data-aid="166308825" id="aside2_p2">“For we saw him, even on the right hand of God; and we heard the voice bearing record that he is the Only Begotten of the Father” (<a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&amp;id=p22-p23#p22">Doctrine and Covenants 76:22–23</a>).</p> [/SIDEBAR]
