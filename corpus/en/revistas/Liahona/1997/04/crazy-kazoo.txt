You will need an empty toilet paper tube or a piece of heavy paper you can form into a roll, a 10 cm x 10 cm piece of cellophane or waxed paper (not plastic wrap), and a rubber band.

1.  Put the cellophane or waxed paper over one end of the roll.

2.  Hold the paper tightly in place with the rubber band.

3.  Hum into the open end of the roll. You can hum tunes or just make strange noises. Make kazoos with your friends and have a whole kazoo band.
