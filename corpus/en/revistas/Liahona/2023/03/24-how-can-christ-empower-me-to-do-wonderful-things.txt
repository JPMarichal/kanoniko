Throughout our lives, as we grow closer to Christ and learn to hear the voice of the Spirit, we may be asked to do things we feel unsure about. Like Peter walking on water, we can be empowered to do what feels impossible so long as we focus on Christ and, as Elder Quentin L. Cook of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles taught, “live by faith and not by fear” (“Live by Faith and Not by Fear,” Liahona, Nov. 2007, 70).

Read Matthew 14:22–33 and outline Peter’s experience.
