As the Light of the World, Jesus Christ lights our path and fills our lives with “joy” and “marvelous light” (see Alma 36:20). Even when the world around us seems dark, we have reason to celebrate because Jesus Christ continues to light the way for those who seek Him. Truly, “the Lord is my light; / He is my joy and my song. / By day and by night / He leads, He leads me along.”
