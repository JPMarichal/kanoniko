Our Savior is the perfect example of obedience to Heavenly Father, declaring, “I seek not mine own will, but the will of the Father which hath sent me” (John 5:30).

The Savior was baptized to show His exact obedience to Heavenly Father. Likewise, when we show our obedience by being baptized, we enter into a covenant with God to be dedicated, obedient disciples of Jesus Christ. (See 2 Nephi 31:5–13.)
