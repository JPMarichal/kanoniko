The Apostle Paul taught the Corinthians what a resurrected body was like. Our bodies will be “raised in incorruption … in glory … [and] in power” (1 Corinthians 15:42–43). He also taught that “death [will be] swallowed up in victory” (1 Corinthians 15:54), but what is this victory? The following quotations can help us understand the victories achieved because of the Resurrection.
