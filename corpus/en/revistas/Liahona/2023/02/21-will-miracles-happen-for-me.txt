Christ performed many miracles during His earthly ministry, such as when He turned water into wine at the marriage feast in Cana (see John 2:1–11). As we see in the New Testament, miracles have always been performed through priesthood power and faith.

Some might wonder, but how do I have the faith that miracles will happen for me?

President Russell M. Nelson taught: “Do the spiritual work to seek miracles. Prayerfully ask God to help you exercise that kind of faith” (“The Power of Spiritual Momentum,” Liahona, May 2022, 100).

Our God is a God of miracles: John 6:5–14; 1 Nephi 17:51; 2 Nephi 27:23; Mormon 9:15, 18–19

Faith and trust in the Lord: Mark 4:36–41; Mormon 9:20–27; Ether 12:12, 18; Moroni 7:33

Priesthood power: Alma 23:6; Mormon 9:17–18
