The Lord is changing teens’ lives through FSY (For the Strength of Youth), a five-day conference for youth ages 14–18. Youth who have attended FSY say they have a stronger testimony of Jesus Christ and a renewed or increased desire to serve a mission, and they are better able to interact with other youth. So what can you and your teen expect from FSY? Here is a closer look at what it’s all about.

  One of my personal highlights at FSY was morning gospel study. I was always able to feel the Spirit, and it was the time most of my questions were answered.

  James R., age 15, California

  At FSY, I learned that when you trust in the Lord, He carries you. Trusting in the Lord is like a shield from all the temptation and darkness in the world.

  Katy B., age 17, Washington

[SIDEBAR] <section> <p data-aid="153903466" id="aside1_p1">Counselors are recommended by bishops and stake presidents. They are young single adults who have been screened and have had background checks. Young single adults are uniquely able to connect with teens and are an essential and valuable part of the FSY experience.</p> </section> <section> <p data-aid="153903478" id="aside1_p2">YSA counselors have received basic training to help teens who may be feeling overwhelmed or need extra support. For some activities, like dances, there are alternate activities that teens can participate in. </p> </section> <section> <p data-aid="153903487" id="aside1_p3">Some teens may feel uncomfortable at FSY during the first day or two and may want to come home. If possible, encourage your teen to stay and communicate any concerns to a counselor. Most teens who stay the full week are glad they stayed.</p> </section> <section> <p data-aid="153903497" id="aside1_p4">As part of the registration process, you can indicate allergies or other special health concerns to the FSY coordinators. For more information, go to <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/youth/childrenandyouth/fsy">fsy.ChurchofJesusChrist.org</a> and select United States or Canada. Click on the Parents tab, and scroll to the link under the heading “Accommodating Youth with Dietary, Disability, Medical, or Other Needs.”</p> </section> [/SIDEBAR]
