When we study the life of Jesus Christ, we gain a deeper understanding of our own nature. The more we follow His example and teachings, the more we’ll be like Him. Through His Atonement we’ll receive the strength, guidance, and power to become converted. As we follow the Lord’s example, we will become converted to His gospel.

President Dallin H. Oaks, First Counselor in the First Presidency, taught, “Now is the time for each of us to work toward our personal conversion, toward becoming what our Heavenly Father desires us to become.”

Consider reading “The Living Christ: The Testimony of the Apostles” (available at ChurchofJesusChrist.org) and highlighting the things He did during His ministry.

How can you follow the Savior’s example and do similar things?

On the table below, you can list some of the things Christ did during His ministry and then list things you can do to follow His example. Two sample statements from “The Living Christ” are provided for you.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------------
  What the Lord Did                                                             What I Can Do
  “Under the direction of His Father, He was the creator of the earth.”         I can help create a more Christ-centered home.
  “He instituted the sacrament as a reminder of His great atoning sacrifice.”   I can remember Him as I partake of the sacrament.
