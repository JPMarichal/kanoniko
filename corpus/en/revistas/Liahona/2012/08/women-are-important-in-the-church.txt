Women are daughters of our Heavenly Father, who loves them.

God placed within women divine qualities of strength, virtue, and love.

Wives are equal to their husbands. Wives and husbands work side by side to meet the needs of the family.

Women of the Church today are strong and faithful.

Much of what we accomplish in the Church is due to the selfless service of women.

Latter-day Saint women are incredible!
