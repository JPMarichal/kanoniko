Dear Parents,

This month’s issue provides insights about the Book of Mormon as the Come, Follow Me curriculum for 2024. The articles below discuss keys for finding light and truth in a world often filled with darkness and confusion.

Consider sharing with your family some ideas from President Eyring’s article on page 4, and discuss the role that light plays in our lives. Review Kamryn’s story about finding the Savior in the dark. How might your family find the Savior’s light through your Book of Mormon study this year?

God is the source of all truth, and we can learn His truth by seeking it diligently. Elder and Sister Silva needed to decide where to live after returning from a mission, so they sought guidance from the Lord (see page 20). When has the Lord given you the guidance you needed?

When visiting an art museum, people often rush to see everything they can, causing them to possibly miss the beauty of the art. Likewise, readers of the Book of Mormon may miss important truths if they rush through reading. What strategies can help us take a careful look at this book of scripture? See page 12 for some ideas.
