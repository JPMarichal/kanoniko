We know from 1 Nephi 2:16 that when Nephi faced uncertainty, he turned to the Lord and the Lord softened his heart. On the other hand, when Laman and Lemuel faced uncertainty, they argued with each other and relied on their brother Nephi for answers instead of relying on the Lord (see 1 Nephi 15:8–9).

Laman and Lemuel show a pattern of what could happen when we rely solely on others for truth rather than turning to the Lord. Nephi became spiritually strong and stayed faithful to God throughout his life, while Laman and Lemuel eventually abandoned their father’s teachings of the Lord. Although Nephi was a trustworthy disciple, even he knew he wasn’t a replacement for seeking truth directly from the Lord (see 1 Nephi 15:6–11).
