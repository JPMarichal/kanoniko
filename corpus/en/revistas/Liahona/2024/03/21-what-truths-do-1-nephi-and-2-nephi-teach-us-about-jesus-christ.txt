Near the end of his writings, Nephi says, “If ye shall believe in Christ ye will believe in these words, for they are the words of Christ” (2 Nephi 33:10). Many truths about the Savior’s character, life, and Atonement are found in what we’ve studied in the Book of Mormon so far this year.

- He is full of patience, kindness, and long-suffering (see 1 Nephi 19:9).

- He will always remember us (see 1 Nephi 21:14–16).

- He invites everyone to come unto Him (see 2 Nephi 26:24, 33).

- He taught His gospel and healed the sick (see 1 Nephi 11:24, 27–31).

- He set an example of obedience by being baptized (see 1 Nephi 10:9; 2 Nephi 31:7–12).

- He was crucified, placed in a tomb, and visited the Nephites after His Resurrection (see 1 Nephi 19:10–11; 2 Nephi 26:1).

- He redeemed us through His sacrifice (see 2 Nephi 2:6–7).

- He performed an “infinite atonement” that conquers physical and spiritual death (2 Nephi 9:5–22).

- He can empower us when we have faith in Him and choose to repent (see 2 Nephi 31:13).
