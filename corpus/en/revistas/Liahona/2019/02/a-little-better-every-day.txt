Heavenly Father can help us make better choices every day. That’s part of what it means to repent! Draw a line to match the choices from yesterday with the better choices from today.

1.  I teased my little sister, even after she said stop.

2.  I got mad and yelled when we lost our football match.

3.  I woke up too late and didn’t say my morning prayers.

4.  I said something mean to a kid when he laughed at me.

5.  I lied to my mom about spilling a pot of water when she asked.

a.  I told Mama I was sorry and told her the truth. Then I helped get more water.

b.  On the way to school, I thought about ways to not get so upset at other people.

c.  I apologized to my coach for getting so mad.

d.  I said sorry to my sister and drew her a funny cat picture.

e.  I went to bed early and woke up with plenty of time to pray.

[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="139168024" id="aside1_p1"><strong>Answers:</strong> 1-D, 2-C, 3-E, 4-B, 5-A</p> [/SIDEBAR]
