Play this game with a partner during conference. First, choose X or O. Then listen for the words on the list below.

Whenever you hear one of the words, take turns writing your letter (X or O) in one of the spaces. Start on the bottom row of the score card. After that, each letter has to be stacked on top of another letter. Whoever gets four in a row is the winner!

- Children

- Commandment

- Faith

- Family

- God

- Jesus

- Love

- Prayer

- Priesthood

- Revelation

- Scriptures

- Temple
