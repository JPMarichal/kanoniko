There are many ways to say “I love you” to our family and friends! Play this game in a group. Use place markers like buttons or pebbles. Roll a die, move forward, and do the action on the space where you land.

- Give someone a hug.

- Say “I love you.”

- Tell about when someone showed you love.

- Say one thing you’ve done to show love.

- Read John 3:16.

- Sing “God Loved Us, So He Sent His Son” (Hymns, no. 187).

- Say one thing you’ll do to show love this week.

- Say one way you know Jesus loves you.

- Tell someone what you love about them.

- Say one way you know Heavenly Father loves you.

- Read John 13:34.

- Give your biggest smile.

- Read Mark 12:29–31.

- Sing “Love One Another” (Hymns, no. 308).

- Draw as many hearts as you can in 10 seconds.

- Write a nice note for someone you love.

- Draw a picture for someone.

- Make a heart shape with your hands.

- Give a high five.

- Sing “Our Savior’s Love” (Hymns, no. 113).

- Say “I love you.”

- Give a compliment.

- Make a heart shape with your arms.
