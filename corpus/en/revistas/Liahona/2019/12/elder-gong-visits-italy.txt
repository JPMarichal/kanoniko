Elder Gerrit W. Gong and Sister Susan Gong traveled to Italy when the Rome Italy Temple was dedicated. It’s the first temple to be built in a place where Jesus’s Apostles taught in Bible times!

Elder Gong, the First Presidency, and the other Apostles had their picture taken in the new visitors’ center. They stood in front of statues of Jesus Christ and His Apostles.

These children came with their families to hear President Russell M. Nelson dedicate the new temple. When they are old enough, they can do temple baptisms there!
