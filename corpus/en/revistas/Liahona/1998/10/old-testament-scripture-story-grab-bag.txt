To play this scripture-story game, remove this page from the magazine. Mount the picture cards on lightweight cardboard, cut them out, and put them into a small, sturdy bag. Have the first player pick one card from the grab bag and tell which Old Testament story the card reminds him or her of. There are no wrong anwers, but the player must share the story that the card reminds him or her of. For example, a sea card might remind a player of the parting of the Red Sea, Noah and the ark, or the Creation. If the player can’t think of a story, the other players may help. Keep playing until at least one story has been shared for each card.
