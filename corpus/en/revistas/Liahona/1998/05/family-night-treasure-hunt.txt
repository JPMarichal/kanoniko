Instructions

Hide your family home evening treat. Then draw a map showing where it is hidden. Mount the map on a piece of cardboard, cut it into 15 puzzle pieces, and put them into an envelope or sack. During family home evening, take turns answering the questions from the Treasure Hunt Questionnaire on the next page. Let your family members look up the scripture references to check or find the answers. When someone answers a question correctly, have that person select a puzzle piece. After all the questions have been answered correctly, put the puzzle together and find the treat!

Treasure Hunt Questionnaire

1.  Who said, “Let us be faithful in keeping the commandments of the Lord”? (See 1 Ne. 4:1.)

2.  What is “most desirable above all things”? (See 1 Ne. 11:22.)

3.  Those who keep the Lord’s commandments are promised great blessings. Name four of those blessings. (See 1 Ne. 15:11; 1 Ne. 17:3; 1 Ne. 20:18; Jarom 1:9; other answers are possible.)

4.  What kind of armor did Lehi tell his sons to put on? (See 2 Ne. 1:23.)

5.  What did Nephi delight in as well as ponder and write for his children? (See 2 Ne. 4:15.)

6.  What are the three steps mentioned in 2 Ne. 9:23 that all people must take to be saved in the kingdom of God?

7.  Whom did the Nephites talk about, preach about, and prophesy about? (See 2 Ne. 25:26.)

8.  “Hearken unto the Spirit which teacheth a man to ____________.” (See 2 Ne. 32:8.)

9.  Whom are we really serving when we serve others? (See Mosiah 2:17.)

10. “Salvation can come unto the children of men, only in and through the name of __________.” (See Mosiah 3:17.)

11. “But there is a ____________, therefore the grave hath no victory, and the sting of death is swallowed up in Christ.” (See Mosiah 16:8.)

12. “Yea, blessed is this people who are willing to bear my ___________; for in my ___________ shall they be called; and they are mine.” (See Mosiah 26:18.)

13. “I have ____________ of my sins, and have been redeemed of the Lord; behold I am born of the Spirit.” (See Mosiah 27:24.)

14. “I say unto you, can ye look up to God at that day with a pure __________ and clean _________? I say unto you, can you look up, having the image of __________ engraven upon your countenances?” (See Alma 5:19.)

15. All that is good comes from whom? (See Alma 5:40.)
