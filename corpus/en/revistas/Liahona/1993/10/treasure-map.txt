To make an old-looking treasure map, you will need: paper (brown wrapping paper works well), a pencil or black pen, and crayons or colored pencils.

1.  For a family home evening, hide a treasure—either the refreshments or an object to be used in a lesson.

2.  Draw a map on the piece of paper that shows where the treasure has been hidden.

3.  Crumple the paper into a tight ball.

4.  Carefully uncrumple the paper and smooth it out.

5.  Repeat steps 3 and 4 five to seven times, until the paper is very old and worn looking.

6.  6. Place the paper between two heavy books to flatten it.
