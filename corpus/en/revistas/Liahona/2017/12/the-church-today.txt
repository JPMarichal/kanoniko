The Saints built a beautiful city in the Salt Lake Valley and spent 40 years building the Salt Lake Temple. The Church has grown a lot since those early days. Now there are 156 finished temples around the world, and the Book of Mormon has been translated into 110 languages! President Thomas S. Monson is our prophet today. How can you help the Church continue to grow?
