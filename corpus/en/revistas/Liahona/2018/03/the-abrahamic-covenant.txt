God made a covenant, or promise, with Abraham and Sarah that they would have children and that God would bless them. Abraham and Sarah didn’t have children for a long time. But they were still obedient. When Abraham and Sarah were almost 100 years old, they had a son! Heavenly Father blesses us when we are obedient, and those blessings come when Heavenly Father knows is best.
