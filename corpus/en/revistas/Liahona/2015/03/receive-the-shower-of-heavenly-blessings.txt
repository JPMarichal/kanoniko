[SIDEBAR] <div class="image-cropper"></div> <p data-aid="127395995" id="aside1_p1">“Perhaps during [hard times], we might find ourselves asking, ‘Do we really need to obey <em>all</em> of God’s commandments?’</p> <p data-aid="127395996" id="aside1_p2">“My response to this question is simple:</p> <p data-aid="127395997" id="aside1_p3">“I think God knows something we don’t—things that are beyond our capacity to comprehend! Our Father in Heaven is an eternal being whose experience, wisdom, and intelligence are infinitely greater than ours. [See <a class="scripture-ref" href="/study/scriptures/ot/isa/55?lang=eng&amp;id=p9#p9">Isaiah 55:9</a>.] …</p> <p data-aid="127395998" id="aside1_p4">“Part of our challenge is, I think, that we imagine that God has all of His blessings locked in a huge cloud up in heaven, refusing to give them to us unless we comply with some strict, paternalistic requirements He has set up. But the commandments aren’t like that at all. In reality, Heavenly Father is constantly raining blessings upon us. It is our fear, doubt, and sin that, like an umbrella, block these blessings from reaching us.</p> <p data-aid="127395999" id="aside1_p5">“His commandments are the loving instructions and the divine help for us to close the umbrella so we can receive the shower of heavenly blessings.”</p> <div class="citation-info"> <p data-aid="127396000" id="aside1_p6">President Dieter F. Uchtdorf, Second Counselor in the First Presidency, “Living the Gospel Joyful,” <em>Liahona,</em> Nov. 2014, 121–22.</p> </div> [/SIDEBAR]
