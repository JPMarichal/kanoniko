My name is Magnolia. I go to a ward where we speak Spanish. One day Mia came to my Primary class. She speaks only English. I wanted to help Mia feel welcome, so I decided to help her. I would be her interpreter!

[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="127394609" id="aside1_p1">An interpreter translates words that someone is speaking into a different language.</p> [/SIDEBAR]

[SIDEBAR] <p data-aid="127394628" id="aside2_p1">Tips from Mia and Magnolia:</p> <p data-aid="127394629" id="aside2_p2">If someone:</p> <ul class="bullet"> <li> <p data-aid="127394630" id="aside2_p3">Is new at church or school, help her feel welcome.</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="127394631" id="aside2_p4">Doesn’t go to church very much, invite him to go with you.</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="127394632" id="aside2_p5">Is being bullied, stand up for her. Tell your teacher.</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="127394633" id="aside2_p6">Seems lonely, invite him to join you.</p> </li> <li> <p data-aid="127394634" id="aside2_p7">Needs a friend, ask her to be your friend.
