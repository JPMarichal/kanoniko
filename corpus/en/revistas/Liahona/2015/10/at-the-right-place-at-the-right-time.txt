Have you ever had someone tell you that something you did or said was just what they needed? Sometimes this happens because Heavenly Father sent you at just the right moment to help. You were in tune with the Spirit, so you could recognize that prompting from Heavenly Father. Stay worthy and willing to help—you never know when He’ll need you to be someone’s angel.
