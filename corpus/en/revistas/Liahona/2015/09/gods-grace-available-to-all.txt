“The grace of God is our great and everlasting hope.

“Through the sacrifice of Jesus Christ, the plan of mercy appeases the demands of justice, ‘and [brings] about means unto men that they may have faith unto repentance’ (Alma 34:15).

“Our sins, though they be as scarlet, can become white as snow. Because our beloved Savior ‘gave himself a ransom for all’ (1 Timothy 2:6), an entrance into His everlasting kingdom is provided unto us. …

“Today and forevermore God’s grace is available to all whose hearts are broken and whose spirits are contrite. …

“… I pray that we will show our love for God and our gratitude for the gift of God’s infinite grace by keeping His commandments and joyfully ‘walk[ing] in [a] newness of life’ (Romans 6:4).”
