John Whitmer, Book of John Whitmer ( 1838)

Chapter 18

KIRTLAND, March 27, 1836.

Previous notice having been given, the Church of Latter-day Saints met in the House of the Lord, etc.

See Messenger and Advocate printed at this place for a full statement of the order, sermon, etc., of the dedication of the House of the Lord, published in the March number commencing on page 74 and ending on page 283 inclusive.
