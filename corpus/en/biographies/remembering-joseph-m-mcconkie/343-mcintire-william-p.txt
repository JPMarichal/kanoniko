McIntire, William P.

(May 29, 1813-January 7, 1882)

Tells of Joseph publicly teaching Don Carlos to speak and gesture naturally when speaking; Joseph taught the doctrine that nothing was created of nothing; tells of participating in rescuing Joseph from the Dixon kidnapping.

Subjects taken from his records written just as he wrote it. "I have before stated that I landed in Nauvoo in 1840. During the winter I heard many valuable items from the mouth of the Prophet, which I will insert hereSubject, Faith. Discussed by Don Carlos Smith "This principle practiced by man and its blessings and results would," he said, "bind the hearts of men together, and give them confidence in each other and the apostle John says, "Thy word is truth," so if we keep his commandments and word, we should all be actuated by the same principle and be as one man and as angels are subject to the same word, we shall have concourse to them and also to all the heavenly throng. Joseph arose to correct him on gestures and said "Don Carlos," to be free from corruptions of the world, the speaker should always speak in his natural tone of voice and not to keep in a loud strain, but to act without affection or assume a tone or gestures that were not natural.

Subject given to Elder Ellis, "And the Lord God made the earth out of nothing," he said "God did not make the earth out of nothing, for it is customary to a rational mind and reason that a something could not be made out of nothing, also it is contrary to the principle and means by which God does work, for instance, God formed man, He formed him of the dust of the earth, so man was organized out of something, so the Lord always takes a something to effect a something else, often he takes man to scourge his fellow man, or water, or fire, or pestilence to destroy man, or even angels, for instance the angel that went forth in the Assyrian camp and destroyed 180 thousand in one night, and the water in the days of Noah, and fire in the days of Lot. Joseph said to Ellis and the congregation, "I for a long time thought on pre-knowledge [and then] had a revelation from the Lord. The Lord rebuked me sharply for creating such a thing and further said there was no reality in such a sign and it was the work of the devil. Joseph said that the Lord told him that Bro. William Law would do well, that he would go and preach the gospel. He also said, as for his own knowledge, the Earth was made out of something, for it was impossible for a something to be made out of a nothing, for fire, water and air are Eternal existant principles, which are the composition of which the Earth is composed; also this Earth has been organized out of other globes that has been dis-organized. In testimony that the Earth was not the first of God's works.

. . . In the summer of 1843 President Joseph Smith and wife, went to visit a relative, 160 miles distant. His enemies in Missouri and Illinois heard of his going there, so they got a false charge against him, pursued and arrested him near Dixon, so the news came to Nauvoo of his perilous situation and volunteers were called for, and I had the privilege of going with my brethren to the rescue of our beloved Prophet and by traveling 4 days and 3 nights we had our Prophet safely back in Nauvoo. It was marvelous to see the workings of the great Almighty on that occasion in strengthening the Brethren & horses to perform [the] journey. The Saints seemingly had rest in a degree until the next spring, when the mob again commenced burning grain and houses, driving the Saints from their homes and until the great burst of persecution which continued until the death of Joseph and Hyrum on June 27th.
