Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Merrill, Franklin

(b. March 17, 1843)

Saw martyred bodies of the Prophet Joseph and Hyrum.

Brother Merrill said he remembers being lifted in his father's arms, to view the bodies of the Church leaders, as they laid in their coffins.

"Franklin Merrill," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
