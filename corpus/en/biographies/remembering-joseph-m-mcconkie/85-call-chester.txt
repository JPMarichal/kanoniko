Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Call, Chester

(b. May 13, 1841)

Remembers seeing Joseph and Hyrum in their caskets.

. . . a son of Anson Call and Mary Flint. His parents were members of the Church and the earliest recollection of the boy Chester was that he was lifted up by his father that he might look upon the martyred Prophet Joseph Smith and his brother Hyrum as they lay in their caskets in Nauvoo.

Untitled manuscript, Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
