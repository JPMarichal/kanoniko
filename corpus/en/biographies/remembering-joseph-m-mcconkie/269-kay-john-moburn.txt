Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Kay, John Moburn

(October 6, 1817-September 27, 1864)

Because of his musical talent, Joseph often invited Kay to entertain visitors.

[In Nauvoo, Brother Kay] became a major in the famous Legion; also a member of the Nauvoo brass band and the police force. Owing to his musical talent and his genial social qualities he was often invited by the Prophet Joseph Smith to assist in the entertainment of visitors.

"John Moburn Kay," in Orson F. Whitney, History of Utah, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: G. Q. Cannon & Sons Co., 1904), 4:452.
