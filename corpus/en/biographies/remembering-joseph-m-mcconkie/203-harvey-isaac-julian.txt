Harvey, Isaac Julian

(1816-1891)

A nonmember who wrote to his wife, Sarah Mellet Harvey in 1839, from Knavestown, Missouri, spreading a somewhat humorous but false piece of gossip regarding Joseph's escape from Liberty Jail.

Tell the folks that Jo Smith and others have made their escape from the sheriff & guard by stealing 2 of their horses. It is said that Smith had 20 thousand dollars in jail with him. The general opinion is the horses was well sold.

"Isaac Julian Harvey to Mrs. Sarah Harvey, 26 April 1839," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
