Higbee, Isaac

(December 13 [or 23], 1797-February 16, 1874)

Isaac Higbee gives Joseph land to settle his debts; later, ordained a bishop by the Prophet in Nauvoo.

When I arrived there [Jackson Co.] I sent Elias Higbee to the land office to enter 80 acres of land on the Big Blue in what was Lyman Wight's settlement. The Bishop divided the land, 20 acres to my father, 30 to Elias, and 30 to myself. After we were driven out of Jackson Co., Joseph Smith was in need of money to pay his Lawyers fees &c. I gave the Bishop leave to sell my land to help Joseph settle his debts, which I presume he did. . . .

. . . Feb. 19, 1841 I was ordained a Bishop under the hands of Joseph Smith the Prophet and appointed to the second ward in the City of Nauvoo.

"Items from the Journal of Isaac Higbee, May 24, 1864," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
