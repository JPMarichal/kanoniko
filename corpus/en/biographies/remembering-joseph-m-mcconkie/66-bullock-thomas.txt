Bullock, Thomas

(December 23, 1816-February 10, 1885)

After the martyrdom Bullock obtained piece of the Prophet's shirt; was also present at the transfiguration of Brigham Young.

At the time of the martyrdom grandfather obtained a piece of the shirt and vest worn by the Prophet, which is still in the family and can be seen at the home of his son in Coalville. Grandfather was at the meeting when the cloak of the Prophet Joseph fell upon Brigham Young. Even his voice was that of the Prophet. It was at that time that Brigham Young was chosen President of the Church.

"Biographical Sketch of Thomas Bullock," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Statement in Bullock's own hand that he possessed portions of the shirt and vest worn by Joseph during the martyrdom.

July 2, 1844

Enclosed is a piece of the shirt worn by the Prophet Joseph Smith at the time of his assassination at Carthage, taken from within two inches of the spot where one of the balls entered his breast. Also a piece of the lining of his vest where a ball passed out [of his] body.

Thomas Bullock, clerk

"Thomas Bullock, In Memory of the Murdered Prophet Joseph Smith 1844," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
