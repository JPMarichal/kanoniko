Hamson, Sarah Ann Smith

(July 22, 1828-March 1903)

Joseph helps bandage arm of a man who was accidentally shot while Joseph was preaching; remembers spirit of persecution at time of the martyrdom; present when Brigham Young transfigured.

Later the family moved to Ohio and then to Nauvoo where they went to hear the Elders speak and the Prophet Joseph explain the principles of the Gospel. One time at an outdoor meeting when the Prophet was speaking, a man was accidentally shot through the arm by a gun that he was leaning on, and the Prophet stepped over to him and asked for a bandage. He bound the arm and blessed the man and in a few days, he was alright again. At one of those meetings Sarah Ann met Geo. E. Hamson, when she was only 16, and they were married in 1844. They became more and more interested in the teachings of the Elders.

. . . [George and Sarah] . . . recalled the persecution of the Prophet and the time when he and his brother Hyrum Smith were killed. They were present at the meeting when Brigham Young was made President and partook of the wonderful spirit there.

"(Biography of) Sarah Ann Smith Hamson," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah, 1-2.
