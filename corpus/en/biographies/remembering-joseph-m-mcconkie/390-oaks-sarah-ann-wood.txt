Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Oaks, Sarah Ann Wood

(April 8, 1827-ca. March 1906)

Knew Joseph; heard him preach; heard him throw mantle of presidency onto Brigham Young.

I was well acquainted with the Prophet Joseph Smith, Jr. I have heard him preach and prophesy many times and have seen his prophecies come to pass. I heard him when he said, "I now thrown this mantle, meaning the mantle of the Presidency, off my shoulder and on the shoulder of Brigham Young," and that he was to be the head of the Twelve Apostles and the Prophet and Seer. I was personally acquainted with Hyrum also. I was in Nauvoo when they were martyred, and I saw their dead bodies. I know they were prophets of the Lord.

Vernal (Utah) Express, Saturday, 3 March 1906, 1; also in Linda M. Dursteler and Dallin H. Oaks, "The William Hyrum Oaks and Janett Bethers Family," LDS Family History Library, Salt Lake City, Utah, 8.
