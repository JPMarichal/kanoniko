Miller, Melena Zundel Brimhall

(November 1, 1833-26 May 1919)

Remembers martyrdom and sitting on the Prophet's lap; the Prophet was her marriage ideal.

"When the Prophet Joseph Smith was martyred at Carthage, Illinois, in 1844, Melena was about eleven years of age. She remembered him as her ideal of a handsome and perfect man, who used to take her on his lap and talk to her kindly when he came to visit the Zundels during the Nauvoo days. In later years her ideal of perfection in marriage in the Hereafter was to be married to the Prophet."

Glen F. Harding, "A Record of the Ancestry and Descendants of John Jacob Zundel, Known as Jacob Zundel," LDS Family History Library, Salt Lake City, Utah, 90.
