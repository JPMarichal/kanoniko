Hancock, Joseph

(March 17, 1899-July 5, 1893)

Childhood playmate of Joseph; Joseph healed him of cholera on Zion's Camp.

Moved to Ontario New York with his parents where he made the acquaintance of Joseph Smith, in his boyhood days.

. . . He assisted in building the Kirtland Temple in 1833. Went to Missouri in Zion's Camp in 1834. Was attacked with the cholera and was healed under the administration of Joseph Smith the Prophet.

. . . When Joseph Smith contemplated going west in 1844 he chose Joseph Hancock to go with him as a hunter for the camp.

"Joseph Hancock (Original Pioneer)," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
