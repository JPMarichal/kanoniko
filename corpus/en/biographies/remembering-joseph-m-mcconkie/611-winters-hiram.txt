Winters, Hiram

(April 5, 1805-October 21, 1889)

First man ordained a seventy in this dispensation; he was ordained by Joseph Smith; he also electioneered for Joseph in 1844.

Bro. Winters returned to Kirtland, Ohio, where he was ordained a Seventy by the Prophet, Joseph Smith, Feb. 28, 1835, he being the first Seventy ordained in this dispensation.

. . . When the Prophet Joseph Smith became a candidate fore the president of the United States Elder Winters was appointed to take charge of three electoral districts and was very diligent in spreading President Smith's "Views of the Powers and Policy of the Government of the United States." While addressing a large congregation in Cleveland, Ohio, advocating President Smith's views, an editor stepped up and handed him a paper containing an account of the martyrdom of the Prophet Joseph Smith. At first, Elder Winters discredited the report, but the news being subsequently confirmed, he ceased his political labor.

Untitled manuscript, Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
