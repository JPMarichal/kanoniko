Strong, William

(December 30, 1827-December 24, 1888)

Baptized by Joseph Smith.

"When a boy, under the teachings of Erastus Snow, his parents joined the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and moved with their children to Nauvoo, Hancock County, Illinois, arriving March 18, 1840. William was baptized in the Mississippi River when about fifteen years of age by the Prophet Joseph Smith . . ." [according to LDS Biographical Encyclopedia]

Harriet Speirs, "A Biography of William Strong, a Member of the Mormon Battalion," June-July 1927, Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
