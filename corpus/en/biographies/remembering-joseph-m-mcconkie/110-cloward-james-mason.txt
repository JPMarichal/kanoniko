Cloward, James Mason

(October 17, 1826-May 27, 1889)

Saw Brigham Young transfigured; remembers sadness of the martyrdom; saw dead bodies of Joseph and Hyrum; home burned by mob for not denouncing Joseph Smith.

James remembered the transfiguration, when the mantle fell upon Brigham Young after the Prophet's death. He remembered when the sad news reached Nauvoo of the martyrdom of the Prophet. He went to meet the companies bringing the bodies, and recalled the weeping and the great sorrow when the bodies were placed in the mansion house. He saw the body of the Prophet and Hyrum lying side by side and the mourners passing by to get a last look at their beloved Prophet.

After the Prophet's death there was only a short time before the mob spirit broke out again, and the Saints were forced to leave their home once again. When the mob rode to their home telling them that they had their choice of either denouncing Joe Smith as a prophet, or have their home burned. They chose the latter.

"Grandparents of Hannetta Ann Davis Loveless, on Her Mother's Side," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
