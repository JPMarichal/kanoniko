Whitney, Horace K.

(b. July 25, 1823)

As a youngster, he attended Hebrew School with the Prophet; he was on a mission to the Eastern States when martyrdom occurred.

When little more than a child he studied Hebrew in the same class with the Prophet Joseph smith and other leading brethren in a school set up in Kirtland.

. . . In 1843 Horace K. was ordained an Elder by the Prophet Joseph Smith, and almost immediately following he was sent on a Mission to the Eastern States. While on this mission the terrible tragedy was enacted at Carthage Jail which brought to a close the earthly careers of the Prophet and Patriarch of the Church. This came as a terrible blow to the young missionary for he loved the Prophet dearer than life itself.

"Horace K. Whitney," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah, 1-3.

Note: Horace Kimball Whitney was the son of Newel K. and Elizabeth Ann Whitney.
