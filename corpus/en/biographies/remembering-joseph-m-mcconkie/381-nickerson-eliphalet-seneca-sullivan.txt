Nickerson, Eliphalet Seneca Sullivan

(b. March [or May] 5, 1818)

Accompanied Joseph and Sidney Rigdon on mission to Mount Pleasant, and later to enlist volunteers for Zion's Camp.

Eliphalet Nickerson received the gospel at Cattaraugus County New York State, town of Dayton; was baptized by Zerrubbabel Snow in April, 1833was soon after ordained [a] Deacon by Edmund Fisher.

His first mission was among the Saints in Kirtland, Ohio, Sept. 1833, then with the Prophet Joseph and Sidney in his own conveyance visited the [Upper?] Canada and labored among the people at Mt. Pleasant. Organized a Church of 13 members and ordained E. F. Nickerson to preside over the Church baptizing his two sons with their households. Returned to Buffalo in Nov. '33.

In the Spring of the following year accompanied the Prophet enlisting volunteers to go up to Missouri to Redeem Zion in Jackson County.

Eliphalet Seneca Sullivan Nickerson, "Family Record of Caroline Stewart," microfilm of holograph, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
