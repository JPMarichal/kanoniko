Clawson, Hiram B.

(November 7, 1826-March 29, 1912)

Joseph supported young Clawson's desire to join Kirtland Debating Society, though Clawson withdrew his application because of opposition of older members.

Hiram, in Nauvoo, wanted to join the Debating Society which was held in a room over Joseph's store; but some of the principal members opposed his admission on accunt of his youth. The Prophet, who was always a warm admirer of lofty aspirations in the young men of his people, stood as Hiram's advocate and would have promoted his admission; but, with a becoming sense of self-respect, young Clawson withdrew his application.

"Hiram B. Clawson," Tullidge's Quarterly Magazine 1, no. 4 (July 1881): 678.
