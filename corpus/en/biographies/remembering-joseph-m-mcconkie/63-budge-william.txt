Budge, William

(May 1, 1828-March 18, 1918)

Even as nonmember living in Scotland, Budge was offended at the martyrdom, feeling it violated his sense of right, justice, and free worship.

It was while the family resided in Glasgow, during the latter part of the year 1844, that William, then sixteen years of age, heard a man on the street reading a paper about the killing of Joseph Smith, the "Mormon." He believed that people should be allowed to worship as they wished without being persecuted for it, and he thought the murder of Joseph Smith cruel and unjust.

Budge Pugmire, "William BudgeCaptain," in Kate B. Carter, comp. Our Pioneer Heritage, 20 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Daughters of the Utah Pioneers, 1958-77), 4:63.
