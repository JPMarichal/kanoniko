Robey, Jeremiah Sr.

(b. April 4, 1809)

Robey was baptized by the Prophet.

Jeremiah Robey, Sr.Was born in Harrison County, West Virginia, April 4, 1809, a son of Jeremiah and Mary Ogden Rabey. He joined the Church in Nauvoo, Illinois and was baptized by the Prophet Joseph Smith."

"Jeremiah Robey, Sr.," in Kate B. Carter, comp., Heart Throbs of the West, 12 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Daughters of the Utah Pioneers, 1939-51), 12:394.
