Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Preston, Harriet A.

(February 11, 1839 [or 1838]-December 25, 1920)

Recalls her feelings at the death of Prophet.

O! the lamentations, the weeping that followed the martyrdom of the prophet and his brother, Hyrum! It was as if the whole people were indeed stricken to the heart.

Harriet A. Preston, "A Moment of Peace," Relief Society Magazine 7, no. 1 (January 1920): 17.

Says Joseph blessed a well so that those who drank from it would receive health.

I remember sitting on the stone steps which led up to the entrance of the temple, and with what reverent admiration I looked upon it, although too young to sense its significance. I recall having gone to a well which our beloved Prophet had blessed to the end that all those who should drink of it should receive health.

Harriet A. Preston, "A Moment of Peace," Relief Society Magazine 7, no. 1 (January 1920): 19.
