Tolton, Mary Ann Tomlinson

(b. October 9, 1831)

A family that went to Nauvoo specifically to see the Prophet.

In May of that same year the family made a pilgrimage to Nauvoo to see the Prophet Joseph Smith.

John Franklin Tolton, "Memories of the Life of John Franklin Tolton: A Brief Record of the Family and Other Matters," commenced 23 August 1887, Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
