Naisbitt, H. W.

(November 7, 1826-February 26, 1908)

Joseph quoted as saying Adam had two wives, Eve and Lilith.

The Scriptures give an account simply of the woman Eve; declaring that this name was given her of Adam, because she was "the mother of all living;" but outside of biblical record there has been handed down from time immemorial the idea that Adam had two wives, the narrators go so far, or rather so near perfecting the tradition so as to give their names, Lilith being said to be the name of one as Eve was the name of the other, and while it may be difficult to harmonize all the Rabbinical and Talmudic versions of this matter, it is said that Joseph Smith the Prophet taught that Adam had two wives. Without however, assuming anything upon this theory, or upon this traditionwhich may be mythical in its characterit is nevertheless, very evident that marriage was ordained of God.

Journal of Discourses, 26:115 (March 8, 1885).
