Hatch, Orin

(May 9, 1830-September 8, 1906)

Heard shots fired at Carthage.

At about 5:00 p. m. on June 27, 1844, Orin was near Carthage and heard the shots ring out when the mob stormed the Carthage jail, killing Joseph Smith and his brother Hyrum and severely wounding John Taylor.

Orin's brother "Ransom was taken prisoner, he too, was near the Carthage jail when the Prophet Joseph Smith was shot, and (he also) heard the firing of guns at that time."

Spencer Fearnley Hatch, Patriarch Orin Hatch,1830-1906: A Biography (N.p.: n.d.).
