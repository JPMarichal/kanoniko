Young, Seymour Bicknell

(October 3, 1837-December 15, 1924)

As a 6-year-old boy, he remembers seeing Joseph go to Carthage and the sorrows attending the martyrdom and funeral of the Prophet and Patriarch.

Brother Seymour well remembers being lifted up in the arms of his mother to obtain a view of Joseph and Hyrum as they passed some fifty rods away on the road to their martyrdom, June 24, 1844. On the morning of the 28th, at five o'clock, their neighbor, the late Pres. Jacob Gates, awakened his mother and her family of little children, and told them that the Prophet Joseph Smith and his brother Hyrum had been murdered the evening before in Carthage jail. Seymour well remembers the sorrow of the Latter-day Saints at the awful event, and the scenes of grief at the funeral and burial of these two great leaders.

"Seymour Bicknell Young," in Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Western Epics, 1971), 1:201.
