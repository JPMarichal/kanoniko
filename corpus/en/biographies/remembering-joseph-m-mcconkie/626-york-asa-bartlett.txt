Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

York, Asa Bartlett

(b. September 23, 1832)

Joseph borrowed a horse from York's father, often when going to see the Governor.

I am happy to say I was well acquainted with the Prophet Joseph Smith and his brother Hyrum. The Prophet Joseph has stayed at my father's home many times. I remember well my father harnessing a horse to the Prophet Joseph's buggy, that horse my father was very proud of and he wanted the Prophet Joseph to enjoy the ride behind this beautiful animal also. Many times when the Prophet Joseph was going to see the Governor my father accompanied him to the governor's home.

"Asa Bartlett York," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
