Nickerson, Freeman

(b. February 5, 1778)

Took Joseph and Sidney in his carriage to Upper Canada on a mission; accompanied Joseph to enlist volunteers for Zion's Camp.

After that [i.e., Sept. 1833] [Freeman] took the Prophet Joseph and Sidney Rigdon in his carriage to Upper Canada and labored among the people of Mount Pleasant, where a branch of the Church was organized with 13 members and Eleazer F. Nickerson as president. After baptizing his three sons with their households Elder Freeman Nickerson returned to Buffalo, N. Y. in 1833. In March, 1834, he accompanied the Prophet Joseph on a special mission enlisting volunteers to go to Missouri, and with his two sons, Uriel and Levi, and his son-in-law, Marshall Moore Hubbard, he went to Missouri in Zion's Camp. After acquitting themselves favorably on this long and perilous journey they returned to Kirtland in August, 1834. In November, 1835 Freeman Nickerson started out on another mission to his friends in Massachusetts. He baptized a goodly number and returned in June, 1836.

"Freeman Nickerson," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
