Eccles, Henry

(September 19, 1823-June 24, 1874)

Friend of Joseph, tarred and feathered by Nauvoo mob; mob raided his home, his wife refused to denounce Joseph, and therefore was cast into street; infant daughter died from consequent exposure.

Henry Eccles was a Master Marble Worker. He and his wife joined the LDS Church in Manchester, Lancaster, England, and came to the United States and settled in Nauvoo, Illinois.

He was a close friend of Prophet Joseph Smith and was among those tarred and feathered by a mob in Nauvoo shortly before the Prophet's death.

Elizabeth, his wife, was still confined to her bed two weeks after the birth of their first baby Sariah, when a mob raided their home, and because she would not denounce the Prophet, they carried her and the child out into the street, the baby died from exposure.
