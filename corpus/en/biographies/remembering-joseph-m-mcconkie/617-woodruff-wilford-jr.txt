Woodruff, Wilford Jr.

(March 22, 1840-May 6, 1921)

Childhood memories of playing with the Prophet.

Although but a child at the martyrdom of the Prophet Joseph Smith, he remembers well the terrible scenes enacted at that time, and no less does he remember the kindness and affection shown him by the Prophet while his father was fulfilling a series of missions in the Eastern States. In his journal he writes: "The Prophet called often at our home, and I remember well, how he used to play games with me; and although his life was filled with so many cares, he never forgot a kind word for the children."

"Wilford Woodruff Jr.," in Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Western Epics, 1971), 1:616.
