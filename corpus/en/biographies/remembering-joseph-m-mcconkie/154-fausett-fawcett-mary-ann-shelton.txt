Fausett [Fawcett], Mary Ann Shelton

(December 25, 1835-August 1900)

Baptized by Joseph Smith; remembers night of martyrdom, and of hearing one of the guards who had been shot cry for water.

She was baptized by the Prophet Joseph Smith in Nauvoo, Ill. The night the Prophet was killed great-grand mother said that she heard the shots and heard a man cry for water, water, water until the voice grew so faint that they could hear it no longer. The next morning the man was found dead on the bank of the river. He was one of the guards that had been shot. My great-grandmother also attended the mock funeral of the Prophet and his brother Hyrum.

"The Biography of Mary Ann Shelton Fausett (Written by her grandson, William L. Van Wagoner, and revised and handed in by her great grand-daughter, H. Margarette Hair Hanson)," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
