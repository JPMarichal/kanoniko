Carter, Dominicus

(June 21, 1806-February 2, 1884)

Stood guard over caskets of Joseph and Hyrum.

CARTER, Dominicus, a veteran member of the Church, was born June 21, 1806, at Scarborough, Cumberland County, Maine, a son of John Carter and Hannah Liby. Dominicus Carter was baptized June 30, 1834 by Elder John Boynton. He united with the Saints in Kirtland, Ohio in 1837 and in 1838 as a member of the Kirtland Camp, taking his family with him. The company arrived at Far West, Missouri, in the fall of the year. Later he located at Nauvoo, from whence he made many missionary trips into the various States. After the martyrdom of the Prophet Joseph Smith and his brother Hyrum, Bro. Carter was one of the brethren who stood guard by the caskets, while the bodies lay in state in Nauvoo and were viewed by thousands of mourning Saints. . . .
