McKee, Persis M. Sweat

(June 29, 1820-October 4, 1918)

As a teen worked in the printing office where the Book of Mormon was being printed; stopped an error from being published; Joseph gave her a personal, autographed copy of the Book of Mormon. Remembers knowing Joseph and Hyrum and their families.

Persis started to work in the printing office [in Kirtland], and to fold the leaves to the second edition ["addition"] of the Book of Mormon. One morning while folding the leaves, she noticed that the type was set wrong, going to the Editor she told him, the press was stopped, the mistake was found and corrected, which saved leaves and a great loss to the company. She gained the love and respect of all whom she labored with.

When the edition [note: original manuscript says "addition"] was finished, the Prophet Joseph Smith gave her a book of her own, folding and autographed it for her. She prized this book very dearly, and would never part with it. It was a very valuable treasure. It was to go to her daughter Sarah L. Hutchison, after she was through with it. Then handed down from generation to generation.

. . . She was intimately acquainted with the Prophet Joseph Smith and his brother Hyrum and their families. Also the first Apostles and the early Prophets of the Church.

"The Life of Persis M. Sweat McKee," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
