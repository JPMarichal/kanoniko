Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Zundel, Abigail Abbott

(February 3, 1842-October 28, 1934)

Remembers Joseph lifting her and her sister up on the oxen in the temple.

The Prophet Joseph Smith and his brother Hyrum lifted my sister Cynthia and myself up on the oxen which held up the font, this pleased us very much and we thought it the most wonderful place we had ever seen."

"History of Abigail Abbott Zundel," in Glen F. Harding, "A Record of the Ancestry and Descendants of John Jacob Zundel, Known as Jacob Zundel," LDS Family History Library, Salt Lake City, Utah, 123.
