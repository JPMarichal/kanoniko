Carling, Asenath E. Browning

(November 17, 1835-January 3, 1899)

As a very young girl, kissed by the Prophet; her husband recalls his family hearing of Word of Wisdom and forsaking tea and coffee.

The Jonathan Browning home still stands in fairly good condition (when Ellen Carling Chamberlain wrote this) in Adams County, Illinois. One of Asenath B. Carling's earliest memories was of the Prophet Joseph Smith coming to her childhood home. He picked her up and kissed her and said, "Whose pretty little girl is this?" She thought this was a great honor and loved to tell her children about it. . . .

When Isaac [B. Carling, her husband; b. November 30, 1831; d. May 24, 1911] was a boy in Nauvoo, his father, John Carling, came home from meeting one day and said, "Mother, the Prophet Joseph has had a revelation. He says tea and coffee are not good for us." Isaac said the family never drank tea and coffee after that.

Ellen Carling Chamberlain, "Asenath E. Browning and Isaac Vanwagoner Carling, a Sketch," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Note: Asenath is the daughter of Jonathan Browning and Elizabeth Stolcup; Ellen Carling Chamberlain is the daughter of Isaac and Asenath.
