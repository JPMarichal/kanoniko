Molen, Jane Elizabeth Hyde

(February 12, 1843-December 25, 1934)

Heard Joseph speak and felt the power thereof; describes general spirit of mourning following martyrdom.

Elizabeth wrote in her diary of hearing the Prophet Joseph Smith speak at a meeting in a grove. Regarding this she says: "A feeling like electricity passed through me and I knew that he was a Prophet of God."

. . . Elizabeth wrote in her diary, "The mourning there was in Nauvoo when the sad news reached us that our beloved Prophet and patriarch had been murdered in cold blood in Carthage Jail, while under the promised protection of the State baffled all description. Every heart was overflowing with sorrow and grief for the inhuman deed."

Jane Woolf Bates, "A Biographical Sketch of Jane Elizabeth Hyde Molen Together with a Part of Her Family Background," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Note: Jane's mother, whose diary is here quoted, was Elizabeth Bullard Hyde, wife of William Hyde.
