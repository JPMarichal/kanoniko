Richards, Louisa Lula Greene

(b. April 8, 1841)

Evan Greene taught an English class attended by the Prophet Joseph Smith.

By nature and education Evan M. Greene [Louisa Lula's father] was well fitted to be a school teacher, and was an exceptionally good one for his time. In Kirtland he taught an English grammar class, which the Prophet Joseph Smith honored with his personal attendance, and with him and others he studied the Hebrew language and various branches of learning. He was also one of the Prophet's trusted clerks.

"Louisa Lula Greene Richards," in Orson F. Whitney, History of Utah, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: G. Q. Cannon & Sons Co., 1904), 4:598.
