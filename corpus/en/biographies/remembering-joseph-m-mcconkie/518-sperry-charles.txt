Sperry, Charles

(June 30, 1824-May 31, 1914)

Tells of troubles surrounding the martyrdom and of guarding the roads against mobbers, though but a boy.

During the trouble before and after Joseph Smith was martyred, mobs threatened to come and burn the town, and it kept us constantly on the watch. I, although (but) a boy, took my turn guarding the roads to keep the mob out of town. The Mormons were a terrible menace to the inhabitants of Hancock county. They threatened to drive the Mormons out after they killed our Prophet, burned our property and raised a great furor in the County and State.

"A Sketch of the Life and History of Charles Sperry, the Son of Jay Sperry and Mary Lamont Who were Married April 26, 1810," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
