Young, Brigham Jr.

(December 18, 1836-April 11, 1903)

Remembers Joseph and Hyrum riding to Carthage.

I remember the time well when the Prophet Joseph Smith and the Patriarch Hyrum Smithtwo of the noblest men that ever stood upon the earthrode to their death. They went to seal their testimonies with their blood, which cannot be refuted by all the world. They gave their lives to sustain this work, and it was necessary, according to the revelations that we have received.

Joseph E. Cardon and Samuel O. Bennion, Testimonies of the Divinity of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, by its Leaders (Independence, Mo.: Zion's Press, 1930), 142.
