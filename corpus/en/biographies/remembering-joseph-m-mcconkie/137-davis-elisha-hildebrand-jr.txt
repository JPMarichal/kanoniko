Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Davis, Elisha Hildebrand Jr.

(February 7, 1850-December 10, 1937)

Tells of Orson Hyde learning, by inspiration, of the death of the Prophet, and of his stunned surprise.

My father, Elisha H. Davis, and Orson Hyde, were together in a hous in New York (both on missions). I do not remember the owner's name. Orson Hyde, who was lying on a couch asleep with his face to the wall. My father was writing at a table when Orson Hyde was startled and jumped up and exclaimed, "Oh Brother Davis, they have murdered the Prophet," which proved true. Afterwards Brother Hyde was called home by President Young and my father left in charge of the work of the Lord. . . .

"Statement of Elisha Hildebrand Davis, Jr., 1916," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah. Grammar and punctuation has been modernized.
