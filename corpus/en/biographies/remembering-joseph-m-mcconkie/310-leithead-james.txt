Leithead, James

(June 14, 1816-March 31, 1907)

At Conference with Joseph in Canada; later asked Joseph where he should live, and Joseph told him to go to Missouri; also built a frame barn for Hyrum.

In the summer of 1837 President Joseph Smith, Sidney Rigdon and apostle Thomas B. Marsh held a conference in that part of Canada [near Scarborough, which is close to Toronto). At that conference I was ordained a priest and was set apart to preside over a small Branch of the Church. In the Spring of 1838, by counsel of the Prophet, I left Canada with my wife for Missouri. Tarrying a short [time] at Kirtland Ohio we proceeded on our journey by the Lakes and Rivers, we arrived at Far West about the First of August. I inquired of the Prophet where to locate and he advised to go to Adam-ondi-Ahman 25 miles north of Far West. . . .

[Spring 1843 arrived in Nauvoo] While at Nauvoo I labored much on the Temple from the start until completed. Was Bass drummer in Marshall Band, built a large frame barn for Hyrum Smith and other work for him. Was intimately acquainted with him and his family. . . .

"Life and Labors of James Leithead," Written 25 January 1901, Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
