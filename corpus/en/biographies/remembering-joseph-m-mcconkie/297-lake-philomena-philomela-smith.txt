Lake, Philomena [Philomela] Smith

(April 13, 1794-March 20, 1873)

Helped build Kirtland and Nauvoo temples; after the martyrdom, he and his wife sought "a testimony as to their future procedure" and then witnessed the transfiguration of Brigham Young.

[Tells of helping to build the Kirtland Temple, and later of helping to work on the Nauvoo Temple. Then speaks of the martyrdom]

. . . After the awful scene at Nauvoo, where they saw the form of the Prophet they had loved so well, laid in the grave, they sought a testimony from God as to their future procedure, and this testimony they received at the great meeting held in the grove at Nauvoo, August 8, 1844. There they witnessed the sublime personality of Joseph Smith as it cast its mantle of splendor around the chosen form of Brigham Young, he being transformed before the eyes of the people, as a heavenly witness to them, that God had caused the majesty of His High Priesthood to fall upon "whom he would," that His work might be continued.

Father and Mother Lake received that testimony and took up their march again with the Saints. . . .

"'Philomena Smith Lake,' in 'Sketch of James Lakes Life,' Written 26 August 1936 by S. C. Richardson, and copied 29 August 1949 by Mary Ellen Lake Jarvis," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives Salt Lake City, Utah, 4.
