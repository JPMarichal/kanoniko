Morrison, John

(June 30, 1802-April 1, 1881[?])

Morrison family tradition explains sallow complexion of young John Morrison as the result of trauma felt by his mother who was pregnant at time of martyrdom.

It is a family tradition that he was associated with the Church for considerable time at Nauvoo, and that John, his third child, born August 15, 1844 at Nauvoo, always had a sallow complexion, was so marked by this mother from viewing the Church martyrs, Joseph and Hyrum Smith at their martyrdom. If there are such things as birth marks, the shock of this incident to a pregnant woman at seven months might have caused this.

Richard W. Morrison, "History of John and Sarah Mark Morrison, of Their Travels from Ireland to Franklin, Idaho," written 10 October 1956, Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
