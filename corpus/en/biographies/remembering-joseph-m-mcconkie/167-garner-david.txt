Garner, David

(January 30, 1818-April 27, 1889)

Witnessed martyrdom; mob burns some 200 LDS homes in Fall, 1845.

David Garner, born in North Carolina, January 30, 1818, and his wife Dolly Durfee Garner, born in New York, March 8, 1816, accepted the gospel and came to Nauvoo, where they witnessed the martyrdom of their beloved Prophet and Patriarch. Edmund Durfee, the father of Dolly Garner, also gave his life for the cause he loved. At his home in Lima, nine miles from Nauvoo, he was getting ready to thrash his grain when the mob came, and after shooting him, burned his home, together with the homes of about 200 saints in the surrounding country. The mobs grew fiercer and finally threatened to burn all the beautiful homes in Nauvoo and drive out all the people. This was in the autumn of 1845. . . .
