Williams, Ezra Granger

(November 17, 1823-August 1, 1905)

Joseph invited 8-year-old Ezra to be baptized and he was; Hyrum confirmed him.

On April 14, 1832 Ezra, with William Kimball, son of Heber C. Kimball, were walking along the river bank, when they met the Prophet Joseph Smith, who asked if they would like to be baptized. They consented and were baptized. They went on home and Hyrum Smith confirmed the baptism.

"Biographical Sketch of the Life of Dr. Ezra Granger Williams by His Daughter, Lucy Godfrey," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Note: Ezra Williams was the son of Frederick G. Williams.
