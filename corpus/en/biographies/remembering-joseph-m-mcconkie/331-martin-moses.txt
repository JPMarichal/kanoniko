Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Martin, Moses

(June 1, 1812-May 5, 1900)

On Zion's Camp Martin saw Joseph wade in mud to help wagons move forward.

The road was so bad that we twice during the day had to unhitch our teams from our wagons and draw them by hand. Here I saw the Prophet wade in mud over the tops of his boot legs and help draw the wagons out.

Journal History, 20 May 1834, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
