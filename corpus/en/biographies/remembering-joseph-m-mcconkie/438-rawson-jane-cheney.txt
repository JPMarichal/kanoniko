Rawson, Jane Cheney

(August 29, 1837-November 29, 1922)

Remembers Joseph inviting her father to wrestle; her father stood guard over Nauvoo following martyrdom.

[Emily Maria Cheney Fose's Aunt, Jane Cheney Rawson, said:] . . . I very well remember seeing the Prophet Joseph Smith, and I remember hearing my father tell when he would go to see him on business the Prophet would say in his jovial way"Now Brother Nathan let's have a wrestling match." We were living in Nauvoo at the time of the Martyrdom of the Prophet Joseph and his Brother Hyrum. My Father stood guard over the Saints the night after the martyrdom.

In Matthias F. Cowley, "A Life Sketch of Emily Maria Cheney Fose and Her Parents," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Remembered Nauvoo, Joseph's store, and the temple.

My parents were among the first to join the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. They suffered the privations and persecutions of the Saints of Missouri and Kirtland. They were among the first to go to Nauvoo, Hancock Co., Illinois, and they were among the last to leave Nauvoo on account of father being called to look after the poor and the widows, to help them start on their journey. Here was my first recollectionI remember the Mississippi River, the rock house where the steam boats used to land. I also remember seeing Joseph Smith's store, and the Nauvoo Temple being built. My father and mother received their endowments in the Nauvoo Temple.

"A Biographical Sketch on the Life of Eliza Jane Cheney Rawson," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
