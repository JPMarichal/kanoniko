Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Taylor, Leonora Cannon

(October 6, 1796-December 9, 1868)

States Joseph invited Emma to go to Carthage, but she refused because of illness; also states that Joseph did not know how the mob intended to kill him only that they did intend to kill him.

Joseph Smith urged Emma to go to Carthage with him. She refused on account of having chills and fever he Replied if they did not hang me I do not know how they kill me.

"Statement by Leonora Cannon Taylor, ca. 1856," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
