Patten, Joanna Hollister

(b. March 18, 1833)

Though young, she remembers the death of the Prophet.

. . . remained there (Ohio) until the Mormons moved to Missouri and from there to Ohio, then back to Nauvoo where I was baptized when eight years old. I was there when the Prophet was killed, have been in the Nauvoo Temple many times and was driven from there by a mob. I was also a witness to the Lord sending quails for us to eat.

"A Biographical Sketch of the Life of Joanna Hollister Patten," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
