Harding, Dwight

(1897-1871)

Dwight and wife, Phoebe, present at the transfiguration of Brigham Young.

Charles Harding's [son of Dwight and Phoebe Holbrook (1810-1874)] parents were in attendance at the special meeting of the Church held in Nauvoo August 8, 1844, in which the apostles asserted their right to lead the church through their president, Brigham Young. They saw the countenance and heard the voice of Brigham Young change to that of Joseph Smith.

Glen F. Harding, "A Record of the Ancestry and Descendants of John Jacob Zundel, Known as Jacob Zundel," LDS Family History Library, Salt Lake City, Utah, 274.
