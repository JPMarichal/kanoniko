Boltin [Bolton], Curtis Edwin

(July 16, 1812-December 6, 1890)

Boltin met Joseph in April 1844; remembers last time he saw Joseph, who was standing on a hill on west side of Nauvoo, holding his son in his arms; describes Joseph as a beautifully formed man, laughing pleasantly.

The following Spring [i.e., April, 1844] I went to Nauvoo myself alone and visited Joseph Smith & Hiram his brother. Received an ordination as an High Priest under the hands of Hiram Smith as also a patriarchal blessing. He ordained me on the 16th May 1844 in the street near the fence north of Joseph's first residence in Nauvoo. I received a certificate from Willard Richards, who at this time lived in the aforementioned house. A few minutes after my ordination I went on board the Steamboat Maid of Iowa on my return home. The last I saw of Joseph Smith he was standing with his youngest boy in his arms, at the brow of the hill on the west side of Nauvoo House in the middle of the street. No one was near him. He was a most beautiful formed man, and was laughing pleasantly to the brethren on board the Steamboatwho were leaving to go a preachingI never in this life shall look upon his like again.

Curtis Edwin Boltin, Reminiscences and Journal, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
