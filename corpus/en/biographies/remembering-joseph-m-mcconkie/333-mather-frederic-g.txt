Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Mather, Frederic G.

(1844-1925)

Mather quotes Joseph playfully suggesting Rigdon's oratory sometimes embellishes.

Smith was in the habit of announcing from his lofty pulpit, "The truth is good enough without dressing up, but Brother Rigdon will now proceed to dress it up."

"The Early Days of Mormonism," Lippincott's Monthly Magazine 26 (August 1880): 210.

Note: Mather gives no source for this quote, and for all we can discern it is his own imagination. Still, Joseph had sufficient wit and humor to have spoken in this gentle tenor.
