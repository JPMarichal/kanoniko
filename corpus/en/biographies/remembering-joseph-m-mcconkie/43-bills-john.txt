Bills, John

(September 19, 1819-February 19, 1850)

As a tailor, made clothes for Joseph and Hyrum.

[John] was president in the 29th Quorum of Seventies, which was organized July 27, 1845. He was also a General in the Nauvoo Legion. Being a tailor, he made the clothes for Joseph and Hyrum and cut out and superintended the making of the uniforms for the first company of the Nauvoo Legion.

William A. Bills Sr., "Family History of William Andrew Bills Sr."; copies in private family possession.
