Herrick, Lester James

(December 14, 1827-April 12, 1892)

As youth, remembers visits of Joseph and Hyrum to his parents home.

In 1842 the [Herrick] family took up their abode at Morley's settlement, where, two years later, they again experienced the wrath of the lawless marauder in the destruction of home and property. Returning to Nauvoo, they remained there until the exodus of 1846. During the life-time of the Prophet and the Patriarch, the Herrick's were often visited by them, and their kindly ministrations left a lasting impression upon the mind of young Lester, who was in his seventeenth year at the time of the martyrdom.

"Lester James Herrick," in Orson F. Whitney, History of Utah, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: G. Q. Cannon & Sons Co., 1904), 4:286.
