Bushman, Martin B.

(February 5, 1841-October 31, 1927)

Though but a babe at the time, remembers sitting on laps of Joseph and Hyrum; also had clear recall of infant memories in Nauvoo Temple as long as sixty years later.

His parents moved to Nauvoo when he was one year old. He had the privilege of seeing many times the Prophet Joseph and Hyrum and of setting on their laps, and the power of attraction was so great in those men that he has the imprint upon his mind just how they looked though he was only three years and four months old when he saw them last and now he is over sixty. His mother took him to see the Temple in September 1846 just before the Saints were driven from Nauvoo. She thought he might remember some things that he saw which he did, for when he went into other Temples when he was older he saw things there just as he had them in his mind that were in [the Nauvoo] Temple.

"Martin B. Bushman," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Writes to friend saying he met Joseph, whom God protects from his enemies.

I have seen Brother Joseph and talked with him and I have herd him preach and I Believe him to be a prophet for his enemies have pursued him verry much he has walked through the midst of them the[y] could not hurt him for God is his stay and gide.

"Martin Bushman to Leonard Pinkle, Dec. 18, 1842," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
