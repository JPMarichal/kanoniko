White, Evander J.

(b. March 5, 1836)

Saw the Nauvoo Expositor destroyed; also saw officers take Joseph and Hyrum to Carthage; he heard Joseph say he was going "like a lamb to the slaughter"; saw Joseph and Hyrum's dead bodies.

I was at Nauvoo when the corner stone of the Temple was laid in 1841 and also when the Temple was finished and dedicated. My father died soon after we moved to Nauvoo. My mother went through the temple at Nauvoo and had her endowments. I was there when the Expositor newspaper was thrown down and the next morning I went and picked up types. I used them to print my name. I sat on the fence and watched the officers when they took the Prophet Joseph to and his brother Hyrum to Carthage and heard Joseph make the remark "I am going like a lamb to the slaughter." The next time I saw him I stood by the road side and watched them as they brought him home dead and went down to his house the next day with my mother and saw Joseph and Hyrum laying side by side dead.

"Evander J. White," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah. Grammar and punctuation have been modernized.
