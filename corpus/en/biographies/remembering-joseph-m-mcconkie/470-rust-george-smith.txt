Rust, George Smith

(January 23, 1834-June 18, 1922)

Joseph visited his parents home; Joseph baptized him in Mississippi.

His father [i.e., George's father] married a widow, Laura Hartson, the spring of 1842. She was an affectionate step-mother and with her the sons had a happy boyhood. The prophet Joseph Smith visited them in their log house.

. . . In August, 1843 he [George] was baptized by the Prophet Joseph in the Mississippi River.

"Brief Biographical Sketch of George Smith Rust," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
