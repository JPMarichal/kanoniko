Miner, Moroni

(June 4, 1835-August 14, 1935)

As an infant, he was blessed by the Prophet; his parents attended the Kirtland Temple dedication.

Moroni Miner, son of Albert Miner and Tamma Durfee, born in Geauga County Ohio, June 4, 1835. Blessed by the Prophet Joseph Smith. His parents were present at the laying of the cornerstone of the Kirtland Temple, assisted in the erection of that edifice and were present at its dedication
