Nelson, Thomas Billington

(May 9, 1835-February 19, 1918)

Baptized by Joseph; he saw the martyrs and remembers the mourning and women praying.

I was baptized when 8 years old by the great Prophet Joseph Smith in the Mississippi River. I well remember the Prophet and his beloved brother and have heard them preach a great many times and well remember the trying scenes of Nauvoo and see both of the great men after they were killed and brought home. Passing through the room where they lay with my parents I well remember the cries and prayers offered up to the Almighty God by the many good mothers for the protection of their husbands and loved ones at home. From the mobs that was seeking to destroy the Saints I see the Prophet meet officers who was hunting him but did [not] know him.

"A Sketch of the Life of Thomas Billington Nelson," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, 2.
