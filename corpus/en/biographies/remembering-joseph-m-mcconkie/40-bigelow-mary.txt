Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Bigelow, Mary

(June 26, 1809-April 19, 1888)

Named a son after the Prophet.

We moved to Nauvoo in the fall of 1843. We went to Brother Matthews, and then down by the levee. Bought land and began to farm. We were all taken sick, had a terrible sicknessfever and ague. We lived through the massacre and I had a son born on the 4 of July, a week after the Prophet was slain. We called his name Joseph Smith Bigelow. He lived nine months and sixteen days and died. . . .

(She tells a story of having a very sick child, and of a vision in which the Savior appeared and told her that she and her baby would get wellwhich occurred as predicted.)

"Autobiography of Mary Bigelow," typescript copy, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
