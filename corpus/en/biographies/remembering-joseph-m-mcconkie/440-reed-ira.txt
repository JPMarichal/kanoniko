Reed, Ira

(January [or June] 25, 1834 [or 1835]-May 8, 1872)

His mother is thought to be the sixteenth person baptized into the Church; his father defended the Prophet in Court.

"Ira Reed, member of the Nauvoo Legion, son of John Reed and Rebecca Barsh [or Bearce], Born Jan. 25, 1834 in Kirtland, Ohio. He started for Utah in 1848 as a boy with his parents one brother and Levi and a sisters Clarissa Hancock, Rebecca (Steed) and Laura (Steed) arriving on 20 Nov. 1848. His parents died on the journey. His mother is supposed to be the 16th member baptized in the church, and his father very strong defender of the Prophet Joseph and at one time defended him in court."

"Ira Reed," Mormon Biographical File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
