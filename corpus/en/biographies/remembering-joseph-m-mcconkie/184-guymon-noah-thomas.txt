Guymon, Noah Thomas

(June 30, 1819-January 7, 1911)

Knew the prophet, and served as bodyguard; was present at transfiguration of Brigham Young.

He too joined the Church at an early age and was a staunch supporter of the Prophet, Joseph Smith. He moved with his family to Nauvoo, where he was privileged to serve as a bodyguard to the prophet. He knew him personally and mourned with the other saints at the martyrdom of him and his brother Hyrum.

It was his privilege also to be in attendance at the historic meeting when the mantle of the Prophet is said to have fallen on the shoulders of Brigham Young as a divine evidence that he was the one to succeed the Prophet as head of the church. Those who have heard him tell of the incident quote him thus, "I was sitting on the ground leaning against a pole that helped support the bowery under which the meeting was held. My back was toward the pulpit as I listened to the proceedings. As Brigham was speaking, his voice suddenly changed to sound like Joseph's. I turned, fully expecting to see the prophet standing before the group." It was sufficient evidence to win his complete loyalty to the new leader, and he prepared to follow him to their new home.

"Typescript Notes From the Journal of Noah Thomas Guymon," 10; copies in private family possession.
