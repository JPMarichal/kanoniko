Benbow, John

(April 1, 1800-May 12, 1874)

Benbow pays passage to America for poor Saints; Joseph visited Benbow's Nauvoo farm; asked if Benbow was willing to give up everything he had and go west with only "what he wore"which Benbow did.

[John Benbow owned a farm at Hill Farm Castle Frome, Ledbury, Herefordshire, Eng., where Wilford Woodruff baptized 600 people; Benbow gave $1500 to Brother Woodruff to have the Book of Mormon published in England.] John Benbow sold his farm, chartered a ship and came to America. He brought all the poor people from that part of the country free. Grandmother came with her Uncle John and Aunt Jane Benbow. John Benbow tried to have a farm in Nauvoo like the one he had in England. He built a beautiful home and laid of his farm with driveways. Having plenty of money he had the very best. He did a great deal of good in the church and for the poor people in the church. The Prophet Joseph Smith would go out to his farm and spend two or three days he asked John Benbow what he would think if he had to go west and leave every thing just as it was and only take what he wore, he thought that would be the worst thing that could happen to him but he had to do it. They left their beautiful home and all April 8th 1947 and moved to Council Bluff.

"Life Sketch of Ellen Benbow Carter," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah. Spelling has been modernized.
