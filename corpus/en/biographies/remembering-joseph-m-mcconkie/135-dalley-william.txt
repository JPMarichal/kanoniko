Dalley, William

(March 2, 1821 [or 1822]-December 31, 1907)

Knew Joseph; as a girl, his wife worked in Joseph's home; remembers martyrdom; William saw transfiguration of Brigham Young, including a "halo of light."

William and his wife were well acquainted with Joseph and Hyrum Smith. She as a girl, worked in the home of the Prophet. They lived at Nauvoo at the time of the mobbings and were driven out with the rest of the Saints. They told many times of the martyrdom of the Prophet Joseph Smith and his brother Hyrum.

William attended the meeting at which the mantle of Joseph fell upon Brigham Young. He said that from the halo of light that shown around Brigham Young that he was chosen by God to lead the Saints to the land of Zion, and would at all times have the guidance and inspiration of God to lead them. He did lead them too, to their land of Zion in "Utah." He was the greatest leader of all time, and with the inspiration of God he builded and founded this land of Zion for the Saints.

"Biography of William Dalley, As Hyrum and Mayhew Dalley remember their father, written by Alvin D. Wilcock," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah. Some modernizations in style have been made to increase clarity.
