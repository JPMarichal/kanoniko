Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Freeze, Lillie

(1855-1936 [or 1937])

Quotes Joseph as saying time will come when only LDS women will be willing to bear children.

We should post ourselves regarding the prophecies that have been predicted. I will mention one in particular that was uttered by the Prophet Joseph Smith, he said the time would come when none but the women of the Latter-day Saints would be willing to bear children.

Lillie Freeze, "Remarks at the Y. L. M. I. Conference of Box Elder Stake," Young Woman's Journal 2, no. 2 (November 1890): 81.
