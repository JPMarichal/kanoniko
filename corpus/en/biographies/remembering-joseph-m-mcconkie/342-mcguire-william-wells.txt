McGuire, William Wells

(June 25, 1809-December 8, 1887)

Heard Joseph testify that he would tell of his own experiences, rather than quote

Peter or Paul.

He heard Joseph Smith preach at the house of Edward Hunter in Chester Co., Pennsylvania January 25, 1840. Joseph said: "Brothers and Sisters, I will not tell you much of what the Lord told Paul, or of what He told Peter, but I will tell you what the Lord told me."

"Statement to Andrew Jenson from W. W. McGuire, June 25, 1886," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
