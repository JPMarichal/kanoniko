Morris, Hyrum Bowles

(December 23, 1821-January 21, 1908)

Served as the Prophet Joseph's bodyguard.

At the age of nineteen Hyrum journeyed to Nauvoo to see Joseph Smith and while there served as a guard to the Prophet.

Leona S. Westover, "Warren WallingCaptain," in Kate B. Carter, comp., Our Pioneer Heritage, 20 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Daughters of the Utah Pioneers, 1958-77), 4:49.
