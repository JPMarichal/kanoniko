Gleason, John S.

(January 13, 1819-December 21, 1904)

Followed Stephen Markham into Nauvoo Expositor office when it was destroyed; present at transfiguration of Brigham Young.

He was personally acquainted with the Prophet Joseph Smith and was active in all the stirring events which transpired predeeding and following the death of the Prophet and his brother Hyrum. He was the second man in the office when the Nauvoo Expositor was destroyed at the command of Joseph smith. He was preceded by Stephen Markham.

. . . After the death of the prophet he was present at the meeting when the cloak of Joseph fell upon Brigham Young and he knew in his heart who was [the] rightful leader of the Church.

"History of John Streator Gleason," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
