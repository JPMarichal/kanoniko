Perry, Philander Jackson

(September 29, 1821-September 27, 1902)

Courted the Prophet's sister; gave Joseph a large sum of money to ease his debts.

While still in the East he was part of the bodyguard of the Prophet Joseph Smith. This was a very dangerous job but he was well qualified to hold it. He knew the Prophet personally and well for he used to court the sister of the Prophet. On one such visit he found Joseph very much perturbed and upon inquiring found him to be very much embarrassed financially. He had sold his farm that day receiving for it a tall hat of money. This he went and obtained it bring it back and handing it to the Prophet said, "It is yours. Use it as you will, you are welcome." This my great grandfather told his son and from him it came to my grandfather and so to me They joined the Church in 1832.

"Biographical Sketch of Philander Jackson Perry," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
