Willis, Alzina Lucinda Lott

(March 4, 1834-August 19, 1910)

Saw the transfiguration of Brigham Young; her mother explained it was to show who they should follow.

A special meeting was called, to be held on Thurs., August 8, 1844. The members of the church were all urged to attend. Among others, Brigham Young addressed the great multitude of saints assembled there. He spoke with great power. When he first arose to speak, we were greatly astonished. President Young stood transfigured before us and we beheld the Prophet, Joseph Smith and heard his voice as plainly as ever we did when he was living among us. My father and mother were there in attendance. I turned to my Mama and said, "Mama, I thought the Prophet was dead?" Mama answered and said "He is, Alzina, and this is the way our Heavenly Father has told us who is to be our next leader and Prophet."

I was 11 years old at the time.

Lorraine W. Silcox, "Zina-Forget-Me-Not: A Biographical Story," Sacramento, California, 1973, 45; copies in private family possession.
