Parker, Joseph Falconer

(April 7, 1841-January 14, 1936)

Childhood memory of seeing both the Prophet and his brother Hyrum.

Father and mother went to Nauvoo, I think to be baptized, when I was just past three years old. Father took me by the hand up the winding stairs. Then we went out to the South door where we saw two men standing in the doorway, and as I remember, my father told me one of them was the Prophet Joseph Smith and I always thought the other one was the Patriarch Hyrum Smith. I also remember the rock oxen with the big brass knubs on their horns and the men were digging a well by the side of the font to pump water into the font.

Signed:

[Signature of Joseph F. Parker]
