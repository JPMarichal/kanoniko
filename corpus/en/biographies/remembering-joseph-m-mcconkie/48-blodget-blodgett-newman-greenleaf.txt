Blodget [Blodgett], Newman Greenleaf

(September 18, 1800-August 24, 1882)

Emiline Blodgett, as a small girl, had the faith that if Joseph looked into her eyes and inquired about her sight, she would be healed. He did, and she was.

Newman Greenleaf and his family knew the Prophet Joseph Smith. Their daughter Emiline told of an incident that happened when she was a small girl, she was bothered with sore eyes. She told her parents she knew if the Prophet Joseph Smith would just look into her eyes and ask her what was the matter with them they would be healed. One day as the Prophet was greeting people around her, he stooped and asked her about her eyes, soon they were completely healed. No one remembers her wearing glasses, she was baptized in 1840/41 by Horace Bliss.

"Newman Greenleaf Blodgett Family History," typescript copy; copies in private family possession.
