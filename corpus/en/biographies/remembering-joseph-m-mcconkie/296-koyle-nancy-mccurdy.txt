Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Koyle, Nancy McCurdy

(b. March 18, 1820)

Present at the transfiguration of Brigham Young.

"She was present at many important happenings in and around Nauvoo. She heard the Prophet on many occasions and attended his funeral. She was present at the meeting when the mantle of the Prophet fell upon Brigham Young. She heard and saw the wonderful manifestations on that occasion."

Ellen Jane Koyle Ross, "John Hyrum Koyle, Senior," personal history; copies in private possession.
