Perry, Sylvester Lyman

(January 2, 1833-July 6, 1908)

Saw Joseph following the Dixon kidnapping and heard him give his last speech.

Sylvester Lyman Perry, son of Stephen and Rhoby Edward, I was born 2 Jan. 1833, Lewis, Essex Co., New York. Later his father moved to Nauvoo where he was baptized and received a blessing by Isaac Morley. He knew Joseph and Hyrum Smith. When Joseph was kidnapped and rescued, he saw him meet his wife, mount his horse and lead the people back to Nauvoo. He heard him give his last public address.
