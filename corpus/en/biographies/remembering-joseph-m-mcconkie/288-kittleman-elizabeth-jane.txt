Kittleman, Elizabeth Jane

(May 26, 1831-December 13, 1917)

As young girl remembers Prophet and Hyrum holding meetings in her grandfather's home.

I was baptized in the summer of 1840 by Elijah Sheets. When I was a small girl, the Prophet Joseph Smith and his brother, Hyrum, came to Grandfather Kittleman's home and held many meetings.

Mary E. Miller Jackson, "John Kittleman and His Family," in Kate B. Carter, comp., Our Pioneer Heritage, 20 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Daughters of the Utah Pioneers, 1958-77), 3:559.
