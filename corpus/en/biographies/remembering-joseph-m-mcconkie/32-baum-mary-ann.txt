Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Baum, Mary Ann

(b. July 17, 1835)

At viewing of bodies of Joseph and Hyrum; remembered story of Joseph repaying a loan from her father.

"After following the Saints, the family finally settled in Navou [sic] where they resided until the death of the Prophet. Her [i.e., Mary Ann's] mother took her to see the Prophet and his brother as they lay side by side in the mansion house. She recalled the time the Prophet came to their home calling her father out telling him, that he was in need of money. It was loaned to the Prophet, and in time it was all paid back."

"Grandparents of Hannetta Ann Davis Loveless, on Her Mother's Side," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
