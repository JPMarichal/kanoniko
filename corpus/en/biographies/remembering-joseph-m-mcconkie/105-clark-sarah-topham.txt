Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Clark, Sarah Topham

(June 30, 1831-January 11, 1918)

One of Joseph's sermons so impressed her she resolved never to quit praying; heard Joseph say he would go as a lamb to the slaughter.

The last sermon I heard the Prophet preach impressed me so that I resolved to never quit praying. I was at the meeting when he told the people that the hounds were after him and that "He was going like a lamb to the slaughter!" I heard him say those words, and Oh the silence and sobbing and tears, and lamenting of the saints gathered in that room that beautiful Sunday morning when these touching, solemn words were uttered! It was a blessing to me that I went to that meeting. It was a blessing to me and my tribe that I heard that last sermon. It impressed me so and I try to impress my children, and others with the values of prayer and trust in our Good Heavenly Father.

"Sketch of the Life of Sara T. Clark, by Samuel R. Brown," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
