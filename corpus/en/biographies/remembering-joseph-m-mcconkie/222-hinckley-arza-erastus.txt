Hinckley, Arza Erastus

(August 13 [or 15], 1826-February 18, 1901)

Was at Far West when Joseph was imprisoned; witnessed mob violate rights of Latter-day Saints.

I went to Far West in the fall of '38 in John E. Payes Co. Was in Far West at the capt [capture] of Pres. Jos. Smith and the first covenant or the edict of Governor Boggs when every man was placed in a howlow [hollow] square and brought up to a table at the point of bainett [bayonette] to sign over his property to pay the expenses of our mobbers, leave the state forth with, or be shot down or leave there religion and go with the mob.

"Life Sketch of Arza Erastus Hinckley," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
