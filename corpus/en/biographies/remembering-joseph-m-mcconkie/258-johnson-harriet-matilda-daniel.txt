Johnson, Harriet Matilda Daniel

(March 12, 1805-June 15, 1884)

Joseph set her apart to be a midwife.

Before the death of the Prophet Joseph Smith, he set apart Harriet Johnson as midwife and between then and the time she died she helped bring into the world about four thousand babies.

"Harriet Matilda Daniel Johnson," in Kate B. Carter, comp., Our Pioneer Heritage, 20. vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Daughters of the Utah Pioneers, 1958-77), 6:431.

Note: For a similar setting apart and experience, see "Life Story of Edward L. Black the son of George Black who was the son of William Y. Black and Jane Johnson as written by Himself." Copies in private family possession.
