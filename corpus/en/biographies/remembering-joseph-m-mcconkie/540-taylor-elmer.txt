Taylor, Elmer

(November 4, 1831-April 1890)

As a boy, saw the mob that murdered the Prophet.

As a boy of thirteen, herding cows on the prairie five miles from Carthage, he saw on the fatal 27th of June, 1844, a portion of the mob that murdered Joseph and Hyrum Smith.

"Elmer Taylor," in Orson F. Whitney, History of Utah, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: G. Q. Cannon & Sons Co., 1904), 4:380.
