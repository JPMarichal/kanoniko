Ockey, Edward

(b. February 27, 1816)

Met by the Prophet at the landing in Nauvoo when he arrived.

On July 1, 1841 he arrived at upper landing in Nauvoo and was met by the Prophet Joseph Smith. He had come from England.

"A Short Account of the Life of Edward Ockey," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
