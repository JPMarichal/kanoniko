Wall, William Madison

(September 30, 1821-September 18, 1869)

Wrestled with Joseph.

William Madison Wall was born Sept. 20, 1821, in Rockingham County North Carolina. He moved to Illinois when a young man and it was there that he accepted the Gospel and was baptized a member of the Latter Day Saint Church in 1842. He and his wife Nancy moved to Nauvoo where he was active in supporting events as well as religious work. He was six feet two inches in height and he and the Prophet Joseph Smith enjoyed many a friendly wrestling match. He helped in building the Nauvoo Temple and he and his wife received their endowments there.

"Life Sketch of William Madison Wall," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
