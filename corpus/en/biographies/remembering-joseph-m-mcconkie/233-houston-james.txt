Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Houston, James

(June 4, 1817-January 3, 1863)

Arrives in Nauvoo; hears Joseph speak, and the Spirit testifies he is a prophet.

[Tells of his conversion in Paisley: his brother came to him in a dream. Then, the missionaries came to town, held a meeting, but he was so engrossed in other conversations that he did not hear them. Then, on a second meeting, he went to hear them but was not able to get into their meeting as they had closed the door to keep people out in order to protect themselves against disturbances such as they had had on earlier occasions. One of the disgruntled investigators left that meeting, and met him waiting outside. "I got into a conversation with one of the hearers who had been at the meeting. He told me of the angel coming to Joseph Smith. The power followed the word it run through me from head to foot. I began to stand up for it and I was astonished at myself."]

[Came to Nauvoo on April 13, 1840] . . . I saw the Prophet Joseph Smith and in a few days more I boarded in the next house and continued there nine months. I can assure he was all he proposed to be. I know he was a true prophet of God, for I have heard him speak and prophesy as he was led by the Holy Ghost. He was a fine and great man I knew this by the Holy Ghost that was in me.

"Religious History of Grandfather James Houston, born 1817," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
