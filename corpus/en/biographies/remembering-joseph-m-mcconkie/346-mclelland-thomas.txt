McLelland, Thomas

(March 28, 1819-May 12, 1890)

Heard Joseph speak on the Godhead; remembers spirit of mourning after martyrdom.

In 1841 when the gospel of Jesus Christ was proclaimed I was baptized by Elder Frances Miller on the 28 of March 1841, [and the] following May 15 was ordained an elder and was called to preside over a Branch of the church in the Ahome [?] place and in '42 I went on a mission to Ardie and was blessed. In 1844 I emigrated with my wife Elizabeth to Nauvoo where I saw the Prophet Joseph Smith and heard him speak with great power on the Godhead and I felt paid for my journeys. I saw the Prophet Joseph & Hyrum his brother after they were assassinated; it was truly a day of mourning and of thick darkness such as I had never experienced since or before

"Thomas McLelland," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
