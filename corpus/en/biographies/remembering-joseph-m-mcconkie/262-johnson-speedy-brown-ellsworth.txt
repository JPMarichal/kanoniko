Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Johnson, Speedy Brown Ellsworth

(b. May 25, 1820)

First meeting with Joseph, and he instructs that they move to Zarahemla.

In the Spring of 1842, she moved to Nauvoo, Illinois. This was the first time that she saw the Prophet Joseph Smith. He gave us instructions to move into Iowa, and to strengthen the Branch of the Church in Zarahemla. . . .

"Sketch of the Life of Speedy Brown Ellsworth Johnson," in Philo Johnson, untitled manuscript, 18 April 1894, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
