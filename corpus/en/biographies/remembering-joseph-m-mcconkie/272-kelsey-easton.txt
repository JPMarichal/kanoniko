Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Kelsey, Easton

(March 19, 1813-March 10, 1899)

Joseph saw ancient Twelve in council and says he knew little by way of comparison.

He (Joseph Smith) was once in Council with the Ancient Twelve, but they were so far advanced in the wonderful things of heaven that I had not a word to say. I merely looked on and listened.

No man can be a minister of the Gospel unless he has the spirit of revelation and prophecy.

"Sayings of Joseph by Those Who Knew Him at Different Times," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Says Joseph used Urim and Thummim to read Bible, and thereby saw "the grand panorama" of what was written.

[Kelsey said he heard Joseph say] After I got through translating the Book of Mormon, I took up the Bible to read with the Urim and Thummim. I read the first Chapter of Genesis and I saw the things as they were done. I turned over the next and the next and the whole passed before me like a grand panorama and so on chapter after chapter until I read the whole of it. I saw it all! Then I think of the sectarian priests boasting of what they knowwhy I have forgotten a thousand times more than ever they knew (This was spoken in the house of Benj. Brown, N.Y., 1832. Sidney Rigdon being along. Related by Lorenzo Brown, 1880.]

"Sayings of Joseph by Those Who Knew Him at Different Times," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
