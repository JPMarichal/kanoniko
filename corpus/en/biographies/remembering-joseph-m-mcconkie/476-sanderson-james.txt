Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Sanderson, James

(June 20, 1804-September 13, 1845)

Stacked corn for the Prophet; his children played with the Prophet's children.

"When arriving at Nauvoo the temple walls were two feet above the ground. James and his son Henry worked each 10th day on the temple. They lived in a story and a half log house one block north of the Prophet Joseph, Sidney Rigdon being between them and Joseph. James and his son stacked corn for the Prophet but got such little work that he asked the Prophet about going to St. Louis to get better work but the Prophet told him he better stay in Nauvoo. So he did but on the 27th of June 1844 the Prophet and Hyrum was murdered. It was such a sad blow to James and family as James' children and the Prophet's children played together with the Prophet and James playing with them occasionally."

"James Sanderson," typescript copy, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah, 1.
