Rawson, Horace Strong

(July 15, 1799-October 10, 1882)

Heard Joseph preach "as I never heard man preach before."

Here in 1836 I saw Joseph the Prophet for the first time, and heard him Preach, as I never heard man preach before, speaking of redemption of Zion, and the restoration of scattered Israel, exhorting us to be faithful, and sealed up the church, to eternal life, and then returned to Kirtland, the church then, moved to Caldwell County, settled down hoping to enjoy peace, but the war commenced again, a jealousy arose to the degree that all western hell boiled over, old Belial and old Boggs his right hand man also old Col. Hinckle the old apostate, all accepted (?) the hellish plan for to destroy their fellow man.

"A Short Sketch of Biography of Horace S. Rawson," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
