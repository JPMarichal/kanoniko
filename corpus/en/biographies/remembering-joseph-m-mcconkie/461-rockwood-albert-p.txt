Rockwood, Albert P.

(June 5, 1805-November 26, 1879)

Served as Joseph's bodyguard.

Rockwood, Albert P. . . . was born June 5, 1805. . . . Later he acted as commander of Joseph Smith's life guard, and when Joseph was kidnapped in Dixon, Ill., in 1843, Elder Rockwood, as acting adjutant of a company of horsemen, rendered efficient service in his rescue.

"Rockwood, Albert P.," in Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Western Epics, 1971), 1:194.
