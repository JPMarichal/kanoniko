Rust, William Walker

(August 14, 1807-September 18, 1894)

Served as occasional bodyguard to Joseph.

[In New England, as a new convert he] migrated to Nauvoo where he first met the Prophet Joseph Smith. That was during the year 1841. During the following three years, Dr. Rust occasionally served as one of the Prophet's body-guards.

"William Walker Rust," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
