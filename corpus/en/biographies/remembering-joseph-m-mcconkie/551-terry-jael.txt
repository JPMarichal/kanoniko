Terry, Jael

(b. May 23, 1812)

Campaigned for Joseph in 1844.

[Baptized in February 1838; was in Missouri when Boggs issued his extermination order] " . . . he went to commerce afterwards and Nauvoo, Ill. Where he lived until the Saints were driven from their homes again.

In the summer of 1844 he went on a mission to the eastern states to electionere [sic] for the Prophet Joseph Smith when he offered himself a candidate for the Presidency of the United States."

"Biographical Sketch of Jael Terry," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
