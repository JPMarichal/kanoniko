Higgins, Nelson

(September 7 [or 1], 1806-November 20, 1890)

Knew and wrestled with Joseph.

He was present at the dedication of the Kirtland Temple and later located in Missouri where he endured the persecutions inflicted upon the Mormon people in the later 1830's. Then he moved to Nauvoo and was living there when the Mormons were again expelled from their homes. He was a close friend of the Prophet Joseph Smith and it was said that on some occasions he and the Prophet engaged in wrestling bouts for amusement. He was a man of athletic build.

"Nelson Higgins," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
