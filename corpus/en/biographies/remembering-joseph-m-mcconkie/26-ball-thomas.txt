Ball, Thomas

(March 29, 1822-July 15, 1905)

Saw Joseph and Hyrum in a dream and heard a hymn sung; later he heard that same hymn, and recognized it from his dream.

Soon after joining the Church Brother Ball had a rather impressive dream. The dream portrayed to him some scenes of Church history, and the Prophet and Patriarch Joseph and Hyrum Smith and others, and an hymn was sung, the tune and the words of which he was utterly unacquainted with and had never heard before. The dream impressed him greatly, and he called the next day upon a sister Bailey, and told her of the dream, and asked her to kindly sing over what church hymn tunes she could think of. She did so, and at length came to the hymn, "When we came to the place where the two martyrs lay," "that is the very tune and words of the hymn I heard in the dream" he said. And he had never heard the words or the tune until he heard them in the dream, and knew them again at once upon Sister Bailey singing it over.

Joseph S. Ball, "The Life of Patriarch Thomas Ball," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
