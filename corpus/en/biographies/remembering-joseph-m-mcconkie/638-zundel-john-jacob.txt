Zundel, John Jacob

(1796-1880)

In a letter to his daughter, he recalls a long conversation with the Prophet Joseph Smith.

"Phebe, dear girl. In your last letter you stated that you contemplate making a visit to Nauvoo. Nothing would give me more pleasure to view some of the scenes of my boyhood days, but I think that it will never be now. In coming up to Quincy [Illinois] that is where your aunt Elizabeth [Elizabeth Jane Harding, 1840-1925, daughter of Dwight Harding who married Isaac E. D. Zundel, son of Jacob Zundel who also lived in Nauvoo] was born. When you get to Nauvoo, if you are permitted to go into the Mansion House, go into the front southwest corner room. There is where I and Lamoni Holbrook sat and had a long conversation with Joseph Smith, the prophet . . ."

"George Harding to Phebe [Harding], circa 1910," in Glen F. Harding, "A Record of the Ancestry and Descendants of John Jacob Zundel, Known as Jacob Zundel," LDS Family History Library, Salt Lake City, Utah, 42.

Worked on the temple; taught Joseph Smith German.

The John Jacob Zundel History . . . says: "At Nauvoo they bought a farm and build a brick house. Here they lived comfortably and enjoyed the Gospel. John Jacob worked on the Nauvoo Temple and also taught the Prophet Joseph Smith the German language . . ."

Glen F. Harding, "A Record of the Ancestry and Descendants of John Jacob Zundel, Known as Jacob Zundel," LDS Family History Library, Salt Lake City, Utah, 52.
