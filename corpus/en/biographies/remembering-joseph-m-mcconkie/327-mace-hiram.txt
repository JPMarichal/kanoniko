Mace, Hiram

(May 5, 1811-August 28, 1896)

First meeting with Joseph, who received his with "kind courtesy"; also saw the martyred bodies of Joseph and Hyrum.

He arrived there (Nauvoo) February 18, 1843, and sold his wagon and horses for a house and city lot. He was much impressed with the beauty and location of the City. Himself and wife visited the Prophet Joseph Smith, who received them with kind courtesy. . . .

. . . He saw the dead bodies of the martyred Joseph and Hyrum Smith, and experienced the darkness of that day.

Deseret Evening News, 21 September 1896. Cited in "Autobiography of Hiram Mace," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Met Joseph; also present when Brigham Young was transfigured.

Father and mother reached Nauvoo Feb. 18, 1843, and later saw Joseph and Hyrum Smith in their blood stained clothes. They were also at the meeting at the time of the transfiguration of Brigham Young into the likeness of Joseph Smith.

"Autobiography of Hiram Mace," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
