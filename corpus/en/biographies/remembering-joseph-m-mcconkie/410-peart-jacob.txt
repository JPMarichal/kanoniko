Peart, Jacob

(June 3, 1801-April 20, 1874)

Joseph administered to his wife; the Prophet sent her to John C. Bennett for medical assistance; she later died.

"On arriving at Nauvoo I called on the Prophet Joseph Smith to come and administer to my wife which he did and advised me to procure the assistance of Elder J. C. Bennett, M.D. which I did. He gave her medicine, but in about 5 days she died."

"Autobiographical Sketch of Jacob Peart," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
