Barton, Eliza Anderson

(March 1, 1835-January 12, 1912)

Remembers martyrdom; saw bodies of Joseph and Hyrum.

Her parents joined the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in 1841, and gathered with the Saints in Nauvoo, passing through the many trials incident to that time. Eliza was acquainted with the Prophet Joseph Smith and vividly remembered the event of the martyrdom of the Prophet and his brother, Hyrum, and saw their bodies after they were prepared for burial.

"Eliza Anderson Barton," in Kate B. Carter, comp., Our Pioneer Heritage, 20 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Daughters of the Utah Pioneers, 1958-77), 6:509.
