Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Curtis, George

(b. October 27, 1823)

Member of Nauvoo Legion; loved to testify of Joseph.

"While living in Nauvoo in 1840, George Curtis at the age of 17 was made a member of the red coat company in the Nauvoo Legion and for six years held himself in readiness to go at a moments notice. He never tired of relating incidents in the life of the prophet Joseph Smith drawing most of them from his personal acquaintance with the prophet, and his testimony that Joseph Smith was a true prophet of the Lord was dearer to him than his life."

"Biography of George Curtis," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Guarded bodies of Joseph and Hyrum.

At the time of the martyrdom of Joseph and Hyrum Smith a reward was offered for the head of the Prophet, he [Lyman Curtis] and his brother George, stood guard over the bodies.

"Zacheus Curtis," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives.
