Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Hammond, Joseph

(June 14, 1822-August 4, 1899)

Joseph Hammond a bodyguard to Joseph.

Her (Susanna's) father [Joseph Hammond] and mother [Mary Lovisa Parker] were staunch members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter day Saints, and her father was a bodyguard to the Prophet Joseph Smith during the early trials of the Church.

"History of James Deans and Susanna Hammond, compiled by Rita C. Lee, a great-granddaughter," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah, 2.
