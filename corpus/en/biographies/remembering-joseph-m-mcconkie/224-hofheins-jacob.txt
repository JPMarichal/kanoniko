Hofheins, Jacob

(December 4, 1812-August 25, 1890)

Witnessed death of Joseph; served as one of his bodyguards.

Jacob while in Nauvoo witnessed the death of the Prophet Joseph Smith. He was one of the bodyguards when the mob was after Joseph Smith . . .
