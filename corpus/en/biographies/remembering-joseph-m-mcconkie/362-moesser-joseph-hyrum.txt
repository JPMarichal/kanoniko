Moesser, Joseph Hyrum

(b. October 11, 1836)

Joseph often visited Moesser home; dangled little Joseph Hyrum on his knee, saying "I love the children"; remembers seeing Joseph and Hyrum in their caskets; Joseph once took the Moesser family through the Nauvoo Temple.

I was baptized in 1845 in the Mississippi River below the Prophet's house by Leonard Soby. The Prophet Joseph Smith was a frequent visitor at our house. He used to take me on his knee and talk and play with we children. My mother would sometimes reprove me for this, but he would say, "I love the children." I saw he and his brother after the martyrdom and when they were laid out in the old Mansion House. One of the brethren lifted me up so I could see them, and now at the age of 86 years past I can remember it as though it was yesterday. I also remember him taking my father and mother and we children through the Nauvoo Temple, remember the oxen that held up the font, then as we went on the top I looked over the railing that was around there. It seemed so far down that I became very frightened. My father was a bodyguard for the Prophet Joseph and his brother Hyrum, and was often called out at night to assist in protecting them.

"A Sketch of the Life of Joseph Hyrum Moesser," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
