Smith, Hyrum

(February 9, 1800-June 27, 1844)

Selected entries from Hyrum's journal give the spirit of the times and mindset: daughter Mary died passing "from time to eternity"; "baptized two into the visible Kingdom of God"; School of the Prophets called the "School of Christ"; Joseph "the Prophet of the Lord that was raised up in the last days."

Mary was called from time to eternity the 29th day of May [1833]. She expired in mine arms. Such a day I never before experienced, and Oh, may God grant that we may meet her again at the great Day of Redemption to hurt no more. . . .

. . . traveled to the town of Springfield. Held a meeting on Thursday the 29th of the 11th month 1832 and baptized two into the visible Kingdom of God.

[January 20, 1833] I Hyrum Smith baptized two into the visible Church of Christ in the beautiful waters of Kirtland. The School of Christ begun in Kirtland, myself spake with tongues and many others.

"Hyrum Smith Diary, 18 Nov. 1832-18 April 1833," BYU Special Collections, Harold B. Lee Library, Provo, Utah.

Receives a blessing from his father and his brother Joseph.

[Around February 21, 1835] I Hyrum Smith received a blessing under the hands of my father, Joseph Smith, Sr., President Rigdon, my brother Joseph, Jr., the Prophet of the Lord that was raised up in the last days. I was blessed with a privilege of obtaining the desire of my heart in all things.

"Hyrum Smith Book of Records 1835-38," BYU Special Collections, Harold B. Lee Library, Provo, Utah.
