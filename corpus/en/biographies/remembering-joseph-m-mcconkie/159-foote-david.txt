Foote, David

(August 7, 1768-August 22, 1845)

Met Joseph, received patriarchal blessing from Father Smith.

David went to Kirtland and became acquainted with the Prophet Joseph Smith, and received a patriarchal blessing by Patriarch Joseph Smith.

"Foote, David," in Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Western Epics, 1971), 1:376.
