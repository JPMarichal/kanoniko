Taylor, Allen

(January 17, 1814-December 5, 1891)

Was one of Joseph's bodyguards.

Allen Taylor was a loved and trusted friend and bodyguard of the Prophet Joseph Smith. Many times the Prophet sent him on secret and important errandssuch as warning the saints about mobs, etc.
