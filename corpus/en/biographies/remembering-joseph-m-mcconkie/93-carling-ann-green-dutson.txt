Carling, Ann Green Dutson

(October 3, 1799-July 16, 1893)

Set apart as a midwife by the Prophet; told to administer herbs.

While living in Nauvoo, the Prophet Joseph Smith laid his hands on Ann's head and set her apart as a midwife, telling her that she would be successful in caring for the sick if she would use herbs exclusively in her work. Some years later in Utah she became known as the "herb doctor." She had an herb garden and prepared her own tea and medicine.
