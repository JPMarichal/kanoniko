Workman, Oliver

(January 7, 1827-July 28, 1904)

Oliver Workman was baptized by Joseph Smith; he also saw Brigham Young transfigured.

Elder Oliver G. Workman said he was an eye-witness of many of the transactions related this evening [i.e., the transfiguration of Brigham Young on Aug. 8, 1844], and could faithfully bear testimony that Joseph Smith was a Prophet of God. He was baptized with fifteen others at Nauvoo by the Prophet Joseph personally. He was also present when the mantle of Joseph fell upon Brigham Young, and joins his testimony with those previously given as to the miraculous manifestation. He had stood up for the life of Joseph Smith when his life was at stake.

Collected Discourses, edited by Brian H. Stuy, 5 vols. (Burbank, California, and Woodland Hills, Utah: B. H. S. Publishing, 1987-92), 5:35.
