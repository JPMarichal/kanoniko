Moses, Julian

(April 11, 1810-April 12, 1892)

Gave the Prophet $500.

Parting with his parents and the rest of the family, excepting his brother James, who accompanied him, he went to Kirtland, Ohio, and there met the Prophet Joseph Smith. Each of the young men had five hundred dollars in cash, the sum total of their world wealth, and this they gave freely to the Prophet, who was in financial straits at the time.

"Julian Moses," in Orson F. Whitney, History of Utah, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: G. Q. Cannon & Sons, Co., 1904), 4:325.
