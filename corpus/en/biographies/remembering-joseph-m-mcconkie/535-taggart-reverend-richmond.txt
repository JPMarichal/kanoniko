Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Taggart, Reverend Richmond

(birth and death dates unavailable)

Taggart writes to a fellow pastor, complaining that Joseph Smith said he saw Jesus and the Apostles. Taggart wrote from Cleveland.

The following curious occurrence occurred last week in Newburg about 6 miles from this Place. Joe Smith the great Mormonosity was there and held forth, and among other things he told them he had seen Jesus Christ and the Apostles and conversed with them , and that he could perform miracles. Now there is in that place an idiot man who brought himself into this situation years ago by drinking he gets nothing [illegible] at present. [A] certain gentleman told Joe that if he would cure this man they would all become Mormonites. They plied him so hard, that he had literally to run away to another Village. I am impressed with this truth, . . . [sic] that the Regular Baptists have as good [illegible] as any Denomination before the people of this State.

"Rev. Richmond Taggart to Rev. R. Goings, March 2, 1833," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah. Spelling and punctuation have been modernized.
