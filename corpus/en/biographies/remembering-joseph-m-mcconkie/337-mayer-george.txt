Mayer, George

(March 2, 1805-July 24, 1896)

Bodyguard for the Prophet.

He was distinctly honored as one of the Bodyguards of the Prophet Joseph Smith.

After the Martyrdom of the Prophet Joseph, he moved with his family to Nauvoo.

"A Sketch of the Life of George Mayer," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
