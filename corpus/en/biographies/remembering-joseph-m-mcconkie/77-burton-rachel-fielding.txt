Burton, Rachel Fielding

(June 27, 1839-July 20, 1914)

Joseph romped and played with the children; Joseph and Hyrum murdered on her fifth birthday.

I was born in Preston, Lancashire, England, 27 June 1839. My father was Joseph Fielding and my mother's name, Hannah Greenwood.

. . . I remember once we went to meeting and afterward went home with Uncle Hyrum Smith's family. The children, our cousins, had a little wagon and we had a nice time with it for a while. Then we went upstairs and had a good time with a rocking horse. In some way in our play I fell down stairs and I yet have a scar, it left on the back of my head.

Uncle Joseph Smith (the Prophet) used to enjoy a romp with the children and he played with us many times.

I remember that we had persecutors who killed my Uncle Hyrum and Brother Joseph, the Prophet, the day I was five years old. I well remember the sorrow and the excitement at that time.

"Autobiography of Rachel Fielding Burton," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
