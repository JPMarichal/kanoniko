Stevens, Lyman

(February 7, 1812-April 18, 1886)

Ordained an elder by Joseph Smith; in 1833 he was sent by Joseph to preside over branches in Missouri; in 1834 he was called to preach again; in 1835 he was sent by Joseph on another mission; in 1836 he was sent back to Missouri.

" . . . I received the gospel March 14th 1831 at the age of 19. In 1832 I was ordained a Priest and sent on a mission to preach in the counties of Lake Erie. I remained there till the summer of 1833. Whilst on the mission I was ordained an Elder under the hands of Joseph Smith Jr. In the month of August 1833 I received a letter from the Prophet Joseph Smith to take charge and lead up four branches of the Church to Jackson County, Missouri, where we arrived in October and was expelled by a mob 10 days after our arrival. In the Spring '34 I was called upon by Parley P. Pratt & Lyman Wight to return to Kirtland preaching by the way. when I arrived safely [?] and remained there till 1835, when I was called & sent on a Mission by the Prophet Joseph to the southern part of Ohio. I filled the mission and returned to Kirtland in the winter following. And was married in January 21 1836. In May following was called by Joseph Smith Jr., to go to Missouri in Company with the Saints from Kirtland as President, and arrived in Caldwell County all well. And remained there until the Saints was driven out of Missouri, to Illinois. Then I was appoint to preside over a branch at Bear Creek County. About that time I was ordained into the second Quorum of Seventies . . ."

"History of Lyman Stevens," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
