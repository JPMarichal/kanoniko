Butterfield, Abel

(December 1, 1881-February 26, 1886)

Worked for Joseph Smith; says Joseph a good man; heard him testify of Moroni's coming and of the trials he endured.

[In a letter written by his brother to his mother, Abel adds these lines of his own:] Dear Mother I improve this opportunity to inform you of my health which is good and has been for the most part of the time since I left Wilton. I have been able to earn twelve dollars a month all winter. I went to work for Joseph Smith jr. immediately after I got to this place. I should be very glad to see you and all the rest of my friends and acquaintances. I am well satisfied with the place and the work which I have imbraced [sic]. I know it is the work of the Lord and it will stand when the heavens and the earth pass away. I have enjoyed my mind [?] quite well the past winter and still feel determined to love the Lord and keep his commandments for in keeping them I find great reward. Joseph Smith is a good man of God and sets good examples and gives instructions. I have heard him tell how he was called to bring forth the book of Mormon and how he has passed through his trials before magistrates I have heard him say preach prophesy. I can say of truth that I have not seen anything but what is according to the Bible. Give my love to Prudence and tell her to be faithful and trust in the Lord and all the rest of the family. Give my respects to Capt. Ditson to his wife, to John, to Sally and the rest of the family. . . .

Your affectionate son

Abel Butterfield

"Jacob Kemp Butterfield to Persis Butterfield, May 10, 1837," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
