Isom, Alice Parker

(January 8, 1843 [or 1848]-August 6, 1924)

Her mother knew the Prophet; heard him preach; saw martyred Prophet and Patriarch.

Mother was in Nauvoo over two years before father came. She suffered with the fever and ague the second year. She knew the Prophet, had heard his sermons and had seen both he and his brother when dead. Father didn't reach Nauvoo until after the Martyrdom.

"Memoirs of Alice Parker Isom," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah, 2.
