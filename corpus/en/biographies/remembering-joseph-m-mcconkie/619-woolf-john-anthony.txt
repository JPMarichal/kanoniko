Woolf, John Anthony

(July 31, 1895-November 7, 1881)

Purchased the farm next to Joseph's in Nauvoo.

Joined the Church in July 1841 and moved to Nauvoo, Ill. in 1844. Here he purchased a large farm adjoining that of the Prophet Joseph's.

"John Anthony Woolf Sr.," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
