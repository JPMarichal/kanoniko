Oaks, Hyrum

(b. 1824)

Young Oaks served as one of Joseph's Bodyguards and lived in Joseph's home for one year.

As a young man of about 20, Hyrum Oaks was among those who served as bodyguards for the Prophet Joseph Smith, whom he loved dearly. According to his obituary, "For one year he lived with the family of the Prophet Joseph Smith, during which time he worked on the temple at Nauvoo." He helped dig the well for the temple. He was working in the pineries of Wisconsin, getting out timber for the temple, when the Prophet Joseph Smith was murdered.

Linda M. Dursteler and Dallin Oaks, "The William Hyrum Oaks and Janett Bethers Family," LDS Family History Library, Salt Lake City, Utah, 7.
