Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

King, Culbert

(January 31, 1836-October 27, 1909)

Culbert's parents, Thomas R. and Matilda Robison King, hid the Prophet from the mobs; also remembers the gloom following the martyrdom.

[King's] parents joined the Church and moved to Illinois, where they became well acquainted with the Prophet Joseph Smith, and often sheltered him when he was being persecuted and well remembers his striking personality and genial manner. He also remembers the gloom and oppression that settled upon the Saints, at the time of the Prophet's death, and beheld with a feeling of awe the reverence, the bodies of the martyr's as they lay in state.

Untitled Manuscript, Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
