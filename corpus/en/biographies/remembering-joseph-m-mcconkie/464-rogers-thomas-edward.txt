Rogers, Thomas Edward

(October 15, 1827-September 16, 1896)

Intimate friend of Joseph; ate and slept with him.

In June 1840 Thomas was baptized by A. L. Lamoreaux. They passed through many of the persecutions in those trying days. Moving from Canada to Kirtland they attended the dedication of the first temple built by the Mormon Church.

The family were in Nauvoo at the time of the martyrdom of Joseph and Hyrum Smith, in 1844.

They had been very intimate with the Prophet and it afforded Thomas a great deal of pleasure in after years to tell of eating and sleeping with the Prophet Joseph.

"Thomas Rogers," 11 November 1915, Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.

Tells of close family friendship with the Prophet, and of seeing dead martyr's bodies.

Our family was very intimate with the Prophet Joseph Smith, and it afforded me a great deal of pleasure, in after years, to tell of the experiences I had in sharing a bed with the Prophet himself, when I was a boy. I was in Nauvoo at the time of the martyrdom of Joseph and Hyrum Smith. I can remember looking through the window and seeing them lying in their coffins and filing past their caskets for the last time, and the sorrow we felt as we gazed at these two wonderful men.

"The Minuteman," in Kate B. Carter, comp. Our Pioneer Heritage, 20 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Daughters of the Utah Pioneers, 1958-77), 10:95.
