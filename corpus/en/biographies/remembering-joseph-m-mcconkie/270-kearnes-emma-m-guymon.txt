Kearnes, Emma M. Guymon

(July 8, 1842-May 31, 1931)

As a baby, blessed by Joseph.

Born in Nauvoo of Mormon parents. Was blessed by Joseph Smith, Prophet of the Church and an intimate friends of father's. He named me for his wife, Emma, and made me a gift of a silver comb. (Father told me this.) Mother died when I was two years old.

"A Brief History of Emma M. Guymon Kearnes," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
