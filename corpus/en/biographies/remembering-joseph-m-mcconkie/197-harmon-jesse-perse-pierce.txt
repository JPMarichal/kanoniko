Harmon, Jesse Perse [Pierce]

(August 11, 1795-December 24, 1877)

Jesse Pierce Harmon a policeman and a bodyguard of Joseph Smith; Joseph promised none would be hurt in destruction of Nauvoo Expositor, and none were.

He [i.e., Uncle Appleton] was baptized in Nauvoo. He and grandfather [Jesse Perse (Pierce) Harmon] were both very active in church and civic affairs. They were policemen and grandfather was a bodyguard of the Prophet Joseph Smith. He was also a major and later a colonel in the Nauvoo Legion. When the 'Nauvoo Expositor,' a paper, was issued by the enemies of the Church, was printed, the City Council declared it a misdemeanor and ordered the police to destroy it. The Prophet promised them that not one of them would be harmed, although it caused great excitement and many threats from the mob, no one was injured.

"A Sketch of the Life of Ansil Perse Harmon by Jane Harmon Pratt (a daughter of Ansel Perse Harmon)," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
