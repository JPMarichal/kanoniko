Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Bruce, Eli

(November 8, 1793-September 24, 1832)

Had a conversation with Joseph Smith Sr., who told him of Joseph's prophetic call and of the Book of Mormon.

Nov. 5 [1830] . . . Had a long talk with the father of the Smith, who, according to the old man's account, is the particular favorite of Heaven! To him Heaven has vouchsafed to reveal its mysteries; he is the herald of the latter-day glory. The old man avers that he is commissioned by God to baptize and preach this new doctrine. He says that our Bible is much abridged and deficient; that soon the Divine will is to be made known to all, as written in the new Bible, or Book of Mormon.

Rob Morris, The Biography of Eli Bruce, Sheriff of Niagara County, New York (Louisville, Ky.: Morris & Monsarrat, 1861), 266-67.

Note: Eli Bruce, former Sheriff of Niagara County, was confined to the Canandaigua Jail at the same time that Joseph Smith Sr., the Prophet's father, was confined because of his inability to pay a debt. The above quotation, taken from Bruce's journal, tells of a conversation he had with the father of the Prophet while in jail together. For Lucy Mack smith's account of the incidents surrounding her husband's imprisonment, see her History of Joseph Smith (Salt Lake City, Utah: Bookcraft, 1958), 179-86.
