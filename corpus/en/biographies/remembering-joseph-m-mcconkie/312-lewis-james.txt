Lewis, James

(January 21, 1814-May 20, 1898)

Met Joseph; heard of martyrdom while on mission in Maine.

[Baptized in Keokuk, Iowa, February 1842] Went to Nauvoo and became acquainted with the Prophet Joseph Smith and the leading men of the Church.

In 1843 traveled with Elder George J. Adams to the Eastern States to gather monies for the Church. Was in the state of Maine when the news came of the martyrdom of the Prophet Joseph Smith. In 1844 returned to St. Louis, Missouri and found employment with the publishing house of Chat Keimle.

"Autobiography of James Lewis," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
