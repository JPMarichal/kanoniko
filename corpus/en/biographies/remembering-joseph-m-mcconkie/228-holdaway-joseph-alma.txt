Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Holdaway, Joseph Alma

(b. March 9, 1832)

Remembers Joseph as a graceful horse rider.

He remembered having seen the Prophet Joseph Smith when a small boy and of noting his graceful poise as he sat on horseback and that is where he might be expected to admire a man as he was always an admirer of horses and though he lost most of his Southern dialect he always said ho's.

"Autobiography of Joseph Alma Holdaway," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
