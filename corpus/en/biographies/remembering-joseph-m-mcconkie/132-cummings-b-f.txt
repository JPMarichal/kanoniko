Cummings, B. F.

(1855-1918)

Sees manuscript of JST, on which Joseph wrote that the Song of Solomon was not inspired.

[Cummings tells of working at Mission Office in Independence, and getting a call from his friend, Frederick M. Smith, who showed him the manuscript of the Joseph Smith Translation. Then:]

I was permitted to handle the manuscript and scan its pages, but of course anything like a critical examination of it was out of the question within the time at my disposal. As I was turning its leaves I came to a page on which was written in a bold hand and large letters, bolder and larger than the rest of the writing on that page, this sentence, which, unless memory is at fault, I here reproduce verbatim: "The song of Solomon is not inspired writing."

B. F. Cummings, "The Prophet's Last Letters," Improvement Era 18, no. 5 (March 1915): 388; also cited in Robert J. Matthews, A Plainer Translation (Provo, Utah: BYU Press, 1975), 87.
