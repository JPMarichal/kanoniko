Garn, Daniel

(December 13, 1802-April 20, 1872)

Served as bodyguard to Prophet; at Carthage Jail when Joseph and Hyrum murdered.

Daniel Garn was born December 13, 1802 at Mt. Clair, Redford County, Pa., the son of John and Susannah Pringle Garn.

He was with the Saints from the beginning of the Church, being the 35th man to be baptized. He was baptized by Hyrum Smith and confirmed by Peter Whitmer. He was prominent in the activities of the Church in Nauvoo. He was a body-guard to the Prophet Joseph Smith and was at the jail when the Prophet was martyred . . .
