Smith, Israel A.

(February 2, 1876-June 14, 1958)

Emma escaped from Missouri with the Inspired Version manuscript under her skirt.

During the troublesome times in Missouri, my grandmother was forced to take her children and go across the state of Missouri to find refuge in Illinois, and she carried this manuscript concealed in her dress. In those days, the women wore voluminous skirts. Grandmother had a belt or girdle made with this manuscript of the Inspired Version suspended from it.

Israel A. Smith, "The Inspired Version," Saints' Herald (14 November 1955): 7; also cited in Robert J. Matthews, A Plainer Translation (Provo, Utah: BYU Press, 1975), 100.
