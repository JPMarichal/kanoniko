Mack, Almira

(April 28, 1805-March 10, 1886)

Confirmed by Joseph Smith

[August 1830]

News: July 23, 1884. "Sister Alvira Covey [Mack] is probably the oldest living member of the Church in the Territory. She was baptized by David Whitmer in August 1830 and was confirmed by her cousin, the Prophet Joseph Smith."
