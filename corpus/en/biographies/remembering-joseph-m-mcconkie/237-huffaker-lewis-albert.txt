Huffaker, Lewis Albert

(March 9, 1841-June 15, 1918)

As youngster saw mob ridicule by "baptizing in the name of Joe Smith"; heard Joseph preach; saw Hyrum's blood on floor of Carthage Jail.

He was with the children who went to the River when the mob came to take possession of the City of Nauvoo. Remembers the occasion when the mob robbed the wagon and baptized some of the brethren in the name of Joe Smith by throwing them in the river. . . . Remembers seeing and hearing Joseph Smith preach also going through the Nauvoo Temple. Was taken by his Father to Carthage jail and saw the blood on the floor and one the well curb.

"Lewis Albert Huffaker," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
