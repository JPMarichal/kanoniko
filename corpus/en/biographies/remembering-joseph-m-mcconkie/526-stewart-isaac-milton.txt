Stewart, Isaac Milton

(March 14, 1815-ca. 1891)

Met Joseph and Hyrum.

When [Stewart] was fifteen he went to Illinois [from New Jersey] and later drifted to Nauvoo. He became acquainted with the Prophet Joseph Smith and Patriarch Hyrum Smith and in time joined the Church.

"Isaac Milton Stewart," in Kate B. Carter, Treasures of Pioneer History, 6 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: Daughters of the Utah Pioneers, 1952-57), 1:449.
