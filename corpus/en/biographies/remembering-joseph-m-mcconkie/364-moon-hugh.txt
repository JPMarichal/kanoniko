Moon, Hugh

(August 5, 1815-September 23, 1870)

Present at transfiguration of Brigham Young.

Hugh Moon was one of the bodyguards of Joseph Smith. He was present at the meeting when Brigham Young was transfigured, and saw and heard what took place. Joseph Smith spoke and said "He would throw the mantle on Brigham Young and that he would lead the people." Brigham Young then spoke to the people and they all thought it was Joseph smith speaking.

"The Book of the Life of Hugh Moon," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
