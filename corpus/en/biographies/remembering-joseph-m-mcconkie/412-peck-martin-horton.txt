Peck, Martin Horton

(May 27, 1806-June 17, 1884)

Upon hearing Joseph he was almost immediately converted; he was present at the transfiguration of Brigham Young.

Many times after he was heard to testify that before the Prophet had spoken five minutes he had received a thorough assurance of the Divinity of his mission, which testimony remained with him all his life. He was baptized in 1833 by Lyman E. Johnson.

. . . After the martyrdom of the Prophet Joseph Smith when the body of the church met to decide who would be the next President, Martin Peck was present at the 2:00 P. M. meeting Aug. 8, 1844 when the mantle of Joseph fell upon Brigham Young.

Vernice Gold Rosenthall, "Biography of Martin Horton Peck," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
