Wilson, Lewis Dunbar Sr.

(June 2, 1805-March 11, 1856)

Ordained an elder because of the counsel of Joseph Smith.

On the 18th of May 1836 he was baptized (about the same time his father and six brothers and their wives were also baptized,) in June following he was ordained to the office of a priest by Elder H. G. Sherwood; soon after he visited Kirtland, and by the counsel of Joseph Smith was ordained an Elder.

George Christian Wilson, "History of Lewis Dunbar Wilson," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
