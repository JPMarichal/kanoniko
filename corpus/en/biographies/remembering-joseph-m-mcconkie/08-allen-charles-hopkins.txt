Mark L. McConkie, Remembering Joseph (Salt Lake City: Deseret Book Company, 2003)

Allen, Charles Hopkins

(October 15, 1830-February 18, 1922 [or 1919])

Met Joseph in Kirtland; heard him speak in Nauvoo; saw Carthage after the martyrdom.

"The spirit of gathering came upon him and he sold his beautiful home for a low price and moved to Kirtland, Ohio, where he met the Prophet Joseph and the Saints. Dec. 31, 1837 another daughter Susan was born."

Untitled Manuscript, Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives.

Note: He later went to Nauvoo and heard the Prophet speak; visited Carthage after the martyrdom, and saw the blood stains on the floor, the ball hole in the doors, and "where the balls that had been shot into the room had knocked the ceiling off."
