Bessey, Anthony Wayne

(b. August 18, 1835)

Illustration of how the martyrdom changed someone's life plans.

The first eight years of his life were spent upon his father's farm, which in 1843 was sold, his parents, who were Latter-day Saints, intending to move to Nauvoo, Illinois. The Prophet's murder changed their plans, and purchasing a lot and building a house at Reading, Massachusetts, twelve miles from Boston, they remained there until 1856, when they again started for the West.

"Anthony Wayne Bessey," in Orson F. Whitney, History of Utah, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City: G. Q. Cannon & Sons Co., 1904), 4:408.
