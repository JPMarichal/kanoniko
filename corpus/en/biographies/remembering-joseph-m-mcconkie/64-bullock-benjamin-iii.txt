Bullock, Benjamin III

(birth and death dates unavailable)

Professor Mitchell recommended to Chandler that he take mummies to Joseph for translation; mummies had record of an Egyptian king, queen, prince, and slave.

[Page 2 says it was Professor Mitchell who recommended to Michael Chandler that he take the mummies to Joseph Smith in Kirtland, June 1835. Also says that it was Benjamin Bullock who took Michael Chandler the 250 miles from New York to Kirtland in his wagon to meet the Prophet Joseph and show him the mummies. Then:]

Springtime, 1833, duty paid at the custom house, New York, Mr. Chandler proceeded to open the coffins for the first time. Two rolls fell to the floor from the breasts of his mummies, which he hoped might be diamonds or other precious gems. "But imagine," he said, "my chagrin and disappointment to find it contained only papyrus or ancient writings, wrapped in linen and saturated in bitumen, same as the bodies." Speculation soared as to the personal of our mummified visitors, but the Prophet alone, through inspiration, could reveal these truths. He told part of his family, at least, one was an Egyptian King and one his Queen, and one was their son, a prince, and a slave. (This information I obtained through my grandfather John S. Fullmer, brother-in-law to the Prophet Joseph Smith).

Clara Fullmer Bullock, "Life Story of Benjamin Bullock, III," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
