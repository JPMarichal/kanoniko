Tippets, John H.

(September 5, 1810-February 14, 1890)

First meeting with Joseph; Joseph counsels Tippetts to stay in Kirtland and urges him to loan money to the Saints in Missouri.

I was married on Tuesday, and started on Wednesday morning for Kirtland, Ohio. We arrived in Kirtland on the 9th of December, 1834, where we rejoiced to see the Prophet Joseph Smith and the saints. We visited Brother Joseph this morning on the 9th of December. We presented our letters of recommendation. Had a counsel in the evening at candle light, it being winter season. We was counseled to stay in Kirtland until the spring of the year (1835) which we readily accepted. The church in Jackson County, Missouri, had been driven from their homes and broken up, and the church generally poor, and the printing office destroyed, Joseph wished us to loan our money to them, which we were very willing to do. Myself and a cousin loaned them $300.00, and paid a donation of $100.00 donated by my cousin Almah Tippets. We remained here through the winter, spring and summer. Obtained a great deal of good instruction. In July (1835, or 1834) my brother returned from Missouri to Kirtland, his being a member of Zion's Camp. Brother Joseph made a wedding and he was married to Caroline Tippets his cousin in the year 1835. Joseph our Prophet called a council, and instructed us to start on our journey to Clay County, Missouri. . . .

"John H. Tippets," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah, 2-3.
