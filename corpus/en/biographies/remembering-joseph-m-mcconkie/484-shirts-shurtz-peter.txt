Shirts [Shurtz], Peter

(August 23, 1808-1880)

Joseph blessed Don Carlos Shirts.

[Peter] was baptized into the L. D. S. Church August 15, 1833. He worked on the Kirtland Temple in 1835 and in that year received a special blessing from Martin Harris. It was on July 29th that my father, Don Carlos, was born at Kirtland, Ohio. Father was named and blessed by Joseph Smith.

Ambrose Shirts, "History of Peter Shirts (Shurtz) and His Descendants," 48; copies in private family possession.

Son blessed by Joseph Smith.

Had a son named Don Carlos, born in 1836, "he was also named and blessed by Joseph Smith."

"Peter Shirts (Shurtz)," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
