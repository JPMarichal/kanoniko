Sessions, Peregrine

(June 15, 1814-June 3, 1893)

One of Joseph's bodyguards.

Mr. Sessions was a member of the Nauvoo police force, and one of the bodyguard of the Prophet Joseph Smith.

"Perrigrine Sessions," in Orson F. Whitney, History of Utah, 4 vols. (Salt Lake City, Utah: G. Q. Cannon & Sons Co., 1904), 4:103.
