Kelsey, Eli Brazee

(October 27, 1819-May 26, 1885)

As a school teacher in Nauvoo, taught the Prophet's children.

He moved to Nauvoo April 1844 where he found employment teaching school here he taught the Prophets children and formed the acquaintance of the Prophet Joseph Smith and Hyrum Smith and was residing there when the two men were martyred. No doubt suffered along with the rest of the Saints, their troubles and persecutions.

"History of the Life of Eli Brazee Kelsey, as Written by Cecil Kelsey Mills of Magna, Utah, Granddaughter of Eli B. Kelsey and Letitia Sheets, His First Wife," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
