Angell, Polly Ann Johnson

(June 4, 1813-April 11, 1876 [or 1878])

Joseph said, "Sisters always first in good works." Sister Polly Angell, the wife of Truman O. Angell, the church architect, relates that she and a band of sisters were working on the "veils," one day, when the Prophet and Sidney Rigdon came in.

"Well, sisters," observed Joseph, "you are always on hand. The sisters always first and foremost in all good works. Mary was first at the resurrection; and the sisters now are the first to work on the inside of the temple."

Edward W. Tullidge, Woman of Mormondom (New York: Tullidge & Crandall, 1877), 76.
