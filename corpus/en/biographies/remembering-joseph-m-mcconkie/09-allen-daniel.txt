Allen, Daniel

(December 9, 1804-January 15, 1892)

Sold his farm and gave all proceeds to the Prophet to redeem Zion; later, bought two city lots in Kirtland from Joseph; lost money in the Kirtland bank failure.

I was Baptized with my wife into the Church of Jesus Christ in June 1834. I sold my farm in Hammburg for six hundred dollars in cash and let Joseph Smith have every dollar of it to redeem Zion by purchasing lands in Jackson County and in the regions round about according to Revelations. I moved to Kirtland Ohio in 1836. I bought two city lots of Joseph Smith for four hundred dollars and build a good frame house and shoe shop. I laid out one thousand $ [?] in Kirtland [scrip.] I was a share holder in the Kirtland Bank when it failed. I lost all my property and then hired a man to move me to Savanna about fifty miles south of Kirtland, where I went to work on the canal for fifty cents a day.

"Daniel Allen Journal," LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah. Spelling and grammar have been modernized.
