Groves, Elisha Hurd

(November 5, 1797-December 29, 1867)

Assigned by Joseph to attend Hebrew school at School of the Prophets.

[Baptized by Samuel Smith. Went to Missouri:] Where I went to the Salt River Branch of the Church where I remained ten (?) days until the arrival of the Prophet Joseph. I was organized in Zion's Camp by James Allred, Capt. of Ten and marched in company to Clay County.

. . . In the Spring of 1835 I got in company with Isaac Higby and we traveled to Kirtland Ohio and baptized 75 persons on our journey and arrived in Kirtland on the 11th of Aug. and worked on the Temple two months and then was called to take a short mission and raised up a small branch about 25 miles from Kirtland and baptized about 20 persons. The Branch was a few miles south of Cleveland and returned to Kirtland about the first of January 1836.

And went into an English grammar School for a few weeks. From there I was taken out by the Prophet Joseph and put into an Hebrew School and on the 19 of January by the counsel of Joseph I married Lucy Simmonds my present wife. . . .

"An Account of the Life of Elisha Hurd Groves," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
