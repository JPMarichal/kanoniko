Peirce, Eli Harvey

(July 29, 1829-August 12, 1858)

Baptized by the Prophet in 1842.

Eli Harvey Peirce, son of Robert and Hannah Peirce, born July 29, 1829 in Uweldon Township, Chester Co., Pennsylvaniamoved to Nauvoo with his brother in 1841; was baptized 27, 1842 by the prophet Joseph Smithreceived his endowments Jan. 24, 1846 in the Temple at Nauvoo.
