Burnham, Wallace Kendall

(January 24, 1838-June 17, 1913)

Remembered hearing Joseph preach; saw the bodies of Hyrum and Joseph after the martyrdom.

He was nearly six years old when the Prophet was martyred but remembered hearing him preach and seeing him and his brother Hyrum after they were brought from Carthage to Nauvoo.

Lydia Stanley Burnham, "Life Sketch of Wallace Kendall Burnham," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
