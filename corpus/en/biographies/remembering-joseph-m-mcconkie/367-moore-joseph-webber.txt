Moore, Joseph Webber

(June 1, 1833-July 24, 1914)

As a boy, met the Prophet; was present at the transfiguration of Brigham Young.

The subject of this sketch was born at Pamfrit, Chautaugua Co., N. Y. June 1st 1833. Was the son of Ethan Allan and Sarah Webber Moore. His parents became identified with the Latter-day Saints in the early thirties. He was baptized in 1841 when 8 years old. The family moved to Kirtland O. and started west in Kirtland Camp. He was acquainted with the Prophet when a boy. He was present at the meeting on the 8th of august when Brigham Young was transformed into the likeness of Joseph Smith. He crossed the plains in 1849 and made his first home in Toole Valley.

"Joseph Webber Moore," Mormon Biography File, LDS Church Archives, Salt Lake City, Utah.
