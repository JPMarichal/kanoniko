Zundel, Emma Maria

(August 28, 1838-June 18, 1926)

Joseph often visited her father's home; Joseph and Hyrum administered to her small brother; she saw Joseph and Hyrum in their caskets.

My father had a beautiful farm, house, and lot [in Nauvoo]. I remember hearing the Prophet Joseph Smith preach. He often came to my father's home in Nauvoo. Father taught Joseph Smith to speak German. The Prophet Joseph Smith and Patriarch Hyrum Smith both came to administer to my small brother Jacob when he was sick unto death. I was at the funeral of Joseph and Hyrum in the Nauvoo Mansion and saw them in their caskets, after their martyrdom. I joined the L.D.S. Church when I was eight years old, was baptized by Benjamin Clapp, and I think he confirmed me, also.

"History of Emma Maria Zundel (1836-1926)," Glen F. Harding, "A Record of the Ancestry and Descendants of John Jacob Zundel, Known as Jacob Zundel," LDS Family History Library, Salt Lake City, Utah, 154.
