Alvord, Sally

(October 8, 1795-December 29, 1866)

As a young girl attended school with the Prophet and thought him possessed of "ordinary ability."

Cenius H. Engle of Hartford, Mich., son of Sally Alvord, recalls that his mother said that on one occasion during the War of 1812, a number of soldiers left baggage at her father's house on a march to Canada, saying that, if they never returned, he might have the things, that her father spent one winter lumbering in Canada and returned in the spring without one cent, the company having failed, also that she attended school with Joseph Smith when a child, and wondered that a boy of such ordinary ability could become the founder of a power religious sect.

Samuel Morgan Alvord, A Genealogy of the Descendants of Alexander Alvord, An Early Settler of Windsor, Conn. and Northampton, Mass. (Webster, New York: A. D. Andrews, Printer, 1908), 132.
