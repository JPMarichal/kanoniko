Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 1 ( 1901)

Austin, Philo Wells

Austin, Philo Wells, Bishop of Dayton Ward, Oneida county, Idaho, was born July 21, 1866, in Centerville, Davis county, Utah. He was baptized June 20, 1875, and ordained successively to the offices of Deacon, Teacher and Elder. The latter ordination he received from Bishop Wm. H. Lewis, at Lewiston, Cache county. Later he was ordained a Seventy by H. M. Rawlins, and he was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 23, 1893, by Joseph F. Smith, and set apart to act as Bishop of Dayton Ward, in the Oneida Stake of Zion. This position he is occupying at the present time.
