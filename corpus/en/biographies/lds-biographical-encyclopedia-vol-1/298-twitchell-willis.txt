Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 1 ( 1901)

Twitchell, Willis

Twitchell, Willis, Bishop of the Manila Ward, in the Woodruff Stake of Zion, was born at Beaver city, Utah, July 11, 1860. He lived with his father on his farm till he was 25 years of age, when he married Rachel A. Potter. He was called to act as superintendent of the Sunday school on Indian Creek, Beaver county, in 1889, which position he held till 1898, when he moved to Lucern valley, where he was chosen superintendent of the Sabbath school. He held that office about one year, or until he was ordained a High Priest and Bishop and set to preside over the Manila Ward in the Woodruff Stake of Zion. Before the organization of the Manila Ward, there was a branch organization in that locality over which Elder Twitchell presided.
