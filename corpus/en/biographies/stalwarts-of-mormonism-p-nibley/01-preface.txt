Preston Nibley, Stalwarts of Mormonism ( 1954)

PREFACE

This volume contains brief sketches of the lives of a number of men and women who were prominent during the early days of the Church in Ohio, Missouri, Illinois and Utah. These people accepted the gospel as it was revealed to the Prophet Joseph Smith and they endeavored to conform their lives to its teachings. They labored without faltering to establish the Church and Kingdom, to build up Zion and "make the desert blossom as a rose." We honor their worthy example and express our gratitude for the service they rendered.

The Author.
