# Darwin B. Christenson

## Biographical Summary

- Born 1935 Firth, Idaho
- Baptized as a child; Aaronic Priesthood as a youth; Melchizedek Priesthood as a young man
- Mission to Brazil 1956-1959
- Married Sandra Jolene Lyon 1962, Idaho Falls Temple; four children
- Bishop; High Councilor; Counselor to Stake President, Temple Recorder
- President of Brazil Sao Paulo North Mission 1981-1984
- Second Quorum of Seventy 2000-2005
