# H. Verlan Andersen

## Biographical Summary

- Born 1914 Logan, Utah
- Baptized as a child; Aaronic Priesthood as a youth; Melchizedek Priesthood as a young man
- Mission to North Central States
- Married Shirley Hoyt 1943; Salt Lake Temple; eleven children
- Bishop, High Councilor, Counselor to Stake President, Patriarch
- Mission (with wife) to Argentina and Peru
- First Quorum of Seventy 1986-1989
- Second Quorum of Seventy 1989-1991
- Died 1992 Orem, Utah
