# George P. Lee

## Biographical Summary

- Born 1943; Towaoc, Colorado.
- Baptized as a child.
- Missionary to Navajo Reservation.
- Married Katherine Hettick; three children.
- First Quorum of the Seventy 1975-1989.
- Excommunicated 1989.
- Died 2010; Provo, Utah.
