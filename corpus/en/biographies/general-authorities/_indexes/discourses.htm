Grampa Bill's General Authority Pages
Discourses and Writings
    General Authorities have many reponsibilities; among them, Administrative, Judicial, and Ambassadorial. But perhaps the most time consuming is counseling the Saints and witnessing to the World through orations and writings.

    This page contains a linked listing of a number of General Authorities whose works are linked in this web site. Included are every Apostle ordained from 1900 forward. Click on the person of choice, then navigate to the bottom of his page for a listing of links to selected discourses and writings. Some of these Brethren have over a hundred talks and writings linked.

    Come back often. We expect to expand this page significantly.
