# Stayner Richards

## Biographical Summary

- Born 1885, Salt Lake City, Utah
- Baptized 1898
- Ordained Elder 1908
- Mission to Great Britain 1908-1910
- Married Jane Foote Taylor 1911; six children
- Ordained High Priest 1914
- Sustained Assistant to the Twelve 1951
- Died 1953 Salt Lake City, Utah
