Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Alley, Emma Turner

Alley, Emma Turner, wife of Stephen Webb Alley, was born Jan. 5, 1845, in Sheffield, England, the daughter of Geo. Turner and Hannah Walles. She was baptized in England when about nine years of age and emigrated to America in 1868, crossing the Atlantic in the ship "John Bright", which sailed from Liverpool, England, June 4th, and arrived in New York July 13, 1868. Continuing the journey by rail as far as Laramie City, on the Union Pacific Railway, she left that place July 27, 1868, in John R. Murdock's mule train which arrived in the Valley Aug. 19, 1868. About six weeks after her arrival in the Valley, on Oct. 3, 1868, she married Stephen W. Alley, by whom she became the mother of six children. Sister Alley has spent her time principally in her home, raising her children in the ways of the Lord. She has always been a most devoted wife to her husband with whom she is still living very happily in the Thirty-first Ward, Salt Lake City.
