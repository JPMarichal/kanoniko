Nielsen, Hans William

Nielsen, Hans William, the first presiding Elder at Axtell, Sanpete county, Utah, was born April 12, 1846, at Skovby, Falster, Denmark, the son of Rasmus Nielsen and Caroline Hansen. He was baptized in 1860 by Niels Christian Heiselt; ordained a Deacon in 1861; ordained a Priest in 1863 and ordained an Elder by Sven J. Jonassen. He labored nearly three years as a local missionary on the island of Fyen, until 1867 when he went to Germany, where he sought and found employment and also did considerable missionary labor in a local way. He emigrated to Utah in 1877 and settled at Oak Creek. Millard county, where his parents, who had preceded him to Utah, already lived. After that he resided a short time at Scipio and later at Leamington, and he finally moved to Axtell in 1906.
