Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Higgins, Mahala Baines

Higgins, Mahala Baines, wife of James Higgins, was born Nov. 7, 1827, in England, the daughter of John Baines and Hannah Steadman. She was baptized in her native land, married James Higgins in 1853 and emigrated with him to America in 1862. As a resident of West Jordan Ward and afterwards of the East Jordan Ward, she has taken a leading part in Relief Society work and throughout been a zealous Church worker. Since 1904, when her husband died, she has earned her own living.
