Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Irons, John Wilbert

Irons, John Wilbert, Bishop of Moroni, Sanpete county, Utah, was born Nov. 21, 1823, in New Jersey, the son of John W. Irons and Hester Applegate. He was raised on a farm and became a Latter-day Saint in 1860. In 1863 he came to Utah, crossing the plains in Capt. Wm. B. Preston's ox train. After spending the winter in Salt Lake City he settled in Moroni, Sanpete county, in the spring of 1864 and resided there until the time of his death. He took an active part in the Black Hawk war as captain of a company of infantry. In 1877 he was appointed Bishop of Moroni, which position he filled until his demise at that place Sept. 17, 1901. With Deborah P. Lippincott, whom he married April 24, 1844, he had four children.
