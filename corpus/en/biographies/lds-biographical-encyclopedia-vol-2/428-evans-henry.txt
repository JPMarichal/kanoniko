Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Evans, Henry

Evans, Henry, a Patriarch in the Summit Stake of Zion, Utah, was born Oct. 25, 1830, in Richland county, Ohio, the son of David Evans and Mary Beck. He was baptized when about eight years old by Rosel Hyde and ordained successively to the offices of Deacon, Elder, Seventy, High Priest and Patriarch, the latter ordination taking place in 1900, by John Henry Smith. Brother Evans acted as Sunday school teacher, Ward teacher, home missionary, second counselor in the Bishopric of the Coalville Ward, member of the High Council, etc. In 1854-56 he filled a mission to the Ute Indians. He emigrated to Utah in 1850; lived in Salt Lake and Utah counties fifteen years, and located permanently in Coalville, Summit county, in 1865, following farming and stock raising as his principal avocations. In the interest of his fellow-citizens he served thirteen years as county treasurer, and was also city assessor and collector, road supervisor, etc. Bro. Evans married two wives, one in 1856 and one in 1858, by whom he became the father of twelve children.
