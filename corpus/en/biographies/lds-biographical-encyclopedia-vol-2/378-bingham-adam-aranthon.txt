Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Bingham, Adam Aranthon

Bingham, Adam Aranthon, Bishop of Riverdale Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, was born Nov. 14, 1865, at Riverdale, Weber county, Utah, the son of Sanford Bingham and Agnes Fife. He was baptized Sept. 6, 1874, by Wm. Stimpson; ordained a Deacon Jan. 15, 1883, by John Russell; ordained a Teacher and later a Priest; ordained an Elder Oct. 24, 1889, by Sanford Bingham, and ordained a High Priest Jan. 20, 1902, by Hyrum M. Smith; filled a mission of 26 months in Colorado in 1897-1900, presiding a part of the time over the West Colorado conference. At home he has labored as president of Y. M. M. I. A. Ward teacher, Sunday school teacher and home missionary. In 1889 he married Anne Stratton, by whom he has had seven children, two sons and five daughters. Bishop Bingham is a farmer by occupation and has always resided in Riverdale.
