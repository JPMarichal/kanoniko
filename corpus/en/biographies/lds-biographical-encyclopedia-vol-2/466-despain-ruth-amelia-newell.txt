Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Despain, Ruth Amelia Newell

Despain, Ruth Amelia Newell, wife of Solomon J. Despain, was born Sept. 22, 1822, in the State of New York, the daughter of Asael Newell and Elizabeth Bushnell. She joined the Church in 1852, being baptized by John Sharp. Shortly afterwards she moved with her parents to Calhoun county, Illinois, and while there she met and married Solomon J. Despain, with whom she emigrated to Utah in 1861 in David H. Cannon's company. They settled in the Granite Ward, where Sister Despain subsequently acted as president of the Relief Society from 1877 to 1896. She was the mother of eleven children, seven boys and four girls. She died Aug. 20, 1901, at Granite.
