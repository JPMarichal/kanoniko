Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Hall, Elizabeth Thorne

Hall, Elizabeth Thorne, wife of Timothy Hall, was born about 1839 in Bedfordshire, England, the daughter of George Thorne and Mary Dowman Luton. She joined the Church in her native land, emigrated to Utah in 1864 and was married to Timothy Hall in March, 1865, after bearing her husband ten children, four of whom are now living. She died in Salt Lake City July 25, 1910.
