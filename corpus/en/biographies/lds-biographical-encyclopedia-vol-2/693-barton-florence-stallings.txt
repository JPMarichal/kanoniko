Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Barton, Florence Stallings

Barton, Florence Stallings, wife of William H. Barton, was born July 22, 1851, in East Mill Creek, Salt Lake county, Utah, the daughter of Joseph Stallings and Caroline Hardford. In 1868 (Nov. 7th) she was married to Brother Barton and became the mother of five children. For a number of years Sister Barton has taken an active part in the Granger Ward Relief Society, devoting her labors especially to the sick among whom she has performed a great work.
