Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Forbush, Loren Ezra

Forbush, Loren Ezra, an Elder in the Union Ward, Salt Lake county, Utah, was born Sept. 7, 1846, in Council Bluffs, Iowa, the son of Rufus Forbush and Sarah Beckstead. Rufus Forbush sen. was born in Massachusetts June 3, 1818, and died in Union, Salt Lake county, Utah, May 30, 1901. His wife was born Nov. 1823, in Canada and died while crossing the plains July 26, 1852. At the age of six Loren E. emigrated with his parents to Utah, crossing the plains in Capt. Jolley's company which arrived in Salt Lake City Sept. 7, 1852. He settled in the Union Ward where he has lived ever since. Here he has assisted materially in the great irrigation system and also in clearing the land for cultivation. In 1866 he took part in the Black Hawk war in Sanpete and had some very narrow escapes, one, especially, in the Thistle Valley skirmish with the Indians. In 1868 (Jan. 4th) he married Isabella Brimhall, who became the mother of eight children. Feb. 28, 1886, he married Catherine McCay Milner, who became the mother of six children. Bro. Forbush was ordained a Seventy many years ago.
