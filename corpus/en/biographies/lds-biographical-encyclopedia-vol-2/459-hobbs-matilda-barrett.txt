Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Hobbs, Matilda Barrett

Hobbs, Matilda Barrett, wife of William Hobbs, was born May 28, 1843, at Pontepool, Monmouthshire, England, the daughter of Wm. Barrett and Phoebe Coburn. She was baptized in 1853 by Henry Thomas and emigrated to America in 1859, crossing the Atlantic in the ship "Wm. Tapscott". On board this vessel she met her future husband for the first time. She crossed the plains in George Rowley's handcart company, helping to pull a cart all the way and fording the streams and rivers. When she arrived in Great Salt Lake City, she was very much fatigued after the long and tiresome journey. She lived temporarily in the Ninth Ward at the home of Bishop Woolley. Oct. 3, 1860, she was married to Wm. Hobbs, to whom she has borne ten children, and proven herself a faithful wife and a firm Latter-day Saint. Her parents joined the Church in England and came to Utah in 1875, settling at Provo, where they lived until their death. Her father died in 1883 and her mother in 1899.
