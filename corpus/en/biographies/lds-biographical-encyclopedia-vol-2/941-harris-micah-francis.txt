Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Harris, Micah Francis

Harris, Micah Francis, Bishop of the Henefer Ward, Summit co., Utah, was born Aug. 3, 1848, in Monmouthshire, England, the son of Thomas Harris and Ann Williams. He was baptized in December, 1866, at Henefer; ordained a Teacher in 1867; ordained an Elder in 1868; ordained a Seventy in 1876 by Joseph Young and set apart for a mission by Orson Pratt. He was ordained a High Priest in 1889 by William W. Cluff and ordained a Bishop May 25, 1901, by Apostle Reed Smoot. Otherwise he has acted as a Ward teacher, first counselor to Bishop John C. Paskett, of the Henefer Ward, president of Y. M. M. I. A., member of the Summit Stake High Council and home missionary. He also filled a mission to the States in 1876, under the direction of James A. Little, laboring in Iowa and Nebraska. As a public officer in secular life he has served as constable, fence viewer, president of the Henefer Irrigation & Canal Company, etc. His principal occupation has been farming and stock raising. During the Walker and Black Hawk Indian wars he was an active member of the home militia. In 1868 (Jan. 15th) he married Mary Jane Bond, who has borne him eleven children.
