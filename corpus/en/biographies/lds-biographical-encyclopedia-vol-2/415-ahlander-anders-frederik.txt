Ahlander, Anders Frederik

Ahlander, Anders Frederik, an active Elder in Provo, Utah Stake, Utah, was born Sept. 13, 1856, at Krogstad parish, Bohus lÃ¤n, Sweden, the son of Jonas Ahlander and Bretta M. Jacobsen. He was baptized March 10, 1877, by Elder Andreas Peterson, ordained a Deacon soon afterwards, and a Priest and an Elder still later. He emigrated to Utah in 1886 and located in Ogden, where he resided until 1889, when he moved to Riverdale, Weber county, remaining there until 1895, when he became a permanent resident of Provo, Utah county. He was ordained a Seventy Oct. 27, 1899, and in 1899-1901 he filled a mission to Scandinavia, laboring in the Christiania conference, Norway. At home Elder Ahlander has always been a faithful Church worker; thus he has acted as Ward teacher, president of a Ward Y. M. M. I. A., president of an Elders quorum and president over the Scandinavian meetings in Provo for a number of years. In 1879 he married Karen Johansen, who bore him four children, and in 1888 (March 5th), after his first wife's death, he married Ludovica A. Herman, who has borne him seven children. Bro. Ahlander is by trade a carriage-maker and blacksmith and is operating a business in those lines in Provo.
