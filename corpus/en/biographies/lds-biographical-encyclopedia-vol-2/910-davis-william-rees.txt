Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Davis, William Rees

Davis, William Rees, the second Bishop of Wales, Sanpete co., Utah, was born Aug. 13, 1869, at Wales, Sanpete co., the son of William Davis and Maria Rees. He was ordained a Deacon when about fifteen years old and acted as secretary of the Deacons quorum; subsequently he filled the position of secretary in the Ward Sabbath school and Y. M. M. I. A. He was ordained an Elder when 21 years of age and acted as secretary of the Elders quorum, was ordained a Seventy Oct. 23, 1896, by Geo. Reynolds in Salt Lake City, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1896-98, laboring as a traveling Elder in the Welsh conference. After his return home he renewed his labors in the interest of the Church, acting in the Sabbath school as superintendent until 1903, when he succeeded the late John E. Rees as Bishop of Wales, which position he still holds.
