Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Ferrin, Josiah Leaman

Ferrin, Josiah Leaman, first counselor to Bishop Henry J. Fuller, of the Eden Ward, Weber co., Utah, was born Sept. 26, 1856, in Ogden, Utah, the son of Josiah M. Ferrin and Martha A. Bronson. He was baptized by A. Moffett; ordained a Deacon by Wm. Halls Sept. 30, 1877; ordained an Elder Dec. 27, 1877, and ordained a High Priest in September, 1895. He married Ellen Dale Stallings Dec. 27, 1877.
