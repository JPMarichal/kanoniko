Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Madsen, Niels

Madsen, Niels, an active Elder in the Riverton Ward, Salt Lake co., Utah, was born January 18, 1853, at Meilby, Randers amt, Denmark, the son of Mads, Madsen and Ane Nielsen Simonsen. He was baptized March 3, 1886, came to Utah in November, 1886, and settled in Riverton. He married Mary Larsen, December 10, 1881, and engaged in farming at Riverton. In 1903 to 1905 he filled a successful mission to Scandinavia, laboring in the Aalborg conference. Brother Madsen is the father of seven children.
