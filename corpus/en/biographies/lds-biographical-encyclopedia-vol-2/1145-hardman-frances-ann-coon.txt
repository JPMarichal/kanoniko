Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Hardman, Frances Ann Coon

Hardman, Frances Ann Coon, wife of Lehi N. Hardman, was born Aug. 15, 1843, in Greene county, Illinois, the daughter of Abraham Coon and Elizabeth Yardbrough. She came to Utah with her parents in 1850 and was married to Lehi N. Hardman June 15, 1860, by whom she became the mother of eleven children, four sons and seven daughters. For many years Sister Hardman has been an active Relief Society worker.
