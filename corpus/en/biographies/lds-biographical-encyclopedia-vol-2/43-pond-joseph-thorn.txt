Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Pond, Joseph Thorn

Pond, Joseph Thorn, second counselor in the Stake Presidency of Bannock Stake, was born in Spanish Fork, Utah, Sept. 19, 1859, and is a son of Skillman Pond and Abigail Thorn. The family moved to Richmond, Utah, in the spring of 1860, where he was baptized at about eight years of age. He was married to Amanda Hendricks April, 1879, and settled in Lewiston, Utah, in 1883. Was ordained an Elder Dec. 18, 1898, by Bishop William H. Lewis; a Seventy by C. H. Monson in 1889; a High Priest and set apart as second counselor to Bishop Wm. Waddoups, in June, 1901, by Apostle Rudger Clawson, and acted in that capacity till March 20, 1903; was set apart as a High Councilor in Bannock Stake in May, 1903, by Prest. Lewis S. Pond and counselor to him in the Stake presidency in Bannock Stake by Apostle John W. Taylor; was ordained a Bishop under the hands of Apostle Charles W. Penrose Sept. 12, 1905, which important position he now holds in association with the Stake Presidency. In civil affairs he held the position of county commissioner of Cache county in 1895, and was elected to the Utah legislature in 1900. Bro. Pond has been in the mission field, having honorably filled an assignment to Kentucky from 1896 to 1899.
