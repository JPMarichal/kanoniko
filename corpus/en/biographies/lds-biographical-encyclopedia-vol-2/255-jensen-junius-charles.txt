Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Jensen, Junius Charles

Jensen, Junius Charles, a Bishop's counselor in Preston, Idaho, was born Jan. 21, 1877, at Preston, Idaho, the son of David Jensen and Julia K. Peterson. After having officiated as a Deacon and afterwards as an Elder, he was ordained a Seventy Feb. 17, 1899, by George Teasdale and ordained a High Priest Feb. 3, 1902, by Joseph S. Geddes; he filled a mission to the northern States in 1899-1901.
