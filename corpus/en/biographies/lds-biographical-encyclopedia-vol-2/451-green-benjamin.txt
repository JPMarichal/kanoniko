Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Green, Benjamin

Green, Benjamin, an active Church worker in the Draper Ward, Jordan Stake, Utah, was born July 24, 1814, at Sutton, near Ashfield, Nottinghamshire, England. His father's name was William Green and he learned the trade of a stockinger when quite young. He first heard the fulness of the gospel preached in 1849 and became a member of the Church by baptism, Feb. 7, 1850. He emigrated to America in 1854 and settled in Draper, where he followed the occupation of a farmer. Crossing the plains he acted as captain of ten under Capt. Wm. A. Empey. While a resident of Draper he labored very faithfully as president of the lesser priesthood and as superintendent of the Ward Sunday school. Advancing from one degree of the Priesthood to another he was finally ordained a High priest. During his life he married two wives. His first wife was Ann Shellcrosslee and after her death he married Harriet Cook. Before emigrating from England he kept an open house for the Elders, who labored as missionaries in his native land. He passed to his final rest at Draper, Utah, Feb. 1, 1896.
