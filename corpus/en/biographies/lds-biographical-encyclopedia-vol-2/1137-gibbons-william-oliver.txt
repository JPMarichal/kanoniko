Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Gibbons, William Oliver

Gibbons, William Oliver, second counselor to Bishop James Vernon, of Rockport, Summit county, Utah, was born at Rockport, Nov. 6, 1870. He was baptized when about eight years old; was ordained a Deacon, Teacher and Elder successively; acted as a Sunday school officer for several years, and as secretary of an Elders quorum, being ordained an Elder in 1895 by Daniel Lewis; filled a mission to the Southwestern States in 1900-1902; was ordained a High Priest June 22, 1902, and set apart as second counselor to Bishop James Vernon of Rockport. In 1903 he was set apart as first counselor to David Seamons of the Rockport Y. M. M. I. A. and in 1904 he became president of said association.
