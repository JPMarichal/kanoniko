Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Butler, Alva

Butler, Alva. (Vol. 1: 578.) Bishop Butler died May 12th, 1909, at Butlerville, Salt Lake county, Utah, survived by a wife and nine children. He remained a faithful and true Latter-day Saint until the last, and died "in the harness."
