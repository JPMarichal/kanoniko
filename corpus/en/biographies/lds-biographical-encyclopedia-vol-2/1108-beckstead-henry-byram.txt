Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Beckstead, Henry Byram

Beckstead, Henry Byram. (See Vol. 1:593.) Bro. Beckstead served four years as a Sunday school superintendent, commencing with 1897. He has also served ten years on the local Old Folks committee and six years as a home missionary in the Jordan Stake of Zion. He has been an acting Ward teacher since he was sixteen years old and for forty years he has served his fellow-citizens as sexton in South Jordan.
