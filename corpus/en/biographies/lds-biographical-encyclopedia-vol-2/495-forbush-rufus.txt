Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Forbush, Rufus

Forbush, Rufus, a veteran Elder in the Union Ward, Salt Lake county, Utah, was born July 12, 1788, at Royalston, Massachusetts, the son of David Forbush and Deliverance Goodell. He joined the Church in the early days and was with the Saints during their persecutions in Illinois. He married Polly Clark, who was also a native of Royalston. In the year 1847 one of his sons, Loren, went with the famous Mormon Battalion, and in 1850 Rufus emigrated with his family to Utah and settled in Union, where he lived until 1864, when he moved to Santaquin, Utah county, Utah; there he lived the remainder of his life. He married two other wives and became the father of thirteen children. His death occurred Sept. 7, 1875, at Santaquin, Utah.
