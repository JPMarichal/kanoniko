Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Fairbourn, Richard Matthew

Fairbourn, Richard Matthew, a High Councilor in the Jordan Stake of Zion, and a resident of the Crescent Ward, Utah, was born Dec. 12, 1869, at Mill Creek, Salt Lake Co., Utah, the son of Edward Fairbourn and Eliza Ann Wright. He received a common school education and worked on his father's farm at Mill Creek until 1901, when he moved to Crescent, where he still resides. He was blessed April 7, 1870, by Reuben Miller, baptized Aug. 25, 1877, by John F. Snedeker, ordained a Deacon, later a Teacher and still later (Jan. 9, 1898) an Elder. He married Myrtle Rhodes Jan. 26, 1898; she became the mother of three children and died March 3, 1906. He married again June 12, 1907, by taking to wife Ida Jensen, daughter of the late Bishop James P. Jensen and Anna Petersen; she has borne, him two children. In 1907-1910 he filled a mission to Great Britain, laboring in the Grimsby conference. Prior to taking his mission he acted as first assistant superintendent in the Ward Sunday school, and since his return he has acted as president of the Y. M. M. I. A. In 1906 (Aug. 19th) he was ordained a High Priest by John Henry Smith and set apart as an alternate High Councilor in the Jordan Stake. In 1903 (Feb. 13th) he became a regular member of said council. The names of Bro. Fairbourn's children are Vera L., Delbert R. Rulon R. Ruth M. and Carol.
