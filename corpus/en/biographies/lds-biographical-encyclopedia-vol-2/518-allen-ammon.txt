Allen, Ammon

Allen, Ammon, president of the 75th quorum of Seventy, was born April 23, 1860, at Ogden, Utah, the son of Alanson D. Allen. He accompanied his parents to Cache valley and resided at Hyrum till the spring of 1866, when he came to Huntsville. Here he was baptized in June, 1869, by SÃ¸ren L. Petersen and confirmed by Bishop Francis A. Hammond. He was ordained a Teacher and an Elder by Bishop Hammond, and a Seventy (in the summer of 1885) by Seymour B. Young. He filled a mission to the Indian Territory in 1885-87 and was set apart as one of the seven presidents of the 75th quorum of Seventy in 1911. In 1881 (Nov. 3rd) he married Isabelle Hislop (daughter of John Hislop and Agnes Rogers), who was born Sept. 22, 1863, at Birkenhead, England. By her he is the father of thirteen children. Brother Allen has always been a faithful and able Church worker and takes an active part in the Huntsville Ward, where he now resides.
