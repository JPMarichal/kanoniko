Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Woodruff, David Patton

Woodruff, David Patton, second counselor to Bishop I. W. Allred of Caldwell, Alberta, Canada, was born April 4, 1854, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Wilford Woodruff and Sarah Brown. He was baptized in 1862 by Wilford Woodruff; ordained an Elder in 1870 by Wm. Smith and ordained a Seventy Nov. 9, 1890, by Geo. W. Hislop; ordained a High Priest by Abraham O. Woodruff July 30, 1899, and became a member of the High Council of the Big Horn Stake, Wyo., in 1901. In 1877 he married Arabell J. Hatch, who bore her husband a number of children. Bro. Woodruff has resided in Randolph, Smithfield and Vernal (Utah), Burlington (Wyo) and Caldwell (Alberta, Canada). He moved to Vernal in 1879 and was among the pioneers of that region of country. He moved to the Big Horn Basin, Wyo., in 1893, and was with the first "Mormon" colonists who settled there.
