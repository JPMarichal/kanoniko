Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Bushman, Preston Ammeron

Bushman, Preston Ammeron, an active Elder in the St. Joseph Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, was born Dec. 11, 1875, at Lehi, Utah county, Utah, removed with his parents to Arizona in 1878 and has resided at St. Joseph ever since, taking an active part in the building up of that town. In 1898-1901 he filled a mission to the Southern States. After his return he labored as a home missionary and took an active part in the Y. M. M. I. A. work as a Stake officer. In 1902 (Oct. 1st) he married Anna Smith, daughter of the late Pres. Jesse N. Smith and Augusta Outzen.
