Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Eardley, Louisa Cooper

Eardley, Louisa Cooper, wife of Bedson Eardley, was born July 14, 1829, in Staffordshire, England, the daughter of James and Mary Cooper. She joined the Church when about twenty-one years old, married Bedson Eardley in 1852 and came to America with her husband. Sister Eardley is now 84 years of age and resides in the Seventh Ward, Salt Lake City.
