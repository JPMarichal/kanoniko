Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Larkin, George William

Larkin, George William, a Patriarch in the Weber Stake of Zion, Utah, was born April 1, 1848, at Chesterton, near Cambridge, Cambridgeshire, England. He joined the Church in England and came to Utah at an early day. He was ordained an Elder Nov. 17, 1863, by John D. T. McAllister; ordained a Seventy Dec. 30, 1883, by William F. Critchlow; ordained a High Priest May 29, 1887, by Franklin D. Richards and ordained a Patriarch July 21, 1901, by Joseph F. Smith. In 1886 (Feb. 17th) he married Barbara Anna McKenzie and in 1869 (Aug. 2nd) he married Ann Lane. By these wives he became the father of eight children.
