Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Lemon, John Knox

Lemon, John Knox, second counselor to Bishop Samuel F. Atwood of Kamas, Summit county, Utah, was born Aug. 19, 1845, in Indiana, the son of William McClure Lemon and Catherine Mayer. He came to Utah as a pioneer in Sept., 1847, crossing the plains in Perrigrine Sessions' fifty and settled at Kamas, Summit county, in 1869, where he has resided ever since.
