Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Smith, Harriet Susannah

Smith, Harriet Susannah, wife of Wm. H. Smith, was born Aug. 14, 1854, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the daughter of John Eddins and Harriet Newman. She was baptized at the age of ten years and married Thomas Parkinson Jan. 25, 1873, by whom she had two children, namely Albert C. and Samuel E. Parkinson. Her husband met with a severe accident, breaking his back, while working in a mine at West Tintic, and died in April, 1876. He was born in England March 6, 1849, emigrated to America in 1858, and continued the journey to Utah in 1860, crossing the plains with handcarts. Sister Harriet being left with the two children by her first husband was married a second time, Oct. 7, 1877. With Wm. H. Smith, her second husband, she is the mother of four children.
