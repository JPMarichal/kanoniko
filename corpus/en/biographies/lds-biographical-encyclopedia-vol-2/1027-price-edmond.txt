Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Price, Edmond

Price, Edmond, an active Elder in the West Jordan Ward, Salt Lake co., Utah, was born Aug. 8, 1854, in Wales, the son of Thos. Price and Mary Perry. He was baptized Aug. 8, 1862, by David E. Jones, emigrated to America in 1871, and after residing in Ohio two years came to Utah in 1873. He was ordained successively to the offices of Deacon Teacher and Priest; ordained an Elder Feb. 3, 1878; ordained a Seventy March 20, 1887, by William H. Freeman, and ordained a High Priest in 1910. In 1896-98 he filled a mission to Great Britain, laboring in Wales. In 1870 he married Emma Brown, who is the mother of nine children.
