Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Alley, George Hubbard

Alley, George Hubbard, a faithful Elder in the Church, was born June 14, 1823, in Lynn, Mass., the son of George Alley and Mary Symonds. He migrated with his father's family to Nauvoo, Ill., in 1842-43, was baptized in Nauvoo by Erastus Snow, and came to Utah in 1848 with his parents. His early life was spent freighting to California and he also helped his brother Stephen on the farm. He was ordained to the office of a Seventy and later to that of a High Priest. He died in Salt Lake City, April 6, 1910.
