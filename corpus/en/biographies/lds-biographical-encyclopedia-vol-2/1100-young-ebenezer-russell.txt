Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Young, Ebenezer Russell

Young, Ebenezer Russell, Bishop of the Wanship Ward, Summit co., Utah, from 1885 to 1901, was born Aug. 29, 1842, at Paterson, Passaic co., New Jersey, the son of Ebenezer Russell Young and Margaret Holden. He came to Utah in 1858 with his father's family; was ordained an Elder in 1869; ordained a Seventy in 1874 and ordained a High Priest and Bishop in 1885. He settled in Wanship, Summit co., Utah, in 1867. In 1866 (May 1st) he married Matilda W. Shreeve, by whom he became the father of five children. Bishop Young is a miller and carpenter by trade, but has also followed farming and stockraising for a living. For many years he acted as a Ward and Sunday school teacher, was a president of a Y. M. M. I. A., a home missionary, etc.
