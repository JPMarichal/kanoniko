Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Larsen, Lars

Larsen, Lars, a prominent Elder in the Big Cottonwood Ward, Salt Lake county, Utah, was born Sept. 19, 1817, on the island of Lolland, Denmark, the son of Lars Madsen and Karen Nielsen. He joined the Church in his native land in the early sixties and received a thorough thrashing by his elder brother because he become a "Mormon"; he learned the trade of a tailor and emigrated to Utah in 1862, walking all the way across the plains. At Big Cottonwood, where he located, he became a successful farmer. He married Karen Sophie Mathiesen, who was born Nov. 20, 1837, in Denmark, and emigrated to Utah in 1862. She bore her husband two children, both boys (Joseph Y. and Lars). Bro. Lars Larsen died June 25, 1880, and his wife died in 1909.
