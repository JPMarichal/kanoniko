Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Butler, Thomas Lambroke

Butler, Thomas Lambroke, a veteran Elder in the Church, was born at Swansea, Wales, May 24, 1813, was baptized Feb. 4, 1849, emigrated to Utah in 1875, and settled in Spanish Fork, Utah county, Utah, where he resided for twenty years and died there as a High Priest July 29, 1895. Prior to his emigrating to America he presided over a branch of the Church at St. Brides, South Wales, for several years.
