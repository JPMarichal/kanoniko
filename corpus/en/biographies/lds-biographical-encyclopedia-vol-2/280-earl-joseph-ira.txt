Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Earl, Joseph Ira

Earl, Joseph Ira, Bishop of Bunkerville Ward, Lincoln county, Nevada, (St. George Stake), was born Sept. 6, 1852, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Sylvester H. Earl and Lois Caroline Owen. He was baptized Sept. 6, 1860; ordained to the Lesser Priesthood soon afterwards; ordained an Elder in 1874, and ordained a High priest Aug. 26, 1888, by Daniel D. McArthur. He acted as superintendent of the Pine Valley Sunday school about three years; served as superintendent of the Bunkerville Sunday school from March 29, 1885, to March 31, 1907, was set apart as second counselor to Bishop Edward Bunker, jun., Aug. 26, 1888, set apart as first counselor to the same Bishop Sept. 16, 1906, and chosen Bishop of the Bunkerville Ward Jan. 9, 1908. In 1880 (March 15th) he married Miss Elethea Calista Bunker, by whom he has had nine children, six girls and three boys. In 1885 (Dec. 11th) he married Agnes Viola Bunker, by whom he has had eight children, two boys and six girls. Elder Earl is a carpenter and blacksmith by avocation and has also engaged in farming and bee keeping.
