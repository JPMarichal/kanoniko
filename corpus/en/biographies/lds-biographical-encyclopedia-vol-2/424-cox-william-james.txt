Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Cox, William James

Cox, William James, a High Councilor in the Woodruff Stake of Zion, was born May 31, 1863, at Basingstoke, Hampshire, England, the son of John Cox and Annie Stiff. He was baptized when about eight years of age and was ordained successively to the offices of Deacon, Teacher, Elder, Seventy (being a member of the 102nd quorum) and High Priest. In 1900-01 he filled a mission to Great Britain, laboring in the Birmingham conference. At home he has acted as president of Y. M. M. I. A., president of a Deacons' quorum, Ward teacher, home missionary and High Councilor. Emigrating to Utah in 1866, he first resided in Davis county, later in Salt Lake county, Utah, and he is now a resident of Woodruff, Rich county, Utah.
