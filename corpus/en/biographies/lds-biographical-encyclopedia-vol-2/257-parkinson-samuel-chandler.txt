Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Parkinson, Samuel Chandler

Parkinson, Samuel Chandler, Bishop of Franklin Ward, Oneida Stake of Zion, Idaho, since 1907, was born Feb. 23, 1853, at St. Louis, Missouri, the son of Samuel R. Parkinson and Arabella Chandler; ordained a Deacon, a Teacher and an Elder successively, the latter ordination taking place in May, 1885, by Pres. Daniel H. Wells. He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1885-1886 and was ordained a High Priest in 1886. He filled another mission to the northwestern States in 1898.
