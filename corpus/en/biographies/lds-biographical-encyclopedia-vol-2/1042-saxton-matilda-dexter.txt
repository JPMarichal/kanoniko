Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Saxton, Matilda Dexter

Saxton, Matilda Dexter, wife of Solomon Saxton, was born Feb. 5, 1827, at Codyner Park, Derbyshire, England, the daughter of John and Lydia Dexter. She was baptized March 11, 1847, and came to Utah in 1861, together with her husband. They located in Coalville, Summit county, where Sister Saxton died July 30, 1908, as a faithful Latter-day Saint. She was president of the Coalville and Upton Ward relief societies for several years. Sister Saxton was the mother of ten children.
