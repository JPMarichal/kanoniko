Call, AnsonPPCall, Anson Charles

Call, AnsonPPCall, Anson Charles, a High Councilor in the Woodruff Stake of Zion, was born March 29, 1854, in Fillmore, Millard county, Utah, the son of Josiah Call and Henriette C. Williams. His father was killed by the Indians in 1858 and Anson moved to Bountiful, Davis county, in 1859. He married Charlotte L. Brown in December, 1872, and settled in 1874 at Woodruff, Rich, county, where he engaged in farming and stock-raising. He has acted as sheriff of Rich county twelve years, and as county commissioner three terms. In Church positions he has labored as a Ward teacher, Sunday school teacher, a home missionary, and president of Y. M. M. I. A. In 1886 (July 25th) he was ordained a Seventy, and later set apart as one of the presidents of the 102nd quorum. In 1889 he was called on a mission to the Northwestern States, but released on account of important home duties. He was ordained a High Priest June 7, 1898, and set apart as a High Councilor in the Woodruff Stake. For several years he has been a member of the Old Folks committee of the Woodruff Stake. He is the father of eleven children.
