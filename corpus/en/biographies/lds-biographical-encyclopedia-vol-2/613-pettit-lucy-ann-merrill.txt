Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Pettit, Lucy Ann, Merrill

Pettit, Lucy Ann, Merrill, wife of Lorenzo Permit, was born July 25, 1828, at Byron, Genesee co., New York. She was married to Bro. Pettit in 1846, came with her husband to Utah in 1848, and shared with him all the various experiences incident to pioneer life. She died Feb. 6, 1879, in Salt Lake City.
