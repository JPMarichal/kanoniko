Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Scott, William Richmond

Scott, William Richmond, an Elder in Sandy Ward, Jordan Stake, (Salt Lake county, Utah) is a son of Robert Scott and Isabella Richmond and was born in Belle Rogh, Antrum county, Ireland. He first heard the Gospel in 1867 and was baptized Jan. 12, 1868, by John Read. His ordinations to the Priesthood took place as follows: Ordained a Priest in 1868, and an Elder in 1870, at which time he was appointed to preside over the Belfast (Ireland) branch of the Church. He emigrated to Utah in 1872 and located in Sandy, which has been his place of residence since that time. Elder Scott has ever been an energetic Church worker, having worked in the Sunday schools and M. I. A., and is at present a president in the council of the 93rd Quorum of Seventy. Among the civil positions he has filled are those of city treasurer and councilman of Sandy city. At present he is operating a successful creamery business.
