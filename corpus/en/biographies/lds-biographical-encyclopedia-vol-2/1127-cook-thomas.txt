Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Cook, Thomas

Cook, Thomas, an active Elder in the North Jordan Ward, Salt Lake county, Utah, was born Nov. 14, 1840, in Gloucestershire, England, the son of John Cook and Ann Denley. He was baptized July 23, 1853, by Wm. Panter; married Anna Harris in May, 1863, and emigrated to America that year, crossing the Atlantic in the ship "Cynosure" and the plains in Thos. E. Ricks' company. After residing temporarily in Salt Lake City, he became a permanent resident of Taylorsville in 1865. He was ordained an Elder in 1864 by Joseph F. Smith, married Mary Ellen Normington March 17, 1866, was ordained a Seventy several years later and finally ordained a High Priest May 27, 1908, by Joseph W. Musser. For a number of years he acted as an officer in the Ward Y. M. M. I. A. and as a Ward teacher. His special calling has been to administer to the sick. By occupation Bro. Cook is a farmer. Four of his sons have filled foreign missions.
