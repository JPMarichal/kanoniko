Willardsen, Anna Katrine SÃ¸rensen, wife of Christian Willardsen, was born Nov. 10, 1849, at Guddumlund, Aalborg amt, Denmark, the daughter of Jens Chris. SÃ¸rensen and Anna Christine Jensen. She was baptized Feb. 23, 1861, by Poul Christian Petersen and emigrated to Utah in 1871, arriving in Salt Lake City Sept. 21st. Nov. 13, 1871, she married Christian Willardsen, to whom she subsequently bore four children, two boys and two girls. After residing in Ephraim until 1898, she moved to a place called Brooklyn, Sevier county, Utah, where she presided over the branch Relief Society about ten years. Sister Willardsen has done a great deal of Temple work for her departed relatives and friends in the Manti and Salt Lake City Temples.
