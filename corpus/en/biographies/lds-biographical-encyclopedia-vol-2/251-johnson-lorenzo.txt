Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Johnson, Lorenzo

Johnson, Lorenzo, second counselor to the Bishop of the Preston Second Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, was born Sept. 18, 1871, at Brigham City, Utah, baptized Sept. 19, 1879, at Hyde Park, Utah; ordained to the lesser Priesthood between the age of 12 and 14 years; ordained an Elder Nov. 15, 1891, by David C. Eames, and ordained a High Priest Aug. 14, 1905.
