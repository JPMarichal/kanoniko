Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Harrison, James Parry

Harrison, James Parry, Bishop of St. John Ward, Malad Stake (Oneida county, Idaho,) is a son of James H. Harrison and Angelina Parry; and was born Jan. 22, 1846, in London, England. He was baptized on his birthday, Jan., 22, 1854 by his father, emigrated to Utah in 1861, located in Malad Valley in 1868, was ordained an Elder Dec. 13, 1869, by John D. T. McAllister and ordained a High Priest by Elijah Box Nov. 20, 1880. In 1886 he went to the Southern States on a mission. Prior to his calling to the office of a Bishop he acted as a Ward teacher, a Sunday school superintendent, an officer in the Y. M. M. I. A. and a president of an Elders quorum. In 1869 (Dec. 13th) he married Elvira Ann Williams who has borne him three sons and five daughters. Nov. 26, 1884, he married Emma Dredge as a second wife, in consequence of which he became a victim of the anti polygamy prosecutions in 1888 and served his six months in the Boise penitentiary. Elder Harrison is by occupation a farmer and stock raiser.
