Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Jones, Alfred Thomas

Jones, Alfred Thomas, second counselor to Bishop Laronzo Day, at Hunter, Salt Lake co., Utah, was born Nov. 29, 1876, at Rockport, Summit co., Utah, the son of Alfred A. Jones and Hannah Maria Gibbs. He was baptized when eight years of age, was ordained to the lesser Priesthood as a boy, received a common school education, was ordained an Elder in 1899, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1900-02, after being ordained a Seventy Feb. 16, 1900. He was ordained a High Priest July 27, 1902, by John R. Winder and set apart as second counselor to Laronzo Day. In 1904 (April 20th) he married Sarah Jane Lester. Bro. Jones has taken an active part in Y. M. M. I. A. work and has acted as a Ward teacher and Sunday school teacher for a number of years.
