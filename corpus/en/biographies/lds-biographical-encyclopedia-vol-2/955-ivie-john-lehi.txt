Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Ivie, John, Lehi

Ivie, John, Lehi, a Utah pioneer and military man, was born June 11, 1833, in Missouri, the son of James Ivie. He shared with the saints in their persecutions in Illinois and migrated to Utah in 1849, became a settler on Pleasant Creek, now Mt. Pleasant, Sanpete co., in 1853. After marrying Mary Catherine Barton, of Farmington, Utah, he moved with his family to Sevier county, where he resided until the time of his demise. He died at Vermillion, May 10, 1909, of old age and general debility, at the home of his daughter, Mrs. Thos. Gledhill. During the latter part of his life Brother Ivie was known as "Uncle John" and "Colonel Ivie." He was a frontiersman to all intent and purposes and did yeoman services in Sanpete, Sevier and other counties in protecting the early settlers from the ravishes of the red men. As a colonel in the Utah militia he was well liked by his men, being brave but cautious; he would never send his men where he dared not go himself, and always tried to obey the orders of his superiors.
