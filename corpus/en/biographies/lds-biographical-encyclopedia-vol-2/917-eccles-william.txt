Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Eccles, William

Eccles, William, a Patriarch in the Weber Stake of Zion, was born April 6, 1825, at Kilpatrick, Scotland, the sen of William Eccles and Margaret Miller. He was baptized Feb. 5, 1842, by Andrew Sprowls; ordained an Elder in 1866; ordained a High Priest by Lester J. Herrick and ordained a Patriarch in 1900 by Apostle Geo. Teasdale. He emigrated to Utah in 1863, resided in Eden Weber co., for twenty years, being among the first settlers of that place, and from 1883 until his death he resided in Ogden. In 1843 (May 5th) he married Sarah Hutchison, who bore him 8 children. Bro. Eccles died in Ogden, Dec. 4, 1903.
