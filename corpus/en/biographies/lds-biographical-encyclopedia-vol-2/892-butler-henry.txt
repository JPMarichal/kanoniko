Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 2 ( 1914)

Butler, Henry

Butler, Henry, a High Councilor in the St. Johns Stake, Arizona, was born June 15, 1844, at Redbourn, Hertford, England, the son of John Butler and Elizabeth Archer. His parents joined the Church when he was a boy, and he emigrated to America with his parents in 1853, crossing the Atlantic in the ship "International" and the plains in Jacob Gates' company. After spending several years in Kaysville, Davis county, Salt Lake City, and other places, he settled more permanently at Payson, Utah county, but afterwards moved to Arizona. He was ordained an Elder Dec. 2, 1872, by Samuel H. B. Smith and married Harriet Belinda Russell. In 1885 (Feb. 1st) he was ordained a Seventy by John R. Hewlett, and he was ordained a High Priest March 9, 1901, by David K. Udall, and set apart as an alternate High Councilor in the St. Johns Stake. He afterwards became a regular member of said High Council.
