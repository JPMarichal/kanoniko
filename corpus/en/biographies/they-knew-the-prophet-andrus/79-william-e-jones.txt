William E. Jones

It would be impossible for me to describe my impressions when I first saw Joseph Smith. I have many times listened to his inspired words; and I never shall forget the words he spoke on the first Sunday after I came to Nauvoo. The Temple was built a few feet above the ground. While preaching he pointed towards it and said, "The Lord has commanded us to build that Temple. We want to build it, but we have not the means. There are people in this city who have the means, but they will not let us have them. What shall we do with such people? I say damn them!"

Then he sat down. On the following day several persons came forward with their means, and this averted the curse which would doubtless otherwise have followed them.

The Juvenile Instructor, XXVII (January 15, 1892), pp. 65-66.
