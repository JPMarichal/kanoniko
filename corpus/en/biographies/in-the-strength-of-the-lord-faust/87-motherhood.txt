MOTHERHOOD

IT IS MY feeling that we grossly underestimate the sacred nature of motherhood. Psychiatric experts remind us that there are certain fundamental, biological facts which influence the psyche of those who bring new life into the world. One says, "The ability of mothers to accept infants after they are born is underrated and underestimated." (Dr. S. Bolter, American Journal of Psychiatry, Oct. 1962. pp. 312-16.) Childbearing is a basic biological and psychological, privileged function of womankind. ("The Sanctity of Life," Ensign, May 1975, 28.)
