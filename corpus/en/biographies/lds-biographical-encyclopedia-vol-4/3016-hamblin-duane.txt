Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hamblin, Duane

Hamblin, Duane, Bishop of the Ramah Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1914 to 1916, was born Aug. 10, 1863, in Washington, Utah, the son of Wm. H. Hamblin and Betsey Leavitt. He was ordained a Bishop Aug. 23, 1914, by David 0. McKay.
