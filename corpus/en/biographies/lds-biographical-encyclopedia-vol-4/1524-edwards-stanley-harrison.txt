Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Edwards, Stanley Harrison

Edwards, Stanley Harrison, presiding Elder of the Hiawatha Branch, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930, was born Oct. 26, 1889, in Sterling, Utah, the son of Harrison M. Edwards and Anna Jensen. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1898, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1913-1915, and was ordained a High Priest and set apart as presiding Elder by Arthur W. Horsley March 13, 1927.
