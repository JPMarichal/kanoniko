Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gilbert, James Harris

Gilbert, James Harris, Bishop of the Bancroft Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1929, was born Dec. 8, 1883, in Twin Creeks, Uinta Co., Wyoming, a son of Jesse Gilbert and Mary Ann Harris. He was baptized Nov. 2, 1892, ordained a High Priest Aug. 26, 1917, by Joseph F. Smith, jun., who also ordained him a Bishop Nov. 22, 1925.
