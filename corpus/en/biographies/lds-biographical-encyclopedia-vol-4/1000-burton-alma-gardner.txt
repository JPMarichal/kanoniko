Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burton, Alma Gardner

Burton, Alma Gardner, president of the Society Islands Mission from 1926 to 1929, was born Dec. 4, 1895, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Willard C. Burton and Mary Jane Gardner. He was baptized Dec. 5, 1903, set apart for his first mission to the Society Islands, Nov. 3, 1915, and returned in 1919. He was called to the Society Islands to preside over the mission in 1926.
