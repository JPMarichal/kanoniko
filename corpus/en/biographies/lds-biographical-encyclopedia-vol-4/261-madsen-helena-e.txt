Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Madsen, Helena E.

Madsen, Helena E., a member of the General Board of Relief Society from Oct. 10, 1892, to Oct. 5, 1902, was the wife of Bishop Christian A. Madsen of Gunnison, Sanpete Co., Utah. Sister Madsen was always very active in public affairs and served respectively as president of the ward Primary Association and of the ward Relief Society until called to be a member of the General Board of Relief Society. She died at Gunnison Nov. 20, 1906.
