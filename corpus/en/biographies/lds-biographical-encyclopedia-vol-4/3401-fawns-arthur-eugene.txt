Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fawns, Arthur Eugene

Fawns, Arthur Eugene, Bishop of the Stirling Ward, Taylor Stake, Canada, from 1904 to 1930+, was born Nov. 27, 1875, in Payson, Utah, a son of David Fowler Fawns and Hannah Olson. He was baptized March 6, 1884, filled a mission to England in 1899-1901, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 1, 1903, by Jos. F. Smith, and a Bishop Nov. 13, 1904, by John W. Taylor.
