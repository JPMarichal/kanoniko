Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Chidester, John Peck

Chidester, John Peck, a member of Zion's Camp, was born Dec. 23, 1831, in Somerfield township, Monroe County, Michigan. Arriving in Utah with a company of saints about 1848, he was one of the first settlers of Spanish Fork. He was commissioned as a captain in the Nauvoo Legion and participated in the Walker Indian War of 1853. He married Susan Foy Oct. 23, 1851, and moved with his family to Southern Utah in 1862, settling at Washington, where he lived until his death which occurred Jan. 10, 1897.
