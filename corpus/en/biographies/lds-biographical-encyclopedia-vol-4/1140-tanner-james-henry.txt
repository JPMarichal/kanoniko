Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tanner, James Henry

Tanner, James Henry, Bishop of the Aetna Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1918 to 1920, was born Dec. 3, 1877, in Cokeville, Uinta Co., Wyoming, the son of John William Tanner and Lucy R. Snyder. He was baptized July 3, 1898; ordained a High Priest Feb. 15, 1899, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 7, 1918.
