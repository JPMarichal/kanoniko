Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gibson, John

Gibson, John, Bishop of the Ogden 16th Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born May 9, 1884, in West Weber, Utah, a son of Thomas Gibson and Catherine Hunter. He was baptized July 7, 1892, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 5, 1926, by Melvin J. Ballard.
