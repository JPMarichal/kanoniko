Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moulton, Thomas Alma

Moulton, Thomas Alma, presiding Elder of the Grovont Branch, Teton Stake, Wyo., from 1921 to 1930+, was born May 10, 1883, in Heber City, Utah, the son of Charles A. Moulton and Rhoda Francia Duke. He was baptized Aug. 16, 1891, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1905-1907, and was set apart Aug. 21, 1921, by Geo. F. Richards to preside over the Grovont Branch.
