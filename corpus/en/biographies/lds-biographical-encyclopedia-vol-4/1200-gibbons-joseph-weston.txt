Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gibbons, Joseph Weston

Gibbons, Joseph Weston, Bishop of the Garden City Ward, Bear Lake Stake, (Rich Co., Utah), Idaho, from 1923 to 1926, was born Oct. 10, 1874, in Laketown, Utah, the son of Joseph Gibbons and Mercy Weston. He was baptized when about eight years old, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1905-1907, and was ordained a Bishop June 3, 1923, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
