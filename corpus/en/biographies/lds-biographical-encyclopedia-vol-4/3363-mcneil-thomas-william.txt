Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcneil, Thomas William

Mcneil, Thomas William, Bishop of the Francis Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1929, was born Aug. 25, 1885, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of Thomas McNeil and Martha Ellen Prescott. He was baptized June 6, 1897, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 6, 1927, by Rudger Clawson.
