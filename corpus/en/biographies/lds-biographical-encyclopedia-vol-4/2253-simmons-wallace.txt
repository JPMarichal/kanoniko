Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Simmons, Wallace

Simmons, Wallace, Bishop of the Manchester Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1927 to 1929, was born Oct. 19, 1883, at Spanish Fork, Utah, the son of Jonathan Simmons and Elizabeth Waugh. He was baptized July 7, 1892, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 26, 1928, by Stephen L. Richards.
