Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sloan, Walter Reed

Sloan, Walter Reed, president of the Northwestern States Mission from 1927 to 1934, was born March 30, 1881, in Manti, Utah, a son of Hugh Russell Sloan and Margaret V. Reed. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1889, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1900-1902, was set apart as president of the Northwestern States Mission Nov. 19, 1926, and arrived in the mission Nov. 30, 1926. Prior to this he had acted as Bishop of the Logan 2nd Ward in Utah from 1911 to 1916.
