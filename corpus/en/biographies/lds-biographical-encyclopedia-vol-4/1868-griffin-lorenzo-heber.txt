Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Griffin, Lorenzo Heber

Griffin, Lorenzo Heber, Bishop of the Escalante North Ward, Garfield Stake, Piute Co., Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born May 17, 1893, at Esealante, Garfield Co., Utah, the son of Charles E. Griffin and Sarah Elizabeth Jarvis. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a Seventy, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1924-1926, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 17, 1928, by George F. Richards.
