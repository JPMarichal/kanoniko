Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mickelson, James Michael

Mickelson, James Michael, Bishop of the Boneta Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1920, was born May 11, 1881, in Manti, Utah, the son of Jens Mickelson and Ann Christina Anderson. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1889, ordained a High Priest Sept. 21, 1913, by William H. Smart, and a Bishop Sept. 17, 1916, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
