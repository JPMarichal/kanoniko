Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, Alma

Williams, Alma, Bishop of the Osgood Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Feb. 5, 1881, in Mayfield, Utah, the son of Samuel Moroni Williams and Esther Susan Milo. He was baptized June 8, 1889, filled a mission to the Central States in 1907-1909, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 14, 1926, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop May 15, 1927, by David O. McKay.
