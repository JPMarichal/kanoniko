Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Madsen, Peter

Madsen, Peter, Bishop of the Provo 5th Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1892, was born April 6, 1824, in Studsdal, Denmark, a son of Mads Petersen Madsen and Mette Marie Madsen. He was baptized June 12, 1853, emigrated to Utah in 1856, was ordained to the Priesthood and filled a mission to the Sandwich Islands in 1886-1888. He was sustained as Bishop Oct. 13, 1877. He died Jan. 16, 1910.
