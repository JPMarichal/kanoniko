Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rigby, James

Rigby, James, Bishop of the Pratt Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1918 to 1926, was born Aug. 25, 1881, at Newton, Utah, the son of William F. Rigby and Sophia Eckersley. He was baptized Aug. 25, 1889, ordained a High Priest May 18, 1913, by Francis M. Lyreart, and a Bishop Aug. 17, 1918, by Stephen L. Richards.
