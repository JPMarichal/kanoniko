Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Oquirrh Stake

Anderson, Henry Claude

Anderson, Henry Claude, Bishop of the Garfield Ward, Oquirrh Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1927, was born June 16, 1888, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Henry J. Anderson and Elizabeth Y. Pyper. He was baptized in July, 1896, ordained an Elder April 26, 1909, by John Cameron, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1909-1911, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 15, 1920, by Charles H. Hyde, and a Bishop June 19, 1923, by Geo. F. Richards.
