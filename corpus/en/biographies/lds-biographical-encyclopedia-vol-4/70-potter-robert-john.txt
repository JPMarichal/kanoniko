Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Potter, Robert John

Potter, Robert John, 3rd Bishop of the Beaver Ward, Bear River Stake, Box Elder Co., Utah, was born Aug. 15, 1895, in Raughton, Cumberland Co., England, the son of Thomas Potter and Margaret Carlile. He emigrated to Utah in 1913, crossing the Atlantic Ocean in the ship [p.55] "Mauretania" and located in Logan, Utah, for a short time, and thence moved to Collinston, Utah (Beaver Ward). He was baptized and confirmed Nov. 26, 1916, by Hyrum Kidman at Collinston; ordained an Elder Aug. 11, 1918, by Bishop Race A. Johnson, at Beaver; married Lottie Lorenzo Rucker Aug. 14, 1918, in the Salt Lake Temple, which union has been blessed with six children, namely, Robert Carleton, Margaret, William Bethel, Leona, Naomi and Elizabeth. Brother Potter acted as second assistant superintendent of the Collinston Sunday school, and on March 4, 1923, he was ordained a High Priest by Arthur R. Capener and set apart as second counselor to Bishop Johnson of the Beaver Ward, which position he held until Nov. 10, 1929, when he was ordained a Bishop by Apostle Richard R. Lyman and set apart to preside over said ward, which position he now occupies. Bishop Potter filled a short-term mission in the Western States Mission in 1925-1926; he is a farmer by occupation.
