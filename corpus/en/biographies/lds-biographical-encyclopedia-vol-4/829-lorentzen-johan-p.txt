Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lorentzen, Johan P.

Lorentzen, Johan P., president of the Icelandic Mission from 1853 to 1854, was born May 23, 1815, in Copenhagen, Denmark, and was baptized April 19, 1851. Bro. Johan P. Lorentzen never emigrated to America, but died in Copenhagen, almost lost and forgotten by his former associates in the Church.
