Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcallister, George Stanley

Mcallister, George Stanley, Bishop of the 11th Ward, Ensign Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born May 26, 1869, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Duncan M. McAllister and. Catherine Perkes. He was baptized June 18, 1877, by John Tingey, ordained a Seventy Dec. 2, 1892, by Seymour B. Young, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1892-1895. On March 22, 1916, he was ordained a High Priest by Nephi L. Morris, and in February, 1921, was ordained a Bishop by Charles W. Penrose.
