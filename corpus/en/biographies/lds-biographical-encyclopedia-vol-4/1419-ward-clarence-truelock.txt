Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ward, Clarence Truelock

Ward, Clarence Truelock, second counselor in the Boise Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1919 to 1926, and first counselor from 1926 to 1930, was born April 26, 1888, in Almo, Cassia Co., Idaho, the son of Charles R. Ward and Margaret E. Mason. He was baptized July 8, 1896, ordained an Elder Dec. 15, 1909, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1909-1912, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 6, 1912, and set apart as first counselor in the Boise Stake Dec. 5, 1926, by Chas. W. Nibley.
