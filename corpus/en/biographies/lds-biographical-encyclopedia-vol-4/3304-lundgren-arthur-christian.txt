Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lundgren, Arthur Christian

Lundgren, Arthur Christian, Bishop of the Monroe North Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born May 28, 1895, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Peter M. Lundgren and Marie Christine Christensen. He was baptized May 28, 1903, ordained a High Priest Nov. 8, 1925, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop May 13, 1928, by Rudger Clawson.
