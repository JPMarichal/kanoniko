Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clark, Joseph Smith

Clark, Joseph Smith, Bishop of the Fielding Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1913, was born Aug. 30, 1878, in Farmington, Cache Co., Utah, the son of Joseph S. Clark and Lucy Maria Robinson. He was baptized in July, 1887, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop by Rudger Clawson Oct. 14, 1906.
