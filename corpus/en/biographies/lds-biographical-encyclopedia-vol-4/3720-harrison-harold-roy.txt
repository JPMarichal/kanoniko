Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harrison, Harold Roy

Harrison, Harold Roy, Bishop of the Kemmerer Ward, Woodruff Stake, Wyo., from 1919 to 1921, was born Dec. 26, 1885, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of Harold Henry Harrison and Clara Mold. He was baptized June 5, 1894, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1904-1906, was ordained a High Priest by Stephen L. Richards, and a Bishop Oct. 25, 1919, by Geo. F. Richards.
