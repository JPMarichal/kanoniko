Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Webb, Eli J.

Webb, Eli J., Bishop of the Lincoln Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1922, was born March 17, 1879, in Lehi, Utah. He was baptized July 21, 1888, ordained a High Priest May 11, 1918, by James E. Steele, and a Bishop Aug. 28, 1920, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
