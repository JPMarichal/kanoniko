Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Platt, James Henry

Platt, James Henry, Bishop of the Wilson Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930, was born June 25, 1886, in Patterson, New Jersey, a son of Benjamin Platt and Mary Ellen Otterway. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1895, ordained an Elder by Joseph H. Grant Oct. 10, 1906, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1906-1909. He was ordained a High Priest April 1, 1917, by Francis W. Stratford, and a Bishop June 20, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
