Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Gustaf Henry

Johnson, Gustaf Henry, Bishop of the Idaho Falls 1st Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930+, was born June 2, 1886, in Hyde Park, Utah, a son of Gustaf Johnson and Anna Sofia Christensen. He was baptized July 4, 1894, ordained an Elder in May, 1905, by Chas. E. Dinwoodey, and filled a mission to Sweden in 1905-1908. He was ordained a High Priest Jan. 8, 1928, by Fred A. Caine, and a Bishop Feb. 11, 1928, by Rudger Clawson.
