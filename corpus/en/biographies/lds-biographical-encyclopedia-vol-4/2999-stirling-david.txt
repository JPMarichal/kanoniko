Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stirling, David

Stirling, David, Bishop of the Leeds Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1928, was born July 23, 1885, in Leeds, a son of William Stirling and Sarah Ann Laney. He was baptized Sept. 7, 1893, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 14, 1913, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
