Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Eaton, John Burton

Eaton, John Burton, Bishop of the Glines Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Dec. 7, 1876, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, a son of Joseph Eaton and Elizabeth Walker. He was ordained a Seventy June 3, 1906, by Geo. A. Smith, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1909-1911, and was ordained a Bishop June 27, 1926, by David O. McKay.
