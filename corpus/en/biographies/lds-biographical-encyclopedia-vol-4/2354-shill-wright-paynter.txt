Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shill, Wright Paynter

Shill, Wright Paynter, Bishop of the Lehi Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1908 to 1914, was born Jan. 12, 1880, in Croyden, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of Charles G. Shill and Harriett Paynter. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 23, 1908, by John Henry Smith.
