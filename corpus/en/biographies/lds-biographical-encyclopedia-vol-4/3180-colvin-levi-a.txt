Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Colvin, Levi A.

Colvin, Levi A., Bishop of the Pleasant View Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1905 to 1912, was born June 12, 1857, in Payson, Utah, a son of Levi O. A. Colvin and Mary E. Davis. He was baptized Oct. 4, 1866, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1895-1897, and was sustained as Bishop Feb. 26, 1905.
