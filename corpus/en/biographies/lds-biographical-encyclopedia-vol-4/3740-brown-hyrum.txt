Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brown, Hyrum

Brown, Hyrum, Bishop of the Chester Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1891 to 1902, was born Oct. 4, 1854, at Union Fort, Utah, a son of Thomas J. Brown and Mary Remington. He was baptized in 1862, set apart as president of the Chester Branch April 15, 1890, and later ordained a Bishop when the branch became a ward. He died Sept. 22, 1932.
