Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Coons, Libeus T.

Coons, Libeus T., a member of Zion's Camp, was born May 13, 1807, He became a convert to "Mormonism" in the early days of the Church, shortly after which he was blessed and set apart by the prophet Joseph Smith to nurse the sick, to whom he often brought great blessings and comfort. As a member of Zion's Camp he traveled to Missouri in 1834, and when the first quorum of Seventy was organized in Kirtland, Ohio, in 1835, he was chosen as one of its members. He passed through the persecutions of the saints in Missouri and Illinois, where he was chosen to be a member of the Prophet Joseph Smith's lifeguard, and always showed himself faithful to the Church. After his arrival in Utah, he resided in a number of places and finally died at Richfield, Utah, July 7, 1872, leaving a wife, and a numerous family of children. He was a kind father, neighbor and friend.
