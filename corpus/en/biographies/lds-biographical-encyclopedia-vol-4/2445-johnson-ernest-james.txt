Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Ernest James

Johnson, Ernest James, second counselor in the Moroni Stake presidency, Utah, from 1929 to 1930, was born April 21, 1891, in Fountain Green, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Alfred Johnson and Marie Jensen Petersen. He was baptized May 22, 1900, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1923-1926, was ordained a High Priest and Bishop of the Fountain Green Ward July 11, 1926, by David O. McKay and set apart as second counselor June 16, 1929, by Rudger Clawson.
