Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Monroe, Theodore M.

Monroe, Theodore M., Bishop of the Lava Hot Springs Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1927, was born Oct. 1, 1864, at Providence, Utah, a son of Marion M. Monroe and Lucinda Busenbark. He was ordained a Bishop Aug. 21, 1920, by David O. McKay.
