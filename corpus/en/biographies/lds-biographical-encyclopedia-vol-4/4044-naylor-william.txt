Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Naylor, William

Naylor, William, a member of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1879 to 1918, was born Aug. 19, 1835, in Little Budworth, Cheshire, England, a son of George Naylor and Mary Sefton. He came to Utah in 1848, and died Feb. 22, 1918, in Salt Lake City, Utah.
