Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Murphy, Castle Henry

Murphy, Castle Henry, Bishop of the Ogden 2nd Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930, was born Oct. 1, 1886, in Ogden, a son of Emanuel Mark Murphy and Eliza Miranda Hadlock. He was baptized July 5, 1896, ordained an Elder June 14, 1909, by Robert McQuarrie and filled a mission to Hawaii in 1909-1913, was ordained a High Priest June 22, 1915, by John Watson, and a Bishop Jan. 11, 1928, by Rudger Clawson. He was set apart for a second mission Aug. 19, 1930, to preside over the Hawaiian Mission.
