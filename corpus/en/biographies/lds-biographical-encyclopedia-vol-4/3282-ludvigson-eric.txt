Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ludvigson, Eric

Ludvigson, Eric, Bishop of the Stifling Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born Dec. 22, 1872, in Manti, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Eric Ludvigson and Anna L. Steck. He was baptized Aug. 7, 1881, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1910-1912, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 3, 1919, by Anthony W. Ivins, and ordained a Bishop May 1, 1920, by Stephen L. Richards.
