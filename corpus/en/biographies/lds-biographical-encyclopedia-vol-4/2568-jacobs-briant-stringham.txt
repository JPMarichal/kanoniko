Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jacobs, Briant Stringham

Jacobs, Briant Stringham, Bishop of the Clearfield Ward, North Davis Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Oct. 4, 1886, in Sugar House, Utah, the son of Henry Chariton Jacobs and Susan Stringham. He was baptized Nov. 14, 1894, and was or-dained a High Priest and Bishop March 25, 1925, by David O. McKay.
