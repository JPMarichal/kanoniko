Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Murdock, Reuben Chase

Murdock, Reuben Chase, first counselor in the Beaver Stake Presidency, Utah, from 1925 to 1930 +, was born June 22, 1894, in Beaver, the son of Geo. C. Murdock and Caroline Farnsworth. He was baptized July 13, 1903, ordained an Elder May 31, 1913, by F. D. Farnsworth, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1913-1915, was ordained a High Priest April 17, 1916, by Rudger Clawson, and set apart as first counselor July 18, 1925, by Stephen L. Richards.
