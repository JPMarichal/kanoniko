Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lambert, Ephraim,

Lambert, Ephraim, first counselor in the Roosevelt Stake presidency, Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born Nov. 4, 1858, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of John Lambert and Elina H. Larsen. He was baptized in 1867, chosen as first counselor June 26, 1920, and was set apart by Rudger Clawson.
