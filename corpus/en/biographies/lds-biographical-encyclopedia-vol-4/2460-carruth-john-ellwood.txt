Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carruth, John Ellwood

Carruth, John Ellwood, Bishop of the Slide Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1919, was born March 12, 1883, in Coalville, Utah, a son of William Carruth and Emma Wilde. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1891, ordained a High Priest Feb. 25, 1912, by George W. Young, and was ordained a Bishop June 15, 1918, by Rudger Clawson.
