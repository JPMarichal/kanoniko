Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rider, Oscar L.

Rider, Oscar L., Bishop of the Blackfoot 2nd Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1924, was born Jan. 28, 1883, in Kanab, Utah, the son of John Rider and Mary McDonald. He was baptized in 1891 by Asa W. Judd, ordained an Elder April 20, 1901, by his father, filled a mission to the Central States in 1901-1903, and was ordained a Bishop May 20, 1917, by Orson F. Whitney.
