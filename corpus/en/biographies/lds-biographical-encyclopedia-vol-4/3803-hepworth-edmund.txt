Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hepworth, Edmund

Hepworth, Edmund, presiding Elder of the Springdale Branch, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1879 to 1880, was born March 7, 1841, in Yorkshire, England, a son of Joseph Hepworth and Mary Hurst. He was baptized in England, came to Utah in 1863, and was appointed presiding Elder June 27, 1879.
