Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mineer, Frank Edgar

Mineer, Frank Edgar, second counselor in the Moapa Stake presidency, Neyada, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Dec. 6, 1897, in St. Johns, Arizona, a son of Emil August Mineer and Frances A. White. He was baptized Feb. 3, 1906, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission in the Northern States in 1917-1920, was ordained a High Priest June 3, 1927, by David O. McKay, and set apart as second counselor Sept. 4, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
