Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pearson, Rulon Vance

Pearson, Rulon Vance, Bishop of the Balboa Ward, San Francisco Stake, Calif., from 1929 to 1930+, was born July 12, 1886, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, the son of Benjamin W. Pearson and Sarah Ellis Hawley. He was baptized April 8, 1895, ordained a High Priest Nov. 17, 1928, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop May 19, 1929, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
