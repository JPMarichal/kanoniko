Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, William

Taylor, William, Bishop of the Ferron Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1879 to 1882, was born Aug. 16, 1824, in Hampton-in-Arden, Warwickshire, England, the son of Richard Taylor and Esther Smith. He was baptized March 10, 1851, in England, came to Utah at an early day, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 20, 1868, and a Bishop Oct. 9, 1879. He died May 19, 1904.
