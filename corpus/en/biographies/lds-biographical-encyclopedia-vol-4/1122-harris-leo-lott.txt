Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Leo Lott

Harris, Leo Lott, Bishop of Woolford Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1913 to 1915, was born April 9, 1886, in Payson, Utah, the son of Dennison Emer Harris and Eunice Stewart. He filled a mission to Great Britain in 1908-1910.
