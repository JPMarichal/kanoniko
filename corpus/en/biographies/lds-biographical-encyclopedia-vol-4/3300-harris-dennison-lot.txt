Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Dennison Lot

Harris, Dennison Lot, Bishop of the Monroe Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1885, was born Jan. 17, 1825, in Windom, Luzerne Co., Pennsylvania, a son of Emer Harris. He emigrated to Utah in 1852, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop in July, 1877, by Erastus Snow. He died June 6, 1885.
