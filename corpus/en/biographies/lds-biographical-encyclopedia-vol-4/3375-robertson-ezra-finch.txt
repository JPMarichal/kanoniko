Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robertson, Ezra Finch

Robertson, Ezra Finch, Bishop of the Wanship Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born July 29, 1886, in Lake Shore, Utah, a son of Ezra W. Robertson and Rebecca Ann Finch. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1894, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 5, 1921, by Richard R. Lyman.
