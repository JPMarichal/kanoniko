Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lambert, Joseph Heber

Lambert, Joseph Heber, Bishop of the Roosevelt Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1914 to 1919, was bern Oct. 27, 1856, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of John Lambert and Elina Hansene Larsen. He filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1889-1892, and was ordained a Bishop Oct. 7, 1914, by Francis M. Lyman.
