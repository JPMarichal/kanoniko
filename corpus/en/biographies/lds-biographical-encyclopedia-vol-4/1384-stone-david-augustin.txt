Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stone, David Augustin

Stone, David Augustin, Bishop of the Aberdeen Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born May 18, 1875, in Salina, Utah, the son of Calvin Augustin Stone and Eliza Emeline Taylor. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1883, was ordained a High Priest May 18, 1919, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., and a Bishop Sept. 29, 1929, by Geo. F. Richards.
