Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Welker, John

Welker, John, Bishop of the Layton Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1884 to 1898, was born March 16, 1826, in Maddison Township, Jackson Co., Ohio, a son of James Welker and Elizabeth Stoker. He came to the Valley in 1852, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 26, 1877, by John Taylor, and a Bishop Nov. 4, 1884, by Brigham Young, jun. He died June 1, 1913.
