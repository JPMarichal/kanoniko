Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dodd, Lewis Abel

Dodd, Lewis Abel, presiding Elder of the Hollister Branch, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1926, was born May 24, 1868, a son of Jacob Dodd and Elizabeth Abel. He was baptized Aug. 7, 1892, and sustained April 19, 1925, as presiding Elder.
