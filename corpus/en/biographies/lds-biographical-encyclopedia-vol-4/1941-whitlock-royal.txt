Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whitlock, Royal

Whitlock, Royal, Bishop of the Axtell Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930, was born April 25, 1894, in Mayfield, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of George Q. Whitlock and Matilda Bunderson. He was baptized May 10, 1904, ordained a High Priest May 6, 1923, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 30, 1924, both ordinations by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
