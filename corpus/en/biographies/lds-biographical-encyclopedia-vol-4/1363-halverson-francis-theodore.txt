Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Halverson, Francis Theodore

Halverson, Francis Theodore, Bishop of the Riverside Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born April 5, 1875, in Marriott, Weber Co., Utah, the son of Simon F. Halverson and Ingeborg Fredricksen. He was baptized in May, 1884, by Henry Tracy, and ordained a Bishop May 18, 1924, by Richard R. Lyman.
