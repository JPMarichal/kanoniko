Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Anderson, Ethel Symons

Anderson, Ethel Symons, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born in Salt Lake City, Utah, the daughter of Charles W. Symons and Arzella Whittaker. She was married in the Salt Lake Temple in 1913 to Gerald Christian Anderson, a son of Nephi Anderson, by whom she had one child, a daughter (Helen), and was then left a widow in 1915. She served in several ward organizations of the 10th Ward, and on the Liberty Stake Y. L. M. I. A. Board from 1917 to 1926, when she was called to the General Board. She has been connected with the music department of the Salt Lake City schools for many years.
