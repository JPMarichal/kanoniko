Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kimber, Charles, sen.

Kimber, Charles, sen., Bishop of the Grouse Creek Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1880 to 1895, was born Aug. 31, 1818, in Berkshire, England, a son of James Kimber and Anna Piggott. He was baptized Oct. 5, 1845, came to Utah in 1868, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 15, 1880, by Oliver G. Snow.
