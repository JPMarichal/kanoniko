Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moore, Clarence E.

Moore, Clarence E., Bishop of the Rigby 2nd Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1923 to 1927, was born Feb. 18, 1884, at Wanship, Summit Co., Utah, a son of Charles Moore and Sarah Nixon. He was baptized when about eight years old, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1904-1906, was ordained a High Priest in February, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 22, 1923, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
