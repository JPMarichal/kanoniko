Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Green, George

Green, George, Bishop of the Kanesville Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1923, was born April 22, 1865, in West Weber, Utah, a son of Ammon Green and Almira Mesick. He was baptized in 1874, ordained a High Priest June 9, 1895, by Lewis W. Shurtliff, and a Bishop Aug. 1, 1908, by Geo. F. Richards.
