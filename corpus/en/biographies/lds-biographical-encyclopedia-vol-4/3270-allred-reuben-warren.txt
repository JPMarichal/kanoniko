Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

South Sanpete Stake

Allred, Reuben Warren

Allred, Reuben Warren, Bishop of the Ephraim Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1854 to 1855, was born Nov. 18, 1815, in Bedford County, Tennessee, a son of James Allred and Elizabeth Warren. He came to Utah in 1849, and was appointed Bishop in 1854.
