Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Park, Allen Eugene

Park, Allen Eugene, president of the Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930, was born Oct. 5, 1885, in Mill Creek, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Allen Thomas Park and Jane Lunn. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 6, 1923, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
