Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whiting, Charles, jun.

Whiting, Charles, jun., Bishop of the Vernon Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1922 to 1930+, was born May 25, 1881, in Brigham City, Ariz., the son of Charles Whiting and Verona Snow. He was baptized in 1889, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 1, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
