Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Star Valley Stake

Barrus, Orrin Orlando

Barrus, Orrin Orlando, Bishop of the Fairview Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyoming, from 1913 to 1924, was born May 29, 1870, in Grantsville, Utah, a son of Benjamin F. Barrus and Lavina Steele. He was baptized May 29, 1878, filled two missions, one to Samoa in 1893-1897 and one to the Southern States in 1908-1910, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 1, 1913, by Francis M. Lyman.
