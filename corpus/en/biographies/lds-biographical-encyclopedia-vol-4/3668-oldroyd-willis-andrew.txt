Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Oldroyd, Willis Andrew

Oldroyd, Willis Andrew, Bishop of the. Lyman Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Nov. 18, 1899, in Lyman, Utah, a son of Archibald Oldroyd and Theda Jane Buchanan. He was baptized July 5, 1908, ordained a High Priest March 23, 1924, by Joseph Eckersley, and a Bishop May 3, 1925, by Richard R. Lyman.
