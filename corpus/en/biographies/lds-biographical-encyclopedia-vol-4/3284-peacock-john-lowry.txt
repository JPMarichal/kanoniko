Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peacock, John Lowry

Peacock, John Lowry, Bishop of the Stirling Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1884 to 1891, was born Aug. 5, 1855, at Manti, Utah, the son of George Peacock and Mary A. Lowry. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a High Priest July 11, 1877, by Erastus Snow and appointed Bishop of the Stirling Ward in 1884.
