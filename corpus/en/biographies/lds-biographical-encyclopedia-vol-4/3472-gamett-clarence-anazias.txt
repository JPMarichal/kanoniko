Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gamett, Clarence Anazias

Gamett, Clarence Anazias, Bishop of the Pleasant Grove 1st Ward, Timpanogos Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Dec. 15, 1884, at Pleasant Grove, Utah, a son of Amazias Gamett and Margaret Poulsen. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest April 24, 1927, by Geo. F. Richards and ordained a Bishop Oct. 21, 1928, by David O. McKay.
