Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Greer, Gilbert Dunlap

Greer, Gilbert Dunlap, Bishop of the Luna (Heber) Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1888 to 1895, was born Jan. 20, 1860, in Bosque County, Texas, the son of Thomas L. Greet and Catherine E. Camp. He was baptized May 2, 1878, filled a mission to the Lamanites in 1879-1880, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 9, 1888, by John Henry Smith.
