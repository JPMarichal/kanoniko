Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ensign Stake

Aldous, Heber Kay

Aldous, Heber Kay, Bishop of the 12th-13th Ward, Ensign Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1929, was born June 26, 1877, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Heber B. Aldous and Susan A. Kay. He was baptized in 1885 by John Cottam, ordained a Seventy in 1897 by John Henry Smith, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1897-1901, was ordained a High Priest May 22, 1904, by Richard W. Young, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 5, 1922, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
