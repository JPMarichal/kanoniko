Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, Jesse Soar

Taylor, Jesse Soar, Bishop of the payson 2nd Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1909, was born Aug. 29, 1845, near Eastwood, Nottinghamshire, England, the son of William Jesse Taylor and Mary Soar. He was baptized in 1854, came to Utah in 1856, and filled a mission to the Muddy in 1868-1871. Later he was ordained a High Priest and Bishop. He died Oct. 6, 1933.
