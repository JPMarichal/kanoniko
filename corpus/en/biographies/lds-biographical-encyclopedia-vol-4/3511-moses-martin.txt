Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moses, Martin

Moses, Martin, Bishop of the Lake View Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1888, was born Aug. 26, 1824, in Campsie, Dumbartonshire, Scotland, a son of Peter Martin Moses and Mary Bell. He was baptized Aug. 26, 1845, emigrated to Utah in 1853, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 24, 1877, by John Taylor.
