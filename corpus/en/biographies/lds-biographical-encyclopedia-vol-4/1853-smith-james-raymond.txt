Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, James Raymond

Smith, James Raymond, Bishop of the Archer Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Jan. 7, 1887, in Richmond, Utah, the son of Isaac Smith and Betsy A. Bateman. He was baptized June 5, 1895, ordained an Elder April 21, 1910, by B. F. Zimmerman, and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1910-1912. He was ordained a High Priest Oct. 18, 1914, by James Blake, and a Bishop Sept. 30, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
