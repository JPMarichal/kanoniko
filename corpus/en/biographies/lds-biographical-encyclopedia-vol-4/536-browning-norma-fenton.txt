Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Browning, Norma Fenton

Browning, Norma Fenton, organist of the general board of Primary Association from 1904 to 1909, is a graduate from the kindergarten department of the University of Utah, receiving her diploma in June, 1907. She is an expert pianist and studied music for many years. She is the wife of Wm. J. Browning.
