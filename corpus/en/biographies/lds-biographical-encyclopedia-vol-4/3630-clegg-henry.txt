Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clegg, Henry

Clegg, Henry, Bishop of the Heber West Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1884 to 1894, was born June 7, 1825, in Preston, England. He was baptized in 1837, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 2, 1884, by Francis M. Lyman. He died Aug. 30, 1894.
