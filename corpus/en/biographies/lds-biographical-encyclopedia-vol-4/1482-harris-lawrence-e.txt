Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Lawrence E.

Harris, Lawrence E., Bishop of the Unity Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1920, and from 1925 to 1930, was born July 7, 1881, in Upton, Utah, the son of Jonathan Harris and Dorcas Dann. He was ordained a Bishop July 25, 1915, by Geo. F. Richards, and set apart Feb. 7, 1925, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., to serve a second term.
