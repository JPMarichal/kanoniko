Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cobbley, James

Cobbley, James, Bishop of the Pleasant Grove 2nd Ward (later the Lindon Ward), Timpanogos Stake, Utah, from 1890 to 1904, was born Sept. 21, 1847, at Windwick, Huntingtonshire, England, a son of Thomas Cobbley and Sarah Smith. He was baptized in 1864, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 20, 1890, by Abraham H. Cannon. He died April 8, 1917.
