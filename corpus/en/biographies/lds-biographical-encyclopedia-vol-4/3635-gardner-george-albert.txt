Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gardner, George Albert

Gardner, George Albert, Bishop of the Wallsburg Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1916 to 1930+, was born Feb. 4, 1872, at Wallsburg, Utah, a son of Charles Gardner and Susannah Rigby. He was baptized June 27, 1880, ordained a Seventy July 21, 1907, by John C. Greer, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1910-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 26, 1916, by Rudger Clawson.
