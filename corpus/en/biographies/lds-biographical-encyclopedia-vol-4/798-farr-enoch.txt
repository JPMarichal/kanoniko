Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Farr, Enoch

Farr, Enoch, president of the Hawaiian Mission from 1885 to 1887, was born Dec. 28, 1845, in Nauvoo, Ill., a son of Lorin Farr and Nancy B. Chase. He was baptized in 1854, after coming to Utah with his parents in 1847. Being called and set apart Oct. 13, 1884, for a mission to the Sandwich Islands, he arrived in Honolulu Nov. 8, 1884, and succeeded Elder Edward Partridge as president of the Hawaiian Mission. He returned to Utah May 30, 1887, and died June 30, 1914.
