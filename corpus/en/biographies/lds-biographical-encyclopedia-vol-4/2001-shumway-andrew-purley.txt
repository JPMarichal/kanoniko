Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shumway, Andrew Purley

Shumway, Andrew Purley, Bishop of the Mendon Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1859 to 1869, was born Feb. 20, 1833, at Millbury, Worcester Co., Massachusetts, the son of Charles Shumway and Julia Ann Hooker. He was baptized when about nine years old, by Joseph Smith, the Prophet, filled two missions to England in 1856-1858 and in 1869-1871, and was ordained a Bishop Dec. 19, 1859, by Orson Hyde. He died June 12, 1909.
