Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harrison, Isaac

Harrison, Isaac, presiding Elder at Sandy, Salt Lake Co., Utah, (now in the East Jordan Stake), from 1872 to 1873, was born Nov. 2, 1815, in Columbian Co., Ohio, was baptized April 18, 1837, by Micha C. Melton, and came to Utah in 1858, having served as a member of the Mormon Battalion. He was a somewhat self-appointed presiding Elder, but labored under the direction of the South Cottonwood Ward. He died March. 2, 1894.
