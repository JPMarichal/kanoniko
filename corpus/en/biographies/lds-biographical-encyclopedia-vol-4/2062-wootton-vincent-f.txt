Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wootton, Vincent F.

Wootton, Vincent F., Bishop of the Coltman Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1926, was born Nov. 21, 1881, at Butler, Utah, a son of Charles R. Wootton and Esther R. Ballard. He was baptized April 30, 1890, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 29, 1921, by James E. Talmage.
