Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pack, Ward Eaton

Pack, Ward Eaton, president of the Hawaiian Mission from 1876 to 1878, was born April 17, 1834, in Jefferson County, N.Y., a son of John Pack and Julia Ires. He was baptized April 17, 1842, set apart April 10, 1876, arrived in Honolulu June 1, 1876, and upon the departure of Elder Alma L. Smith, June 2, 1876, assumed the presidency of the Mission. He returned to Utah April 1, 1878. Elder Pack died Nov. 16, 1907, at Vernal, Utah.
