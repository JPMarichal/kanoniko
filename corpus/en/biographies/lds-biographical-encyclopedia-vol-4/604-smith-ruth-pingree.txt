Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Ruth Pingree

Smith, Ruth Pingree, a member of the general board of the Primary Association from 1929 to 1933, was born June 24, 1907, at Coalville, Utah, a daughter of Frank Pingtee and Pauline Taggart. She was baptized June 27, 1915, by Earl Calderwood, graduated from the L. D. S. High School and later received a B.A. degree at the University of Utah. While at the L. D. S. High School she received the Heber J. Grant award for scholarship. She was organist of the Sunday School and Primary Association of the 11th Ward, Salt Lake City, for a time, organist of the University Ward from 1925 to 1930, counselor in the Ensign Stake Y. L. M. I. A. in 1927-1929, and president of the University Ward Y. L. M. I. A. from 1933 to 1936. On June 5, 1929, she was married to Joseph Fielding Smith (son of Hyrum M. Smith and Ida Bowman) and is the mother of three children, namely, Ruth, Ida and Raoul. At present she is teacher of the literary department of the branch of the Church at Madison, Wisconsin, where her husband is studying art.
