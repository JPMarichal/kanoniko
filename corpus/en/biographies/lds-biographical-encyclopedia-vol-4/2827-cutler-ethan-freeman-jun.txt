Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cutler, Ethan Freeman, jun.

Cutler, Ethan Freeman, jun., Bishop of the Inkom Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1914 to 1921, was born July 23, 1877, at Brigham City, Utah, a son of Ethan F. Cutler and Lucy Ann Williams. He was baptized July 4, 1885, ordained a High Priest July 1, 1901, by Noah S. Pond, and a Bishop Feb. 15, 1914, by Geo. F. Richards.
