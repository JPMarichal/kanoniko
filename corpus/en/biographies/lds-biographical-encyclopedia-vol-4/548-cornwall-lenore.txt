Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cornwall, Lenore

Cornwall, Lenore, a member of the general board of the Primary Association since 1932, is a daughter of Alma M. Cornwall and Lenora Jane Hill. She graduated from the Granite High School, Salt Lake Co., Utah, in 1921, received a B.A. degree from the University of Utah and taught school for some time. She was organist and teacher in a ward Primary Association, officer in the Y. L. M. I. A., and later a member of a stake Y. L. M. I. A. board. She filled a mission in California from June, 1930, to August, 1932, during which she served as mission supervisor of Primary Associations and president of the Y. L. M. I. A.
