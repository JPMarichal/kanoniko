Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, William Weldon

Taylor, William Weldon, president (pro tem.) of the Australian Mission from 1913 to 1914, was born Dec. 9, 1883, in Provo, Utah, the son of William Joseph Taylor and Abby Jane Scott. He was baptized June 25, 1891, set apart for his mission to Australia June 5, 1912, and returned Sept. 22, 1914.
