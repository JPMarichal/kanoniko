Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beesley, Robert Heber

Beesley, Robert Heber, Bishop of the Sunset Ward, North Davis Stake, Utah, in 1917, was born Nov. 15, 1865, in Kaysville, Utah, the son of William Beesley and Hannah Flint. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1875, ordained a High Priest May 18, 1916, by David O. McKay, and a Bishop Jan. 21, 1917, by Heber J. Grant. He died June 24, 1917.
