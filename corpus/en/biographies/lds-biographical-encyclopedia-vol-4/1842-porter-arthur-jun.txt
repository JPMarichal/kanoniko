Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Porter, Arthur, jun.

Porter, Arthur, jun., second counselor in the Fremont Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born April 20, 1876, in Auckland, New Zealand, the son of Arthur Porter and Louisa Koebel. He came to Utah in 1885, and after being ordained to the priesthood he filled a mission to Switzerland in 1896-1899. He was ordained a High Priest and set apart as second counselor in the Fremont Stake presidency Sept. 27, 1925, by Melvin J. Ballard.
