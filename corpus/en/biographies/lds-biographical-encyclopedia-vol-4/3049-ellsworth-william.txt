Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ellsworth, William,

Ellsworth, William, first counselor in the St. Joseph Stake presidency, Arizona, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Aug. 17, 1872, in Leeds, Utah, a son of James Henry Ellsworth and Eliza R. Snow McCleave. He was baptized Aug. 17, 1880, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1916-1918, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 12, 1910, by E. S. Carpenter, and set apart as first counselor in the Stake presidency Sept. 9, 1928, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
