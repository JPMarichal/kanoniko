Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jenson, Andrew

Jenson, Andrew, a member of the Old Folks Central Committee since 1883, secretary from 1883 to 1915, and vice chairman since 1915, was born Dec. 11, 1850, in Torslev parish, Hjorring amt, Denmark, a son of Christian Jensen and Kirsten Andersen. (See Bio Ency., Vol. 1, p. 261.)
