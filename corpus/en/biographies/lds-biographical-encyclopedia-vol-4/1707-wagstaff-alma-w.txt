Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wagstaff, Alma W.

Wagstaff, Alma W., Bishop of the Tabiona Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1920, was born Sept. 18, 1862, in Salt Lake City, the son of William Wagstaff and Emily Limb. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 23, 1916, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
