Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beardall, John William

Beardall, John William, Bishop of the Parleys Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930, was born Dec. 12, 1868, in Calverton, Nottinghamshire, England, the son of John Beardall and Sarah Stevenson. He was ordained a Bishop April 2, 1924, by Richard R. Lyman.
