Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stuart, William C.

Stuart, William C., Bishop of the North Pocatello Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1925, was born March 20, 1895, at American Fork, Utah, a son of James J. Stuart and Grace Waterfall. He was baptized in the year 1903, ordained a High Priest May 26, 1919, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., and a Bishop Nov. 28, 1920, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
