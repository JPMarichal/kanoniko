Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Joseph F.

Smith, Joseph F., president of British Mission from 1874 to 1875 and from 1877 to 1878. (See Bio Ency., Vol. 1, p. 66, and Vol. 3, p. 78.)

Joseph F. Smith presided over the Church from Oct. 17, 1901 to Nov. 19, 1918. On the latter date he died in Salt Lake City.
