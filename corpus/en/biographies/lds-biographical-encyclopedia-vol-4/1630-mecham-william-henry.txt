Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mecham, William Henry

Mecham, William Henry, Bishop of the Park Valley Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1892 to 1899, was born Dec. 31, 1852, in Little Cottonwood, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Erastus D. Mecham and Martha Jones. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 25, 1892, by Lorenzo Snow, and died in Logan in February, 1922.
