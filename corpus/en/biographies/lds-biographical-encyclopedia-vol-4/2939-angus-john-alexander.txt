Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Angus, John Alexander

Angus, John Alexander, Bishop of the Ioka Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1920, was born Sept. 14, 1862, in Spanish Fork, Utah Co., Utah, a son of John Angus and Betsy Hislop. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1872, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 23, 1912, by David O. McKay.
