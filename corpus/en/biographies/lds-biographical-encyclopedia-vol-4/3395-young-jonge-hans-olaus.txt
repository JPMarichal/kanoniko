Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Young (Jonge), Hans Olaus

Young (Jonge), Hans Olaus, Bishop of the Parley's Park Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1898 to 1901, was born Oct. 21, 1838, in Frederikstad, Norway, the son of Hans Olsen Jonge and Anna A. Sandberg. He was baptized April 24, 1857, came to Utah that same year, was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 8, 1898, by John Henry Smith, and filled a mission to Scafidinavia in 1900-1901. He died March 23, 1919.
