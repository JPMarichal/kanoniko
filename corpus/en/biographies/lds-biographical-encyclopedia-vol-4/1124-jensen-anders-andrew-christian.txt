Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Anders (Andrew) Christian

Jensen, Anders (Andrew) Christian, Bishop of the Aetna Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1920 to 1930+, was born Oct. 4, 1868, in Aasted, Hjorring amt, Denmark, the son of Hans Christian Jensen and Caroline M. Andreasen. He was baptized Dec. 25, 1882, emigrated to Utah in 1886, and later settled in Canada. He was ordained a High Priest in 1902 by Edw. J. Wood and a Bishop May 2, 1920, by Rudger Clawson.
