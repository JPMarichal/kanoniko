Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomas, Martin Adams

Thomas, Martin Adams, Bishop of the Downey Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1923, was born Nov. 4, 1875, in Richmond, Utah, a son of Martin W. Thomas and Eliza Adams. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a Seventy in 1907, by Charles H. Hart, and filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1908-1909. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 9, 1917, by Hyrum M. Smith.
