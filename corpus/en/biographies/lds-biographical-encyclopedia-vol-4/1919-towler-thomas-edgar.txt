Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Towler, Thomas Edgar

Towler, Thomas Edgar, Bishop of the Farmers Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1924, and of the Whittier Ward from 1924 to 1930+, was born Sept. 3, 1885, in Mill Creek, Utah, the son of Daniel Towler and Sarah A. Durnford. He was baptized Oct. 5, 1893, ordained an Elder in 1906, by Ben R. Eldredge, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1906-1908, was ordained a High Priest by Thos. J. Yates and ordained a Bishop Sept. 10, 1923, by Richard R. Lyman.
