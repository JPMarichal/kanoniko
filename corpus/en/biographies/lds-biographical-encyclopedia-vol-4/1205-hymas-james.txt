Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hymas, James

Hymas, James, Bishop of the Lanark Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1904 to 1908, was horn March 14, 1849, in Raleigh, Essex, England, the son of William Hymas and Mary Ann Alkins. He emigrated to America in 1861, arrived in Utah in 1862, located in Bear Lake Valley in 1864, and was ordained a Bishop in 1904.
