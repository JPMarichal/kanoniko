Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jenson, Nils Lorenzo

Jenson, Nils Lorenzo, Bishop of the Grant Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930, was born April 10, 1887, in West Jordan, Utah, the son of Nils Jenson and Maria Johnson. He was baptized July 10, 1895, was ordained a High Priest Mar. 12, 1922, by Wm. B. Erekson, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 16, 1924, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
