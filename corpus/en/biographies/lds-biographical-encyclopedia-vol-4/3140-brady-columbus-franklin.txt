Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

San Luis Stake

Brady, Columbus Franklin

Brady, Columbus Franklin, Bishop of the Manassa Ward, San Luis Stake, Colorado, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Aug. 16, 1893, in Manassa, Colorado, the son of Christopher C. Brady and Nancy Raynor. He was baptized June 1, 1902, ordained a High Priest Sept. 19, 1926, by Stephen L. Richards, and a Bishop Sept. 22, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
