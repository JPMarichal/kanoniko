Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lazenby, Moroni

Lazenby, Moroni, president of the North Sevier Stake of Zion, Utah, from 1921 to 1924, was born Jan. 12, 1872, in Minersville, Utah, a son of John Thomas Lazenby and Anna Tether. He was baptized when nine years old, filled a mission to Australasia in 1894-1897, was ordained a High Priest by Francis M. Lyman, and was set apart as president of the North Sevier Stake Jan. 20, 1921, by Geo. F. Richards.
