Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stewart, William Thomas

Stewart, William Thomas, president of the New Zealand part of the Australasian Mission, from 1883 to 1886, and from 1891 to 1893, was born Oct. 18, 1853, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Levi Stewart and Marjorie Wilkerson. He was baptized in November, 1861, was active in the Church, as well as being a prominent member of the southern Utah community, served on three missions for the Church, the first among the Indians in the southwest, and two later missions in New Zealand, being set apart for the first one April 30, 1883, and returning May 25, 1886. He arrived in New Zealand on his second mission Aug. 13, 1891, to act as president of the Australasian Mission, and left there Dec. 30, 1893, to return home. Elder Stewart died Aug. 22, 1935, in Alamo, Nevada.
