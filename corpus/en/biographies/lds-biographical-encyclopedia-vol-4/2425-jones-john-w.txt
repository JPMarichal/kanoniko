Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, John W.

Jones, John W., Bishop of the Montpelier 4th Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1929, was born Sept. 8, 1890, in Minersville, Utah, the son of John F. Jones and Altheda Ennes. He was ordained a High Priest Jan. 2, 1921, by Edward C. Rich, and a Bishop March 11, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards.
