Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wallace, Elen

Wallace, Elen, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born in Salt Lake City, Utah, the daughter of Henry Wallace and Elen Harper. She was educated in the public schools, the L. D. S. University and the University of Utah, served in the presidency of the Y. L. M. I. A. in the 7th Ward, was a member of the Salt Lake Stake Board for one year and in 1904 became a member of the General Board. In 1905 she was chosen to act as a member of the first Bee-Hive Committee, of the first Summer Home Committee, and of the M. I. A. Colors Committee. She saw the beginning of numerous movements which have since become far-reaching.
