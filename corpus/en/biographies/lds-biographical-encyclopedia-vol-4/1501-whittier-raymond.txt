Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whittier, Raymond

Whittier, Raymond, Bishop of the Delco Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1929, was born Oct. 6, 1888, in Milton, Utah, the son of James Whittier and Annie Madson. He was baptized in August, 1895, ordained a High Priest Feb. 8, 1925, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop Nov. 6, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
