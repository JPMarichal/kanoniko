Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Godfrey, James Clifton

Godfrey, James Clifton, Bishop of the Ucon Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1913 to 1914, was born Aug. 23, 1873, in Murray, Utah, a son of James Godfrey and Sophia Clifton. He was baptized Aug. 21, 1881, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1909-1911, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 4, 1913, by Hyrum M. Smith. He died Dec. 14, 1914, in Ucon, Idaho.
