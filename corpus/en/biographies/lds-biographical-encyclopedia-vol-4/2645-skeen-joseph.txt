Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Skeen, Joseph

Skeen, Joseph, Bishop of the Warren Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born April 29, 1876, in Plain City, Utah, a son of Lyman S. Skeen and Electa P. Dixon. He was baptized July 3, 1884, and after being ordained a Seventy June 16, 1897, by Brigham H. Roberts, he filled a mission to the Southern States in 1897-1899. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 12, 1909, by John V. Bluth, and a Bishop June 21, 1925, by Reed Smoot.
