Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dean, Joseph Henry

Dean, Joseph Henry, president of the Samoan Mission from 1888 to 1890, was born Oct. 16, 1855, in Taunton, Somersetshire, England, a son of Joseph Dean and Catherine Knott. He was baptized in 1863, filled a mission to the Sandwich Islands in 1877-1881, was called again to fill a mission to the Sandwich Islands, being set apart May 21, 1887, and was called from Hawaii June 18, 1888, to open up the work in Samoa. He arrived in Samoa June 17, 1888, with his wife and child, and was released July 14, 1890, sailing for home Aug. 16, 1890.
