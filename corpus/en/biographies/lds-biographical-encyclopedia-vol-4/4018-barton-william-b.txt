Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barton, William B.

Barton, William B., a member of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1903 to 1923, was born July 21, 1836, at Sutton Mill, on the Douglas river, near Wigan, Lancashire, England, a son of John Barten and Elizabeth Bell. He died Oct. 1, 1923, in Salt Lake City, Utah. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 667.)
