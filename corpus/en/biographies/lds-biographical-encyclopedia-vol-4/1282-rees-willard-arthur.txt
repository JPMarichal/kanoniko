Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rees, Willard Arthur

Rees, Willard Arthur, Bishop of the Adamsville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1923, was born Jan. 27, 1876, in Adamsville, Utah, the son of Watkin Rees and Jane Williams. He was baptized May 18, 1884, ordained a High Priest April 18, 1915, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., and ordained a Bishop July 20, 1919, by Richard R. Lyman.
