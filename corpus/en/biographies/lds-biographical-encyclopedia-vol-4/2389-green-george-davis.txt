Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Green, George Davis

Green, George Davis, Bishop of the Paul Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Oct. 17, 1892, in Kanesville, Utah, a son of George Green and Charlotte I. Bartholomew. He was baptized July 6, 1901, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1919-1920, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 22, 1921, by Richard R. Lyman.
