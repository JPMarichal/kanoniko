Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beckstrom, William Charles

Beckstrom, William Charles, Bishop of the Spanish Fork 1st Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1918 to 1930, was born June 5, 1882, at Spanish Fork, Utah, the son of John N. Beckstrom and Mary C. Hansen. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1891, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 2, 1918, by Richard R. Lyman.
