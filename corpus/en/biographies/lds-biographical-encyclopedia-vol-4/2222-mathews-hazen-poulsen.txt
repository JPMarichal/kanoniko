Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mathews, Hazen Poulsen

Mathews, Hazen Poulsen, Bishop of the Providence 2nd Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1927, was born May 24, 1887, in Providence, Utah, a son of Alma Morris Mathews and Sophie Poulsen. He was baptized June 11, 1895, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1912-1914, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 14, 1924, by James E. Talmage.
