Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Scofield, Richard

Scofield, Richard, Bishop of the West Layton Ward, North Davis Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1924, was born Aug. 16, 1871, in Harlaxton, Lincolnshire, England, the son of George Scofield and Jane Barsby. He was baptized in 1883, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 24, 1919, by David O. McKay.
