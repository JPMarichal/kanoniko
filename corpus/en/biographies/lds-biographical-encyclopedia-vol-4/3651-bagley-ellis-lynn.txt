Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bagley, Ellis Lynn

Bagley, Ellis Lynn, Bishop of the Loa Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Oct. 20, 1898, in Koosharem, Utah, a son of Edward Carroll Bagley and Pauline Martinsen. He was baptized July 7, 1907, ordained an Elder Nov. 10, 1922, by his father, filled a mission to the Western States in 1922-1925, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 9, 1930, by Geo. W. 0kerlund, and a Bishop July 6, 1930, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
