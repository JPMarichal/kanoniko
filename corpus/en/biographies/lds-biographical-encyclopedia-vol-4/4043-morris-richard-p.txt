Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Morris, Richard P.

Morris, Richard P., a member of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1924 to 1925, was born Dec. 23, 1855, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Richard V. Morris and Hannah Phillips. He died April 2, 1925, in Salt Lake City, Utah.
