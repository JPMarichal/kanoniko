Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Asahel Henry

Smith, Asahel Henry, Bishop of the Hunt Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1910 to, 1917, was born Dec. 5, 1880, in Snowflake, Arizona, a son of Jesse N. Smith and Augusta Outsen. He was ordained a Bishop May 25, 1910, by Francis M. Lyman.
