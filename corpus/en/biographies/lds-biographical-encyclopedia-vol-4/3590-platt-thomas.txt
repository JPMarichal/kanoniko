Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Platt, Thomas

Platt, Thomas, Bishop of the Summerville Ward, Union Stake, Oregon, from 1903 to 1906, was born Oct. 9, 1869, in Pinto, Washington Co., Utah, a son of Benjamin Platt and Mary Graves. He was baptized Oct. 9, 1877, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1898-1901, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 7, 1903, by Pres. Joseph F. Smith.
