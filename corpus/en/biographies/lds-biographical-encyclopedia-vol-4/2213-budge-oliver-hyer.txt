Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Budge, Oliver Hyer

Budge, Oliver Hyer, president of the Logan Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930, was born April 3, 1872, in Paris, Idaho, a son of William Budge and Ann Hyer. He was baptized when nine years old, ordained a Seventy Oct. 7, 1896, by Christian D. Fjeldsted and filled a mission to Switzerland and Germany in 1896-1899. He was set apart as stake president June 5, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
