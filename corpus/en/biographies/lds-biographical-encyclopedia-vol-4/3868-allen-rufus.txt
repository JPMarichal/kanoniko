Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Allen, Rufus

Allen, Rufus, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born March 22, 1814, in Litchfield County, Conn., a son of Gideon Allen and Rachel Hand. He returned to Winter Quarters with Pres. Brigham Young the same fall and after his arrival in the Valley a second time located in Ogden, Weber County, where he died during the winter of 1888-1889. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 583.)
