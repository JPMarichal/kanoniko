Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lundquist, Joseph Lavern

Lundquist, Joseph Lavern, Bishop of the La Belle Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1928 to 1930 +, was born Oct. 28, 1903, at La Belle, Idaho, a son of Frank Lundquist and Matilda Matson. He was baptized July 5, 1913, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 25, 1928, by George Albert Smith.
