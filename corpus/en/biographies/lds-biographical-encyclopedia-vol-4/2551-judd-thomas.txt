Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Judd, Thomas

Judd, Thomas, Bishop of the Lund Ward, Nevada Stake, Nevada, from 1901 to 1902, was born Sept. 1, 1845, in Birkenhead, Cheshire, England, the son of Samuel Judd and Catherine Hindes. He was baptized April 26, 1864, acted as Bishop in the White Pine Ward from Aug. 18 to Oct. 13, 1901, and then presided over the Lund Ward. He died June 7, 1922.
