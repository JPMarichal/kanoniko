Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rymer, Sidney Childers

Rymer, Sidney Childers, Bishop of the Torrey Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1929, was born Feb. 24, 1885, in Troup, Smith Co., Texas, the son of George L. Rymer and Elizabeth C. Taylor. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 21, 1927, by Stephen L. Richards.
