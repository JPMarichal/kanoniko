Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wakefield, Lansing Ira

Wakefield, Lansing Ira, Bishop of the Taylor Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1920 to 1925, was born Nov. 8, 1878, in Brigham City, Ariz., a son of Joseph Buck Wakefield and Aretha M. Bates. He was baptized Nov. 14, 1886, ordained a High Priest Jan. 18, 1920, by Samuel F. Smith, and a Bishop July 24, 1920, by 0rson F. Whitney.
