Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sproul, Andrew

Sproul, Andrew, Bishop of the Washington Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1888 to 1908, was born Aug. 28, 1842, in Paisley, Renfrew, Scotland, a son of Andrew Sproul and Mary McSpurrin. He was baptized in Scotland, came to Utah in 1862, from St..Louis, Mo., and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 17, 1888, by John W. Taylor. He died April 25, 1886.
