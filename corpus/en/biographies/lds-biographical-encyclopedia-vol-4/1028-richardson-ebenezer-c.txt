Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Richardson, Ebenezer C.

Richardson, Ebenezer C., president of the South African Mission from 1857 to 1861, was called at the April conference, 1857, in Salt Lake City, Utah, to fill a mission to Europe. He arrived in Liverpool Aug. 4, 1857, and was appointed Aug. 22, 1857, to labor in the Cape of Good Hope Colony, South Africa. He sailed for Africa Sept. 29, 1857.
