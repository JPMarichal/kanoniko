Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Remington, Fuller Rasmussen

Remington, Fuller Rasmussen, Bishop of the Naples Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1922, was born Jan. 17, 1891, in Merrill (Naples), Utah, a son of Joseph Fuller Remington and Amanda Rasmussen. He was baptized in January, 1899, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 21, 1919, by Geo. F. Richards.
