Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whiting, Lorenzo Snow

Whiting, Lorenzo Snow, Bishop of the Crystal Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1923 to 1930 +, was born Dec. 13, 1883, in Springville, Utah, a son of Lorenzo S. Whiting and Flora Waterman. He was set apart as Bishop Aug. 12, 1923, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
