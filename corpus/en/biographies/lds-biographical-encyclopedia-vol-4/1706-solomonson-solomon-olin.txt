Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Solomonson, Solomon Olin

Solomonson, Solomon Olin, Bishop of the Arcadia Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1923, was born Aug. 3, 1877, at Norre Hald, Aalborg amt, Denmark, the son of Johan Erik Solomonsen and Else Christensen. He came to Utah in 1888, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 19, 1915, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop Sept. 15, 1917, by Geo. Albert Smith.
