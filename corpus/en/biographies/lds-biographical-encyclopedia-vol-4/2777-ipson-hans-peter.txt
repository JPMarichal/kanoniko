Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ipson, Hans Peter

Ipson, Hans Peter, Bishop of the Panguitch North Ward, Panguitch Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1930+, was born May 13, 1877, in Panguitch, a son of Niels Peter Ipson and Inger K. Madsen. He was baptized June 14, 1885, ordained a High Priest Sept. 4, 1913, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop May 28, 1916, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
