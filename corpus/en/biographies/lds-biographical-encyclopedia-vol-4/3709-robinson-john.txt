Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robinson, John

Robinson, John, Bishop of the West Jordan Ward, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1852 to 1853, was born Dec. 27, 1792, in Cushing, Lincoln (now Knox) Co., Missouri. He emigrated to Utah in 1847, and was ordained a Bishop Jan. 19, 1852.
