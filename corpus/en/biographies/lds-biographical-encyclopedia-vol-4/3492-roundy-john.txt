Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Roundy, John

Roundy, John, Bishop of the Knightsville Ward, Tintic Stake, Juab Co., Utah, from 1901 to 1909, was born May 18, 1864, at Springville, Utah, a son of Loren H. Roundy and Jane Koyle. He was baptized June 5, 1898, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 4, 1901, by Reed Smoot.
