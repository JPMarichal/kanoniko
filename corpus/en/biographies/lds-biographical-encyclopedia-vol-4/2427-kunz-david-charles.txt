Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kunz, David Charles

Kunz, David Charles, Bishop of the Montpelier 2rid Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1927, was born Dec. 5, 1881, in Nounan, Idaho, the son of Christian Kunz and Caroline Buhler. He was ordained a Bishop March 10, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards.
