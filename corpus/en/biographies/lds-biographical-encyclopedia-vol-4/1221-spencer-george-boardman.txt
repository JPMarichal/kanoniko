Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Spencer, George Boardman

Spencer, George Boardman, Bishop of the Paris 1st Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1881 to 1885, was born Feb. 21, 1840, in Middlefield, Mass., the son of Orson Spencer and Catherine Curtis. He was baptized in 1849, moved to Paris in 1874, was chosen as Bishop in 1882 and served until 1885. He was ordained a High Priest in 1873, filled a mission to Switzerland in 1864-1867, and from 1867 to 1871 filled a special mission to the Muddy Valley. On Aug. 7, 1881, he was ordained a Bishop by John Taylor.
