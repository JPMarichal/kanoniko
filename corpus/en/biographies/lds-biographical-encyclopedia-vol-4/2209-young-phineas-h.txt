Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Young, Phineas H.

Young, Phineas H., Bishop of the Salt Lake City 2nd Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1864 to 1871, was born Feb. 16, 1799, in Hopkinton, Middlesex Co., Mass., the son of John Young and Nabby Howe. He was baptized April 5, 1832, and filled several missions for the Church. He came to Utah in July, 1847, and was ordained a Bishop Dec. 1, 1864. He died Oct. 10, 1879.
