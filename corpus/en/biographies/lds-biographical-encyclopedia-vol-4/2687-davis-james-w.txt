Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davis, James W.

Davis, James W., Bishop of the Clifton Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1911 to 1914, was born Dec. 23, 1882, at Clifton, Idaho, a son of Riley Davis and Jenette McMurrin. He was baptized May 6, 1891, ordained a High Priest July 2, 1905, by Chas. D. Conslind, and a Bishop June 11, 1911, by David O. McKay. He died in May, 1914, in Salt Lake City.
