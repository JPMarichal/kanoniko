Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ward, George Plant

Ward, George Plant, Bishop of the Iona Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1895 to 1896, was born Aug. 20, 1854, in Walton, Northamtonshire, England, a son of George P. Ward and Sarah Plant. He was baptized in June, 1868, and ordained a Bishop June 9, 1895, by John Henry Smith. He died Aug. 17, 1900.
