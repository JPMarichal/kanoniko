Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Northwestern States Mission

Presidents: George C. Parkinson, general director, 1897-1898; Lewis S. Pond, local assistant to President Parkinson; Franklin S. Bramwell, 1898-1902; Nephi Pratt, 1902-1909; Melvin J. Ballard, 1909-1919; Heber C. Iverson, 1919-1923; Brigham S. Young, 1923-1927; William R. Sloan, 1927-1934.

Ballard, Melvin J.

Ballard, Melvin J., president of the Northwestern States Mission from 1909 to 1919. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 419, and Vol. 3, p. 744.)
