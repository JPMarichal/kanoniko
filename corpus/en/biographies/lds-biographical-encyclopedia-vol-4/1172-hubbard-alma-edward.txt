Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hubbard, Alma Edward

Hubbard, Alma Edward, Bishop of the Bench Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1907 to 1915, was born April 13, 1874, in Willard, Box Elder Co., Utah, the son of Charles W. Hubbard and Mary Edwards. He was ordained a High Priest May 1, 1894.
