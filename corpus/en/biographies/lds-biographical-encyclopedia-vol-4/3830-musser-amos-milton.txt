Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Musser, Amos Milton

Musser, Amos Milton, treasurer of the Genealogical Society of Utah from 1894 to 1909 and vice-president of that organization from 1907 to 1909, was born May 20, 1830, in Donegal township, Lancaster Co., Pennsylvania, and died Sept. 24, 1909, in Salt Lake City, Utah. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 381; Vol. 3, p. 765.)
