Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thatcher, Nathan Davis

Thatcher, Nathan Davis, Bishop of the Thatcher Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1907 to 1912, was born Oct. 3, 1867, in Logan, Utah, the son of John B. Thatcher and Rachel Hanna Davis. He was baptized by Christian Larsen, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 11, 1907, by Geo. F. Richards.
