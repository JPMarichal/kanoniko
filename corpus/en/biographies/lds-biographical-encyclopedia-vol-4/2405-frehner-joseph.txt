Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Frehner, Joseph

Frehner, Joseph, presiding Elder of the Littlefield Branch, Moapa Stake, Nevada, from 1916 to 1921, was born May 18, 1886, in Littlefield, Arizona, a son of Henry Frehner and Henrietta Reber. He was baptized Oct. 13, 1894, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission in the Central States in 1912-1914, and was ordained a High Priest Dec. 2, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
