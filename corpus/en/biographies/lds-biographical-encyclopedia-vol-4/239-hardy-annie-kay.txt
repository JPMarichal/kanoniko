Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hardy, Annie Kay

Hardy, Annie Kay, a member of of the General Board of Relief Society, was born Feb. 1, 1855, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a daughter of John M. Kay and Ellen Pardington. She was among the first to graduate from the University of Deseret, and was a woman of great intelligence, had a kindergarten school at her home when only 12 years old, and when quite young taught school in the old 12th Ward. She was a member of the General Board of Primary from 1896 to 1899, secretary of the Ensign Stake Relief Society from 1904 to 1912, and was appointed a member of the General Board of Relief Society in 1909. She was also a member of the board of trustees of the Agricultural College of Utah and of the Reapers' Club of Salt Lake City. Sister Hardy was secretary and treasurer of the Utah. Commission for the adult blind and an active worker in that organization. As a young woman she was married to Rufus H. Hardy and became the mother of three sons.
