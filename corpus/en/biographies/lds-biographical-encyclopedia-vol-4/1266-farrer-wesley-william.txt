Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Farrer, Wesley William

Farrer, Wesley William, Bishop of the Beaver West Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born April 29, 1886, in Beaver, the son of James Wesley Farrer and Mary Smith. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1894, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1908-1910, was ordained a High Priest July 20, 1913, by Patriarch Hyrum G. Smith, and ordained a Bishop by Geo. F. Richards Oct. 10, 1925.
