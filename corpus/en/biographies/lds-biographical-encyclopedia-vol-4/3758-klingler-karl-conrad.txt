Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Klingler, Karl Conrad

Klingler, Karl Conrad, second counselor in the presidency of the Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Nov. 30, 1891, at Rexburg, Fremont Co., Idaho, a son of Frederick L. Klingler and Christine G. Walz. He was baptized when eight years of age, and ordained a High Priest June 5, 1921, by C. E. Lloyd.
