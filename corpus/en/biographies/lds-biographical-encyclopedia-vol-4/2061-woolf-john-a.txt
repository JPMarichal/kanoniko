Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Woolf, John A.

Woolf, John A., Bishop of the Ucon Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1909 to 1913, was born Nov. 22, 1861, in Moroni, Utah, the son of James WooIf and Malinda J. Bradley. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1871, and was ordained a Bishop May 9, 1909, by Orson F. Whitney.
