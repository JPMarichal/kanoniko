Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lunt, John E.

Lunt, John E., first counselor in the Juab Stake presidency, Utah, from 1914 to 1924, was born May 3, 1880, in Nephi, Juab Co., Utah, a son of Alfred Lunt and Pricilla Pitt. He waS baptized Sept. 6, 1888, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1900-1902, was ordained a High Priest May 6, 1906, by Stirling Williams, and set apart as first counselor Aug. 9, 1914, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
