Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Facer, George

Facer, George, Bishop of the Willard Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1882 to 1892, was born July 4, 1834, in Eynesbury, Huntingdonshire, England, the son of Henry Facer and Mary Jarvis. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1854, by James B. Price, came to Utah in 1860, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 9, 1877, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 3, 1882, by Lorenzo Snow.
