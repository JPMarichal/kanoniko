Christensen, Olive Derbridge

Christensen, Olive Derbridge, a member of the general board of the Primary Association, was sustained as assistant secretary Sept. 24, 1897, and as general secretary Dec. 29, 1905. She was honorably released from membership on the board May 31, 1911. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 4, p. 89.)
