Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barton, Walter K.

Barton, Walter K., president of the Franklin Stake of Zion, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Dec. 14, 1877, in Sterling, Utah, the son of Walter Kilshaw Barton and Susan Angeline Petty. He was baptized July 1, 1886, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 30, 1911, by James Bodily, acted as second counselor in the Oneida Stake presidency from 1920 to 1922, and as first counselor from 1922 to 1927. He was chosen as president of the Franklin Stake Nov. 27, 1927.
