Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rasmussen, Peter

Rasmussen, Peter, Bishop of the Salina Ward, North Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1864 to 1867, was born Dec. 31, 1835, in Thorslunde, Holbak amt, Denmark, a son of Rasmus Rasmussen and Katrine Andersen. He was baptized March 19, 1852, emigrated to Utah in 1853, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 12, 1864, by Orson Hyde. He died Feb. 7, 1926.
