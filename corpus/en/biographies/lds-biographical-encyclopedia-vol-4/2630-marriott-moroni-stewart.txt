Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Marriott, Moroni Stewart

Marriott, Moroni Stewart, Bishop of the Marriott Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1902 to 1908, was born Oct. 31, 1857, in Marriott, a son of John Marriott and Elizabeth Stewart. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1866, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1885-1888, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 25, 1902, by Jos. F. Smith.
