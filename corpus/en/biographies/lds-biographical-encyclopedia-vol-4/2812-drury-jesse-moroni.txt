Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Drury, Jesse Moroni

Drury, Jesse Moroni, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 5th Ward, Pioneer Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Nov. 11, 1885, in Mill Creek, Utah, the son of Thomas Drury and Sarah Ann Morris. He was baptized June 4, 1895, and ordained a Bishop April 27, 1930, by Geo. F. Richards.
