Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Frandsen, James Ernest

Frandsen, James Ernest, Bishop of the Redmond Ward, North Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1924, was born Dec. 12, 1887, in Redmond, Utah, a son of James Fraudsen and Marinda Snow. He was baptized when nine years old, and after being ordained an Elder by Wm. H. Seegmiller in 1906, he filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1906-1908. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 17, 1921, by Hyrum G. Smith.
