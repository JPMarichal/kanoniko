Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Saxton, John Solomon

Saxton, John Solomon, Bishop of the Upton Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1910, was born March 7, 1860, in Henry County, Ill., a son of Solomon Saxton and Matilda Dexter. He was baptized Oct. 2, 1870, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 23, 1901, by Reed Smoot.
