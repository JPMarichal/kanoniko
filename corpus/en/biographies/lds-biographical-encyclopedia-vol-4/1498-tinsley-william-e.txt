Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tinsley, William E.

Tinsley, William E., Bishop of the Unity Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1925, was born May 16, 1887, at West Weber, Utah, the son of Edwin Tinsley and Amanda Cole. He was ordained a Bishop Aug. 14, 1921, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
