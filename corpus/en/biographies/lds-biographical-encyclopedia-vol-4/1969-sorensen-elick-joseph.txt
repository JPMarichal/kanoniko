Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sorensen, Elick Joseph

Sorensen, Elick Joseph, Bishop of the Mar Vista Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1928 to 1930, was born Nov. 15, 1891, in Bear River City, Utah, the son of Joseph Sorensen and Selena Sohlean. He was baptized May 6, 1900, and ordained a High Priest in 1928 by Stephen L. Richards.
