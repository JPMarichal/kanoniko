Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Call, Elbert Vanson

Call, Elbert Vanson, Bishop of the Wapello Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1916 to 1920, was born Aug. 3, 1881, in Bountiful, Davis Co., Utah, the son of Chester V. Call and Annie J. Barlow. He was baptized by Truman H. Barlow, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 14, 1915, by Orson F. Whitney.
