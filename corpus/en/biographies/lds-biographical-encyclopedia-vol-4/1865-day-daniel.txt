Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Day, Daniel

Day, Daniel, Bishop of the Marion Ward, Garfield Stake, Piute Co., Utah, from 1922 to 1927, was born July 8, 1872, at Kanarra, Iron Co., Utah, the son of Thomas Day and Charlotte N. Clark. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest Nov. 27, 1920, by David A. Smith, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 26, 1922, by Stephen L. Richards.
