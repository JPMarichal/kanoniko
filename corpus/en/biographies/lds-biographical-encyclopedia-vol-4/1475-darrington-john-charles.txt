Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Darrington, John Charles

Darrington, John Charles, Bishop of the Declo Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Aug. 12, 1880, at Elba, Idaho, the son of John Darrington and Sarah Jane Lowe. He was baptized Aug. 20, 1888, ordained a Seventy Oct. 9, 1900, and filled a mission to Colorado in 1900-1903. He acted as presiding Elder from 1913 until the ward was organized, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 13, 1916, by Wm. T. Harper, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 17, 1929, by Stephen L. Richards.
