Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Siddoway, Robert

Siddoway, Robert, Bishop of the Rockport Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1921, was born Dec. 25, 1853, in Sunderland, England, a son of Robert Siddoway and Elizabeth Dawson. He was ordained a Bishop July 8, 1917, by Orson F. Whitney. He died July 25, 1921.
