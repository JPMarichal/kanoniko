Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Iverson, Victor E.

Iverson, Victor E., Bishop of the Washington Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born March 7, 1897, in Washington, a son of Edward M. Iverson and Eveline Boggs. He was baptized June 30, 1906, ordained a High Priest Sept. 13, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard, and a Bishop Sept. 20, 1925, by Rudger Clawson.
