Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Blackburn, Thomas

Blackburn, Thomas, president of the Springfield Branch, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1930+, was born July 18, 1858, in Provo, Utah, the son of Elias H. Blackburn and Sarah J. Goff. He moved to Loa, Utah, in 1876, and acted as a counselor in the bishopric of that ward, filled a mission in the Eastern States in 1896-1897, acted as Bishop of the Loa Ward from 1898 to 1900, moved to Blackfoot and in 1913 to Springfield, Idaho, where he was set apart as presiding Elder Oct. 24, 1920.
