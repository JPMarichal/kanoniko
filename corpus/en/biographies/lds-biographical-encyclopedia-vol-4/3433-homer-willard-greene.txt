Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Homer, Willard Greene

Homer, Willard Greene, Bishop of the Tetonia Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1902 to 1907, and Bishop of the Darby Ward from 1909 to 1911, was born Dec. 30, 1873, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of Edmund Homer and Malissa Greene. He was baptized Nov. 10, 1893, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 2, 1902, by Pres. Joseph F. Smith.
