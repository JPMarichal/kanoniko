Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dana, Clarence

Dana, Clarence, Bishop of the Mesa 2nd Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1914 to 1930+, was born July 10, 1879, in Mesa, Maricopa Co., Arizona, a son of George C. Dana and Amanda A. Phelps. He was baptized in 1886, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Central States in 1899-1901, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 8, 1912, by James E. Talmage, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 9, 1914, by Rudger Clawson.
