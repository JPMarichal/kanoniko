Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Henry W.

Smith, Henry W., Bishop of the Farnum Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1909 to 1914, was born June 27, 1877, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Henry Smith and Lydia E. Kershaw. He was baptized in July, 1885, ordained a High Priest Oct. 20, 1906, by Mark Austin, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 24, 1909, by David O. McKay.
