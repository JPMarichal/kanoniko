Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wood, Walter John

Wood, Walter John, Bishop of the Bates Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1930+, was born March 27, 1882, in Elba, Idaho, a son of Samuel Leigh Wood and Mary Ann Cockerill. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1890, ordained a High Priest March 10, 1918, by Don C. Driggs, and a Bishop Aug. 18, 1922, by John A. Widtsoe.
