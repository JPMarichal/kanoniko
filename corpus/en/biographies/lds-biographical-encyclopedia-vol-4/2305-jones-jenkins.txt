Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, Jenkins

Jones, Jenkins, Bishop of the Malad Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1890 to 1896, was born July 17, 1840, in Glamorganshire, Wales, a son of Thomas Jones and Elizabeth Jenkins. He was baptized Feb. 14, 1864, emigrated to Utah in 1866, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 12, 1888, by Moses Thatchef, and ordained a Bishop June 16, 1890, by Lorenzo Snow. He died Jan. 6, 1919.
