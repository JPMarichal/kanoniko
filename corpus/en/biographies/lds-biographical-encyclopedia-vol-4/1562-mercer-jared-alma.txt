Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mercer, Jared Alma

Mercer, Jared Alma, Bishop of the Oakley 1st Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930, was born Feb. 15, 1882, in American Fork, Utah, the son of Ammon Mercer and Sarah Amelia Shelley. He was baptized May 1, 1890, filled a misson to the Western States in 1902-1904, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 19, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
