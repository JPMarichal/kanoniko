Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hill, Isaac

Hill, Isaac, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 2nd Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1854 to 1864, was born Sept. 28, 1806, near Beaver Brighton, Beaver Co., Pennsylvania. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 8, 1844, by Isaac Higbee, in Nauvoo, Ill., and a Bishop Dec. 28, 1854, by Edward Hunter.
