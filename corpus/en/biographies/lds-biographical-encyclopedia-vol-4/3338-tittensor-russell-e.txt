Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tittensor, Russell E.

Tittensor, Russell E., Bishop of the Bedford Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1928 to 1930+, was born July 22, 1892, in Thayne, Wyo., a son of Thomas E. Tittensor and Mary E. Goaslind. He was baptized July 22, 1900, ordained an Elder by Roswell R. Dana, jun., filled a mission to Australia in 1913-1915, was ordained a High Priest June 18, 1922, by Melvin J. Ballard, who also ordained him a Bishop June 17, 1928.
