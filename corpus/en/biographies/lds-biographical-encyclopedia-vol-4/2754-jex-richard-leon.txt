Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jex, Richard Leon

Jex, Richard Leon, Bishop of the Spanish Fork 1st Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born April 7, 1887, in Spanish Fork, the son of Richard H. and Ruth Jex. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1895, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 28, 1929, by Stephen L. Richards.
