Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Frehner, Henry

Frehner, Henry, presiding Elder of the Littlefield Branch, Moapa Stake, Nevada, from 1900 to 1916, was born Sept. 14, 1857, in Heisen, Switzerland, a son of Johann J. Frehner and Anna Elizabeth Loch. He was baptized Dec. 18, 1871, and died July 26, 1925.
