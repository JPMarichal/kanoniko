Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bunker, Edward, jun.

Bunker, Edward, jun., Bishop of the Bunkerville Ward, Moapa Stake, Nevada, from 1883 to 1908, was born Feb. 1, 1847, in Garden Grove, Iowa, a son of Edward Bunker and Emily Abbott. He was baptized in November, 1856, ordained a High Priest Jan. 11, 1879, by John D. T. McAllister, who also set him apart as Bishop May 2, 1883.
