Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whitear, Eli

Whitear, Eli, Bishop of the Milton Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1888, was born April 25, 1827, in Preston, Candover, Hampshire, England, a son of John Whitear, and Rachel Hitchcock. He was baptized Feb. 21, 1854, emigrated to Utah in 1854, and was ordained a Bishop July 1, 1877, by Lorenzo Snow.
