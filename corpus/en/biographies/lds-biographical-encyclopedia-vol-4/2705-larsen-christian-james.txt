Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larsen, Christian James

Larsen, Christian James, Bishop of the Glendale Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1923 to 1930, was born Jan. 17, 1894, in Logan, Utah, a son of Lars C. Larsen and Elizabeth A. Hebden. He was baptized May 11, 1902, filled a mission to Samoa in 1916-1920, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 13, 1922, by Richard R. Lyman.
