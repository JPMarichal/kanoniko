Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, Thomas Evan

Jones, Thomas Evan, Bishop of the Lehi Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1882 to 1895, was born July 30, 1844, in South Wales, Great Britain, a son of David Jones and Eleanor Evan. He was baptized in May 1863, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 10, 1882, by Erastus Snow.
