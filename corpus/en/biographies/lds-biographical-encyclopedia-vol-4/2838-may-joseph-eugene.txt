Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

May, Joseph Eugene

May, Joseph Eugene, Bishop of the Rockland Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born May 24, 1882, in Calls Fort, Utah, a son of James May and Martha Allen. He was baptized May 24, 1890, ordained an Elder Jan. 6, 1907, filled a mission to the Western States in 1907-1909, and was ordained a Bishop May 24, 1925, by Geo. F. Richards.
