Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taggart, George Albert

Taggart, George Albert, Bishop of the Morgan Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1926, was born Nov. 25, 1872, in Morgan, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of George H. Taggart and Jessie McNiven. He was baptized April 23, 1881, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 27, 1925, by John A. Widtsoe.
