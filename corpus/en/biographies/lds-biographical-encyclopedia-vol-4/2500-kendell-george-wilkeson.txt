Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kendell, George Wilkeson

Kendell, George Wilkeson, Bishop of the South Weber Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1891 to 1909, and from 1910 to 1914, was born April 21, 1856, in South Weber, Davis Co., Utah, a son of William Kendell and Johanna Peek. He was baptized when about twelve years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 25, 1891, by Franklin D. Richards. He died Oct. 24, 1924.
