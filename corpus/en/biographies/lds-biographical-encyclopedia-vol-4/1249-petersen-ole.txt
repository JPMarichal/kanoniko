Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Petersen, Ole

Petersen, Ole, Bishop of the Fielding Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1922, was born March 14, 1863, in Tjustrup, Soro amt, Denmark, the son of Rasmus Petersen and Anna Kierstine Christensen. He emigrated to Utah in 1876, and, after residing in different places, he located in Clarkston, Cache Valley, Utah, and filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1888-1890. He was ordained a High Priest June 22, 1902, by Alma Merrill, moved to Fielding in 1910, and was ordained a Bishop March 23, 1919, by George Albert Smith.
