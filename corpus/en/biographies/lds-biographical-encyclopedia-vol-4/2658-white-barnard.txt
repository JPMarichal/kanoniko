Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

White, Barnard

White, Barnard, Bishop of the Ogden 3rd Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1891 to 1901, was born Jan. 16, 1839, in London, England, the son of William White and Mary Ann Syer. He was baptized May 22, 1854, ordained a High Priest in 1877, and a Bishop Jan. 24, 1891, by Franklin D. Richards. He died March 8, 1912.
