Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robison, Parker Pratt

Robison, Parker Pratt, Bishop of the Fillmore 1st Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1928, was born April 9, 1893, in Fillmore, Millard Co., Utah, a son of Franklin A. Robison and Isabella Eleanor Pratt. He was baptized July 6, 1901, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 14, 1920, by George F. Richards.
