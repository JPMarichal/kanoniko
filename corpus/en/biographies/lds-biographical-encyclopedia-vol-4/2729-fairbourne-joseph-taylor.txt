Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fairbourne, Joseph Taylor

Fairbourne, Joseph Taylor, president of the Bacchus Branch, Oquirrh Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930, was born Aug. 4, 1886, in Salt Lake County, Utah, a son of Joseph Watson Fairbourne and Minnie Sarah Taylor. He was baptized June 8, 1895, ordained an Elder April 4, 1909, by William Hudson, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1909-1911, and was set apart as presiding Elder of the Bacchus Branch in March, 1929.
