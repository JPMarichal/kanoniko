Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lyman, Francis M.

Lyman, Francis M., president of the British Mission from 1901 to 1904. During his administration he made a visit to Egypt and the Holy Land. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 136 and Vol. 3, p. 755.)

Francis M. Lyman died as president of the Council of Twelve Apostles November 18, 1916, in Salt Lake City, Utah. His remains were buried in Tooele, Utah.
