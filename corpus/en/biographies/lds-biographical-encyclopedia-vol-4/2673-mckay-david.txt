Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mckay, David

Mckay, David, Bishop of the Huntsville Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1885 to 1905, was born May 3, 1844, in Caithness County, near Thurso, Scotland, a son of Wm. McKay and Helen Oman. He came to Utah in 1856, and filled a mission to Scotland in 1881-1883, presiding over that Mission. He was set apart to preside over the Huntsville Ward March 22, 1885. He died Nov. 10, 1917.
