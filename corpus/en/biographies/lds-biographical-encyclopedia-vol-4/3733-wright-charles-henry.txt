Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wright, Charles Henry

Wright, Charles Henry, presiding Elder of the Kemmerer Branch, Woodruff Stake, Wyo., from 1907 to 1912, was born Sept. 26, 1872, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Charles Wright and Annie Galloway. He was baptized Oct. 7, 1880, and ordained a High Priest March 22, 1903, by Archibald McKinnon.
