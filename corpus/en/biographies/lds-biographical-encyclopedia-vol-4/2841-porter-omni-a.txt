Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Porter, Omni A.

Porter, Omni A., Bishop of the North Pocatello Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1920, was born March 7, 1895, in Basalt, Idaho, a son of Abinadi Porter and Annie L. Jensen. He was baptized July 18, 1903, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 26, 1919, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
