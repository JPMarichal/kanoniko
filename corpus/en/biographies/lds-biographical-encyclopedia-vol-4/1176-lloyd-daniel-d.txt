Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lloyd, Daniel D.

Lloyd, Daniel D., Bishop of the Central Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1901 to 1903, was born Feb. 8, 1863, in Farmington, Utah, the son of Thomas Lloyd and Susanna Stone. He was a successful pioneer of both Utah and Idaho and filled a mission to California in 1913. He was the first Bishop of the Central Ward and also served as Bishop of the Mt. Sterling Ward in Hyrum Stake. He died April 1, 1935.
