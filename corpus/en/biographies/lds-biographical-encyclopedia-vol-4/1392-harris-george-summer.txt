Blaine Stake

Harris, George Summer

Harris, George Summer, Bishop of the Blaine Ward, Blaine Stake, Idaho, from 1892 to 1909, was born Feb. 20, 1866, in Coalville, Utah, the son of Chauncey S. Harris and Susannah Waring. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1877, was set apart as presiding Elder June 5, 1892, and was ordained a Bishop March 22, 1896, by Francis M. Lyman,
