Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

George, Preal

George, Preal, Bishop of the Kanosh Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Aug. 25, 1897, in Kanosh, Millard Co., Utah, a son of Alonzo George and Clara Edith Barney. He was baptized Sept. 2, 1905, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1917-1919, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 30, 1925, by Melvin J. Ballard.
