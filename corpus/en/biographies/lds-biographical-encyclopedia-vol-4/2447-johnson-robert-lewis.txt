Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Robert Lewis

Johnson, Robert Lewis, Bishop of the Fountain Green Ward, Moroni Stake, Utah, from 1862 to 1883, was born Aug. 17, 1819, in London, Canada, a son of Edward Johnson and Sarah Brown. He was baptized Feb. 22, 1842, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 11, 1862, by Orson Hyde. He died in June, 1901.
