Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

California Mission

Presidents: Samuel Brannan, 1846-1849; Charles C. Rich, 1849-1851; Parley P. Pratt, 1851-1852; Amasa M. Lyman and Charles C. Rich, 1853-1854; Parley P. Pratt (second term), 1854-1855; George Q. Cannon, 1856-1858. (After the year 1858 no organized missionary labor was done in California until 1892.) John L. Dalton, 1892-1894; Karl G. Maeser, January-August, 1894; Henry S. Tanner, 1894-1896; Ephraim H. Nye, 1896-1901; Joseph E. Robinson, 1901-1919; Joseph W. McMurrin, 1919-1932.

Brannan, Samuel

Brannan, Samuel, president of the California Mission from 1846 to 1849, died in California May 14, 1889. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 606.)
