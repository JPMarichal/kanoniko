Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moss, James Edward

Moss, James Edward, Bishop of the Holladay Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Sept. 4, 1875, in Salt Lake City, the son of Thomas Moss and Fanny Goodman. He was ordained a High Priest in 1915, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop Sept. 4, 1927, by Geo. Albert Smith.
