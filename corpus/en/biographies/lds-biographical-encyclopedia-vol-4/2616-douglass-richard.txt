Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Douglass, Richard

Douglass, Richard, presiding Elder of the West Weber Branch, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1864 to 1867, was born Feb. 27, 1828, in Downham, Lancashire, England, a son of George Douglass and Ellen Briggs. He emigrated to America in 1842, came to Utah in 1852, and was appointed presiding Elder in 1864. He died Feb. 16, 1914.
