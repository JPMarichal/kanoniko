Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gledhill, Fred Ovie

Gledhill, Fred Ovie, presiding Elder of the McCornick Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1923, was born Aug. 28, 1892, in Vermillion, Sevier Co., Utah, a son of Thomas Gledhill and Lilly Belle Ivie. He was baptized Sept. 12, 1900, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1914-1916, and was ordained a Bishop May 13, 1922, by James E. Talmage.
