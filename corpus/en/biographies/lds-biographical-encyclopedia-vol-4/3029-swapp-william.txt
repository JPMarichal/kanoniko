Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Swapp, William

Swapp, William, Bishop of the Luna Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1886 to 1888, was born Oct. 28, 1892, a son of Melvin Swapp and Margaret A. Mortensen. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 7, 1886, by Jesse N. Smith.
