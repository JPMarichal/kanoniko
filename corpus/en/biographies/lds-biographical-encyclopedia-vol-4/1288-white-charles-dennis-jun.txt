Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

White, Charles Dennis, jun.

White, Charles Dennis, jun., Bishop of the Beaver East Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1923 and from 1925 to 1930+, was born March 27, 1881, in Beaver, the son of Charles D. White and Mary A. Greenwood. He was ordained a Bishop July 20, 1919, by Richard R. Lyman.
