Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Hans

Hansen, Hans, Bishop of the Showlow Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, .from 1884 to 1903, was born April 4, 1837, in Karlebo, Fredriksborg amt, Denmark, a son of Niels Hansen and Mette Kjerstine Larsen. He was baptized June 24, 1855, emigrated to Utah in 1856, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 13, 1884, by Francis M. Lyman. He died Sept. 19, 1909.
