Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thatcher, Howard E.

Thatcher, Howard E., Bishop of the Trout Creek (Lago) Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1921, and Bishop of the Thatcher 2nd Ward from 1921 to 1924, was born Dec. 15, 1873, in Logan, Utah, the son of John B. Thatcher and Rachel H. Davis. He was baptized Dec. 15, 1881, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 16, 1914, by Anthony W. Ivins, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 19, 1921.
