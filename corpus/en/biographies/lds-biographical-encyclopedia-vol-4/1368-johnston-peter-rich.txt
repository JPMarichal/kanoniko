Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnston, Peter Rich

Johnston, Peter Rich, 1st Counselor in the Blackfoot Stake Presidency, Idaho, in 1930+, was born June 7, 1897, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Peter G. Johnston and Alice Duckworth. He was baptized July 9, 1905, moved to Blackfoot in 1903, was ordained an Elder in 1915, filled a mission to Australia in 1915-1919, and was ordained a High Priest Feb. 23, 1930, and set apart by Richard R. Lyman.
