Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Steed, Joseph Evan

Steed, Joseph Evan, Bishop of the Woolford Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Nov. 12, 1872, in Farmington, Utah, the son of John Wilford Steed and Ann Jenkins. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a Seventy by Seymour B. Young Feb. 17, 1897, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1897-1899. In 1900 he located at Cardston, Canada; was ordained a High Priest Oct. 28, 1917, by Thomas Duce, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 22, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
