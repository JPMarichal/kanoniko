Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merrill, Joseph Harris

Merrill, Joseph Harris, president of the Samoan Mission from 1902 to 1903, was born March 10, 1868, in Smithfield, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Horatio Harris Merrill and Martha Mouritsen. He was baptized July 5, 1877, filled a mission to Samoa in 1891-1893, and another one in 1902 to the same field, to act as president of the mission. He was set apart Nov. 30, 1901 and returned Feb. 12, 1903. He married Katie Eliza Hale Sept. 6, 1889, who died June 29, 1890. On June 21, 1894, he married Grace Emma Hale. They are the parents of nine children.
