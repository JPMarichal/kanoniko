Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nelson, David

Nelson, David, Bishop of the Emmett Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1903 to 1913, was born Oct. 2, 1855, in Lehi, Utah, the son of Jens Nielsen and Karen Christine Bentzen. He was baptized at Lehi, in 1864, ordained a High Priest Feb. 7, 1885, by Wm. F. Rigby, moved to Emmett June 10, 1902, and was ordained a Bishop April 10, 1904, by Geo. Albert Smith.
