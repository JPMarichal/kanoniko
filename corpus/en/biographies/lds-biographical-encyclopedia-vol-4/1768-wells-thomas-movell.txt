Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wells, Thomas Movell

Wells, Thomas Movell, Bishop of the VictOr Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born March 28, 1907, at Desert Lake, Utah, the son of Samuel Wells and Viola M. Dodge. He was baptized July 4, 1915, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 27, 1930, by Orson F. Whitney.
