Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Price, Daniel Shepherd

Price, Daniel Shepherd, Bishop of the Paris 2nd Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1927, was born Feb. 3, 1880, in Paris, the son of Robert Price and Christina Shepherd. He was baptized May 3, 1888, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 24, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards.
