Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ensign, George Calvin

Ensign, George Calvin, Bishop of the Sunset Ward, North Davis Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Oct. 22, 1887, in Ogden, Utah, a son Of Datus H. Ensign and Wealthy Richards. He was baptized Oct. 25, 1895, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 29, 1926, by David O. McKay.
