Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fullmer, Oscar Myron

Fullmer, Oscar Myron, Bishop of the Abraham Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1903 to 1907, was born Sept. 13, 1852, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Alma L. Fullmet and Sarah Ann Follett. He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1881-1883, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 23, 1903, by Orrin P. Miller. He died Jan. 25, 1934.
