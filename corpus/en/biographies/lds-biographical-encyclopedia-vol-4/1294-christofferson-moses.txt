Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christofferson, Moses

Christofferson, Moses, Bishop of the Cornish Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Dec. 18, 1890, the son of John C. Christofferson and Madie Madson. He was baptized May 7, 1899 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 5, 1929, by Geo. F. Richards.
