Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Seeley, Justin Burt

Seeley, Justin Burt, Bishop of the Mt. Pleasant North Ward, North Sanpete Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1911, was born April 14, 1882, in Indianola, Utah, a son of William H. Seeley and Charlotte Reynolds. He was baptized when eight years old, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 12, 1909, by John Henry Smith.
