Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jolley, Haskell Shurtliff

Jolley, Haskell Shurtliff, Bishop of the Lovell Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1901 to 1910, was born May 17, 1861, in Salem, Utah, the son of Henry B. M. Jolley and Cynthia Ann Shurtliff. He was baptized May 17, 1869, by his father, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1887-1889, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 1, 1892, by Anthon H. Lund, moved to Big Horn in 1900, and there became Bishop of the Lovell Ward.
