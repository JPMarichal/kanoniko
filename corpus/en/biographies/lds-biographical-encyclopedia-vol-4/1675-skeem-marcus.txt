Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Skeem, Marcus

Skeem, Marcus, Bishop of the Oasis Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1926, was born Feb. 24, 1877, in Skanderup, Denmark, the son of Hans Peter Skeem and Cecilie Svenning. He came to Utah with his parents in 1880, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 1, 1908, by Francis M. Lyman.
