Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burton, Ira Junius

Burton, Ira Junius, Bishop of the Park Valley Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1928, was born April 19, 1897, in Park Valley, Utah, a son of Joseph H. Burton and Martha Mecham. He was baptized June 10, 1905, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 28, 1923, by Rudger Clawson.
