Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Allen, Thomas Lonsdale

Allen, Thomas Lonsdale, president of the Summit Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1925, was born June 2, 1849, in Antrim, Ireland, a son of Robert B. Allen and Jane Ferguson. He was baptized in 1862, came to Utah in 1869, was ordained a High Priest April 21, 1901, and set apart as first counselor. He was set apart as president Aug. 6, 1921, by Geo. F. Richards.
