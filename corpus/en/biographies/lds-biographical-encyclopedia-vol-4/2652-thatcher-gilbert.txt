Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thatcher, Gilbert

Thatcher, Gilbert, Bishop of the Plain City Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1920, was born April 16, 1876, in Logan, Utah, a son of John B. Thatchef and Rachel Davis. He was baptized May 6, 1886, was called on a mission to the Southern States in 1902, and was sustained as Bishop April 13, 1913.
