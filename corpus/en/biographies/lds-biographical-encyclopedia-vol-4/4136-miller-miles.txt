Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Miller, Miles

Miller, Miles, a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company E, was born July 26, 1818, at Clarendon, Orleans Co., N.Y., a son of Josiah Miller and Amanda Morgan. He marched to California with the Battalion, and when he came back settled in Ferron, Emery Co. He married Rachel Ewing, and nine children were born to them. He died March 2, 1900, at Nephi, Juab Co., Utah.
