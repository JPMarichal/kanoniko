Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harper, Seth F.

Harper, Seth F., Bishop of the Basin Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1923, was born Nov. 25, 1892, the son of Wm. T. Harper and Annie Lovelind. He was baptized Nov. 25, 1900, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 6, 1919, by Richard R. Lyman.
