Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Loosle, Reuben Oddo

Loosle, Reuben Oddo, Bishop of Clarkston Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Feb. 20, 1895, in Clarkston, Utah, the son of John Wm. Loosle and Elizabeth Buttars. He was baptized April 21, 1903, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 12, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard.
