Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Weiler, Joseph

Weiler, Joseph, president of the Netherlands Mission from 1864 to 1867, was born Nov. 17, 1836, in Chester County, Penn., a son of Jacob Weiler and Anna Maria Malin.

He was set apart for his mission April 22, 1864, and landed Oct. 31, 1964, having labored in England first. With the arrival of Elder Weiler, the Netherlands, which had theretofore formed a part of the Swiss and German Mission, became a separate mission. (See also "Historical Record", Vol. 5, p. 32.)
