Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Parkinson, David

Parkinson, David, Bishop of the Portage Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1918 to 1929, was born Jan. 31, 1867, in Wellsville, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Thomas Parkinson and Elizabeth King. He was baptized Sept. 16, 1892, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1902-1904, was ordained a High Priest March 24, 1907, by George F. Richards, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 18, 1918, by Orson F. Whitney.
