Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Park, Samuel

Park, Samuel, Bishop of the Tooele South Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born July 18, 1899, in Tooele, Utah, a son of Joseph Robert Park and Caroline Hoagland. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1907, ordained an Elder Dec. 27, 1920, by Peter M. Clegg, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1921-1923, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 17, 1928, by George F. Richards, and a Bishop Jan. 13, 1929, by Orson F. Whitney.
