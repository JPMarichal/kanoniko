Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fairbanks, David

Fairbanks, David, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 1st Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1849 to 1851, was born March 14, 1810, in Peru, Vermont, the son of Joseph Fairbanks and Polly Brooks. He joined the Church in 1842, came to the "Valley" in the fall of 1847, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 22, 1849.
