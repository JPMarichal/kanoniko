Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Young, William Goodall

Young, William Goodall, Bishop of the Big Cottonwood Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1874 to 1877, was born Feb. 21, 1827, in Ontario Co., New York, the son of Lorenzo D. Young and Persis Goodall. He was chosen as a Bishop Aug. 2, 1874. He died Dec. 26, 1883.
