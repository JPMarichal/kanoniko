Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grant, William Albert

Grant, William Albert, Bishop of the Granger Ward, Oquirrh Stake, Utah, from 1914 to 1920, was born March 5, 1878, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of John Grant and Emma Eames. He was baptized when eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 29, 1914, by Francis M. Lyman.
