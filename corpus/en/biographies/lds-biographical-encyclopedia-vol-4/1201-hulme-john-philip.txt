Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hulme, John Philip

Hulme, John Philip, Bishop of the Bloomington Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, in 1928-1930, was born March 25, 1884, in Bloomington, Idaho, the son of William Hulme and Ann Maria Briscoe. He was baptized in June, 1892, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 5, 1913, by Wm. L. Rich, and ordained a Bishop May 27, 1928 by Melvin J. Ballard.
