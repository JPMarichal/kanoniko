Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bringhurst, William Augustus

Bringhurst, William Augustus, Bishop of the Toquerville Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1874 to 1912, was born Jan. 26, 1839, in Lyonville, Chester Co., Penn., a son of Samuel Bringhurst and Eleanor Beitler. He came to the Valley in 1847, was baptized in August of that year, in the Platte River, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 15, 1874, by Brigham Young. He died March 8, 1912.
