Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cox, Eli Ashton

Cox, Eli Ashton, Bishop of the Jameston Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born March 22, 1898, in Taylor, Idaho, a son of Levi Cox and Elnora Arave. He was baptized Sept. 2, 1906, ordained an Elder Dec. 29, 1919, by Isaac Sorensen, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1920-1922, was ordained a High Priest May 21, 1922, by Stephen L. Richards, and sustained as Bishop April 13, 1924.
