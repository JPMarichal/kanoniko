Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beecher, Elihu Ulysses

Beecher, Elihu Ulysses, Bishop of the Elba Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1926 to 1930 +, was born April 21, 1883, at Elba, Idaho, a son of Asa Moroni Beecher and Clarissa Paulina Pethingill. He was baptized July 2, 1891, ordained an Elder Oct. 1, 1906, by his father, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1906-1907, was ordained a High Priest May 16, 1915, by John A. Elison, and ordained a Bishop June 6, 1926, by Stephen L Richards.
