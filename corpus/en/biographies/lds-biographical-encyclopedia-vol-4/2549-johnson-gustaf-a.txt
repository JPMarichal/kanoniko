Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Gustaf A.

Johnson, Gustaf A., Bishop of the McGill Ward, Nevada Stake, Nevada, from 1923 to 1926, was born Nov. 5, 1877, in Eskilstuna, Sodermanland, Sweden, the son of John Johnson and Caroline C. Johnson. He was baptized March 1, 1906, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 21, 1923, by Francis W. Stratford.
