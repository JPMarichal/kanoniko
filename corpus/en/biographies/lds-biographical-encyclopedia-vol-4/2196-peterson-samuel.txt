Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Samuel

Peterson, Samuel, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 2nd Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1890, was born Dec. 1, 1826, on the island of Bornholm, Denmark, the son of Peter P. Peterson and Ellen M. Hansen. He was baptized in 1852, came to Utah in 1853, was ordained a Seventy March 15, 1858, filled a mission to Sweden in 1867-1870, and was ordained a Bishop June 8, 1877, by John Taylor.
