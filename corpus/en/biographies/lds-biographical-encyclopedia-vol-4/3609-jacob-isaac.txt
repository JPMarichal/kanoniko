Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jacob, Isaac

Jacob, Isaac, Bishop of the Bonneville Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1925, was born May 11, 1876, in Midway, Utah, a son of Joseph Jacob and Ellen Gerber. He was baptized April 12, 1885, filled a mission to England in 1908-1910, was ordained a High Priest July 30, 1911, by Charles W. Penrose, and a Bishop Oct. 13, 1923, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
