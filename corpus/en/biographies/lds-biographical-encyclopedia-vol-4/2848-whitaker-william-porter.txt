Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whitaker, William Porter

Whitaker, William Porter, Bishop of the Pocatello 4th Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1924, was born April 2, 1882, at Centerville, Utah, a son of Thomas W. Whitaker and Hannah Waddoups. He was baptized July 6, 1890, ordained a High Priest June 24, 1912, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop Feb. 27, 1921, by James E. Talmage.
