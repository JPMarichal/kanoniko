Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, Roy

Taylor, Roy, Bishop of the La Point Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1925, was born Feb. 18, 1872, in Levan, Utah, a son of Martin V. Taylor and Joannah Jennings. He was baptized June 6, 1886, ordained a High Priest Sept. 10, 1916, by Don B. Colton, and sustained as Bishop Dec. 31, 1916.
