Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mccrary, John M.

Mccrary, John M., first counselor in the Malad Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1892 to 1902, was born in Cynthiana, Posey Co., Indiana, a son of James McCrary and Matilda Forest. He was baptized in May, 1850, emigrated to Utah that year, ordained a High Priest by Peter Boise, crossed the plains as a hand-cart missionary to Canada in 1857, and was set apart as first counselor June 13, 1892, by John W. Taylor.
