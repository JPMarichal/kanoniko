Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wood, Franklin Bryner

Wood, Franklin Bryner, Bishop of the Cedar East (or First) Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Oct. 13, 1881, in Cedar City, Utah, the son of George H. Wood and Elizabeth A. Bryner. He was baptized June 27, 1890, filled a mission to the Central States in 1906-1908, was ordained a High Priest June 19, 1908, by Francis M. Lyman, and ordained a Bishop March 16, 1924, by George Albert Smith.
