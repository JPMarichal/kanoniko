Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cannon, Walter

Cannon, Walter, second counsel, or in the St. George Stake presidency, Utah, from 1925 to 1930, was born July 5, 1888, in Pinto, Utah, a son of David Henry Cannon and Rhoda Ann Knell. He was baptized July 7, 1896, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1913-1915, and was ordained a High Priest in September, 1916, by Anthony W. Ivins. He was set apart as second counselor June 14, 1925, by Geo. F. Richards.
