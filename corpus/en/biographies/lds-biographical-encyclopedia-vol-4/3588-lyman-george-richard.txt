Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lyman, George Richard

Lyman, George Richard, second counselor in the Union Stake presidency, Oregon, from 1928 to 1930, was born April 15, 1873, in Parowan, Iron Co., Utah, a son of Oscar Morris Lyman and Phoebe Medora Benson. He was baptized July 19, 1896, ordained a Seventy April 17, 1900, by Francis M. Lyman, and a High Priest March 12, 1911, by Francis M. Lyman, served as Bishop of the La Grande Ward, from 1922 to 1924, and of the La Grande 1st Ward from 1924 to 1925. He was set apart as second counselor in the Union Stake presidency June 17, 1928.
