Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Henrie, Alphonso

Henrie, Alphonso, Bishop of the Manti Center Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, in 1930+, was born Feb. 21, 1894, in Manti, Utah, a son of Jerome B. Henrie and Thea Lund. He was baptized Sept. 16, 1902, ordained a High Priest Aug. 12, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 31, 1930, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
