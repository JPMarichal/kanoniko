Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bills, David

Bills, David, Bishop of the Riverton Ward, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1927, was born April 13, 1873, in South Jordan, Utah, a son of William A. Bills and Emmeline Beckstead. He was baptized when about eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 14, 1921, by George F. Richards.
