Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gibby, Richard Stevenson

Gibby, Richard Stevenson, Bishop of the Provo 3rd Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1891 to 1895, was born Jan. 6, 1867, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of William Gibby and Catherine Stevenson. He was baptized Aug. 26, 1877, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1886-1888, and was ordained a Bishop May 17, 1891, by Heber J. Grant.
