Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pickett, Eugene

Pickett, Eugene, Bishop of the Marion Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930, was born Sept. 22, 1885, in Marion, the son of Moroni Pickett and Frances A. Clegg. He was baptized July 5, 1894, filled a mission to Ireland in 1907-1910 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 18, 1926, by Richard R. Lyman.
