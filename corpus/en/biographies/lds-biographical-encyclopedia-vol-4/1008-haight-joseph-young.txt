Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Haight, Joseph Young

Haight, Joseph Young, president of the Society Islands Mission from 1900 to 1902, was born Oct. 9, 1865, in Farmington, Utah, the son of Hurton D. Haight and Louisa Leavitt. He was baptized July 5, 1872, set apart for his mission April 10, 1899, and returned in September, 1902.
