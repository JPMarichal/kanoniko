Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Perkins, Edward Martin

Perkins, Edward Martin, Bishop of the Mapleton Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1891 to 1908 and from 1910 to 1912, was born Jan. 10, 1860, in Provo, Utah, the son of Joseph Perkins and Margaret Martin. He was baptized March 16, 1882, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1888-1890, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 13, 1891, by Marriner W. Merrill.
