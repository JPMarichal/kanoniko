Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Morris, Clarence

Morris, Clarence, Bishop of the Ogden 14th Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1924, was born March 16, 1887, in Hunter, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Joseph N. Morris and Sarah A. Grove. He was baptized Sept. 30, 1895, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 27, 1919, by David O. McKay.
