Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nielsen, Lars Peter

Nielsen, Lars Peter, Bishop of the Ovid Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho from 1915 to 1916, was born Jan. 9, 1861, in Dronninglund, Hjorring amt, Denmark, the son of Andera M. Nielsen and Marie A. Sorensen. He emigrated to Utah in 1869, was baptized in Huntsville, Utah, in 1869, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1888-1890, was ordained a High Priest in November 1890, by Wm. Budge, and a Bishop Dec. 19, 1915, by Jos. F. Smith, jun. Bishop Nielsen was accidentally killed Dec. 6, 1916.
