Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stookey, Mahonri Moriancumr

Stookey, Mahonri Moriancumr, Bishop of the Clover Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1912, was born Sept. 24, 1870, in Clover, Tooele Co., Utah, a son of Enos Stookey and Jamima E. Child. He was baptized June 10, 1881, ordained a Seventy Nov. 5, 1896, by Francis M. Lyman, ordained a High Priest Oct. 14, 1900, by Hugh S. Gowans, and sustained as Bishop June 15, 1901.
