Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Spendlove, Joseph Franklin

Spendlove, Joseph Franklin, Bishop of the Milton Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1926, was born March 14, 1877, in Littleton, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of Joseph Spendlove and Harriet Payne. He was baptized July 2, 1885, ordained a High Priest Sept. 7, 1901, by Lyman Porter, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 11, 1921, by Richard R. Lyman.
