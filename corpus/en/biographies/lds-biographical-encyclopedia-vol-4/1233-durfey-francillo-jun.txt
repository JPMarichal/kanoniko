Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Durfey, Francillo, jun.

Durfey, Francillo, jun., presiding Elder and Bishop of Beaver Dam Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1872 to 1908, was born Feb. 1, 1850, at Mound Fort, Weber Co., Utah, the son of Francillo Durfey and Cintha Harrington. He was baptized in February, 1858, in Fort Limhi, Oregon (now Idaho), came to Beaver Dam, Utah, with his father's family in 1868, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 27, 1892.
