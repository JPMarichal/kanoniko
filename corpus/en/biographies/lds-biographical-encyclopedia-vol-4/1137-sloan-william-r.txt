Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sloan, William R.

Sloan, William R., Bishop of the Kimball Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1903 to 1908, was born March 30, 1881, in Manti, Utah, the son of Hugh R. Sloan and Margaret Reid. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1889, by Hans Dennison, and ordained a High Priest Aug. 23, 1903, by Heber S. Allen.
