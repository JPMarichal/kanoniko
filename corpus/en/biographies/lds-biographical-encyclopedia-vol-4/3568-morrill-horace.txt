Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Morrill, Horace

Morrill, Horace, Bishop of the Tridell (Liberty) Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1929, was born July 18, 1863, in Johnson, Iron Co., Utah, a son of Laban Morrill and Permelia Drury. He was baptized Aug. 18, 1861, ordained an Elder by Thomas J. Jones, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1887-1889, was ordained a High Priest in 1911, and a Bishop Aug. 27, 1916, by Heber J. Grant.
