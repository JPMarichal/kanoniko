Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cheever, George Alber

Cheever, George Albert Smith, Bishop of the Payson 4th Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born July 8, 1892, in Provo, Utah, the son of Joseph E. Cheever and Grace Libby Smith. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1900, ordained a High Priest Dec. 5, 1925, by Geo. Albert Smith, and a Bishop Oct. 27, 1929, by James E. Talmage.
