Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brinton, David Branson, sen.

Brinton, David Branson, sen., Bishop of the Big Cottonwood Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1900, was born Feb. 23, 1850, in Savannah, MO., the son of David Brinton and Harriet W. Dilworth. He came to Utah in 1850, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 27, 1877, by Daniel H. Wells.
