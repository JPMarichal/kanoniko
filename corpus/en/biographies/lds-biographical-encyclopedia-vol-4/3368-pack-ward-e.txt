Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pack, Ward E.

Pack, Ward E., second counselor in the Summit Stake presidency, Utah, from 1881 to 1901, was born April 17, 1834, in Watertown, New York, a son of John Pack and Julia Ives. He was baptized April 17, 1842. He died Nov. 16, 1907.
