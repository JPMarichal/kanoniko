Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Eldredge, John Sunderlin

Eldredge, John Sunderlin, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born April 30, 1831, at Brutus, Cayuga Co., N.Y., a son of Alanson Eldredge and Esther Sunderlin. After his arrival in Great Salt Lake Valley as one of Pres. Brigham Young's famous company of pioneers, he took an active part in raising crops and forwarding pioneer industries; it is claimed that he was among the first to use the plow in preparing the arid land on the site of the present Salt Lake City. He filled a mission to Australia in 1852-1856. He died suddenly, while plowing on his farm at Charleston, Wasatch Co., Utah, May 7, 1873, being survived by a large family.
