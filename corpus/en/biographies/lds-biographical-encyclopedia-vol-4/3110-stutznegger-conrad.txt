Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stutznegger, Conrad

Stutznegger, Conrad, Bishop of the Center Ward, Salt Lake Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Aug. 26, 1880, in Manti, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of John Stutznegger and Barbara Catherine Ramsauer. He was baptized Feb. 5, 1890, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Switzerland and Germany in 1902-1904, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 15, 1920, by Charles W. Penrose, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 20, 1925, by Richard R. Lyman.
