Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stock, John

Stock, John, Bishop of the Fish Haven Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1882 to 1893, was born Oct. 12, 1820, in Cape of Good Hope, South Africa, the son of Robert Stock and Susanna Pierce. He was baptized Feb. 14, 1855, by Leonard I. Smith, emigrated to America in 1860, settled in Bear Lake Valley in 1864, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 14, 1877, and a Bishop Aug. 9, 1882, by Francis M. Lyman. He died Oct. 18, 1896.
