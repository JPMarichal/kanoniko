Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jeppson, Hans Peter

Jeppson, Hans Peter, Bishop of the Bramwell Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1902 to 1903, was born Sept. 11, 1864, in Brigham City, Utah, the son of Jeppa Hans Jeppson and Gunill Maria Henderson. He was baptized in August, 1874, and chosen Bishop Sept. 14, 1902.
