Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tullis, John Hardman

Tullis, John Hardman, Bishop of the Newcastle Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Sept. 6, 1879, at Pinto, Washington Co., Utah, the son of David Wilson Tullis and Martha Eccles. He was baptized June 30, 1889, ordained a High Priest March 21, 1914, by Lucius N. Marsden, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 4, 1927, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
