Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Greenhalgh, Albert Augustus

Greenhalgh, Albert Augustus, Bishop of the Santaquin 2nd Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Nov. 5, 1878, in Santaquin, the son of Ezekiel Greenhalgh and Emma Caroline Arenson Nelson. He was baptized June 16, 1887, ordained a High Priest Dec. 4, 1927, by Geo. Albert Smith, and a Bishop June 23, 1929, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
