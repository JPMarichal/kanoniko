Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Morgan Stake

Anderson, Everett Elydore

Anderson, Everett Elydore, Bishop of the North Morgan Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1928, was born Jan. 4, 1893, in Lehi, Utah Co., Utah, a son of Brigham J. Anderson and Rachel Woodhouse. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a High Priest Sept. 17, 1922, by James E. Talmage, and was ordained a Bishop March 29, 1925, by. Joseph F. Smith, jun.
