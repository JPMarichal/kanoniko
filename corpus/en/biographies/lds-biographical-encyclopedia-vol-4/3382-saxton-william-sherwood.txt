Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Saxton, William Sherwood

Saxton, William Sherwood, Bishop of the Upton Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930, was born Feb. 8, 1865, in Coalville, Utah, a son of Solomon Saxton and Matilda Dexter. He was baptized when eight years old, and ordained a Bishop in April, 1920, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
