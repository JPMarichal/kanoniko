Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hacking, Orson Ezra

Hacking, Orson Ezra, Bishop of the Cedar Valley Ward, Lehi Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1922, was born Sept. 16, 1882, in Cedar Valley, the son of John Sampson Hacking and Jane Clark. He was baptized Sept. 14, 1890, and ordained an Elder Dec. 8, 1901, by Eli Bennett.
