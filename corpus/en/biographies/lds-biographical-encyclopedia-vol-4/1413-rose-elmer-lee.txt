Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rose, Elmer Lee

Rose, Elmer Lee, Bishop of the Bramwell Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1930+, was born Dec. 25, 1872, in Farmington, Utah, the son of Erastus F. Rose and Josephine Elnora Robinson He was baptized in 1880, filled a mission in the Eastern States in 1909-1911, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 2, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard.
