Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kofoed, Orson

Kofoed, Orson, Bishop of the Clifton Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Jan. 28, 1882, in Weston, Idaho, a son of Hans A. Kofoed and Thora Mary Anderson. He was baptized April 3, 1890, ordained a High Priest Sept. 5, 1920, by Rudger Clawson, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 14, 1929, by Geo. F. Richards.
