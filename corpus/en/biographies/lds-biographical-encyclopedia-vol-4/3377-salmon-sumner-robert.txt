Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Salmon, Sumner Robert

Salmon, Sumner Robert, Bishop of the Coalville Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born June 9, 1899, in Coalville, Utah, a son of John H. Salmon and Sarah Elizabeth Fisher. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1920-1923, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 25, 1925, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
