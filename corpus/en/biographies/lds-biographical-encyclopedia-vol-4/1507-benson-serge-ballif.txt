Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Benson, Serge Ballif

Benson, Serge Ballif, Bishop of the Logan 4th Ward, Cache Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930, was born in October, 1877, in Logan, Utah, the son of (George Taft Benson and Louisa Ballif. He was baptized in 1885, ordained a Seventy Oct. 8, 1900, by John Henry Smith, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1900-1902, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 5, 1920.
