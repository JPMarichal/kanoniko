Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Teton Stake

Brower, Joshua Albert

Brower, Joshua Albert, Bishop of the Victor Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1925, was born Feb. 1, 1885, in Richmond, Utah, a son of Joshua Brower and Amelia Allen. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 19, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard.
