Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Flanigan, Alma Emmett

Flanigan, Alma Emmett, Bishop of the Virgin City Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Oct. 17, 1898, in Springdale, Utah, a son of David A. Flanigan and. Clarinda M. Stout. He was baptized in 1907, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 17, 1928, by David O. McKay.
