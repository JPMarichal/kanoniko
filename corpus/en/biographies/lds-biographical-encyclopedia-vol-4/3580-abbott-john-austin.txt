Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Union Stake

Abbott, John Austin

Abbott, John Austin, Bishop of the Cove Ward, Union Stake, Oregon, from 1903 to 1909 was born Aug. 12, 1871, in Square Town (or Plymouth), Utah, a son of Myron Abbott and Laurie Josephine Allen. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1898-1902, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 7, 1903, by Anthon H. Lund.
