Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Slater, James Alma

Slater, James Alma, Bishop of the Slaterville Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1899 to 1922, was born April 11, 1856, at Slaterville, a son of Richard Slater and Ann Cotbridge. He was baptized June 6, 1867, was ordained a Seventy in the spring of 1884 and filled a mission to New Zealand in 1884-1887. He was ordained a High Priest Oct. 2, 1892, by Lewis W. Shurtliff, and a Bishop Dec. 19, 1899. He died March 4, 1934.
