Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lillywhite, Benjamin

Lillywhite, Benjamin, Bishop of the Greenville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1880 to 1885, was born Sept. 25, 1843, the son of Benjamin and Margaret Lillywhite. He was ordained a Bishop April 11, 1880, by John R. Murdock.
