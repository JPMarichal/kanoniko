Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hall, Hubert Frank

Hall, Hubert Frank, Bishop of the Annis Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Nov. 13, 1871, in Ogden, Utah, a son of Benjamin Franklin Hall and Mary Sorepta Loke. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1917, ordained a High Priest March 1, 1924, by Hyrum G. Smith, and ordained a Bishop May 26, 1928, by Stephen L. Richards.
