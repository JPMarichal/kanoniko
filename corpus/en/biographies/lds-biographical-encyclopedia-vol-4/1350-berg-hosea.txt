Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Blackfoot Stake

Berg, Hosea

Berg, Hosea, Bishop of the Tilden Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1907 to 1910, was born July 4, 1873, in Christiania, Norway, the son of Halvor H. Berg and Caroline Larsen. He was baptized in 1883 by Thomas Farrer, ordained a High Priest in May, 1899, and a Bishop Feb. 6, 1910.
