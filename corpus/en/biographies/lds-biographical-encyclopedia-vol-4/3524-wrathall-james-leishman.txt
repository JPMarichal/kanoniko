Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wrathall, James Leishman

Wrathall, James Leishman, Bishop of the Grantsville Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1890 to 1906, was born Sept. 22, 1860, in Grantsville, Utah, a son of James Wrathall and Mary Leishman. He was baptized June 11, 1871, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 28, 1890.
