Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Scott, Franklin

Scott, Franklin, Bishop of the Oaxaca Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1894 to 1899, was born Dec. 1, 1851, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Andrew H. Scott and Sarah A. Roe. He was ordained a High Priest in 1877 by Peter Madsen, and a Bishop March 11, 1894, by Brigham Young, jun. He was killed Aug. 7, 1901.
