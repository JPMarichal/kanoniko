Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carlile, Frederick Giles

Carlile, Frederick Giles, Bishop of the Heber 2nd Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Nov. 17, 1887, at Heber, Utah, a son of John Carlile and Sarah Elizabeth Crook. He was baptized Aug. 16, 1896, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1911-1914, was ordained a High Priest July 27, 1919, by Anthony W. Ivins, and a Bishop April 7, 1928, by Stephen L. Richards.
