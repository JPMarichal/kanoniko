Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Marshall, William Calvin

Marshall, William Calvin, Bishop of the La Point Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1927, was born Sept. 19, 1866, in Nester, Carroll Co., Virginia. He was ordained a High Priest Jan. 25, 1925, and a Bishop Sept. 26, 1926, by Richard R. Lyman.
