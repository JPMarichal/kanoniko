Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harker, Heber Lawrence

Harker, Heber Lawrence, Bishop of the Hill Spring Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Jan. 1, 1880, at Vernon, Tooele Co., Utah, the son of Heber Willard Harker and Margaret A. Terry. He was baptized when 8 years old, moved in 1905 to Cardston, where he was ordained an Elder by Wm. E. Yancey, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1909-1911; was ordained a High Priest by Edward J. Wood June 25, 1916, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 25, 1929, by George Albert Smith.
