Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kjar, Karl

Kjar, Karl, Bishop of the Sunset Ward, San Francisco Stake, Calif., from 1927 to 1930+, was born March 17, 1887, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Joseph J. Kjar and Ida Johanson. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 10, 1927, by Rudger Clawson.
