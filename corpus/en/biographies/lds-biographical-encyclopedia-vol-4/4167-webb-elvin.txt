Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Webb, Elvin

Webb, Elvin, an Elder who died while filling a foreign mission, was born Oct. 11, 1903, in Fillmore, Millard Co., Utah, a son of William James Webb and Eliza Jane Melville. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1912, by Daniel A. Morris, and later ordained an Elder. He was residing at Hinckley, Utah, when he was called to fill a mission to the Netherlands. Responding to the call he was set apart Feb. 2, 1926. He arrived in Rotterdam Feb. 21, 1926, and a short time later (on March 29, 1926) he died of peritonitis. He was taken suddenly ill one morning and passed away while being taken to a hospital. A special memorial service was held for Elder Webb in the saints' hall, "Excelsior", in Rotterdam. His last words were "Tell mother that all is well", and all was well, for Elder Webb had been attending to his missionary duties up to his last day on earth. His remains were shipped home accompanied by Elders Edward P. Young and William R. Wilcken.
