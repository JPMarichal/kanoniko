Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Esplin, Marion

Esplin, Marion, Bishop of the Shelley 1st Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Dec. 1, 1880, in Orderville, Utah, a son of John James Esplin. He was baptized in 1888, ordained a High Priest May 5, 1918, by Richard R. Lyman, and a Bishop Feb. 17, 1924, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
