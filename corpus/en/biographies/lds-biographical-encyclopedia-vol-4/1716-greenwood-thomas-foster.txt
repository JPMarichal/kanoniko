Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Greenwood, Thomas Foster

Greenwood, Thomas Foster, Bishop of the East Midvale Ward, East Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930, was born April 5, 1873, in Union, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Thomas Greenwood and Ann Henrietta Parry. He was baptized Sept 10, 1881, ordained a Seventy in August, 1907, by David Archibald, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1908-1911, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 13, 1921, by Rudger Clawson.
