Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kidman, Lewis Rudolph

Kidman, Lewis Rudolph, presiding Elder of the Redondo Branch, Hollywood Stake, California, in 1930, was born May 8, 1892, in Petersboro, Utah, the son of William Kidman, jun., and Elizabeth Petersen. He was ordained a High Priest Jan. 10, 1932, by Chas. H. Norberg.
