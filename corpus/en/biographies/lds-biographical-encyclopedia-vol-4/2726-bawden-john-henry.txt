Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bawden, John Henry

Bawden, John Henry, Bishop of the Magna Ward, Oquirrh Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1920, was born March 22, 1885, in Granger, Utah, a son of John Howard Bawden and Alice Jane Freestone. He was baptized March 28, 1893, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 6, 1916, by Geo. F. Richards.
