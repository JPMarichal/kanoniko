Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

May, Richard C.

May, Richard C., president of the Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born June 27, 1876, in Calls Fort, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of James May and Martha Allen. He was baptized in Sept., 1898, ordained a High Priest Nov. 10, 1910, by Francis M. Lyman, and set apart as stake president May 11, 1924, by George F. Richards.
