Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lesueur, James Warren

Lesueur, James Warren, president of the Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1912 to 1926, was born May 31, 1878, at Montpelier, Bear Lake Co., Idaho, a son of John Taylor Lesueur and Geneva Casto. He was baptized Oct. 7, 1886, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1898-1900, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 28, 1903, by J. T. Lesueur, and set apart as president March 9, 1912, by Francis M. Lyman.
