Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clark, Leigh William

Clark, Leigh William, Bishop of the Mesa 3rd Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1925 to 1927, was born Oct. 21, 1900, in Lehi, Maricopa Co., Arizona, a son of Joseph W. Clark and Mary Noble. He was baptized Oct. 21, 1908, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to England in 1921-1923, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 23, 1925, by David O. McKay.
