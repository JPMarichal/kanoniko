Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ogden, Frank Marsh

Ogden, Frank Marsh, Bishop of the Richfield 1st Ward, Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, from 1921 to 1930, was born Sept. 18, 1878, in Richfield, Utah, the son of Thomas Ogden and Ann Marsh. He was baptized in 1886, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1902-1904, was ordained a High Priest May 1, 1917, by Robert D. Young, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 25, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard.
