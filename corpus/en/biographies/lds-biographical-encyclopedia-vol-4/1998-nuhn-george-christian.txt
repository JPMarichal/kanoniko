Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nuhn, George Christian

Nuhn, George Christian, Bishop of the Avon Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1920 to 1930, was born Jan. 10, 1890, at San Francisco, California, the son of George C. Nuhn and Vilate Cole. He was baptized Feb. 16, 1910, ordained an Elder March 11, 1910, by Heber J. Grant, filled a mission to Holland in 1910-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 18, 1920, by David O. McKay.
