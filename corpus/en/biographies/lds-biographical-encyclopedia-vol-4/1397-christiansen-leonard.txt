Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christiansen, Leonard

Christiansen, Leonard, presiding Elder of the Ontario Branch, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born March 31, 1879, in Deseret, Utah, the son of Niels John Christiansen and Ellen Stark. He was baptized May 6, 1887, by his father, and sustained as branch president Feb. 19, 1928.
