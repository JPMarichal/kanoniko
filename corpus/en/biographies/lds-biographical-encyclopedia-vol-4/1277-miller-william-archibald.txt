Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Miller, William Archibald

Miller, William Archibald, Bishop of the Milford Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930, was born March 11, 1875, at Greenville, Beaver Co., Utah, the son of David Miller and Margaret Fife. He was baptized March 18, 1883, by Wm. Edwards, located in Milford in 1899, was ordained a High Priest April 20, 1913, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 19, 1928, by Orson F. Whitney.
