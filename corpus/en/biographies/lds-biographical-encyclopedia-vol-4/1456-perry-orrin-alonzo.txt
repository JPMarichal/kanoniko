Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Perry, Orrin Alonzo

Perry, Orrin Alonzo, presiding Elder of Perry, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1875 to 1877, and Bishop from 1877 to 1896, was born Sept. 11, 1817, in Lewis, Essex Co., New York, the son of Gustavus A. Perry and Junus Wing. He was baptized early in 1839, emigrated to Far West, Mo., suffered in the Missouri and Illinois persecutions, arrived in Utah in 1855, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 9, 1877, by Lorenzo Snow. The original name, Three Mile Creek, was changed to that of Perry in his honor.
