Bluth, John V.

Bluth, John V. (Continued from Vol. 2, p. 244.)

In September, 1921, when President James Wotherspoon was called on a mission to Great Britain, Brother Blurb was chosen president of the North Weber Stake. He selected as his counselors, Francis W. Stratford and Thomas M. Irvine. This position he held until January, 1931, when he was called to preside over the Canadian Mission, with headquarters at Toronto. He was honorably released from this mission, turning over the affairs of the mission to his successor July 15, 1935.

From 1901 to 1916 he continued as deputy county clerk, after which he became affiliated with the Scoville Brothers in their broom, printing and lithographing business and later in their oil ventures, until his call to the Canadian mission.

Since commencing his activities at the Church Historian's Office in 1936 he has completed his concordance to the Doctrine and Covenants, which he commenced many years ago.
