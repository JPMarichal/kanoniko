Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Perkins, Reuben Josiah

Perkins, Reuben Josiah, presiding Elder of Walker (Clay Springs) Branch, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1916 to 1919, was born in October, 1856, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of Jesse N. Perkins. He was baptized Dec. 15, 1866, ordained a High Priest June 6, 1908, by James M. Flake, and set apart as presiding Elder May 21, 1916, by Samuel F. Smith. Elder Perkins died Sept. 7, 1919.
