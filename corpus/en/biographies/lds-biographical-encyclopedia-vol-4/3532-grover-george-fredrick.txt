Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grover, George Fredrick

Grover, George Fredrick, presiding Elder of the Hollister Branch, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1925, was born Jan. 8, 1884, at Oakley, Idaho, a son of Walter L. Grover and Celia Millard. He was baptized July 22, 1893, and ordained a High Priest June 1, 1924, by Richard R. Lyman.
