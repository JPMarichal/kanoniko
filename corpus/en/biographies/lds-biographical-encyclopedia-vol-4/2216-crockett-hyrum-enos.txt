Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Crockett, Hyrum Enos

Crockett, Hyrum Enos, Bishop of the Logan 7th Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1917, was born April 6, 1873, in Logan, Utah, the son of Alvin Crockett and Mary Sophia Reed. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 2, 1913, by Geo. F. Richards. He died at Logan Dec. 23, 1935.
