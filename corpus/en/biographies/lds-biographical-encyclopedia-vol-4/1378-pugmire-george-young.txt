Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pugmire, George Young

Pugmire, George Young, Bishop of the Tilden Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1896 to 1907, was born Sept. 20, 1866, in St. Charles, Idaho, the son of George M. Pugmire and Marie Young. He was baptized when eight years old, moved to Tilden in 1895, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 14, 1896, by John Henry Smith.
