Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mecham, Joseph Lyman, sen.

Mecham, Joseph Lyman, sen., Bishop of the Milton Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1889 to 1892, was born Sept. 26, 1856, at E T City, Tooele Co., Utah, a son of Joseph L. Mecham and Maria Tuttle. He was baptized Sept. 7, 1865, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1887-1888, and was ordained a Bishop in May, 1889, by John W. Taylor.
