Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lyman Stake

Rollins, Henry Melvin

Rollins, Henry Melvin, Bishop of the Lyman Ward, Lyman Stake, Wyoming, from 1916 to 1926, was born May 26, 1886, in Minersville, Utah, a son of James Watson Rollins and Harriet Ann Eyre. He was baptized in 1894, ordained a Seventy May 2, 1909, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1909-1911, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 11, 1912, by Daniel Gerrard, and ordained a Bishop April 30, 1916, by Rudger Clawson.
