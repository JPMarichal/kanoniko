Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olpin, Joseph,

Olpin, Joseph, first counselor in the presidency of the Timpanogos Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1928 to 1929, was born Sept. 24, 1897, at Pleasant Grove, Utah, a son of Edwin D. Olpin and Inez Robison. He was baptized Sept. 30, 1905, ordained an Elder in May, 1916, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1916-1919, was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 21, 1927, by George F. Richards, and set apart to preside over the Lindon Ward, which position he held until chosen first counselor in the Timpanogos Stake presidency July 1, 1928.
