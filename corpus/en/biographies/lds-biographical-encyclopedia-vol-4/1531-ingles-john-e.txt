Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ingles, John E.

Ingles, John E., Bishop of the Scoffold Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1902, was born Aug. 24, 1851, at Paisley, Scotland, the son of Robert Ingles and Elizabeth Eccles. He filled a mission to Scotland in 1921-1923 and was ordained a Bishop July 28, 1901, by Reed Smoot.
