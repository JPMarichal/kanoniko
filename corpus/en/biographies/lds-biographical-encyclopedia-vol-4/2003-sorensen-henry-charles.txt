Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sorensen, Henry Charles

Sorensen, Henry Charles, Bishop of the Mendon Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Feb. 6, 1886, at Mendon, Utah, the son of Isaac Sorensen and Mary (Marie) Jacobsen. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a Seventy Jan. 24, 1912, by Chas. H. Hart, filled a mission to the Central States in 1915-1917, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 28, 1925, by Hyrum G. Smith.
