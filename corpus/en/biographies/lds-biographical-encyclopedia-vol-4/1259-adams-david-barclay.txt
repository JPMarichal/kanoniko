Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beaver Stake

Adams, David Barclay

Adams, David Barclay, Bishop of the Greenville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1869 to 1872, was born May 4, 1814, in Scotland. He was baptized April 6, 1844, by Joseph Clemens, emigrated and located in Beaver. On March 20, 1869, he was appointed Bishop of the Beaver 3rd Ward, comprising Greenville and Adamsville, and presided thus until Jan. 15, 1872. He died Aug. 4, 1881.
