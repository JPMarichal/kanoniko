Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Joseph Robert

Harris, Joseph Robert, Bishop of the Woodruff Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1891 to 1905, was born April 11, 1857, in Kaysville, Davis Co., Utah, a son of Joseph Harris and Charlotte Green. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a High Priest Sept. 16, 1889, and ordained a Bishop June 16, 1891, by Lorenzo Snow.
