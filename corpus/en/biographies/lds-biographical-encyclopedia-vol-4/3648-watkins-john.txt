Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Watkins, John

Watkins, John, Bishop of the Midway Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1892 to 1902, was born April 13, 1834, at Kent, England, a son of Thomas John Edward Watkins and Sarah Jordan. He was baptized in 1852, and ordained a High Priest March 30, 1872, by John Jordan. He died Dec. 23, 1902, in Midway.
