Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robinson, Samuel John

Robinson, Samuel John, Bishop of the Dublan Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1899 to 1907, was born Dec. 10, 1863, in Payson, Utah, the son of Jos. Robinson and Jemima Parks. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 3, 1900, and called on a mission to Great Britain in 1928.
