Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

England, James

England, James, Bishop of the La Grande Ward, Union Stake, Oregon, from 1901 to 1902, and Bishop of the Union ward of the same stake, from 1902 to 1905, was born May 24, 1851, in Bradpool, Dorsetshire, England, a son of John England and Jane Paverd. He was baptized in 1862, emigrated to Utah in that year, was ordained a High Priest June 17, 1900, by Franklin S. Bramwell, ordained a Bishop June 9, 1901, by Mathias F. Cowley, and set apart as Bishop of Union Ward March 9, 1902.
