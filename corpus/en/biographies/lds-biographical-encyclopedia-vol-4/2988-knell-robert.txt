Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Knell, Robert

Knell, Robert, Bishop of the Pinto Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1876 to 1897, was born Sept. 22, 1828, in Millbrook, near Southampton, England, the son of John C. Knell and Rhoda Fry. He was baptized Oct. 17, 1848, emigrated to Utah in 1850, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 18, 1877, by Wilford Woodruff.
