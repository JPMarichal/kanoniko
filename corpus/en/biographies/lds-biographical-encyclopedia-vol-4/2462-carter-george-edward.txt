Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carter, George Edward

Carter, George Edward, Bishop of the Porterville Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1920, was born April 1, 1867, in Round Valley, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of Samuel Carter and Sarah Davis. He was baptized in 1875, ordained a High Priest by Francis M. Lyman and ordained a Bishop June 3, 1917, by Hyrum M. Smith.
