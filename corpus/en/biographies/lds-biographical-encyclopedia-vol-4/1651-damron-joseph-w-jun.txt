Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Damron, Joseph W., jun.

Damron, Joseph W., jun., Bishop of the Deseret Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1925, was born June 9, 1866, at Petersburg, Utah, the son of Joseph W. Damron and Margaret P. Freeman. He filled a mission to Samoa in 1891-1892, and to the Society Islands in 1892-1895, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 3, 1908, by Francis M. Lyman.
