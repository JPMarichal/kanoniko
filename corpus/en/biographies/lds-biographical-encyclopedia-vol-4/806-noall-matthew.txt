Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Noall, Matthew

Noall, Matthew, president of the Hawaiian Mission from 1892 to 1895, was born Feb. 1, 1864, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Simon Noall and Rebecca Squire. He was baptized June 6, 1872, ordained a Seventy in 1884, and filled a mission to the Sandwich Islands in 1885-1889. He left Salt Lake City Nov. 17, 1891, on a second mission to preside over the Hawaiian Mission; he arrived in Honolulu Dec. 18, 1891, and returned Nov. 4, 1895.
