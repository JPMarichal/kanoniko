Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tooele Stake

Anderson, George Noel

Anderson, George Noel, Bishop of the Clover Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Jan. 3, 1888, in Grantsville, Tooele Co., Utah, a son of Gustave Anderson and Emily J. Hunter. He was baptized when about eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 5, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
