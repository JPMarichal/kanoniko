Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hendrickson, Iver

Hendrickson, Iver, Bishop of the Farhum Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1919 to 1928, was born Feb. 1, 1865, in Gunnison, Utah, a son of Iver C. Hendriksen and Mette K. Hansen. He was baptized April 2, 1873, ordained a High Priest Feb. 1, 1914, by Daniel G. Miller, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 26, 1919, by Rudger Clawson.
