Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merril, Austin Taylor

Merril, Austin Taylor, presiding Elder from 1889 to 1893, and Bishop from 1893 to 1895, of the Glendale Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, was born May 7, 1869, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of Austin T. Merril and Mary Hatton. He was baptized in 1877, and was sustained as Bishop Oct. 1, 1893.
