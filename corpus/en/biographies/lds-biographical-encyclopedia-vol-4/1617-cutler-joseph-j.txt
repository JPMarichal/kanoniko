Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cutler, Joseph J.

Cutler, Joseph J., Bishop of the Snowville Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1927, was born Feb. 25, 1884, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Jonathan C. Cutler and Rebecca Nilson. He was baptized May 5, 1892, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 17, 1915, by David O. McKay.
