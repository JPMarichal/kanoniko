Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shipley, George Dixon

Shipley, George Dixon, Bishop of the Sutherland Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1917, was born March 28, 1872, in Ogden, Utah, the son of Thomas Shipley and Mary Ann Dixon. He was baptized July 1, 1880, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 22, 1914, by James E. Talmage.
