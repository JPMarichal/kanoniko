Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dorius, John Nikolai

Dorius, John Nikolai, second counselor in the presidency of the South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1922 to 1924, was born March 26, 1876, in Ephraim, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Carl C. N. Dorius and Julia Petrine Peterson. He was baptized July 14, 1884, ordained a Seventy in 1910, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1919-1920, and was ordained a High Priest and set apart as second counselor in the South Sanpete Stake presidency Feb. 5, 1922, by Melvin J. Ballard.
