Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, James Naylor

Jones, James Naylor, presiding Elder of the Fairview Branch, North Sanpete Stake, Utah, from 1859 to 1864, was born April 9, 1810, in Maryland, a son of Thomas Jones and Mary Naylor. He came to Utah in 1848, and was set apart as presiding Elder by Welcome Chapman in 1861. He died Aug. 14, 1865.
