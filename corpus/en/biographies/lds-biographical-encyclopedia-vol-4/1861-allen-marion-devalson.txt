Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Garfield Stake

Allen, Marion Devalson

Allen, Marion Devalson, Bishop of the Kingston Ward, Garfield Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1930+, was born Oct. 17, 1883, at Kingston, Piute Co., Utah, the son of Rufus A. Allen and Sarah Ann Higgins. He was baptized Oct. 17, 1891, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 26, 1917, by Orson F. Whitney.
