Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Black, Edward Webb

Black, Edward Webb, Bishop of the Emery Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Dec. 19, 1881, in Orderville, Utah, a son of William M. Black and Louisa Ann Washburn. He was baptized in 1890, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 20, 1922, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
