Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dean, Charles Edwin

Dean, Charles Edwin, Bishop of the Redmesa Ward, Young Stake, Colorado, from 1923 to 1930+, was born Aug. 26, 1893, in Mill Creek, Utah, a son of Joseph Henry Dean and Florence Ridges. He was baptized Dec. 1, 1901, ordained a High Priest Aug. 20, 1917, and a Bishop Dec. 8, 1923, by Geo. F. Richards.
