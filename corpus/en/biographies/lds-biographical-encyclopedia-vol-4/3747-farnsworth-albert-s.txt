Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Farnsworth, Albert S.

Farnsworth, Albert S., Bishop of the Chester Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1902 to 1904, was born Jan. 4, 1870, at Beaver, Utah, a son of Philo T. Farnsworth and Agnes Patterson. He was ordained a Bishop in 1902.
