Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rawlins, Joseph Sharp

Rawlins, Joseph Sharp, Bishop of the South Cottonwood Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1871 to 1900, was born April 9, 1823, near Carlton, Greene Co., Illinois, a son of Joseph Rawlins and Jane Sharp. He came to Utah in 1848, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 6, 1872.
