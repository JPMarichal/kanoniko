Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shepherd, Edwin Tracy

Shepherd, Edwin Tracy, Bishop of the Paris 2nd Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Sept. 30, 1882, in Paris, Bear Lake Co., Idaho, the son of Lorenzo Tracy Shepherd and Sarah Clifton. He was baptized Sept. 30, 1890, was ordained a High Priest June 22, 1919, by Orson, F. Whitney, and a Bishop Feb. 27, 1919, by Geo. A. Smith.
