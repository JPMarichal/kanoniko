Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Workman, Abram Smith

Workman, Abram Smith, presiding Elder of Hatch Branch, Panguitch Stake, Utah, from 1878 to 1887, was born Oct. 29, 1852, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Jacob L. Workman and Rebecca W. Turner. He was baptized Oct. 28, 1860, and was ordained a High Priest Jan. 17, 1897, by David Cameron. He died Oct. 16, 1918.
