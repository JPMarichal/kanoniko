Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kienke, John

Kienke, John, Bishop of the Mona Ward, Juab Stake, Utah, from 1886 to 1892, was born April 6, 1831, in Mecklenburg-Schwerin, Germany, a son of Christopher Kienke and Margaret Buhse. He was baptized Sept. 12, 1852, filled a mission to Germany in 1878-1880, and was chosen as Bishop of the Mona Ward in 1886.
