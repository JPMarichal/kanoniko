Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Winters, Niels Erastus

Winters, Niels Erastus, Bishop of the Otto Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1909 to 1917 (presiding Elder from 1906 to 1909), was born March 20, 1874, in Montpelier, Bear Lake Co., Idaho, the son of Frantz Martin Winters and Johanna Kristine Larsen. He was baptized Sept. 10, 1882, moved to Big Horn in 1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 28, 1909, by Francis M. Lyman.
