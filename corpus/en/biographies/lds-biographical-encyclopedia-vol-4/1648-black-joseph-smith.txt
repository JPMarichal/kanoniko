Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Black, Joseph Smith

Black, Joseph Smith, Bishop of the Deseret Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1891, was born July 14, 1836, at Lisburn, Antrim Co., Ireland. He came to America in 1840 with his father, was baptized in June, 1844, and ordained a Bishop July 24, 1877, by Erastus Snow. He died Aug. 13, 1910.
