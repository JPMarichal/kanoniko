Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cordon, Ralph Roland

Cordon, Ralph Roland, first counselor in the Teton Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1919 to 1930+, was born Feb. 23, 1866, in Willard, Utah, a son of Alfred Cordon and Mary Ann Voss. He was baptized in 1874, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1909-1911, and was set apart May 17, 1919, as first counselor in the presidency of the Teton Stake by David O. McKay. He died July 29, 1936, at Driggs, Idaho.
