Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Matthews, David Henry

Matthews, David Henry, Bishop of the Matthews Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1886 to 1898, was born Dee. 28, 1851, in San Bernardino, Calif., a son of Joseph Matthews and Polly Matthews. He was baptized when about eight years old, came to Utah in the winter of 1857-1858, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 21, 1886.
