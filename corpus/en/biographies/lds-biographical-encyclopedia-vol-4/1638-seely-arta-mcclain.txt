Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Seely, Arta Mcclain

Seely, Arta Mcclain, Bishop of the Rosette Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1928, was born Nov. 3, 1874, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Isaac J. Seely and Elizabeth Jane Fisher. He was baptized June 17, 1883, by John B. Fagg, ordained a Seventy and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1897-1898, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 31, 1913, by Francis M. Lyman.
