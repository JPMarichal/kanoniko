Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Read, Thomas William

Read, Thomas William, Bishop of the Hooper Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1925, was born Feb. 28, 1883, in Hooper, the son of Thomas R. Read and Martha E. Hardy. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1891, filled a mission to California in 1902-1904, and was ordained a Bishop July 15, 1923, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
