Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Day, Henry Earl

Day, Henry Earl, second counselor in the Oquirrh Stake presidency, Utah, from 1923 to 1929 and first counselor from 1929 to 1930, was born March 10, 1892, in Hunter, Utah, a son of Lorenzo Day and Elizabeth J. Russell. He was baptized May 5, 1900, filled a mission to the Central States in 1913-1915, and was ordained a High Priest Dec. 12, 1920, by Jos. F. Smith, jun. He was set apart as first counselor in the stake presidency Feb. 3, 1929, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
