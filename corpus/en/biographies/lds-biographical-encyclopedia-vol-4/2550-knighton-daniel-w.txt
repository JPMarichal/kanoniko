Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Knighton, Daniel W.

Knighton, Daniel W., Bishop of the McGill Ward, Nevada Stake, Nevada, from 1926 to 1927, was born Nov. 5, 1890, in East Bountiful, Utah, the son of George Knighton and Eliza Johnson. He was baptized May 24, 1900, and ordained a High Priest add Bishop Sept. 19, 1926, by David O. McKay.
