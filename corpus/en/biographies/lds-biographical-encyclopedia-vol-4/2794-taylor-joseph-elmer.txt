Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, Joseph Elmer

Taylor, Joseph Elmer, Bishop of the Harmony Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born July 24, 1896, at Harmony, Utah, a son of Joseph A. Taylor and Margaret A. Pace. He was baptized Aug. 7, 1904, and sustained as Bishop March 7, 1926.
