Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

McAllister, Duncan M.

McAllister, Duncan M., one of the directors of the Genealogical Society of Utah from 1908 to 1917, was born April 18, 1842, in Glasgow, Scotland, and died May 15, 1931, in Salt Lake City, holding the office of a Patriarch. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 651.)
