Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Morgan, Frederick Willard

Morgan, Frederick Willard, Bishop of the Pratt Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1897 to 1908, was born Aug. 12, 1863, in Salt Lake City, a son of Joseph R. Morgan and Mary J. Turner. He was baptized when nine years old, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1890-1892, was ordained a High Priest July 12, 1896, and a Bishop Aug. 14, 1897, by John Henry Smith.
