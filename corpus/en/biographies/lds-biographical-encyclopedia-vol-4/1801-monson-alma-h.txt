Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Monson, Alma H.

Monson, Alma H., Bishop of the Preston 6th Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1929, was born Sept. 9, 1889, at Whitney, Idaho. He was baptized July 2, 1897, ordained a High Priest Sept. 24, 1922, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop Nov. 29, 1924, by Geo. Albert Smith.
