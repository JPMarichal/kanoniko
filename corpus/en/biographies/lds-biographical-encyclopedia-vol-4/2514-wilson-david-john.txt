Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilson, David John

Wilson, David John, Bishop of the Ogden 12th Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Oct. 27, 1887, in Midway, Wasatch Co., Utah, a son of James Brigham Wilson and Margaret Powell. He was baptized March 15, 1896, ordained a High Priest May 22, 1922, by David O. McKay, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 28, 1924, by Reed Smoot.
