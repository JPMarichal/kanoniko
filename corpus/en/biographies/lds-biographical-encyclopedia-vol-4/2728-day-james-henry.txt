Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Day, James Henry

Day, James Henry, Bishop of the Garfield Ward,. Oquirrh Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1929, was born Nov. 30, 1876, in Draper, Utah, a son of James H. Day and Lucy Stringfellow. He was baptized May 9, 1885, ordained a High Priest June 19, 1923, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop Sept. 15, 1927, by Orson F. Whitney.
