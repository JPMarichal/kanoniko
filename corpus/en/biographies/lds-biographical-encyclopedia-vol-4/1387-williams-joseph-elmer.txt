Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, Joseph Elmer

Williams, Joseph Elmer, 2nd counselor in the presidency of the Blackfoot Stake in 1930+, was born Oct. 11, 1892, in Beaver, Utah, the son of Joseph Willfares and Frida C. Anderson. He was baptized in 1900 by James Gerrard, filled a mission to the Central States in 1912-1914, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 27, 1916, by James Duckworth, and was set apart as 2nd counselor in the Blackfoot Stake presidency by Rudger Clawson May 25, 1930.
