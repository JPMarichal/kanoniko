Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bennett, Hiram Bell

Bennett, Hiram Bell, Bishop of the Meadow Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1901, was born March 1, 1823, in Sumner County, Tennessee, a son of William Bennett and Elizabeth Bell. He was baptized about 1841, emigrated to Utah in 1852, and was ordained a Bishop July 22, 1877, by Erastus Snow.
