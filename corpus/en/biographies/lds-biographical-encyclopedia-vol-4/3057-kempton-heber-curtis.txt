Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kempton, Heber Curtis

Kempton, Heber Curtis, Bishop of the Eden Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1921 to 1923, was born April 20, 1890, in Eden, a son of Alvin B. Kempton and Sarah K. Curtis. He was baptized June 5, 1898, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 9, 1921, by Geo. Albert Smith.
