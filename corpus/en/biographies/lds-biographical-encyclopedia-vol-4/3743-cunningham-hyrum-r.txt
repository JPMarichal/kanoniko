Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cunningham, Hyrum R.

Cunningham, Hyrum R., Bishop of the Ashton (Vernon) Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1909 to 1921, was born Jan. 12, 1855, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Andrew Cunningham and Lucinda Rawlins. He was baptized Feb. 23, 1879, and ordained a High Priest June 17, 1907. He was ordained a Bishop Jan. 11, 1909, by David O. McKay.
