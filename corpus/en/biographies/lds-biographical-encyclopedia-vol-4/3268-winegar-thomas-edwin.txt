Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Winegar, Thomas Edwin

Winegar, Thomas Edwin, Bishop of the West Bountiful Ward, South Davis Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born March 15, 1890, in West Bountiful, Davis Co., Utah, a son of William W. Winegar and Myra Pace. He was baptized April 2, 1898, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1911-1913, and was ordained a Bishop June 24, 1923, by Rudger Clawson.
