Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sawley, Edward

Sawley, Edward, Bishop of the East Coalville Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1909, was born March 12, 1854, in Burnley, Lancashire, England, a son of William Sawley and Margaret Hartley. He was baptized in 1885, came to Utah in 1887, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 27, 1908, by Geo. F. Richards.
