Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whitney, George Luke

Whitney, George Luke, first counselor in the Moapa Stake presidency, Nevada, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Aug. 3, 1874, in Panaca, Lincoln Co., Nevada, a son of George Burton Whitney and Lovina Syphus. He was baptized Aug. 3, 1882, ordained a High Priest Sept. 18, 1908, by George Albert Smith, and set apart as first counselor Sept. 1, 1929.
