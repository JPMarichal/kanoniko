Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Muir, George Henry

Muir, George Henry, Bishop of the Gray's Lake Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1896 to 1915, was born Oct. 10, 1865, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of William S. Muir and Lucy Darke. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 12, 1897, by John Henry Smith.
