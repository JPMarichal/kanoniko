Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mauss, Michael

Mauss, Michael, Bishop of the Murray 1st Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1914, was born Nov. 2, 1871, in Papillion, Sarpy Co., Neb., the son of Jacob Mauss and Lena Kelsch. He was baptized Oct. 15, 1888, and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1897-1899. He was ordained a High Priest Oct. 28, 1900, by Iteber J. Grant, and a Bishop Feb. 28, 1909, by Hyrum M. Smith.
