Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carver, Parley Pratt

Carver, Parley Pratt, Bishop of the Glencoe Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1923, was born Jan. 17, 1867, in Plain City, Utah, a son of John Carver and Mary Ann Eames. He was ordained a High Priest Feb. 5, 1910, by Joseph Belnap, and a Bishop June 15, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard.
