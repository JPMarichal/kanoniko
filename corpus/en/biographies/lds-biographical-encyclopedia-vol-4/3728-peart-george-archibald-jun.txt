Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peart, George Archibald, jun.

Peart, George Archibald, jun., Bishop of the Randolph Ward, Woodruff Stake, Wyo., from 1918 to 1922, was born Aug. 16, 1874, in Randolph, Utah, a son of Geo. Alma Peart and Jane McFail. He was baptized when eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 28, 1918, by Orson F. Whitney.
