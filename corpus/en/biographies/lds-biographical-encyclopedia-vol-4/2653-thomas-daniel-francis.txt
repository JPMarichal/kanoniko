Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomas, Daniel Francis

Thomas, Daniel Francis, Bishop of the Lynne Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1875 to 1890, was born April 13, 1826, in Caermarthenshire, Wales, a son of Daniel Thomas and Hannah Willlares. He was baptized May 16, 1847, emigrated to America in 1885, and was ordained a Bishop May 29, 1877, by Franklin D. Richards. He died July 3, 1890, at Lynne, Utah.
