Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Roundy, William A.

Roundy, William A., Bishop of the Baker Ward, Union Stake, Oregon, from 1919 to 1921, was born Jan. 15, 1885, in St. David, Arizona, a son of Jared C. Roundy and Nellie White. He was ordained a Seventy July 15, 1911, by Osborne Richins, and a High Priest and Bishop June 24, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard.
