Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Norton, Alanson

Norton, Alanson, Bishop of the McCammon Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1895 to 1898 (presiding Elder from 1890 to 1895), was born March 26, 1814, in Granville, New York, a son of Allen Norton and Lucy Wilkinson. He was baptized Feb. 16, 1843, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 20, 1895, by Jos. F. Smith. He died Aug. 22, 1904.
