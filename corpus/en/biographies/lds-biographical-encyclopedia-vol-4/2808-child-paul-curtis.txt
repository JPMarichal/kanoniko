Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Child, Paul Curtis

Child, Paul Curtis, Bishop of the Poplar Grove Ward, Pioneer Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1930, was born March 31, 1892, in Ogden, Utah, a son of Lester A. Child and Emma Goodale. He was baptized July 1, 1900, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 1, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
