Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burbridge, George Wm.

Burbridge, George Wm., president of the Society Islands Mission from 1929 to 1933, was born Sept. 2, 1896 in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of George E. Burbidge and Minnie M. Brinton. He was baptized Oct. 2, 1904, set apart July 13, 1915, for a mission to the Society Islands, and returned in 1919. He was called on a second mission to the islands in 1929, this time to preside.
