Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Westover, John Lycurgus

Westover, John Lycurgus, Bishop of the Joseph City Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1916 to 1930+, was born Oct. 4, 1880, in Joseph City, a son of Henry W. Despain and Joanna M. Brickson. He was baptized Oct. 5, 1888, ordained a High Priest Sept. 28, 1913, by Samuel F. Smith, and a Bishop May 13, 1916, by Geo. F. Richards. He filled a short term mission to the Eastern States in 1929-1930.
