Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Young, Archibald Graham

Young, Archibald Graham, Bishop of the Richfield Ward, Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, from 1899 to 1904, was born May 22, 1865, at Kirkintilloch, Dumbartonshire, Scotland, the son of Archibald Miller Young and Mary Graham. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a Seventy March 6, 1895, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1895-1897, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 25, 1899, by Francis M. Lyman.
