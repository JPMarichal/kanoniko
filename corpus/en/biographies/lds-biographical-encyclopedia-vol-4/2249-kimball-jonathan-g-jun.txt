Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kimball, Jonathan G., jun.

Kimball, Jonathan G., jun., Bishop of the San Pedro Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, in 1930+, was born June 20, 1890, in Logan, Utah, a son of Jonathan G. Kimball and Jennie K. Knowlton. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1898, ordained a High Priest April 26, 1925, by Pres. Heber J. Grant, and a Bishop Feb. 22, 1931, by Melvin J. Ballard.
