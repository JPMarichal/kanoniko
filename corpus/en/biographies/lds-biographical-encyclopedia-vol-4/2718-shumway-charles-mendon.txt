Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shumway, Charles Mendon

Shumway, Charles Mendon. Bishop of the Treasureton Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1911 to 1928, was born July 15, 1872, in Mendon, Utah, a son of Andrew P. Shumway and Mary Christian. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest Jan. 25, 1904, by William C. Parkinson, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 9, 1911, by Geo. Albert Smith.
