Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hubbard, David

Hubbard, David, Bishop of the Elba Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1910 to 1915, was born Nov. 30, 1861, in Willard, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of Charles Hubbard and Mary Edwards. He was baptized April 17, 1871, ordained a High Priest Aug. 8, 1897, by Moroni Pickett, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 6, 1910, by Orson F. Whitney.
