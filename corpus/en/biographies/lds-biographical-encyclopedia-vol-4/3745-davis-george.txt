Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davis, George

Davis, George, Bishop of the Wilford Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1887 to 1893, was born Nov. 4, 1833, at Stoke, Abbott, Dorsetshire, England, a son of William Davis and Lucy Davis. He was baptized Feb. 18, 1849, ordained a High Priest July 24, 1877, by Franklin D. Richards, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 21, 1887, by Lorenzo Snow. He died June 5, 1912.
