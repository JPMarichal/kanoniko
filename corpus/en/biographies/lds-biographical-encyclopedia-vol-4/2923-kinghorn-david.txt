Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kinghorn, David

Kinghorn, David, Bishop of the Lewisville Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1905 to 1913, was born May 6, 1862, at Bellville, St. Clair Co., Illinois, a son of Alexander Kinghorn, sen., and Jane Campbell. He was baptized May 31, 1874, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 7, 1905, by Hyrum M. Smith.
