Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bringhurst, George Roberts

Bringhurst, George Roberts, Bishop of the Lovell West Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1917 to 1918, was born March 3, 1876, in Toquerville, Washington Co., Utah, the son of Wm. A. Bringhurst and Selinda Palmer. He was baptized May 16, 1884, ordained an Elder Nov. 5, 1899, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1899-1902. He was ordained a Bishop May 27, 1917, by Anthony W. Ivins.
