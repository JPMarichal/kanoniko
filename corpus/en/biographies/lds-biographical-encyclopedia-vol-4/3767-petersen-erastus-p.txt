Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Petersen, Erastus P.

Petersen, Erastus P., Bishop of the Twin Groves Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1920 to 1929, was born Sept. 6., 1893, at Mt. Pleasant, Utah, a son of Frederik Petersen and Annie Marsine Jacobson. He was baptized Oct. 12, 1901, ordained a High Priest July 27, 1919, by James E. Talmage, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 30, 1920, by George F. Richards.
