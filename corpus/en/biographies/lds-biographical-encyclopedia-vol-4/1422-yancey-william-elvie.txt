Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Yancey, William Elvie

Yancey, William Elvie, Bishop of the Weiser Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1928, was born Sept. 4, 1872, in Mississippi, the son of Jasper Yancey and Sarah Francis Tunnell. He was baptized April 28, 1899, was ordained a High Priest June 25, 1916, by C. Oscar Winklet, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 13, 1925, by Melvin J. Ballard.
