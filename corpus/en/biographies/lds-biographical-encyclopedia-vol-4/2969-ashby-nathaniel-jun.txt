Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ashby, Nathaniel, jun.

Ashby, Nathaniel, jun., Bishop of the St. George 4th Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1869 to 1877, was born May 25, 1835, in Salem, Mass., a son of Nathaniel Ashby and Susan A. Hammond. He was baptized by Erastus Snow, filled a mission to Hawaii in 1881-1882, and died at sea one and one-half days out of San Francisco early in 1882, on his return from his mission.
