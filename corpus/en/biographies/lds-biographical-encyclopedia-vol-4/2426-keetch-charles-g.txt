Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Keetch, Charles G.

Keetch, Charles G., Bishop of the Wardboro Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1891 to 1909, was born Sept. 12, 1861, in Echo Canyon, Utah, the son of Charles G. Keetch and Mercy Barker. He was baptized Aug. 3, 1873, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 11, 1891, by Franklin D. Richards.
