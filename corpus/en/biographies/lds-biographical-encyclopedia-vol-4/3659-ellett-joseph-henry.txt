Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ellett, Joseph Henry

Ellett, Joseph Henry, Bishop of the Giles Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1907 to 1910, was born June 20, 1872, in Holden Utah, a son of John J. Ellett and Mary Turner. He was baptized in September, 1880, filled a mission to Montana in 1898-1899, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 7, 1907, by Orson F. Whitney.
