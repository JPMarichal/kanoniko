Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomas, Joseph Francis

Thomas, Joseph Francis, second counselor in the San Luis Stake presidency, Colorado, from 1890 to 1893, was born April 4, 1858, in Burks Garden, Tazwell Co., Virginia, the son of Abijah M. Thomas and Malinda Dalton. He was baptized Aug. 14, 1876, ordained a High Priest Aug. 18, 1890, and set apart as second counselor in the San Luis Stake presidency by Geo. Q. Cannon.
