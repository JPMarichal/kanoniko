Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Steiner, Casper

Steiner, Casper, Bishop of the Rexburg 2nd Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1887 to 1893, was born April 20, 1846, in Bern, Switzerland, the son of John Steiner and Anna Maria Christen. He was baptized in 1860, came to Utah in 1861, and moved to Rexburg in 1884. He was ordained a Bishop Aug. 21, 1887, by Lorenzo Snow.
