Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Orme, Joseph Cross

Orme, Joseph Cross, Bishop of the Tooele North Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1915, was born Aug. 1, 1860, in Tooele Co., Utah, a son of Samuel Washington Orme and Sarah Cross. He was baptized Nov. 6, 1869, ordained a High Priest Feb. 26, 1897, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop Dec. 29, 1912, by George F. Richards.
