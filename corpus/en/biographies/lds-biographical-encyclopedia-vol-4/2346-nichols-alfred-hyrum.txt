Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nichols, Alfred Hyrum

Nichols, Alfred Hyrum, Bishop of the Gilbert Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1928 to 1930+, was born March 7, 1897, in Oaxaca, Mexico, a son of E. S. Nichols and Viola V. Scott. He was baptized April 2, 1905, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 19, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
