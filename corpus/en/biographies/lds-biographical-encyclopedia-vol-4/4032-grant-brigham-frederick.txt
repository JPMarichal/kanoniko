Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grant, Brigham Frederick

Grant, Brigham Frederick, a member of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1906 to 1936, was born Oct. 17, 1856, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Jedediah M. Grant and Maryetta Kesler. He died Aug. 30, 1936, in Los Angeles, California.
