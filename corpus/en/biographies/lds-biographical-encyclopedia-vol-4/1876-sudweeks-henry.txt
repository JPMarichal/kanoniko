Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sudweeks, Henry

Sudweeks, Henry, Bishop of the Circleville Ward, Garfield Stake, Garfield Co., Utah, from 1920 to 1927, was born May 3, 1881, at Kingston, Garfield Co., Utah, the son of Richard Henry Sudweeks and Hannah E. Gibbs. He was baptized May 3, 1889, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 28, 1920, by Hyrum G. Smith.
