Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beeley, Arthur Lawton

Beeley, Arthur Lawton, a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A., from 1920 to 1935, was born Aug. 28, 1890, in Stockport, Cheshire, England, the son of William Beeley and Elizabeth Ann Lawton. He came to Salt Lake at the age of 18, was baptized June 15, 1907, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1913-1915, and was a member of the 45th quorum of Seventy. He acted as principal of the Emery Academy in Castle Dale, went to the University of Utah in 1917, was in Europe during the first nine months of the World war and was in Paris the day France declared war on Germany. He is now a member of the faculty of the University of Utah. His further activities, etc., can be found in "Who's Who in America."
