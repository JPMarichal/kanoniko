Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Reynolds, Amasa Owen

Reynolds, Amasa Owen, Bishop of the Luna Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1919 to 1930, was born July 2, 1879, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of Braman Reynolds and Gertrude Van Etten. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest Nov. 19, 1905, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop Oct. 21, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard.
