Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Reed, John Beaverly

Reed, John Beaverly, first counselor in the San Luis Stake presidency, Colo., from 1924 to 1930+, was born Oct. 25, 1875, in Lexington, Tenn., a son of John L. Reed and Prescilla Adair. He came to Colorado in 1880, was baptized Aug. 13, 1883, ordained a High Priest Nov. 15, 1914, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop Dec. 10, 1922. He presided over the Sanford Ward as Bishop from 1922 to 1924. On Dec. 6, 1924, he was chosen as first counselor in the stake presidency and was set apart by Geo. F. Richards.
