Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hollingworth, Benjamin Hanson

Hollingworth, Benjamin Hanson, Bishop of the Center Ward, Salt Lake Stake, from 1922 to 1925, was born Oct. 8, 1896, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Benjamin Hatton Hollingworth and Emma Hanson. He was baptized Dec. 2, 1904, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1916-1918, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 10, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
