Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taggart, Henry Milton

Taggart, Henry Milton, Bishop of the Hawthorne Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born March 9, 1875, in Richville, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of Geo. Washington Taggart and Clarissa Marina Rogers. He was baptized April 8, 1883, ordained a Seventy April 19, 1899, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1899-1901, and was ordained a High Priest June 18, 1919, by Anthon H. Lurid, and a Bishop March 5, 1924.
