Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rogerson, Daniel Edwin

Rogerson, Daniel Edwin, presiding Elder of the Fort Hall Branch, Blackfeet Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born March 25, 1880, in Heber City, Utah, the son of Daniel Rogerson and Lydia Holmes Fuller. He was baptized May 2, 1889, ordained an Elder April 19, 1902, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1902-1904, moved to Fort Hall, in 1917, and on June 21, 1924, he was set apart as presiding Elder of the Fort Hall Branch by James Duckworth.
