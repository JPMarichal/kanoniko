Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

North Sevier Stake

Bird, Kelsey Walter

Bird, Kelsey Walter, Bishop of the Salina 1st Ward, North Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1916, was born March 10, 1868, in Mendon, Utah, a son of Kelsey Bird and Ann Muir. He was baptized in 1877, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1908-1910, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 23, 1912, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
