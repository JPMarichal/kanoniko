Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Little, Edwin Sobieski

Little, Edwin Sobieski, Bishop of the Clawson (Leigh) Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1895 to 1898, was born Nov. 5, 1860, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of George E. Little and Martha Taylor. He was baptized when eight years old, moved to Teton Valley in 1889, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 9, 1895, by John Henry Smith.
