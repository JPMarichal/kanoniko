Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Charles Leo

Smith, Charles Leo, Bishop of the Hazelton Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1929, was born April 29, 1892, in Moroni, Utah, a son of George A. Smith and Eleanor R. Morris. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1900, and ordained a Bishop June 17, 1928, by Orson F. Whitney.
