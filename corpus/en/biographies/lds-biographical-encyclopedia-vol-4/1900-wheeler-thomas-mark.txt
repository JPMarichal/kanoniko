Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wheeler, Thomas Mark

Wheeler, Thomas Mark, Bishop of the Sugar House Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born May 8, 1881, in Sugar House, Salt Lake Co., Utah, the son of Thomas Wheeler and Frances R. C. Elkins. He was baptized April 29, 1890, ordained a High Priest Dec. 3, 1916, by Frank Y. Taylor, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1919-1921, and was ordained a Bishop Dec. 1, 1925, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
