Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cluff, Leonard Bruce

Cluff, Leonard Bruce, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 30th Ward, Pioneer Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1929, was born July 31, 1886, in Provo, Utah, a son of Jerry Cluff and Lydia Snow. He was baptized in June, 1895, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 31, 1926, by Geo. Albert Smith.
