Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Joseph

Jensen, Joseph, second counselor in the presidency of the Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1917, was born Nov. 29, 1868, at Mantua, Box Elder Co., Utah, the son of Peter Jensen and Anne Marie Hansen. He was baptized March 25, 1877, and when the Bear River Stake was organized Oct. 11, 1908, he was chosen as second counselor to Pres. Milton H. Welling and set apart to that position by Rudger Clawson.
