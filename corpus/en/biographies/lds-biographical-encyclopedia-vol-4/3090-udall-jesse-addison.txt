Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Udall, Jesse Addison

Udall, Jesse Addison, Bishop of the Thateher Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1929 to 1930+, was born June 24, 1893, in Eagar, Arizona, a son of David K. Udall and Ida F. Hunt. He was baptized Aug. 3, 1901, ordained a High Priest by Richard R. Lyreart in 1920, and a Bishop March 17, 1929, by Geo. Albert Smith.
