Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ryckman, Lloyd Mason

Ryckman, Lloyd Mason, presiding Elder of the Redondo Branch, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1928 to 1930, was born March 25, 1892, at Shier, Michigan, the son of Albert Joseph Ryckman and Mabel M. Mason. He was baptized May 3, 1924, and ordained a High Priest Jan. 31, 1926, by John F. Corbridge.
