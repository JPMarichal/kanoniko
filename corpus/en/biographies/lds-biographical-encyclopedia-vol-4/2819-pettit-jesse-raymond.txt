Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pettit, Jesse Raymond

Pettit, Jesse Raymond, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 5th Ward, Pioneer Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1918, was born July 25, 1886, in Salt Lake City, a son of Edwin Pettit and Rebecca Hill. He was baptized July 30, 1894, and ordained a Bishop April 23, 1911, by Francis M. Lyman.
