Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christensen, George

Christensen, George, Bishop of the Kamas Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1920, was born July 4, 1867, in Milton, Utah, a son of Lars Peter Christensen and Annie Mary Peterson. He was sustained as Bishop June 25, 1916, and ordained a Bishop and set apart Aug. 6, 1916, by James E. Talmage.
