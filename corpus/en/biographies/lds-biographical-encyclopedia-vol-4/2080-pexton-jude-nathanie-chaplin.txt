Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pexton, Jude Nathanie Chaplin

Pexton, Jude Nathaniel Chaplin, second counselor in the Juab Stake presidency, Utah, from 1921 to 1924, was born Dec. 5, 1882, in Nephi, Juab Co., Utah, a son of James Pexton and Mary Chaplin. He was baptized June 3, 1891, and ordained a High Priest and set apart as second counselor Feb. 13, 1921, by James E. Talmage.
