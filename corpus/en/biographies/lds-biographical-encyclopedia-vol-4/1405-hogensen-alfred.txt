Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hogensen, Alfred

Hogensen, Alfred, Bishop of the Boise 1st Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1923 to 1926, was born April 28, 1875, in Christiania, Norway, a foster-son of Christian Hogensen and Petria Larsen. He emigrated to America in June, 1884, was baptized July 10, 1884, ordained a Seventy April 27, 1897, and filled a mission to the Indian Territory in 1897-1899. He moved to Boise from Montpelier in 1906, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 22, 1914, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop May 2, 1920. He acted as Bishop of the Boise Ward from that date until the ward was divided March 4, 1923, when he was set apart to preside over the Boise 1st Ward by Rudger Clawson.
