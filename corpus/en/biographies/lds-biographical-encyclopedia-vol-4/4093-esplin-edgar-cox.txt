Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Esplin, Edgar Cox

Esplin, Edgar Cox, an Elder who died in the missionary field, was bern March 8, 1887, in Orderville, Kane Co., Utah, a son of Henry W. Esplin and Philena Cox. He was baptized April 5, 1895, by Fred J. Carroll, and later ordained a Seventy. When called te fill his mission in the Eastern States, he was residing in Orderville, Utah. Responding to the call, he was set apart Nov. 24, 1925. Elder Esplin was a short-term missionary, and had been in his field of labor less than a month when he died of pneumonia Dec. 7, 1925, in Charlesten, West Virginia. The body of Elder Esplin was brought home by Elder Sidney R. Godfrey.
