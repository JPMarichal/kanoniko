Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cowan, Peter Burt

Cowan, Peter Burt, Bishop of the Nephi South Ward, Juab Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Sept. 19, 1882, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of John S. Cowan and Elizabeth Ann Condie. He was baptized when about eight years old, filled a mission to Hawaii in 1902-1906, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 8, 1914, by Rudger Clawson, who also ordained him a Bishop Oct. 4, 1924.
