Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, John E.

Harris, John E., Bishop of the Moore Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, in 1920 (from March to December), was born April 19, 1886, in Cornish, Utah, a son of William J. Harris and Jessie L. Freckleton. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 18, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard, and a Bishop March 28, 1920, by Geo. F. Richards.
