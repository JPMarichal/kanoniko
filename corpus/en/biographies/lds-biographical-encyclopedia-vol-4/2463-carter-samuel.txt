Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carter, Samuel

Carter, Samuel, Bishop of the West Porterville Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1882 to 1897, was born Sept. 1, 1836, in Wardington, Oxfordshire, England, a son of John Carter and Mary Temms. He was baptized in October, 1851, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 19, 1882, by Franklin D. Richards.
