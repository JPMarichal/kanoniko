Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hogan, Ira

Hogan, Ira, Bishop of the Thatcher Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1898 to 1905, was born Nov. 24, 1854, in West Bountiful, Davis Co., Utah, the son of Goudy Hogan and Bergitta Nilson. He was baptized by John Allsop, located in Gentile Valley in 1881, and was called to act as Bishop of the Thatcher Ward when the Bannock Stake was organized, July 24, 1898. He died at Soda Springs, Idaho, April 16, 1905.
