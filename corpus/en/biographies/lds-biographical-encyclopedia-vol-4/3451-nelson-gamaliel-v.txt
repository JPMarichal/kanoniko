Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nelson, Gamaliel V.

Nelson, Gamaliel V., Bishop of the Driggs Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1927, was born Dec. 8, 1871, at Oxford, Idaho, a son of Wm. G. Nelson and Elvira Vail. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1880, ordained a High Priest June 4, 1896, by Mathias F. Cowley, and a Bishop Oct. 24, 1925, by Orson F. Whitney. He died Oct. 12, 1928, at Driggs, Idaho.
