Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lee, Alfred Dorris

Lee, Alfred Dorris, Bishop of the Murtaugh Ward, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1925, was born Jan. 13, 1890, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of A. O. Lee and Elizabeth Corbett. He was baptized Aug. 30, 1913, ordained a High Priest Dec. 14, 1919, by Lawrence G. Kirkman, and a Bishop in 1921. He died Nov. 1, 1925.
