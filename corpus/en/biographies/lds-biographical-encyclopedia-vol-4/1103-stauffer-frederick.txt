Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stauffer, Frederick,

Stauffer, Frederick, president of the Turkish Mission from 1889 to 1891, was born Oct. 24, 1866, in Willard, Utah, a son of John Stauffer and Elizabeth Mussler. He was baptized when about nine years old, set apart April 20, 1889, for a mission to Turkey, and returned Dec. 23, 1891.
