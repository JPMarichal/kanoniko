Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ricks, Orson

Ricks, Orson, Bishop of the Hibbard Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born April 23, 1873, in Logan, Utah, the son of Thomas E. Ricks and Elizabeth J. Shoup. He was baptized Nov. 1, 1883, and was ordained a High Priest Nov. 2, 1913, by James E. Talmage, and a Bishop Sept. 29, 1929, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
