Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Collings, John

Collings, John, Bishop of the Matthews Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Nov. 25, 1890, in Paris, Idaho, the son of James Collings and Frances Rich. He was baptized May 6, 1899, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 26, 1925, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
