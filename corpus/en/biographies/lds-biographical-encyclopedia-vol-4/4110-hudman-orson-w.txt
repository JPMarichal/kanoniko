Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hudman, Orson W.

Hudman, Orson W., Bishop of the Coltman Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1916 to 1921, was born June 16, 1868, at Slaterville, Utah, the son of John Hudman and Emma Manning. He was baptized about 1879, was ordained a High Priest May 4, 1913, by Hyrum M. Smith, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 15, 1916, by Orson F. Whitney.
