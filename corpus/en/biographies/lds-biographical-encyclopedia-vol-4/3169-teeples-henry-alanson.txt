Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Teeples, Henry Alanson

Teeples, Henry Alanson, Bishop of the Burrville Ward, Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, from 1891 to 1900, was born Nov. 8, 1842, in Nauvoo, Illinois, the son of George B. Teepies and Huldah Colby. He was ordained a High Priest Feb. 22, 1891, by William H. Seegmiller, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 22, 1892.
