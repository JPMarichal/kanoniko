Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Choules, Albert

Choules, Albert, president of the Teton Stake of Zion, Idaho, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Dec. 29, 1877, in Wilton, Wiltshire, England, a son of George Choules and Mary Jane Pearce. He came to Utah in 1882, was baptized May 19, 1887, ordained a Seventy Feb. 27, 1903, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to England in 1903-1905, and was ordained a High Priest May 18, 1919, by David O. McKay. He was set apart as stake president Feb. 20, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard.
