Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lewis, Henry Arundel

Lewis, Henry Arundel, Bishop of the Georgetown Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1877 to 1900, was born June 26, 1838, in Hull, Yorkshire, England, the son of Henry Lewis and Jane Arundel. He was baptized when eleven years old in England, emigrated to America in 1850, and located in Idaho in 1871; was ordained a Bishop Aug. 26, 1877.
