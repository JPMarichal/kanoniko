Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Angus Elmer

Peterson, Angus Elmer, Bishop of the Hollywood Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1928 to 1930+, was born July 16, 1884, in Lehi, Utah, the son of John R. Peterson and Agnes E. Sorensen. He was baptized Aug. 20, 1893, ordained a High Priest March 11, 1923, by Pres. Heber J. Grant, and ordained a Bishop July 14, 1928, by Richard R. Lyman.
