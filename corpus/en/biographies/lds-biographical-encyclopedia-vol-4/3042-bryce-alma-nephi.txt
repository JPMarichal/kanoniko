Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bryce, Alma Nephi

Bryce, Alma Nephi, Bishop of the Bryce Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1892 to 1902, was born Oct. 24, 1861, in Mill Creek, Utah, the son of Ebenezer Bryce and Mary Ann Park. He was chosen as Bishop Oct. 9, 1892, and died in El Paso, in December, 1915.
