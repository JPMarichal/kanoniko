Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hawkins, Riego Stay

Hawkins, Riego Stay, Bishop of the Granite Ward, East Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Aug. 30, 1879, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Riego Hawkins and Charlotte E. Stay. He was baptized Aug. 30, 1887, ordained to the Priesthood and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1898-1900, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 7, 1926, by Heber J. Grant, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 8, 1928, by Rudger Clawson.
