Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Campbell, Joseph

Campbell, Joseph, Bishop of the Providence 2nd Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1923, was born Oct. 26, 1861, in Providence, a son of Jos. Hyrum Campbell and Elizabeth Mathews. He was baptized June 18, 1871, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1891-1893, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 1, 1909, by Hyrum M. Smith.
