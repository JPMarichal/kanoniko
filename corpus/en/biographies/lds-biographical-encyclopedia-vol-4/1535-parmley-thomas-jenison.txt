Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Parmley, Thomas Jenison

Parmley, Thomas Jenison, Bishop of the Pleasant Valley Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1888 to 1920, was born Oct. 22, 1855, in Durham, Grimsby, England, the son of Joseph Parmley and Mary Jenison. He was baptized May 26, 1869, emigrated to Utah in 1881, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 17, 1888, by Heber J. Grant.
