Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, Thomas

Taylor, Thomas, Bishop of the Elba Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1887 to 1910, was born Sept. 8, 1850, in Lee County, Iowa, the son of Benjamin Taylor and Mary Ann Cole. He was baptized in 1860, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 24, 1887, by John W. Taylor. He died Aug. 26, 1921.
