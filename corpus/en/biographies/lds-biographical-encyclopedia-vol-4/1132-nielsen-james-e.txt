Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nielsen, James E.

Nielsen, James E., Bishop of the Kimball Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1913 to 1918, was born Feb. 22, 1880, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, the son of Mads Nielsen and Mette Marie Christensen. He was baptized when eight years old and ordained a Bishop July 27, 1913, by Pres. Joseph F. Smith. He died in Cardston about 1926.
