Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Foutz, Alma Luff

Foutz, Alma Luff, Bishop of the Burnham or Fruitland Ward, Young Stake, New Mexico, from 1921 to 1928, was born March 9, 1882, in Moava, Arizona, a son of Joseph L. Foutz and Emma E. Crossland. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained an Elder Aug. 23, 1909, by Henry Thomas Stolworthy, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1911-1913, was ordained a High Priest March 13, 1921, by Henry Thomas Stolworthy, and a Bishop Aug. 28, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard.
