Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stanley, George Burton

Stanley, George Burton, Bishop of the Heber 1st Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1923 to 1930, was born Aug. 29, 1899, at Park City, Utah, a son of George Franklin Stanley and Harriet Hales. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1907, ordained a High Priest Oct. 29, 1922, by Stephen L. Richards, and ordained a Bishop April 15, 1923, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
