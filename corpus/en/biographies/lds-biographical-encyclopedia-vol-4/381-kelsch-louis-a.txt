Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kelsch, Louis A.

Kelsch, Louis A., a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A. from 1905 to 1909, was born Nov. 28, 1856, in Vinningen, Germany, the son of Louis Kelsch and Saraphine Kelsch. He came to Utah when he was nine years old, was baptized Oct. 25, 1877, and filled a mission to the Northern States, acting as president of that mission from 1896 to 1901. He also filled a mission to Japan in 1901-1902, and died Oct. 16, 1917.
