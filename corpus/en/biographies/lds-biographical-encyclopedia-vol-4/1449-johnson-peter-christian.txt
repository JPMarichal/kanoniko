Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Peter Christian

Johnson, Peter Christian, Bishop of the Mantua Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1917, was born Sept. 18, 1859, in Tikjobing, Frederiksborg amt, Denmark, the son of Lars Jorgensen and Anna Marie Hansen. He was baptized July 10, 1870, by Jens Peter Andreasen; emigrated with his mother to Utah in 1870, and located in Mantua, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 6, 1908, by Francis M. Lyman, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 25, 1911, by Geo. F. Richards.
