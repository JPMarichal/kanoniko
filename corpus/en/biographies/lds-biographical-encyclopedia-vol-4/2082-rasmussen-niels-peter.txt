Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rasmussen, Niels Peter

Rasmussen, Niels Peter, Bishop of the Levan Ward, Juab Stake, Utah, from 1892 to 1901, was born Oct. 27, 1843, near Nykjobing, on the island of Falster, Denmark, a son of Peter Rasmussen and Bodil Olsen. He was baptized July 3, 1861, emigrated to Utah in 1862, was ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Denmark in 1878-1880, was ordained a High Priest July 20, 1890, by George Q. Cannon and ordained a Bishop April 17, 1892, by Anthon H. Lund. He died Aug. 9, 1922.
