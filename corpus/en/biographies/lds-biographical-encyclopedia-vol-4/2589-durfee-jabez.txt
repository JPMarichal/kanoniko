Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Durfee, Jabez

Durfee, Jabez, Bishop of the Aurora Ward, North Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1881 to 1883, was born May 10, 1828, in Williams, Oswego Co., N.Y., a son of Edmund Durfee and Lancy Pickle. He was ordained a High Priest in August, 1880, by Albert K. Thurber, and a Bishop Feb. 27, 1881. He died Dec. 27, 1883.
