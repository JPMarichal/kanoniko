Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Partridge, William Clayton

Partridge, William Clayton, Bishop of the Cowley Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1901 to 1910, and president of the Big Horn Stake from 1910 to 1916, was born Oct. 2, 1862, in Farmington, Davis Co., Utah, the son of Edward Partridge and Sarah Lucretia Clayton. He was baptized in June, 1871, by Ralph Rowley, moved to Fillmore in 1864, filled a mission to Hawaii in 1882-1885, moved to Big Horn in 1900, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 14, 1900, by Jedediah M. Grant, and a Bishop July 19, 1901, by Abraham O. Woodruff.
