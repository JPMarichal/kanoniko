Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, George Usher

Smith, George Usher, Bishop of the Burton Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1887 to 1891, was born March 27, 1842, in Durham, England, the son of Thomas Smith and Mary J. Usher. He came to Utah in 1859, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 21, 1887.
