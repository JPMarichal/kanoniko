Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Holland, Joseph

Holland, Joseph, Bishop of the Shelley Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1906 to 1909, was born Oct. 29, 1867, in Ogden, Utah, a son of John Holland and Mary Burton. He was baptized in 1877, ordained a High Priest May 10, 1902, by Geo. Teasdale, and a Bishop Jan. 14, 1906, by Rudger Clawson.
