Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hunt, William Ammon

Hunt, William Ammon, Bishop of the Clay Springs Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1921 to 1930+, was born June 9, 1887, in Pine, Arizona, the son of Alma Moroni Hunt and Rosetta Smutz. He was baptized Aug. 8, 1895, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 7, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
