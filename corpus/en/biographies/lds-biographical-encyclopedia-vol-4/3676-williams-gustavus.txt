Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, Gustavus

Williams, Gustavus, Bishop of the Teasdale Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930, was born Feb. 3, 1880, in Escalante, Utah, a son of Sylvester Williams and Mary Coleman. He was baptized April 1, 1889, ordained a High Priest May 9, 1915, by David O. McKay, and a Bishop Aug. 15, 1926, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
