Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dunn, Charles Welch

Dunn, Charles Welch, second counselor in the Logan Stake presidency, Utah, in 1930+, was born April 16, 1885, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Charles O. Dunn and Martha Jane Welch. He was baptized June 11, 1893, was ordained a High Priest July 20, 1912, by Hyrum G. Smith, and set apart as second counselor in the Stake presidency Oct. 19, 1930, by Rudger Clawson.
