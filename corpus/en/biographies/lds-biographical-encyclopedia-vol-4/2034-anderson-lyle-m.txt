Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Anderson, Lyle M.

Anderson, Lyle M., Bishop of the Ammon Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born June 16, 1897, in Mantua, Utah, a son of Joseph Anderson and Mary C. Christensen. He was baptized July 5, 1905, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1919-1921, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 9, 1930, by Stephen L. Richards.
