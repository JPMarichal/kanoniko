Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Big Horn Stake

Anderson, Charles Gustave

Anderson, Charles Gustave, presiding Elder of the Penrose Branch, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1928 to 1930+, was born June 1, 1877, in Richfield, Sevier Co., Utah, the son of Gustave Anderson and Mary Larson. He was baptized June 1, 1886, ordained a Seventy Oct. 19, 1898, by Francis M. Lyman, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1898-1901, and was ordained a High Priest Sept. 29, 1912.
