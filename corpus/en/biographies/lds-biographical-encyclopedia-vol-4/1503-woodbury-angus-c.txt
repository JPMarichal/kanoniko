Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Woodbury, Angus C.

Woodbury, Angus C., Bishop of the View Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1916 to 1919, was born May 20, 1873, the son of Orrin Woodbury and Ann Cannon. He was baptized in 1881 and was ordained a Bishop July 30, 1916, by David O. McKay.
