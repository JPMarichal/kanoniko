Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grant, Joseph Hyrum, jun.

Grant, Joseph Hyrum, jun., Bishop of the Salt Lake City 33rd Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1928, was born July 20, 1876, in Bountiful, Utah, the son of Jos. H. Grant and Eva Eliza Eldredge. He filled a mission to Colorado in 1897-1899, was ordained a Bishop Feb. 21, 1926, by Pres. Heber J. Grant, and died in Salt Lake City in February, 1929.
