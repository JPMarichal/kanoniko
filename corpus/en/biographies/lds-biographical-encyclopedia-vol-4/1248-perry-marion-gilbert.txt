Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Perry, Marion Gilbert

Perry, Marion Gilbert, Bishop of the Deweyville Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Dec. 11, 1887, at Slaterville, Weber Co., Utah, the son of Stephen Washington Perry, jun., and Laura Etta Rogers. He was baptized Sept. 6, 1896, by his father, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 22, 1926.
