Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mccardell, James

Mccardell, James, Bishop of the Inglewood Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1924 to 1926, was born May 21, 1888, at Nephi, Utah, the son of John McCardell and Margaret Fowles. He was baptized July 12, 1905, ordained a High Priest July 27, 1924, by Wm. J. Reeves, and a Bishop Nov. 9, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard.
