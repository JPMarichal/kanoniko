Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peacock, Brigham James, jun.

Peacock, Brigham James, jun., Bishop of the Emery Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1925, was born April 8, 1882, in Manti, Utah, the son of Brigham J. Peacock and Sarah E. Cox. He was baptized March 10, 1891, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 12, 1922, by Geo. Albert Smith.
