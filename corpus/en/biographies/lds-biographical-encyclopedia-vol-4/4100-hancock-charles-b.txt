Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hancock, Charles B.

Hancock, Charles B., a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company C, was born Dec. 23, 1823, in Columbia County, Ohio, a son of Solomon Hancock and Alta Adams. He was baptized April 6, 1833, by his father, in Jackson County, Mo., ordained a Seventy in Illinois in 1842, marched with the Battalion to California, and after being discharged from that service he came to the "Valley," arriving Oct. 30, 1847. He camped on the Jordan River during the first winter he spent in the Valley, to watch the Indians, worked on the Church Farm for three winters, and was called to settle at Payson in 1850. Once he went back to the Sweetwater to help bring in immigrating saints, was the second Bishop of Payson Ward from 1854 to 1861, and helped to build up the settlement. He moved to Ogden, and later to Harrisville, Utah, where he died Jan. 24, 1904.
