Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Baker, Henry Friend

Baker, Henry Friend, Bishop of the. Minersville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1908, was born Nov. 20, 1867, in Minersville, the son of William Baker and Frances Friend. He was baptized Aug. 9, 1877, ordained a High Priest by Francis M. Lyman in June, 1894, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1902-1903, and was ordained a Bishop Oct. 15, 1906, by Francis M. Lyman.
