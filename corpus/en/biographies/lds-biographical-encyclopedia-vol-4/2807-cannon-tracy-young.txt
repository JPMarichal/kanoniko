Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cannon, Tracy Young

Cannon, Tracy Young, Bishop of the Cannon Ward, Pioneer Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born July 23, 1879, in Salt Lake City, Utah, an adopted son of George Q. Cannon. His father's name was Mark Croxall. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1887, ordained a High Priest in November, 1915, by Wm. McLachlan, and a Bishop Dec. 14, 1930, by Geo. Albert Smith.
