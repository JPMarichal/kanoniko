Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Egbert, Samuel Parley

Egbert, Samuel Parley, Bishop of the Ashton (Vernon) Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1904 to 1907, was born Oct. 27, 1876, in Lewiston, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Robert N. Egbert and Josephine McCausker. He was ordained a Bishop in 1904.
