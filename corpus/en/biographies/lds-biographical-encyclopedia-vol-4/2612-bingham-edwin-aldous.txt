Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bingham, Edwin Aldous

Bingham, Edwin Aldous, Bishop of the Wilson Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1920, was born Aug. 31, 1864, in Bingham Fort (Lynne), Utah, the son of Brigham H. Bingham and Angeline T. Aldous. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1875, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1902-1905, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 8, 1906, by Lewis W. Shurtliff, and a Bishop Aug. 11, 1908, by David O. McKay.
