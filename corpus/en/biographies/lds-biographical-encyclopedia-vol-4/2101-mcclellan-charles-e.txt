Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcclellan, Charles E.

Mcclellan, Charles E., second counselor in the Juarez Stake presidency, Mexico, from 1908 to 1912, was born Feb. 8, 1875, in Payson, Utah, a son of William C. McClellan and Almeda Day. He was chosen as second counselor in the Stake presidency March 8, 1908.
