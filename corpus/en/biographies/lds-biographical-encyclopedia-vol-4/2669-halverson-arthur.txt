Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Halverson, Arthur

Halverson, Arthur, Bishop of the Ogden 6th Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born April 27, 1880, in Marriott, Utah, a son of Simeon Halverson and Ingeborg Fredrickson. He was baptized July 5, 1888, ordained an Elder in May, 1901, by James Ritchie, filled a mission to Norway in 1901-1903, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 15, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
