Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christensen, Christian Miller

Christensen, Christian Miller, Bishop of the Central Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930, was born Feb. 3, 1889, in Logan, Utah, the son of Ferdinand C. Christensen and Elsie Mary Miller. He was baptized June 5, 1897, ordained a High Priest Oct. 28, 1928, and set apart as Bishop by Geo. F. Richards.
