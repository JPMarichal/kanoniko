Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Griffeth, George Andrew

Griffeth, George Andrew, Bishop of the Dayton Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Sept. 20, 1870, in Hyde Park, Utah, a son of Geo. Andrew Griffeth and Mary E. Thurman. He was baptized Sept. 20, 1878, was ordained an Elder Dec. 4, 1898, by Moroni W. Pratt, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1898-1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 15, 1925, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
