Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beebe, Leroy Winslow

Beebe, Leroy Winslow, Bishop of the Virgin City Ward,. Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1886 to 1905, was born Jan. 25, 1840, in Sheridan, New York, a son of William A. Beebe and Louisa Newton. He was baptized when eight years old (in 1848) in Iowa, came to Utah in 1852, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 17, 1886, by Wilford Woodruff.
