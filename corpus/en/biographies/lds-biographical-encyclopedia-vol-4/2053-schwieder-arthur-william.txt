Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Schwieder, Arthur William

Schwieder, Arthur William, Bishop of the Iona Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Aug. 21, 1887, in Lewiston, Minnesota, a son of Gotlieb Schwieder and Henrietta Rubin. He was baptized May 21, 1907, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 28, 1921, by Hyrum G. Smith, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 22, 1928, by Rudger Clawson.
