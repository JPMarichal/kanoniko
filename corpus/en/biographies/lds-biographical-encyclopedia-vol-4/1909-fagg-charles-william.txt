Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fagg, Charles William

Fagg, Charles William, Bishop of the Wilford Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Nov. 27, 1883, in East Mill Creek, Utah, the son of John Birch Fagg and Caroline Esther Elizabeth Simper. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1892, ordained a Seventy April 3, 1911, by Seymour B, Young, ordained a High Priest Aug. 24, 1913, by Frank Y. Taylor and ordained a Bishop June 11, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard.
