Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Flynn, Clifford Clinton

Flynn, Clifford Clinton, Bishop of the Buhl Ward, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1926, was born March 4, 1891, in Cressville, Tenn., a son of Thomas S. Flynn and Flora Brown. He was baptized Oct. 12, 1921, and ordained a High Priest Dec. 14, 1924, by Jesse W. Richens.
