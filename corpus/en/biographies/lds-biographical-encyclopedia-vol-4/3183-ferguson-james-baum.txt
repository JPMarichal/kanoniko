Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ferguson, James Baum

Ferguson, James Baum, Bishop of the Edgemont Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Aug. 20, 1889, in Provo, Utah, a son of Fredrick Ferguson and Elizabeth Baum. He was baptized Aug. 14, 1898, ordained a High Priest May 23, 1926, by Thomas N. Taylor, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 18, 1930, by George Albert Smith.
