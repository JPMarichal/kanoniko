Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Noble, Edward A.

Noble, Edward A., Bishop of the Alpine Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1880 to 1890, was born Feb. 2, 1841, in Montrose, Iowa, a son of Joseph B. Noble and Mary Adeline Belman. He was baptized in 1850, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop by Brigham Young, jun., Sept. 26, 1880. He died Nov. 28, 1909, in St. Johns, Arizona.
