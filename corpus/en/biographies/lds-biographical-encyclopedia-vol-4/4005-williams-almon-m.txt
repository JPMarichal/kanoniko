Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, Almon M.

Williams, Almon M., one of the original pioneers of Utah, arrived in Great Salt Lake Valley with Pres. Brigham Young in July, 1847. Later the same year he returned to Winter Quarters in William Clayton's company, and in 1848 is mentioned as one of the signers of a petition to the U. S. government to establish a post-office on the Pottawattamie lands. He probably did not come back to Utah, as no further mention is made in reference to him.
