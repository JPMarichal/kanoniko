Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Mons

Peterson, Mons, Bishop of the Moab Ward, San Juan Stake, Utah, from 1893 to 1896, was born April 30, 1861, near Stockholm, Sweden. He came to Utah when seven years old, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 13, 1893, by Brigham Young, jun.
