Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hunt, Mitchell Wiggins

Hunt, Mitchell Wiggins, Bishop of the Twin Falls Ward, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, in 1930 (from Jan. 26 to Oct. 26), was born June 10, 1897, in Richmond, Utah, a son of Enoch Jefferson Hunt, sen., and Eliza Rebecca Whitaker. He was baptized Nov. 14, 1905, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 26, 1930, by Melvin J. Ballard.
