Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Winter, Timothy J.

Winter, Timothy J., Bishop of the Rexburg 3rd Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1887 to 1892, was born Dec. 6, 1859, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Thomas W. Winter and Myra Clayton. He was baptized in 1868, moved to Rexburg in January, 1886, and was ordained a Bishop in April, 1887, by Franklin D. Richards.
