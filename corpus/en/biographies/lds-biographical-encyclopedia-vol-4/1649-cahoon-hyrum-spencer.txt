Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cahoon, Hyrum Spencer

Cahoon, Hyrum Spencer, Bishop of the Deseret Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1903 to 1908, was born Nov. 24, 1853, in Murray, Utah, the son of Daniel S. Cahoon and Jane Amanda Spencer. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 23, 1903, by George Teasdale.
