Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Durtschi, Alfred

Durtschi, Alfred, Bishop of the Pratt Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Oct. 2, 1886, in Wimmis, Canton Bern, Switzerland, the son of Edward Durtschi and Rosina Katherina Hiltbrand. He was baptized Aug. 20, 1905, came to Utah Oct. 17, 1905, moved to Idaho in 1909, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 17, 1918, by Stephen L. Richards, and a Bishop May 9, 1926, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
