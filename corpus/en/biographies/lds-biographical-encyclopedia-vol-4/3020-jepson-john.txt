Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jepson, John

Jepson, John, Bishop of the Alpine Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1921 to 1930+, was born March 25, 1868, in Fountain Green, Utah, the son of Martin Olsen Jepson and Hannah Anderson. He was baptized June 7, 1877, filled a mission to England in 1897-1899, was ordained a High Priest by David K. Udall, and a Bishop Aug. 1, 1921, by Richard R. Lyman.
