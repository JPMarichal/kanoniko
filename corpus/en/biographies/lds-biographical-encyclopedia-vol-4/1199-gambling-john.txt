Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gambling, John

Gambling, John, Bishop of the Sharon Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, in 1926-1930+, was born Jan. 27, 1887, in Liberty, Bear Lake Co., Idaho, the son of Thomas Gambling and Elizabeth Alkins. He was baptized Aug. 18, 1895, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 27, 1926, by Rudger Clawson.
