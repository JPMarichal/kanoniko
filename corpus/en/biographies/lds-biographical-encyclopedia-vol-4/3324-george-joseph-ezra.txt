Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

George, Joseph Ezra

George, Joseph Ezra, Bishop of the Auburn Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1928 to 1930+, was born March 12, 1887, in Ramah, New Mexico, a son of Henry George and Elizabeth Shelton. He was baptized May 4, 1895, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 17, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
