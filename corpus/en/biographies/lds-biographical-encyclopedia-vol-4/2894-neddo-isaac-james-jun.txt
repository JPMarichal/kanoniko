Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Neddo, Isaac James, jun.

Neddo, Isaac James, jun., Bishop of the Malta Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1916 to 1925, was born May 1, 1882, at St. John, Tooele Co., Utah, a son of Isaac James Neddo and Pauline S. Burridge. He was baptized June 1, 1905, ordained a High Priest Aug. 30, 1914, by William T. Jack, and ordained a Bishop April 22, 1916, by George F. Richards.
