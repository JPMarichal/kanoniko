Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gates, Brigham Cecil

Gates, Brigham Cecil, a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A. from 1918 to 1929, was born Aug. 17, 1887, in Laie, Oahu, Hawaii, the son of Jacob F. Gates and Susa Young. He was baptized Aug. 17, 1895, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1907-1910, was director of the music department of the Latter-day Saints College, director of music at the Utah Agricultural College, assistant leader of the Tabernacle choir and the first director of the McCune School of Music in Salt Lake City.
