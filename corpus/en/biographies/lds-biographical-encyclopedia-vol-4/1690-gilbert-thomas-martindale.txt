Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gilbert, Thomas Martindale

Gilbert, Thomas Martindale, Bishop of the Arcadia Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born Feb. 12, 1896, at Joseph, Sevier Co., Utah, the son of Leandrew Jackson Gilbert and Eliza Jane Farnsworth. He was baptized in September, 1904, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 14, 1923, by Melvin J. Ballard.
