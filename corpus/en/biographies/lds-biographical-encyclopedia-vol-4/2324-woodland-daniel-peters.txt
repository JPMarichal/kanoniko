Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Woodland, Daniel Peters

Woodland, Daniel Peters, first counselor in the Malad Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1898 to 1900, also from 1928 to 1930+, was born Feb. 17, 1882, in Woodland, Bannock Co., Idaho, a son of William W.Woodland and Laura Peters. He was baptized April 9, 1892, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Japan in 1905-1908, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 21, 1928, by George F. Richards, and was sustained as first counselor Oct. 21, 1928.
