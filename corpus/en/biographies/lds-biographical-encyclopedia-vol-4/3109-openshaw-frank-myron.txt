Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Openshaw, Frank Myron

Openshaw, Frank Myron, Bishop of the 22nd Ward, Salt Lake Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930, was born April 5, 1897, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Jonathan E. Openshaw and Bessie Lees. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 11, 1926, by Richard R. Lyman.
