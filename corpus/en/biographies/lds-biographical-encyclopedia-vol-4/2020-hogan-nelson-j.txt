Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hogan, Nelson J.

Hogan, Nelson J., Bishop of the Hatch Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1902 to 1917, was born Aug. 12, 1860, in Bountiful, Davis Co., Utah, a son of Goudy Hogan and Ann Nelson. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 27, 1902, by Hyrum M. Smith.
