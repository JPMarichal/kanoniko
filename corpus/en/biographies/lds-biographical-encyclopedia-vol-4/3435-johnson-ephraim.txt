Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Ephraim

Johnson, Ephraim, presiding Elder of the Wilson Branch, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1930+, was born March 20, 1876, in Eden, Utah, a son of Peter Johnson and Ann M. Madison. He was baptized June 5, 1884, and sustained as presiding Elder of Wilson Branch July 10, 1921.
