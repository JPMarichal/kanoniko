Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pocatello Stake

Allen, Heber Charles

Allen, Heber Charles, Bishop of the American Falls Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1920, was born in Utah, Oct. 10, 1881, the son of Andrew Allen and Elizabeth Lasley. He was baptized Oct. 16, 1889, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 26, 1917, by Rudger Clawson.
