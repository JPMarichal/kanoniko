Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tanner, Ebenezer, jun.

Tanner, Ebenezer, jun., Bishop of the Milford Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1909, was born Aug. 8, 1869, in Holden, Millard Co., Utah, the son of Ebenezer Tanner and Mary Ann Bigler. He was baptized by Ansel Harmon when about eight years old and ordained a Bishop Oct. 15, 1906, by Francis M. Lyman.
