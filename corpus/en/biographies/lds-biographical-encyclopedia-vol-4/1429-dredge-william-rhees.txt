Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dredge, William Rhees

Dredge, William Rhees, Bishop of the Brigham City 6th Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born May 28, 1880, in Malad, Idaho, the son of Jesse R. Dredge and Ellen Rhees. He was baptized May 28, 1888, ordained a Seventy Nov. 22, 1899, and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1899-1901. He was ordained a High Priest Oct. 23, 1921, by Jos. N. Stohl, and ordained a Bishop April 14, 1929, by Rudger Clawson.
