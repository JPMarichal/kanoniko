Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rhodehouse, John William

Rhodehouse, John William, Bishop of the Egin 1st Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1907 to 1915, and Bishop of the Heman Ward, in the same Stake, from 1915 to 1917, was born Aug. 11, 1882, at Oxford, Idaho, a son of William H. Rhodehouse and Mary Truscott. He was baptized Aug. 11, 1890, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 16, 1907, by Rudger Clawson.
