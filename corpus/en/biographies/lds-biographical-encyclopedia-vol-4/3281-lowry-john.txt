Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lowry, John

Lowry, John, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 2nd Ward from 1849-1850, and Bishop of the Manti Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1850 to 1855, was born Aug. 10, 1799, at Nashville, Tennessee, a son of William Lowry and Polly Norris. "Bishop Lowry's" name appears for the first time as Bishop of the Manti settlement under date of Aug. 26, 1850. He died Jan. 7, 1867.
