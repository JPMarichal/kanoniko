Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brown, Henry Alonzo

Brown, Henry Alonzo, Bishop of the Plano Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, in 1930+, was born June 30, 1880, in Oxford, Idaho, the son of Joseph Smith Brown and Sarah Shumway. He was baptized in May, 1889, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to California in 1905-1906, was ordained a High Priest in June, 1912, by James Blake, and a Bishop Sept. 28, 1930, by Melvin J. Ballard.
