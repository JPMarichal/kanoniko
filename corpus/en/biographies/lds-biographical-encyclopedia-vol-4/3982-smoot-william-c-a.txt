Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smoot, William C. A.

Smoot, William C. A., one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born Jan. 30, 1828, in the State of Tennessee. He was baptized Feb. 8, 1836, by Henry G. Sherwood. After his arrival in Great Salt Lake Valley, Bro. Smoot spent the winter of 1847-1848 in the North Fort and later settled permanently in the Sugar House Ward. In 1856 he was set apart as a counselor to Abraham O. Smoot, Bishop of the Sugar House Ward. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 2, p. 711.)
