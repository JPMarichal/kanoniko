Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Alice Kimball

Smith, Alice Kimball, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born Sept. 6, 1858, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the daughter of Heber C. Kimball and Ann Alice Gheen, and twin sister of Andrew Kimball. She received her education in the public schools and the Deseret University of Utah, served as a teacher in Sunday Schools, was counselor in the 19th Ward Y. L. M. I. A., president of the Primary in that ward, and was called to the General Board in 1896. In April, 1905, she was appointed to act as general treasurer, served in a General Board capacity for 30 years, and was released because of poor health. In 1914 she attended the International Council of Women at Rome, Italy.
