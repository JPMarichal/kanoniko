Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cox, Solomon Lindsay, jun.

Cox, Solomon Lindsay, jun., presiding Elder of the Redondo Branch, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1926 to 1928, was born March 14, 1862, in Lehi, Utah, the son of Solomon L. Cox and Lucy Mack Smith. He was baptized Dec. 15, 1873, ordained a High Priest by John C. Cutler, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 21, 1926, by Geo. W. McCune.
