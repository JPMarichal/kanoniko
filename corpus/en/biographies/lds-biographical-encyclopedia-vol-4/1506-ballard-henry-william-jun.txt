Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cache Stake

Ballard, Henry William, jun.

Ballard, Henry William, jun., Bishop of the Benson Ward, Cache Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Dec. 1, 1888, in Logan, Utah, a son of Henry William Ballard and Alvira Davidson. He was baptized Sept. 21, 1897, was ordained an Elder Jan. 5, 1910, by Wallace Wesley Roundy, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1911-1913 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 5, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard.
