Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Roghaar, John

Roghaar, John, Bishop of the Grace 2nd Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, since 1920, was born July 29, 1884, in Amsterdam, Holland, the son of Andries Roghaar and Grietje Kater. He was baptized Nov. 28, 1893, by Evert Neuteboom, emigrated to Utah in 1895, filled a mission to Holland in 1904-1907, located in Grace, Idaho, in 1911, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 15, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
