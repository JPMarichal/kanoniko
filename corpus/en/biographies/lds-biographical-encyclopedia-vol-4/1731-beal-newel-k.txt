Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beal, Newel K.

Beal, Newel K., Bishop of the Ferron Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1914, was born April 1, 1867, in Manti, Utah, the son of John Alma Beal and Levina Esther DeMill. He was baptized in 1878, ordained to the Priesthood, and called on a mission to the Southern States in 1899. He was ordained a High Priest Feb. 18, 1906, by Reuben G. Miller, and a Bishop April 9, 1906, by Francis M. Lyman.
