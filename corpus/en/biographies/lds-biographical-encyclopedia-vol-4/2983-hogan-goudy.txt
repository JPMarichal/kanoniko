Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hogan, Goudy

Hogan, Goudy, Bishop of the Leeds Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1876 to 1877, was born Sept. 16, 1829, in Telemarken, Norway, a son of Erik G. M. Hogan and Helga Knudsen. He was baptized Feb. 12, 1843, emigrated to Utah in 1848, having come from Norway in 1837 and settled in Nauvoo, Ill. He filled a mission to Norway in 1877-1879, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 13, 1877. He died Jan. 30, 1898.
