Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hyer, Saul Edward

Hyer, Saul Edward, Bishop of the Lewiston 1st Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Aug. 4, 1892, in Lewiston, Utah, the son of Andrew Lars Hyer and Ellen Gilbert. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1900, ordained an Elder May 10, 1914, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1915-1918, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 18, 1926, by Orson F. Whitney.
