Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Lars

Jensen, Lars, presiding Elder of the Riverton Branch, West Jordan Stake, from 1881 to 1883, was born May 10, 1814, in Helsingor, Denmark. He emigrated to Utah in 1859, and was appointed presiding Elder in 1881. He died April 23, 1883, as a Patriarch.
