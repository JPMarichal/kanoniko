Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Walker, Fredrick William

Walker, Fredrick William, Bishop of the Ogden 16th Ward (West Ogden), North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1917, was born Jan. 11, 1872, in West Weber, Utah, the son of Samuel Walker and Sarah Dixon. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1880, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 10, 1915, by David O. McKay.
