Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gunnison Stake

Bardsley, Leo Duffin

Bardsley, Leo Duffin, second counselor in the Gunnison Stake presidency, Utah, from 1923 to 1924, was born July 11, 1889, in Centerfield, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Joseph Bardsley and Alice Duffin. He was baptized when about eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and set apart as second counselor May 6, 1923, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
