Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olson, Lorenzo Emil

Olson, Lorenzo Emil, Bishop of the Declo Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1916 to 1927, was born Nov. 6, 1867, at Moroni, Sanpete Co., Utah, the son of Jens C. Olson and Karen Marie Hansen. He was baptized in 1876, ordained a High Priest July 15, 1913, by David O. McKay, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 13, 1916, by David O. McKay. He died Dec. 12, 1927, at Declo, Idaho.
