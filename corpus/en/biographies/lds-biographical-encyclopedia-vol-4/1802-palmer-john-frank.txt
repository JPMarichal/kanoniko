Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Palmer, John Frank

Palmer, John Frank, Bishop of the Preston 5th Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was borri Aug. 1, 1892, in Croydon, Utah, the son of John Palmer and Eliza Wainwright. He was baptized April 30, 1910, and after being ordained to the Priesthood he filled a mission to Great Britain in 1912-1914. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 1, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
