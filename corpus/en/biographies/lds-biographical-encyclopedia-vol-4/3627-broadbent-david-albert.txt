Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Broadbent, David Albert

Broadbent, David Albert, president of the Wasatch Stake, Wasatch, Co., Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born May 14, 1871, at Goshen, Utah Co., Utah, a son of Thomas Broadbent and Mary Jane Nuttall. He was baptized Aug. 7, 1879, ordained a Seventy Jan. 5, 1898, by J. Golden Kimball, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1898-1900, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 7, 1904, by George Albert Smith, and chosen as president of the Wasarch Stake March 11, 1928.
