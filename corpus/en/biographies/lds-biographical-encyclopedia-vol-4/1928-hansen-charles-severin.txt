Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Charles Severin

Hansen, Charles Severin, Bishop of the Centerfield Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1926, was born March 20, 1891, in Centerfield, Utah, a son of Peter C. Hansen and Christine Madsen. He was baptized in 1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 6, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards.
