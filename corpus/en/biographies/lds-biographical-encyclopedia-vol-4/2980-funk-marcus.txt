Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Funk, Marcus

Funk, Marcus, Bishop of the Washington Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1881 to 1888, was born Dec. 3, 1842, at Eskegaard, on the island of Bornholm, Denmark, a son of Diderick Funk and Kirstine Madsen. He was ordained a Bishop March 21, 1881, by Pres. Wilford Woodruff. He died Nov. 19, 1926, in Trenton, Utah.
