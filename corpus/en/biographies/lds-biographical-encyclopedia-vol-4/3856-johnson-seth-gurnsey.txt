Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Seth Gurnsey

Johnson, Seth Gurnsey, a member of Zion's Camp, was born Feb. 14, 1805, in Royalton, Mass., a son of Ezekiel Johnson and Julia Hills. He was well educated, held the office of an Elder in the Church and had labored as a missionary. He died Feb. 19, 1835, at Kirtland, Ohio, of consumption, while teaching school at Willoughby.
