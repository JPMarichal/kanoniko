Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fuhriman, Godfrey Jared

Fuhriman, Godfrey Jared, presiding Elder of the Ridgedale Branch, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1925, was born Aug. 22, 1883, in Providence, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Godfrey Fuhriman and Bertha Frederick. He was baptized Aug. 22, 1891, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Germany in 1911-1913, and was set apart as presiding Elder June 28, 1915. He died April 6, 1924.
