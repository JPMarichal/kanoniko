Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

West, Erastus

West, Erastus, Bishop of the Darlington Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1923, was born Jan. 10, 1867, in Parowan, Utah, a son of John A. West and Betsey Jane Fish. He was baptized in 1877, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 18, 1919, by Rudger Clawson.
