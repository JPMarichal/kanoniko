Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Howell, James William

Howell, James William, Bishop of the McCammon Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1923 to 1930, was born July 29, 1865, in Kaysville, Utah, a son of James Howell and Rosanna Monk. He was baptized Oct. 1, 1873, ordained a High Priest Dec. 17, 1899 by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop Sept. 9, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards.
