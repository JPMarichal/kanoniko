Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Charles Shreeve

Peterson, Charles Shreeve, Presiding Elder at Alpine, Alpine Stake, Utah, in 1852, was born July 28, 1818, at Mount Holly, Burlington Co., New Jersey, the son of Andrew Peterson and Martha Murdock. He was an early settler in Weber Valley, Utah, where he acted as Bishop. Later, he figured prominently in Arizona. He died Sept. 26, 1889, in Mesa, Arizona.
