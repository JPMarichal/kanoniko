Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Yeates, Alma

Yeates, Alma, Bishop of the Nibley Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1920 to 1924, was born May 31, 1881, in Millville, Cache Co., Utah, the son of Frederick Yeates and Sarah Webb. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1889, ordained a Seventy Dec. 28, 1910, by Charles H. Hart, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1911-1913, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 18, 1920, by Orson F. Whitney.
