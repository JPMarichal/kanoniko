Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Freeze, Lillie (Lelia) Tuckett

Freeze, Lillie (Lelia) Tuckett, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born in Salt Lake City, Utah, the daughter of Henry Tucker and Mereel E. Westwood. She went to St. George in 1861, but later returned to Salt Lake City, was active in the various auxiliary organizations of the 11th Ward, especially in the Retrenchment Association, and edited the 11th Ward "Star". She married James P. Freeze and bore four children. In October, 1889, she was called to the General Board of the Y. L. M. I. A., and wrote for the "Young Ladies Journal" and the "Woman's Exponent". She spoke in the Tabernacle on the 50th anniversary of the Church and prepared an article on M. L A. work, used at the World's Fair in 1893.
