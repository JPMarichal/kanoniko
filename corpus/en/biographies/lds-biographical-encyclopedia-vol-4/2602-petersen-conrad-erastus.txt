Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Petersen, Conrad Erastus

Petersen, Conrad Erastus, Bishop of the Salina 1st Ward, North Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1930+, was born Aug. 2, 1892, in Redmond, Utah, a son of Erastus Peter Petersen. He was baptized Oct. 30, 1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 27, 1919, by Stephen L. Richards.
