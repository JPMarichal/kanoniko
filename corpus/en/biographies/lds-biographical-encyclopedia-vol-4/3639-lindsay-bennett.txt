Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lindsay, Bennett

Lindsay, Bennett, Bishop of the Center Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1917 to 1930+, was born Oct. 23, 1882, at Center, Utah, a son of James Lindsay and Agnes Watson. He was baptized Nov. 11, 1906, ordained an Elder Oct. 13, 1907, by Charles Jensen, filled a mission to the Central States in 1911-1913, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 22, 1915, by Edward D. Clyde, and ordained a Bishop July 29, 1917, by James E. Talmage.
