Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thompson, Daniel,

Thompson, Daniel, first counselor in the Millard Stake presidency, Utah, from 1882 to 1902, was born Dec. 25, 1824, in Canada, a son of William Thompson and Elizabeth Macaulay. He was baptized about 1849, came to Utah in 1851, was ordained Bishop of the Scipio Ward March 9, 1869, by George A. Smith, and chosen as first counselor May 28, 1882.
