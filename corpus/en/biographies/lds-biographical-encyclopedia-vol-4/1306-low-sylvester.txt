Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Low, Sylvester

Low, Sylvester, Bishop of the Smithfield 2nd Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Dec. 22, 1862, in Providence, Cache Co., Utah, the son of Sylvester Low and Ann A. Patten. He was baptized Sept. 17, 1871, ordained a Seventy Nov. 18, 1898, by J. Golden Kimball, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1898-1900, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 29, 1926, and a Bishop April 15, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
