Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Morley, George F.

Morley, George F., Bishop of the Freedom Ward, Moroni Stake, Utah, from 1904 to 1907, was born Sept. 30, 1852, in Manti, Utah, a son of Isaac Morley and Abiah Bradley. He was baptized Aug. 30, 1863, and ordained a Bishop March 6, 1904, by Anthon H. Lund. He died Jan. 10, 1915.
