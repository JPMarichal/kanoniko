Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fullmer, Halsey Dean

Fullmer, Halsey Dean, Bishop of the Clawson Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1905 to 1913, was born June 15, 1865, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Almon Linnes Fullmet and Sarah Ann Follett. He was baptized in 1890, ordained a High Priest Sept. 2, 1901, by John Henry Smith, and sustained as Bishop Jan. 29, 1905.
