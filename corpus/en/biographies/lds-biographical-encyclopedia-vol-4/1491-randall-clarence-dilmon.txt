Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Randall, Clarence Dilmon

Randall, Clarence Dilmon, a Bishop of the Pella Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Jan. 7, 1888, in Ogden, Utah, the son of Charles Franklin Randall and Sarah Ann Smith. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1898, ordained an Elder April 21, 1912, by Wm. T. Jack, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1919-1920, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 10, 1924, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
