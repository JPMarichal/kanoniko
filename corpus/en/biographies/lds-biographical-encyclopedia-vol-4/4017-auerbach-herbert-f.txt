Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Auerbach, Herbert F.

Auerbach, Herbert F., a member of the Old Folks Central Committee since 1927, was born Oct. 4, 1882, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Samuel H. Auerbach and Eveline Brooks. He is one of the most prominent men in Utah in civic and business affairs, and in favor of any movement inaugurated toward making life happy for the Old Folks.
