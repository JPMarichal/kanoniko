Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Butler, John Lowe

Butler, John Lowe, Bishop of the Acequia Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1922, was born June 5, 1874, in Panguitch, Utah, a son of John L. Butler and Nancy F. Smith. He was baptized June 25, 1882, and was sustained as Bishop Aug. 22, 1920.
