Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Joseph Alonzo

Johnson, Joseph Alonzo, Bishop of the Victor Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1913 to 1916, was born April 25, 1874, in Richmond, Utah, a son of Joseph Johnson and Ruth A. Alliston. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 16, 1913, by Rudger Clawson.
