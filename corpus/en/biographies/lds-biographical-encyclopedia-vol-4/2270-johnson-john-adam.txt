Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, John Adam

Johnson, John Adam, Bishop of the Arco Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1923 to 1930, was born May 20, 1881, in Richmond, Utah, a son of Niels Johnson and Margaret Rasmussen. He was baptized Sept. 5, 1889, filled a mission to Norway in 1903-1905, was ordained a High Priest May 7, 1922, by Richard R. Lyman and a Bishop Feb. 4, 1923, by Melvin J. Ballard.
