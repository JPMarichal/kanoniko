Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hanson, Soren J.

Hanson, Soren J., Bishop of the Twin Groves W a r d, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1914 to 1920, was born Oct. 14, 1866, at Mt. Pleasant, Utah, a son of Soren Jacobson Hanson and Mary Ann Mickelson. He was baptized July 10, 1875, ordained a High Priest Jan. 11, 1909, by George F. Richards, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 23, 1915, by Anthony W. Ivins.
