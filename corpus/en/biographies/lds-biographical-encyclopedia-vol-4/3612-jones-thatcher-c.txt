Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, Thatcher C.

Jones, Thatcher C., Bishop of the Provo 3rd Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1921, was born Sept. 29, 1888, in Lehi, Utah, a son of Edward E. Jones and Letitia Wheatley. He was baptized Dec. 3, 1896, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1910-1912, and was ordained a Bishop Jan. 25, 1920, by Richard R. Lyman.
