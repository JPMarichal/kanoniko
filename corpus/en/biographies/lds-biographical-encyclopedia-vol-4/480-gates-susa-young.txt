Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gates, Susa Young

Gates, Susa Young, member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born March 18, 1856, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the daughter of Brigham Young and Lucy Bigelow. She was the first editor of the "Young Woman's Journal" in 1889 and was made a member of the General Board of the Y. L. M. I. A. the same year. (See L. D. S. Biographical Encyclopedia, Vol. 2, p. 626.) She died May 27, 1933, at Salt Lake City.
