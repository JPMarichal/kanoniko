Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lowry, James William

Lowry, James William, Bishop of the Freedom Ward, Moroni Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1904, was born Feb. 25, 1863, in Moroni, Utah, a son of Abner Lowry and Louisa Bradley. He was baptized June 9, 1872, and ordained a Bishop June 27, 1900, by Anthon H. Lund.
