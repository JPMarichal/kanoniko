Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burt, Charles Willden

Burt, Charles Willden, Bishop of the Cardston 1st Ward, Alberta, Canada, from 1914 to 1918, was born Jan. 18, 1877, in St. George, Utah, the son of William Burr and Louisa Willden. He was baptized when 9 years old, ordained an Elder May 3, 1903, by Wm. H. Steed, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1907-1909, and was ordained a Bishop by Heber J. Grant May 19, 1918.
