Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Andrew Lysander

Peterson, Andrew Lysander, Bishop of the Mr. Pleasant South Ward, North Sanpete Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Dec. 23, 1887, in Fairview, Utah, a son of Peter Peterson and Celestia M. Terry. He was baptized when eight years old, and was ordained a High Priest June 24, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards, and was chosen and sustained as Bishop in March, 1926.
