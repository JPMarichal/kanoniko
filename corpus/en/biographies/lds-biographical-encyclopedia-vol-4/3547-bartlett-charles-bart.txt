Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bartlett, Charles Bart

Bartlett, Charles Bart, Bishop of the Liberty (Tridell) Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1916, was born Aug. 27, 1869, in Mountain Dell, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Charles C. Bartlett and Ane Katrine Jensen. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to Australia in 1892-1895, and again in 1902-1905, when he presided over the Australian Mission. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 26, 1911, by David O. McKay.
