Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christensen, James G.

Christensen, James G., Bishop of the Hyrum 3rd Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born March 20, 1884, at Hyrum, Utah, the son of Niels C. Christensen and Karen Maria Sorensen. He was baptized May 4, 1892, at Hyrum, ordained a Seventy Sept. 20, 1909, by Charles H. Hart, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1909-1912, was ordained a High Priest July 22, 1917, by James E. Talmage, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 13, 1924, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
