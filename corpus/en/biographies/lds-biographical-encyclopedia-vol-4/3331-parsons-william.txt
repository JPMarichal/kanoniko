Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Parsons, William

Parsons, William, Bishop of the Cottonwood (Smoot) Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1889 to 1903, was born Dec. 4, 1849, in Lake Branch, Pottawattamie Co., Iowa, the son of William Parsons and Mary Jenkins. He came to Utah in 1852, was baptized when eight years old, settled in Star Valley in 1887, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 2, 1889, by Heber J. Grant.
