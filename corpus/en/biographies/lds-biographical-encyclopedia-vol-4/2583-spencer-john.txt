Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Spencer, John

Spencer, John, Bishop of the Indianola Ward, North Sanpete Stake, Utah, from 1881 to 1890, was born Aug. 29, 1836, in Lancashire, England, a son of Richard Spencer and Mary Enshaw. He was baptized when eleven years old in Pottawattamie County, Iowa, came to Utah in 1852, was called to preside over the Indian Mission in Skull Valley in 1877, and baptized many Indians. He was ordained a Bishop by Pres. John Taylor, and died March 29, 1922.
