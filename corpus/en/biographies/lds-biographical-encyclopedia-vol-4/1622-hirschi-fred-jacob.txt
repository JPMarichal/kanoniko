Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hirschi, Fred Jacob

Hirschi, Fred Jacob, Bishop of the Rosette Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born July 21, 1883, in Rosette, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of Christian Hirschi and Anna Berger. He was baptized Oct. 16, 1883, by William H. Mecham, ordained an Elder Nov. 7, 1908, by Joseph Eli Jensen, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1909, was ordained a High Priest by Heber J. Grant May 29, 1910, and ordained a Bishop April 22, 1928, by Stephen L. Richards.
