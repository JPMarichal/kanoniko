Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Judd, James

Judd, James, first counselor in the presidency of the Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Nov. 15, 1884, in St. George, Utah, a son of Thomas Judd and Mary Jane Ashworth. He was baptized Nov. 15, 1892, ordained an Elder June 24, 1906, by Edward H. Snow, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1914-1916, was ordained a High Priest and was set apart as first counselor in the Stake presidency Dec. 8, 1929, by David O. McKay.
