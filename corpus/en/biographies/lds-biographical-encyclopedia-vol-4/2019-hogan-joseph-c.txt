Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hogan, Joseph C.

Hogan, Joseph C., Bishop of the Hatch Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1923, was born Sept. 11, 1892, in Chesterfield, Idaho, a son of Nelson J. Hogan and Josephine Bergensen. He was baptized in November, 1900, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1913-1915, and was ordained a Bishop Dec. 4, 1921, by Richard R. Lyman. He died Aug. 9, 1923.
