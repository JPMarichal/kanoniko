Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Risenmay, George Henry

Risenmay, George Henry, Bishop of the Woodville Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Oct. 27, 1890, in Logan, Utah, a son of Christian Risenmay and Wilhelmina Closner. He was baptized Nov. 1, 1898, ordained a High Priest Feb. 19, 1916, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop Nov. 4, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
