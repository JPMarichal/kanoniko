Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Raft River Stake

Allen, David Orlo

Allen, David Orlo, Bishop of the Almo Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1907 to 1912, was born April 21, 1872, in Huntsville, Weber Co., Utah, a son of Allenson D. Allen and Chastina Hadlock. He was baptized May 7, 1880, ordained a High Priest Nov. 24, 1905, by William T. Jack, and ordained a Bishop June 10, 1907, in Salt Lake City, Utah.
