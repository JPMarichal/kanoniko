Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nygren, Evald Edmund

Nygren, Evald Edmund, Bishop of the Murray 2nd Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1928, was born Nov. 16, 1885, in Sweden, the son of Frederick B. Nygren and Emma Anderson. He filled a mission to the Northern States in 1922-1924, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 16, 1924, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
