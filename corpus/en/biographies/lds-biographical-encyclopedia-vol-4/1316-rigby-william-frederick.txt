Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rigby, William Frederick

Rigby, William Frederick, Bishop of the Newton Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1884, was born Jan. 29, 1833, at Saddleworth, Yorkshire, England, the son of Joseph Rigby and Margaret Littleworth. He emigrated to Utah in 1857, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1887-1889, was ordained a Bishop in the fall of 1867 and placed in charge of the Clarkston Ward. He presided in Clarkston three years, moved to Newton and became Bishop of that Ward. He died March 13, 1901.
