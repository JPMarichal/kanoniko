Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Simon Long

Smith, Simon Long, Bishop of Clarkston Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1870 to 1876, was born in 1833 in England, near London, was baptized in England and emigrated to Utah in 1858. He was chosen a Bishop July 10, 1870, but later left the Church and joined the Re-organites. He died in St. Joseph, Missouri, April 29, 1898.
