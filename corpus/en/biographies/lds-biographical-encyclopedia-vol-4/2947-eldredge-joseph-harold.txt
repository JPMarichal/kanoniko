Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Eldredge, Joseph Harold

Eldredge, Joseph Harold, Bishop of the Myron Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born Dec. 19, 1890, in Coalville, Utah, a son of Ira E. Eldredge and Sarah Burr Staker. He was baptized Aug. 27, 1899, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 17, 1923, by James E. Talmage.
