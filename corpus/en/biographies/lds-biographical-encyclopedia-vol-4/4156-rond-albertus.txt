Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rond, Albertus

Rond, Albertus, an Elder who died in the missionary field, was born Nov. 24, 1862, in Zwolle, Overysel, Netherlands, a son of Coenraad Rond and Egbertdina Jansen. He was baptized May 21, 1899, in Amsterdam, by Frank E. Hansen, and served as a counselor in the superintondency of the Amsterdam Sunday School, and also as a counselor in the presidency of the Amsterdam Branch No. 1. He emigrated to Utah in May, 1909. While residing in Ogden, Utah, he was ordained a High Priest and called on a mission to the Netherlands, being set apart Nov. 4, 1921. He had served almost a year in the ministry when he suffered a heart attack, from which he died in Amsterdam Sept. 15, 1922. His wife, Hendrika J. Rond, accompanied him to their native land and was with him during his last hours. His body was brought back to Utah by two companions, Elders Joseph Van Leeuwen and Hendrik Bell, sen. Services were held for him in the Ogden Tabernacle Oct. 22, 1922.
