Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nelson, Victor Daniel

Nelson, Victor Daniel, Bishop of the Darlington Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1923 to 1930+, was born Aug. 24, 1889, in Ferron, Utah, a son, of Hyrum A. Nelson and Mary A. Lowry. He was baptized Sept. 5, 1897, ordained an Elder Sept. 19, 1910-by Christian Petersen, filled a mission to Norway in 1910-1912 and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 4, 1923, by Melvin J. Ballard.
