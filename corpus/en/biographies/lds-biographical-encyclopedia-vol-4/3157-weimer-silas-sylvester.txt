Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Weimer, Silas Sylvester

Weimer, Silas Sylvester, Bishop of the Manassa 2nd Ward, San Luis Stake, Colo., from 1920 to 1928, was born Nov. 12, 1873, in Garrett Co., Maryland, the son of Simon Weimer and Mary E. Keefer. He was baptized June 6, 1887, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 30, 1920, by Geo. F. Richards.
