Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bloomfield, Richard Henry

Bloomfield, Richard Henry, Bishop of the Ramah Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1919 to 1929, was born March 6, 1873, in Hyde Park, Utah, a son of John Bloomfield and Elizabeth A. Barton. He was baptized April 15, 1881, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 20, 1919, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
