Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nelson, Nels C.

Nelson, Nels C., Bishop of the Burley 2nd Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1924, was born Feb. 14, 1860, in Goshen, Utah, the son of Nels C. Nelson and Catherine Jensen. He was ordained a Bishop Feb. 27, 1921, by Rudger Clawson.
