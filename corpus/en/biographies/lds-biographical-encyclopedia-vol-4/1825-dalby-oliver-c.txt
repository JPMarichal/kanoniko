Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dalby, Oliver C.

Dalby, Oliver C., Bishop of the Rexburg 1st Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1907 to 1914, was born Nov. 11, 1871, in Levan, Utah, the son of Christian Christiansen and Anna Marie Jensen. After being ordained to the Priesthood he filled a mission to Denmark in 1900-1902, moved to Rexburg in 1905, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 16, 1907, by Rudger Clawson.
