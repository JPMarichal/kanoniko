Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Horrocks, Roger

Horrocks, Roger, Bishop of the Hayden Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1917, was born Dec. 23, 1871, in Heber, Wasarch Co., Utah, a son of Roger Horrocks and Sarah A. Taylor. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 4, 1910, by William H. Smart, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 10, 1911, by Francis M. Lyman.
