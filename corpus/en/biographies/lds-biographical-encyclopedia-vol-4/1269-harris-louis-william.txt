Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Louis William

Harris, Louis William, Bishop of the Beaver East Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1905 to 1909, was born Jan. 4, 1889, in Beaver, the son of Louis W. Harris and Annie Bakes. He was baptized June 3, 1897, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 22, 1905, by Francis M. Lyman.
