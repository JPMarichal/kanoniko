Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burk, William Isaiah

Burk, William Isaiah, Bishop of the Alpine Ward, St. Johns Stake, Ariz., from 1904 to 1908, was born Nov. 28, 1874, in Farmington, Utah, the son of Hubert Rosell Burk and Lois Hamblin. He was baptized Dec. 11, 1882, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 14, 1904.
