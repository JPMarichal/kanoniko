Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rawson, William John

Rawson, William John, Bishop of the Nibley Ward, Union Stake, Oregon, from 1901 to 1906, was born Aug. 21, 1873, in Harrisville, Weber Co., Utah, a son of Archer Morrison Rawson and Margaret Angeline Pace. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1881, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 9, 1901, by Mathias F. Cowley.
