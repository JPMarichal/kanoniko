Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Indian Territory Mission

Presidents: Henry W. Miller, 1855-1856; Washington N. Cook, 1856-1858; Henry Eyring, 1858-1860; Matthew W. Dalton, March, 1877-November, 1877; George Teasdale, April-September, 1883; Matthew W. Dalton (second term), 1883-1884; Andrew Kimball, 1885-1897; William T. Jack, 1897-1898.

Note: The name of the Indian Territory Mission was changed to the Southwestern States Mission in March, 1898.

Cook, Washington N.

Cook, Washington N., president of the Indian Territory Mission from 1856 to 1858. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 620.)
