Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cook, Clarence

Cook, Clarence, Bishop of the Garden City Ward, Bear Lake Stake, (Rich Co., Utah), in 1926-1930+, was born Nov. 27, 1889, in Garden City, the son of David Savage Cook and Lydia Ann Nelson. He was baptized July 2, 1898, was ordained an Elder by Ernest W. Irwin and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1911-1913, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 27, 1926, by Rudger Clawson.
