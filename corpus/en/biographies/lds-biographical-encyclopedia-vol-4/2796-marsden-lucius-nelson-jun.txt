Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Marsden, Lucius Nelson, jun.

Marsden, Lucius Nelson, jun., Bishop of the Parowan East Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1926 to 1929, was born June 7, 1896, at Parowan, Utah, the son of Lucius Nelson Marsden and Mary Matheson. He was baptized July 2, 1904, ordained an Elder in October, 1916, by Wilford Day, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1916-1919, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 16, 1921, by Stephen L. Richards, and ordained a Bishop in March, 1926, by Hyrum G. Smith.
