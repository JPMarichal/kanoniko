Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merrill, William Porter

Merrill, William Porter, Bishop of the Bluebell Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, was born Aug. 30, 1869, in Paradise, Utah, the son of Porter William Merrill and Harriet A. Remington. He was baptized Sept. 6, 1877, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 10, 1911, by Orson F. Whitney.
