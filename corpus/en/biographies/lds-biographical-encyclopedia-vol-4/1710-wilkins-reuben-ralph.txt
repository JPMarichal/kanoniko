Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilkins, Reuben Ralph

Wilkins, Reuben Ralph, Bishop of the Mountain Home Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930, was born Aug. 28, 1890, in Peoa, Utah, the son of Oscar Wilkins and Elizabeth Durrah. He was baptized Sept. 17, 1899, ordained a High Priest March 20,1926, by Geo. Albert Smith, and a Bishop May 11, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
