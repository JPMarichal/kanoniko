Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hoglund, Alvin A.

Hoglund, Alvin A., Bishop of the Garvanza Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1923 to 1930, was born Jan. 26, 1893, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Otto Hoglund and Anna C. Jacobson. He was baptized Oct. 31, 1903, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 12, 1923, by Richard R. Lyman.
