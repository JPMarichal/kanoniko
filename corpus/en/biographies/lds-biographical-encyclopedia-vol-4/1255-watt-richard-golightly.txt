Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Watt, Richard Golightly

Watt, Richard Golightly, Bishop of the Thatcher Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1924, was born Nov. 26, 1860, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of George D. Watt and Elizabeth Golightly. He was baptized Sept. 23, 1886, by Henry Thornley, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop by Heber J. Grant May 6, 1911. He died Oct. 6, 1933.
