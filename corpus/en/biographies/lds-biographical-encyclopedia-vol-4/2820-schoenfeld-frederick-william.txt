Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Schoenfeld, Frederick William

Schoenfeld, Frederick William, Bishop of the Brighton Ward, Pioneer Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1911, was born Feb. 14, 1831, in Eilerburg, Prussia, the son of Friedrich Schoenfeld and Sophia Krebs. He was baptized May 23, 1856, emigrated to Utah in 1864, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 29, 1877, by Daniel H. Wells. He died Jan. 4, 1919.
