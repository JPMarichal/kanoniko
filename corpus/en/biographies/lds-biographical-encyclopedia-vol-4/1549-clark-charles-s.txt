Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clark, Charles S.

Clark, Charles S., president of Cassia Stake, Idaho, front 1928 to 1930+, was born Jan. 16, 1892, in Grantsville, Utah, the son of Thomas H. Clark and Rachel S. Hale. He was ordained an Elder Dec. 1, 1912, filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1912-1915, ordained a High Priest July 29, 1918, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., and set apart as president of the Cassia Stake Oct. 20, 1928, by Stephen L. Richards.
