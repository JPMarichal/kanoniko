Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shumway, James Jardine

Shumway, James Jardine, Bishop of the Taylor Ward, Snowflake Stake, Ariz., from 1904 to 1910, was born Feb. 21, 1866, in Mendon, Utah, a son of Chas. Shumway and Elizabeth Jardine. He was baptized Feb. 21, 1874, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 31, 1904, by Matbias F. Cowley.
