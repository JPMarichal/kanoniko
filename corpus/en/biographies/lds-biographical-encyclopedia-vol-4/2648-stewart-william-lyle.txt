Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stewart, William Lyle

Stewart, William Lyle, Bishop of the Warren Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1896 to 1908, was born Nov. 19, 1846, in Glasgow, Scotland, a son of Archibald Stewart and Esther Lyle. He emigrated to Utah in 1854, was baptized in 1854, filled a mission to Scotland in 1888-1890, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 10, 1896, by Geo. Q. Cannon. He died March 15, 1917.
