Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shelley Stake

Arave, Joseph Clarence

Arave, Joseph Clarence, Bishop of the Taylor Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Sept. 14, 1891, in Taylor, Idaho, a son of David Eli Arave and Mary Ann Fowles. He was baptized Sept. 13, 1902, ordained a High Priest May 31, 1914, by Joseph H. Dye, and a Bishop Feb. 2, 1930, by Orson F. Whitney.
