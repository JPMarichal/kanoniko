Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whitehead, William J.

Whitehead, William J., Bishop of the Claresholm Ward, Lethbridge Stake, Canada, from 1911 to 1920, was born April 8, 1864, in Franklin, Idaho, the son of William Whitehead and Margaret Green. He was baptized June 13, 1875, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 19, 1911, by Thomas Duce, and a Bishop Nov. 26, 1911.
