Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Evans, Thomas Benjamin

Evans, Thomas Benjamin, president of the Ogden Stake of Zion, Utah, from 1908 to 1919, was born Feb. 3, 1859, in Trecynon, Wales, a son of Thomas Evans and Mary Benjamin. He filled a mission to England and Wales in 1890-1892, and was set apart as president of the Ogden Stake July 26, 1908, by Geo. F. Richards. He died April 4, 1919, at Ogden.
