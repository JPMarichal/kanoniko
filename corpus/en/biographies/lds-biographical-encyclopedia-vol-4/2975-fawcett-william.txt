Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fawcett, William

Fawcett, William, Bishop of the St. George 1st Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1878 to 1879, was born Dec. 13, 1814, in Malton, Yorkshire, England, a son of George Fawcett and Mary Spink. He was baptized Jan. 1, 1840, and labored as a missionary before coming to Utah in 1850. He was ordained a High Priest Jan. 4, 1853, in Salt Lake City, was called to the Dixie Mission in 1861, and was acting Bishop for six months. He died at St. George Jan. 17, 1904.
