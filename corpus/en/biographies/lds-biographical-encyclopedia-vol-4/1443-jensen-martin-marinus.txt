Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Martin Marinus

Jensen, Martin Marinus, Bishop of the Mantua Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1910, was born Dec. 8, 1858, in Union, Iowa, the son of Peter Christian Jensen and Ant Christina Peterson. He was baptized Aug. 28, 1870, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1883-1885, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 6, 1908, by Francis M. Lyman. He died Nov. 21, 1910.
