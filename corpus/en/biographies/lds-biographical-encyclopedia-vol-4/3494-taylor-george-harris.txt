Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, George Harris

Taylor, George Harris, Bishop of the Knightsville Ward, Tintic Stake, Juab Co., Utah, from 1915 to 1917, was born Feb. 9, 1882, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Thomas E. Taylor and Emma Harris. He was baptized when eight years of age, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 20, 1916, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
