Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Calder, Robert

Calder, Robert, Bishop of the Garden City Ward, Bear Lake Stake, (Rich Co., Utah), Idaho, from 1879 to 1897, was born Oct. 12, 1834, in Edinburgh, Scotland, the son of Peter Calder and Ann Miller. He emigrated with his father's family to Nauvoo, Illinois, in 1845, and came to Utah in 1850. He was called to Garden City to act as Bishop in 1879. He died April 7, 1906, at Garden City.
