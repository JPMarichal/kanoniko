Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brown, Scott B.

Brown, Scott B., Bishop of the Weiser Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born June 22, 1885, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Homer Manley Brown and Lydia Jane Brown. He was baptized May 1, 1893, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1908-1910. He was ordained a Bishop March 10, 1928, by Rudger Clawson.
