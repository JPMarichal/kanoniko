Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hollywood Stake

Brown, William George

Brown, William George, Bishop of the Mar Vista Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1928 to 1930+, was born March 10, 1897, in Evanston, Wyoming, the son of James Brown and Christina Hunter. He was baptized July 15, 1905, ordained an Elder Feb. 5, 1917, by Albert W. Phips, and filled a mission to California in 1919-1922. He was called to act as Los Angeles Stake Clerk in 1923, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 26, 1928, by Stephen L. Richards.
