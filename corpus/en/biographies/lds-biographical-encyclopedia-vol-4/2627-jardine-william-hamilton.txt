Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jardine, William Hamilton

Jardine, William Hamilton, Bishop of the Taylor Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1926, was born Feb. 11, 1865, in Wellsville, Utah, a son of James Jardine and Isabel White. He was baptized Nov. 2, 1873, and was ordained a High Priest May 31, 1908, by Lewis W. Shurtliff, and a Bishop by Orson F. Whitney March 14, 1909.
