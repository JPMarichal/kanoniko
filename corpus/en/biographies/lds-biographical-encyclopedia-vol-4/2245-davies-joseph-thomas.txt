Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davies, Joseph Thomas

Davies, Joseph Thomas, Bishop of the Manchester Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1929 to 1930+, was born June 11, 1901, at Portage, Utah, a son of William E. Davies and Lucy J. Glover. He was baptized July 11, 1909, ordained a High Priest Feb. 26, 1928, by Leo. J. Muir, and a Bishop Sept. 7, 1930, by Rudger Clawson.
