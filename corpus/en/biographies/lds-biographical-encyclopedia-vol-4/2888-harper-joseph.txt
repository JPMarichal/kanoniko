Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harper, Joseph,

Harper, Joseph, first counselor in the presidency of the Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1915 to 1930+, was born March 15, 1872, at Calls Fort, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of Thomas Harper and Hannah Jones. He was baptized when about eight years of age, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1909-1913, was ordained a High Priest by Heber J. Grant, and set apart as first counselor in the Raft River Stake presidency April 27, 1915, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
