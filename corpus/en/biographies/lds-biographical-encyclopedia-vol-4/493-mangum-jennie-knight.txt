Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mangum, Jennie Knight

Mangum, Jennie Knight, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born Nov. 7, 1885, the daughter of Jesse and Amanda Knight. She was educated in the public schools of Provo and the B. Y. University, was married in 1905 to W. Lester Mangum, is the mother of three sons and four daughters, has served in various capacities in ward and stake in the Y. L. M. I. A. and Relief Society, and was called to the General Board in March, 1924, being set apart by Pres. Heber J. Grant. She was released in 1929.
