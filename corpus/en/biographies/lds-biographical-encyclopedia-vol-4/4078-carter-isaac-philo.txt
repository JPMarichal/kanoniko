Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carter, Isaac Philo

Carter, Isaac Philo, a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company B, was born March 11, 1828, in Rutland County, Vermont, a son of Gideon Carter and Hilah Burwell. He enlisted in the Battalion at Council Bluffs without being a member of the Church, and marched to California. He arrived in the "Valley" in 1849, but went back to San Bernardino in 1853, where he joined the Church, and married Amasa M. Lyman's daughter. He returned to Utah in 1858 when the San Bernardino settlement was broken up, and settled in Beaver, Utah, in 1859. He died July 27, 1913, near Heyburn, Idaho.
