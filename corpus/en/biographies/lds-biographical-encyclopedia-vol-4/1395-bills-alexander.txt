Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bills, Alexander

Bills, Alexander, Bishop of the Nampa Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1913 to 1914, was born Sept. 4, 1857, in Little Cottonwood, Utah, the son of William A. Bills and Emmeline Beckstead. He moved to Idaho in 1914, and acted as presiding Elder over the Nampa Branch until it was organized as a ward Dec. 7, 1913, when he was chosen as Bishop.
