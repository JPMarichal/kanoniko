Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bentley, Joseph Charles

Bentley, Joseph Charles, president of the Juarez Stake of Zion, Mexico, from 1916 to 1929, was born Aug. 31, 1859, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Richard Bentley and Elizabeth Price. He was baptized in 1867, ordained an Elder Jan. 25, 1877, filled a mission to England in 1879-1881, was ordained a High Priest in 1882, and went to Mexico in 1892. He acted as Bishop of the Juarez Ward from 1898 to 1916 and was set apart as president of the Juarez Stake May 14, 1916, by Anthony W. Ivins.
