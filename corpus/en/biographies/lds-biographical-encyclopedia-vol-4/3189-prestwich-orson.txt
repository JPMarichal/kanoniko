Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Prestwich, Orson

Prestwich, Orson, Bishop of the Sharon Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1928, was born Nov. 15, 1881, in Moroni, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Cyrus W. Prestwich and Lucy Morley. He was baptized Feb. 20, 1891, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 31, 1921, by George F. Richards.
