Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sorensen, Wilford Halversen

Sorensen, Wilford Halversen, Bishop of the Marsh Center Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Oct. 13, 1887, in Mendon, Utah, the son of Christian Sorensen and Caroline Halversen. He was baptized Aug. 16, 1896, ordained an Elder Nov. 7, 1910, by M.D. Bird, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1910-1913, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 10, 1921, by Geo. T. Hyde, and a Bishop in 1924 by Richard R. Lyman.
