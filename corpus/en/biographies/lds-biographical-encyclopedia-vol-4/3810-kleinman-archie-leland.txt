Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kleinman, Archie Leland

Kleinman, Archie Leland, Bishop of the Toquerville Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1928, was born Sept. 23, 1900, in Arimo, Idaho, a son of Conrad M. Kleinman and Eliza B. Bringhurst. He was baptized June 6, 1909, ordained an Elder June 10, 1924, by John T. Batty, filled a mission to the Central States in 1924-1926, was sustained as Bishop Nov. 6, 1927, and ordained a High Priest Nov. 20, 1927, by John H. Cottam.
