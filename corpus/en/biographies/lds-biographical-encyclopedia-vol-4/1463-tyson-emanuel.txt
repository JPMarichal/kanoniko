Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tyson, Emanuel

Tyson, Emanuel, Bishop of the Brigham City 6th Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1929, was born May 8, 1874, in Randolph, Rich Co., Utah, the son of William Tyson and Sarah Caldwell. He was baptized in 1882, by Charles South, ordained a Seventy by J. Golden Kimball Dec. 3, 1897, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1897-1900, located in Brigham City in 1897, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 23, 1921, by Rudger Clawson.
