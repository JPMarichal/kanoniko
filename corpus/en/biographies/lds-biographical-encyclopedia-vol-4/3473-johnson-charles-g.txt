Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Charles G.

Johnson, Charles G., Bishop of the Windsor Ward, Timpanogos Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1924, was born Nov. 1, 1872, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Anders Gustave Johnson and Charlotta C. Anderson. He was baptized June 26, 1881, ordained a Seventy April 3, 1902, by J. Golden Kimball, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1902-1904, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 5, 1915, by Orson F. Whitney.
