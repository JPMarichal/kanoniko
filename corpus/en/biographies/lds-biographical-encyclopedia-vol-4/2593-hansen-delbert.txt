Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Delbert

Hansen, Delbert, Bishop of the Redmond Ward, North Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1928, was born March 25, 1890, in Redmond, the son of James Hansen and Elizabeth Frandsen. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained an Elder in June, 1920, by James A. Christensen and filled a mission to the Central States in 1920-1922. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 20, 1924, by Hyrum G. Smith.
