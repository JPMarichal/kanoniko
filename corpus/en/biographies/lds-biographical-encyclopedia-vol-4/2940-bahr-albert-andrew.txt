Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bahr, Albert Andrew

Bahr, Albert Andrew, Bishop of the Cedarview Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born May 24, 1884, in Lehi, Utah Co., Utah, a son of William Andreas Bahr and Harriet Jackson. He was baptized Sept. 16, 1893, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 4, 1928, by David O. McKay.
