Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Edlefsen, Edlef B.

Edlefsen, Edlef B., Bishop of the Driggs Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1910 to 1912, was born April 21, 1860, in Emmelev, Denmark, a son of Niels Christian Edlefsen and Maren Hansen. He was baptized in 1868, ordained a High Priest Sept. 9, 1895, by John Henry Smith and sustained as Bishop July 17, 1910.
