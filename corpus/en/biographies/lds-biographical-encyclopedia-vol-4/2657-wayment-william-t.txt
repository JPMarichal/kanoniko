Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wayment, William T.

Wayment, William T., Bishop of the Warren Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1925, was born April 29, 1858, in Whaddon, Cambridge, England, the son of William Wayment and Martha Brown. He was baptized May 20, 1872, emigrated to Utah, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1897-1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 27, 1908, by David O. McKay.
