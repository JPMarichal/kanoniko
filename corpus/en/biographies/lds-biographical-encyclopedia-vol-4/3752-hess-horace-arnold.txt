Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hess, Horace Arnold

Hess, Horace Arnold, Bishop of the Ashton Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Sept. 19, 1880, in Farmington, Davis Co., Utah, a son of John W. Hess and Sarah Miller. He was baptized Sept. 23, 1888, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1902-1903, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 31, 1921, by Stephen L. Richards.
