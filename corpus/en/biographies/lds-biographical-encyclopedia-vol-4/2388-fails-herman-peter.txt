Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fails, Herman Peter

Fails, Herman Peter, second counselor in the Minidoka Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930, was born May 9, 1883, in Wood County, Ohio, a son of Henry R. Fails and Martha Jane Rechner. He was baptized June 8, 1902, emigrated to Utah in 1902, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 3, 1913, by Rudger Clawson, and was set apart as second counselor May 11, 1924, by Richard C. May.
