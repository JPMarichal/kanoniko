Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomander, Daniel Theodore

Thomander, Daniel Theodore, Bishop of the Mapleton Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1914 to 1915, was born Nov. 28, 1870, in Ephraim, Utah, the son of Peter Thomander and Ingeborg Pehrson. He was baptized Aug. 10, 1879, ordained a High Priest Sept. 22, 1907, by Reuben H. Miller, and a Bishop June 6, 1914, by Geo. F. Richards.
