Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Benson Stake

Andersen, William

Andersen, William, Bishop of the Richmond Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1930+, was born June 13, 1873, in Richmond, the son of Jorgen (George) Anderson and Armina Carson. He was baptized Oct. 4, 1881, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1896-1899, was ordained a High Priest March 1, 1908, by Thomas Moore, and ordained a Bishop July 23, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
