Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Romney, Gaskell

Romney, Gaskell, Bishop of the Yale Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930, was born Sept. 21, 1871, in St. George, Utah, the son of Miles P. Romney and Hannah E. Hill. He was baptized Oct. 18, 1879, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1900-1902, and was ordained a High Priest by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop Jan. 31, 1926, by Geo. Albert Smith.
