Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Watkins, Arthur Vivian

Watkins, Arthur Vivian, president of the Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Dec. 18, 1886, in Midway, Wasarch Co., Utah, a son of Arthur Watkins and Emily A. Gerber. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Eastern States, and was set apart as president Sept. 14, 1929, by Stephen L. Richards.
