Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Paskett, Philip J.

Paskett, Philip J., Bishop of the Basalt Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1928, was born Dec. 9, 1878, in Grouse Creek, Utah, a son of Philip Paskett and Emma Richins. He was baptized July 17, 1887, and sustained as Bishop June 15, 1924.
