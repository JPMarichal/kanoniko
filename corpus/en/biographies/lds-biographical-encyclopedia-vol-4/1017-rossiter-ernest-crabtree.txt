Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rossiter, Ernest Crabtree

Rossiter, Ernest Crabtree, president of the Society Islands Mission from 1915 to 1919, was born Oct. 16, 1882, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of William A. Rossiter and Eliza Ann Crabtree. He was baptized when eight years old, was ordained a Seventy Jan. 10, 1905, by Seymour B. Young, and filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1905-1907. In 1907-1908 he filled a mission to the Northern States. He acted as president of the Y. M. M. I. A. in the 10th Ward, Salt Lake City, married Venus Robinson, June 28, 1911, was called to preside over the Tahitian Mission or Society Islands Mission in 1915, and left home with his wife Feb. 17, 1915; he returned in January, 1920.
