Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

North Weber Stake

Allred, John Allen

Allred, John Allen, Bishop of the Slaterville Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1890, was born Aug. 29, 1834, in Monroe Co., Mo., a son of Isaac Allred and Julia Ann Taylor. He was baptized in October, 1842, came to Utah in 1851, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 28, 1877, by Franklin D. Richards. He died April 9, 1904.
