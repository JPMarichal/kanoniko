Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cook, Henry Freeman

Cook, Henry Freeman, Bishop of the Cedar Valley Ward, Lehi Stake, Utah, from 1876 to 1882, was born Jan. 12, 1815, at Homer, New York, baptized in 1843 and came to Utah in 1852. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 30, 1857, and died April 14, 1882, in Cedar Fort.
