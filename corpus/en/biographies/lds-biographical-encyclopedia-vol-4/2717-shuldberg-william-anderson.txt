Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shuldberg, William Anderson

Shuldberg, William Anderson. Bishop of the Winder Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1909 to 1913, was born Dec. 1, 1873, in Munkthorp, Sweden, a son of Peter A. Shuldberg and Anna Anderson. He was baptized May 18, 1893, came to Utah that same year, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 30, 1907, by Francis M. Lyman, who also ordained him a Bishop Sept. 4, 1909. He filled a mission to Sweden in 1913-1915.
