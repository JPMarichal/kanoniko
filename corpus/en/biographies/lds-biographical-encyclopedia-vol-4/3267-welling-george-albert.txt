Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Welling, George Albert

Welling, George Albert, Bishop of the North Farmington Ward, South Davis Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Nov. 11, 1875, in Farmington, Davis Co., Utah, a son of Job Welling and Phebe L. Holmes. He was baptized July 27, 1884, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1897-1899, and was ordained a Bishop Jan. 9, 1925, by Rudger Clawson.
