Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilson, Joseph Ellis, jun.

Wilson, Joseph Ellis, jun., Bishop of the Lund Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1930, was born June 15, 1878, in Clifton, Idaho, a son of Joseph Ellis Wilson and Lerona Abigail Monroe Martin. He was baptized June 15, 1886, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1897-1899, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 9, 1906, by Seymour B. Young, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 26, 1922, by George F. Richards.
