Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dewey, William Alfred

Dewey, William Alfred, Bishop of the Cambridge Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1907 to 1915, was born June 28, 1861, in Call's Fort, Utah, a son of John C. Dewey and Harriet May. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest Jan. 20, 1894, by Marriner W. Merrill, and ordained a Bishop June 16, 1907, by Geo. F. Richards.
