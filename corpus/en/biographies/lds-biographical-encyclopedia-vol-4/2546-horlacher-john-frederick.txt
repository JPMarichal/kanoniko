Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Horlacher, John Frederick

Horlacher, John Frederick, first counselor in the Nevada Stake presidency, Nevada, from 1926 to 1930, was born Dec. 7, 1884, in Rublinger, Wurtemberg, Germany, a son of Michael Horlacher and Marie Schuler. He was baptized in April, 1904, in Dublin, Ireland, came to America in 1906, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 19, 1926, by Rudger Clawson and set apart as first counselor in the Nevada Stake presidency.
