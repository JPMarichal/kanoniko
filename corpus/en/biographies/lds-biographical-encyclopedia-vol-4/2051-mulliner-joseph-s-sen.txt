Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mulliner, Joseph S., sen.

Mulliner, Joseph S., sen., Bishop of the Iona Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1890 to 1895, was born Dec. 10, 1857, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Samuel Mulliner and Mary Richardson. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 19, 1890, by John W. Taylor, and died in 1923.
