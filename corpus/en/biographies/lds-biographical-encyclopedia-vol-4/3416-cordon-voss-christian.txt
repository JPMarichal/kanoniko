Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cordon, Voss Christian

Cordon, Voss Christian, Bishop of the Chapin Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born April 9, 1894, in Treasureton, Idaho, a son of Ralph R. Cordon and Annie S. Shumway. He was baptized April 9, 1902, ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 6, 1926, by David O. McKay, and filled a mission to California in 1928-1930.
