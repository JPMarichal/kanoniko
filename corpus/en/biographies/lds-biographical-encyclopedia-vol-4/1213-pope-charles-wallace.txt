Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pope, Charles Wallace

Pope, Charles Wallace, Bishop of the Garden City Ward, Bear Lake Stake, (Rich Co., Utah), Idaho, from 1910 to 1923, was born Dec. 27, 1872, in Randolph, Rich Co., Utah, the son of Charles Holmes Pope and Sarah Maria McCann. He was baptized when eight years old, was ordained a High Priest in August, 1900, by James H. Hart, and a Bishop Dec. 11, 1910, by Jos. R. Shepherd.
