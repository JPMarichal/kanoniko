Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Simpson, Henry

Simpson, Henry, Bishop of the Cedarville Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1916 to 1917, was born April 12, 1864, in Leicester, England, the son of Charles Simpson and Susannah Moore. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1904, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 4, 1916, by Heber J. Grant.
