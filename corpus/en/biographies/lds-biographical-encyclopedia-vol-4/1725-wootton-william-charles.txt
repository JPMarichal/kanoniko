Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wootton, William Charles

Wootton, William Charles, Bishop of the Butler Ward, East Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1930, was born Nov. 11, 1879, in South Cottonwood, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Charles R. Wootton and Esther Ballard. He was baptized June 6, 1888, ordained a Seventy Oct. 17, 1900, by George Teasdale, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1900-1902, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 10, 1919, by James E. Talmage.
