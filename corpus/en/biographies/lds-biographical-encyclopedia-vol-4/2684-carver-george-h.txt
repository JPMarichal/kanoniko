Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Oneida Stake

Carver, George H.

Carver, George H., Bishop of the Preston 3rd Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1902 to 1917, was born Nov. 11, 1854, in Kaysville, Utah a son of John Carver and Mary Ann Eames. He was baptized Aug. 10, 1866, filled two missions, one to the Southern States in 1879-1881 and the other to the Northwestern States in 1898-1899. He was ordained a Bishop Feb. 3, 1902, by Marriner W. Merrill. He died June 21, 1922.
