Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomson, William

Thomson, William, Bishop of the Bingham Ward, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1894 to 1901, was born in October, 1857, in Stirlingshire, Scotland, a son of William Thomson and Margaret Wilson. He was baptized March 22, 1868, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 7, 1899, by Francis M. Lyman.
