Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Weber Stake

Beus, James Richard

Beus, James Richard, Bishop of the Hooper Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1923, was born April 5, 1869, in Ogden, Utah, a son of James Beus and Clarinda C. Hill. He was ordained a Bishop July 13, 1913.
