Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Andrew C., jun.

Jensen, Andrew C., jun., Bishop of the Moore Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1906 to 1910, was born Jan. 15, 1875, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Andrew C. Jensen, sen., and Annie M. Carlson. He was baptized July 9, 1883, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 13, 1906, by Geo. F. Richards.
