Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Law, Preston Floyd

Law, Preston Floyd, Bishop of the Twin Groves Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born April 4, 1907, at Twin Groves, Idaho, a son of Charles H. Law and Sarah Hill. He was baptized June 7, 1915, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 14, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
