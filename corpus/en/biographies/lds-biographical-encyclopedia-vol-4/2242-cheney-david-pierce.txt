Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cheney, David Pierce

Cheney, David Pierce, Bishop of the Belvedere Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1923 to 1930, was born July 10, 1879, in Springville, Utah, a son of David Cheney and Lodima Hutchings. He was baptized Aug. 4. 1887, ordained a Seventy July 20, 1900, by Mathias F. Cowley, filled a mission to Holland and Belgium in 1900-1903, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 21, 1905, by Hyrum M. Smith, and presided over the Chapin Ward, Teton Stake.
