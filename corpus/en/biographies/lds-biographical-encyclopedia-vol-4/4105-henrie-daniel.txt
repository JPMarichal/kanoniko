Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Henrie, Daniel

Henrie, Daniel, a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company D, was born Nov. 15, 1825, in Hamilton County, Ohio, a son of William Henrie and Myra Mayall, both natives of Virginia. His parents joined the Church in 1842, and moved to Nauvoo, where Daniel was baptized. He marched to California with the Mormon Battalion, and made his way to Utah in 1849. On Oct. 29, 1849, he married Amanda Bradley, and settled in Davis County. While on a visit to Manti he was detained by Indian troubles and later served in the Walker and Black Hawk Indian wars, and was also captain of Company A of the Nauvoo Legion. He served as sheriff one year and as city treasurer for three yesrs, besides holding many prominent positions in the Church, acting as senior president of the 45th quorum of Seventy for nearly forty years. By his first wife he had fourteen children, and by his second wife twelve children. He died June 28, 1914, in Manti, Utah.
