Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnston, Peter Green

Johnston, Peter Green, Bishop of the Blackfeet Ward, Blackfeet Stake, Idaho, from 1904-1905, was born Aug. 15, 1864, in Gramsay, Orkney, Scotland, the son of Wm. Johnston and Isabella Green. He was baptized June 23, 1878, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 23, 1904, by Francis M. Lyman. He died Jan. 7, 1931, at Blackfoot.
