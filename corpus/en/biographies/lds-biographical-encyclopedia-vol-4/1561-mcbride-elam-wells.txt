Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcbride, Elam Wells

Mcbride, Elam Wells, Bishop of the Oakley 3rd Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1901 to 1904, was born Sept. 18, 1854, in Grantsville, Utah, the son of James McBride and Olive Cheeney. He was baptized May 30, 1863, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1881-1882 and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 9, 1901, by Rudger Clawson. He died June 14, 1927.
