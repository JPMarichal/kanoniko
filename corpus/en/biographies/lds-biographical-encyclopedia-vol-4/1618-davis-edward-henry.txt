Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davis, Edward Henry

Davis, Edward Henry, Bishop of the Arbon Ward, Curlew Stake, Idaho, from 1914 to 1916, was born April 4, 1873, in Samaria, Oneida Co., Idaho, a son of David R. Davis and Elizabeth Griffiths. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 17, 1914, by George Albert Smith.
