Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rigby, Joseph Eckersley

Rigby, Joseph Eckersley, Bishop of the Hibbard Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1906 to 1929, was born July 27, 1867, in Wellsville, Utah, the son of William F. Rigby and Sophia Eckersley. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1875, was ordained a Seventy and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1902-1904, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 14, 1906, by Charles W. Penrose.
