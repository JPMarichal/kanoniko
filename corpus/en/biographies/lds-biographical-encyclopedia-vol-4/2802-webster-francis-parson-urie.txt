Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Webster, Francis Parson Urie

Webster, Francis Parson Urie, Bishop of the Cedar East Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1920 to 1922, was born May 22, 1893, in Cedar City, Utah, the son of John J. G. Webster and Eliza A. Urie. He was baptized June 9, 1901, ordained an Elder in 1916 by Francis M. Lyman, filled a mission to Australia in 1916-1919, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 21, 1920, by George F. Richards.
