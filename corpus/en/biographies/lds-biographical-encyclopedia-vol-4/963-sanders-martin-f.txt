Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sanders, Martin F.

Sanders, Martin F., president of the Samoan Mission from 1903 to 1905, was born Nov. 16, 1874, in Fairview, Utah, a son of Moses M. Sanders, jun., and Elizabeth Ellen Cheney. He was baptized in July, 1883, set apart for a mission to Samoa June 18, 1899, and returned Dec. 19, 1904.
