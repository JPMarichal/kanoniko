Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Miller, Abiah Wadsworth

Miller, Abiah Wadsworth, Bishop of the Starline Ward, Lethbridge Stake, Canada, from 1929 to 1930+, was born July 7, 1883, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of William Miller and Lucinda Wadsworth. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1891, was ordained an Elder Nov. 5, 1909, and filled a mission to Samoa in 1910-1914. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 18, 1929, by George Albert Smith.
