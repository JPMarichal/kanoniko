Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rawlings, Walter, jun.

Rawlings, Walter, jun., Bishop of the Tetonia Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1918 to 1919, was born Dec. 19, 1891, at Fairview, Idaho, the son of Walter Rawlings and Lottie Inglet. He was baptized July 6, 1901, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1911-1913, and was ordained a High Priest July 16, 1916, by James F. Griggs, and a Bishop March 6, 1918, by Rudger Clawson.
