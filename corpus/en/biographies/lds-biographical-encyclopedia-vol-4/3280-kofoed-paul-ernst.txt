Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kofoed, Paul Ernst

Kofoed, Paul Ernst, Bishop of the Ephraim Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1856 to 1859, was born June 7, 1811 or 1813, on the Isle of Bornholm, Denmark. As a sailor he crossed the Atlantic 23 times. He was baptized in the summer of 1847, was appointed to preside as a Bishop over the Ephraim Ward by Erastus Snow in 1856, but was never ordained a Bishop.
