Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Judd, Jerome John

Judd, Jerome John, Bishop of the Chuichupa Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1926 to 1930, was a son of Hyrum Jerome Judd and Susan Boyce Judd. He was baptized in 1877, moved to Mexico in 1885, was ordained a High Priest June 6, 1926, by John Thomas Whetten, and a Bishop Nov. 14, 1926, by Melvin J. Ballard.
