Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Gertrude Pearson

Smith, Gertrude Pearson, a member of the general board of the Primary Association since 1929, was born Oct. 6, 1887, in Salt Lake City, Utah. She was baptized Oct. 6, 1895, became a member of the Liberty Stake Sunday School board, was a member of the Wasatch Stake Y. L. M. I. A. board, a member of the Granite Stake Sunday School board, and a chorister and supervisor of the Granite Stake Religion classes from 1926 to 1929. She received a normal diploma from the University of Utah, and was a teacher in the Salt Lake City schools for five years. For a time she was supervisor of schools in Heber City, Utah, and for four years was instructor in the Lincoln County High School at Panaca, Nevada. Later she became a teacher of the Home Economics Department at the L. D. S. College in Salt Lake City and president of the Parent-Teachers' Association of the Irving Junior High School. On Aug. 16, 1911, she was married to James Richard Smith and is the mother of one daughter (Sarah Gertrude Smith).
