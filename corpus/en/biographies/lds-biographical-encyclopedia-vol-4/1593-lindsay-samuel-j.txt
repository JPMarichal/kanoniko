Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lindsay, Samuel J.

Lindsay, Samuel J., Bishop of the Taylorsville Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930, was born June 28, 1881, in Taylorsville, the son of Joseph S. Lindsay and Emma Bennion. He was baptized July 4, 1889, filled a mission to the Central States in 1905-1907, and was ordained a High Priest Dec. 13, 1914, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop Oct. 2, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard. He died Jan. 20, 1932.
