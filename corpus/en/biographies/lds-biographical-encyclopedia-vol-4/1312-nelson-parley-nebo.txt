Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nelson, Parley Nebo

Nelson, Parley Nebo, Bishop of the Richmond Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1921, was born Feb. 22, 1874, in Richmond, the son of Christian Nielsen and Mary Israelson. He was baptized March 2, 1882, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 22, 1917, by James E. Talmage. He died June 10, 1922.
