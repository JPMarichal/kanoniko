Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Christian, jun.

Jensen, Christian, jun., president of the Samoan Mission from 1912 to 1913, was born Nov. 17, 1868, in Ephraim, Utah, a son of Christian and Barbara Jensen. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to Samoa in 1894-1897, and was set apart for a second mission to Samoa Nov. 21, 1911, to act as president of the Mission.
