Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pincock, George Albert

Pincock, George Albert, Bishop of the Wilford Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1898 to 1903, was born March 1, 1865, in Ogden, Utah, a son of John Pincock and Isabella Douglas. He was baptized May 5, 1873, ordained a High Priest July 16, 1893, by Thomas E. Ricks, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1898, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 1, 1898, by John Henry Smith.
