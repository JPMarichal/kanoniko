Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Edwards, Harrison Martin, jun.

Edwards, Harrison Martin, jun., presiding Elder of the Rochester Branch, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930, was born Aug. 27, 1866, in Manti, Utah, the son of Harrison Martin Edwards and Nahomi Padres. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1884, and was ordained a High Priest May 6, 1923, by James E. Talmage, and set apart as presiding Elder of Rochester Branch.
