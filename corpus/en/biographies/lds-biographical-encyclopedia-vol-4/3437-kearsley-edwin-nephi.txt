Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kearsley, Edwin Nephi

Kearsley, Edwin Nephi, Bishop of the Victor Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born April 30, 1872, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Edwin S. Kearsley and Harriet Cooper. He was baptized in 1880, ordained a High Priest Feb. 18, 1923, by Rudger Clawson, who also ordained him a Bishop May 11, 1929.
