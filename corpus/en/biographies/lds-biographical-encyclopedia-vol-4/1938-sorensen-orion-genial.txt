Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sorensen, Orion Genial

Sorensen, Orion Genial, Bishop of the Axtell Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born June 28, 1897, in Ephraim, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Niels Peter and Amelia Sophie Sorensen. He was baptized July 15, 1915, ordained a High Priest March 10, 1929, by Orson F. Whitney, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 14, 1930, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
