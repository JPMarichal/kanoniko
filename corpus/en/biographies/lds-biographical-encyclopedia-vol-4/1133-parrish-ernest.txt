Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Parrish, Ernest

Parrish, Ernest, Bishop of Mountain View Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1918 to 1930, was born Aug. 13, 1877, in Centerville, Davis Co., Utah, the son of Joel Parrish and Emma Ford. He was baptized in 1887; moved to Canada in 1900; was ordained a High Priest by Thomas Duce about 1907, and ordained a Bishop by George Albert Smith.
