Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Schoenfeld, Edward

Schoenfeld, Edward, president of the Swiss and German Mission from 1870 to 1872, was born March 8, 1832, in Magdeburg, near Leipzig, Saxony, Germany, the son of Wilhelm Schoenfeld and Sophia Krebs. He was baptized in the river Elbe Oct. 14, 1855, by Franklin D. Richards, went to England in 1856, labored in the London Conference for one year, and emigrated to Utah in 1857. From February, 1870, to July, 1872, Elder Schoenfeld was president of the Swiss and German Mission and editor of the "Stern". He died Sept. 2, 1914, in Salt Lake City, beloved by all who knew him.
