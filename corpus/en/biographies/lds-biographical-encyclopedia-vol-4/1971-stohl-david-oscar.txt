Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stohl, David Oscar

Stohl, David Oscar, Bishop of the Ocean Park Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1923 to 1928, was born Oct. 15, 1882, at Brigham City, Utah, the son of Ola N. Stohl and Christina Johnson. He was baptized May 7, 1891, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 19, 1923, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
