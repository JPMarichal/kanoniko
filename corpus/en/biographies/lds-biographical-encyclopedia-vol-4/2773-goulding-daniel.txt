Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Goulding, Daniel

Goulding, Daniel, Bishop of the Henrieville Ward, Panguitch Stake, Utah, from 1889 to 1905, was born March 31, 1831, in Winterburn, Gloucestershire, England, a son of John Goulding and Rosanna Bray. He was baptized in July, 1845, in Wales, emigrated to Utah in 1868 from Australia, and was ordained and set apart to preside over the Henrieville Ward by Francis M. Lyman June 6, 1889.
