Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tolman, Abinadi

Tolman, Abinadi, Bishop of the Honeyville Ward, Box Elder Co., Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Oct. 22, 1884, at Honeyville, the son of Benjamin H. Tolman and Eliza B. Grant. He was baptized in December, 1892, by his father. After being ordained an Elder by Ole N. Stohl, he filled a mission to the Southern States in 1902-1903, filled a second mission to the Netherlands and Switzerland in 1906-1907, was ordained a High Priest May 12, 1917, by Helger J. Packer, and ordained a Bishop July 8, 1928, by James E. Talmage.
