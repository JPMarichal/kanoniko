Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Richards, Ralph Ashton

Richards, Ralph Ashton, Bishop of the Oakley Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Feb. 28, 1903, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Albert D. Richards and Emily T. Ashton. He was baptized Aug. 12, 1911, ordained an Elder April 1, 1923, by John Frandsen, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1923-1925, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 17, 1929, by Rudger Clawson.
