Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tate, Alma Vining

Tate, Alma Vining, Bishop of the Thatcher West Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1927 to 1929, was born Sept. 14, 1895, in Spanish Fork, Utah, a son of Martin Van Buren Tare and Margaret Vining. He was baptized Aug. 11, 1904, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 30, 1927, by Stephen L. Richards.
