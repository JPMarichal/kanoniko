Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Woodruff Stake

Bell, Benjamin

Bell, Benjamin, presiding Elder of the Almy Branch, Woodruff Stake, Wyoming, from 1900 to 1901, was born July 31, 1868, in Frostburg, Allegany Co., Maryland, a son of William Bell and Ann Wheelden (or Whedon). He was baptized Dec. 13, 1891, and ordained a High Priest Feb. 3, 1902, by John M. Baxter. He died in Kemmeter.
