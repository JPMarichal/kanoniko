Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dunn, Thomas

Dunn, Thomas, Bishop of the North Ogden Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1851 to 1863, was born Jan. 2, 1822, at Phelps, Ontario Co., N.Y., a son of James Dunn and Sally Barker. He was baptized May 28, 1839, and came to Utah in 1847, as one of the Mormon Battalion. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 22, 1852, by Lorin Farr, and a Bishop on the same date by Brigham Young.
