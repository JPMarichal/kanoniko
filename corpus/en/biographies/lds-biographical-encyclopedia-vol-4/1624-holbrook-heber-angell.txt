Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Holbrook, Heber Angell

Holbrook, Heber Angell, Bishop of the Holbrook Ward, Curlew Stake, Idaho, from 1902 to 1909, was born June 5, 1867, in Bountiful, Davis Co., Utah, a son of Joseph Holbrook and Caroline Frances Angell. He was baptized June 5, 1875, and was sustained as a Bishop Oct. 26, 1902. He died Dec. 29, 1931.
