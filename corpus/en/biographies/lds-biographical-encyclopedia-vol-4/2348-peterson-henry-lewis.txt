Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Henry Lewis

Peterson, Henry Lewis, Bishop of the Chandler Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1913 to 1928, was born Aug. 5, 1878, in Coalville, Summit Co., Utah, a son of Charles Peterson and Clara Jane Lewis. He was baptized Sept. 2, 1886, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1907-1908, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 6, 1913, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
