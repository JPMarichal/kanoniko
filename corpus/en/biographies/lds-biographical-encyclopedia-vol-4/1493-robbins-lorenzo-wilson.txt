Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robbins, Lorenzo Wilson

Robbins, Lorenzo Wilson, Bishop of the Burley Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1906 to 1915, was born Jan. 9, 1867, in Weston, Idaho, the son of Wilson C. Robbins and Minnie Kofoed. He was baptized Jan. 12, 1879, ordained a High Priest March 30, 1901, by Wm. T. Jack, and ordained a Bishop June 8, 1906, in Salt Lake City.
