Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Covington, Joseph W.

Covington, Joseph W., Bishop of Lankershim Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1928 to 1929, was born Aug. 8, 1879, in Orderville, Utah, the son of John T. Covington and Johanna Luridbind. He was baptized Oct. 13, 1887, ordained a High Priest April 22, 1923, by Geo. Albert Smith, and a Bishop Jan. 29, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
