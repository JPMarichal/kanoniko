Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomas, Elijah Murray

Thomas, Elijah Murray, Bishop of the Pinedale Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1902 to 1914, was born April 26, 1852, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Elijah Thomas and Ann Heywood. He was baptized by Mosiah Hancock, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 21, 1902.
