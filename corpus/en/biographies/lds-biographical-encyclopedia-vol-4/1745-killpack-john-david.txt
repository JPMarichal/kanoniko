Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Killpack, John David

Killpack, John David, Bishop of the Ferron Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1904 to 1906, was born May 8, 1857, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of William Joseph Killpack and Eliza Elizabeth Savage. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 14, 1882, by John Henry .Smith, and a Bishop April 18, 1904, by Hyrum M. Smith.
