Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kloepfer, Frank Philip

Kloepfer, Frank Philip, Bishop of the Boise 1st Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930, was born Nov. 5, 1887, in Ogden, Utah, the son of Philip Kloepfer and Louise Lehman. He was baptized Jan. 25, 1906, was ordained a High Priest June 2, 1929, by John Wells, and ordained a Bishop March 9, 1930, by Rudger Clawson.
