Miscellaneous Biographies

Adams, Orson B.

Adams, Orson B., a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company C, was born March 9, 1815, in New York State, and came to Utah the year following the march of the Battalion to California. In 1850 he went to Parowan as a pioneer, and later extended his pioneer work to Washington County, Utah, settling at Harrisburg. He died Feb. 4, 1901. His companion in the Battalion, John Steele, spoke at the services, and told of the fine character of Brother Adams; said he was a most diligent and straightforward man. Brother Adams acted as Bishop of the Harrisburg Ward in 1878.
