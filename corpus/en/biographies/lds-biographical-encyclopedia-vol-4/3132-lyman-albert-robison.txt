Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lyman, Albert Robison

Lyman, Albert Robison, first counselor in the San Juan Stake presidency, Utah, from 1912 to 1928, was born Jan. 10, 1880, in Fillmore, Utah, the son of Platte D. Lyman and Adelia Robison. He was baptized June 16, 1888, ordained an Elder April 2, 1899, filled a mission to England in 1899-1900, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 10, 1908, by Walter C. Lyman, and set apart as first counselor May 21, 1912, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
