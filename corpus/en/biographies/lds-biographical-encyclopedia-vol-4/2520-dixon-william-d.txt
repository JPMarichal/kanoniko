Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dixon, William D.

Dixon, William D., Bishop of the Payson 1st Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1924, was born Feb. 20, 1874, in Payson, Utah, the son of Charles H. Dixon and Matilda Douglas. He was baptized May 4, 1882, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1898, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 20, 1921, by Orson F. Whitney.
