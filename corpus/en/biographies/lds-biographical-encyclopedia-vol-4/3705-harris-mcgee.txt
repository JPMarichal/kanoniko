Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Mcgee

Harris, Mcgee, presiding Elder of the Herriman Branch, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1855 to 1858, was born Nov. 8, 1800, in Wilson Co., Tennessee, a son of William and Margaret Harris. He was sustained as presiding Elder Feb. 11, 1855.
