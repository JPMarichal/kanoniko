Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gardner, Franklin Richards

Gardner, Franklin Richards, Bishop of the Afton North Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1919 to 1930+, was born July 23, 1888, in West Jordan, Utah, a son of Archibald Gardner and Marie Larsen. He was baptized July 23, 1896, ordained an Elder Oct. 30, 1909, by Osborne Low, filled a mission to South Africa in 1910-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 22, 1919, by James E. Talmage.
