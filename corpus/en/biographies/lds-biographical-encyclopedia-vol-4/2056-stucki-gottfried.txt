Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stucki, Gottfried

Stucki, Gottfried, Bishop of the Coltman Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born March 26, 1890, in Bern, Switzerland, a son of Gottlieb Stucki and Anna Gamfeler. He was baptized Aug. 20, 1900, emigrated to Utah in 1901, was ordained an Elder Feb. 10, 1913, and filled a mission to the Western States in 1914-1917. He was ordained a High Priest May 29, 1921, by James E. Talmage, and a Bishop Aug. 15, 1926, by Richard R. Lyman.
