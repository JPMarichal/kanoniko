Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sinclair, William A. H.

Sinclair, William A. H., Bishop of the Bramwell Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1916 to 1919, was born June 20, 1880, in Portage, Morgan Co., Utah, the son of William Sinclair and Mary Carter Hoskins. He was baptized June 25, 1888, by James Halford, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 5, 1916, by Anthony W. Ivins.
