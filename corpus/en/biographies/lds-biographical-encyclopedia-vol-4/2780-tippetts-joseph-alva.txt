Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tippetts, Joseph Alva

Tippetts, Joseph Alva, Bishop of the Tropic Ward, Panguitch Stake, Utah, from 1904-1906, was born March 3, 1870, in Spanish Fork, Utah, a son of Joseph Tippetts and Sarah Losee. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1891-1892, and was ordained a Bishop April 24, 1904, by David Cameron.
