Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ashby, Rodney Badger

Ashby, Rodney Badger, Bishop of the Leamington Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1920, was born Oct. 26, 1872, in Holden, Utah, the son of Wm. H. Ashby and Nancy M. Badger. He was baptized May 22, 1881, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1898, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 15, 1900, by Francis M. Lyman.
