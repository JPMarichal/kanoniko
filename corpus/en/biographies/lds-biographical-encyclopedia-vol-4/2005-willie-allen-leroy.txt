Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Willie, Allen Leroy

Willie, Allen Leroy, Bishop of the Mendon Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1920 to 1921, was born Jan. 11, 1889, at Mendon, Utah, the son of John S. Willie and Eliza Hunsaker. He was baptized July 17, 1898, ordained an Elder in August, 1911, by Andrew M. Israelsen, filled a mission to Germany in 1911-1914, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 18, 1920, by David O. McKay.
