Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Schwendiman, John

Schwendiman, John, Bishop of the Newdale Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born April 7, 1877, in Bern, Switzerland, the son of Samuel Schwendiman and Magdalena Straubhaar. He emigrated to Idaho in 1886, filled a mission to Switzerland in 1903-1906, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 21, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
