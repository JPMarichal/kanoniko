Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Eugene Severin

Hansen, Eugene Severin, Bishop of the East Garland Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1930, was born Feb. 21, 1881, in Collinston, Box Elder Co., Utah, the son of Willard Snow Hansen and Maria Larsen. He was baptized when eight years old in Brigham City. After being ordained an Elder by Charles Kelly, he filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1902-1904, and was ordained a Bishop March 23, 1919, by Geo. Albert Smith.
