Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Loveland, David

Loveland, David, Bishop of the Darby Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1905 to 1908, was born Aug. 15, 1861, in Call's Fort, Box Elder Co., Utah. He was baptized in 1872, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 20, 1905, by Orrin P. Miller.
