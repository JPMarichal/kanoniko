Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jolley, Henry Brya

Jolley, Henry Bryan Manning, Bishop of the Mt. Carmel Ward, Kanab Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1902, was born Oct. 11, 1813, in Pitt County, North Carolina, the son of Henry Jolley and Frances Manning. He was baptized March 12, 1842, in Tennessee, came to Utah in 1852, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1880-1881, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 5, 1877, by Erastus Snow.
