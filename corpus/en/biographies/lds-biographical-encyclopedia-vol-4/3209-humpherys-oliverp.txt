Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Humpherys, Oliverp

Humpherys, Oliver, Bishop of the Shelley 2nd Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Jan. 12, 1888, in Dingle, Idaho, a son of Samuel Humpherys and Hannah Martha Clifton. He was baptized in June, 1896, ordained an Elder in October, 1908, by his father, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1908-1912, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 17, 1924, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., and a Bishop Nov. 3, 1929, by Richard R. Lyman.
