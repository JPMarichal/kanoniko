Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stewart, Andrew Jackson Barett

Stewart, Andrew Jackson Barett, Bishop of the Benjamin Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1886 to 1900, vas born Feb. 28, 1861, in Payson, Utah, the son of Benjamin Franklin Stewart and Elizabeth J. Davis. He was baptized March 4, 1869, and ortained a High Priest and Bishop June 12, 1886, by Franklin D. Richards.
