Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Major, Frederick Reynaldo

Major, Frederick Reynaldo, Bishop of the Wales Ward, Moroni Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1929, was born July 21, 1888, in BoDavis Co., Utah, a son of William D. Major and Virginia F. McMaster. He was baptized July 11, 1897, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 14, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
