Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wootton, George A.

Wootton, George A., Bishop of the Price Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1922, was born June 18, 1870, in Midway, Utah, the son of Attewall Wootton and Cynthia J. Jewett. He was baptized Sept. 28, 1879, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 8, 1921, by James E. Talmage.
