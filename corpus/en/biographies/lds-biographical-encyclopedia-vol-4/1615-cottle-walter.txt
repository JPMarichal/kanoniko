Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cottle, Walter

Cottle, Walter, Bishop of the Black Pine Ward, Curlew Stake, Idaho, from 1913 to 1920, was born Oct. 1, 1884, in Plain City, Weber Co., Utah, a son of William H. Cottie and Elizabeth S. Dummer. He was baptized Sept. 12, 1893, ordained a High Priest Aug. 27, 1911, by F. W. Stratford, and .a Bishop Nov. 23, 1913, by Orson F. Whitney.
