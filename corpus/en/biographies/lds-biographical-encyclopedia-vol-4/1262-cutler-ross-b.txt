Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cutler, Ross B.

Cutler, Ross B., Bishop of the Adamsville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1924, was born Feb. 10, 1876, in Glendale, Kane Co., Utah, the son of Royal James Cutler and Margaret Butler. He was called on a mission to the Southern States Feb. 17, 1897, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 15, 1906, and ordained a Bishop July 29, 1923, by James E. Talmage.
