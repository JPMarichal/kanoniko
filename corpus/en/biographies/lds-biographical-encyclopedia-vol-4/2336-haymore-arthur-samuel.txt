Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Haymore, Arthur Samuel

Haymore, Arthur Samuel, Bishop of the Gilbert Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1918 to 1926, was born Feb. 1, 1878, in Payson, Utah Co., Utah, a son of Franklin D. Haymore and Lucinda A. Taylor. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Mexico in 1910-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 17, 1918, by David O. McKay.
