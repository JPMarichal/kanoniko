Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Zion Park Stake

Ballard, James Monroe

Ballard, James Monroe, Bishop of the Grafton Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1887 to 1900, and from 1903 to 1907, was born Sept. 26, 1847, in Lee County, Iowa, a son of John Harvey Ballard and Charlotte Pincock. He came to Utah in 1852, was baptized in 1856, ordained an Elder May 8, 1871, by Elias Smith and a High Priest and Bishop by Moses Thatcher Nov. 27, 1887. He died May 3, 1925.
