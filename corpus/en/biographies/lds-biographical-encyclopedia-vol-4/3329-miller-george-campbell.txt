Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Miller, George Campbell

Miller, George Campbell, Bishop of the Thayne Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1913 to 1925, was born May 10, 1878, at Hot Springs, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of John S. Miller and Elizabeth Hodge. He was baptized March 23, 1902, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 1, 1913, by Geo. Albert Smith.
