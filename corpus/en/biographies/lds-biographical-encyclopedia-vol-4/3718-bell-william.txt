Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bell, William

Bell, William, Bishop of the Cumberland Ward, Woodruff Stake, Wyoming, from 1905 to 1907, and presiding Elder from 1902 to 1905, was born May 29, 1857, in Frostburg, Allegany Co., Maryland, a son of William Bell and Ann Wheelden (or Whedon). He was baptized in 1880 in Derbyshire, England, having gone with his parents to England in 1875. He presided over the Tupton Branch, returned to America in 1885, and after presiding over the Almy Branch, Wyoming, he was ordained a High Priest by John M. Baxter and a Bishop Aug. 6, 1905, by Rudger Clawson. He died Jan. 6, 1935.
