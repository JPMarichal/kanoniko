Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pettigrew, William Helaman

Pettigrew, William Helaman, Bishop of the Nephi First Ward, Juab Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1912, was born March 5, 1860, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of David Pettigrew and Caroline Cope. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1890-1892, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 22, 1901, by George Teasdale.
