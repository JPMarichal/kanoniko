Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brown, George Washington

Brown, George Washington, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born Jan. 25, 1827, in Newbury, Cuyahoga Co., Ohio, a son of Nathaniel Brown and Avis Hill. After arriving in Great Salt Lake Valley, he returned to Winter Quarters with Pres. Brigham Young and came again to Utah with his mother, a brother and a sister, in 1850. Later he made his home at Charlesten, and was ordained a High Priest by Pres. John Taylor and set apart as an officer in the Wasatch Stake of Zion. He died at Charleston Dec. 20, 1906, survived by a large family. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 608.)
