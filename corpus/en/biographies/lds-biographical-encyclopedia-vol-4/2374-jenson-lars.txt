Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jenson, Lars

Jenson, Lars, Bishop of the Scipio Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, in 1929, was born June 15, 1873, in Norhede, Denmark, a son of Jens C. Skjelsgaard and Anna Christine Pleidrup. He was baptized April 14, 1902, ordained a High Priest Oct. 30, 1908, by Anthony W. Ivins, and was ordained a Bishop Dee. 2, 1928, by Orson F. Whitney.
