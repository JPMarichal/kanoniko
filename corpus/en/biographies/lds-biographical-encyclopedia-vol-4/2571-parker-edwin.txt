Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Parker, Edwin

Parker, Edwin, Bishop of the South Hooper (West Point) Ward, North Davis Stake, Utah, from 1883 to 1893, was born May 1, 1843, in Preston, Hampshire, England, the son of William Parker and Maria Blake. He was baptized April 15, 1855, came to the Valley in 1861, and was ordained a Bishop Dec. 29, 1883, by Geo. Q. Cannon. He died at Hooper Oct. 18, 1935.
