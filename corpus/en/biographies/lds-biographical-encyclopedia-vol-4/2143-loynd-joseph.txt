Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Loynd, Joseph

Loynd, Joseph, Bishop of the Springville 4th Ward, Kolob Stake, Utah, from 1892 to 1910, was born Dec. 19, 1843, in Hyde, Cheshire, England, a son of James Loynd and Elizabeth Thompson. He was baptized May 1, 1851, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 17, 1892, by George Q. Cannon.
