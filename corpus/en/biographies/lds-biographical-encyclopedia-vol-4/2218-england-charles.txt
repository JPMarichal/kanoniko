Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

England, Charles

England, Charles, Bishop of the Logan 2nd Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1930+, was born Oct. 6, 1863, in Tooele, Utah, a son of Wm. England and Eliza Seamons. He was baptized July 7, 1872, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 6, 1887, by Lorenzo Snow, and filled two missions, one to England in 1889-1891, and one to the Southwestern States in 1891-1893. He was ordained a Bishop Oct. 14, 1917, by Stephen L. Richards.
