Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ricks, Alfred

Ricks, Alfred, Bishop of the Sugar Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1905 to 1927, was born Nov. 28, 1869, in Logan, Utah, the son of Thomas E. Ricks and Ellen M. Yallop. He was ordained a High Priest March 23, 1900, by John Henry Smith, and a Bishop June 30, 1905. He died Oct. 24, 1927, in Sugar City, Idaho.
