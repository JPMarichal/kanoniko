Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Vorkink, William

Vorkink, William, Bishop of the Glendale Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1928 to 1930, was born Feb. 12, 1890, at Dordrecht, Holland, the son of Hendrik G. Vorkink and Jobanna B. Gobel. He was baptized July 19, 1901, ordained a High Priest June 27, 1919, by Charles W., Penrose, and a Bishop July 15, 1928, by Richard R. Lyman.
