Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shepherd, Vincent

Shepherd, Vincent, Bishop of the Kamas Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1924, was born Oct. 20, 1866, in Radcliff, Northumberland, England, a son of Geo. Carr Shepherd and Mary Wood. He was baptized April 23, 1909, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1915-1917, and was chosen as Bishop in 1920.
