Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dillman, Ray E.

Dillman, Ray E., Bishop of the Roosevelt Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born April 22, 1890, in Vernal, Utah, a son of Simon Peter Dillman and Julia Ellen Davis. He was baptized June 4, 1898, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1918-1920, was ordained a High Priest by Richard R. Lyman, and ordained a Bishop May 5, 1929, by George F. Richards.
