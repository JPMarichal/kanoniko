Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Claridge, David Harmon

Claridge, David Harmon, Bishop of the Bryce Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Ariz., from 1902 to 1909, was born June 30, 1873, in Mt. Carmel, Utah, the son of Samuel Claridge and Rebecca Hughes. He was baptized in 1881, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 16, 1902, by Hyrum M. Smith.
