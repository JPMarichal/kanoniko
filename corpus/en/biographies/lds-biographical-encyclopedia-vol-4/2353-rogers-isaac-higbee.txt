Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rogers, Isaac Higbee

Rogers, Isaac Higbee, Bishop of the Lehi Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1914 to 1919, was born Nov. 27, 1875, in Provo, Utah, a son of Henry C. Rogers and Emma Higbee. He was baptized Dec. 6, 1883, and sustained as Bishop Nov. 22, 1914.
