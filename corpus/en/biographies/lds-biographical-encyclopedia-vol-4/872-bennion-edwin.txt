Bennion, Edwin

Bennion, Edwin, president of the Netherlands Mission from 1893 to 1895, was born April 8, 1868, at Taylorsville, Utah, a son of John Bennion and Mary Turpin. He was baptized in May, 1876, ordained a Seventy Oct. 14, 1892, by Seymour B. Young, and filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1892-1895, being set apart, together with his wife, Mary Emma Lindsay Bennion, Oct. 14, 1892. On May 21, 1893, he succeeded Alfred L. Farrell as president of the mission. He was succeeded by Asa W. Judd.

From 1900 to 1903 he served as second counselor, and from 1903 to 1911 as first counselor in the presidency of the Granite Stake, Utah. He died at Draper, Utah, April 3, 1930.
