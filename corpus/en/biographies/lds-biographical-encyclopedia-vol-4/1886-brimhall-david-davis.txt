Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brimhall, David Davis

Brimhall, David Davis, Bishop of the Emerson Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930, was born May 23, 1886, in Spanish Fork, Utah, the son of Emer Myer Brimhall and Angeline Davis. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1896, ordained an Elder Nov. 5, 1904, by James Anderson, filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1907-1910, and was ordained a Bishop Oct. 18, 1926, by Geo. Albert Smith.
