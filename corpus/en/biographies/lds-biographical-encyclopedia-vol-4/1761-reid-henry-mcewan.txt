Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Reid, Henry Mcewan

Reid, Henry Mcewan, Bishop of the Orangeville Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1921, was born Dec. 18, 1852, in Edinburgh, Scotland, the son of William D. Reid and Jane McEwan. He came to Utah at an early age and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 14, 1906, by Hyrum M. Smith. He died Feb. 10, 1926.
