Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bigler, James

Bigler, James, Bishop of the Davisville Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1919, was born March 24, 1866, in Nephi, Juab Co., Utah, a son of Jacob G. Bigler and Amy Loretta Chase. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained an Elder, Seventy and High Priest successively, and ordained ,a Bishop Feb. 23, 1917, by Hyrum M. Smith.
