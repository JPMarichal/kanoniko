Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Turner, Amos Howarth

Turner, Amos Howarth, Bishop of the South Cottonwood Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1929, was born Oct. 23, 1876, in South Cottonwood, the son of William K. Turner and Jane Howarth. He was baptized July 12, 1885, ordained a Seventy Sept. 5, 1900, by J. Golden Kimball, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1900-1902. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 20, 1927, by Stephen L. Richards.
