Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clegg, Robert Williams

Clegg, Robert Williams, Bishop of the Bench Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, since 1922, was born June 28, 1892, at Perry, Oneida (now Bannock) County, Idaho, the son of Benjamin Clegg and Anna J. Williams. He was baptized in 1900, ordained a High Priest May 18, 1918, by Orson F. Whitney, and ordained a Bishop May 17, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
