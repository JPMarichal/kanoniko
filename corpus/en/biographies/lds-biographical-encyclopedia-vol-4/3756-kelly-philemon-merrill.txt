Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kelly, Philemon Merrill

Kelly, Philemon Merrill, Bishop of the St. Anthony 1st Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Feb. 17, 1877, at American Fork, Utah, a son of William Kelly and Christine Peterson. He was baptized June 14, 1885, ordained a Seventy Oct. 19, 1900, by J. Golden Kimball, filled a mission to Germany in 1900-1903, and to California in 1927-1929. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 1, 1928, by Richard R. Lyman.
