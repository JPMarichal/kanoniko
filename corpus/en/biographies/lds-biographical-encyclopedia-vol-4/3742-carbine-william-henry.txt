Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carbine, William Henry

Carbine, William Henry, Bishop of the St. Anthony Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1901 to 1906, was born March 29, 1871, at Clarkston, Cache Co., Utah, a son of William V. Carbine and Sarah Jane Miller. He was baptized March 30, 1879, ordained a Seventy Oct. 7, 1897, by Elias S. Kimball, filled a mission to Montana in 1897, was ordained a High Priest March 26, 1900, by John Henry Smith, and ordained a Bishop July 22, 1901, by Rudger Clawson.
