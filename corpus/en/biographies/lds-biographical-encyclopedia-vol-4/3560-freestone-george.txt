Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Freestone, George

Freestone, George, Bishop of the Ashley Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1887 to 1898, was born Aug. 13, 1837, on Prince Edward's Island, Canada, the son of Thomas Freestone and Ann Fall. He was baptized in 1854, and was ordained a Bishop May 9, 1887, by John Henry Smith. He died Aug. 26, 1920.
