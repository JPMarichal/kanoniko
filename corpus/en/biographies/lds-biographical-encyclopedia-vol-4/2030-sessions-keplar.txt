Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sessions, Keplar

Sessions, Keplar, first counselor in the Idaho Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1916 to 1930, was born Dec. 3, 1855, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of Perregrine Sessions and Lucina Call. He was baptized in 1863, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1892-1894, and was set apart as first counselor Nov. 19, 1916, by Reed Smoot.
