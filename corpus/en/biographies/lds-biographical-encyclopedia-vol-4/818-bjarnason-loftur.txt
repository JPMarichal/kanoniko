Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bjarnason, Loftur

Bjarnason, Loftur, president of the Icelandic Mission from 1903 to 1906, was born March 15, 1879, in Spanish Fork, Utah Co., Utah, the son of Gisle E. Bjarnason and Haldora Jonson. He was baptized when about eight years of age and later emigrated to Utah. Called to fill a mission to Iceland, he arrived in Reykjavik April 21, 1903, and labored diligently and with some success part of the time without a missionary companion. On Elder Jon Johanneson's departure for home, Elder Bjarnason succeeded him as president of the mission, being appointed to that position, June 16, 1903. Bro. Bjarnason was a faithful Elder, but had to seek employment in a store on Vestmanoen in order to meet expenses. He baptized eight persons and left for home in 1906.
