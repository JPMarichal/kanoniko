Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harbertson, James Moffat

Harbertson, James Moffat, Bishop of the Ogden 14th Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born March 23, 1892, in South Weber, Davis Co., Utah, a son of John William Harbertson and Mary Moffat. He was baptized July 1, 1900, ordained a High Priest April 27, 1919, by David O. McKay, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 28, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
