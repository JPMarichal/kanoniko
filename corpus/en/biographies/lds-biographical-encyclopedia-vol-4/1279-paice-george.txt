Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Paice, George

Paice, George, Bishop of the Beaver West Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1925, was born Jan. 13, 1870, at Lasham, Hampshire, England, the son of George Paice and Louise Weston. He emigrated to America in 1888 and located temporarily in Wisconsin, came to Utah in 1890 and located in Beaver. Becoming a convert to "Mormonism," he was baptized Aug. 11, 1890, filled a mission to England in 1897-1899, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 22, 1905, by Francis M. Lyman, and ordained a Bishop April 20, 1919, by David O. McKay.
