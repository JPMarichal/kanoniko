Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Low, Thomas Scott Budge

Low, Thomas Scott Budge, Bishop of the Kimball Ward, Alberta Stake. Canada, from 1918 to 1923, was born Jan. 26, 1871, in Providence, Cache Co., Utah, the son of William Walker Low and Helen Budge. He was baptized in May, 1879, and located in Kimball Ward in 1902; was ordained a High Priest by Pres. Joseph F. Smith.
