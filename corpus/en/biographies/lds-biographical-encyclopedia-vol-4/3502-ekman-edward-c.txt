Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ekman, Edward C.

Ekman, Edward C., Bishop of the Batesville Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1912, was born Jan. 14, 1867, in Drammen, Norway, a son of Johan F. Ekman and Eliza Scow. He was baptized Nov. 20, 1877, filled a mission to Norway in 1905-1908, was ordained a High Priest April 2, 1899, by Hugh S. Gowans, and a Bishop June 28, 1908, by Francis M. Lyman.
