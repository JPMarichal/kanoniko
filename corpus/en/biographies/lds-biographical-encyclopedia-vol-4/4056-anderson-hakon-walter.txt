Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Anderson, Hakon Walter

Anderson, Hakon Walter, an Elder who died while filling a mission, was born May 31, 1898, in Pima, Graham Co., Arizona, a son of Hakon J. Anderson and Sarah E. Anderson. He was baptized June 1, 1906, by Ray M. Hanchett, and later ordained an Elder. While still a resident of Pima, Graham Co., Arizona, he was called on a mission to the Northern States. He accepted the call and was set apart July 9, 1918. A short time after arriving in his field of labor he was stricken with pneumonia following an attack of influenza, from which he died Oct. 31, 1918, in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. From the time he arrived in the field, he magnified his calling to the entire satisfaction of those placed over him, rendering able and valiant service in the cause of the Master. He was an inspiration to all who knew him, and was loved and respected for his agreeable disposition,his clean and sincere manhood, and his love for right and righteousness of life. His sister Nora, also a missionary of exceptional ability, was at his bedside at the time of his death, and accompanied the body of her brother to their home in the West.
