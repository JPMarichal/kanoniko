Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wall, Lawrence Constantine

Wall, Lawrence Constantine, Bishop of the Randlett Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1928, was born Aug. 20, 1885, in Big Cottonwood, Utah, a son of Joseph J. Wall and Hedvig T. Petersen. He was baptized Aug. 20, 1893, ordained a High Priest Dec. 18, 1920, by Ephraim Lambert, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 18, 1921, by Stephen L. Richards.
