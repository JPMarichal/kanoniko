Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Petersen, Wilford Christian

Petersen, Wilford Christian, Bishop of the Ferron Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1927, was born Nov. 2, 1889, in Ferron, the son of Christian Petersen and Margrethe Lorentzen. He was baptized July 10, 1898, ordained an Elder May 3, 1909, by Newel K. Beal, and filled a mission to Samoa in 1909-1912. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 9, 1914, by Heber J. Grant, and a Bishop Nov. 5, 1917, by Stephen L. Richards.
