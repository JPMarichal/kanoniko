Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Woodruff, Helen Winters

Woodruff, Helen Winters, a member of the General Board of the Y. W, M. I. A., was born Sept. 24, 1873, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, a daughter of Oscar and Mary Ann Winters, and is a sister of Augusta W. Grant. She graduated from the B. Y. University, taught school in Salina and Coalville, Utah, and later studied at the University of Utah. She was married to Abraham Owen Woodruff in 1897 in the Salt Lake Temple, and was called to the General Board in 1898. She was the mother of four children. In 1904 she accompanied her husband (Apostle Woodruff) to Mexico, where, in June, in the City of Mexico, she died of smallpox. Her husband died a month later in El Paso, Texas, of the same disease, as he was returning to the United States with his children.
