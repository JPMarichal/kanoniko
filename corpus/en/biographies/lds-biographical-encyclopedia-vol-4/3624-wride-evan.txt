Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wride, Evan

Wride, Evan, Bishop of the Provo 2nd Ward, Utah Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1889 to 1898, was born Feb. 17, 1843, in Lanishen Parish, Glamorganshire, South Wales, a son of Thomas Wride and Ann James. He was baptized in 1868, emigrated to Utah that same year, filled a mission to Wales in 1879-1880, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 28, 1880, and a Bishop June 2, 1889, by Franklin D. Richards. He died Aug. 2, 1920.
