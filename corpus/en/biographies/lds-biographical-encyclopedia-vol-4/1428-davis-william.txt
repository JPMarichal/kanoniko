Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davis, William

Davis, William, Bishop of the Brigham City Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1851 to 1855, was born Sept. 12, 1795, in Union Township, Westmoreland Co., Pennsylvania. He was baptized by Simeon Carter in December, 1832, came to Utah in 1848, and settled in Brigham City in 1851, acted as presiding Elder of Brigham City in 1851, and was ordained a Bishop, and set apart to preside over the Box Elder settlement April 30, 1852. He died Nov. 22, 1883, in Brigham City.
