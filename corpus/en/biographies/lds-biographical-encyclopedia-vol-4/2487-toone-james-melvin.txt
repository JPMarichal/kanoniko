Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Toone, James Melvin

Toone, James Melvin, Bishop of the Croydon Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1926, was born Sept. 13, 1888, in Croydon, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of William H. Toone and Hannah Webb. He was baptized when eight years of age, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1913-1916, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 9, 1917, by David O. McKay.
