Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jameson, Charles

Jameson, Charles, a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company E, was born April 9, 1803, at Little York, Pennsylvania, a son of David Jameson and Magdaline Miller. He was baptized April 6, 1834, by David Evans, in Ohio, was in the Haun's Mill Massacre and was badly wounded, marched to California with the Battalion, and came to the Valley in 1847. He came from Iowa and settled in Provo in 1850; moved to Minersville in 1871, where he resided until his death Sept. 20, 1886, leaving one wife and seven children.
