Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hatch, Fred Stevens

Hatch, Fred Stevens, second counselor in the Los Angeles Stake presidency, California, from 1927 to 1928, was born Dec. 24, 1869, in Little Genesee, N.Y., the son of Geo. W. Hatch and Livera A. Hatch. He was baptized in June, 1920, and set apart as second counselor May 22, 1927.
