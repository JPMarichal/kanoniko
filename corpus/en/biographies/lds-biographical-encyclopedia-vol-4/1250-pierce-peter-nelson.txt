Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pierce, Peter Nelson

Pierce, Peter Nelson, Bishop of the Penrose Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1917, was born April 7, 1865, at Billingsby, Sweden, the son of Benjamin Pierce and Hannah Nelson. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 6, 1911, by Heber J. Grant.
