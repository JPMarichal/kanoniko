Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rasmussen, Raymond Roscoe

Rasmussen, Raymond Roscoe, Bishop of the Murray 2nd Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born July 2, 1894, at Membres, New Mexico, the son of Joseph E. Rasmussen and Sarah Poulsen. He was ordained a High Priest Sept. 4, 1927, by Geo. Albert Smith, and a Bishop Feb. 20, 1928, by Orson F. Whitney.
