Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Egbert, Joseph S.

Egbert, Joseph S., one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born March 10, 1818, in Sullivan County, Indiana, a son of John Egbert and Susan Hahn. As a member of Pres. Brigham Young's famous company of pioneers in 1847, he was also one of Orson Pratt's advance company, and entered Salt Lake Valley on July 22. He returned to Winter Quarters with Pres. Brigham Young later the same year and came back with his family in 1849. He made his home in Kaysville, Davis Co., Utah, where he established a hotel and acted in several civic offices. On several occasions he returned to the Missouri River with Church teams to assist emigrants across the plains. He died May 24, 1898, at Ogden, Utah. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 70.)
