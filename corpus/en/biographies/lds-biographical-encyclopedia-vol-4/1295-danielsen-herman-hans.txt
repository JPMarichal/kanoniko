Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Danielsen, Herman Hans

Danielsen, Herman Hans, Bishop of the Lewiston 2nd Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1907 to 1921, was born Aug. 3, 1862, in Bogelund, Soro amt, Denmark, the son of Daniel Rasmussen and Marie Hendriksen. He emigrated to Utah in 1878, was baptized Oct. 19, 1878, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1903-1905, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 14, 1905, by Alma Merrill, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 1, 1907, by Heber J. Grant.
