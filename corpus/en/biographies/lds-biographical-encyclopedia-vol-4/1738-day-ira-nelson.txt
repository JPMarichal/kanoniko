Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Day, Ira Nelson

Day, Ira Nelson, Bishop of the Lawrence Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1921, was born Nov. 11, 1878, in Mt. Pleasant, Utah, the son of Ira A. Day and Polly Ann Noakes. He was baptized March 6, 1887, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 12, 1917, by Hyrum M. Smith.
