Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johns, Walter Mathias

Johns, Walter Mathias, Bishop of the Naf Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1913 to 1915, and Bishop of the Almo Ward, Raft River Stake, from 1918 to 1921, was born April 1, 1875, in North Ogden, Utah, a son of Jonathan Johns and Elizabeth Jane Bishop. He was baptized when about eight years of age, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 2, 1913, by Francis M. Lyman.
