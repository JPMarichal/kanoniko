Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcarthur, Wilford Woodruff

Mcarthur, Wilford Woodruff, Bishop of the St. George West Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930, was born April 30, 1884, in St. George, a son of Daniel D. McArthur and Mary F. Calloway. He was baptized May 10, 1892, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 20, 1925, by Rudger Clawson.
