Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gwilliam, Henry Beynon

Gwilliam, Henry Beynon, Bishop of the West Point (South Hooper) Ward, North Davis' Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1883, was born May 7, 1835, in Shrewbury, England. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 24, 1877, by Franklin D. Richards, and died Sept. 22, 1907, in Hooper, Utah.
