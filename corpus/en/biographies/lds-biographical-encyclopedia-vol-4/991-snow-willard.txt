Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Snow, Willard

Snow, Willard, president of the Scandinavian Mission from 1852 to 1853, was born May 6, 1811, in St. Johnsbury, Caledonia Co., Vermont, the son of Levi and Lucina Snow, and a brother of Erastus Snow. He was baptized June 18, 1833, by Orson Pratt, and moved to Kirtland, Ohio, in 1834, was a member of Zion's Camp in 1834, was ordained a Seventy Feb. 28, 1835, and performed several missions to different parts of the United States.

He arrived in the Valley in 1847, and was called on a mission to Europe in 1851. While laboring as a missionary in England, he was called to preside over the Scandinavian Mission in 1852. He died on the North Sea en route from Denmark to England Aug. 21, 1853.
