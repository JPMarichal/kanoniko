Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jorgensen, George Edgar

Jorgensen, George Edgar, Bishop of the Price Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Aug. 26, 1884, in Burrville, Utah, the son of John Smith and Lena Winklet. He was baptized Sept. 11, 1898, ordained an Elder June 12, 1908, and filled a mission to Germany in 1908-1911. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 10, 1912, and a Bishop June 22, 1930, by Stephen L. Richards.
