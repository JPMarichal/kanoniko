Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beckstead, William Warren

Beckstead, William Warren, Bishop of the American Falls Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1917, was born March 26, 1871, in Riverton, Utah, a son of G. M. Beckstead and Elinor Davis. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 11, 1909, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop May 16, 1915, by Francis M. Lyman. In 1894-1896 he filled a mission to the Southern States.
