Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lamph, David Daniel

Lamph, David Daniel, Bishop of Burley 2nd Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1929, was born April 27, 1892, in Cleveland, Utah, the son of William T. Lamph and Jennie C. Bennett. He was ordained a Bishop Sept. 1, 1929.
