Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

McKay, James Gunn

McKay, James Gunn, a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A. from 1922 to 1935, was born April 9, 1881, in Huntsville, Utah, the son of Angus McKay and Williamena McKay. He was baptized April 29, 1889, ordained a Seventy, Sept. 7, 1906, by Rulon S. Wells, and filled a mission to South Africa in 1906-1909.
