Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Romney, George Samuel

Romney, George Samuel, president of the Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+ was born Nov. 12, 1874, in St. George, Utah, the son of Miles Park Romney and Hannah Hood Hill. He was baptized in 1882, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1897-1900, was ordained a High Priest by Anthony W. Ivins, and set apart as president of the Fremont Stake Sept. 27, 1925, by Melvin J. Ballard. He died Dec. 19, 1935, in Rockford, Ill.
