Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ellertson, Ephraim

Ellertson, Ephraim, Bishop of the Mona Ward, Juab Stake, Utah, from 1904 to 1910, was born Jan. 20, 1860, in Spanish Fork, Utah Co., Utah, a son of John Ellertson and Christine G. Lund. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1893-1894 (released early on account of illness) and was ordained a Bishop Oct. 30, 1904, by Reed Smoot.
