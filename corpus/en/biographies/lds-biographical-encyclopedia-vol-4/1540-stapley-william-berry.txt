Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stapley, William Berry

Stapley, William Berry, Bishop of the Castle Gate Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Feb. 3, 1889, in Kanarra, Utah, the son of James L. Stapley and Harriet Berry. He was baptized June 6, 1897, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1913-1916, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 25, 1921, by James E. Talmage, and ordained a Bishop June 22, 1924, by Richard R. Lyman.
