Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bryan, William Henry

Bryan, William Henry, Bishop of the Batesville Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1924, was born April 10, 1876, in Batesville, Tooele Co., Utah, a son of George Woodward Bryan and Margaret Simpson. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1885, ordained an Elder May 25, 1899, by Edward C. Ekman, filled a mission to Australia in 1897-1900, was ordained a High Priest July 24, 1904, by Francis M. Lyman and a Bishop Oct. 19, 1919, by Anthony W. Ivins.
