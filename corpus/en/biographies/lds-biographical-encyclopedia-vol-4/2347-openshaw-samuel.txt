Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Openshaw, Samuel

Openshaw, Samuel, Bishop of the Nephi Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1888 to 1900, was born in Brightmet, Lancashire, England, a son of William Openshaw and Ann Greenhalgh. He was baptized April 24, 1847, ordained a High Priest May 28, 1876, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 23, 1888, by John Henry Smith.
