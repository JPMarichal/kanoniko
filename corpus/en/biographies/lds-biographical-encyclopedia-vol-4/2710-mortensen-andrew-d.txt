Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mortensen, Andrew D.

Mortensen, Andrew D., Bishop of the Glendale Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1905 to 1917, was born July 15, 1874, in Spring City, Utah, a son of Andrew Mortensen and Annie Catherine Hansen. He was baptized Sept. 7, 1882, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 17, 1906, by Francis M. Lyman.
