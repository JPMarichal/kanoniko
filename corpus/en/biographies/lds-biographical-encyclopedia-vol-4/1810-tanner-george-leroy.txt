Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tanner, George Leroy

Tanner, George Leroy, Bishop of the Whitney Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Nov. 30, 1891, in North Powder, Oregon, the son of William Tanner and Lillias Speirs. He was baptized Nov. 30, 1899, ordained an Elder June 20, 1915, by Geo. T. Benson, jun., and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1915-1917. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 3, 1922, by Hyrnm G. Smith, and a Bishop March 10, 1929, by David O. McKay.
