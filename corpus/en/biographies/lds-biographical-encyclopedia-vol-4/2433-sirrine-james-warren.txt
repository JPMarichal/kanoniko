Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sirrine, James Warren

Sirrine, James Warren, Bishop of the Dingle Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1929, was born Aug. 24, 1888, in Dingle, Idaho, the son of Geo. Edward Sirrine and Sarah Oakey. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to England in 1909-1911, and acted as presiding Elder of the Cokeville Branch, Wyoming, in 1912-1913. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 14, 1919, by David 0. McKay.
