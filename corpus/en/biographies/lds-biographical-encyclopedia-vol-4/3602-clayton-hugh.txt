Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clayton, Hugh

Clayton, Hugh, Bishop of the Provo 6th Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1902 to 1903, was born Sept. 15, 1871, in Preston, Lancashire, England, a son of William Clayton and Alice Affleck. He was baptized March 22, 1882, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 30, 1902, by Rudger Clawson.
