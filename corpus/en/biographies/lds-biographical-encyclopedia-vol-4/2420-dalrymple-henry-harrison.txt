Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dalrymple, Henry Harrison

Dalrymple, Henry Harrison, Bishop of the Preston Ward (now Wardboro), Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1877 to 1889, was born Nov. 24, 1824, in Bolton, Warren Co., New York, a son of Henry Harrison and Maria C. Dalrymple. He came to Utah in 1859, and was ordained a Bishop in August, 1877.
