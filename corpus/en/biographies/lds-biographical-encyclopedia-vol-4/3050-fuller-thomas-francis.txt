Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fuller, Thomas Francis

Fuller, Thomas Francis, Bishop of the Eden Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1923 to 1929, was born May 16, 1889, in Eden, a son of Thomas A. Fuller and Annie M. Colvin. He was baptized March 5, 1898, and ordained a Bishop May 24, 1923, by James E. Talmage.
