Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brown, James, jun.

Brown, James, jun., first counselor in the Woodruff Stake presidency, Wyoming, from 1928 to 1930+, was born July 15, 1883, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of James Brown and Christina Hunter. He was baptized July 15, 1891, ordained a High Priest July 20, 1924, by Hyrum G. Smith, and set apart as first counselor in the Woodruff Stake presidency July 1, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
