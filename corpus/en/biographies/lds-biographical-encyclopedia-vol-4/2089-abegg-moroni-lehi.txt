Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Juarez Stake

Abegg, Moroni Lehi

Abegg, Moroni Lehi, second counselor in the Juarez Stake presidency, Mexico, from 1929 to 1930+, was born April 14, 1891, in Payson, Utah, a son of Arnold Abegg and Louisa Mort Haag. He was ordained a High Priest in 1916, by Anthony W. Ivins, and was set apart as second counselor in the Juarez Stake presidency Sept. 8, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
