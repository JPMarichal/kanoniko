Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mendenhall, James M.

Mendenhall, James M., Bishop of the Hazelton Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1928, was born Aug. 16, 1883, in Dayton, Idaho, a son of George Madison Mendenhall and Celestia Mecham. He was baptized Aug. 16, 1891, ordained to the Priesthood, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1904 (receiving an early release on account of sickness), was ordained a High Priest Sept. 3, 1916, by James E. Talmage, and ordained a Bishop June 6, 1926, by George Albert Smith.
