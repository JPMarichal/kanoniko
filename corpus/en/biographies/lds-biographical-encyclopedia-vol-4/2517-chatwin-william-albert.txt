Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Chatwin, William Albert

Chatwin, William Albert, Bishop of the Santaquin 2nd Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1929, was born Sept. 3, 1875, in Santaquin, the son of William Chatwin and Irena Carter. He was baptized Nov. 11, 1883, ordained a High Priest March 15, 1903, by Isaac H. Grace, and a Bishop Dec. 28, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
