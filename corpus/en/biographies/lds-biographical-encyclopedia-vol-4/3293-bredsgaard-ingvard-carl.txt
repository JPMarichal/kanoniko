Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bredsgaard, Ingvard Carl

Bredsgaard, Ingvard Carl, Bishop of the Monroe South Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1927, was born Jan. 15, 1874, in Aarhus, Denmark, a son of Jacob Bredsgaard and Anna Nielsen. He was baptized in 1889, emigrated to Utah in 1891, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 20, 1922, by David O. McKay, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 24, 1926, by the same Apostle.
