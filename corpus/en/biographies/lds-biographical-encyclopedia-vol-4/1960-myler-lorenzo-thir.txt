Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Myler, Lorenzo, thir

Myler, Lorenzo, third Bishop of the Inglewood Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1927 to 1930 +, was born Sept. 9, 1897, at Diamond, Juab Co., Utah, the son of John Alfred Keyte and Louise M. Rose. He was baptized Jan. 30, 1908, and ordained a Deacon Nov. 13, 1911, by B. F. Tanner; later he was ordained a High Priest and acted as second counselor to Bishop James McCardell of the Inglewood Ward.
