Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Layton, Jesse Monroe

Layton, Jesse Monroe, Bishop of the Solomonville Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1920 to 1922, was born Dec. 27, 1884, in Eden, Arizona, the son of Christopher Layton and Hannah M. S. Sims. He was baptized in 1893, ordained an Elder by Andrew Kimball, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1904-1907, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 21, 1921, by Geo. Albert Smith.
