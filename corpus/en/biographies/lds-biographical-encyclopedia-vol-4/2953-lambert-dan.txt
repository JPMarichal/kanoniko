Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lambert, Dan

Lambert, Dan, Bishop of the Roosevelt W a r d, Roosevelt S t a k e, Utah, from 1908 to 1911, was born March 2, 1861, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of John Lambert and Elina H. Larsen. He was baptized Sept. 5, 1878, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1893-1895, and was sustained a Bishop Aug. 20, 1908.
