Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dunn, Samuel A.

Dunn, Samuel A., Bishop of the North Pocatello Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born March 12, 1898, in College Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, a son of Charles O. Dunn and Letitia Smith. He was baptized March 26, 1906, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 25, 1928, by Richard R. Lyman.
