Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brown, Lorenzo John

Brown, Lorenzo John, Bishop of the Nutriosa Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1889 to 1896, was born May 20, 1854, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Lorenzo Brown and Frances Crosby. He was baptized when eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 8, 1890, by John Henry Smith. He died Dec. 22, 1935.
