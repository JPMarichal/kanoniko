Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Worsencroft, William Lorenzo

Worsencroft, William Lorenzo, president of the Samoan Mission in 1899, was born Oct. 11, 1869, in Payson, Utah, a son of Samuel Worsencroft and Jane Hardy. He was baptized Dec. 17, 1877, set apart for a mission to Samoa Dec. 5, 1896; was appointed president of the Mission in January, 1899, and was released Jan. 26, 1900.
