Eardley, Roscoe W.

Eardley, Roscoe W., Bishop of the Salt Lake City 3rd Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1911 and from 1920 to 1924, was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 25, 1906, by Pres. Jos. F. Smith. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 4, p. 235.)
