Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Miller, James Nathan

Miller, James Nathan, Bishop of the Spring Glen Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1905, was born March 3, 1870, in Coalville, Utah, the son of William Miller and Margaret Neibaur. He was ordained a Bishop July 5, 1900, by George Teasdale.
