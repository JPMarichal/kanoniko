Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomas, Emanuel

Thomas, Emanuel, Bishop of the Byron Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1913 to 1919, was born Nov. 25, 1866, at Provo, Utah, the son of Emanuel Thomas and Mary Ellis. He was baptized by Philip Thomas, and ordained .a Bishop Feb. 23, 1913, by Geo. F. Richards.
