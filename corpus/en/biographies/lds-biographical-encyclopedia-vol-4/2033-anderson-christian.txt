Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Idaho Falls Stake

Anderson, Christian

Anderson, Christian, Bishop of the Ammon Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1899 to 1913, was born Dec. 11, 1863, in Mantua, Utah, a son of Thomas C. Anderson and Sidse Nielsen. He was baptized Dec. 21, 1877, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 25, 1899, by John Henry Smith.
