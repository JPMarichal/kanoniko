Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Isaac

Hansen, Isaac, Bishop of the Benjamin Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1904, was born April 5, 1862, in Spanish Fork, Utah, the son of Jens Hansen and Karen Petersen. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 18, 1900.
