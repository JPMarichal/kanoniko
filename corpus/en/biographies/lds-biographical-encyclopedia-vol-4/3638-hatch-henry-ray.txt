Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hatch, Henry Ray

Hatch, Henry Ray, second counselor in the presidency of the Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1917 to 1919, was born April 20, 1886, at Ashley, Uintah Co., Utah, a son of Abram C. Hatch and Maria H. Luke. He was baptized Oct. 28, 1909, ordained a High Priest Oct. 24, 1915, by Heber J. Grant, and set apart as second counselor in the stake presidency Sept. 27, 1917, by Heber J. Grant.
