Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Simper, Ernest Daniel

Simper, Ernest Daniel, Bishop of the Moulton Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Oct. 6, 1874, in Murray, Utah, the son of Daniel Simper and Mary Ann Panter. He was baptized July 24, 1881, ordained a High Priest April 23, 1916, by John. A. Elison, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 30, 1921, by George F. Richards.
