Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ogden Stake

Belnap, Arias Guy

Belnap, Arias Guy, Bishop of the Ogden 20th Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Sept. 6, 1893, in Hooper, Utah, a son of Hyrum Belnap and Anna C. Bluth. He was baptized in 1901, ordained an Elder in 1913, filled a mission to the Swiss-German Mission in 1913-1916, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 27, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
