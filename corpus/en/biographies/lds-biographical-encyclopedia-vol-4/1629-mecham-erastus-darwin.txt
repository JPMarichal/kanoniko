Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mecham, Erastus Darwin

Mecham, Erastus Darwin, Bishop of the Park Valley Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1879 to 1892, was born July 20, 1826, in Hopkinton, St. Lawrence Co., New York. He was baptized in January, 1838, served as a member of the Mormon Battalion, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 17, 1879.
