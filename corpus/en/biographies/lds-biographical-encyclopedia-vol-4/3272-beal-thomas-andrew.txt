Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beal, Thomas Andrew

Beal, Thomas Andrew, Bishop of the Ephraim North Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1912 to 1914, was born July 20, 1874, in Ephraim, Utah, a son of Henry Beal and Christine Bjerggaard. He was baptized in 1882, ordained a High Priest Dec. 31, 1911, by Lewis Anderson, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 3, 1912, by Anthon H. Lund.
