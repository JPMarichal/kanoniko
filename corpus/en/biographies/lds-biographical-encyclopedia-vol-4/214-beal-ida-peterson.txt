Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beal, Ida Peterson

Beal, Ida Peterson, member of the General Board of Relief Society since Aug. 17, 1927, and also chairman of the Music Committee of that organization, was born Feb. 7, 1876, at Ephraim, Utah, a daughter of Canute Peterson and Charlotte Eckstrom. She was educated at the Sanpete Stake Academy (now Snow College) and at the B. Y. U. and studied music under Prof. A. C. Lund. On June 19, 1901, she was married in the Manti Temple to Thomas A. Beal of the University of Utah, and in 1925-1926, spent ten months in Europe with him studying and traveling. Music and gardening are her hobbies.
