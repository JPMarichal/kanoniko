Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Quist, Joseph Franklin

Quist, Joseph Franklin, Bishop of the Holladay Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1927, was born Dec. 13, 1882, in Big Cottonwood, Utah, the son of John Anderson Quist and Mary Hendeen. He filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1902-1905, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 17, 1921, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
