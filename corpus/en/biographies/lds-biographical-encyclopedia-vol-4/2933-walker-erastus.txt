Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Walker, Erastus

Walker, Erastus, presiding Elder of the Bybee Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1900 to 1908, was born May 22, 1867, at Deseret, Millard Co., Utah, a son of William Walker and Olive S. Bingham. He was baptized when about eight years old, and appointed presiding Elder of the Bybee Ward some time in 1900.
