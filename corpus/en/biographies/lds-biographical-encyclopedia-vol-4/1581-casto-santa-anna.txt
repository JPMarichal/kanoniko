Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Casto, Santa Anna

Casto, Santa Anna, Bishop of the Big Cottonwood Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1905 to 1911, was born May 7, 1850, in Council Bluffs, Iowa, the son of William Casto and Racheline Cornog. He came to Utah in 1851, and was ordained a High Priest July 15, 1877, by Daniel H. Wells. He died Oct. 18, 1904.
