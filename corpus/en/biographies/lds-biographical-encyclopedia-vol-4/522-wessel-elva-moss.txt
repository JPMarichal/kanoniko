Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wessel, Elva Moss

Wessel, Elva Moss, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born Oct. 3, 1903, in Syracuse, Utah, the daughter of John W. Moss and Martha Burningham. She was baptized in 1911 by Clarence Smedley, received her education in Davis County, the Weber College, and the University of Utah. She filled a mission to California in 1922-1924, and was called to the General Board in April, 1926, as field secretary. After acting in this capacity until 1929, she went to New York to attend the Columbia University. She married William C. Wessel July 8, 1930, which union has been blessed with two children, namely, William C., and Shirley Marlerie. She was released from the General Board in June, 1930, and since her marriage has lived in New York City, where she is at present serving as second counselor of the Y. W. M. I. A. in the New York Stake. She has held positions in the Primary, the Sunday School, the Relief Society and the M. L A. since she was fourteen years old. For the past four years she has been actively affiliated with the National Council of Women of the United States, acting as corresponding secretary. She is at present attending the Columbia University, majoring in speech education, and is teaching camp programing in that institution.
