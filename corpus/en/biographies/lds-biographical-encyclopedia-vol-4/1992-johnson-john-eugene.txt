Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, John Eugene

Johnson, John Eugene, Bishop of the Nibley Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1924 to 1929, was born Feb. 23, 1881, at Hyrum, Utah, the son of John Johnson and Jane Curtis. He was baptized March 7, 1889, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 13, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
