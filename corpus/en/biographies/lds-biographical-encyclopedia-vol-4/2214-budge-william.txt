Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Budge, William

Budge, William, Bishop of the Providence Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1864 to 1870, was born May 1, 1828, in Lanark, Scotland, a son of William Budge and Mary Scott. He was baptized Dec. 31, 1848, labored for ten years as a local missionary in Scotland, emigrated to Utah in 1860, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 10, 1864, by Pres. Brigham Young. He acted as president of the Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1870 to 1906, and died March 18, 1919. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 451.)
