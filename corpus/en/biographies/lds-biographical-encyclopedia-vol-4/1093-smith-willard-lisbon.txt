Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Willard Lisbon

Smith, Willard Lisbon, president of the Tongan Mission from 1917 to 1920, was born Sept. 2, 1891, in Farmington, Utah, a son of Willard Gilbert Smith and Mariah Lamb. He was set apart for a mission to Samoa Jan. 13, 1915, and from Samoa he was sent to preside over the Tongan Mission in 1917; he was released April 9, 1920.
