Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Porter, Orson Merit

Porter, Orson Merit, Bishop of the Otto Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1917 to 1930+, was born July 26, 1869, in Porterville, Utah, the son of Alma Porter and Minerva Deuel. He was baptized July 26, 1877, by his father, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1890-1892, moved to Big Horn in 1901, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 25, 1917, by Stephen L. Richards.
