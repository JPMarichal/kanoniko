Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Old Folks Central Committee

Armstrong, William W.

Armstrong, William W., a member of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1927 to 1930+, was born Sept. 18, 1865, in Darlingten, Wisconsin, a son of William H. Armstrong and Georgia Wright. He died Oct. 23, 1932, in Salt Lake City, Utah. Mr. Armstrong was a successful banker and in full sympathy with the policy of honoring the Old Folks.
