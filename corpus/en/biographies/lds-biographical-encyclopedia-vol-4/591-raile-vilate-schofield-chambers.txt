Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Raile, Vilate Schofield Chambers

Raile, Vilate Schofield Chambers, a member of the general board of the Primary Association from 1913 to 1932, is a daughter of Nephi Y. Schofield and Vilate Ellen Romney. She became the wife of George Chambers, who, after she had borne him two children (Frank George and Robert L. Chambers), was accidentally killed. Some time later Sister Chambers was married to Dr. Henry Raile and became the mother of a daughter (Ramona Raile).
