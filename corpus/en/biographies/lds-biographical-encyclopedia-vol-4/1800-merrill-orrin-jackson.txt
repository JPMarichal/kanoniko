Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merrill, Orrin Jackson

Merrill, Orrin Jackson, Bishop of the Mapleton Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1908 to 1910, was born June 22, 1856, in Cottonwood, Utah, the son of Samuel Bemus Merrill and Elizabeth Runyah. He moved to Cache Valley in 1860, and to Mapleton in 1880, and was ordained a High Priest Dec. 13, 1891, by Geo. C. Parkinson, and a Bishop in 1908.
