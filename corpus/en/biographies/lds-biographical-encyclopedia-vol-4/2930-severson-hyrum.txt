Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Severson, Hyrum

Severson, Hyrum, Bishop of the Garfield Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1908 to 1930+, was born July 2, 1869, at Brinton, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Halvor Syvertsen and Matea Evansdatter. He was baptized in 1898, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 5, 1908, by Francis M. Lyman.
