Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Andersen, Lars Strib

Andersen, Lars Strib, Bishop of the Ephraim North Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1877 to 1889, was born April 16, 1829, at Avlby, Odense amt, Fyen, Denmark, a son of Anders Larsen and Anne Katrine Hansen. He was baptized Feb. 6, 1852, ordained a High Priest March 17, 1867, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1873-1875, was ordained a Bishop July 4, 1877, and filled another mission to Scandinavia in 1887-1889.
