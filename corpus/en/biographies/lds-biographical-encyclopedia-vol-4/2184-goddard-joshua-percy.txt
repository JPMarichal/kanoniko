Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Goddard, Joshua Percy

Goddard, Joshua Percy, Bishop of the 31st Ward, Salt Lake City, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1926, was born Dec. 6, 1879, in Kanosh, Utah, the son of Benjamin Goddard and Martha A. Nield. He was baptized in May, 1888, filled a mission to Germany in 1899-1901, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 7, 1913, by Francis M. Lyman.
