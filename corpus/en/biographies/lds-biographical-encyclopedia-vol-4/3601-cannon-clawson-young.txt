Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cannon, Clawson Young

Cannon, Clawson Young, Bishop of the Provo 5th Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1929, was born Oct. 27, 1885, in Mill Creek, Utah, a son of Geo. Q. Cannon and Caroline Young. He was baptized Oct. 27, 1893, ordained a Seventy by J. Golden Kimball, filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1905-1908, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 24, 1925, by Orson F. Whitney.
