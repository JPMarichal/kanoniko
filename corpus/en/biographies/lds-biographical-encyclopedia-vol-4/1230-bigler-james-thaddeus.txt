Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bigler, James Thaddeus

Bigler, James Thaddeus, Bishop of the Riverside Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1923, was born Jan. 22, 1868, in Farmington, Davis Co., Utah, the son of Adam Biglet and Isabella Miller. He was baptized when about eight years old. He moved to the so-called Bear River Flat about 1885, was ordained an Elder and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1909-1911, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 23, 1919, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
