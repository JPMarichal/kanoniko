Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clark, Lawrence D.

Clark, Lawrence D., Bishop of the Pocatello 4th Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Sept. 22, 1899, at Hooper, Utah, a son of Arthur B. Clark and Mary C. Rasmussen. He was baptized Sept. 22, 1907, ordained a High Priest Aug. 23, 1924, by Rudger Clawson, and ordained a Bishop in 1926.
