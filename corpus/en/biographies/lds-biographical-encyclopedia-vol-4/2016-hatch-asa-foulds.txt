Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hatch, Asa Foulds

Hatch, Asa Foulds, presiding Elder of the Kelly Branch, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Nov. 20, 1899, in Hatch, Idaho, a son of Thomas Asa Hatch and Sarah Elizabeth Parkin. He was baptized May 3, 1908, ordained to the PrieSthood, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1923-1925, and was ordained a High Priest Nov. 17, 1929, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
