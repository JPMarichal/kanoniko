Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hadfield, John, jun.

Hadfield, John, jun., Bishop of the Grouse Creek Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Sept. 15, 1883, at Glossop, Derbyshire, England, a son of John Hadfield and Mary Shaw. He was baptized in August, 1900, and came to Utah that same year. He filled a mission to Great Britain in 1909-1911, was ordained a High Priest April 24, 1916, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop June 5, 1927, by Richard R. Lyman.
