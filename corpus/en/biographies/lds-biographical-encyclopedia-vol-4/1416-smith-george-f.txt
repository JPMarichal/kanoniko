Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, George F.

Smith, George F., Bishop of the Emmett Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1913 to 1930, was born July 14, 1875, in South Cottonwood, Utah, the son of Geo. Smith and Catherine Wooten. He was baptized May 4, 1896, filled a mission in 1905-1907 to Great Britain, was ordained a High Priest March 26, 1905, by Charles W. Nibley, and sustained as Bishop of the Emmett Ward, April 13, 1913.
