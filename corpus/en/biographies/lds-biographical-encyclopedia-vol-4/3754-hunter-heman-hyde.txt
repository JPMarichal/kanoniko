Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hunter, Heman Hyde

Hunter, Heman Hyde, Bishop of the Egin Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1902 to 1907, was born April 10, 1864, in Grantsville, Utah, a son of Edward Hunter and Martha Ann Hyde. He was ordained a Seventy March 23, 1895, by George Reynolds, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1896, and was sustained as Bishop of the Egin Ward June 29, 1902.
