Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Welling, Arthur

Welling, Arthur, president of the North Central States Mission from 1929 to 1934, was born Dec. 18, 1877, in Farmington, Davis Co., Utah, a son of Job Y. Welling and Marietta Holmes. He filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1902-1904, and was set apart for his second mission June 11, 1929, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
