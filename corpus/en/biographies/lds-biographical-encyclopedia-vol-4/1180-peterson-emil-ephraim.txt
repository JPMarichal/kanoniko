Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Emil Ephraim

Peterson, Emil Ephraim, Bishop of the Grace 1st Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, since 1929, was born March 16, 1881, in Sweden, the son of Peter Peterson and Christina Olson. He was baptized May 2, 1889, emigrated to America in 1884 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 28, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
