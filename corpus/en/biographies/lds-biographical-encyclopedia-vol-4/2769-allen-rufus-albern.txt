Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Allen, Rufus Albern

Allen, Rufus Albern, Bishop of the Junction Ward, Panguitch Stake, Utah, from 1887 to 1895, was born Oct. 12, 1856, at Harmony, Washington Co., Utah, a son of Rufus Chester Allen and Lavenia Hoops Yearsley. He was baptized May 7, 1865, at Mountain Dell, Washington Co., Utah, ordained a High Priest Nov. 26, 1883, by William King, and ordained a Bishop March 29, 1887, by Heber J. Grant.
