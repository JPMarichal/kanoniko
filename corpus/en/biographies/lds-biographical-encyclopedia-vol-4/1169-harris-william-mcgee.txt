Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, William Mcgee

Harris, William Mcgee, Bishop of the Trout Creek Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho; from 1891 to 1915, was born May 23, 1862, at Richmond, Cache Co., Utah, the son of William Harris and Emeline Whittle. He was baptized in May, 1874, located in Gentile Valley in 1884, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 5, 1891, by Marriner W. Merrill.
