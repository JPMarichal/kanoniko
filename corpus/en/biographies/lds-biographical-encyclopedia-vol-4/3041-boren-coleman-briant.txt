Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Boren, Coleman Briant

Boren, Coleman Briant, Bishop of the Matthews Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Ariz., from 1898 to 1901, was born Dec. 29, 1849, in Mt. Pisgah, Iowa, a son of Coleman Boren and Malinda Keller. He was ordained a Bishop Feb. 7, 1898, by John Henry Smith.
