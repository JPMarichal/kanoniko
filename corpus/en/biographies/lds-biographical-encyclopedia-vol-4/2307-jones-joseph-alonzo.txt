Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, Joseph Alonzo

Jones, Joseph Alonzo, Bishop of the Cherry Creek Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1903 to 1911, was born April 19, 1871, in Willard, Utah, a son of John D. Jones and Sarah J. James. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest June 22, 1902, by William H. Gibbs, and when the ward was reorganized in 1903, he was made Bishop.
