Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Minidoka Stake

Anderson, John E.

Anderson, John E., Bishop of the Acequia Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1918 to 1920, was born May 15, 1873, in Meadow, Millard Co., Utah, a son of Haakon Anderson and Mary A. Nielsen. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 5, 1918, by Stephen L. Richards.
