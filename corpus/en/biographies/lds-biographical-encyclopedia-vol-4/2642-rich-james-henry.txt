Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rich, James Henry

Rich, James Henry, Bishop of the Slaterville Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1927, was born April 2, 1882, in Richville, Utah, a son of James T. Rich and Emma Hemming. He was baptized April 13, 1890, ordained a High Priest July 15, 1913, by James Wotherspoon, and a Bishop by Rudger Clawson April 1, 1923.
