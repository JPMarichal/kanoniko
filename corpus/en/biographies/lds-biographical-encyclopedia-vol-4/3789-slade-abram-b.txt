Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Slade, Abram B.

Slade, Abram B., Bishop of the Kline Ward, Young Stake, Colorado, from 1923 to 1926, was born April 19, 1878, in Pine Valley, Utah, a son of Henry Slade and Amanda M. Burgess. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest May 25, 1913, by Geo. F. Richards, who also ordained him a Bishop Dec. 8, 1923.
