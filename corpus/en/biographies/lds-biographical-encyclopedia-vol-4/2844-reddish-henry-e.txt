Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Reddish, Henry E.

Reddish, Henry E., Bishop of the Pocatello 2nd Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1908 to 1917, was born April 26, 1876, in Derbyshire, England, a son of Henry Reddish and Eliza Hurst. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 13, 1908, by Francis M. Lyman.
