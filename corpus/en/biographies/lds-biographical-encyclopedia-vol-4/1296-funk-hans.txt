Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Funk, Hans

Funk, Hans, Bishop of the Newton Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1884 to 1892, was born May 15, 1839, on the island of Bornholm, Denmark, the son of Frederik E. Funk and Kirsten Madsen. He was baptized Nov. 4, 1855, by Chr. G. Larsen, emigrated to Utah in 1861, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 11, 1884, by Wm. B. Preston.
