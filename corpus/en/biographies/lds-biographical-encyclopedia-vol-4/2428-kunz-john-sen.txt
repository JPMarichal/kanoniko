Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kunz, John, sen.

Kunz, John, sen., presiding Elder of the Bern Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1878 to 1890, was born. Jan. 2, 1823, in Bern, Switzerland, the son of Johann Kunz and Rosine Catherine Klossner. He came to Utah in 1870 and was ordained a Bishop June 3, 1878, by Charles C. Rich. He died Feb. 16, 1890.
