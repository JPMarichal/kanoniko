Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cook, John William

Cook, John William, Bishop of the Southgate Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930, was born Dec. 9, 1872, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of William Cook and Mary Davis. He was baptized April 11, 1896, ordained a High Priest Aug. 8, 1920, by Geo. E. Woolley, and a Bishop Oct. 19, 1925, by Rudger Clawson.
