Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hancock, Jesse Alonzo

Hancock, Jesse Alonzo, Bishop of the Eden Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Oct. 1, 1897, in Eden, the son of Jacob A. Hancock and Rozella Tyler. He was baptized Oct. 1, 1905, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1916-1918, and was ordained a Bishop March 17, 1929, by Geo. Albert Smith.
