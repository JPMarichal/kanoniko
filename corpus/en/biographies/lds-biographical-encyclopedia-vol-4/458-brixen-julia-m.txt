Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brixen, Julia M.

Brixen, Julia M., a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born in Sweden. She was baptized at the age of 13 (her parents having accepted the Gospel in Sweden), and came to Utah as a child and worked in a Salt Lake hotel and assisted her family to emigrate. She was married to Andrew Brixen, Aug. 16, 1880, and served as first counselor to Agnes Campbell in the 20th Ward Y. I. M. I. A. in 1898. In 1901 she became president, and was called to the General Board of the Y. L. M. I. A. in May, 1898. She has traveled extensively in the United States and Europe.
