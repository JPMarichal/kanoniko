Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larkin, Joseph James

Larkin, Joseph James, president of the Curlew Stake, (Utah-Idaho) from 1922 to 1924, was born Oct. 5, 1869, in Ogden, Weber Co., Utah, a son of Joseph Larkin and Harriet Chatterly. He was baptized June 27, 1878, ordained a Seventy Nov. 25, 1896, by Christian D. Fjeldsted and filled a mission to the Central States in 1896-1899. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 13, 1905, by Oleen N. Stohl.
