Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Ole Bertrand

Peterson, Ole Bertrand, president of the Society Islands Mission from 1922 to 1925, was born Sept. 7, 1889, in Lehi, Utah, the son of John R. Peterson and Agnes E. Sorenson. He was baptized July 2, 1899, was set apart Oct. 11, 1910, for his first mission and returned in 1914. He was called on a second mission to the Society Islands June 30, 1922, this time to preside. He returned home in 1925.
