Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Willard Richards

Smith, Willard Richards, first counselor in the Curlew Stake presidency from 1924 to 1930+, was born July 31, 1872, in Farmingten, Utah, a son of Lot Smith and Alice Ann Richards. He was baptized Oct. 3, 1880, by his father, ordained a High Priest Oct. 19, 1924, and set apart aa first counselor by Melvin J. Ballard.
