Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Yellowstone Stake

Bates, Ernest George

Bates, Ernest George, Bishop of the Chester Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1920 to 1926, was born June 26, 1889, at Lyman, Fremont Co., Idaho, a son of Thomas Y. Bates and Nora Foster. He was baptized July 1, 1901, ordained a Seventy May 21, 1911, by Joseph W. McMurrin, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1909-1911, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 24, 1920, by Orson F. Whitney.
