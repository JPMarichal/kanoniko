Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dewey, John Cyrus, jun.

Dewey, John Cyrus, jun., Bishop of the Fairview Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1889 to 1895, was born April 12, 1859, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of John C. Dewey and Mary Allen. He was baptized May 9, 1869, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 2, 1889, by Heber J. Grant. He died Oct. 8, 1895.
