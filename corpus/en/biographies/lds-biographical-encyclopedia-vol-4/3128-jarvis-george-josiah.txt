Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jarvis, George Josiah

Jarvis, George Josiah, Bishop of the Monticello Ward, San Juan Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1920, was born Oct. 13, 1881, in Snowflake, Arizona, a son of Samuel Walter Jarvis and Frances G. De Friez. He was baptized Oct. 13, 1889, filled a mission to the Southwestern States in 1901-1903, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 3, 1917, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
