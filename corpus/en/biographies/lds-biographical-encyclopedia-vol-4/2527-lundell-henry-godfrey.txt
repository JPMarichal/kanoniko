Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lundell, Henry Godfrey

Lundell, Henry Godfrey, Bishop of the Benjamin Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Jan. 19, 1890, in South Cottonwood, Utah, the son of Andrew G. Lundell and Charlotte Mattson. He was baptized July 3, 1898, ordained a Seventy Jan. 19, 1911, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to Sweden in 1911-1913, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 27, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard.
