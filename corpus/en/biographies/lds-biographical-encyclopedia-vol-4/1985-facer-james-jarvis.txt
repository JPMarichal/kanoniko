Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Facer, James Jarvis

Facer, James Jarvis, Bishop of the Hyrum 3rd Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1907 to 1917, was born June 10, 1866, at Willard, Box Elder Co., Utah, the son of George Facer and Sarah Thompson. He was baptized Sept. 7, 1876, ordained a Seventy Nov. 16, 1898, by George Reynolds, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1898-1900, was ordained a High Priest June 4, 1904, by David Murray, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 25, 1904, by Francis M. Lyman.
