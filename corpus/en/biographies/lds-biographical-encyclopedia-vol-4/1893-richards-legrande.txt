Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Richards, LeGrandPPRichards, Legrande

Richards, LeGrandPPRichards, Legrande, Bishop of the Sugar House Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1925, was born Feb. 6, 1886, in Farmington, Utah, the son of George F. Richards and Alice A. Robinson. He was ordained to the Priesthood and filled two missions to the Netherlands in 1905-1908 and in 1914-1916, presiding over the mission during his second term. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop by Charles W. Penrose June 29, 1919.
