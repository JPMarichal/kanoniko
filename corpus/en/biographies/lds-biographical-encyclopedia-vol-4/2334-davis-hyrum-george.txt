Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davis, Hyrum George

Davis, Hyrum George, Bishop of the Lehi Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1905 to 1908, was born March 17, 1875, in Big Cottonwood, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of William G. Davis and Emily Nix. He was baptized May 3, 1883, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southwestern States in 1902-1903, and was sustained as Bishop Nov. 28, 1905.
