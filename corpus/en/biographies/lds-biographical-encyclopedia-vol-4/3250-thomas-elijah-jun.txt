Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomas, Elijah, jun.

Thomas, Elijah, jun., Bishop of the Pinedale Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Nov. 24, 1876, at Leeds, Utah, a son of Elijah Murray Thomas and Emily Hampton. He was baptized Oct. 14, 1885, ordained a High Priest Aug. 30, 1914, by David O. McKay, and a Bishop Nov. 1, 1921, by Geo. Albert Smith.
