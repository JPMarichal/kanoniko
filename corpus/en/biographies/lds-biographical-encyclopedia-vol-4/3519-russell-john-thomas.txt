Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Russell, John Thomas

Russell, John Thomas, Bishop of the St. John Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1905 to 1915, was born Jan. 27, 1857, in Taylorsville, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of William G. Russell and Elizabeth Vickrey. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 9, 1905, by Francis M. Lyman.
