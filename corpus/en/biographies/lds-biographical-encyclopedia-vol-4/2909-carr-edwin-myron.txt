Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carr, Edwin Myron

Carr, Edwin Myron, Bishop of the Annis Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1916 to 1924, was born June 3, 1870, at Eden, Weber Co., Utah, a son of David Cart and Melvina A. Butler. He was baptized June 13, 1883, ordained a High Priest Nov. 5, 1904, by Robert L. Bybee, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 2, 1916, by Geo. Albert Smith.
