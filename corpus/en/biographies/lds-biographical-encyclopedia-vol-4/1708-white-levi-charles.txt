Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

White, Levi Charles

White, Levi Charles, Bishop of the Utahn Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1922, was born March 5, 1868, in St. Joseph, Nevada, the son of John Chambers White and Mary Ann Ingham. He was baptized when eight years old, and was ordained a High Priest June 1, 1896, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop June 30, 1918.
