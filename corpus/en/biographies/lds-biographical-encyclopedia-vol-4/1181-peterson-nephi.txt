Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Nephi

Peterson, Nephi, Bishop of the Lago Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1919, was born Aug. 1, 1887, in Mink Creek, Idaho, the son of Peter Peterson and Christina Olson. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1895, was ordained a High Priest in November, 1914, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., and was ordained a Bishop May 16, 1915, by Orson F. Whitney. He filled a mission to Sweden in 1907-1919.
