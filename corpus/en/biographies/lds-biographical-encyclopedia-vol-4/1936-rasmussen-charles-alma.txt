Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rasmussen, Charles Alma

Rasmussen, Charles Alma, first counselor in the Gunnison Stake presidency, Utah, from 1923 to 1926, was born April 2, 1889, in Ephraim, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Rasmus Rasmussen and Hannah Cooper. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1899, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 6, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards.
