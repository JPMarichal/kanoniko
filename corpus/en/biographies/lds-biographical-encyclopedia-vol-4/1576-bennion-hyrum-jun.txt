Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bennion, Hyrum, jun.

Bennion, Hyrum, jun., second counselor in the presidency of the Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1927, was born April 3, 1879, in Taylorsville, the son of Hyrum Bennion and Eliza Ann Harker. He was baptized June 2, 1887, ordained a High Priest Dec.12, 1907, and a Bishop Dec. 14, 1914, by Francis M. Lyman. He acted as Bishop of the Taylorsville Ward from 1914 to 1921.
