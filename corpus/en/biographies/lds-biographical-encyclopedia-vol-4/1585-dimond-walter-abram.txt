Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dimond, Walter Abram

Dimond, Walter Abram, Bishop of the Bennion Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1914 to 1919, was born July 18, 1872, in Crewkerne, Somersetshire, England, the son of Henry Dimond and Elizabeth Jane Webber. He was baptized March 22, 1889, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1905 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 27, 1914, by Francis M. Lyman.
