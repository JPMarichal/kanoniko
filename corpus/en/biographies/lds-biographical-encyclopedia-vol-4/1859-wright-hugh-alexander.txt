Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wright, Hugh Alexander

Wright, Hugh Alexander, Bishop of the Rexburg 1st Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Feb. 14, 1878, in American Fork, Utah, the son of Frederick Wright and Margaret Thornton. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1891, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1914-1917, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 27, 1925, by Melvin J. Ballard.
