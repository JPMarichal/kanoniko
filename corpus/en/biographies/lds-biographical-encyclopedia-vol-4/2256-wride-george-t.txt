Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wride, George T.

Wride, George T., Bishop of the Matthews Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1923 to 1925, was born Nov. 7, 1874, in Payson, Utah, a son of Barry Wride and Hannah Selman. He was baptized Nov. 2, 1882, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 12, 1922, by Richard R. Lyman.
