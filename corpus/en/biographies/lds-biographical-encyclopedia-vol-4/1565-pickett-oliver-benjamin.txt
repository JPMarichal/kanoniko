Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pickett, Oliver Benjamin

Pickett, Oliver Benjamin, Bishop of the Oakley 3rd Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1904 to 1914, was born in Laketown, Utah, the son of Moroni Pickett and Amelia Clegg. He filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1902-1904 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 4, 1904, by Hyrum M. Smith. He died Jan. 19, 1933.
