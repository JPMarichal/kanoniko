Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Murdock, Keith

Murdock, Keith, Bishop of the Garfield Ward, Oquirrh Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born April 10, 1900, in Heber City, Utah, the son of Nelson Murdock and Lavina E. Averett. He was baptized May 2, 1908, ordained a Seventy Sept. 6, 1921, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to California in 1921-1924, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 5, 1929, by David O. McKay.
