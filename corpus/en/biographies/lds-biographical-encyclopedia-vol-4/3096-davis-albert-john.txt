Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davis, Albert John

Davis, Albert John, Bishop of the Center Ward, Salt Lake Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1922, was born Oct. 14, 1866, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Albert W. Davis and Melissa J. Lambson. He was baptized Oct. 14, 1874, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Hawaii in 1891-1894, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 25, 1906, by Hyrum M. Smith, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 14, 1916, by Anthon H. Lund.
