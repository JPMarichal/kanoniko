Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gummersall, George Hodgson

Gummersall, George Hodgson, Bishop of the Ivins Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1923, was born April 24, 1887, in Franklin, Idaho, a son of Smith Gummersall and Phebe Hodgson. He was baptized Sept. 24, 1895, ordained a High Priest Jan. 2, 1918, by Rudger Clawson, and ordained a Bishop March 4, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
