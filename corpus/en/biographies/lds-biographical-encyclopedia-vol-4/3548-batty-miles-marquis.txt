Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Batty, Miles Marquis

Batty, Miles Marquis, Bishop of the Glines Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1914 to 1926, was born June 12, 1865, in Wanship, Utah, a son of Miles Batty and Mary Henrietta Mecham. He was baptized June 7, 1886, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1907-1909, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 19, 1914, by Orson F. Whitney.
