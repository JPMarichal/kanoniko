Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Martin

Jensen, Martin, Bishop of the Redmond Ward, North Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1912, was born Jan. 7, 1866, at Gunnison, Utah, the son of Christian Jensen and Barbara Christensen. He was baptized in 1878, ordained a Seventy May 18, 1898, by Chr. D. Fieldsted, and filled a mission to Denmark in 1898-1900. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 29, 1902, by Jos. F. Smith.
