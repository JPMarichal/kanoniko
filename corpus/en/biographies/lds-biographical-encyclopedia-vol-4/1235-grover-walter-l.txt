Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grover, Walter L.

Grover, Walter L., Bishop of the Garland Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1898 to 1908, was born Dec. 25, 1860, in Farmington, Davis Co., Utah, the son of Thomas Grover and Elizabeth Walker. He was baptized when about eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 15, 1898, by Rudger Clawson. He presided over the Sunset Branch (later Garland Ward) from 1893 to 1898.
