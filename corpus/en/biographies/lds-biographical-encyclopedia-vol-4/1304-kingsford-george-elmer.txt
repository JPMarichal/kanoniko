Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kingsford, George Elmer

Kingsford, George Elmer, Bishop of the Smithfield 2nd Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1928, was born Jan. 10, 1894, at Coveville, Cache Co., Utah, the son of George A. Kings-ford and Isabel Clements. He was baptized Jan. 10, 1902, ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 22, 1921, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., and a Bishop April 18, 1926, by Orson F. Whitney.
