Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merkley, Ray Edgar

Merkley, Ray Edgar, Bishop of the Wapello Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1926, was born May 8, 1889, at St. Charles, Idaho, the son of Christopher A. Merkley and Lydia L. Allred. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1898, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 14, 1915, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop May 15, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
