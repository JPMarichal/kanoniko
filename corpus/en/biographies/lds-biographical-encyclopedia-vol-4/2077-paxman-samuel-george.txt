Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Paxman, Samuel George

Paxman, Samuel George, first counselor in the Juab Stake presidency, Utah, in 1930+, was born Feb. 21, 1896, at Nephi, Juab Co., Utah, a son of James W. Paxman and Julia Sudberry. He was baptized July 2, 1904, ordained to the PrieSthood, filled a mission to Canada in 1919-1922, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 17, 1924, by Orson F. Whitney, and set apart as first counselor Sept. 14, 1930, by Rudger Clawson.
