Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hurst, Albert Leroy

Hurst, Albert Leroy, Bishop of the Lynndyl Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1923, was born March 22, 1880, in Fairview, Utah, the son of Philip Hurst and Rebecca A. Sanderson. He was baptized June 22, 1888, ordained a High Priest May 25, 1918, by Alonzo A. Hinckley, and a Bishop June 29, 1919.
