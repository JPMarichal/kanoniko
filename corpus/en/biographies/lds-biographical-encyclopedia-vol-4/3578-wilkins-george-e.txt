Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilkins, George E.

Wilkins, George E., Bishop of the Vernal 2nd Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1927, was born Nov. 1, 1876, in Peoa, Utah, the son of Oscar Wilkins and Elizabeth Durrah. He was baptized April 16, 1885, ordained a Seventy April 11, 1898, by Albert R. Smith, filled a mission to the Southwestern States in 1898-1900, was ordained a High Priest May 7, 1905, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop Sept. 10, 1911, by Francis M. Lyman.
