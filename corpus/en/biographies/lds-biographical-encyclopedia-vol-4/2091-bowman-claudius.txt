Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bowman, Claudius

Bowman, Claudius, first counselor in the Juarez Stake presidency, Mexico, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Sept. 6, 1890, in Kanab, Utah, a son of Henry Eyring Bowman and Mary Gubler. He was baptized June 2, 1900, moved to Mexico in 1900, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 30, 1924, by John T. Whetten and was set apart as first counselor in the Juarez Stake presidency Sept. 8, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
