Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Forsgren, John Clifford

Forsgren, John Clifford, Bishop of the Preston 4th Ward, Oneida Stake, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Oct. 18, 1893, in Riverdale, Idaho, a son of Eli Forsgren and Frances Smith. He was baptized Sept. 5, 1903, filled a mission to Australia in 1915-1919, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 1, 1929, by Geo. F. Richards.
