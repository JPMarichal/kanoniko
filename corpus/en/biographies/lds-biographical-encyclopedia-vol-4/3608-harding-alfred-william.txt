Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harding, Alfred William

Harding, Alfred William, Bishop of the Pioneer Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1927, was born Feb. 14, 1863, in Kingswood, Gloucestershire, England, a son of Edward G. Harding and Sarah Ann Meeks. He came to Utah in 1874, was baptized July 20, 1879, filled a mission to Samoa in 1889-1894, was ordained a High Priest June 9, 1895, by David John, and a Bishop April 27, 1913, by David O. McKay.
