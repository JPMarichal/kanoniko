Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Andrus, Robert

Andrus, Robert, Bishop of the Ucon Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1930+, was born Sept. 12, 1873, in Draper, Utah, a son of Milo Andrus and Jane Munday. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1883, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1909-1911, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 23, 1900, by Heber J. Grant, and a Bishop May 21, 1916, by David O. McKay.
