Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Call, Irvin

Call, Irvin, Bishop of the West Layton Ward, North Davis Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Oct. 9, 1892, in Bountiful, Utah, the son of David Call and Eliza Dittmore. He was baptized April 23, 1901, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1914-1916, was ordained a High Priest May 12, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards, and a Bishop Aug. 19, 1924, by Richard R. Lyman.
