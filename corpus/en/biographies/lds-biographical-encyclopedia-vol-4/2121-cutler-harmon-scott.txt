Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cutler, Harmon Scott

Cutler, Harmon Scott, Bishop of the Kanab Ward, Kanab Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1905, was born Sept. 11, 1862, in American Fork, Utah, the son of Royal J. Cutler. He was ordained a Bishop Dec. 15, 1901, by Abraham O. Woodruff.
