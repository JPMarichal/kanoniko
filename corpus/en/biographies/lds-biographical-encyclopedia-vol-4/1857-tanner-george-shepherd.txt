Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tanner, George Shepherd

Tanner, George Shepherd, Bishop of the Salem Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930, was born Jan. 26, 1897, in Joseph, Arizona, the son of Henry M. Tanner and Eliza E. Parkinson. He was baptized Jan. 28, 1905, ordained an Elder June 20, 1918, by his father, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1919-1921, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 18, 1926, by Geo. F. Richards.
