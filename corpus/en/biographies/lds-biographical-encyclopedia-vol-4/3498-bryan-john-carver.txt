Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bryan, John Carver

Bryan, John Carver, Bishop of the Batesville Ward. Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1915, and Bishop of the Tooele NorthWardfrom 1929 to 1930+, was born April 25, 1875, in Batesville, Tooele Co., Utah, a son of George W. Bryan and Margaret Simpson. He was baptized Sept. 6, 1883, ordained a High Priest Nov. 26, 1911, by Joseph C. Orme, ordained a Bishop Dec. 28, 1912, by George F. Richards, and set apart as Bishop of the Tooele North Ward June 30, 1929, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
