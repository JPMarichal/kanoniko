Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rees, Thomas D., jun.

Rees, Thomas D., jun., second counselor in the San Luis Stake presidency, Colo., in 1908, was born Sept. 11, 1877, in Wales, Utah, a son of Thomas D. Rees and Elizabeth Rees. He was baptized Nov. 1, 1885, ordained a High Priest, and set apart as second counselor Jan. 19, 1908, by Hyrum M. Smith.
