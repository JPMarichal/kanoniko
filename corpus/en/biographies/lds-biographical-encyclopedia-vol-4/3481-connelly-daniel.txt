Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Connelly, Daniel

Connelly, Daniel, Bishop of the Eureka Ward, Tintic Stake, Juab Co., Utah, from 1900 to 1908, was born June 19, 1861, in North Leith, Scotland, a son of Daniel Connelly and Jessie Ramsay. He was baptized Feb. 12, 1881, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 23, 1900, by Reed Smoot.
