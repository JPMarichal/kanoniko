Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merrill, Olonzo D.

Merrill, Olonzo D., president of the Tongan Mission from 1892 to 1895, was born Dec. 13, 1867, in Richmond, Utah, a son of Marriner W. Merrill and Cyrene Stanley. He was baptized when nine years old, set apart April 13, 1892, for a mission to Tonga, and presided over the Mission from Oct. 24, 1892 to July 22, 1895.
