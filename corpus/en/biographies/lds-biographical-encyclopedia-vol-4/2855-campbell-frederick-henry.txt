Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Campbell, Frederick Henry

Campbell, Frederick Henry, Bishop of the Lava Hot Springs Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1916, was born May 16, 1887, in Weston, Idaho, a son of Adam R. Campbell and Elizabeth Beebe. He was baptized June .1, 1895, and ordained a High Priest June 26, 1910, by Noah S. Pond, and a Bishop Feb. 19, 1916, by Rudger Clawson.
