Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nielsen, Niels Peter

Nielsen, Niels Peter, Bishop of the Fairview South Ward, North Sanpete Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born May 17, 1876, in Fairview, a son of Niels Nielsen and Christine Christiansen. He was baptized June 22, 1884, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 16, 1924, by Hyrum G. Smith.
