Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cook, John W.

Cook, John W., Bishop of the Taylor Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1929, was born Sept. 18, 1872, in Taylorsville, Utah, a son of Thomas Cook and Mary Ellen Normington. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1880, and ordained a High Priest Nov. 9, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
