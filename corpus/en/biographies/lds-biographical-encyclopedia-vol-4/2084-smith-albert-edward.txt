Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Albert Edward

Smith, Albert Edward, Bishop of the Nephi North Ward, Juab Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Oct. 24, 1886, at Manassa, Conejos Co., Colorado, a son of Albert R. Smith and Irene U. Haskell. He was baptized June 6, 1895, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1910-1912, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 15, 1914, by George Albert Smith, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 4, 1927, by Richard R. Lyman.
