Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christenson, Joseph

Christenson, Joseph, secretary of the Genealogical Society of Utah from 1900 to 1907, librarian from 1910 to 1929, and vice-president since 1934, was born April 19, 1865, at American Fork, Utah Co., Utah, a son of Joseph Christenson and Johanna Harling. He is recorder in the Salt Lake Temple. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 2, p. 607.)
