Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Anderson, Gustaf Erik

Anderson, Gustaf Erik, Bishop of the Central Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1924, was born Sept. 6, 1873, in Dalarne, Sweden, the son of Andera Gustaf Anderson and Hadda Romlin. He emigrated to Utah in 1891, was baptized at Kanesville, near Ogden, Utah, the same year, and was ordained a High Priest Aug. 14, 1915, and a Bishop by James E. Talmage Aug. 19, 1917.
