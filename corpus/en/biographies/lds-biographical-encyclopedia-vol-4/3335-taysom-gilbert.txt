Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taysom, Gilbert

Taysom, Gilbert, Bishop of the Alton South Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1919 to 1921, was born May 30, 1891, in Afton, a son of Abram J. Taysom and Hannah Neild. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1900, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1914-1916, was ordained a High Priest and Bishop in 1917, by Orson F. Whitney and presided over the Kemmerer Ward, Wyo., from 1917 to 1919. He was set apart as Bishop of Alton South Ward June 22, 1919, by James E. Talmage.
