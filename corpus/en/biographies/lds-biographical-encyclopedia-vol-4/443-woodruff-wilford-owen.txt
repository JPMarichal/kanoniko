Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Woodruff, Wilford Owen

Woodruff, Wilford Owen, a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A. from 1931 to 1935, was born Oct. 31, 1899, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Abraham Owen Woodruff and Helen May Winters. He was baptized in 1907 and filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1919-1922. He has been active in furthering home industry by advertising and marketing home products.
