Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Orme, Silas Cross

Orme, Silas Cross, Bishop Of the Tooele Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1905 to 1911, was born Dec. 26, 1864, in Tooele, Utah, a son of Samuel W. Orme and Sarah Cross. He was baptized March 28, 1874, ordained an Elder Oct. 1, 1894, by Hugh S. Gowans, filled a mission to Colorado in 1902-1904, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 29, 1905, by Francis M. Lyman.
