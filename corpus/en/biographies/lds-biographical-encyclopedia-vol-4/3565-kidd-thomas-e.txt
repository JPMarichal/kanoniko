Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kidd, Thomas E.

Kidd, Thomas E., Bishop of the Ashley Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1919, was born Oct. 27, 1887, at Riverton, Utah, a son of Alexander B. Kidd and Sarah E. Wright. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1896, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1912-1914, and was ordained a Bishop June 20, 1915, by Geo. F. Richards.
