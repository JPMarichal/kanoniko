Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Huntington, Edward Ray

Huntington, Edward Ray, Bishop of the Palmyra Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born March 17, 1889, in Spanish Fork, Utah Co., Utah, the son of John Albert Huntington and Sarah Jane Banks. He was baptized June 26, 1898, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 27, 1929, by George F. Richards.
