Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Payne, Junius Edward

Payne, Junius Edward, Bishop of the Virden Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Oct. 3, 1887, in Aurora, Utah, a son of Harry M. Payne and Helen Amelia Buchanan. He was baptized Oct. 3, 1897, ordained a High Priest Nov. 26, 1916, by Hyrum M. Smith, and a Bishop May 23, 1926, by Richard R. Lyman.
