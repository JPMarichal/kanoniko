Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Farr, Winslow, jun.

Farr, Winslow, jun., Bishop of the Ogden 3rd Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1891, was born May 11, 1837, in Vermont, a son of Winslow Farr and Olive H. Freeman. He was baptized May 11, 1845, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 28, 1877, by Franklin D. Richards. He died Feb. 18, 1913.
