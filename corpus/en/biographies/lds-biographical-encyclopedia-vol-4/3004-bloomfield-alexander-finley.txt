Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bloomfield, Alexander Finley

Bloomfield, Alexander Finley, Bishop of the Ramah Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1916 to 1919, was born Aug. 4, 1887, in Juarez, Mexico, a son of John Bloomfield and Elizabeth A. Barton. He was baptized Oct. 3, 1895, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 6, 1916, by Geo. Albert Smith.
