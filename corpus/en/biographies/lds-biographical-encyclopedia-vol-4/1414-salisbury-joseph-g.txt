Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Salisbury, Joseph G.

Salisbury, Joseph G., Bishop of the Boise 2nd Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from March, 1923, to October, 1923, was born Oct. 16, 1888, at Wellsville, Utah, the son of Joseph H. Salisbury and Matilda J. Gibbs. He filled a mission to Switzerland and Germany in 1910-1913, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 4, 1923, by Rudger Clawson.
