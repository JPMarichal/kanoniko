Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Facer, William John

Facer, William John, Bishop of the Willard Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1903 to 1910, was born July 13, 1861, in Willard, the son of George Facet and Mary Prior. He filled a mission to Great Britain in 1900-1903, was called to Willard to act as Bishop in 1903, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 8, 1903, by Rudger Clawson.
