Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dunyon, Phares Wells

Dunyon, Phares Wells, Bishop of the Berkeley Ward, San Francisco Stake, Calif., from 1928 to 1930+, was born Oct. 21, 1874, in Tooele City, Utah, the son of Isaac Newton Dunyon and Evelyn Wells. He was baptized June 6, 1895, ordained a Seventy by Brigham Young, jun., filled a mission to the Southwestern States Mission in 1896-1898, was ordained a High Priest July 10, 1927, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop May 20, 1928, by David O. McKay.
