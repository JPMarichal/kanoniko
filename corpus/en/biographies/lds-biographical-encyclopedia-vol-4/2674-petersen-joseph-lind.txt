Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Petersen, Joseph Lind

Petersen, Joseph Lind, Bishop of the Huntsville Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1926, was born July 15, 1876, in Huntsville, a son of Soren L. and Louisa Petersen. He filled three missions to Denmark, presiding over the mission in 1921-1926. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 7, 1917, by David O. McKay.
