Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stratton, Charles Abel

Stratton, Charles Abel, Bishop of the Hinckley Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born March 24, 1874, in Virgin City, Utah, the son of James Albert Stratton and Emma Maria Gardiner. He was baptized when eight years old, and ordained a Bishop March 21, 1920, by David O. McKay.
