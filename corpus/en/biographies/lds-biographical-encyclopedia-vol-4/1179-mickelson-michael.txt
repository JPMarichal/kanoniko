Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mickelson, Michael

Mickelson, Michael, Bishop of the Lago (Trout Creek) Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1920, and later serving a second term commencing in 1930, was born Aug. 28, 1873, in Denmark, the son of Anton Peter Mikkelsen and Gitte Jensen. He emigrated to America in 1881, was baptized March 3, 1899, ordained a High Priest June 24, 1906, by Jos. T. Pond, and ordained a Bishop May 18, 1919, by Richard R. Lyman.
