Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Andrew

Johnson, Andrew, president of the Swedish Mission from 1926 to 1927, was born Aug. 9, 1871, in Hagglingo, Malmohus lan, Skane, Sweden, a son of Jons Swenson and Elna Pearson. He was baptized March 31, 1917, in Utah, to which state he migrated from Nebraska in 1892, having left his native land, the same year. In 1897 he married Ellen Anderson, and made his home in Murray, Utah, where he still resides. He filled a mission to Sweden in 1925-1927, laboring first in Skane, and later acted as assistant editor of "Nordstjarnan," the last year of his mission acting as president of the Swedish Mission, succeeding Pres. John H. Anderson.

Elder Johnson has presided over the Scandinavian organization in the Murray Second Ward. (See History of the Scandinavian Mission, p. 481.)
