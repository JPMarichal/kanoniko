Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Corbett, Joseph Frederick

Corbett, Joseph Frederick, Bishop of the Bancroft Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1918 to 1925, was born Oct. 3, 1883, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Joseph E. Corbett and Louisa E. Gorringe. He was baptized in May, 1892, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1905-1907, was ordained a High Priest March 7, 1908, by William H. Mendenhall, and ordained a Bishop March 2, 1918, by Heber J. Grant.
