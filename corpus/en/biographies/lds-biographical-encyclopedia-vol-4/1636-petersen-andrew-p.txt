Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Petersen, Andrew P.

Petersen, Andrew P., Bishop of the Stone Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1922, was born Dec. 23, 1880, in Slagelse, Denmark, a son of Niels A. Petersen and Johanne Larson. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1889, by Joseph M. Smith, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 5, 1917, by George D. Smith.
