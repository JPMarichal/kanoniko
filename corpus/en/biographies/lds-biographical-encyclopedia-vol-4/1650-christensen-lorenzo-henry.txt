Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christensen, Lorenzo Henry

Christensen, Lorenzo Henry, Bishop of the Delta 3rd Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Jan. 2, 1887, in Oak City, Utah, the son of Anton Christensen and Castina Lovell. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1895, filled two home missions in Millard Stake, was ordained a High Priest March 22, 1924, by Alonzo A. Hinckley, and a Bishop March 24, 1929, by Geo. Albert Smith.
