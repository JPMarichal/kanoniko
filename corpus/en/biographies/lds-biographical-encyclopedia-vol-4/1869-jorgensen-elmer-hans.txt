Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jorgensen, Elmer Hans

Jorgensen, Elmer Hans, Bishop of the Widtsoe Ward, Garfield. Stake, Piute Co., Utah, from 1918 to 1920, was born March 10, 1886, at Smithfield, Utah, the son of Jacob Jorgensen and Melvina M. Munk. He was baptized May 3, 1894, in Smithfield, ordained a High Priest Nov. 22, 1914, by Heber J. Grant, and ordained a Bishop March 2, 1918, by George Albert Smith.
