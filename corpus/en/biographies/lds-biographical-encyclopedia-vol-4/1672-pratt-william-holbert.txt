Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pratt, William Holbert

Pratt, William Holbert, Bishop of the Hinckley Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1891 to 1902, was born May 8, 1844, in Tessomingo County, Mississippi, the son of Jonathan B. Pratt and Susanna Holbert. He was baptized in 1857, in Grantsville, Utah, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 21, 1891, by Anthon H. Lund. He died July 16, 1902.
