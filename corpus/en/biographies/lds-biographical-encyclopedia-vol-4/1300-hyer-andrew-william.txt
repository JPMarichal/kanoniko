Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hyer, Andrew William

Hyer, Andrew William, Bishop of the Lewiston 1st Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1926, was born Aug. 9, 1882, in Lewiston, Utah, the son of Andrew Lars Hyer and Ellen Gilbert. He was baptized Aug. 9, 1890, ordained a Seventy June 19, 1904, filled a mission to Germany in 1906-1909, was ordained a High Priest May 2, 1920, and a Bishop Oct. 23, 1921.
