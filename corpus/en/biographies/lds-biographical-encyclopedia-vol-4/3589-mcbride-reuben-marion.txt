Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcbride, Reuben Marion

Mcbride, Reuben Marion, Bishop of the Imbler Ward, Union Stake, Oregon, from 1916 to 1925, was born May 12, 1864. He was ordained a High Priest Sept. 28, 1913, by Lehi Wright, and sustained as Bishop March 12, 1916.
