Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Butterworth, Isaiah

Butterworth, Isaiah, Bishop of the Thayne Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1901 to 1905, was born July 8, 1868, in Winwich parish, Huntingtonshire, England, a son of William Butterworth and Mary Rose. He emigrated to Utah in 1872, was baptized in August, 1876, ordained a High Priest in Clinton Ward, Weber Stake, in 1894, and a Bishop Aug. 18, 1901, by Jos. F. Smith.
