Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Richardson, Ovey Albert

Richardson, Ovey Albert, Bishop of the Montwel Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born Dec. 24, 1886, in Woodland, Summit Co., Utah, a son of John W. Richardson and Maria Anderson. He was baptized Aug. 18, 1895, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 16, 1924, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
