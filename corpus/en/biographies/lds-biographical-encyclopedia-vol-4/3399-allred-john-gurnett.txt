Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Allred, John Gurnett

Allred, John Gurnett, Bishop of the Raymond 1st Ward, Taylor Stake, Canada, from 1915 to 1919, was born Sept. 11, 1876, in Lehi, Utah, a son of James H. Allred and Kate Jones. He was baptized Nov. 2, 1884, and ordained a Bishop May 3, 1915, by Geo. Albert Smith.
