Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christensen, Erastus Milton

Christensen, Erastus Milton, second counselor in the presidency of the Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1929, was born July 5, 1889, in Fountain Green, Utah, a son of Anton Erastus Christensen and Anna Christine Jensen. He was baptized July 5, 1897, ordained a High Priest Aug. 20, 1922, by Hyrum G. Smith, and sustained as Bishop of the Shelley 1st Ward June 4, 1922. He held that position until Feb. 17, 1924, when he was set apart as second counselor by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
