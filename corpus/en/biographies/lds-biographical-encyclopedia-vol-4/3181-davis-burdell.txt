Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davis, Burdell

Davis, Burdell, Bishop of the Pleasant View Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1917, was born Jan. 15, 1858, in Provo, Utah, a son of Josuah Davis and Susanna Cole. He was baptized in 1866, ordained a High Priest April 15, 1905, by John Henry Smith, and ordained a Bishop April 28, 1912, by Francis M. Lyman. He died May, 27, 1919.
