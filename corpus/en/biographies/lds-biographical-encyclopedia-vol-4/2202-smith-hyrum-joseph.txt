Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Hyrum Joseph

Smith, Hyrum Joseph, Bishop of the Liberty Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1907 to 1915, was born April 27, 1868, in Leicester, England, the son of Charles Dickens Smith and Mary Darkins. He was baptized in April, 1880, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 22, 1907, by Francis M. Lyman.
