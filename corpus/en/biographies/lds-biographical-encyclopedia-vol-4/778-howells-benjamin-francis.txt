Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Howells, Benjamin Francis

Howells, Benjamin Francis, president of the French Mission in 1914, was born March 5, 1888, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Thomas F. Howells and Mary Parrish. He was baptized in March, 1896, set apart for a mission to Switzerland and Germany July 22, 1912, and was made president of the French Mission May 7, 1914. When war was declared in August of that year he and most of the American missionaries were released to return home.
