Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cameron, Jonathan William

Cameron, Jonathan William, Bishop of the Torrey Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1909, was born Aug. 6, 1874, in Panguitch, Utah, a son of Jonathan Cameron and Frances Louisa Cooper. He was baptized Oct. 5, 1882, and ordained a Bishop May 13, 1906, by Geo. Albert Smith.
