Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ferrin, Arthur Moroni

Ferrin, Arthur Moroni, Bishop of the Liberty Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1918, was born Dec. 10, 1881, in Eden, Utah, a son of Josiah L. Ferrin and Ella D. Stallings. He was baptized June 5, 1890, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 22, 1907, by Lewis W. Shurtliff, and a Bishop June 27, 1909, by Francis M. Lyman.
