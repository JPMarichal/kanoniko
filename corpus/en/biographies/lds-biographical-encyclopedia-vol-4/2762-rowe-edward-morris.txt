Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rowe, Edward Morris

Rowe, Edward Morris, first counselor in the presidency of the Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1924 to 1929, was born Dec. 9, 1878, in Spanish Fork, Utah Co., Utah, the son of Owen Rowe and Ann Creer. He was baptized when about eight years of age, and set apart as first counselor in the Palmyra Stake presidency Nov. 23, 1924, by John A. Widtsoe.
