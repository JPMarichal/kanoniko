Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Giles, John Thomas

Giles, John Thomas, Bishop of the Bonneville Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1921, was born June 1, 1865, in Provo, Utah, a son of John and Elizabeth Giles. He was baptized in August, 1873, filled a mission to Hawaii in 1890-1893 and was ordained a High Priest Jan. 7, 1894, by David John.
