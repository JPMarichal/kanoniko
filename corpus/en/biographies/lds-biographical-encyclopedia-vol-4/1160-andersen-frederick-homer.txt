Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bannock Stake

Andersen, Frederick Homer

Andersen, Frederick Homer, Bishop of the Cleveland Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Jan. 27, 1893, in Bear River City, Box Elder Co., Utah, the son of Andrew Andersen and Sophia Johnson. He was baptized April 1, 1901, and was ordained a High Priest Feb. 5, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
