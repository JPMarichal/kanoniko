Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Marchant, Abraham, jun.

Marchant, Abraham, jun., presiding Elder of the Peoa Branch, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1862 to 1877, and Bishop of the Peoa Ward from 1877 to 1881, was born March 17, 1816, in N e w to n (Bath), Somersetshire, England, a son of Abraham Marchant and Mary Pranket. He was baptized and labored as a missionary before emigrating to Utah in 1854, and was ordained a Bishop in 1877. He died Oct. 5, 1881, in Peoa, Utah.
