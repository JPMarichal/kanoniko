Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Campbell, Wallace

Campbell, Wallace, Bishop of the Annis Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1924 to 1928, was born Nov. 30, 1893, in Lewisville, Fremont Co., Idaho, a son of Eli Campbell and Hannah Ash. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1902, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 1, 1924, by Hyrum G. Smith.
