Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Porter, Sanford

Porter, Sanford, Bishop of the Centerville Ward, South Davis Stake, Utah, from 1852 to 1855, was born March 7, 1790, in Brimfield, Massachusetts, a son of Nathan Porter and Susannah West. He was baptized in 1831, emigrated to Utah in 1847, and was ordained a Bishop Jan. 7, 1852.
