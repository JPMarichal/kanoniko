Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Roe, Thomas William

Roe, Thomas William, presiding Elder of the Stone Branch, Curlew Stake, from 1900-1902 and Bishop of the Stone Ward from 1902 to 1915, was born March 31, 1869, in Derby, Derbyshire, England, a son of John Roe and Catherine Byatt. He was baptized Nov. 15, 1882, by John Sutton, emigrated to Utah, was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 25, 1902, by Rudger Clawson, and chosen second counselor in the presidency of the Curlew Stake in 1915. He died April 5, 1920.
