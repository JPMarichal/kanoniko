Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christensen, Erastus Snow

Christensen, Erastus Snow, president of the San Luis Stake, Coto., from 1908 to 1914, was born Feb. 22, 18944, in Fountain Green, Utah, a son of Martin Christensen and Mary J. Christensen. He was baptized April 6, 1882, and set apart as president of the Stake Jan. 19, 1908, by Geo. F. Richards.
