Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Young, Clifford Earle

Young, Clifford Earle, president of the Alpine Stake, Utah, since 1928, was born Dec. 7, 1883, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Seymour B. Young and Ann Elizabeth Riter. He was baptized May 3, 1892, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1905-1908. He was chosen president of the Alpine Stake July 1, 1928, and set apart by PreS. Heber J. Grant.
