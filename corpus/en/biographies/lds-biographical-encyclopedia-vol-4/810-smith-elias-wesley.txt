Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Elias Wesley

Smith, Elias Wesley, president of the Hawaiian Mission from 1919 to 1922, was born April 21, 1886, in Laie, Oahu, Hawaiian Islands, a son of Pres. Jos. F. Smith and Julina Lambson. He was baptized April 21, 1894, ordained an Elder and filled a mission to the Hawaiian Islands in 1907-1910. Being called on a second mission to Hawaii, he was set apart to preside over the Hawaiian Mission May 8, 1919, and acted in that capacity until his return.
