Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mclean, David Ka

Mclean, David Kay Moffat, Bishop of the Soda Springs Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1926, was born June 7, 1874, in Meadowville, Rich Co., Utah, a son of John McLean and Janet Moffat. He was baptized May 13, 1883, ordained a High Priest Nov. 15, 1914, by Joseph F. Smith, jun., and ordained a Bishop Feb. 25, 1917, by Hyrum M. Smith.
