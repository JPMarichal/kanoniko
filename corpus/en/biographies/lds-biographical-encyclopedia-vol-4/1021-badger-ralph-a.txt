Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Badger, Ralph A.

Badger, Ralph A., president of the South African Mission from 1906 to 1908, was born April 8, 1880, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Rodney C. Badger and Louisa Noble Ashby. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1888, set apart for his mission March 10, 1905, and returned July 10, 1908.
