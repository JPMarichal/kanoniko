Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Huntington, Oliver Baker, jun.

Huntington, Oliver Baker, jun., Bishop of the Springville 1st Ward, Kolob Stake, Utah, from 1903 to 1924, was born May 30, 1856, at Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Oliver Boardman Huntington and Hannah Mendenhall Sanders. He was baptized Nov. 12, 1865, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 25, 1903, by Reed Smoot. He died April 6, 1932.
