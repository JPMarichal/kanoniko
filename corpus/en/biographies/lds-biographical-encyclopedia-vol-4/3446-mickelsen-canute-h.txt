Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mickelsen, Canute H.

Mickelsen, Canute H., Bishop of the Cache Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1929, was born Nov. 10, 1875, in Denmark. He was baptized April 1, 1899, ordained a High Priest by Wm. H. Mendenhall, and a Bishop Oct. 25, 1925, by Orson F. Whitney.
