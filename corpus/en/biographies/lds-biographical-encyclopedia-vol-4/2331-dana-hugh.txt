Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dana, Hugh

Dana, Hugh, Bishop of the Mesa 3rd Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Aug. 16, 1875, in Montpelier, Idaho, a son of Charles Carl Dana and Hannah Ellen Oderkirk. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1898-1900, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 16, 1928, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
