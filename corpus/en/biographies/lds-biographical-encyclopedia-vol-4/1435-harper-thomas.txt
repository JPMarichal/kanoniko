Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harper, Thomas

Harper, Thomas, Bishop of the Harper Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1866 to 1899, was born March 5, 1831, in Rickall, Yorkshire, England, the son of John Harper and Eliza Odell. He was baptized, Sept. 27, 1851, by Curtis E. Bolton, emigrated to America in 1853, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 9, 1877, by Lorenzo Snow. He died Nov. 7, 1899.
