Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Maricopa Stake

Bird, Reaves Alexander

Bird, Reaves Alexander, Bishop of the Papago Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1912 to 1914, was born Oct. 13, 1880, in Vernal, Uintah Co., Utah, a son of Bradford K. Bird. He was baptized in 1889, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 8, 1912, by George Albert Smith.
