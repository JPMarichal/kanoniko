Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Huskinson, John Willard

Huskinson, John Willard, Bishop of the Teton Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born July 23, 1872, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of William Huskinson and Jennette Cousins. He was baptized Feb. 27, 1881, was ordained a Seventy March 21, 1909, by Charles H. Hart, filled a short mission to the Central States in 1914, was ordained a High Priest June 26, 1916, by Mark Austin, and a Bishop Oct. 5, 1926, by Geo. Albert Smith.
