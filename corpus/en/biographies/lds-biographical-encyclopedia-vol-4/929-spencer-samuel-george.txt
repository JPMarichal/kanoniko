Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Spencer, Samuel George

Spencer, Samuel George, president of the Northern States Mission from 1895 to 1896, was born Feb. 14, 1864, in Salt Lake City,. Utah, a son of Daniel Spencer and Mary Jane Cutcliffe. He was baptized Aug. 19, 1877, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1885-1887, and was set apart as president of the Northern States Mission Jan. 4, 1895. He returned home Dec. 3, 1896.
