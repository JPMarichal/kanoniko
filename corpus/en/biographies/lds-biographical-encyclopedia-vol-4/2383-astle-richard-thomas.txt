Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Astle, Richard Thomas

Astle, Richard Thomas, Bishop of the Rupert 2nd Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1924, was born Sept. 2, 1871, in Montpelier, Idaho, a son of John Astle and Isabel Bradshaw. He was baptized July 18, 1880, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1911-1913, was ordained a High Priest March 3, 1906, by Aroet L. Hale, and sustained as Bishop June 27, 1920.
