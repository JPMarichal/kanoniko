Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilkins, Joseph David

Wilkins, Joseph David, Bishop of the Franklin Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1913 to 1922, was born April 25, 1877, in Coffee County, Alabama, the son of John T. Wilkins and Virginia Wilkerson. He was baptized Feb. 8, 1888, filled a mission to the Central States in 1910-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 15, 1913, by Hyrum M. Smith.
