Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nations, Thomas Jefferson

Nations, Thomas Jefferson, Bishop of the Franklin Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1903 to 1913, was born Feb. 17, 1849, in Georgia, the son of Israel Nations and Jane Cannally. He was baptized April 6, 1882, sustained as Bishop Nov. 8, 1903, and set apart by Andrew Kimball.
