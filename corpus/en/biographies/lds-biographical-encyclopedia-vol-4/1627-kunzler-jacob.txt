Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kunzler, Jacob

Kunzler, Jacob, Bishop of the Rosette Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1913, was born March 13, 1851, in St. Margrethen, Switzerland, a son of Jacob and Mary Kunzlet. He was baptized March 18, 1880, and ordained a Bishop May 29, 1910, by Heber J. Grant.
