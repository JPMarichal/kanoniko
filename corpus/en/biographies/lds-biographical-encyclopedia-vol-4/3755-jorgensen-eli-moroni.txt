Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jorgensen, Eli Moroni

Jorgensen, Eli Moroni, Bishop of the St. Anthony 1st Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1925 to 1928, was born Sept. 16, 1884, at Bear River City, Utah, a son of Rasmus Jorgensen and Bertha C. Peterson. He was baptized Sept. 9, 1892, ordained a Seventy Nov. 3, 1912, by J. T. B. Mason, filled a mission to Mexico in 1919, was ordained a High Priest April 20, 1924, by Hyrum G. Smith, and sustained as Bishop of the St. Anthony 1st Ward Jan. 11, 1925.
