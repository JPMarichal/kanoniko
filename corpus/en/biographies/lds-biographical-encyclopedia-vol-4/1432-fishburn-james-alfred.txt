Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fishburn, James Alfred

Fishburn, James Alfred, Bishop of the Brigham City 5th Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born April 14, 1877, in Brigham City, the son of Robert L. Fishburn and Laura Matilda Noble. He was baptized Sept. 20, 1885, ordained a Seventy Jan. 12, 1900, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1900-1902, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 18, 1921, by James E. Talmage.
