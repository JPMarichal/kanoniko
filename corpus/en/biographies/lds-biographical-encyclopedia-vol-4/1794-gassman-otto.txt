Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gassman, Otto

Gassman, Otto, Bishop of the Weston Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1902 to 1911, was born July 6, 1862, in Providence, Utah, the son of Hans Heinrich Gassman and Catherine Knopfell. He was baptized about 1871, came to Weston when six years old, and was chosen Bishop in 1902.
