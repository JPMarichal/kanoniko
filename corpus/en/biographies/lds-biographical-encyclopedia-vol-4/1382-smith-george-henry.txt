Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, George Henry

Smith, George Henry, Bishop of the Riverside Ward, Blackfeet Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1919, was born April 23, 1860, at Lehi, Utah, the son of Samuel T. Smith and Jane Dean. He was baptized July 11, 1869, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 14, 1915, by Orson F. Whitney.
