Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hill, Leonard Allison

Hill, Leonard Allison, Bishop of the Burrville Ward, Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, from 1902 to 1904, was born April 3, 1868, in Payson, Utah Co., Utah, the son of Allison H. Hill and Susannah Chase. He was baptized Sept. 7, 1876, ordained a High Priest Dec. 29, 1900, by William H. Seegmiller, and ordained a Bishop in 1902.
