Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Flamm, Henry

Flamm, Henry, president of the Swiss and German Mission from 1877 to 1879, was born Nov. 28, 1837, in Metzingen, Wurtemberg, Germany, the son of John Daniel Flamm and Elizabeth Barbara Flamm. He emigrated to America in 1853, was baptized Sept. 17, 1861, came to Utah in 1862, and was ordained a High Priest Feb. 2, 1883. After acting as president of the Swiss and German Mission from 1877 to 1879, he was called to settle in the Snake River Valley in Idaho. He died Aug. 19, 1913, at Rexburg, Idaho.
