Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Warner, Elisha

Warner, Elisha, Bishop of the Spanish Fork 2rid Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born April 24, 1889, in Spanish Fork, Utah, the son of James Warner and Dorcas Patterson. He was baptized July 3, 1898, ordained a High Priest Dec. 14, 1924, by Henry A. Gardner, filled a short term mission to the Northern States in 1926, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 14, 1925, by Rudger Clawson.
