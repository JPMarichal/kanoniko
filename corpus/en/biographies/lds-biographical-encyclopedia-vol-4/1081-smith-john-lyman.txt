Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, John Lyman

Smith, John Lyman, president of the Swiss and Italian Mission from 1856 to 1857, was born Nov. 17, 1828, at Portsdam, St. Lawrence Co., New York, the son of John Smith and Clarissa Lyman. He was baptized in 1836 by John Smith; suffered persecutions with the Saints in Nauvoo, and other places, came to the "Valley" in 1847; filled two missions to Europe, took part in the Indian wars in Utah, and died Feb. 24, 1898, in St. George, Utah.
