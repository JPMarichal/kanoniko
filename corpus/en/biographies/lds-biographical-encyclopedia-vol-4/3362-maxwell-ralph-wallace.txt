Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Maxwell, Ralph Wallace

Maxwell, Ralph Wallace, first counselor in the presidency of the Summit Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1924, was born May 11, 1862, in Peoa, a son of Ralph Maxwell and Isabel McGavin. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest Aug. 6, 1894, by Alma Eldredge, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1904-1906, and was set apart as first counselor in the stake presidency in June, 1921, by Geo. F. Richards. He died May 23, 1933, in Salt Lake City.
