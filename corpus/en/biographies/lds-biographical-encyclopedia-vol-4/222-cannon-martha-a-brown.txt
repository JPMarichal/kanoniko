Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cannon, Martha A. Brown

Cannon, Martha A. Brown, a member of the General Board of Relief Society from 1892 to 1907, was born in Ogden, Utah, a daughter of Francis A. Brown. She was educated in the Ogden public schools and the University of Utah and taught school in Weber County. She was an officer in the Young Ladies' Retrenchment Association of Weber Stake. She loved children, and because of her philanthropic work in their behalf the Martha Society of Ogden, an organization for the benefit of underprivileged children, is named for her. Sister Cannon established an annual Old Ladies' Day, when she entertained with dinner and program all the women over 70 in Ogden. She was a delegate to the National Council of Women and traveled extensively in the interest of the Relief Society. In April, 1878, she was married to Frank J. Cannon and became the mother of four children.
