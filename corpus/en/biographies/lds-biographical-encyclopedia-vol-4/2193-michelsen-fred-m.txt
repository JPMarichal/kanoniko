Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Michelsen, Fred M.

Michelsen, Fred M., first counselor in the Liberty Stake presidency, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Oct. 26, 1874, in Salt Lake City, the son of James M. Michelsen and Carolina Larson. He was baptized June 5, 1883, and ordained a High Priest Feb. 26, 1904, by Geo. Albert Smith.
