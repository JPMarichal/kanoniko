Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Lewis M.

Jensen, Lewis M., Bishop of the Imbler Ward, Union Stake, Oregon, from 1907 to 1909 and from 1913 to 1914, was born May 10, 1873, in Manti, Utah, a son of Hans Jensen and Marie Christensen. He was baptized June 2, 1881, ordained a High Priest March 20, 1904, by Lehi Wright, and a Bishop June 17, 1907, by Pres. Joseph F. Smith. He was sustained as Bishop for a second term Sept. 28, 1913.
