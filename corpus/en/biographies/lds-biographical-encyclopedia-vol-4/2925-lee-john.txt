Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lee, John

Lee, John, Bishop of the Grant Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1913 to 1930, was born Nov. 30, 1875, at Huntsville, Weber Co., Utah, a son of Niels Peter Lee and Mary Larson. He was baptized June 5, 1884, ordained a High Priest Dec. 2, 1906, by Heber Andrus, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1910-1912, and was sustained as Bishop of Grant Ward May 25, 1913.
