Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christensen, Hyrum

Christensen, Hyrum, Bishop of the Mayfield Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1917, was born Sept. 26, 1866, in Salina, Sevier Co., Utah, a son of Peter C. and Ane Kristine Christensen. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a Seventy by Heber J. Grant, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1898-1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop, and set apart to preside over the Mayfield Ward April 7, 1906, by Anthon H. Lund.
