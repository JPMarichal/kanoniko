Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

England, William T.

England, William T., Bishop of the Moreland Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1907 to 1927, was born Jan. 25, 1874, in Plain City, Utah, the son of James England and Laura Thuesen. He was baptized Aug. 28, 1882, by Lewis W. Shurtliff, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 11, 1908, by George Albert Smith.
