Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cahoon, George Edward

Cahoon, George Edward, Bishop of Leavitt, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1921 to 1930, was born Feb. 23, 1877, in Manti, Utah, the son of James Cordon Cahoon and Ellen Spencer Wilson. He was baptized May 7, 1885, ordained a Seventy May 20, 1906, by Wm. H. Steed, filled a mission to the Central, States in 1907-1909, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 20, 1921, by Rudger Clawson.
