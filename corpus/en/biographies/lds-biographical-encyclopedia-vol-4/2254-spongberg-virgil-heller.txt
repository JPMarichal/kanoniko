Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Spongberg, Virgil Heller

Spongberg, Virgil Heller, Bishop of the Virginia Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Dec. 4, 1894, in Preston, Idaho, a son of Charles J. Spongberg and Evelyn Heller. He was baptized Aug. 8, 1903, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 14, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard, and a Bishop Aug. 23, 1925, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
