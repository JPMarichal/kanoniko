Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tingey, Thomas Josiah, jun.

Tingey, Thomas Josiah, jun., Bishop of the Woodruff Ward, Woodruff Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1930+, was born May 16, 1888, in Woodruff, a son of Thomas J. Tingey and Annie Cox. He was baptized May 17, 1896, ordained an Elder Nov. 20, 1904, by his father, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1909-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 27, 1913, by Geo. F. Richards.
