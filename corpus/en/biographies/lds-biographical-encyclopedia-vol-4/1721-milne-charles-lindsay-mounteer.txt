Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Milne, Charles Lindsay Mounteer

Milne, Charles Lindsay Mounteer, Bishop of the Union Ward, East Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1918, was born June 3, 1871, in Woodhouse, Normanton, Yorkshire, England, emigrated to Utah with his parents in 1873, was baptized June 6, 1880, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1902-1904, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 20, 1910, by Francis M. Lyman.
