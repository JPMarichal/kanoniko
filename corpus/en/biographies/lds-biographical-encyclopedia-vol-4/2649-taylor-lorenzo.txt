Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, Lorenzo

Taylor, Lorenzo, Bishop of the Farr West Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Sept. 16, 1885, at Harrisville, Utah, a son of Joseph Allen Taylor and Mary Lake. He was baptized July 5, 1894, was ordained an Elder Feb. 4, 1904, by James Martin, filled a mission to Hawaii in 1904-1908, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 3, 1928, by Orson F. Whitney.
