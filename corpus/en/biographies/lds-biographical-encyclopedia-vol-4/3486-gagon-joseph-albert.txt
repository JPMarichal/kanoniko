Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gagon, Joseph Albert

Gagon, Joseph Albert, Bishop of the Knightsville Ward, Tintic Stake, Juab Co., Utah, from 1917 to 1924, was born Dec. 15, 1879, at Vernal, Uintah Co., Utah, a son of William H. Gagon and Lydia Ann Taylor. He was baptized when nine years old, ordained a Seventy in 1912, by Charles H. Hart, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1913-1915, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 26, 1916, by Rudger Clawson, and ordained a Bishop April 21, 1918, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
