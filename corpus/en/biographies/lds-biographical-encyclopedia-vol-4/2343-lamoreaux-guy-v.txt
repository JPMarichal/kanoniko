Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lamoreaux, Guy V.

Lamoreaux, Guy V., Bishop of the Chandler Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1928 to 1930+, was born July 6, 1887, in Preston, Idaho, a son of Archibald Orrell Lamoreaux and Lydia L. Crockett. He was baptized Oct. 20, 1895, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1911-1913, was ordained a High Priest in November, 1914, by Rudger Clawson, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 16, 1928, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
