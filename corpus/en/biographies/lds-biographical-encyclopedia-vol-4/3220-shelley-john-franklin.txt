Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shelley, John Franklin

Shelley, John Franklin, Bishop of the Shelley Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1895 to 1906, was born Aug. 30, 1862, in American Fork, Utah. He was baptized when eight years old, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 10, 1895, by Heber J. Grant.
