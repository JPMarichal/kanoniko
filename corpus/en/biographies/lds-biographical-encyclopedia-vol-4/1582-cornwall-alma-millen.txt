Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cornwall, Alma Millen

Cornwall, Alma Millen, Bishop of the Mill Creek Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Feb. 18, 1881, in Mill Creek, the son of Joseph Cornwall and Charlotte Carter. He was baptized July 4, 1889, ordained a High Priest Feb. 15, 1914, and a Bishop Feb. 13, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
