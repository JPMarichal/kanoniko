Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Koldewyn, Zwier Willem

Koldewyn, Zwier Willem, temporary president of the Netherlands Mission in 1882 and again from 1884 to 1885, was born March 18, 1830, at Deventer, Netherlands, a son of Jan Koldewijn and Elizabeth Veugelink.

He was baptized at Deventer April 7, 1872, by Jan Poorte and ordained a Deacon June 2, 1872, by Sybren van Dyk. When Pres. van Dyk left Holland in June, 1882, Brother Koldewyn succeeded him as president pro tem. The new president, Peter J. Lammers, arrived in November of that year, and when he had finished his mission in November, 1884, Brother Koldewyn again became president pro tem., serving this time until November, 1885, when John W. F. Volker arrived to take charge of the mission. Zwier Willera Koldewyn died at Ogden, Utah, May 14, 1889.
