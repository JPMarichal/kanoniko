Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

White, Levi Charles

White, Levi Charles, Bishop of the Giles Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1896 to 1907, was born March 5, 1868, in St. Joseph, Nevada, a son of John C. White and Mary Ann Ingram. He was baptized in 1887, and was ordained a Bishop May 31, 1896.
