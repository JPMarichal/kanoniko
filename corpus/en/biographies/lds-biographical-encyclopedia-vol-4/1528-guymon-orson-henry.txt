Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Guymon, Orson Henry

Guymon, Orson Henry, second counselor in the Carbon Stake presidency, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born April 6, 1878, in Fountain Green, Utah, the son of William Albert Guymort and Marcella Orilla Fowles. He filled a mission to the Central States in 1913-1915, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 19, 1915, by Arthur W. Horsley, and set apart as second counselor in the Carbon Stake presidency Sept. 29, 1929.
