Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hull, Alvin C.

Hull, Alvin C., Bishop of the Whitney Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1929, was born Feb. 12, 1880, in Preston, Idaho, the son of Robert McClellan Hull and Mary Ann Chadwick. After being ordained to the Priesthood, he filled a mission to the Central States in 1904-1906. He was ordained a High Priest March 7, 1915, by Orrin P. Miller, and a Bishop Dec. 3, 1922, by Hyrum G. Smith.
