Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Slater, Enos Arnold

Slater, Enos Arnold, Bishop of the Slaterville Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Oct. 16, 1889, at Slaterville, a son of James A. Slater and Mary E. Allred. He was baptized Oct. 16, 1897, filled a mission to the Central States in 1910-1912, was ordained a High Priest in April, 1923, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop June 3, 1928, by Orson F. Whitney.
