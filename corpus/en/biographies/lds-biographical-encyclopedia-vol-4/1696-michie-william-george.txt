Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Michie, William George

Michie, William George, Bishop of the Redcliff Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Oct, 19, 1875, in Salt Lake County, Utah, the son of Robert Michie and Frances Ports. He was ordained a High Priest Oct. 20, 1912, by William H. Smart, and a Bishop Feb. 28, 1925.
