Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wayment, Alma E.

Wayment, Alma E., Bishop of the Paul Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1921, was born May 13, 1879, in Plain City, Utah, a son of Samuel Wayment and Castina Chapman. He was baptized May 26, 1887, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1902-1904, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 3, 1915, by James E. Talmage, and was sustained as Bishop in 1917.
