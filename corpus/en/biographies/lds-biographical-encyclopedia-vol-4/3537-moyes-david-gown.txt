Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moyes, David Gown

Moyes, David Gown, Bishop of the Murtaugh Ward, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born April 13, 1889, in Huntsville, Utah, a son of William G. Moyes and Sarah Ann Allen. He was baptized June 5, 1897, ordained a High Priest Feb. 7, 1921, by Rudger Clawson, who also ordained him a Bishop Oct. 25, 1925.
