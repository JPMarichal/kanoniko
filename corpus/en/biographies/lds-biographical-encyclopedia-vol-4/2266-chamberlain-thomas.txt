Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Chamberlain, Thomas

Chamberlain, Thomas, Bishop of the Arco Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1923, was born May 24, 1876, in Orderville, Utah, a son of Thomas Chamberlain and Laura Fackrell. He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1899-1901 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 27, 1920, by Geo. F. Richards.
