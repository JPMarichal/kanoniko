Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Malad Stake

Beeton, James Andrew

Beeton, James Andrew, Bishop of the Woodruff Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, in 1930+, was born Aug. 7, 1897, in Marsh Valley, Oneida Co., Idaho, a son of William Beeton, jun., and Margaret Ann Knudsen. He was baptized Aug. 7, 1905, ordained a High Priest Jan. 11, 1931, by Thomas W. Richards, and ordained a Bishop April 12, 1931, by Richard R. Lyman.
