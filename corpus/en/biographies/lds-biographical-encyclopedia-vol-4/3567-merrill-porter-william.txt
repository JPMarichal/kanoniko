Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merrill, Porter William

Merrill, Porter William, presiding Elder in Naples (Merrill) Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, in 1884, was born Sept. 10, 1846, in Farmington, Iowa, a son of Hosea Merrill and Anna Saxton. He came to Utah in 1850, and was appointed presiding Elder in January, 1884, by Bishop Jeremiah Hatch. He died Aug. 29, 1884.
