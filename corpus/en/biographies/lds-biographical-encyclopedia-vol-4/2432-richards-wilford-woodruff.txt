Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Richards, Wilford Woodruff

Richards, Wilford Woodruff, Bishop of the Georgetown Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1901 to 1906, was born May 8, 1866, in Farmington, Utah, the son of Franklin D. Richards and Mary Thompson. He was baptized July 25, 1875, filled a mission to Virginia in 1891-1893, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 23, 1901, by Geo. Teasdale. He died Sept. 6, 1912, in Paris, Idaho.
