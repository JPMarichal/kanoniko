Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Westerberg, Charles Axel

Westerberg, Charles Axel, Bishop of the Glencoe Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1923 to 1929, was born July 8, 1876, in Vingaker, Sweden, a son of Erick E. Westerberg and Caroline Olson. He came to Utah in 1887, was ordained a High Priest March 9, 1918, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop Feb. 11, 1923, by Hyrum G. Smith.
