Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stone, David Lehi

Stone, David Lehi, Bishop of the West Daniels Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1912 to 1920, and Bishop of the Daniels Ward from 1928 to 1930, was born Jan. 5, 1884, in Logan, Utah, a son of Enoch Stone and Fanny Burrows. He was baptized March 23, 1892, ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 24, 1912, by David O. McKay, and was set apart as Bishop of the Daniels Ward Jan. 12, 1929, by Orson F. Whitney.
