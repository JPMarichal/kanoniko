Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Franklin Stake

Allen, Alexander Alma

Allen, Alexander Alma, Bishop of the Weston Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1877 to 1886, was born Sept. 28, 1846, in Nauvoo, Illinois, the son of Ezra H. Allen and Sarah B. Fisk. He came to Utah in 1852, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1885-1886, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 22, 1876, by Brigham Young, jun., and a Bishop May 21, 1877, by the same Apostle. He died July 31, 1916.
