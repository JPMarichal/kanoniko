Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lee, Hyrum

Lee, Hyrum, Bishop of the Bybee Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1912 to 1930+, was born Feb. 15, 1878, at Huntsville, Weber Co., Utah, a son of Niels Peter Lee and Mary Larson. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1886, ordained a High Priest March 4, 1911, by Anthony W. Ivins, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 30, 1912, by James E. Talmage.
