Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Welling, Job

Welling, Job, president of the Australasian Mission from 1875 to 1876, was born Jan. 9, 1833, in Andlum, Cheshire, England, a son of John Welling and Ann Key. Becoming a convert to "Mormonism" in his native land, he was baptized Jan. 9, 1852, and emigrated to Utah in 1856, settling in Farmington. He was set apart for a mission April 11, 1875, and returned from his mission March 13, 1877, after circumnavigating the globe. For several years he acted as second counselor to Bishop Hess of the Farmington Ward, and later as a counselor in the High Priests' quorum of the Davis Stake. He died at his residence at Farmington March 7, 1886, of heart disease, leaving a wife and 21 children. His first wife died some years previous.
