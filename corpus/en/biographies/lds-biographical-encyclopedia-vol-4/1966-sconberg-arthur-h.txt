Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sconberg, Arthur H.

Sconberg, Arthur H., first counselor in the Hollywood Stake presidency, California, from 1929 to 1930, was born Jan. 29, 1880, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Henry Seonberg and Emma Johnson. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1888, and ordained a High Priest Aug. 28, 1927, by David O. McKay.
