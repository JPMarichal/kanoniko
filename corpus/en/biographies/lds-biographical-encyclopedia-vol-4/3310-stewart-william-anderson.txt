Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stewart, William Anderson

Stewart, William Anderson, Bishop of the Inverury Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1879, was born June 2, 1830, in Tuscaloosa County, Alabama, a son of George Stewart and Ruth Baker. He was baptized in 1847, ordained a High Priest July 16, 1877, by Orson Hyde, who also set him apart as Bishop July 16, 1877. He died Nov. 29, 1911.
