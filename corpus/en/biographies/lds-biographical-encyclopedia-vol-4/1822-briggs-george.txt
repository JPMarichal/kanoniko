Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Briggs, George

Briggs, George, Bishop of the Archer Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1902 to 1907, was born Oct. 15, 1858, in Tupton, Derbyshire, England, the son of Charles Briggs and Mary A. Worrell. He joined the Church in June, 1876, and came to Utah soon after. He filled a mission to Great Britain in 1910-1911, and was ordained a High Priest Aug. 17, 1884, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop in 1902, by Abraham Owen Woodruff. He died Jan. 23, 1919.
