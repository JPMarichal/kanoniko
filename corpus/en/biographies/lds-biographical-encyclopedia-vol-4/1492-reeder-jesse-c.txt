Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Reeder, Jesse C.

Reeder, Jesse C., Bishop of the View Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1910 to 1916, was born July 23, 1876, in Brigham City, Utah, the son of George B. Reeder and Caroline Madsen. He was baptized Nov. 2, 1884, and ordained a Bishop April 17, 1910, having previously acted as presiding Elder of the Branch.
