Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Roderick, John P.

Roderick, John P., Bishop of the Samaria Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930, was born Dec. 11, 1880, in Samaria, Oneida Co., Idaho, a son of William Roderick and Annie M. Price. He was baptized in 1889 and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 10, 1927, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
