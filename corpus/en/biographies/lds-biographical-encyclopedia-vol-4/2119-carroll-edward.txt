Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carroll, Edward

Carroll, Edward, Bishop of the Orderville Ward, Kanab Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born Jan. 23, 1875, at Heber City, Utah, the son of Chas. N. Carroll and Kezia Giles. He was baptized May 3, 1883, and was ordained a High Priest Sept. 2, 1900, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop Dec. 10, 1920, by Geo. F. Richards.
