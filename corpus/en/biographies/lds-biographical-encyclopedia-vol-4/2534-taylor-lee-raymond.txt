Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, Lee Raymond

Taylor, Lee Raymond, president of the Nebo Stake of Zion, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born March 8, 1884, in Goshen, Utah, the son of Zachariah Shadrack Taylor and Nellie Olsen. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1892, filled a mission to California in 1904-1905, and was set apart as president of the Nebo Stake Nov. 13, 1924, by John A. Widtsoe.
