Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olson, Olof Angus

Olson, Olof Angus, Bishop of the Oxford Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born June 21, 1886, in Oxford, a son of Olof Regal and Betty Adelia O1son. He was baptized July 5, 1894, filled a mission to Sweden in 1912-1914, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 5, 1920, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop Oct. 18, 1928, by Richard R. Lyman.
