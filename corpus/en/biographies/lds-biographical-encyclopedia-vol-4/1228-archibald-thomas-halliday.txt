Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bear River Stake

Archibald, Thomas Halliday

Archibald, Thomas Halliday, Bishop of the Plymouth Ward, Bear River Stake, (Box Elder Co.) Utah, from 1891 to 1913, was born Jan. 24, 1858, in Croftheath, Linniskinshire, Scotland, the son of William Russell Archibald and Elizabeth Halliday. He emigrated to Utah with his parents in 1853, located in Plymouth in 1885, and was ordained a Bishop by Lorenzo Snow June 15, 1891.
