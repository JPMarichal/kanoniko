Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larsen, James Berkeley

Larsen, James Berkeley, president of the Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Feb. 27, 1889, in Mt. Pleasant, Utah, a son of Andrew Larsen and Christina Mathesen. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1897, and ordained a High Priest Aug. 27, 1916, by Orson F. Whitney.
