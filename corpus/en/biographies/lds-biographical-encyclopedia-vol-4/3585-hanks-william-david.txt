Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hanks, William David

Hanks, William David, president of the Union Stake, Oregon, from 1918 to 1930, was born Jan. 14, 1874, in Paragonah, Iron Co., Utah, a son of George William Hanks and Elizabeth Edwards. He was baptized July 16, 1882, ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 12, 1909, by Anthony W. Ivins, served as Bishop of the Union Ward from 1910 to 1914, and was set apart as stake president March 17, 1918, by Stephen L. Richards.
