Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gardner, Clarence

Gardner, Clarence, president of the Star Valley Stake, Wyoming, from 1913 to 1930+, was born Nov. 6, 1875, in West Jordan, Utah, the son of Archibald Gardner and Mary (Marie) Larsen. He was baptized July 3, 1884, ordained a High Priest Aug. 12, 1894, by Francis M. Lyman, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1898-1900, and was set apart as president of the Stake June 1, 1913, by Francis M. Lyman.
