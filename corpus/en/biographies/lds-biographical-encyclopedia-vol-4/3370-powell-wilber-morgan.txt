Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Powell, Wilber Morgan

Powell, Wilber Morgan, Bishop of the Upton Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born June 21, 1901, in Coalville, Utah. He was baptized July 3, 1910, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 6, 1930, by Orson F. Whitney.
