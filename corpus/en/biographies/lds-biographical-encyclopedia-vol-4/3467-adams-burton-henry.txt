Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Timpanogos Stake

Adams, Burton Henry

Adams, Burton Henry, Bishop of the Pleasant Grove 2nd Ward, Timpanogos Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born May 27, 1889, at Fountain Green, Utah, a son of William Henry Adams and Melissa Jane Caldwell. He was baptized July 4, 1897, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 21, 1928, by David O. McKay.
