Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nielsen, Jesse H.

Nielsen, Jesse H., Bishop of the Idaho Falls 2nd Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930+, was born May 10, 1885, in Mantua, Utah, a son of Lars P. Nielsen and Sarah Hansen. He was baptized May 10, 1893, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 12, 1928, by Rudger Clawson.
