Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, George Henr

Harris, George Henry Burriston, Bishop of the Salem Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1884 to 1900, was born April 10, 1857, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, the son of Geo. H. A. Harris and Ann Burriston. He came to Salem in 1883, as one of the first settlers, and was chosen a member of the High Council of the Bannock Stake. He was appointed Bishop Nov. 23, 1884.
