Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Condie, Parley M.

Condie, Parley M., second counselor in the Oneida Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1920 to 1921, and first counselor from 1921 to 1929, was born June 9, 1889, in Croydon., Utah, a son of Thomas Condie and Hannah Swann. He was baptized July 4, 1897, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1915-1918, and was ordained a High Priest June 6, 1920, by Richard R. Lyman, and set apart as 2nd counselor in the Oneida Stake. On Aug. 14, 1921, he was promoted to first counselor and set apart by James E. Talmage.
