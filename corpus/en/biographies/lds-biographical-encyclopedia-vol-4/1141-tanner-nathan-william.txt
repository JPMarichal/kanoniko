Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tanner, Nathan William

Tanner, Nathan William, Bishop of the Aetna Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1903 to 1918, was born Oct. 19, 1870, in Wanship, Summit Co., Utah, the son of John William Tanner and Lucy R. Snyder. He was baptized April 6, 1896, by Wm. H. Branch, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 23, 1902, by Joseph F. Smith.
