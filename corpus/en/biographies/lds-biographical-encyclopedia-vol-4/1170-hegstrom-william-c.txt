Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hegstrom, William C.

Hegstrom, William C., Bishop of the Central Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1926, was born Nov. 15, 1877, in Sweden, the son of Claus F. Hegstrom and Lovina Olson. He was baptized Oct. 11, 1890, emigrated to America, and was ordained a Seventy. He was sustained as Bishop May 25, 1924, and died May 21, 1926.
