Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Baker, Stephen Martindale

Baker, Stephen Martindale, Bishop of the Sevier Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born April 24, 1891, in Joseph, Sevier Co., Utah, a son of Orange T. Baker. He was baptized June 9, 1900, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 15, 1927, by Rudger Clawson.
