Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nish, William R.

Nish, William R., Bishop of the Park Valley Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1923, was born Sept. 10, 1884, in Clarkston, Utah, a son of Malcom Nish and Maria J. Godfrey. He was baptized Sept. 10, 1892, ordained a Seventy April 18, 1911, by Rulon S. Wells, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1911-1913, was ordained a High Priest in 1918, by David O. McKay and ordained a Bishop Aug. 1, 1920, by Stephen L. Richards.
