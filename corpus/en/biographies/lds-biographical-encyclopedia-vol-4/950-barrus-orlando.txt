Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barrus, Orlando

Barrus, Orlando, president of the Samoan Mission in 1896, was born May 29, 1870, in Grantsville, Utah, a son of B. F. Barrus and Lovinia Ann Steele. He was baptized when eight years old, set apart Aug. 11, 1893, for a mission to Samoa and returned Jan. 20, 1897. He acted as president of the mission from April 19 to Dec. 25, 1896.
