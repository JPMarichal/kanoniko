Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Holmes, Jonathan H.

Holmes, Jonathan H., a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company D, was born March 11, 1806, in Georgetown, Essex Co., Mass., was baptized by John F. Boynton in 1832, and came to Kirtland, Ohio, in 1834. There he made his home with the Prophet Joseph Smith. On April 13, 1837, he married Miss Rosetta Garter, at the home of the Prophet Joseph. Wilford Woodruff married Miss Phoebe W. Carter at the same time. Bro. Holmes remained with the saints until their expulsion from Nauvoo, and while on his way to the Rocky Mountains he enlisted with the Mormon Battalion and served through the campaign. He joined his family in the mountains (Utah) after his discharge, and became a resident of Farmington. He served as a member of the Davis Stake High Council at the time of his death Aug. 18, 1880.
