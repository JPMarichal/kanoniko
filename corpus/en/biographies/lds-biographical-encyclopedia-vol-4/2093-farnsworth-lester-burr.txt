Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Farnsworth, Lester Burr

Farnsworth, Lester Burr, Bishop of the Garcia Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1917 to 1928, was born Aug. 23, 1879, in Tuba City, Arizona, a son of Alonzo Farnsworth and Mary Ann Staker. He was baptized Aug. 23, 1887, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 13, 1917, by Anthony W. Ivins.
