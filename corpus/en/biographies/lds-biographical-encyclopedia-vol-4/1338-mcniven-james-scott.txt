Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcniven, James Scott

Mcniven, James Scott, Bishop of the Burlington Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1901 to 1919, was born June 21, 1848, in Arel, Pertshire, Scotland, the son of John McNiven and Janet McNiven. He was baptized in 1859 by Robert Hogg, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 26, 1901, by Abraham O. Woodruff.
