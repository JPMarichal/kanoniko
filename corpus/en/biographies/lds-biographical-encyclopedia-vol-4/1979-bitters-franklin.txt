Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bitters, Franklin

Bitters, Franklin, Bishop of the Millville Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born March 10, 1897, at Logan, Cache Co., Utah, the son of Frangott Bitters and Bertha Grelett. He was baptized March 28, 1905, ordained an Elder in March, 1918, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1918-1920, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 16., 1929, by George Albert Smith.
