Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bowring, John Franklin

Bowring, John Franklin, Bishop of the Brigham City 3rd Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born July 2, 1866, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Henry Ebenezer Bowring and Annie Newman. He was baptized in 1874, moved to Brigham City in 1877, filled a mission to Germany in 1901-1903, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 19, 1907, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 3, 1926, by John A. Widtsoe.
