Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jorgensen, Hyrum Andrew

Jorgensen, Hyrum Andrew, Bishop of the Peoa Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born May 14, 1874, in Peoa, the son of Anders Jorgensen and Inger Nielsen. He was baptized June 8, 1884, ordained an Elder and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1917-1918, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 28, 1923, by Thomas L. Allen, and ordained a Bishop July 26, 1925, by Rudger Clawson.
