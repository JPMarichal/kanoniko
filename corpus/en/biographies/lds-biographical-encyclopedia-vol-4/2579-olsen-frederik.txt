Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olsen, Frederik

Olsen, Frederik, Bishop of the Spring City Ward, North Sanpete Stake, Utah, from 1868 to 1882, was born July 26, 1824, in Kjolby, Denmark, a son of Ole Hansen and Bertha Olsen. He was baptized in 1854, came to Utah in 1857 and was ordained a Bishop May 2, 1868, by Orson Hyde. He died Sept. 2, 1920. (See also Emery Stake.)
