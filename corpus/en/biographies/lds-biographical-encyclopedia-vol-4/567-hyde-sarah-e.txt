Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hyde, Sarah E.

Hyde, Sarah E., a member of the general board of the Primary Association from 1892 to 1901, was born June 18, 1862, at Moroni, Utah, a daughter of Rasmus Justesen and Sarah Ann Shepherd. She was baptized in 1872 by Samuel Bunnel. She became the wife of Charles Albert Hyde and resided at Spring City, Sanpete Co., Utah, where her three children were born, namely, Grace Mabel, Maud and Barney Hugh.
