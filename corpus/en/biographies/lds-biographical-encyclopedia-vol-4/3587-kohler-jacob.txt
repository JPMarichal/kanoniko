Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kohler, Jacob

Kohler, Jacob, Bishop of the Union Ward, Union Stake, Oregon, from 1919 to 1930+, was born Jan. 7, 1869, in Ruderswyl, Canton Bern, Switzerland, a son of Jacob Kohler and Anna Barbara Schank. He was baptized June 8, 1895, ordained an Elder June 5, 1896, by Fred Thener, a Seventy June 26, 1907, by George E. Stoddard, a High Priest May 28, 1910, by Lehi Wright, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1916-1919, and was set apart as Bishop Sept. 23, 1919.
