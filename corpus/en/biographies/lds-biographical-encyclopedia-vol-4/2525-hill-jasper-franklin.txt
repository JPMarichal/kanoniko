Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hill, Jasper Franklin

Hill, Jasper Franklin, Bishop of the Payson 2nd Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930, was born Sept. 2, 1877, in Payson, Utah, the son of Allison H. Hill and Lurancy Chase. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1885, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 22, 1920, by Geo. F. Richards.
