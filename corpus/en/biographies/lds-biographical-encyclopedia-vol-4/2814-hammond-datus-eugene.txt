Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hammond, Datus Eugene

Hammond, Datus Eugene, president of the Pioneer Stake of Zion, Utah, from 1925 to 1930, was born Jan. 13, 1886, in Millville, Utah, the son of Datus R. Hammond and Sarah M. Gamble. He was baptized Jan. 22, 1895, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1906-1907, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 29, 1917, by Charles W. Penrose, and set apart as president of the Pioneer Stake in 1925.
