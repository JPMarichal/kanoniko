Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wade, Charles Franklin

Wade, Charles Franklin, Bishop of the Liberty Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1907 to 1909, was born Dec. 8, 1853, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Edward D. Wade and Belinda Hickenlooper. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 22, 1907, by David O. McKay.
