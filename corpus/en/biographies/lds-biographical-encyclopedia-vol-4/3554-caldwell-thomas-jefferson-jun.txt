Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Caldwell, Thomas Jefferson, jun.

Caldwell, Thomas Jefferson, jun., Bishop of the Jensen Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1925, was born March 5, 1868, in Fountain Green, Utah, a son of Thomas J. Caldwell and Mary Ann Petersen. He was baptized when nine years old, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1897-1898, and was ordained a Bishop May 18, 1916, by Rudger Clawson.
