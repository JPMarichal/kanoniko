Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clarke, Peter Drew

Clarke, Peter Drew, second counselor in the Lethbridge Stake presidency, Canada, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Jan. 6, 1896, in American Fork, Utah, the son of Peter G. Clarke and Margaret Vilate Green. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1904, ordained an Elder Jan. 3, 1916, by Ira C. Fletcher, and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1916-1917. He was ordained a High Priest Oct. 31, 1926, by Rudger Clawson and set apart as second counselor in the Lethbridge Stake presidency.
