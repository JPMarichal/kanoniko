Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wright, Joseph Brigham

Wright, Joseph Brigham, Bishop of the Midvale Ward, East Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1902 to 1915, was born May 17, 1861, in Lichfield, Staffordshire, England, a son of Joseph Wright and Elizabeth Smith. He was baptized when eighteen years old, emigrated to Utah in 1883, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 15, 1895, by Joseph E. Taylor, and was ordained a Bishop April 7, 1902, by John R. Winder.
