Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, James Milton

Taylor, James Milton, presiding Elder of the South Delta Ward or Delta 3rd Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1924, was born May 10, 1871, in Salem, Utah, the son of James Allen Taylor and Louisa Jane Taylor. He was baptized Feb. 19, 1883, was ordained a High Priest May 18, 1893, by Francis M. Lyman, and set apart as presiding Elder March 24, 1918, by Heber J. Grant.
