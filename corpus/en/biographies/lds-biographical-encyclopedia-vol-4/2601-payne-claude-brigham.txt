Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Payne, Claude Brigham

Payne, Claude Brigham, Bishop of the Vermillion Ward, North Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Oct. 5, 1878, in Glenwood, Utah, the son of Edward Payne and Emma Powell. He was baptized Nov. 7, 1886, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 18, 1926, by Stephen L. Richards.
