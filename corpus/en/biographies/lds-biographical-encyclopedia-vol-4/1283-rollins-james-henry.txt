Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rollins, James Henry

Rollins, James Henry, Bishop of the Minersville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1860 to 1869, was born May 27, 1816, in Lima, Livingston Co., New York, the son of John P. Rollins and Keziah Van Benthuizen. He was baptized in June, 1832, in Rock Creek, Jackson Co., Missouri, by John Carroll, came to Utah in 1848, and was ordained a Bishop April 7, 1860, by Amasa M. Lyman. He died Jan. 7, 1899, at Bridger, Wyoming.
