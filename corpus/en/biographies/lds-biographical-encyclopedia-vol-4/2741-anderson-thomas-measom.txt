Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Anderson, Thomas Measom

Anderson, Thomas Measom, Bishop of the Lake Shore Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Dec. 12, 1886, at Lake Shore, Utah Co., Utah, the son of George Anderson and Rosetta Measom. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1895, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 23, 1924, by Stephen L. Richards.
