Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Peter E.

Johnson, Peter E., Bishop of the Nampa 2nd Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Jan. 28, 1878, at Mineral Point, Wisconsin, the son of Ole C. Johnson and Nellie M. Larson. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1886, ordained a Seventy Nov. 5, 1901, filled a mission to the Middle States in 1901-1904, was ordained a High Priest and Bishop by Orson F. Whitney, presided over the Paul Ward, Minidoka Stake, from 1915 to 1917, and set apart as Bishop of the Nampa 2nd Ward Feb. 21, 1926.
