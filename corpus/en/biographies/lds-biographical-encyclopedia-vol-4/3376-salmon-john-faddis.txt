Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Salmon, John Faddis

Salmon, John Faddis, Bishop of the Grass Creek Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1902, was born Sept. 12, 1871, in Coalville, Utah, the son of Robert Salmon and Agnes Scott. He was baptized Sept. 12, 1880, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 23, 1901, by Reed Smoot.
