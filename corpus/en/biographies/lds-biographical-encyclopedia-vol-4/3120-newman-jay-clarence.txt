Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Newman, Jay Clarence

Newman, Jay Clarence, Bishop of the San Francisco Ward, San Francisco Stake, Calif., in 1927, was born Dec. 1, 1899, in Holladay, Utah, the son of Thomas S. Newman and Caroline Marie Wayman. He was baptized Jan. 4, 1908, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 10, 1927, by Rudger Clawson.
