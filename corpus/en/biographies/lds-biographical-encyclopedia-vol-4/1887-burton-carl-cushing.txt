Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burton, Carl Cushing

Burton, Carl Cushing, Bishop of the Highland Park Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born May 16, 1885, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Willard C. Burton and Mary J. Gardner. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood and filled a mission to Germany in 1907-1910. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 27, 1914, and a Bishop Nov. 17, 1928, by James E. Talmage.
