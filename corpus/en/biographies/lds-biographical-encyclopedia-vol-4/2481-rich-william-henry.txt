Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rich, William Henry

Rich, William Henry, second counselor in the Morgan Stake presidency from 1893 to 1900, and first counselor from 1900 to 1923, was born Sept. 4, 1857, in Centerville, Davis Co., Utah, a son of John Henry Rich and Lydia Pond. He was baptized May 15, 1869, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1885-1887, was ordained a High Priest April 21, 1893, and set apart as second counselor by Heber J. Grant. On Sept. 9, 1900, he was set apart as first counselor. He died Sept. 29, 1923.
