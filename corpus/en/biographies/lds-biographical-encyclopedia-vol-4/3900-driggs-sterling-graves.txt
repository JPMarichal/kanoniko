Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Driggs, Sterling Graves

Driggs, Sterling Graves, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born Feb. 12, 1822, in Pennsylvania, a son of Uriel Driggs and Hannah Ford. He joined the Church in Ohio and emigrated to Nauvoo, Ill., in 1840. After his arrival in Great Salt Lake Valley he assisted to establish the saints in their new home. In 1851 he accompanied Apostle Amasa M. Lyrman on an expedition to California and assisted in the founding of the settlement of San Bernardino. He married Sarah Rodgers in Salt Lake City May 29, 1855, and returned to California, remaining at San Bernardino until that settlement was vacated in 1857 when he settled at Parewan, Utah, where he died Dec. 3, 1860, from injuries received while operating a threshing machine.
