Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Corbridge, John T.

Corbridge, John T., Bishop of the Santa Monica Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1928 to 1930, was born Oct. 21, 1880, in Bountiful, Utah, the son of W. H. Corbridge and Emma Howard. He was baptized Nov. 17, 1895, ordained a Seventy May 7, 1900, by J. Golden Kimball, and filled a mission to the Society Islands in 1900-1904. He was ordained a High Priest Feb. 25, 1923, by Pres. Heber. J. Grant, who also ordained him a Bishop Feb. 26, 1928.
