Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Matthews, Daniel Alonzo

Matthews, Daniel Alonzo, Bishop of the Matthews Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1918 to 1923, was born July 18, 1887, in Matthews, a son of D. H. Matthews and Clara Wall. He was baptized Oct. 20, 1895, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 24, 1918, by Orson F. Whitney.
