Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barney, Joseph Orson

Barney, Joseph Orson, Bishop of the Clawson Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1925, was born Feb. 19, 1870, in Kanosh, Utah, the son of Joseph Smith Barney and Amanda Azelia Oviatt. He was baptized in April, 1879, ordained a High Priest Oct. 25, 1904, by Rudger Clawson, and ordained a Bishop May 13, 1917, by Hyrum M. Smith.
