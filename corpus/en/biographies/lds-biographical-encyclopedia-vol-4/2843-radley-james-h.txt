Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Radley, James H.

Radley, James H., Bishop of the Pocatello 4th Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1926, was March 31, 1882, at Birkenhead, Cheshire, England, a son of James H. Radley and Kate Taylor. He was baptized Jan. 29, 1908, ordained a High Priest Aug. 3, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard, and a Bishop Aug. 23, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
