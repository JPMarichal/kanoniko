Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hulet, Oscar Jones

Hulet, Oscar Jones, Bishop of the Summit Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, in 1930+, was born April 20, 1899, in Summit, Utah, a son of Oscar Willard Hulet and Susannah Jones. He was baptized July 6, 1907, ordained a High Priest Jan. 8, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 1, 1930, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
