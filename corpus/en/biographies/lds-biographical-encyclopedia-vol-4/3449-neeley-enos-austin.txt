Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Neeley, Enos Austin

Neeley, Enos Austin, Bishop of the Palisade Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born May 19, 1873, in Bear River City, Utah, a son of William Neeley and Mary C. Nelson. He was baptized May 19, 1881, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1897-1900, was ordained a High Priest May 16, 1916, by George S. Young, and a Bishop June 14, 1925.
