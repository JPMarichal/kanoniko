Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fish, Silas L.

Fish, Silas L., Bishop of the Snowflake Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1910 to 1916, was born Jan. 17, 1880, in Snowflake, a son of Joseph Fish and Adelaide M. Smith. He was baptized Jan. 17, 1888, ordained a Seventy Dec. 28, 1900, by Geo. Reynolds, filled a mission to Samoa in 1900-1904, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 15, 1910, by Rudger Clawson.
