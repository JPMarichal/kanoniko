Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shumway, William Howard

Shumway, William Howard, Bishop of the Snowflake Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arzona, from 1916 to 1919, was born July 20, 1877, in Johnson, Kane Co., Utah, a son of Charles Shumway and Elizabeth Jardine. He was baptized March 4, 1886, ordained a High Priest Nov. 15, 1909, by Samuel F. Smith, and a Bishop May 13, 1916, by Geo. F. Richards.
