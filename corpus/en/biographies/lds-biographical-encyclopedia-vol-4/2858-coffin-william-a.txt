Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Coffin, William A.

Coffin, William A., Bishop of the Cambridge Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1891 to 1894, was born Nov. 15, 1846, in Bonaparte, Iowa, a son of Wm. B. Coffin and Habagle Starbuck. He was called on a mission to the Southern States in 1889, and was ordained a Bishop Dec. 6, 1891, by Geo. C. Parkinson, and died at Cambridge, Idaho.
