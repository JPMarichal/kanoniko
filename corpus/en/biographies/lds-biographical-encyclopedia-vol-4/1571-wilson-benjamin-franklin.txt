Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilson, Benjamin Franklin

Wilson, Benjamin Franklin, Bishop of the Oakley 3rd Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1914 to 1917, was born July 15, 1872, in Eden, Utah, the son of Robert Wilson and Ann Blood. He was baptized in 1880, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 10, 1914, by Wm. T. Jack, and a Bishop Oct. 25, 1914, by David O. McKay.
