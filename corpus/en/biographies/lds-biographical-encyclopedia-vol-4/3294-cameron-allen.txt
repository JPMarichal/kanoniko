Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cameron, Allen

Cameron, Allen, presiding Elder of the Marysvale Branch, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1916, and Bishop of the Marysvale Ward from 1916 to 1917, was born April 8, 1885, in Panguitch, Garfield Co., Utah, a son of Benjamin Cameron and Sarah Elizabeth Allen. He was baptized June 18, 1893, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1904-1906, was sustained as presiding Elder Feb. 26, 1911, and was ordained a Bishop Dec. 17, 1916, by Anthony W. Ivins.
