Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burt, Andrew Hill

Burt, Andrew Hill, Bishop of the 21st Ward, Ensign Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1883, was born Oct. 21, 1828, in Dumfermline, Fifeshire, Scotland, a son of Andrew Burr and Isabella Hill. He was baptized May 19, 1848, by John Sharp, emigrated to Utah in 1852, and was ordained a Bishop July 5, 1877, by Orson Pratt. He died Aug. 25, 1883.
