Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Blake, Herbert J.

Blake, Herbert J., Bishop of the Emerson Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1926, was born April 10, 1885, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of Thos. Blake and Bettie Platt. He was baptized May 4, 1893, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 20, 1922, by Anthony W. Ivins.
