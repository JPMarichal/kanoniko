Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davey, Charles Edmund

Davey, Charles Edmund, Bishop of the Cannon Ward, Pioneer Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1930, was born April 19, 1872, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Charles Davey and Sarah Ann Butterworth. He was baptized in June, 1880, ordained a High Priest Nov. 18, 1901, by Joseph F. Smith, and a Bishop May 26, 1918, by Anthon H. Lund.
