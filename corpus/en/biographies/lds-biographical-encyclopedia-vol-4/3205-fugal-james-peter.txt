Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fugal, James Peter

Fugal, James Peter, Bishop of the Woodville Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1910 to 1916, was born Nov. 15, 1875, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, a son of Christian Fugl and Margaret Jensen. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1885, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 13, 1910, by Hyrum M. Smith.
