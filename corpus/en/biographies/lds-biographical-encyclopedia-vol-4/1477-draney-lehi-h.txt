Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Draney, Lehi H.

Draney, Lehi H., Bishop of the View Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1928, was born Oct. 19, 1882, at Plain City, Utah, the son of Samuel P. Draney and Angeline Richardson. He was baptized Nov. 2, 1890, ordained a High Priest Feb. 25, 1923, by John A. Widtsoe, and ordained a Bishop May 3, 1926, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
