Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Scholes, Walter Adam

Scholes, Walter Adam, Bishop of the River Heights Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born April 8, 1895, in Laie, Oahu, Hawaii, a son of Walter Scholes and Phoebe L. Adams, while his parents were laboring as missionaries in Hawaii. He was ordained a High Priest April 18, 1925, by Melvin J. Ballard, and a Bishop Oct. 20, 1928, by Orson F. Whitney.
