Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Zundel, Isaac David

Zundel, Isaac David, Bishop of the Woodruff Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1905 to 1914, was born Sept. 18, 1868, in Willard, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of Isaac E. D. Zundel and Elizabeth Jane Harding. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a High Priest March 9, 1902, by Oliver C. Hoskins, and ordained a Bishop June 25, 1905, by Rudger Clawson.
