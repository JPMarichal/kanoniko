Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ivie, Lloyd Oscar

Ivie, Lloyd Oscar, president of the Japan Mission from 1921 to 1923, was born Oct. 9, 1890, in Salina, Utah, a son of James Oscar Ivie and Anna Catherine Mortensen. He was baptized Oct. 9, 1898, filled a mission to Japan in 1911-1915, and was set apart for a second mission to Japan, to act as president of that mission, Jan. 18, 1921. He returned from this second mission in January, 1924.
