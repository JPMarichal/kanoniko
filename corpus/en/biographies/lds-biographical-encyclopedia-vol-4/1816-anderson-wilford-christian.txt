Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fremont Stake

Anderson, Wilford Christian

Anderson, Wilford Christian, Bishop of the Independence Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Aug. 26, 1889, in Redmond, Sevier Co., Utah, the son of Andrew P. Anderson (Andreas Hansen) and Karen Petersen. He was baptized Aug. 15, 1897, moved to Idaho in 1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 27, 1925, by Melvin J. Ballard.
