Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sinclair, David Raymond

Sinclair, David Raymond, Bishop of the Victor Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1893 to 1901, was born Sept. 20, 1861, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Peter Sinclair and Catherine Cook. He was baptized when eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 24, 1893.
