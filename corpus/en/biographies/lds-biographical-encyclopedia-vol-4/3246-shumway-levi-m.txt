Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shumway, Levi M.

Shumway, Levi M., Bishop of the Taylor Ward, Snowflake Stake, Ariz., from 1915 to 1918, was born Nov. 15, 1859, in Wellsville, Utah, a son of Charles Shumway and Louisa Minnersley. He was ordained a High Priest Oct. 30, 1904, by Jesse N. Smith, and a Bishop Aug. 13, 1915, by Rudger Clawson.
