Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Parker, William Russell

Parker, William Russell, Bishop of the Orton Ward, Lethbridge Stake, Canada, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Sept. 23, 1887, in Liberty, Idaho, the son of Oscar F. Parker and Annie Watkins. He was baptized Oct. 2, 1895, ordained an Elder Jan. 9, 1911, by John S. Morgan, filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1912-1914, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 13, 1922, by James E. Talmage.
