Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilson, Morris, jun.

Wilson, Morris, jun., Bishop of the La Verkin Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1904 to 1928, was born Sept. 13, 1874, in Mountain Dell, Utah, a son of Morris Wilson and Sarah Amelia Isom. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1898, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 25, 1904, by Rudger Clawson.
