Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thurber, Albert Daniel

Thurber, Albert Daniel, Bishop of the Dublan Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1907 to 1912, was born May 13, 1854, in Palmyra, Utah, a son of Albert K. Thurber and Thurza M. Berry. He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1880-1881, and was ordained a Bishop Dec. 10, 1907. He died Jan. 22, 1931.
