Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moffitt, John Clifton

Moffitt, John Clifton, Bishop of the Boneta Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1923, was born June 10, 1896, in Manti, Utah, the son of John W. Moffitt and Evelyn Cox. He moved with his parents to Boneta in 1907, filled a mission to California in 1916-1918, was ordained a High Priest June 29, 1919, by David A. Smith, and a Bishop June 27, 1920, by Rudger Clawson.
