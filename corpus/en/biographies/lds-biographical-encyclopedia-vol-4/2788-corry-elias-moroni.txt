Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Corry, Elias Moroni

Corry, Elias Moroni, Bishop of the Cedar 2nd Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Feb. 21, 1884, at Cedar City, Utah, the son of William H. Corry and Elizabeth L. Parry. He was baptized Feb. 21, 1892, filled a mission to California and Oregon in 1903-1905, and was sustained as Bishop June 26, 1921.
