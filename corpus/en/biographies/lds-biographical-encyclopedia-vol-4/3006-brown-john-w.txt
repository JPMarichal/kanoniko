Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brown, John W.

Brown, John W., second counselor in the St. Johns Stake presidency, Arizona, from 1915 to 1922, was born Nov. 2, 1858, in Kaysville, Utah, a son of Thomas D. Brown and Mary L. Willes. He filled a mission to Great Britain in 1886-1888, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 19, 1905, by Francis M. Lyman and set apart as second counselor in May, 1915, by Geo. F. Richards. He died Nov. 3, 1926, at St. Johns, Arizona.
