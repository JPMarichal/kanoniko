Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Octavius

Smith, Octavius, Bishop of the Darby Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1924, was born Jan. 23, 1878, in Hyrum, Utah. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1887, ordained a High Priest March 19, 1916, by James F. Griggs, and a Bishop May 21, 1921, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
