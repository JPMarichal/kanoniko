Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jackson, Alma Obray, sen.

Jackson, Alma Obray, sen., Bishop of the Avon Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1906 to 1926, was born July 24, 1851, at Council Bluffs, Iowa, the son of Henry C. Jackson and Ann Oades. He was baptized when about eight years old, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 16, 1906, by Orson F. Whitney. He died April 30, 1934.
