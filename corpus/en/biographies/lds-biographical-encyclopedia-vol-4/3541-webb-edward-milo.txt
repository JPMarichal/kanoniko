Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Webb, Edward Milo

Webb, Edward Milo, president of the Hollister Branch, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1924, was born Feb. 27, 1869, in Deseret, Utah, a son of Edward Webb and Elizabeth Horne. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1896, and sustained as presiding Elder March 24, 1921. He died April 4, 1924.
