Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sears, William Gailey

Sears, William Gailey, president of the Samoan Mission from 1899 to 1903, was born Feb. 17, 1873, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Isaac Sears and Sarah Jane Galley. He was baptized Feb. 17, 1881, was ordained a Seventy Nov. 11, 1893, by Heber J. Grant, and set apart for his first mission to Samoa, where he labored until 1896. He was set apart to preside over the Mission April 14, 1899, and returned home in 1903.
