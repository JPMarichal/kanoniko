Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stokes, Archie

Stokes, Archie, Bishop of the Unity Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1921, was born July 24, 1886, in Draper, Utah, the son of Thomas Stokes and Ellen L. Canfield. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1894, ordained a High Priest March 6, 1905, by David R. Langlois, and a Bishop Feb. 22, 1920.
