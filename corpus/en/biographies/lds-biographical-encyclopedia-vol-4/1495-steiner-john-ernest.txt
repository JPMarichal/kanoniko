Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Steiner, John Ernest

Steiner, John Ernest, Bishop of the Springdale Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1927, was born May 30, 1869, in Chaude-de-Fonds, Switzerland. He was baptized in April, 1877, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 18, 1901, by Helaman Pratt, and ordained a Bishop May 11, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard.
