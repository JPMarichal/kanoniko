Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Draper, William

Draper, William, presiding Elder of the Draper Ward, East Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1852 to 1856, was born Nov. 25, 1818, in Easterton, Wilts, England, a son of William Draper and Lydia Lathtop. He came to Utah in 1852, and bore the title of Bishop, he having acted as Bishop on the frontiers before his arrival in the Valley. He died June 29, 1898.
