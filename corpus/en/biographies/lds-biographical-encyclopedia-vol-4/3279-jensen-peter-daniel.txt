Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Peter Daniel

Jensen, Peter Daniel, Bishop of the Ephraim South Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1917 to 1930+, was born May 17, 1869, at Ephraim, Utah, a son of Jens P. Jensen and Dorthea Gregerson. He was baptized July 29, 1877, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 2, 1918, by Anthon H. Lurid.
