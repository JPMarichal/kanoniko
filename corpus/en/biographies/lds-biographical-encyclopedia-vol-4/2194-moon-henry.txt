Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moon, Henry

Moon, Henry, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 1st Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1856 to 1870, was born March 29, 1819, at Chorley, Lancashire, England, the son of Robert Moon and Ann Walton. He was baptized Oct. 6, 1838, came to Utah in 1850, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 21, 1856. He filled a mission to the United States in 1871-1872.
