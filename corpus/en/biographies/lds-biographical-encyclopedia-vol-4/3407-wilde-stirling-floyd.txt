Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilde, Stirling Floyd

Wilde, Stirling Floyd, Bishop of the Welling Ward, Taylor Stake, Canada, from 1929 to 1930+, was born June 30, 1887, in Provo, Utah, the son of Abraham M. Wilde and Louisa Chittenden. He was baptized July 14, 1895, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 20, 1929, by Geo. Albert Smith.
