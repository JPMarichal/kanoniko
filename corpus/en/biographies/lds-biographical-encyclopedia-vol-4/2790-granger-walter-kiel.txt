Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Granger, Walter Kiel

Granger, Walter Kiel, Bishop of the Cedar 3rd Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Oct. 11, 1888, at St. George, Washington Co., Utah, a son of Walter Granger and Anna Kiel. He was baptized Oct. 11, 1896, ordained an Elder Sept. 14, 1909, by John Henry Smith, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1909-1911, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 5, 1926, by Rudger Clawson.
