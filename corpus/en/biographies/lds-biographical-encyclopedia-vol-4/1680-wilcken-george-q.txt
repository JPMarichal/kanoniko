Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilcken, George Q.

Wilcken, George Q., Bishop of the Abraham Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930, was born May 24, 1894, in Mill Creek, Utah, the son of Charles Henry Wilcken and Haddie V. Carlisle. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 27, 1927, by Orson F. Whitney..
