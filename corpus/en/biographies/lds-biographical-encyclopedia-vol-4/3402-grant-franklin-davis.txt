Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grant, Franklin Davis

Grant, Franklin Davis, Bishop of the Stifling Ward, Taylor Stake, Canada, from 1902 to 1904, was born Sept. 18, 1859, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of George D. Grant and Susan Fairchild. He was baptized May 12, 1868, ordained a High Priest Dec. 11, 1897, by Lorenzo Snow, and a Bishop Sept. 12, 1902, by John W. Taylor.
