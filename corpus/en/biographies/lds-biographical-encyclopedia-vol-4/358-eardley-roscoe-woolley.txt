Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Eardley, Roscoe Woolley

Eardley, Roscoe Woolley, a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A. from 1913 to 1922, was born Feb. 13, 1880, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of James Walter Eardley and Catherine Adella Woolley. He was baptized June 8, 1888, ordained a Seventy Oct. 13, 1899, by George Reynolds, and filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1899-1901. He filled a second mission to the Netherlands in 1911-1913, as mission president.
