Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, John Hyrum

Taylor, John Hyrum, Bishop of the Orangeville Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Feb. 1, 1879, in Manti, Utah, the son of Jos. Hyrum Taylor and Martha Ann Tatton. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 13, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard.
