Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, David Almon

Johnson, David Almon, Bishop of the Moab Ward, San Juan Stake, Utah, from 1897 to 1903, was born April 8, 1861, in Santaquin, Utah, a son of Geo. W. Johnson and Eveline Burdick. He was baptized May 2, 1886, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 25, 1897, by John Henry Smith.
