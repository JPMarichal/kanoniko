Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Snelgrove, Charles Rich

Snelgrove, Charles Rich, Bishop of the Lincoln Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930, was born June 30, 1887, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Charles Richard Snelgrove and Emily Brooksbank. He was baptized July 6, 1901, ordained an Elder March 14, 1926, by Shipley D. Burton, filled a mission to Canada in 1926-1928, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 11, 1928, by James E. Talmage.
