Presidents: Cyrus H. Wheelock, 1878-1879; William Moroni Palmer, 1880-1889; John E. Booth, acting, 1883-1884; Dennis E. Harris, pro tem, 1888-1889; John E. Booth, 1889-1890; Charles W. Booth, John E.

Booth, John E., president of the Northern States Mission from 1883 to 1884, and from 1889 to 1890, died March 28, 1920, in Provo, Utah. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 494.)
