Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olson, Albert

Olson, Albert, second counselor in the Big Horn Stake presidency, Wyoming, from 1920 to 1930, was born Aug. 2, 1874, near Minneapolis, Minnesota, the son of Olof Olson and Karen Borg. He moved with his parents to Utah in 1876, and located in Nephi, moved to Big Horn in 1901, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 12, 1917, by Richard C. May, and set apart as second counselor in the Big Horn Stake presidency Feb. 20, 1921, by Geo. F. Richards.
