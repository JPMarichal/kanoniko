Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dewsnup, Claude Mace

Dewsnup, Claude Mace, Bishop of the Richmond Ward, San Francisco Stake, Calif., from 1927 to 1930+, was born May 16, 1886, in Deseret, Utah, the son of Hyrum Dewsnup and Emily Mace. He was baptized May 16, 1894, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 10, 1927, by Rudger Clawson.
