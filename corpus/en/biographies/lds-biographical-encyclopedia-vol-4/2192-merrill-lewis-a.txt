Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merrill, Lewis A.

Merrill, Lewis A., Bishop of the Salt Lake City 31st Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1911, was born June 23, 1874, in Richmond, Utah, the son of Marriner W. Merrill and Almira Lewis. He was baptized June 23, 1882, ordained a High Priest March 30, 1907, by Hugh J. Cannon, and a Bishop in 1908. He died June 1, 1915, in Salt Lake City.
