Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ellison, James E.

Ellison, James E., Bishop of the Raymond 1st Ward, Taylor Stake, Canada, from Feb. 14 to May 2, 1915, was born Sept. 9, 1873, in Kaysville, Utah, a son of Ephraim P. Ellison and Elizabeth Whitesides. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1883.
