Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Woolley, Ranald Mills

Woolley, Ranald Mills, Bishop of the Magna Ward, Oquirrh Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1923, was born Aug. 12, 1883, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Frank Albert Woolley and Eliza Kimball. He was baptized in 1891, ordained a High Priest March 3, 1916, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop Feb. 22, 1922, by Stephen L. Richards.
