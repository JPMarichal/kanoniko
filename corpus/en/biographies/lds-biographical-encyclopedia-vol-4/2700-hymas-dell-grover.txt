Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hymas, Dell Grover

Hymas, Dell Grover, Bishop of the Treasureton Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Aug. 31, 1894, in Treasureton, a son of Benjamin Hymas and Hannah Thurston. He was baptized Aug. 31, 1902, and ordained a High Priest May 16, 1926, by Stephen L. Richards; ordained a Bishop Feb. 19, 1928, by James E. Talmage.
