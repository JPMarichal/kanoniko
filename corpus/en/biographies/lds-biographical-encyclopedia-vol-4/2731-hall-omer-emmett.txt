Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hall, Omer Emmett

Hall, Omer Emmett, Bishop of the Pleasant Green Ward, Oquirrh Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1928, was born May 5, 1890, in Great Bend, Kansas, a son of Edmond Estep Hall and Anna Elizabeth Hurst. He was baptized June 7, 1903, came to Utah in 1911, was ordained a High Priest June 21, 1921, by James E. Talmage, and a Bishop June 20, 1923, by Melvin J. Ballard.
