Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Frei, Edward Rudolph

Frei, Edward Rudolph, Bishop of the Santa Clara Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1927, was born Sept. 25, 1867, in Santa Clara, a son of Rudolph Frei and Margarette Nageli. He was baptized Jan. 4, 1875, ordained a High Priest Nov. 29, 1901, by Abraham O. Woodruff, and ordained a Bishop in 1912.
