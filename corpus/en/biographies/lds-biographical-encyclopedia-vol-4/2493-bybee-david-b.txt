Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bybee, David B.

Bybee, David B., presiding Elder of the Uintah Branch, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1861 to 1864, was born Sept. 17, 1832, in Byram County, Kentucky. He was appointed presiding Elder in June,, 1861, and died Feb. 22, 1893.
