Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Despain, Alvin Augustus

Despain, Alvin Augustus, Bishop of the Granite Ward, East Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1920, was born Feb. 22, 1877, in Granite, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of William Joseph Despain and Sarah C. Egbert. He was baptized in May, 1885, ordained to the Priesthood and filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1911-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 19, 1917, by Heber J. Grant.
