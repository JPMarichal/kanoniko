Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whetten, Clifford Leon

Whetten, Clifford Leon, Bishop of the Chuichupa Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1923 to 1926, was born May 15, 1898, in Colonia Garcia, Mexico, the son of John Thomas Whetten and Agnes Belzora Savage. He was baptized June 3, 1906, ordained a High Priest Oct. 21, 1923, by Joseph C. Bentley, and a Bishop Nov. 26, 1923, by Richard R. Lyman.
