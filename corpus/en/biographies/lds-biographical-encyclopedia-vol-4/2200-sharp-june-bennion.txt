Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sharp, June Bennion

Sharp, June Bennion, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 31st Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930, was born Jan. 17, 1892, in Salt Lake City, the son of John Adam Sharp and Rebecca Bennion. He was baptized March 3, 1900, ordained an Elder by Edward H. Eardley May 13, 1912, and filled a mission to South Africa in 1912-1915. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 19, 1926, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
