Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robinson, Lot

Robinson, Lot, Bishop of the American Fork 4th Ward, Alpine Stake, Utah, since 1928, was born Jan. 19, 1880, in American Fork, the son of Wm. Walker Robinson and Sarah Eckersley. He was baptized July 5, 1888, was ordained a Seventy July 18, 1900, by Mathias Cowley, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1900-1902, was ordained a High Priest April 27, 1924, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 5, 1928, by James E. Talmage.
