Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Seamons, James William

Seamons, James William, Bishop of the Hyde Park Ward, Cache Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1930+, was born March 11, 1878, in Hyde Park, Utah, a son of Samuel Seamons and Sarah Hurren. He was baptized March 11, 1886, ordained a Seventy July 28, 1897, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1897-1899, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 11, 1923, by Rudger Clawson.
