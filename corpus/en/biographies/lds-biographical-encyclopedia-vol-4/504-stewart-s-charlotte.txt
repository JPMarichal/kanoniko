Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stewart, S. Charlotte

Stewart, S. Charlotte, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., is the daughter of William M. Stewart and Sarah E. Taylor. She was educated in the public schools, received an A. B. degree from the University of Utah, an M. A. degree from the University of New York and has nearly completed work for a doctor's degree at the Leland Stanford University, Calif. She has served as supervisor of health and physical education for girls in the Salt Lake high schools for seventeen years; was also supervisor of municipal recreation for the same period, is responsible for the institution of many public recreation projects such as, Christmas cheer week, civic opera, tennis courts, art programs, swimming, story telling, festivals, etc. She has been active in the auxiliary organizations since she was sixteen years old, served in the Sunday School, the M. I. A. as ward counselor, ward president, stake board member, and was called to the General Board in 1914. She was a member of the Deseret Gymnasium Board for eighteen years, and has assisted in the education of her brothers and sisters.
