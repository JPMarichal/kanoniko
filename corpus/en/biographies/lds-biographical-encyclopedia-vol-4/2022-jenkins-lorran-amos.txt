Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jenkins, Lorran Amos

Jenkins, Lorran Amos, Bishop of the Ivins Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Jan. 7, 1892, in Newton, Cache Co., Utah, a son of John Jenkins and Annie Clark. He was baptized Jan. 7, 1900, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 17, 1929, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
