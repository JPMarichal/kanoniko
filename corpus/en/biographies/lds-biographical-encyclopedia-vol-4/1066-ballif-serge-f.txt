Swiss and German Mission

From 1850 to 1851, and from 1898 to 1904 there was a distinct Swiss Mission. From 1851 to 1857 the Swiss Mission constituted the principal part of the Swiss and Italian Mission. From 1857 to 1861 the same mission was called the Swiss, Italian and German Mission. From 1867 to 1898 and from 1904 to 1930 it was called the Swiss and German Mission-sometimes written Swiss-German Mission. (See the Swiss Mission and the German Mission under their respective titles.)
