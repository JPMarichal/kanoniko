Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pugmire, Edward Mckay

Pugmire, Edward Mckay, Bishop of the St. Charles Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1906 to 1914, was born Oct. 20, 1861, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Jonathan Pugmire and Elizabeth McKay. He was baptized June 20, 1870, located in Bear Lake Valley with his parents in 1864, being one of the first settlers of St. Charles; filled a mission to Great Britain in 1893-1894, and a mission to California in 1902-1903. He was ordained a High Priest by Wm. Budge and a Bishop Dec. 2, 1907, by Rudger Clawson.
