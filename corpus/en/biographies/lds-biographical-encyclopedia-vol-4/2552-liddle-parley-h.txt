Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Liddle, Parley H.

Liddle, Parley H., Bishop of the McGill Ward, Nevada Stake, Nevada, from 1912 to 1914, was born Oct. 28, 1883, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Edward J. McGill and Maria A. Sears. He was baptized Oct. 31, 1891, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 26, 1912, by David O. McKay.
