Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Judd, Daniel Knapp

Judd, Daniel Knapp, Bishop of the Fredonia Ward, Kanab Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1929, was born Nov. 2, 1884, in Kanab, the son of Zadok Knapp Judd and Ada Howell. He was baptized in 1893, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 4, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
