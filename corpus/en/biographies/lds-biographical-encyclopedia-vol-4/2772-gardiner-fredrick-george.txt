Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gardiner, Fredrick George

Gardiner, Fredrick George, Bishop of the Panguitch South Ward, Panguitch Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Oct. 18, 1879, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, a son of Joseph Henry Cardiner and Eliza Ann Watts. He was baptized Nov. 26, 1888, ordained a High Priest May 28, 1916, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop June 5, 1921, by Geo. Albert Smith.
