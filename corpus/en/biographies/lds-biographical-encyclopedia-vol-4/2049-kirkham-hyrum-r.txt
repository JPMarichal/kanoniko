Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kirkham, Hyrum R.

Kirkham, Hyrum R., second counselor in the presidency of the Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1926, was born July 15, 1882, at Lehi, Utah, the son of George Kirkham and Mary Russon. He was baptized July 20, 1890, ordained a High Priest May 4, 1913, by Hyrum M. Smith, and a Bishop Aug. 22, 1915, by Pres. Jos. F. Smith. He served as Bishop of the Ucon Ward in 1915-1916.
