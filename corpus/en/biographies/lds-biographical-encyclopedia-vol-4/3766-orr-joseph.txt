Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Orr, Joseph

Orr, Joseph, Bishop of the Egin Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1915 to 1930, was born Jan. 8, 1877, at Freedom, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Richard Charles Orr and Caroline Derricott. He was baptized when about ten years old, and ordained a Bishop in January, 1915, by Anthony W. Ivins.
