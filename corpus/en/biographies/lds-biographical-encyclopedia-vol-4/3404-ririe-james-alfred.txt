Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ririe, James Alfred

Ririe, James Alfred, Bishop of the Magrath 2nd Ward, Taylor Stake, Canada, from 1922 to 1930, was born Jan. 21, 1885, in West Weber, Utah, the son of James B. Ririe and Elizabeth Morgan. He was baptized July 7, 1892, filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1904-1907, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 15, 1922, by Melvin J. Ballard.
