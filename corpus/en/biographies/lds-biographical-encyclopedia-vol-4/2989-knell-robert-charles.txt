Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Knell, Robert Charles

Knell, Robert Charles, Bishop of the Pinto Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1897 to 1903, was born Jan. 1, 1861, at Kaysville, Utah, a son of Robert Knell and Mary Crook. He was baptized when nine years old, ordained a Seventy Nov. 12, 1887, by Horace S. Eldredge, filled a mission to England in 1887-1889, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 15, 1897, by Francis M. Lyman.
