Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Humpherys, Hyrum Thomas

Humpherys, Hyrum Thomas, Bishop of the Paris 1st Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1898 to 1912, was born May 15, 1850, in Mansfield, Nottinghamshire, England, the son of Thomas Humpherys and Mary Sudbury. He emigrated to America in 1854, arrived in Utah in 1856, located in Paris, Idaho, in 1866, was ordained a High Priest by Wm. L. Rich, and ordained a Bishop in 1899 by Heber J. Grant. He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1887-1888.
