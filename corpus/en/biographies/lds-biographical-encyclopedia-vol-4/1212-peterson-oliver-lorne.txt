Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Oliver Lorne

Peterson, Oliver Lorne, Bishop of the Ovid Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, in 1925-1930+, was born April 12, 1888, at Ovid, the son of Andrew H. Peterson and Mary S. Larsen. He was baptized July 2, 1896, was ordained an Elder and called on a mission to the Northwestern States in 1899, was ordained a High Priest June 3, 1923, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., and ordained a Bishop Nov. 22, 1925, by Orson F. Whitney.
