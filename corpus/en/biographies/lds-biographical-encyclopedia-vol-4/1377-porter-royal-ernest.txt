Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Porter, Royal Ernest

Porter, Royal Ernest, presiding Elder of the Rich Branch, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1927 and in 1930+, was born Dec. 25, 1887, in Porterville, Utah, the son of John P. Porter and Amy Z. Porter. He was baptized June 28, 1896, was set apart as presiding Elder Oct. 1, 1922, by Pres. James Duckworth, and again set apart on May 25, 1930, to the same position by Rudger Clawson.
