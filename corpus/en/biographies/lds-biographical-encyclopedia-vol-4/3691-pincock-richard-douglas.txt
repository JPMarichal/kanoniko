Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pincock, Richard Douglas

Pincock, Richard Douglas, Bishop of the Ogden 2nd Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, in 19307, was born March 14, 1876, in Ogden, Utah, a son of John Pincock and Isabella Douglas. He was baptized June 29, 1884, ordained a High Priest Jan. 6, 1928, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop July 20, 1930, by Rudger Clawson.
