Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whiting, Lucius Burr

Whiting, Lucius Burr, Bishop of the Ballard Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1927, was born Oct. 1, 1878, at Springville, Utah. He was baptized Feb. 3, 1895, ordained a High Priest April 26, 1924, by Geo. F. Richards, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 18, 1924. The Ballard Ward was disorganized in 1927.
