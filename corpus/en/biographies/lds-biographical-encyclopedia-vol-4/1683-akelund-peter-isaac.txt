Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Akelund, Peter Isaac

Akelund, Peter Isaac, Bishop of the Altonah Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1924, was born July 17, 1858, in Ostersund, Sweden, the son of Eric Isak Eklund and Brita Pherson. He came to Utah in 1869, was baptized in 1870, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1886-1888 and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 30, 1916, by Jos. F. Smith, jun. Bro. Akelund acted as Bishop of Castle Dale from 1907-1912.
