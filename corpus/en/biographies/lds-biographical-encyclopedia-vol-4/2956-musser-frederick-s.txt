Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Musser, Frederick S.

Musser, Frederick S., Bishop of the Midview Ward, Duchesne Stake, from 1913 to 1915, and of the Myron Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1918, was born Oct. 1, 1878, in Lake View, Tooele Co., Utah, a son of A. Milton Musser and Annie Seegmiller. He was baptized Oct. 10, 1886, ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 21, 1913, by James E. Talmage, and set apart as Bishop of the Myron Ward Sept, 19, 1915.
