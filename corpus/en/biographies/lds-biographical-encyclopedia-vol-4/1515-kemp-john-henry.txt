Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kemp, John Henry

Kemp, John Henry, Bishop of the North Logan (formerly Greenville) Ward, Cache Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1930+, was born Sept. 14, 1878, in Lewiston, Cache Co., Utah, the son of John Kemp and Rachel B. Strickland. He was baptized Sept. 14, 1886, by his father, ordained an Elder April 18, 1902, by Joseph F. Pond, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 4, 1912, by Pres. Joseph F. Smith.
