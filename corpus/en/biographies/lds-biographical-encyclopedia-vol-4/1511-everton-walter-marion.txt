Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Everton, Walter Marion

Everton, Walter Marion, second counselor in the Cache Stake presidency, Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born Oct. 22, 1876, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of Marion C. Everton and Elizabeth Pitcher. He was baptized Oct. 22, 1884, by his father, ordained a Seventy June 16, 1896, by B. H. Roberts, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1896-1898, and was ordained a High Priest June 5, 1917, by George F. Richards.
