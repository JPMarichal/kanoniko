Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shumway, Wallace Everett

Shumway, Wallace Everett, Bishop of the Shumway Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1915 to 1919, was born May 15, 1879, in Upper Kanab, Utah, a son of Wilson G. Shumway and Maria Averett. He was baptized Nov. 3, 1887, ordained a Seventy Sept. 19, 1906, by Brigham H. Roberts, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1908-1910, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 13, 1915, by Rudger Clawson.
