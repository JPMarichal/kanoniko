Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Twede, Herman

Twede, Herman, Bishop of the Spring Lake Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930, was born Aug. 22, 1873, in Provo, Utah, the son of Chr. Fred. N. Twede and Christlane Petersen. He was baptized Jan. 21, 1897, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 25, 1922, by Melvin J. Ballard.
