Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rowley, John Emerson

Rowley, John Emerson, Bishop of the Mountain Home Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1929, was born Sept. 28, 1892, in Meadow, Utah, the son of John T. Rowley and Lucy M. Golding. He was baptized Oct. 3, 1903, and was ordaineda High Priest June 18, 1927, by David O. McKay, and a Bishop May 6, 1928, by Geo. F. Richards.
