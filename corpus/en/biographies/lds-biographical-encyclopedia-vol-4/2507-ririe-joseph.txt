Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ririe, Joseph,

Ririe, Joseph, first counselor in the Mount Ogden Stake presidency, Utah, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Feb. 27, 1872, in West Weber, Weber Co., Utah, a son of James Ririe and Ann Boyack. He was baptized Sept. 10, 1882, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1901-1903, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 5, 1910, and set apart as first counselor May 21, 1922, by David O. McKay.
