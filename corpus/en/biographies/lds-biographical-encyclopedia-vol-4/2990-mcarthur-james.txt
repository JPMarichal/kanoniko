Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcarthur, James

Mcarthur, James, Bishop of the St. George West Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1902 to 1925, was born Jan. 29, 1871, in St. George, a son of Daniel D. McArthur and Elizabeth Bullock. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1899-1901, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 14, 1902, by Rudger Clawson.
