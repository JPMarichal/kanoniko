Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Caldwell, Joseph Edwin, jun.

Caldwell, Joseph Edwin, jun., Bishop of the Molen Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1920, was born May 21, 1875, in Gunnison, Utah, the son of Joseph Edwin Caldwell and Anna P. Fjeldsted. He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1898-1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 6, 1910, by Geo. F. Richards.
