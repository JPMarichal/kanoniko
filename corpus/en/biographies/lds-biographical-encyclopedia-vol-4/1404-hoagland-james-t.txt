Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hoagland, James T.

Hoagland, James T., Bishop of the Brainwell Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1908 to 1916 (presiding Elder from 1907 to 1908) was born Jan. 25, 1876, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Edward T. Hoagland and Sarah E. Lish. He was baptized in August, 1884, filled a mission to California in 1898-1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 12, 1908, by John Henry Smith.
