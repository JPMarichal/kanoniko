Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Farr, Willard Ballantyne

Farr, Willard Ballantyne, Bishop of the Holbrook Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, in 1920, was born Nov. 10, 1888, in St. Johns, Arizona, a son of Willard Farr and Mary E. Ballantyne. He was baptized Dec. 5, 1896, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 24, 1920, by Orson F. Whitney.
