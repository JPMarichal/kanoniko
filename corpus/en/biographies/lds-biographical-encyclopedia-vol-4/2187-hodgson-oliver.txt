Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hodgson, Oliver

Hodgson, Oliver, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 3rd Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1906, was born March 8, 1857, in Ovenden, Yorkshire, England, the son of Henry Hodgson and Mary Shaw. He was baptized in 1865 at Bradford, came to Utah in 1866, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1893-1895 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 11, 1901, by Joseph F. Smith. He was killed Feb. 18, 1932, by a street car.
