Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wood, William

Wood, William, a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company C, was born Feb. 3, 1823, in Hereford, Herefordshire, England, a son of John Wood and Ann Lawrence. He was baptized Sept. 23, 1840, by William Green in England, emigrated to America in 1842, and arrived in Nauvoo May 6, 1843. After being exiled from Nauvoo with the other saints in 1846, he enlisted in the Battalion at Council Bluffs and marched all the long, long way to California. He finally arrived in the "Valley" in 1848. In 1852 he went to San Bernardino, California, but came back when that settlement was broken-up in 1857. He located in Minersville March 6, 1860.
