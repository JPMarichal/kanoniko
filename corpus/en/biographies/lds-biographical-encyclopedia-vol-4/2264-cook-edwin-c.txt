Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cook, Edwin C.

Cook, Edwin C., Bishop of the Leslie Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1926, was born Dec. 19, 1887, in Dingle, Idaho, a son of George Henry Cook and Elizabeth H. Nate. He filled a mission to the Western States in 1909-1912, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 20, 1914, by Joseph R. Shepherd, and a Bishop Nov. 4, 1922, by Geo. Albert Smith. He had acted as Bishop of the Dingle Ward, Montpelier Stake, from 1917 to 1919.
