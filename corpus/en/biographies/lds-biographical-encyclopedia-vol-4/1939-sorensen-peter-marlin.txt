Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sorensen, Peter Marlin

Sorensen, Peter Marlin, Bishop of the Axtell Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1915, was born Aug. 29, 1888, in Ephraim, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Niels Peter and Amelia Sophie Sorensen. He was baptized Sept. 7, 1897, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 3, 1912, by Francis M. Lyman.
