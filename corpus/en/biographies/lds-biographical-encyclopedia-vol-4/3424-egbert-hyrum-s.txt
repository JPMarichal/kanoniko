Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Egbert, Hyrum S.

Egbert, Hyrum S., Bishop of the Tetonia Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1907 to 1915, was born Dec. 30, 1860, in West Jordan, Utah, a son of Samuel Egbert and Margaret M. Beckstead. He was baptized Oct. 29, 1871, ordained a High Priest in May, 1901, by Abraham O. Woodruff, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 11, 1907, by Francis M. Lyman.
