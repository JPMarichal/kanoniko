Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larsen, Holger M.

Larsen, Holger M., president of the Danish Mission from 1929 to 1934, was born Nov. 4, 1888, in Copenhagen, Denmark, the son of Niels Larsen and Ida Camilia Bolle. He was baptized Oct. 20, 1897, and later emigrated to Utah. Elder Larsen filled his first mission to Denmark in 1909- 1911. On his second mission he was joined by his wife, Rachel S. Larsen, in the fall Of 1930. She was born May 1, 1894, at Soda Springs, Idaho, a daughter of Ludwig Suhrke and Susan Bossart. Their home is in Logan, Cache Co., Utah.
