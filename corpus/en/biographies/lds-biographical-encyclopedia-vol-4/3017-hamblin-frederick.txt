Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hamblin, Frederick

Hamblin, Frederick, Bishop of the Alpine Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1890 to 1891, was born Feb. 12, 1841, in Spring Prairie, Wisconsin, the son of Isaiah Hamblin and Daphne Haynes. He was baptized in 1850, and ordained a High Priest and a Bishop in 1890 by David K. Udall.
