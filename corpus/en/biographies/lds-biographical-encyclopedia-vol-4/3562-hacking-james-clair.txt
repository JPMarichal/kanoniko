Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hacking, James Clair

Hacking, James Clair, Bishop of the La Point Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Jan. 1, 1878, in Cedar Fort, Utah, a son of James Hacking and Anna Maria Glines. He was baptized July 11, 1886, ordained a Seventy in June, 1897, by Geo. Reynolds, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1897-1899, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 16, 1927, by Geo. Albert Smith.
