Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Deloney, Hyrum W.

Deloney, Hyrum W., Bishop of the Jackson Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1914 to 1915, was born May 12, 1887, in Evanston, Wyoming, the son of Charles DeLoney and Clara Jane Burton. He was sustained as Bishop Aug. 22, 1914, when the Jackson Branch was organized as a Ward.
