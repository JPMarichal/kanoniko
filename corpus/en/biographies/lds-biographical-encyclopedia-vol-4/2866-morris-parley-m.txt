Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Morris, Parley M.

Morris, Parley M., Bishop of the Merrill Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1930+, was born June 20, 1885, in Portage, Utah, a son of John S. Morris and Esther Williams. He was baptized June 20, 1893, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 24, 1919, by Hyrum G. Smith.
