Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larsen, Jacob Raphael

Larsen, Jacob Raphael, Bishop of the Goshen Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born April 6, 1892, in Mt. Pleasant, Utah, a son of Andrew Larsen and Christina Mathesen. He was baptized Aug. 15, 1903, ordained a High Priest Feb. 17, 1924, and a Bishop May 5, 1929, by Geo. Albert Smith.
