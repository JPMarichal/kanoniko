Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nielsen, Peter Aagaard

Nielsen, Peter Aagaard, Bishop of the 15th Ward, Salt Lake Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1927, was born Dec. 23, 1884, in Kvottrup, Faarup parish, Aarhus amt, Denmark, a son of Poul C. Nielsen and Sine Aagaard. He was baptized June 15, 1903, emigrated to Utah in 1893, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 17, 1920, by Richard R. Lyman.
