Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Hyrum

Peterson, Hyrum, Bishop of the Samaria Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1913 to 1914, was born Jan. 1, 1868, in Bear River City, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of John Peterson and Helena Olsen. He was baptized when ten years old, was ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1909-1911, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 26, 1913, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
