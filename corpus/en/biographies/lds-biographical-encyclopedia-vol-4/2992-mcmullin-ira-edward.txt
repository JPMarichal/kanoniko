Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcmullin, Ira Edward

Mcmullin, Ira Edward, Bishop of the Leeds Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Aug. 30, 1888, in Leeds, a son of Ira L. McMullin and Helen Leany. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1896, ordained a High Priest Nov. 24, 1912, by Edward H. Snow, and a Bishop Sept. 23, 1928, by David O. McKay.
