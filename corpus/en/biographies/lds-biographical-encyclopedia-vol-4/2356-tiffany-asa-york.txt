Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tiffany, Asa York

Tiffany, Asa York, Bishop of the Papago Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1915 to 1930+, was born Feb. 24, 1881, at Provo, Utah, a son of George M. Tiffany and Sarah Jane York. He was baptized Feb. 24, 1889, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1901-1905, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 22, 1915, by Heber J. Grant.
