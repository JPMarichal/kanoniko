Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Humpherys, Samuel

Humpherys, Samuel, Bishop of the Dingle Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1886 to 1914, was born Jan. 31, 1846, in Mansfield, Nottinghamshire, England, the son of Thomas Humphreys and Mary Sudberry. He emigrated to Utah in 1853, was baptized in 1857, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 27, 1886, by Wm. Budge.
