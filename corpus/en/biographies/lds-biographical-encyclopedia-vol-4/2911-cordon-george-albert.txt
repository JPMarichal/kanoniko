Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cordon, George Albert

Cordon, George Albert, Bishop of the Rigby Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1886 to 1917, was born Nov. 3, 1860, at Willard, Box EIder Co., Utah, a son of Alfred Cordon and Eraely Pridmore. He was baptized when about eleven years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 22, 1886, by John W. Taylor.
