Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ellison, James Edward

Ellison, James Edward, Bishop of the Layton Ward, North Davis Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1930+, was born Sept. 9, 1873, in Kaysville, Utah, a son of Ephraim P. Ellison and Elizabeth Whitesides. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1883, filled a mission to California in 1906-1908, was ordained a High Priest March 1, 1915, by David F. Fawns, and a Bishop Aug. 8, 1915, by Francis M. Lyman.
