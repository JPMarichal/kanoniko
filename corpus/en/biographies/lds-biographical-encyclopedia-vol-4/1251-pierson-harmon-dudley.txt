Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pierson, Harmon Dudley

Pierson, Harmon Dudley, Bishop of the Plymouth Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1880 to 1884, was born in April, 1816, in Connecticut, the son of Daniel Pierson and Julia Lester. He joined the Church at an early day, located in Nauvoo, Ill., served as a member of the Mormon Battalion, and came to Utah with the so-called sick detachment of said Battalion in 1847. He died July 3, 1892, in North Plymouth.
