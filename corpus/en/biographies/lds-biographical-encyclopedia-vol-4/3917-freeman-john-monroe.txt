Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Freeman, John Monroe

Freeman, John Monroe, one of the original pioneers of Utah, resided for a time in Nauvoo, where he became a member of the 31st Quorum of Seventy, organized in Nauvoo in October, 1845. He did not remain long in Great Salt Lake Valley, but went to Carson Valley, Nevada, in 1850, where he died of cholera.
