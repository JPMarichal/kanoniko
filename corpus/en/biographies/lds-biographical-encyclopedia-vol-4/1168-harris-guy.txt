Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Guy

Harris, Guy, Bishop of the Lago (Trout Creek) Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1926, was born April 28, 1896, in Lago, Idaho, the son of William McGee Harris and Harriet A. Carson. He was baptized April 28, 1904, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 19, 1921, by Geo. F. Richards.
