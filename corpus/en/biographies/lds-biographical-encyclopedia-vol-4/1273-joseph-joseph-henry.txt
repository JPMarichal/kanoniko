Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Joseph, Joseph Henry

Joseph, Joseph Henry, Bishop of the Adamsville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1876 to 1886, was born Nov. 17, 1830, in Stannely, Carmarthenshire, Wales, the son of Henry Joseph and Ann Thomas. He was baptized in 1849, located in Adamsville in 1867, and was ordained a Bishop prior to Jan. 6, 1876. He died Dec. 17, 1907.
