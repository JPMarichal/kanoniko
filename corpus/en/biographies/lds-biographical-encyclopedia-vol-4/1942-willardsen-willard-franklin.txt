Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Willardsen, Willard Franklin

Willardsen, Willard Franklin, Bishop of the Mayfield Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1926, was born Dec. 19, 1876, in Ephraim, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Willard Willardsen and Mary Ann Thorston. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1886, ordained a Seventy Feb. 15, 1904, by Joseph W. McMurrin, filled a mission to California in 1914-1915, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 6, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards.
