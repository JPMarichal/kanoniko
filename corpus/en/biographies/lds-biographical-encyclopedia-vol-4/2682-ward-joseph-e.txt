Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ward, Joseph E.

Ward, Joseph E., Bishop of the Liberty Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1904 to 1906, was born Nov. 25, 1875, at North Ogden, Weber Co., Utah, a son of James Ward and Harriet Brown. He was baptized when eight years of age, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1915-1916, and was sustained as Bishop Feb. 21, 1904.
