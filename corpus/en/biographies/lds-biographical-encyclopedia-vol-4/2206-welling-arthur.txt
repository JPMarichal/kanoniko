Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Welling, Arthur

Welling, Arthur, Bishop of the Liberty Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1926, was born Dec. 18, 1877, in Farmington, Utah, the son of Job Welling and Mariette Holmes. He was baptized July 25, 1886, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 24, 1920, by Rudger Clawson.
