Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bingham, Calvin Perry

Bingham, Calvin Perry, Bishop of the Mountain Dell Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1890 to 1900, was born Jan. 28, 1852, in Pottawattamie Co., Iowa, a son of Calvin Bingham and Lucretia Thorne. He came to Utah in 1854, was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest Nov. 17, 1889, by Reuben S. Collett, and a Bishop Nov. 15, 1890. He died June 4, 1922.
