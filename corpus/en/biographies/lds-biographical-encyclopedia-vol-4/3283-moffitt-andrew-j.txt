Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moffitt, Andrew J.

Moffitt, Andrew J., Bishop of the Manti Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1861 to 1874, was born May 7, 1818, at Inniskillen, County Fermanagh, Ireland, the son of James and Isabella Moffitt. He was baptized July 24, 1852, and was installed as Bishop April 21, 1861.
