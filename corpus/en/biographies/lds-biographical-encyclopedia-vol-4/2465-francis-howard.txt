Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Francis, Howard

Francis, Howard, Bishop of the Morgan Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Nov. 2, 1894, in Morgan, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of Samuel Francis, jun., and Sarah Jane Ursenbach. He was baptized Nov. 10, 1902, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 28, 1926, by George F. Richards.
