Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Finch, George Nephi

Finch, George Nephi, Bishop of the Eureka Ward, Tintic Stake, Juab Co., Utah, from 1921 to 1927, was born May 11, 1891, in Coshen, Utah Co., Utah, a son of Hyrum Finch and Mary Ann Garbett. He was baptized in June, 1899, ordained a High Priest Jan. 16, 1921, by James E. Talmage, who also ordained him a Bishop April 17, 1921.
