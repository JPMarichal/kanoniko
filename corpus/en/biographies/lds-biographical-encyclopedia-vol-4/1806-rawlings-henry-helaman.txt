Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rawlings, Henry Helaman

Rawlings, Henry Helaman, Bishop of the Fairview Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1930, was born April 8, 1893, in Fairview, the son of Ether Rawlings and Loretta Jane Inglet. He was baptized Nov. 1, 1903, ordained an Elder June 1, 1913, by Samuel W. Parkinson, and filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1913-1914. He was ordained a High Priest June 15, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard, who also ordained him a Bishop Sept. 12, 1920.
