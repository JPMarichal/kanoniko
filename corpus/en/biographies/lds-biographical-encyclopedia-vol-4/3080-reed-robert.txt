Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Reed, Robert

Reed, Robert, Bishop of the Graham Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1921 to 1927, was born Nov. 30, 1878, in Lexington, Tenn., a son of John T. Reed and Prescilla Adair. He was baptized in May, 1887, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 20, 1922, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
