Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Reid, Edgar Thomas

Reid, Edgar Thomas, Bishop of the Manti North Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born March 13, 1877, at Manti, Utah, the son of William T. Reid and Mary Adelaide Cox. He was baptized when about eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 5, 1923, by George F. Richards.
