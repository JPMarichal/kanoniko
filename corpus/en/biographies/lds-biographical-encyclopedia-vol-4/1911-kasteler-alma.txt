Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kasteler, Alma

Kasteler, Alma, Bishop of the Burton Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born May 9, 1892, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son Of Christian Kasteler and Marie Hostetler. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1900, and after being ordained an Elder Feb. 24, 1913, he filled a mission to Switzerland, Germany and the United States in 1913-1915. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 15, 1929, by James E. Talmage.
