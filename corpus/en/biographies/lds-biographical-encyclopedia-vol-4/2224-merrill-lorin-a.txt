Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merrill, Lorin A.

Merrill, Lorin A., Bishop of the Logan 7th Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1907 to 1913, was born April 4, 1876, in Richmond, Utah, a son of Marriner W. Merrill and Elmira J. Bainbridge. He was baptized May 1, 1884, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 25, 1908, by Heber J. Grant.
