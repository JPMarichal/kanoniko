Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Woodward, Cecil

Woodward, Cecil, Bishop of the Franklin Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1923, was born Dec. 24, 1881, in Franklin, the son of William Woodward and Rebecca Wright. He was baptized Dec. 24, 1889, ordained a High Priest Dec. 7, 1912, by James Johnson, and a Bishop Sept. 7, 1919, by Geo. F. Richards.
