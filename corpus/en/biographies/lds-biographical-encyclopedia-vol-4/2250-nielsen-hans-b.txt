Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nielsen, Hans B.

Nielsen, Hans B., Bishop of the Adams Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1923 to 1925, was born Dec. 31, 1876, in Hyrum, Utah, a son of Hans Enoch Nielsen and Mary Osborne. He was baptized Aug. 8, 1886, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 11, 1923, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
