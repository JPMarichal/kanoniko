Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Poulton, Ralph

Poulton, Ralph, Bishop of the Provo 6th Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1903 to 1916, was born Oct. 23, 1872, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of James Poulton and Caroline Harris. He was baptized in 1880, ordained a High Priest April 20, 1902, by Jos. B. Keeler, and a Bishop April 19, 1903, by Reed Smoot.
