Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, George Y., jun.

Smith, George Y., jun., Bishop of Smithfield 1st Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1917, was born April 8, 1869, the son of George Y. Smith and Johanna Luckie. He was baptized July 5, 1887, ordained an Elder Feb. 29, 1892, set apart for a mission to Great Britain April 8, 1892, and was ordained a Bishop Jan. 25, 1915, by Jos. F. Smith, jun. He died in Logan July 29, 1936.
