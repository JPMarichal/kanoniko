Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robinson, Carl

Robinson, Carl, Bishop of the Freedom Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1919 to 1930+, was born May 3, 1895, in Richmond, Utah, a son of John Robinson and Hilda Erickson. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained an Elder May 10, 1914, by A. Carlos McCoombs, filled a mission to California in 1914-1916, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 22, 1919, by James E. Talmage.
