Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Eckersley, George Teasdale

Eckersley, George Teasdale, second counselor in the Wayne Stake presidency, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born May 6, 1895, in Loa, Utah, a son of Joseph Eckersley and Sarah Ellen Wilkenson. He was baptized June 7, 1903, and ordained a High PrieSt March 23, 1924, by Stephen L. Richards.
