Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nicholls, Samuel Frederick

Nicholls, Samuel Frederick, Bishop of the McKinley Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930, was born Sept. 9, 1894, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Frederick William Nicholls and Anna Johnson. He was baptized Sept. 28, 1902, ordained a High Priest June 20, 1922, by Frank Y. Taylor, and a Bishop Aug. 1, 1924, by Geo. Albert Smith. He was elected auditor of Salt Lake City in 1929.
