Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Asay, Aaron

Asay, Aaron, Bishop of the Hatch Ward, Panguitch Stake, Utah, from 1892 to 1900 (presiding Elder from 1887 to 1892), was born Oct. 7, 1855, in Trenton, New Jersey, a son of Joseph Asay. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 30, 1892, by Francis M. Lyman.
