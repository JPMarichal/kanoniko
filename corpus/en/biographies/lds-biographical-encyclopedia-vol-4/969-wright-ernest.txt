Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wright, Ernest

Wright, Ernest, president of the Samoan Mission from 1916 to 1918, was born April 9, 1874, in Ogden, Utah, a son of Joseph Wright and Elizabeth Westwood. He was baptized in 1884, filled a mission to Samoa in 1896-1899, and a mission to Great Britain in 1904-1906. He was set apart as president of the Samoan Mission Feb. 2, 1916, and returned home April 3, 1918.
