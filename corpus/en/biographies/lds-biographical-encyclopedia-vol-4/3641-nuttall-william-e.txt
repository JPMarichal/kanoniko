Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nuttall, William E.

Nuttall, William E., Bishop of the Provo 3rd Ward, Utah, from 1862 to 1864, and Bishop of the Wallsburg Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1877 to 1886, was born Oct. 29, 1826, in Carlisle, Cumberland, England, a son of William Nuttall and Mary Langhorn. He was set apart to preside over the Wallsburg Ward July 15, 1877, by Franklin D. Richards.
