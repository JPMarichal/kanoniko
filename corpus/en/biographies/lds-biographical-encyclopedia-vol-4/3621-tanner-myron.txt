Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tanner, Myron

Tanner, Myron, Bishop of the Provo 3rd Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1864 to 1891, was born Jan. 4, 1826, in Bolton, Warren Co., N.Y., a son of John Tanner and Elizabeth Beswick. He was sustained as Bishop Oct. 25, 1864. He died Jan. 11, 1903.
