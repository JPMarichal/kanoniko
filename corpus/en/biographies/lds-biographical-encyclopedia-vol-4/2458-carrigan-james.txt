Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carrigan, James

Carrigan, James, presiding Elder of the Peterson Branch, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1879 to 1883, and Bishop of the Peterson Ward from 1884 to 1889, was born in 1841, in Ireland. He emigrated to Utah in 1862, after laboring as a missionary in England for seven years, and was ordained a Bishop in 1884.
