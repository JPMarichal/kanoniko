Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thornell, Jesse

Thornell, Jesse, second counselor in the North Sevier Stake presidency, Utah, from 1921 to 1924, was born Oct. 10, 1878, in Newpoint, Mo., the son of James Thornell and Martha Dyke. He was baptized in August, 1901, was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 20, 1919, by Geo. F. Richards and presided over the Salina 2nd Ward until Jan. 30, 1921, when he was set apart as second counselor in the Stake presidency by Geo. F. Richards.
