Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilson, Marion Lyman

Wilson, Marion Lyman, Bishop of the Pacheco Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, in 1930+, was born Oct. 26, 1898, in Logan, Utah, the son of Joseph E. Wilson and Lerona S. Monroe. He was baptized in 1906, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 2, 1930, by Stephen L. Richards.
