Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Greenwood, Carlos Barnard

Greenwood, Carlos Barnard, Bishop of the Inverury Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1905 to 1909, was born Oct. 31, 1879, in Inverury, Utah, a son of Barnard H. Greenwood and Eunice Howd. He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1902-1904, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 17, 1905, by Rudger Clawson.
