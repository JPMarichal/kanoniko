Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Orme, Samuel Washington

Orme, Samuel Washington, Bishop of the Wilford Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1903 to 1916, was born Sept. 19, 1858, at Tooele, Utah. He was sustained as Bishop of the Wilford Ward April 12, 1903, and died April 10, 1924.
