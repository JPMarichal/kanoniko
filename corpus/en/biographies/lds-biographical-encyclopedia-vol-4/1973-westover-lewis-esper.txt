Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Westover, Lewis Esper

Westover, Lewis Esper, president of the El Segunda Branch, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1929 to 1930+, was born March 10, 1890, in Sanford, Colorado, the son of Lewis Burton Westover and Eliza Jane Funk. He was baptized March 10, 1898, ordained to the Priesthood and filled a mission to the Society Islands in 1912-1915.
