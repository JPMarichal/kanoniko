Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cannon, William Telle

Cannon, William Telle, Bishop of the Liberty Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1920, was born Sept. 7, 1870, in Salt Lake City, a son of George Q. Cannon and Eliza L. Telle. He was baptized Sept. 5, 1878, filled a mission to the Netherlands and Belgium in 1899-1901, was ordained a High Priest June 25, 1916, by Hugh J. Cannon, and a Bishop Aug. 24, 1919, by Charles W. Penrose.
