Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Morgan, Nicholas Groesbeck

Morgan, Nicholas Groesbeck, a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A. from 1912 to 1935, was born Nov. 9, 1884, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of John Morgan and Helen M. Groesbeck. He attended the public schools, University of Utah, and graduated from the law course of Georgetown University, at Washington, D.C. in 1910, after which he practiced law in Salt Lake City. He married Ethel S. Tate, Jan. 8, 1909, and they have four children, viz., Dorothy, Helen, Marjorie and Nicholas Groesbeck, jun.
