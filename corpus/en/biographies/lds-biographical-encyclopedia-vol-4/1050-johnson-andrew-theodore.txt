Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Andrew Theodore

Johnson, Andrew Theodore, president of the Swedish Mission from 1912 to 1913, was born Jan. 2, 1881, in Wanship, Summit Co., Utah, a son of Andrew Johnson and Emma Rasmussen. He was baptized May 1, 1890, and filled a mission to Sweden in 1910-1913, about a year of which time he presided over the mission. He resides in Vernal, Uintah Co., Utah. (See History of the Scandinavian Mission p. 418.)
