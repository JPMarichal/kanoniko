Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barton, Stephen Smith

Barton, Stephen Smith, Bishop of the Paragonah Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1897 to 1908, was born Jan. 3, 1839, at Lebanon, St. Clair Co., Illinois, the son of John Barton and Sally Penn. He was baptized Feb. 14, 1847, at Lebanon, Illinois, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 25, 1897, by Francis M. Lyman.
