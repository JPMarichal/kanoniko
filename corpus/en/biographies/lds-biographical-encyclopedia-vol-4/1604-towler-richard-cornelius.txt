Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Towler, Richard Cornelius

Towler, Richard Cornelius, Bishop of the Mill Creek Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1914 to 1916, was born Sept. 5, 1880, in Mill Creek, the son of Daniel Towler and Sarah Ann Dunford. He was ordained a Bishop Feb. 22, 1914.
