Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pratt, William Franklin

Pratt, William Franklin, Bishop of the Hinckley Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1902 to 1912, was born April 27, 1867; in Duncan, Washington Co., Utah, the son of William H. Pratt and Ann Burgess. He was baptized June 11, 1876, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1894-1896, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 24, 1902, by Mathias F. Cowley.
