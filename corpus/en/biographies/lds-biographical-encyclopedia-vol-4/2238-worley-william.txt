Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Worley, William

Worley, William, Bishop of the Logan 1st Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born July 19, 1864, in Birmingham, England, a son of Henry Worley and Catherine Willmore. He came to Utah in 1869, was baptized in June, 1877, was ordained a High Priest April 28, 1900, by Marriner W. Merrill, and a Bishop June 5, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
