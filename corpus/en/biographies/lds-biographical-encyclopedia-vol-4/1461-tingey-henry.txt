Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tingey, Henry

Tingey, Henry, Bishop of the Brigham City 1st Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1896, was born Nov. 18, 1819, in Biggleswade, Bedfordshire, England, the son of James Tingey and Elizabeth Bonis. He was baptized Jan. 11, 1849, by Thomas Smith, emigrated to Utah in 1849, located in Brigham City in 1876, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 9, 1877, by Lorenzo Snow. He died Feb. 14, 1896.
