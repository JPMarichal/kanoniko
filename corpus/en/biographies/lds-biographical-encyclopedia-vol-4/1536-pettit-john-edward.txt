Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pettit, John Edward

Pettit, John Edward, first counselor in the Carbon Stake presidency, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born July 10, 1864, in Dunstall Green, Suffolk, England. He was baptized Aug. 21, 1881, emigrated to Utah in 1887 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 29, 1904, by John Henry Smith. He was set apart as first counselor in the Carbon Stake presidency Jan. 9, 1929, by Richard R. Lyman.
