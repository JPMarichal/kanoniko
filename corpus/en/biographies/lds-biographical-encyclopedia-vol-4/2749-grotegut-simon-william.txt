Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grotegut, Simon William

Grotegut, Simon William, Bishop of the Spanish Fork 4th Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1912 to 1925, was born Dec. 1, 1875, at Spanish Fork, the son of Carl L. Grotegut and Kirsten Siresen. He was baptized Aug. 7, 1884, ordained a High Priest Feb. 21, 1904, by Jonathan S. Page, jun., and ordained a Bishop Nov. 24, 1912, by Orson F. Whitney.
