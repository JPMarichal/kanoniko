Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Asay, Eleazer, jun.

Asay, Eleazer, jun., Bishop of the Leslie Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Feb. 8, 1892, in Monroe, Utah, the son of Eleazer Asay and Emmeline Zenetta Windsor. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1900, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 24, 1926, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
