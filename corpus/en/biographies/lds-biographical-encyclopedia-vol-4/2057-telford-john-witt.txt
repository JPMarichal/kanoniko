Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Telford, John Witt

Telford, John Witt, Bishop of the Lincoln Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1926, was born April 7, 1872, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of Robert Telford and Maria Spooner. He was baptized July 31, 1881, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1906-1909, was ordained a High Priest March 9, 1919, by Thomas W. Lee, and a Bishop Nov. 26, 1922, by John A. Widtsoe.
