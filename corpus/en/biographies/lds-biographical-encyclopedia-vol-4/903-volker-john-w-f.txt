Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Volker, John W. F.

Volker, John W. F., president of the Netherlands Mission from 1885 to 1889, was born Aug. 5, 1859, at Amsterdam, Netherlands, a son of Jan Willem Frederik Volker and Anna Catherina Scherrewitz. He was baptized March 6, 1877, by Peter J. Lammers, and filled two missions to the Netherlands, the first one in 1882-1883. Elder Volker, on his second mission, translated Parley P. Pratt's "Voice of Warning" and also the Book of Mormon into the Dutch or Netherlands language, the first book coming off the press in its second edition in 1889, the latter work (the first edition in the Dutch language,) being published in 1890, after Elder Volker's return home.

Shortly before his death, Elder Volker sojourned once more in the Netherlands for some time, visiting relatives and the places where he had labored as a missionary. He died March 8, 1932, at his home in Ogden, Utah.
