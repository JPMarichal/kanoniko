Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Parowan Stake

Adams, James Leech

Adams, James Leech, Bishop of the Parowan Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1911 to 1916, was born March 2, 1880, in Parowan, Utah, a son of William Adams, jun., and Mary Jane Richards. He was baptized March 21, 1888, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 24, 1911, by James E. Talmage.
