Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, Jared

Taylor, Jared, presiding Elder of the Marysvale Branch, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1890 to 1895, was born Jan. 26, 1845, in Elba, Genessee Co., New York, a son of Stephen Taylor and Martha Turner. He was baptized Jan. 2, 1889, and sustained as presiding Elder June 29, 1890. He died Aug. 6, 1900.
