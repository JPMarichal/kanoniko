Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Iversen, Heber Charles

Iversen, Heber Charles, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 2nd Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1899 to 1919, was born July 1, 1868, in Salt Lake City, the son of Soren Iversen and Caroline Mogensen, He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1893-1895, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 25, 1899, by Joseph F. Smith.
