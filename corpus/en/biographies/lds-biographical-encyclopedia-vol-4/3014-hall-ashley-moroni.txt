Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hall, Ashley Moroni

Hall, Ashley Moroni, Bishop of the Eagar Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Dec. 23, 1881, in Vernal, Utah, a son of John Oscar Hall and Lucy Ann Prisby. He was baptized May 29, 1894, ordained a Seventy May 28, 1910, by Anthony W. Ivins, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1912-1914. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 23, 1914, by David O. McKay, and a Bishop May 3, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
