Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robinson, Oliver Legrande

Robinson, Oliver Legrande, Bishop of the Egin (Brighton) Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1891 to 1902, was born Oct. 28, 1860, at Farmington, Utah, a son of Oliver Lee Robinson and Lucy Miller. He was baptized in October, 1868, was ordained a High Priest June 9, 1895, by John W. Taylor, and became Bishop in 1891.
