Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clark, John

Clark, John, Bishop of the Upton Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1882 to 1901, was born March 14, 1833, in Lincoln, England, a son of Thos. Clark and Ann Beck. He was baptized Jan. 29, 1849, emigrated to Utah in 1866, was ordained a High Priest in May, 1879, and a Bishop May 14, 1882, by Jos. F. Smith. He died Jan. 22, 1923.
