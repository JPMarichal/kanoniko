Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Crabtree, Charles Samuel

Crabtree, Charles Samuel, Bishop of the Idaho Falls Ward in 1907-1919 and Bishop of the Idaho Falls 1st Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from March to August, 1919, was born July 7, 1857, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Charles Crabtree and Elizabeth Aston. He was baptized in 1865, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 5, 1907, by Francis M. Lyman.
