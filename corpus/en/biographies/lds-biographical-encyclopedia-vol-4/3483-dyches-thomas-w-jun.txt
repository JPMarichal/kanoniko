Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dyches, Thomas W., jun.

Dyches, Thomas W., jun., Bishop of the Silver Ward, Tintic Stake, Juab Co., Utah, from 1926 to 1929, was born Sept. 23, 1877, in Wales, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Thomas W. Dyches and Sarah Ann Thomas. He was baptized when about eight years old, filled a mission to California in 1914-1916, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 16, 1927, by David O. McKay.
