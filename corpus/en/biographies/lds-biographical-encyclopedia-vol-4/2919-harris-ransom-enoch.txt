Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Ransom Enoch

Harris, Ransom Enoch, Bishop of the Perry Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1923 to 1930, was born June 30, 1872, at Portage, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of Enoch Harris and Jane A. Hoskins. He was baptized July 19, 1880, ordained a Seventy June 21, 1908, by Heber J. Grant, filled a mission to the Western States in 1909-1911, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 19, 1908, by William Selch, jun., and ordained a Bishop Sept. 9, 1923, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
