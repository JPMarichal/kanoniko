Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Musser, Don C. W.

Musser, Don C. W., president of the Turkish Mission from 1892 to 1894, was born Aug. 28, 1869, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of A. Milton Musser and Mary White. He was baptized when eight years old, set apart April 10, 1891, to fill a mission to Switzerland and Germany, and on Feb. 29, 1892, he was released from that mission and appointed to preside over the Turkish Mission.
