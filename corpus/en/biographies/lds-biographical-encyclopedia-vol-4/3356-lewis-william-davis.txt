Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lewis, William Davis

Lewis, William Davis, Bishop of the Park City Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1916, was born Sept. 7, 1858, in Provo, Utah, a son of William John Lewis and Jane Davis. He was baptized Sept. 7, 1866, sustained as Bishop of Vineyard ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, Dec. 24, 1899, serving until March 1, 1904. He was set apart as Bishop of the Park City Ward Oct. 10, 1909, by Heber J. Grant. He died April 27, 1922.
