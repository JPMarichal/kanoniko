Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Geary, Charles Calvin

Geary, Charles Calvin, second counselor in the Morgan Stake presidency, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Sept. 7, 1891, in Morgan, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of Hyrum P. Geary and Isabel M. Gibby. He was baptized Oct. 23, 1899, ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 14, 1921, by Richard R. Lyman and was set apart as second counselor Oct. 2, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
