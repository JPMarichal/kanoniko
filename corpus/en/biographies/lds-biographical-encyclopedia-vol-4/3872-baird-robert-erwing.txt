Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Baird, Robert Erwing

Baird, Robert Erwing, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born May 15, 1817, in Londonderry, Ireland, a son of James Baird and Elizabeth Erwing. He located in Weber County, served as a justice of the peace and a city councilman in Ogden and was a president of the 33rd Quorum of Seventy. He acted as presiding Elder of the Lynn District in Weber Coumty and died at Lynn, Aug. 24, 1875, leaving a large family.
