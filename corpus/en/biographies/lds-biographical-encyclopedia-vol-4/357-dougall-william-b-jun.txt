Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dougall, William B., jun.

DOUGALL, William B., jun., a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A., from 1902 to 1906, was born May 7, 1869, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of William B. Dougall and Maria Young. He was baptized in 1877, was manager of the Deseret Telegraph Company, worked on the Deseret News staff, filled a mission to England in 1892-1893, and was an expert pianist. He died April 10, 1906.
