Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Zabriskie, George Albert

Zabriskie, George Albert, Bishop of the Highland Ward, Alpine Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1920, was born Nov. 15, 1856, in Provo, Utah, the son of Alva Zabriskie and Susan Roberts. He was baptized in 1868 by Wm. J. Roberts, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 3, 1908, by Jos. B. Keeler, and ordained a Bishop July 25, 1915, by Francis M. Lyman. He died November 9, 1930, in American Fork.
