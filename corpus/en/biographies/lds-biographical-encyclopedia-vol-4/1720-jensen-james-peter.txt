Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, James Peter

Jensen, James Peter, Bishop of the Sandy 2nd Ward, East Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born Dec. 9, 1881, in Draper, Utah, a son of Jens Peter Jensen and Ann Petersen. He was baptized Aug. 17, 1890, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1902-1904, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 12, 1920.
