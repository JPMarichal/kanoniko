Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stoney, John Henry

Stoney, John Henry, Bishop of the Junction Ward, Garfield Stake, Garfield Co., Utah, from 1914 to 1915, was born April 3, 1871, at Minersville, Beaver Co., Utah, the son of Robert Steney and Sarah Johnson. He was baptized when about eight years old, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 2, 1914, by George F. Richards.
