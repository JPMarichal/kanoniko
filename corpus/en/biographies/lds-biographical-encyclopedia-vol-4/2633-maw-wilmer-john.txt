Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Maw, Wilmer John

Maw, Wilmer John, Bishop of the Plain City Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1926, was born Jan. 17, 1892, in Plain City, the son of John Maw and Annie C. Poulsen. He was baptized July 1, 1900, ordained a Seventy Nov. 18, 1914, by J. Golden Kimball, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1914-1919, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 28, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
