Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Van Dyke, Willia David, jun.,

Van Dyke, William David, jun., Bishop of the Ogden 3rd Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1914, was born Aug. 17, 1879, in Ogden, Utah, the son of William D. Van Dyke, sen., and Martha Evans. He filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1902-1904, and was ordained a Bishop Jan. 1, 1911, by David O. McKay. He died May 22, 1914, at Ogden.
