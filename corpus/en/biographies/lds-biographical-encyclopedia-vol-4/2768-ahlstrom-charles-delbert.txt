Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Panguitch Stake

Ahlstrom, Charles Delbert

Ahlstrom, Charles Delbert, Bishop of the Henrieville Ward, Panguitch Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1923, was born March 21, 1874, in Ephraim, Utah, a son of Peter Ahlstrom and Mary Larson. He filled a mission to Sweden in 1898-1900 and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 29, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
