Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stephens, Clarence Lee

Stephens, Clarence Lee, Bishop of the Victor Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1928, was born Dec. 2, 1887, at Menan, Idaho, a son of William N. Stephens and Susan Ellsworth. He was ordained a Bishop Feb. 7, 1926, by David O. McKay.
