Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nebo Stake

Broadbent, William Francis

Broadbent, William Francis, Bishop of the Santaquin 1st Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Feb. 2, 1887, in Mona, Utah, the son of James T. Broadbent and Sarah Alice Jarvis. He was baptized when about eight years old and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 5, 1927, by Rudger Clawson.
