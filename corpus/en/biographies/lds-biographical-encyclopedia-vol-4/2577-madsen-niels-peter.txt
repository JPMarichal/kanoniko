Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Madsen, Niels Peter

Madsen, Niels Peter, Bishop of the Mt. Pleasant North Ward, North Sanpete Stake, Utah, from 1878 to 1890, was born Sept. 17, 1832, in Svinninge, Sjaelland, Denmark, a son of Lars Madsen and Bodel Nielsen. He was baptized Dec. 2, 1854, emigrated to Utah in 1856, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 20, 1878, by Erastus Snow. He died Sept. 30, 1909, in Mt. Pleasant.
