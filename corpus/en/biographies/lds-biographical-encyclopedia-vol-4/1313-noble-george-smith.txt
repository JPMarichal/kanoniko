Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Noble, George Smith

Noble, George Smith, Bishop of the Amalga Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930 +, was born March 1, 1893, at Smithfield, Utah, the son of William A. Noble and Mary Jane Smith. He was baptized Aug. 31, 1901, ordained a Seventy and filled a mission to the Western States in 1919-1921, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 29, 1922, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop Jan. 10, 1925, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
