Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Allen, William Land Nuttle

Allen, William Land Nuttle, Bishop of the 21st Ward, Ensign Stake, Utah, from 1883 to 1893, was born May 22, 1825, in Kingston-on-Hull, Yorkshire, England, a son of Thomas Allen and Mary Nuttle. He was baptized Oct. 27, 1848, by Thomas Jackson, was ordained an Elder Jan. 21, 1849, and labored as a local Elder in Hull, England. On March 16, 1851, he was appointed to preside over the Hull Branch. He emigrated to Utah in 1853, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 22, 1856, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 16, 1883, by Angus M. Cannon. He died Nov. 16, 1893, in Salt Lake City, Utah.
