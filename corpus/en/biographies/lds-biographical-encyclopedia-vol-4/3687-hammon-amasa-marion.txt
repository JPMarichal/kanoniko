Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hammon, Amasa Marion

Hammon, Amasa Marion, Bishop of the Roy Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 19307, was born Sept. 24, 1884, in South Hooper, Utah, a son of Levi Baron Hammon and Martha Jane Belnap. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1892, ordained an Elder Nov. 19, 1905, by Nils C. Flygare, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1906-1908, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 11, 1925, by David O. McKay.
