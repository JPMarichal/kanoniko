Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ricks, Henry H. Peter

Ricks, Henry H. Peter, Bishop of the Teton Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1926, was born Jan. 19, 1898, the son of John Ricks and Julia F. Ricks. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 17, 1922, by Melvin J. Ballard.
