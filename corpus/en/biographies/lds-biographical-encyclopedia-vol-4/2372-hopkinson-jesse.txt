Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hopkinson, Jesse

Hopkinson, Jesse, Bishop of the Kanosh Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, from 1889 to 1897, was born Sept. 17, 1848, in Berry, Lancashire, England, a son of Thomas Hopkinson and Nancy Nuttall. He was baptized July 7, 1873, emigrated to Utah in 1886, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 26, 1889, by Francis M. Lyman.
