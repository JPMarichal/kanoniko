Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nelson, Lars L.

Nelson, Lars L., Bishop of the Provo 2nd Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1902 to 1929, was born Oct. 1, 1864, in Provo, Utah, a son of Lars P. Nelson and Mary M. Lofvendahl. He was baptized Nov. 3, 1872, filled a mission to Sweden in 1890-1892, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 20, 1902, by Reed Smoot. He died Sept. 15, 1933, in Provo.
