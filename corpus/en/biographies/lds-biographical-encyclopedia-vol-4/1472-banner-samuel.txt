Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burley Stake

Banner, Samuel

Banner, Samuel, president of the Unity Branch, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1907 to 1915, was born March 5, 1880, in Derbyshire, England, the son of Samuel Banner and Ellen Bradford. He was baptized by James Judd, and ordained a High Priest April 15, 1906, by Thomas L. Allen.
