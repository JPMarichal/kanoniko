Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Archibald, David Watson

Archibald, David Watson, Bishop of the Salem Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, in 1930+, was born Aug. 23, 1889, in Rexburg, Idaho, the son of Robert R. Archibald and Isabella Watson. He was baptized in 1899, ordained an Elder in the spring of 1909, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1909-1911, and was ordained a High Priest Sept. 25, 1921, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop May 11, 1930.
