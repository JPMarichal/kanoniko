Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Miles, Franklin Godbe

Miles, Franklin Godbe, Bishop of the St. George South Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930, was born April 7, 1865, in St. George, a son of Samuel Miles and Hannah M. Colburn. He was baptized April 30, 1873, ordained a High Priest March 10, 1907, by Geo. F. Whitehead, and a Bishop Sept. 20, 1925, by Stephen L. Richards.
