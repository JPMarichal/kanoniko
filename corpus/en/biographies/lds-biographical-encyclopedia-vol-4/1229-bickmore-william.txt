Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bickmore, William

Bickmore, William, Bishop of the Penrose Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1921, was born Oct. 15, 1878, in Paradise, Cache Co., Utah, the son of David N. Bickmore and Elizabeth N. Arthur. He was baptized Oct. 7, 1886, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 5, 1920, by Anthony W. Ivins.
