Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cheney, David Pierce

Cheney, David Pierce, Bishop of the Boyle Heights Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, in 1923, was born July 10, 1879, in Springville, Utah, a son of David Cheney and Lodena Hutchings. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1887, and ordained a Bishop March 11, 1923, by Heber J. Grant. He acted in this capacity until Aug. 26, 1923, when the ward was divided and he became Bishop of the Belvedere Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California.
