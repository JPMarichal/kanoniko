Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hammer, Josiah Austin

Hammer, Josiah Austin, Bishop of the Cardston Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1895 to 1908, was born Oct. 9, 1855, at Uintah, Weber Co., Utah, the son of Josiah Hammer and Elizabeth Osborn. He was baptized Feb. 21, 1864, and located in Cardston, Canada, in 1887, where he died May 19, 1922.
