Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Greenwood, William Snow

Greenwood, William Snow, Bishop of the Highland Ward, Alpine Stake, Utah, since 1922, was born July 23, 1874, in St. George, Utah, the son of Jacob Greenwood and Melissa Snow. He was baptized June 24, 1884, and was ordained a Bishop Jan. 29, 1923, by Rudger Clawson. He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1898.
