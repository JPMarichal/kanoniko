Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Parkinson, George Arthur

Parkinson, George Arthur, Bishop of the Adamsville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1925, was born May 21, 1866, in Beaver, Utah, the son of Thomas Parkinson and Mary A. Bryant. He was baptized March 4, 1877, by his father, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 12, 1924, by John A. Widtsoe.
