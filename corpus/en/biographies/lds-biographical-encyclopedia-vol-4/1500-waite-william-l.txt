Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Waite, William L.

Waite, William L., Bishop of the Burley 4th Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1924, was born Aug. 23, i872, at Bountiful, Utah, the son of John A. Waite and Margaret Barnes. He was baptized Nov. 3, 1881, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 28, 1921, by Rudger Clawson.
