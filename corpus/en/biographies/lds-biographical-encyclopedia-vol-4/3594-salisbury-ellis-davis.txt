Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Salisbury, Ellis Davis

Salisbury, Ellis Davis, Bishop of the Alicel Ward, union Stake, Oregon, from 1901 to 1906, was born Aug. 4, 1851, in Flintshire, Wales, a son of Joshua Salisbury and Martha Davis. He was baptized in 1860, emigrated to Utah in 1860, filled a special mission to St. Johns, Arizona, in 1884-1886, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 9, 1901, by Mathias F. Cowley. He died Feb. 20, 1915.
