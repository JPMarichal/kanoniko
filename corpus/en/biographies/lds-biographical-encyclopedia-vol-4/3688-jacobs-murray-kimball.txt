Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jacobs, Murray Kimball

Jacobs, Murray Kimball, Bishop of the Riverdale Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930, was born Sept. 3, 1880, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Henry C. Jacobs and Susan Stringham. He was baptized Feb. 5, 1889, ordained a Seventy Nov. 24, 1904, by Thos. Hull, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1904-1906, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 12, 1915, by Alva L. Scoville, and ordained a Bishop July 15, 1923, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
