Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Murphy, Emanuel B.

Murphy, Emanuel B., Bishop of the Upalco Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1930+, was born Oct. 14, 1854, in Weakly, Tennessee, the son of Emanuel B. Murphy and Mary Easters. He was baptized in 1862, ordained a High Priest, June 7, 1903, by George M. Young, and (already a Bishop) set apart Sept. 15, 1917, by Anthony W. Ivins.
