Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Portneuf Stake

Blanchard, Asael D.

Blanchard, Asael D., Bishop of the Woodland Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1912 to 1915, was born Jan. 24, 1883, at Logan, Utah, a son of Thos. Blanchard and Amelia Davis. He was baptized July 2, 1891, and was ordained a Bishop and set apart to preside over the Woodland Ward June 22, 1912.
