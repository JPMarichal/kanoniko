Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cramer, Eric Adolph

Cramer, Eric Adolph, Bishop of the Ashley Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1924, was born Sept. 30, 1888, in Huntsville, Utah, a son of John C. Cramer and Anna C. Larson. He was baptized July 4, 1897, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 21, 1919, by Geo. F. Richards.
