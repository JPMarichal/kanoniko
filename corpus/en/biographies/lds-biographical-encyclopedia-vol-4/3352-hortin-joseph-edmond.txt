Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hortin, Joseph Edmond

Hortin, Joseph Edmond, Bishop of the Rockport Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1917, was born Feb. 4, 1871, in Rockport, the son of John Hortin and Maria Wilkelson. He was baptized Aug. 16, 1881, ordained a High Priest Aug. 30, 1915, by Moses W. Taylor and ordained a Bishop Jan. 30, 1916, by Rudger Clawson.
