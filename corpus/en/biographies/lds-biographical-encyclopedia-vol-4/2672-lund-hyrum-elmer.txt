Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lund, Hyrum Elmer

Lund, Hyrum Elmer, Bishop of the Ogden 4th Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1927, was born Dec. 15, 1883, in Plain City, Utah, a son of Willard R. Lund and Bertha Anderson. He was baptized July 7, 1892, ordained a High Priest Feb. 9, 1917, by David O. McKay, and a Bishop Aug. 10, 1919, by Geo. F. Richards.
