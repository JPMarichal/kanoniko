Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sevey, George Thomas

Sevey, George Thomas, Bishop of the Chuichupa Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1911 to 1912, and from 1919 to 1923, was born Aug. 7, 1881, in Pine Valley, Utah, the son of Geo. W. Sevey and Martha N. Thomas. He was baptized Sept. 9, 1890, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 1, 1911.
