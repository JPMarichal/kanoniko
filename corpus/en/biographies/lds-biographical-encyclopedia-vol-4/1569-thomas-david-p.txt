Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomas, David P.

Thomas, David P., Bishop of the Oakley 4th Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1906 to 1915, was born Feb. 17, 1858, in Brigham City, Utah, the son of Wm. C. Thomas and Margaret Phillips. He was ordained a Bishop Jan. 28, 1906, by Francis M. Lyman, and died April 19, 1924.
