Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, John Christian

Peterson, John Christian, Bishop of the Welling Ward, Taylor Stake, Canada, from 1908 to 1924, was born Jan. 28, 1874, in Huntsville, Utah, the son of Christian Peterson and Emma Backman. He was baptized when eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 18, 1908, by Geo. F. Richards.
