Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pedersen, Olof Ignatius

Pedersen, Olof Ignatius, Bishop of the Logan 6th Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born Dec. 16, 1865, in Christiania, Norway, a son of Ole Pedersen and Ingeborg Hansen. He came to Utah in 1883, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1900-1902, and was ordained a Bishop June 5, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
