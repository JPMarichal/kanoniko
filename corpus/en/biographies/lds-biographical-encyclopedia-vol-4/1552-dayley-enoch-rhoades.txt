Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dayley, Enoch Rhoades

Dayley, Enoch Rhoades, Bishop of the Spring Basin (or Basin) Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1887 to 1892, was born Sept. 19, 1837, in Grand River Township, Caldwell Co., Missouri, the son of James Dayley, sen., and Isabella McBride. He was baptized in April, 1850, at Keg Creek, Iowa, came to Utah in 1852, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 22, 1887, by John W. Taylor.
