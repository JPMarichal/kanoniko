Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Atwood, Frank Dan

Atwood, Frank Dan, Bishop of the Pleasant Grove 3rd Ward, Timpanogos Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born June 12, 1882, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Millen Dan Atwood and Sarah Wanlass. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1891, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 7, 1929, by Richard R. Lyman.
