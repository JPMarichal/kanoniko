Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Seth Alvin

Johnson, Seth Alvin, Bishop of the Penrose Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1911 to 1915, was born Nov. 9, 1864, at Virgin, Washington Co., Utah, the son of Seth Johnson and Lydia A. White. He was baptized Oct. 5, 1873, by his father, ordained a High Priest Nov. 27, 1910, by Hyrum M. Smith, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 3, 1911.
