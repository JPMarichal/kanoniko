Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dutson, Willis Eddy

Dutson, Willis Eddy, Bishop of Oak City Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Oct. 28, 1897, in Oak City, Millard Co., Utah, the son of Eddy Quayle Dutson and Annie B. Anderson. He filled a mission to California in 1924-1926, and was ordained a High Priest and a Bishop March 23, 1930.
