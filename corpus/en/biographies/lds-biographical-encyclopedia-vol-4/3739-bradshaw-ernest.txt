Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bradshaw, Ernest

Bradshaw, Ernest, Bishop of the Egin Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont County, Idaho, in 1930+, was born in June, 1891, at Alma, Uinta Co., Wyoming, a son of Edward Bradshaw and Sarah E. Dexter. He was baptized June 10, 1899, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 29, 1930, by David 0. McKay.
