Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Magleby, John Ephraim

Magleby, John Ephraim, president of the South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1928, was born Nov. 27, 1862, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Hans Olsen Magleby and Gjertrud Marie Christensen (Roe). He was baptized July 6, 1871, ordained a Seventy Feb. 24, 1885, by Wm. M. Palmer, and filled a mission to New Zealand in 1885-1889. On a second mission to New Zealand, he presided over the New Zealand mission from 1898-1902. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 14, 1902, by George Teasdale, and set apart as president of the South Sevier Stake Jan. 30, 1921, by Richard R. Lyman.
