Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Warner, Homer Candland

Warner, Homer Candland, a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A. since 1929, was born Dec. 11, 1896, in Uintah, Weber Co., Utah, the son of William A. Warnet and Minnie Candland. He attended the public schools and graduated from the Ogden High School in 1914, and from the University of Utah in 1918 with an A. B. degree in Commerce and Finance. He was a 2nd lieutenant in the heavy artillery during the World war, taught school in the Davis High School and was physical director at the Deseret Gymnasium from 1920 to 1922; has also worked in scouting and "M" Men work and is at present in charge of the athletic program for "M" men of the Church. He married Grace Richards, June 16, 1921, who has borne him the following children: Homer, Richard, Emma Lou, and Gill.
