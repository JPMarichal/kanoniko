Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grow, Horace I.

Grow, Horace I., Bishop of the Roberts Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1920 to 1928, was born Oct. 8, 1877, at Huntsville, Weber Co., Utah, a son of William Grow and Esther Coffin. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1893, ordained a High Priest by Hyrum M. Smith, and ordained a Bishop March 5, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard.
