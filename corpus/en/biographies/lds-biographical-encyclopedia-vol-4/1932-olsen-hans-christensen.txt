Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olsen, Hans Christensen

Olsen, Hans Christensen, Bishop of the Hamilton Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1927, was born Sept. 11, 1880, in Ephraim, Utah, a son of James Olsen and Annie Catherine Christensen. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest June 1, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 30, 1925, by Stephen L. Richards.
