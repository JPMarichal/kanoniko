Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hess, James Henry

Hess, James Henry, Bishop of the Fielding Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1894 to 1906, was born March 6, 1858, in Farmington, Davis Co., Utah, the son of John W. Hess and Mary Ann Steed. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest in 1883, and ordained a Bishop by Francis M. Lyman Oct. 14, 1894. He died March 11, 1931.
