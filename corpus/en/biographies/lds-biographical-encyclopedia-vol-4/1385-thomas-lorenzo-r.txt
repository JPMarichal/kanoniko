Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomas, Lorenzo R.

Thomas, Lorenzo R., Bishop of the Blackfoot 2nd Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1909 to 1917, was born May 31, 1870, in Haverley, Staffordshire, England, the son of James Thomas and Elizabeth Richardson. He was ordained a Bishop May 9, 1909, by Heber J. Grant.
