Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Frandsen, George

Frandsen, George, Bishop of the Price Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1882 to 1896, was born May 31, 1834, on the isle of Langeland, Denmark, the son of Frands Jorgensen and Ane Marie Rasmussen. He was baptized Nov. 27, 1855, emigrated to Utah in 1856, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 20, 1882.
