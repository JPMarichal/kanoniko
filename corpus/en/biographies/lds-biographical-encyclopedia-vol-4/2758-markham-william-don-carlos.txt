Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Markham, William Don Carlos

Markham, William Don Carlos, Bishop of the Leland Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1900 to 1908, was born Jan. 6, 1855, at Palmyra, Utah, the son of Stephen Mark ham and Mary Curtis. He was baptized June 14, 1868, ordained a Seventy March 9, 1884, by William Stoker, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1885-1887, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 10, 1891, by George D. Snell, and ordained a Bishop April 29, 1900, by Reed Smoot. He died May 9, 1908.
