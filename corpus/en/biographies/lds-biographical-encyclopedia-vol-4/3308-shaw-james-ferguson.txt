Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shaw, James Ferguson

Shaw, James Ferguson, Bishop of the Brooklyn Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1904 to 1909, was born Feb. 21, 1848, in Garnkirk, Lanarkshire, Scotland, a son of Alexander Shaw and Elizabeth Ferguson. He was baptized in 1858, ordained a High Priest May 22, 1898, by George Teasdale, and a Bishop in 1904. He died May 1, 1932.
