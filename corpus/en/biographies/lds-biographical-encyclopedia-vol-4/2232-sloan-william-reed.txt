Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sloan, William Reed

Sloan, William Reed, Bishop of the Logan 2nd Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1916, was born March 30, 1881, in Manti, Utah, the son of Hugh Russell Sloan and Margaret Violet Reed. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1889, ordained a Seventy July 13, 1900, by J. Golden Kimball, and filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1900-1902. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 23, 1903, by Heber S. Allen, and a Bishop by Mathias F. Cowley (Kimball Ward, Canada). He acted as president of the Northwestern States Mission in 1927-1934.
