Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sorenson, Soren Christian

Sorenson, Soren Christian, Bishop of the Lehi Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1895 to 1905, was born Feb. 16, 1859, at Hadsund, Aalborg amt, Denmark, a son of Mads Sorenson and Kirsten Christensen. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 25, 1895, by Brigham Young, jun. He died July 7, 1933.
