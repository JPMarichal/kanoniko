Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Romney, Park

Romney, Park, Bishop of the Cornish Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1922, was born March 25, 1883, at St. Johns, Arizona, the son of Miles P. Romney and Catherine Jane Cottam. He was baptized by Gaskell Romney, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 20, 1918, by Hyrum G. Smith.
