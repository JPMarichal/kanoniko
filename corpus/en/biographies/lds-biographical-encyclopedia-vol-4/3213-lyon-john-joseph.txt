Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lyon, John Joseph

Lyon, John Joseph, Bishop of the Firth Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born April 10, 1881, in Fairview, Idaho, a son of Charles Lyon and Mary Balls. He was baptized in May, 1897, ordained an Elder Oct. 12, 1902, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1905-1907, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 19, 1917, by Geo. Albert Smith, and a Bishop Nov. 10, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
