Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bagley, Edward Carroll

Bagley, Edward Carroll, Bishop of the Koosharem Ward, Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born Feb. 28, 1873, in Payson, Utah Co., Utah, the son of Edward Alma Bagley and Mary McClellan. He was baptized May 5, 1881, ordained a High Priest March 13, 1904, by Wm. H. Seegmiller, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 26, 1920, by Hyrum G. Smith.
