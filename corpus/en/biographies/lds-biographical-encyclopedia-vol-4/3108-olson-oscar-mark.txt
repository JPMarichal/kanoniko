Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olson, Oscar Mark

Olson, Oscar Mark, Bishop of the 24th Ward, Salt Lake Stake, Utah, since 1930, was born May 15, 1886, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of John Fred Olson and Anna Olson. He was baptized June 4, 1894, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Sweden in 1911-1914, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 28, 1930, by Richard R. Lyman.
