Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olsen, Henning

Olsen, Henning, Bishop of the Castle Dale Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1882 to 1896, was born Jan. 3, 1830, in Ostrup, Denmark, the son of Ole Petersen and Marie Skradder. He came to Utah in 1861 and lived in Spring City until 1882, when he was called to Castle Dale to preside. He was ordained a Bishop Aug. 13, 1882, by Erastus Snow. He died Jan. 12, 1904.
