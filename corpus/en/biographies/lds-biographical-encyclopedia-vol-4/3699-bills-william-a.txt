Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bills, William A.

Bills, William A., Bishop of the South Jordan Ward, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1900, was born Aug. 5, 1835, in Pittsburg, Pennsylvania. He emigrated to Utah in 1848, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 17, 1877, by Daniel H. Wells.
