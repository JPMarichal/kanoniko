Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, John T.

Peterson, John T., Bishop of the Ovid Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1918 to 1925, was born Oct. 10, 1870, in Ovid, Idaho, the son of Thomas C. Peterson and Casty Peterson. He was baptized July 27, 1879, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 13, 1908, and ordained a Bishop June 22, 1918.
