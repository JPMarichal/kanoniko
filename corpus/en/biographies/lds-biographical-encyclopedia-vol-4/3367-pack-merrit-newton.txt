Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pack, Merrit Newton

Pack, Merrit Newton, Bishop of the Kamas Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1916, was born May 1, 1856, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of John Pack and Julia Ives. He was baptized Sept. 23, i886, ordained a High Priest May 19, 1901, by Joseph F. Smith, and sustained as Bishop Jan. 16, 1910.
