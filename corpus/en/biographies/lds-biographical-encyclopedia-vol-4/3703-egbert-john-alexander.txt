Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Egbert, John Alexander

Egbert, John Alexander, Bishop of the West Jordan Ward, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1892 to 1921, was born March 28, 1842, in Hancock County, Ill., a son of Samuel and Margaret M. B. Egbert. He was baptized April 20, 1851, ordained a Seventy June 15, 1884, by William W. Taylor, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 1, 1891, by Lorenzo Snow.
