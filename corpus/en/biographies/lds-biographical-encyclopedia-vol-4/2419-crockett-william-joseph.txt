Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Crockett, William Joseph

Crockett, William Joseph, Bishop of the Montpelier 2nd Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1922, was born Feb. 10, 1868, in Logan, Utah, a son of Alvin Crockett and Mary S. Reed. He was baptized May 4, 1876, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1911-1913, was ordained a High Priest May 3, 1914, by John U. Stucki, and a Bishop Dec. 23, 1917, by Rudger Clawson.
