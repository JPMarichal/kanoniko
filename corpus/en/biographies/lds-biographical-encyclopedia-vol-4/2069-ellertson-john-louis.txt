Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ellertson, John Louis

Ellertson, John Louis, Bishop of the Mona Ward, Juab Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1902, was born Nov. 13, 1857, in Spanish Fork, Utah Co., Utah, a son of John Ellertson and Christine G. Lurid. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission in the Northern States in 1896-1898, was sustained as Bishop Jan. 21, 1901, and ordained a Bishop by Anthon H. Lund.
