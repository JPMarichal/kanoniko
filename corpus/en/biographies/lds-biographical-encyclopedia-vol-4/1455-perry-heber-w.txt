Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Perry, Heber W.

Perry, Heber W., Bishop of the Perry Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1924, was born Oct. 25, 1866, in Three-Mile Creek, or Perry, the son of Henry E. Perry and Elizabeth Zabriske. He was baptized May 4, 1876, was ordained a High Priest Oct 24, 1896, by Adolph Madsen, and ordained a Bishop June 6, 1914, by Orson F. Whitney.
