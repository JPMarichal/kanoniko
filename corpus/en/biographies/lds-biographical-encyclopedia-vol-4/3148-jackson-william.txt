Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jackson, William

Jackson, William, Bishop of the Manassa 1st Ward, San Luis Stake, Colo., from 1925 to 1928, was born Aug. 21, 1871, in Nephi, Utah, a son of Samuel Jackson and Hannah M. Jaques. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1879, filled a mission to the Central States in 1889-1890, and a mission to England in 1909-1911. He was ordained a High Priest Nov. 25, 1916, by Hyrum S. Harris, and a Bishop June 14, 1925, by Orson F. Whitney.
