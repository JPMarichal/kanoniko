Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Spaulding, Ira N.

Spaulding, Ira N., Bishop of the Uintah Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1878 to 1882, was born April 16, 1809, in Crown Point, Essex Co., N.Y., a son of Stephen G. Spaulding and Phoebe Lawrence. He was baptized Feb. 12, 1842, ordained a High Priest Dec. 15, 1877, by Robert McQuarrie, and a Bishop July 21, 1878, by Franklin D. Richards.
