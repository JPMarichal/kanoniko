Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Evans, Jonah

Evans, Jonah, Bishop of the Samaria Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1880 to 1897, was born Feb. 26, 1838, in South Wales, a son of John Evans and Esther Williams. He was baptized April 5, 1860, emigrated to Utah in 1863, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 31, 1880. He died Jan. 22, 1897.
