Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Maw, Henry Thomas

Maw, Henry Thomas, Bishop of the Plain City Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1913, was born Feb. 12, 1870, in Plain City, a son of Abraham Maw and Eliza Tripp. He was baptized Aug. 24, 1879, filled a mission to the Central States in 1905-1907, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 25, 1910, by David O. McKay.
