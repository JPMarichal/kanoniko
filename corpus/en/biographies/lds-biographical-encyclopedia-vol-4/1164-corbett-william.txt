Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Corbett, William

Corbett, William, Bishop of the Turner Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1913 to 1917, was born Oct. 24, 1873, at Smithfield, Utah, the son of John Corbett and Emily Woodward. He was baptized Sept. 6, 1883, ordained a High Priest May 12, 1907, by John Henry Smith, and filled a mission to the Central States in 1911-1912.
