Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Quayle, Philip

Quayle, Philip, Bishop of the Treasureton Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1902 to 1905, was born June 29, 1861, in Logan, Utah, a son of James Quayle and Elizabeth Gillions. He was baptized in 1871, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1898-1901, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 19, 1902, by Marriner W. Merrill. He died in 1930, in Willard, Utah.
