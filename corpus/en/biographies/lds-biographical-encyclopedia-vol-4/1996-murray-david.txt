Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Murray, David

Murray, David, Bishop of the Mount Stirling Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1906 to 1913, was born Feb. 23, 1849, at Crofthead, Linlithgowshire, Scotland, the son of Robert Murray and Jessie Archibald. He was baptized when about twelve years old, filled a mission to Scotland in 1889-1891, and was ordained a Bishop April 9, 1906, by Francis M. Lyman. He died March 21, 1913.
