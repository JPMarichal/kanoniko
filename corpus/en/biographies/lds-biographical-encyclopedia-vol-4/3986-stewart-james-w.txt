Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stewart, James W.

Stewart, James W., one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born May 19, 1825, in Fayette County, Alabama, a son of George Stewart and Ruth Baker. He and his parents and other members of the family were baptized May 25, 1842, by Benjamin Clapp in Alabama. When the pioneer company arrived at the Platte River, he, with nine other men was left to operate a ferry which he had helped to construct and on which the on-coming companies were to cross the river. After his arrival in Great Salt Lake Valley he assisted to construct irrigation ditches. Later, he filled a mission to the Southern States, where he met Jane Grover, a daughter of Thomas Grover, whom he married and located in Farmingten. Later he lived in Morgan County and died March 22, 1913, in Cokeville, Wyoming, at the home of one of his daughters.
