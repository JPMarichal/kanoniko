Wells, LouiePPWells, Louie, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born Aug. 27, 1862, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the daughter of Daniel H. and Emmeline B. Wells. She was the first secretary of the General Board of the Y. L. M. I. A. in 1880, and being a gifted writer she contributed to the "Woman's Exponent," "The Contributor," and other publications of the day. She was a member of the Home Opera Company and worked under Prof. George Careless. She died in San Francisco, Calif., May 16, 1887.
