Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Freer, Joseph Slack

Freer, Joseph Slack, Bishop of the Twin Groves Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1909 to 1914, was born. Sept. 22, 1863, at Leicestershire, England, a son of William Freer and Martha Slack. He was baptized Nov. 12, 1871, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 11, 1909, by George F. Richards. He died July 7, 1917, at Layton, Utah.
