Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcmillan, David

Mcmillan, David, Bishop of the Kemmerer Ward, Woodruff Stake, Wyo., from 1902 to 1906, was born March 10, 1860, in Beleree, County of Down, Ireland, a son of Hugh McMillan and Catherine Fowler. He was baptized Feb. 1, 1879, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 2, 1902, by John W. Taylor.
