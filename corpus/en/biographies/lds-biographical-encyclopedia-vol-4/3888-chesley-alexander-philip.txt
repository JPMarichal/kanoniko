Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Chesley, Alexander Philip

Chesley, Alexander Philip, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born Oct. 22, 1814, in Fanquier Co., Vermont, a son of John and Elizabeth Chesley. He was an active member of the Church during the life of the Prophet Joseph Smith. After coming to Utah he resided for a time in Provo, where he acted as constable. He filled a mission to Australia in 1856-1857. Later he lived at Fillmore and represented Millard County in the Utah Territorial Legislature. He was a farmer and stock raiser by vocation and had several children. When last heard of he was in San Juan Valley, California.
