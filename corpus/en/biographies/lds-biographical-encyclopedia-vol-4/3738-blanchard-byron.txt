Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Blanchard, Byron

Blanchard, Byron, Bishop of the Chester Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1904 to 1915, was born Feb. 3, 1872, in Springville, Utah, a son of Alma Moroni Blanchard and Emma Bocock. He was baptized Nov. 15, 1885, ordained an Elder March 5, 1899, by John Watson, filled a mission to Colorado in 1902-1904, and was chosen Bishop of the Chester Ward in 1904, shortly after he returned from his mission.
