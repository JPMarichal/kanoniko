Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Curtis, Moses Monroe

Curtis, Moses Monroe, Bishop of the Graham Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1882 to 1883, was born Oct. 28, 1840, in Nauvoo, Illinois, a son of Moses Curtis and Aurelia Jackman. He was baptized in 1848, came to Utah in 1850, and was ordained a High Priest by Pres. Brigham Young. He died May 4, 1907.
