Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hibbard, George A.

Hibbard, George A., Bishop of the Hibbard Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1895 to 1906, was born July 18, 1857, in Farmington, Utah, the son of George Hibbard and Anna Williams. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 9, 1895, by John Henry Smith.
