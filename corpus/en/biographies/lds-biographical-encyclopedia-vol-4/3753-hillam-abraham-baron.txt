Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hillam, Abraham Baron

Hillam, Abraham Baron, Bishop of the Marysville Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1920 to 1930+, was born Oct. 18, 1876, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Abraham B. Hillam and Mary Baron. He was baptized Oct. 18, 1884, ordained a High Priest April 30, 1916, by Charles E. Lloyd, and ordained a Bishop April 24, 1920, by Orson F. Whitney.
