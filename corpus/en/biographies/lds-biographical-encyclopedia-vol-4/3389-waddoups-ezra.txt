Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Waddoups, Ezra

Waddoups, Ezra, Bishop of the Park City Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1918, was born Jan. 24, 1882, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of Thos. Waddoups and Mary Call. He was baptized Jan. 24, 1890, sustained as Bishop Aug. 13, 1916, and set apart Sept. 13, 1916, by Francis M. Lyman.
