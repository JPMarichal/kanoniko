Reynolds, William Henry

Reynolds, William Henry, Bishop of the Luna Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1898 to 1903 and from 1913 to 1919, was born Nov. 1, 1858, in Shropshire, England, a son of John Reynolds and Elizabeth Morris. He was baptized Nov. 1, 1866, in Newark, New Jersey, ordained a High Priest in 1888 and a Bishop in February, 1898, by John Henry Smith. He had acted as presiding Elder since Aug. 15, 1895,
