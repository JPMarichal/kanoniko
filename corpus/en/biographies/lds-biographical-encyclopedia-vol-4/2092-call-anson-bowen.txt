Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Call, Anson Bowen

Call, Anson Bowen, Bishop of the Dublan Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1915 to 1930+, was born Oct. 20, 1863, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of Artson Call and Ann Mariah Bowen. He was baptized in October, 1872, moved to Mexico in 1890, was ordained a Seventy Feb. 25, 1893, by B. H. Roberts, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1895-1897. He was ordained a Bishop May 11, 1915, by Anthony W. Ivins.
