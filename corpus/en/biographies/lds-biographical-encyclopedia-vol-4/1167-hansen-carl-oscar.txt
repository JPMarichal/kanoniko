Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Carl Oscar

Hansen, Carl Oscar, Bishop of the Turner Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, in 1926, was born Nov. 15, 1883, in Brigham City, Utah, the son of Niels Hansen and Emma Olson. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1892, and was ordained a High Priest May 15, 1915, by Orson F. Whitney.
