Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tuckett, John

Tuckett, John, Bishop of the Springville 1st Ward, Kolob Stake, Utah, from 1892 to 1898, was born April 26, 1834, in London, England, a son of Charles Tuckett and Jane Pattenden. He was called on the Las Vegas mission in 1856, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 17, 1892, by George Q. Cannon. He died Nov. 13, 1931.
