Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, George Albert

Smith, George Albert, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was bern June 26, 1817, at Potsdam, St. Lawrence Co., New York, a son of Patriarch John Smith and Clarissa Lyman. He was a member of the Council of Twelve Apostles at the time he made the pioneer journey, and died in Salt Lake City, Sept. 1, 1875. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 37.)
