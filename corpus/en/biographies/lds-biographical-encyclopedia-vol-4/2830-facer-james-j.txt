Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Facer, James J.

Facer, James J., Bishop of the North Pocatello Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1927, was born Jan. 10, 1866, in Willard, Utah, a son of George Facer and Sarah Thompson. He was baptized Oct. 7, 1876, and was ordained a High Priest by David Murray. He was sustained as Bishop July 12, 1925.
