Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lafount, Harold Arundel

Lafount, Harold Arundel, Bishop of the Hawthorne Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1924, was born Jan. 5, 1880, in Birmingham, Warwickshire, England, the son of Robert Arthur Lafount and Ethel Hewitt. He emigrated to America when about 11 years old and was baptized March 10, 1893, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1902-1903, and was ordained a Bishop June 18, 1919, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
