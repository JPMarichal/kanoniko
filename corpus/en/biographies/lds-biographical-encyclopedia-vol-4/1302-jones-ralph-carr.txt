Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, Ralph Carr

Jones, Ralph Carr, Bishop of the Newton Ward, Benson Stake, Utah from 1928 to 1930+, was born Oct. 25, 1901, at Newton, the son of George Leopold Jones and Mary Wright. He was baptized July 2, 1910, ordained an Elder Aug. 19, 1923, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1923-1925, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 15, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
