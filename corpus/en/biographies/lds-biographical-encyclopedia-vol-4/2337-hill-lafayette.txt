Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hill, Lafayette

Hill, Lafayette, Bishop of the Mesa 4th Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1921 to 1927, was born March 6, 1876, in Toquerville, Washington Co., Utah, a son of William Hill and Mary J. Price. He was baptized in 1884, ordained a High Priest June 4, 1906, by George Teasdale, and sustained as Bishop Nov. 7, 1920.
