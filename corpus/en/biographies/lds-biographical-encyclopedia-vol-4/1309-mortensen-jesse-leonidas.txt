Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mortensen, Jesse Leonidas

Mortensen, Jesse Leonidas, Bishop of the Amalga Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1925, was born April 21, 1873, in Parowan, Iron Co., Utah, the son of Hans J. Mortensen and Emily Donetta Alexandria. He was baptized June 2, 1881, and was ordained a Bishop April 21, 1918, by Richard R. Lyman.
