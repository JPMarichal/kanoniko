Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gibbs, James Hyrum

Gibbs, James Hyrum, Bishop of the Portage Ward. Malad Stake Idaho from 1899 to 1909, was born June 15, 1860, in South Wales, a son of John D. Gibbs and Julia A. Tompkins. He was baptized June 15, 1868, ordained to the Priesthood and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1898, and was ordained a Bishop Dec. 17, 1899, by Francis M. Lyman.
