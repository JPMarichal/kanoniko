Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Charles N.

Smith, Charles N., Bishop of the Rockville Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1868 to 1891, was born March 14, 1821, in Cradley, Herefordshire, England, a son of Thomas Smith and Mary Tringham. He was baptized in August, 1840, filled a mission to Canada in 1875-1876, and was appointed to preside over the Rockville Ward May 10, 1868, by Erastus Snow.
