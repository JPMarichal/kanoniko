Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Horsley, Ernest Preston

Horsley, Ernest Preston, a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A., from 1919 to 1935, was born May 19, 1875, in Three Mile Creek, Box Elder Co., Utah, the son of William Horsley and Elizabeth Welch. He was baptized in April, 1883, ordained a Seventy, Nov. 12, 1897, by Seymour B. Young, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1897-1899.
