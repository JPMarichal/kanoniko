Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nielsen, Carl Erastus

Nielsen, Carl Erastus, Bishop of the Axtell Ward, Gunnison Stake, from 1915 to 1920, was born Feb. 13, 1887, in Scipio, Utah, a son of Hans W. Nielsen and Magdelene Rasmussen. He was baptized Aug. 3, 1895, ordained a High Priest Aug. 3, 1912, by Joseph F. Smith, jun., and ordained a Bishop by Orson F. Whitney Jan. 31, 1915.
