Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stoker, William E.

Stoker, William E., Bishop of the Price Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1929, was born April 27, 1875, in Spanish Fork, Utah, the son of Michael Stoker and Aimira Jane Wilson. He was baptized June 7, 1882, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 8, 1921, and a Bishop April 7, 1923, by George F. Richards.
