Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moss, William

Moss, William, Bishop of the West Bountiful Ward, South Davis Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1915, was born June 21, 1855, in Bountiful, Davis Co., Utah, a son of John Moss and Rebecca Wood. He was baptized June 21, 1863, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 27, 1909, by Francis M. Lyman. He died Nov. 8, 1933.
