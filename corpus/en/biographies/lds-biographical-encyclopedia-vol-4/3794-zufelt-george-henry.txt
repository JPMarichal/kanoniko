Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Zufelt, George Henry

Zufelt, George Henry, Bishop of the Kline Ward, Young Stake, Colorado, from 1926 to 1928, was born Nov. 21, 1900, at Jewerr, New Mexico, a son of Reuben Zufelt and Isabella McBride. He was baptized when eight years old, and sustained as Bishop Oct. 31, 1926.
