Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Klingler, Alma

Klingler, Alma, Bishop of the Rexburg 3rd Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, in 1930+, was born Aug. 12, 1902, in Rexburg, the son of Fredrick L. Klingler and Christine G. Walz. He was baptized Aug. 30, 1910, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 27, 1925, by Geo. S. Romney, and a Bishop April 6, 1930, by Rudger Clawson.
