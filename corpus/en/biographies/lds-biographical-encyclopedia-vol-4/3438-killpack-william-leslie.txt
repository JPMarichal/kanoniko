Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Killpack, William Leslie

Killpack, William Leslie, Bishop of the Driggs Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born March 26, 1886, in Molen, Utah, a son of John David Killpack and Helen Elizabeth Peacock. He was baptized June 7, 1894, filled a mission to California in 1911-1913, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 9, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
