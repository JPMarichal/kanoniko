Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stowell, Heber John

Stowell, Heber John, Bishop of the Spring Glen Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1889 to 1892, was born July 14, 1860, in Ogden, Utah, the son of Wm. R. R. Stowell and Cynthia Jane Parks. He was baptized in August, 1868, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 11, 1889.
