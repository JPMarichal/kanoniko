Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Petersen, Christian Andreas

Petersen, Christian Andreas, Bishop of the Centerfield Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born March 24, 1880, in Copenhagen, Denmark, a son of Christian Petersen and Johanne Pedersen. He was baptized Dec. 28, 1891, emigrated to Utah in 1897, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 4, 1918, by Rudger Clawson and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 29, 1927, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
