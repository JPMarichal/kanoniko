Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Palmyra Stake

Allen, Wilbur Bird

Allen, Wilbur Bird, Bishop of the Salem Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930, was born April 10, 1881, in Glenwood, Sevier Co., Utah, the son of Joseph William Allen and Fannie Kindred. He was baptized June 2, 1893, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 21, 1920, by George F. Richards.
