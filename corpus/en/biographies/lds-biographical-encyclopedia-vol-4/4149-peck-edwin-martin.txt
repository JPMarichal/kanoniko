Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peck, Edwin Martin

Peck, Edwin Martin, a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company D, was born July 24, 1828, in Caledonia County, Vermont, a son of Martin H. Peck and Susan Cluff. His father's family moved to Nauvoo, and after the saints were exiled from their homes there in 1846, he traveled with the body of the Church to Council Bluffs, Iowa, where he joined the Battalion in July, 1846. After his arrival in California he' was honorably discharged July 16, 1847, but four days later with forty-six other members of the Battalion re-enlisted for service six months longer. After his discharge he came to the "Valley" and made his home in Provo. He died in Coshen, Utah Co., Aug. 22, 1903.
