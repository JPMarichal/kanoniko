Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, Thomas J.

Jones, Thomas J., first counselor in the presidency of the Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born June 5, 1858, in Cedar City, Iron Co., Utah. He was set apart as first counselor in the Parowan Stake presidency May 31, 1925, by John A. Widtsoe. He died Jan. 8, 1931.
