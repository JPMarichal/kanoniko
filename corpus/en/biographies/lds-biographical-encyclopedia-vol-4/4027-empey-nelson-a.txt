Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Empey, Nelson A.

Empey, Nelson A., a member of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1885 to 1904, was born May 7, 1837, in Preston, Ontario, Canada, a son of William Empey and Mary Ann Morgan. He died Sept. 4, 1904, in Salt Lake City, Utah.
