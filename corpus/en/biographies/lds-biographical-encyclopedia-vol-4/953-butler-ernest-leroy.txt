Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Butler, Ernest LeRoy

Butler, Ernest LeRoy, president of the Samoan Mission from 1924 to 1927, was born Feb. 13, 1885, in Ogden, Utah, a son of M. W. Butler and Matilda Stowell. He was baptized Feb. 26, 1893, filled his first mission to Samoa in 1909-1912, and was set apart for a second mission to Samoa Nov. 1, 1921.
