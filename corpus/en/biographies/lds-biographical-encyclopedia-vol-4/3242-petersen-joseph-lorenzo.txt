Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Petersen, Joseph Lorenzo

Petersen, Joseph Lorenzo, Bishop of the Pinedale Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1914 to 1916, was born Dec. 11, 1887, in Pinedale, a son of Niels Petersen and Marie Mortensen. He was baptized May 18, 1896, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 30, 1914, by David O. McKay.
