Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dunn, John Madden

Dunn, John Madden, Bishop of Kimball Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1898 to 1903, was born March 3, 1864, in Willard, Box Elder Co., Utah, the son of James Dunn and Mary Jane Madden. He was baptized March 28, 1874, moved to Canada in 1893, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 1, 1900, by Francis M. Lyman. He died in November, 1919, in Lethbridge, Canada.
