Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Berg, Carl

Berg, Carl, Bishop of the Castle Dale Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born 0ct. 22, 1869, in Gjerrild, Randers amt, Denmark, the son of Andrew Berg and Engeline Petersen. He was baptized in 1877, came to Utah in 1885, was ordained to the Priesthood, and filled a mission to Denmark in 1907-1909. He was ordained a High Priest May 8, 1910, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop May 28, 1929, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
