Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kimball, Joseph

Kimball, Joseph, Bishop of the Meadowville Ward, Bear Lake Stake, (Rich Co., Utah), Idaho, from 1876 to 1892, was born Dec. 22, 1851, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Heber C. Kimball and Prescinda Lathrop Huntington. He was baptized when about eight years old, and located in Meadowville in 1869. He was ordained a Bishop Aug. 26, 1877, by Albert Cartington. Elder Kimball died March 29, 1936, in Salt Lake City, Utah.
