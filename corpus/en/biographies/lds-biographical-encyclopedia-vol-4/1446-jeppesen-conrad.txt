Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jeppesen, Conrad

Jeppesen, Conrad, Bishop of the Mantun Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1930+, was born Dec. 29, 1871, in Mantua, the son of Rasmus M. Jeppesen and Emelie Bravant. He was baptized April 11, 1880, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 2, 1917, by James E. Talmage.
