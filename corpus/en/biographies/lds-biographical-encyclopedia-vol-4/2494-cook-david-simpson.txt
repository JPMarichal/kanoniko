Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cook, David Simpson

Cook, David Simpson, Bishop of the South Weber Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1890, was born Jan. 19, 1829, in Kincardine, Perthshire, Scotland, a son of David Cook and Margaret Simpson. He was baptized Jan. 3, 1846, and ordained . Bishop June 29, 1877, by Franklin D. Richards. He died May 5, 1890.
