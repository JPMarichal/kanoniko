Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rynearson, Edward M.

Rynearson, Edward M., Bishop of the Mill Creek Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1927, was born Jan. 25, 1886, in Mill Creek, the son of A. M. Rynearson and Emma Redman. He was baptized June 7, 1894, was ordained a High Priest Feb, 22, 1914, by Hyrum G. Smith, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 13, 1916, by Francis M. Lyman.
