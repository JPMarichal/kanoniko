Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rawlings, Walter, jun.

Rawlings, Walter, jun., Bishop of the Preston 6th Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930, was born Dec. 18, 1891, in Fairview, Idaho, the son of Walter Rawlings and Lottie May Elizabeth Inglet. He was baptized July 6, 1901, filled a mission to England in 1911-1913, and was ordained a High Priest July 16, 1916, and a Bishop Sept. 15, 1929, by Geo. F. Richards.
