Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dana, Roswell Roy, jun.

Dana, Roswell Roy, jun., Bishop of the Thayne Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1925 to 1930+, was born March 11, 1889, in Willard, Utah, a son of Roswell R. Dana and Melvina Eunice Davis. He was baptized July 3, 1897, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1908-1911, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 23, 1917, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop in December, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard. He presided over the Bedford Ward from 1921 to 1925 (June 14) when he was set apart as Bishop of the Thayne Ward by Rudger Clawson.
