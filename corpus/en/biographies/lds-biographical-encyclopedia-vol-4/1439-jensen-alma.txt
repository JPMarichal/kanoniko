Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Alma

Jensen, Alma, Bishop of the Corinne Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1914 to 1920, was born June 12, 1866, in Mantua, Box Elder Co., Utah, the son of Peter C. Jensen and Anna C. Petersen. He was baptized Aug. 9, 1874, ordained a Seventy by Rudger Clawson, filled a mission to Norway in 1906-1908, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 22, 1914, by Francis M. Lyman.
