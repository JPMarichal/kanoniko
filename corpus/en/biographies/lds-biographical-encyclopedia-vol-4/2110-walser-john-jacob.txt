Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Walser, John Jacob

Walser, John Jacob, Bishop of the Juarez Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1916 to 1922, was born April 20, 1849, in Herisau, Appenzell, Switzerland, the son of John J. and Anne Walser. He was baptized April 20, 1859, filled two foreign missions and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 19, 1916, by Anthony W. Ivins.
