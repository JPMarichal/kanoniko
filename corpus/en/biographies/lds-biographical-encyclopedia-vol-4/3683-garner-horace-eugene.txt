Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Garner, Horace Eugene

Garner, Horace Eugene, Bishop of the Ogden 1st Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born June 29, 1876, in Ogden, a son of Frederick Garner and Ann Horrocks. He was baptized Nov. 6, 1885, ordained a Seventy April 10, 1899, by John Henry Smith, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1899-1901, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 13, 1908, and a Bishop Oct. 28, 1923, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
