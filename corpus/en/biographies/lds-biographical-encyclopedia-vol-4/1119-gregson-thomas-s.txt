Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gregson, Thomas S.

Gregson, Thomas S., Bishop of the Kimball Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1909 to 1913, was born July 29, 1870, in Haslingden, Lancashire, England, the son of John Gregson and Rosannah Sinnett. He was baptized Dec. 6, 1883, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 4, 1909, by Henry L. Hinman.
