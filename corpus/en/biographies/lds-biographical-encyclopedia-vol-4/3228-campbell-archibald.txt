Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Campbell, Archibald

Campbell, Archibald, Bishop of the Winslow Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Feb. 28, 1897, in Snowflake, the son of Franklin Campbell and Annie Frost. He was baptized July 3, 1904, ordained an Elder in 1915, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1915-1919, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 21, 1927, by Geo. F. Richards.
