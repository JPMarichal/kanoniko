Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Horton, John Earl

Horton, John Earl, Bishop of the McGill Ward, Nevada Stake, Nevada, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Oct. 1, 1897, in Greenville, Utah, a son of John Alma Hotton and Harriet Heslington. He was baptized July 5, 1905, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1916-1919, .was ordained a High Priest July 20, 1919, by Hyrum G. Smith, and a Bishop Oct. 16, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
