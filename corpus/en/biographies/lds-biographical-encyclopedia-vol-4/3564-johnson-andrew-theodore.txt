Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Andrew Theodore

Johnson, Andrew Theodore, Bishop of the Vernal 2nd Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930, was born Jan. 2, 1881, in Wanship, Summit Co., Utah, a son of Andrew Johnson and Emma Rasmussen. He was baptized in 1889, filled a mission to Sweden in 1910-1914, was ordained a High Priest March 29, 1925, by Geo. F. Richards, and sustained as Bishop Jan. 16, 1927.
