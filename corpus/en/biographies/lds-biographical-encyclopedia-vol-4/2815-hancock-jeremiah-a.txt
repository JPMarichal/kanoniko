Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hancock, Jeremiah A.

Hancock, Jeremiah A., first counselor in the Pioneer Stake presidency, Utah, from 1925 to 1929, was born April 3, 1871, in Payson, Utah, a son of Isaac A. Hancock and Margaret M. Bingham. He was baptized Aug. 7, 1879, ordained a High Priest May 1, 1921, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., and set apart as first counselor in the Stake presidency in 1925.
