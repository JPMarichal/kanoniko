Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Glad, Alfred Peter Andersen

Glad, Alfred Peter Andersen, Bishop of the 28th Ward, Salt Lake Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Nov. 2, 1890, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Niels Peter Andersen and Alphine C. Alfsen. He was baptized Dec. 3, 1898, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 16, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
