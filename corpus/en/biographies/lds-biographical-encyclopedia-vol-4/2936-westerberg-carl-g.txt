Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Westerberg, Carl G.

Westerberg, Carl G., Bishop of the Clark Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1908 to 1910, was born June 16, 1871, at Hyrum, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Carl P. Westerberg and Annie E. Peterson. He was baptized May 6, 1885, ordained a Seventy Nov. 9, 1898, by J. G. Kimball, filled a mission to the Western States in 1898-1901, and was sustained and ordained a Bishop July 5, 1908.
