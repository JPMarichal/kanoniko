Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Adams, John, jun.

Adams, John, jun., Bishop of the Oakley 3rd Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1928, was born July 22, 1858, in Tooele, Utah, the son of John Adams and Mary Caroline Howells. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest March 4, 1917, by Wm. T. Jack, and a Bishop July 29, 1917, by Rudger Clawson.
