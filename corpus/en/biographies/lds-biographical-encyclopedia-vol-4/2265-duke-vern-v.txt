Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Duke, Vern V.

Duke, Vern V., Bishop of the Moore Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1923, was born Nov. 3, 1891, in Santaquin, Utah, a son of Robert S. Duke and Margaret E. Van Ausdal. He was baptized Sept. 7, 1902, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 12, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
