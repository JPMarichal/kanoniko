Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mitchell, Joseph Clayton

Mitchell, Joseph Clayton, Bishop of the Parowan East Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Nov. 16, 1885, in Parowan, Utah, the son of William Cook Mitchell and Hulda Dalton. He was baptized Nov. 16, 1893, ordained a Seventy Dec. 30, 1919, by T. K. Holyoak, filled a mission to the Central States in 1928, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 23, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
