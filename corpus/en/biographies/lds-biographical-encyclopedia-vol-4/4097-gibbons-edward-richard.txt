Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gibbons, Edward Richard

Gibbons, Edward Richard, an Elder who died in the missionary field, was born May 14, 1894, in St. Johns, Apache Co., Arizona, a son of Richard Gibbons and Clara Isabell Wilhelm. He was baptized Aug. 7, 1904, and later ordained te the office of an Elder. When he was called on his mission, he was living in Tempe, Maricopa Co., Arizona. After accepting his call he was set apart Nov. 21, 1916, to labor in the Western States Mission, but when a part of this mission was added to the Mexican Mission he came under Pres. Rey L. Pratt's jurisdiction, and while serving in this field of labor he contracted a case of influenza from which he died Nov. 3, 1918, in Rameo, Colorado. The father of Elder Gibbons went to Colorado and brought the body of his son home to Tempe, Arizona, where it was buried.
