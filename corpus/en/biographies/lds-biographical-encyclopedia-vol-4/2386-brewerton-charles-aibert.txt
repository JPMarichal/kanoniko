Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brewerton, Charles Aibert

Brewerton, Charles Aibert, Bishop of the Acequia Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Jan. 24, 1883, in Elba, Cassia Co., Idaho, a son of Charles Albert Brewerton and Sarah H. Hardy. He was baptized in May, 1891, ordained to the Priesthood, filled missions to the Society Islands in 1899-1901 and in 1903-1906. He acted as presiding Elder of the Jackson Branch, Idaho, from the time of its organization in 1899 until 1917, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 19, 1922, by Hyrum G. Smith.
