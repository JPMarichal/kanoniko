Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kimball, David Patten

Kimball, David Patten, first counselor in the St. Joseph Stake presidency, Arizona, in 1883, was born Aug. 23, 1839, in Nauvoo, Illinois, a son of Heber Chase Kimball and Vilate Murray. He filled a mission to England in 1863-1865, acted as presiding Elder of St. David in 1881-1883, and died Nov. 22, 1883, in St. David, Arizona.
