Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Green, Edward Saxe

Green, Edward Saxe, Bishop of the Kanesville Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born Nov. 26, 1872, in West Weber, Utah, a son of Ammon Green and Almira Mesick. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1881, ordained a Seventy Sept. 17, 1897, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1897-1900, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 1, 1902, by Charles F. Middleton, and a Bishop July 15, 1923, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
