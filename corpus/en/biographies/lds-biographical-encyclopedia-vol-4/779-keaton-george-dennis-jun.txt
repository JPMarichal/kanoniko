Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Keaton, George Dennis, jun.

Keaton, George Dennis, jun., president of the French Mission from 1856 to 1858, was born June 27, 1828, in Buckleberry, Berkshire, England, a son of George Dennis and Mary Jane Keaton. He was appointed to fill a mission in Italy Dec. 11, 1852, from England, and labored in that mission till June, 1854. On Dec. 8, 1855, he was appointed president of the French Mission, and as late as July, 1858, was still acting as president of that mission.
