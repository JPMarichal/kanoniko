Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilde, Albert Alanson

Wilde, Albert Alanson, presiding Elder of the Melba Branch, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born June 28, 1885, in Mink Creek, Idaho, the son of Albert A. Wilde and Tora Keller. He was baptized June 28, 1893, by Andrew A. Larson, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1904-1907, and was sustained as branch president July 21, 1929.
