Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Miller, Austin Porter

Miller, Austin Porter, Bishop of the Miller Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1929, was born March 1, 1888, in Riverton, Utah, the son of Orrin P. Miller and Elizabeth Ann Morgan. He was baptized May 24, 1896, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1909-1911, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 18, 1914, by Francis M. Lyman, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 26, 1917, by Heber J. Grant.
