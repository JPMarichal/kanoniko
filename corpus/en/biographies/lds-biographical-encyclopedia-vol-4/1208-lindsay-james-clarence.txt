Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lindsay, James Clarence

Lindsay, James Clarence, Bishop of the Ovid Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1918, was born February 22, 1873, in Paris, Idaho, the son of Philemon Lindsay and Marintha Athay. He was baptized July 10, 1882, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 19, 1915, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 23, 1917, by Hyrum M. Smith.
