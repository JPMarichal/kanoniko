Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Booth, Wayne Chipman

Booth, Wayne Chipman, Bishop of the Highland Ward, Alpine Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1922, was born April 16, 1892, in. Alpine, Utah Co., Utah, the son of Robert Ebenezer Booth and Lovenia Jane Chipman. He was baptized April 15, 1900, by his father, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 4, 1920, by Rudger Clawson. He died April 27, 1927, at American Fork.
