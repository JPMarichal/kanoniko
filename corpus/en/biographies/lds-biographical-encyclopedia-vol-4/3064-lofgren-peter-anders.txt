Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lofgren, Peter Anders

Lofgren, Peter Anders, Bishop of the St. David Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1888 to 1901, was born Jan. 18, 1847, at Orja, Sweden, a son of Anders Pehrson Lofgren and Sisa Bengtson. He was baptized April 8, 1860, emigrated to Utah in 1862, was ordained a High Priest July 7, 1884, by Christopher Layton, and a Bishop Feb. 12, 1888, also by Christopher Layton. He died March 6, 1922.
