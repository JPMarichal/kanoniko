Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Webb, Edward Milo

Webb, Edward Milo, Bishop of the Inkom Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1906 to 1914, was born Feb. 27, 1869, at Deseret, Utah, a son of Edward Webb and Eliza Ann Home. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1896, and ordained a Bishop July 1, 1906, by Orson F. Whitney. He died in 1924.
