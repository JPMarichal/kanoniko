Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robison, Joseph V.

Robison, Joseph V., second counselor in the Millard Stake presidency, from 1877 to 1890, was born Dec. 30, 1832, in Clay, Onondago Co., N.Y., a son of Joseph Robison and Lucretia Hancock. He was baptized in 1848, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1871-1873, and was set apart as second counselor July 22, 1877, by Wilford Woodruff.
