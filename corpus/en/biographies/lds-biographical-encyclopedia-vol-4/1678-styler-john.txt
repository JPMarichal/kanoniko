Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Styler, John

Styler, John, Bishop of the Oasis Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1891 to 1908, was born Feb. 28, 1855, in Switzerland. He came to Utah in 1861, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1883-1884, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 21, 1891, by Francis M. Lyman.
