Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hunter, Oscar F.

Hunter, Oscar F., a member of the Old Folks Central Committee, from 1913 to 1931, was born May 8, 1852, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Bishop Edward Hunter and Laura Shimer Kaufman. He died Aug. 24, 1931, in Salt Lake City, Utah. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 2, p. 643.)
