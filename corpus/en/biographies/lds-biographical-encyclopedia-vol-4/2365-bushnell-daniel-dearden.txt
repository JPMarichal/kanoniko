Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bushnell, Daniel Dearden

Bushnell, Daniel Dearden, Bishop of the Meadow Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1928, was born Dec. 18, 1892, in Meadow, Utah, a son of Daniel B. Bushnell and Susan Dearden. He was baptized June 8, 1901, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1915-1918, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 11, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
