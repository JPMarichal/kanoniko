Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Bernice Rawlins

Harris, Bernice Rawlins, Bishop of the Salem Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1905 to 1921, was born March 6, 1867, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, the son of George H. A. Harris and Sarah Loader. He was baptized Aug. 8, 1875, and later ordained a High Priest. He was ordained a Bishop Jan. 29, 1906, by Geo. Albert Smith.
