Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Skousen, Orson Pratt

Skousen, Orson Pratt, Bishop of the Thatcher West Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1922 to 1927, was born Sept. 19, 1877, at St. Joseph, Arizona, a son of James N. Skousen and Anna Christina Jensen. He was baptized in 1885, ordained a High Priest Nov. 9, 1919, by Andrew Kimball, and a Bishop Aug. 20, 1922, by Geo. F. Richards.
