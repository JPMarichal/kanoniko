Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Porter, George Myron

Porter, George Myron, Bishop of the Otto Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1901 to 1906, was born March 17, 1871, at Porterville, Morgan Co., Utah, the son of Alma Porter and Minerva Deuel. He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1899-1901, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 10, 1901, by Marriner W. Merrill.
