Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harper, Reese Morris

Harper, Reese Morris, Bishop of the Oakley 4th Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1929, was born Feb. 19, 1863, in Calls Fort, Utah, the son of Thomas Harper and Hannah Jones. After being ordained a Seventy Oct. 11, 1892, by Seymour B. Young he filled a mission to the Indian Territory in 1892-1894, and a second mission to Great Britain in 1904-1906. He was ordained a High Priest May 31, 1907, by Wm. T. Jack, and a Bishop April 25, 1915, by Rudger Clawson.
