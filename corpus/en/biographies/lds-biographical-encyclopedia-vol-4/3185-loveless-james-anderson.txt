Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Loveless, James Anderson

Loveless, James Anderson, Bishop of the Timpanogos Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1924, was born Oct. 30, 1880, in Timpanogos, Utah Co., Utah, a son of James Anderson Loveless and Julia Elkins. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1912-1914, was ordained a High Priest in November, 1917, by Orson F. Whitney, and ordained a Bishop in March, 1920, by Rudger Clawson.
