Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor Stake

Allen, Heber Franklin

Allen, Heber Franklin, Bishop of the Raymond 1st Ward, Taylor Stake, Canada, from 1926 to 1930+, was born March 17, 1894, in Cardston, Alberta, Canada, a son of Heber C. Allen and Amy L. Leonard. He was baptized March 17, 1902, ordained an Elder Sept. 27, 1914, by William Redd, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1914-1917, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 2, 1925, by Hyrum G. Smith, and a Bishop Oct. 24, 1926, by Orson F. Whitney.
