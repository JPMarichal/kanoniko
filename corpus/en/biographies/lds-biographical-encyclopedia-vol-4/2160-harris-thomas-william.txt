Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Thomas William

Harris, Thomas William, Bishop of the Tabor Ward, Lethbridge Stake, Canada, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Aug. 10, 1868, in Kaysville, Utah, the son of Thomas E. Harris and Mary Ann Payne. He was baptized Aug. 10, 1877, ordained a High Priest June 26, 1898, by Rudger Clawson, and sustained as Bishop Aug. 14, 1927.
