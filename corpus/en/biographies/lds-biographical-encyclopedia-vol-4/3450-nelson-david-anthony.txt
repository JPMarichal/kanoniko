Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nelson, David Anthony

Nelson, David Anthony, Bishop of the Chapin Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1909 to 1915, was born Dec. 19, 1862, in Goshen, Utah, a son of Christian Nielsen and Ann Josephine Evans. He was baptized June 7, 1871, ordained a Seventy Nov. 11, 1895, by John Henry Smith, was called on a mission to Australasia in 1895, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 16, 1909, by Don C. Driggs.
