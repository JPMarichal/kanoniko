Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, William W.

Williams, William W., Bishop of the Samaria Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1912 to 1913, was born April 2, 1881, in Samaria, Idaho, a son of Jeremiah Hodge and Ruth Williams. He was baptized in 1889, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 3, 1912, by George F. Richards.
