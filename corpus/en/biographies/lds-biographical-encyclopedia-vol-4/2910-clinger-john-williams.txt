Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clinger, John Williams

Clinger, John Williams, Bishop of the La Belle Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1915to1922, was born Dec. 3, 1871, in Provo, Utah Co., Utah, a son of James H. Clinger and Pauline Williamson. He was baptized July 18, 1880, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 28, 1915, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
