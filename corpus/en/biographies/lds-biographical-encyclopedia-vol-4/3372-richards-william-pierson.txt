Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Richards, William Pierson

Richards, William Pierson, Bishop of the Oakley Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1912, was born May 18, 1864, in Wanship, Utah, a son of Franklin D. Richards and Susan S. Pierson. He was baptized July 21, 1877, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 27, 1901, by Reed Smoot.
