Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Swallow, Richard Thomas

Swallow, Richard Thomas, second counselor in the Nevada Stake presidency, Nevada, from 1926 to 1930, was born April 10, 1880, in Shoshone, Nevada, the son of George Swallow and Annie Day. He was baptized in March, 1904, filled a mission to California in 1905-1906, and was ordained a High Priest and set apart as second counselor in the Nevada Stake presidency Sept. 19, 1926, by Rudger Clawson.
