Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nielson, Gustave (Gustaf)

Nielson, Gustave (Gustaf), Bishop of the Cardston 1st Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1928 to 1930+, was born May 4, 1870, in Gardstanga, Malmohus lan, Sweden, the son of Niels Nielson and Sidsa Peterson. He emigrated with his parents to Utah in 1873; was baptized May 2, 1878; ordained a High Priest May 19, 1918, by Henry L. Hinman, and sustained as Bishop of the Cardston 1st Ward Aug. 22, 1928.
