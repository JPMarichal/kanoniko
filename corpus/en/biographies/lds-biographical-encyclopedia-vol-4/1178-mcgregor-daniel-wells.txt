Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcgregor, Daniel Wells

Mcgregor, Daniel Wells, Bishop of the Mound Valley Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, in 1930+, was born Nov. 10, 1889, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of James D. McGregor and Mary Perry. He was baptized Nov. 10, 1897, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 13, 1930, by Melvin J. Ballard.
