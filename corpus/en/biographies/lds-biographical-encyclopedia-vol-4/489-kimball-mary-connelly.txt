Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kimball, Mary Connelly

Kimball, Mary Connelly, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born Feb. 19, 1876, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the daughter of John Connelly and Elizabeth Jessop. She received her A. B. degree from the University of Utah in 1898, taught school for nine years in the elementary and high schools of Salt Lake City, served as president of the Y. L. M. I. A. in the 21st and 27th wards, was a member of the Salt Lake Stake Board, and was called in 1904 to the General Board of Y. L. M. I. A. In 1907 she became editor of the "Young Woman's Journal" and served for 16 years. She was married to Andrew Kimball June 5, 1923, released from the Y. L. M. I. A. Board in 1930 and called to the General Board of Relief Society in 1930, and became editor of the "Relief Society Magazine."
