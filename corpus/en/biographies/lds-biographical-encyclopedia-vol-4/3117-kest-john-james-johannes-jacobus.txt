Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kest, John James (Johannes Jacobus)

Kest, John James (Johannes Jacobus), Bishop of the Martinez Ward, San Francisco Stake, California, from 1927 to 1930, was born Feb. 26, 1896, at Vlissingen (Flushing), Netherlands, a son of Johannes Robertus Kest and Joziena Cornelia Lute. He was baptized Sept. 10, 1908, at Rotterdam, by Jan Eggen, emigrated to Utah in 1911, and later moved to California. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July10, 1927, by Geo. Albert Smith.
