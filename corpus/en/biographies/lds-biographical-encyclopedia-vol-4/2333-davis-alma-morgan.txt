Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davis, Alma Morgan

Davis, Alma Morgan, Bishop of the Mesa 4th Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1927 to 1930+, was born March 21, 1883, in Holladay, Utah, a son of William G. Davis and Esther Harrison. He was baptized June 4, 1891, ordained a High Priest Nov. 7, 1920, by Orle S. Stapley, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 14, 1927, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
