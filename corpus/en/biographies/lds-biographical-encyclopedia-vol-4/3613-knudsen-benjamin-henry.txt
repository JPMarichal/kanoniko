Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Knudsen, Benjamin Henry

Knudsen, Benjamin Henry, Bishop of the Provo 2nd Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Dec. 11, 1887, in Provo, a son of Herman Knudsen and Bertha K. A. Evert. He was baptized Aug. 29, 1896, filled a mission to the Swiss-German Mission in 1914-1916, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 26, 1919, by Jos. B. Keeler, and a Bishop Sept. 14, 1930, by Reed Smoot.
