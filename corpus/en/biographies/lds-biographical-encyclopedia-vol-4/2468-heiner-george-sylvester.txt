Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Heiner, George Sylvester

Heiner, George Sylvester, first counselor in the Morgan Stake presidency, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born March 2, 1886, in Pleasant Grove, Utah Co., Utah, a son of Daniel Heiner and Sarah J. Coulam. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood and filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1907-1909, was ordained a High Priest and set apart as second counselor Dec. 9, 1923, by Melvin J. Ballard. He was promoted to the position of first counselor Dec. 31, 1926.
