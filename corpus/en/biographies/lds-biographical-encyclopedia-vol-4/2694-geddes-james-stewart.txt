Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Geddes, James Stewart

Geddes, James Stewart, Bishop of the Banida Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1914 to 1918, was born May 18, 1873, in Plain City, Utah, a son of W. S. Geddes and Martha Stewart. He was baptized July 7, 1881, and was ordained a High Priest May 7, 1906, by Geo. C. Parkinson, and a Bishop June 7, 1914, by Geo. F. Richards.
