Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hymas, Benjamin

Hymas, Benjamin, Bishop of the Treasureton Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1892 to 1902 and from 1905 to 1911, was born May 26, 1846, in Essex, England, a son of William Hymas and Mary Ann Atkins. He was baptized about 1856 in England, and on Dec. 11, 1892, he was set apart as Bishop over the newly organized Ward. He died May 15, 1925.
