Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jolly, Bryant Heber, jun.

Jolly, Bryant Heber, jun., Bishop of the Brooklyn Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1904, was born Oct. 4, 1870, in Harmony, Washington Co., Utah, a son of Bryant Heber Jolly and Orissa Ann Angelia Taylor. He was baptized Dec. 19, 1878, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1897-1898 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 23, 1900, by Anthon H. Lund.
