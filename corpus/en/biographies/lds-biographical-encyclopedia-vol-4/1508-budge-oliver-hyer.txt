Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Budge, Oliver Hyer

Budge, Oliver Hyer, first counselor in the presidency of the Cache Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1920, was born April 3, 1873, in Paris, Idaho, a son of William Budge and Ann Hyer. He was ordained a Seventy Oct. 7, 1896, by Christian D. Fieldsted, and filled a mission to Switzerland and Germany in 1896-1899.
