Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Van Wagoner, John, jun.

Van Wagoner, John, jun., Bishop of the Midway 1st Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1913 to 1926, was born Dec. 16, 1873, at Midway, Utah, a son of John Van Wagoner and Margaret Ann Fawcett. He was baptized July 15, 1883, ordained a High Priest Jan. 26, 1912, by Joseph R. Murdock, and chosen Bishop of the Midway 1st Ward Oct. 26, 1913.
