Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Quinn, James Alfred

Quinn, James Alfred, Bishop of the Pocatello 5th Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Sept. 19, 1881, in Manassa, Colorado, a son of William G. Quinn and Susan C. Dotson. He was baptized Dec. 19, 1895, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1907-1908, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 21, 1921, by James E. Talmage.
