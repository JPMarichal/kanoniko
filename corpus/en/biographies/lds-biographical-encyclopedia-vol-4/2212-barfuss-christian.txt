Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barfuss, Christian

Barfuss, Christian, Bishop of the River Heights Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1928, was born Aug. 11, 1862, in Bern, Switzerland, a son of Christian Barfuss and Maria Aschbacher. He was baptized Jan. 17, 1884, came to Utah that same year, was ordained a High Priest by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop April 24, 1921, by James E. Talmage.
