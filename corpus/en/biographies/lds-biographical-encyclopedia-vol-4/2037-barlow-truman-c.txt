Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barlow, Truman C.

Barlow, Truman C., Bishop of the Iona Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1913 to 1919, was born Oct. 29, 1879, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of T. H. Barlow and Fanny Call. He was ordained a Bishop Nov. 2, 1913, by David O. McKay.
