Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Owen, Evan Richard

Owen, Evan Richard, Bishop of the Wellsville Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1900 to 1909, was born June 14, 1867, at Wellsville, Utah, the son of Evan Owen and Elizabeth Walters. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a Seventy July 9, 1897, by J. Golden Kimball, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1897-1899, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 25, 1900, by Francis M. Lyman.
