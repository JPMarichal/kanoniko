Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Newbold, Joseph

Newbold, Joseph, Bishop of the Logan 4th Ward, Cache Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1913, was born April 12, 1858, in Breashows, England, a son of John Newbold and Elizabeth Deakin. He was baptized Dec. 22, 1867, by John Newbold, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 28, 1906, by Rudger Clawson.
