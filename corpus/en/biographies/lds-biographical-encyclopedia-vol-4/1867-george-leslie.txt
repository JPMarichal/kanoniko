Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

George, Leslie

George, Leslie, Bishop of the Escalante North Ward, Garfield Stake, Piute Co., Utah, from 1921 to 1928, was born Aug. 18, 1876, at Kanosh, Millard Co., Utah, the son of William George and Mary Jane Prows. He was baptized Nov. 6, 1884, ordained a High Priest March 15, 1901, by Francis M. Lyman, and ordained a Bishop in 1921.
