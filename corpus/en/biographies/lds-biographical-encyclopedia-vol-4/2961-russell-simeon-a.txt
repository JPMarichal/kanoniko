Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Russell, Simeon A.

Russell, Simeon A., Bishop of the Cedarview Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1918, was born Oct. 10, 1875, in Fillmore, Millard Co., Utah, a son of Allen Russell and Flora A. Howd. He was baptized July 3, 1884, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 21, 1913, by James E. Talmage.
