Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Powell, Albert Harris

Powell, Albert Harris, second counselor in the Nebo Stake presidency, Utah, from 1924 to 1930, was born Feb. 13, 1883, in Payson, Utah, the son of William Powell and Christlane Petersen. He was baptized when eight years old, and after being ordained a Seventy in 1908 by J. Golden Kimball, he filled a mission to Great Britain in 1908-1911. He was ordained a High Priest by Orson F. Whitney, and was set apart as second counselor in the stake presidency Nov. 23, 1924, by John A. Widtsoe. He died at Salt Lake City July 17, 1930.
