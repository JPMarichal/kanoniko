Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Billings, Geerge Pierce

Billings, Geerge Pierce, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born July 25, 1827, in Kirtland, Ohio. Soon after his arrival in Salt Lake Valley he went to California, but later, responding to a call from the Church, filled a mission to Carson Valley, Nevada. He finally made his residence in Manti, Utah, where he died Dec. 2, 1896, survived by a large family.
