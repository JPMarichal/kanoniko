Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larson, Lars Peter

Larson, Lars Peter, Bishop of the Leland Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1913 to 1921, was born May 8, 1863, in Glostrup, Sjaelland, Denmark, the son of Lars Larsen and Johanne Jensen. He was baptized when about eight years of age, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 24, 1913, by Francis M. Lyman.
