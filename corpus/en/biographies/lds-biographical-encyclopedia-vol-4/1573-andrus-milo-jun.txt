Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cottonwood Stake

Andrus, Milo, jun.

Andrus, Milo, jun., Bishop of the Big Cottonwood Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1905 to 1911, was born Sept. 30, 1848, in Liverpool, England, the son of Milo Andrus and Sarah Ann Miles. He emigrated to Utah in 1850, was baptized when eight years old, and ordained a High Priest July 15, 1877, by Daniel H. Wells.
