Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thatcher, John B., jun.

Thatcher, John B., jun., Bishop of the Bedford Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1895 to 1905, was born May 22, 1859, in Payson, Utah, a son of John B. Thatcher and Rachel Hannah Davis. He was baptized when young, filled a mission to the States in 1881-1882, and was ordained a High Priest Aug. 4, 1895, by George Osmond. Prior to 1905, he presided over the Bedford Branch.
