Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcgrath, James Vernon

Mcgrath, James Vernon, Bishop of the Franklin Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Sept. 14, 1906, in Manassa, Colorado, a son of James Franklin McGrath and Laura E. Elledge. He was baptized Oct. 3, 1914, filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1926-1929, was ordained a High Priest by his father in December, 1929, and a Bishop by Harry L. Payne.
