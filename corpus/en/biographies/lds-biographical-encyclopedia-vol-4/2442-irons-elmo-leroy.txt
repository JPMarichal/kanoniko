Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Irons, Elmo Leroy

Irons, Elmo Leroy, Bishop of the Moroni East Ward, Moroni Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Sept. 15, 1886, in Moroni, Utah, a son of Will L. Irons and Jane Jolly. He was baptized Nov. 18, 1896, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1919-1921, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 12, 1922, by James W. Anderson, and ordained a Bishop April 8, 1928, by Richard R. Lyman.
