Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dalton, Frederick William

Dalton, Frederick William, second counselor in the Boise Stake presidency: Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born March 5, 1896, in Plain City, Utah, the son of Frederick William Dalton and Annie Dorothy Benson. He was baptized June 2, 1904, was ordained a High Priest March 10, 1929, by Geo. F. Richards, and set apart as second counselor in the stake presidency March 10, 1929.
