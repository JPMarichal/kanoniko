Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Crowther, Thomas A.

Crowther, Thomas A., second counselor in the San Luis Stake presidency, Colo., from 1893 to 1905, and first counselor from 1905 to 1908, was born March 29, 1858, in Cedar City, Utah, the son of Thomas Crowther and Jane Jewkes. He was baptized in 1866, ordained a High Priest Jan. 29, 1893, and set apart as second counselor by Francis M. Lyman.
