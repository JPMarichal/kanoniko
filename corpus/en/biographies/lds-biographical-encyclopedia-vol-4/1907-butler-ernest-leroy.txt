Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Butler, Ernest Leroy

Butler, Ernest Leroy, Bishop of the Belvedere Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930, was born Feb. 13, 1885, in Ogden, Utah, the son of Myron W. Butler and Matilda Stowell. He was baptized Feb. 13, 1893, ordained a Seventy July 19, 1927, filled two missions to Samoa, one in 1909-1912, and another in 1921-1927, part of the time (1924-1927) presiding over the mission, and was sustained as a Bishop Jan. 27, 1929. He died March 17, 1933.
