Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bair, Hyrum Lester

Bair, Hyrum Lester, Bishop of the Coveville Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1930, was born Sept. 29, 1872, in Richmond, Utah, the son of Hyrum Bair and Mary E. Van Orden. He was baptized May 3, 1883, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1894-1896, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 24, 1909, and a Bishop April 22, 1917, by David O. McKay.
