Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Forsyth, Nell

Forsyth, Nell, Bishop of the Teasdale Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Nov. 11, 1881, in Thurber, Utah, a son of Thomas Robert Forsyth and Fredonia M. Gohene. He was baptized June 4, 1896, ordained a High Priest Nov. 4, 1926, by Joseph F. Smith, jun., and was sustained as Bishop Jan. 12, 1930.
