Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Spencer, George Sterling

Spencer, George Sterling, Bishop of the Forest Dale Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930, was born Oct. 13, 1870, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Claudius Victor Spencer and Matilda Price. He was baptized June 1, 1882, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Netherlands in 1894-1896 and presiding over the mission five months. He was ordained a High Priest Nov. 29, 1914, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop Dec. 24, 1928.
