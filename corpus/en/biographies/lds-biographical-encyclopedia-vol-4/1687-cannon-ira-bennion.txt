Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cannon, Ira Bennion

Cannon, Ira Bennion, second counselor in the Duchesne Stake presidency, Utah, from 1919 to 1920, was born Feb. 11, 1889, in Taylorsville, Utah, the son of Angus M. Cannon and Maria Bennion. He was baptized July 4, 1897, ordained a High Priest Oct. 26, 1913, by Nephi L. Morris, and a Bishop June 28, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard. He acted as Bishop of the Mt. Emmons Ward until June 27, 1920, when he was chosen as second counselor in the Duchesne Stake presidency, and was set apart to that position by Rudger Clawson.
