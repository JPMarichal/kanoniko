Genealogical Society of Utah

The following is a complete list of the officers or directors of the Genealogical Society of Utah, which was organized in 1894.

Anderson, Nephi

Anderson, Nephi, secretary of the Genealogical Society of Utah from 1922 to 1923, was born Jan. 22, 1866, in Christiania, Norway, a son of Christian Andersen and Petronelle Nielsen. He died in Salt Lake City Jan. 6, 1923. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 296.)
