Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mccullough, Thomas Jefferson

Mccullough, Thomas Jefferson, Bishop of the Alpine Ward, Alpine Stake, Utah, from 1856 to 1893, was born Aug. 18, 1826, in White County, Tenn., the son of Thomas McCullough and Mary Hefner. He was baptized April 9, 1842, emigrated to Utah in 1852, was ordained a High Priest May 18, 1856, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 15, 1856, by Edward Hunter. He died April 10, 1893.
