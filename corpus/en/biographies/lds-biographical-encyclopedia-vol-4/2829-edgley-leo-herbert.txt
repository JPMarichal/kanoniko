Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Edgley, Leo Herbert

Edgley, Leo Herbert, Bishop of the Pocatello 2nd Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1930, was born May 10, 1884, in London, England. He was baptized July 7, 1892, ordained a High Priest Nov. 25, 1917, by Heber J. Grant, and a Bishop Feb. 26, 1921, by James E. Talmage.
