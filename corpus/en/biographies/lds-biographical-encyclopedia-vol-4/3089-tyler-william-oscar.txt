Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tyler, William Oscar

Tyler, William Oscar, Bishop of the Ashurst Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Nov. 21, 1893, in Central, Arizona, a son of Oscar Taylor and Mary Ann Virginia Juan. He was baptized Nov. 21, 1901, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 21, 1922, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
