Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kerr, John Bankhead

Kerr, John Bankhead, Bishop of the Wellsville 2nd Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1920 to 1929, was born Jan. 28, 1888, at Wellsville, Utah, the son of David A. Kerr and Malinda Bankhead. He was baptized April 1, 1896, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1909-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 18, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
