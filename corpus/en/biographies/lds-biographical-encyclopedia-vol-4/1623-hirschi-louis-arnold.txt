Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hirschi, Louis Arnold

Hirschi, Louis Arnold, Bishop of the Park Valley Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Nov. 17, 1896, in Park Valley, Utah, a son of David Hitachi and Mary Zaugg. He was baptized June 30, 1906, ordained a High Priest Sept. 4, 1920, by Jonathan C. Cutler, and a Bishop April 20, 1930, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
