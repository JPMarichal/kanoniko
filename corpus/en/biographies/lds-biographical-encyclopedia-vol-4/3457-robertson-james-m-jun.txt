Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robertson, James M., jun.

Robertson, James M., jun., presiding Elder of the South Park Branch, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Aug. 18, 1894, in Park City, Utah, a son of James M. Robertson and Rebecca A. Wilson. He Was baptized in August, 1905, filled a mission to the Central States in 1915-1917, and was ordained a High Priest Oct. 24, 1925, by Orson F. Whitney.
