Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sevier Stake

Anderson, James

Anderson, James, Bishop of the Richfield 3rd Ward, Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, from 1915 to 1919, was born Aug. 3, 1883, in Elsinore, Sevier Co., Utah, a son of Jens Christian Andersen and Johanne Slyter. He was baptized Aug. 31, 1892, filled a mission to Japan in 1905-1907, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 19, 1915, by Orson F. Whitney.
