Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Maxwell, James A.

Maxwell, James A., Bishop of the Peoa Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1924, was born July 28, 1873, in Peoa, a son of Ralph Maxwell and Isabel McGavin. He was baptized May 27, 1883, ordained a High Priest Feb. 27, 1916, by Geo. W. Young, and a Bishop Oct. 28, 1923, by Richard R. Lyman. Bishop Maxwell died July 14, 1928.
