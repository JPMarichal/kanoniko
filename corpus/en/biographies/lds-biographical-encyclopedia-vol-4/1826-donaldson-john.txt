Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Donaldson, John

Donaldson, John, Bishop of the Teton Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1884 to 1893, was born Sept. 30, 1842, in London, England, the son of Wm. Donaldson and Rachel Notman. He was baptized in June, 1862, in England, came to Utah in 1863, filled a mission to England in 1880-1882, and was ordained a Bishop June 10, 1884, by Wilford Woodruff. He died July 23, 1926.
