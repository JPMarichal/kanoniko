Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Erastus P.

Peterson, Erastus P., Bishop of the Levan Ward, Juab Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born May 17, 1889, at Levan, Juab Co., Utah, a son of Erick Peterson and Anna Sorensen. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1897, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Denmark in 1913-1915, was sustained as Bishop March 18, 1923, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop in Salt Lake City by George Albert Smith.
