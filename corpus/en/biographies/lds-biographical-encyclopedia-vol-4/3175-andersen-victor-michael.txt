Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Andersen, Victor Michael

Andersen, Victor Michael, Bishop of the Vineyard Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born June 29, 1879, in Aalborg, Denmark, a son of Peter Christian Andersen and Marlane Thomine Michaelsen. He was baptized April 13, 1888, emigrated to Utah in. 1893, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 25, 1920, by Thomas N. Naylor and ordained a Bishop Jan. 18, 1930, by George Albert Smith.
