Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pendleton, John Benjamin

Pendleton, John Benjamin, Bishop of the Garfield Ward, Oquirrh Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1923, was born Oct. 18, 1876, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Joshua Pendleton and Zelpha Stewart. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1885, ordained a Seventy Jan. 20, 1900, by Rulon S. Wells, and filled a mission to Hawaii in 1900-1901. He was ordained a High Priest June 23, 1901, by Reed Smoot, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 30, 1909, by Rudger Clawson, to preside over the Grass Creek Ward. He was sustained as Bishop of the Garfield Ward Sept. 24, 1916, and set apart to that position by Geo. Albert Smith.
