Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whitlock, Charles Clyde

Whitlock, Charles Clyde, Bishop of the Mayfield Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1929, was born Dec. 29, 1882, in Ephraim, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Charles Whitlock and Anne E. Peterson. He was baptized March 10, 1891, ordained a High Priest Aug. 5, 1923, by James E. Talmage and ordained a Bishop May 30, 1926, by Rudger Clawson.
