Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gardner, William H.

Gardner, William H., Bishop of the West Jordan Ward, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1919, was born April 20, 1877, in West Jordan, Utah, a son of Duncan Gardner and Elizabeth Irving. He was baptized June 4, 1885, ordained a Seventy May 12, 1899, by George Teasdale, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1899-1901, was ordained a High Priest May 28, 1911, by Francis M. Lyman, and sustained as Bishop April 23, 1911.
