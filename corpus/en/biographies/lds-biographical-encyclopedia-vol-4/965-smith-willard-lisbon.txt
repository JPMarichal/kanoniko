Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Willard Lisbon

Smith, Willard Lisbon, president of the Samoan Mission from 1927 to 1934, was born Sept. 2, 1891, in Farmington, Utah, a son of Willard G. Smith and Mariah Lamb. He was baptized Aug. 3, 1900, filled a mission to Samoa in 1915-1920, and was set apart to preside over that Mission Dec. 9, 1926.
