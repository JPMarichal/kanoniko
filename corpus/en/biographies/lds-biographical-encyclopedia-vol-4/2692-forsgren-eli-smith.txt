Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Forsgren, Eli Smith

Forsgren, Eli Smith, Bishop of the Riverdale Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Sept. 25, 1890, in Riverdale, a son of Eli Forsgren and Frances Smith. He was baptized Sept. 15, 1900, was ordained an Elder Sept. 11, 1910, by James R. McNeil, filled a mission to France in 1910-1913, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 11, 1922, by Richard R. Lyman.
