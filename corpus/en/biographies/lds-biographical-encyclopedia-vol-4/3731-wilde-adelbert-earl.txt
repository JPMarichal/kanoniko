Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilde, Adelbert Earl

Wilde, Adelbert Earl, Bishop of the Kemmerer Ward, Woodruff Stake, Wyo., from 1926 to 1930+, was born June 24, 1890, in Thayne, Wyo., a son of Thomas Henry Wilde and Rosetta Priscilla Nelson. He was baptized June 24, 1898, ordained an Elder by Reuben F. Burton in 1913, and filled a mission to California in 1913-1915. He was ordained a High Priest Sept. 21, 1924, and a Bishop Jan. 16, 1927, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
