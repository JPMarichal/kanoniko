Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bingham, Heber Raymond

Bingham, Heber Raymond, Bishop of the Linrose Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Nov. 20, 1884, in Wilson, Utah, the son of Brigham Heber Bingham and Catherine R. Wilson. He was baptized March 28, 1893, ordained a Seventy in 1906, filled a mission to Germany in 1906-1909, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 24, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
