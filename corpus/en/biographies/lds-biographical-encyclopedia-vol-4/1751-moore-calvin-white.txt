Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moore, Calvin White

Moore, Calvin White, Bishop of the Lawrence Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1889 to 1900, was born July 21, 1827, in Palmer, Hampden Co., Mass., the son of Ethan A. Moore and Sarah Webber. He was baptized in 1835, in Kirtland, Ohio, served as a member of the Mormon Battalion in 1846-1847 and came to Utah in 1848. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 12, 1889, by John Henry Smith.
