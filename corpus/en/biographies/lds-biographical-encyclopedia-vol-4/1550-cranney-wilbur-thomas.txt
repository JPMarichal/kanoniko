Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cranney, Wilbur Thomas

Cranney, Wilbur Thomas, Bishop of the Marion Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1926, was born Jan. 4, 1872, in Logan, Utah, the son of Hyrum King Cranney and Elizabeth White. He was baptized in May 1881, and ordained a High Priest May 6, 1916, by Wm. T. Harper, and a Bishop Oct. 31, 1920, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
