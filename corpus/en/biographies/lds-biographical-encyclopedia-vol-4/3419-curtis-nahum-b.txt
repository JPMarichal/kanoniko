Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Curtis, Nahum B.

Curtis, Nahum B., Bishop of the Victor Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1901 to 1913, was born June 30, 1863, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Foster Curtis and Clarissa Bemas. He was baptized Oct. 13, 1872, ordained a High Priest May 31, 1893, by Wm. F. Rigby, and a Bishop Sept. 2, 1901, by John Henry Smith.
