Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, Leo Daniels

Williams, Leo Daniels, Bishop of the Cherry Creek Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Dec. 24, 1902, in Malad, Idaho, a son of John S. Williams and Annie Eliza Daniels. He was baptized July 1, 1911, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 22, 1928, by Stephen L. Richards.
