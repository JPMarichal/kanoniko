Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nelson, Ephraim

Nelson, Ephraim, Bishop of the Moroni West Ward, Moroni Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Sept. 30, 1865, in Moroni, Utah, a son of Jens Christian Nielsen and Anna Andersen. He was baptized Aug. 27, 1876, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1900-1902, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 12, 1915, by James E. Talmage and ordained a Bishop Dec. 18, 1921, by George F. Richards.
