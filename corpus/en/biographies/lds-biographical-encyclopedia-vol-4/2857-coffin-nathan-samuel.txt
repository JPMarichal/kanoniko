Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Coffin, Nathan Samuel

Coffin, Nathan Samuel, Bishop of the Marsh Center Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1903 to 1919, was bornI Sept. 11, 1870, in Huntsville, Utah, a son of Nathan H. Coffin and Lydia Ann Cook. He was baptized April 2, 1899, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1900-1901, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 7, 1903.
