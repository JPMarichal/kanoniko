Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

King, William

King, William, president of the Hawaiian Mission from 1887 to 1890, was born April 8, 1834, in Cicero, Onondaga Co., N.Y., a son of Thomas R. King and Matilda Robison. He was baptized in 1843, came to Utah at an early day, and was identified with some of the foremost interests of the Territory. Twelve years of his life were spent in missionary labors among the natives of the Sandwich Islands. He also filled a mission to the United States. He was set apart for his mission to the Sandwich Islands Oct. 18, 1886, and being made president April 6, 1887, he acted in that capacity until Nov. 13, 1889. He sailed from Honolulu May 9, 1890, for his home in Utah, accompanied by his wife.
