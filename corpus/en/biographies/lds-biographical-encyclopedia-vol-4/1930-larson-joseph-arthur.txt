Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larson, Joseph Arthur

Larson, Joseph Arthur, Bishop of the Hamilton Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1925, was born Dec. 18, 1874, in Gunnison, Utah, a son of John Larson and Anna Erickson. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1883, ordained a High Priest May 6, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 3, 1923, by James E. Talmage.
