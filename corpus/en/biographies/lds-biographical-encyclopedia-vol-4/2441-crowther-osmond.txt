Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Crowther, Osmond

Crowther, Osmond, Bishop of the Fountain Green Ward, Moroni Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930, was born Aug. 13, 1898, in Sanford, Conejos Co., Colorado, a son of James F. Crowther and Mary Olsen. He was baptized Sept. 2, 1906, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1919-1920, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 16, 1929, by Rudger Clawson.
