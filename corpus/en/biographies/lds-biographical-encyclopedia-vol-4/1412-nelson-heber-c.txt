Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nelson, Heber C.

Nelson, Heber C., presiding Elder of the Bramwell branch, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1906 to 1907, was born Feb. 3, 1871, in Bloomington, Bear Lake Co., Idaho, the son of Christian Nelson and Ann Josephine Evans. He was sustained as presiding Elder Dec. 9, 1906.
