Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rigby Stake

Agren, Pehr Alfred

Agren, Pehr Alfred, Bishop of the Lewisville Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1923 to 1928, was born Jan. 31, 1877, at Harrisville, Weber Co., Utah, a son of Pehr Agren and Johanna Felt. He was baptized May 10, 1885, ordained a High Priest June 1, 1913, by John W. Hart, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 23, 1923, by Richard R. Lyman.
