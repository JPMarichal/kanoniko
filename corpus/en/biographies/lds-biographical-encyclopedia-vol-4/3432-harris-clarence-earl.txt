Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Clarence Earl

Harris, Clarence Earl, Bishop of the Tetonia Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1927, was born May 24, 1879, in Parowan, Utah, a son of Charles Harris and Louisa Hall. He was baptized in November, 1888, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1913-1916, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 27, 1916, by James E. Talmage, and a Bishop in 1919.
