Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Miller, Christian Marius

Miller, Christian Marius, Bishop of the Lawrence Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1913, was born May 5, 1857, in Nexo, Bornholm, Denmark, the son of Peter P. Miller and Petrea C. Rebo. He was baptized in November, 1868, in Denmark, came to Utah in 1890, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1898-1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 17, 1900, by George Teasdale.
