Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jacobsen, Soren Magnus

Jacobsen, Soren Magnus, Bishop of the Wales Ward, Moroni Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930, was born Jan. 2, 1881, in Braband, Aarhus amt, Denmark, a son of Anders Peter Jacobsen and Ane Marie Madsen. He was baptized Jan. 7, 1890, emigrated to Utah in 1884, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 14, 1924, by Rudger Clawson, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 8, 1929, by George F. Richards.
