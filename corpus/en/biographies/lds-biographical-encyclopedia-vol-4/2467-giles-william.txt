Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Giles, William

Giles, William, Bishop of the Milton Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1893 to 1921, was born June 3, 1867, in Milton, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of John H. Giles and Ann Kingman. He was baptized Nov. 15, 1875, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop in June, 1893, by Francis M. Lyman.
