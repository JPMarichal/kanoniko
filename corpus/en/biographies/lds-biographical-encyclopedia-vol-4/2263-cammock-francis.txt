Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cammock, Francis

Cammock, Francis, Bishop of the Ballard Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, in 1924, was born Nov. 29, 1879, in Mallory, Nottinghamshire, England. He was baptized April 16, 1890, ordained a High Priest Aug. 24, 1913, by Francis M. Lyman, and ordained a Bishop April 26, 1924, by Geo. F. Richards.
