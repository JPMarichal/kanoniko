Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Buchanan, Osmond

Buchanan, Osmond, Bishop of the Blackfoot 1st Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1917, was born Sept. 25, 1871, in Glenwood, Sevier Co., Utah, the son of Archibald Buchanan and Maria Larson. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1880, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1899-1901, settled in Blackfoot in 1901, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 8, 1902, by H. P. Smith, and a Bishop May 7, 1915, by Geo. Albert Smith.
