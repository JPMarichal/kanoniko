Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nevada Stake

Arnoldsen, Hyrum Smith

Arnoldsen, Hyrum Smith, Bishop of the Preston Ward, Nevada Stake, Nevada, from 1901 to 1912, was born July 27, 1866, in Moroni, Utah, a son of Lars Chr. Arnoldsen and Else Mortensen. He filled a mission to the Northern States in 1896-1898, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 12, 1901, by Anthon H. Lurid.
