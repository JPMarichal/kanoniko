Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Perkins, Daniel

Perkins, Daniel, Bishop of the Bluff Ward, San Juan Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1928, was born Jan. 9, 1887, in Bluff, the son of Hyrum Perkins and Rachel Cory. He was baptized Jan. 13, 1895, ordained a Seventy Feb. 27, 1911, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1911-1914, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 27, 1923, by Geo. F. Richards.
