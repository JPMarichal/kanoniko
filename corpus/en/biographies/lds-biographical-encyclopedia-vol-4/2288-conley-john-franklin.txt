Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Conley, John Franklin

Conley, John Franklin, Bishop of the Portage Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1909 to 1918, was born Oct. 18, 1865, in Farmington, Davis Co., Utah, a son of John Conley and Lydia Thornton. He was baptized Dec. 1, 1874, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Samoa in 1895-1899, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 17, 1899, by Francis M. Lyman, and ordained a Bishop April 6, 1909, by Rudger Clawson.
