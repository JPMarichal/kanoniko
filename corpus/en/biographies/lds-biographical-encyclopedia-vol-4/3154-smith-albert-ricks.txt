Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Albert Ricks

Smith, Albert Ricks, president of the San Luis Stake of Zion, Colo., from 1892 to 1905, was born April 1, 1862, in Paragonah, Utah, the son of Silas S. Smith and Sarah Ann Ricks. He was set apart as president of the San Luis Stake Feb. 17, 1892, by Lorenzo Snow.
