Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Frodsham, James R.

Frodsham, James R., Bishop of the Kimball Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1927 to 1928, was born Dec. 23, 1883, the son of James Frodsham and Alta Rudd. He was baptized in 1891 and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 7, 1927, by David O. McKay.
