Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mangum, Corel Henry

Mangum, Corel Henry, Bishop of the Widtsoe Ward, Garfield Stake, Piute Co., Utah, from 1925 to 1929, was born April 21, 1883, at Pahria, Kane Co., Utah, the son of James Mangum and Mary Ann Smith. He was baptized when about eight years old, at Pahria, ordained a High Priest Feb. 3, 1907, by Mahonri M. Steele, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 30, 1925, by Orson F. Whitney.
