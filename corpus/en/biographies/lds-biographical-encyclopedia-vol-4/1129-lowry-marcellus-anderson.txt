Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lowry, Marcellus Anderson

Lowry, Marcellus Anderson, Bishop of Taylorville Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1917 to 1930+, was born April 7, 1877, in Fanshaw, Hanover Co., Virginia, the son of Benjamin Franklin Lowry and Victoria Eddleton. He was baptized in 1891, moved to Canada in 1899, was ordained a High Priest by George Teasdale, and ordained a Bishop by George Albert Smith in 1917.
