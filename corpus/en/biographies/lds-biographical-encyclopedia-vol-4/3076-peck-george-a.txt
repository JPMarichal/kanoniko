Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peck, George A.

Peck, George A., Bishop of the Bryce Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1909 to 1924, was born Jan. 16, 1884, in Nutriosa, Arizona, a son of George O. Peck and Barbara E. Bryce. He was baptized April 6, 1892, ordained a High Priest Dec. 1, 1907, by Geo. Albert Smith, and a Bishop June 6, 1909, by Hyrum M. Smith.
