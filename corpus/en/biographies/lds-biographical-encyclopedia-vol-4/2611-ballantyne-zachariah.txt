Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ballantyne, Zachariah

Ballantyne, Zachariah, Bishop of the West Weber Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1888 to 1893, was born Oct. 13, 1856, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Richard Ballantyne and Mary Pearce. He was ordained a Bishop by Franklin D. Richards.
