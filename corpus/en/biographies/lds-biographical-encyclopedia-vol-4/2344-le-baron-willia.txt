Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Le Baron, Willia

Le Baron, William Johnson, first counselor in the Maricopa Stake presidency from 1902 to 1905, was born Feb. 6, 1856, in Payson, Utah Co., Utah, a son of David Tulley Le Baron and Esther Malita Johnson. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest Sept. 27, 1891, and set apart as first counselor Dec. 4, 1902..
