Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Chugg, Nathaniel B.

Chugg, Nathaniel B., Bishop of the Nibley Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Aug. 8, 1888, at Providence, Cache Co., Utah, the son of William Chugg and Eliza Fredrick. He was baptized Sept. 2, 1896, ordained a High Priest April 13, 1924, by Rudger Clawson, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 16, 1929, by George Albert Smith.
