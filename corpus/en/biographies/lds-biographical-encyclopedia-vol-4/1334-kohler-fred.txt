Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kohler, Fred

Kohler, Fred, Bishop of the Byron Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1901 to 1913, was born Nov. 29, 1887, in Switzerland, the son of Christian Reinhart Kohler and Rosina Lechty. After emigrating to America, he was baptized Nov. 29, 1887, in the Endowment House, Salt Lake City, Utah, was ordained a Seventy Nov. 11, 1890, by John Morgan, filled a mission to Switzerland and Germany in 1890-1893, went to Big Horn in 1900, was ordained a High Priest May 6, 1900, by Abraham O. Woodruff, and a Bishop May 26, 1901, by the same Apostle.
