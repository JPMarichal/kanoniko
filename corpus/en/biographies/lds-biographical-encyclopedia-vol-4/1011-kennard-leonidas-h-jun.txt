Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kennard, Leonidas H., jun.

Kennard, Leonidas H., jun., president of the Society Islands Mission from 1920 to 1922, was born Oct. 26, 1869, in Farmington, Utah, the son of Leonidas H. Kennard, sen., and Joanah Louisa Gleason. He was baptized in 1877, set apart for his first mission July 2, 1896, and returned home in 1899. He Was set apart Jan. 9, 1920, as president and returned in 1922. (See also Bear River Stake.)
