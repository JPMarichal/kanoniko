Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wight, Thaddeus

Wight, Thaddeus, Bishop of the Harper Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1920, was born April 14, 1854, in West Jordan (Wight's Fort), Utah, the son of Lyman Wight and Harriet Bateman. He was baptized in January, 1866, by his father. He filled a mission to the Southern States in 1897-1899 and was ordained a High Priest Dec. 22, 1899, by Geo. Kelley, and a Bishop May 29, 1910, by Heber J. Grant.
