Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fisher, Frederic Merle

Fisher, Frederic Merle, Bishop of the Rexburg 3rd Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930, was born Jan. 31, 1887, at Bountiful, Utah, the son of John Fisher and Harriet Knighton. He moved to Rexburg in 1910, and was ordained a High Priest in December, 1912, by James Blake, and a Bishop Sept. 27, 1925, by Melvin J. Ballard.
