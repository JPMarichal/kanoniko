Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Maughan, Edward Leroy

Maughan, Edward Leroy, Bishop of the Mapleton Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930, was born March 24, 1895, in Logan, Utah, the son of Peter W. Maughan and Mary L. Naef. He was baptized March 31, 1903, moved to Mapleton in 1921, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 31, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard.
