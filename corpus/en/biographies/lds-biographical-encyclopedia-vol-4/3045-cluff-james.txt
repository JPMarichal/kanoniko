Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cluff, James

Cluff, James, Bishop of the Central Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1889 to 1902, was born Oct. 7, 1866, in Heber City, Utah, the son of Moses M. Cluff and Rebecca Langman. He was baptized June 22, 1877, and ordained a Bishop by John Henry Smith Dec. 29, 1889. He died about 1910.
