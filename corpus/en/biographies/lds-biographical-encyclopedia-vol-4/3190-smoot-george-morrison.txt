Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smoot, George Morrison

Smoot, George Morrison, Bishop of the Vineyard Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1904 to 1908, was born Jan. 9, 1864, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Abraham O. Smoot and Anna Mouritzen. He was baptized in May, 1872, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1897-1899, was ordained a High Priest in February, 1892, and sustained as Bishop March 27, 1904.
