Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brimhall, Logan

Brimhall, Logan, Bishop of the Taylor Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1925 to 1930, was born Oct. 12, 1892, in Mesa, Arizona, a son of Norman Andrew Brimhall and Mary Agnes Willis. He was baptized Oct. 12, 1900, ordained an Elder May 17, 1914, by his father, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1914-1916, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 21, 1925, by Orson F. Whitney.
