Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Porter, Charles Worthy

Porter, Charles Worthy, Bishop of the Fairview Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1895 to 1898, was born Dec. 17, 1867, in Porterville, Utah, the son of Alma Porter and Minerva Dewell. He was baptized in 1876, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 20, 1895, by John Henry Smith.
