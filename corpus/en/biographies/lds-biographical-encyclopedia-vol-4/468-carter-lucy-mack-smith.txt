Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carter, Lucy Mack Smith

Carter, Lucy Mack Smith, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born April 14, 1890, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the daughter of Joseph F. Smith and Alice Kimball. She was educated in the public schools and the L. D. S. University, was active all her life in Church and community affairs, and is the mother of seven children. After her release from the General Board, which her family duties necessitated, she was active in the Highland Park Ward until her death which occurred Nov. 24, 1933.
