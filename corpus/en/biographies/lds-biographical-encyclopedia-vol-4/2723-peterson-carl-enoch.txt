Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Carl Enoch

Peterson, Carl Enoch, Bishop of the Glencoe Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1918 to 1919, was born Dec. 22, 1889, in Mink Creek, Idaho, a son of Peter Peterson and Christina Olson. He was baptized Dec. 22, 1897, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 9, 1918, by Orson F. Whitney.
