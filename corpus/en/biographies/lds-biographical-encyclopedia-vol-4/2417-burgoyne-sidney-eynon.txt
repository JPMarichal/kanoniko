Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burgoyne, Sidney Eynon

Burgoyne, Sidney Eynon, Bishop of the Montpelier 3rd Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1930, was born Sept. 25, 1888, in Montpelier, a son of Edward L. Burgoyne and Binhie Cederlund. He was baptized in October, 1896, and having been ordained an Elder in 1908, he filled a mission to the Central States in 1912-1914. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 20, 1914, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop March 11, 1923, by Stephen L. Richards.
