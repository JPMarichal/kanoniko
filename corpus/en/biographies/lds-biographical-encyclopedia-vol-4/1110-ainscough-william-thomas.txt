Alberta Stake

Ainscough, William Thomas

Ainscough, William Thomas, presiding Elder from 1906 to 1913 and Bishop from 1915 to 1919 of the Woolford Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, was born July 10, 1854, in Cottonwood, Salt Lake Co., Utah, the son of William Ainscough and Mary Clark. He was baptized in July, 1866, ordained a High Priest by Henry L. Hinman May 13, 1906 and ordained a Bishop April 8, 1915, by Geo. Albert Smith.
