Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Waters, William Birch

Waters, William Birch, Bishop of the Bingham Ward, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1913, was born Sept. 25, 1858, in Springville, Utah, a son of John Waters and Sarah Birch. He was baptized June 13, 1868, and was ordained a High Priest and set apart as second counselor Oct. 23, 1899, by Charles W. Penrose. He died Nov. 18, 1935.
