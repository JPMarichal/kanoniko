Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Powell, Thomas Ezra

Powell, Thomas Ezra, Bishop of the Marriott Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1918, was born May 2, 1875, in Marriott, a son of John Davis Powell and Sarah Joyce. He was baptized July 3, 1884, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 18, 1908, by Francis M. Lyman.
