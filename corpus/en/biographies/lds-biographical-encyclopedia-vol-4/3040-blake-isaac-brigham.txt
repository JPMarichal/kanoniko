Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Blake, Isaac Brigham

Blake, Isaac Brigham, Bishop of the Pima Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1927 to 1930, was born April 14, 1877, in Wanship, Utah, a son of William Blake and Mary Lake. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1885, filled a mission to England in 1910-1912, was ordained a High Priest in 1912 by Andrew Kimball, and a Bishop Feb. 26, 1927, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
