Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hillstead, Robert Henry

Hillstead, Robert Henry, Bishop of the Fairview Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1924 to 1930, was born May 15, 1870, in Lincolnshire, England, a son of John B. Hillstead and Charlotte Gray. He came to Utah in 1870, was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest Aug. 30, 1913, by Heber J. Grant, and a Bishop Sept. 21, 1924, by John A. Widtsoe.
