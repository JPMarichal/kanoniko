Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stallings, Virgil B.

Stallings, Virgil B., Bishop of the Eden Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, frorh 1924 to 1927, was born Nov. 8, 1874, in Eden, a son of Joseph Stallings and Charlotte Hussey. He was baptized June 1, 1886, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 17, 1924, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
