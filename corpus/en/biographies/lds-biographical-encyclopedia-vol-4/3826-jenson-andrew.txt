Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jenson, Andrew

Jenson, Andrew, a member of the board of directors of the Genealogical Society of Utah from 1894 to 1896, was born Dec. 11, 1850, in Torslev parish, Hjorring amt, Denmark, a son of Christian Jensen and Kirsten Andersen. He holds the position of Assistant Church Historian. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 261; Vol. 2, p. 542.)
