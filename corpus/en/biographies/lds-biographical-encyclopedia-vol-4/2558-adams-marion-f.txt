Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

North Davis Stake

Adams, Marion F.

Adams, Marion F., Bishop of the Layton Ward, North Davis Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1915, was born April 5, 1869, in Kaysville, Utah, the son of Geo. W. Adams and Mary Ann Pilling. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 25, 1910, by Orson F. Whitney.
