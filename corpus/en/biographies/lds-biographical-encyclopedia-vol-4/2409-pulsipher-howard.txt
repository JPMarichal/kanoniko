Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pulsipher, Howard

Pulsipher, Howard, Bishop of the Mesquite Ward, Moapa Stake, Nevada, from 1929 to 1930+, was born. Jan. 30, 1894, in Hebron, Utah, a son of John David Pulsipher and Ann Elizabeth Bowles. He was baptized May 4, 1902, ordained a High Priest June 3, 1927, by Willard L. Jones, and ordained a Bishop June 2, 1929, by Orson F. Whitney.
