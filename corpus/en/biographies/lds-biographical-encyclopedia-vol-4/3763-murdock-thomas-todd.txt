Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Murdock, Thomas Todd

Murdock, Thomas Todd, Bishop of the Farnum Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born March 4, 1866, in Heber City, Utah, a son of John Murry Murdock and Ann Steele. He was baptized in 1874, filled a mission to the Central States in 1907-1909, was ordained a High Priest July 30, 1922, by Daniel G. Miller, and was sustained as Bishop of the Farnum Ward Dec. 16, 1928.
