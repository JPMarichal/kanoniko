Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mecham, Moroni

Mecham, Moroni, Bishop of the Kimball Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1905 to 1909, was born March 25, 1868, in Richville, Utah, a son of Leonidas Clinton Mecham and Margaret E. Champlean. He was baptized in April, 1876, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 21, 1905, by Rudger Clawson.
