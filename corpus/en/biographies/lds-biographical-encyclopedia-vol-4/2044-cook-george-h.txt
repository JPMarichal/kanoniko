Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cook, George H.

Cook, George H., Bishop of the Milo Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930, was born Nov. 10, 1877, in South Weber, Utah, a son of David S. Cook and Janette Hunter. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1886, filled a mission to England in 1914-1916, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 24, 1917, by James E. Talmage, who also ordained him a Bishop May 24, 1924.
