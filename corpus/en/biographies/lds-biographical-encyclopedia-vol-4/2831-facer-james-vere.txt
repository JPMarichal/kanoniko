Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Facer, James Vere

Facer, James Vere, Bishop of North Pocatello Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1928, was born May 28, 1889, in Willard, Utah, a son of James J. Facet and Myrtle Lishenberg. He was baptized June 1, 1897, ordained a High Priest Nov. 28, 1920, by Jos. F. Smith, jun., and chosen as Bishop May 15, 1927.
