Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grant, Joseph Hyrum

Grant, Joseph Hyrum, president of the Davis Stake, Utah, from 1902 to 1915, was born Oct. 17, 1853, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Jedediah M. Grant and Susan Noble. He was baptized Oct. 17, 1861, ordained a High Priest July 22, 1877, by Franklin D. Richards, served as Bishop of the West Bountiful Ward from 1885 to 1890, and as second counselor in the Davis Stake presidency from 1894 to 1902. He died Nov. 17, 1917.
