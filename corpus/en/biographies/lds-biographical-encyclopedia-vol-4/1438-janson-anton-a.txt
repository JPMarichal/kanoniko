Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Janson, Anton A.

Janson, Anton A., Bishop of the Brigham City 3rd Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1890 to 1899, was born March 26, 1826, in Galstad, Elfsborgslan, Sweden, the son of Johan Janson and Ebba Peterson. He was baptized Nov. 9, 1854, by John Gronbeck, emigrated to Utah in 1858, located in Brigham City in 1859, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 19, 1877, and ordained a Bishop March 24, 1889.
