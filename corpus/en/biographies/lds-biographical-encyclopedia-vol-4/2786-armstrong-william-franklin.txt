Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Armstrong, William Franklin

Armstrong, William Franklin, Bishop of the Enoch Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, in 1930+, was born Jan. 23, 1885, at Enoch, Utah, the son of Joseph H. Armstrong and Mary Ann Smith. He was baptized June 1, 1893, and ordained a High Priest and a Bishop March 9, 1930, by Richard R. Lyman.
