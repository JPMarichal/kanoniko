Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Young, Walter Ernest

Young, Walter Ernest, Bishop of the Juarez Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Nov. 22, 1887, in Manassa, Colorado, the son of James A. Young and Lillian A. Hurst. He was baptized in 1896, filled a mission to Mexico in 1910-1913, was ordained a High Priest May 11, 1915, by Anthony W. Ivins, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 15, 1922, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
