Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nilson, Andrew Matts

Nilson, Andrew Matts, Bishop of the Osmond Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1901 to 1915, was born May 15, 1860, near Christianstad, Sweden, a son of Matts Swenson and Ella Anderson. He was baptized May 5, 1881, after coming to Utah in 1879, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 18, 1901, by Jos. F. Smith.
