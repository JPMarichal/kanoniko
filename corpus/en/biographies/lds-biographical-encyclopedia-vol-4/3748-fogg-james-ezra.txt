Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fogg, James Ezra

Fogg, James Ezra, second counselor in the Yellowstone Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1909 to 1913, was born Oct. 8, 1868, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of James E. Fogg and Martha Woodard. He acted as Bishop of the St. Anthony Ward, Yellowstone Stake, from 1906 to 1909, and was set apart as second counselor Jan. 10, 1909, by Geo. F. Richards. He died Aug. 18, 1912, at Ogden, Utah.
