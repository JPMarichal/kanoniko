Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lee, John Amasa

Lee, John Amasa, Bishop of the Lebanon Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1912 to 1923, was born March 10, 1860, in Washington, Utah, the son of John D. Lee and Rachel A. Woolsey. He was baptized in 1868, and set apart as Bishop Dec. 31, 1911, by Andrew Kimball.
