Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stucki, Ezra Spori

Stucki, Ezra Spori, Bishop of the Paris 1st Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1922, was born Feb. 25, 1895, in Paris, Idaho, the son of John U. Stucki and Anna Clara Spori. He was baptized Feb. 25, 1903, was ordained an Elder by his father, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1914-1917, and was ordained a High Priest and a Bishop Aug. 24, 1919, by Geo. F. Richards.
