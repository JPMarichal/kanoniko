Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Martin, James, jun.

Martin, James, jun., Bishop of the Farr West Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1898 to 1909, was born June 7, 1846, in Princess Rock, Devonshire, England, a son of James Martin and Mary Ann Stockdale. He emigrated to Utah in 1861, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 6, 1890, by Charles F. Middleton, and ordained a Bishop July 24, 1898, by Franklin D. Richards. He died Jan. 19, 1931.
