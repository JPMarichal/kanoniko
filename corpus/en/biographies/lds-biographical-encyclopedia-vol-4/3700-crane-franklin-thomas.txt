Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Crane, Franklin Thomas

Crane, Franklin Thomas, Bishop of the Herriman Ward, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1930+, was born Feb. 3, 1875, in Herriman, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of James Crane and Elizabeth Stewart. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained an Elder Aug. 11, 1901, by Valentine Smith, filled a mission to the Central States in 1906-1908, and was ordained a High Priest and. Bishop Jan. 29, 1916, by Francis M. Lyman.
