Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, George Calvin

Williams, George Calvin, Bishop of the Luna Ward, St. Johns Stake, Ariz., from 1883 to 1885, was born Feb. 27, 1836, near Chattanooga, Tennessee, a son of Geo. W. Williams and Elizabeth Davis. He was baptized Oct. 16, 1881, went to Nutriosa in 1877, and was ordained a Bishop Nov. 18, 1883, by Brigham Young, jun.
