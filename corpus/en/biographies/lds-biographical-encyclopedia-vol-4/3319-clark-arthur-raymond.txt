Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clark, Arthur Raymond

Clark, Arthur Raymond, Bishop of the Etna Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1919 to 1929, was born Dec. 3, 1878, in Richville, Utah, a son of Arthur Benjamin Clark and Helen Margaret Ross. He was baptized in April, 1887, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1926-1927 (short term), was ordained a High Priest July 15, 1900, by Abraham O. Woodruff, and a Bishop June 22, 1919, by James E. Talmage.
