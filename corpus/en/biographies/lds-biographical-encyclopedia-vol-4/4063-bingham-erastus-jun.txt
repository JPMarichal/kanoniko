Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bingham, Erastus, jun.

Bingham, Erastus, jun., a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company B, was born Sept. 30, 1822, at St. Johnsbury, Vermont, a son of Erastus Bingham, sen., and Lucinda Gates. After his arrival in Utah he lived in Ogden and Huntsville. During the polygamy troubles he went with his second family to Arizona, while his first family remained in Weber County. He died in Mesa, Arizona.
