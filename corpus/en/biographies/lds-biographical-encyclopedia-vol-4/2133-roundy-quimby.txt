Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Roundy, Quimby

Roundy, Quimby, Bishop of the Alton Ward, Kanab Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Jan. 8, 1888, in Upper Kanab, Utah, a son of William Heber Roundy and Malinda Parker. He was baptized Sept. 21, 1899, ordained an Elder Aug. 13, 1911, by Jonathan B. Heaton, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1913-1915, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 25, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
