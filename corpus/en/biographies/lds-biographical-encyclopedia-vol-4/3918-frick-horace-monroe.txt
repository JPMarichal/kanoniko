Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Frick, Horace Monroe

Frick, Horace Monroe, one of the original pioneers of Utah, returned to Winter Quarters with Pres. Brigham Young in the fall of 1847. He did not make his home with the saints in the Rocky Mountains, but went to California, where he resided at San Bernardino. In 1860 he visited friends at Manti, Utah, at which time he was still living at San Bernardino. Nothing further is known of him.
