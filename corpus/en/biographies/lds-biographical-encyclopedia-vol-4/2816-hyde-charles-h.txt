Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hyde, Charles H.

Hyde, Charles H., first counselor in the Pioneer Stake presidency, Utah, from 1916 to 1925, was born Nov. 3, 1861, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Charles Walker Hyde and Cecilia Funk. He filled several missions, the first one to the Southern States in 1902-1903, and he presided over the Australian Mission in 1911-1913 and in 1924-1928. He was set apart as first counselor in the Pioneer Stake presidency Jan. 28, 1917. He died in Salt Lake City Jan. 3,1936.
