Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Toone, Claudius Conrad

Toone, Claudius Conrad, Bishop of the Croydon Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Dec. 25, 1902, in Croydon, Morgan Co., Utah,, a son of John Wilford Toone and Harriet B. Grover. He was baptized June 17, 1911, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1924-1926, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 26, 1926, by Hyrum G. Smith.
