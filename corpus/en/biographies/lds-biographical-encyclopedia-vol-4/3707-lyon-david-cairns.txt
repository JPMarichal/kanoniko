Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lyon, David Cairns

Lyon, David Cairns, Bishop of the Bingham Ward, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Jan. 6, 1881, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of David Ross Lyon and Mary Cairns. He was baptized Feb. 4, 1895, ordained an Elder by John H. Russell, filled a mission to Holland in 1909-1912, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 11, 1923, by Richard R. Lyman, and a Bishop Feb. 8, 1925, by Rudger Clawson.
