Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Snell, George Dixon

Snell, George Dixon, Bishop of the Spanish Fork Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1874 to 1891, and Bishop of the Spanish Fork 1st Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1891 to 1902, was born March 18, 1836, at Sackville, Westmoreland Co., New Brunswick, the son of Cyrus Snell and Rhoda Barnes. He was baptized Feb. 12, 1858, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 10, 1874.
