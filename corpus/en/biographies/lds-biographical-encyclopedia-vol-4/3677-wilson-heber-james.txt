Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilson, Heber James

Wilson, Heber James, Bishop of the Teasdale Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1903 to 1907, was born Aug. 28, 1860, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Robert Wilson and Mary Ann Baldwin. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to England in 1897-1898, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 2, 1903, by Abraham O. Woodruff.
