Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bailey, Harvey Clive

Bailey, Harvey Clive, Bishop of the Escalante South Ward, Garfield Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born March 21, 1890, at North Ogden, Weber Co., Utah, the son of John Wesley Bailey and Desdimona Rose. He was baptized July 9, 1899, ordained an Elder Jan. 6, 1917, by Ambrose Shirts and filled a mission to the Western States in 1919-1921. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 28, .1921, by George F. Richards, who also ordained him a Bishop Oct. 28, 1923.
