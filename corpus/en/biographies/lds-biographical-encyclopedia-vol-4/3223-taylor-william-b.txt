Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, William B.

Taylor, William B., Bishop of the Kimball Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1910 to 1930, was born Dec. 14, 1876, in Cottonwood, Utah, a son of James A. Taylor and Sarah L. Boyes. He was baptized June 4, 1885, filled a mission to the Society Islands in 1898-1901, was ordained a High Priest May 4, 1907, by Elias S. Kimball, and a Bishop Aug. 14, 1910, by Charles W. Penrose.
