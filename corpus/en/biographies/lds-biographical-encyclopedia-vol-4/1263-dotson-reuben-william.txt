Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dotson, Reuben William

Dotson, Reuben William, Bishop of the Minersville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1906, was born Nov. 29, 1854, in Atlanta, Miss., the son of Wm. L. H. Dotson and Henrietta Landrum. He was baptized March 19, 1879, and was ordained a Bishop Jan. 6, 1902, by Abraham O. Woodruff. He died Oct. 1, 1928.
