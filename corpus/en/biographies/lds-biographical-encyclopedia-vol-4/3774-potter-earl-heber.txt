Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Potter, Earl Heber

Potter, Earl Heber, Bishop of the Chester Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Feb. 9, 1884, at Albion, Cassia Co., Idaho, a son of Heber Carlos Potter and Julia Huphines. He was ordained a High Priest Jan. 10, 1925, and was ordained a Bishop Jan. 15, 1927, by Stephen L. Richards.
