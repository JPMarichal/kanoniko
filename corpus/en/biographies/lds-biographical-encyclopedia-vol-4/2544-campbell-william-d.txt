Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Campbell, William D.

Campbell, William D., Bishop of the McGill Ward, Nevada Stake, Nevada, from 1922 to 1923, was born Sept. 25, 1879, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of William Campbell and Agnes Brown. He was baptized Oct. 4, 1887, ordained a High Priest July 25, 1919, by Francis W. Stratford, and a Bishop May 21, 1922, by James E. Talmage.
