Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Spilsbury, Archie Price

Spilsbury, Archie Price, Bishop of the Toquerville Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1918, was born Jan. 10, 1882, in Toquerville, Utah, a son of Geo. Moroni Spilsbury and Roselia J. Haight. He was baptized March 6, 1890, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 14, 1912, by Heber J. Grant.
