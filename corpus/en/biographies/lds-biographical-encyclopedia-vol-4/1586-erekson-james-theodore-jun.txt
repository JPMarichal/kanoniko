Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Erekson, James Theodore, jun.

Erekson, James Theodore, jun., Bishop of the South Cottonwood Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Nov. 2, 1894, at South Cottonwood, Salt Lake Co., Utah, the son of James Theodore Erekson and Janet B. McMillan. He was baptized Feb. 8, 1903, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1915-1917, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 20, 1927, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop Jan. 6, 1929.
