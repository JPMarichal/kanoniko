Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Parley Pratt

Jensen, Parley Pratt, Bishop of the Toponce Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930, was born April 24, 1880, in Honeyville, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of Denmark Jensen and Lucina Johnson. He was baptized April 29, 1888, ordained a High Priest Nov. 23, 1924, by Orson F. Whitney, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 22, 1926, by Richard R. Lyman.
