Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pomeroy, Francis M.

Pomeroy, Francis M., one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born Feb. 22, 1822, at Somers, Tollard Co., Corm., a son of Martin Pomeroy and Sybil Hunt. After arriving in Great Salt Lake. Valley, he engaged in many pioneer enterprises, operated a saw mill, shingle and lath business, served as a missionary to the Indians and died at Mesa, Arizona, Feb. 28, 1883, survived by a large family. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 488.)
