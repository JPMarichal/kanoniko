Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Snow, Perry Edward

Snow, Perry Edward, Bishop of the Ferron Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Oct. 26, 1901, in Castle Dale, Utah, the son of Edw. G. Snow and Georgine Shiner. He was baptized July 3, 1910, was ordained to the Priesthood, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1925-1927, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 28, 1929, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
