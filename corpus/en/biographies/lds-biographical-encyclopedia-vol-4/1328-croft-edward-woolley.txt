Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Croft, Edward Woolley

Croft, Edward Woolley, president of the Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1916 to 1930+, was born Dec. 1, 1872, in Tooele, Utah, the son of Thomas Croft and Elizabeth Richardson. He was baptized in August, 1881, and about that time moved to Paris, Idaho, was ordained a Seventy Nov. 29, 1896, by John W. Taylor and filled a mission to the Northern States from Dec. 2, 1896 to March 20, 1899, was ordained a High Priest by Hyrum M. Smith Nov. 27, 1910, and set apart as president of the Big Horn Stake Aug. 27, 1916, by Rudger Clawson.
