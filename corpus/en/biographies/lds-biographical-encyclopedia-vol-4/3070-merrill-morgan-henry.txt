Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merrill, Morgan Henry

Merrill, Morgan Henry, second counselor in the St. Joseph Stake presidency, Arizona, from 1884 to 1888, was born Feb. 17, 1850, in Big Cottonwood, Utah, the son of Philemon Merrill and Cyrene Dustin. He was baptized in 1858, ordained a High Priest and Bishop and set apart in 1884 to preside over the St. David Ward by Moses Thatcher. He died July 31, 1896.
