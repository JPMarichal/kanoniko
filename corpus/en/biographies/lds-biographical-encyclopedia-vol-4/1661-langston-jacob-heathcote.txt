Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Langston, Jacob Heathcote

Langston, Jacob Heathcote, Bishop of the Lynndyl Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1914 to 1915, was born Jan. 20, 1863, An Rockville, Utah, the son of John Langston and Clarinda Phillips. He was baptized June 25, 1871, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 10, 1892, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop March 22, 1914, by James E. Talmage.
