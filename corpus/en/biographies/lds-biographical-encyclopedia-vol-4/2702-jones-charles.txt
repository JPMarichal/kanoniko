Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, Charles

Jones, Charles, Bishop of the Dayton Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1925, was born Aug. 22, 1880, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of Robert J. Jones and Eliza Ann Collett. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1890, ordained a High Priest March 8, 1914, by James Johnson, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 5, 1920, by Rudger Clawson.
