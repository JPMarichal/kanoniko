Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Millard Stake

Allen, Jonathan A.

Allen, Jonathan A., Bishop of the McCornick Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1927, was born Nov. 3, 1883, at Pine Creek, Gila Co., Arizona, a son of. Jonathan A. Allen and Jane Nelson. He was baptized Nov. 29, 1891, ordained a High Priest March 15, 1914, by Anthony W. Ivins, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 11, 1923, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
