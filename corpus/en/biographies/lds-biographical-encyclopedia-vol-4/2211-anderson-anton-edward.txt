Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Logan Stake

Anderson, Anton Edward

Anderson, Anton Edward, second counselor in the Logan Stake presidency, Utah, in 1930+, was born Feb. 20, 1873, in Logan, Utah, a son of Anthon Anderson and Mary Henderson. He was baptized in April, 1881, was ordained an Elder Nov. 3, 1895, by Niels C. Edlefsen, and filled a mission to England in 1904-1906. He was ordained a High Priest May 7, 1911, by Heber J. Grant, and set apart as second counselor in the Logan Stake presidency, Oct. 19, 1930.
