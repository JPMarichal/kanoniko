Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Neff, Eugene Jefferson

Neff, Eugene Jefferson, president of the Hawaiian Mission from 1922 to 1926, was born March 29, 1883, in East Mill Creek, Utah, a son of Amos Herr Neff and Eliza Anne Hughes. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1892, filled a mission to Hawaii in 1904-1908, and was set apart to preside over the Hawaiian Mission Feb. 2, 1923.
