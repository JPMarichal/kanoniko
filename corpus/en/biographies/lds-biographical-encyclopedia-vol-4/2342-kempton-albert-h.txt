Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kempton, Albert H.

Kempton, Albert H., Bishop of the Gilbert Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1926 to 1928, was born Sept. 21, 1876, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Ira T. Kempton and Elvina M. Olson. He was baptized Sept. 5, 1886, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1898-1900, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 8, 1926, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 7, 1926, by Stephen L. Richards.
