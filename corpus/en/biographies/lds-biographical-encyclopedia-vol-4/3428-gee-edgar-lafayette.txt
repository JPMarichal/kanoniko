Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gee, Edgar Lafayette

Gee, Edgar Lafayette, Bishop of the Palisade Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1925, was born Dec. 28, 1877, in Tooele, Utah, the son of Erastus R. Gee and Geneva E. Telford. He was baptized in 1895, ordained a High Priest June 22, 1919, by Ralph R. Cordon, and a Bishop May 22, 1921, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
