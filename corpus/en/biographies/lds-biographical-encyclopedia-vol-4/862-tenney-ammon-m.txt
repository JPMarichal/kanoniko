Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tenney, Ammon M.

Tenney, Ammon M., president of the Mexican Mission from 1887 to 1889, and from 1901 to 1903, was born Nov. 16, 1844, in Lee County, Iowa, the son of Nathan C. Tenney and Olive Strong. He came to Utah in 1848, went to California with his father who was called to San Bernardino, and there learned the Spanish language. He was ordained an Elder by Pres. Brigham Young and sent with Jacob Hamblin as interpreter among the Indians, and spent 15 years among the Red Men. He was ordained a Seventy by Daniel H. Wells. He died Oct. 28, 1925, in Safford, Arizona.
