Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Palmer, James

Palmer, James, Bishop of the Stone Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1928, was born June 16, 1887, in Southminster, Essex, England, a son of James Watts Palmer and Elizabeth E. Miller. He was baptized by his father, emigrated to America in 1889, was ordained an Elder, filled a mission to England in 1908-1910, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 6, 1922, by David O. McKay.
