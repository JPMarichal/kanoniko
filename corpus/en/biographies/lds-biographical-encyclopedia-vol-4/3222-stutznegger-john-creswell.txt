Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stutznegger, John Creswell

Stutznegger, John Creswell, Bishop of the Kimball Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, in 1930+, was born Sept. 23, 1899, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of John Stutznegger and Rachel A. King. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1908, ordained a High Priest Jan. 15, 1927, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop May 4, 1930, by Melvin J. Ballard.
