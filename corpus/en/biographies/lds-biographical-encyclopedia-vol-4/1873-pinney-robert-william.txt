Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pinney, Robert William

Pinney, Robert William, Bishop of the Widtsoe Ward, Garfield Stake, Piute Co., Utah, from 1920 to 1925, was born March 29, 1870, at Toquerville, Washington Co., Utah, the son of R.C. Pinney and Susan D. Smith. He was baptized in June, 1879, ordained a High Priest in 1893, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop Nov. 27, 1920, by Hyrum G. Smith.
