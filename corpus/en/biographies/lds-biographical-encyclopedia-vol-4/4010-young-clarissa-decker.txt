Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Young, Clarissa Decker

Young, Clarissa Decker, one of the three original pioneer women of Utah, and a wife of Pres. Brigham Young, was born July 22, 1828, in Freedom, Catteraugus Co., N.Y., a daughter of Isaac Decker and Harriet Page Wheeler. After her arrival in Great Salt Lake Valley, she remained with the saints while her husband returned to Winter Quarters, and was an example of patience and industry to the pioneer women who followed closely after the original company of pioneers. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 804.)
