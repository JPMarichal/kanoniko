Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grange, Franklin Charles

Grange, Franklin Charles, Bishop of the Huntington Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1929, was born June 24, 1887, in Huntington, Utah, the son of Samuel Grange and Esther Stevenson. He was baptized in 1895, was ordained a Seventy May 13, 1922, by Geo. F. Richards, and a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 3, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
