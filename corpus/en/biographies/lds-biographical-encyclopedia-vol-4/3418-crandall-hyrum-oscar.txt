Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Crandall, Hyrum Oscar

Crandall, Hyrum Oscar, Bishop of the Driggs Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1901 to 1904, was born April 26, 1844, in LaHarpe, Hancock Co., Illinois. He was chosen Bishop of the Driggs Ward Sept. 23, 1901. He died April 29, 1904.
