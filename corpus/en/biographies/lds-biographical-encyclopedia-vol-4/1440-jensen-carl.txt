Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Carl

Jensen, Carl, Bishop of the Bear River City Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1881 to 1899, was born July 6, 1840, in Glomso, Soro amt, Denmark, the son of Jens Carlsen and Marine D. Simonsen. He was baptized April 24, 1865, emigrated to Utah in 1865, and became an early settler in Bear River City in 1866. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 30, 1881, by Lorenzo Snow.
