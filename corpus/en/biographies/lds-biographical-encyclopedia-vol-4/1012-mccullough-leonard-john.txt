Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

McCullough, Leonard John

McCullough, Leonard John, president of the Society Islands Mission from 1919 to 1920, was born May 8, 1897, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of John S. McCullough and Edna Susan Chugg. He was baptized when eight years old, and was set apart for his mission Nov. 20, 1917.
