Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shaffer, William

Shaffer, William, Bishop of the Maeser Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1887 to 1896, was born March 9, 1827, in Rushville, Rush Co., Illinois, a son of Henry Shaffer and Eva Baird. He was baptized in August, 1852, came to the Valley that year, and was ordained a Bishop May 9, 1887, by John Henry Smith. He died Jan. 31, 1898, in Maeser, Utah.
