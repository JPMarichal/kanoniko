Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Crook, Frederick

Crook, Frederick, Bishop of the Heber 3rd Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1903 to 1930, was born Aug. 17, 1869, at Heber, Utah, a son of John Crook and Mary Giles. He was baptized Aug. 22, 1881, ordained a Seventy Feb, 7, 1900, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1900-1902, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 8, 1903, by Mathias F. Cowley.
