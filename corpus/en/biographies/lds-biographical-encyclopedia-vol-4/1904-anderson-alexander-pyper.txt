Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Anderson, Alexander Pyper

Anderson, Alexander Pyper, Bishop of the Waterloo Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1926.to 1930, was born June 21, 1893, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Henry J. Anderson and Elizabeth Jane Pyper. After being ordained an Elder he filled a mission to New Zealand in 1920-1922. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop in 1926 by Rudger Clawson.
