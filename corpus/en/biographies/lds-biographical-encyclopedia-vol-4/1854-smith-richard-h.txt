Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Richard H.

Smith, Richard H., Bishop of the Rexburg 3rd Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1910 to 1921, was born Jan. 14, 1863, at McMerry, Scotland, the son of Richard Smith and Ellen Hogg. He came to Utah in 1866, was baptized in October, 1872, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1907-1909, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 13, 1910, by Orson F. Whitney.
