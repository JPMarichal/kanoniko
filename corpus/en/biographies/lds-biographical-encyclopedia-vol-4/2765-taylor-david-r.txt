Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, David R.

Taylor, David R., Bishop of the Salem Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1888 to 1910, was born Aug. 29, 1850, in Holly Springs, Surry Co., North Carolina, the son of Thomas Taylor and Mary A. Danley. He was baptized in May, 1869, in North Carolina, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1886-1888, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 21, 1888, by Francis M. Lyman. He died Aug. 1, 1935.
