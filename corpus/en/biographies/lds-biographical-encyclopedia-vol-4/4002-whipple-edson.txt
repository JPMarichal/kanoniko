Whipple, Edson

Whipple, Edson, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born Feb. 5, 1805, at Dummerston, Vermont, a son of Timothy Whipple and Elizabeth Safford. He was baptized by Benjamin Winchester in Philadelphia, Pa., June 15, 1840. He became one of the pioneers of Parowan and drew the plan for that city, and with another brother, erected the first thresher there. He died at Colonia Juarez, Mexico, May 11, 1894. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 560.)
