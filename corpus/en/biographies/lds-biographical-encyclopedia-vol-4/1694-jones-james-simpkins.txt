Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, James Simpkins

Jones, James Simpkins, Bishop of the Tabiona Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1916, was born March 17, 1877, in Enoch, Utah, the son of John Lee Jones and Rachel Simpkins. He was baptized May 3, 1885, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 10, 1911, by Orson F. Whitney.
