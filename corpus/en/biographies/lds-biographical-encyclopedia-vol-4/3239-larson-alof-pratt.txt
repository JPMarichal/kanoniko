Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larson, Alof Pratt

Larson, Alof Pratt, Bishop of the Lakeside Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1912 to 1916, was born Nov. 7, 1882, in Snowflake, the son of Alof Larson and May Hunt. He was baptized Dec. 4, 1890, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 1, 1912, by Hyrum M. Smith.
