Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nicholes, Joseph Kelly

Nicholes, Joseph Kelly, president of the St. George Stake, Utah, from 1925 to !930, was born Oct. 10, 1887, in American Fork, Utah, the son of Joseph Nicholes and Eleanor Kelly. He was baptized in 1895, filled a mission to Denmark in 1909-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and set apart as president of the St. George Stake June 14, 1925, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
