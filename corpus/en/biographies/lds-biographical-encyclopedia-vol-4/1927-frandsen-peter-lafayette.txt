Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Frandsen, Peter Lafayette

Frandsen, Peter Lafayette, presiding Elder of the Clarion Branch, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1925, and Bishop of the Clarion Ward, from 1925 to 1930+, was born May 26, 1885, in Mount Pleasant, Utah, a son of Niels Peter Frandsen and Caroline Simpson. He was baptized July 5, 1895, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 30, 1925, by Stephen L. Richards.
