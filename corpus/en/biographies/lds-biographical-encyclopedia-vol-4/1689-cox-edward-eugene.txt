Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cox, Edward Eugene

Cox, Edward Eugene, Bishop of the Boneta Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born Feb. 18, 1877, in Manti, Utah, the son of Wm. Arthur Cox and Mary C. Anderson. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1885, ordained a High Priest June 27, 1920, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop Jan. 10, 1923, by Melvin J. Ballard.
