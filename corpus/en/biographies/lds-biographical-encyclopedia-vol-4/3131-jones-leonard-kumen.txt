Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, Leonard Kumen

Jones, Leonard Kumen, second counselor in the San Juan Stake presidency, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Nov. 6, 1889, in Bluff, Utah, the son of Kumen Jones and Mary Nielson. He was baptized Nov. 6, 1897, ordained a High Priest Aug. 17, 1918, by Anthony W. Ivins, and a Bishop April 6, 1926, by Richard R. Lyman, and set apart Dec. 2, 1928, by Richard R. Lyman as second counselor in the Stake presidency. He acted as Bishop of the Blanding Ward from 1925 to 1928. Bro. Jones died July 25, 1931, in Blanding, Utah.
