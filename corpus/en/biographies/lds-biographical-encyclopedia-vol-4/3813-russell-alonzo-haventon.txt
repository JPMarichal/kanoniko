Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Russell, Alonzo Haventon

Russell, Alonzo Haventon, presiding Elder of the Grafton Branch, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1868 to 1887, was born July 31, 1821, in Royalton, Vermont, the son of Thomas B. Russell and Esther Lyman. He was baptized in 1841, came to the Valley in 1852, and was sustained as Bishop of the Grafton Branch when it became a Ward in 1877.
