Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Blood, Russell Henry

Blood, Russell Henry, president of the French Mission from 1924 to 1926, was born Oct. 27, 1900, at Kaysville, Utah, a son of Henry H. Blood and Minnie A. Barnes. He was baptized Oct. 27, 1908, was set apart Nov. 10, 1921, for the British Mission, was appointed president of the French Mission Dec. 27, 1923, and acted in that capacity until his release in July, 1925.
