Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shumway, Ernest Hill

Shumway, Ernest Hill, Bishop of the Thatcher West Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1919 to 1922, was born June 5, 1892, in Shumway, Arizona, a son of James J. Shumway and Annettie Hatch. He was baptized June 5, 1900, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 21, 1919, by David O. McKay.
