Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Richards, Silas Leroy

Richards, Silas Leroy, Bishop of the Central Park Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930, was born Oct. 25, 1882, in Union, Utah, the son of Silas Newton Richards and Mary Norman Goulder. He was baptized March 28, 1893, ordained an Elder Nov. 9, 1902, by John A. Berrett, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1902-1905, and was ordained a High Priest Dec. 30, 1914, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop May 23, 1925, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
