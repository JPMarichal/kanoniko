Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ericksen, Louis E.

Ericksen, Louis E., Bishop of the Glencoe Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1909 to 1918, was born June 22, 1866, in Wingaker, Sweden, a son of Erick Ericksen and Christina Olson. He was baptized April 16, 1882, and ordained a High Priest Feb. 26, 1910, by John Henry Smith.
