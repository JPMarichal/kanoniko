Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clements, Ada

Clements, Ada, a member of Zion's Camp, was born Dec. 24, 1801, in Hebron, New York, a daughter of Justice and Eve Winchell. She was married to Albert Clements Jan. 28, 1822, at Fort Ann; joined the Church soon after her marriage and passed through the Missouri persecutions, together with her husband. She came to Utah in 1852, with her children, while her husband remained in the states till 1872, when he joined his family in Utah. Sister Clements died at Oxford, Bingham Co., Idaho, March 4, 1890. She was the mother of nine children.
