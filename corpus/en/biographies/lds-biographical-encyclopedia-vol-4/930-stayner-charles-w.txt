Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stayner, Charles W.

Stayner, Charles W., president of the Northern States Mission from 1890 to 1893, was born June 21, 1840, in London, England, a son of Thomas C. Staynet and Elizabeth Pill. He came to Utah at the age of fourteen, was baptized in 1855, filled two missions to Great Britain, the last one in 1880-1882; was set apart Oct. 8, 1889, for a mission to the Northern States and acted as president of the mission from 1890 to 1893. He returned home April 18, 1894, and died Dec. 26, 1899, in East Bountiful, Utah.
