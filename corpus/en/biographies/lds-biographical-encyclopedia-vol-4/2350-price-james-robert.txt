Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Price, James Robert

Price, James Robert, president of the Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1926 to 1930+ , was born April 3, 1885, in Charleston, Wasatch Co., Utah, a son of George Price and Mary Hannah Daybell. He was baptized Sept. 15, 1893, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1905, 1907, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 22, 1914, by J. W. Lesueur, and set apart as president in November, 1926, by Stephen L. Richards.
