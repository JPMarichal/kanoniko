Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Worthington, Samuel Wilson

Worthington, Samuel Wilson, Bishop of the Boise 1st Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1929, was born March 3, 1874, in Payson, Utah Co., Utah, the son of Samuel Worthington and Mary Wilson. He was ordained a High Priest Nov. 3, 1913, by Francis M. Lyman, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 12, 1926, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
