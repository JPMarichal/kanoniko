Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Frank Tilton

Johnson, Frank Tilton, Bishop of the Hurricane North Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Aug. 3, 1878, at Lee's Ferry, Arizona, a son of Warren Marshall Johnson and Samantha Nelson. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a Seventy by Francis M. Lyman, filled a short mission to the Western States in 1925-1926, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 25, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard, and a Bishop Sept. 23, 1928, by David 0. McKay.
