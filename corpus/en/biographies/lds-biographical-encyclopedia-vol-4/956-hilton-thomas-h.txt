Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hilton, Thomas H.

Hilton, Thomas H., president of the Samoan Mission from 1894 to 1895, was born Dec. 6, 1870, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of David Hilton and Mary Ann Aftleek. He was baptized March 1, 1881, and set apart March 25, 1892, for a mission to Samoa; he arrived April 17, 1892, and on April 28, 1894, Ransom M. Stevens, president of the Samoan Mission, died. Bro. Hilton was made president July 12, 1894, by appointment from the First Presidency and returned to his home in Utah Aug. 16, 1895.
