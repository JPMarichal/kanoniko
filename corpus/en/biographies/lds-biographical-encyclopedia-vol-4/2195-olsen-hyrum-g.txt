Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olsen, Hyrum G.

Olsen, Hyrum G., Bishop of the Liberty Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1919, was born Dec. 14, 1868, in Christiania, Norway, the son of Guldbrand Olsen and Karen Simonsen. He was baptized in July, 1878, ordained a High Priest Dec. 22, 1907, by Hugh J. Cannon, and ordained a Bishop June 6, 1915, by Charles W. Penrose.
