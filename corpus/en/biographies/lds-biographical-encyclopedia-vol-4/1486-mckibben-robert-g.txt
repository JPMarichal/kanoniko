Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mckibben, Robert G.

Mckibben, Robert G., Bishop of the Burley 3rd Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1922, was born Aug. 10, 1873, in Galveston, Texas, the son of John McKibben and Gertrude Bingham. He was baptized May 28, 1920, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 27, 1921, by Rudger Clawson.
