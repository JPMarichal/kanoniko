Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

St. George Stake

Angell, Solomon

Angell, Solomon, presiding Elder of the Leeds Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1869 to 1876, was born April 21, 1806, at Florence, Oneida Co., N.Y. He was baptized in 1834, came to Utah in 1848, and was appointed presiding Elder May 9, 1869, by Erastus Snow. He died Sept. 20, 1881.
