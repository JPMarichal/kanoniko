Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Short, Ira Temple

Short, Ira Temple, presiding Elder of the Emerson Branch, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1926, and Bishop of the Emerson Ward from 1926 to 1930+, was born July 6, 1891, in Farr West, Weber Co., Utah, a son of Temple Short and Margaret Fisher. He was baptized about 1907, ordained a High Priest April 11, 1926, by Richard C. May, and was sustained as Bishop on the same day.
