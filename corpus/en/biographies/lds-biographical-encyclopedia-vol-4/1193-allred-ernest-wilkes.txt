Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bear Lake Stake

Allred, Ernest Wilkes

Allred, Ernest Wilkes, Bishop of the St. Charles Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, in 1928-1930+, was born June 16, 1877, in St. Charles, the son of William L. Allred and Sarah Ann Wilkes. He was baptized Sept. 6, 1885, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 27, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
