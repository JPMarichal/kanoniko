Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hall, Archibald Sanford

Hall, Archibald Sanford, presiding Elder of the Mount View Branch, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1915, and Bishop of the Mount View Ward, from 1915 to 1924, was born Feb. 14, 1886, in St. Johns, Apache Co., Arizona, a son of Mark Hall, jun., and Phebe A. Elder. He was baptized June 7, 1894, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 14, 1915, by Orson F. Whitney.
