Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jorgensen, George Daniel

Jorgensen, George Daniel, Bishop of the 22nd Ward, Salt Lake Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born March 13, 1887, in Arendal, Norway, a son of Thorbjorn Jorgensen and Anne Pedersen. He was baptized Aug. 13, 1901, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 24, 1930, by Stephen L. Richards.
