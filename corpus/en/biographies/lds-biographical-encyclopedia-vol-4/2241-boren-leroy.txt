Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Boren, Leroy

Boren, Leroy, Bishop of the San Pedro Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1925 to 1930, was born July 3, 1882, in Wallsburg, Utah, a son of Ephraim DeCabe Boren and Eliza R. McPhee. He was baptized June 16, 1893 and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 26, 1925, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
