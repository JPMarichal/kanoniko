Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Willard, James Joseph

Willard, James Joseph, Bishop of the Twin Groves Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1900 to 1909, was born March 31, 1863, in Salt Lake City, Utah. He was baptized April 9, 1871, and was ordained a Bishop Jan. 27, 1901, by John W. Taylor.
