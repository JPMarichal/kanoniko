Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clark, Parley James

Clark, Parley James, Bishop of the Liberty Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Aug. 19, 1893, in Liberty, a son of Andrew A. Clark and Mary Alice Lindsay. He was baptized Sept. 30, 1902, ordained a High Priest May 13, 1923, by Melvin J. Ballard, and a Bishop Feb. 23, 1930, by Geo. Albert Smith.
