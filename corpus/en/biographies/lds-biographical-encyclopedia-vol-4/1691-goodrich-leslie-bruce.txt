Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Goodrich, Leslie Bruce

Goodrich, Leslie Bruce, Bishop of the Bluebell Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Dec. 7, 1884, in Morgan County, Utah, the son of George Albert Goodrich and Aliza Ann Taggart. He was baptized Sept. 17, 1893, was ordained a High Priest by Geo. F. Richards, and was ordained a Bishop June 14, 1925, by Geo. Albert Smith.
