Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Savage, Charles Roscoe

Savage, Charles Roscoe, one of the members of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1875 to 1909, was born Aug. 16, 1832, in Southampton, England, a son of John and Ann Savage. He died Feb. 3, 1909, in Salt Lake City, Utah. He is recognized as the father of the Old Folks movement and a statue was erected in his honor at the southeast corner of the Temple Block in July, 1936. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 708.)
