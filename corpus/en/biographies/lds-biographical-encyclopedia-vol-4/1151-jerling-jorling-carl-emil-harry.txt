Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jerling (Jorling), Carl Emil Harry

Jerling (Jorling), Carl Emil Harry, Bishop of the Highland Ward, Alpine Stake, Utah, since 1930, was born Oct. 9, 1899, in Vingaker, Sodermanland, Sweden, the son of Emil A. Jorling and Emma Maria Person. He emigrated to Utah with his parents in 1907, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 1, 1928, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
