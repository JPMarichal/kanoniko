Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rowan, Charles Elliot, jun.

Rowan, Charles Elliot, jun., president of the Garfield Stake, Garfield Co., Utah, from 1920 to 1930, was born Oct. 18, 1885, at Coyote, Garfield Co., Utah, the son of Charles Elliot Rowan and Delilah King. He was baptized Jan. 2, 1894, ordained an Elder Sept. 19, 1902, by William King, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1903-1905, was ordained a High Priest April 30, 1916, by John N. Henrie, and set apart as stake president Aug. 29, 1920, by Rudger Clawson.
