Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Randall, Melvin Howard

Randall, Melvin Howard, president of the Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born Dec. 10, 1879, in Centerville, Davis Co., Utah, a son of Melvin H. Randall and Frankie Phelps. He was baptized June 9, 1888, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1900-1902, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 23, 1906, by Daniel Heiner and set apart as president Dec. 9, 1923, by Melvin J. Ballard.
