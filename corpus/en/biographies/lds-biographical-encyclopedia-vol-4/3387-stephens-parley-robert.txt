Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stephens, Parley Robert

Stephens, Parley Robert, Bishop of the Henefer Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Aug. 26, 1898, in Henefer, Utah, a son of William Thomas Stephens and Hannah Edith Richins. He was baptized Aug. 26, 1906, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 6, 1930, by Orson F. Whitney.
