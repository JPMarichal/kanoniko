Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Weech, David H.

Weech, David H., Bishop of the Pima Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1901 to 1902, was born Sept. 27, 1875, in Goshen, Utah, a son of Hyrum Weech and Sarah Dall. He was baptized Aug. 7, 1884, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 18, 1901, by Mathias F. Cowley.
