Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kloepfer, Henry Philip

Kloepfer, Henry Philip, Bishop of the Kuna Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Oct. 25, 1880, in Logan, Utah, the son of Henry Kloepfer and Eliza Harlacher. He was baptized Nov. 1, 1888, ordained a Seventy April 17, 1901, by Heber J. Grant, filled a mission to Germany in 1901-1904, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 7, 1920, by Stephen L. Richards, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 5, 1925, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
