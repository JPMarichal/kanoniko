Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Griffin, William Henry, jun.

Griffin, William Henry, jun., Bishop of the Logan 4th Ward, Cache Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Jan. 11, 1872, at Newton, Cache Co., Utah, son of William Hyrum Griffin and Elizabeth Treherne. He was baptized April 25, 1880, by his father, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1897-1900 and was ordained a Bishop by David O. McKay.
