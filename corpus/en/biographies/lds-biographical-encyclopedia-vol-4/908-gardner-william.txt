Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gardner, William

Gardner, William, president of the New Zealand Mission from 1914 to 1918, died Feb. 4, 1932, in Salt Lake City. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 379.) Previously (from 1893 to 1896) he had presided over the Australian Mission. (See page 307)
