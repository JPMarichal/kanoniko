Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wood, Edward Glen

Wood, Edward Glen, Bishop of the Glenwood Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Aug. 8, 1893, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Edward James Wood and Mary Ann Solomon. He was baptized Aug. 8, 1901, moved to Canada the same year, filled a mission to Samoa in 1914-1917, and was ordained a Bishop April 24, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
