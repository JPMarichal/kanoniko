Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robertson, Joseph Leon

Robertson, Joseph Leon, presiding Elder of the Riverton Branch, Blackfeet Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born June 5, 1890, at Lake Shore, Utah, the son of Ezra W. Robertson and Rebecca Ann Finch. He was baptized June 5, 1898, and filled a mission to New Zealand in 1914-1917. He was set apart as presiding Elder of Riverton Branch Nov. 16, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
