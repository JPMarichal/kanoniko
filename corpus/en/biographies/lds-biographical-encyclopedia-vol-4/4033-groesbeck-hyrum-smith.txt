Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Groesbeck, Hyrum Smith

Groesbeck, Hyrum Smith, a member of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1903 to 1915, and vice chairman for two years, was born July 19, 1854, in Springfield, Illinois, a son of Nicholas Groesbeck and Elizabeth Thompson. He died March 23, 1915, in Salt Lake City, Utah.
