Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ward, James

Ward, James, Bishop of the North Ogden Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1894 to 1912, was born June 12, 1840, in Pewsy, Wiltshire, England, a son of William and Sarah Ward. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1856, came to Utah in 1861, and was ordained a High Priest Aug. 20, 1882.
