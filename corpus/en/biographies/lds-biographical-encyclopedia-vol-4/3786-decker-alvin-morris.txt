Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Decker, Alvin Morris

Decker, Alvin Morris, Bishop of the Mancos Ward, Young Stake, Colorado, from 1920 to 1928, was born April 12, 1880, in Bluff, Utah, a son of Nathaniel Alvin Decker and Emma t Morris. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a Seventy Aug. 15, 1909, by Jos. W. McMurrin, ordained a High Priest April 22, 1911, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop Aug. 21, 1920, by Rudger Clawson.
