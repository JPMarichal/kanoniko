Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Oldham, Samuel Price

Oldham, Samuel Price, Bishop of the Rexburg 4th Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born April 6, 1873, in Paradise, Utah, the son of Samuel Oldham and Mary Jane Price. He was baptized May 10, 1881, ordained a Seventy in August, 1895, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1898. He was ordained a High Priest Aug. 5, 1912, by James E. Talmage, and a Bishop Sept. 30, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
