Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Alpine Stake

Abel, George Edward, jun.

Abel, George Edward, jun., Bishop of the American Fork 3rd Ward, Alpine Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Aug. 15, 1886, in American Fork, Utah, the son of Geo. Edward Abel and Mary Ellen Shelley. He was baptized Sept. 6, 1894, and was ordained a High Priest Oct. 24, 1928, by Clifford E. Young. Elder Abel filled a mission to Holland in 1906-1909.
