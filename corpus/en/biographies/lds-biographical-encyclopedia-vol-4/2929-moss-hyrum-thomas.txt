Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moss, Hyrum Thomas

Moss, Hyrum Thomas, Bishop of the Ririe Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Aug. 29, 1880, at Enterprise, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of Hyrum Ralph Moss and Fanny Wardley. He was baptized in September, 1888, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1919-1921, and was ordained a Bishop March 5, 1922, by Melvin J. Ballard.
