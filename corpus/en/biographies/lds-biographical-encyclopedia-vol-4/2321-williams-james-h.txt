Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, James H.

Williams, James H., Bishop of the Samaria Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, in 1930+, was born Jan. 29, 1893, in Samaria, Oneida Co., Idaho, a son of Jeremiah Hodge and Ruth Williams. He was baptized Feb. 3, 1901, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 29, 1930, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
