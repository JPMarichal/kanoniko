Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merrill, Adrian A.

Merrill, Adrian A., Bishop of the Murtaugh Ward, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1920, was born Dec. 12, 1890, in Richmond, Utah, a son of Alma Merrill and Rebecca Hendricks. He was baptized Jan. 10, 1899, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 27, 1919, by Stephen L. Richards.
