Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mccann, Thomas J.

Mccann, Thomas J., acting Bishop of the Turner Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1927, was born July 14, 1866, in Smithfield, Utah, the son of Joseph Nephi McCann and Ellen Cantwell. He was baptized July 25, 1875, and was killed in the explosion in the Turner Ward meeting house Jan. 26, 1927.
