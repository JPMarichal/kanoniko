Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Crouch, Ebenezer

Crouch, Ebenezer, Bishop of the Lanark Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, from 1897 to 1902, was born Sept. 23, 1850, in Tunbridge Wells, Kent, England, the son of Ebenezer Crouch and Sarah Russell. He emigrated with his parents to Utah in 1856, was ordained a High Priest by Samuel Francis at Morgan, Utah, and ordained a Bishop by James H. Hart in 1897.
