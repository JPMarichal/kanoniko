Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peirce, Eli Harvey

Peirce, Eli Harvey, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born July 29, 1827, at Uwchland, Chester Co., Pa., a son of Robert Peirce and Hannah Harvey. He was baptized March 27, 1842, by the Prophet Joseph Smith and after his arrival in Utah he became the second Bishop of Brigham City. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 2, p. 686.)
