Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Willardsen, Albertus

Willardsen, Albertus, Bishop of the Mayfield Ward, Gunnison Stake, from 1917 to 1920, was born July 27, 1891, in Ephraim, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Joseph Willardsen and Anna Forester. He was baptized Aug. 15, 1899, filled a mission to Great Britain .in 1911-1913, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 6, 1917, by Pres. Joseph F. Smith.
