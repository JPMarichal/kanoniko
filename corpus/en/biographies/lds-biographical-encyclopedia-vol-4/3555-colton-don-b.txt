Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Colton, Don B.

Colton, Don B., president of the Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1921, was born Sept. 15, 1876, in Mona, Utah, a son of Sterling Driggs Colton and Nancy A. Wilkins. He was baptized March 4, 1893, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1896-1898, and was set apart Sept. 18, 1910, by Francis M. Lyman as president of the Uintah Stake.
