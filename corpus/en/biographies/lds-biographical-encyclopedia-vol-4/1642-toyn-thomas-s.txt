Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Toyn, Thomas S.

Toyn, Thomas S., Bishop of the Arbon Ward, Curlew Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1925, was born Feb. 12, 1882, in Grouse Creek, Utah, a son of David H. Toyn and Martha Jane Davis. He was baptized Aug. 15, 1891, ordained a Seventy May 14, 1901, by Seymour B. Young, ordained a High Priest Oct. 28, 1923, by James E. Talmage, and sustained as a Bishop in 1924.
