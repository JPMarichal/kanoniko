Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Poulsen, Niels Christian

Poulsen, Niels Christian, Bishop of the Richfield 1st Ward, Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, from 1917 to 1921, was born April 14, 1875, at Richfield, Utah, the son of Niels Poulsen and Caroline Jacobsen. He was baptized June 6, 1883, ordained a High Priest March 24, 1912, by Joseph F. Smith, jun., filled a mission to the Northern States in 1915-1917, and was ordained a Bishop March 18, 1917, by Hyrum M. Smith.
