Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lazenby, Moroni

Lazenby, Moroni, second counselor in the Wayne Stake presidency, Utah, from 1912 to 1916, was born Jan. 12, 1872, in Minersville, Utah, a son of John T. Lazenby and Annie Tether. He was baptized in April, 1882, ordained a Seventy in 1894, by Walter H. Jeffries, and filled a mission to Australia in 1894-1897. He was sustained as second counselor Dec. 30, 1911.
