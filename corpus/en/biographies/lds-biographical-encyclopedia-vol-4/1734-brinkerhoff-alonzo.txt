Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brinkerhoff, Alonzo

Brinkerhoff, Alonzo, Bishop of the Emery Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1896 to 1922, was born July 8, 1864, in St. George, Utah, the son of James Brinkerhoff and Rebecca Halk. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1893-1895, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 9, 1896.
