Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pingree, Job

Pingree, Job, president of the Eastern States Mission from 1893 to 1895, was born Nov. 21, 1837, in Acton, Beauchamp, Worcestershire, England, the son of Job Pingree and Charlotte Fairant. He was baptized in August, 1856. He was set apart for his mission Jan. 6, 1893, and returned Dec. 31, 1894.
