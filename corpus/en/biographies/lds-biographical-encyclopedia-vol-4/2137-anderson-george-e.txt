Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kolob Stake

Anderson, George E.

Anderson, George E., Bishop of the Springville 2nd Ward, Kolob Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1903, was born Oct. 28, 1860, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of George Anderson and Mary Ann Thorne. He was baptized Jan. 6, 1869, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1906-1913, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 18, 1900, by George Teasdale.
