Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Patterson, Charles Gold

Patterson, Charles Gold, Bishop of the American Fork 1st Ward, Alpine Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1903, was born May 14, 1871, at Shelby, North Carolina, a son of John H. Patterson and Marguerite M. Gold. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop by Geo. Teasdale July 14, 1901.
