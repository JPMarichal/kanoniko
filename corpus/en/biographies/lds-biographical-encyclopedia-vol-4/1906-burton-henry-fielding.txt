Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burton, Henry Fielding

Burton, Henry Fielding, Bishop of the Farmers Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1886 to 1914, was born Feb. 21, 1858, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Robert T. Burton and Sarah Anna Garr. He was baptized when eight years old and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 12, 1886, by Angus M. Cannon. He died Feb. 16, 1920.
