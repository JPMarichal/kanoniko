Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Riggs, John Lyle

Riggs, John Lyle, Bishop of the Mesa 2nd Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1912 to 1914, was born Nov. 25, 1879, in Shunesburg, Utah, a son of William T. Riggs and Clarice M. Millett. He was baptized when about eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and a Bishop Sept. 8, 1912, by James E. Talmage.
