Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Horsley, Arthur William

Horsley, Arthur William, president of the Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1930+, was born June 18, 1860, in Totten, Middlesex Co., England, the son of Samuel P. Horsley and Sarah Barrows. He was baptized Aug. 14, 1870, emigrated to Utah in 1877, and was set apart as president of the Carbon Stake April 13, 1913, by Francis M. Lyman.
