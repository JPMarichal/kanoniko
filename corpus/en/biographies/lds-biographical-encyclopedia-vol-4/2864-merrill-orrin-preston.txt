Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merrill, Orrin Preston

Merrill, Orrin Preston, Bishop of the Downey Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1917, was born Aug. 5, 1876, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of Orrin J. Merrill and Elizabeth White. He was baptized Nov. 5, 1884, ordained an Elder in January, 1900, by Thomas Morgan, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1900-1903, was ordained a High Priest in 1904 by Geo. C. Parkinson, and a Bishop Nov. 20, 1915, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
