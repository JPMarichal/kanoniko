Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Anthon Marius

Hansen, Anthon Marius, Bishop of the Brigham City 4th Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Nov. 6, 1871, in Christiania, Norway, the son of Hans Martin Hansen and Bertha Karine Nielsen. He was baptized in Norway in 1884, and emigrated with his mother the same year to Utah, was ordained a SeVenty June 14, 1899, by Chr. D. Fieldsted, and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1899-1901. He was ordained a Bishop by Rudger Clawson April 4, 1925.
