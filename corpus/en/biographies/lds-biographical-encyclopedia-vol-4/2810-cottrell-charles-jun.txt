Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cottrell, Charles, jun.

Cottrell, Charles, jun., Bishop of the Salt Lake City 30th Ward, Pioneer Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1926, was born Dec. 7, 1864, in Reading, Berkshire, England, a son of Charles Cottrell and Leonora Whitman. He was baptized in 1873, ordained a High Priest Feb. 16, 1902, by Joseph E. Taylor, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 31, 1919, by Charles W. Penrose.
