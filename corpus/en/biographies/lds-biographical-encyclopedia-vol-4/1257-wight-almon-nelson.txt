Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wight, Almon Nelson

Wight, Almon Nelson, Bishop of the Thatcher Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born April 19, 1885, in Brigham City, Utah, the son of Almon Lyman Wight and Mariane Nielsen. He was baptized when about eight years of age, and ordained a Bishop May 11, 1924, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
