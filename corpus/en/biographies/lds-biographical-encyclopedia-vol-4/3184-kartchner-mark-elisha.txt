Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kartchner, Mark Elisha

Kartchner, Mark Elisha, Bishop of the Grand View Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born June 6, 1876, in Panguitch, Piute (now Garfield) Co., Utah, a son of Mark Elisha Kartchner and Phebe Palmer. He was baptized June 6, 1884, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1897-1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 28, 1922, by David O. McKay.
