Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Oblad, Zachariah James

Oblad, Zachariah James, Bishop of the Park City Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Oct. 3, 1890, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Jobart Fred Oblad and Marie Magdaline Larsen. He was baptized Oct. 1, 1898, ordained a High Priest July 25, 1920, by John Lamb, and a Bishop June 26, 1925, by Geo. F. Richards.
