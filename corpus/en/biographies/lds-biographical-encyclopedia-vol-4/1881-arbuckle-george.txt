Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Arbuckle, George

Arbuckle, George, Bishop of the Emerson Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1919, was born April 8, 1859, in Glasgow, Scotland, a son of James Arbuckle and Agnes Thompson. He was baptized in 1867, ordained a High Priest Jan. 12, 1902, by Mathias F. Cowley, and a Bishop Feb. 28, 1909, by Anthon H. Lund.
