Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Layton, Harry Wilford

Layton, Harry Wilford, Bishop of the Central Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1926 to 1930, was born Oct. 7, 1876, in Kaysville, Utah, the son of Christopher Layton and Hannah M. S. Sims. He was baptized in Eden, Ariz., ordained a High Priest June 6, 1909, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop Nov. 6, 1926, by Melvin J. Ballard.
