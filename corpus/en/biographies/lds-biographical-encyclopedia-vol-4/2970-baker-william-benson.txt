Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Baker, William Benson

Baker, William Benson, Bishop of the Central Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1924, was born May 22, 1862, in Mill Creek, Utah, a son of George Baker and Mary Ann Randall. He was baptized in 1870, filled a mission to Ireland in 1899-1901, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 14, 1902, by Edward H. Snow, and set apart as Bishop April 24, 1918.
