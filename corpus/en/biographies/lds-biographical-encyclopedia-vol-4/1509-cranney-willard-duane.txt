Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cranney, Willard Duane

Cranney, Willard Duane, Bishop of the Petersboro Ward, Cache Stake, Utah, from 1887 to 1896, was born Aug. 24, 1848, in Sheboygan, Wisconsin, the son of H. K. Cranney and Elizabeth Boughy. He was baptized by L. M. Durfey in 1862 and ordained a Bishop July 24, 1887, by Moses Thatcher.
