Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Valentine, August

Valentine, August, Bishop of the Brigham City 2nd Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1892 to 1904, was born Aug. 27, 1837, on the island of Bornholm, Denmark, the son of Valentin Valentinsen and Johanne Andersen. He emigrated to Utah with his parents in 1852-1853, and located in Brigham City, where he was baptized and ordained to the Priesthood. He filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1883-1885, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 22, 1892, by Lorenzo Snow.
