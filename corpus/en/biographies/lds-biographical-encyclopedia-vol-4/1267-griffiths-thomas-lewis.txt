Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Griffiths, Thomas Lewis

Griffiths, Thomas Lewis, Bishop of the Adamsville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1911, was born Oct. 6, 1873, in Lehi, Utah Co., Utah, the son of John E. Griffiths and Margaret Jones. He was baptized Oct. 6, 1882, and filled a mission to South Africa in 1903-1905. He was ordained a Bishop Jan. 15, 1906, by George Albert Smith.
