Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brown, Moroni Franklin

Brown, Moroni Franklin, Bishop of the Ogden 1st Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1890 to 1897, was born Sept. 23, 1857, in Fort Supply, Wyo., a son of James M. Brown and Adelaide Exervia. He was baptized Oct. 4, 1866, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 2, 1890, by Franklin D. Richards. He died Sept. 18, 1897, in San Diego, Calif.
