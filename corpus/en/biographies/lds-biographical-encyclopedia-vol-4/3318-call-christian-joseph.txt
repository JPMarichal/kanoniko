Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Call, Christian Joseph

Call, Christian Joseph, Bishop of the Afton South Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1921 to 1926, was born Dec. 21, 1889, in Afton, a son of Anson Vasco Call and Lucy E. King. He was baptized Aug. 16, 1898, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1915-1917, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 17, 1922, by Melvin J. Ballard.
