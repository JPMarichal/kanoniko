Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Koyle, John H.

Koyle, John H., Bishop of the Leland Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1908 to 1913, was born Aug. 14, 1864, at Spanish Fork, Utah Co., Utah, .the son of John Koyle and Adlinda Hillman. He was baptized when about eight years of age, ordained a High Priest in 1900, by Reed Smoot, and ordained a Bishop May 31, 1908, by Rudger Clawson.
