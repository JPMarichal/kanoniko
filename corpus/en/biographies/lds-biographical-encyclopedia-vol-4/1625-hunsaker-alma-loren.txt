Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hunsaker, Alma Loren

Hunsaker, Alma Loren, Bishop of the Holbrook Ward, Curlew Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930, was born May 15, 1892, in Honeyville, Utah, a son of Robert S. Hunsaker and Minnie M. Wheatley. He was baptized May 15, 1900, ordained a High Priest May 19, 1925, by Rudger Clawson, and ordained Bishop of Holbrook Ward, July 17, 1927, by Melvin J. Ballard.
