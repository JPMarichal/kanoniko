Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wood, Charles Samuel

Wood, Charles Samuel, Bishop of the View Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1925, was born July 11, 1861, in Ogden, Utah, the son of Charles Wood and Alice Herrick. He was baptized in June, 1872, ordained a High Priest July 22, 1900, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 23, 1919, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
