Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fernelius, Adolph Gustaf

Fernelius, Adolph Gustaf, Bishop of the Uintah Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born July 12, 1873, in Danielson, Meeker Co., Minnesota, a son of Peter Gustaf Fernelius and Johanna C. Lindberg. He was baptized July 12, 1882, ordained a High Priest by Lewis W. Shurtliff, and a Bishop Nov. 11, 1928, by Francis M. Lyman.
