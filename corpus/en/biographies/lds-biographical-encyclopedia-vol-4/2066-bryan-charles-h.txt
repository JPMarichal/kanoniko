Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bryan, Charles H.

Bryan, Charles H., Bishop of the Nephi Ward, Juab Stake, Utah, from 1861 to 1868, was born Dec. 14, 1807, in Jefferson Co., Kentucky, a son of John Bryan and Elizabeth Hinkle. He was ordained a Bishop and set apart May 17, 1861, by John Taylor.
