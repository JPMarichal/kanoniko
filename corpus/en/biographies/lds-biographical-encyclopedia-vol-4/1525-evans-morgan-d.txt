Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Evans, Morgan D.

Evans, Morgan D., Bishop of the Castle Gate Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1921, was born Jan. 2, 1863, the son of Benjamin Evans and Mary Eynon. He was baptized in 1872, ordained a High Priest Jan. 14, 1902, by Hyrum M. Smith, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 1, 1911, by Patriarch Hyrum G. Smith. He acted as presiding Elder from 1909 to 1911, in Castle Gate.
