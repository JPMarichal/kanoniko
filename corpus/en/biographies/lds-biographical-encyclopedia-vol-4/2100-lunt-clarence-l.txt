Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lunt, Clarence L.

Lunt, Clarence L., Bishop of the Pacheco Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1920 to 1930, was born July 16, 1896, in Colonia Paeheco, a son of Henry Lunt and Sarah Ann Lunt (Lunt was her maiden name). He was baptized July 16, 1904, and was ordained a Bishop Oct. 14, 1921, by George Albert Smith.
