Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sessions, Harvey

Sessions, Harvey, Bishop of the Marion Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1910 to 1920, was born June 13, 1859, in Bountiful, Utah, the son of Perrigrene Sessions and Lucina Call. He filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1910-1911, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 6, 1893, by Moroni Pickett, and a Bishop June 13, 1911.
