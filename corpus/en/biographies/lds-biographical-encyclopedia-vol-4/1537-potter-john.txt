Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Potter, John

Potter, John, Bishop of the Sunnyside Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1906, was born Dec. 6, 1861, in Kearsley, Lancashire, England, the son of Samson E. Potter and Sarah Chatterton. He was baptized in 1879, emigrated to Utah in 1884, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 17, 1900, by John W. Taylor.
