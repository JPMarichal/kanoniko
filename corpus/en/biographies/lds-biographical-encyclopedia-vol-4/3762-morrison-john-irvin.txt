Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Morrison, John Irvin

Morrison, John Irvin, Bishop of the Farnum Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1906 to 1909, was born April 2, 1870, at Franklin, Oneida Co., Idaho, a son of Andrew Morrison and Mary Ann Smith Whitworth. He was baptized Jun.e 5, 1879, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 20, 1906, by Rudger Clawson.
