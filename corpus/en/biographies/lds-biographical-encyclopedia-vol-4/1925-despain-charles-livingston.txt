Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Despain, Charles Livingston

Despain, Charles Livingston, Bishop of the Axtell Ward, Gunnison Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1924, was born Nov. 8, 1874, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Orson A. Despain and Janet Livingston. He was baptized June 6, 1883, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 7, 1920, by Richard R. Lyman.
