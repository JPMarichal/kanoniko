Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gardner, John Alfred

Gardner, John Alfred, Bishop of the Mendon Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1921 to 1925, was born Aug. 27, 1878, at Mendon, Utah, the son of Alfred Gardner and Mary E. Coon. He was baptized Aug. 27, 1886, ordained a High Priest May 15, 1910, by William H. Maughan and ordained a Bishop April 24, 1921, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
