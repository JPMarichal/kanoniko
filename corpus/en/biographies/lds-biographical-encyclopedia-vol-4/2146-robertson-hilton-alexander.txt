Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robertson, Hilton Alexander

Robertson, Hilton Alexander, Bishop of the Springville 4th Ward, Kolob Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930, was born Aug. 2, 1891, a son of Alexander Robertson and Lucy Smith. He was baptized May 12, 1900, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Japan in 1921-1924, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 23, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
