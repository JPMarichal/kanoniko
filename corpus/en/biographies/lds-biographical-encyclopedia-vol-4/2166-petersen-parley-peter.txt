Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Petersen, Parley Peter

Petersen, Parley Peter, presiding Elder of the Champion Branch, Lethbridge Stake, Canada, from 1918 to 1930, was born July 4, 1882, at Spring City, Utah, the son of Iver Peter Petersen and Caroline Margrethe Sorensen. He was baptized July 3, 1890, moved to Canada in 1902 and was set apart as presiding Elder of the Champion Branch in July, 1918, by Bishop George E. Toone of the Lethbridge Ward.
