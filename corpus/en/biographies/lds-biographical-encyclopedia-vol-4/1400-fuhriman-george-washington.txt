Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fuhriman, George Washington

Fuhriman, George Washington, Bishop of the Kuna Ward, Boise Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1925, was born Jan. 15, 1879, in Providence, Utah, the son of Jacob Fuhriman and Mary Loosle. He was baptized March 5, 1887, and after acting as presiding Elder from 1918, he was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 7, 1920, by Stephen L. Richards.
