Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Orme, Joseph Roy

Orme, Joseph Roy, Bishop of the Wilford Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born June 3, 1894, in Tooele City, Utah, a son of Samuel W. Orme and Mary A. Smith. He was baptized July 6, 1902, ordained a High Priest April 20, 1924, by David A. Smith, and ordained a Bishop July 25, 1926, by Rudger Clawson.
