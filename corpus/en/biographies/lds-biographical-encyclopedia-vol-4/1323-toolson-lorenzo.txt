Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Toolson, Lorenzo

Toolson, Lorenzo, Bishop of the Smithfield 2nd Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1923, was born Aug. 18, 1863, in Smithfield, the son of Lars Toolson and Ingrid Tuveson. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1872, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 20, 1919, by James E. Talmage. He died June 24, 1923, in Smithfield, Utah.
