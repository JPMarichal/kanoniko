Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Keyes, Alma

Keyes, Alma, Bishop of the Uintah Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1895 to 1917, was born Aug. 10, 1839, in Adams County, Illinois, a son of Harrison Keyes and Eliza Herrick. He was baptized Sept. 13, 1857, ordained a High Priest July 19, 1886, by Franklin D. Richards, and was ordained Bishop Aug. 11, 1895, by Franklin D. Richards. He died Sept. 8, 1918.
