Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hammer, Jasper John

Hammer, Jasper John, Bishop of the Woodville Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1904 to 1906, was born Sept. 2, 1871, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of Austin Hammer and Sarah Jane Payne. He was baptized when eight years old, acted as presiding Elder at Woodville from 1894 to 1904, and was sustained as Bishop July 24, 1904, when the branch was organized as a ward.
