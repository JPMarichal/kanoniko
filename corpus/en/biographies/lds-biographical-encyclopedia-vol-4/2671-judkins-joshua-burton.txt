Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Judkins, Joshua Burton

Judkins, Joshua Burton, Bishop of the Liberty. Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1892 to 1904, was born June 17, 1847, in Franklin Co., Maine, a son of Newell Judkins and Rachel Hoyt. He was baptized Aug. 26, 1876, came to Utah that same year, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 10, 1892, by Franklin D. Richards. He died Sept. 24, 1913.
