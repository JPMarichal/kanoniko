Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clayton, Raymond H.

Clayton, Raymond H., Bishop of the Payson 3rd Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1926, was born Dec. 29, 1880, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of James Clayton and Cornelia Home. He was baptized Jan. 1, 1889, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1902-1904, and was ordained a High Priest May 9, 1909, by Heber J. Grant, and a Bishop Dec. 28, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard.
