Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clayton, John

Clayton, John, Bishop of the 3rd Ward, Salt Lake City, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1920, was born Jan. 7, 1880, in Preston, England, the son of William Clayton and Alice Clayton. He was baptized in 1890 by John Kay, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 1, 1911, by Anthon H. Lund.
