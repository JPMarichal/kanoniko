Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Albert Swen

Jensen, Albert Swen, Bishop of the Mountain Home Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1928, was born Dec. 9, 1875, in Peoa, Utah, the son of Ole and Annie Jensen. He was baptized May 8, 1884, ordained a High Priest Sept. 26, 1920, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop June 23, 1923, by Melvin J. Ballard.
