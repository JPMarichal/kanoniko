Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Valentine, Nephi James

Valentine, Nephi James, Bishop of the Brigham City 2nd Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1929, was born Nov. 15, 1867, in Brigham City, the son of Valentin Valentinsen and Johanne Andersen. He was baptized June 8, 1877, ordained to the Priesthood and was set apart for a mission to Scandinavia Oct. 8, 1897. He was ordained a High Priest Nov. 27, 1904, by Rudger Clawson and a Bishop April 9, 1922, by Melvin J. Ballard.
