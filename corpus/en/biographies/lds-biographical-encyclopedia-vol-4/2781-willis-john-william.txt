Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Willis, John William

Willis, John William, Bishop of the Henrieville Ward, Panguitch Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1920, was born Jan. 12, 1885, in Hillsdale, Utah, a son of William P. Willis and Mary Merrill. He was baptized in 1895, ordained an Elder Nov. 8, 1906, by Geo. Romney, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1906-1908, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 27, 1916, by Anthony W. Ivins.
