Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fullmer, John Wesley

Fullmer, John Wesley, Bishop of the Abraham Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1927, was born July 5, 1890, in Mound Valley, Idaho, the son of Oscar M. Fullmet and Harriet P. Williams. He was baptized July 5, 1898, filled a mission to Australia in 1911-1913, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 19, 1915, by Alonzo A. Hinckley, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 22, 1918, by Richard R. Lyman.
