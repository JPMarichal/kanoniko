Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Theurer, Frederich

Theurer, Frederich, Bishop of the Providence Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1889 to 1909, was born Sept. 29, 1839, in Argay, Switzerland, a son of Michael Theurer and Barbara Horlacher. He was baptized Feb. 5, 1857, in Switzerland, emigrated to America in 1859, and filled two missions to Switzerland and Germany in 1874-1876 and 1885-1887. Later he was ordained a High Priest and Bishop. He died Feb. 8, 1922.
