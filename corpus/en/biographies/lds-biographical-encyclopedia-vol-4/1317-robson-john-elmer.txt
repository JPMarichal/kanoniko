Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robson, John Elmer

Robson, John Elmer, Bishop of the Trenton Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1930, was born Sept. 6, 1884, in Plain City, Utah, the son of James P. Robson and Eliza Jane Skeen. He was baptized Sept. 6, 1892, ordained a High Priest June 2, 1918, by Alma Merrill, and ordained a Bishop June 18, 1922, by Rudger Clawson.
