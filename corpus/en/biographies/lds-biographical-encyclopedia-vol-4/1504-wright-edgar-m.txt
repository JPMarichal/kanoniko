Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wright, Edgar M.

Wright, Edgar M., Bishop of the Burley 2nd Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1921, was born July 21, 1876, in Bennington, Idaho, the son of Amos R. Wright and Catherine Roberts. He was baptized July 21, 1884, filled a mission in 1908-1910, to Great Britain, and was ordained a Bishop July 27, 1919.
