Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Waters, Elmer Evan

Waters, Elmer Evan, Bishop of the Clark Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1928 to 1930, was born Aug. 8, 1893, at Burrville, Sevier Co., Utah, a son of George B. Waters and Laura Ann Burr. He was baptized Aug. 3, 1903, ordained a High Priest March 3, 1918, by William W. Selck, jun., and ordained a Bishop Feb. 25, 1928, by George Albert Smith.
