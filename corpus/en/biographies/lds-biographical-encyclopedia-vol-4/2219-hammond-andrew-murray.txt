Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hammond, Andrew Murray

Hammond, Andrew Murray, Bishop of the Providence 1st Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born May 28, 1875, in Providence, Utah, a son of Milton D. Hammond and Kirsti Transtrom. He was baptized May 28, 1883, ordained a Seventy May 4, 1898, by John Henry Smith, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1898-1900, was ordained a High Priest May 2, 1909, by Hyrum M. Smith, and a Bishop April 24, 1921, by James E. Talmage.
