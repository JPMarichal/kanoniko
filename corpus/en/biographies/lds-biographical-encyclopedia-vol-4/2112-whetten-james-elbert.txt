Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whetten, James Elbert

Whetten, James Elbert, Bishop of the Garcia Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1928 to 1930, was horn July 18, 1881, in Snowflake, Arizona, the son of James Thomas Whetten and Agnes B. Savage. He was baptized Sept. 5, 1889, ordained a High Priest Nov. 24, 1917, by Joseph C. Bentley, and a Bishop by Melvin J. Ballard.
