Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Covington, Robert Duchey

Covington, Robert Duchey, Bishop of the Washington W a r d, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1858 to 1869, was born Aug. 20, 1815, in North Carolina, a son of Thomas D. Corington and Jane Thomas. He was baptized Feb. 3, 1843, came to Nauvoo in 1845, and to the Valley in 1847, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 1, 1858, by Geo. A. Smith.
