Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wanner, Serge N.

Wanner, Serge N., an Elder who died while filling a foreign mission, was born March 8, 1908, in Preston, Franklin Co., Idaho, a son of John George Wanner and Regina Nuffer. He was baptized April 29, 1916, by J. W. Corbridge, was ordained an Elder, and resided at Preston, Idaho, when he was called on a mission to New Zealand. He responded to his call and was set apart April 24, 1928, and arrived in his field of labor May 20, 1928. He labored in the Bay, of Islands, Whangerei, and Wellington districts, and on the South Island. While still in the ministry he was seriously stricken with septicaemia from the effects of which he died Oct. 4, 1929. His body was returned home for burial. Elder Wanner was twenty-one years old at the time of his death.
