Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Yearsley, Nathan David

Yearsley, Nathan David, Bishop of the Woodruff Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1914 to 1921, was born Oct. 13, 1865, in Ogden, Weber Co., Utah, a son of Nathan Yearsley and Ruthinda Stewart. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a High Priest March 19, 1899, by Anthon H. Lund and ordained a Bishop Feb. 22, 1914, by James E. Talmage.
