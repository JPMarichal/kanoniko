Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Elijah Martin

Jensen, Elijah Martin, Bishop of the Vermillion Ward, North Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1919, was born March 27, 1880, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, the son of James C. Jensen and Annie Jessie Jepson. He was baptized Oct. 14, 1888, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1904-1906, and was ordained a High Priest Jan. 12, 1913, by John Christensen, and a Bishop Sept. 22, 1917, by James E. Talmage.
