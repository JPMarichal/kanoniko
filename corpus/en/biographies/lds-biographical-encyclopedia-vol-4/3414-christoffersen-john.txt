Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christoffersen, John

Christoffersen, John, Bishop of the Cache Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1925, was born Nov. 4, 1884, in Richmond, Utah, a son of John C. Christoffersen and Mette Marie Madsen. He was baptized Nov. 1, 1892, ordained a High Priest by Dan C. Driggs, and a Bishop May 19, 1919, by David O. McKay.
