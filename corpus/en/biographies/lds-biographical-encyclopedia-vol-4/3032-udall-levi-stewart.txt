Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Udall, Levi Stewart

Udall, Levi Stewart, president of the St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Jan. 20, 1891, in St. Johns, Arizona, a son of David K. Udall and Eliza Luella Stewart. He was baptized Feb. 11, 1899, ordained a High Priest July 11, 1920, by his father, and set apart April 30, 1922, as president of the Stake by Rudger Clawson.
