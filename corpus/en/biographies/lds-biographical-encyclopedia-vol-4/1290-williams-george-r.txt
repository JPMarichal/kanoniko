Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, George R.

Williams, George R., Bishop of the Minersville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1919, was born May 5, 1880, in Greenville, Beaver Co., Utah, the son of David Williams and Margaret Reese. He was baptized May 3, 1888, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 28, 1917, by Geo. Albert Smith.
