Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whitehead, Joseph Samuel

Whitehead, Joseph Samuel, Bishop of the Burrville Ward, Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, from 1890 to 1891, was born April 1, 1848, in Henderson Co., Illinois, the son of William Whitehead and Jane Hardman. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a High Priest July 20, 1884, and sustained as Bishop of the Burrville Ward Dec. 14, 1890.
