Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bradley, George Washington

Bradley, George Washington, Bishop of the Moroni Ward, Moroni Stake, Utah, from 1859 to 1868, was born Jan. 15, 1813, in Salem, Washington Co., N.Y., a son of James P. Bradley and Albeah Richmond. He was ordained a Bishop July 14, 1859, by Geo. Albert Smith and died March 8, 1891.
