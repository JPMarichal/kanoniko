Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Calder, Hyrum Bennion

Calder, Hyrum Bennion, Bishop of the Vernal 1st Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1927, was born May 26, 1873, in Mill Creek, Utah, a son of George Calder and Mary Bennion. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1881, ordained a Seventy Sept. 11, 1897, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1897-1898, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 18, 1910, by Francis M. Lyman.
