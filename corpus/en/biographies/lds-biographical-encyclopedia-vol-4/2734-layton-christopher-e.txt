Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Layton, Christopher E.

Layton, Christopher E., Bishop of the Hunter Ward, Oquirrh Stake, Utah, from 1899 to 1902, was .born Sept. 6, 1867, in Kaysville, Utah, the son of Charles Layton and Sarah A. Crockett. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 25, 1899, by John Henry Smith.
