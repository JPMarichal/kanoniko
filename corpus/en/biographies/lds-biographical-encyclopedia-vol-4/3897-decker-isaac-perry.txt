Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Decker, Isaac Perry

Decker, Isaac Perry, a little boy who at seven years of age accompanied his mother (then the wife of Lorenzo D. Young) on the famous journey across the plains and mountains with the original pioneers of Utah. Isaac Perry Decker was born Aug. 7, 1820, in Winchester, Scott Co., Ill., a son of Isaac Decker and Harriet Page Wheeler. The boy was an eye witness to the early struggles of the pioneers to raise crops, remembered the invasion of the crickets and the providential arrival of the sea gulls. In middle life he made his home in Prove, where his son C. F. Decker served as mayor for some time, and others of his children played important parts in the development of the state. Elder Decker died of infirmities incident to his age, Jan. 24, 1916.
