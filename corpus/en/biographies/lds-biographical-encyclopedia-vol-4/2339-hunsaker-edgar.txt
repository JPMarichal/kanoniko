Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hunsaker, Edgar

Hunsaker, Edgar, Bishop of the Alma Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1906 to 1920, and Bishop of the Mesa 3rd Ward of said stake, from 1920 to 1925, was born Oct. 17, 1872, in Honeyville, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of Alexander Hunsaker and M. C. Johnson. He was baptized Sept. 16, 1881, ordained a High Priest June 14, 1903, by Mathias F. Cowley, and set apart as Bishop Nov. 7, 1920, by Rudger Clawson.
