Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rammell, Parley Samuel

Rammell, Parley Samuel, Bishop of the Tetonia Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1916 to 1918, was born Dec. 4, 1884, in Providence, Utah, a son of Charles H. Rammell and Caroline Berger. He was baptized Dec. 4, 1892, ordained a High Priest Dec. 29, 1912, by John Learham, and a Bishop May 21, 1916, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
