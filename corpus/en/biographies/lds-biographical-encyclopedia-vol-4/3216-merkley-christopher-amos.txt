Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Merkley, Christopher Amos

Merkley, Christopher Amos, Bishop of the Kimball Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1909 to 1910, was born Feb. 28, 1859, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Christopher Merkley and Harissa Fairbanks. He was baptized in August, 1867, ordained a High Priest Aug. 15, 1899, by Geo. Osmond, and a Bishop Dec. 26, 1909, by John Henry Smith. He died Aug. 4, 1935.
