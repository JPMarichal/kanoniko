Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Satterthwaite, Orson Pratt

Satterthwaite, Orson Pratt, Bishop of the Logan 11th Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Aug. 2, 1875, in Pine Valley, Warren Co., Indiana, a son of Joshua W. Satterthwaite and Frances Jane Stowe. He came to Utah in 1882, was baptized in 1883, ordained a High Priest Dec. 11, 1910, by Jos. R. Shepherd, and ordained a Bishop April 20, 1930, by Richard R. Lyman.
