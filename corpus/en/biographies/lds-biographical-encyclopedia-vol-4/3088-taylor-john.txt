Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, John

Taylor, John, Bishop of the Pima Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1885 to 1901, was born Oct. 25, 1850, in Mill Creek, Utah, a son of Allen Taylor and Hannah Egbert. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest Dec. 16, 1883, by Heber J. Grant, and a Bishop Dec. 3, 1885, by Erastus Snow. He died May 29, 1923.
