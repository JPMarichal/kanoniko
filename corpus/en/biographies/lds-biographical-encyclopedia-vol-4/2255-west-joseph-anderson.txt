Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

West, Joseph Anderson

West, Joseph Anderson, Bishop of the Adams Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1927 to 1930, was born Oct. 5, 1867, in Parowan, Utah, a son of John A. West and Mary J. Robinson. He was baptized Oct. 5, 1875, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 29, 1907, by Nephi L. Morris, and a Bishop Sept. 4, 1927, by Geo. F. Richards.
