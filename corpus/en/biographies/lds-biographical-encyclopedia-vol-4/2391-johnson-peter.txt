Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Peter

Johnson, Peter, Bishop of the Paul Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1917, was born Jan. 28, 1876, at Mineral Point, Iona Co., Wisconsin, a son of Ole C. Johnson and Nellie M. Larson. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1886, ordained a High Priest in 1914, by Orson F. Whitney, and sustained as Bishop Sept. 26, 1915.
