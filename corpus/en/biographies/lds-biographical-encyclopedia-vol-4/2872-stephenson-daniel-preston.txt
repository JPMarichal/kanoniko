Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stephenson, Daniel Preston

Stephenson, Daniel Preston, Bishop of the Topaz Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1918 to 1930+, was born July 29, 1879, in Richfield, Utah, the son of Archibald Stephenson and Susan Simpkins. He was baptized in 1887, filled a mission to the Central States in 1904-1905, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 25, 1918, by Anthony W. Ivins.
