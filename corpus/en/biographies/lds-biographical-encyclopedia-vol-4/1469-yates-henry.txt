Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Yates, Henry

Yates, Henry, Bishop of the Harper Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born June 6, 1881, in Harper, the son of Joseph Yates and Elizabeth Batey. He was baptized in September, 1899, ordained an Elder Sept. 29, 1903, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1914-1916, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 5, 1919, by Rudger Clawson.
