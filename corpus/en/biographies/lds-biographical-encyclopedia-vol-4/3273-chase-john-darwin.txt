Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Chase, John Darwin

Chase, John Darwin, Bishop of the Ephraim Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, in 1856, was born Aug. 10, 1815, at Bristol, Addison Co., Vermont, a son of Abner Chase and Amy Scott. He was baptized Oct. 1, 1840, filled a mission to England in 1861-1863, and was set apart to preside over the Ephraim Ward Jan. 8, 1856, by Erastus Snow.
