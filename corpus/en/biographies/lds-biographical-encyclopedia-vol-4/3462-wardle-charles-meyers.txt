Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wardle, Charles Meyers

Wardle, Charles Meyers, Bishop of the Bates Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1922, was born Dec. 18, 1870, at South Jordan, Utah, a son of Isaac John Wardie and Sophia Meyers. He was baptized May 17, 1879, and ordained a Bishop May 18, 1919, by David O. McKay.
