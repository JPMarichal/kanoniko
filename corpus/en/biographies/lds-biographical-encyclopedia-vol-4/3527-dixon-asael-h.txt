Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Twin Falls Stake

Dixon, Asael H.

Dixon, Asael H., Bishop of the Buhl Ward, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1924 and from 1926 to 1929, was born Aug. 3, 1888, in Alton, Wyo., a son of Harvey Dixon and Kittie Pritchett. He was baptized Feb. 6, 1899, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 28, 1919, by Rudger Clawson.
