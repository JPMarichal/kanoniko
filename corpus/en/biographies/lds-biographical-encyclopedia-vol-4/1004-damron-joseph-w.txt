Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Damron, Joseph W.

Damron, Joseph W., president of the Society Islands Mission in 1892 and again from 1893 to 1895, was born June 9, 1866, at Petersburg, Millard Co., Utah, the son of Joseph Damrun and Margaret P. Freeman. He was baptized June 9, 1874, was ordained a Seventy and set apart for his mission Nov. 7, 1891, by Abraham H. Cannon, and returned June 17, 1895.
