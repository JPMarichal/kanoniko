Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kirkman, Lawrence Gomer

Kirkman, Lawrence Gomer, president of the Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1930, was born May 31, 1874, in Springville, Utah, a son of John Kirkman and Annie Jones. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 17, 1911, by Francis M. Lyman, presided over the Twin Falls Ward from 1911 to 1919, and was set apart as president of the stake July 26, 1919, by Rudger Clawson.
