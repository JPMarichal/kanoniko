Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sudweeks, Joseph Hyrum

Sudweeks, Joseph Hyrum, Bishop of the Kimberly Ward, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1921, was born Dec. 21, 1858, in Nephi, Utah, a son of Henry Sudweeks and Sarah Sweet. He was baptized Dec. 21, 1866, ordained a High Priest May 10, 1909, by Francis M. Lyman, and a Bishop Oct. 23, 1915, by Geo. Albert Smith. He died Dec. 18, 1934, in Kimberly, Idaho.
