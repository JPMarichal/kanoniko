Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nelson, Joseph Everet

Nelson, Joseph Everet, Bishop of the Marion Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, in 1913, was born Aug. 26, 1863, in Provo, Utah, a son of Joseph E. Nelson and Sarah Ann Richmond. He was baptized Sept. 19, 1875, and ordained a High Priest by James E. Talmage.
