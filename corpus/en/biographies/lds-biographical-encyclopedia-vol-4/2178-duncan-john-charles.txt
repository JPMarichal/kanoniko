Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Duncan, John Charles

Duncan, John Charles, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 1st Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1930+, was born Jan. 8, 1882, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Adam Duncan and Isabella Robb. He was baptized May 5, 1891, ordained an Elder Jan. 14, 1901, by Charles S. Martin, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1902-1904. He was ordained a High Priest by Arnold Schulthess, and a Bishop Oct. 10, 1909, by Geo. F. Richards.
