Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lee, Lafayette Cox

Lee, Lafayette Cox, second counselor in the Deseret Stake presidency, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born July 31, 1894, in St. George, Utah, the son of Milton LaFayette Lee and Mary Elizabeth Cox. He was set apart as second counselor Sept. 29, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
