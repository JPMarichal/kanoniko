Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Garn, Daniel

Garn, Daniel, Bishop pro tem. of the Salt Lake City 9th Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1849 to 1851, was born Dec. 13, 1802, in St. Clair, Bedford Co., Pennsylvania. He filled a mission to Germany in 1852-1854, and was appointed as Bishop pro tem. in 1849. He died April 20, 1872.
