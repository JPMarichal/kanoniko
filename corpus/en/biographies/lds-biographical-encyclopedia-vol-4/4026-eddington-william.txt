Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Eddington, William

Eddington, William, a member of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1879 to 1913, was born Nov. 27, 1821, in Portsea, Portsmouth, Hampshire, England, a son of James Eddington and Eliza Seaton. He died March 3, 1913. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 2, p. 620.)
