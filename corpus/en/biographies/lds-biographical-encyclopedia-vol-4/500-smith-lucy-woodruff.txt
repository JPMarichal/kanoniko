Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Lucy Woodruff

Smith, Lucy Woodruff, a member of the General Board of the Y. W. M. I. A., was born Jan. 10, 1869, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the daughter of Wilford Woodruff and Emily J. Smith. In 1887 she was chosen to act as ward president of the Y. L. M. I. A. of the 17th Ward, and in 1894 she was elected treasurer of the Salt Lake Stake Y. L. M. I. A. When the stake was divided in 1902, she was chosen to act as second counselor to Nellie C. Taylor in the, Salt Lake Stake Y. L. M. I. A. presidency and was called to the General Board in 1908. She was married May 25, 1892, to George Albert Smith and is the mother of three children, viz., Emily S. Stewart, Edith S. Elliott and George Albert, jun.
