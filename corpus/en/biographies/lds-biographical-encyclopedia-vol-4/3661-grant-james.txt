Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Grant, James

Grant, James, Bishop of the Thurber Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1901, was born Nov. 30, 1857, in Mill Creek, Utah, a son of David Grant and Elizabeth Williams. He was baptized June 16, 1867, and was sustained as Bishop Aug. 12, 1900.
