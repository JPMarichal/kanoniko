Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Day, Rufus

Day, Rufus, Bishop of the Fillmore Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1920, was born Aug. 10, 1869, in Didmartin, England, a son of John Day and Mary Clark. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1880, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1898, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 9, 1917, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
