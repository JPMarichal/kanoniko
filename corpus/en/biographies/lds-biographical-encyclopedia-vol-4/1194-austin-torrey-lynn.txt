Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Austin, Torrey Lynn

Austin, Torrey Lynn, Bishop of the Liberty Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, in 1929 to 1930 +, was born Dec. 9, 1882, in Liberty, the son of Edwin N. Austin and Emma Wood. He was baptized Jan. 27, 1891, was ordained a High Priest by Hyrum G. Smith Nov. 30, 1924, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 15, 1929, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
