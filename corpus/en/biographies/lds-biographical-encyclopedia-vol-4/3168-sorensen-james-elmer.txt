Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sorensen, James Elmer

Sorensen, James Elmer, Bishop of the Glenwood Ward, Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, in 1930+, was born Nov. 8, 1892, at Glenwood, Utah, the son of Jens Sorensen and Ellen Petersen. He was baptized July 8, 1900, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 22, 1930, by George Albert Smith.
