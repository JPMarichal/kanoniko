Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hutchinson, Alonzo

Hutchinson, Alonzo, presiding Elder of the Almy Branch, Woodruff Stake, Wyo., in 1920, was born Feb. 6, 1875, in Hucknall-Torkard, Nottinghamshire, England, a son of Abraham Hutchinson and Elizabeth Lester. He was baptized April 19, 1885, after coming to America in 1884, and was ordained a High Priest March 22, 1903, by John M. Baxter.
