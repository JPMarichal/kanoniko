Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Irvine, Thomas Maycock

Irvine, Thomas Maycock, second counselor in the North Weber Stake presidency, Utah, from 1920 to 1930+, was born July 26, 1885, in Payson, Utah, a son of John D. Irvine and Louise Marie Maycock. He was baptized Aug. 3, 1893, filled a mission to California in 1916-1918, and was ordained a High Priest Nov. 1, 1908, by Orson F. Whitney, and sustained as second counselor in the North Weber Stake presidency Sept. 11, 1921. He was promoted to first counselor in 1931.
