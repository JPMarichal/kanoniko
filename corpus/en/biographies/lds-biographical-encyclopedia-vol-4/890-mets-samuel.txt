Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mets, Samuel

Mets, Samuel, temporary president of the Netherlands Mission from 1863 to 1864, was born Dec. 30, 1836, at Zwyndrecht, Holland, a son of Philip Mets and Leah Ova. He moved to Rotterdam in 1862, his name being entered in the register of population (bevolkings register) July 21 of that year. About that time he accepted the restored Gospel, which was introduced in the Netherlands in 1861. When A. W. van der Woude left for Utah June 1, 1863, Samuel Mets-who presided over the Rotterdam branch-was left in charge of the affairs of the mission, it seems, and Joseph Weiler, the next president sent from Utah, found him at his post on his (Elder Weiler's) arrival Oct. 31, 1864. Bro. Weiler wrote at that time: "He (Samuel Mets) bids me welcome to all he has, and says as long as he has anything I shall share. I like his appearance very much, and he feels like doing all he can to help to roll on the Work in which he is engaged" (Mill. Star, Vol. 26, p. 734). Brother Mets died April 26, 1903, in Rotterdam.
