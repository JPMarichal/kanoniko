Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mitchell, Daniel J.

Mitchell, Daniel J., Bishop of the Francis Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1899 to 1908, was born Jan. 18, 1853, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Benjamin Trotter Mitchell and Lois J. Judd. He was baptized when eight years old, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 11, 1899, by Francis M. Lyman
