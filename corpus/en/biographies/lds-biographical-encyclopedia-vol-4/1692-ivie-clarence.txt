Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ivie, Clarence

Ivie, Clarence, Bishop of the Strawberry Ward, Duchesne Stake, from 1918 to 1930, was born Aug. 12, 1887, in Riverdale, Wasarch Co., Utah, the son of Hyrum L. Ivie and Esther Robbins. He was baptized Aug. 18, 1895, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 30, 1918, by Heber J. Grant.
