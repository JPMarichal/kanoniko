Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jorgensen, Elmer H.

Jorgensen, Elmer H., Bishop of the Alterra Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1929, was born March 10, 1886, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of Jacob Jorgensen and Melvina M. Munk. He was baptized May 3, 1894, ordained a High Priest Nov. 22, 1914, by Heber J. Grant, and sustained as Bishop July 17, 1927. He died April 19, 1929.
