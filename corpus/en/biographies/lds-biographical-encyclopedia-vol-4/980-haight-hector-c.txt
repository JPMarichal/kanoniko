Haight, Hector C.

Haight, Hector C., president of the Scandinavian Mission from 1856 to 1858, died June 26, 1879, in Farmington, Davis Co., Utah. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 387.)

Elder Haight was a Utah pioneer of 1847, who had crossed the plains in Perregrine Sessions' Fifty of Orson Spencer's Hundred. He was the first settler of Farmington, Davis Co., Utah, but by a change in the boundary lines between Farmington and Kaysville his farm on Haight's Creek became a part of Kaysville, where his son, the late Judge Haight, resided for many years.
