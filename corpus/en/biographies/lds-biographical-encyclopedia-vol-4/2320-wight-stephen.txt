Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wight, Stephen

Wight, Stephen, Bishop of the Pleasant View Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1908 to 1913, was born April 1, 1854, in Mill Creek, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Stephen Wight and Lucy E. Waterberg. He was ordained a High Priest June 13, 1897, by William H. Gibbs, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 27, 1908, by George F. Richards. He died in 1924.
