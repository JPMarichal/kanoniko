Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kirkman, John

Kirkman, John, a member of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1879 to 1910, was born Nov. 1, 1830, in Manchester, Lancashire, England, a son of Henry Kirkman and Sarah Holmes. He died Nov. 15, 1910, in Salt Lake City, Utah.
