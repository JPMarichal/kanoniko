Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Miles, Jeddie Leroy

Miles, Jeddie Leroy, Bishop of the Banida Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1922 to 1929, was born March 1, 1881, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of Edwin R. Miles and Jane Mouritsen. He was baptized Oct. 3, 1889, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 11, 1922, by Richard R. Lyman.
