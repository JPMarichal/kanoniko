Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Martindale, Alvin

Martindale, Alvin, presiding Elder of the Elysian Park Branch, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Dec. 4, 1899, in St. George, Utah, the son of John Albert Martindale and Elizabeth Jane Wright. He was baptized July 10, 1910, and ordained a High Priest Sept. 11, 1927, by C. B. Steward.
