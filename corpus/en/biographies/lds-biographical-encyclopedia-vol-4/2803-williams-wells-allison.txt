Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, Wells Allison

Williams, Wells Allison, Bishop of the Kanarra Ward, Parowan Stake, Iron Co., Utah, in 1930+, was born Dec. 15, 1895, in Kanarra, Utah, the son of Reese Jones Williams and Martha Marie Davis. He was baptized June 26, 1904, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 14, 1930, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
