Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hintze, Ferdinand F.

Hintze, Ferdinand F., president of the Turkish Mission from 1887 to 1889 and from 1899 to 1900, was born May 13, 1854, at Roskilde, Denmark, a son of Anders Hintze and Karen Sophie Nikolaisen.

He was baptized May 13, 1862, emigrated to Utah in 1864, and filled missions to the Northwestern States in 1877-1878 and 1879-1880. He filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1885-1887, and one to Turkey in 1897-1900. On the latter mission he published 29,000 tracts in the Turkish and Armenian languages. In 1906 he published the Book of Mormon in Turkish. Elder Hintze died March 9, 1928.
