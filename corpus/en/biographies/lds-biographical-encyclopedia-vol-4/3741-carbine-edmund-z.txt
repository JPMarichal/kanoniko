Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carbine, Edmund Z.

Carbine, Edmund Z., Bishop of the Parker Ward, yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1892 to 1902, was born Sept. 15, 1864, at East Weber, Davis Co., Utah, a son of William V. O. Carbine. He was baptized in 1873, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 12, 1893.
