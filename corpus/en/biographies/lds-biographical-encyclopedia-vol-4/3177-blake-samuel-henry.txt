Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Blake, Samuel Henry

Blake, Samuel Henry, Bishop of the Vineyard Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1929 and first counselor in the Sharon Stake presidency, Utah, from 1929 to 1930 3, was born Oct. 12, 1885, at Centre Creek, Wasatch Co., Utah, a son of William Blake and Mary Lake. He was baptized Oct. 12, 1885, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1906-1908, was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 27, 1920, by Richard R. Lyman, and was chosen first counselor in September, 1929.
