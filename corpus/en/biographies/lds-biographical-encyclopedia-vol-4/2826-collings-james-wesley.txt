Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Collings, James Wesley

Collings, James Wesley, Bishop of the American Falls Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1930 was born Aug. 6, 1878, in Paris, Idaho, a son of James Collings and Frances P. Rich. He was baptized Oct. 6, 1887, ordained a Seventy Sept. 28, 1900, by Anthon H. Lund, filled a mission to Australia in 1900-1903, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 23, 1920, by Rudger Clawson.
