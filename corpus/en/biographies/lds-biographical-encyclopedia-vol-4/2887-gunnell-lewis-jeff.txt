Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gunnell, Lewis Jeff

Gunnell, Lewis Jeff, Bishop of the Naf Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1920 to 1929, was born Dec. 18, 1881, a son of Francis Gunnell and Emma Jeff. He was baptized April 4, 1889, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 6, 1921, by Rudger Clawson.
