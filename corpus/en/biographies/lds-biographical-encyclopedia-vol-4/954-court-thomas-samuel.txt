Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Court, Thomas Samuel

Court, Thomas Samuel, president of the Samoan Mission from 1905 to 1908, was born March 4, 1871, at West Jordan, Utah, a son of Owen T. Court and Louisa S. Swinyard. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1879, filled a mission to Samoa in 1893-1896, and was set apart for a second mission to Samoa Sept. 2, 1904.
