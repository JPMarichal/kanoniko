Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Quist, David Emanuel

Quist, David Emanuel, Bishop of Claresholm Ward, Lethbridge Stake, Canada, from 1920 to 1930+, was born Oct. 13, 1884, in Big Cottonwood, Utah, the son of John Anderson Quist and Mary C. Henden. He was baptized March 30, 1893, ordained an Elder Nov. 1, 1908, by Charles Cole, and filled a mission to Sweden in 1909-1911. He was ordained a High Priest July 9, 1913, by Henry L. Hinman, and a Bishop May 8, 1921, by George F. Richards.
