Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Richter, Fredrick Emil

Richter, Fredrick Emil, an Elder who died while filling a foreign mission, was born Dec. 20, 1858, in Boderetz, Saxony, Germany, a son of Karl August Richter and Wilhelmine Sireroger. He was baptized June 10, 1903, and lator ordained a High Priest. He emigrated to America and made his home in Salt Lake City, Utah. When called on his mission he was residing in the Wandamere Ward, Grant Stake, in Salt Lake City, Utah, where he was a very active worker. He accepted the call to labor in the Swiss and German Mission, being set apart July 27, 1923. He had been in the ministry almost twelve months when he died Sept. 8, 1924, of blood poisoning and a weak heart, in Zwickau, Germany. He was sick only a few days. His remains were brought home to Salt Lake City, accompanied by Brother and Sistor Albert Frederick, who were honorably released for this purpose.
