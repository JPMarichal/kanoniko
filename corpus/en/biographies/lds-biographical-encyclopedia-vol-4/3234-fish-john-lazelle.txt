Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fish, John Lazelle

Fish, John Lazelle, Bishop of the Lakeside Ward, Snowflake Stake, Ariz., from 1920 to 1930+, was born Oct. 28, 1868, in Parowan, Utah, the son of Joseph Fish and Mary C. Stelle. He was baptized June 27, 1877, and ordained a Bishop April 24, 1920, by Geo. F. Richards.
