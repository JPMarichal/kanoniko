Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Heese, Frank H.

Heese, Frank H., Bishop of the Blackfoot Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1903 to 1904, was born May 21, 1865, at Apenrade, Schlesvig-Holstein, Germany. He was baptized in July, 1899, was appointed presiding Elder in 1902, and acted until February, 1903, when he was chosen as Bishop.
