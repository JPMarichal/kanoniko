Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Loveland, Thomas

Loveland, Thomas, Bishop of the Albion Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1915 to 1924, was born Feb. 20, 1879, at Call's Fort, Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of Don Carlos Loveland and Eliza Rosetta Harper. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a Seventy Oct. 10, 1901, by Mathias F. Cowley, filled a mission to the Society Islands in 1901-1904, and was ordained a Bishop April 19, 1915, by Rudger Clawson.
