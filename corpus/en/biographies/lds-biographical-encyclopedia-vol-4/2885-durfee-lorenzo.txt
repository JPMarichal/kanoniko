Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Durfee, Lorenzo

Durfee, Lorenzo, Bishop of the Almo Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1912 to 1918, was born Aug. 31, 1865, at Providence, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Henry D. Durfee and Jane Barker. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1884, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1904-1906, was ordained a High Priest July 27, 1907, by Geo. Albert Smith, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 4, 1912.
