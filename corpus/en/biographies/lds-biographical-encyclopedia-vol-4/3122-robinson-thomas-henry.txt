Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robinson, Thomas Henry

Robinson, Thomas Henry, Bishop of the Dimond Ward, San Francisco Stake, Calif., from 1927 to 1930+, was born Sept. 18, 1887, in Mona, Juab Co., Utah, a son of Eli Robinson and Julia Etlertsen. He was baptized when eight years old, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 20, 1927, by Rudger Clawson.
