Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

East Jordan Stake

Beckstead, Henry

Beckstead, Henry, Bishop of the Midvale 2nd Ward, East Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Dec. 18, 1892, in South Jordan, Utah, a son of Byrum H. Beckstead and Anna Jane Holt. He was baptized Dec. 28, 1901, ordained a Seventy in 1910, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1916-1918, was ordained a High Priest May 8, 1927, by Stephen L. Richards, and a Bishop on July 15, 1928, by Orson F. Whitney.
