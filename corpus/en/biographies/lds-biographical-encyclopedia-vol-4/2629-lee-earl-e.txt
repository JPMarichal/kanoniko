Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lee, Earl E.

Lee, Earl E., Bishop of the Ogden 15th Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Sept. 2, 1896, in Ogden, a son of Thomas Lee and Elizabeth A. Butterworth. He was baptized Nov. 8, 1904, and was ordained a High Priest Sept. 14, 1920, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop March 14, 1926, by David O. McKay.
