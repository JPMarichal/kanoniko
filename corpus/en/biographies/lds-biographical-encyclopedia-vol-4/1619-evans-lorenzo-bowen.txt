Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Evans, Lorenzo Bowen

Evans, Lorenzo Bowen, Bishop of the Arbon Ward, Curlew Stake, Idaho,from 1923 to 1924, was born March. 24, 1868, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Jonah Evans and Frances Bowen. He was ordained a High Priest May 5, 1917, by John C. Cutler, and a Bishop July 29, 1923, by Rudger Clawson.
