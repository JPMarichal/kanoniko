Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Terry, William Zimmerman

Terry, William Zimmerman, Bishop of the Ogden 18th Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born March 28, 1872, in Lewiston, Utah, a son of William A. Terry and Susan Zimmerman. He was baptized March 28, 1880, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Germany in 1896-1899, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 24, 1909, by Heber J. Grant. He presided over the Coalville Ward, Summitt Stake, in 1909-1912.
