Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Graham, Archie J.

Graham, Archie J., Bishop of the Byron Ward, Big Horn Stake, Wyoming, from 1928 to 1930, was born March 26, 1887, in Bennington, Bear Lake Co., Idaho, the son of William B. Graham and Margaret Hope Williams. He was baptized June 6, 1895, filled a short-term mission to the Northern States, moved to the Big Horn country in 1900, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 17, 1928, by David O. McKay.
