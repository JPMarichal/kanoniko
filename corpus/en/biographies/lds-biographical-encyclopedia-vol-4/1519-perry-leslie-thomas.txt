Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Perry, Leslie Thomas

Perry, Leslie Thomas, Bishop of the Logan 9th Ward, Cache Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Feb. 17, 1885, in Three-Mile Creek (Perry), Box Elder Co., Utah, a son of Henry M. Perry and Fanny Young. He was baptized July 23, 1893, and was ordained a High Priest July 26, 1914, by George F. Richards.
