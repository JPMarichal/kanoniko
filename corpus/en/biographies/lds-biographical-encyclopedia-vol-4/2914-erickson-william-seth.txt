Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Erickson, William Seth

Erickson, William Seth, Bishop of the Lewisville Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Oct. 8, 1895, at Blackfoot, Bingham Co., Idaho, a son of John Erickson and Sarah Walker. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1905, ordained a Seventy Feb. 5, 1921, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1921-1923, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 26, 1928, by Stephen L. Richards.
