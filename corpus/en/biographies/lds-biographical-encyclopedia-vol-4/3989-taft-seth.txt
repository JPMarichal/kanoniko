Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taft, Seth

Taft, Seth, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born Aug. 11, 1790, a son of Seth and Lydia Taft. After his arrival in Great Salt Lake Valley he became Bishop of the 9th Ward in Salt Lake City and died in Salt Lake City Nov. 23, 1863, as a Patriarch. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 2, p. 798.)
