Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olsen, Niels Albert

Olsen, Niels Albert, Bishop of the Kimberly Ward, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born July 13, 1880, in Villingebak, Frederiksborg amt, Denmark, a son of Andreas Olsen and Ane Jensine Nielsen. He came to Utah in 1899, was baptized July 1, 1916, ordained a High Priest Aug. 25, 1918, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop Aug. 1, 1926, by Geo. F. Richards.
