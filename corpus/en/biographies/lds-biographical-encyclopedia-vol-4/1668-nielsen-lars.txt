Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nielsen, Lars

Nielsen, Lars, Bishop of the Learnington Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1883 to 1900, was born March 20, 1828, in South Orslev, Maribo amt, Denmark, the son of Niels Christensen and Anna M. Rasmussen. He was baptized March 30, 1860, in Denmark, emigrated to Utah in 1863, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 9, 1883, by Francis M. Lyman.
