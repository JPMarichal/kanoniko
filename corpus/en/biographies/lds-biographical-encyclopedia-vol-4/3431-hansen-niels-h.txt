Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Niels H.

Hansen, Niels H., Bishop of the Cache Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1916 to 1917, was born May 13, 1867, in Merlose, Holbak amt, Denmark. He was baptized July 1, 1875, ordained a High Priest June 6, 1909, by Don C. Driggs, and a Bishop May 21, 1916, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
