Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bronson, Clark

Bronson, Clark, Bishop of the Midway 1st Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born April 21, 1885, at Midway, Utah, a son of Everice Bronson and Cynthia Van Wagoner. He was baptized in March, 1893, ordained a Seventy Nov. 16, 1919, by Brigham H. Roberts, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1920-1922, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 28, 1926, by Richard R. Lyman.
