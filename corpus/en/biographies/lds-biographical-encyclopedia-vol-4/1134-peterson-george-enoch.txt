Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, George Enoch

Peterson, George Enoch, Bishop of the Beazer Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1919 to 1930+, was born May 5, 1873, in Mr. Pleasant, Utah, the son of Anders (Andrew) Peterson and Fannie McLean. He was baptized May 29, 1881; ordained a High Priest Jan. 9, 1916, by Sterling Williams, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 3, 1919, by Orson F. Whitney.
