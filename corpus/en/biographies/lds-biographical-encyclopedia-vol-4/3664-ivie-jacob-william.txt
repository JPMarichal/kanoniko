Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ivie, Jacob William

Ivie, Jacob William, Bishop of the Loa Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930, was born May 2, 1883, in Loa, a son of Jacob Ivie and Lydia Okerlund. He was baptized May 2, 1891, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 8, 1927, by Orson F. Whitney.
