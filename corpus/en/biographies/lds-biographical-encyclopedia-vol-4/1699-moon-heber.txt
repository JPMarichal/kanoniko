Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Moon, Heber

Moon, Heber, Bishop of the Redcliff Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1925, was born Jan. 8, 1883, in Woodland, Utah, the son of Henry Moon and Mary Ann Thayne. He filled a mission to the Western States in 1903-1905, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 30, 1918, by Heber J. Grant.
