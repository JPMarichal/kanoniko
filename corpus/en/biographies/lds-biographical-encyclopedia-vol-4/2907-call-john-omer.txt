Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Call, John Omer

Call, John Omer, Bishop of the Rigby 2rid Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1927 to 1930+, was born March 16, 1887, in Rigby, Idaho, a son of Cyril J. Call and Mary E. Wood. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1895, ordained an Elder May 5, 1907, by Charles Call, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1907-1909, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 22, 1923, by Pres. Heber J. Grant, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 20, 1927, by David O. McKay.
