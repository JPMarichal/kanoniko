Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stewart, William Urban

Stewart, William Urban, Bishop of the Adamsville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1914 to 1919, was born Oct. 10, 1868, in Beaver, Utah, the son of Urban Van Stewart and Mary Ann Jones. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 16, 1915, by Orson F. Whitney.
