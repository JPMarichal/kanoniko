Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Monson, John F.

Monson, John F., Bishop of the Aberdeen Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1925 to 1929, was born Oct. 4, 1873, in Mr. Pleasant, Utah, the son of Peter Monsen and Hannah C. Christensen. He was baptized June 18, 1882, was ordaned a High Priest Dec. 3, 1916, by Nofear Davis, and a Bishop May 9, 1925, by Rudger Clawson.
