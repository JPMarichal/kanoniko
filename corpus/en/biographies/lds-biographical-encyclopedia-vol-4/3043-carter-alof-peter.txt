Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Carter, Alof Peter

Carter, Alof Peter, Bishop of the Matthews Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1923 to 1929, was born March 2, 1891, in Matthews, the son of Edwin T. Carter and Betsy Larson. He was baptized May 7, 1899, and ordained a Bishop May 27, 1923, by James E. Talmage.
