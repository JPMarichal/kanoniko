Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, Wallace

Johnson, Wallace, Bishop of the Marysvale Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1921, was born May 26, 1892, in Johnson, Kane Co., Utah, a son of Nephi Johnson and Julia Shumway. He was baptized June 30, 1900, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1911-1913, and .was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 20, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
