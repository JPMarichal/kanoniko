Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Facer, George Henry

Facer, George Henry, Bishop of the Cherry Creek Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1902 to 1903, was born Nov. 13, 1858, in Eynesburry, Huntingtonshire, England, a son of George Facer and Mary Prior. He was baptized Nov. 13, 1866, emigrated with his parents in 1860, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 22, 1902, by Matthias F. Cowley.
