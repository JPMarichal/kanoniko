Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Simper, Alfred

Simper, Alfred, Bishop of the Davis Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1930+, was born Aug. 3, 1884, in East Mill Creek, Utah, a son of Thomas R. Simper and Martha Elizabeth Swain. He was baptized Sept. 18, 1893, ordained a High Priest Jan. 5, 1913, by James M. Shaffer, and a Bishop June 15, 1913, by Geo. F. Richards.
