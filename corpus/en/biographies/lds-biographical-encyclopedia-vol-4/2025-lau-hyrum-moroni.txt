Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lau, Hyrum Moroni

Lau, Hyrum Moroni, Bishop of the Soda Springs Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1927, was born Nov. 10, 1870, in Providence, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Daniel P. Lau and Dorothy Zollinger. He was baptized July 30, 1881, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Germany, in 1899-1901, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 26, 1902, by Louis S. Pond, and ordained a Bishop May 23, 1926, by Rudger Clawson.
