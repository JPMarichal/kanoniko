Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hymas, Ernest David

Hymas, Ernest David, Bishop of the Lanark Ward, Bear Lake Stake, Idaho, in 1921-1930+, was born Feb. 4, 1883, in Liberty, Idaho, the son of James Hymas and Sarah C. Evans. He was baptized April 6, 1891, was ordained an Elder in 1905 by Robert Price, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1905-1907, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 30, 1908, by James Nye and ordained a Bishop Aug. 28, 1921, by Rudger Clawson.
