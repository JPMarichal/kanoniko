Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johnson, William Derby, jun.

Johnson, William Derby, jun., Bishop of the Diaz Ward, Juarez Stake, Mexico, from 1887 to 1911, was born May 2, 1850, in Council Bluffs, Iowa, a son of Wm. D. Johnson, sen., and Jane C. Brown. He was baptized in 1858, was ordained a High Priest April 17, 1877, by Erastus Snow, and a Bishop Nov. 9, 1886, by Geo. Teasdale. He acted as Bishop of Kanab Ward in 1877-1884 and as a counselor in the Kanab Stake presidency in 1884-1887. He died June 2, 1910.
