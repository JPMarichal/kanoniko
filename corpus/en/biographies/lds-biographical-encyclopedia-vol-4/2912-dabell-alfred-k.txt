Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dabell, Alfred K.

Dabell, Alfred K., Bishop of the Grant Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1892 to 1906, was born May 14, 1852, at Bradford, Northamptonshire, England, a son of Alonzo Dabell and Harriett Lambert. He was baptized May 14, 1860, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 14, 1892, by John Henry Smith.
