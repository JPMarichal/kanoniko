Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cook, Joshua Holden

Cook, Joshua Holden, Bishop of the Lyman Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1909, was born May 16, 1857, in Nephi, Utah, a son of David Cook and Mary Ann Holden. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1893-1895, and was sustained as Bishop Aug. 11, 1900.
