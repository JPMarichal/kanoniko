Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Snyder, William Henry

Snyder, William Henry, Bishop of the Blackfoot 2nd Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930, was born Nov. 22, 1871, in Fairfield, Utah, the son of Henry Snyder and Sophia Hardy. He was baptized in 1880 by James Finn, ordained an Elder in 1897 by Bishop Eli Bennett and filled a mission in the Swiss-German Mission in 1904-1907. He was ordained a High Priest May 27, 1917, by James Duckworth and ordained a Bishop Nov. 16, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
