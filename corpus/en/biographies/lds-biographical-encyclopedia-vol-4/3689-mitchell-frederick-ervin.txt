Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mitchell, Frederick Ervin

Mitchell, Frederick Ervin, Bishop of the Clinton Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1925, was born Aug. 17, 1881, in Riverdale, Utah, a son of James Mitchell and Maren Jensen. He was baptized Oct. 24, 1889, ordained a Seventy March 11, 1900, by J. Golden Kimball, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1902-1904, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 25, 1910, by Charles F. Middleton, and a Bishop March 2, 1915, by Pres. Jos. F. Smith.
