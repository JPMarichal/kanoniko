Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Phippen, Melvin Earl

Phippen, Melvin Earl, Bishop of the Albion Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born April 16, 1893, at Albion, Idaho, the son of Wilford F. Phippen and Emma L. Williams. He was baptized June 22, 1901, ordained an Elder Sept. 28, 1913, by William O. Pratt, filled a mission to the Central States in 1913-1915, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 26, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard.
