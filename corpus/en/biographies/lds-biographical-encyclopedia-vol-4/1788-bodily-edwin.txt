Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bodily, Edwin

Bodily, Edwin, Bishop of the Fairview Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1904 to 1919, was born Dec. 12, 1851, in Port Elizabeth, South Africa, the son of Robert Bodily and Jane Pittam. He was baptized in South Africa, came to Utah, and filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1885-1888. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 28, 1888, by Geo. C. Parkinson, and sustained as Bishop Dec. 27, 1903.
