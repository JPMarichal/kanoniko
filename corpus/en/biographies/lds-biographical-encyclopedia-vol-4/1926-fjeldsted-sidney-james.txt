Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fjeldsted, Sidney James

Fjeldsted, Sidney James, second counselor in the Gunnison Stake presidency, Utah, in 1930+, was born Oct. 19, 1887, in Centerfield, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Andrew C. Fieldsted and Matilda Larson. He was baptized June 30, 1896, ordained a High Priest Aug. 5, 1923, by James E. Talmage, and set apart as second counselor March 9, 1930, by David O. McKay.
