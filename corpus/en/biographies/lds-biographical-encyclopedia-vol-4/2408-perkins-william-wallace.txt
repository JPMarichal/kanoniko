Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Perkins, William Wallace

Perkins, William Wallace, Bishop of the Overton Ward, Moapa Stake, Nevada, from 1915 to 1916, was born June 23, 1879, in St. George, Utah, a son of William A. Perkins and Rachel Lamb. He was baptized in February 1887, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southwestern States in 1900-1903, was ordained a High Priest June 9, 1912, by George F. Richards, and sustained as Bishop of Overton Feb. 21, 1915.
