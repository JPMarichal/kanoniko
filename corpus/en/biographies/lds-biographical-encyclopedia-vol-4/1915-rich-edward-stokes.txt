Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rich, Edward Stokes

Rich, Edward Stokes, Bishop of the East Mill Creek Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1929, was born Dec. 10, 1886, in Almo, Idaho, the son of Samuel Rich and Mary Ann Stokes. He was baptized Dec. 31, 1894, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1905-1907, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 8, 1924, by Geo. Albert Smith.
