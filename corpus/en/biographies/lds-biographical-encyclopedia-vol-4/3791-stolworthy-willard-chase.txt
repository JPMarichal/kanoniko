Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stolworthy, Willard Chase

Stolworthy, Willard Chase, first counselor in the Young Stake presidency from 1924 to 1930+, was born Sept. 16, 1891, in Orderville, Utah, a son of Henry Thomas Stolworthy and Johanna Elizabeth Covington. He was baptized in September, 1899, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1912-1913, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 21, 1914, by Rudger Clawson, and set apart as first counselor in the stake presidency March 22, 1925.
