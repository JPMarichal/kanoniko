Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Richards, Ralph C.

Richards, Ralph C., Bishop of the Riverside Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1926, was born April 14, 1886, in Plymouth (now Fielding), Box Elder Co., Utah, the son of Myron J. Richards and Julia Ann Peterson. He was baptized April 14, 1894, ordained a High Priest Nov. 23, 1915, by Davidd O. McKay, and ordained a Bishop June 2, 1923, by Rudger Clawson.
