Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bodily, Henry James

Bodily, Henry James, Bishop of the Preston 1st Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1914 to 1918, was born Oct. 23, 1875, in Fairview, Idaho, the son of Edwin Bodily and Matilda Roberts. He was baptized Aug. 24, 1885, and ordained a High Priest Sept. 6, 1908, by Charles D. Goaslind, and a Bishop June 6, 1914, by Geo, F. Richards.
