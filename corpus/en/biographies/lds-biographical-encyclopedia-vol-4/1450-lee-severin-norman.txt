Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lee, Severin Norman

Lee, Severin Norman, president of the Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1930+, was born Oct. 4, 1875, in Brigham City, Utah, the son of Severin N. Lee and Emma L. Ensign. He was baptized Oct. 21, 1883, ordained a Seventy Oct. 6, 1896, by Chr. D. Fieldsted, and filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1896-1898.
