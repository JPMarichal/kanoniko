Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Christensen, Christian Franklin

Christensen, Christian Franklin, Bishop of the Kanosh Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, from 1897 to 1908, was born Aug. 26, 1864, in Panguitch, Utah, a son of Hans Christensen and Magdalene Christensen. He was baptized in 1872, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1882-1885, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 21, 1889, by Abraham H. Cannon, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 21, 1897, by George Teasdale.
