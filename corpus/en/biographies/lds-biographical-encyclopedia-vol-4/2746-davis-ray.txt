Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Davis, Ray

Davis, Ray, Bishop of the Salem Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1929 to 1930 +, was born Dec. 6, 1886, in Salem, the son of James J. Davis and Harriet Matilda Kelsey. He was baptized June 6, 1895, ordained a Seventy Nov. 29, 1908, by Heber J. Grant, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1915-1917, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 16, 1925, by George F. Richards, who also ordained him a Bishop Jan. 27, 1929.
