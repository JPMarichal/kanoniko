Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cutler, Jonathan Corkins

Cutler, Jonathan Corkins, president of the Curlew Stake, in Utah and Idaho, from 1915 to 1922, was born Sept. 14, 1864, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Sheldon Bealy Cutler and Sarah Wight. He was baptized Oct. 27, 1872, filled a mission in the Northern States in 1898-1900, acted as Bishop of the Snowville Ward from 1905 to 1915, and was set apart as president of the Curlew Stake May 17, 1915, by Rudger Clawson. He died June 22, 1922.
