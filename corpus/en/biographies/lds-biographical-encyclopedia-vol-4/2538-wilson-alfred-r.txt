Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilson, Alfred R.

Wilson, Alfred R., Bishop of the Payson 1st Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1928, was born Oct. 1, 1882, in Payson, Utah, the son of Thomas H. Wilson and Caroline Marchant. He was baptized June 2, 1892, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 28, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
