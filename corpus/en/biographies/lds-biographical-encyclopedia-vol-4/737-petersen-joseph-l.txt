Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Petersen, Joseph L.

Petersen, Joseph L., president of the Danish Mission from 1926 to 1929, was born in Huntsville, Weber Co., Utah, the son of Soren L. and Louisa Petersen. Elder Petersen has spent most of his life in Huntsville, where he is engaged in mercantile business. In 1902 he married Miss Ida A. Anderson. He has filled three missions to Denmark, the first in 1897-1899, the second in 1904-1906, and the third in 1926-1929, when he was called to preside over the Danish Mission. He also served as Bishop of the Huntsville Ward in 1916-1926. (See History of the Scandinavian Mission, p. 492.)
