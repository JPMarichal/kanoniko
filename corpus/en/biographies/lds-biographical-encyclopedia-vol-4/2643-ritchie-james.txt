Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ritchie, James

Ritchie, James, Bishop of the Marriott Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1902, was born May 12, 1833, in Saltcoats, Ayrshire, Scotland, a son of James Ritchie and Elizabeth Anderson. He was baptized in Salt Lake City when twenty-one years old, having emigrated to Utah in 1853. He filled a mission to Great Britain in 1885-1886, and was ordained a Bishop May 28, 1877, by John Taylor. He died June 6, 1913.
