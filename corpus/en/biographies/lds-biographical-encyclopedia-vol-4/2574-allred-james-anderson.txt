Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

North Sanpete Stake

Allred, James Anderson

Allred, James Anderson, Bishop of the Spring City Ward, North Sanpete Stake, Utah, from 1882 to 1904, was born Nov. 22, 1819, in Bedford County, Tenn., a son of William Allred and Sarah Warren. He was baptized in 1839, came to Utah in 1861, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 17, 1868, by James Allred, and a Bishop Oct. 3, 1882, by John Henry Smith. He died April 3, 1904.
