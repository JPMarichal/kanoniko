Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lay, Hark

Lay, Hark, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was a negro servant of Wm. Lay, a wealthy member of the Church in Monroe County, Mississippi, who was sent with three other negroes, servants of William Crosby, John H. Bankhead and John Brown, in charge of Bro. John Brown, with two good wagons and supplies, to assist Pres. Brigham Young on the pioneer journey. Hark Lay at that time was about 22 years of age. He remained in the western country and died at Union, Salt Lake Co., Utah, about 1890.
