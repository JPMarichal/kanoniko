Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whitney, Orson K.

Whitney, Orson K., one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born Jan. 30, 1830, at Kirtland, Ohio, a son of Newel K. and Elizabeth Ann Whitney. After his arrival in Great Salt Lake Valley, Bro. Whitney took part in numerous expeditions in connection with Indian depredations and was a daring and adventurous frontiersman. He died in Salt Lake City July 31, 1884.
