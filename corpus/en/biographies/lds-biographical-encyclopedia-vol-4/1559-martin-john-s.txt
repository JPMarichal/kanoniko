Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Martin, John S.

Martin, John S., Bishop of the Basin Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born March 2, 1889, in Tooele, Utah, the son of John G. Martin and Mary Myers. He was ordained an Elder April 12, 1908, filled a mission to the Central States in 1908-1910, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 19, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
