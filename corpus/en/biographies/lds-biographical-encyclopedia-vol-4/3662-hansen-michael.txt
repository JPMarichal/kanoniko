Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Michael

Hansen, Michael, Bishop of the Loa Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1918, was born July 7, 1849, in Bjerre, Denmark, a son of Hans Dennesen and Kjerstine Mikkelsdatter. He was baptized Dec. 15, 1881, came to Utah in 1885, was ordained an Elder April 27, 1884, by John B. Hesse, filled a mission to Denmark in 1910-1912, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 27, 1893, by Marriner W. Merrill, and a Bishop May 14, 1916, by Francis M. Lyman.
