Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Houston, Isaac

Houston, Isaac, Bishop of the Alpine Ward, Alpine Stake, Utah, from 1852 to 1856, was born Oct. 5, 1799, in New Hampshire. He was baptized Oct. 28, 1840, was ordained a High Priest in Nauvoo, Illinois, and migrated to Utah in 1851. He died Aug. 23, 1856, in Alpine.
