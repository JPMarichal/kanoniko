Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Maycock, George Ray

Maycock, George Ray, president of the Kolob Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930, was born Sept. 18, 1879, in Springville, Utah, a son of George Henry Maycock and Julia L. Guymon. He was baptized Feb. 2, 1888, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1900-1901, was ordained a High Priest and Bishop of the Springville 4th Ward Oct. 10, 1915, by James E. Talmage, and was set apart as stake president Nov. 23, 1924.
