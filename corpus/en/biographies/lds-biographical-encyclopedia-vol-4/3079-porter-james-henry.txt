Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Porter, James Henry

Porter, James Henry, Bishop of the Thatcher East Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1927 to 1929, was born June 21, 1875, in Scipio, Millard Co., Utah, a son of James Henry Porter and Emma U. Bennett. He filled a mission to the Northern States in 1909-1911 and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 26, 1927, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
