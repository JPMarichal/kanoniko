Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shorten, David George

Shorten, David George, Bishop of the Ogden 18th Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1926, was born July 29, 1883, in Ogden, Weber Co., Utah, a son of George Shorten and Elizabeth Ellis. He was baptized July 29, 1891, ordained a High Priest Dec. 19, 1915, by A. W. Horsley and a Bishop July 28, 1924, by George F. Richards.
