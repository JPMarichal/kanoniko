Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

South Davis Stake

Barlow, Wilford Walter

Barlow, Wilford Walter, Bishop of the Bountiful 1st Ward, South Davis Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1915, was born Oct. 26, 1880, in Centerville, Davis Co., Utah, a son of Wilford W. Barlow and Laura Jackson. He was ordained a High Priest Feb. 14, 1909, by Joseph H. Grant, and a Bishop March 27, 1909, by Francis M. Lyman.
