Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hanson, Alma

Hanson, Alma, second counselor in the Teton Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Aug. 20, 1872, at Brigham City, Utah. He was baptized April 3, 1881, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 12, 1908, by Geo. Albert Smith. He presided over the Bates Ward from 1908 to 1919, and was set apart as second counselor Feb. 20, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard.
