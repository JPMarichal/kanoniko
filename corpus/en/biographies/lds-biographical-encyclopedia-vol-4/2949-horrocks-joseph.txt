Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Horrocks, Joseph

Horrocks, Joseph, Bishop of the Bennett Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1924, was born Dec. 7, 1873, in Heber, Wasatch Co., Utah, a son of Roger Horrocks and Sarah Ann Taylor. He was baptized in 1881, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 21, 1913, by James E. Talmage.
