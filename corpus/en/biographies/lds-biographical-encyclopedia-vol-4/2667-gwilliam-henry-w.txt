Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gwilliam, Henry W.

Gwilliam, Henry W., Bishop of the Ogden 4th Ward, Ogden Stake, Utah, in 1908, and of the Ogden 6th Ward, from 1908 to 1911, was born Feb. 21, 1857, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Henry B. Gwilliam and Elizabeth Palmer. He was baptized in May 1865, was ordained a High Priest May 29, 1887, by Franklin D. Richards, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 23, 1908, by Pres. Jos. F. Smith. He died Sept. 22, 1907.
