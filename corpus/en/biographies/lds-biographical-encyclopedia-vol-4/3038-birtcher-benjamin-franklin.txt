Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Birtcher, Benjamin Franklin

Birtcher, Benjamin Franklin, Bishop of the Globe Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1929 to 1930+, was born March 20, 1886, in pisgah, Preston Co., W. Va., a son of Lockard L Birtcher and Minerva J. Knotts. He was baptized July 24, 1905, came to Utah in 1907, was ordained an Elder Sept. 29, 1912, by Heber C. Iverson, and filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1913-1915. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 19, 1920, by Andrew Kimball, and a Bishop Sept. 12, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
