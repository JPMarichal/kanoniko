Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hatch, Robert Oral

Hatch, Robert Oral, Bishop of the Cornish Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1922 to 1926, was born March 15, 1891, in Franklin, Oneida Co., Idaho, the son of Hyrum Hatch and Esther Gregory. He was baptized March 15, 1899, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 29, 1922, by Geo. F. Richards.
