Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Schaub, Karl Conrad

Schaub, Karl Conrad, Bishop of the Logan 10th Ward, Cache Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1930+, was born Dec. 12, 1869, in Zizers, Canton Graubunden, Switzerland, a son of Conrad Karl Schaub and Marie Alker. He was baptized Aug. 8, 1887, by Adolph Baer, emigrated to Utah with his father in 1888, and was ordained a Bishop Oct. 14, 1917, by Stephen L. Richards.
