Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Chadwick, James

Chadwick, James, Bishop of the Whitney Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, from 1889-1893, was born June 23, 1842, near Oldham, Yorkshire, England, the son of Joseph Chadwick and Mary Whitehead. He came to Utah in 1860, located in Whitney in 1874, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 15, 1889, by Moses Thatcher.
