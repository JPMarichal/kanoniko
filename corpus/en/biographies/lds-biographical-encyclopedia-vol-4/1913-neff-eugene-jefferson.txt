Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Neff, Eugene Jefferson

Neff, Eugene Jefferson, Bishop of the East Mill Creek Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born March 29, 1883, in East Mill Creek, a son of Amos Herr Neff and Eliza Annie Heughes. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1892, ordained an Elder Aug. 14, 1900, by James G. Smith, filled two mission to Hawaii, one in 1904-1908, and another in 1923-1927 when he presided over the mission. He was ordained a High Priest in August, 1924, by Anthony W. Ivins, and a Bishop May 6, 1928.
