Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Todd, Thomas Richardson

Todd, Thomas Richardson, Bishop of the Cedarview Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1928, was born Aug. 1, 1883, in Heber City, Wasatch Co., Utah, a son of Thomas Todd and Harriet B. Richardson. He was baptized Aug. 15, 1892, ordained a High Priest Dec. 8, 1912, by William H. Smart, and ordained a Bishop April 14, 1918, by Heber J. Grant.
