Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hill, Abraham Mormon

Hill, Abraham Mormon, Bishop of the Miller Ward, Grant Stake, Utah, from March 17 to Nov. 17, 1907, was born March 28, 1868, in Mill Creek, Utah, the son of William H. Hill and Mary Sorensen. He was baptized June 10, 1877, was ordained a Seventy April 21, 1903, by Rulon S. Wells, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1903-1905. He died May 6, 1919, in Mill Creek.
