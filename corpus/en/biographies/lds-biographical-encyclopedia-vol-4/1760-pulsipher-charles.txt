Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pulsipher, Charles

Pulsipher, Charles, Bishop of the Huntington Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1883 to 1891, was born April 20, 1830, in Spafford, Onondago Co., New York, the son of Zerah Pulsipher and Mary Brown. He was baptized in May, 1838, came to Utah in 1848, and was ordained a Bishop June 3, 1883, by Wilford Woodruff. He died Nov. 19, 1915.
