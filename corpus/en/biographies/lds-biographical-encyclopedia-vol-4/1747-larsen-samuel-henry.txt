Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larsen, Samuel Henry

Larsen, Samuel Henry, Bishop of the Castle Dale Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1928, was born Dec. 8, 1865, in Spring City, Utah, the son of Christen G. Larsen and Marie Caroline Sorensen. He was baptized Aug. 16, 1874, was ordained a Seventy Nov. 5, 1907, by Rudger Clawson, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1907-1909, and was ordained a High Priest Sept. 18, 1910, by Lars P. Oveson, and a Bishop Aug. 11, 1912, by Orson F. Whitney.
