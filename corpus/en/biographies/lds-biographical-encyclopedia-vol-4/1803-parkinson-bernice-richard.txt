Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Parkinson, Bernice Richard

Parkinson, Bernice Richard, Bishop of the Franklin Ward, Franklin Stake, Idaho, in 1930+, was born April 12, 1893, in Franklin, the son of Samuel C. Parkinson and Maryann Hobbs. He was baptized June 7, 1902, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1915-1917, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 16, 1919, by Jos. S. Geddes, and a Bishop Feb. 23, 1930, by Orson F. Whitney.
