Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Van Wagener, David

Van Wagener, David, Bishop of the Midway Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1877 to 1892, was born July 18, 1836, in Wynocke, Hudson (now Pasaic) Co., New Jersey, a son of John H. Van Wagener and Eliza Smith. He was baptized at Winter Quarters in June, 1847, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 15, 1877, by John Taylor.
