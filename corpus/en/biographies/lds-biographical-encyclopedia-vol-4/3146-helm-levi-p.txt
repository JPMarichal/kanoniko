Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Helm, Levi P.

Helm, Levi P., president of the San Luis Stake of Zion, Colorado, from 1905 to 1908, was born Oct. 22, 1851, in Sandusky Co., Ohio, the son of Abraham Helm and Mary Richards. He was baptized Oct. 13, 1861, and set apart as president of the stake July 16, 1905, by Francis M. Lyman. He died April 3, 1934.
