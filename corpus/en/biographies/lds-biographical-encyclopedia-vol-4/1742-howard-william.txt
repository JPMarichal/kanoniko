Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Howard, William

Howard, William, second counselor in the Emery Stake presidency, Utah, from 1888 to 1899, was born Jan. 13, 1847, in Belfast, Ireland, the son of William Howard and Elizabeth Anderson. He came to America in 1853, was baptized March 29, 1857, filled a mission to Europe in 1868-1869, and was ordained a High Priest Sept. 12, 1886, by John Henry Smith. He died April 6, 1914.
