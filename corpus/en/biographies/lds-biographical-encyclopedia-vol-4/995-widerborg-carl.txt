Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Widerborg, Carl

Widerborg, Carl, president of the Scandinavian Mission from 1858 to 1860, died March 12, 1869, in Ogden. Utah. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 814.)

Elder Widerborg was one of the most successful missionaries and one of the ablest and most fluent speakers known in the Scandinavian Mission.
