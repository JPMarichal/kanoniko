Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Unander, Ralph Peter

Unander, Ralph Peter, Bishop of the Burley 1st Ward, Burley Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930, was born Sept. 23, 1890, in Ogden, Utah, the son of Peter Unander and Margaret Isabel Golightly. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1903, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 5, 1927, by David O. McKay.
