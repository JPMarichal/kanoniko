Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Holbrook, Joseph Hyrum

Holbrook, Joseph Hyrum, Bishop of the Auburn Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1891 to 1894, was born Feb. 8, 1854, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Joseph Holbrook and Caroline Augell. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a High Priest July 1, 1889, and a Bishop Aug. 29, 1891, by William Budge.
