Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Budge, Oliver H.

Budge, Oliver H., president of the German-Austrian Mission from 1930 to 1934, was born April 3, 1873, a son of Wm. Budge and Ann Hyer. He was baptized when nine years old by Robert Price, ordained a Seventy Oct. 7, 1896, by Chr. D. Fjeldsted, and filled a mission to Switzerland and Germany in 1896-1899. On his second mission (1930-1934), when he presided, he was accompanied by his wife, Margaret Sutton Budge, who was born April 15, 1877, in Paris, Idaho, a daughter of John A. Sutton and Margaret H. Shepherd.
