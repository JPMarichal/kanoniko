Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bennett, John Harvey

Bennett, John Harvey, Bishop of the Kimball Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1924 to 1927, was born July 4, 1870, at New Harmony, Kane Co., Utah, the son of George Bennett and Nancy Melvina Taylor. He was baptized June 27, 1880, by William Blood, and was ordained a Bishop April 30, 1924, by Stephen L. Richards.
