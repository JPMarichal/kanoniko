Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, John Davis

Jones, John Davis, presiding Elder of the Cherry Creek Branch, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1873 to 1880, and Bishop of the Cherry Creek Ward from 1880 to 1885, was born Nov. 2, 1831, in South Wales, a son of Benjamin Davis Jones and Elizabeth Evans. He was baptized Sept. 10, 1846, emigrated to Utah in 1849, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 31, 1880, by Lorenzo Snow.
