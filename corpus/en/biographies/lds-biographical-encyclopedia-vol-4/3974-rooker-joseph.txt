Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rooker, Joseph

Rooker, Joseph, one of the original pioneers of Utah, remained in Salt Lake Valley for some time and was a member of a committee to take teams to meet the on-coming companies, relieve them of their loads and assist them to reach their destination. Later he resided at Black Rock, Salt Lake Co., and was a member of the 9th Quorum of Seventy. About 1857 he went to California.
