Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jeppson, Royal M.

Jeppson, Royal M., Bishop of the Blackfoot 1st Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1924, was born Oct. 27, 1885, in Millville, Utah, the son of Joseph Jeppson and Annie L. Cederholm. He was baptized May 2, 1893, ordained a High Priest Nov. 18, 1917, by Hyrum G. Smith, and a Bishop Dec. 19, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
