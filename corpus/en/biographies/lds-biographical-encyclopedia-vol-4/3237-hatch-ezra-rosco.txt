Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hatch, Ezra Rosco

Hatch, Ezra Rosco, Bishop of the Holbrook Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1917 to 1920, was born Oct. 30, 1888, at Taylor, Arizona, a son of John Hatch and Mary Jane Standifird. He was baptized Nov. 5, 1896, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 11, 1917, by Orson F. Whitney.
