Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, Philetusp

Jones, Philetus, Bishop, of the Rockville Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Jan. 13, 1876, in Grafton, Utah, a son of Charles H. Jones and Viola M. Russell. He was baptized about 1884 and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 17, 1921, by Hyrum G. Smith.
