Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hafen, Johan Georg, jun.

Hafen, Johan Georg, jun., Bishop of Santa Clara Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1884 to 1912, was born Oct. 17, 1838, in Scherzingen, Canton Thurgau, Switzerland, a son of Johan G. Hafen and Marie Magdalene Hafen. He was baptized April 17, 1860, emigrated to Utah in 1861, filled a six months' mission to the Southern States in 1877, and a mission to Switzerland and Germany in 1882-1884. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 9, 1884, by Erastus Snow.
