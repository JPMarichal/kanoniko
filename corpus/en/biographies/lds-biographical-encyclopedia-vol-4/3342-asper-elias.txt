Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Asper, Elias

Asper, Elias, Bishop of the Echo Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1894, was born March 7, 1820, in York County, Penn., a son of Geo. Asper and Salome Firestine. He came to Utah in 1861, and was ordained a Bishop July 9, 1877, by Lorenzo Snow. He died March 20, 1894.
