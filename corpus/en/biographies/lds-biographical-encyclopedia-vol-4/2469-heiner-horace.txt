Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Heiner, Horace

Heiner, Horace, Bishop of the North Morgan Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, in 1930, was born May 1, 1888, in Morgan, Morgan Co., Utah, a son of George Heiner and Sarah Jane Loggart. He was baptized May 1, 1896, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Australia in 1910-1912, and was ordained a High Priest March 8, 1925, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
