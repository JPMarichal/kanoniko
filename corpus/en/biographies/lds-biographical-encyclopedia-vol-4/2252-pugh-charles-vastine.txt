Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pugh, Charles Vastine

Pugh, Charles Vastine, Bishop of the Long Beach Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, from 1924 to 1927, was born Oct. 2, 1885, in Clarinda, Iowa, a son of Daniel Smith Pugh and Anna Marie Draper. He was baptized May 2, 1914, was ordained a High Priest May 13, 1923, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop Nov. 9, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard.
