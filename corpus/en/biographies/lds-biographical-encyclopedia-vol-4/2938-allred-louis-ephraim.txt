Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Roosevelt Stake

Allred, Louis Ephraim

Allred, Louis Ephraim, Bishop of the Hayden Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1920, was born Oct. 2, 1878, in Chester, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Ephraim L. Allred and Harriet B. Bronson. He was baptized Oct. 7, 1886, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northwestern States, in 1910-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 15, 1917, by George Albert Smith.
