Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bramwell, George Walter, jun.

Bramwell, George Walter, jun., Bishop of the Plain City Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1883 to 1906, was born Dec. 19, 1853, in Portsmouth, England, the son of George Bramwell and Mary Ann Stevenson. He was baptized in 1864, emigrated to Utah in 1869, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1882, being released early on account of illness. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 22, 1883.
