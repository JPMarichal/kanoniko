Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Willard

Hansen, Willard, Bishop of the Huntington Park Ward, Los Angeles Stake, California, in 1930, was born Feb. 27, 1876, in Brigham City, Utah, the son of Willard S. Hansen and Maria Larson. He was baptized May 4, 1884, ordained a High Priest Nov. 16, 1905, by Frank Y. Taylor, and a Bishop in 1923. He acted as Bishop of the Long Beach Ward from 1923 to 1924.
