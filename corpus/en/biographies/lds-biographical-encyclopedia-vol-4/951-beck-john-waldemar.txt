Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Beck, John Waldemar

Beck, John Waldemar, president of the Samoan Mission from 1895 to 1896, was born Jan. 10, 1869, in Paris, Idaho, a son of Anders Beck and Sophia C. Hanson. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1876, and was set apart for his mission to Samoa Dec. 30, 1892. He acted as president of the mission from March 23, 1895, to April 19, 1896, and returned home May 12, 1896.
