Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Duchesne Stake

Akelund, Lester Eric

Akelund, Lester Eric, Bishop of the Mountain. Home Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1923, was born June 20, 1889, in Fremont, Utah, the son of Peter Isak Akelund and Minerva E. Zufelt. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1897, filled a mission to Samoa in 1910-1914, was ordained a High Priest Sept. 15, 1917, by Anthony W. Ivins, and a Bishop Sept. 26, 1920, by Orson F. Whitney.
