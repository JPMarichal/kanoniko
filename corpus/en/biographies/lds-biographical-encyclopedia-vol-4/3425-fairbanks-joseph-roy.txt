Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Fairbanks, Joseph Roy

Fairbanks, Joseph Roy, Bishop of the Driggs Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1913 to 1915, was born April 16, 1876, in Glenwood, Utah, a son of Cornelius M. Fairbanks and Emily A. Davis. He was ordained a Seventy Oct. 7, 1897, by B. H. Roberts, filled a mission to Colorado in 1897-1900, and was sustained as Bishop Nov. 16, 1913.
