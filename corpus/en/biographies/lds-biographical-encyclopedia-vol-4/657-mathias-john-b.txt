Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mathias, John B.

Mathias, John B., president pro tem. of the Australian Mission from June to November 1900, was born Oct. 29, 1875, in Nephi, Utah, a son of Jonah Mathias and Abigail Burbanks. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1884, was set apart for his mission to Australia May 12, 1899, departed from Australia Feb. 26, 1901, and returned to Utah March 2, 1901.
