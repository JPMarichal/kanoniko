Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Savage, Levi Mathers

Savage, Levi Mathers, Bishop of the Woodruff Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1891 to 1918, was born Jan. 11, 1851, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Levi Savage and Jane Mathers. He was baptized July 15, 1860, ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 23, 1878, by Erastus Snow, and set apart to preside over the Sunset Ward. He was set apart as Bishop of the Woodruff Ward Oct. 19, 1891, by John Henry Smith. Bishop Savage died March 15, 1935.
