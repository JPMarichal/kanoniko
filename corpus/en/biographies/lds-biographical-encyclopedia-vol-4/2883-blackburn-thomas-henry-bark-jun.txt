Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Blackburn, Thomas Henry Bark, jun.

Blackburn, Thomas Henry Bark, jun., Bishop of the Yost Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1903 to 1907, was born Aug. 8, 1877, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Thomas H. Blackburn and Roxy L. Biglet. He was baptized Aug. 8, 1886, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1896-1898, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop in October, 1903, by Mathias F. Cowley.
