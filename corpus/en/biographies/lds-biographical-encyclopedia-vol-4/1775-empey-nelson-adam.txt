Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Empey, Nelson Adam

Empey, Nelson Adam, Bishop of the 13th Ward, Ensign Stake, Utah, from 1890 to 1904, was born May 7, 1837, in Preston, Ontario, Canada, a son of William Empey and Mary Ann Morgan. He was baptized when about eight years old by his father in Nauvoo, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1877-1878, and was ordained a High Priest Dec. 31, 1881. On April 8, 1891, he was ordained a Bishop by George Q. Cannon. He died Sept. 4, 1904.
