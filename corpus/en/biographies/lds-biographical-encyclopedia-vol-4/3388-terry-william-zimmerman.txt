Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Terry, William Zimmerman

Terry, William Zimmerman, Bishop of the Coalville Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1912, was born March 28, 1872, in Lewiston, Utah, a son of William A. Terry and Susan Zimmerman. He was baptized March 28, 1880, ordained a Seventy Aug. 15, 1896, by Chas. W. Penrose and filled a mission to Switzerland and Germany in 1896-1898. He was sustained as a Bishop Feb. 7, 1909, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 24, 1909, by Heber J. Grant.
