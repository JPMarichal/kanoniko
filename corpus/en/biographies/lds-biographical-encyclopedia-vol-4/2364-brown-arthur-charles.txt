Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brown, Arthur Charles

Brown, Arthur Charles, Bishop of the Scipio Ward, Millard Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1928, was born July 27, 1899, at Scipio, Utah, a son of Jorgen P. Brown and Anne Peterson. He was baptized Aug. 24, 1907, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Central States in 1919-1922, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 8, 1925, by Richard R. Lyreart,
