Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jacob, Elmer A.

Jacob, Elmer A., Bishop of the Lynndyl Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1919, was born Nov. 25, 1883, in Daniels, Utah, the son of Joseph Jacob and Ellen Gerber. He was baptized in 1892, ordained a High Priest June 27, 1915, by Hyrum G. Smith, and a Bishop June 23, 1918, by Anthon H. Lund.
