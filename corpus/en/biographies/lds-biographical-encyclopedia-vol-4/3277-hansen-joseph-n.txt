Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hansen, Joseph N.

Hansen, Joseph N., Bishop of the Ephraim North Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Dec. 29, 1894, at Ephraim, Utah, a son of Anders Nikolai Hansen and Jensine Christine Petersen. He was baptized Nov. 12, 1883, ordained a High Priest Sept. 26, 1914, by Joseph Y. Jensen, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 21, 1926, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
