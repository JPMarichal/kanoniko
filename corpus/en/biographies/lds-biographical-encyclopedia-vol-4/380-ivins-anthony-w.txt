Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ivins, Anthony W.

Ivins, Anthony W., a member of the General Board of Y. M. M. I. A. from 1909 to 1929, and general superintendent from 1919 to. 1921, died in Salt Lake City, Sept. 27, 1934. (See Bio. Ency. Vol. 1, p. 311, and Vol. 3, p. 750.)
