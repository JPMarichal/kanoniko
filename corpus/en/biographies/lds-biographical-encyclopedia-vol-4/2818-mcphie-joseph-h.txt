Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcphie, Joseph H.

Mcphie, Joseph H., Bishop of the Salt Lake City 25th Ward, Pioneer Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born May 4, 1888, in Almy, Wyoming, a son of Martin C. McPhie and Sarah Cain. He was baptized July 20, 1896, ordaified a High Priest June 28, 1921, by Anthony W. Ivins, and a Bishop Feb. 14, 1926, by Geo. Albert Smith.
