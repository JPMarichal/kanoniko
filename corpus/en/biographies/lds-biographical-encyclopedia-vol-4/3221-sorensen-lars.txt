Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sorensen, Lars

Sorensen, Lars, Bishop of the Goshen Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1900 to 1906, was born April 24, 1856, in Holbak, Denmark, a son of Jens Sorensen and Johanna Johannesen. He was baptized April 24, 1870, emigrated to Utah in 1874, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 24, 1900, by Heber J. Grant. He died Jan. 29, 1926.
