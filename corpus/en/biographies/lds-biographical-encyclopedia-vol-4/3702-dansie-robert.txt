Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dansie, Robert

Dansie, Robert, Bishop of the Herriman Ward, West Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1887 to 1896, was born Feb. 5, 1825, in Boxford, Suffolk, England, a son of James Dansie and Sarah Chenery. He was baptized Oct. 8, 1849, emigrated to Utah in 1862, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1879, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 12, 1887, by Heber J. Grant. He died Nov. 10, 1929.
