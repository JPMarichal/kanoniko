Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gibson, Robert Orson

Gibson, Robert Orson, Bishop of the St. Thomas Ward, Moapa Stake, Nevada, from 1912 to 1930+, was born Dec. 19, 1880, in Duncan, Kane Co., Utah, a son of Moses W. Gibson and Lydia Ann Badger. He was baptized Dec. 30, 1888, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southwestern States in 1900-1903, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 9, 1912, by George F. Richards.
