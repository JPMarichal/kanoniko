Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Byington, Charles Norton

Byington, Charles Norton, Bishop of the Lava Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1914 to 1930+, was born March 29, 1868, in Calls Fort, Utah, a son of Joseph H. Byington and Hannah Molland. He was baptized June 10, 1877, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 18, 1914, by Orson F. Whitney.
