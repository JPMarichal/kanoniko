Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thomas, James Richard

Thomas, James Richard, Bishop of the Logan 11th Ward, Logan Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1928, was born Nov. 17, 1865, in Paradise, Utah, the son of Wm. Nash Thomas and Elizabeth Lallis. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1898-1900 as a Seventy, was ordained a High Priest March 6, 1920, by Geo. F. Richards, and a Bishop Jan 5, 1920, by Geo. F. Richards.
