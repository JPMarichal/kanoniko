Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Green, Peter Bertelsen

Green, Peter Bertelsen, Bishop of the Lewisville Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1913 to 1923, was born Feb. 4, 1864, at Plain City, Weber Co., Utah, a son of Peter C. Green and Elsie M. Bertelsen. He was baptized in 1872, ordained a Seventy by Brigham H. Roberts, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1891-1893, was ordained a High Priest March 17, 1906, by Lewis W. Shurtliff, and ordained a Bishop June 1, 1913, by Anthony W. Ivins.
