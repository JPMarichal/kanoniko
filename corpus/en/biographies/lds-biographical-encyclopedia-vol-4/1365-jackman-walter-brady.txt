Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jackman, Walter Brady

Jackman, Walter Brady, Bishop of the Rose Ward, Blackfeet Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born April 20, 1878, in Juab Co., Utah, the son of Sylvester Martin Jackman and Martha Catherine Gardner. He was baptized Aug. 4, 1892, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 15, 1928, by Rudger Clawson.
