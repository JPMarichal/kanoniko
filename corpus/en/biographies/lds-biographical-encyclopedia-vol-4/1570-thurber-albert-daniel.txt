Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Thurber, Albert Daniel

Thurber, Albert Daniel, Bishop of the Oakley 1st Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1916 to 1921, was born May 13, 1854, in Palmyra, Utah. He was set apart as Bishop July 30, 1916, by David O. McKay, having previously acted as a Bishop in Mexico. He died Jan. 22, 1931.
