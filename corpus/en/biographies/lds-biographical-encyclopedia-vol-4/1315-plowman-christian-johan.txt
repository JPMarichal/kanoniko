Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Plowman, Christian Johan

Plowman, Christian Johan, Bishop of the Smithfield 1st Ward, Benson Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1929, was born Oct. 4, 1862, in Bedsted, Thisted amt, Denmark, the son of Jorgen J. Plowman and Mette Marie Lauritsen. He emigrated with his parents to Utah in 1866, and located in Mantua; later he moved to Smithfield (1870). He filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1887-1889, and was ordained a Bishop Oct. 26, 1917, by Rudger Clawson.
