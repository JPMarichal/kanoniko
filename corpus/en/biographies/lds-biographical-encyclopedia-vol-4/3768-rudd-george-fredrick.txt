Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rudd, George Fredrick

Rudd, George Fredrick, Bishop of the Parker Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Fremont Co., Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born April 17, 1888, at Parker, Idaho, a son of Joseph S. Rudd and Viiate E. Rudd. He was baptized July 2, 1896, ordained a High Priest Oct. 31, 1920, by George F. Richards, and ordained a Bishop July 19, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
