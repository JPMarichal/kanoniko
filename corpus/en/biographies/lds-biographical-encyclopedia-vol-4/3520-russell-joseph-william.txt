Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Russell, Joseph William

Russell, Joseph William, Bishop of the Clover Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1913, was born April 8, 1887, in St. John, Tooele Co., Utah, a son of Joseph S. Russell and Margaret Charles. He was baptized Aug. 23, 1896, ordained a High Priest Oct. 30, 1910, by Charles R. McBride, and sustained as Bishop March 24, 1912.
