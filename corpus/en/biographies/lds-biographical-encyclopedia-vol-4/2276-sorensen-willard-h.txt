Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sorensen, Willard H.

Sorensen, Willard H., Bishop of the Moore Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1920, was born March 19, 1889, in Trout Creek, Idaho, the son of John P. Sorensen and Sena C. Mickelsen. He was baptized July 31, 1897, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 18, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard.
