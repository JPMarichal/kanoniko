Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Skinner, Clawson Nephi

Skinner, Clawson Nephi, Bishop of the Boyle Heights Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1923 to 1928, was born May 21, 1899, in Nounan, Bear Lake Co., Idaho, the son of Nephi A. Skinner and Etta Mae Morgan. He was baptized Oct. 4, 1907, moved to California in 1923, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 11, 1923, by Jos, F. Smith, jun.
