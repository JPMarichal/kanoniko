Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barney, Walter

Barney, Walter, a member of the Mormon Battalion, Company C, was born Jan. 7, 1819, in Ohio, a son of Charles Barney and Mercy Yoeman. He arrived in Utah in 1847. Later he was a pioneer of Sanpete and Sevier counties, Utah, and an Indian war veteran. He died April 15, 1917, at Monroe, Sevier Co., Utah.
