Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wells, Thomas

Wells, Thomas, Bishop of the Victor Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1918 to 1929, was born Sept. 6, 1860, in Mill Creek, Utah, the son of Samuel Wells and Sarah Ann Hattesley. He was baptized Oct. 11, 1868, filled a mission to the Western States in 1908-1909, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 12, 1909, by John H. Pace, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 10, 1918, by Rudger Clawson.
