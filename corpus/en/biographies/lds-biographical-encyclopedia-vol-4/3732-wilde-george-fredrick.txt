Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilde, George Fredrick

Wilde, George Fredrick, Bishop of the Cumberland W a r d, Woodruff Stake, Wyo., from 1907 to 1928, was born Nov. 25, 1869, at Coalville, Utah, a son of Fredrick Wilde and Louise Farnsworth. He was baptized when nine years old, ordained a High Priest by Peter McKinnon, and a Bishop April 28, 1907, by Francis M. Lyman.
