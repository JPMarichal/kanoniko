Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hemsley, Richard, jun.

Hemsley, Richard, jun., Bishop of the PIano Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1905 to 1908, and from 1910 to 1914, was born Oct. 12, 1864, in Mill Creek, Utah, the son of Richard Hemsley and M. Christine Jensen. He was baptized April 1, 1886, filled a mission to Scandinavia in 1908-1910, was ordained a High Priest May 26, 1902, by Thomas E. Bassett, and set apart as Bishop Sept. 3, 1905.
