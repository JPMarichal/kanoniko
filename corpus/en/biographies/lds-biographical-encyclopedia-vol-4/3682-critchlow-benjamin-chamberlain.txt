Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Critchlow, Benjamin Chamberlain

Critchlow, Benjamin Chamberlain, Bishop of the Ogden 1st Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1878 to 1890, was born Dec. 20, 1835, in Leechburg, Penn., a son of William Critchlow and Harriet Hawkins. He was baptized in 1845, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 22, 1878, by Franklin D. Richards. He died at Hyrum, Utah, Aug. 31, 1924.
