Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Elliot, Robert Lester

Elliot, Robert Lester, Bishop of the Edgemont Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1929, was born Nov. 11, 1883, in Richfield, Sevier Co., Utah, a son of James W. Elliot and Sarah Fraser. He was baptized March 3, 1892, ordained a High Priest April 28, 1918, by Joseph William Knight, and was ordained a Bishop May 23, 1926, by George Albert Smith.
