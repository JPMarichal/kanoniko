Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wainwright, William

Wainwright, William, Bishop of the Springville 2nd Ward, KoIob Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1929, was born March 19, 1875, at Manchester, England, a son of Henry Wainwright and Lucy Hall. He was baptized April 11, 1903, emigrated to Utah in 1904, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 23, 1924, by Rudger Clawson.
