Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larsen, Lars Christian, jun.

Larsen, Lars Christian, jun., Bishop of the Glendale Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1895 to 1905, was born June 27, 1867, in Logan, Utah, a son of Lars C. Larsen and Clara C. Jensen. He was baptized Aug. 3, 1876, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 1, 1893, by Jos. S. Geddes, and ordained a Bishop June 9, 1895.
