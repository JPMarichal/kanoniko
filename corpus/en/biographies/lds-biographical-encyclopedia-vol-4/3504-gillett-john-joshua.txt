Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gillett, John Joshua

Gillett, John Joshua, Bishop of the Tooele North Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1918, was born January 24, 1873, at E T City, Tooele Co., Utah, a son of Samuel Gillett and Nioma Chapels. He was baptized Sept. 23, 1901, ordained a High Priest March 6, 1911, by Hugh S. Gowans, and a Bishop March 7, 1915, by George F. Richards.
