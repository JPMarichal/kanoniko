Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rasmussen, Arelius Peter

Rasmussen, Arelius Peter, Bishop of the Midvale 1st Ward, East Jordan Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Nov. 2, 1886, in Draper, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Peter Christian Rasmussen and Mette Marie Jensen. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1895, ordained a High Priest Nov. 30, 1915, by Francis M. Lyman, ordained Bishop of the Midvale Ward Nov. 8, 1927, by Stephen L. Richards, and set apart as Bishop of the Midvale First Ward July 15, 1928, by Orson F. Whitney.
