Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Reddish, Fredrick Herbert

Reddish, Fredrick Herbert, Bishop of the Lund Ward, Idaho Stake, Idaho, from 1900 to 1915, was born Dec. 28, 1866, in Old Whittington, England, a son of Henry Reddish and Eliza Hurst. He was baptized when about eight years old, emigrated to Utah in 1880, was ordained a High Priest Oct. 27, 1895, by Abraham H. Cannon, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 21, 1899, by Joseph F. Smith.
