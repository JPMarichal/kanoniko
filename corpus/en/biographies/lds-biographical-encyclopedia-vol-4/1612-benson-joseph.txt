Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Benson, Joseph

Benson, Joseph, presiding Elder of the Summit Branch, Curlew Stake, Idaho, from 1913 to 1915, and Bishop of the Summit Ward from 1915 to 1924, was born July 13, 1866, at Logan, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Ezra T. Benson, and Mary Knight. He was baptized when about eight years old by Eli Bell, sen., ordained a Seventy and set apart for a mission to Germany by Seymour B. Young Sept. 30, 1902. He was ordained a High Priest by Hyrum G. Smith in May, 1916.
