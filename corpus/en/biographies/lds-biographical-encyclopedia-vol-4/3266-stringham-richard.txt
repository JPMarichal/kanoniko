Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stringham, Richard

Stringham, Richard, second counselor in the South Davis Stake presidency, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, and Bishop of the Bountiful 1st Ward from 1918 to 1924, was born June 7, 1870, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Briant Stringham and Martha Ashby. He was set apart as second counselor March 23, 1924, by Melvin J. Ballard.
