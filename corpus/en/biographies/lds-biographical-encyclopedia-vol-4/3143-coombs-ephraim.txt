Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Coombs, Ephraim

Coombs, Ephraim, Bishop of the Richfield Ward, San Luis Stake, Colo., from 1891 to 1905, was born Dec. 21, 1849, at Stafford, England, a son of George Coorobs and Eliza Astbury. He was baptized May 10, 1858, emigrated to Utah in 1864, was set apart as president of the Richfield B r a n c h April 22, 1888, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 13, 1891, by Francis M. Lyman. He died May 29, 1920.
