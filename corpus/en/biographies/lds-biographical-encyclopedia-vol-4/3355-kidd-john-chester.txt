Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kidd, John Chester

Kidd, John Chester, Bishop of the Upton Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1910 to 1915, was born Dec. 25, 1883, at Riverton, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of John B. Kidd and Annie Staley. He was baptized Aug. 15, 1893, filled a mission to Holland in 1907-1910, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 28, 1911, by Heber J. Grant.
