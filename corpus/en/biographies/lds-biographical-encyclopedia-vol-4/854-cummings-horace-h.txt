Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mexican Mission

Presidents: Daniel W. Jones, 1876-1877; Helaman Pratt, 1877-1879; Moses Thatcher, 1879-1880; James Z. Stewart pro tem., February-December, 1880; Moses Thatcher (second term), 1880-1881; August H. F. Wilcken, 1882-1883; Anthony W. Ivins, 1883-1884; Helaman Pratt (second term), 1884-1887; Horace H. Cummings, in 1887; Ammon M. Tenney, 1887-1889.

After the re-opening of the mission in 1901: Ammon M. Tenney (second term), 1901-1903; Hyrum S. Harris, 1903-1904; Talma E. Pomeroy, 1904-1905; Hyrum S. Harris (second term), 1905-1907; Rey L. Pratt, 1907-1930.

Cummings, Horace H.

Cummings, Horace H., president of the Mexican Mission in 1887. (See Bio. Ency. Vol. 1, p. 713.)
