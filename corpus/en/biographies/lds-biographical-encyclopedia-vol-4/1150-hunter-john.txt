Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hunter, John

Hunter, John, Bishop of the American Fork 3rd Ward, Alpine Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930, was born Oct. 22, 1866, in American Fork, the son of Ebenezer Hunter and Agnes Martin. He was baptized in 1875, ordained a Seventy and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1897-1899. He was ordained a Bishop July 22, 1928, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
