Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gwilliam, Franklin Stanford

Gwilliam, Franklin Stanford, presiding Elder of the Weiser Branch, Boise Stake Idaho, from 1915 to 1916, and Bishop of the Weiser Ward from 1916 to 1925, was born Nov. 26, 1888, at Hooper, Utah, the son of Benjamin T. Gwilliam and Amelia Stanford. He was baptized Aug. 14, 1897, ordained a High Priest July 19, 1914, by Rudger Clawson, and a Bishop June 25, 1916.
