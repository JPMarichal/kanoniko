Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cassia Stake

Adams, John Fredrick

Adams, John Fredrick, Bishop of the Oakley 1st Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1928, was born May 14, 1882. He was baptized Dec. 7, 1901, ordained a High Priest July 3, 1916, and a Bishop May 1, 1921, by Geo. F. Richards.
