Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Richins, Osborne

Richins, Osborne, Bishop of the Union Ward, Union Stake, Oregon, from 1914 to 1919, was born Nov. 5, 1877, at Mud Springs, Kane Co., Utah, a son of Wellington Richins and Alice Ida Bond. He was baptized Dec. 30, 1885, ordained a Seventy Dec. 9, 1897, by George Teasdale, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1897-1899, and a mission to the Central States in 1905-1907. He was ordained a High Priest April 27, 1912, by Franklin S. Bramwell, and a Bishop Sept. 6, 1914, by Francis M. Lyman.
