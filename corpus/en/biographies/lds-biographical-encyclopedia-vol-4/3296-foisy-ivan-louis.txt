Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Foisy, Ivan Louis

Foisy, Ivan Louis, Bishop of the Marysvale Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born Dec. 8, 1896, in Marysvale, Piute Co., Utah, a son of Edward Foisy and Catherine L. Beckstead. He was baptized Aug. 12, 1905, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1917-1919, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 20, 1921, by Melvin J. Ballard, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 18, 1923, by George Albert Smith.
