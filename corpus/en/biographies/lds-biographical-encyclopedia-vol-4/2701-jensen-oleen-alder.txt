Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Oleen Alder

Jensen, Oleen Alder, Bishop of the Glendale Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, in 1930+, was born Nov. 24, 1899, in Preston, Idaho, a son of Hyrum D. Jensen and Margaret Alder. He was baptized Nov. 24, 1907, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 10, 1930, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
