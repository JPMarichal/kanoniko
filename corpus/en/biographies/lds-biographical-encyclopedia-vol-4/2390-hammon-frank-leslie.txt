Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hammon, Frank Leslie

Hammon, Frank Leslie, Bishop of the Rupert 2nd Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Aug. 21, 1893, in South Hooper, Davis Co., Utah, a son of Levi Byrum Hammon and Martha Jane Belna. He was baptized June 6, 1902, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Australia in 1912-1914, was ordained a High Priest May 9, 1915, by Lewis W. Shurtliff, and sustained as Bishop April 13, 1924.
