Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Clawson, Edwin

Clawson, Edwin, Bishop of the Hyrum 2nd Ward, Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1922 to 1930+, was born Dec. 21, 1888, at Logan, Cache Co., Utah, the son of Carl John Clawson and Hedda Mathilda Anderson. He was baptized Sept. 2, 1899, ordained a Seventy Sept. 20, 1909, by Charles H. Hart, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1911-1913, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 22, 1922, by George Albert Smith.
