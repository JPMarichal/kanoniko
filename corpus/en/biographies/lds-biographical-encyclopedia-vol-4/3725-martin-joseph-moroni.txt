Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Martin, Joseph Moroni

Martin, Joseph Moroni, presiding Elder of the Hilliard Branch, Woodruff Stake, Wyo., from 1918 to 1930+, was born Jan. 22, 1881, in South Normanton, Derbyshire, England, a son of Joseph B. Martin and Ann West. He came to America May 17, 1882, was baptized Nov. 9, 1889, and ordained a High Priest May 24, 1929, by Samuel Rex.
