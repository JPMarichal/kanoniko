Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barnes, Herschel

Barnes, Herschel, president of the Jackson Branch, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1928 to 1930+, was born April 20, 1894, a son of Lorenzo D. Barnes and Algina Cable. He was baptized July 15, 1904, and sustained as presiding Elder April 29, 1928.
