Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tew, Warren S.

Tew, Warren S., Bishop of the Ballard Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1923, was born Jan. 28, 1891, at Mapleton, Utah, the son of William T. Tew and Clara Eliza Snow. He was baptized Aug. 27, 1899, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 18, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard.
