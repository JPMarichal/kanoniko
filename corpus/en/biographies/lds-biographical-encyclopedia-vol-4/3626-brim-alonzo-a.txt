Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wasatch Stake

Brim, Alonzo A.

Brim, Alonzo A., Bishop of the Center Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1898 to 1900, was born March 10, 1853, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Alfred W. Brim and Mary Ann Merrill. He filled a mission to the Sandwich Islands in 1883-1886, was ordained a High Priest May 5, 1894, by Francis M. Lyman, and ordained as Bishop of the Center Ward Nov. 12, 1898, by the same Apostle. He died Jan. 4, 1925.
