Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dunbar, William C.

Dunbar, William C., president of the French Mission from 1854 to 1856, was born Oct. 26, 1822, in Inverness, Scotland, a son of James and Ann Dunbar. He was baptized Dec. 28, 1840, and labored in England as a missionary in 1846-1852. He emigrated to Utah in 1852. He was ordained a Seventy and called to England to fill a mission, was appointed president of the French Mission Dec. 2, 1854, and was released Dec. 8, 1855. Bro. Dunbar was a famous comedian, vocalist, and bagpipe performer of the early days of Salt Lake City; he was also one of the founders of the "Salt Lake Herald." Elder Dunbar died in Salt Lake City June 8, 1905.
