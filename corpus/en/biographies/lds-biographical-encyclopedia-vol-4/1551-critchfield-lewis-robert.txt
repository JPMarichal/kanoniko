Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Critchfield, Lewis Robert

Critchfield, Lewis Robert, Bishop of the Oakley 3rd Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was bern April 29, 1894, in Oakley, Idaho, the son of Lewis A. Critchfield and Jane Wilson. He was baptized June 6, 1903, filled a mission to the Central States in 1915-1919, was ordained a High Priest May 25, 1919, by Pres. Wm. T. Jack, and a Bishop Jan. 13, 1929, by Melvin J. Ballard.
