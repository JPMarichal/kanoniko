Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Tyler, Daniel

Tyler, Daniel, president of the Swiss and Italian Mission from 1854 to 1856, was born Nov. 23, 1816, in Semproneous, Cayuga Co., New York, the son of Andrew Tyler and Elizabeth Comins. He was baptized Jan. 16, 1833, ordained a High Priest Sept. 24, 1844, and sent on a mission; ordained a Patriarch Dec. 10, 1873, by George A. Smith in Beaver, Utah. Daniel Tyler was a member of the famous Mormon Battalion. He died Nov. 7, 1906, in Beaver, Utah.
