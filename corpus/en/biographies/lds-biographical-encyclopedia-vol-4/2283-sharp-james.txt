Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sharp, James

Sharp, James, Bishop of the Millburne Ward, Lyman Stake, Wyoming, from 1928 to 1930+, was born June 28, !872, in Heber, Utah, a son of Jonathan Sharp and Annie Jowett. He was baptized Dec. 18, 1892, was ordained a High Priest May 16, 1915, by Thomas Tingey, and a Bishop Jan. 20, 1929, by Orson F. Whitney.
