Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Boggs, Francis

Boggs, Francis, one of the original pioneers of Utah, was born May 17, 1807 in Belmont, Ohio, a son of Alexander Boggs and Hannah Martin. He was baptized May 17, 1841, by John Cairn and took an active part in building up Nauvoo, Ill. He resided in Salt Lake City for some time and served one term in the Utah territorial legislature. He was a pioneer of southern Utah and lived at Washington, Utah, where he died Jan. 22, 1889. His wife, Eveline Martin, bore him eleven children, the family doing much to forward a number of pioneer enterprises in Iron and Washington counties, Utah. (Bio. Ency., Vol. 3, p. 322.)
