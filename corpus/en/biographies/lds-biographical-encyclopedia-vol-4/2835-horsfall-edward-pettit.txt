Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Horsfall, Edward Pettit

Horsfall, Edward Pettit, Bishop of the Pocatello 3rd Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1923 to 19307, was born Jan. 31, 1889, in Oceanside, Nassau Co., New York, the son of Charles George Horsfall and Mary Brown Pettit. He was baptized May 1, 1897, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 24, 1923, by Richard R. Lyman.
