Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sessions, J. Wyley

Sessions, J. Wyley, president of the South African Mission from 1921 to 1926, was born Dec. 11, 1885, in Marion, Cassia Co., Idaho, the son of Harvey Sessions and Alice Bryson. He was baptized in July, 1894, was set apart Nov. 9, 1920, for a mission to South Africa, and set apart as president of the South African Mission in London, England, in January, 1921, by Apostle George Albert Smith.
