Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

White, Samuel Orson, jun.

White, Samuel Orson, jun., Bishop of the Beaver West Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1905 to 1908, was born April 6, 1877, in Beaver, Utah, the son of Samuel Orson White and Mary Ellen Woodhouse. He was baptized April 6, 1885, by his father, filled a mission to the Southern and Northern States in 1898-1901, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 11, 1903, by Geo. Teasdale, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 22, 1905, by Francis M. Lyman.
