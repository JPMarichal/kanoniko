Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Iverson, Orson Alphonzo

Iverson, Orson Alphonzo, Bishop of the Bear River City Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1928, was born Nov. 13, 1886, in Bear River City, the son of Niels Iverson and Marie Bentine Wilson. He was baptized Nov. 27, 1894, ordained an Elder Jan. 3, 1908, filled a mission to the North Central States in 1909-1912, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 1, 1912, by David O. McKay, and ordained a Bishop April 4, 1926, by Richard R. Lyman.
