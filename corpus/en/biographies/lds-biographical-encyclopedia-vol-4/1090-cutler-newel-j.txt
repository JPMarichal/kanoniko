Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cutler, Newel J.

Cutler, Newel J., president of the Tongan Mission from 1929 to 1933, was born Jan. 12, 1893, in Glenwood, Sevier Co., Utah, a son of Jonathan C. Cutler and Rebecca Nelsen. He was baptized May 19, 1901, filled a mission to Tonga in 1913-1916, and was set apart for another mission to Tonga Aug. 16, 1929, to act as president.
