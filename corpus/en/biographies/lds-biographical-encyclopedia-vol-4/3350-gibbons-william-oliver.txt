Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gibbons, William Oliver

Gibbons, William Oliver, Bishop of the Rockport Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1930+, was born Nov. 6, 1870, in Rockport, a son of Thos. Gibbons and Sarah Elizabeth Lack. He was baptized Nov. 2, 1879, ordained an Elder Nov. 17, 1895, by Daniel Lewis, and filled a mission to the Central States in 1900-1902. He was ordained a High Priest June 22, 1902, by Moses W. Taylor, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 28, 1921, by Geo. F. Richards.
