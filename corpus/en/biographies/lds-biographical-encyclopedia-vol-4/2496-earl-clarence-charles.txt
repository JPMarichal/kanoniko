Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Earl, Clarence Charles

Earl, Clarence Charles, Bishop of the South Weber Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1930+, was born Jan. 4, 1884, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Joseph Wright Earl and Elizabeth Poll. He was baptized July 6, 1893, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 18, 1915, by Francis M. Lyman.
