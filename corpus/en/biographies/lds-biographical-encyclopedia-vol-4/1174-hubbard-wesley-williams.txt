Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hubbard, Wesley Williams

Hubbard, Wesley Williams, Bishop of the Williams Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, since 1929, was born May 2, 1902, in Logan, Utah, the son of Alma Edward Hubbard and Manna Williams. He was baptized May 2, 1910, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 27, 1929, by David O. McKay. He filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1924-1926.
