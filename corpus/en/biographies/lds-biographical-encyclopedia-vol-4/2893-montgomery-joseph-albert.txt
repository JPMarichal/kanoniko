Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Montgomery, Joseph Albert

Montgomery, Joseph Albert, Bishop of the Yost Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1907 to 1918, was born July 15, 1870, at North Ogden, Utah, a son of Robert Montgomery and Annie Chadwick. He was baptized Nov. 26, 1891, ordained a High Priest in October, 1903, and ordained a Bishop July 27, 1907, by Geo. Albert Smith.
