Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Livingston, Abram

Livingston, Abram, second counselor in the Moroni Stake presidency, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Aug. 2, 1887, in Fountain Green, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of William Livingston and Lillias Dick. He was ordained a High Priest April 4, 1927, by Richard R. Lyman and set apart as second counselor June 16, 1929, by Rudger Clawson.
