Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sutton, David J.

Sutton, David J., Bishop of the Pocatello 3rd Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1923, was born June 3, 1874, in Paris, Idaho, a son of John A. Sutton and Margaret Shepherd. He was baptized June 4, 1882, ordained a High Priest Oct. 1, 1910, and a Bishop Dec. 5, 1915, by Francis M. Lyman.
