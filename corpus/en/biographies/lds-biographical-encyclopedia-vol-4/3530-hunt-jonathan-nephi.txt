Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hunt, Jonathan Nephi

Hunt, Jonathan Nephi, Bishop of the Buhl Ward, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born July 11, 1877, in Hebron, Utah, a son of Jonathan Hunt and Susan C. Nanny. He was baptized in 1885., ordained a Seventy in May, 1903, filled a mission to the Central States in 1903-1905 and was ordained a Bishop Oct. 27, 1929, by Stephen L. Richards.
