Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lowe, Alexander H.

Lowe, Alexander H., Bishop of the Windsor (Lindon 2nd) Ward, Timpanogos Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Jan. 25, 1878, at Hooper, Weber Co., Utah, a son of Alexander Lowe and Elizabeth Adamson. He was baptized March 5, 1906, ordained an Elder March 4, 1907, by Joseph H. Storrs, filled a mission to England in 1907-1909, was ordained a High Priest June 23, 1920, by James H. Clark, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 31, 1924, by James E. Talmage.
