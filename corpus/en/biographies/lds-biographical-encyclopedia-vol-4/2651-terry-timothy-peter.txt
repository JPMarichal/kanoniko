Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Terry, Timothy Peter

Terry, Timothy Peter, Bishop of the Ogden 10th Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1925, was born Nov. 17, 1858, at Wilson, Utah, a son of Peter Hartswell Terry and Esther A. Hadlock. He was baptized July 2, 1868, and after being ordained a Seventy he filled a mission to Great Britain in 1893-1895. He was ordained a High Priest by Nils C. Flygare, and a Bishop March 30, 1913, by Francis M. Lyman.
