Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kunz, John, jun.

Kunz, John, jun., Bishop of the Bern Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1890 to 1915, was born Feb. 7, 1844, in Diemtigen, Canton Bern, Switzerland, the son of Johann Kunz and Rosina Knutti. He was baptized Nov. 15, 1868, came to Idaho in 1873, and filled a mission to Switzerland and Germany in 1884-1886. He was ordained a High Priest June 15, 1890, by Wm. Budge, and a Bishop Nov. 10, 1890, by John W. Taylor. He died Jan. 16, 1918.
