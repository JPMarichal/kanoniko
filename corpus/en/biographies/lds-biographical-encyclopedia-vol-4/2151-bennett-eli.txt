Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lehi Stake

Bennett, Eli

Bennett, Eli, Bishop of the Cedar Valley Ward, Lehi Stake, Utah, from 1882 to 1906, was born Nov. 26, 1831, in Bedford County, Tennessee, the son of Richard Bennett and Mary Bell. He arrived in Great Salt Lake Valley in 1852, was ordained a High Priest Jan. 11, 1877, by Abraham O. Smoot and ordained a Bishop in 1882 by Joseph F. Smith.
