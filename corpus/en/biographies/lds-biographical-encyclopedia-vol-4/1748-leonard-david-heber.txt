Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Leonard, David Heber

Leonard, David Heber, Bishop of the Huntington Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1916 to 1924, was born July 13, 1869, in St. John, Utah, the son of David Henry Leonard and Emma Eliza Child. He was baptized when eight years old, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1906-1907 (six months), and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 14, 1916, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
