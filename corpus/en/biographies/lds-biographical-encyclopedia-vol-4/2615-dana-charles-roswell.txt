Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dana, Charles Roswell

Dana, Charles Roswell, Bishop of the Ogden 16th Ward, North Weber Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1923, was born March 10, 1887, in Ogden, a son of Chauncey R. Dana and Mary Ellen Clark. He was baptized Aug. 27, 1905, ordained a High Priest Jan. 10, 1917, by Charles Wotherspoon and ordained a Bishop Oct. 14, 1917, by David O. McKay.
