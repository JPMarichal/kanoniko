Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Steed, Henry Lee

Steed, Henry Lee, president of the South African Mission from 1908 to 1909, was born July 11, 1862, in Farmington, Utah, the son of Henry Steed and Lydia Rebecca Reed. He was baptized when eight years old, and was set apart Jan. 30, 1908, for his mission by Pres. Joseph F. Smith.
