Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cook, Amos Roy

Cook, Amos Roy, Bishop of the West Point (South Hooper) Ward, North Davis Stake, Utah, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Aug. 17, 1885, at Bountiful, Utah, the son of David Cook and Hannah Holt. He was baptized Aug. 20, 1893, and after being ordained a Seventy he filled a mission to Great Britain in 1913-1914. He was ordained a High Priest May 25, 1919, by Francis H. Nalder, and a Bishop Nov. 13, 1927, by Hyrum G. Smith.
