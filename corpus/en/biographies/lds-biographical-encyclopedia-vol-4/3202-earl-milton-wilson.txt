Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Earl, Milton Wilson

Earl, Milton Wilson, Bishop of the Taylor Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1921 to 1926, was born Nov. 26, 1875, in Farmington, Utah, a son of Milton W. Earl and Sophrona Hess. He was baptized April 21, 1893, ordained a High Priest Aug. 17, 1919, by Joseph H. Dye, and a Bishop Nov. 20, 1921, by Richard R. Lyman.
