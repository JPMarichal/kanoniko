Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Webster, William Fredrick

Webster, William Fredrick, president of the Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Oct. 3, 1881, in Loa, Utah, a son of William M. Webster and Margaret Mathews. He was baptized July 2, 1892, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1912-1914, was ordained a High Priest May 14, 1916, by Francis M. Lyman, and acted as first counselor in the stake presidency from 1924 to 1926 (Aug. 8), when he was set apart as president by Richard R. Lyman.
