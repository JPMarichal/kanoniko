Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, Stanley F.

Taylor, Stanley F., a member of the Old Folks Central Committee from 1925 to 1931, was born Dec. 29, 1866, in Moroni, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Stanley Taylor and Hannah Howard. He died Feb. 20, 1931, in Salt Lake City, Utah.
