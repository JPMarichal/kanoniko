Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rowley, John Thomson

Rowley, John Thomson, Bishop of the Spring Glen Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1910, was born Nov. 7, 1847, in Glasgow, Scotland, the son of Ralph Nephi Rowley and Mary Ann Thomson. He emigrated to Utah in 1850, was baptized in 1855, ordained a High Priest Oct. 15, 1905, by John H. Pace, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 15, 1906, by Hyrum M. Smith. He died Jan. 31, 1925.
