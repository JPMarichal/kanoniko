Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Rees, David James

Rees, David James, Bishop of the Adamsville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1914, was born June 18, 1871, at Adamsville, the son of David D. Rees and Elizabeth Grimshaw. He was baptized June 15, 1880, by his father, and ordained a Bishop April 22, 1911, by Geo. F. Richards.
