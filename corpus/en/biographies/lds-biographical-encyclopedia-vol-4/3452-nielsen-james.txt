Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nielsen, James

Nielsen, James, Bishop of the Cache Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1917 to 1919, was born Dec. 9, 1870, in Weston, Idaho, a son of Thomas L. Nielsen and Andtea Jensen. He was baptized Dec. 9, 1878, ordained a Seventy July 22, 1891, by Seymour B. Young and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1897-1899. He was ordained a High Priest in 1902 by Abraham Owen Woodruff, and a Bishop May 1, 1917, by Anthony W. Ivins.
