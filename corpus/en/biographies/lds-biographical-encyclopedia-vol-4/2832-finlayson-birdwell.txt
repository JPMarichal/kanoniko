Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Finlayson, Birdwell

Finlayson, Birdwell, Bishop of the Pocatello 1st Ward, Pocatello Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 19307, was born Sept. 12, 1893, in Payson, Utah, a son of Fredrick James Finlayson and Josephine Hickman. He was baptized Jan. 5, 1902, ordained a High Priest Oct. 15, 1923, by William A. Hyde, and a Bishop May 23, 1926, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
