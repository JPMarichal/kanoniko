Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Decker, Zachariah B., jun.

Decker, Zachariah B., jun., Bishop of the Taylor Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1894 to 1904, was born March 11, 1850, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Zachariah B. Decker and Nancy Bean. He was baptized when eleven years old, called the Arizona Mission in 1881, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 5, 1894, by Brigham Young, jun. He died April 20, 1903.
