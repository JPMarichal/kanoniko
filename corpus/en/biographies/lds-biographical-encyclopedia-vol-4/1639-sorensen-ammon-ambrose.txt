Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sorensen, Ammon Ambrose

Sorensen, Ammon Ambrose, Bishop of the Summit Ward, Curlew Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930, was born Dec. 26, 1883, in Mendon, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Jacob F. Sorensen and. Susan Hancock. He was baptized Aug. 21, 1892, ordained a High Priest May 7, 1916, by Joseph J. Larkin, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 18, 1925, by Hyrum G. Smith.
