Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Hyrum Daniel

Jensen, Hyrum Daniel, president of the Norwegian Mission from 1929 to 1933, was born Feb. 22, 1868, in Franklin, Idaho, a son of David Jensen and Serena Peterson. He was baptized May 2, 1878, ordained a Seventy and set apart for a mission to Scandinavia Feb. 8, 1895, by B. H. Roberts; filled a second mission to Scandinavia in 1911-1913. April 2, 1929, he was set apart to preside over the Norwegian Mission and was honorably released Aug. 30, 1933. His wife, Margaret A. Jensen (born Aug. 10, 1864, at Kaysville, Utah, a daughter of Alfred Alder and Susan Field) accompanied Elder Jensen on his third mission as a missionary.
