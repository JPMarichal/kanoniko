Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jorgensen, Abraham

Jorgensen, Abraham, Bishop of the Logan 9th Ward, Cache Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1924, was born Jan. 9, 1859, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Hans Jorgensen and Dorthea Christiansen. He was ordained a High Priest Feb. 19, 1906, by Ed. W. Smith.
