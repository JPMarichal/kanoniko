Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Chamberlain, William H., jun.

Chamberlain, William H., jun., president of the Society Islands Mission from 1899 to 1900, was born Feb. 12, 1870, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Wm. H. Chamberlain and Frances H. Jones. He was baptized in 1879, set apart for a mission to the Islands June 23, 1897, and returned May 11, 1900.
