Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stoddard, William Henry

Stoddard, William Henry, Bishop of the Grant Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1917, was born Nov. 3, 1880, in Richmond, Utah, a son of Geo. A. Stoddard and Hannah E. Bowman. He was baptized July 3, 1890, ordained a High Priest March 22, 1908, and a Bishop Nov. 20, 1915, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
