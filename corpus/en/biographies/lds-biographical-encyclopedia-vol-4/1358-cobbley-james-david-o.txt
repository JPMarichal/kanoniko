Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cobbley, James David O.

Cobbley, James David O., Bishop of the Riverside Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1912 to 1915, was born July 14, 1874, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, the son Of James Cobbley and Emma Thorne. He was baptized July 2, 1882, set apart for a mission to California March 17, 1897, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 18, 1912, by Hyrum M. Smith.
