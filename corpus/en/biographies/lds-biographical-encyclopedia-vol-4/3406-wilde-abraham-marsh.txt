Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilde, Abraham Marsh

Wilde, Abraham Marsh, presiding Elder of the Welling Branch, Taylor Stake, Canada, from 1903 to 1908, was born May 13, 1857, in Bedford Leigh, Lancashire, England, the son of Isaac Wilde and Elizabeth Marsh. He was baptized July 20, 1865, emigrated to Utah in 1876, and was set apart as presiding Elder of the Welling Branch Feb. 18, 1903, by Heber S. Allen.
