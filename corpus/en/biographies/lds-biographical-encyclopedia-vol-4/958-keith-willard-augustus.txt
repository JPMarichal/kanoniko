Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Keith, Willard Augustus

Keith, Willard Augustus, president of the Samoan Mission from 1918 to 1920, was born July 26, 1886, in Havelock, Canada, a son of Asa H. Keith and Elminn Freeze. He was baptized March 6, 1914, and set apart Nov. 1, 1916, for a mission to Samoa.
