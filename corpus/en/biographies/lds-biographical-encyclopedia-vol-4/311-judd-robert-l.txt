Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Judd, Robert L.

Judd, Robert L., a member of the general board of Deseret Sunday School Union since 1920, a prominent jurist and general council for the Church, was born July 6, 1885, in Salt Lake City, Utah. (See Biographical Encyclopedia, Vol. 4, p. 100.)
