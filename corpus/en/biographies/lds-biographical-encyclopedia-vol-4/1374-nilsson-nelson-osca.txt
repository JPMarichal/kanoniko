Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nilsson (Nelson), Osca

Nilsson (Nelson), Oscar Edwin, Bishop of the Sterling Ward, Blackfoot Stake, Idaho, from 1912 to 1913, was born in Salt Lake City, Utah, Jan. 28, 1871, the son of Olaus F. Nilsson and Anna Erickson. He was baptized Sept. 4, 1904, was ordained a High Priest July 14, 1907, by Rudger Clawson, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 6, 1910, to preside over the Tilden Ward, which in 1912 became the Sterling Ward.
