Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wright, Amos Russell

Wright, Amos Russell, Bishop of the Bennington Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1881 to 1911, was born Jan. 11, 1840, at Exeter, Scott Co., Ill., the son of Jonathan E. Wright and Rebecca Wheeler. He was baptized when eight years old, came to Utah in 1850 and to Idaho in 1864, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 6, 1881, by Pres. John Taylor. He died Feb. 24, 1915, in Bennington, Idaho.
