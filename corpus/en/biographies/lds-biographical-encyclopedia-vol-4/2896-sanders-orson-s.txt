Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sanders, Orson S.

Sanders, Orson S., Bishop of the Malta Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Sept. 10, 1886, in South Cottonwood, Salt Lake Co., Utah, the son of Orson Sanders and Rosa Shepherd. He was baptized Feb. 6, 1897, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 6, 1926, by Stephen L. Richards.
