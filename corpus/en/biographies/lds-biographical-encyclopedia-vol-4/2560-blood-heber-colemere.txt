Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Blood, Heber Colemere

Blood, Heber Colemere, Bishop of the Clearfield Ward, North Davis Stake, Utah, from 1915 to 1917, was born Oct. 20, 1873, in Kaysville, Utah, the son of William Blood and Sarah J. Colemere. He was baptized May 21, 1882, ordained a Seventy Dec. 6, 1895, by Brigham H. Roberts, and filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1898. He was ordained a High Priest Nov. 27, 1910, by Joseph H. Grant, and a Bishop Aug. 5, 1915, by Francis M. Lyman.
