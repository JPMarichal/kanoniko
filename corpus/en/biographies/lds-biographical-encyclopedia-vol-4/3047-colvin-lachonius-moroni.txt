Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Colvin, Lachonius Moroni

Colvin, Lachonius Moroni, presiding Elder of the Eden Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1893 to 1898, was born Aug. 16, 1846, in Pottawattamie Co., Iowa, the son of David S. Colvin and Harriet A. Delabaugh. He was appointed presiding Elder pro tem. Sept. 8, 1893, by Christopher Layton.
