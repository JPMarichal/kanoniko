Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Winterton, Harold Vernon

Winterton, Harold Vernon, Bishop of the Woodland Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born July 16, 1903, in Charleston, Utah, the son of Hyrum S. Winterton and Sarah Van Wagoner. He was baptized July 16, 1911, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 6, 1930, by Orson F. Whitney.
