Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Varley, William, jun.

Varley, William, jun., Bishop of the Vineyard Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1920, was born Dec. 11, 1861, in Bountiful, Davis Co., Utah, a son of William Varley and Mary Ellen McDough. He was baptized March 25, 1877, ordained a High Priest Aug. 14, 1904, by David John, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 19, 1913, by George F. Richards.
