Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Christian Peter

Jensen, Christian Peter, Bishop of the Manassa Ward, San Luis Stake, Colo., from 1922 to 1925, was born Feb. 23, 1884, in Moroni, Utah, the son of Christen Jensen and Carrie Peterson. He was baptized July 7, 1892, filled a mission to the Central States in 1906-1908, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 15, 1914, by Geo. Albert Smith, and ordained a Bishop Dec. 9, 1922, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
