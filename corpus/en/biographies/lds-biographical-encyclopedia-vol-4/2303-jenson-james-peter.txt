Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jenson, James Peter

Jenson, James Peter, Bishop of the Malad Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1908 to 1912, and Bishop of the Malad First Ward, from 1912 to 1915, was born Oct. 12, 1875, in Newton, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Jens Peter and Mary Jenson. He was baptized Oct. 12, 1883, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 27, 1908, by Rudger Clawson.
