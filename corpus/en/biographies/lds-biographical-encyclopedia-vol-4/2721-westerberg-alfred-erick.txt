Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Westerberg, Alfred Erick

Westerberg, Alfred Erick, Bishop of the Glencoe Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born April 4, 1874, in Westervik, Sweden, a son of Erick E. Westerberg and Caroline Olson. He was baptized Jan. 2, 1887, emigrated to Utah that same year, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 18, 1928, by Taylor Nelson, and a Bishop Sept. 14, 1929, by Geo. F. Richards.
