Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Roskelley, Marriner W.

Roskelley, Marriner W., Bishop of the Alhambra Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Aug. 11, 1888, in Smithfield, Utah, the son of Samuel Roskelley and Sarah Maud Burton. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1896, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1911-1913, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Jan. 29, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
