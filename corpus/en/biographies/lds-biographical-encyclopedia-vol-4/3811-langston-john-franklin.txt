Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Langston, John Franklin

Langston, John Franklin, Bishop of the Rockville Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1900 to 1906, was born Sept. 15, 1852, in Pottawattamie County, Iowa, a son of John Langston and Clarinda Phillips. He came to Utah in 1854, was baptized when eight years old, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 25, 1900, by Daniel D. McArthur.
