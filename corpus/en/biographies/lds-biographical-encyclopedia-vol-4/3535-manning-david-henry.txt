Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Manning, David Henry

Manning, David Henry, Bishop of the Murtaugh Ward, Twin Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1920 to 1921, was born April 1, 1889, in Farmington, Utah, a son of David E. Manning and Margaret Wilcox. He was baptized July 4, 1897, ordained a High Priest Dec. 14, 1919, by Raymond McClellan, and a Bishop June 6, 1920, by Orson F. Whitney.
