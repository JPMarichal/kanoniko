Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hirschi, Claudius

Hirschi, Claudius, president of the Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Sept. 13, 1892, in Rockville, Utah, a son of David Hirschi and Mary M. Petty. He was baptized Sept. 13, 1900, ordained a High Priest June 15, 1924, by Geo. F. Richards, and was chosen as president of the Zion Park Stake Dec. 8, 1929.
