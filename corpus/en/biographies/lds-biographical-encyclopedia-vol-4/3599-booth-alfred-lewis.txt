Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Booth, Alfred Lewis

Booth, Alfred Lewis, Bishop of the Provo 4th Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1924, was born June 17, 1864, in Alpine, Utah, a son of Richard T. Booth and Elsie Edge. He was baptized Aug. 3, 1872, ordained a High Priest Dec. 9, 1900, and a Bishop Jan. 13, 1907, by Pres. Jos. F. Smith.
