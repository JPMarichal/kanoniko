Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Terry, Charles Alphonzo

Terry, Charles Alphonzo, Bishop of the St. George 3rd Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1878 to 1890, was born Nov. 10, 1821, in Richfield, N.Y., the son of Otis Terry and Cynthia Ruggles. He was baptized in 1843, came to Utah in 1850, was ordained a High Priest April 17, 1877, by Wilford Woodruff, and a Bishop June 16, 1878, by Erastus Snow. He died April 14, 1908.
