Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Garn, Micah A.

Garn, Micah A., Bishop of the Fielding Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Sept. 13, 1869, in Centerville, Davis Co., Utah, the son of Micah Garn and Fannie Wood. He was baptized in May, 1881, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1907-1909, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 24, 1930, by Rudger Clawson.
