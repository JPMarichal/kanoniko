Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Busenbark, Leland J.

Busenbark, Leland J., Bishop of the Torrey Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1929 to 1930+, was born May 10, 1898, in Fairview, Utah, a son of John Busenbark and Dora Dreyor. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1906, ordained a High Priest Aug. 21, 1927, by Stephen L. Richards, and a Bishop July 6, 1930, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
