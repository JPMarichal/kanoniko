Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Webb, John Edmund

Webb, John Edmund, Bishop of the Ioka Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Aug. 8, 1886, in Woodland, Summit Co.; Utah, a son of Ether Webb and Emma S. Jones. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1894, ordained a High Priest Aug. 27, 1911, and ordained a Bishop Nov. 4, 1928, by David O. McKay.
