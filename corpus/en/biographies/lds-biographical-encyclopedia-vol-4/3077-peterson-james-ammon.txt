Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, James Ammon

Peterson, James Ammon, Bishop of the Kimball Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Aug. 20, 1894, in Graham, Arizona, a son of Peter C. Peterson and Mary Anderson. He was baptized Feb. 1, 1903, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 26, 1928, by Melvin J. Ballard.
