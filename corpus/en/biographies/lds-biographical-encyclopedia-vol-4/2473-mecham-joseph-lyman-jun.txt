Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mecham, Joseph Lyman, jun.

Mecham, Joseph Lyman, jun., Bishop of the Milton Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from June 27 to December 19, 1926, was born May 26, 1883, in Milton, Morgan County, Utah, the son of Joseph Lyman Mecham and Annie Giles. He was baptized Aug. 8, 1891, ordained a High Priest and Bishop and set apart to preside over the Milton Ward June 27, 1926, by George Albert Smith.
