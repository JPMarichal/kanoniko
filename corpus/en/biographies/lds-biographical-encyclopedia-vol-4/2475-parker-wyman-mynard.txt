Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Parker, Wyman Mynard

Parker, Wyman Mynard, Bishop of the North Morgan Ward, Morgan Stake, Utah, from 1877 to 1881, was born April 2, 1828, in Franklin, Delaware Co., N.Y., a son of Wyman Parker and Maria Mynard. He was baptized July 4, 1850, emigrated to Utah in 1850, was ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1876-1878, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 1, 1877, by Franklin D. Richards. (See also Parker Ward, Yellowstone Stake, Idaho.)
