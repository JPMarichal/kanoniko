Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barker, Fred Ellis

Barker, Fred Ellis, president of the Australian Mission from 1898 to 1900, was born May 6, 1861, in Ogden, Utah, a son of Simon Barker and Jemima Newey. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1869, set apart for his mission to Australia Dec. 22, 1897, and returned April 28, 1902. He died Nov. 12, 1922, in Salt Lake City.
