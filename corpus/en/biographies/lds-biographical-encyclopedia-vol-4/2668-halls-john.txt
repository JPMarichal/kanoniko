Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Halls, John,

Halls, John, first counselor in the Ogden Stake presidency, Utah, from 1919 to 1930+, was born July 11, 1872, in Huntsville, Utah, the son of William Halls and Louisa C. Enderby. He was baptized May 10, 1881, was ordained a Bishop Oct. 28, 1908, by David O. McKay, and presided over Huntsville Ward from 1908 to 1916. He was set apart as second counselor in the Ogden Stake presidency in 1918 and was set apart as first counselor May 11, 1919, by David O. McKay.
