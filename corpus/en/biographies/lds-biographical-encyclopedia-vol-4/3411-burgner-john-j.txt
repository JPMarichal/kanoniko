Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burgner, John J.

Burgner, John J., Bishop of the Darby Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1927, was born Jan. 3, 1879, in Midway, Utah, a son of John Burgner and Mary Murr. He was baptized Aug. 19, 1888, and ordained a High Priest May 22, 1921, by Jos. F. Smith, jun. He died Jan. 15, 1927, at Idaho Falls, Idaho.
