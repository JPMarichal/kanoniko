Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hopkinson, Albert E.

Hopkinson, Albert E., Bishop of the Sunnyside Ward, Carbon Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Aug. 10, 1877, in Paplewick, Nottinghamshire, England, the son of Mathew Hopkinson and Margaret Boresford. He emigrated to Utah in 1900, was baptized Nov. 18, 1893, ordained a High Priest Jan. 28, 1916, and ordained a Bishop March 28, 1926, by Stephen L. Richards.
