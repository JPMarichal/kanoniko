Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burgess, Ernest Hungate

Burgess, Ernest Hungate, Bishop of the Roosevelt 1st Ward, Roosevelt Stake, Utah, from 1923 to 1929, was born Jan. 3, 1884, in Pine Valley, Washington Co., Utah, a son of George Martin Burgess and Rhoda Ann Dykes. He was baptized when eight years of age, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Germany in 1904-1907, was ordained a High Priest June 17, 1922, by George F. Richards, and ordained a Bishop June 17, 1923, by James E. Talmage.
