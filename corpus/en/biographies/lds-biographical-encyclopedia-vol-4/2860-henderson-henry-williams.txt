Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Henderson, Henry Williams

Henderson, Henry Williams, Bishop of the Garden Creek Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1911 to 1917, was born Aug. 20, 1875, in Logan, Utah, a son of James Henderson and Mary Williams. He was baptized July 1, 1884, ordained a Seventy Nov. 6, 1907, by Rudger Clawson, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1907-1909, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 16, 1911, by James E. Talmage.
