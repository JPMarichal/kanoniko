Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Benson, George Taft, jun.

Benson, George Taft, jun., first counselor in the Franklin Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930+, was born June 24, 1875, in Logan, Utah, the son of George Taft Benson and Louise Ballif. He was baptized June 24, 1883, was ordained a High Priest May 7, 1915, by Orrin P. Miller, and set apart Nov. 27, 1927, as counselor in the stake presidency. He died Aug. 13, 1934.
