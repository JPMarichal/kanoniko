Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Willard Gilbert

Smith, Willard Gilbert, Bishop of Leavitt Ward, Alberta Stake, Canada, from 1908 to 1921, was born Sept. 8, 1870, in Littleton, Morgan Co., Utah, the son of Willard G. Smith and Cordella Thurston. He was baptized Sept. 8, 1878, by Lyman Mecham, and ordained a High Priest by Willard G. Smith, sen.
