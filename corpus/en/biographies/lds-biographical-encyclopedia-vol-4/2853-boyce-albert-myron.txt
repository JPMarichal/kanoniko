Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Boyce, Albert Myron

Boyce, Albert Myron, Bishop of the McCammon Ward, Portneuf Stake, Idaho, from 1902 to 1909, was born March 16, 1854, in Spanish Fork, Utah, a son of John Boyce and Mary. A. Barzee. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 8, 1902, by Mathias F. Cowley.
