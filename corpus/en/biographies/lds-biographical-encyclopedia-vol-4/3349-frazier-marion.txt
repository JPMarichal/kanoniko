Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Frazier, Marion

Frazier, Marion, Bishop of the Oakley Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1894 to 1900, was born June 20, 1856, in Fort Supply, Wyoming, a son of Thomas L. Frazier and Rachel M. Young. He was baptized in 1867, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 5, 1894, by Brigham Young, jun.
