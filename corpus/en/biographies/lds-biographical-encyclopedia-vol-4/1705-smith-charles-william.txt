Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Charles William

Smith, Charles William, Bishop of the Midview Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Feb. 9, 1870, in Midway, Utah, the son of Benjamin Mark Smith and Elizabeth Agnes Wood. He was baptized March 30, 1879, was ordained a High Priest by James E. Talmage, and a Bishop Jan. 5, 1930.
