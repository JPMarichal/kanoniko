Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ward, Asael E.

Ward, Asael E., Bishop of the Almo Ward, Raft River Stake, Cassia Co., Idaho, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Oct. 12, 1890, at Almo, Idaho, the son of George A. Ward and Mary Ellen Brewerton. He was baptized March 1, 1899, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 5, 1926, by George F. Richards.
