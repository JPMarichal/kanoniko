Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Heywood, Joseph Neal

Heywood, Joseph Neal, Bishop of the Alpine Ward, St. Johns Stake, Arizona, from 1891 to 1896, was born Nov. 18, 1851, in Nephi, Utah, a son of Jos. L. Heywood and Martha Spence. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained an Elder July 17, 1875, by Wm. J. Smith, filled a mission to New Zealand in 1888-1891, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 5, 1891, by David K. Udall.
