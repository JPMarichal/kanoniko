Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Kooyman, Frank I.

Kooyman, Frank I., president of the Netherlands Mission from 1929 to 1933, is the author of "De Profeet der Mormonen, een eerlijk Waarheidsgetuige" (The Mormon Prophet an Honest Witness of Truth) and other writings in the Dutch or Netherlands language. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 4, p. 101.)
