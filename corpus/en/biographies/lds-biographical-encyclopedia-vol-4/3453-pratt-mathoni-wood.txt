Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pratt, Mathoni Wood

Pratt, Mathoni Wood, Bishop of the Pratt Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1889 to 1897, was born July 6, 1856, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Parley P. Pratt and Mary Wood. He was baptized Oct. 5, 1864, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 22, 1889, by Francis M. Lyman.
