Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Easton, Robert

Easton, Robert, Bishop of the Greenville Ward, Beaver Stake, Utah, from 1872 to 1880, was born July 25, 1822, at Cardrie, Scotland, and ordained a Bishop March 24, 1872, by Wm. Fotheringham. He died May 25, 1887, at Greenville, Utah.
