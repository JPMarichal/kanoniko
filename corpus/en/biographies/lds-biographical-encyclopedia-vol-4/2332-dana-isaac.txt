Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dana, Isaac

Dana, Isaac, Bishop of the Mesa 1st Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1912 to 1930+, was born June 22, 1866, in Montpelier, Bear Lake Co., Idaho, a son of Charles Carl Dana and Hannah Ellen Odekirk. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1893-1895, was ordained a High Priest March 8, 1902, and ordained a Bishop Sept. 8, 1912, by James E. Talmage.
