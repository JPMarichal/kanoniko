Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Johanneson, Jon

Johanneson, Jon, president of the Icelandic Mission from to 1903, was born June 2, 1862, at Hrutafirdi, Hunavatus, Iceland, the son of Johannes Johnson and Sophia Gunhaugson. He was baptized May 13, 1894, emigrated to Utah and made his home for many years in Cleveland, Emery Co., Utah. To fill a mission in Iceland, he left Salt Lake City, Aug. 18, 1900, and arrived in Reykjavik Sept. 28, 1900. During his missionary labors he baptized ten new members, ordained three and blessed thirteen children. He also aided five to emigrate to America. At the close of his mission, there was a membership in Iceland of thirty-nine souls. While in Iceland, Elder Johanneson, besides his other duties, composed and published a number of tracts. Elder Johanneson died in Salt Lake City Feb. 17, 1930.
