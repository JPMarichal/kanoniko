Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bean, George Washington

Bean, George Washington, first counselor in the presidency of the Sevier Stake, Sevier Co., Utah, from 1888 to 1894, was born April 1, 1831, in Centre Township, Adams Co., Illinois, the son of James Bean and Elizabeth Lewis. He was baptized July 12, 1841, ordained a Seventy in Nauvoo, Illinois, in November, 1845, filled a mission to the Lamanites in New Mexico in 1855-1856, was ordained a High Priest in 1877, and sustained as first counselor in the Sevier Stake presidency May 27, 1888. He was set apart to that position by John W. Taylor.
