Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hazen, Samuel B.

Hazen, Samuel B., Bishop of the Center Ward, Salt Lake Stake, Utah, in 1916, was born Jan. 1, 1867, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Robert Hazen and Mary A. Bainbridge. He was baptized March 1, 1881, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission in Indian Territory in 1906-1908, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 2, 1916, by Pres. Joseph F. Smith.
