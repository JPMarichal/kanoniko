Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pratt, Jonathan Burgess

Pratt, Jonathan Burgess, Bishop of the Hinckley Ward, Deseret Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1920, was born June 28, 1869, in Duncan's Retreat, Washington Co., Utah, the son of Wm. H. Pratt and Ann Burgess. He was ordained a High Priest and. Bishop Dec. 22, 1912, by Heber J. Grant.
