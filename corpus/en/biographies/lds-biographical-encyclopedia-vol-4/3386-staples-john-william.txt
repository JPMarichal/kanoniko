Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Staples, John William

Staples, John William, Bishop of the Cluff Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1912 to 1930+, was born March 26, 1880, in Coalville, Utah, the son of James Brigham Staples and Sarah Ellen Ray. He was baptized Aug. 3, 1888, ordained an Elder April 2, 1905, by Wm. G. Johnston, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1905-1907, and was ordained a High Priest May 2, 1909, by David O. McKay, and a Bishop Nov. 3, 1912, by Francis M. Lyman.
