Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

St. Joseph Stake

Allred, Edsil Myron

Allred, Edsil Myron, Bishop of the Central Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1909 to 1926, was born Jan. 6, 1860, in Spring City, Utah, a son of Joseph A. Allred and Rhoda Palmer. He was baptized in 1868, and ordained a Bishop June 6, 1909, by John Henry Smith.
