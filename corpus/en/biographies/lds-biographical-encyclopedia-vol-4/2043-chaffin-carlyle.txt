Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Chaffin, Carlyle

Chaffin, Carlyle, Bishop of the Lincoln Ward, Idaho Falls Stake, Idaho, from 1926 to 1930, was born May 26, 1894, in Cedar City, Utah, a son of John R. Chaffin and Ellen J. Murrie. He was baptized May 26, 1902, ordained an Elder April 12, 1914, commenced a mission to Holland (serving five months), was transferred when the World war broke out and finished his mission term in the Eastern States in 1914-1916. He was ordained a High Priest Nov. 26, 1922, by Heber C. Austin, and a Bishop May 16, 1926, by Orson F. Whitney.
