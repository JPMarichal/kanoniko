Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olsen, Jesse Jensen

Olsen, Jesse Jensen, Bishop of the Hazelton Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born April 13, 1883, in Paradise, Cache Co., Utah, a son of Gideon E. Olsen and Caroline Jensen. He was baptized May 6, 1891, ordained a High Priest in 1921 by Joseph P. Payne, and ordained a Bishop March 16, 1930, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
