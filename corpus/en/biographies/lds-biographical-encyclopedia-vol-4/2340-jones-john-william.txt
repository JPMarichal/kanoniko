Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jones, John William

Jones, John William, Bishop of the Lehi Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1919 to 1930+, was born Feb. 2, 1878, in Adamsville, Beaver Co., Utah, a son of Thomas E. and Lucretia Jones. He was baptized Feb. 2, 1886, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1906-1908, and was sustained as Bishop Aug. 2, 1919.
