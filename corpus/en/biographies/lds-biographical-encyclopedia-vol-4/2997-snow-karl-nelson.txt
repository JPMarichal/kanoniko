Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Snow, Karl Nelson

Snow, Karl Nelson, Bishop of the St. George East Ward, St. George Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930, was born Jan. 17, 1894, in St. George, a son of Edward H. Snow and Hannah Nelson. He was baptized Feb. 11, 1902, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 20, 1925, by Rudger Clawson.
