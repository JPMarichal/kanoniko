Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Madsen, Spencer

Madsen, Spencer, Bishop of the Lake View Ward, Sharon Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Nov. 4, 1895, in Lake View, Utah, a son of Peter Madsen, jun., and Bertha Knudsen. He was baptized July 31, 1904, ordained a High Priest July 30, 1921, by George F. Richards, and ordained a Bishop June 3, 1928, by Richard R. Lyman.
