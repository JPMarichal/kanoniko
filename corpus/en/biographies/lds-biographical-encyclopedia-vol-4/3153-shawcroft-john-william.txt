Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shawcroft, John William

Shawcroft, John William, Bishop of the Richfield Ward, San Luis Stake, Colo., from 1905 to 1908, was born Dec. 13, 1874, in Fountain Green, Utah, a son of John Shawcroft and Anna Marie Jensen. He was baptized June 4, 1883, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1895-1897, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 16, 1905, by Francis M. Lyman.
