Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lost River Stake

Andersen, J. Wesley

Andersen, J. Wesley, Bishop of the Moore Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, from 1923 to 1930, was born Nov. 3, 1887, at Brigham City, Utah, a son of Jonathan N. Andersen and Ellen M. Tingey. He was baptized Nov. 3, 1895, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 18, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard, and ordained a Bishop Oct. 28, 1923, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
