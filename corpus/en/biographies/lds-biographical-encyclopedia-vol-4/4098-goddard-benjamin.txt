Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Goddard, Benjamin

Goddard, Benjamin, president of the Temple Square Mission and manager of the Bureau of Information in Salt Lake City, Utah, from 1902 to 1929, was born July 27, 1851, in Huddersfield, Yorkshire, England, and died in Salt Lake City Dec. 5, 1930. (See page 239.)
