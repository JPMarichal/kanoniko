Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cutler, Thomas R.

Cutler, Thomas R., Bishop of the Lehi Ward, Utah County, Utah, from 1879 to 1903, was born June 2, 1844, in Sheffield, England, a son of Thomas Cutler and Elizabeth Robinson. He arrived in Utah in 1864, and became a prominent and successful business man and ecclesiastical leader. He died April 3, 1922, at Long Beach, Calif.
