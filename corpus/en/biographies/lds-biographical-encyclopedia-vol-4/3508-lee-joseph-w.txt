Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lee, Joseph W.

Lee, Joseph W., Bishop of the Metcur Ward, Tooele Stake, Utah, from 1906 to 1908, was born June 26, 1862, in Santa Clara, Washington Co., Utah, a son of Samuel F. Lee and Ann White. He was baptized in 1871, and ordained a Bishop March 11, 1906, by Francis M. Lyman.
