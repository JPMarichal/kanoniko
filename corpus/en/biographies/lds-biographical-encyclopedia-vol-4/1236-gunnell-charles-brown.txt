Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gunnell, Charles Brown

Gunnell, Charles Brown, Bishop of the Howell Ward, Bear River Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930, was born March 5, 1887, in Wellsville, Cache Co., Utah, the son of Charles E. Gunnell and Sarah Ann Brown. He was baptized June 6, 1895, was ordained an Elder March 23, 1903, by Daniel I. Stewart and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1909-1911; he was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 11, 1928, by Geo. F. Richards.
