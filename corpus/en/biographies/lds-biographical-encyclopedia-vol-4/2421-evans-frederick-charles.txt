Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Evans, Frederick Charles

Evans, Frederick Charles, Bishop of the Raymond Ward, Montpelier Stake, Idaho, from 1915 to 1930+, was born Sept. 21, 1872, in Kaysville, Utah, a son of Frederick Henry Evans and Emma Raymond. He was baptized Sept. 13, 1885, ordained a Seventy March 23, 1900, by J. Golden Kimball, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1900-1902, was ordained a High Priest March 9, 1904, by Wm. L. Rich, and a Bishop Sept. 19, 1915, by Heber J. Grant.
