Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Maughan, Willard W.

Maughan, Willard W., second counselor in the presidency of the Cache Stake, Utah, from 1901 to 1906, was born Aug. 31, 1854, in E T City, Tooele Co., Utah, a son of Peter and Mary Ann Maughan. He was ordained a High Priest Dec. 19, 1870, by Peter Maughan. He died April 30, 1924.
