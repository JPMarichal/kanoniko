Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Miller, John Henry

Miller, John Henry, Bishop of the Holbrook Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1915 to 1917, was born June 3, 1887, in Snowflake, Arizona, a son of Ninian Miller and Sarah E. Kartchner. He was baptized June 3, 1896, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 15, 1915, by Geo. F. Richards.
