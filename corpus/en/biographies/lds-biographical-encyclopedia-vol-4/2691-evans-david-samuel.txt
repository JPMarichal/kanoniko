Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Evans, David Samuel

Evans, David Samuel, Bishop of the Riverdale Ward, Oneida Stake, Idaho, from 1910 to 1922, was born June 13, 1882, in Logan, Utah, a son of David P. Evans and Sarah Smith. He was baptized Sept. 2, 1890, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1903-1905, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 4, 1910, by Hyrum M. Smith.
