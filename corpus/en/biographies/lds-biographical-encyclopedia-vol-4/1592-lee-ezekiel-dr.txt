Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lee, Ezekiel (Dr.)

Lee, Ezekiel (Dr.), Bishop of the Big Cottonwood Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1851 to 1856, was born in Chesterfield, Massachusetts, the son of Captain Charles Lee. In 1843 he embraced the Gospel, and was appointed president of the Comstock Branch, Michigan. He came to Utah in 1848. He died June 12, 1877.
