Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bunker, Bryan Lamond

Bunker, Bryan Lamond, Bishop of the Las Vegas Ward, Moapa Stake, Nevada, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Nov. 8, 1898, in Bunkerville, Lincoln Co., Nevada, a son of Martin Allen Bunker and Helen Euphemia McNeil. He was baptized Nov. 8, 1906, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1919-1921, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 2, 1929, by Orson F. Whitney.
