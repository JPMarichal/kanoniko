Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Goodrich, Byron

Goodrich, Byron, Bishop of the Naples Ward, Uintah Stake, Utah, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Nov. 23, 1887, in Vernal, Utah, a son of Geo. A. Goodrich and Eliza A. Taggart. He was baptized May 6, 1896, ordained a High Priest Jan. 27, 1923, by Albert G. Goodrich, and a Bishop June 27, 1925, by David O. McKay.
