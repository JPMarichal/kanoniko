Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Belliston, William Thomas

Belliston, William Thomas, Bishop of the Murray 1st Ward, Cottonwood Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1928, was born Oct. 1, 1892, in Nephi, Utah, the son of Thomas Wm. Belliston and Elizabeth Foster. He was baptized Aug. 31, 1901, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 21, 1926, by Rudger Clawson.
