Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Buxton, John M.

Buxton, John M., Bishop of the Cache Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1929 to 1930+, was born Oct. 25, 1900, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of joseph Buxton and Tacy Harper. He was baptized Oct. 31, 1908, ordained an Elder in November, 1920, and filled a mission to the Central States in 1920-1923. He was ordained a High Priest Oct. 24, 1925, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop May 11, 1929, by Rudger Clawson.
