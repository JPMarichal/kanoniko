Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mabey, Albert, jun.

Mabey, Albert, jun., Bishop of the Provo 5th Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1924, was born Feb. 4, 1878, in Bountiful, Utah, a son of Albert Mabey and Celestia Janet Wood. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a Seventy by John Henry Smith, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1898-1900, was ordained a High Priest Dec. 9, 1917, by Jos. B. Keeler, and a Bishop April 24, 1920, by Rudger Clawson.
