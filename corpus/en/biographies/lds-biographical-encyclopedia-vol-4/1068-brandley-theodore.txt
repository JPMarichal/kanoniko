Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brandley, Theodore

Brandley, Theodore, president of the Swiss and German Mission from 1890 to 1891, was a counselor in the Stake presidency and later a Patriarch in the Taylor Stake of Zion. He died May 6, 1928, at Stirling, Alberta, Canada. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 387.)
