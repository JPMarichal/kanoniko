Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Haight, Hector Caleb

Haight, Hector Caleb, Bishop of the Oakley 1st Ward, Cassia Stake, Idaho, from 1901 to 1916, was born Aug. 18, 1869, in Farmington, Utah, the son of Hotton D. Haight and Louise Leavitt. He was baptized in 1887, Ordained a Bishop Aug. 9, 1901, by Rudger Clawson, and died March 23, 1916.
