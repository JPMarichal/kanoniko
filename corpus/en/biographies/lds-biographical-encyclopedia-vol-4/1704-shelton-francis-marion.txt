Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Shelton, Francis Marion

Shelton, Francis Marion, Bishop of the Duchesne Ward, Duchesne Stake, Utah, from 1920 to 1922, was born Aug. 22, 1882, in Heber, Utah, the son of Charles Shelten and Susan Jane Wilkins, He was baptized about August, 1891, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 26, 1920, by Orson F. Whitney.
