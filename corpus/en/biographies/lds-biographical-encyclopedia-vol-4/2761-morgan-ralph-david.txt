Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Morgan, Ralph David

Morgan, Ralph David, Bishop of the Spanish Fork 3rd Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1917 to 1930, was born Feb. 28, 1882, in Spanish Fork, Utah Co., Utah, the son of David Morgan and Elizabeth Prior. He was baptized Aug. 6, 1896, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 27, 1917, by Orson F. Whitney.
