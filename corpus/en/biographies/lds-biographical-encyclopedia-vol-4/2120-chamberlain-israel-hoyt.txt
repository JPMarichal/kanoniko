Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Chamberlain, Israel Hoyt

Chamberlain, Israel Hoyt, Bishop of the Kanab Ward, Kanab Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1922 and from 1928 to 1930+, was born Aug. 31, 1884, in Orderville, Utah, the son of Thomas Chamberlain and Ellen A. Hoyt. He was baptized in September, 1893, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 7, 1913, by Rudger Clawson.
