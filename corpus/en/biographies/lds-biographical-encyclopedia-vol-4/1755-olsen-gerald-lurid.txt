Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Olsen, Gerald Lurid

Olsen, Gerald Lurid, Bishop of the Emery Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born March 29, 1900, in Emery, Utah, the son of Geo. T. Olsen and Catherine M. Edwards. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1911, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 12, 1922, by Alma G. Jewkes, arid a Bishop Jan. 28, 1928, by Geo. F. Richards.
