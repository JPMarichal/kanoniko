Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilson, Robert Lorenzo

Wilson, Robert Lorenzo, Bishop of the Payson 3rd Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Dec. 24, 1878, in Payson, Utah, the son of Thomas Henry Wilson and Caroline Marchant. He was baptized June 6, 1889, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 14, 1926, by Geo. F. Richards.
