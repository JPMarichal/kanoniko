Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Knighton, James

Knighton, James, Bishop of the Moore Ward, Lost River Stake, Idaho, in 1930+, was born Feb. 18, 1882, in South Bountiful, Utah, a son of William Knighton and Mary Ann Stacey. He was baptized June 22, 1890, was ordained a High Priest May 7, 1922, by Richard R. Lyman, and a Bishop April 20, 1930, by David O. McKay.
