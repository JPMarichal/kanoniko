Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Garff, George Peter

Garff, George Peter, Bishop of the Wallsburg Ward, Wasatch Stake, Wasatch Co., Utah, from 1903 to 1912, was born June 17, 1870, at South Jordan (now Riverton), Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Peter N. Garff and Antomina Sorensen. He was ordained a Seventy May 23, 1896, by Edward Stevenson, filled a mission to the Sandwich Islands in 1896-1899, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 11, 1903, by Rudger Clawson.
