Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Nickle, Francis Clement

Nickle, Francis Clement, Bishop of the Marysvale Ward, South Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1921 to 1923, was born April 30, 1875, in Fayette County, Illinois. He was baptized Nov. 1, 1914, ordained a High Priest July 11, 1920, by John Christensen, and sustained as Bishop Nov. 13, 1921.
