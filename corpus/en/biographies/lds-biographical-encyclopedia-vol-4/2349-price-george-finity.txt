Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Price, George Finity

Price, George Finity, Bishop of the Phoenix Ward, Maricopa Stake, Arizona, from 1926 to 1928, and Bishop of the Phoenix 2rid Ward from 1928 to 1930+, was born June 28, 1886, in Charleston, Utah, a son of George Price and Mary H. Daybell. He was baptized Aug. 1, 1894, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to Great Britain in 1899-1901, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 9, 1926, by David O. McKay, and sustained as Bishop in the same year.
