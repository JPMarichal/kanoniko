Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brinkerhoff, George Cutler

Brinkerhoff, George Cutler, Bishop of the Thurber Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1928, was born Feb. 21, 1887, in Thurber, a son of George Brinkerhoff and Ida Estella Cutler. He was baptized May 2, 1895, ordained a Seventy Feb. 9, 1908, by Charles H. Hart, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1912-1914, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 23, 1924, by Stephen L. Richards.
