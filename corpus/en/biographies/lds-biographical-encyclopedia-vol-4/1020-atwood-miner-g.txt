Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Atwood, Miner G.

Atwood, Miner G., president of the South African Mission from 1864 to 1865, was born March 18, 1823, in Mansfield, Connecticut, a son of Daniel Atwood and Polly Sawyer. He embraced the Gospel on July 21, 1849, being baptized by Cyril Brown; emigrated to Utah in 1850, crossing the plains in Wilford Woodruff's company. He was chosen counselor to Bishop Leonard G. Hardy of the Salt Lake City 12th Ward in 1856, went on a mission to the Southern States in 1857, and was set apart for a mission to South Africa May 11, 1862; he returned in June, 1865. His last mission was to Arizona in 1874, He was a member of the High Council of the Salt Lake Stake of Zion, being called to that position at the April Conference in 1869. He died May 17, 1887, in Salt Lake City, Utah.
