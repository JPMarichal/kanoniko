Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Sargent, William

Sargent, William, Bishop of the Hoytsville Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1886 to 1910, was born Aug. 1, 1844, in Dowsdale, Lincolnshire, England, the son of John Sargent and Hannah Farrow. He was baptized May 21, 1868, came to Utah in 1868, and was ordained a Bishop Aug. 15, 1886, by John Henry Smith.
