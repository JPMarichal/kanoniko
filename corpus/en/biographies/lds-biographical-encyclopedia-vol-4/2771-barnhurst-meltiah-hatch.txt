Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Barnhurst, Meltiah Hatch

Barnhurst, Meltiah Hatch, Bishop of the Hatch Ward, Panguitch Stake, Utah, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Feb. 24, 1893, in Hatch, a son of Samuel James Barnhurst and Laura Ann Hatch. He was baptized Aug. 10, 1901, ordained a High Priest Aug. 31, 1924, by Wm. J. Henderson, and a Bishop Sept. 3, 1924, by Richard R. Lyman.
