Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Porter, Alva Sylvanus

Porter, Alva Sylvanus, Bishop of the Eden Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1898 to 1900, was born May 26, 1860, in Centerville, Utah, a son of Lyman W. Porter and Electa Mariah Kilburn. He was baptized in May, 1868, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 7, 1898, by John Henry Smith. He acted as Bishop of Central Ward from 1902 to 1909.
