Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Williams, Christopher

Williams, Christopher, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 3rd. Ward, Liberty Stake, Utah, from 1849 to 1856, was born March 7, 1789, in Plattekill, Ulster Co., New York, the son of Ichabod Williams and Sybil Clark. He was ordained a High Priest by Witlard Snow in Salt Lake City, and was ordained a Bishop Feb. 22, 1849.
