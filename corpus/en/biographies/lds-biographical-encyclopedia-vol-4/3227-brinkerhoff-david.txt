Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Brinkerhoff, David

Brinkerhoff, David, Bishop of the Tuba Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1886 to 1900, was born May 22, 1855, at Centerville, Utah, a son of James Brinkerhoff and Eliza Jane Henderson. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained a Bishop in 1886, by Jesse N. Smith, and when the settlement of Tuba was broken up in 1903, because of claims made by the Indians as to the lands, he moved to Woodruff, Arizona, while others of the saints removed to different settlements.
