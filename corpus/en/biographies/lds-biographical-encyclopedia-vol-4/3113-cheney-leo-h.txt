Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Cheney, Leo H.

Cheney, Leo H., Bishop of the Elmhurst Ward, San Francisco Stake, Calif., from 1927 to 1930, was born June 5, 1896, in Fruitland, New Mexico, the son of Franklin Cheney and Edith Hilton. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 10, 1927, by Rudger Clawson.
