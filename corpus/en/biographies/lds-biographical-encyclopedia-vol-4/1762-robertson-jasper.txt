Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Robertson, Jasper

Robertson, Jasper, Bishop of the Orangeville Ward, Emery Stake, Utah, from 1882 to 1906, was born May 8, 1847, in Walkersville, Green Co., Ill., the son of Nichols Robertson and Jane Hunter. He was baptized in June, 1862, en route across the plains, located in Fountain Green, Sanpete Co., Utah, in 1863, and in Orangeville in 1880. He was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 13, 1882, by Erastus Snow, and died Jan. 17, 1916, in Orangeville, Utah.
