Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Smith, Charles, jun.

Smith, Charles, jun., Bishop of the Heyburn Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1930+, was born Dec. 24, 1883, in Centerville, Davis Co., Utah, a son of Charles L. Smith and Pamelia E. Thompson. He was baptized Aug. 28, 1892, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Nov. 15, 1919, by George F. Richards.
