Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hunsaker, Lorenzo

Hunsaker, Lorenzo, Bishop of the Globe Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1907 to 1928, was born March 21, 1859, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Abraham Hunsaker and Margaret Sweeten. He was baptized when nine years old, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1879-1881, and was ordained a Bishop Dec. 1, 1907, by Geo. F. Richards.
