Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Myers, Wilford Jacob

Myers, Wilford Jacob, Bishop of the Riverton 2nd Ward, West Jordan Stake, from 1927 to 1930+, was born March 25, 1890, in Cottonwood, Salt Lake Co., Utah, a son of Jacob Myers and Annie M. Johnson. He was baptized May 15, 1898, ordained a Seventy Nov. 23, 1911, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1911-1913, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Oct. 23, 1927, by Joseph F. Smith, jun.
