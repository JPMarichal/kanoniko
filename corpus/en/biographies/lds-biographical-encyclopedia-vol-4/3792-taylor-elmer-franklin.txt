Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Taylor, Elmer Franklin

Taylor, Elmer Franklin, president of the Young Stake, Colorado and New Mexico, from 1924 to 1930+, was born Feb. 23, 1871, in Santaquin, Utah, a son of Norman Taylor and Lydia Forbush. He was baptized about 1879, ordained an Elder Dec. 23, 1892, by John B. Maiben, and a High Priest Jan. 14, 1906, by John R. Young, and ordained a Patriarch March 9, 1924, by James E. Talmage.
