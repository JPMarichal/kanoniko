Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mckell, Robert Wilson

Mckell, Robert Wilson, Bishop of the Spanish Fork 1st Ward, Palmyra Stake, Utah Co., Utah, from 1917 to 1918, was born Nov. 16, 1856, in Spanish Fork, Utah, the son of Robert McKell and Elizabeth Boyack. He was baptized July 14, 1867, ordained a High Priest Feb. 6, 1892, by David John, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 13, 1901, by Stephen L. Richards. He died May 12, 1911.
