Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Danish-Norwegian Mission

Presidents: Jens M. Christensen, 1905-1907; Soren Rasmussen, 1907-1909; Andrew Jenson, 1909-1912; Martin Christoffersen, 1912-1914; Hans J. Christiansen, 1914-1919; Carl E. Peterson, 1919-1920.

Christensen (James Miller) Jens Moller

Christensen (James Miller) Jens Moller, president of the Danish-Norwegian Mission from 1905 to 1907, died Jan. 7, 1915, in Salt Lake City, Utah. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 686 and History of the Scandinavian Mission, p. 411.)
