Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

St. Johns Stake

Ashcroft, Josiah Emer

Ashcroft, Josiah Emer, Bishop of the Ramah Ward, St. Johns Stake, Ariz., from 1908 to 1914, was born Sept. 7, 1865, in Hyde Park, Utah, a son of Henry Ashcroft and Elizabeth Ann Barton. He was baptized July 12, 1874, and ordained a Bishop Aug. 22, 1908, .by Hyrum M. Smith.
