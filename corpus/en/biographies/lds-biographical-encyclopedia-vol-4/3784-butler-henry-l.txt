Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Butler, Henry L.

Butler, Henry L., Bishop of the Kline Ward, Young Stake, from 1907 to 1913, was born March 23, 1874, in Payson, Utah, a son of Henry Butler and Harriet B. Russell. He was baptized in 1882, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 28, 1907, by John Henry Smith.
