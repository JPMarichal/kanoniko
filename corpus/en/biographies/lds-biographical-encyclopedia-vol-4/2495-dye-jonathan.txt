Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Dye, Jonathan

Dye, Jonathan, presiding Elder pro tem. of the Uintah Branch, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1869 to 1870, was born Oct. 10, 1829, in Herrford, England, a son of Thomas Dye and Sarah Gower. He was baptized May 26, 1852 and died April 24, 1907.
