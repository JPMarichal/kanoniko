Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hale, Howard Hoskins

Hale, Howard Hoskins, Bishop of the 12th-13th Ward, Ensign Stake, Utah, in 1930+, was born Feb. 10, 1876, in Portage, Utah, a son of Martin Van Buren Hale and Martha Arnold. He was baptized May 11, 1884, by Enoch Harris, ordained a Seventy by George Teasdale Nov. 21, 1900, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1900-1902, was ordained a High Priest May 8, 1927, by George H. Wallace, and ordained a Bishop Jan. 25, 1930, by James E. Talmage.
