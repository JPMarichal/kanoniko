Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Meikle, Robert Gilbert

Meikle, Robert Gilbert, Bishop of the Clawson Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1898 to 1903, was born June 5, 1868, in Smithfield, Utah, a son of James Meikle and Harriet L. Peacock. He filled a mission to New Zealand in 1890-1894, and was ordained a Bishop Sept. 3, 1898, by John Henry Smith.
