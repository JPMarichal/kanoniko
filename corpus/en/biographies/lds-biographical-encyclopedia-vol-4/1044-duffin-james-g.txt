Presidents: William T. Jack, 1898-1900; James G. Duffin, 1900-1904.

Duffin, James G.

Duffin, James G., president of the Southwestern States Mission from 1900 to 1904, died May 20, 1921. (See Bio. Ency., Vol. 1, p. 537.)
