Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gerstner, John Joseph

Gerstner, John Joseph, Bishop of the Salt Lake City 32rid Ward, Pioneer Stake, Utah, from 1911 to 1924, was born July 24, 1865, in Zurich, Switzerland, the son of Joseph Gerstnet and Helena Sophia Wagener. He came to Utah in 1874, was baptized in 1875, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 11, 1911, by Francis M. Lyman.
