Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larson, Andrew Olof

Larson, Andrew Olof, Bishop of the Alhambra Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1923 to 1928, was born Sept. 11, 1887, in Molen, Emery Co., Utah, the son of Niels C. Larson. He was baptized in 1897, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 12, 1923, by Richard R. Lyman.
