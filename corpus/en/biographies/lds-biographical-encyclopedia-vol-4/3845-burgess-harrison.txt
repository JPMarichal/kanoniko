Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Burgess, Harrison

Burgess, Harrison, a member of Zion's Camp, was born Sept. 3, 1814, in Putnam, Washington Co., N.Y., a son of William Burgess. He was baptized Oct. 1, 1832, filled a mission to Vermont in 1833, went to Missouri with Zion's Camp in 1834, was ordained a Seventy Feb. 28, 1835, by Sidney Rigdon, and filled a mission to England before coming to the "Valley." In 1862 he was called to the Southern Utah Mission and settled in Pine Valley. There he acted as a counselor to Bishop Wm. Snow, and after the death of Bishop Snow, acted as Bishop for a short time. He died Feb. 10, 1883, in Pine Valley, Utah.
