Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Peter Canute

Peterson, Peter Canute, Bishop of the Ephraim West Ward, South Sanpete Stake, Sanpete Co., Utah, from 1923 to 1930+, was born Feb. 23, 1876, at Ephraim, Utah, the son of Peter Cornelius Petersen and Hanna Thomson. He was baptized June 22, 1884, ordained a Seventy in May, 1920, by Anthon H. Lund, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 4, 1923, by George F. Richards.
