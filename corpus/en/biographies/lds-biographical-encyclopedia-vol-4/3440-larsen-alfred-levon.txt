Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Larsen, Alfred Levon

Larsen, Alfred Levon, Bishop of the Darby Ward, Teton Stake, Idaho, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Nov. 21, 1899, in Gunnison, Utah, a son of Chas. A. Larsen and Annie Peterson. He was baptized Oct. 3, 1908, filled a mission to the Central States in 1920-1922, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop May 1, 1927, by Jos. F. Smith, jun.
