Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Borup, David John

Borup, David John, Bishop of the Rupert 1st Ward, Minidoka Stake, Idaho, from 1919 to 1930, was born Dec. 5, 1881, in Goshen, Utah, a son of Christen Carl Borup and Caroline Thomas. He was baptized in June, 1895, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 16, 1919, by Melvin J. Ballard.
