Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jensen, Orestus

Jensen, Orestus, Bishop of the Bear River City Ward, Box Elder Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930 +, was born June 6, 1886, in Brigham City, Utah, the son of Olof Jensen and Mary Ralphs. He was baptized June 7, 1894, by his father, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop July 8, 1928, by James E. Talmage.
