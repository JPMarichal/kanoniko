Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Stuart, David Calvin

Stuart, David Calvin, Bishop of the Ogden 19th Ward, Weber Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930, was born May 25, 1873, in Wardboro, Bear Lake Co., Idaho, the son of David Crockett Stuart and Parthena L. Gunnison. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained an Elder Sept. 28, 1894, by Wm. Budge, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1914-1916, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Aug. 1, 1926, by David O. McKay.
