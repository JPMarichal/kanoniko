Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ridges, Wilford Owen

Ridges, Wilford Owen, Bishop of the Ogden 9th Ward, Mount Ogden Stake, Utah, from 1909 to 1923, was born Sept. 5, 1866, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a son of Joseph H. Ridges and Agatha Walker. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1875, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1892-1895, was ordained a High Priest Nov. 28, 1898, by Wm. C. Parkinson and a Bishop July 4, 1909, by David O. McKay.
