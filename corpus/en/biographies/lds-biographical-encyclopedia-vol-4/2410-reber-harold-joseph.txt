Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Reber, Harold Joseph

Reber, Harold Joseph, Bishop of the Littlefield Ward, Moapa Stake, Nevada, from 1927 to 1930+, was born Feb. 24, 1895, in Santa Clara, Washington Co., Utah, a son of Joseph Henry Reber and Amelia Anna Enz. He was baptized Sept. 5, 1903, ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Northwestern States in 1920-1922, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop Feb. 27, 1927, by Stephen L. Richards.
