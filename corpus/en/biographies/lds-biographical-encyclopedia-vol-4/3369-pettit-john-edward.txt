Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Pettit, John Edward

Pettit, John Edward, Bishop of the Grass Creek Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1902 to 1908, was born July 10, 1864, in Dunstall Green, Suffolk, England, a son of Edward Wade Pettit and Harriet Pettit. He was baptized Aug. 27, 1881, emigrated to America in 1887, and was sustained and set apart as Bishop in 1902.
