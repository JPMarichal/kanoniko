Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Watkins, Joseph Hammond, jun.

Watkins, Joseph Hammond, jun., Bishop of the Logan 7th Ward,. Logan Stake, Utah, from 1917 to 1930+, was born Dec. 12, 1885, in Brigham City, Utah, a son of Jos. II. Watkins and Ella Hancock. He was baptized Dec. 12, 1893, ordained an Elder Feb. 7, 1907, by Martin Jensen, filled a mission to the Eastern States in 1907-1909, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 3, 1917, by Hyrum M. Smith.
