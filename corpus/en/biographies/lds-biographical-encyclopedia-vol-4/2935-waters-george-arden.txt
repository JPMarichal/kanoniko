Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Waters, George Arden

Waters, George Arden, Bishop of the Rigby 2nd Ward, Rigby Stake, Jefferson Co., Idaho, from 1920 to 1923, was born Aug. 28, 1880, at Burrville, Sevier Co., Utah, a son of George Birch Waters and Laura Ann Burr. He was baptized June 6, 1889, ordained a High Priest Dec. 2, 1917, by George F. Richards, and ordained a Bishop Feb. 29, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard.
