Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Gottfredsen, Jacob

Gottfredsen, Jacob, Bishop of the Vermillion Ward, North Sevier Stake, Utah, from 1899 to 1903, was born Dec. 3, 1862, in Mt. Pleasant, Utah, a son of Jens Gottfredsen and Karen Marie Pedersen. He was ordained a High Priest June 2, 1896, by John Henry Smith.
