Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ricks, Peter Jeppeson

Ricks, Peter Jeppeson, first counselor in the Fremont Stake presidency, Idaho, from 1925 to 1930+, was born Nov. 16, 1886, at Rexburg, ths son of Jos. Ricks and Sophie Nielsen. He was baptized Nov. 16, 1894, ordained an Elder by John D. Spiers, and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1907-1909. He was ordained a High Priest May 17, 1914, by Eli McEntire, and a Bishop March 27, 1921, by Rudger Clawson (Rexburg 3rd Ward). He was set apart as first counselor in the Stake presidency Sept. 25, 1925, by Geo. S. Romney.
