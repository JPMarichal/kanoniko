Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Lloyd, Joseph Francis

Lloyd, Joseph Francis, Bishop of the Emerson Ward, Granite Stake, Utah, from 1919 to 1922, was born June 27, 1876, in Wellsville, Utah, the son of Thomas Lloyd and Susannah Stone. He was ordained a Bishop June 27, 1919, by Pres. Heber J. Grant.
