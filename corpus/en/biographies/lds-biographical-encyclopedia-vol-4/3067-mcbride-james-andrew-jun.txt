Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Mcbride, James Andrew, jun.

Mcbride, James Andrew, jun., Bishop of the Bryce Ward, St. Joseph Stake, Arizona, from 1924 to 1930, was born Dec. 7, 1867, in Santaquin, Utah, a son of James Andrew McBride and Elizabeth Clark. He was ordained a Bishop Feb. 14, 1925, by Geo. Albert Smith.
