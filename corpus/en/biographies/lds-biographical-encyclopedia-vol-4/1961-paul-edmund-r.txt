Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Paul, Edmund R.

Paul, Edmund R., Bishop of the Lankershim Ward, Hollywood Stake, California, from 1924 to 1928, was born April 10, 1879, at Mendon, Utah, the son of Walter Paul and Emma Westover. He was baptized May 5, 1887, and ordained a High Priest Sept. 27, 1924, by Geo. W. McCune.
