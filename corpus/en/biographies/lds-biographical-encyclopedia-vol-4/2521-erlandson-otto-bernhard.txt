Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Erlandson, Otto Bernhard

Erlandson, Otto Bernhard, Bishop of the Payson 1st Ward, Nebo Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Sept. 20, 1867, in Malmo, Sweden, the son of Elias Erlandson and Kjersti Lundsted. He emigrated to America in 1870, was baptized June 1, 1876, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 8, 1928, by Stephen L. Richards.
