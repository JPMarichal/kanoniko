Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Peterson, Charles Henry

Peterson, Charles Henry, Bishop of the Smoot Ward, Star Valley Stake, Wyo., from 1913 to 1929, was born Sept. 13, 1866, in Peterson, Utah, the son of Geo. H. Peterson and Eliza Wilde. He was baptized in 1874, ordained a High Priest by Heber J. Grant, and a Bishop June 6, 1913, by Hyrum M. Smith.
