Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Jackson, Samuel, jun.

Jackson, Samuel, jun., second counselor in the San Luis Stake presidency, Colo., from 1908 to 1919 and first counselor from 1919 to 1924, was born Sept. 15, 1869, in Nephi, Utah, the son of Samuel Jackson and Hannah M. Jaques. He was baptized Sept. 5, 1877, ordained a High Priest July 26, 1908, and set apart as second counselor by John Henry Smith.
