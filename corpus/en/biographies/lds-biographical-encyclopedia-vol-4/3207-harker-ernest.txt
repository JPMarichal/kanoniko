Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harker, Ernest

Harker, Ernest, Bishop of the Taylor Ward, Shelley Stake, Idaho, from 1914 to 1919, was born March 30, 1872, at Taylorsville, Utah, a son of Job Harker and Mary Griffiths. He was baptized June 5, 1890, filled a mission to the Central and Southern States in 1903-1904, and was ordained a High Priest Oct. 13, 1907, by Orson F. Whitney, and a Bishop Aug. 16, 1914, by Geo. F. Richards.
