Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Harris, Archibald

Harris, Archibald, Bishop of the Saint John Ward, Malad Stake, Idaho, from 1918 to 1929, was born Jan. 3, 1885, in Woodruff, Oneida Co., Idaho, a son of Everett Harris and Margaret Johns. He was ordained a High Priest May 6, 1917, and ordained Bishop Aug. 18, 1918, by Orson F. Whitney.
