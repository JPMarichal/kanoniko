Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Poulson, Christian Hyrum

Poulson, Christian Hyrum, Bishop of the Grace Ward, Bannock Stake, Idaho, from 1904 to 1914, was born April 28, 1859, in Salt Lake City, Utah, the son of Niels C. Poulson and Helga Oleson. He was baptized Nov. 24, 1867, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop June 19, 1904, by Hyrum M. Smith. He filled a mission to Denmark and England in 1890-1892.
