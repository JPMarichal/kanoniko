Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Denham, Walter Hampton

Denham, Walter Hampton, Bishop of the Shumway Ward, Snowflake Stake, Arizona, from 1919 to 1927, was born Aug. 31, 1885, in Boles, Monroe Co., Kentucky, a son of Franklin P. Denham and Dicia Ann Wood. He was baptized Sept. 1, 1893, was ordained a High Priest Aug. 13, 1915, by James M. Flake, and a Bishop April 24, 1919.
