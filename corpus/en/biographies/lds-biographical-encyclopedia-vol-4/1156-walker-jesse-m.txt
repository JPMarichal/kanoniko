Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Walker, Jesse M.

Walker, Jesse M., 2nd counselor in the stake presidency of the Alpine Stake, Utah, since 1928, was born Dec. 15, 1885, in Pleasant Grove, Utah, the son of Apollus Benjamin Walker and Jane Holman. He was baptized Aug. 5, 1885, was ordained a High Priest Feb. 8, 1918, by James E. Talmage, and acted as Bishop of the American Fork 3rd Ward from 1924 to 1928.
