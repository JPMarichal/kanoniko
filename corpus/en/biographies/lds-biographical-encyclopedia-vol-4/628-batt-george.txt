Batt, George

Batt, George, president of the Australasian Mission from 1880 to 1881, was born Aug. 20, 1854, in Houghton, Hampshire, England, a son of George Batt and Hannah Ray. He was baptized June 8, 1875, emigrated to Utah, and was set apart Oct. 10, 1878, for a mission to Australasia. He arrived there Feb. 28, 1879, and when Pres. Elijah F. Pearce was released Dec. 25, 1880, Bro. Batt was left in charge of the Mission. He was succeeded Jan. 20, 1881, by Wm. M. Bromley, and returned to Utah May 28, 1881.
