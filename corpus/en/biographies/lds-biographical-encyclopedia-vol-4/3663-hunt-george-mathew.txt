Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Hunt, George Mathew

Hunt, George Mathew, Bishop of the Thurber Ward, Wayne Stake, Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Nov. 4, 1886, in Monroe, Utah, a son of Moroni Hunt and Emily Casto. He was baptized July 31, 1899, filled a mission to Germany in 1910-1912, and was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 22, 1928, by Rudger Clawson.
