Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Wilde, William S.

Wilde, William S., Bishop of the Grass Creek Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1908 to 1909, was born Feb. 9, 1864, in Coalville, Utah, a son of Henry Wilde and Jane Batchelor. He was baptized July 5, 1873, and ordained a Bishop March 19, 1908, by John Henry Smith.
