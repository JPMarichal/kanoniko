Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Crawford, Daniel

Crawford, Daniel, Bishop of the Springdale Ward, Zion Park Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born March 25, 1879, in Rockville, Utah, a son of Wm. Robinson Crawford and Cornelia Gifford. He was baptized June 3, 1888, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Sept. 18, 1926, by John A. Widtsoe.
