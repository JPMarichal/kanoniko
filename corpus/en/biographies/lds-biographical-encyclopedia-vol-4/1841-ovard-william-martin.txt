Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Ovard, William Martin

Ovard, William Martin, Bishop of the Rexburg 2nd Ward, Fremont Stake, Idaho, from 1924 to 1930, was born April 20, 1876, in North Morgan, Utah, the son of Joseph Ovard and Susannah Heiner. He was ordained a High Priest June 23, 1918, by Thomas E. Ricks, and a Bishop June 21, 1924, by James E. Talmage.
