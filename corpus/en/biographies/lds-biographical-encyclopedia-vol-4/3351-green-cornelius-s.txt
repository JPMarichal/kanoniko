Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Green, Cornelius S.

Green, Cornelius S., Bishop of the Marion Ward, Summit Stake, Utah, from 1913 to 1915, was born Nov. 16, 1860, in Mill Creek, Utah, a son of Cornelius Green and Karen C. Hartsen. He was baptized Nov. 23, 1875, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop in the fall of 1913.
