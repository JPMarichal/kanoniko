Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Bickmore, Danford Mcarthur

Bickmore, Danford Mcarthur, president of the Hyrum Stake, Cache Co., Utah, from 1928 to 1930+, was born Jan. 3, 1875, at Wellsville, Utah, the son of David N. Rickmore and Elizabeth McArthur. He was baptized when about eight years old, ordained a Seventy June 15, 1898, by Seymour B. Young, filled a mission to the Northern States in 1898-1900, was ordained a High Priest July 18, 1920, by Melvin J. Ballard, and set apart as president of the Hyrum Stake Sept. 30, 1928, by Stephen L. Richards.
