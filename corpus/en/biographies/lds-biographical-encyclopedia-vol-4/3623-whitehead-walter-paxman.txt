Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Whitehead, Walter Paxman

Whitehead, Walter Paxman, Bishop of the Provo 1st Ward, Utah Stake, Utah, from 1926 to 1930+, was born Feb. 28, 1876, in Grays, Kent, England, a son of Walter Whitehead and Sarah Allsworth. He was baptized Oct. 12, 1885, emigrated to Utah in 1888, filled a mission to England in 1908-1909, was ordained a High Priest April 27, 1913, by Geo. Albert Smith, and a Bishop in 1926.
