Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 4 ( 1936)

Snow, Edwin Marion

Snow, Edwin Marion, second counselor in the Kolob Stake presidency, Utah, from 1924 to 1928, was born Nov. 21, 1859, in Manti, Sanpete Co., Utah, a son of Warren S. Snow and Elizabeth Whiting. He labored in the Mapleton Ward bishopric for twenty-one years, and was chosen as second counselor Nov. 23, 1924. He died Dec. 11, 1928.
