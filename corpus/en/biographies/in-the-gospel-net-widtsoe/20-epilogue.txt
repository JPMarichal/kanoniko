John A. Widtsoe, In the Gospel Net: The Story of Anna Karine Gaarden Widtsoe ( 1941)

Epilogue

The woman whose story has been told here was one of tens of thousands who in these latter days earnestly sought truth, happily found it, courageously accepted it, and were led into new places, where, despite many trials, they discovered new and greater joys in living.

The story herein told, may, therefore, be read as the saga of a glorious multitude who have been caught in the gospel net. Before this host of radiant men and women, we bow in admiration and gratitude. May the memory of their certain faith and diligent works ever rest in the hearts of the coming generations!
