Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Mace, John

Mace, John, an Elder who died while filling a foreign mission, was born May 13, 1806, in Stockport, Cheshire, England, the son of William Mace and Fanny Slate. He received the gospel and was baptized in October, 1842, by Elder Henry Shaw. In 1865 he came to Utah, leaving his family in England, and on May 29, 1867, he was set apart by President George A. Smith for a mission to his native country and to gather his family to Zion. He labored faithfully in the missionary field, mostly in the Leeds conference. He died in Leeds Jan. 19, 1869, at the age of sixty-three years, being a High Priest at the time of his demise.
