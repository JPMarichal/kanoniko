Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Heaton, Israel H.

Heaton, Israel H., second counselor in the Kanab Stake presidency, was born Jan. 30, 1880, at Orderville, Kane county, Utah, the son of Jonathan Heaton and Clarissa Amy Hoyt. He was baptized April 8, 1888, by John J. Esplin. After completing the district school he attended the B. Y. Academy at Provo during the years 1898-1900. In 1900 (Nov. 28th) he married Charlotte Cox of Orderville, and moved to Upper Kanab in the spring of 1901, where he continued his occupation as a farmer and as secretary of the Ward Y. M. M. I. A. and as school trustee for three years. He was ordained an Elder Nov. 23, 1900, and soon afterwards chosen as first counselor to Pres. Henry C. Esplin in the presidency of the Elders of the Kanab Stake. He also filled a home mission in the Nebo Stake of Zion in the interest of Y. M. M. I. A. He was ordained a Seventy Sept. 9, 1906, and filled a mission to Australia in 1907-1909. He labored successfully in the South Wales and South Australian conferences. About two years after his return home, or on Dec. 5, 1910, he was ordained a High Priest and set apart as second counselor to Wm. W. Seegmiller, president of the Kanab Stake, by Geo. F. Richards. He moved his family from Mocassin, Arizona, to Kanab, April 28, 1913. Bro. Heaton has always been a willing and conscientious Church worker.
