Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Harvey, Ann Coope

Harvey, Ann Coope, wife of John Harvey, was born April 13, 1826, at Stockport, Lancashire, England, the daughter of Richard Coope and Esther Stafford. She was baptized March 21, 1841, by John Wood; emigrated to Utah in the early fifties and was married to John Harvey, July 23, 1854, to whom she bore six children. She was an active worker in the Ward Primary Association, and acted as president of that association from 1889 to 1900. She also served as a counselor and secretary in the Ward Relief Society. Sister Harvey died April 8, 1910, beloved by all who knew her; she had been a most diligent and faithful Church worker all her life and a devoted and loving wife and mother.
