Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Borup, Peter, junior

Borup, Peter, junior, Bishop of the Eureka Ward (Tintic Stake), Juab county, Utah, was born Nov. 15, 1869, at Goshen, Utah county, Utah, the son of Peter Borup and Christine Christensen. He removed with his parents to Panguitch in 1878, where he was baptized by Bishop J. C. Davis when nine years old. He received considerable schooling in Goshen and Panguitch. His father died in October, 1878, in Panguitch, after which Peter returned to Goshen, where he resided until 1885, when he located in Eureka. In 1897 (May 12th) he married Laura Mattinson, who has borne him five children, namely, Leta L., Klea Louise, Carl M., Fern and Helen. He worked as an engineer for the Bullion Beck Mining Co, for fifteen years. Bro. Borup's first office in the Priesthood was that of a Deacon; afterwards he was ordained an Elder and filled a mission to Norway in 1906-1908, laboring in the Bergen conference, part of the time as president of the same. Before going on his mission he acted as second counselor in the Eureka Ward Y. M. M. I. A. In 1908 (Aug. 30th), soon after his return from his mission, he was ordained a High Priest by Rudger Clawson and set apart to preside over the Eureka Ward, which position he still holds. Bishop Borup has taken an active part also in secular affairs and now acts as a member and treasurer of the Carnegie Library Board of Eureka.
