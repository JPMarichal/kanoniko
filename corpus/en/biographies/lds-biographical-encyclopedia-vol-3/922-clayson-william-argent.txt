Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Clayson, William Argent

Clayson, William Argent, an Elder who died in the missionary field, was born April 3, 1873, at Payson, Utah, the son of Nathan Clayson and Annie Harriett Butler. He was baptized in April, 1881, by Thomas Daniels, and was later ordained an Elder. While residing in Colonia Juarez, Mexico, he was called on a mission to the Eastern States, being ordained a Seventy by George Teasdale and set apart for that mission Jan. 3, 1900. He was laboring as a missionary in West Virginia when he took sick and died June 30, 1901.
