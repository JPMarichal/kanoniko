Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Haight, Julia Van Orden

Haight, Julia Van Orden, wife of Hector C. Haight, was born Jan. 19, 1811, the daughter of Peter Van Orden and Mary Crocker. She was married to Hector C. Haight in 1829 and migrated to Utah with her husband in 1847. She became the mother of seven children and died in May, 1869, at Farmington, Utah.
