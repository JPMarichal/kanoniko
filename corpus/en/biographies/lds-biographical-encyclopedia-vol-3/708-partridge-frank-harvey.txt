Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Partridge, Frank Harvey

Partridge, Frank Harvey, Bishop of Fillmore (Millard Stake), Millard county, Utah, was born Aug. 12, 1875, at Fillmore, the son of Edward Partridge and Elizabeth Buxton. He was baptized in 1883 by Christian Anderson; ordained a Deacon and afterwards an Elder; ordained a Seventy Dec. 29, 1906, by Francis M. Lyman, and a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 30, 1906, by Apostle Francis M. Lyman. Previous to this he had acted as president of an Elders' quorum. He served as Bishop until October, 1909, and in 1912 he was chosen as a member of the High Council of the Millard Stake, a position which he still holds. He also served as first assistant Stake superintendent of Sunday schools of the Millard Stake during the years 1917 and 1918. Bishop Partridge has also been active in secular affairs. He acted as justice of the peace in 1917-1918 and as city treasurer from 1918 to 1919. In 1898 (Oct. 26th) he married Savilla Adell Melville (daughter of Brigham Melville and Savilla Bishop), who was born Oct. 8, 1880, at Fillmore. After bearing her husband two children (Savilla and Dell Fenton), she died on Jan. 10, 1906. In 1908 (April 1st) Bishop Partridge married Harriet A. Whicker ( daughter of Isaac Whicker and Elizabeth Starley), who was born Jan. 18, 1885. She became the mother of five children, namely: Agnes, Eldon E., Ronald R., Orvil and Carroll.
