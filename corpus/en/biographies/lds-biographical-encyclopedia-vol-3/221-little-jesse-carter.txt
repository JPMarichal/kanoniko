Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Little, Jesse Carter

Little, Jesse Carter (continued from Vol. 1, page 242), Bro. Little acted for a number of years as U. S. marshal for Utah, was the first sexton of Salt Lake City, which city he also served as assessor and collector. In 1839 (Sept. 28th) he married Eliza Greenwood French (daughter of Col. Whitecomb French and Mary Kendall), who was born Sept. 2, 1818, and died Oct. 23, 1899; she became the mother of eleven children. About the year 1856 Jesse C. Little married Emily Hoagland and Mary M. Holbrook. By these two wives he had sixteen children, eleven by Emily and five by Mary.
