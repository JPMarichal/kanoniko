Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Farr, Lucian Cordian Jr.

Farr, Lucian Cordian Jr., Bishop of Arimo (Portneuf Stake), Bannock county, Idaho, was born June 7, 1882, at Logan, Utah, the son of Lucian C. Farr and Sarah A. E. Holden. He was baptized by his father when about eight years old and was subsequently ordained to the offices of Deacon, Priest, Elder and Seventy. The latter ordination was by Chas. H. Hart. He was ordained a Bishop May 17, 1913, by Hyrum M. Smith, and set apart to preside over the Arlmo Ward as its first Bishop. In his younger days heacted as a counselor in the presidency of a Deacons' Quorum, was secretary of the Ward Y. M. M. I. A. and later served on the Stake Board. He has been a constant worker in Sunday School and Y. M. M. I. A. matters. Otherwise he is a farmer by occupation.
