Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Mendenhall, William Henry

Mendenhall, William Henry, president of the Bannock Stake (continued from Vol. 1, page 769 ). Bro. Mendenhall served as second counselor to Pres. Pond until Jan. 18, 1904, when he was chosen as first counselor to Pres. Pond, and at a conference held May 21, 1916, he was chosen and set apart as president of the Bannock Stake of Zion, which position he still holds. Bro. Mendenhall served four years as a representative in the Idaho State legislature, serving in the house during the 12th session and as senator during the 13th session. He has also acted as clerk of the Thatcher Rural High School No. 2, and served as school trustee a number of terms. His wife, Naomi Herd, was born Oct. 29, 1873, at Darwin, Lancashire, England, the daughter of James Herd and Grace Knowles; she has borne her husband nine children, namely, Vernon Herd, Lovell James, Wm. Thomas, Murat H., Millen H., Naomi H., Ruth H., Elease H., and Kay H.
