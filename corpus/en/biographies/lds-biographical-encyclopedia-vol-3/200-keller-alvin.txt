Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Keller, Alvin

Keller, Alvin, Bishop of the Tremonton Ward, (Bear River Stake), Box Elder county, Utah, was born July 1, 1874, at Mantua, Box Elder county, Utah, the son of Jens M. Keller and Margaret Larsen. He was baptized Sept. 3, 1882, by Peter C. Jensen; ordained a Deacon Nov. 6, 1886, by Peter Jensen; ordained a Teacher March 2, 1890 by Lars J. Halling; ordained a Priest Dec. 9, 1894, by Julius Keller; ordained an Elder Sept. 3, 1898, by Wm. L. Watkins, and ordained a Seventy Nov. 19, 1910, by J. Golden Kimball. He was set apart as superintendent of the Tremonton Sunday school August 16, 1908; was set part as presiding Elder of the Tremonton branch July 7, 1912; was chosen as Bishop of the Tremonton Ward Nov. 29, 1914, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop Dec. 13, 1914, by Pres. Joseph F. Smith. In 1898 (Sept. 21st) he married Annie J. Keller, who has borne him six children, namely, Jean Alvin and Justin Peter (twins), born June 2, 1899; Verne James, born July 11, 1901; Doyle Joseph, born Feb. 23, 1906; Lisle Hyrum, born March 22, 1909, and Marguerite, born June 25, 1912.
