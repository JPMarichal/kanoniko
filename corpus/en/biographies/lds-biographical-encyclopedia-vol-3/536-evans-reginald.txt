Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Evans, Reginald

Evans, Reginald, Bishop of the Kemmerer Ward (Woodruff Stake), Wyoming, was born March 13, 1879, at Lye, Worcestershire, England, the son of Emanuel Evans and Betsy Hatton. Becoming a convert to "Mormonism" he was baptized November 4, 1899, by Elder Daniel Clarke. He emigrated to America in 1901, crossing the Atlantic in the steamship "New England." Later he located at Kemmerer, Wyoming; he was ordained an Elder Oct. 29, 1906, by Archibald McKinnon, and filled a mission to Great Britain in 1907-1909, laboring in the Birmingham conference. About one year after his return from that mission (in November, 1910) he was chosen as presiding Elder of the Kemmerer branch which position he held until April 27, 1913, when he was ordained a High Priest and Bishop and set apart to preside over the Kemmerer Ward by Apostle Geo. F. Richards. In 1912 (June 5th) he married Elizabeth Parsons of Ogden, Utah, which marriage has been blessed with two children, namely, Milton Joseph (born April 11, 1913) and Elizabeth Emma (born June 25, 1915).
