Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Burnham, James

Burnham, James, one of the Elders of the Church who died while in the missionary field, was an American by birth and became a convert to "Mormonism" in the early days of the Church. In 1840-42 he filled a mission to Great Britain, laboring in England and Wales, and in 1843 he was called on a mission to the Eastern States, where he was exposed to the inclemency of the weather, which, coupled with excessive labor, brought on quick consumption, with which he died March 22, 1843, in Richmond, Mass. He left a wife and several children.
