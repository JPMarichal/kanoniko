Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Allred, James

Allred, James, a member of Zion's Camp and presiding Elder of the Allred settlement (now Spring City, Sanpete county, Utah), in 1852-53, was born Jan. 22, 1784, in North Carolina, the son of William Allred and Elizabeth Thresher. He became a member of the Church at an early day, being baptized Sept. 10, 1832, in Missouri, by Geo. M. Hinkle and was a member of Zion's Camp in 1834, and served as a body guard to the Prophet Joseph Smith. He passed through all of the persecutions of the Saints in Missouri and Illinois, and he was ordained a High Priest by the Prophet Joseph Smith. In 1851 he arrived in Utah and was one of the founders of Spring City in 1852. Bro. Allred, or "Father" Allred, as he was known by his associates, was always a faithful member of the Church. He raised a large family and died in Spring City, Sanpete county, June 10, 1876.
