Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Stoker, Regena Hogan

Stoker, Regena Hogan, wife of David Stoker, was born July 10, 1844, in Lee county, Iowa, the daughter of Erick G. M. Hogan and Holga Nesteby. She came to Utah with her parents in 1848, crossing the plains in Pres. Brigham Young's company. As a girl she passed through all the experiences connected with crickets, grasshoppers and Indians, and she became the wife of David Stoker March 3, 1866. She bore her husband eight children, of whom seven are still living. For a number of years Sister Stoker acted as a counselor in the Ward Y. L. M. I. A. and has also been a most diligent and successful Relief Society worker. She was also a teacher in the Sabbath school for about 25 years.
