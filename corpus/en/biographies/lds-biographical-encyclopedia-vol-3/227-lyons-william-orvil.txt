Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Lyons, William Orvil

Lyons, William Orvil, Bishop of Grassy Lake (Taylor Stake), Alberta, Canada, was born July 27, 1878, at Clarkston, Cache county, Utah, the son of Caleb W. Lyons and Sarah Minerva Rice. He was baptized August 6, 1885 by Thomas Godfrey at Clarkston, married Anna M. Smith in Fremont county, Idaho, June 15, 1902, and removed to Alberta, Canada the same year. From 1906 to 1910 he acted as Sunday school superintendent of the Taber Ward, Alberta, Canada. He then moved to Grassy Lake where he opened up a harness business April 22, 1910. He was ordained a Bishop by Orson F. Whitney, August 10, 1912, and set apart to preside over the Grassy Lake Ward which position he still holds. Bishop Lyon has ever taken part in public affairs and has since his early youth worked diligently for the benefit of his fellow citizens, having held several positions of responsibility in the interests of the communities where he has resided.
