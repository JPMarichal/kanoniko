Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Stewart, John Riley

Stewart, John Riley, Bishop of Torrey Ward (Wayne Stake), Wayne county, Utah, from 1901 to 1906, was born Aug. 27, 1873, in Beaver, Beaver county, Utah, the son of Urban Van Stewart and Ellen Adams. He was baptized May 21, 1882, at Adamsville; ordained a Deacon Nov. 30, 1884, by Joseph H. Joseph; ordained a Priest Aug. 30, 1897, by Bishop George Coleman at Teasdale, Wayne county; ordained an Elder Oct. 14, 1897, by Willis E. Robison at Loa; ordained a Seventy Oct. 19, 1897, by Apostle John Henry Smith; received his blessings in the Salt Lake Temple Nov. 20, 1897, and filled a mission to the Northern States in 1897-1900, laboring principally in Michigan. After laboring as presiding Elder and assistant Sunday school superintendent at Grover, he was ordained a High Priest and Bishop April 5, 1901, by Francis M. Lyman and set apart to preside over the Torrey Ward, which position he filled until May, 1906. In 1903 (Aug. 14th) he married Ellen Amelia Covington (daughter of John Thomas Covington and Elizabeth Adams), who was born Dec. 10, 1884, in Orderville, Utah. The children by this marriage are the following: Ellen Elizabeth, born April 26, 1904; John Riley jun., born Oct. 25, 1905; Golda, born Dec. 21, 1907; Cecil Van, born March 7, 1910; Gerold William, born March 21, 1911, and Junius Kent, born July 17, 1913. His wife died July 17, 1916, in childbed. Bro. Stewart acted as second counselor to President Gearson S. Bastion from 1906 to 1910 and then as first counselor to Joseph Eckersley from 1910 to 1915. He is a farmer and stock raiser by avocation.
