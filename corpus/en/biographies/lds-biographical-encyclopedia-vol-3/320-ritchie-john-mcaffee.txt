Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Ritchie, John McAffee

Ritchie, John McAffee (Continued from Vol. 2, page 11), John M. Ritchie acted as counselor to Bishop N. C. Murdock of the Charleston Ward from 1898 to 1901, and from 1901 to 1904 he acted as a counselor to Bishop Wm. Daybell. He was elected county commissioner in November, 1914. In connection with his farming Bro. Ritchie is a specialist in raising pure-bred horses and Hereford cattle, and is meeting with great success in improving the standard of his animals. Bishop Ritchie is the father of nine children, namely, Mabel, Joseph W., Lacey, Vera J., Elden L., P. Clyde, Horace J., Nerdell W. and Relva.
