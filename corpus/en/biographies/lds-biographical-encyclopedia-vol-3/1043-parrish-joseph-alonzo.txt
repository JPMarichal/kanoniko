Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Parrish, Joseph Alonzo

Parrish, Joseph Alonzo, Bishop of Peterson (Morgan Stake), Morgan county, Utah, was born Aug. 20, 1873, at Centerville, Davis county, Utah, the son of Joel Parrish and Emma Ford. He was baptized in January, 1881, by Thomas Howells, and ordained successively to the offices of Deacon, Priest and Elder. He was ordained a Seventy Sept. 27, 1908, by Charles Tucker, and ordained a High Priest and Bishop March 6, 1909, by Hyrum M. Smith and set apart to preside over the Peterson Ward, which position he held until March 10, 1912, when he was set apart to preside over the Mountain Green Ward, which position he still holds. In 1902-1903 Bro. Parrish filled a mission to the Southern States. At home he has held several civil offices; thus he acted four years as a commissioner in Morgan county. His main occupation is that of a farmer and stock raiser. In 1906 (Feb. 21st) he married Ida C. Cook (daughter of David S. Cook), who bore him eight children, namely, Ruth, David, Wallace, Elizabeth, Gordon, Mary, Joseph, and Josephine.
