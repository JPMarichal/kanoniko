Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Chamerlain, Henry

Chamerlain, Henry, Bishop of Orderville (Kanab Stake), Kane county, Utah, was born July 6, 1879, at Orderville, the son of Thomas Chamberlain and Ann Carling. He was baptized and confirmed by his father August 21, 1887; ordained a Deacon by H. W. Esplin Feb. 8, 1892; ordained an Elder by Isaiah Bowers Sept. 26, 1897, and ordained a High Priest by Hyrum M. Smith Sept. 6, 1910, and set apart as an alternate member of the High Council; ordained a Bishop by Geo. F. Richards Nov. 30, 1910, and set apart to preside over the Orderville Ward. While yet quite young he presided over a quorum of Deacons, labored as a home missionary and acted as choir leader. In 1901-1903 he filled a mission to the Northern States with headquarters in Chicago. While laboring in Chicago he acted as superintendent of the branch Sunday school and also as chorister of the branch. In 1899 (Oct. 11) he married Mary Esplin (daughter of Henry W. Esplin and Philems Cox), born Dec. 13, 1880, at Orderville. Bishop Chamberlain is a farmer by occupation and a much respected citizen.
