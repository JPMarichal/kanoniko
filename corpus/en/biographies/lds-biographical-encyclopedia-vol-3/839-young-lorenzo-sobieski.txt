Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Young, Lorenzo Sobieski

Young, Lorenzo Sobieski, the only survivor of the orginal company of Utah pioneers of 1847, was born March 9, 1841, in Winchester, Scott county, Ill., the son of Lorenzo Dow Young and Persis Goodall. He arrived in Salt Lake Valley with his parents in July, 1847. In 1872 he married Sarah A. Black, who bore him 12 children. He now lives at Shelley, Idaho.
