Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Porter, Aaron Benjamin

Porter, Aaron Benjamin, Bishop of the Centerville Ward, Davis county, Utah, was born April 27, 1851, at Centerville the son of Nathan T. Porter and Rebecca Ann Cherry. He was baptized May 27, 1860, by Chas. C. Rich when about nine years old; was ordained an Elder at the age of eighteen years, and was called on a mission to Arizona in 1876, where he helped to locate the settlement of Sunset on the Little Colorado river. He returned to Utah the same year. After his return he was ordained a High Priest and set apart to preside over the Centerville Ward. Prior to that date he had acted as president of the Ward Y. M. M. I. A. In a civil capacity Bishop Porter has served as selectman of Davis county two terms, acted as school trustee, road supervisor, etc. Highly respected and beloved by all who knew him, Bishop Porter died Nov. 15, 1910, at Rexburg, Idaho, remaining true and steadfast in the faith till the last.
