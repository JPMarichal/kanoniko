Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Williams, Ada Evans

Williams, Ada Evans, wife of Ebenezer A. Williams, was born May 1, 1838, at Witchurch, Glamorganshire, Wales, the daughter of William Evans and Elizabeth Bowering. She was baptized in December, 1849, by Sidney Bailey, and emigrated to Utah in 1853, in the same company as Elder Williams, whose wife she became on Jan. 19, 1856. She bore her husband ten children, five boys and five girls, and has always been a faithful Relief Society worker. She Was made a teacher in the Kaysville Relief Society when that was first organized in 1871 and later she served as a counselor in said Society for about fifteen years. She was the first president of the Y. L. M. I. A. of Kaysville, and for a few years acted also as president of the Suffrage Association of Kaysville. She has been president of the Columbian Club and vice president of the Suffrage Associations in Davis county. In 1893 she took a Sunday school course in the B. Y. University at Provo, and for many years was a very active worker in the Sunday schools of the Kaysville Ward.
