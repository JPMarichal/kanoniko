Anderson, Pernilla Johnson

Anderson, Pernilla Johnson, wife of Anton Edward Anderson, was born Sept. 16, 1850, at Annelov, SkÃ¥ne, Sweden, the daughter of Eskild Johnson, and Hanna Larson. She was baptized when eight years of age by Elder M. Rosenberg, emigrated with her parents to Utah in 1863, crossing the Atlantic in the ship "Electric," which sailed from Hamburg, April 18, 1863, and arrived at New York June 5, 1863. After crossing the plains in Capt. John F. Sanders' company (which arrived in Salt Lake City Sept. 5, 1363) she settled at Provo, but moved in 1865 to Santaquin, where she became acquainted with Bro. Anton Edward Anderson, to whom she was married March 1, 1875. For many years Sister Anderson has been a most faithful and devoted Relief Society worker. She is the mother of eight children.
