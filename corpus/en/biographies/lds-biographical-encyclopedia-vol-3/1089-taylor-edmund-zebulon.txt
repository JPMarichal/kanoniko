Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Taylor, Edmund Zebulon

Taylor, Edmund Zebulon, an Elder who died in the missionary field, was born March 14, 1859, in Slaterville, Weber county, Utah, the son of Wm. Warren Taylor ad Julia Aner Carbine. Later he moved with his parents to New Harmony, Washington county, and became a member of the 9th quorum of Seventy. In the fall of 1882 he married a daughter of Bishop Pleasant G. Taylor of Harrisville, by whom he had three children, two boys and a girl. He was set apart for a mission to the Southern States by Apostle John Henry Smith, Feb. 14, 1887, and was president of the North Carolina conference at the time of his death, which was caused by chills and fever, Aug. 13, 1888. Elder Taylor bore an excellent character. He was a reliable and moral young man and made a good missionary. The body was taken to Ogden, Utah, for burial.
