Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Archibald, Andrew Leavitt

Archibald, Andrew Leavitt, one of the Elders of the Church who died while filling a foreign mission, was born April 27, 1897, at Cardston, Alberta, Canada, the son of Andrew R. Archibald and Martha E. Leavitt. He was baptized when eight years old, ordained to the Priesthood when quite young, and resided in the Cardston First Ward, when he was called on a mission to Samoa in 1914. He was set apart for this mission Nov. 18, 1914, and the next day started for his field of labor. On his arrival in Samoa, he took hold of the work of the ministry with diligence and zeal, and labored faithfully until he was stricken with typhoid fever, with which he died March 21, 1917.
