Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Harvey, Richard Coope

Harvey, Richard Coope, second counselor to Wm. Blake of the Center Ward, was born Oct. 5, 1865, at Center Creek, Wasatch county, Utah, the son of John Harvey and Ann Coope. He was baptized when about eight years of age and subsequently ordained to the Priesthood, filled a mission to the Southern States in 1890-1892, laboring principally in Virginia, where he acted as conference president one and a half years. At home he acted for several years as superintendent of the Ward Sunday school and president of the Ward Y. M. M. I. A. He also served as constable of the Center precinct one term. In 1894 ( (April 28th) he was ordained a High Priest and set apart as second counselor to Bishop Wm. Blake, which position he held until 1900, when he moved to Alberta, Canada, locating near Raymond.
