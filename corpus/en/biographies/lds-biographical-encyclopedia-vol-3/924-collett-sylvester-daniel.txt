Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Collett, Sylvester Daniel

Collett, Sylvester Daniel, one of the Elders of the Church who died while filling a foreign mission, was born Dec. 15, 1866, in Smithfield, Cache county, Utah, the son of Reuben Collett and Elthara Rosetta Merrill. He was baptized Aug. 2, 1878, by Andrew P. Scow. In 1887 he was called on a mission to the Republic of Mexico, being ordained a Seventy and set apart under the hands of Apostle Franklin D. Richards, April 1, 1887. He died from an attack of typhoid fever, while in the discharge of his duties in Mexico City May 5, 1889, only twenty-two years old.
