Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Burt, Albert Cleon

Burt, Albert Cleon, an Elder who died while filling a foreign mission, was born Sept. 17, 1890, at Brigham City, Box Elder county, Utah, the son of Albert Burr and Lenora Nelson. He was baptized Sept. 17, 1898, by J. P. C. Johnson, and later ordained to the Priesthood. While residing at Bear River City, Box Elder county, Utah, he was called on a mission to New Zealand. He was set apart for this mission Nov. 21, 1911, and started immediately for his appointed field of labor, where he was engaged faithfully in missionary work until he was attacked with sickness (typhoid fever), from which he died at Thames, near Auckland, Jan. 8, 1913. His remains were shipped home by way of Vancouver, B.C., in care of Elder Ephraim E. Wright (Elder Burt's missionary companion). Impressive funeral services were held at Brigham City Feb. 10, 1913.
