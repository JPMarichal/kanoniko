Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Condie, Thomas A.

Condie, Thomas A., counselor in the bishopric of the Croyden Ward, Morgan county, Utah, was born April 19, 1865, at Croyden, Utah, the son of Thos. Condie and Hannah Swann. He was baptized June 28, 1874, at Croyden, by his father; ordained a Deacon Jan. 19, 1878; ordained a Teacher Feb. 5, 1883, by Ephraim Swann; ordained a Priest Feb. 24, 1884, by Chas. Turner; ordained a Seventy Oct. 19, 1884, by Seymour B. Young; ordained a High Priest Feb. 14, 1897, and set apart as first counselor to Bishop Geo. R. Thackeray of the Croyden Ward by Francis M. Lyman. He married Alice Rebecca London in the Salt Lake Temple Dec. 8, 1897. She is the daughter of John London and bore her husband five children, namely, Olive, born May 2, 1899; Alice Hannah, born May 8, 1905; Lula, born Dec. 15, 1907; Edna, born August 4, 1909, and Thomas Anson, born Feb. 7, 1913. Bro. Condie's occupation is that of a farmer. He graduated in the normal course of the Brigham Young College at Logan in May, 1892, and the following year had charge of the L. D. S. Seminary at Franklin, Idaho. Owing to conflicting water claims at Lost creek he took a leading part in organizing the Croyden Irrigation Company and was later instrumental in the adjudication of the water rights of Lost creek. He served as county attorney of Morgan county in 1901-1902, and when the Morgan county schools were consolidated Nov. 10, 1908, he was appointed representative for the Croyden precinct and served as vice president of the district for two years.
