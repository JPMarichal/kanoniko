He was baptized in Denmark May 18, 1857, by Christen L. Hansen; was ordained to the Priesthood and labored as a missionary in Denmark in 1857-1859. In 1861 he married Sophia Nielsen, with whom he emigrated to Utah in 1862. He located in Santaquin, Utah county, Utah. In 1830-1882 he filled a mission to Scandinavia, laboring in his native land, Sweden. After his return home, he was ordained a High Priest. His wife died March 17, 1915. She bore him a daughter who was born in 1862 and died in 1868. Bro. Samuelson died in Santaquin in June, 1919, as a faithful member of the Church.
