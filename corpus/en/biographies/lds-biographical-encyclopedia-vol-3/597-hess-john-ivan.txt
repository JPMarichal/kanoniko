Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Hess, John Ivan

Hess, John Ivan, second counselor to Bishop James H. Robinson, of Farmington, Davis county, Utah, was born May 25, 1891, at Plymouth, Box Elder county, Utah, the son of John Fred Hess and Eleanor Udy. He was baptized by Jonathan D. Wood when eight years of age; was ordained a Deacon, Priest and Elder successively; was ordained a High Priest April 20, 1915, by Francis M. Lyman and set apart as second counselor to Bishop James H. Robinson. In 1911-1913 he filled a mission to the Central States. In 1914 (Jan. 28th) he married Elda Maud Edmonds (daughter of Edward Edmonds and Ellen M. Woolley), which marriage has been blessed with two children.
