Andrew Jenson, LDS Biographical Encyclopedia, vol. 3 ( 1920)

Moss, Melvina Rushton

Moss, Melvina Rushton, wife of Daniel Moss, was born July 31, 1853, at St. Louis, Mo., the daughter of Fredric James Rushton and Eliza Hundy. She migrated to Utah with her parents in 1855, crossing the plains in Richard Ballantyne's company, which arrived in Salt Lake Sept. 25, 1855. She first lived in Salt Lake City, and was baptized Nov. 22, 1863, by Daniel Wood, after moving to Bountiful or Woods Cross, where she has lived ever since and where she has taken an active part in the Relief Society and Church work generally. During the past seven years she has presided over the South Bountiful Relief Society. Previous to that she acted as a counselor to Sister Mary Ann Page in said Society.
