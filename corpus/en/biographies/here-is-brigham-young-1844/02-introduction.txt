S. Dilworth Young, Here Is Brigham . . . Brigham Young: The Years to 1844 ( 1964)

INTRODUCTION

As time goes on, more and more material comes to light on the little known early life of Brigham Young. This biography makes no attempt to trace exhaustively all of the threads of his activity. More nearly, it attempts to place him in the setting of his everyday life. There will come others who will analyze and weigh and measure. It is quite evident that after 1832 Brigham Young was moved by one motive: Determination to obey the will of God as spoken thru the Prophet, and to support that Prophet with all that he possessed of time, talent, and means. It is needless to point out that the intimate detail of family living is imaginary. This I have tried to convey in the text.

Many people have given me material, referred me to sources, or related family incidents. It would be impossible to name them all. Special help has come from the following: Mrs. Cecil K. Miller, Mrs. Melinda Ellsworth, Mrs. Genevieve R. Curtis, Elders Earl E. Olson and Lauritz G. Petersen of the Church Historian's Office, Irwin Clawson, Jay Heslop, Mrs. Helen Spencer Williams, and Mrs. Hortence Young Hammond. To them I am grateful.

August 10, 1964
