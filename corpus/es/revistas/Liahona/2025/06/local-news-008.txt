Before and after the mission, Institute classes have truly been a blessing to my life. Keeping closer to the Savior is harder these days, but coming to Institute has brought me hope, joy, and comfort while facing the challenges of this world! Having a safe zone filled with respect and love for everyone’s journey in this gospel is welcomed!

Institute classes have blessed me in my daily life to truly remember I am His daughter, and I have so many opportunities to be forgiven and become more Christlike. I invite you to join your local Institute; this can change your life drastically and make your life so much happier with fulfilling and understanding your purpose in this temporary place we call home.

I know the Lord’s plan is as perfect as His Gospel! I am happy to be a member of His Church, and I am forever grateful for our living prophet in our dispensation. The Book of Mormon is indeed another testament of Jesus Christ, and I pray you may find yourself through Jesus Christ.
