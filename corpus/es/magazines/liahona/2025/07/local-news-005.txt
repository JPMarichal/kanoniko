I was invited to the FSY that took place in Chimaltenango, Guatemala, in December of 2024. At first, I thought the location was too far away from my home and that I would need to spend one week of my Christmas vacation. Later I made up my mind and decided I would go.

As we arrived at the campgrounds, I sensed the presence of the Spirit, and those traveling with me felt it too. As the days passed, I witnessed miracles and the presence of God’s Spirit in every activity. I felt fortunate. The Sabbath day was one of my favorite parts; I could feel the spirit and the love of God. As I listened, I learned and gained experience as a youth. I honestly did not want to leave. FSY experience has strengthened my testimony. It has helped me to grow as a member of the church and be more active.
