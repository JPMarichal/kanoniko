Bruce R. McConkie, Doctrinal New Testament Commentary, vol. 1 ( 1965)

Woman Anoints Jesus' Feet in Simon's House

Luke 7 3636. Sat down to meat] Reclined on a couch with his feet extending outward, as was the custom of the day.

Luke 7 3737. A woman] Not Mary Magdalene and not Mary of Bethany (Matt. 26:6-13; John 12:2-8), both of whom were righteous women of good character. A sinner] Presumably an unvirtuous woman.

Luke 7 3838. Banquets were held in open type houses; spectators or visitors often came in and viewed the proceedings.

Luke 7 4646. Oil was cheap, ointment expensive.

Luke 7 4747. In effect Jesus is saying: 'Her sins were many, but she believed in me, has repented of her sins, was baptized by my disciples, and her sins were washed away in the waters of baptism. Now she has sought me out to exhibit the unbounded gratitude of one who was filthy, but is now clean. Her gratitude knows no bounds and her love is beyond measure, for she was forgiven of much. Had she been forgiven of but few sins, she would not have loved me so intensely.'

Luke 7 4848. Jesus reaffirms the forgiveness previously gained through repentance and baptism. He is not forgiving sins contrary to the law which he himself has ordained, which law is that men must believe the gospel, repent, and be baptized for the remission of sins.

Luke 7 5050. 'Thy faith hath saved thee from thy sins so that thou art now clean and pure in the Father's sight. By enduring in righteousness to the end thou shalt have eternal salvation in the Father's kingdom.'
