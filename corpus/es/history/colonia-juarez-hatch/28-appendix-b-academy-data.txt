IV OFFICIAL SONG AND YELL

Words for the official school song were written by Guy C. Wilson shortly after the turn of the century, and set to music by Arthur J. Done. The song has ever been the means of voicing loyalty to the institution, and an influence in keeping its ideals uppermost in the minds of students and all others associated with the school. The singing of "Alma Mater" opens every school program, stirs the congregation at every commencement exercise, and reviews love for the Academy at every alumni gathering.

Our Alma Mater, hail to thee, thy praise we sing!

Thy sons and daughters here their homage pay,

We'll make thy glory known

Through every land and zone

By deeds of worth, by acts of valor done,

And naught but fame thy name shall know.

CHORUS

Then cheer today for the J.S.A.

We've come to work, we mean to stay.

We'll raise thy standard, win the day.

Hip, hip, hurray! for the J.S.A.

With hearts and hands we'll join to raise thy standard high.

Thy colors fair shall e'er unsullied be,

We'll ever pray for thee

Our hearts are brave and free,

Thy precepts rare shall e'er remembered be

And naught but praise thy name shall know.

The official school yell—often given immediately following the singing of the school song.

Bif, Baf, Bean! White and Green!

Bravest colors ever seen!

Bif, Baf, Bay! J.S.A.

Stands for character!
