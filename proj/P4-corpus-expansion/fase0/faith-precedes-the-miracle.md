# Fase 0 --- Faith Precedes the Miracle (Spencer W. Kimball)

> Fecha: 2026-04-23.

## Que es

1972 Deseret Book. Classic Kimball treatise on the primacy of faith before divine intervention. His most-cited book. Written as Q12 (called Oct 1943), before his Oct 1973 call to Acting President and Dec 1973 call as Church President.
