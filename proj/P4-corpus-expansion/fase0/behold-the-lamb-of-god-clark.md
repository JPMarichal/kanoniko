# Fase 0 --- Behold the Lamb of God (J. Reuben Clark, Jr.)

> Fecha: 2026-04-23.

## Que es

1962 Deseret Book. J. Reuben Clark Jr.'s magnum opus on the Atonement. Clark was First Counselor to Heber J. Grant, George Albert Smith, and David O. McKay (1933-1961). Written in his final years (he died 1961, published posthumously 1962).
